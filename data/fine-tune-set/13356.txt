Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>African Style Chicken Peanut Soup With Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Sweet potato
• Russet potato
• Onion
• Garlic
• Red bell pepper
• Jalapeno peppers
• Cumin
• Vegetable oil
• Butter
• Cooked chicken
• Salsa
• Chicken broth
• Black beans
• Peanut butter
• Salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. Heat oil and butter in a large dutch oven over medium-high heat
2. Add in both potatoes onion, garlic, red bell pepper, jalapeno pepper and cumin
3. Saute for about 5-6 minutes
4. Add in chicken, salsa, cumin, chicken broth and black beans
5. Simmer for about 25-30 minutes
6. Add in peanut butter, season with salt and pepper to taste cook stirring for 3 minutes<|endoftext|>
