Please give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Easy Fancy Rack Of Lamb

Ingredients:
• Rack of lamb
• Pepper
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Dijon mustard
• Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
• Garlic
• Black pepper
• Fresh rosemary
• Fresh thyme

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. In a small bowl, combine: breadcrumbs, rosemary, thyme, garlic, 1 teaspoon salt, 1 / 4 teaspoon pepper and 2 tablespoons of olive oil
3. Set aside
4. Season rack of lamb with salt and pepper
5. Use an oven proof skillet to heat 2 tablespoons olive oil over high heat
6. Sear rack of lamb for two minutes per side
7. Set aside to cool slightly
8. Cover bones with foil- to prevent discoloration during roasting
9. Brush dijon over rack of lamb and evenly coat with breadcrumb mixture
10. Place lamb, bone side down back into pan and roast in oven for approximately 12-18 minutes - use a meat thermometer placed into the center of the meat to check doneness
11. Remove meat from oven and let rest for 5-10 minutes, covered before cutting rack between ribs<|endoftext|>
