Ingredients:
• Instant rice
• Water
• Onion
• Peas and carrots
• Soy sauce
• Egg
• Oil

Steps:
1. In a small pot, bring the water to a boil and then stir in rice
2. Cover with a tight-fitting lid and set aside
3. In a large skillet, place 2 tablespoons of oil to get hot over med-high heat
4. Scramble egg, stir-frying the heck outta that sucker to make sure it's good and broken up
5. Put it into your serving bowl for holding
6. Put the other 2 tablespoons of oil in the skillet and fry the onion until almost transparent
7. Add the peas and carrots, stir-frying those to thaw
8. Heat thoroughly
9. By this time, the rice should be ready
10. Pour it into the skillet and add the egg
11. Pour in the soy sauce as well
12. With a quick hand, toss ingredients over med-high heat and stir quickly
13. "fry" the rice until all the soy sauce is distributed and the color and strength is to your liking
14. Dish this stuff up and serve it with your favorite other chinese dishes or just do what i do and make a meal outta this stuff!<|endoftext|>
