Ingredients:
• Apples
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter
• Bisquick baking mix

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Lightly spray an 8 x 8-inch baking dish with non stick spray
3. Peel and core the apples, then slice them thinly
4. Heat up a skillet over medium heat and put butter into the skillet
5. Once it's melted, throw in the apple slices
6. Once the apples are softened slightly, add tablespoon ground cinnamon and cup sugar
7. Stir until the sugar is melted
8. Turn off the burner
9. In a medium bowl, mix the 2 cups of bisquick mix with 1 cups of water until it has a slightly thick consistency
10. Add the apple mixture into the bowl of bisquick mix and stir together until blended
11. If it is too soupy, then add more mix at your discretion
12. Pour the mixture into the baking dish and put it in the oven
13. Set the timer for 35 minutes
14. Once it's done, allow to cool slightly before serving with a side of applesauce<|endoftext|>
