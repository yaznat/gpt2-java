What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Amazing Buttermilk Garlic Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Idaho potatoes
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Butter
• Buttermilk

Steps:
1. Add potatoes and garlic to a pot and cover with water
2. Add salt and bring to a boil
3. Reduce heat and simmer until fork tender
4. Drain and add back to the pot
5. Mash with the remaining ingredients
6. Add the buttermilk gradually in case you need less<|endoftext|>
