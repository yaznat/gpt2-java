Ingredients:
• Short-grain rice
• Cornstarch
• Milk
• Sugar
• Rose water
• Almonds
• Pistachio nut
• Ground cinnamon
• Syrup

Steps:
1. Blend the ground rice and the cornstarch together into a smooth paste, adding a little of the cold milk
2. Bring the remaining milk to a boil in a saucepan
3. Add the sugar, lower heat enough to maintain a gentle simmer
4. Gradually add the ground rice mixture to the milk, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon
5. Simmer on very low heat for 10-15 minutes or until the mixture has thickened, stirring often and being very careful not to allow it to burn- especially on the bottom of the pan
6. Add the rosewater and half of the ground almonds, simmer 5 minutes
7. Let cool a few minutes and then pour into a serving bowl or 4 serving dishes
8. Sprinkle remaining ground almonds and all of the ground pistachios on top and dust with some ground cinnamon
9. Serve with syrup or honey on the side
10. Grind the raw rice in a blender or food processor<|endoftext|>
