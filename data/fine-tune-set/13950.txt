Ingredients:
• Dried currant
• Tawny port
• Breadcrumbs
• Of fresh mint
• Flat leaf parsley
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Pine nuts
• Eggs
• Boneless leg of lamb

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, soak the currants in enough port to cover for at least 2 hours and up to overnight
2. When ready to roast the lamb, drain the currants
3. Discard the port
4. Position a rack in the lower middle of the oven and heat the oven to 400f
5. In a large bowl, mic the bread crumbs, mint, parsley, olive oil, pine nuts, and currants
6. Season to taste with salt and pepper, add the beaten eggs and mix well
7. Until the lamb and unroll it
8. Lay the meat flat and pat dry with paper towels
9. Trim off any excess fat and, if necessary, butterfly portions of the lamb to make it roughly rectangular and evenly thick
10. Sprinkle the inside of the lamb with salt and pepper and pat the stuffing evenly over the meat
11. Roll the lamb tightly, from one short end to the other, and tie the roast snugly at 1 inch intervals with kitchen twine
12. Sprinkle the outside of the lamb with more salt and pepper and then set it, seam side up on a rack in a small roasting pan
13. Gather any stuffing that has escaped and poke it back in at the ends of the lamb roll
14. Roast until an instant read thermometer inserted into a thick part of the roast reads 125f to 130f for medium rare
15. Let rest for 15 minutes, then carve into medium thin slices<|endoftext|>
