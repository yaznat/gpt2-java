Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Veggie Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Mushroom
• Green pepper
• Green onions
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat frying pan, spray with nonstick spray or coat the bottom of the pan with oil, or butter
2. Beat together eggs, mushrooms, green peppers and green onions, mix together
3. When pan is hot enough add egg mixture and cook until almost set, until you can flip it over with out it falling apart, cook on the other side until set
4. Top 1 / 2 of the omelette with cheese and fold over
5. Cook until cheese is melted
6. If you are really hungery then add a little water and cover, this will make the cheese melt faster
7. Serve with toast, hashbrowns, or even rice
8. Can use any color peppers, or even use regular onions too
9. Change the cheese if you want<|endoftext|>
