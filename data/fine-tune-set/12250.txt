Ingredients:
• Hot water
• Dry onion soup mix
• Margarine
• Black pepper
• Potatoes
• Parsley flakes

Steps:
1. Mix water, soup mix, margarine, and pepper
2. Stir well
3. Place potatoes in a greased 2 quart casserole dish
4. Pour water mixture over potatoes
5. Covering potatoes well
6. Bake at 350, covered, for about 2hours, or until potatoes are tender
7. Stir occasionally
8. Uncover and sprinkle parsely over potatoes for last 30 minutes of baking to brown potatoes
9. It may not take the full 2 hours to cook<|endoftext|>
