What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Crock Pot Spicy Lamb Shanks

Ingredients:
• Oranges
• Beef broth
• Ground cardamom
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Ground turmeric
• Pepper
• Carrots
• Boiling onions
• Garlic cloves
• Lamb shanks
• Cinnamon sticks
• Water
• Cornstarch
• Kalamata olives
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Using a vegetable peeler, remove the orange part of the peel from 1 of the oranges
2. Cut the peel into thin strips
3. Set aside
4. Squeeze the juice from both oranges to make about 2 / 3 cup
5. In a small bowl stir together orange juice, broth, cardamom, cumin, salt, turmeric, and pepper
6. Set aside
7. Place carrots, onions, and garlic in the bottom of a 5- to 6-quart crockery cooker
8. Top with lamb shanks, strips of orange peel, and cinnamon sticks
9. Pour orange juice mixture over all
10. Cover and cook on low-heat setting for 8 to 9 hours or until lamb pulls easily from the bone
11. When ready to serve, transfer the lamb shanks and vegetables to a serving dish using a slotted spoon
12. Skim the fat from the cooking liquid
13. Remove and discard the cinnamon sticks
14. Measure 1 1 / 2 cups juices
15. Transfer to a small saucepan
16. Combine water and cornstarch
17. Add to saucepan
18. Cook and stir over medium heat until thickened and bubbly
19. Cook and stir 2 minutes more
20. Spoon sauce over lamb and vegetables
21. Sprinkle with olives and cilantro
22. Makes 4 to 6 servings<|endoftext|>
