Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Apple
• Cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Pepper
• Salt
• Lemon juice
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Wash and prick sweet potatoes
2. Microwave on high for about 15 minutes
3. More if needed, they need to be cooked through
4. Let cool sweet potatoes cool
5. Preheat oven to 350f
6. Cut a thin slice, about 1 / 2 inch from the top of each potato
7. Carefully scoop out meat, being sure not to puncture or tear potato skin
8. Combine potato meat, butter, brown sugar, apple, cinnamon, ginger, pepper, salt and lemon juice, mix well
9. Carefully spoon mixture into shells, top each potato with 1 / 2 of the pecans
10. Place potatoes in baking dish
11. Bake for about 25 minutes
12. Stuffing must be hot and tender
13. Top with remaining pecans<|endoftext|>
