Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Asian Ground Beef Casserole

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cream of chicken soup
• Bean sprouts
• Water
• Worcestershire sauce
• Instant rice
• Chow mein noodles
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Brown groundbeef and onion and drain
2. Mix soups and rest of ingrediants together except chow mein noodles
3. Place in a buttered or sprayed casserole dish
4. Bake for 30 minutes at 350 degrees with lid or covered
5. Remove cover and top with chow mein noodles and bake another 10 to 20 mins longer
6. Enjoy! i like to sprinkle soy sauce over it and serve it with a nice green salad<|endoftext|>
