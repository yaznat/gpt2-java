Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Peking House Special Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Cooked beef
• Cooked chicken
• Cooked shrimp
• Eggs
• Onion
• Bean sprouts
• Carrot
• Peas
• Green onion
• Garlic
• Salt
• Sugar
• Light soy sauce
• Dark soy sauce
• White pepper
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Heat oiled or sprayed wok or large pan over medium-high heat
2. Add vegetables and garlic and cook, stirring continuously, until they are hot, but still crisp
3. Add in rice, eggs, meat, and soy sauce
4. Continue to stir and cook until hot
5. Stir in sugar, salt, and pepper
6. Serve<|endoftext|>
