Give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Amazing Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onions
• Garlic
• Parsley
• Parmesan cheese
• Butter
• Cooking spray
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Put all your ingredients- aside from ground beef- in a big bowl and stir everything together
2. Melt the butter before adding it
3. Break the ground beef up into little pieces and add to the bowl and mix everything together thoroughly
4. I make the mix into as dense a log as possible and then cut it into 6 pieces to ensure uniform size in the patties
5. Form your patties
6. Spray each patty lightly with cooking spray and then season with salt and pepper
7. Use your hands to gently pat the salt and pepper into each patty
8. Fire up the grill and grab a cold one<|endoftext|>
