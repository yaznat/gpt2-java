Can you give me a popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Watermelon Orange Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Watermelon
• Orange juice
• Water
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Stir sugar into the orange juice until sugar is dissolved
2. Put everything in a blender and blend until well mixed
3. Pour into popsicle moulds and freeze until firm<|endoftext|>
