Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Sugar
• Black pepper
• Garlic
• Green onions
• Toasted sesame seeds
• Beef sirloin tip

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, black pepper, garlic, green onions and sesame seeds
2. Slice beef thinly into x 2 inch pieces
3. Add meat to soy sauce mixture and mix well
4. Cover and refrigerate 1-2 hours
5. Preheat oven to broil
6. Broil on broiler rack or grill meat for 2-3 minutes per side or until brown
7. Serve with vegetable side dishes and rice<|endoftext|>
