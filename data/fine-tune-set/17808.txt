What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon And Cilantro Breakfast Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• White sugar
• Baking powder
• Egg
• Milk
• Canola oil
• Cilantro
• Cooked bacon
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Line 12 muffin cups in a muffin tin or spray muffin tin with a thin layer of non-stick cooking spray
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine all-purpose flour, whole wheat flour, white sugar and baking powder
4. Whisk egg
5. Combine egg, milk and canola oil
6. Add egg mixture to flour mixture
7. Combine until lumps disappear
8. Do not over-mix
9. Let set for 10 minutes
10. Add cilantro, bacon and cheddar cheese to the batter
11. Carefully combine well
12. Bake for 20 minutes, or until a cake tester comes out clean<|endoftext|>
