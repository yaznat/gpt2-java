Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Beef With Snow Peas

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Rice wine
• Brown sugar
• Cornstarch
• Vegetable oil
• Fresh gingerroot
• Garlic
• Beef round steak
• Fresh snow peas

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine the soy sauce, rice wine, brown sugar and cornstarch
2. Set aside
3. Heat oil in a wok or skillet over medium high heat
4. Stir-fry ginger and garlic for 30 seconds
5. Add the steak and stir-fry for 2 minutes or until evenly browned
6. Add the snow peas and stir-fry for an additional 3 minutes
7. Add the soy sauce mixture, bring to a boil, stirring constantly
8. Lower heat and simmer until the sauce is thick and smooth
9. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
