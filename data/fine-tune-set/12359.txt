Please give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>After Thanksgiving Turkey Soup

Ingredients:
• Turkey carcass
• Onions
• Carrots
• Celery ribs
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Half-and-half cream
• Long grain rice
• Salt
• Chicken bouillon granule
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place turkey carcass in a soup kettle or dutch oven and cover with water
2. Bring to a boil
3. Reduce heat
4. Cover and simmer for 1 hour
5. Remove carcass
6. Cool
7. Set aside 3 quart broth
8. Remove turkey from bones and cut into bite-size pieces
9. Set aside
10. In a soup kettle or dutch oven, saute the onions, carrots and celery in butter until tender
11. Reduce heat
12. Stir in flour until blended
13. Gradually add 1 quart of reserved broth
14. Bring to a boil
15. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened
16. Add cream, rice, salt, bouillon, pepper, remaining broth and reserved turkey
17. Reduce heat
18. Cover and simmer for 30-35 minutes or until rice is tender<|endoftext|>
