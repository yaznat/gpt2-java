Please give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Apple Cheese Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Apple
• Flour tortillas
• Fat-free american cheese
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Peel and dice apple
2. Spray frying pan with cooking spray and saute apple until lightly browned remove apple from pan and re-spray
3. Place in pan 1 flour tortilla and scoop 1 / 2 apple, 1 / 2 splenda, 1 / 2 cinnamon, and 1 cheese slice in center
4. Cover pan and cook on medium heat until underside is golden and cheese melts
5. Fold tortilla in half and remove to serving plate
6. Repeat process for second tortilla
7. Serve warm with fat free whipped topping
8. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
