What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Muffins Mix Or Just Make The Muffins Yourself

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Raisins
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Apple juice
• Oil
• Applesauce
• Margarine

Steps:
1. In a bowl, mix together first 5 ingredients
2. Place in an air tight container
3. Package raisins and nuts in a separate bag
4. Mix topping ingredients in a bowl
5. Package in a small plastic bag
6. Wrap nicely for a gift, and attach the following
7. To prepare, preheat oven to 350
8. Mix together apple cinnamon muffin mix, raisins and / or nuts, 1 cup apple juice, 2 tbl oil, 1 / 2 cup applesauce
9. Stir until just combined
10. Spoon into lightly greased muffin tins and bake for 20-25 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
11. While still warm, dip in melted margarine and then topping<|endoftext|>
