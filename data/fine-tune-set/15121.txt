Can you give me a pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus Pizza

Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• Asparagus
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Fontina cheese
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Shape dough into a ball by pulling sides down and folding into bottom at centre
2. Cover with tea towel and let rest for 20 minutes
3. In a large bowl, mix asparagus, half of the oil, the salt & pepper
4. Set aside
5. On a lightly floure surface, shape dough by pressing with finger
6. Place on cornmeal dusted baking sheet
7. Stir remaining oil with garlic spread over dough
8. Top with asparagus, and cheeses
9. Bake at 425f until crust is golden, 20 to 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
