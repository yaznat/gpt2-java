Ingredients:
• Medium shrimp
• Turmeric powder
• Mustard oil
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Mustard seeds
• Clove
• Cinnamon sticks
• Bay leaf
• Cayenne pepper
• Coconut milk
• Fresh coriander leaves
• Salt

Steps:
1. Make a paste of turmeric, 1 tablespoon mustard oil, and salt
2. Apply the mixture to the shrimp
3. Cover and refrigerate for at least an hour
4. Heat the remaining oil in a medium-sized saute pan over medium heat and saute the marinated shrimp for 3 minutes
5. Remove from heat and keep aside
6. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium heat
7. Add mustard seeds, cloves, cinnamon stick, and bay leaf
8. Cook, stirring constantly until it is toasted and fragrant
9. Add chopped onions and cook till it is translucent
10. Add cayenne pepper and 1 / 2 cup water and cook until the oil separates
11. Lower the heat and add the coconut milk and the sauteed shrimp
12. Simmer until the shrimp are cooked
13. Transfer to a serving dish and garnish with fresh coriander leaves and eat it with basmati rice<|endoftext|>
