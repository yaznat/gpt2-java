What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Asian Beef Chicken Lo Mein

Ingredients:
• Ground sirloin
• Ramen noodles
• Vegetables
• Ground ginger
• Green onions
• Water

Steps:
1. In a large nonstick skillet, brown ground sirloin or quickly stir fry chicken strips until no longer pink
2. Use a drop of vegetable is necessary
3. Remove with a slotted spoon
4. Season the meat with one of the seasoning packets from the noodles
5. Set aside
6. In the same skillet, combine water, vegetables, noodles, ginger and remaining seasoning packet
7. Bring to a boil, reduce heat
8. Cover and simmer 3 minutes until noodles are tender, stirring occasionally
9. Return meat to skillet
10. Stir in green onion<|endoftext|>
