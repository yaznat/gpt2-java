Give me a wrap recipe.<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Weiners

Ingredients:
• Little smokies sausages
• Bacon
• Light brown sugar

Steps:
1. Line large baking sheet with foil
2. Cut bacon into thirds, resulting in several 3" strips
3. Wrap each weiner with bacon, line them loosely in the pan as you go
4. Cover with brown sugar, it'll seem like a lot of sugar, but it cooks down
5. Bake 375 for 45 minutes
6. Broil at the end until top is crisped
7. Hurry and get your share before everyone eats them all!<|endoftext|>
