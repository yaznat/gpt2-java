Give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>American Hamburger Goulash Soup

Ingredients:
• Ground sirloin
• Celery ribs
• Carrots
• Onion
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Frozen baby peas
• Potatoes
• Diced tomatoes
• Red wine
• Beef consomme
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt and pepper
• Bay leaf
• Shell macaroni
• Parmesan cheese
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. In a large pot, brown the ground beef with onion and garlic
2. Drain any fat
3. Add in all remaining ingredients except parmesan cheese, wine, and pasta and simmer for 3 hours or more
4. 1 hour before serving add uncooked pasta
5. 1 / 2 an hour before serving add red wine
6. Serve with grated parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
