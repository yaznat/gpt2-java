Ingredients:
• Sweetcorn
• Butter
• Flour
• Cornmeal
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Whole milk
• Eggs
• Egg white
• Margarine
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Melt butter in small skillet and saute corn for 3 minutes
2. Set aside
3. Sift together the flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt
4. Slowly add the buttermilk, milk, eggs and melted margarine
5. Gently stir until combined but not smooth
6. Fold in the bacon, corn and cheddar
7. Cook in waffle maker<|endoftext|>
