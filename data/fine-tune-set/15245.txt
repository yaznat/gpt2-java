Please give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Breakfast Pizza Somersize

Ingredients:
• Bulk sausage
• Cheddar cheese
• Eggs
• Diced tomato
• Fresh mushrooms

Steps:
1. Combine the sausage and 1 1 / 2 cups cheese and spread in the bottom of a pie plate or a 9" x 9" casserole dish
2. Pour the eggs over the top of the meat mixture and top with remaining cheese
3. Sprinkle tomatoes and mushrooms over the top
4. Bake at 350 degrees until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean, about 20-30 minutes<|endoftext|>
