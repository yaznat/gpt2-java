Give me a lamb recipe.<|endoftext|>A Pot Of Lamb Chops

Ingredients:
• Breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh parsley
• Olive oil
• Baby potatoes
• Onion
• Lamb chops
• White wine
• Water
• Tomatoes
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 190c
2. Mix together breadcrumbs, parmesan, garlic and parsley
3. I use my processor, processing the crumbs first
4. Brush bottom of casserole dish with oil
5. Place all of the potatoes in a layer and then top with all of the onions in a layer
6. Just cover this with equal parts wine and water
7. Season with salt and pepper to taste
8. Next, layer with 1 / 3 tomatoes and 1 / 3 crumbs mixture
9. Layer with 6 chops
10. Layer again with 1 / 3 tomatoes and 1 / 3 crumbs
11. Then a add the rest of the chops
12. Layer with the last of tomatoes and crumbs
13. Sprinkle with olive oil
14. Bake in oven for 1- 1 1 / 2 hours until chops are tender<|endoftext|>
