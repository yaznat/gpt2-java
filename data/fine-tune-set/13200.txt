Ingredients:
• Chicken breast
• Chicken broth
• Shallot
• Sea salt
• Fresh black pepper
• Baby portabella mushrooms
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Flour
• Instant minced garlic
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Crepes

Steps:
1. In med
2. Sauce pan combine chicken broth shallot, salt, pepper & chicken and simmer till chicken is cooked through
3. When done, remove chicken with tongs and reserve the broth
4. Chop or dice chicken and set aside
5. Saute garlic with olive oil over med high heat till fragrant 2-3 minutes
6. Add mushrooms and continue to saute till soft 5-6 min
7. Remove from heat and set aside
8. In large pot melt butter over med high heat
9. Add chicken and mushrooms and mix well
10. Let flavors blend for 2-3 minute add flour and coat chicken and mushroom pieces ~ the mix should look a little dry, that's perfect
11. Cook another 2-3 minutes
12. Add chicken broth reserve
13. Mix well and bring to a boil
14. Simmer until nice and thick
15. Add dried garlic
16. Once you get to a nice, well blended thickness turn down heat and add the cheese
17. Blend well
18. When well mixed spoon into warm crepes
19. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
