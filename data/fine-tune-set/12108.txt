Give me a pie recipe.<|endoftext|>Amish Crazy Quilt Pie

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Sweetened flaked coconut
• Granulated sugar
• Butter
• Salt
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients into a blender, put blender on medium speed and count to ten --
2. Pour into a 9" deep dish pie pan
3. During the baking process, the flour drops to form the crust
4. And the remaining ingredients will form the filling --
5. Bake at 350 for 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
