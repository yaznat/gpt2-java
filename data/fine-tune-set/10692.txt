What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Aussie Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Pasta
• Bacon
• Canned green beans
• Mushroom
• Green onions
• Cherry tomatoes
• Light olive oil
• Sour cream
• Garlic steak seasoning
• Mustard

Steps:
1. Combine sour cream, olive oil, mustard & steak seasoning together to create a dressing
2. Combine remaining ingredients together in a large bowl
3. Pour dressing over salad & mix gently to combine
4. Serve chilled<|endoftext|>
