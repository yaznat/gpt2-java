Ingredients:
• Cooked sushi rice
• Nori
• Cucumber

Steps:
1. Lay sheet of nori on bamboo mat
2. Cover nori with layer of sushi rice, spreading rice about 1 / 8-1 / 4 inch thick
3. Lay long strips of cucumber on center of rice
4. Roll into long sushi roll using the bamboo mat to aid
5. Cut roll into smaller pieces using sharp knife -- serve with soy sauce<|endoftext|>
