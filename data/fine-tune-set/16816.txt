Can you give me a milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Now That S A Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Coconut milk
• Peach yogurt
• Unsweetened orange juice
• Orange ice cream
• Peach
• Nectarine
• Ice cubes
• Ice cube
• Nectarines

Steps:
1. Into the blender pour the following ingredients
2. Coconut milk, yogurt, orange juice and ice cream, processing until smooth
3. Next add the peach slices, the nectarine slices and the ice cubes and process until smooth
4. Pour the mixture into chilled glasses and garnish with the nectarine slices<|endoftext|>
