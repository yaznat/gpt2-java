Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Salsa
• Green peppers
• Onion
• Reduced-fat sharp cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Spray large skillet with cooking spray
2. Add chicken and cook on medium-high heat 5 minutes, stirring frequently
3. #add green pepper and onion and cook 4 to 5 minutes or until crisp-tender
4. Pour in salsa and cook an additional 3 minutes or until heated through
5. Place 1 / 4 cup of the chicken mixture and 1 / 4 cup of the cheese down center of each tortilla then fold tortilla over to enclose filling
6. Place on preheated griddle or skillet sprayed with cooking spray and cook 2 minutes on each side or until heated through
7. Fold over or cut-into wedges
8. Serve with sour cream if desired
9. You can also add 1 / 2 cup corn or black beans to the filling
10. Top with shredded romaine lettuce, diced tomatoes and cilantro<|endoftext|>
