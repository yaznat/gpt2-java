Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Brown sugar
• Soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Ketchup
• Cornstarch
• Sambal oelek
• Firm tofu
• Canola oil
• Green onions
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Salt and pepper
• Broccoli
• Steamed rice

Steps:
1. Sauce: in a bowl, combine all the ingredients
2. Set aside
3. Stir-fry: in a large bowl, dust the tofu with the cornstarch
4. Shake to remove any excess
5. Set aside on a plate
6. In a wok, heat the oil
7. Fry the tofu, half at a time, until lightly browned
8. Season with salt and pepper
9. Remove the tofu from the wok with a slotted spoon and set aside on a plate lined with paper towels
10. In the wok, fry the onions, ginger, and garlic for 1 to 2 minutes
11. Add oil, if needed
12. Add the sauce and bring to a boil
13. Add the tofu and cook, stirring to warm through, and coat thoroughly with the sauce
14. Serve immediately over rice
15. Serve with broccoli<|endoftext|>
