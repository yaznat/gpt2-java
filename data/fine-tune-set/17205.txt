Give me a recipe for tea.<|endoftext|>Another Russian Tea

Ingredients:
• Water
• Whole cloves
• Cinnamon sticks
• Tea bags
• Orange juice
• Pineapple juice
• Lemon juice
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Boil water with cloves and cinnamon rapidly for 20 minutes
2. Remove from heat, cover, and add 4 tea bags, and let "steep" for at least 4 hours
3. After "steeping" is complete, in another pan combine: 2 cups orange juice 2 cups pineapple juice 1 cup lemon juice 2 cups sugar
4. Heat gently, stirring to melt and mix sugar
5. Drain "solids" from tea mixture
6. Combine all together and heat to enjoy<|endoftext|>
