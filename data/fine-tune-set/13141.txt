What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Breaded Fish Fillets A La Microwave

Ingredients:
• Fish fillet
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Fold the thinner fillet ends under to the center and place and place spoke fashion around the outside of a 9 or 12 inch dish
2. Combine remaining ingredients and spread evenly over the fillets
3. Cook on high 4-6 minutes or until done<|endoftext|>
