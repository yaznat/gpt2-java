Ingredients:
• Refrigerated biscuits
• Spaghetti sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Garlic
• Onion
• Ground sausage
• Pepperoni
• Mushroom

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. In a 9 x 13" baking dish, line the bottom with pepperoni, in a mixing bowl quarter biscuits, add the rest of the ingredients, mix well pour on top of the pepperoni, sprinkle some extra cheese on top
3. Bake 30- 35 minutes or until sides are golden brown<|endoftext|>
