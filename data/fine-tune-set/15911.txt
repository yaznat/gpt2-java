What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Chorizo And Egg Burritos

Ingredients:
• Chorizo sausage
• Eggs
• Onion
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese
• Sour cream
• Hot sauce
• Avocado

Steps:
1. Fry a chopped onion and chorizo together until onion is tender and translucent
2. Add 6 to 8 beaten eggs after everything is nice and brown
3. Keep'em moving until cooked
4. Serve it on a sour cream coated pan-grilled tortilla, add a bit of grated monterrey jack and avocado
5. Serve with more sour cream and hot sauce<|endoftext|>
