What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Ann S Roasted Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Pepper
• Rosemary
• Thyme leaves
• Russet potatoes
• White vinegar
• Dijon mustard
• Shallot
• Basil leaves

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375f
2. Mix 6 tablespoons of oil, the garlic cloves, salt, pepper, rosemary, and thyme
3. Add potatoes and toss
4. Spread out onto a baking sheet and bake for 55 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. When potatoes are golden brown remove the pan from the oven and let cool
6. Place potatoes in a bowl and scrape the pan drippings into measuring cup
7. Add enough oil to the drippings to make 6 tablespoons of oil
8. Whisk together vinegar and mustard in a small bowl
9. Gradually add the oil and mix in the shallots
10. Pour the dressing over the potatoes and combine
11. Season with salt and pepper, if needed
12. Add shopped basil
13. Let stand at room temperature for one hour before serving<|endoftext|>
