What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>A Really Simple Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• White potatoes
• Frozen peas
• Fresh carrot
• Pickles
• Mayonnaise
• Horseradish
• Basil
• Oregano
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Peel the potatoes first, and then boil in water for 30-40 minutes, or until soft
2. Let cool, before cubing them
3. In a large bowl combine the potatoes with the peas, carrots, and pickled
4. In a smaller bowl combine the mayonnaise, horseradish, salt and pepper to taste, basil and oregano
5. Add the mayonnaise mixture a little at a time and mix well
6. If you like it to be creamier make a little more mayonnaise
7. Serve chilled<|endoftext|>
