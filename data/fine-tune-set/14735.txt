Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Arroz Con Pollo Dominican Style Chicken Rice

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Apple cider vinegar
• Tomato paste
• Oregano
• Red onion
• Red pepper
• Green pepper
• Corn oil
• Long grain rice
• Water

Steps:
1. Throw all the ingredients "except" rice & water into a large pot
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring every so often
3. When liquid starts to diminish add the rice & water
4. Stir and bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat, cover & simmer for approximately 20 minutes
6. Enjoy! : )<|endoftext|>
