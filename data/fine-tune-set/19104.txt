Ingredients:
• Whole milk
• Heavy cream
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Pistachio nuts
• Rose water
• Vanilla extract
• Saffron
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a medium thick-bottomed bot, slowly heat the milk to boiling while stirring
2. Add the vanilla extract and saffron
3. Continue to cook on low heat, stirring occasionally while doing the next step
4. Beat the egg yolks with the sugar until smooth and foamy
5. Very carefully and slowly, pour the egg / sugar mixture into the milk
6. Make sure to stir it rapidly with a fork or use a whisk while pouring
7. Continue heating the mixture on low heat while stirring constantly with a wooden spoon until thickened
8. Pour the custard mixture into a bowl, and refrigerate until well chilled
9. Once cold, stir in the cream, rose water or extract, and chopped pistachios
10. Churn in an ice cream maker until finished<|endoftext|>
