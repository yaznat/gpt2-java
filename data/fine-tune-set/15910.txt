Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Chorizo sausage
• Eggs
• Chunky salsa
• Mexican blend cheese
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Cook chorizo - in wide skillet, saute chorizo sausage, stirring it and breaking it up so that the finished product is small and crumbly
2. Remove to paper towels to drain
3. Clean skillet of any blackened bits
4. Prepare egg mixture - whisk together eggs, salsa, salt and pepper in a large bowl
5. Don't add water or milk because you want firm scrambled eggs
6. Scramble eggs - pour the egg mixture into the hot skillet and scramble the eggs
7. When the eggs are nearly done, turn down the heat and add the drained sausage and shredded cheese
8. Remove from heat
9. Heat tortillas - put 5 tortillas between damp paper towels and microwave 45 seconds
10. Repeat for other 5 tortillas
11. Assemble - put egg mixture onto warm tortilla and roll up
12. Place each completed tortilla in a piece of waxed paper for easy freezing and removal
13. To reheat - microwave 2 minutes at 50% power<|endoftext|>
