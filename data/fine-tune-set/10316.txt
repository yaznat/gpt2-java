Ingredients:
• Shortening
• Brown sugar
• White sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Nuts
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Mix shortening, butter or margarine and sugar,
2. Add vanilla and eggs
3. In a separate bowl, mix flour, baking soda and salt
4. Combine flour mixture with butter mixture
5. Stir in nuts and chocolate chips
