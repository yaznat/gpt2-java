Give me a recipe for donuts.<|endoftext|>Donut Holes

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Pancake mix
• Sugar
• Egg
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Heat oil to 375 degrees
2. In a large bowl, combine pancake mix and cup sugar
3. Add water and egg and stir until dough has formed
4. In a small bowl combine remaining sugar and cinnamon
5. Using a 1 tablespoon sized cookie scoop, drop balls of dough into hot oil
6. Fry 1-2 minutes on each side until golden brown
7. Place cooked donut holes on plate lined with paper towels
8. Roll donut holes in cinnamon-sugar mixture before serving<|endoftext|>
