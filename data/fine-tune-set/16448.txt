Ingredients:
• Egg roll wraps
• Jalapeno jack cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Refried beans
• Sunflower oil

Steps:
1. Mix cheeses together in a bowl
2. Place wrappers, one at a time on your counter
3. Put a half cup of cheese in the center of the wrapper
4. Add 1 1 / 2 tbsp refried beans
5. Now to roll them, have a corner of the wrapper pointing toward you, fold in the two sides, moisten all around the edge with water, bring up the far corner, seal with water, continue to wrap the wrapper by rolling the folded edge toward the one corner that is sticking out, seal the flap to the wrapper with water, before this final step think of an envelope and you are rolling the bottom of the envelope toward the flap
6. Now you can store the wrappers in a plastic bag in the fridge until you want to cook them or cook them now
7. You can now fry them in the heated oil, the oil should be hot enoughif you put a corner of the wrapperin it it sizzles
8. Cook on one side and turn when bottom is golden
9. Cook until both sides are golden
10. Or brush each wrapper with oil and put on a cookie sheet
11. Bake in 375f degrees oven until they are nicely browned
12. Serve with salsa<|endoftext|>
