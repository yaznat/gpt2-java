What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Guacamole Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Guacamole
• Black beans
• Red onion
• Tomatoes
• Red bell pepper
• Fresh cilantro
• Cumin
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper
• Chili powder
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Tortillas

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients except cheese and tortillas
2. Spread mixture onto 5 tortillas
3. Sprinkle with cheese
4. Top with remaining 5 tortillas
5. Heat on skillet until bottom tortilla starts to get crispy, then flip and repeat
6. Cut into triangles with pizza cutter
7. Serve with salsa, sour cream, etc<|endoftext|>
