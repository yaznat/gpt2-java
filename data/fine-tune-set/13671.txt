Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Ground black pepper
• Coarse salt
• Chili powder
• Cayenne powder
• Arugula
• Pico de gallo
• Black beans
• Tomatoes
• Red onion
• Green onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Jalapeno peppers
• Fresh lemon juice
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f mix paprika, black pepper, salt, chili powder and cayenne together in a bowl
2. Thoroughly rub both sides of each piece of pork with spice mixture
3. Heat an oven -safe skillet over medium-high and pan-seer each piece of pork on both sides until golden brown
4. Pan-searing seals in the meat's juices
5. Transfer pan to oven until pork is completely done (6 to 8 minutes for each inch of thickness
6. Pico de gallo
7. Combine all ingredients
8. Garnish with arugula add pork in equal portions and sprinkle 1 / 2 pico de gallo on the top
9. Serve<|endoftext|>
