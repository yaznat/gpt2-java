Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Blackberry Banana Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Unbleached flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Wheat bran
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Egg
• Milk
• Bananas
• Blackberry

Steps:
1. Mix together all dry ingredients
2. Add milk and beaten egg
3. Add 2 mashed bananas and mix until well blended, but lumpy
4. Fold in blackberries
5. Pour onto a medium hot lightly greased griddle
6. Flip when top is bubbly<|endoftext|>
