Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Apples
• Pecans
• Butter
• Milk
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, beat eggs until foamy
2. Gradually add sugar
3. Blend in oil and vanilla
4. Combine flour, salt and baking soda
5. Add to egg mixture
6. Stir in apples and pecans
7. Pour into a greased 10-in
8. Tube pan
9. Bake at 350 for 1 hour and 15 minutes or until the cake tests done
10. Cool in pan on a wire rack for 10 minutes
11. Remove cake to a serving platter
12. For topping, combine all ingredients in a saucepan
13. Boil 3 minutes, stirring constantly
14. Slowly pour over warm cake<|endoftext|>
