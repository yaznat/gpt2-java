What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Amish Friendship Starter Oatmeal Cookies

Ingredients:
• Amish starter
• Eggs
• Molasses
• Vanilla
• Butter
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Oatmeal
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Chocolate chips
• Raisins
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Combine mixture 1 in a bowl or large glass measuring cup
2. Mix ingredients well with a fork
3. Combine mixture 2 in a large bowl
4. I use my kitchen aide mixer
5. Beat until creamy
6. Add mixture 1 to mixture 2
7. Combine mixture 3 and mix thoroughly
8. Add mixture 3 to mixtures 1 and 2 in the large bowl
9. Add mixture 4 to the large bowl and mix until just combined
10. Spoon resulting dough onto an ungreased cookie sheet and bake at 375 for 10-13 minutes
11. It usually takes twelve minutes for me
12. Cool on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
