Ingredients:
• Cabbage kimchi
• Lean ground beef
• Sesame oil
• Soy sauce
• Green onions
• Garlic
• Black pepper
• Cooked rice
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Chop kimchi into small pieces
2. Mix meat and seasonings together
3. Cook meat until brown
4. Add rice and kimchi to meat in pan and stir fry for 2-3 minutes to heat rice and kimchi up
5. Serve with fried egg on top of each serving<|endoftext|>
