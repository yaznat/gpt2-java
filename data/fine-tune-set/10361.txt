What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Almond Sandwich Cookies

Ingredients:
• Refrigerated sugar cookie dough
• All-purpose flour
• Almond extract
• Granulated sugar
• Powdered sugar
• Butter
• Milk
• Red food coloring

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f in large bowl, break up cookie dough
2. Stir or knead in flour and almond extract until well blended
3. Shape dough into 180 balls
4. Place balls 1 inch apart on ungreased cookie sheets
5. Press bottom of glass dipped into granulated sugar on each ball until 1 / 4 inch thick
6. Prick top of each with a fork
7. Bake 6 to 8 minutes until set but not brown
8. Cool 1 minute
9. Remove from cookie sheets to cooling rack
10. Cool completely, about 15 minutes
11. Meanwhile, in small bowl, beat filling ingredients with electric mixer on low speed until smooth and creamy
12. Spread about 1 teaspoon filling on bottoms of half of the cookies
13. Top each with another cookie, bottom side down
14. Press gently
15. Store in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
