What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>A Super Slow Cooker Chicken Cacciatore

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Pasta
• Bay leaf
• Diced tomatoes
• Sliced mushrooms
• Onion
• Celery
• Dried italian herb seasoning
• Minced garlic cloves
• Fresh parsley
• Dry white wine
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. In a large heavy duty plastic bag, combine the flour, salt, italian seasoning and pepper
2. Add chicken, a few pieces at a time, and shake to coat
3. Place chicken pieces in slow cooker stoneware
4. Add onion, mushrooms, celery, garlic, tomatoes, pasta sauce, wine, bay leaf and parsley to slow cooker
5. Cover and cook on low for 5 to 7 hours or high for 2 1 / 2 to 4 hours or until chicken is tender and no longer pink
6. Discard bay leaf
7. Serve sprinkled with parmesan cheese
8. Yield: 6-8 servings<|endoftext|>
