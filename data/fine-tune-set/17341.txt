What's a good tea recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Tea Bread

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Shortening
• Eggs
• Banana
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cream sugar and shortening together
2. Add eggs then bananas
3. Add dry ingredients
4. Bake in a loaf pan for 1 hour at 350 degrees fahrenheit<|endoftext|>
