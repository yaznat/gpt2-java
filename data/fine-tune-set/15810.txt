Give me a burrito recipe.<|endoftext|>Black Bean And Sweet Potato Burritos Slow Cooker

Ingredients:
• Chili powder
• Dried oregano
• Ground cumin
• Sweet potato
• Black beans
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Jalapeno pepper
• Green pepper
• Frozen corn
• Fresh lime juice
• Cilantro
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Combine chili powder, oregano, and cumin in small bowl and set aside
2. Layer sweet potato, beans, half of chili powder mix, garlic, onion, jalapeno pepper, green pepper, remaining half of chili powder mix and corn in slow cooker
3. Cover and cook on low 5 hours ore until sweet potato is tender
4. Stir in lime juice and cilantro
5. Spoon 2 tbs cheese in tortilla and top with 1 cup filling
6. Fold tortilla to close
7. Microwave until cheese melts<|endoftext|>
