What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Bermese Squash And Tofu Stew

Ingredients:
• Yellow split peas
• Firm tofu
• Kosher salt
• Canola oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Chili pepper
• Ground coriander
• Ground cumin
• Delicata squash
• Salt
• Water
• Cherry tomatoes
• Tamarind pulp
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Cube the squash unpeeled into 1 inch cubes
2. Use the small skinny chiles
3. In small saucepan, bring 4 cups water to boil
4. Add peas and simmer until tender but not mushy
5. About 35 minutes
6. Drain and set aside
7. Wrap tofu in dishtowel or paper towels and press
8. Cube into 1 / 2 inch cubes
9. Salt liberally
10. In large skillet heat oil over med heat add tofu and onion and cook until onion is golden brown about 12 to 14 minutes
11. Use spatula and turn tofu and onion once or twice
12. Add garlic, ginger, chilies, coriander and cumin and saute for 2 minutes
13. Stir in squash, salt and water
14. Cover and cook for 10 minutes or until squash is tender
15. Add the peas, tomatoes, tamarind pulp and heat through
16. Season to taste
17. Transfer to seving dish and garnish with cilantro or mint<|endoftext|>
