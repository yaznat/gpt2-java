Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Salt
• Pepper
• Catsup
• Garlic powder
• Process american cheese
• Tomatoes
• Bermuda onions
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Mix ground beef lightly with salt, pepper, and garlic powder shape into patties about 1' thick
2. Pan fry or grill over hot coals med
3. Heat 8 minute on each side for med
4. 9-10 minute for med well
5. Brush tops lightly with catsup
6. Cover with cheese slices
7. I leave them on the heat for a few seconds to let the cheese melt
8. Place on butterd buns
9. Serve with lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion slices, mustard and mayo as condiments on the side<|endoftext|>
