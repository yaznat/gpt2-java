What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Flour Blueberry Muffins Best Ever

Ingredients:
• Almond flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Walnut oil
• Honey
• Almond extract
• Vanilla extract
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 350 degrees
2. Mix dry ingredients
3. Mix liquid ingredients in separate bowl
4. Add liquid ingredients to dry ingredients and gently mix
5. Gently fold in blueberries
6. Put into prepared muffin cups
7. If making mini muffins cook for 15 minute or when toothpick comes out clean
8. If making big muffins cook for 20-23 minute or when toothpick comes out clean
9. Cool for 5 minutes and enjoy
10. Yum!<|endoftext|>
