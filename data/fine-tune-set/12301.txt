Ingredients:
• Corn
• Black beans
• Diced tomatoes with green chilies
• Chicken broth
• Cooked chicken
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Corn tortilla strips

Steps:
1. Throw all ingredients into a pot, heat, and serve!
2. Sour cream, salsa, green onion, and hot sauce are also great toppings
3. I was told canned chicken may be substituted for fresh chicken, although i have not tried it that way before
