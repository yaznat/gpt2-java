Ingredients:
• Green onion
• Button mushroom
• Cherry tomatoes
• Olive oil
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet
2. Add green onion and mushrooms
3. Stir-fry over a high heat for 1 minute
4. Add the tomatoes and cook for a further 2 minutes or until the mushrooms have cooked
5. Season with salt and pepper and serve<|endoftext|>
