Ingredients:
• Catfish fillets
• Ketchup
• Barbecue sauce
• Jamaican jerk spice
• Margarine
• Balsamic vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dijon mustard
• Garlic
• Dried tarragon
• Cracked black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Rinse fillets and arrange in a lightly-greased 9 x 13-inch pan
3. Melt margarine in a saucepan
4. Add all of the remaining ingredients to the melted margarine
5. Mix well and stir occasionally for 5-10 minutes
6. Pour mixture over fish fillets
7. Bake 15-18 minutes until fish flakes easily<|endoftext|>
