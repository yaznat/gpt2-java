Ingredients:
• Broccoli rabe
• Italian sausage
• Sun-dried tomato
• Garlic
• Mozzarella cheese
• Fresh basil
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Pizza dough
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Spread dough evenly on a pizza pan
2. Coat generously with olive oil
3. Sprinkle surface with chopped garlic
4. Rinse and cut broccoli rabe into 3-4 inch pieces
5. Blanch in boiling water for 2 minutes
6. Drain
7. Sprinkle cheese on pizza then sausage, sun-dried tomatoes and broccoli rabe
8. Sprinkle basil and crushed red pepper
9. Bake until crust is brown in a 325f oven<|endoftext|>
