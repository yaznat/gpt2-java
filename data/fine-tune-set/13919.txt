Ingredients:
• Leg of lamb
• Garlic clove
• Rosemary sprig
• Parsley sprig
• Sage sprig
• Fresh thyme sprig
• Dry sherry
• Walnut oil
• Potatoes
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place the lamb on a cutting board, use a sharp knife make a long cut through the flesh to the bone
2. Remove the bone and cut away the excess fat
3. Cut through the thickest part of the meat to open it out as flat as possible
4. Make several cuts in the lamb with a sharp knife and push silvers of garlic and sprigs of herbs into the slits
5. Place the meat in a bowl and pour sherry and oil over the meat
6. Chop remaining herbs and scatter over meat
7. Cover and let marinate in the refrigerator for 30 minutes
8. Remove lamb from marinade and season with salt and pepper
9. Place on a medium-hot barbecue and cook for 30-35 minutes, turning and basting with reserved marinade
10. Or tie up lamb and roast on a spit for 1 1 / 2 hours or until done
11. Scrub potatoes and then cut them into thick slices
12. Brush with marinade and place them around the lamb
13. Cook for 15-20 minutes
14. Turning occassionally or until golden brown<|endoftext|>
