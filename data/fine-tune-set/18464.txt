Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Double Chocolate Waffles Oamc

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Flour
• Cocoa powder
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat waffle iron
2. In a small bowl, mix together the flour, cocoa, sugar, baking powder and salt
3. In a medium bowl beat egg yolks with milk and vanilla
4. Add melted butter, a little at a time, whisking the whole time so the eggs don't cook
5. Add dry ingredients and stir just until moistened
6. In another bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form
7. Fold the beaten whites into the waffle batter very gently so as not to deflate the egg whites
8. Stir in chocolate chips
9. Pour batter onto waffle iron using about 1 / 4 cup per waffle
10. Bake until waffles are cooked through and crisp
11. This will vary depending upon your waffle iron
12. Serve warm
13. To freeze: cool to room temperature and place waffles separated by waxed paper on a cookie sheet
14. Freeze until firm and then package in freezer bags
15. To serve: remove from freezer bag and toast in toaster until crisp<|endoftext|>
