Ingredients:
• Low-carb milk
• Strawberries
• Splenda sugar substitute

Steps:
1. You will need: 1 small cuisinart
2. Dump berries into the cuisinart first
3. Add the packet of sweetener of your choice
4. Cover the berries and sweetener with the carb countdown dairy beverage
5. Leave enough space under the lid because this stuff really, really fluffs up!
6. Put the cover on and blend in short spurts
7. About 30-60 seconds depending on how smooth you want the fruit to be
8. The longer you blend this the fluffier this will come out
9. For a beverage, scoop this out into a glass and pour some more carb countdown directly into glass to thin out mixture and drink
10. For a "cool whip" like topping, take a spatula and scrape out the mixture and add to your dessert and smooth out artistically to make a beautiful, tasty topping
11. To make this into ice cream, scrape out mixture and pour into a bowl and place in freezer for 30-60 minutes depending on how firm you like your ice cream
12. Eat and enjoy<|endoftext|>
