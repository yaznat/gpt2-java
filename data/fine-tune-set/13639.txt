What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Roast Pork And Apple Dinner

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Onion
• Sliced mushrooms
• Balsamic vinaigrette
• Minute white rice
• Condensed chicken broth
• Water
• Red apples
• Dried thyme leaves

Steps:
1. Spray large nonstick skillet with cooking spray
2. Add chops
3. Cook on medium-high heat 4 minutes on each side or until browned
4. Add onion, mushrooms and dressing
5. Cook an additional 3 minutes
6. Stir in rice, broth, water, apples and thyme
7. Reduce heat to medium
8. Simmer 10 minutes or until heated through<|endoftext|>
