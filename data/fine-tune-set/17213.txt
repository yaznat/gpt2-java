Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Scallion
• Green bell pepper
• Crushed pineapple in syrup
• Pecans
• Seasoning salt
• Cracker

Steps:
1. To cream cheese add scallions, peppers, pineapple, pecans and seasoned salt to bowl of mixer
2. Mix to smooth consistency
3. Serve with your favorite crackers or assortment
4. Store in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
