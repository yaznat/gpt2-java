Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Magic Omelette

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Eggs
• Water
• Cheese
• Ham
• Chives
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Heat a non-stick skillet until butter sizzles
2. Scramble eggs
3. Add to skillet
4. Sprinkle eggs evenly with cheese, ham, chives, salt and pepper
5. Cover and cook over low flame until eggs solidify, about 5 minutes
6. Slide from skillet to plate, folding omelette in half with edge of skillet<|endoftext|>
