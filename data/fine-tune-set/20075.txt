Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt and pepper
• Tabasco jalapeno sauce
• Venison sausage
• Sharp cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Mix eggs, milk, salt and pepper and tabasco together and whisk into a froth
2. Go overboard if you want, the fluffier the eggs are the better they seem to taste
3. Once eggs are in frying pan and cooking for a minute, add sausage
4. Right before folding eggs into omelette, add cheese so it doesnt get too melted
5. Use whatever kind of cheese you like
6. I usually use sharp cheddar, but you can use a mexican blend or i sometimes use monterey jack
7. Add two pieces of toast and a nice cup of chickory coffee and you've got a pretty good breakfast!<|endoftext|>
