Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• Agave syrup
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Milk
• Cream cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325 and coat a 9x13 pan with non-stick spray
2. Whisk together flour, powder, and salt
3. With the mixer on medium speed, beat butter and agave syrup until light and fluffy
4. Add eggs one at a time
5. Beat in vanilla
6. On low, beat in flour mixture alternately with milk
7. Pour into prepared pan
8. Bake at 325 for 45 min, or until tests clean
9. Cool completely
10. Prepare frosting by beating together last 3 ingredients until smooth
11. Spread on cooled cake<|endoftext|>
