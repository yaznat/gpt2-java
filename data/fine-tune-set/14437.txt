What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Anasazi Bean Veggie Burgers

Ingredients:
• Anasazi beans
• Olive oil
• Yellow onion
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Carrot
• Cumin
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt and pepper
• Egg
• Dried breadcrumbs

Steps:
1. Cook the beans in water for approximately 1 hour, until soft but not mushy
2. Drain and cool
3. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a frying pan
4. Saute the onion, bell peppers and carrot until soft, about 10 minutes
5. Cool
6. Process the beans in a food processor until roughly chopped
7. Remove the beans and repeat that step for the vegetables
8. Combine beans and vegetables in a large mixing bowl, season with cumin, cayenne pepper and salt and pepper to taste
9. Add the egg and then work the bread crumbs into the bean mixture, starting with small amounts and gradually adding more
10. It should hold together and have a texture similar to pie dough
11. If the mixture seems a little dry, add up to 1 tablespoon additional olive oil
12. Form patties
13. Starting with a cold pan, saute with 1 tablespoon olive oil until golden, approximately 3 minutes on each side
14. Add a slice of cheddar and serve with mayonnaise<|endoftext|>
