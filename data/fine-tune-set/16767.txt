What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Dulce De Leche Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Caramel sauce
• Kahlua
• Dulce de leche ice cream
• 2% low-fat milk
• Toffee pieces
• Ice

Steps:
1. In a blender mix all ingredients together
2. Meanwhile, swirl caramel sauce around the rim of a glass
3. Pour mixture into glass and sprinkle the ground chocolate pieces on top
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
