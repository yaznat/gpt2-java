Ingredients:
• Tuna in water
• Sweet red pepper
• Green onions
• Cilantro
• Honey
• Lime juice
• Low-fat mayonnaise
• Black pepper
• Hot pepper sauce
• Cumin
• Salt
• Pita bread
• English cucumber
• Alfalfa sprouts

Steps:
1. Combine tuna, red pepper, green onion and cilantro in a bowl
2. Whisk together the honey, lime juice, mayo, black pepper, hot pepper sauce, cumin and salt
3. Pour over the tuna mixture and toss to combine
4. Open pita pockets and line each with 1 / 4 of the cucumber slices and 1 / 4 of the alfalfa sprouts
5. Divide the tuna mixture evenly among the pita pockets<|endoftext|>
