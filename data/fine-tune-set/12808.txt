What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Honey Steak

Ingredients:
• Beef sirloin steaks
• Red peppers
• Black pepper
• Garlic
• Onion
• Honey

Steps:
1. Thinly slice onion
2. Combine all ingredients well
3. Marinate steak for 7 to 8 hours
4. Remove meat from marinade and grill over very hot fire
5. To serve, heat leftover marinade and pour over grilled meat<|endoftext|>
