Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>57 Zesty Honey Chicken

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• Heinz 57 steak sauce
• Honey
• Garlic powder
• Hot pepper sauce

Steps:
1. Grill chicken until cooked through brushing with sauce the last 5 minutes
2. For sauce, combine 57 sauce and remaining ingredients
3. Divide sauce in half
4. Brush chicken often with one half of sauce during last 5 minutes of cooking time
5. Serve chicken with remaining sauce for dipping
6. Makes about 1 1 / 2 cups of sauce<|endoftext|>
