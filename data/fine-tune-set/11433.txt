What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Allergen Free Gluten Free Bread

Ingredients:
• Sorghum flour
• Potato starch
• Millet flour
• Xanthan gum
• Salt
• Yeast
• Warm water
• Olive oil
• Honey
• Egg beaters egg substitute
• Rice vinegar

Steps:
1. 1
2. Mix 1t honey and warm water
3. Water should feel warm-to-hot to the touch but not burning
4. 2
5. Add yeast and mix well
6. Let the mixture sit until it gets bubbly / frothy
7. 3
8. Mix all dry ingredients together
9. 4
10. When yeast liquid is frothy, add to dry ingredients
11. 5
12. Add olive oil, 2t honey, cider vinegar and mixed egg replacer and beat until a smooth batter forms
13. Dough should be along the consistency of cookie dough, not like wheat bread dough
14. 6
15. Scrape into a lightly oiled loaf pan and smooth with wet fingers or spatula
16. 7
17. Loosely cover pan
18. 8
19. Preheat over to 350 and put loaf pan on top of stove other other warm place to rise
20. 9
21. Let rise 20 minutes for rapid rise yeast and 30ish minutes for regular yeast
22. 10
23. Bake 35-45 minutes
24. 11
25. Cool on wire rack<|endoftext|>
