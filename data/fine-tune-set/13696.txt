Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic salt
• Onion
• Beef broth
• Bud light beer
• Olive oil
• Flour

Steps:
1. Salt, pepper, garlic salt chops, then dredge in flour
2. Place in frying pan with oil, make sure the oil is already hot
3. Brown on both sides, add onions, cook for about 2 minutes
4. Add beef broth and beer, cover and let simmer about 45 minutes until chops are very tender<|endoftext|>
