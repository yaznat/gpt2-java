Ingredients:
• Turkey ham
• Cranberry sauce
• Cream cheese
• Dried cranberries
• Tortillas
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Spread cream cheese on 1 of the tortilla, and cranberry sauce / jam on the other
2. Arrange the turkey ham on 1 of the tortilla
3. Sprinkle dried cranberry all over it
4. Place the other tortilla over the one with turkey ham, sprinkle some grated cheese over the tortilla if desired and bake for about 4 minute
5. Cut into wedges and serve warm<|endoftext|>
