What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Allen S Loaded Potato Soup

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onions
• Carrot
• Celery
• Condensed chicken broth
• Water
• White pepper
• Flour
• Butter
• Whipping cream
• Velveeta cheese
• Bacon
• Green onion

Steps:
1. In a 4-qt
2. Large, heavy pot combine potatoes, onion, carrots, celery, broth, water and pepper
3. Bring to boiling
4. Reduce heat
5. Cover and simmer 20 minutes or until vegetables are tender
6. Meanwhile, cook bacon until crisp
7. Stir together flour and melted butter
8. Stir into soup mixture
9. Cook and stir until slightly thickened and bubbly
10. Cook and stir 1 minute more
11. Stir in whipping cream
12. Heat through
13. Ladle into bowls
14. Sprinkle with cheese, bacon and green onions
15. Serve<|endoftext|>
