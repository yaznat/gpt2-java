What's a good tea recipe?<|endoftext|>Apricot Tea Loaf

Ingredients:
• Dried apricots
• Brewed tea
• Butter
• Caster sugar
• Egg
• Self raising flour

Steps:
1. Soak apricots in hot tea for 1 hour till tea is absorbed
2. Preheat oven to 180 degrees
3. Grease and line loaf pan - 13cms x 25cms
4. With electric mixer, cream butter and sugar
5. Add egg and beat well
6. Fold in flour and apricots
7. Spoon into prepared pan
8. Bake 40-45 minutes
9. Cool in tin for 5 minutes then turn onto wire rack to cool<|endoftext|>
