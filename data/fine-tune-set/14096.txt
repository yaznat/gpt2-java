What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>5 Spice Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Tamari soy sauce
• Water
• Five-spice powder
• Star anise
• Molasses

Steps:
1. Cut cakes in half horizontally
2. Combine rest of ingredients in pan large enough to accommodate slices of tofu flat and bring to a boil
3. Add the tofu carefully and lower heat and simmer for 10 minutes, uncovered
4. Remove from heat, cover and marinate 3 hours, turning over occasionally
5. Drain and use or store up to a week, wrapped in plastic<|endoftext|>
