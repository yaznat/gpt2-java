Ingredients:
• Breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Almonds
• Garlic
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Catfish fillets

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 475f
2. In a medium bowl, combine bread crumbs, cheese, almonds, garlic, salt and pepper
3. Place the olive oil in a shallow dish
4. Brush fillets in olive oil
5. Dredge fillets in breadcrumb mixture
6. Bake fillets for 15 minutes or until fish flakes easily when tested with a fork<|endoftext|>
