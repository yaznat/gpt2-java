Please give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Another Leftover Ham Casserole

Ingredients:
• Cooked ham
• Potatoes
• Flour
• Milk
• Pepper
• Butter

Steps:
1. Layer potatoes on bottom of 9 x 13 casserole dish
2. Layer ham chunks on top of potatoes
3. Sprinkle flour over layer
4. Repeat layers finishing with potatoes on top
5. Sprinkle with flour and put butter tabs on
6. Pour milk over casserole
7. Pepper to taste
8. Bake at 325f for 1 hour or until potatoes are soft
9. Sprinkle crushed chips or bread crumbs on top
10. Serve and smile<|endoftext|>
