What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Artichoke And Basil Hero Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Fresh basil
• Olive oil
• Parmesan cheese
• Capers
• White wine vinegar
• Dijon-style mustard
• Garlic clove
• French bread
• Artichoke hearts
• Provolone cheese
• Tomatoes
• Fresh spinach
• Salami

Steps:
1. To make the basil / parmesan mixture: in a blender or food processor bowl combine fresh basil, parmesan cheese, capers, wine vinegar, mustard and garlic cloves
2. Process until almost smooth
3. Cover and refrigerate for 8-24 hours
4. Cut the bread in half lengthwise
5. Hollow out each half
6. Spread the prepared basil mixture over each bread half, then layer artichoke hearts then provolone cheese, tomato then top with spinach
7. Cover with the bread top
8. Cut the sandwich crosswise into about 6-8 pieces<|endoftext|>
