Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Fresh lime juice
• Fresh oregano
• Medium shrimp
• Ice
• Tomato juice
• Hot sauce
• Onion
• Tomatoes
• English cucumber
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Fresh cilantro
• Avocado
• Lime wedges

Steps:
1. Combine broth, 1 t lime juice and oregano in a large saucepan
2. Bring to a simmer
3. Cook 5 minutes and discard oregano
4. Add shrimp to pan
5. Cook 3 minutes or until done
6. Transfer shrimp mixture to a large bowl
7. Stir in ice, tomato juice and hot sauce
8. Let stand 1 minute or until ice melts
9. Stir in remaining 1 / 4 cup lime juice, onion, tomato, cucumber, pepper and salt
10. Cool, cover and chill
11. When ready to serve, stir in cilantro
12. Divide shrimp mixture evenly among 6 bowls
13. Top with avocado
14. Garnish with lime wedges<|endoftext|>
