Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Lime juice
• Fresh ginger
• Scotch bonnet pepper
• Fish fillets
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Combine oil, garlic, lime juice, ginger, and scotch bonnet in a non-reactive bowl
2. Add fish and turn to coat well
3. Cover and refrigerate 1 hour
4. Light grill
5. Remove fish from marinade and scrape off most of the garlic and ginger pieces
6. Season with salt and pepper and grill about 3 minutes on each side, or until fish is nicely browned and just cooked through<|endoftext|>
