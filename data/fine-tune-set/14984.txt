Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Mrs C S Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Calrose rice
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Green onions
• Carrots
• Cabbage
• Soy sauce
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Steam or cook rice according to package directions
2. Cool
3. Fry bacon in large fry pan or wok until crisp
4. Move bacon to side of pan and add eggs to pan and scramble
5. Add all vegetables to pan, and stir fry with bacon and eggs until crisp-tender
6. Add rice to pan and mix well
7. Add soy sauce, salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
