What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Another Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Salt
• Vinegar
• Green beans
• Yellow beans
• Lima beans
• Garbanzo beans
• Red kidney beans
• Green pepper
• Celery
• Onions

Steps:
1. Combine sugar, salt vinegar in pan, bring to boil for 1 min
2. Let cool
3. Toss all other ingredients together and pour the vinegar mixture over them
4. Marinate for 24 hrs in refrigerator, stirring occasionally<|endoftext|>
