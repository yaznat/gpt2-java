Ingredients:
• Butter
• Granulated sugar
• Velveeta cheese
• All-purpose flour
• Apples

Steps:
1. Cream butter and sugar in a mixing bowl, add cheese and combine well
2. Add flour and mix well-batter will be stiff
3. Place apples in a buttered baking dish - about 1 1 / 2 quart size
4. Spread the cheese / flour mixture over the apples covering the apples well
5. Bake 325: for about 30-45 minutes
