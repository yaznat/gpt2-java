Please give me a recipe for a popsicle.<|endoftext|>Watermelon Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Watermelon
• Fresh raspberry
• Sugar
• Fresh lemon juice
• Light corn syrup

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in blender
2. Puree until smooth
3. Strain into 2-cup glass measuring cup, pressing on solids to extract as much liquid as possible
4. Pour puree into popsicle molds, dividing equally
5. Freeze overnight<|endoftext|>
