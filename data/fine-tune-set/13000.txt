Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Fish Nuggets

Ingredients:
• Tilapia fillets
• Skim milk
• Fresh breadcrumb
• Cornmeal
• Ground pepper
• Cooking spray
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees
2. Put the fresh bread crumbs on a cookie sheet and place them in the oven until they are toasted
3. Take them out and set them aside to cool
4. Turn the oven up to 425 degrees
5. Cut the tilapia into slices about one inch thick
6. Put the tilapia in a bowl and add the milk
7. Stir well
8. In a large ziploc type bag add the toasted bread crumbs, cornmeal, and pepper
9. Using a slotted spoon, remove the fish from the milk and put the pieces into the bag
10. Seal it and then shake it up so all the pieces are coated
11. Spray a cookie sheet with cooking spray
12. Place the nuggets on the baking sheet
13. Sprinkle the tops with salt
14. Bake until the fish flakes easily with a fork, 15-25 minutes<|endoftext|>
