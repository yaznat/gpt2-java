Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Apricot Pork Chops Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Apple
• Dried apricot
• Cranberries
• Onion
• Celery
• Apple juice
• Brown sugar
• Wine
• Salt and pepper
• Cornstarch
• Cold water

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients
2. Cover and cook 7 to 9 hours on low
3. About 30 minutes before serving, pour liquid into a separate container to skim off excess fat
4. Stir in cornstarch mixture and return broth to crockpot
5. Continue cooking on low until smooth and thickened<|endoftext|>
