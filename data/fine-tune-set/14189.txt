What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Cauliflower And Tofu Pizza Crust Gluten Free Low Carb

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Cauliflower
• Egg
• Mixed italian herbs
• Salt
• Pizza sauce
• Pizza toppings
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Place a block of tofu in a bowl and top with a heavy weight to drain water as much as possible
2. Place cauliflower florets in a food processor and process until a rice-like texture is achieved
3. Fill a medium pan with an inch of water and bring to a boil
4. Add the processed cauliflower and cook about 4 5 minutes
5. Drain water and cool
6. Preheat oven to 400f degrees
7. Place the cooled cauliflower in a nut milk bag or wrap with cheesecloth and squeeze excess water out as much as possible
8. In a bowl, place drained tofu and use your hands to mash it well
9. Add cauliflower, beaten egg, herb, and salt and mix well
10. Press the dough out onto a baking sheet lined with parchment paper
11. The thickness of the dough should be around 1 / 2 inch
12. Bake for about 30 40 minutes
13. The crust should be firm and slightly brown
14. Remove the crust from oven
15. Top with pizza sauce, toppings and cheese
16. Bake for another 10 20 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned
17. Infuse love and serve!<|endoftext|>
