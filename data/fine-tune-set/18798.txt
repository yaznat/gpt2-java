What's a good custard recipe?<|endoftext|>4 In 1 Creamy Custard Drizzle Dip Dressing Or Sauce

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Pineapple juice
• Orange juice
• Lime juice
• Eggs
• Cream cheese

Steps:
1. Mix sugar, cornstarch & salt
2. Gradually mix in the fruit juices & stir to combine
3. Cook in top of double boiler over simmering water for 20 min, stirring often
4. Remove 1 / 4 cup of hot mixture & whisk into beaten eggs
5. Return mixture to pan & cook 5 min more
6. Beat hot mixture into cream cheese & refrigerate till well-chilled
