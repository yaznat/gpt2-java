What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>A Date With Rum Cake Vegan

Ingredients:
• Applesauce
• Baking powder
• Flour
• Brown sugar
• Ginger
• Cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Dates
• Dark rum
• Mandarin orange
• Soymilk
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Chop dates finely until you have 1 cup, then add to them enough rum to saturate them
2. Mix together the applesauce and baking powder in a small bowl then set aside
3. In a large bowl, combine ingredients from flour to salt and set aside
4. Add to the bowl containing the flour mixture the applesauce mixture, dates, oranges, oil and soy milk
5. Spoon batter into pan and bake in a 350 degree oven for 30-35 minutes
6. Cool and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
