Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Braised Lamb Shanks With Guinness Barley

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt & pepper
• Lamb shanks
• Oil
• Mixed vegetables
• Guinness stout
• Diced tomatoes with basil and oregano
• Beef stock
• Water
• Pearl barley
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180c season flour with salt and pepper, then coat shanks
2. Heat oil in a large casserole dish
3. Brown shanks on all sides and remove from dish
4. Empty mixed veggies into pot and heat for 5 minutes
5. Add beer and stir to loosen sediment on the bottom of dish
6. Stir in tomatoes, stock and bring to the boil
7. Add barley, stir well
8. Return shanks to the dish
9. Transfer casserole to the oven
10. Cook for about 2 hours, add more water if needed
11. Spoon onto deep plates to serve and sprinkle with parsley<|endoftext|>
