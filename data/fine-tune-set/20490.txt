Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>Asian Pea Pods Casserole Microwave

Ingredients:
• Frozen pea pods
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Green onions
• Butter
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Ground ginger
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Microwave pea pods in 1-qt
2. Covered glass casserole for 2 minutes, or until thawed
3. Stir in remaining ingredients
4. Microwave, uncovered, 4 minutes, or until pea pods are crisp tender
5. Stir again, adjust seasonings if necessary, and serve<|endoftext|>
