Ingredients:
• Leg of lamb
• Of fresh mint
• Lemons, juice of
• Garlic
• Ground coriander
• Cayenne pepper
• Paprika
• Ground cumin
• Fresh ground pepper
• Olive oil
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. Place the lamb in a non-aluminium container
2. Combine all the ingredients for the marinade and rub over the meat
3. Cover and refrigerate overnight or let stand for 2-3 hours at room temperature
4. Bring the lamb to room temperature, if refrigerated
5. Heat the oven
6. Remove the lamb from the marinade and sprinkle with salt
7. Cook the meat until rare, remove from the oven and allow a few minutes before carving
8. Sprinkle with a little chopped mint before serving<|endoftext|>
