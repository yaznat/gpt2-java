Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Whole wheat bread cubes
• Butter
• Apples
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Ground cinnamon
• Raisins
• Brown sugar
• Ground nutmeg
• Cooking spray
• Corn syrup

Steps:
1. Spray a shallow 1 1 / 2 quart baking dish with cookin spray
2. In a dish, combine apples, bread cubes and raisins
3. In a medium bowl, beat together eggs, milk, brown sugar, vanilla, cinnamon and nutmeg until sugar is dissolved
4. Pour over apple mixture
5. Cover and refrigerate several hours or overnight
6. Preheat oven to 350f bake pudding until knife inserted in center comes out clean, about 45-55 minutes
7. Serve hot, warm or chilled
8. Cinnamon sauce: in glass bowl or measuring cup, stir together corn syrup and cinnamon
9. Microwave on high 1 minute
10. Pour over pudding<|endoftext|>
