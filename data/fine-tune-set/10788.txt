Please give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>3 P S Salad Peas Pickle Peanut

Ingredients:
• Sweet peas
• Sweet gherkins
• Sweet pickle juice
• Unsalted peanuts
• Mustard
• Sugar
• Cream

Steps:
1. Drain peas and place in bowl
2. Put peanuts and pickles in food processor and grind until gravel-like
3. Add to bowl
4. Mix in the pickle juice
5. Combine mustard, sugar, and cream together
6. Allow to sit 10-15 minutes for the sugar to dissolve
7. Mix in with the 3 p's
8. Serve at room temperature<|endoftext|>
