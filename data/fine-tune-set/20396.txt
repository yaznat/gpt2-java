Ingredients:
• Orzo pasta
• Olive oil flavored cooking spray
• Eggplant
• Fresh tomatoes
• Flour
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Butter
• Beef bouillon
• Cream
• Dried oregano
• Cinnamon
• Parmesan cheese
• Kalamata olive

Steps:
1. Spread orzo in bottom of 13-inch x 9-inch pan coated with olive oil flavored cooking spray
2. Lightly coat both sides eggplant slices with cooking spray and saut in batches over medium heat in a large skillet until lightly browned on both sides
3. Set aside
4. Meanwhile, dust tomato slices with flour and, in a separate pan, fry in batches in 1 / 4 inch oil until lightly browned on both sides
5. Set aside
6. Re-spray skillet you cooked eggplant in and saut onion slices with garlic until soft and golden
7. Spread half the tomatoes in a layer over the orzo, then half the onion-garlic mixture, then half the eggplant slices
8. Repeat layers
9. Make sauce: melt butter and blend in flour until smooth
10. Add bouillon slowly, stirring constantly, until thickened
11. Add cream, oregano and cinnamon
12. Simmer very gently for about 1 minute
13. Pour sauce over casserole
14. Sprinkle casserole with the parmesan cheese and scatter chopped olives over the top
15. Bake at 400f about 15 minutes until top is crispy brown<|endoftext|>
