Ingredients:
• Tofu
• Salt and pepper
• Soy sauce
• Ginger
• Sesame oil
• Fettuccine pasta
• Vegetable oil
• Mushrooms
• Broccoli

Steps:
1. Season the tofu with salt and pepper
2. In a small bowl, combine the soy sauce, 1 tablespoon ginger and the sesame oil
3. In a large pot, bring 4 quarts salted water and the remaining ginger to a boil
4. Add the pasta and cook until al dente
5. Drain, reserving 2 cups of cooking water
6. Return the pasta to the pot
7. Meanwhile blanche the broccoli
8. In a large nonstick skillet, heat the vegetable oil over medium-high heat
9. Dd the tofu in a single layer and cook, undisturbed, until browned on one side, about 5 minutes
10. Repeat turning until all sides are browned
11. Transfer cooked tofu to a plate
12. Add the mushrooms to the skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned about 5 minutes
13. Add the broccoli, mushrooms, ginger-soy mixture and reserved pasta water to the pasta and toss to coat
14. Divide the pasta and broth among 4 bowls
15. Top with tofu<|endoftext|>
