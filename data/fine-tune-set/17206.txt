Give me a tea recipe.<|endoftext|>Another Southern Iced Tea

Ingredients:
• Water
• Family-size tea bags
• Sugar

Steps:
1. In a 2 qt pot, bring about 5 cups of water to a full boil
2. Add the four family size tea bags and remove from heat
3. Set your timer for 5 minutes
4. During this time add sugar to gallon sized tea pitcher
5. When 5 minutes are up, add tea to sugar in pitcher and press gently on tea bags to remove left in liquid and stir
6. Discard tea bags
7. While stirring, add rest of water to pitcher filling to about two or three inches from top
8. (watching color, i do about three inches from top
9. Pour over a tall glass of ice and enjoy the goodness!<|endoftext|>
