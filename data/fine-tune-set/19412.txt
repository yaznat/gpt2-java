Can you give me a donut recipe?<|endoftext|>Sweet Cider Donuts

Ingredients:
• Baking mix
• Egg
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Apple cider
• Vanilla extract
• Flour

Steps:
1. Preheat oil in deep fryer to 375 degrees f
2. Beat egg, sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, cider and vanilla together
3. Add flour and baking mix
4. On floured surface, knead dough several times and roll to 1 / 2-inch thick
5. Cut with donut cutter and drop into hot oil
6. Cook until golden brown
7. Drain on paper towel
8. Frost or roll in a cinnamon-sugar mixture<|endoftext|>
