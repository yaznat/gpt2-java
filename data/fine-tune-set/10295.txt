Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Ground ginger
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Salt
• Crystallized ginger
• Dark brown sugar
• Vegetable shortening
• Unsalted butter
• Egg
• Light molasses
• Turbinado sugar

Steps:
1. Combine first 6 ingredients in medium bowl
2. Whisk to blend
3. Mix in crystallized ginger
4. Using electric mixer, beat brown sugar, shortening and butter in large bowl until fluffy
5. Add egg and molasses and beat until blended
6. Add flour mixture and mix just until blended
7. Cover and refrigerate 1 hour
8. Preheat oven to 350f
9. Lightly butter 2 baking sheets
10. Using wet hands, form dough into 1 1 / 4-inch balls
11. Place balls on prepared sheets, spacing 2 inches apart
12. Chill cookies in refrigerator for 10 minutes
13. Remove cookies on sheets from the refrigerator and place in oven
14. Bake cookies until cracked on top but still soft to touch, about 12 minutes
15. Do not overbake
16. Cool on sheets 1 minute
17. Carefully transfer to racks and cool
18. Can be made 5 days ahead
19. Store airtight at room temperature
20. These freeze well for up to 2 months, stored airtight<|endoftext|>
