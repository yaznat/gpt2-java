Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Low Fat Mc Donald S French Fries

Ingredients:
• Salt
• Russet potato
• Extra virgin olive oil

Steps:
1. Bring water to boil in a saucepan over high heat
2. Add one teaspoon of salt
3. Preheat oven to 400f
4. Peel potatoes and slice into 1 / 4 inch fries
5. Weigh the potatoes and measure out 9 ounces
6. Transfer potatoes to boiling water and cook exactly 2 1 / 2 minutes
7. Drain in colander and let cool
8. Transfer cooled potatoes to a mixing bowl and add oil and 1 / 2 teaspoon of salt
9. Mix gently to coat
10. Spread in a single layer on a non stick baking sheet
11. Bake for 20-25 minutes until crisp and golden
12. Turn every five minutes during the last half of cooking
13. Add the remaining salt if desired
14. Chef alexander states that this recipe is equal to a medium order of fries and has 5 grams of fat<|endoftext|>
