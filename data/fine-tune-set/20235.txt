What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Cha Traop Dot Cambodian Eggplant Pork Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Eggplants
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic cloves
• Ground pork
• Shrimp
• Fish sauce
• Sugar
• Pepper
• Scallions

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Prick eggplants with a fork, then put on baking sheet and bake for 45 minutes
3. Allow to cool slightly, then peel and discard the skins
4. Mash the flesh with a fork until smooth
5. Set aside
6. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
7. Add the garlic until golden
8. Stir in the pork and shrimp, breaking up any clumps
9. Add the fish sauce, sugar and ground pepper
10. When well mixed, add the eggplant and continue stir-frying until warmed through
11. Add the scallions, stir well, and remove from heat and serve over rice<|endoftext|>
