Give me a recipe for a taco.<|endoftext|>Carne Asada Tacos

Ingredients:
• Flank steaks
• Vinegar
• Soy sauce
• Olive oil
• Cumin
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Chili powder
• Paprika
• Onion powder
• Oregano
• Limes
• Garlic cloves
• Corn
• Cheese
• Guacamole
• Sour cream
• Pico de gallo

Steps:
1. In a gallon size ziploc bag, combine all ingredients with flank steak and mix really well
2. Let marinate for at least 6 hours - overnight if you can
3. Grill steak until desired degree of doneness
4. Let sit for at least 5-10 minutes! once the meat has cooled, slice across grain into small chunks
5. Serve on warmed tortillas with toppings of your choice! enjoy!<|endoftext|>
