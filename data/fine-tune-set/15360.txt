Ingredients:
• Canned chick-peas
• Dried apricot
• Shallot
• Salt
• Ground ginger
• Ground cumin
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground red pepper
• Ground turkey
• Cooking spray
• Multi-grain hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Prepare grill
2. Place chickpeas in a food processor
3. Pulse 3 times or until chopped
4. Combine chickpeas, apricots, and next 7 ingredients
5. Divide mixture into 6 equal portions, shaping each into a 1 / 2-inch-thick patty
6. Place patties on a grill rack coated with cooking spray
7. Grill 6 minutes on each side or until a meat thermometer registers 165
8. Remove from grill
9. Let stand 5 minutes
10. Place buns, cut sides down, on grill rack
11. Grill 1 minute or until toasted
12. Place 1 patty on bottom half of each bun
13. Top each serving with 1 tablespoon tzatziki sauce and top half of bun<|endoftext|>
