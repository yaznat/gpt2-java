Give me a rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Amazing Crab Rice Cakes

Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Minute white rice
• Eggs
• Crabmeat
• Parmesan cheese
• Butter

Steps:
1. Bring broth to a boil in small saucepan
2. Stir in rice
3. Cover
4. Remove from heat
5. Let stand 5 minutes
6. Fluff with fork
7. Beat eggs lightly in medium bowl
8. Add rice, crabmeat and cheese
9. Mix well
10. Divide and shape mixture into 8 patties
11. Let stand 5 minutes
12. Melt butter in large skillet on medium heat
13. Add patties
14. Cook 5 minutes on each side or until golden brown on both sides and heated through<|endoftext|>
