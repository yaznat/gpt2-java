Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Mushroom stems and pieces
• Water
• Instant beef bouillon
• Pepper
• Enchilada sauce
• Green chilies
• Red chilies
• Corn chips
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Dip container of 3 cups beef-mushroom frozen mix in hot water just to loosen
2. In 2-quart saucepan, heat frozen mix, water and enchilada sauce to boiling
3. Reduce heat
4. Cover and simmer, stirring frequently, until mix is thawed, about 20 minutes
5. Preheat oven to 350f rinse green and red chilies to remove seeds
6. Cut chilies into small pieces
7. Arrange 2 cups of the corn chips, and half each of the meat mixture, chilies and cheese in ungreased baking dish, 10x6x1 3 / 4 inches
8. Repeat with remaining meat mixture, chilies and cheese
9. Sprinkle with remaining corn chips
10. Bake uncovered until casserole is bubbly, 30 to 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
