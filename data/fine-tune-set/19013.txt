Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Salt
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Milk
• Nutmeg
• Unbaked pie shells

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Scald milk
3. Set aside
4. In large mixing bowl slightly beat eggs, blend in sugar, salt, vanilla and almond extract
5. Gradually stir in scalded milk
6. Do not overbeat as this will create an unwanted foam
7. Pour into prepared pie shell and lightly sprinkle freshly grated nutmeg over the top
8. Bake 30-35 minutes or until knife inserted in center of pie comes out clean
9. Remove from oven and let cool 30-60 minutes before slicing and serving<|endoftext|>
