What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Amish Casserole

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Lawry's seasoned salt
• Black pepper
• Dark brown sugar
• Condensed tomato soup
• Condensed cream of celery soup
• Noodles
• Cheese

Steps:
1. In a skillet, brown the ground beef and onion
2. Drain off any excess grease
3. Season with lawry's seasoned salt and black pepper
4. According to taste
5. Add the tomato soup and brown sugar to the cooked beef, and stir
6. Cook the noodles according to pkg
7. Directions, and then drain them
8. Add the cream of chicken soup to the hot-cooked noodles, and stir
9. In a casserole baking dish
10. Add the beef / soup mixture
11. Top with the slices of velveeta cheese
12. Add the noodle / soup mixture ontop of the velveeta cheese slices
13. Cover, and bake for 35 minutes in a 350 degree oven<|endoftext|>
