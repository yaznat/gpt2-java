Give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Omelette In A Bag

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Ziploc bag
• Cheese
• Ham
• Onion

Steps:
1. Start the water boiling in a pot
2. Put two eggs in the quart sized bag
3. Insert other ingredients
4. Close bag tightly and shake the ingredients together
5. When the water boils, put the omelette in the water for 13 minutes<|endoftext|>
