What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Apple Curry Tuna Melt

Ingredients:
• Tuna
• Apple
• Mayonnaise
• Green onion
• Dijon mustard
• Curry powder
• Salt and pepper
• Whole wheat bread
• Walnuts
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, add the first 7 ingredients
2. Stir to combine
3. Spread 1 / 4 cup of the mixture on each slice of toasted bread
4. Sprinkle evenly with walnuts
5. Top with a slice of cheese
6. Place tuna melts on a baking sheet
7. Broil 5 inches away from the heat until the cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
