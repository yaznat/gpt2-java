What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Apple Filled Sweet Potato

Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Brown sugar
• Margarine
• Nutmeg
• Orange peel
• Apple
• Pecans
• Pecan halves

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375f
2. Prick sweet potatoes with a fork to allow steam to escape
3. Bake for 35 to 45 minutes or until fork tender
4. Cut thin lenghwise slice from top of each sweet potato
5. Scoop out inside, leaving a thin shell
6. Set shells aside
7. In large mixer bowl place hot sweet potatoes and remaining ingredients, except apple, chopped pecan and pecan halves
8. Beat at medium speed, scraping bowl often until well mixed and no lumps remain
9. By hand, stir in apple and chopped pecans
10. Place shells on a 15x10x1" baking pan
11. Fill shells with sweet potato mixture
12. If desired, garnish each potato with pecan halves
13. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes or until heated through
14. Spoon hot sweet potato mixture into serving bowl
15. If desired toast pecan halves and use to garnish sweet potato mixture<|endoftext|>
