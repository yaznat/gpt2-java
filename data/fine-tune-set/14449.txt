Ingredients:
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Green beans
• Onion
• Cheddar cheese
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Stirring frequently, fry slivered onions in the olive oil till evenly dark colored
2. In microwave safe casserole dish, stir together can of cream of chicken soup and drained fried onions
3. Microwave on high for 1 minute
4. Add contents of can of drained green beans to casserole and stir together
5. Microwave on high for 2 minutes
6. Stir in cheddar cheese
7. Microwave 1 minute
8. The casserole is now ready to serve<|endoftext|>
