Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Half-and-half cream
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Cooked ham
• Green pepper
• Sweet red pepper
• Green onions
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. In a large bowl, beat eggs and cream until light and fluffy
3. Stir in cheese, ham, green pepper and onions
4. Butter a 9" x 9" oven proof casserole dish and pour in the egg mixture
5. Bake in preheated 400 f oven for 25 minutes or until golden brown on top
6. Let sit for 5 minutes before cutting
7. Cut into pieces and serve hot<|endoftext|>
