Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breast
• Oil
• Chicken broth
• Tomato sauce
• Taco seasoning mix
• Corn
• Minute rice
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Saute chicken in oil until done
2. Stir in chicken broth, tomato sauce, taco mix & corn
3. Bring to a boil,
4. Cover and simmer for 5 mins
5. Stir in rice, cover and remove from heat
6. Sprinkle cheese on top and serve with tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
