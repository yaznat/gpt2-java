Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Water
• Tomato sauce
• Potatoes
• Corn
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Hot pepper sauce

Steps:
1. Brown beef and onion well, drain off excess grease
2. Add remaining ingredients
3. Bring to a boil, then simmer for about 1 hour or until potatoes are cooked<|endoftext|>
