Ingredients:
• Meatballs
• Grape jelly
• Chili sauce
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. In a pot combine the grape jelly with the chili sauce
2. Add in the meatballs and simmer for about 45 minutes uncovered or until the sauce has thickened
3. If you are making my parmesan meatball recipe use 2-1 / 2 to 3 pounds lean ground beef or use half beef and ground pork shape
4. Shape into about 1-inch balls
5. Place on a baking sheet
6. Bake in a 350 degree f oven for 20-25 minutes or until cooked through
7. Place in the prepared sauce<|endoftext|>
