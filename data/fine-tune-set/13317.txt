Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Butter
• White wine
• Lemon, zest of
• Lemon juice
• Salt
• Pepper
• Whipping cream
• Mushrooms
• Parmesan cheese
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Melt butter in frying pan, add chicken and saute over medium heat for 10 minutes until chicken is browned on both sides
2. Remove chicken to a 9 x 13-inch oven-proof dish
3. Drain butter from pan and add wine, lemon peel, and lemon juice
4. Cook for 1 minute, then add salt, pepper and cream
5. Stir and heat, but do not boil
6. Pour sauce over chicken and sprinkle with mushrooms and parmesan cheese
7. Bake, uncovered, at 325 degrees for 30 minutes
8. Garnish with parsley<|endoftext|>
