What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Baked Chili Chicken

Ingredients:
• Chicken coating mix
• Chili powder
• Garlic powder
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
2. Combine coating mix, chili powder, and garlic powder
3. Use to coat chicken as directed on package
4. Place on baking sheet
5. Bake for 20 minutes or until chicken is cooked through
6. Serve with rice and a salad<|endoftext|>
