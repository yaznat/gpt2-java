Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Breadsticks
• Parmesan cheese
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Cut bacon in half length wise
2. Wrap half sliced bacon around bread stick diagonally
3. Combine parmesan and 2 tablespoons of parsley [if wanted] in a shallow dish [set aside]
4. Place 4 bread sticks on double layer of paper towel in microwave oven for 2 to 3 minutes until bacon is cooked through
5. Immediately roll bread sticks in parmesan and parsley
6. Do to remaining bread sticks<|endoftext|>
