Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Frosted flakes
• Cinnamon
• Confectioners' sugar
• Chocolate syrup
• Cherries
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot to 350
2. While the oil is heating, take the frosted flakes, and put them into a ziploc bag
3. Smash them until they are pea-size pieces
4. Pour the frosted flakes into a large bowl
5. Take the scoop of ice cream, and drop it into the bowl
6. Roll the ice ceam around in the cereal until it is well coated, pressing the cereal into the ice cream
7. Take the cereal covered ice cream ball, and drop it into the hot oil for 30-45 seconds
8. Take the ice cream out of the oil, plate, and dust with cinnamon and confectionary sugar for a mexican fried ice cream, or drizzle chocolate on ice cream and top with a cherry for a chinese fried ice cream
9. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
