Ingredients:
• Oil
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a heavy large frying pan heat the oil to 365 degrees
2. Oil is hot enough if a small test piece of dough dropped in the oil begins cooking almost immediately and rises to the top
3. Mix together dry ingredients in a large bowl
4. Gradually add warm milk and mix to make a soft dough
5. When the dough has pulled together, cover and let sit in a warm place for 10 to 15 minutes
6. Break off a golf ball sized piece of dough and pat it out flat in your hand, no thicker than 1 / 4 inch
7. Place 2 or 3 flats at a time into the hot oil and when they begin to bubble flip them over and fry until they're a nice light golden brown
8. Remove and drain on paper towels then serve hot
9. You can make larger flats
10. Just make sure they're no thicker than 1 / 4" and fry one at a time<|endoftext|>
