What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Venison Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground venison
• Onion
• Celery
• Green pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Chili seasoning mix
• Cumin powder
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Cilantro
• Canned tomatoes
• Salsa
• Pinto beans

Steps:
1. Brown venison with 2 to 3 tablespoons olive oil, with the chopped onions, celery, green peppers, and garlic
2. Put chopped tomatoes and salsa in a large pot
3. Add meat mixture, beans and all the spices
4. Adjust spices to suite your taste
5. Simmer for 1 to 2 hours
6. Serve with corn bread
7. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
