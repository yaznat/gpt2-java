Ingredients:
• Dark sesame oil
• Broccoli florets
• Large shrimp
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh shiitake mushroom
• Sugar
• Rice vinegar
• Ketchup
• Cornstarch
• Red chili paste
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Cooked rice
• Green onion tops

Steps:
1. Heat 1 teaspoon sesame oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add broccoli
3. Saut 4 minutes
4. Add 1 teaspoon sesame oil, shrimp and minced garlic
5. Saut 4 minutes or until the shrimp are done
6. Combine sugar, rice vinegar, ketchup, cornstarch and red chili paste
7. Add to skillet
8. Cook 1 minute, stirring constantly
9. Stir in sliced water chestnuts
10. Serve over rice
11. Sprinkle with thinly sliced green onion tops<|endoftext|>
