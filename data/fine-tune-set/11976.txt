Ingredients:
• Plain yogurt
• Cornflour
• Oil
• Onions
• Fresh ginger
• Fresh garlic
• Green chilies
• Chicken thighs
• Ground coriander
• Ground cumin
• Ground turmeric
• Ground cayenne pepper
• Canned tomatoes
• Fresh coriander
• Salt
• Garam masala

Steps:
1. Stir cornflour into yoghurt
2. Put aside
3. In a large saucepan, heat oil over medium-high heat
4. Add onions and saute until beginning to colour
5. Reduce heat to medium and saute onions until dark golden brown
6. Stir in ginger, garlic and chillies
7. Saute for a further two minutes
8. Increase heat to medium-high and add chicken
9. Brown well
10. Reduce heat to medium
11. Sprinkle ground coriander, cumin, turmeric and cayenne pepper over chicken
12. Mix well and cook for 3 minutes
13. Add tomatoes, yogurt mixture, 1 / 2 cup fresh coriander and salt
14. Mix well, cover and bring to boil
15. Reduce heat to low and simmer until chicken is cooked
16. Turn off heat
17. Stir in garam masala and three tablespoons fresh coriander
18. Garnish with remaining coriander and serve with rice and / or indian breads<|endoftext|>
