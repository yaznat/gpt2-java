What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Artichoke Breakfast Burritos

Ingredients:
• Bulk italian sausage
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Jalapeno
• Tomatoes
• Artichoke hearts
• Salt and pepper
• Eggs
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. In a skillet over medium heat, cook sausage until done
2. Drain grease
3. Add olive oil to sausage
4. Add onion, garlic and jalapeno
5. Cook until onion is soft
6. Add tomatoes and artichokes
7. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 3-4 minutes
8. Season with salt and pepper
9. Add eggs and cook, stirring until eggs are done
10. Heat tortillas
11. Fill middle of each tortilla with artichoke mixture
12. Sprinkle with cheese
13. Fold ends, then roll up tortilla<|endoftext|>
