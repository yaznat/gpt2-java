Give me a pie recipe.<|endoftext|>30 Minute Pot Pie

Ingredients:
• Miracle whip
• Milk
• Flour
• Onions
• Meat
• Cheese
• Pie shell

Steps:
1. Mix salad dressing, milk and flour
2. Microwave on high 2-3 minutes until sauce thickens
3. Add onion, veggies, meat and cheese to sauce
4. Remove frozen pie shell from tin tray
5. Pour filling into empty tray
6. Place frozen pie shell over the top of filling, oinch edges to seal in juices
7. Score pastry and place on cookie sheet
8. Bake pie in preheated 400 f oven for 30 minutes or until pastry becomes golden brown<|endoftext|>
