Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Cocoa powder
• Flaked coconut
• Almond extract
• Whole almond
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Instructions:
2. Sprinkle almond extract on coconut and toss to blend
3. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt
4. Layer ingredients in order given in a 1-quart wide-mouth canning jar
5. Press each layer firmly in place before adding next ingredient
6. Attach the following instructions on a gift tag:
7. Almond joy brownies:
8. Makes 2 dozen brownies
9. Empty jar of brownie mix into large mixing bowl
10. Use your hands to thoroughly blend mix
11. Add:
12. 3 / 4 cup butter or margarine, melted
13. 4 large eggs, slightly beaten
14. Mix until completely blended
15. Spread batter in a sprayed 9 x 13-inch baking pan
16. Bake at 350 f for 30 minutes
17. Cool completely in pan, then cut into 2-inch squares<|endoftext|>
