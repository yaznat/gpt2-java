Give me a salad recipe.<|endoftext|>Amanda S World Famous Krab Salad

Ingredients:
• Imitation crabmeat
• Miracle whip
• Celery
• Scallion
• Lemon juice
• Dill weed
• Garlic salt
• Lemon pepper

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, flake crab meat
2. Drizzle lemon juice and stir until mixed
3. Set aside for ten minutes to let lemon juice soak inches
4. While waiting, coarsely chop celery and scallions and set aside
5. Returning to crab, sprinkle dill weed, lemon pepper and garlic salt over crab
6. Add chopped celery and scallions to bowl
7. Stir in miracle whip to desired consistency
8. Refrigerate up to 8 hours for best taste<|endoftext|>
