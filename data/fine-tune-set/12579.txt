Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Basic Beef Stew Very Hearty

Ingredients:
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Warm water
• All-purpose flour
• Seasoning salt
• Garlic salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Paprika
• Stew meat
• Worcestershire sauce
• Bay leaves
• Onion
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Celery

Steps:
1. In a small dish, dissolve beef bouillon in warm water
2. In a plastic zipper bag, mix together flour, salts, pepper, and paprika
3. Add meat and shake to coat meat
4. Spray slow-cooker with non-stick cooking spray and add contents of bag
5. Add worcestershire sauce and place bay leaves on top
6. Slowly pour beef broth over beef mixture and add onion, potatoes, carrots, and celery
7. Cover and cook on low setting for 10-12 hours or on high setting for 4-6 hours
8. Stir to combine ingredients before serving<|endoftext|>
