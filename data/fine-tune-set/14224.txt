Give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Comfort Tofu Loaf

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Bread
• Onion
• Garlic powder
• Tomato ketchup
• Nutritional yeast
• Tamari soy sauce
• Italian herb seasoning
• Black pepper
• Carrot
• Celery

Steps:
1. First, preheat oven to 350f
2. Put all the ingredients in a food processor, except for the carrot and celery
3. Blend until smooth
4. Add the carrot and celery, and blend in the tofu mixture
5. Put in a loaf pan and bake for one hour
6. You can also put ketchup on top of the loaf<|endoftext|>
