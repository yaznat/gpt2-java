What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>Adrienne S Chocolate Chip Cake

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Sugar
• Flour
• Eggs
• Lemon juice
• Baking powder
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Margarine

Steps:
1. In small mixing bowl, beat 5 egg whites until high and stiff, place in the refrigerator
2. In large mixing bowl, mix up everything but the chocolate chips (use an electric mixer
3. Wait until mixture is well blended and then add the chips
4. Then, mix in the egg whites by hand using a spatula
5. Be gentle
6. Pour the entire mixture in a bundt pan, well greased up with something like pam original with flour-i find that works best
7. Bake at 350 degrees for approximately one hour, the testing stick should come out slightly damp
8. Place cake on a cooling rack, still right side up in bundt pan
9. After 20 minutes, turn over onto the serving platter and let cool
10. Any mistakes can be covered up with powdered sugar
11. Avoid drafts-it can fall
12. This cake's prep time is only 15 minutes and it serves a lot of people
13. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
