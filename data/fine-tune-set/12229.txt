Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Onion
• Celery
• Chicken broth
• Sour cream
• Velveeta cheese
• White pepper
• Salt
• Liquid smoke
• Cheddar cheese
• Chives
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Peel potatoes and cut into large chunks
2. Put potatoes in soup pot along with onion, celery, and chicken broth
3. If there is not enough broth to cover the veggies, add a little water
4. Cover, bring to a boil, and let simmer for approximately 20 minutes, until vegetables soften
5. If you like thicker soup, drain off some of the liquid
6. If you like thinner soup, leave it as is and proceed
7. Use a potato masher to mash up the spuds
8. Mash more or less depending on how creamy or chunky you like your soup
9. Add white pepper, salt, and liquid smoke to taste
10. Add sour cream and velveeta, heat and stir until cheese melts
11. Dip the soup into 4 bowls
12. Top each bowl with shredded cheddar cheese, chives, and bacon bits
