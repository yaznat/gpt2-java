Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Corn
• Green beans
• Potatoes
• Celery
• Onion
• Garlic
• Carrots
• Water
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Diced tomatoes
• Rice
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a large stockpot, brown ground chuck
2. When almost done, add onions, garlic, celery, and carrots
3. Drain meat
4. Add rest of the ingredients except rice
5. Bring to a boil and then simmer for about 1 hour
6. Add the rice and cook until done
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
