Ingredients:
• Smoked paprika
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Dried oregano
• Lean ground beef
• Cajun seasoning
• Hot sauce
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Garlic
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Hamburger buns
• Ranch dressing
• Roma tomatoes
• Bacon
• Arugula leaves

Steps:
1. Combine cajun seasoning ingredients in a sml bowl & mix well
2. Place beef, seasoning, hot sauce & garlic in a large bowl & mix until just combined
3. Form the mixture into 8 equally-sized patties
4. Place oil in a lrg skillet over med-heat
5. In batches as needed, place patties in hot skillet & cook until browned
6. Place a quarter of cheese on top of ea burger to melt
7. When cheese melted, set burgers aside & keep warm
8. Place buns split-side-down into same skillet until toasted
9. Spread dressing onto insides of warm toasted rolls
10. Layer bottom roll w / arugula leaves, add a burger, tomato slice & a halved bacon slice
11. Close w / top roll<|endoftext|>
