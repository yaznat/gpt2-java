Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Milk
• Egg
• Fresh blueberries
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Grease a 9-inch square pan
3. Set aside
4. In a medium mixing bowl, blend together 1 1 / 2 cups flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, oil, milk, egg and 1 cup blueberries
5. Beat thoroughly for 30 seconds and spread into prepared pan
6. Combine 1 / 3 cup flour, brown sugar, cinnamon and butter until crumbly
7. Sprinkle mixture evenly over batter and top with the remaining berries
8. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes or until tested done when wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean
9. Do not overbake
10. Cool slightly
11. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
