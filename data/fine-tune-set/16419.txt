Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Water chestnut
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Toothpick

Steps:
1. Mix soy sauce and brown sugar
2. Place drained waterchestnuts into soy sauce / brown sugar mixture
3. I will put this all into a ziplock bag or a container with a lid so i can"turn or roll" them about every half hour, making sure the sauce stays well mixed and waterchestnuts are getting well coated
4. Place into fridge and let sit for 2 hours, minimum
5. After a minimum of 2 hours, cut bacon in half
6. Wrap a waterchestnut with piece of bacon and stick with toothpick to hold in place
7. Place wrapped waterchestnuts in shallow baking dish
8. After all are wrapped, drizzle just a little bit of the soy sauce / brown sugar mixture over the wrapped chestnuts
9. Bake at 375 degrees until bacon looks"crisp, " usually about 20 minutes or so<|endoftext|>
