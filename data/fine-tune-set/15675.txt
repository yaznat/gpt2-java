Ingredients:
• Limes
• Tomatoes
• Avocado
• Cilantro
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Cooked shrimp
• Jalapeno
• Garlic clove
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. Finely grate rind from limes to measure 1 tbls
2. Juice limes to measure 1 / 4 cup
3. Place rind and juice in a large bowl
4. Add tomato and remaining ingredients this is where i also added a jalapeno -- chopped really fine!
5. Toss well to combine
6. Cover and chill for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Heat tortillas
8. Once again -- recipe says it will feed 6 -- embarrassed to say -- it didn't!
9. Update
10. I really enjoyed this mixture on a bed of lettuce! made a great salad!<|endoftext|>
