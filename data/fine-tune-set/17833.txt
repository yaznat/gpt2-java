Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs

• Quick-cooking oats
• Baking powder
• Baking soda

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, cream butter and sugar
2. Add eggs, bananas, apple and honey
3. Mix well
4. Combine dry ingredients
5. Stir into creamed mixture just until moistened
6. Fill paper-lined muffin cups two-thirds full
7. Bake at 350 degrees f for 15-18 minutes or until muffins test done
8. Cool in pan 10 minutes before removing to a wire rack<|endoftext|>
