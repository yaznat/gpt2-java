Ingredients:
• White onion
• Red onion
• Olive oil
• White beans
• Black beans
• Corn kernels
• Chopped tomatoes
• Chicken broth
• Relish
• Cumin
• Salt
• Celery

Steps:
1. In a large pot, heat oil and saut onions
2. Add rest through chili relish
3. Bring to a boil
4. Reduce heat & simmer 15-20 minute
5. Add cumin and salt to taste, add celery & simmer another 5 minute<|endoftext|>
