What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Muffins

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Swiss cheese
• Chives
• Egg
• Milk
• Dijon mustard
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Spray regular size muffin containers with cooking spray
3. In a bowl, sift together, flour, baking powder and salt
4. Stir in sugar, cheese and chives
5. Crumble the bacon, and add to dry ingredients
6. In a bowl combine the egg with milk, mustard and dijon
7. Stir just until blended, then mix into the dry ingredients just until combined
8. Spoon mixture into prepared muffin cups
9. Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until golden brown
10. Remove from oven
11. Cool 2 minutes
12. Remove from pans and serve warm<|endoftext|>
