Ingredients:
• Fresh ginger
• Fresh lemongrass
• Fresh lime juice
• Fish sauce
• Water
• Sweet chili sauce
• English cucumber
• Scallion
• Tomatoes
• Fresh jalapenos
• Fresh cilantro
• Of fresh mint
• Fresh basil
• Firm tofu
• Butter lettuce leaves

Steps:
1. I would strongly suggest to begin by using only 1 teaspoon of the fish sauce in this recipe and then adjust seasoning accordingly
2. For vegetarian version, omit the fish sauce
3. )
4. Dressing: puree the first 5 ingredients in a blender
5. Allow the mixture to stand at least 5 minutes or up to 1 hour
6. Stir in chili sauce
7. Strain the dressing into a small bowl, pressing down the solids with a spatula to release the liquids
8. Discard solids
9. Tofu: toss together the first 7 ingredients
10. Next add the tofu and salad dressing, tossing to coat well
11. For each serving, arrange 1 or 2 lettuce leaves on a plate
12. Divide the tofu mixture onto the lettuce leaves<|endoftext|>
