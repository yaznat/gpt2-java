Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Frozen blueberries
• Vanilla ice cream
• Milk
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Place the banana, blueberries, ice-cream, milk & maple syrup in a blender & blend until smooth
2. Pour among serving glasses top with extra blueberries and drizzle with a little extra maple syrup
3. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
