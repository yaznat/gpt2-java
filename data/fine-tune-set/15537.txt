What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Amazing Salmon Tacos

Ingredients:
• Salmon
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Olive oil
• Yellow onion
• Limes
• Diced tomatoes with green chilies
• Fresh cilantro
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Toss cubed salmon in cumin and chili powder
2. Set aside
3. Heat large skillet over medium and cook yellow onion 2 minutes, until it begins to soften
4. Turn heat up to medium high
5. Add salmon to skillet
6. Lightly sear salmon cubes for about 2-3 minutes, turning gently
7. Important: do not over cook the salmon
8. This juicy recipe will keep steaming the salmon
9. The salmon should still be firm in the middle of the cube when you proceed to the next step
10. Return heat setting to medium
11. Add canned tomatoes and chilies, cilantro and green onions
12. Gently incorporate and heat through
13. Squeeze quartered limes over the salmon mixture, give it a quick stir, and plate up the salmon
14. Serve with warm flour tortillas, quality salsa, sour cream, sliced avocado, and crisp romaine lettuce<|endoftext|>
