Ingredients:
• Butter
• Vegetable oil
• Confectioners' sugar
• White sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Lemon extract
• All-purpose flour
• Cream of tartar
• Baking soda
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees f
2. Sift together the flour, cream of tartar, baking soda and salt
3. Set aside
4. In a large bowl, cream together the butter, oil, confectioners' sugar and white sugar until well blended
5. Stir in the eggs, vanilla and lemon extracts
6. Gradually mix in the dry ingredients until well blended
7. Roll the dough into walnut sized balls
8. Place the cookies 2 inches apart onto the cookie sheet
9. Flatten cookies to 1 / 8 inch thickness using the bottom of a glass dipped in sugar
10. Colored sugar may be used if you do not plan to frost cookies later
11. Bake for 9 to 12 minutes in the preheated oven, or until the edges are golden brown
12. Allow cookies to cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes before removing to a wire rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
