Ingredients:
• Magic shell ice cream topping
• Graham cracker crust
• Maraschino cherries
• Vanilla ice cream
• Peanut butter cups

Steps:
1. Following package directions, drizzle half of the ice cream topping over the crust
2. Gently spread to coat bottom and sides
3. Freeze until firm
4. Meanwhile, set aside six cherries for garnish
5. Chop remaining cherries
6. In a large bowl, combine ice cream and chopped cherries
7. Spread into prepared crust
8. Sprinkle with peanut butter cups
9. Drizzle with remaining ice cream topping
10. Garnish with reserved cherries
11. Cover and freeze for 2 hours or until firm
12. Remove from freezer 15 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
