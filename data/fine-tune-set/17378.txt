Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Sour cream
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Almond extract
• Sliced almonds
• Flaked coconut
• Ground cinnamon
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f grease and flour 12-cup bundt pan or 10-inch tube pan
2. Combine 1 cup sugar, butter and eggs in large mixer bowl
3. Beat at medium speed, scraping bowl often, until creamy
4. Reduce speed to low
5. Add all remaining coffee cake ingredients
6. Continue beating, scraping bowl often, until well mixed
7. Combine all filling ingredients in small bowl
8. Spoon half of batter into prepared pan
9. Sprinkle with filling
10. Spoon remaining batter into pan over filling
11. Bake for 35 to 45 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
12. Cool completely
13. Invert cooled cake onto serving plate
14. Stir together all glaze ingredients in small bowl
15. Drizzle over cooled coffee cake
16. Garnish with sliced almonds
17. Cool completely
18. Cover
