Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Skim milk
• Part-skim mozzarella cheese
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Whisk together egg whites and skim milk in a medium bowl
2. Add salt or pepper to mixture if desired
3. Pour olive oil onto non-stick skillet and put on medium heat for a few seconds
4. Pour egg mixture into skillet and allow approximately 4 minutes to cook
5. Once the egg mixture is off white and there is no liquid in the center, add cheese to the top and allow it to melt
6. Once the cheese is melted, flip one side of the omelette to the other side, and voila!
7. Serve with toast, fruit, yogurt, or as-is<|endoftext|>
