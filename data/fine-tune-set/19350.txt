Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Tapioca starch
• Cocoa
• Sugar
• Xanthan gum
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Dry buttermilk
• Eggs
• Coconut oil
• Water
• Unsweetened cocoa
• Hot water
• Confectioners' sugar
• Vanilla
• Peanuts
• Sweetened flaked coconut
• Nonpareils

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Spray a 6 hole donut pan with non-stick cooking spray
3. In a medium bowl blend together the flour, tapioca starch, cocoa, sugar, gum, baking powder, salt, cinnamon and buttermilk powder
4. In a small bowl beat together the eggs, oil and 2 tablespoons water
5. Pour the mixture into the dry ingredients and mix until well combined
6. If mixture is to thick add in additional water until a smooth, thick, cake batter consistency is achieved
7. Spoon the mixture into the prepared pan, smoothing with a spatula
8. Place in pre-heated oven and bake for 10 to 12 minutes
9. Allow to rest in pan a minute or two and then turn out onto a wire rack to cool for a few minutes
10. In a small bowl, blend together the coconut oil, cocoa, 1 1 / 2 tablespoons hot water, sugar and vanilla
11. Stir until smooth adding in additional water as needed
12. Place the cooling rack with donuts on a cookie sheet and spoon the chocolate glaze over the donuts allowing the glaze to drip down sides
13. Immediately top with topping of choice
