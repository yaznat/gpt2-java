What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Baked Fish With A Cheese And Tarragon Crust

Ingredients:
• Sour cream
• Tarragon
• Green onion
• Garlic
• Tabasco sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Swiss cheese
• Parsley
• Fish steaks

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 500f
2. Lightly oil a baking sheet
3. Combine sour cream, tarragon, green onion, garlic, hot sauce, worcestershire sauce and pepper to taste
4. In another bowl, combine bread crumbs, both cheeses, and parsley
5. Dip fish in sour cream mixture then roll in bread crumb mixture
6. Place fish on baking sheet and bake for 12 minutes<|endoftext|>
