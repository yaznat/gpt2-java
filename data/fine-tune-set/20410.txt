What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Chicken Casserole

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Mayonnaise
• Plain yogurt
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Chicken broth
• White pepper
• Salt
• Lemon juice
• Onions
• Cooked rice
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Sliced almonds
• Celery
• Butter
• Corn flakes

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f degrees
2. Mix chicken, mayonnaise, yogurt, soup, broth, pepper, salt, lemon juice, onion, rice, water chestnuts, 1 c
3. Almonds, and celery together
4. Put into a lg
5. Buttered casserole dish
6. Mix remaining almonds, butter, and corn flakes together and top casserole with mixture
7. Bake for 35-45 minutes<|endoftext|>
