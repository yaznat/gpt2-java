Ingredients:
• Fresh blueberries
• Light brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Margarine
• Granulated sugar
• Egg
• Egg white
• Lemon zest
• Self-rising flour
• Reduced-fat milk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Grease and flour 13 x 9-inch caking pan
3. Toss 2 cups blueberries, brown sugar, and cinnamon in small bowl until berries are coated
4. Cream margarine / butter and granulated sugar with electric mixer at high speed until light and fluffy, about 4 minutes
5. Add egg and egg white and beat 2 minutes
6. Beat in lemon zest
7. Reduce speed to low
8. Add flour alternatively with milk, one-third at a time, stopping mixer occasionally to scrape bowl with rubber spatula
9. Do not overbeat! if you do, you will have a very though cake
10. Spread half of batter into pan and sprinkle with blueberry mixture
11. Spoon remaining batter on top, spreadinng evenly
12. Swirl through batter several times with knike, then top with remaining 1 / 2 cup of blueberries
13. Bake until toothpick inserted in center comes out with moist crumbs clinging, 40 to 45 minutes
14. Cool in pan on wire rack 15 minutes
15. Cut into 16 equal pieces
16. Serve and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
