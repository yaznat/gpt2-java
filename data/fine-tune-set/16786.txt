What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Healthy Strawberry Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Frozen strawberries
• Milk
• Soft tofu
• Frozen orange juice concentrate
• Ground flax seed
• Wheat germ

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in the blender
2. Process until smooth anad frothy
3. Pour into a tall glass, and drink immediately
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
