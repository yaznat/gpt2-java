Give me a chicken recipe.<|endoftext|>Adobo Chicken Pie

Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken breast
• Dry mustard
• Flour
• Cornmeal
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• 2% low-fat milk
• Canola oil
• Egg
• Black beans
• Sweet whole kernel corn
• Green chilies
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Chipotle chiles in adobo

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. In a large bowl
3. Mix mustard, flour, corn meal, sugar, baking powder, and salt
4. Stir in the milk, oil, and egg then mix until dry ingredients are just moistened
5. Chop 2 of your adobo peppers into little pieces and add them to the mix
6. Stir mix well to blend
7. Pour half the corn meal batter into an 8x8 glass baking dish and spread along the bottom
8. Gently press your mashed black beans on top of the corn meal batter in the dish
9. In a medium bowl
10. Mix sour cream, 1 / 2 cup of the corn, your third adobo chile, cheese, and green chiles
11. Stir well then spread on top of the black bean layer
12. Place shredded chicken on top of the sour cream layer
13. Finally, spread the other half of the corn meal batter on top
14. Bake for 25 minutes or until top is golden
15. Let cool for a couple minutes then serve!<|endoftext|>
