Give me a recipe for sushi.<|endoftext|>Sushi Now Sushi Rice

Ingredients:
• Long-grain white rice
• Rice vinegar
• Sugar
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cook rice according to package directions
2. Stir in remaining ingredients until combined
3. Cool to room temperature
4. I advise starting with just a teaspoon of salt and adding as needed as it depends on the vinegar that you have etc
5. That will determine how salty this rice will be
6. I have found playing around by adding bit by bit of the vinegar and sugar will give you the taste you're looking for<|endoftext|>
