What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>101 Super Cookies

Ingredients:
• Rice krispies
• Rolled oats
• Flaked coconut
• Chocolate chips
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Margarine
• Egg
• Oil
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Cream of tartar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Do not use beaters!
3. Combine rice krispies, oats, coconut and chocolate chips
4. Set aside
5. In a large bowl cream both sugars with margarine, add eggs then add oil and vanilla
6. Combine flour, baking soda and cream of tarter and stir into rice kripies mixture
7. Add to creamed mixture and mix until ll ingredients are blended
8. Drop or form into balls and place on a lightly greased cookie sheet, flattening with a fork
9. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until lightly browned<|endoftext|>
