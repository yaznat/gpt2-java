Ingredients:
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Butter
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Line an 8 x 8 inch baking pan with foil
3. Combine cocoa, sugar, vanilla, salt, and melted buttter
4. Mix with mixer until well combined
5. Add eggs one at a time
6. Add flour and mix until just blended
7. Fold in nuts
8. Spread evenly in lined baking dish
9. Bake at 325 degrees for about 28 to 30 minutes
10. Toothpick inserted in the center will be a little fudgy
11. Cool brownies completely
12. Lift from baking dish and remove the foil
13. Cut into squares and serve<|endoftext|>
