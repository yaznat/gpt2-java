Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Taco seasoning mix
• Tomato sauce
• Whole kernel corn
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Bisquick baking mix
• Milk
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Chopped tomato
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325f grease rectangular pan, 13- x 9- x 2-inches
2. Cook ground beef and onion in 10-inch skillet, stirring frequently, until beef is brown
3. Drain
4. Stir in dry seasoning mix, tomato sauce, and corn
5. Spoon into pan
6. Sprinkle with cheese
7. Stir bisquick mix, milk and eggs until smooth
8. Pour over beef mixture
9. Bake about 35 minutes or until knife inserted in center comes out clean
10. Cool 10 minutes before cutting
11. Serve with remaining ingredients<|endoftext|>
