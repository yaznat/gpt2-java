What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Beef In Crusty Rolls

Ingredients:
• Catsup
• Vinegar
• Lemon juice
• Chili powder
• Lemon pepper
• Jalapeno
• Tomato sauce
• Onions
• Garlic clove
• Worcestershire sauce
• Brown sugar
• Dry mustard
• Pepper
• Beef
• French rolls
• American cheese

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients except beef, rolls and cheese in 1-qt casserole
2. Cover and microwave on high util hot and bubbly, 2 to 3 minutes
3. Stir
4. Mix beef into sauce
5. Cover and microwave until beef is hot, 3 to 5 minutes fill rolls with beef mixture and cheese slices and serve<|endoftext|>
