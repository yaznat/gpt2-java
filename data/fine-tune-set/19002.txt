Ingredients:
• Heavy whipping cream
• Garlic cloves
• Egg yolks
• Parmesan cheese
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees
2. Butter six 4-ounce ramekins
3. Combine the cream and garlic in a heavy saucepan
4. Bring to a boil over medium heat
5. Reduce the heat to low and cook until the garlic is very soft, about 15 minutes
6. Strain the liquid through a strainer placed over a bowl
7. Press the garlic through the strainer and into the cream
8. Whisk to combine
9. Add the egg yolks one at a time, whisking continuously
10. Add the cheese, salt and pepper
11. Pour into the prepared ramekins
12. Place the ramekins in a baking dish and fill with water half way up the ramekins
13. Bake until set, 20 to 30 minutes
14. Let cool slightly before unmolding
15. Serves 6<|endoftext|>
