Ingredients:
• Butter
• Apples
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Flour
• Lumpia skins
• Oil
• Condensed milk
• Cream cheese
• Coffee

Steps:
1. For the creamy coffee sauce, combine all ingredients in a blender or food processor and blend until smooth
2. Chill
3. Melt butter / margarine in a pan
4. Add apples, and sugar and cook over moderate heat
5. Stirring occasionally until apples become tender
6. Add cinnamon and flour
7. Mix well
8. Cook until thickened
9. Set aside and let cool
10. Put about 1 to 2 tablespoons of apple filling in a lumpia wrapper
11. Roll up and seal well
12. Deep-fry in hot oil until golden and drain in paper towels
13. Drizzle with chilled sauce
14. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
