What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Arroz Brasileiro Rice With Tomatoes And Onions

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Long-grain rice
• Chicken stock
• Water
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Pepper
• Curry powder

Steps:
1. In a heavy 3-quart saucepan over medium heat, warm the olive oil for about a minute,
2. Add the onion and saut, stirring constantly, for about 5 minutes or until the onion is soft and transparent, but not yet brown
3. Pour in the rice and stir for about 3 minutes, until all the grains are coated with the oil
4. Add the boiling chicken stock and water to the rice
5. Add the tomatoes, curry powder, pepper and salt, and return the mixture to the boil, making sure to stir occasionally
6. Cover the saucepan and reduce the heat to a simmer
7. Cook for 20 minutes or until the rice has absorbed all the liquid
8. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
