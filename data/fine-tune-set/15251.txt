Ingredients:
• Bulk italian sausage
• Frozen hash brown potatoes
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Salt
• Pepper
• Sliced mushrooms
• Eggs
• Tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook sausage until no longer pink
2. Add potatoes, onion, green pepper, salt and pepper
3. Cook over medium-high heat for 18 to 20 minutes or until the potatoes are browned
4. Stir in mushrooms
5. Beat in eggs
6. Pour over the potato mixture
7. Arrange tomato slices on top
8. Sprinkle with cheese
9. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for 10-15 minutes or until eggs are completely set
10. Serve with sour cream and salsa if desired<|endoftext|>
