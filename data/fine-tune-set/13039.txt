Ingredients:
• Parmesan cheese
• All-purpose flour
• Paprika
• Salt
• Pepper
• Egg
• Milk
• Orange roughy fillets

Steps:
1. In a shallow bowl combine the parmesan cheese, flour, paprika, salt and pepper
2. In another bowl beat the egg and milk
3. Dip fish fillets into egg mixture then coat with parmesan mixture
4. Arrange in a greased 13x9x2-inch baking dish
5. Bake uncovered at 350f for 25-30 minutes or until fish flakes with a fork<|endoftext|>
