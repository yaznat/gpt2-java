Ingredients:
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Carrots
• Sliced mushrooms
• Crushed tomatoes
• Kidney beans
• Chili seasoning mix
• Crushed pineapple

Steps:
1. Throw all the ingredients in a crock pot
2. Stir
3. Cook on high for 6 hours
