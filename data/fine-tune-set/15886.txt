What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Chicken Burrito Soup

Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Water
• Taco seasoning
• Instant minced garlic
• Dried parsley flakes
• Frozen whole kernel corn
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Onion
• Cooked chicken breast
• Red kidney beans
• Stewed tomatoes
• Noodles

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, combine chicken broth, water, taco seasoning, garlic, & parsley flakes
2. Add corn, red& green peppers, onion, chicken, kidney beans, & undrained tomatoes
3. Mix well to combine
4. Bring mixture to a boil
5. Stir in uncooked noodles
6. Lower heat, cover& simmer for about 15 min or until vegetables& noodles are tender, stirring occasionally<|endoftext|>
