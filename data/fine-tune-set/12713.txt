What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Poor Mans Steak

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Milk
• Cracker crumb
• Pepper
• Salt
• Onion
• Mushroom soup
• Water

Steps:
1. Mix all together, except soup and water or milk, and shape into a narrow loaf
2. Let sit in refrigerator overnight, or for at least 8 hours, to blend flavors
3. Slice loaf and fry in a skillet on both sides until brown
4. Put slices in baking dish
5. Mix 1 can mushroom soup with 1 cup of water or milk, and pour over each piece
6. Bake at 325 f for one hour<|endoftext|>
