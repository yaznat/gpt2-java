Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Boneless pork butt
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Curry powder
• Ground coriander
• Ground cumin
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Ground ginger
• Cinnamon
• Bay leaf
• Salt
• Chicken broth
• Tomato paste
• Chunky peanut butter
• Plum tomatoes
• Green bell pepper
• Cilantro
• Unsalted peanuts

Steps:
1. In a large pot, heat 1 tablespoons of the oil on high heat
2. When hot, add the pork cubes and cook until soft
3. Stir in the garlic, curry powder, coriander, cumin, and crushed red pepper
4. Cook 1 minute
5. Add ginger, cinnamon, bay leaf, salt, chicken broth, and tomato paste
6. Bring liquid to a boil, cover and simmer on low for 45 minutes
7. Add peanut butter and blend well
8. Cook stew another 3 minutes, uncovered to blend
9. Stir in chopped tomato and bell pepper
10. Simmer 2-3 minutes, just until the vegetables soften but still retain shape
11. Before serving, taste to correct seasonings
12. You may wish to add a final splash of lemon juice to refresh the flavors<|endoftext|>
