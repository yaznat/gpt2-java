Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onions

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Slice each potato, crosswise, into 4 thick slices
3. Slice each onion, crosswise, into 3 thick slices
4. Put potatoes together, placing an onion slice between each of the potato slices
5. Wrap potato tightly in foil
6. Bake 1 hour<|endoftext|>
