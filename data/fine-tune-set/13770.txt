• Pork tenderloin
• Butter
• Apricot jam
• Green onions
• Dried mustard
• Cider vinegar

Steps:

2. Melt 1 tablespoon butter over medium-high heat in a large skillet
3. Saute pork about 2 minutes on each side
4. Remove pork from pan
5. Add 1 teaspoon butter, jam, onion, mustard and vinegar to pan
6. Cover and simmer 3 to 4 minutes
