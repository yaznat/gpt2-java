Ingredients:
• Asparagus
• Water
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Pepper
• Manchego cheese
• Eggs
• Lemon juice
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Wash the asparagus and trim them into 1-inch pieces
2. Bring water to a boil in a saucepan and add the asparagus pieces
3. Cook for about 2 to 3 minutes or until tender
4. Drain the asparagus and set aside
5. Heat the olive oil over low heat in a 12-inch skillet
6. Add the chopped onion and garlic and cook until tender
7. Stir in the asparagus, salt, pepper, and grated cheese
8. Reduce the heatt to low and pour the beaten eggs into the skillet
9. Do not stir the mixture
10. Cook, covered, until the omelette sets, around 15 to 20 minutes
11. Slice into four portions and sprinkle with lemon juice
12. Garnish with sliced tomato and serve<|endoftext|>
