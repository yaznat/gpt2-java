Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Potatoes
• Cornstarch
• Coarse sea salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Paprika
• Cayenne pepper
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 440 f
2. Combine the garlic and oil in a small pan, warming it on medium heat until the garlic is fragrant, about 1 minute
3. Transfer 5 tablespoons of the garlic infused oil to the baking sheet, coating it well
4. Cut the fries into wedges and drop them into simmering water for about 5 or 6 minutes
5. Lay on paper towel or napkin to dry
6. Add the potatoes to the pan with the garlic mixture, add onion powder if using, and toss to coat
7. Combine the cornstarch, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and cayenne in a small bowl
8. Sprinkle over the hot potatoes and toss well to coat
9. Arrange the potatoes in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet and bake, turning once, until deep golden brown and crisp, 30 to 40 minutes
10. Sprinkle with chopped parsley
11. Serve with ketchup, mayo or sour cream
12. I like to mix a little ketchup and mayo together, sometimes adding a touch of bbq sauce
