Ingredients:
• Beef ribs
• Salt
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Chili powder
• Paprika
• Cumin
• Cayenne pepper
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Ketchup
• Molasses
• Water
• Worcestershire sauce
• Vinegar
• Butter
• Onion
• Dry mustard
• Tabasco sauce

Steps:
1. Mix all the dry spices for the rub
2. Rub on beef ribs and slow cook in a 250f oven for 3-4 hours
3. Whisk all the bbq sauce ingredients together
4. Baste with bbq sauce and grill for 10-15 minutes<|endoftext|>
