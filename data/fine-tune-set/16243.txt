What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Gouda And Turkey Wrap With Bacon

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Flour tortillas
• Gouda cheese
• Meat
• Apple
• Baby greens

Steps:
1. Heat a large nonstick frying pan over medium heat
2. Add bacon
3. Cook, turning often, until bacon is crispy, 6-8 minutes
4. Drain on kitchen paper, and set aside
5. Arrange wraps on a clean work surface
6. Place 1 / 4 of the cheese and turkey slices in the center of each wrap
7. Top turkey with 1 / 4 of the apple slices, 1 piece of bacon each and 1 / 4 of the salad greens
8. Roll wraps, folding in sides, to enclose filling<|endoftext|>
