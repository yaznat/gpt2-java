Can you give me a cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Cocktail Wieners

Ingredients:
• Little smokies sausages
• Grape jelly
• Seafood cocktail sauce

Steps:
1. In a saucepan over medium heat, combine the cocktail sauce and grape jelly
2. Mix in the little smokie sausages
3. Cook 5 minutes, or until the sausages are hot
4. An optional way would be to put all ingredients into a crock pot on low and slowly bring up to temperature<|endoftext|>
