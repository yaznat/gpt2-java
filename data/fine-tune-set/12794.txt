What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Balsamic Steak Sauce

Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Butter
• Sugar
• Shallots

Steps:
1. Mince shallots
2. Place butter in pan over medium heat and saute shallots until clear
3. Add sugar and balsamic vinegar
4. Bring to boil and stir constantly until sauce thickens slightly
5. Immediately ladle over freshly grilled steaks<|endoftext|>
