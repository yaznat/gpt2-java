Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Cabbage
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Ground mustard

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until crisp
2. Remove to paper towels and drain, reserving 1 tablespoon drippings
3. Stir-fry cabbage in drippings for 5 minutes
4. Add garlic powder, salt, mustard and bacon
5. Cook and stir until heated through<|endoftext|>
