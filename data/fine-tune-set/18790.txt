Give me a recipe for pudding.<|endoftext|>Bird S Apple Bread Pudding Flambe

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Egg yolks
• Whipping cream
• Milk
• Sugar
• French bread
• Butter
• Raisins
• Red delicious apples
• Brandy
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f wisk all custard ingredients
2. Set aside
3. In frying pan, melt butter
4. Add raisins, sugar, and apples
5. Cook on med heat for about 5 minutes
6. Add applejack brandy and carefully flambe till alcohol is cooked off
7. Then set pan aside to cool
8. Place bread slices in bottom of 9 x 13 pan
9. Place apple mixture over bread
10. Pour custard over the apple mixture
11. Place in water bath and bake for 45 minutes
12. Cool 20 minutes to serve warm, 3 hours in fridge to serve later or to serve chilled
13. Dust lightly with powdered sugar before serving<|endoftext|>
