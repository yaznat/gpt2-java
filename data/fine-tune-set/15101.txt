Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic powder
• Mozzarella cheese
• Onion powder
• Spaghetti sauce
• Cooked macaroni
• Pepperoni

Steps:
1. Brown beef and onion in oil
2. Cook until onion is tender
3. In a bowl, combine spaghetti sauce, garlic and onion powders
4. Mix well and combine with the macaroni and the beef and onion mixture
5. In a 2-quart casserole, layer the meat / macaroni mixture with the shredded cheese and pepperoni
6. Ending with the cheese
7. Bake, uncovered, in a preheated 350 degree oven for 30 minutes or until hot and bubbly<|endoftext|>
