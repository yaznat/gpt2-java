Ingredients:
• Cheese-filled tortellini
• Fresh spinach
• Green onions
• Ripe olives
• Red bell pepper
• Baby shrimp
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• Caesar salad dressing
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Cook and drain tortellini as directed on package
2. Rinse with cold water
3. Drain
4. Place spinach in boiling water
5. Cover and cook 1 minute
6. Drain
7. Immediately rinse with cold water
8. Drain
9. Place tortellini, spinach, onions, olives, bell pepper and shrimp in large glass or plastic bowl
10. Toss lightly
11. Mix remaining ingredients
12. Toss with tortellini mixture
13. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour to blend flavors but no longer than 24 hours<|endoftext|>
