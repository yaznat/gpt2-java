Please give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>Apple Mustard Glazed Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients:
• Syrup
• Soy sauce
• Apple cider vinegar
• Grainy mustard
• Pork tenderloin

Steps:
1. Stir first 4 ingredients together to make the glaze
2. Pour half the glaze over the pork and stand for 15-30 minutes
3. Roast or grill pork at 350 degrees for 20-25 mins per side
4. Heat remaining glaze on stovetop or in microwave
5. Drizzle cooked pork with remaining glaze or serve on side as a dip<|endoftext|>
