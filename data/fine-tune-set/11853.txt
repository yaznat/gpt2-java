Ingredients:
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Cinnamon stick
• Green cardamoms
• Cloves
• Bay leaf
• Ginger paste
• Garlic paste
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Ground coriander
• Salt
• Green pepper
• Canned tomatoes
• Prawns
• Green chilies
• Fresh coriander

Steps:
1. Fry onion in oil or ghee until just tender
2. Add cinnamon stick, cardamoms, cloves and bay leaf
3. Fry for one minute and then add ginger and garlic pastes
4. Add chilli, cumin, coriander and salt
5. Fry for half a minute
6. Add chopped green pepper and tomatoes, then bring to boil
7. Turn down heat and simmer for 10 - 15 minutes
8. Add prawns and let them 'poach' in the sauce for three minutes or so, or until done
9. Add chopped green coriander leaves and chopped chillies
10. Serve with rice<|endoftext|>
