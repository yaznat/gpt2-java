Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Ice Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Hersheys chocolate syrup
• Whipping cream
• Graham cracker pie crusts

Steps:
1. Combine syrup and sweetened condenced milk
2. Set aside
3. Whip cream until stiff peaks form
4. Slowly fold in milk mixture
5. Pour into pie shells and freeze<|endoftext|>
