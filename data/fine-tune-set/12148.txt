What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Apple And Dried Fruit Spice Pie

Ingredients:
• Granny smith apples
• Dried cherries
• Dried cranberries
• Golden raisin
• Dark raisin
• Dried currant
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Ground ginger
• Allspice
• Kosher salt
• Orange
• Lemon
• Light brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Cornstarch
• Dark rum
• Double crust pie crusts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f with an oven rack in lowest position
2. Working in 2 batches, & using a food processor, pulse apples into pea-size pieces, then transfer the apples to a large mixing bowl
3. Put cherries, cranberries & raisins in food processor & whirl just until fruit starts to break up, then add this fruit mixture to the apples
4. Stir in currants, spices, salt, zests, sugars, cornstarch
5. Pour this mixture into a 9
6. 5-inch deep dish pie pan with a bottom crust in it
7. Prepare a lattice top with 1 / 2-inch to 3 / 4-inch wide dough strips & complete a lattice top using them
8. Trip excess dough flush with edge of the pie pan, then use floured fingers to press edge of pie & seal strips to bottom edge
9. Bake about 1 1 / 2 hours, or until golden brown & bubbling, & if necessary, put a a drip pan under the pie for the last 30 minutes
10. Cool to room temperature before serving
11. Refrigerate any leftovers<|endoftext|>
