Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Applesauce Muffins

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Applesauce
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground allspice
• Ground cloves
• Walnuts
• Cinnamon sugar

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, cream butter and sugar
2. Add eggs and vanilla
3. Mix well
4. Stir in applesauce
5. Combine the flour, baking soda and spices
6. Stir into creamed mixture
7. Fold in nuts
8. Fill greased or paper-lined muffin cups three-fourths full
9. Bake at 350 for 20-25 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean
10. Cool for 5 minutes before removing from pans to wire racks
11. Sprinkle with cinnamon-sugar if desired
12. Yield: about 2 dozen<|endoftext|>
