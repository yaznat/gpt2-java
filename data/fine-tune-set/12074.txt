Please give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Almost Apple Pie For One

Ingredients:
• Apple
• Flour
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Light margarine

Steps:
1. Slice and core apple
2. Combine flour, spices & sweetener in bowl
3. Toss apple in dry mixture
4. Pour in pan
5. Drop margarine in dots on top
6. Bake at 350 for 20 minutes or until apples are soft<|endoftext|>
