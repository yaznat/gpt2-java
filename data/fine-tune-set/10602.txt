Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Pignoli

Ingredients:
• Pine nuts
• Clarified butter
• Heavy whipping cream
• Butter
• Ground nutmeg
• Salt
• Fresh ground white pepper
• Angel hair pasta

Steps:
1. Toast pine nuts slowly in a skillet, watching carefully not to burn
2. Remove from skillet when nuts are tan in color
3. Cook pasta in boiling water until al dente, drain
4. Combine heavy cream, clarified butter, 2 1 / 3 tablespoons butter, nutmeg, salt and pepper in a saucepan
5. Heat thoroughly
6. Add pasta to the heated sauce
7. Let sauce reduce to a creamy consistency
8. Top with nuts and serve<|endoftext|>
