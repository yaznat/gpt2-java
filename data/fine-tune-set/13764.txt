What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Apricot Glazed Pork Kabobs

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin
• Apricot preserves
• Ground ginger
• Orange liqueur
• Butter

Steps:
1. Stir together preserves, ginger, liqueur and butter, simmer in a small saucepan until butter is melted
2. Place pork cubes in heavy plastic bag, pour 3 / 4 cup apricot mixture over to coat
3. Marinate at least 30 minutes
4. Thread pork onto 4-6 skewers that have been soaked in water for 60 minutes, grill over hot coals 10-12 minutes, turning occasionally and basting with marinade
5. Heat remaining apricot sauce to boiling and serve alongside kabobs<|endoftext|>
