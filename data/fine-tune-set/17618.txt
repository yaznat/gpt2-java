Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>A Pail Full Of Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• Boiling water
• Natural bran
• Brown sugar
• White sugar
• Oil
• Molasses
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• All-bran cereal
• Wheat germ

Steps:
1. Mix boiling water and bram and let stand
2. Cream oil, sugars and eggs
3. Add buttermilk, bran and molasses
4. Mix dry ingredients together
5. Fold dry ingredients into wet ingredients
6. Pour into ice cream pail and store in fridge for at least 24 hours prior to using
7. Bake at 400 for 20-25 minutes
8. Do not stir mix each time you use it
9. It will darken slightly on the surface<|endoftext|>
