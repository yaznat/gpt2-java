What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Antipasto Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Rotelle pasta
• Pitted ripe olives
• Green pepper
• Hard salami
• Red onion
• Parmesan cheese
• Parsley
• Italian dressing

Steps:
1. Cook macaroni according to the package
2. Drain, and then rinse with cold water until it is cool
3. In a large bowl, combine the remaining ingredients
4. Add macaroni, and toss well
5. Chill, or serve at room temperature<|endoftext|>
