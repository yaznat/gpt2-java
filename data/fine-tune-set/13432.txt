Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Adobo Beef Tacos With Pickled Red Onions

Ingredients:
• Steaks
• Corn tortillas
• Queso fresco
• Fresh cilantro
• Red onion
• White wine vinegar
• Salt
• Honey
• Smoked paprika
• Adobo seasoning
• Ground chipotle chile pepper

Steps:
1. Combine pickled red onions ingredients in medium bowl
2. Stir well
3. Cover and refrigerate while preparing beef
4. Combine seasoning ingredients in small bowl
5. Press evenly onto beef steaks
6. Cover and refrigerate 15 minutes to 2 hours
7. Place steaks on grid over medium, ash-covered coals
8. Grill, covered, 10 to 14 minutes for medium rare to medium doneness, turning once
9. Remove from grill
10. Let stand 5 minutes
11. Meanwhile drain pickled onions well
12. Carve steaks into thin strips
13. Top tortillas evenly with steak, queso fresco and pickled onions
14. Sprinkle with cilantro, as desired<|endoftext|>
