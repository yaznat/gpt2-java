Ingredients:
• Baby spinach
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh lime juice
• Light brown sugar
• Soy sauce
• Red onion
• Steak
• Fresh cilantro
• Unsalted peanuts

Steps:
1. Wash the spinach and spin dry
2. Place the spinach in a large salad bowl and set aside
3. In a small bowl, mix 2 tablespoons of the olive oil, garlic, lime juice, brown sugar, and soy sauce
4. Set aside
5. In a large cast-iron skillet, heat 1 tablespoon of the olive oil over med-highheat
6. When the oil is hot but not smoking, add the onion and saute for 2 minutes, stirring constnatly
7. Transfer the hot onion to the salad bowl on top of the spinach
8. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil to the pan
9. Sear the steak until med-rare, about 3 minutes per side
10. Remove the pan from the heat, immediately transfer the steak to a cutting board, and let it rest
11. Let the skillet cool for a few minutes
12. Add the soy-lime mixture to the pan and turn the heat to med-high
13. Deglaze the pan by stirring constantly and loosening any bits of steak that cling to the bottom
14. Decrease heat to low to keep the sauce warm
15. Slice the meat as thin as possible, cutting against the grain
16. Arrange the meat slices over the onion and spinach
17. Drizzle with the warm sauce and sprinkle with the cilantro and peanuts
18. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
