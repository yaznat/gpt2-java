Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Veggie shreds cheese
• Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
• Eggs
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Ground sage
• Ground coriander
• Oregano
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Red onion
• Fresh ground pepper
• Adobo seasoning
• Ketchup
• Sea salt
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Thaw meat in advance and place in a large mixing bowl
3. Stir in bread crumbs, sage, coriander, adobo, oregano, pepper and salt
4. Use hands to mix in eggs, ketchup, soy sauce, soy cheese and red onion divide mixture in half and form into 2 loaves
5. Place loaves side by side in a medium sized lasagna pan
6. Use fingers to open the top of the loaves and sprinkle in the garlic and add 1 / 2 a tbs of olive oil to each loaf and squeeze closed
7. Close loaves with hands and top with ketchup if desired
8. Cook at 350 for 1 hour
9. Let stand 5-10 minutes and serve warm!
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
