Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Burrito Sauce

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Flour
• Chicken broth
• Tomato sauce
• Garlic salt
• Green chili peppers

Steps:
1. In a skillet, beat butter and saute onion
2. Blend in flour
3. Gradually add broth, stirring to keep smooth
4. Add tomato sauce, garlic salt and chili
5. Cover and simmer 20 minutes
6. Spoon over your favorite burrito<|endoftext|>
