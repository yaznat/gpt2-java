What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Baked Whole Fish

Ingredients:
• Fish
• Lemons
• Onions
• Celery
• Salt and pepper
• Onion salt
• Lemon juice
• Bacon
• Butter

Steps:
1. Soak the fish in cold, salted water for 15-30 minutes
2. Drain and dry slightly
3. Preheat oven to 400
4. Season cavity with lemon slices, celery, onion, salt, and pepper and 2 tbs lemon juice
5. Close the fish and place bacon strips on top or around fish
6. Sprinkle with salt, pepper, onion salt and remainder of lemon juice
7. Cover with pats of butter and bake until flesh is white and flakes apart with fork
8. The fish may be broiled at the end to brown the bacon
9. Cut into 1 1 / 2 inch wide sections and serve with lemon wedges
10. Serves 2-4, depending on size of fish<|endoftext|>
