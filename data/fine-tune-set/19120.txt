Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Pepper Cinnamon Honey With Fruit And Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Honey
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Cinnamon stick
• Apricots
• Vanilla ice cream
• Cookies

Steps:
1. Heat the honey, pepper, and cinnamon stick in a small pot over low heat
2. When ready to serve, add the fruit to the honey mixture and toss
3. Put a scoop of ice cream into each serving dish and top with the honey mixture
4. Garnish with cookies and serve<|endoftext|>
