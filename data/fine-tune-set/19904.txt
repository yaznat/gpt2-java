What's a recipe for an omelette?<|endoftext|>Apple Walnut And Brie Omelette For Two

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Granny smith apple
• Walnuts
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Water
• Brie cheese

Steps:
1. In a 10 in non stick skillet melt 1 t
2. Of the butter over medium heat
3. Add the apples, walnuts, cinnamon, nutmeg and saute for 3 or 4 minutes
4. Remove from skillet and keep warm
5. Melt remaining butter while whisking the eggs with water
6. Pour into skillet
7. Tilt and rotate pan as the egg cooks
8. Add the apple-walnut mixture to one side of the omlette, top with cheese and fold uncovered portion of omlette over top of the apple-walnut and cheese mixture
9. Reduce heat to low and let set for 1-2 minutes
10. Using a plastic spatula cut omlette in half and serve<|endoftext|>
