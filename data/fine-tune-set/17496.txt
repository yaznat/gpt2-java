Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Crisco
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Black walnut flavoring
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Black walnut

Steps:
1. Mix as for cake
2. For filling: mix together the ingredients
3. Spread 1 / 2 batter in long flat pan
4. Sprinkle 1 / 2 filling over this
5. Pour remaining batter over, spread rest of filling on top
6. Spoon 1 / 2 cup melted butter on top
7. Bake 350f for about 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
