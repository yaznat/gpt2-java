Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Cauli Fried Rice Low Carb

Ingredients:
• Raw cauliflower
• Unsalted butter
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Green onions
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Grate the cauliflower using the medium sized holes of a grater or grater attachment of the food processor
2. With your hands squeeze out as much water as you can
3. Over medium heat, melt the butter in a large, heavy frying pan and add the garlic
4. Saute until the garlic sizzles
5. Add the grated cauliflower, sprinkle with salt and stir and fry until its tender-crisp, about 5-8 minutes, the length of time will depend on the cauliflower
6. Stir in the green onion and pepper
7. Check the seasoning and serve<|endoftext|>
