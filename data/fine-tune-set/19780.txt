Give me a fries recipe.<|endoftext|>Crunchy Baked Fries

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Vegetable oil
• Seasoning salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Peel potatoes and cut them into evenly sliced "fry" wedges
2. Set oven at 475 degrees
3. Place potatos in large bowl and cover with warm tap water for 10 minutes
4. Meanwhile, coat 18 x 12 non stick baking sheet with 4 tablespoons of oil, and sprinkle evenly with 3 / 4 teaspoon of seasoned salt and 1 / 4 teaspoon of pepper
5. Drain potatoes, and dry them completely on a triple layer of paper towels
6. Wipe out empty bowl, return potatoes, and toss with remaining 1 tablespoon of oil
7. Arrange potatoes in a single layer on baking sheet
8. Cover tightly with foil, and bake 10 minutes
9. Remove foil, and return to the oven for another 10-15 minutes
10. In the last 5 minutes, check for doneness by using a spatula to check for a golden brown color underneath
11. Turn the fries, and return to the oven for a golden color on the flip sides
12. Dab with a paper towel to remove excess oil, and sprinkle with seasoned salt and pepper to taste
13. Yum!<|endoftext|>
