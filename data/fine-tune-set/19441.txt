Give me a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>1 Bowl 1 Person Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Potato
• Butter
• Milk
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place the potato on a pot and fill it with water
2. Put it on the stove and wait for the potato to be tender
3. Drain the water and place the potato in the same pot
4. With a fork mash the potato, it will look dry don't you worry
5. Put salt and pepper, butter and milk, mix it all and watch the creaminess appear
6. You don't even have to serve it on a dish, you can pig out on it in the same pot!
7. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
