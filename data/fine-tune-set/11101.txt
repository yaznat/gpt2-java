Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Awesome Breakfast Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Bagels
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Cheddar cheese
• Avocado
• Cream cheese
• Chives
• Butter
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Cook bacon according to instructions on package then set aside
3. Meanwhile mix cream cheese and chives and set aside
4. Once oven is ready, butter bagels and sprinkle with garlic
5. Bake for 4 minutes
6. Remove and set aside
7. Add 1 tbl of butter to a pan and cook eggs according to your taste
8. Once eggs are done, start layering your sandwich
9. I "butter" the bagel with cream cheese mixture first, then place the egg, then criss-cross the 3 slices of bacon, then add a slice of cheese and finally a slice of avocado
10. Finally while the oven is still warm i put the sandwich in to melt the cheese or i just microwave for 25 seconds<|endoftext|>
