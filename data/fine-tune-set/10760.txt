What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>Sweet Broccoli Salad

Ingredients:
• Broccoli
• Bacon
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Red onion
• Vinegar
• Sugar
• Mayonnaise
• Miracle whip

Steps:
1. Combine first 4 ingredients in a large bowl and mix
2. Combine remaining ingredients to make dressing
3. Pour dressing over broccoli mixture and mix well
4. Refrigerate
5. If this is going to sit a while before serving, i suggest you wait to add the bacon until just before you serve to keep the bacon crisp<|endoftext|>
