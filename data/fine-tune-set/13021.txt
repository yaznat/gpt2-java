What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Baked Fish With Parmesan Sour Cream Sauce

Ingredients:
• Orange roughy fillets
• Sour cream
• Parmesan cheese
• Paprika
• Salt
• Pepper
• Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Place filets in single layer in a lightly greased 13x9" pan
2. Stir together sour cream, parmesan cheese, paprika, salt and pepper
3. Spread mixture evenly over filets
4. Sprinkle with bread crumbs, and drizzle with butter
5. Bake at 350 for 20-25 minutes or until fish flakes with fork<|endoftext|>
