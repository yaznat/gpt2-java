Ingredients:
• Oil
• Seasoning salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Chuck steaks
• Onions
• Garlic
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Water
• Tomato sauce
• Lemon juice
• Ketchup
• Brown sugar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Mustard powder

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large skillet or electric frypan over medium-high heat
2. In a bowl combine the water with tomato sauce, lemon juice, ketchup, brown sugar, worcestershire sauce and mustard powder until combined
3. Set aside
4. Season both chuck steaks with salt and pepper
5. Brown both sides of the steak in hot oil then remove to a plate
6. Add in onion
7. Saute for about 3 minutes
8. Add in garlic and crushed chili flakes
9. Cook stirring for another 1 minute
10. Pour in the sauce over and around the meat
11. Cover and simmer on low heat for about 1-1 / 2 hours or until meat is tender
