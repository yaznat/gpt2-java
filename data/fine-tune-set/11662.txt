Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Sour cream
• Vanilla
• Cool whip topping
• Graham cracker crust

Steps:
1. Beat cream cheese until smooth
2. Gradually beat in sugar
3. Blend in sour cream and vanilla
4. Fold in whipped topping
5. Blend well
6. Spoon into crust
7. Chill until set
8. At least 4 hours
9. Garnish with your favorite topping or eat plain<|endoftext|>
