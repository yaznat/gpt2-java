Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Baked Finnish Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Eggs
• Milk
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f
2. Pour the melted butter into a 9x13 baking pan
3. Beat eggs until foamy but not whippy, until well-blended
4. Beat with milk, flour, sugar and salt
5. Pour into pan with butter and bake 20-23 minutes
6. Great with jam, powdered sugar & cinnamon and maple syrup, really anything you want to top it with!<|endoftext|>
