Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Chive & onion cream cheese
• Salt
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Flatten chicken to 1 / 2 inch thickness
2. Spread 3 tbsp cream cheese over each chicken breast
3. Sprinkle with salt if desired
4. Roll chicken breasts up
5. Wrap each with bacon strip
6. Place seam side down in a greased 13x9 inch baking pan
7. Bake uncovered, at 400 degrees for 35-40 minutes, or until juices run clear
8. Broil 6 inches from the heat for 5 minutes or until bacon is crisp<|endoftext|>
