Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Bacon
• Oil
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt
• Cheese
• Thyme

Steps:
1. If sheep cheese is unavailable, use another sharp cheese, such as aged monterey jack
2. Heat the oven to 360f degrees
3. Peel and cut the potatoes in thin slices
4. Peel and thinly slice the onion
5. Cut the bacon lengthwise in strips and lightly fry them in a pan
6. Remove and allow to drain on a paper towel
7. Oil bottom and sides of a 9-inch by 12-inch glass baking dish
8. Place a layer of potatoes on the bottom
9. Lay julienned onions and a couple strips of bacon on top to create another layer
10. Repeat this process until all potatoes, onions and bacon have been used
11. Beat the eggs and milk together, and pour over the potato mixture
12. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and place in hot oven for 60 minutes
13. Remove from oven and sprinkle the grated cheese over the top
14. Return to oven uncovered for another 5-10 minutes
15. When cheese has melted, remove from oven and decorate with thyme if desired
16. Cut into squares and serve<|endoftext|>
