Ingredients:
• Dry red wine
• Dijon mustard
• Sea salt
• Black pepper
• Lamb shanks
• Garlic
• Onions
• Carrots
• Lemon, zest of
• Fresh rosemary

Steps:
1. Combine the red wine, dijon mustard, salt, pepper in a small dish
2. Place the lamb shanks in the crock pot
3. Scatter the remaining ingredients around and on top of the shanks
4. Cover and cook on high for 6 hours
5. Use tongs to reverse the position of the shanks, top to bottom
6. Reduce the setting to low and cook for an additional 6 hours
7. Using a slotted spoon to transfer the shanks to a serving platter
8. Skim the fat from the cooking juices, taste and add salt and pepper, if needed
9. Pour the juices over the shanks, and serve<|endoftext|>
