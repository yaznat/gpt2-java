What's a good smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Coconut Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Coconut milk
• Ice cube
• Pineapple
• Cinnamon
• Honey

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in blender container
2. Cover
3. Blend on high speed until smooth
4. Pour into tall glasses
5. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
