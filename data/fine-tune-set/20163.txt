What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Beef Mushroom Teriyaki Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Water
• Sugar
• Onions
• Vinegar
• Garlic
• Ground ginger
• Flank steaks
• Fresh mushrooms
• Oil
• Cabbage
• Bean sprouts
• Sliced water chestnuts

Steps:
1. Marinade: combine soy sauce, water, sugar, onions, vinegar, garlic and ground ginger
2. Add beef to marinade cover and refrigerate for two hours or overnight
3. In skillet, heat oil and saute mushrooms for 3 minutes
4. Add cabbage and saute for 2 minutes
5. Add beef and marinade and stir fry for 5 minutes
6. Add bean sprouts and water chestnuts and stir fry 5 minutes or until meat is done<|endoftext|>
