Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Eggs
• Dark brown sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Sour cream
• Vanilla
• Apple
• Cranberries
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Mix flour, baking soda, cinnamon and salt in a large bowl
2. In a separate bowl, whisk eggs, sugar, oil, sour cream and vanilla until smooth
3. Stir in apple, cranberries and walnuts
4. Fold into dry ingredients
5. Bake at 350f, for 20 to 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
