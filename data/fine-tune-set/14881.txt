Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Dee S Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Soy sauce
• Bacon
• Oil
• Onion
• Scallion
• Celery
• Chicken breast
• Pork
• Egg
• Frozen peas

Steps:
1. Add soy sauce to rice
2. Add more if you desire
3. , less if you want
4. In fry pan add bacon and fry
5. When cooked remove from pan
6. Add oil to bacon grease
7. Add meat to oil and cook until almost done
8. Add onions, scallions and celery
9. Fry until lightly browned
10. Add peas
11. Add rice
12. In separate bowl beat egg
13. Add to cooking rice and stir quickly to mix egg
14. Take off heat cover pan let egg cook<|endoftext|>
