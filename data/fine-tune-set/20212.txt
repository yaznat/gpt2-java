Ingredients:
• Lite olive oil
• Garlic
• Zucchini
• Green cabbage
• Onion
• Salt & pepper
• Sugar
• Celery seed

Steps:
1. Saute the garlic in the oil, do not allow to burn
2. Add veggies& stir fry for 2-3 minutes
3. Add seasonings and continue to stir fry for about 3 minutes<|endoftext|>
