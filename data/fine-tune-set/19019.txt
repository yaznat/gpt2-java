Ingredients:
• Milk
• Unsalted butter
• Sugar
• Farina
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Phyllo pastry

Steps:
1. Scald milk in a large saucepan
2. Stir in 4 tablespoons butter and sugar
3. Gradually add farina, stirring constantly, and bring mixture slowly to a boil
4. Remove from heat
5. Beat eggs in a bowl until they are fluffy and a light yellow
6. Slowly stir hot farina mixture into egg mixture
7. Add vanilla extract
8. Set aside to cool
9. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
10. Line a 9 x 12-inch baking pan with 1 sheet of phyllo with some of it hanging over the edge of the pan
11. Brush with butter
12. Be sure to keep unused phyllo pastry covered with plastic wrap or a damp towel
13. Layer 5 more sheets of buttered phyllo in the pan so that bottom and sides are completely covered
14. Pour in cooled custard
15. Cover with 1 sheet of phyllo
16. Brush with butter
17. Layer 5 more individually buttered sheets of phyllo on top, and fold in overhang
18. With a sharp knife, score through top layers of phyllo, marking off 2 1 / 4-inch squares or diamond shapes
19. Bake for 10 minutes
20. Reduce oven temperature to 350 degrees and bake for 45 minutes, or until golden<|endoftext|>
