Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Bell peppers
• Garlic cloves
• Cayenne
• Tomatoes
• Oregano
• French bread
• Butter
• Eggs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Basil

Steps:
1. Heat 4 t olive oil in a large heavy frying pan
2. Add the onions and cook over a gentle heat, stirring occasionally, for about 5 minutes till they are softened but not colored
3. Add the peppers, garlic and cayenne to the pan
4. Cook for another 5 minutes, stirring, then add the tomatoes, seasoning and oregano
5. Cook over moderate heat for 15-20 minutes till peppers are soft and most of the liquid has evaporated
6. Preheat oven to 400
7. Cut the bread in half lengthwise, trim off ends and cut into six equal pieces, brush with remaining olive oil
8. Place bread on baking trays and bake for 8-10 minutes till crisp and just turning golden
9. Heat the butter in a pan till it bubbles, add eggs and cook, stirring, till softly scrambled
10. Turn off heat and stir in the pepper mixture
11. Divide evenly among the pieces of bread and sprinkle with basil, if using
12. Serve hot or warm<|endoftext|>
