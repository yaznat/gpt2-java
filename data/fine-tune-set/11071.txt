Ingredients:
• Bulk italian sausage
• Mozzarella cheese
• Pita bread

Steps:
1. Brown the sausage
2. Allow to cool
3. Mix in the cheese
4. Stuff the pita bread halves with the sausage mixture
5. Wrap individually in foil
6. Heat in a 350f oven for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
