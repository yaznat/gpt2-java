Ingredients:
• Dried apricot
• Orange juice
• Apricot preserves
• Brown sugar
• Curry powder
• Olive oil
• Boneless pork chops
• Salt and pepper
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Place apricots in a bowl
2. Pour orange juice over apricots
3. Add preserves, brown sugar, and curry powder and stir gently
4. Set aside
5. Heat large skillet over medium high heat
6. Add oil
7. Cook chops about 3 minutes on each side or until browned
8. Season lightly with salt and pepper
9. Reduce heat to low
10. Add apricot and juice mixture
11. Cover and simmer about 5 minutes or until chops are done
12. Remove from heat and sprinkle with green onions<|endoftext|>
