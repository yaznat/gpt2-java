What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Beer Batter Fish

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Beer
• Eggs
• Ground pepper
• Onion powder
• Turmeric
• Fillets of sole
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Blend flour, beer, eggs, pepper, onion powder, and turmeric well, but do not over beat
2. Batter should "plop" from a spoon
3. If it is too thin, thicken with additional flour
4. Dry each piece of fish with paper towels
5. Heat oil to 375 'f in an automatic deep-fryer
6. Dip fish pieces in batter and deep-fry for 5-10 minutes
7. Turn occasionally
8. Drain fish on paper towels and serve immediately with tartar sauce<|endoftext|>
