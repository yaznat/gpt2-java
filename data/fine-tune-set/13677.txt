Generate a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>All In One Pork Chops Bake

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Shake-n-bake for pork
• Potato
• Italian dressing
• Barbecue sauce
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees fahrenheit
2. Coat chops with coating mix
3. Place chops in a foil lined 9 x 13 inch baking pan
4. Add potatoes around chops in pan
5. Drizzle dressing over potatoes, toss lighty to coat
6. Bake 30 minutes
7. Put 2 tablespoons of the barbeque sauce and 1 / 4 cup cheese on each chop
8. Return to oven and bake 5 minutes or until cheese is melted
9. Yummy served with a salad and applesauce<|endoftext|>
