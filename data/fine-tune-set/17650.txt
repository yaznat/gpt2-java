Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Amazing Apple Nut Muffins

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Apple juice
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• Sugar
• Flour
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Pecans
• Apple
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. In a large bowl, beat egg with juice / milk, oil and vanilla
3. Stir in flour, sugars, baking powder, and salt until moistened
4. Stir in nuts and apple
5. Fill large, paper-lined muffin cups with batter
6. Mix the last amount of sugar and cinnamon and sprinkle on muffins
7. Bake for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
