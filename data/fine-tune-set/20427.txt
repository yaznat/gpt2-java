Can you give me a casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Breakfast Casserole

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Sweet onion
• Eggs
• Frozen hash brown potatoes
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Cottage cheese
• Swiss cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook bacon and onion until bacon is crisp
2. Drain
3. In a large bowl, combine the remaining ingredients
4. Stir in bacon mixture
5. Transfer to a greased 13-in
6. X 9-in
7. Baking dish
8. Bake, uncovered, at 350 for 35-40 minutes or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean
9. Let stand for 10 minutes before cutting<|endoftext|>
