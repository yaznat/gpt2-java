Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Red onion
• Green bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Corn kernels
• Tomatoes
• Fresh cilantro
• Seasoned rice vinegar
• Apple cider
• Lemons, juice of
• Garlic cloves
• Ground cumin
• Coriander
• Red pepper flakes
• Chipotle chile in adobo

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine beans, onion, bell peppers, corn, tomatoes, and cilantro
2. In a small bowl, whisk together vinegars, lemon juice, garlic, cumin, coriander, red pepper flakes, and chipotle
3. I use a hand held immersion blender for this
4. Pour over salad and toss gently to mix<|endoftext|>
