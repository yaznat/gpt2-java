Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Celery ribs
• Onions
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic clove
• Tomato juice
• Chicken bouillon cubes
• Water
• Dry onion soup mix
• Balsamic vinegar
• Cauliflower
• Zucchini
• Cabbage
• Fresh spinach leaves
• Frozen cut green beans

Steps:
1. Over medium heat add oil, celery and onions to a large stock pot
2. Saute until tender
3. Add garlic, salt & pepper, cook an additional 2 - 3 minutes
4. Add tomatoe juice, bouillon cubes, water, onion soup mix and balsamic vinegar
5. Add cauliflower and cook for approximately 5 minutes
6. Stir in zucchini, cabbage
7. Cook for 5 - minutes
8. Stir in spinach and green beans
9. Cook until desired doneness is reached
10. You may add a bit of water to achieve the soupy consistency that you like<|endoftext|>
