What's a recipe for a smoothie?<|endoftext|>Banana Split Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Crushed pineapple
• Milk
• Strawberry
• Honey
• Ice cubes
• Whipped topping

Steps:
1. In a blender, combine the first five ingredients
2. Cover and process until smooth
3. Gradually add ice, blending until slushy
4. Pour into chilled glasses<|endoftext|>
