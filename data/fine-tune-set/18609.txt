Ingredients:
• Nonfat milk
• Brown sugar
• Reduced fat margarine
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Baking apple
• Rolled oats
• Vanilla extract
• Ground nutmeg
• Cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Lightly coat a 1 quart baking dish with oil spray
3. Combine the milk, sugar, margarine or butter, cinnamon, and salt in a 2 quart saucepan
4. Cook, stirring constantly, over high heat for 4 minutes to scald the milk
5. Add the apple and the oats
6. Continue cooking on high for 1 minuted
7. Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla
8. Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish
9. Bake for 15 minutes
10. Remove from the oven, stir thoroughly
11. Sprinkle the top with nutmeg
12. Return to the oven and bake for 15 more minutes
13. Serve warm with cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
