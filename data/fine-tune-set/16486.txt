Ingredients:
• 1% low-fat milk
• Malted chocolate drink mix
• French vanilla non-dairy coffee creamer
• Coffee
• Ice

Steps:
1. In a blender, blend on high: milk, drink mix, non-dairy creamer, coffee for about 2 minutes
2. Add ice and blend until smooth about 5 minutes
3. Serve immediately and enjoy<|endoftext|>
