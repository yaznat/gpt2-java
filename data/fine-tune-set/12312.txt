Ingredients:
• Butter
• Yellow onion
• Italian-style diced tomatoes
• Chicken stock
• Heavy cream
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Melt butter in 3qt sauce heavy bottomed sauce pan
2. Add onion and sautee for 2 minutes
3. Add the tomatoes, chicken stock, and cream to onions
4. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 5 minutes
5. Pour into food processor, blender, or use and imursion blender to mix until smooth
6. Pour into bowls, add your dollop of sour cream and enjoy<|endoftext|>
