Ingredients:
• Frozen blueberries
• Cinnamon
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Icing sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Toss blueberries with cinnamon
3. Set aside
4. In large bowl, cream butter and sugar for 1 minute
5. Beat in eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition
6. Stir in vanilla
7. Sift together flour, baking powder and salt
8. Fold into butter mixture alternately with milk, making three additions of each
9. Fold in blueberry mixture, leaving some cinnamon streaks
10. Spoon into greased 6-cup bundt pan, spreading batter higher at edges
11. Bake at 350 degrees for 40-45 minutes or until top of cake springs back when pressed
12. Let cool on rack 15 minutes
13. Invert and unmould
14. Sprinkle with icing sugar and serve warm<|endoftext|>
