What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Pineapple Fried Rice From Cooked Leftover Rice And Chicken

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Garlic
• Eggs
• White rice
• Fried chicken
• Spring onions
• Soy sauce
• Salt
• White pepper powder
• Sugar
• Pineapple
• Raisins
• Cashews

Steps:
1. Put oil in a wok
2. Add garlic
3. When it turns slightly brown, add eggs or vegetables, rice, chicken or vegetables, spring onions, salt, soy sauce, white pepper powder and sugar
4. Mix well
5. Add in raisins and pineapples
6. Mix well
7. Garnish with fried cashews and serve<|endoftext|>
