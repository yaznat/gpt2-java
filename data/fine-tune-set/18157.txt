Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Flour
• Skim milk
• Salt
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Apple

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425
2. Beat eggs vigorously 30 seconds
3. Stir in flour, milk, mix to blend well but do not over mix
4. Divide butter into 6 muffin tins, place in oven until melted
5. Place 1 tsp brown sugar in each muffin tin
6. Divide apple among muffins
7. Pour batter equally on top of apples
8. Bake for 10-15 minutes or until top is golden brown<|endoftext|>
