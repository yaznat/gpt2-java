What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>High Protein Diet Waffles

Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Splenda sugar substitute
• No-sugar-added jam

Steps:
1. Whisk ingredients into bowl and pour into a waffle maker
2. Pour batter so that it is contained within the grids
3. Top off with jam or other suggested topping<|endoftext|>
