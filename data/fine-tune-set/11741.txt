Ingredients:
• Butter
• Caster sugar
• Lemon zest
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Almond meal
• Pears
• Cinnamon
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Grease a 30cm flan tin or a pie plate that you can directly slice and serve from
2. Preheat oven to 18oc
3. Using electric beaters, beat butter, sugar and lemon zest until creamy and pale in colour
4. Continu beating and add the eggs, one at a time
5. Fold in the flour, baking powder and almond meal
6. Fold in the xanthan or guar gum at this point if you are using gluten-free flour that does not already contain a gum
7. Spoon half of this mixture into the flan tin
8. Spread to cover
9. Reserve the remaining half of the mixture
10. Arrange the pear slices over the base
11. Spread the pears out evenly
12. They usually need to be slightly overlapped
13. Sprinkle the cinnamon over the pears
14. Spread the remaining cake batter over the top of the pear layer and then scatter over the flaked almonds
15. Bake in the pre-heated oven for around 30 minutes or until tested as cooked with a skewer
16. Allow the cooked cake to stand for at least 10 minutes prior to serving
17. Serve warm with fresh cream, warm custard or ice-cream<|endoftext|>
