Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew Pressure Cooker

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Stew meat
• Potatoes
• Carrot
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Paprika
• Ground black pepper
• Flour
• Condensed beef broth
• Tomato soup
• Celery
• Kitchen bouquet
• Bay leaf
• Garlic clove
• Sliced mushrooms

Steps:
1. Cut stew meat into smaller pieces if desired
2. Place in pressure cooker
3. Sprinkle with worcestershire sauce, kitchen bouquet, and paprika
4. Sprinkle with flour
5. Layer potatoes, then carrots, then celery, then mushrooms on top
6. Add pressed garlic and bay leaf
7. Pour tomato soup and beef broth over all
8. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
9. Cook on high pressure for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
