Can you give me a quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Calzonedilla Italian Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Flour tortilla
• Part-skim mozzarella cheese
• Garlic powder
• Basil leaves
• Oregano leaves
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Parmesan cheese
• Tomato sauce
• Extra virgin olive oil

Steps:
1. Heat a large skillet on medium-high
2. Add olive oil
3. When skillet is heated, put tortilla in pan
4. Add mozzarella cheese, then herbs, spices and optional ingredients to one half of tortilla
5. Fold tortilla over onto ingredients
6. Use a lid from a large pot or skillet to cover
7. Cook for about 30-45 seconds, or until tortilla starts to get light brown spots
8. Flip tortilla, cover and cook for an additional 30-45 seconds, also until light brown spots
9. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
