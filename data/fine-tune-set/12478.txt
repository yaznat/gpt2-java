What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>African Peanut Stew Elephant Stew

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Beef
• Flour
• Nutmeg
• Chili powder
• Onions
• Garlic
• Water
• Red pepper
• Tomato sauce
• Chunky peanut butter
• Peas and carrots
• Steamed rice
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. In a large heavy kettle, heat 3 tablespoons oil
2. Roll beef cubes in flour, then brown in hot oil
3. While this is cooking, add nutmeg and chili powder
4. When meat is browned, add onion, garlic, water, red pepper to taste, and tomato sauce
5. Simmer until meat is tender, about 1 hour
6. If desired, add carrots and peas while stew is simmering
7. A few minutes before serving, heat peanut butter and remaining 2 tablespoons oil
8. Stir peanut butter mixture into beef stew over low heat
9. Let simmer 15 minutes, until peanut butter is absorbed
10. Serve over steamed rice with hot sauce on the side<|endoftext|>
