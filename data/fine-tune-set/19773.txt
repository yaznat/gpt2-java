Ingredients:
• Potato
• Milk
• Cornstarch
• Breadcrumbs
• Oil

Steps:
1. Cut potato into french fry shapes
2. Heat oil to 325f
3. Drop spuds into oil
4. Cook them in the oil for about 5 minutes, until they are semi-cooked and kind of floppy looking
5. Remove from oil, drain and cool
6. When spuds have cooled, re-heat oil to 375f
7. Toss milk and cornstarch into a plastic bag
8. Put bread crumbs into another bag
9. Take semi-fried spuds and put them into the milk bag
10. Remove from milk bag then place into the bead crumb bag, shaking to coat
11. Refry for about 2 or 3 minutes
12. Place on wire rack to drain<|endoftext|>
