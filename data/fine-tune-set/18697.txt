Ingredients:
• Short-grain rice
• Milk
• Dried apricot
• Vanilla bean
• Lemon zest
• Sugar
• Egg yolks

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 300 degrees f
2. In a covered heavy saucepan, bring the rice, milk, apricots, vanilla scrapings, and lemon zest to simmer over medium heat
3. Immediately reduce the heat to as low as possible, cover tightly, & simmer gently
4. Stirring occasionally, until the rice is very tender, 20-25 minutes & turn off
5. Stir the sugar into the cooked rice mixture
6. Stir in the egg yolks
7. Pour the rice pudding into a greased baking dish and bake for 20 mintues until slightly browned on the top
8. Remove from the oven and let cool
9. Chill until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
