What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Grilled Chicken And Black Bean Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Grilled chicken breasts
• Canned black beans
• Frozen corn
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Monterey jack and cheddar cheese blend
• Taco seasoning
• Flour tortillas
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Saute green peppers and onions in vegetable oil until softened
2. Combine cooked peppers and onion with beans, corn, cheeses, and seasoning
3. Spread some mixture over one half of a tortilla
4. Fold tortilla over to form a half moon and press around the edges
5. Lightly spray or brush a skillet with vegetable oil
6. Grill the quesadilla on medium to medium high heat
7. Each side takes about 3 minutes
8. Do the same for the reaming mixture and tortillas
9. It's easiest to use a pizza cutter to cut them into wedges
10. Serve with sour cream and salsa<|endoftext|>
