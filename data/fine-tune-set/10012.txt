What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Cheesy Chili N Rice Skillet

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Chili with beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Water
• White rice
• Velveeta cheese

Steps:
1. Brown meat in large skillet on medium-high heat
2. Drain
3. Add chili, tomatoes and water
4. Stir
5. Bring to boil
6. Stir in rice and prepared cheese product
7. Cover
8. Remove from heat
9. Let stand 5 minutes
10. Stir until prepared cheese product is melted<|endoftext|>
