What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>Bacon And Black Bean Smash

Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Bacon
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Black beans
• Salt and pepper
• Ground cumin
• Hot sauce
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Heat a medium skillet over medium high heat
2. Add extra-virgin olive oil and bacon
3. Render fat 2 minutes then add onion and garlic, cook 5 minutes
4. Add 1 can of beans to the pan and season them with salt, pepper and cumin then smash them with a potato masher, combining them with bacon and onions
5. When they are combined, add remaining can of beans and combine with smashed beans
6. Add hot sauce to your taste and serve<|endoftext|>
