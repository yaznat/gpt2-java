What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Apple Nut Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Shortening
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Apple
• Sour cream
• Walnuts
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Grease pan
2. In a bowl, stir together dry ingredients
3. In a large bowl, beat together shortening and sugar
4. Add egg and vanilla
5. Core, peel and chop the apple
6. Stir of the flour mixture into the sugar mixture
7. Add the sour cream and then the rest of the flour
8. Stir in apple
9. Spread evenly in pan
10. Chop the nuts
11. Mix with cinnamon, brown sugar and butter
12. Sprinkle on top
13. Bake at 350 for 25-30 minutes<|endoftext|>
