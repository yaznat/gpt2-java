Ingredients:
• Ground lean pork
• Oatmeal
• Ground sage
• Salt
• Pepper
• Dried thyme
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Egg
• Green onion
• Bacon

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine oats, sage, salt, pepper and thyme
2. Stir in applesauce, egg and green onion
3. Mix well
4. Stir in ground pork until well blended
5. Form into a loaf
6. Wrap bacon strips around pork loaf
7. Secure with a toothpick
8. Bake at 350 f for 45 minutes or until done<|endoftext|>
