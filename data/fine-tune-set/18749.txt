Ingredients:
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Butter
• Milk chocolate chips
• Bananas
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees
2. Butter a baking sheet
3. In a medium bowl, stir the graham-cracker crumbs and melted butter together
4. Press the crumb mixture onto the baking sheet in a circle that is approximately 8 inches in diameter
5. Bake for 8 minutes
6. Set aside to cool
7. Use a small spoon to drizzle the melted chocolate chips thickly over the baked crumbs on the baking sheet
8. Let the crunch sit until the chocolate is firm
9. [the chocolate holds the crunch together in large pieces so it does not crumble when it is transferred to the pudding
10. I always have a problem with this step, but if it crumbles it doesn't alter the flavor: -)]
11. In the bottom of a 2-quart serving bowl, spread 1 / 2 of the banana slices
12. Spread about 1 / 2 of the pudding over the bananas and sprinkle about 1 / 3 of the graham crunch over the pudding
13. Repeat the layering once
14. Spread the whipped cream over the crunch and sprinkle with the remaining crunch
15. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight and serve cold<|endoftext|>
