Ingredients:
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Sugar
• Butter
• Cinnamon
• Cream cheese
• Sour cream
• Heavy cream
• Salt
• Eggs
• Egg yolks
• Lemon juice
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Mix cracker crumbs and 1 / 4 cup of sugar
3. Stir in butter and cinnamon until crumbs are moist
4. Pat evenly onto bottom and sides of 8-inch springform pan
5. Bake 10 minutes and remove from oven
6. Let cool
7. Reduce oven heat to 275
8. Cream remaining 3 / 4 cup sugar with softened cream cheese
9. Add remaining ingredients in order listed
10. Mix until smooth
11. Pour into crust and bake 1 1 / 2 hours, or until cake is set
12. Remove from oven and cool until sides pull away from springform pan
13. Unmold and chill in refrigerator, covered loosely, until 30 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
