Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Everyday Waffles

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Beat egg whites with an electric hand-mixer until stiff
2. Set aside
3. Combine wet ingredients and beat lightly
4. Combine flour, baking powder, and salt, and stir into wet ingredients
5. Fold in egg whites so that a few fluffs of egg white remain visible
6. Bake until done in a hot waffle iron that has been very lightly sprayed with no-stick cooking spray<|endoftext|>
