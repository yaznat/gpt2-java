Ingredients:
• Carrots
• Green onion
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Fresh cilantro leaves

Steps:
1. Place a large nonstick skillet coated with cooking spray over medium-high heat until hot
2. Add shredded carrot and onions
3. Saute 5 minutes or until tender
4. Sprinkle 2 tablespoons shredded cheese over each of 4 tortillas, and divide carrot mixture evenly over tortillas
5. Top each tortilla with 1 / 2 cup chicken, 2 tablespoons cilantro leaves, and 2 tablespoons cheese, and top with a tortilla
6. Place skillet coated with cooking spray over medium heat until hot
7. Add 1 quesadilla, and cook 2 minutes on each side or until quesadilla is browned
8. Repeat with the remaining quesadillas
9. Cut each quesadilla into 6 wedges
10. Arrange 4 quesadilla wedges on each of 6 plates
11. Top each serving with 2 / 3 cup fruit salsa and about 3 tablespoons avocado cream<|endoftext|>
