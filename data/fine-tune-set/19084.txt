Give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Banana Ice Cream Dairy Free

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Vanilla extract
• Ice cream cones

Steps:
1. Peel ripe bananas and freeze them on a plate, in a ziplock, or wrapped in parchment paper for 5-10 hours until solid
2. Break bananas and add to blender with vanilla
3. Add 1-3 tbsp of mix-ins or toppings
4. Blend on high until creamy
5. Stir as needed
6. Add a tiny bit of water to thin if blender keeps getting stuck
7. Serve into bowls or ice cream cones<|endoftext|>
