What's a good smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>A Berry Nice Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Frozen blueberries
• Frozen sliced strawberries
• Honey
• Vanilla yogurt
• 2% low-fat milk
• Strawberry
• Blueberries
• Whipped topping

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in blender, except garnish and whipped topping
2. Blend until smooth
3. Pour into glasses and add topping and berries for garnish<|endoftext|>
