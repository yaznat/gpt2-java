Ingredients:
• Rice
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Wine vinegar
• Garlic clove
• Onion
• Salt and pepper
• Piquillo peppers
• Parsley
• Green olives
• Pitted black olives

Steps:
1. Bring 2 cups of water to the boil
2. Add the rice and a pinch of salt
3. Cover and cook slowly for 18 -25 minutes, until rice is tender
4. Cool the rice
5. Make a vinaigrette sauce by combining the olive oil, vinegar, garlic and onion
6. Season to taste with salt and pepper
7. In a bowl combine the rice, with the vinaigrette sauce, peppers, parsley, and olives
8. Serve cooled<|endoftext|>
