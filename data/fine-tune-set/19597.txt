Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Bacon
• Cooking oil
• Cheese
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Cook the unpeeled potatoes in boiling salted water until just tender
2. Drain, cool, and refrigerate overnight
3. Peel and grate the potatoes on a coarse grater
4. Season the potato shreds with salt and pepper
5. Fry the bacon in a large heavy skillet until the fat renders
6. If needed, add enough oil to coat the bottom of the pan
7. Add the potatoes, and mix to combine with the bacon
8. Gently press the mixture into the pan to form a cake
9. Cook for 20 minutes over medium heat until the bottom is golden and crusty
10. Invert a plate over the pan, and turn out the rosti
11. Add a little more oil to the pan, and slide the rosti back in, cooked side up
12. Cook until the other side is golden, about 10 minutes
13. Sprinkle with the grated cheese, cover, and let sit for about 2 minutes, until the cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
