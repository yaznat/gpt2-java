Give me a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>2 Baby Potatoes With Rosemary

Ingredients:
• Small potatoes
• Olive oil
• Fresh rosemary
• Salt
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Rosemary sprig

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Cut each potato in half or into quarters if large
3. Place potatoes in large bowl & toss with olive oil, chopped rosemary, salt, and pepper
4. Put into roasting pan
5. Roast potatoes 30 to 40 minutes, turning occasionally with metal spatula, until golden and fork-tender
6. Garnish with rosemary sprigs<|endoftext|>
