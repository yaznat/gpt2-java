What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Crock Pot Green Chile Burrito Taco Filling

Ingredients:
• Rump roast
• Onion
• Taco seasoning mix
• Dried oregano leaves
• Garlic powder
• Diced green chilies
• Tomato sauce

Steps:
1. Layer ingredients, meat first, in crock pot
2. Cook on low for 8-10 hours
3. Do not stir for first 5 hours
4. Shred meat after 7 hours
5. Serve on flour or corn tortillas
6. Top with sour cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
