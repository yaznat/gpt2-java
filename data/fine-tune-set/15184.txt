Ingredients:
• Sweet onion
• Barbecued pork
• Whole wheat pizza crust
• Cheddar cheese
• Jalapeno pepper

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400f
2. Separated onion into rings
3. Heat a medium non-stick skillet over medium heat
4. Add onion rings and cook 3 minutes to soften slightly
5. Meanwhile, heat container of bbq pork in microwave as package directs for 2 minutes
6. Lay pizza crust on pizza pan or cookie sheet
7. Spread with softened onions, bbq pork, and top with cheddar cheese and jalapeo peppers
8. Bake at 400f for 12 minutes or until pork is heated through and cheese is melted
9. Cut into wedges and serve hot<|endoftext|>
