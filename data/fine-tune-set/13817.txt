Ingredients:
• Ground pork
• Eggs
• Green onions
• Water chestnuts
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger powder
• Pepper
• Sesame oil
• Oyster sauce
• Sugar
• Soy sauce
• Japanese eggplants
• Wonton wrappers

Steps:
1. Combine pork or turkey, eggs, green onions water chestnuts garlic, ginger powder, pepper, sesame oil, oyster sauce, sugar and soy sauce in a large bowl
2. Mix well
3. Cut a wedge out of the eggplant
4. Stuff the pork mixture into the newly created pocket
5. If desired, put the wedge back on top of the eggplant
6. This can be made into "pork hash" burgers or made into pork hash
7. To make pork hash, place about 1 tablespoon of the mixture on to the middle of a wrapper
8. Wrap up the sides, leaving the top open and unsealed
9. Put stuffed eggplant and pork hash into a steamer, and steam for about 30 minutes
10. Serve with soy sauce mixed with dry powdered mustard, or sweet and thick teriyaki sauce or chinese black bean sauce<|endoftext|>
