Ingredients:
• White sugar
• Flour
• Egg
• Molasses
• Water
• Vanilla
• Brown sugar
• Baking soda
• Cream of tartar
• Butter
• Shortening
• 9-inch pie shells

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, combine white sugar, 4 tbsp flour, egg, molasses, water, and vanilla
2. Bring to a full, rolling boil
3. Set aside to cool
4. In a large bowl, combine 2 cups flour, brown sugar, baking soda, cream of tartar, butter, and shortening
5. Rub together with fingers to make crumbs
6. Pour 1 / 2 of cooked mixture into each unbaked shell
7. Cover each pie with crumbs
8. Bake at 350 degrees for 40-45 minutes<|endoftext|>
