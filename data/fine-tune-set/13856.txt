Give me a pork recipe.<|endoftext|>Awesome Pork Roast

Ingredients:
• Pork roast
• Beer
• Italian dressing
• Dry onion soup mix
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. Place pork roast in crock pot
2. Add dry onion soup mix
3. Pour italian dressing, beer and worcestershire sauce over top of roast
4. Cover and cook on low 8 hours
5. Roast can be shredded and put on rolls for sandwiches
6. Also can be a meat and potatoes meal<|endoftext|>
