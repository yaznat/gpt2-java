Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic
• Red onion
• Soft tofu
• Water
• Tamari
• Dried sweet basil leaves
• Cooked pasta
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Heat the olive oil in a small pan
2. Add garlic and onion and saute over medium-low heat until the onion is translucent
3. In food processor, blend the tofu until it reaches a uniform consistency
4. Pour the blended tofu into saute pan with the garlic and onion, and cook for 5 minutes over medium heat
5. Add water, tamari, and basil to the pan and cook for 5 minutes more
6. Pour the tofu sauce over cooked pasta
7. Toss well and sprinkle with parsley
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
