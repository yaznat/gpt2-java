• Ground beef
• Butter
• Celery
• Hot sauce
• Sugar
• Cumin
• Onion
• Salt
• Pepper
• Pinto beans
• Canned tomatoes
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:

2. Drain well
3. Push to one side of pan
4. Add butter and saute celery and onion a few minutes
5. Stir into meat and add remaining ingredients
