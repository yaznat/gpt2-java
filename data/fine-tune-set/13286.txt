Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>7 Vegetable Chicken Stew With Dumplings

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Onions
• Celery
• Rutabagas
• Sweet potatoes
• Chicken stock
• Dried thyme
• Dried sage
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Frozen peas
• All-purpose flour
• Fresh parsley
• Butter
• Baking powder
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a large pot, combine chicken, all veg except peas, stock and spices
2. Bring to a boil over high heat
3. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, covered, for 20 minutes
4. Stir in peas
5. Meanwhile, in a food processor or by hand, make dumplings: combine flour, parsley, butter, baking powder and salt until mixture is in coarse crumbs
6. Stir in milk, then drop by tablespoonfuls onto the hot stew
7. You should have about 6 mounds
8. Cover pot and simmer-- don't boil hard and don't lift the lid-- for 15 minutes or until dumplings have risen<|endoftext|>
