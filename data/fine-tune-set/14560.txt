Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Baked beans
• Apricot preserves
• Apple cider vinegar
• Spicy brown mustard

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Chop 3 slices bacon
3. In ovenproof pot over medium-high heat, cook chopped bacon, onion and garlic, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3 minutes
4. Remove from heat
5. Stir in beans, preserves, vinegar and mustard
6. Cut remaining bacon slices in half
7. Arrange over baked beans
8. Bake 1 hour or until bacon is crisp<|endoftext|>
