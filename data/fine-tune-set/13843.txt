What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Autumn Harvest S Best Pork Roast

Ingredients:
• Pork loin
• Coarse black pepper
• Salt
• Apples
• Apple juice
• White wine
• Peach jam
• Brown sugar
• Ground ginger

Steps:
1. Rub pork loin with pepper and salt to taste
2. Brown sides in frying pan
3. Line apples on the bottom of a crockpot
4. Lay pork loin on top of apples
5. Whisk the remaining ingredients together and pour over pork loin and apples
6. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours
7. 00 a bottle in the grocery store
8. However, beringer's chenin blanc or a nice german white wine would be lovely as well
9. If you use plain peach jam be sure to add 1 / 2 tsp cinnamon and a pinch of ground clove for a wonderful flavor<|endoftext|>
