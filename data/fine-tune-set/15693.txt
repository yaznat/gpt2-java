What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken And Bean Tacos

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• Taco seasoning mix
• Green chilies
• Tomato sauce
• Ground cumin
• Coriander seed
• Cannellini beans
• Taco shells
• Cheddar cheese
• Lettuce
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Add chicken thighs to a 4 quart slow cooker
2. Sprinkle with taco seasoning
3. Sprinkle chopped chiles on top of chicken
4. In a bowl, add tomato sauce, cumin, and coriander seed
5. Stir to combine
6. Pour mixture over the top of the chicken
7. Top with beans
8. Cover, cook on low for 7 hours
9. Remove chicken from crock pot and set aside
10. Mash beans with a potato masher
11. Shred chicken and return to slow cooker
12. Stir to mix well
13. To assemble taco: spoon about 3 tablespoons of chicken filling into each taco shell
14. Then add 1 tablespoon cheese and lettuce, then add 2 teaspoons sour cream and salsa
15. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
