What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>Homemade Vegetable Soup From A Can

Ingredients:
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Diced tomatoes
• Zucchini
• Corn
• Potatoes
• Wax beans
• Green beans
• Carrots

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in large pot
2. Bring to a boil and simmer for 3 minutes
3. Serve
4. I use the tomatoes that come flavored with basil, oregano, and garlic
5. You can substitute whatever vegetables you like
6. If you don't want to use the zucchini, add a can of tomato sauce to give it some substance<|endoftext|>
