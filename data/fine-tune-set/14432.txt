• Dijon mustard
• Great northern beans

Steps:
1. Saut onion and bell pepper in a pan sprayed with cooking spray or a little olive or vegetable oil until they begin to soften
2. Stir in tomatoes and tomato sauce
3. Bring to a boil, cooking until tomatoes break up, stirring often
4. If you prefer smaller tomato pieces help to break them up using the back of your spoon
