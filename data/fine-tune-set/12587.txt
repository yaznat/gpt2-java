Ingredients:
• Cannellini beans
• Chopped tomatoes
• Garlic
• Oregano
• Olive oil
• Red bell pepper
• Yellow bell pepper
• Red chile
• Fresh cilantro
• Fresh parsley
• Orange
• Lime
• Tabasco sauce
• Tequila

Steps:
1. Drain and rinse beans
2. Mash 1 / 4 of them
3. Combine beans, tomatoes, garlic, oregano and oil in medium saucepan over medium low hear
4. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until tomatoes and oil have boiled together and mixture is slightly thickened, 15-20 minutes combine remaining ingredients in small bowl and mix well
5. Serve warm beans topped with salsa<|endoftext|>
