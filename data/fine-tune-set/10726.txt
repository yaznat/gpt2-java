Ingredients:
• Radiatore
• Butter
• Shallot
• Shiitake mushroom caps
• Cremini mushrooms
• Morels
• Bacon
• Fresh thyme
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Dry sherry
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Asiago cheese
• Cooking spray
• Fresh thyme sprig

Steps:
1. Reheat oven to 375
2. Cook pasta according to the package directions, omitting salt and fat
3. Drain well
4. Set cooked pasta aside
5. Cook bacon and set aside to cool
6. Melt butter in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
7. Add shallots
8. Saut 3 minutes
9. Add mushrooms, 1 tablespoon thyme, salt, pepper, and garlic
10. Saut 8 minutes or until mushrooms are tender
11. Add sherry
12. Cook 1 minute, stirring frequently
13. Remove from heat
14. Place flour in a dutch oven over medium-high heat
15. Gradually add milk, stirring constantly with a whisk
16. Bring mixture to a boil
17. Cook 1 minute or until slightly thick, stirring constantly with a whisk
18. Remove from heat
19. Add 1 / 2 cup cheese, stirring until melted
20. Add pasta and mushroom mixture to cheese mixture, crumble in the bacon, tossing well to combine
21. Spoon pasta mixture into an 8-inch square baking dish lightly coated with cooking spray
22. Sprinkle evenly with remaining 1 / 2 cup cheese
23. Bake at 375 for 30 minutes or until cheese melts and begins to brown
24. Garnish with thyme sprigs, if desired<|endoftext|>
