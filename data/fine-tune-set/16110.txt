What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Chili Cheese Dog Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Hot dogs
• Flour tortillas
• Chili
• Ketchup
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tsp olive oil over medium heat
2. Slice hot dogs into 1 / 2 inch slivers
3. Add to skillet and brown on both sides
4. Remove and set aside
5. Add remaining olive oil to skillet
6. While oil is heating, assemble quesadillas: place 1-2 tbsp chilli onto one half of each quesadilla
7. Top with hot dog slivers, tsp ketchup, and 1 / 3 cup of cheese, and fold
8. Transfer quesadillas to skillet, one at a time
9. Brown on both sides until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
