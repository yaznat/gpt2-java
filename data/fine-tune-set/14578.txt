Ingredients:
• Lime juice
• Garlic cloves
• Cilantro
• Green chilies
• Salt
• Onion
• Butter
• Rice
• Chicken broth

Steps:
1. Chop veggies / herbs coarsely and then fry in 1 t butter or oil
2. Add rice and stir to coat and then add broth, salt and lime juice
3. Bring to rapid boil, turn down as low as possible, cover and simmer about 20 minutes - do not stir! fluff with fork<|endoftext|>
