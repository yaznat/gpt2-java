Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>1 2 Way Brownies

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Shortening
• Sugar
• Chocolate chips
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Over low heat, in small saucepan, melt the margarine, shortening, sugar, and chocolate chips
2. Remove from heat, add egg and vanilla
3. Stir in flour, cocoa powder, and baking powder
4. Grease an 8x8 inch pan with nonstick cooking spray and pour in batter
5. Bake at 325 degrees for 12-15 minutes<|endoftext|>
