Give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Aloha Burgers

Ingredients:
• Pineapple slices
• Teriyaki sauce
• Ground beef
• Sweet onion
• Butter
• Lettuce leaves
• Onion hamburger buns
• Swiss cheese
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Drain pineapple juice into a small bowl
2. Add teri sauce
3. Place 3 tbsp in a resealable plastic bag
4. Add pineapple and rotate to coat
5. Set aside
6. Shape beef into four patties
7. Place in a 8" square baking dish
8. Pour the remaining teriyaki sauce mixture over patties
9. Marinate for 5-10 minutes, turning once
10. In a skillet, saute onion in butter until tender, about 5 minutes
11. Grill or broil burgers until no longer pink
12. Place pineapple on grill or under broiler to heat through
13. Layer lettuce and onion on bottom of buns
14. Top with burgers, cheese, pineapple and bacon
15. Replace tops
16. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
