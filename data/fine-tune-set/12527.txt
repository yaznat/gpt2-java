What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Argentina Style Beef Stew

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Beef stew meat
• Carrots
• Onions
• Tomatoes
• Potatoes
• Winter squash
• Green bell pepper
• Fresh thyme
• Garlic cloves
• Beef broth
• Head of cabbage

Steps:
1. Fry bacon in a large skillet until crisp
2. Remove bacon to a paper towel line plate to drain
3. Crumble bacon and chill until serving time
4. Brown beef in the hot bacon drippings
5. Drain off the fat
6. In a 6-quart slow cooker, add in the carrots, onions, tomatoes, potatoes, squash or pumpkin, sweet pepper, dried thyme, and garlic
7. Add in the meat and pour the broth over all
8. Cover and cook on low for 8-10 hours
9. Turn to high
10. Stir in cabbage and fresh thyme
11. Cover and cook for 15-20 minutes or until the cabbage is just tender<|endoftext|>
