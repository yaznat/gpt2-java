Give me a lamb recipe.<|endoftext|>Char Siu Rack Of Lamb

Ingredients:
• Fresh rosemary
• Garlic
• Szechuan peppercorns
• Dark soy sauce
• Soy sauce
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Rack of lamb
• Hoisin sauce
• Honey
• Mango
• Red bell peppers
• Onions
• Fresh mint leaves
• Fresh lemon juice
• Plum sauce
• Cooked rice
• Mint leaf

Steps:
1. To prepare marinade: combine rosemary, garlic, peppercorns, soy sauces, salt and oil in a large bowl
2. Add lamb and turn to coat
3. Cover and refrigerate 2-3 hours
4. To prepare glaze: combine hoisin sauce, soy sauce and honey in a bowl
5. Mix and set aside
6. To prepare salsa: combine mango, bell pepper, onion, mint, soy sauces, honey, lemon juice and plum sauce in a bowl
7. Mix well
8. Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve
9. Preheat broiler with rack placed 4 to 5 inches from heating element
10. Place lamb, meat side up, in a foil-lined baking pan
11. Broil lamb, meat side up, for 10 to 12 minutes
12. Turn, brush with glaze, and broil for 5 to 7 minutes more
13. Turn meat again, brush with glaze, and broil for 3 to 4 minutes more
14. Slice rack between ribs
15. Serve with rice and salsa and garnish with mint leaves<|endoftext|>
