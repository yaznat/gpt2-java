Ingredients:
• Asparagus
• Fresh green beans
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon
• Fresh tarragon
• Fresh parsley leaves

Steps:
1. Cook asparagus and green beans in 1 inch salted boiling water, covered, for 5 minutes
2. Drain, cool and arrange on a serving plate
3. Combine the dip ingredients in a small bowl and garnish with sprigs of parsley and tarragon<|endoftext|>
