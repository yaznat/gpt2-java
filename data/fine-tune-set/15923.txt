Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Green onion
• Garlic cloves
• Pinto beans
• Frozen whole kernel corn
• Cooked brown rice
• Tomatoes
• Cotija cheese
• Chipotle chile in adobo
• Phyllo pastry sheets
• Light sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. In a medium saucepan, heat oil over medium heat
3. Add the green onions and garlic
4. Cook about 3 minutes or just until tender, stirring occasionally
5. Remove from heat
6. Stir in beans, corn, rice 1 / 3 cup of the tomatoes, cheese, and chili peppers
7. Set aside
8. Unroll phyllo dough
9. Remove one sheet of the phyllo dough and place on a flat surface
10. Lightly coat the phyllo sheet with cooking spray
11. Lay another sheet of phyllo dough on top he first sheet and lightly coat with cooking spray
12. Repeat with the remaining phyllo sheets to make two more stacks
13. Cut each phyllo stack in half crosswise to make six stacks total
14. To assemble burritos, spoon about 2 / 3 cup of the bean mixture along one side of each phyllo stack, leaving about 1 1 / 2inches of space on each end
15. Fold short sides up and over filling
16. Roll up phyllo around filling, starting athe long side
17. Place burritos, seam sides down, on a large baking sheet
18. Coat tops of burritos with cooking spray
19. Bake 20 minutes or until tops are golden brown and filling is heated through
20. Serve with the remaining 1 / 2 cup chopped tomato and sour cream<|endoftext|>
