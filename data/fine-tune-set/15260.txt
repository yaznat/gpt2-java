What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Broccoli Pizza Crust

Ingredients:
• Cooked broccoli
• Eggs
• Mozzarella cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Combine all and press evenly into well greased in to a pan or cookie sheet sprinkle with freshly ground black pepper and basil
2. Bake at 450 degrees until browned and bubbly
3. Top like a pizza and rebake to cook toppings<|endoftext|>
