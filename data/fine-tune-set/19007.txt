Ingredients:
• Butter
• White flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Brown sugar
• Rhubarb
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Salt
• Milk
• Flour
• Egg whites
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Butter a 13x9 pan
3. Cream butter with flours and sugar, spread in prepared pan, pressing into corners and bottom
4. Bake for 5-10 minutes
5. While that is cooking, cut up the rhubarb
6. In a blender or a mixer bowl, beat eggs until foamy and fluffy, add sugar and continue mixing, then add other ingredients until thick and rich
7. Set aside
8. When crust is baked, spread rhubarb pieces evenly
9. Pour custard over rhubarb
10. Return pan to the oven for 45 minutes
11. While that is cooking, prepare meringue
12. Beat eggs until forming soft peaks, adding sugar 2 teaspoons at a time, then salt and vanilla last, mixing until stiff
13. At end of baking time, remove pan from oven, turn heat up to 425
14. Spread meringue over rhubarb / custard, smooth with a spoon into pretty swirls
15. Brown for five minutes
16. Set the timer--it can burn very quickly!
17. Variations: skip the meringue and use whole eggs in the custard
18. Switch out some of the rhubarb for strawberries or raspberries
19. Use all white flour instead of wholewheat in the crust<|endoftext|>
