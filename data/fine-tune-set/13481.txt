What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Apple Corned Beef Brisket For The Slow Cooker

Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Onion
• Apple
• Corned beef brisket
• Cabbage
• Apple cider
• Brown sugar
• Dijon mustard
• Ground cloves
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Place red potatoes, onion, and apple in a large slow cooker
2. Top with corned beef brisket
3. Place cabbage over top
4. In a small bowl, whisk together cider, brown sugar, mustard, cloves and pepper
5. Pour into slow cooker
6. Cook on low for 6 - 8 hours, or high for about 3 - 4 hours<|endoftext|>
