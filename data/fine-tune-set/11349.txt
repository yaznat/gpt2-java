Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Sugar
• Egg
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Vanilla
• Nuts

Steps:
1. In a large bowl mash your bananas until they are very pasty
2. Add all other ingredients
3. Mix well
4. Pour all ingredients into a greased loaf pan
5. Bake at 350f for 50-60 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the middle of the pan comes out clean<|endoftext|>
