What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Chicken And Cheddar Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Mild green chilies
• Rotisserie-cooked chicken
• Extra-sharp cheddar cheese
• Taco sauce
• Fresh cilantro stem
• Salsa
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 450f
2. Place 1 tortilla on large baking sheet
3. Spread with 1 tablespoon chiles
4. Top with one-quarter of chicken, cheese and taco sauce
5. Place another tortilla on top
6. Repeat with remaining tortillas, chiles, chicken, cheese and sauce, for a total of 4 separate quesadillas
7. Bake in 450f oven 8 minutes, until crispy and cheese melted
8. Remove from oven
9. Let stand for 5 minutes
10. Cut into quarters
11. Garnish with cilantro, salsa and sour cream<|endoftext|>
