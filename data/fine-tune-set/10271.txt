Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Rolled oats
• Applesauce
• Vanilla extract
• Ground flax seeds
• Dried cherries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Using a fork, mash the bananas in a bowl
3. Stir in oats, applesauce, dried cherries, flax and vanilla extract
4. Mix batter well
5. Drop by rounded spoonfuls onto a lined cookie sheet
6. Bake for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
