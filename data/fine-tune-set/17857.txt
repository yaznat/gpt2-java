Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>88 Calorie Brownies

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Vanilla extract
• Egg whites
• Sugar
• Unbleached all-purpose flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Instant espresso powder
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Semisweet mini chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f spray an 8x8 inch non-stick cake pan with cooking spray
2. In a medium mixing bowl, using a sturdy whisk or spatula, mix the applesauce, vanilla, egg whites and sugar until they are well combined
3. Add the flour, cocoa powder, espresso powder, baking powder, and salt
4. Stir the until just combined and no lumps remain
5. Pour into prepared pan
6. Sprinkle the chocolate chips chips evenly over the top
7. Bake for 20-22 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out dry
8. Transfer the pan to a cooling rack and allow them to cool 5 minutes
9. Cut into 12 equal brownies<|endoftext|>
