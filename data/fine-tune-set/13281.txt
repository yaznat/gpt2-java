What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>6 Ingredient Chicken Stew

Ingredients:
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Sliced peaches in juice
• Apricot-pineapple preserves
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Chicken quarters
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Pour first 4 ingredients into the crock pot, add chicken, then top off with water until chicken is covered
2. Cook on high for about 5 hours, or on low for 7-8
3. Stir in corn starch at the end of cooking time to thicken
4. Serve over elbow macaroni<|endoftext|>
