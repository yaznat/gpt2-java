Give me a taco recipe.<|endoftext|>Cabin Fever Game Night Taco Dip

Ingredients:
• Refried beans
• Salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Cream cheese
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• Garlic powder
• Avocado
• Onion
• Lemon juice
• Tomatoes
• Black olives
• Green onion
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Mix beans & salsa, spread on cookie sheet or serving tray
2. Sprinkle cheddar over this
3. Press lightly into bean-salsa mixture
4. In a medium mixing bowl, beat cream cheese, sour cream, mayo & garlic powder until very smooth
5. Spread over bean-cheese in pan
6. In a blender or by hand, mix avocado, onion and lemon juice until very smooth
7. Spread this over half the cream cheese layer
8. By now your kitchen should have messes on every counter!
9. Over the top, in a festive arrangement, sprinkle on your choice of the toppings
10. Serve with tortilla chips
11. Leftovers: slop together and serve over tortilla chips or spoon into a taco shell or on a tortilla skin
12. This is even good when heated up<|endoftext|>
