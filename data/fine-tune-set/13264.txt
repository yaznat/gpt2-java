Give me a chicken recipe.<|endoftext|>40 Cloves Of Garlic And Chicken

Ingredients:
• Fresh thyme
• Fresh rosemary
• Fresh sage
• Fresh italian parsley
• Garlic
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Whole chickens
• Celery
• French bread

Steps:
1. Place 1 sprig each of thyme, rosemary, sage and parsley in chicken cavity
2. Place celery in bottom of stoneware pot
3. Put chicken on top of celery
4. Add garlic around chicken
5. Chop remaining herbs
6. Sprinkle herbs, salt and pepper over chicken
7. Cover and cook on low 8- 10 hours or on high 4- 6 hours
8. To serve, place chicken, garlic and celery on serving platter
9. Squeeze roasted garlic out of skins onto toasted french bread slices and spread with a knife<|endoftext|>
