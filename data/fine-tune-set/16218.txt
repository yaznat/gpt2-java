Give me a quesadilla recipe.<|endoftext|>Light Luscious Vegetarian Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Baby spinach
• Pine nuts
• Shallots
• Gouda cheese
• Black beans
• Flour tortillas
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in a medium frying pan
2. Wilt baby spinach in the oil until tender, but so that it still is separate leaves
3. Set aside to cool for 3 minutes
4. In a large bowl mix by hand: pine nutes, gouda, shallots, and black beans
5. Add baby spinach once cooled
6. Spoon 3 tablespoons of mixture onto a flour tortilla
7. Top with another tortilla
8. Fry tortillas in frying pan for 2 minutes per side or until golden brown
9. Cut quesadillas into quarters and serve with salsa or guacamole<|endoftext|>
