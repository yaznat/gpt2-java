Please give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>Al Kabsa Traditional Saudi Rice Meat Dish

Ingredients:
• Lamb
• Basmati rice
• Carrot
• Green pepper
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Ground cumin
• Ground coriander
• Salt and black pepper
• Butter
• Pine nuts
• Saffron
• Green cardamoms
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground allspice
• White pepper
• Dried limes
• Water
• Beef stock cube
• Chopped tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Celery

Steps:
1. Fry meat until browned on all sides
2. Add enough water to the pot so that it covers the meat by 1
3. 5"-2", bring to a boil then cook gently over medium heat for 1 1 / 2 - 2 hours or until tender
4. Reserve stock & set meat aside
5. Soak rice for 15 minutes
6. Drain, rise under running water then drain well
7. Add butter to a large stock pot or casserole, fry the onion, pepper, carrot & spices
8. Cook on low heat for about 10 minutes, or until tender
9. Add rice to vegetables in the pot, cook for 5 minutes on medium heat
10. Add meat stock until stock covers rice
11. Cover pot
12. Cook on low heat for 30 minutes or until tender - add a little more stock or water if rice seems to become dry
13. Sauce:
14. Fry onion, garlic and tomato in the butter
15. Add tomato paste, celery, salt, cinnamon, water & stock cube
16. Cook on medium heat until slightly thickened and celery is tender
17. Serve in a large dish or on a platter
18. Place the rice first, top with meat & garnish with pine nuts
19. Serve the sauce in a jug with a spoon
20. Saudies enjoy kabsa with a hot sauce called 'shattah' --<|endoftext|>
