Ingredients:
• Butter
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Milk
• Salt
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325 degrees
2. Melt butter in a 10x13" glass dish in oven, tilting dish to coat bottom
3. Beat eggs, sour cream, milk and salt together
4. Stir in green onion
5. Bake until eggs are set, but still moist, about 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
