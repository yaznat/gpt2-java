Ingredients:
• Lentils
• Yellow onion
• Cilantro
• Tomatoes
• Coconut milk
• Tomato sauce
• Curry powder
• Turmeric
• Ginger
• Ground cloves
• Cumin
• Cayenne
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cover bottom of saucepan with vegetable oil
2. Fry onion about 5 minutes, add cilantro and continue frying over medium heat, until onion is golden
3. Add tomato and let get a little soft
4. Add rest of ingredients, including spices and let simmer for about 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
