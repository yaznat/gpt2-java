Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Chili sauce
• Brown sugar
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Italian seasoning
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Top round steaks

Steps:
1. Mix all the ingredients except for the steak in a shallow glass dish or a food storage bag
2. Add steak, turning to coat
3. Cover the dish or seal the bag
4. Refrigerate at least 8 hours or overnight, turning occasionally
5. Remove steak from the marinade
6. Reserve marinade
7. Grill over direct high heat for 5 minutes
8. Turn steak and sear the other side
9. Move steak to a cooler part of the grill
10. Cover and grill until desired doneness, turning and brushing with reserved marinade once or twice
11. Discard any remaining marinade
12. To serve, cut beef into slices across the grain into slices<|endoftext|>
