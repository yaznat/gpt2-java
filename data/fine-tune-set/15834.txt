What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Breakfast Burritos Oamc

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Bulk sausage
• Chunky salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Scramble eggs in a large skillet until cooked through
2. Stir in cooked sausage and salsa
3. Warm tortillas in the microwave for 20-30 seconds until warm and flexible
4. Place 1 / 2 cup of filling mixture and 1 / 8 cup cheese into each tortilla and roll up burrito-style
5. Freeze burritos in a single layer on lightly greased cookie sheets
6. When fully frozen, wrap individually
7. Place wrapped burritos in a large zipped freezer bags and place back in the freezer
8. To serve: cook from the frozen state in the microwave for about 2 minutes or until heated through
9. Or, thaw burritos, wrap in foil, and bake in the oven at 350 degrees for 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
