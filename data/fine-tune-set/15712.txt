Ingredients:
• Tortilla chips
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Eggs
• Mayonnaise
• Taco seasoning mix
• All-purpose flour
• White chicken meat
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Sour cream
• Sliced ripe olives
• Whole kernel corn
• Green onion
• Fresh tomato
• Picante sauce

Steps:
1. Combine tortilla chips and butter
2. Stir well
3. Firmly press into bottom of 10 spring form pan
4. Set aside
5. Beat cream cheese until creamy
6. Add eggs one at a time beating well after each addition
7. Add mayonnaise, taco seasoning and flour
8. Beat at low speed until smooth
9. Stir in chicken and cheese
10. Pour batter into prepared pan
11. Bake at 325 for 55 minutes
12. Spread sour cream on top of cheesecake and bake 10 additional minutes
13. Let cool to room temperature in pan on wire rack
14. Cover and chill at least 8 hours
15. To serve carefully remove sides of pan
16. Arrange toppings on cheesecake
17. Serve with picante sauce<|endoftext|>
