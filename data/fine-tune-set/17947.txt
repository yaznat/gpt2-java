Ingredients:
• Unbaked pastry shells
• Chocolate chips
• Butter
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Biscuit mix
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Bake the pastry shell for 10 minutes
3. Remove from oven
4. Reduce temperature to 325
5. In a saucepan over low, melt the chocolate chips with butter
6. In large mixer bowl, beat chocolate mixture with remaining ingredients except nuts until smooth
7. Add nuts and pour mixture into baked pastry shell
8. Bake 35 to 45 minutes or until center is set
9. Let cool a bit before serving<|endoftext|>
