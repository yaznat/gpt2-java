Ingredients:
• Great northern beans
• White chicken meat
• Pace picante sauce
• Monterey jack pepper cheese

Steps:
1. Put the ingredients in a slow cooker
2. Cook on high for 2 hours and low for 2 hours
3. Stir every 1 / 2 hour
4. Serve with tortilla chips and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
