Ingredients:
• Frozen pizza dough
• Alfredo sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Plum tomatoes
• Cooked bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
2. Coat a 12-inch pizza pan with cooking spray
3. Press dough onto pan
4. Spread alfredo sauce over dough, leaving a half-inch border
5. Sprinkle with 1 / 2 cup cheese
6. Top with spinach, tomatoes, and bacon
7. Sprinkle with remaining 1 / 2 cup cheese
8. Bake until heated through and cheese is melted and lightly browned, 15-18 minutes<|endoftext|>
