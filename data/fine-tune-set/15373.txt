Please give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Asian Style Turkey Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Scallion
• Soft breadcrumbs
• Fresh cilantro
• Soy sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Fresh orange juice
• Fresh lemon juice
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine ground turkey, scallions, bread crumbs, 2 tb of cilantro, 1 tb of the soy sauce, ginger, and garlic
2. Season to taste with salt and pepper
3. Mix well
4. Divide turkey mixture into 8 equal portion
5. Shape into patties
6. Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat until hot
7. Add 4 of the patties and cook for 7 to 8 minutes
8. Turn patties over and cook another 8 minutes or until done
9. Repeat with remaining 4 patties
10. Keep the first batch warm in a warm oven
11. For the sauce, in a small saucepan combine orange juice, lemon juice, and the remaining 1 tb soy sauce
12. Let bubble until reduced by half
13. Whisk in the butter and the remaining 1 tb cilantro
14. Pour over burgers<|endoftext|>
