What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Andrea S Easy Ham Salad For Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Cooked ham
• Black pepper
• Sweet onion
• Light mayonnaise
• Sweet relish
• Mustard

Steps:
1. Gently combine all ingredients till well mixed
2. Refrigerate several hours until well chilled to let flavors blend
3. Serve on your favorite bread<|endoftext|>
