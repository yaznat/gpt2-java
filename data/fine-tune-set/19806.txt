Give me a fries recipe.<|endoftext|>Easy Home Fries

Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Dried rosemary
• Garlic powder
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Scrub the potatoes to clean them, then chop them into bite-sized pieces
2. Place all potato pieces onto a microwave-safe plate and cover with plastic wrap
3. Place plate in the microwave and cook for approximately 4 minutes
4. While the potatoes are cooking in the microwave, place a griddle on the stove top and turn the burner on to medium heat
5. Add a small amount of olive oil to the pan
6. When the potatoes are done in the microwave, carefully remove hot plate and take off plastic wrap
7. Watch out for steam
8. Drizzle the rest of the olive oil over the potatoes and then sprinkle the dried rosemary and garlic powder over them
9. When the griddle is heated, dump the potatoes onto it
10. After 2-3 minutes flip them over using a spatula
11. Continue to flip and stir them every couple of minutes as they cook, until they are done to your tastes
12. I usually leave them on for a total of 10 minutes
13. Remove from griddle and serve<|endoftext|>
