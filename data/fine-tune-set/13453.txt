What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Amazing Beef

Ingredients:
• Beef roast
• Pepperoncini peppers
• Garlic

Steps:
1. Cut slits in beef and insert a clove of garlic in each slit
2. You may also used chopped garlic if you wish
3. Place the roast into crockpot
4. Dump entire jar of peppers and liquid over it
5. Cook 8-10 hours on low<|endoftext|>
