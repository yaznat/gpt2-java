Ingredients:
• Ricotta cheese
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cooking oil
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. In a large bowl beat ricotta cheese with an electric mixer on medium speed until smooth
2. Add eggs and vanilla
3. Beat until combined
4. Add flour, granualted sugar, baking powder, and salt & beat on low speed until just combined
5. Let batter stand 30 minutes
6. Drop batter by well-rounded teaspoons, four or five at a time, into deep hot fat
7. And cook 2 1 / 2 to 3 minutes or until golden brown turning once
8. Remove doughnuts with slotted spoon and drain on paper towels
9. Repeat with remaining batter
10. Cool completely
11. Shake doughnuts in a bag with powdered sugar, granulated sugar, or cinnamon / sugar mixture
12. Makes 3 dozen<|endoftext|>
