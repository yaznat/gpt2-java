Give me a custard recipe.<|endoftext|>Cappuccino Custard Sugar Free

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Salt
• Milk
• Splenda granular
• Vanilla
• Instant coffee granules
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Place six 4-ounce custard cups into a 9x13 inch baking pan
3. Whisk together the eggs, salt, milk, splenda, vanilla, coffee granules, and cinnamon
4. Pour, through a sieve, into the custard cups
5. Fill baking pan with 1 inch of hottest tap water
6. Bake for 25-40 minutes or until set on the edges but still slightly jiggly in the center
7. Remove custards from water bath and allow to cool on a wire rack
8. Cover with plastic wrap and chill<|endoftext|>
