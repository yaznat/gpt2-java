Ingredients:
• Beef brisket
• Salt and pepper
• Tomato sauce
• Water
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Red wine vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Brown sugar
• Honey
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Lemon
• Liquid smoke

Steps:
1. Sprinkle roast with salt and pepper
2. Roast in a covered roaster at 325 degrees for 3 to 4 hours or until tender
3. Cool and refrigerate a short while for easier slicing
4. Mix remaining ingredients together except for the lemon
5. Cut off excess fat on roast and slice meat thinly
6. Cut across the grain
7. Place layers of meat in a 9 by 12 casserole dish and top each layer with sauce
8. I usually get 2 layers of meat with a 5 pound brisket
9. Put thinly sliced lemon over the top layer, cover and bake at 350 degrees for 1 hour or until heated through
10. Remove lemon slices before serving
11. This freezes well
12. Thaw overnight and then reheat
