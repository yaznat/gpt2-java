Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Spring Vegetables Ww

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• White pearl onions
• Baby carrots
• Leeks
• Boneless beef loin
• Low sodium beef broth
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Tiny new potatoes
• Green beans
• Flat leaf parsley
• Fresh dill
• Lemon zest
• Fresh lemon juice
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. In large nonstick saucepan, heat oil
2. Add onions, carrots and leeks
3. Cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, 6-7 minutes, until onions are lightly browned
4. Remove vegetable mixture from saucepan
5. Set aside
6. In same saucepan, cook beef over medium heat, stirring frequently, 8-10 minutes, until beef is browned on all sides and cooked through
7. Add beef and chicken broths
8. Bring liquid to a boil
9. Reduce heat to low
10. Simmer, covered, stirring occasionally, 1-1 1 / 2 hours, until beef is tender
11. Add potatoes, green beans and reserved vegetable mixture to beef mixture
12. Simmer, covered, 15-20 minutes, until vegetables and beef are very tender
13. Stir in parsley, dill, zest, juice and pepper
14. Divide evenly among 4 bowls and serve
15. Serving provides: 1 / 4 fat, 4 vegetables, 3 proteins, 1 bread, 10 optional calories
16. Per serving: 395 calories, 8 g total fat, 3 g saturated fat, 63 mg cholesterol, 163 mg sodium, 51 g total carbohydrate, 6 g dietary fiber, 31 g protein, 119 mg calcium
