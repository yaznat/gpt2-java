Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate squares
• Unsalted butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Cream cheese
• Egg
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Walnut pieces

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Coat an 8"x8"x2" baking pan with non-stick cooking spray
3. Set aside
4. In a saucepan, melt the unsweetened chocolate and butter over low heat
5. Remove from heat
6. Stir in 1-1 / 3 cups of sugar and the 1 / 2 teaspoon vanilla extract
7. Cool the mixture for 15 minutes
8. Beat in eggs and 3 / 4 cup flour
9. In a mixing bowl, mix the remaining 1 / 3 cup sugar, cream cheese the 1 egg and 4 teaspoons all-pupose flour & 1 / 4 teaspoon vanilla extract
10. In prepared pan, spread 2 / 3 of the chocolate batter in the pan
11. Spoon cream cheese layer over batter
12. Dollop with remaining chocolate batter
13. Bake in a 350f oven for 20 minutes
14. Sprinkle with semi-sweet chocolate pieces
15. Bake for 12 minutes more
16. Cool
17. Cover
18. Store in refrigerator
19. Let the chilled dessert stand at room temperature for 30 minutes before serving
