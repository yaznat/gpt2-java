Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Oregano
• Black pepper
• Eggs
• Skim milk
• Tomato sauce
• Garlic powder
• Red pepper flakes
• Italian sausage
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Mushrooms
• Fresh garlic
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Have ready a 12-14" round pizza pan with a rim, lightly greased with olive oil
3. Combine flour, salt, oregano, pepper, eggs, and mikl until smooth
4. Pour crust batter into the pan, tilting the pan to coat evenly
5. You want the entire bottom covered and it will appear to be very very low and thin
6. Sprinkle cooked sausage all over the batter, then do the same with the garlic, onions, green peppers, and mushrooms if using, but do not add sauce or cheese yet
7. Bake on a low rack in preheated 425 f oven for 25-30 minutes until deep golden brown
8. Meanwhile mix together the sauce ingredients
9. When pizza is ready, drizzle the sauce over the pizza as evenly as you can and sprinkle cheese over the top, bearing in mind that if you add extra sauce / cheese the pizza will not be as crispy
10. Bake another 10-15 minutes or until sauce is heated through and cheese has melted
11. Cut into squares and serve, this is going to have a very thin crust and depending on how many toppings you added, might be a bit floppy so have a fork handy<|endoftext|>
