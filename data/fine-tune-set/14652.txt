What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Alternative Rice Crispy Treats

Ingredients:
• Honey
• Chunky peanut butter
• Crispy brown rice cereal
• Dried cherries
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Combine honey and peanut butter in a large pot and heat over medium-low heat until melted, about 2-3 minutes
2. Add brown rice cereal and cherries and stir to combine until mixture is sticky
3. Press into a 9 by 13-inch pan sprayed with cooking spray
4. Let cool, then chill in refrigerator for 30 minutes
5. Per serving:
6. Calories 160
7. Total fat 5 g
8. Protein 3 g
9. Carb 26 g
10. Fiber 2 g
11. Cholesterol 0 mg
12. Sodium 35 mg<|endoftext|>
