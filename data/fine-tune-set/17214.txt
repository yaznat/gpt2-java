Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Celery
• Red bell peppers
• Dried dill weed
• Dry ranch dressing mix
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Wash chicken breasts and pat dry
3. Place on large foil-lined baking sheet and bake until cooked through, about 30-35 minutes
4. Remove and set aside to cool
5. Cut into chunks and transfer to large bowl
6. Add celery, red pepper, dill, ranch dressing and mayonnaise
7. Stir gently but thoroughly to blend
8. Refrigerate to allow flavors to meld for at least a couple of hours before serving<|endoftext|>
