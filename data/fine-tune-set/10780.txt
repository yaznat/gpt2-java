Can you give me a salad recipe?<|endoftext|>24 Hour Fruit Salad

Ingredients:
• Pasta
• Pineapple tidbits
• Lemon, juice of
• Eggs
• Flour
• Salt
• Sugar
• Fruit cocktail
• Cool whip
• Colored miniature marshmallows

Steps:
1. Cook macaroni per package directions to al dente
2. Drain, rinse and cool
3. Combine juice of fruit cocktail, lemon, 2 cups pineapple juice, eggs, flour and salt
4. Boil over medium heat, stirring constantly until thickened
5. Pour this mixture over the cooled pasta, stir, and store covered in the refrigerator overnight
6. The next day, add pineapple tidbits, fruit cocktail, cool whip and marshmallows
7. Refrigerate until serving time<|endoftext|>
