Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Black beans
• Corn kernel
• Salsa
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, heat the oil over medium heat
2. Add the onion, cover, and cook until softened, about 5 minutes
3. Add the beans and mash them until broken up
4. Add the corn and salsa, stirring to combine
5. Simmer, stirring, until the bean mixture is hot about 5 minutes
6. To assemble burritos, place 1 tortilla on a work surface and spoon about 1 / 2 cup of the filling mixture down the center
7. Roll up tightly, tucking in the sides
8. Repeat with the remaining ingredients
9. Serve seam side down<|endoftext|>
