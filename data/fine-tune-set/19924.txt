Ingredients:
• Red onion
• Garlic cloves
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Goat cheese
• Rosemary
• Sage
• Oregano
• Marjoram
• Thyme
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Rocket
• Olive oil
• Lemon zest

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in a pan and saut the onion and garlic until they have softened and just lightly browned
2. Add the sugar and continue cooking until the consistency of the contents of the pan is sticky like a jam
3. Remove from the pan
4. If you want to use the same pan, rinse or wash it as necessary and wipe it clean with kitchen towelling
5. Whisk the eggs lightly and pour into a pan
6. When the base of the omelette is beginning to set, add the cheese, onions and garlic, your choice of herbs and salt and pepper to taste
7. Cook the top of the omelette under a grill
8. Slide the omelette onto the serving plates and serve immediately with your choice of salad greens drizzled with olive oil and sprinkled with lemon zest<|endoftext|>
