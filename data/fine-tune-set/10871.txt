What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Adapted Columbia Restaurant 1905 Salad Recipe

Ingredients:
• Romaine lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Sweet onion
• Swiss cheese
• Ham
• Green olives
• Garlic cloves
• Oregano
• Worcestershire sauce
• White wine vinegar
• Lemon juice
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper
• Parmesan cheese
• French baguette
• Unsalted butter
• Garlic clove
• Smoked paprika
• Sea salt
• Manchego cheese

Steps:
1. Preparation:
2. Preheat the oven to 325f and line a baking sheet with parchment
3. Combine the butter, olive oil, garlic, paprika and sea salt in a small bowl
4. Spread on the rounds of bread and top each with a portion of the grated manchego
5. Bake until toasty and crisp, 5 to 7 minutes
6. Toss the lettuce, tomatoes, onion, swiss cheese, ham and olives in a large salad bowl
7. In a small bowl, combine the garlic, oregano, worcestershire, vinegar and lemon juice
8. Gradually whisk in the olive oil to form an emulsion
9. Add the dressing to salad and toss well
10. Season with salt and pepper to taste, sprinkle with the grated parmesan and serve with smoked paprika-manchego croutons<|endoftext|>
