Ingredients:
• Mushroom
• Chicken broth
• All-purpose flour
• Evaporated milk
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Tomatoes
• Water-packed artichoke hearts
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Paprika

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook mushrooms and broth over medium heat until tender, about 3 minutes
2. Remove mushrooms with a slotted spoon and set aside
3. Whisk flour and milk until smooth
4. Add to skillet
5. Bring to a boil
6. Cook and stir for 2 minutes
7. Remove from the heat
8. Stir in spinach, tomatoes and mushrooms
9. Place half of the artichoke in an ungreased 13-in
10. X 9-in
11. X 2-inch baking dish
12. Top with half of the spinach mixture
13. Repeat layers
14. Combine sour cream, mayonnaise, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt and pepper
15. Dollop over casserole
16. Sprinkle with paprika if desired
17. Bake, uncovered, at 350 for 25-30 minutes or until bubbly
18. Yield: 12-14 servings<|endoftext|>
