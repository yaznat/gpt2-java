Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Buttermilk
• Fresh lime juice
• Taco seasoning
• Onion powder
• Fresh cilantro
• Kosher salt
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Canola oil
• Black beans
• Avocado
• Plum tomato
• Romaine lettuce
• Tortilla chips
• Fresh cilantro leaves
• Lime wedges

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400f line a baking sheet with aluminum foil
2. Taco-ranch dressing: whisk together all the dressing ingredients in a medium bowl
3. Refrigerate until chilled, at least 30 minutes
4. Chicken: mix the taco seasoning, onion powder, and kosher salt in a small bowl
5. Season the chicken on both sides with the spice mixture and brush with the canola oil
6. Place the chicken on the prepared baking sheet and bake until opaque in the center, 15 to 20 minutes
7. Salad: combine the beans, avocado, tomato, and taco seasoning in a large bowl and toss gently
8. Add the lettuce, chips, and cilantro
9. Add 1 / 2 cup of the dressing and toss, adding additional dressing if necessary
10. Divide between 2 plates
11. Slice the chicken and fan on top of the salads
12. Garnish each with a lime wedge<|endoftext|>
