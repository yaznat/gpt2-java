Give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>Ambrosia Sweet Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Orange
• Lemon
• Crushed pineapple
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Shredded coconut
• Maraschino cherry

Steps:
1. Place sweet potatoes in a baking dish
2. Slice orange and lemon into thin slices
3. Alternate layering slices on top of sweet potatoes
4. Combine drained pineapple, brown sugar and melted butter
5. Pour mixture over sweet potatoes
6. Sprinkle with shredded coconut
7. Decorate with cherries
8. Bake in a 350f oven for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
