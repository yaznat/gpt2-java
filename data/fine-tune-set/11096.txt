Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado Crabmeat Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Crabmeat
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Dill weed
• White pepper
• Rye bread
• Butter
• Avocados
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Combine first 5 ingredients in a small bowl
2. Mix well and chill
3. Toast bread on both sides and lightly butter one side of each slice
4. Spread crab mixture evenly over each toast
5. Top sandwiches with with avocado slices and crumbled bacon<|endoftext|>
