Ingredients:
• Tapioca flour
• Brown rice flour
• Bean flour
• Baking powder
• Dry buttermilk
• Baking soda
• Sea salt
• Honey
• Vanilla
• Vegetable oil
• Warm water
• Egg whites

Steps:
1. Beat egg whites to stiff peaks, set aside
2. Spray griddle with cooking spray and preheat to 365f
3. Mix ingredients in order given, folding in egg whites last
4. Pour 2"-3" circles of batter onto preheated griddle
5. Or -- pour 1 cup of batter into a preheated waffle iron that has been sprayed with a nonstick vegetable spray
6. Cook until golden brown<|endoftext|>
