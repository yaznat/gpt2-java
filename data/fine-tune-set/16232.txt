Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Flour tortillas
• Bacon dip
• Lettuce leaves
• Cooked turkey breast
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Cook bacon til crisp
2. Drain on paper towels
3. Crumble and set aside
4. Spread each tortilla with dip to 1 / 2 inch of edges
5. Layer lettuce, bacon, turkey and tomato on each tortilla
6. Roll up
7. May add cheese it you like!<|endoftext|>
