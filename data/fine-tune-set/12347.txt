Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Red bell pepper
• Jalapeno
• Garlic
• Low sodium vegetable broth
• Chickpeas
• Tomatoes
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Light coconut milk
• Brown rice
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. In a medium stockpot, heat the oil over medium heat
2. Add the onion, bell pepper, and chili
3. Cook, stirring, until softened, about 5 minutes
4. Add the garlic and cook, stirring constantly, 1 minute
5. Add the broth, chickpeas, tomatoes, curry powder, salt, and black pepper
6. Bring to a boil over high heat
7. Reduce the heat and simmer gently, uncovered, stirring occasionally, about 10 minutes
8. Add the remaining ingredients and cook, stirring occasionally, until heated through, about 5 minutes
9. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
