Please give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Easy Meat Burritos

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Potatoes
• Diced green chilies
• Salt
• Ground pepper
• Ground cumin
• Onion powder
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Flour tortillas
• Cheese
• Guacamole
• Salsa
• Black olives
• Onion
• Chopped tomato
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef in a large skillet over medium heat breaking it into small pieces
2. Drain potatoes and add to the ground beef and mix well
3. Add the diced green chilies to the meat mixture and mix well
4. Add spices to taste and continue to cook until meat is done
5. Heat flour tortillas by placing them in the microwave for a few seconds until soft
6. Hint: to keep your tortillas from drying out, place a damp paper towel over the tortillas while heating in the microwave
7. Place a portion of the meat mixture on a warm tortilla and add your favorite toppings
8. Roll up in a burrito style or fold like a soft taco and enjoy<|endoftext|>
