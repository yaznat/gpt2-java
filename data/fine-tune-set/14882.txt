Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Dejah S Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Vegetable oil
• Diced onions
• Chicken
• Eggs
• Salt
• Msg
• Soya sauce

Steps:
1. Fry the egg, like a pancake
2. When cooled, dice
3. Set aside
4. Heat up the wok with the vegetable oil
5. When a light haze forms over the oil, sprinkle the salt over the entire wok surface
6. Carefully add the onions and diced meat
7. Stir-fry for a minute
8. If using green onions, leave them out in this step
9. Add the cooled rice, mixing well, turning the rice over continually until all is heated through
10. Sprinkle soya sauce over the rice--mixing until the rice is all the same desired colour
11. Not too much soya as it is salty, and you don't want it black!
12. Toss in cut up pre-cooked egg
13. Top with green onions
14. Options: add mushrooms, peas, blanched diced carrots in step #2
15. For authentic chinese version, using raw eggs, after frying the onions, meat, etc in step #2, break 2 raw eggs into the mixture, stir-frying until eggs are broken up and cooked
16. Then proceed to step #3
17. Add 2 tbsp curry powder in step #2 to spice it up!
18. If using fresh uncooked meat, cook the meat in the hot oil in step #2 before adding the onions
19. The amount of veg and meat given is just a guideline
20. Adjust according to your taste<|endoftext|>
