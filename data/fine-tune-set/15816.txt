Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Gluten free bread
• Chicken
• Tomatoes
• Frozen corn
• Salsa
• Mixed salad greens
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Wrap tortillas in the foil, and bake for 10 minutes
3. Put chicken, corn and salsa in skillet and cook on medium until bubbly, stirring often
4. Top each tortilla with chicken mixture, greens and tomato
5. Roll up, and cut in half to serve<|endoftext|>
