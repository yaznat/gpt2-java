Ingredients:
• Italian sausage
• Onion
• Garlic
• Red pepper
• Salsa
• Corn kernel
• Cilantro
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Parmesan cheese
• Ground black pepper
• Mozzarella cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Medium cheddar

Steps:
1. Remove sausage from casings and crumble into medium skillet
2. Add onion and garlic
3. Cook over medium heat stirring occasionally until meat is no longer pink
4. Slice 3 thin rings from bottom of red pepper, set aside to garnish
5. Chop remainder of pepper and stir into sausage
6. Add salsa, corn and cilantro
7. Set aside
8. Squeeze excess moisture from spinach
9. Toss with parmesan cheese, pepper and mozzarella cheese
10. Place one tortilla on lightly greased pizza pan or baking sheet
11. Spread generously 2 / 3 cup sausage mixture and top with 1 / 3 cup cheddar cheese
12. Cover with another tortilla
13. Distribute generously 1 cup spinach mixture on tortilla
14. Repeat layers ending with a tortilla
15. Press down lightly
16. Cover loosely with foil
17. Bake in 375 degree oven for 40 minutes
18. Uncover and sprinkle with remaining 1 / 3 cup cheddar cheese and red pepper rings
19. Continue to bake for 10 minutes or until heated through and cheese is melted
20. Let stand 10 minutes before cutting into wedges
21. Serve with additional salsa, sour cream or guacamole
22. Bake for a few minutes extra if cold<|endoftext|>
