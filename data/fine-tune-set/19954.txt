What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Crustless Stir Fry Quiche Or Scrambled Omelette

Ingredients:
• Yellow onion
• Ham
• Spinach
• Fresh parsley
• Eggs
• Milk
• Oil
• Salt & pepper
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Chop the onion and ham into 1 cm squares
2. Separate the stem and leaves of the parsley, then chop each
3. Heat a stir-fry pan to medium-high, add oil
4. Cook the onion till it softens a bit
5. Don't caramelize it! then add ham and the stem portion of the parsley
6. Stir for a minute or two
7. Add spinach, stir and cover
8. If the frying pan becomes too dry, add a little bit of milk
9. Meanwhile, use a fork to beat eggs in a medium bowl, then add the milk to the eggs
10. As you whisk the mixture, add salt and pepper
11. Put in the parsley leaves as well
12. When the spinach shrinks, reduce the heat to medium
13. Add the egg mixture, and mix evenly
14. Stir the bottom of the pan as the "quiche" cooks
15. When about half of the liquid is cooked, mix in cheese, reserving some for top
16. Cover and let the eggs steam in the pan
17. Don't be tempted to open the lid - or the top won't cook!
18. Remove the quiche wholly, or scramble it and transfer to a pie dish
19. Top with cheese & enjoy =)<|endoftext|>
