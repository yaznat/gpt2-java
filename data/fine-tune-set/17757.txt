What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Oat Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Oat bran
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Nuts
• Raisins
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. Line 12 muffin cups with paper liners, or grease muffin tins
3. In a large bowl, mix together dry ingredients, including nuts & raisins if using
4. In another bowl, mix together vanilla, oil, eggs & applesauce until well blended
5. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients
6. Add wet ingredients & mix until well blended
7. Let mixture sit for 10 minutes
8. Spoon mixture into paper-lined muffin cups and bake 15-20 minutes or until golden brown & toothpick comes out clean
9. Let muffins cool in pan for 5 minutes
10. Then remove to a wire rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
