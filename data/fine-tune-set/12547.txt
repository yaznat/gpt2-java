Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Autumn Stew

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Water
• Tomatoes
• Carrot
• Celery
• Potatoes
• Salt
• Brown bouquet sauce
• Pepper
• Bay leaf
• Basil

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, cook and stir meat until brown
2. Drain off fat
3. Cook and stir onions with meat until onions are tender, almost 5 minutes
4. Stir in all remaining ingredients
5. Heat to boiling
6. Reduce heat
7. Cover and simmer at least 40 minutes or till vegetables are tender<|endoftext|>
