Ingredients:
• Warm water
• Olive oil
• Honey
• Whole wheat flour
• Parmesan cheese
• Salt
• Active dry yeast

Steps:
1. Place ingredients as specified for your bread machine
2. Use the "dough" cycle
3. When bread machine is finished, punch down dough and let rest for about 5 minutes
4. Spread dough on a 14 inch round pizza pan, prick with fork and bake for 10 minutes at 425 degrees
