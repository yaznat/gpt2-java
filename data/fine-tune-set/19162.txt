What's a recipe for ice cream?<|endoftext|>Camel Tracks Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Whipping cream
• Milk
• Sugar
• Egg yolks
• Vanilla
• Instant coffee
• Peanut butter cup

Steps:
1. Combine the first 3 ingredients in a microwave safe bowl
2. Microwave on high for approximately 7 minutes, until almost boiling lightly beat the yolks in a small bowl
3. Whisk while gradually adding 1 cup of the hot mixture into the yolks
4. Whisk the yolks back into the hot mixture
5. Microwave on high for an additional 1-2 minutes
6. Whisk the mixture every 30 seconds while cooking
7. To check if the custard is sufficiently cooked, dip a spoon into the custard
8. Draw your finger across the back of the spoon, you should see the path of your finger drawn through the custard
9. Mix the vanilla and coffee into the custard
10. Chill the custard well
11. Freeze the chilled custard in an ice cream maker, following the manufacturer's recommendations
12. Add the chunks of reese's squares or peanut butter cups during final 2 minutes of freezing<|endoftext|>
