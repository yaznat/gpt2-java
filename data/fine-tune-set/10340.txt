Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Fisher chef's naturals whole almonds
• Ground allspice
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Butter
• Powdered sugar
• Almond extract
• Granulated sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. In medium bowl combine flour, chopped almonds, allspice, cinnamon and salt
3. Set aside
4. In large bowl cream butter and powdered sugar using electric mixer
5. Add almond extract
6. Stir in flour mixture
7. Mix until just combined
8. Shape level tablespoons of cookie dough to form balls
9. Place on baking sheet, spacing 1 inch apart
10. Gently press 1 almond into center of each ball
11. Sprinkle granulated sugar over cookies
12. Bake 16 to 18 minutes
13. Remove from oven
14. Cool cookies 3 minutes on baking sheet
15. Transfer cookies to cooling rack
16. Cool completely<|endoftext|>
