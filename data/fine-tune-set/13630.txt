Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Milk
• Oats
• Onions
• Salt
• Pepper
• Chili powder
• Catsup
• Water
• Vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Paprika

Steps:
1. Barbecue sauce:
2. In saucepan mix all ingredients for barbecue sauce
3. Simmer uncovered for 30 minutes
4. Preheat oven to 350
5. Mix all ingredients except barbecue sauce
6. Shape mixture into 16 patties
7. Place in ungreased baking pan, 13 x 9 x 2 inches
8. Pour barbecue sauce over patties
9. Bake uncovered 1 hour<|endoftext|>
