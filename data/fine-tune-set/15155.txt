Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Balsamic vinegar
• Granulated sugar
• Asparagus
• Tomatoes
• Dried basil
• Salt and pepper
• Whole wheat pizza crust
• Goat cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. In a frying pan, heat oil over medium-high heat
3. Saute onion and garlic until softened and golden, about 8 minutes
4. Add vinegar and sugar
5. Stir
6. Add asparagus
7. Cook for 3 minutes
8. Add tomato, basil, salt, and pepper
9. Cook for 1 minute
10. Spread asparagus mixture over whole-wheat pizza crust
11. Top with goat cheese
12. Place pizza directly on middle oven rack
13. Bake for 6 to 8 minutes or until crust browns
14. Broil for 2 minutes or until cheese begins to bubble<|endoftext|>
