Ingredients:
• Frozen cheese ravioli
• Boneless chicken breast halves
• Olive oil
• Alfredo sauce
• Fresh mushrooms
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Mozzarella cheese
• Seasoned bread crumbs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
2. Cook ravioli as directed
3. Drain and set aside
4. In a skillet, heat oil and add chicken breasts and saute until lightly browned on both sides
5. When chicken is browned push to one side of skillet and add mushrooms and cook about 2 minutes, just enough to slightly flavor and softened
6. Remove pan from heat
7. In a bowl combine, sauce, mushrooms and tomatoes
8. Add ravioli
9. Toss to coat
10. Transfer to a greased shallow 5 cup baking dish
11. Place chicken breasts on top of ravioli
12. Place a slice of cheese on each chicken breast
13. Sprinkle with bread crumbs
14. Bake, uncovered at 425 degrees for 15 to 20 minutes or until cheese is melted and golden on top<|endoftext|>
