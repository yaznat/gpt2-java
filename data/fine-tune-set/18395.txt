Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Buttermilk Pumpkin Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Kosher salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Ground cloves
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Butter
• Fresh pumpkin puree
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat the waffle iron to the desired setting
2. Sift together the flour, brown sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and spices, in a medium mixing bowl
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs, buttermilk, pumpkin, and butter, until smooth
4. Working in batches, whisk the dry ingredients into the wet, until well combined
5. Pour the batter onto the center of the lower grid, and spread it evenly to the edges
6. The entire lower grid should be filled
7. Flip the lid, and cook until ready
8. Drizzle with syrup or confectioners sugar, and serve warm!<|endoftext|>
