Ingredients:
• Cinnamon rolls
• Chocolate milk
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Lightly coat six 8-oz
3. Ramekins with nostick spray
4. Place in a large baking pan
5. Place bottoms of rolls in ramekins
6. Whisk milk and eggs to blend
7. Pour half over roll bottoms
8. Add roll tops
9. Pour on remaining mixture
10. Place ramekins in a shallow baking pan
11. Add boiling water to pan to come halfway up sides of ramekins
12. Bake 35 minutes or until a knife inserted near centers comes out clean
13. Remove ramekins to a wire rack
14. Cool to room temperature
15. Serve or refrigerate up to 2 days
16. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
