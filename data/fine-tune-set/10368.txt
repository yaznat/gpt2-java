What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Almond Toffee Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Icing sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Almond extract
• Whole wheat flour
• White flour
• Baking soda
• Cream of tartar
• Salt
• Toffee pieces
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees
2. In a mixing bowl, cream butter and 1 cup sugar and 1 cup icing sugar
3. Add the oil, eggs and extract
4. Mix well
5. In another bowl, combine the whole wheat flour and white flour, baking soda, cream of tartar and salt
6. Gradually add to the creamed mixture
7. Stir in almonds and toffee bits
8. Shape into 1-inch balls
9. Roll in icing sugar
10. Place on ungreased baking sheets, and flatten with a fork
11. Bake for 12-14 minutes, or until lightly browned
12. Sprinkle with additional icing sugar while warm<|endoftext|>
