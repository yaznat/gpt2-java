What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Cashew Chicken Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Ground ginger
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Garlic cloves
• Carrot
• Celery
• Broccoli florets
• Fresh snow pea
• Cashews
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In a skillet, heat 3 t
2. Chicken broth
3. Meanwhile, combine the cornstarch, soy sauce, ginger and remaining broth until smooth
4. Set aside
5. Add chicken to the skillet
6. Stir-fry over medium heat until no longer pink, about 3 - 5 minutes
7. Remove and keep warm
8. Add garlic, carrots and celery
9. Stir fry for 3 minutes
10. Add broccoli and peas
11. Stir fry for 4-5 minutes or until crisp-tender
12. Stir broth mixture
13. Add to the skillet with chicken
14. Cook and stir for 2 minutes
15. Stir in cashews
16. Serve over rice, if desired<|endoftext|>
