Ingredients:
• Plum tomatoes
• Chicken breasts
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Canola oil
• White onion
• Garlic clove
• Jalapeno pepper
• Lime juice
• Fresh cilantro
• Scallions
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. Heat a medium skillet over high heat until very hot
2. Add tomatoes and cook, turning occasionally with tongs, until charred on all sides, 8 to 10 minutes
3. Transfer to a plate to cool slightly
4. Cut the tomatoes in half crosswise
5. Squeeze to discard seeds
6. Remove cores and chop the remaining pulp and skin
7. Cut chicken into 1-inch chunks
8. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
9. Add 1 teaspoon oil to the pan and heat over high heat until very hot
10. Add the chicken and cook, stirring occasionally, until it is browned and no longer pink in the middle, 3 to 5 minutes
11. Transfer to a plate
12. Reduce the heat to medium and add the remaining 1 teaspoon oil
13. Add onion and cook, stirring, until softened, about 2 minutes
14. Add garlic and jalapeo and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 30 seconds
15. Add lime juice, the chicken and tomatoes
16. Cook, stirring, until heated through, 1 to 2 minutes
17. Stir in cilantro and scallions
18. Divide the chicken mixture among tortillas
19. Serve with lime wedges<|endoftext|>
