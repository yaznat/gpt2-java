Give me a beans recipe.<|endoftext|>Baby Lima Beans And Corn

Ingredients:
• Frozen baby lima beans
• Corn kernels
• Bacon drippings
• Water
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. In a medium size saucepan bring water to a boil
2. Place frozen lima beans, bacon drippings, salt and pepper in water
3. Cover with a lid
4. Turn heat down to very low heat and let simmer for an hour and 15 minutes
5. Keep a watchful eye and do not let water boil out
6. Pour drained can of corn kernels into bean mixture and simmer 15 more minutes
7. Serve and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
