Give me a coffee recipe.<|endoftext|>Black Coffee Barbecue Sauce

Ingredients:
• Brewed coffee
• Ketchup
• Red wine vinegar
• Dark brown sugar
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Dark molasses
• Hot dry mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Cumin
• Chili powder

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan and simmer over low heat for 20 minutes
2. Cool, then puree in a blender or food processor until smooth
3. This can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks in a covered container<|endoftext|>
