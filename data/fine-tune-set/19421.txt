What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Ara S Potato Oup

Ingredients:
• Cream of chicken soup
• Cream of celery soup
• Potatoes
• Onions
• Celery
• Carrots
• Water
• Butter
• Milk
• Bouillon cubes
• Potato flakes

Steps:
1. Cook potatoes, onions, celery, and carrots in water until tender
2. Meanwhile, in other pan, put in milk, butter, cream of chicken, cream of celery, and bullion cubes on low heat
3. When potatoes and the vegetables are tender, pour in the milk, butter, the cream of chicken and cream of celery, with the bullion cubes into the pan with the potatoes
4. Put on medium heat and stir in potato flakes occasionally
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
