Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Paprika

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large frying pan
2. Add potatoes and onions
3. Season to taste with salt, pepper and optional garlic powder
4. Fry potatoes for about 5 minutes, turning occasionally
5. Sprinkle paprika over potatoes and continue cooking and turning them for another 5-10 minutes until heated through
6. Serve with ketchup<|endoftext|>
