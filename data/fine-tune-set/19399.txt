Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Soymilk
• Apple cider vinegar
• Pure vanilla extract
• Egg
• Margarine

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 f
2. In a large bowl, combine dry ingredients with a whisk to mix thoroughly
3. Combine wet ingredients in a small sauce pan over medium low heat and mix until earth balance is melted
4. This mixture should not get too hot, you should be able to stick your finger in the mixture
5. It should feel slightly warm
6. Add wet to dry and mix until just combined
7. It should form a very soft dough
8. Using a tablespoon measure, scoop out dough into your ungreased nonstick mini-donut pan
9. Smooth out the top of the dough with your fingers, this will make for more even, prettier donuts, but isnt crucial
10. The dough should sit just below the rim of the pan
11. Bake for 12 minutes
12. They should not be browned on top, but a tester will come out clean
13. Invert hot pan over a cutting board or cooling rack to release donuts<|endoftext|>
