What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Fried Rice For 2

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Cooked rice
• Soy sauce
• Green onion
• Egg
• Chicken

Steps:
1. Heat oil or bacon drippings in an 8 inch skillet
2. Add rice
3. Stir-fry until rice is hot and mixed with oil and drippings
4. Add soy sauce, mix well
5. If rice is dry, add 1 to 2 tablespoons water
6. Cover and cook gently until water is absorbed
7. Stir in green onion
8. Push rice to side of skillet
9. Beat egg separately or break into skillet and quickly scramble with a fork
10. As egg sets, stir gently to let uncooked egg glow undereath
11. Stir cooked egg into rice, breking egg into small pieces
