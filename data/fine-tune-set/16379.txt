Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Bacon
• Cheese
• Meat
• Vegetables

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Spray muffin tin
3. Line each spot with a bacon around the edges
4. Whip all eggs
5. Mix in cheese and other desired toppings
6. Evenly pour the egg mix into each spot, filling about 1 / 2 way or 3 / 4 way
7. Bake about 25 minutes or until bacon is cooked
8. Pop out and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
