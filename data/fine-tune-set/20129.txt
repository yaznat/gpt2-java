What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Vegetable Stir Fry With Sugar Snap Peas And Baby Bok Choy

Ingredients:
• Vegetable stock
• Hoisin sauce
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Honey
• Garlic clove
• Ginger
• Vegetable oil
• Sweet onion
• Red bell peppers
• Green bell peppers
• Sugar snap peas
• Baby bok choy
• Dried shiitake mushrooms
• Pork tenderloin
• Green onion

Steps:
1. In a small dish, whisk together the sauce ingredients and set aside
2. In a large wok or skillet heat add a small amount of vegetable oil and heat over medium high heat
3. When a drop of water sizzles in the pan add the onions and cook until lightly browned
4. Stir in the peppers and peas
5. Cut the baby bok choy in fourths lengthwise and then in 1 inch lengths
6. Add to wok and stir-fry until the bok choy begins to turn dark green, about 3 minutes
7. Add drained mushrooms, sliced pork if using and sauce
8. Cook just until warmed through<|endoftext|>
