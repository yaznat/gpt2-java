Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Bourbon
• Powdered sugar
• Butter
• Chocolate chips
• Water

Steps:
1. Bake brownies in 9 x 13 inch pan according to package direction
2. Remove from oven and immediately pour 1 / 3 cup bourbon over hot brownie
3. Cool frosting: mix 2 cups powdered sugar, 1 stick butter and 3 tbsp bourbon until creamy
4. Spread over cooled brownies
5. Melt chocolage chips, 1 tbsp butter and 3 tbsp water together
6. With a teaspoon, drizzle melted chocolate mixture over white frosting
7. Refrigerate
8. When completely cooled, cut into small bars
9. Freezes well!<|endoftext|>
