Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Banana Berry Brownie Pizza

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Water
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Strawberries
• Firm banana
• Semisweet chocolate

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine brownie mix, water, oil, and egg until well blended
2. Spread into a greased and floured 12-in pizza pan
3. Bake at 350 for 25 minutes
4. In a mixing bowl, beat the cream sheese, sugar, egg, and vanilla until combined
5. Spread over brownie crust
6. Bake 15 minutes longer or until topping is set
7. Cool on a wire rack
8. Just before serving, arrange strawberries and bananas over topping
9. Drizzle with chocolate
10. Refrigerate leftovers<|endoftext|>
