Ingredients:
• Fish fillets
• Fat-free mayonnaise
• Plain low-fat yogurt
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Lemon pepper seasoning
• Breadcrumbs
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Place fillets in 13 x 9-inch baking dish sprayed lightly with cooking spray
3. Combine mayonnaise, cheese and seasoning in bowl
4. Mix well and spoon over fillets
5. Spray breadcrumbs lightly with cooking spray and toss to blend
6. Sprinkle over cheese topping
7. Bake uncovered for 9 to 11 minutes
8. Toast in broiler for 30 seconds or until breadcrumbs are golden brown<|endoftext|>
