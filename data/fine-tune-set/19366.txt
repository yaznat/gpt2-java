Ingredients:
• Coffee
• Milk
• Vanilla ice cream
• Doughnuts
• Ice cubes
• Cinnamon
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a blender and process until smooth
2. Mix the cinnamon and sugar in a shallow dish
3. Wet the rim of the glass with a little coffee or milk and coat it in cinnamon sugar before pouring the milkshake inches<|endoftext|>
