Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>Award Winning Texas Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground sirloin
• Beef broth
• Chicken broth
• Hunts tomato sauce
• Granulated onion
• Cayenne
• Beef bouillon granules
• Salt
• Chicken bouillon granules
• Gebhardt chili powder
• Dark chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Granulated garlic
• Sazon goya
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Brown ground sirloin and drain
2. Add beef and chicken broth and tomato sauce to meat and bring to a boil
3. Add first spices 2 tsp granulated onion, 1 / 2 tsp cayenne, 2 tsp wylers beef granules, 1 / 4 tsp salt, 2 tsp wylers chicken granules, 1 tbsp light chili powder, 2 tbsp dark chili powder
4. Cover and cook 1 hour
5. After 1 hour add: 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp granulated garlic, 2 tbsp dark chili powder, 1 tbsp light chili powder, 1 packet sazon goya
6. Adjust liquid with remainder of chicken broth, if necessary
7. Cover and cook 30 minutes
8. After 2nd spice dump and 30 minutes add: 1 tbsp dark chili powder, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 / 4 tsp granulated garlic, 1 / 4 tsp cayenne, 1 / 4 tsp brown sugar
9. Reduce heat to a slow boil
10. Cook 10 minutes
11. Adjust salt, cayenne, and light chili powder to taste<|endoftext|>
