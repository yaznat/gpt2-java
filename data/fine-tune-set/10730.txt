What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacony Blue Cheese Chicken Pasta

Ingredients:
• Rigatoni pasta
• Butter
• White wine
• Yellow onions
• Chicken breast halves
• Salt and pepper
• Olive oil
• Blue cheese
• Fresh rosemary
• Bacon
• Parmesan cheese
• Garlic salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Begin by cooking the pasta per package instructions
2. In a large sauce pan over medium heat add 3 tablespoons butter and, if desired, a splash of white wine
3. Add to the sizziling butter the sliced onions, stirring every 5 minutes for a total of 25 minutes, or until they turn a caramel color
4. Season breasts with salt and pepper
5. Broil chicken breasts halves about 12 minutes, flipping halfway through until cooked
6. Careful not to overcook
7. Breasts may, also, be grilled or pan fried
8. When the pasta is cooked, drain and place in a large bowl while hot
9. Stir in the olive oil
10. Over the pasta, evenly sprinkling the blue cheese letting the cheese melt
11. Slice the chicken into strips
12. Place over pasta and cheese
13. Sprinkle over 1 tablespoon fresh chopped rosemary and the cooked, chopped bacon
14. Add the caramelized onions to the pasta and chicken, gently toss and season with a few shakes of garlic salt and pepper
15. Top each serving with parmesan<|endoftext|>
