Can you give me a casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Aaron Tippin S Mexican Casserole

Ingredients:
• Corn tortilla
• Chili beans
• Cheddar cheese
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Water
• Yellow onion

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Brown the meat and onion in a skillet
3. Add the taco seasoning and water, and let simmer for 5 minutes
4. In a baking dish, put one layer of tortillas, then 1 / 3 meat mixture on top, followed by layer of beans and layer of cheese
5. Repeat two more times, ending with cheese
6. Bake for 30 minutes, or until cheese is melted and beginning to brown<|endoftext|>
