Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Arkansas German Baked Pancake

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Butter
• Granny smith apples
• Honey
• Powdered sugar
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Make apple filling: melt butter in a large skillet over med-high heat
2. Add in apples
3. Cook, stirring constantly, 12 minutes, until tender
4. Gently stir in honey
5. Cook just until thoroughly heated
6. Keep warm
7. Make pancake: heat a 12-inch cast-iron skillet in a 450 oven for 5 minutes
8. Combine the eggs, milk, and vanilla in a bowl
9. Beat at medium speed using an electric mixer, until smooth
10. Add in flour and salt
11. Beat 5 minutes
12. Melt butter in skillet
13. Pour batter into hot skillet
14. Bake at 450 for 15 minutes
15. Decrease temperature to 350
16. Bake 8 minutes or until puffed and browned
17. Remove from oven
18. Spoon apple filling over pancake
19. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and dollop with whipped cream
20. Cut into wedges and serve<|endoftext|>
