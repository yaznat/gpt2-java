What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>10 Grain Sorta Healthy Cookies

Ingredients:
• Ten-grain pancake mix
• Sugar
• Crisp rice cereal
• Butter
• Egg
• Maple extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Mix all ingredients
3. Roll into small balls
4. Place on a cookie sheet
5. Bake for 10 minutes
6. Cool for one minute, put on cooling rack
7. Give them to kids with a little less guilt<|endoftext|>
