Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Onion
• Broth
• Water
• Cumin
• Rotel
• Diced tomatoes
• Dried cilantro
• Sour cream
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Place onion, water, broth and cumin in a stock pot and bring to a boil
2. Reduce to medium heat
3. When onions are soft, place beans and rotel in the stock pot
4. Simmer for 20 minutes
5. Blend with a hand blender
6. Add diced tomatoes and cilantro
7. Simmer for 10-15 more minutes
8. Serve topped with a bit of sour cream and crushed tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
