What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>4 5 Calorie Iced Coffee

Ingredients:
• Instant coffee
• Equal sugar substitute
• Water
• Ice

Steps:
1. Put the coffee and equal into a blender
2. Add the ice then the water to the blender
3. Thouroughly blend the ice until it has the consistency of sludge
4. Be sure to stop blending frequently and mix the top ice to the bottom
5. When thourougly blended spoon out of the blender and enjoy<|endoftext|>
