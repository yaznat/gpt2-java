Give me a pie recipe.<|endoftext|>String Pie

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Garlic clove
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Spaghetti sauce
• Spaghetti
• Parmesan cheese
• Eggs
• Butter
• Cottage cheese
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Cook beef, onion, green pepper and garlic in a large skillet until browned
2. Drain
3. Stir in red pepper flakes and spaghetti sauce and set aside
4. In a large bow, mix spaghetti, parmesan cheese, eggs and butter
5. Spread in a greased casserole dish
6. Spread cottage cheese over the top
7. Pour sauce mixture over cottage cheese
8. And sprinkle with mozzarella cheese
9. Bake at 350 degrees for about 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
