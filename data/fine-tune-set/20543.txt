What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Bachelors Tuna Casserole

Ingredients:
• Tuna fish
• Dry pasta
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Green peas
• Shredded cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a 5-quart stove-top pot, bring water to a boil
2. Add pasta and hold at a boil until tender
3. Then turn off the heat and drain pasta in the 5-quart pot
4. Add tuna, peas, and can of soup
5. Stir gently to not break the pasta
6. Pour mixture into a 9x12-inch baking dish or an 8x8 baking dish
7. Distribute cheese evenly over the pasta mix
8. At this point, you can let it cool and cover it after 20 minutes, refrigerate and prepare for dinner the following evening, or put it directly into a 350f oven and bake uncovered for 30 minutes
9. Carefully remove from oven with hot mitts and serve
10. Can be served alone or with hot bread and a green salad<|endoftext|>
