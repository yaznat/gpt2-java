Ingredients:
• Butter
• Caster sugar
• Eggs
• Self-raising flour
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Measure out all the ingredients, put in to a large bowl and beat until well blended
3. Divide this between the two tins
4. Level out and bake in the oven for approx 30-35 minutes, until well risen and the top of the sponges spring back when lightly pressed with your finger
5. Leave them to cool for 5 minutes in the tin before you turn them out
6. Remove the paper and let them completely cool on a cake rack
7. When cold, sandwich them together with your favorite filling
8. Sprinkle caster or powdered sugar on the top<|endoftext|>
