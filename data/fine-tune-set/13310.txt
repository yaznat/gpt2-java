Give me a chicken recipe.<|endoftext|>A 1 Bombay Chicken

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Boneless chicken breasts
• Cooking oil
• Onion
• Golden raisin
• Basmati rice
• Water
• Flaked coconut
• Salted peanuts
• Curry powder

Steps:
1. Prepare / measure all ingredients first
2. Mix flour, salt and pepper and coat chicken
3. Melt margaine in wok over medium high heat
4. Brown chicken and set aside
5. Reduce heat to medium
6. Do not clean wok
7. Cook onion in wok until tender but not brown
8. Add raisins and heat until they puff
9. Add curry and stir until mixed
10. Stir in rice, coconut and peanuts
11. Mix well
12. Add water
13. Spoon rice mixture into ungreased baking dish
14. Place chicken on top - skin side up
15. Bake at 350f about 45 min or until chicken is cooked
16. Serve in baking dish<|endoftext|>
