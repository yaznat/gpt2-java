Ingredients:
• Slivered almonds
• Amaretto
• Brewed coffee
• Whipped cream
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. In a coffee cup, stir together the coffee, amaretto, and brown sugar
2. Stir to dissolve sugar
3. Top with a dollop of whipped cream and sprinkle with almonds, if desired<|endoftext|>
