Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Corn Waffles With Cilantro Lime Butter

Ingredients:
• Self-rising flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Vegetable oil
• Frozen whole kernel corn
• Butter
• Fresh cilantro
• Lime rind
• Fresh lime juice

Steps:
1. For the cilantro-lime butter: mix together all the ingredients in a small bowl
2. Cover and chill until ready to serve
3. For the waffles: add the first 3 ingredients to a large mixing bowl
4. Stir to combine
5. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients
6. In another bowl, add the eggs, buttermilk, and vegetable oil
7. Whisk to combine
8. Add in the corn
9. Stir to combine
10. Add the corn mixture to the dry ingredients
11. Stir just until moistened
12. Cook in a pre-heated oiled waffle iron until golden
13. Serve warm with cilantro-lime butter<|endoftext|>
