Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Oil
• Refried beans
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Jalapenos
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Pam cooking spray
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Trim breast and pound to 1 / 4 inch cutlets
2. Completely coat cutlets in a 50 / 50 mixture of chili powder and cumin
3. In a large pre-heated oil-coated skillet, fry cutlets until cooked through over med-high heat
4. Let cutlets cool for 10-15 minutes then cut into bite-size strips
5. Heat refried beans over low heat while letting chicken cool and cutting vegetables
6. Dice tomato into bite-size pieces
7. Mince onion and jalapenos
8. When chicken is able to be handled comfortably and beans are heated through, you can begin assembly
9. Spread 1 / 4 of the refried beans on one side of tortilla leaving about a 1 / 2 to 1 inch of uncoated tortilla around the edges
10. Fill the center of bean coated tortilla with chicken, tomatoes, onions, jalapenos, and cheese
11. Roll up the tortilla into the shape of a burrito
12. In a large skillet preheated to medium and sprayed with pam or lightly coated with butter, placed burritos in the pan with the folds face down
13. Cover and fry for about 5 minutes
14. Flip and cook for 5 more minutes while covered
15. Tortilla shell should become nicely toasted
16. Serve with salsa and or guacamole on the side
17. I also make some spanish rice to go along with this dish as well
18. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
