Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Onion
• Garlic
• Dark sesame oil
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Chicken broth
• Tomato paste
• Stewed tomatoes
• Chunky peanut butter

Steps:
1. In a large pot, saute chicken, onion and garlic in sesame oil for about 10 minutes, until onion is tender
2. Add curry powder, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes and saute 1 minute more
3. Add chicken broth, tomato paste, stewed tomatoes and peanut butter, stirring until well-combined
4. Heat until very hot but not boiling
5. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
