Ingredients:
• Old-fashioned oatmeal
• Walnuts
• Dried apricot
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Honey
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Put paper liners in 12 muffin cups
3. In a large mixing bowl, mix oats, walnuts, apricots, baking powder, & salt
4. In a small bowl whisk together milk, honey, eggs, & vegetable oil
5. Add the wet to the dry and mix til just combined
6. Let sit for 3-5 minutes
7. Fill muffin cups 3 / 4 full
8. Bake 13 minutes or until toothpick inserted in middle comes out clean<|endoftext|>
