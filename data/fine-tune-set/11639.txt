Ingredients:
• Pretzel
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Brown sugar
• Cornstarch
• Chocolate chips
• Peanut butter
• Egg substitute
• Vanilla extract
• Peanuts
• White chocolate chips
• Chocolate

Steps:
1. Crust: mix pretzels and butter until pretzel crumbs are moistened and press into bottom of a 4 inch springform pan
2. Filling: combine cream cheese, brown sugar, and cornstarch
3. Beat until smooth
4. Beat in melted chocolate, peanut butter, egg substitute and vanilla
5. Beat well after each addition
6. Add peanuts if desired
7. Bake at 350f for 10 minutes
8. Reduce heat to 225 and bake for an additional 40-50 minutes or until the center no longer looks wet or shiny
9. Remove cake from oven and run a knife around the inside edge of the pan
10. Turn the oven off and return the cake to the oven for an additional 20 minutes
11. Chill, uncovered, overnight
12. Topping: in small saucepan melt white chocolate and peanut butter over low heat, stirring constantly
