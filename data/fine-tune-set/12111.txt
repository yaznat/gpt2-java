Ingredients:
• Condensed milk
• Real maple syrup
• Salt
• Pie shells
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan combine maple syrup, condensed milk and the pinch of salt
2. Heat over very low heat, stirring constantly until bubbles form in center of pan
3. Cool slightly
4. Pour into baked pie shell
5. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours
6. Top with whipped cream or cool whip when ready to serve<|endoftext|>
