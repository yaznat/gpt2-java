What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus Omelette Wraps

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Fresh sage
• Fresh thyme
• Garlic cloves
• Pecorino cheese
• Asparagus
• Extra virgin olive oil

Steps:
1. Beat the eggs in a bowl
2. Add the milk, sage, thyme, garlic, pecorino and season with cracked black pepper
3. Lay the asparagus lengthways in a pan with just enough salted boiling water to cover the spears
4. Cook for 2 minutes until they are tender but still crisp
5. Heat a large non stick flat pan with a little olive oil
6. Pour a ladle of the egg mixture into the pan and roll the pan around until the egg is thinly layered over the base and it is cooked on one side
7. Reduce the heat and flip to cook the egg on the other side
8. Repeat until all the egg mixture is used up
9. Fill the crepes with asparagus and serve with an extra sprinkle of grated pecorino
10. Then trim the broken end with a sharp knife and the asparagus is ready to cook<|endoftext|>
