Ingredients:
• Red onion
• Olive oil
• Caraway seed
• Eggs
• Water
• Jarlsberg cheese
• Ham

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. In a large, oven-proof frying pan, slowly saute the red onion and caraway seeds in olive oil over medium heat until onion begins to brown
3. While the onion browns, whisk together eggs, water, shredded jarlsberg cheese, and diced ham
4. When onion has become translucent and brown around the edges, fold egg mixture into onions in frying pan and allow to cook at medium an additional 2 or 3 minutes to brown the bottom of the omelette
5. Transfer frying pan to upper rack of preheated oven and cook until nicely browned, about 15 to 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
