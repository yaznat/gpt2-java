Ingredients:
• Frozen orange juice concentrate
• Fresh lime juice
• Curry powder
• Firm tofu

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
2. Combine orange juice, lime juice and curry powder in a 9 x 13 glass or ceramic baking dish
3. Drain tofu
4. Cut block of tofu horizontally into three pieces
5. Then, with pieces stacked on top of each other, cut tofu on the diagonal from one corner to the other
6. Repeat the cuts on the other two corners
7. You should have 12 triangles of tofu
8. Place tofu pieces in a single layer in the baking dish and turn to coat with the marinade
9. Bake the tofu for about 25 minutes, turning once, until the marinade is absorbed and the tofu is browned
10. Serve immediately or use cold or at room temperature as a sandwich filler<|endoftext|>
