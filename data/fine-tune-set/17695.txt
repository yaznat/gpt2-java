Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Crunch Cakelets Muffins

Ingredients:
• Shortening
• Sugar
• Egg
• Flour
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Milk
• Apple

Steps:
1. Cream shortening and sugar until well blended
2. Add egg
3. Beat until smooth
4. Add dry ingredients with milk and stir just until blended
5. Stir in apples
6. Drop in 12 greased muffin tins
7. Sprinkle with brown sugar
8. Bake at 375 for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
