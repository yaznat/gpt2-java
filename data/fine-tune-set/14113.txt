Ingredients:
• Sesame seeds
• Minced garlic clove
• Firm tofu
• Corn flakes
• Vegetable broth
• Oatmeal
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350
2. Spray a 8x8 square baking pan with nonstick spray and set aside
3. Grind the sesame seeds into a coarse meal using a food processor or spice / coffee grinder
4. Place in a large mixing bowl
5. Mix and mash together with garlic, tofu, corn flakes, oatmeal, pepper, and soy sauce, adding only as much liquid as needed to create a soft, moist loaf that holds together and is not runny
6. Add more binder / carbohydrate as needed if the loaf seems too wet
7. Press mixture into the prepared pan and bake for 45 minutes to 1 hour, or until cooked through
8. Let the loaf cool in the pan for 10 to 15 minutes, then turn out onto a plate or platter and slice
9. Serve with potatoes, vegetables, and vegetarian gravy, if desired<|endoftext|>
