Ingredients:
• White bread
• Mayonnaise
• Bananas

Steps:
1. Spread a tablespoon on one side of each slice on bread
2. Peel and cut the bananas into 1 / 4-inch round slices
3. Lay the slices on two of the slices of bread and then top with the other half
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
