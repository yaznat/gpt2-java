What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Sour Cream Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Egg
• Sour cream
• Milk
• Butter
• Banana

Steps:
1. Sift together dry ingredients and set aside
2. Mix together beaten egg yolk, sour cream, milk, butter and banana
3. Add to flour mixture, mixing well
4. Fold in egg white which has been whipped until stiff
5. Pour into oiled waffle iron and bake until brown<|endoftext|>
