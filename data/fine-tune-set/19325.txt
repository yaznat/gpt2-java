Ingredients:
• Sugar-free jello
• Kool-aid
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Hot water
• Cold water

Steps:
1. Combine jello, kool-aid and splenda with hot water and stir until dissolved
2. Add cold water and stir for 2-3 minutes
3. Pour into ice cube trays or popcicle molds, freeze and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
