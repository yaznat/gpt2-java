Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground ginger
• Cinnamon
• Allspice
• Clove
• Salt
• Pepper
• Eggs
• Molasses
• Butter
• Tart apples
• Fresh lemon juice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 350 degrees f
2. Combine flour, brown sugar, 1 / 3 cup of the granulated sugar, baking powder, baking soda, 1 tsps of the ginger, remaining spices and salt and pepper in a bowl
3. Add 2 of the eggs, molasses and 6 tblsps of the butter and blend until just combined
4. Scrape dough into buttered 9 or 10 inch springform pan and, with lightly-floured hands, pat it evenly onto the bottom and 2 inches up sides of pan
5. Arrange the apple slices, cored side down, in concentric circles over the gingerbread
6. Sprinkle them with remaining tsp ginger and bake for 30 minutes, or until apples are browned lightly
7. Whisk together remaining 2 eggs, cup sugar, 4 tblsps butter, rind and lemon juice and pour evenly over apples, being careful not to let any drip onto sides of pan
8. Bake another 20-25 minutes, or until custard is just set
9. Serve at room temperature<|endoftext|>
