Ingredients:
• Candy cane
• Whipping cream
• Milk
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Crack both eggs into a bowl and whisk until light and fluffy
2. Slowly add sugar and whisk till blended
3. Add whipping cream and milk and whisk together
4. Add the vanilla extract and mix
5. Crush candy canes and add to mixture
6. Put mixture into ice cream maker and wait till done
7. If you want you can save extra candy canes and add them when the ice cream is ready so you have candy cane chunks or you can not add the candy canes in the mixture and just put it in before it's put in the freezer<|endoftext|>
