Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Barley
• Beef broth
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef, add vegetables, tomatoes, barley, and broth
2. Salt and pepper to taste
3. Let it simmer for at least 30 minutes
4. This is great for a crock pot, as the longer it cooks, the better it is<|endoftext|>
