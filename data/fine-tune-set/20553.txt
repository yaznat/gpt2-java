Ingredients:
• French bread
• Gruyere cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Bacon
• Scallions
• Eggs
• Milk
• White wine
• Dijon mustard
• Sour cream
• Black pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Spray 9x13 pan with cooking spray
2. Cover bottom with bread, sprinkle with cheeses, bacon and scallions
3. Beat together eggs through cayenne pepper until foamy
4. Pour over bread, cheese, bacon and scallion mixture
5. Cover with foil and bake at 350f for 20 minutes
6. Remove foil and sprinkle with parmesan cheese
7. Bake uncovered for 30 more minutes<|endoftext|>
