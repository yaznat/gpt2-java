What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>10 Minute German Chocolate Pie

Ingredients:
• German sweet chocolate
• Milk
• Sugar
• Cream cheese
• Cool whip
• Graham cracker crust

Steps:
1. Heat chocolate and 2 tablespoons of milk over low heat, stirring until chocolate is melted
2. Beat sugar into cream cheese
3. Add remaining milk and chocolate
4. Beat until smooth
5. Fold in cool whip
6. Put in crust and freeze for 4 hours<|endoftext|>
