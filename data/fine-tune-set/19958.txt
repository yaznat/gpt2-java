What's a recipe for an omelette?<|endoftext|>Dubliner Flat Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Potatoes
• Cheese
• Cooked shrimp
• Chives
• Butter
• Salt and pepper
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Chop the tomato into small cubes,
2. Beat the eggs, season with pepper and salt
3. In a large pan, melt the butter
4. Add the potato and shrimp and cook for 2 minutes
5. Add eggs and let cook for a few minutes
6. Add tomato and grated cheese
7. Finish under the grill or cover pan for a minute or two, until cheese melts and eggs are cooked to your liking
8. Garnish with chives
9. Serve with homemade brown bread and butter
10. Finish breakfast with more brown bread, butter and irish marmalade!<|endoftext|>
