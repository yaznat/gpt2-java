Ingredients:
• Lemon, juice and zest of
• Catsup
• Horseradish
• Flour
• Cornmeal
• Blackening seasoning
• Kosher salt
• Oysters
• Eggs
• Oil

Steps:
1. Mix all sauce ingredients together and refrigerate
2. Mix together flour, cornmeal, blackened seasoning and kosher salt
3. Dip each oyster in the eggs, then into the flour mixture
4. Allow to sit while heating the oil
5. Heat 1 / 2 inch oil in a cast iron skillet until almost smoking
6. Without crowding or touching, fry on both sides until golden
7. Remove to a platter covered in paper towels to drain
8. Serve with a side of sauce and a lemon wedge to garnish<|endoftext|>
