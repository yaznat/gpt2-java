Give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>20 Minute Chicken Alfredo Recipe

Ingredients:
• Half-and-half cream
• Philadelphia cream cheese
• Kraft 100% parmesan cheese
• Butter
• Fettuccine pasta
• Boneless chicken breasts
• Black pepper
• Italian seasoning

Steps:
1. Season boneless chicken breast with black pepper and italian seasoning to taste
2. Cook as desired
3. Boil noodles per directions
4. In a medium, non-stick sauce pan place half & half, butter, and cream cheese into pan
5. Stir constantly on low / low-medium setting and gradually add parmesan cheese
6. Sauce will thicken as desired, usually 15-20 minutes
7. Drain noodles, cut chicken into strips and top with alfredo sauce
8. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
