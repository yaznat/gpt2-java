Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Alpine Village House Soup Chicken Supreme

Ingredients:
• Water
• Celery salt
• Accent seasoning
• Ground chicken
• Onion
• Yellow food coloring
• Salt
• Pepper
• Kitchen bouquet
• Chicken bouillon
• Carrots
• Roux
• Vegetable oil
• All-purpose flour

Steps:
1. To prepare, boil all soup ingredients together for about 30 minutes
2. To prepare the roux, heat the vegetable oil until smoking
3. Add the flour and stir with a wire whisk
4. The oil and flour roux should be the consistency of mashed potatoes
5. Add the roux to the soup and use the wire whisk to blend
6. Serve hot and enjoy
7. Puree in blender for a creamier texture<|endoftext|>
