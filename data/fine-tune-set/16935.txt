What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Best Ever Fruit Cocktail Cake

Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Fruit cocktail in juice
• Flaked coconut
• Butter
• Evaporated milk
• Pure vanilla extract
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Cake:
2. Beat sugar, eggs and oil together
3. Add flour, salt and baking soda
4. Add fruit cocktail with the juice
5. Mix well
6. Pour batter into a greased and floured 9x13x2 inch pan
7. I use kittencal's professional pan coating
8. Sprinkle coconut over batter
9. Bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 45 minutes
10. Frosting:
11. Place butter, sugar and evaporated milk in a medium size saucepan
12. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly, for 5 to 6 minutes or until it begins to thicken
13. Remove from heat
14. Add vanilla extract, coconut and nuts
15. Spread mixture onto cake while both are still hot
16. Serve warm or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
