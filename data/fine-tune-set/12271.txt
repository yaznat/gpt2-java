Ingredients:
• Soup mix
• Water
• Ham
• Leeks
• Garlic cloves
• Carrots
• Celery
• Basil
• Thyme
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place beans in a colander and sort through them, picking out any rocks or debris
2. Rinse the beans with water and then drain the water away
3. Place beans in crock pot and add the 6 cups of water
4. Put the lid on the crock pot and leave the beans to soak in the cold water overnight or for at least 8 hours
5. Pour the beans and water into the colander
6. Rinse gently, drain and return beans to the crock pot
7. Add the rest of the ingredients to the crock pot and stir to combine
8. Set the crock pot on low and cook for 3-5 hours or until beans and veggies are soft
9. Serve with fresh garlic bread and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
