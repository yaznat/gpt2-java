Please give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>A African Bean Soup

Ingredients:
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Onion
• Sweet potatoes
• Carrot
• Celery
• Red bell pepper
• Crushed tomatoes
• Vegetable stock
• Garbanzo beans
• Fresh cilantro
• Peanut butter
• Curry powder

Steps:
1. A food processor or mandolin cuts 10 min off of prep time
2. Heat 1 / 2 cup water and soy sauce in a large pot
3. Add onion and sweet potatoes
4. Cook over high heat for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Add carrot, celery and bell pepper
6. Cover and cook 3 minutes stirring occasionally
7. Stir in tomatoes, stock, and garbanzo or other beans
8. Blend peanut butter with 1 / 2 cup water, add to soup with curry powder
9. Stir
10. Cover and simmer for 10 minutes
11. Stir in cilantro and save some for garnish
12. Can be served over rice if desired<|endoftext|>
