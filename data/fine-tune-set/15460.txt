What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Mex Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Egg
• Worcestershire sauce
• Hamburger buns
• Mayonnaise
• Avocados
• Cooked bacon
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce leaves
• Onions
• Pickles

Steps:
1. Mix ground chuck with egg, worcestershire, salt and pepper
2. Form into 4 patties, 1 / 2 inch thick
3. Cook on grill 5-6 minutes on each side, or in a skillet for 4-5 minutes each side
4. Toast 4 buns and spread with mayo, add avacado slices, and cooked bacon<|endoftext|>
