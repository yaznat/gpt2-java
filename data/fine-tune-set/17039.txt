What's a good cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Cocktail Franks With Barbecue Sauce

Ingredients:
• Tomato paste
• Water
• Onions
• Worcestershire sauce
• Brown sugar
• White vinegar
• Molasses
• Lemon juice
• Dry mustard
• Salt
• Cocktail franks

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, combine all ingredients except franks
2. Bring to a boil stirring frequently and reduce heat
3. Add franks and simmer 10 minutes
4. Keep warm in a crockpot, fondue pot or chafing dish
5. Serve with toothpicks<|endoftext|>
