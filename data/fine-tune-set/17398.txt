Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Cooking apples
• Pecans
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg

Steps:
1. Combine 2 cups sugar, oil, and eggs in a large bowl, beating with a whisk until blended
2. Stir in the apple, 1 cup pecans, and vanilla
3. Combine the flour, soda, salt, 1 teaspoon cinnamon, and nutmeg
4. Add to the apple mixture, stirring until blended
5. Spread the batter into a greased and floured 13 x 9 x 2 inch pan
6. Combine the remaining 1 / 3 cup sugar, 1 / 2 cup pecans, and 1 tsp cinnamon
7. Sprinkle evenly over the batter
8. Bake at 350 degrees for 45 to 50 minutes or until a wooden pick inserted in the center of the cake comes out clean
9. Cool in the pan on a wire rack
10. Cut into squares and serve<|endoftext|>
