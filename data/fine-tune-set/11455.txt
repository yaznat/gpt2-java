Give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>Almond Shortbread

Ingredients:
• Real butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Flour
• Almonds
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cream butter, sugar and vanilla
2. With your almonds, either just crush them or you can put them into a blender / food processor to either make very small pieces or ground
3. Its your preference as to how you would like them
4. Mix the almonds with the flour and salt
5. When the butter is creamed, add the flour mix
6. It will seem crumbley, keep mixing until it comes together
7. When mixed well, separate into 4 equal balls
8. I use a pizza pan because i can do the whole batch on one pan
9. Lightly grease pan
10. Take balls and press until about 1 / 4-inch thick
11. Cut into six pieces
12. Bake for 12-14 minutes
13. You don't want them browned, just set well
14. When they are done, take them out and cut again and put on cooling rack still together
15. Let cool
16. Store in tight container
17. I use ziplock bags<|endoftext|>
