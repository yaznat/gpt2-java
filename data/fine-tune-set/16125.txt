What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Copycat Applebee S Chicken Quesadilla Grande

Ingredients:
• Shortening
• Flour tortillas
• Chipotle hot sauce
• Grilled chicken
• Lettuce
• Sour cream
• Green onions
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Brush one side of each tortilla with shortening
2. Place one tortilla, shortening side down on work surface
3. Spread chipotle sauce evenly on one tortilla
4. If chicken was not freshly grilled, then microwave chicken approximately 45 seconds, then distribute evenly on top of sauce on tortilla
5. Evenly put quesa filling on top of chicken
6. Then, cover with the other tortilla, shortening side up
7. Brown on griddle or in non stick pan evenly on both sides until internal filling is thoroughly heated
8. Use shredded lettuce, sour cream, green onion and salsa as accompaniments
9. I like to add crumbled bacon on top<|endoftext|>
