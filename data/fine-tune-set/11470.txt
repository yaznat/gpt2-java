Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Amanda S Vegan Flat Bread

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Oil
• Soymilk
• Olive oil
• Herbs
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients
2. Add oil and soy milk, mix
3. Knead on floured board for about 1 - 2 minutes
4. Press onto oiled cookie sheet
5. Smear topping on
6. Bake at 375 for about 20 minutes or until golden on top<|endoftext|>
