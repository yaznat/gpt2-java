Please give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Berks County Bread Machine Pizza Crust

Ingredients:
• Water
• Olive oil
• Flour
• Salt
• Active dry yeast

Steps:
1. Add the ingredients to the bread machine in the order given by the manufacturer
2. Process on the "dough" cycle for 1 1 / 2 hours then turn out onto a floured surface
3. Divide into equal sized dough balls
4. Knead each ball in your hands for a few minutes, then set under a towel for 15-20 minutes to rest
5. Stretch or roll each dough ball seperately into a 14-inch circle, and prebake on a lightly oiled pizza pan, or cornmeal dusted pizza stone for approximately 5 minutes in a 375 degree oven
6. Refrigerate crusts up to a week
7. Freeze for even longer if necessary<|endoftext|>
