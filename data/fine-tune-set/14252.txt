Give me a tofu recipe.<|endoftext|>Curried Tofu Cutlets

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Curry powder
• Dried thyme
• Dried sage
• Pepper
• Salt
• Firm tofu
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Cut tofu into two patties
2. In a shallow dish, mix together flour, curry powder, herbs and salt and pepper
3. Lay tofu in flour mixture and coat on both sides
4. In a skillet, on medium high heat, heat oil and butter for 1 minute
5. Lay tofu in skillet
6. Cook for approximately two minutes per side until brown and crispy
7. Squeeze lemon juice over 'cutlets' and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
