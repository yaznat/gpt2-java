What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Asian Pork Chicken Noodle Skillet Pampered Chef

Ingredients:
• Carrots
• Red bell pepper
• Green onions with tops
• Pork tenderloin
• Sesame oil
• Asian seasoning
• Oriental-flavor instant ramen noodles
• Oil
• Water

Steps:
1. Combine meat with sesame oil, asian seasoning mix and 2 of the instant noodle seasoning packets
2. Mix well
3. Add oil to a large skillet
4. Heat over medium-high heat
5. Add half the meat
6. Cook & stir 2-3 minutes / until browned
7. Remove from skillet and keep warm
8. Repeat with remaining meat
9. Add carrots and bell pepper to skillet
10. Cook 2 minutes / until tender crisp
11. Add water and remaining seasoning packets
12. Stir to loosen browned bits from the bottom of the skillet
13. Add noodles and white parts of onion to skillet
14. Cover
15. Simmer 4-5 minutes / until noodles are softened
16. Add the meat
17. Remove skillet from heat and let stand covered 3 / 4 minutes
18. Garnish with green onion tops<|endoftext|>
