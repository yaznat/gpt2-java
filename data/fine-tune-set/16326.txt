Ingredients:
• Jalapenos
• Cream cheese
• Cajun seasoning
• Bacon

Steps:
1. 1) cut jalapenos longways and scoop out the insides
2. 2) mix cream cheese and cajun seasoning together
3. 3) spread a good amount of cream cheese mixture in each of the jalapeno halves
4. 4) wrap each half in a strip of bacon
5. Secure with toothpicks on each end
6. 5) place on medium heated grill
7. And cook until bacon is done on each side and jalapeno is tender<|endoftext|>
