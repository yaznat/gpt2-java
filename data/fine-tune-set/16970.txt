Ingredients:
• Water
• Superfine sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Lemon wedge
• Ice cube
• Spiced rum
• Fresh lemon juice
• Orange curacao
• Orange rind twists

Steps:
1. Chill two martini glasses in freezer
2. Bring 1 / 4 cup water to boil in heavy small saucepan over high heat
3. Add 1 / 4 cup sugar and stir until dissolved
4. Cool sugar syrup completely
5. On small plate, stir remaining 1 tablespoon sugar and cinnamon to blend
6. Rub rims of chilled glasses with lemon wedge
7. Dip rims into cinnamon sugar to coat lightly
8. Combine ice, rum, lemon juice, curacao, and 2 tablespoons sugar syrup in cocktail shaker
9. Shake mixture until cocktail shaker becomes frosty
10. Strain mixture into prepared glasses
11. Garnish with orange peel twists and serve<|endoftext|>
