What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Chicken With Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Skinless chicken thighs
• Barbecue sauce
• Clear honey
• Oyster sauce
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Long-grain white rice
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Peanut oil
• Frozen peas
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Mix together the chicken thighs, barbecue sauce, honey, oyster sauce, olive oil and 2 cloves chopped garlic
2. Cover and marinate in the fridge for 2-4 hours
3. Preheat the oven to 375f transfer the marinated chicken to a roasting tin
4. Roast in the oven for 40 minutes, turning now and then, until the chicken is cooked through
5. Meanwhile, rinse the rice to wash out excess starch
6. Place in a medium-sized heavy saucepan
7. Add 1 1 / 4 cups water and season with salt
8. Bring to the boil, reduce heat, cover tightly and simmer over a very low heat for 15 minutes until the water has been absorbed and the rice is tender
9. Spread the rice out on a large plate to cool
10. Heat a wok until very hot
11. Add the oil and heat through
12. Add the garlic and stir-fry for one minute
13. Add the cooled rice and mix thoroughly with the garlic
14. Crack the eggs into the rice and add the cooked peas
15. Stir-fry until the eggs are cooked
16. Season with salt and pepper
17. Serve the barbecue chicken with the fried rice<|endoftext|>
