What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Fried Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Nutritional yeast
• Shoyu
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Slice tofu into cutlets about 1 / 4 inch thick
2. Press the water from the tofu cutlets for about 15 minutes
3. Coat cutlets in nutritional yeast
4. In a large skillet heat oil over medium-low heat
5. When oil shimmers add tofu, dropping a small amount of shoyu onto each cutlet
6. When the cutlets get brown on the bottom flip and brown on the other side
7. Serve with gravy and enjoy<|endoftext|>
