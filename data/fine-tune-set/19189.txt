Give me a ice cream recipe.<|endoftext|>Carrot Cake Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Pecans
• Heavy cream
• Brown sugar
• Unflavored gelatin
• Golden raisin
• Light cream cheese
• Maple syrup
• Cinnamon
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Skim evaporated milk
• Carrot

Steps:
1. Cook about 1 1 / 4 cup sliced fresh carrots in the microwave or stovetop until soft
2. Blend in food processor or blender with enough water or milk to make a puree
3. Measure out 3 / 4 cup carrot puree, set aside
4. Heat the 1 cup of heavy cream with the brown sugar in a medium size saucepan on medium heat, stirring often
5. When cream mixture is hot, whisk vigorously while adding the gelatin powder
6. Add in raisins and cream cheese and continue to heat for about 4 minutes, stirring often
7. Remove saucepan from heat
8. Pour hot mixture into a large glass bowl
9. Add maple syrup, cinnamon, vanilla, and salt
10. Whisk in evaporated milk, and pureed carrot
11. Chill in fridge for about 1 hour or 1 / 2 hour in freezer, till ice cream mixture is cold
12. The ice cream mixture will be thicker at this point
13. Ice cream maker: follow manufacturers directions, adding chopped pecans near the end of the freezing process
14. Freezer method:
15. Skip the previous chill step
16. Add in raisins and pecans
17. Pour ice cream mixture in a covered shallow pan and freeze until almost solid
18. Break up frozen mixture and process in food processor or blender until soft
19. Repeat 1 to 2 more times<|endoftext|>
