Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Cooked pasta
• Eggs
• Milk
• Fresh parsley
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a small frying pan and fry the garlic and pasta for 2-3 mins until heated through
2. Beat together the eggs, milk, parsley and a little seasoning
3. Pour over the pasta and cook gently for 3-4 mins until the egg is almost set on top
4. Sprinkle the cheese over and place under a hot grill for 2-3 mins until golden and bubbly<|endoftext|>
