Ingredients:
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Chickpeas
• Green pepper
• Fresh tomato
• Chili powder
• Flour tortillas
• Low-fat cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Combine spinach, chick peas, peppers, tomatoes and chili powder
2. Spread mixture over half of
3. Each tortilla ending with 1 slice of cheese
4. Fold tortillas in half and place on baking sheet
5. Bake at 350f for 5 to 8 minutes or until cheese melts
6. Serve with low fat or fat free sour cream or salsa
