Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Fresh lemon juice
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Fresh lemon zest

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Butter a 9 x 5 x 3 inch loaf pan
3. Cream butter with sugar for 6 minutes, until light
4. Beat in eggs, one at a time
5. Blend in bananas and lemon juice
6. Sift together flour, baking soda and salt
7. Blend into banana mixture along with lemon zest
8. Turn into prepared pan
9. Bake for 1 hour or until tested done
10. Remove, cool for 15 minutes and turn out of pan
11. Cool completely<|endoftext|>
