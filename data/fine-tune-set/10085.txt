Ingredients:
• Pork roast
• Tomatoes
• Green chili peppers
• Jalapenos
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Garlic clove
• Garlic powder
• Cumin powder
• Salt & pepper
• Water

Steps:
1. Brown meat in a little oil
2. Heat water, add spices, meat, chilies, onion, etc
3. Bring to a slow boil
4. Turn down and simmer 2 to 3 hours
5. Thicken with flour and water
6. If you desire more "spice" add picante sauce or more chilies and jalapenos<|endoftext|>
