Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazingly Soft Peanut Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Creamy peanut butter
• Granulated sugar
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking soda
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Peanut butter chips
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven to 325 degrees fahrenheit
2. Cream butter, peanut butter, and sugars together until smooth
3. Add eggs and vanilla, beat well
4. Stir in flour and baking soda until well combined
5. Mix in chips and nuts by hand
6. Using a cookie scooper or a spoon, scoop up 1 1 / 4 inch balls of dough and place 2'' apart on a greased cookie sheet
7. Bake for 15 minutes or until cookies are firm around the edges but do not wait until the tops are brown
8. Wait 3-4 minutes before transferring to wire rack to cool
9. You could use any kind of nuts you want and even switch up the chips
10. If you put the cookies into a zip lock bag place a piece of sandwich bread in with them to keep them soft<|endoftext|>
