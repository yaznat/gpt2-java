Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Cooked pumpkin
• Bisquick baking mix
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Ginger
• Salad oil

Steps:
1. Beat eggs in small mixer bowl on high speed 5 minutes or until thick and lemon colored
2. Stir in remaining ingredients
3. Pour batter by tablespoonfuls onto medium-hot un-greased griddle
4. Bake until puffed and bubbles begin to break
5. Turn and bake other side until golden brown
6. About 5 dozen 2-inch pancakes
7. Serve with sausages and rum flavored syrup
8. Maple rum flavored syrup: heat 1 cup maple flavored syrup and tablespoon butter or margarine
9. Remove from heat
10. Stir in 1 / 2 teaspoon rum flavoring
11. Serve warm
