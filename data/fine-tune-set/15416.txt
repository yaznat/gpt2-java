Ingredients:
• Lean hamburger
• Hidden valley ranch dressing mix
• Barbecue sauce
• Bacon
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Cheddar cheese
• Onion
• Lettuce leaves
• Tomatoes
• Mayonnaise
• Ranch salad dressing
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. In bowl, mix together the hamburger and dressing mix packet well
2. Shape into 3 patties, set in fridge to chill and firm up
3. Meanwhile, in large frying pan, cook bacon sprinkling with the pepper on both sides, drain grease saving 1-2 tbl
4. Set bacon aside wrapped in paper towel
5. Fry hamburgers in same pan with a little bacon grease til done
6. Coat with bbq sauce, top with cheddar slices, cover, heat til cheese is melted
7. Place on bottom buns, top with bacon, onion, lettuce and tomato
8. Mix the prepared dressing with the mayo and spread on top buns, place on top
9. Other bbq sauces might not work well<|endoftext|>
