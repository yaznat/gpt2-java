What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Asian Style Beef And Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Potato
• Rump steak
• Fresh coriander leaves
• Fresh mint leaves
• Mixed salad greens
• Red onion
• Bean sprouts
• Red chile
• Toasted sesame seeds
• Coconut milk
• Lime juice
• Fish sauce

Steps:
1. Dressing:
2. Combine all ingredients in a screw top jar
3. Salad:
4. Cook potatoes until tender
5. Drain and slice lengthways into wedges
6. Cook steak about 4 minutes per side on a preheated bbq plate
7. Allow to rest for 5 minutes before slicing thinly across the grain
8. Mix salad ingredients with the beef and potatoes, folding the dressing through gently
9. Sprinkle with the sesame seeds and serve<|endoftext|>
