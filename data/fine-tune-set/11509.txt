What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>Andouille Sausage And Corn Bread Stuffing

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Andouille sausage
• Bulk sausage
• Onions
• Celery
• Red bell peppers
• Green onion
• Fresh thyme
• Hot pepper sauce
• Dried rubbed sage
• Bread stuffing mix
• Low sodium chicken broth

Steps:
1. Melt butter in heavy large skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add all sausages and cook until brown and cooked through, breaking up bulk sausage with back of fork, about 5 minutes
3. Add onions, celery and red bell peppers
4. Cover and cook until vegetables are tender, stirring occasionally, about 12 minutes
5. Stir in green onions, thyme, hot pepper sauce and sage
6. Transfer sausage mixture to large bowl
7. Stir stuffing mix into sausage mixture
8. Season with salt and pepper
9. Preheat oven to 350f
10. Generously butter 13x9x2-inch glass or ceramic baking dish
11. Add enough broth to stuffing to moisten
12. Transfer stuffing to prepared dish
13. Cover with buttered foil, buttered side down
14. Bake until heated through, about 30 minutes
15. Uncover and bake until top is crisp and golden, about 20 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
