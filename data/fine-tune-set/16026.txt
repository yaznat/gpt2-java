What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Ranch Quesadilla Wedges

Ingredients:
• Green bell pepper
• Olive oil
• Yellow bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Onion
• Barbecue sauce
• Ranch dressing
• Butter
• Flour tortillas
• Barbecued beef
• Mild cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Stir fry peppers and onions in oil
2. Combine barbecue sauce and ranch dressing
3. Mix well
4. Spread butter evenly on 1 side of each tortilla
5. Layer barbecue beef, pepper and onion blend, and cheese evenly on non-buttered side of 4 tortillas
6. Cover with remaining tortillas, buttered side up
7. Grill each quesadilla in large skillet over medium heat for 3 minutes on each side or until golden and cheese melts
8. Cut into wedges
9. Serve with barbecue ranch dipping sauce<|endoftext|>
