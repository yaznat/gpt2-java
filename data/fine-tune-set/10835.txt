Ingredients:
• Thousand island dressing
• Vinegar
• Hard-boiled eggs
• Fresh lemon juice
• Prepared mustard
• Sugar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Elbow macaroni
• Celery
• Green peppers
• Onion

Steps:
1. Combine first 6 ingredients, in this step include only the egg yolks leaving the whites
2. This is your sauce
3. Chop the whites of the eggs
4. Stir in remaining ingredients
5. Cover, chill<|endoftext|>
