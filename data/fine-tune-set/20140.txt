Ingredients:
• Chicken strips
• Cabbage
• Asparagus
• Red bell pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Ginger
• Soy sauce
• Red pepper flakes
• Cherry jam

Steps:
1. Heat the oil and add add the cabbage, soy sauce, jam and red pepper flake
2. Cook on high 3-5 minutes until cabbage has started to wilt
3. Add the chicken, ginger, asparagus and bell pepper
4. Cook on high heat the entire time and stop once the asparagus is slightly tender, about 10 minutes
5. Serve with white or brown rice<|endoftext|>
