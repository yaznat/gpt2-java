What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Authentic Refried Beans

Ingredients:
• Dried pinto bean
• Water
• Bacon grease
• Garlic powder
• Chicken bouillon powder
• Chili powder

Steps:
1. Put beans and water into dutch oven
2. Soak at least six hours
3. Pour beans into colander and rinse thoroughly
4. Add two cups water back into the dutch oven, along with bacon grease, garlic powder, bouillon and chili powder
5. Bring to a boil
6. Cover and reduce heat, simmering for about four to six hours
7. Stir occasionally
8. You'll know when the frijoles are done because they'll be very tender
9. Mash until about only half are left whole<|endoftext|>
