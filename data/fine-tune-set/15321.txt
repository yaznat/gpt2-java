Give me a burger recipe.<|endoftext|>All Gone Onion Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground round
• Dry onion soup mix
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Granulated garlic
• Lemon pepper
• Salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Beer
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Mix all together and shape into 12 patties
2. Grill over hardwood coals until done to your liking
3. Place on hamburger rolls and top with your choice of condiments<|endoftext|>
