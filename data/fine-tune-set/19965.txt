What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Egg Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Onion
• Green chili
• Tomatoes
• Coriander leaves
• Salt
• Red chili powder
• Oil

Steps:
1. Beat the eggs, chopped onions, tomatoes, green chilies, coriander, salt & chili powder
2. Beat the mixture until foamed
3. Heat oil in a frying pan and add the mixture
4. Cook over low heat till mixture is firm and flip it and fry the other side
5. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
