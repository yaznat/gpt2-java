Ingredients:
• Oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Assorted fresh vegetables
• Reduced-sodium fat-free chicken broth
• Instant rice
• Italian dressing

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large skillet on medium heat
2. Add chicken
3. Cook and stir until lightly browned, stirring occasionally
4. Add vegetables
5. Cook and stir 3 to 5 minute or until crisp-tender
6. Stir in broth
7. Bring to boil
8. Stir in rice and dressing
9. Cover
10. Reduce heat to low
11. Cook 5 minute or until liquid is absorbed and chicken is cooked through<|endoftext|>
