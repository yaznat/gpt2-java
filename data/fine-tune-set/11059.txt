What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pita Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Pita bread
• Peanut butter
• Flax seed meal
• Granola cereal
• Apple

Steps:
1. Heat the pita
2. Cover it in peanut butter
3. Sprinkle the flaxseed meal and granola on top
4. Spread the apples in the middle
5. Fold up the sides and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
