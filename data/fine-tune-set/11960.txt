Ingredients:
• Whole boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Butter
• Dijon mustard
• Prepared mustard
• Honey
• Curry powder
• Lemon juice
• Salt
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Place chicken in pan
2. Melt butter in a saucepan and add remaining ingredients
3. Stir until smooth
4. Pour over chicken and bake, uncovered at 350f 45 min
5. , basting occasionally, until tender and golden<|endoftext|>
