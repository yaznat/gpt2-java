What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Apricot Pork Roast With Stuffing

Ingredients:
• Herb stuffing cubes
• Chicken broth
• Dried apricot
• Onion
• Boneless pork loin roast
• Apricot jam
• Balsamic vinegar

Steps:
1. Spray inside of crock pot with cooking spray
2. Mix stuffing, broth, apricots and onions in slow cooker
3. Place pork in crock pot
4. Brush with jam and vinegar
5. Cover and cook on low 8 hrs or until tender
6. Stir stuffing before serving<|endoftext|>
