Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Easy Vegetarian Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Cooked white rice
• Sesame oil
• Carrots
• Yellow sweet onion
• Sweet corn
• Green pepper
• Soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Black pepper
• Ground ginger
• Garlic powder
• Eggs

Steps:
1. In a large wok or saute' pan, stir fry the carrots, onion, corn, and green peppers for about 2 minutes
2. Add the soy sauce, hoisin sauce, black pepper, ginger, garlic powder, and rice
3. Toss to combine
4. Make a well in the center of the rice and scramble the eggs
5. Toss to combine and remove from heat
6. You can add chopped chicken, pork, beef, or even tofu to this recipe for a twist
7. Serving suggestions: my recipe for light lemon chicken, beef and brocolli, egg rolls, and sesame chicken<|endoftext|>
