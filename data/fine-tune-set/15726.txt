Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Limes, juice and zest of
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Ground cumin
• Red onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt and pepper
• Corn tortillas
• Extra-sharp cheddar cheese
• Avocado
• Mango
• Red onions
• Fresh cilantro leaves
• Fresh lime juice
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Coarse sea salt

Steps:
1. Chop cilantro, zest and juice limes, mince garlic and chop red onion into small chunks
2. In a small bowl, mix together the lime zest and juice, olive oil, minced garlic, cumin, minced red onion, red pepper flakes, chopped cilantro and a pinch of salt and pepper
3. Put the chicken breasts into a ziploc bag and pour in the mixture, making sure chicken is coated in the mixture
4. Put the bagged chicken in the refrigerator and let it marinate for 2-4 hours
5. Grill the chicken breasts until thoroughly cooked
6. Let the meat rest for at least 5 minutes, and then slice the breast chicken into thin slices
7. While the chicken is resting, throw the tortillas on the grill, and grill them until slightly charred and pliable, about 20 seconds per side
8. Top the tortilla with the sliced chicken, mango avocado salsa, cheddar cheese and some fresh cilantro
9. To make the mango avocado salsa:
10. Combine all the above ingredients, add the tablespoon of olive oil and the 1 / 2 teaspoon salt
11. Gently combine all the ingredients together
12. Serve over tacos<|endoftext|>
