Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Artichoke Rice Salad

Ingredients:
• Chicken rice-a-roni
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Mayonnaise
• Curry powder
• Green olives
• Green pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Green onions
• Black olives
• Dried cranberries
• Roasted sunflower seeds
• Seasoned rice vinegar

Steps:
1. Cook rice a roni according to package directions, but omit the butter
2. Mix all ingredients carefully in a large bowl
3. Taste and adjust seasonings
4. Refrigerate until ready to serve
5. Garnish with sliced green onions, if desired<|endoftext|>
