What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Baked Fish With Potatoes And Onion Rings

Ingredients:
• Baking potato
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Dried oregano leaves
• Fish fillet
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 375 degrees
2. Arrange half of potatoes in buttered 3 quart casserole dish
3. Top with half of onion and half of bell pepper
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Sprinkle with 1 / 4 tsp oregeno
6. Arrange fish in one layer over vegetebles
7. Arrange remaining potatoes, onion and bell pepper over fish
8. Season with salt and pepper and add remaining 1 / 4 teaspoons oregeno
9. Melt butter in medium saucepan over medium heat
10. Stir in flour
11. Cook until bubbly, stirring constantly
12. Gradually stir in milk
13. Cook until thickened, stirring constantly
14. Pour white sauce over casserole
15. Cover and bake at 375 for 40 minutes or until potatoes are tender
16. Sprinkle with cheese and bake uncovered for 5 minutes more until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
