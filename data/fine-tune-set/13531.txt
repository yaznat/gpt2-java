What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Au Gratin Beef Bake

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Celery ribs
• Green pepper
• Au gratin potatoes
• Tomato soup
• Worcestershire sauce
• Water
• Milk

Steps:
1. I large skillet, cook the beef, onion, celery and green pepper until meat is no longer pink and the veggies are tender
2. Drain
3. In a greased 2-1 / 2 quart baking dish, combine beef mixture, potatoes with contents of sauce mix, soup and worcestershire sauce
4. Stir in the water and milk
5. Bake uncovered at 400 degrees for 45-50 minutes or until potatoes are tender<|endoftext|>
