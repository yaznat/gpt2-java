Ingredients:
• Rolled oats
• Apples
• Milk
• Dates
• Coconut
• Cinnamon
• Slivered almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Lightly grease an 8x8 inch baking pan
3. Finely grate the apples, and set aside
4. Scatter 1 / 3 of the oats over the bottom of the pan
5. Scatter 1 / 2 the raisins and coconut over the oats
6. Place another 1 / 3 of the oats over the raisins
7. Spread the apples evenly over the oats
8. Sprinkle 1 / 2 tspn cinnamon over the apples
9. Pour over 1 cup of milk
10. Scatter remaining raisins and coconut over
11. Scatter remaining oats over raisins and coconut
12. Sprinkle 1 / 2 teaspoon cinnamon over oats
13. Pour remaining cup of milk over
14. Scatter almonds evenly over casserole
15. Bake for 35-40 minute will be quite soft<|endoftext|>
