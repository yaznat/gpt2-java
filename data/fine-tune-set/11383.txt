What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>A Different Garlic Bread

Ingredients:
• French bread
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Italian spices
• Roma tomato
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Place slices of bread on a stone or cookie sheet
2. Brush with a little olive oil or melted butter
3. In a small sauce pan, add olive oil or butter
4. When hot, add italian seasonings and garlic
5. Cook about 2 to 3 minutes until garlic is softened
6. Be careful not to burn garlic
7. Add sliced tomatoes on top of sliced bread
8. Brush mixture on top of bread and tomatoes
9. Add cheese
10. Bake for about 15 minutes or until cheese is bubbly
11. This is good with any italian dish! i love it with spaghetti!<|endoftext|>
