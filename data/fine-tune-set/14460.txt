What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>Apple Topped Baked Beans

Ingredients:
• Baked beans
• Apple
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Place beans in 1 1 / 2 quart casserole
3. Sprinkle with apple and dot with butter
4. Sprinkle with brown sugar
5. Top with bacon pieces<|endoftext|>
