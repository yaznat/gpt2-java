What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Coffee Gravy

Ingredients:
• Wheat flour
• Chuck roast
• Bacon grease
• Cream of asparagus soup
• Onion and mushroom soup mix
• Strong coffee
• Dry red wine
• Water
• Beef bouillon
• Horseradish sauce
• Soy sauce
• Seasoning salt
• Dried oregano
• Ground thyme
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Carrots
• Russet potatoes
• Turnips

Steps:
1. Dredge meat in flour to coat
2. In a dutch oven, heat the grease over medium heat, and brown the beef
3. Whisk together the soup, soup mix, coffee, wine, water, beef base, horseradish sauce, soy sauce, seasoning salt, oregano, thyme, garlic, and onions
4. Add the mixture to the meat in the pot
5. Simmer, covered, over low heat for 30 minutes
6. Add the carrots, potatoes, and turnips, and simmer, covered, until beef is tender
7. Uncover the stew and bring to a boil
8. Cover and cook over medium heat 10-15 minutes more
9. With a slotted spoon, remove the meat and vegetables to a serving platter
10. Stir a couple of tablespoons of water into the reserved flour, and add to the liquid in the pot
11. Boil, stirring constantly, until thickened
12. Pour gravy over meat and vegetables<|endoftext|>
