Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Stir Fry For Ginger Lovers

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breast
• Soy sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Vinegar
• Red pepper flakes
• Cornstarch
• Oil
• Onion
• Celery
• Gingerroot
• Bell pepper
• Bok choy
• Bean sprouts

Steps:
1. Cut chicken into 1 / 2 inch cubes
2. Prepare marinating sauce and add to chicken cubes
3. Sit in refrigerator for at least 2 hours
4. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok
5. Drain chicken keeping the juice
6. Stir fry the chicken for 4 minutes over high heat
7. Push the chicken to the side and add onion, celery, bell pepper and ginger
8. Add reserved marinating juice
9. Cook, stirring constantly for 5 minutes
10. Add bok choy
11. Cook another 2 minutes,
12. Add the sprouts and stir-fry another 2 minutes
13. Serve while sprouts are still crisp
14. Cooking time does not include marinating time<|endoftext|>
