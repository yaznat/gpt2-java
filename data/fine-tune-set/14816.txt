What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Aromatic Vegetable Fried Rice Su Cai Chao Fan

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Garlic clove
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Salt
• Sesame oil
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Celery
• Green beans
• Carrot
• Bean sprouts
• Bamboo shoots
• Spring onions
• Green capsicum
• Vegetable stock
• Soy sauce
• Coriander leaves

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tbs peanut oil in a saucepan and saut the chopped garlic for 10 seconds
2. Add the cooked rice and salt and toss to mix
3. Heat through, remove from heat and set aside
4. In a separate saucepan, heat th 2 tbs sesame oil and 1 tbs peanut oil and saute the ginger and grated garlicfor 15 seconds over medium heat
5. Add the celery, green beans and carrot and stir-fry over high heat for 2-3 minutes
6. Add the bean sprouts and bamboo shoots and stir for 1 minute
7. Stir in the rice, spring onions and green capsicum and toss to mix
8. Stir-fry for one minute
9. Add the vegetable stock, soy sauce and salt
10. Continue to stir over high heat for 1-2 minutes until the stock is absorbed, garnish with coriander leaves and serve<|endoftext|>
