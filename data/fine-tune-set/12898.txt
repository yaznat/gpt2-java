Ingredients:
• Garlic bread
• Onions
• New york strip steaks
• Peppercorn
• Fresh parsley
• Balsamic vinegar
• Blue cheese
• Butter
• Garlic cloves
• Potato chips

Steps:
1. Place garlic bread on a large sheet pan, and bake according to package directions
2. Slice onions thin and microvave covered on high for 5 minutes
3. Heat a pan on medium-high for 5 minutes, while the onions are in the microwave
4. Season steaks with rub, and cook 5-10 minutes, or until browned
5. Turn steaks over and repeat, until done, it should have a medium-rare doneness
6. Remove steak from pan, and lightly cover with foil
7. Chop parsley, and then add the parsley, onions, and vinegar to pan
8. Cook 3-4 minutes or until the onions are carmelized
9. Slice steal into 1 / 2 inch pieces, and place steak and onion mixture over one garlic bread half
10. Sprinkle with cheese, and top with other garlic bread half
11. Cut sandwich into 6 pieces
12. For the seasoned chips:
13. Combine 2 tablespoons butter, melted with 2 pressed garlic cloves
14. Add 1 / 4 cup freshed parsley
15. Toss 6 ounces plain potato chips with butter mixture
16. Bake on medium sheet pan at 450 degrees for 3-5 minutes<|endoftext|>
