What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Boston Fish Chowder

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Celery
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Unsifted flour
• Water
• Chicken flavor instant bouillon
• Potatoes
• Fish fillets
• Half-and-half
• Chopped pimiento

Steps:
1. In large kettle or dutch oven, cook bacon until crisp
2. Remove and crumble
3. In drippings, cook celery, onion and garlic until tender
4. Stir in flour
5. Gradually add water and bouillon, stirring until smooth and well blended
6. Bring to a boil and add potatoes
7. Reduce heat and cook 10 minutes
8. Stir in fish
9. Cook 15 minutes
10. Add cream and pimiento
11. Heat through
12. Do not boil
13. Garnish with bacon
14. Refrigerate leftovers<|endoftext|>
