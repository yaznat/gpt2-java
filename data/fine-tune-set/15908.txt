Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Chorizo sausage
• Green bell pepper
• Butter
• Eggs
• Adobo seasoning
• Black beans
• Pico de gallo
• Chihuahua cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. In a deep pan, heat the oil over medium-high heat
2. Add sausage and bell pepper
3. Cook for about 8-10 minutes, crumbling the sausage every now and then
4. When sausage is cooked through, remove from pan and drain of fat
5. Wipe the grease from the pan, then melt the butter over medium-high heat
6. Add the beaten eggs and adobo seasoning, and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring often, until the eggs are scrambled and cooked through
7. Remove from heat
8. Add the drained black beans and pico de gallo to the pot
9. Stir to combine well
10. Now, prepare your burritos
11. Warm tortillas in microwave for 30 seconds until warm and flexible
12. Place 3 / 4 cup scrambled egg mixture into lower center of tortilla
13. Top with about 1 1 / 2 tablespoons of cheese
14. Roll up tortilla into a burrito
15. Repeat with each tortilla, until done
16. Wrap each burrito in plastic wrap, and store all of them in a large container or zip-lock bag in the freezer
17. When ready to eat, remove frozen burritos from wrapping and then wrap in a paper towel
