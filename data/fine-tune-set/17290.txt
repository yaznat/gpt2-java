Ingredients:
• Cinnamon stick
• Green cardamom pods
• Whole cloves
• Gingerroot
• Black peppercorns
• Water
• Black tea bags
• Dark brown sugar
• Low-fat milk

Steps:
1. Place the cinnamon stick, cardamom, cloves, ginger, peppercorns and water in a saucepan
2. Bring to a boil, then cover and reduce the heat to simmer for 5 minutes
3. Remove from heat and allow the mixture to steep for 10 minutes
4. Return to a boil
5. Remove from heat, add the tea bags and let steep for 3 to 5 minutes
6. Strain the tea and return to the saucepan
7. Add brown sugar and milk while stirring over low heat for one minute
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
