Give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>Asian Style Glazed Green Beans

Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Hoisin sauce
• Rice wine vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Sesame seeds
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Combine and mix hoisin sauce, vinegar, brown sugar, sesame seeds and red pepper flakes together in a bowl
2. In a saute pan over medium heat, combine the sauce from the bowl and the green beans
3. Stir to incorporate
4. Saute until heated through, about 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
