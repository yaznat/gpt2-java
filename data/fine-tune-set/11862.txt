Ingredients:
• Water
• Medium shrimp
• Onion
• Butter
• Peanut oil
• Flour
• Curry powder
• Chicken broth
• Milk
• Sugar
• Salt
• Gingerroot
• Fresh lemon juice
• Roasted peanuts
• Scallion
• Raisins
• Coconut
• Fresh cilantro
• Cooked bacon

Steps:
1. In large pot of boiling water, cook the shrimp for about 3 to 5 minutes or until shrimp turns pink
2. Drain, rinse with cold water, devein and set aside
3. Cook onion in butter and peanut oil in large skillet over medium heat until softened
4. Lower heat and add flour and curry powder
5. Stir with whisk until smooth and no lumps remain
6. Cook for 1 minute stirring constantly until mixture thickens
7. Gradually stir in broth and milk
8. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until thickened
9. Stir in sugar, salt, fresh ginger, and lemon juice
10. Add shrimp and cook until heated through
11. Serve shrimp with hot cooked white rice garnished with any or all of the suggested toppings<|endoftext|>
