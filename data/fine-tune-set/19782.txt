Ingredients:
• Corn flake crumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Italian herb seasoning
• Chili powder
• Garlic salt
• Potatoes
• Egg

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375 degrees
2. Coat two baking sheets with vegetable cooking spray
3. Lightly beat egg in shallow bowl
4. In another shallow bowl, mix crumbs and seasoning blend of your choice
5. Dip potato wedges into egg
6. Coat completely with crumb mixture
7. Arrange in single layer on baking sheets
8. Bake 20 minutes, then turn potatoes over and continue to bake 10 to 15 minutes longer, until potatoes are browned and crisp and insides are tender when tested with toothpick or fork
9. Serve immediately, plain or with lemon or ketchup<|endoftext|>
