Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Lemon juice
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Sour cream
• Water
• All-purpose flour
• Dry buttermilk
• Granulated sugar
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Semisweet chocolate chunks

Steps:
1. In large mixing bowl combine banana and lemon juice
2. Add the eggs, oil, sour cream and water
3. Mix until well-blended
4. In small bowl combine the flour, buttermilk blend, sugar, salt, baking powder and baking soda
5. Stir the dry ingredients into the banana mixture just until combined
6. Heat skillet or griddle until a drop of water dances on it,
7. Then lightly grease skillet
8. Drop 2 to 3 tablespoons of batter for each pancake onto skillet
9. When small bubbles break the surface,
10. Sprinkle 4 to 6 chocolate chunks onto each pancake
11. Turn pancakes over and cook for 1 minute,
12. Or until bottom is lightly browned
13. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
