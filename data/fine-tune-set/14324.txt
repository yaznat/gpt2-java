Give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Green Scrambled Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Soy sauce
• Turmeric
• Pepper
• Dried oregano
• Olive oil
• Greek olives
• Sun-dried tomato
• Spinach

Steps:
1. Preheat a little olive oil in a large saucepan
2. Add tofu, soy sauce, turmeric, pepper and oregano
3. Saute on medium high heat for 10 or 12 minutes or until moisture has evaporated
4. Add the remaining ingredients, cover and cook for 2 to 4 more minutes until spinach is tender<|endoftext|>
