What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Cookies

Ingredients:
• Rolled oatmeal
• Whole wheat flour
• Cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Liquid egg substitute
• Vanilla
• Apple

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine oatmeal, flour, cinnamon, baking powder, nutmeg and salt
2. In another bowl, whisk applesauce, splenda, liquid egg, and vanilla
3. Pour over oatmeal mixture and stir to combine
4. Add finely chopped apple and stir
5. Bake on parchment paper-lined cookie sheet in 275 oven for about 25 minutes
6. Let cool completely<|endoftext|>
