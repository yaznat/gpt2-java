Can you give me a smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Chocolate Peanut Butter Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Ovaltine
• Banana
• Peanut butter
• Water
• Sugar
• Cashews
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Put all ingredients in a blender
2. Blend until mixed
3. Put cashews in if wanted
4. Blend, but leave cashews chunky
5. Pour into a glass
6. Put cinnamon on top of the poured smoothie<|endoftext|>
