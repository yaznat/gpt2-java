Ingredients:
• Frozen baby carrots
• Lemon, zest of
• Orange juice
• Dark corn syrup
• Cornstarch
• Hot water

Steps:
1. Put everything is a medium saucepan except cornstarch and water
2. Simmer for 10 minutes
3. Mix cornstarch with hot water
4. Add to pot
5. Simmer another 5 minutes
6. These carrots are great with fish<|endoftext|>
