Can you give me a quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Bean Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Canned black beans
• Whole wheat tortilla
• Frozen spinach
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Spread black beans on half of the tortilla
2. Spread the spinach on top of the black beans
3. Spread the cheese on top of the spinach
4. Fold tortilla in half
5. When time to heat up, cover tortilla with a napkin and heat in the microwave until heated through 1 - 1
6. 5 minutes
7. Top with salsa and enjoy: )<|endoftext|>
