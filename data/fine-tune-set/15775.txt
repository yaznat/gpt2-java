What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Tuna Burritos

Ingredients:
• Tuna in water
• Celery
• Green onions
• Light mayonnaise
• Dill pickle relish
• Dijon mustard
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a bowl, mix the tuna, celery, green onions, mayonnaise, relish, mustard, salt and pepper
2. Spoon 3 / 4 cup onto the centre of each tortilla
3. Sprinkle each with 1 / 4 cup of the cheese
4. Fold the bottom edge over the filling, then the sides
5. Roll up
6. Bake, seam side down, on a small greased rimmed baking sheet in a 400 degree f oven until golden and the cheese is melted, about 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
