Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Bacon
• Orange marmalade
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Ground ginger
• Garlic clove
• Vegetable oil cooking spray

Steps:
1. Cut the chicken breasts into 24 pieces
2. Wrap each cube with a piece of bacon and secure it with a toothpick
3. Mix marmalade, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic in a large zip-lock bag or a dish and add chicken nuggets
4. Cover and chill for 2 hours, turning occasionally
5. Coat a broiler pan with cooking spray and place the nuggets on it
6. Bake at 450 degrees for 10 minutes
7. Turn them over and bake 10 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
