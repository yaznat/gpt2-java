Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Olive oil
• Fresh basil
• Dijon mustard
• Garlic
• Sugar
• Boneless beef top sirloin steaks
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a resealable plastic bag, combine vinegar, oil, basil, mustard, garlic and sugar
2. Mix well
3. Add steak, turn to coat and marinate in refrigerator 15 minutes to 2 hours, turning occasionally
4. When ready to cook, remove steak and discard marinade
5. Grill steak 17 to 21 minutes for medium-rare to medium doneness, turning occasionally
6. Season with salt and pepper, to taste
7. Slice steak crosswise and serve<|endoftext|>
