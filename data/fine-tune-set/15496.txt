Please give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Barley Burgers

Ingredients:
• Barley
• Water
• Tomato paste
• Carrots
• Celery
• Garlic powder
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Bring the water to a boil in a medium sized pot
2. Add barley, reduce heat to medium, and cook covered fifty minutes until barley is tender
3. Drain any excess liquid
4. Mix cooked barley with remaining ingredients in a large bowl
5. Form seven burgers with your hands and cook in an oiled large frying pan over med-high heat for ten mins on each side
6. Serve warm with lettuce on a bun<|endoftext|>
