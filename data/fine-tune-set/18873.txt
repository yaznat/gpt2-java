What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Cape Malay Type Slightly Spiced Baked Custard

Ingredients:
• Cream
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon
• Star anise
• Ground mixed spice
• Eggs
• Egg yolks
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 300 deg f / 150 deg celsius grease 6 ramekins or cups which can hold about 1 cup each
2. Mix the cream, milk and spices in a small saucepan, and bring to boiling point
3. Remove from heat
4. Whisk the eggs, egg yolks and brown sugar until really well mixed and the sugar almost melted
5. Add the warm creamy mixture, while whisking, to the egg mixture, keeping up whisking
6. Whisk until the sugar has melted
7. Using a fine sieve, pour the custard mixture through the sieve to get rid of any small lumps
8. Divide into the prepared ramekins
9. Place a clean kitchen cloth on the bottom of a deep oven tin or dish, and on that, place the filled ramekins
10. Carefully pour in boiling water to about halfway up the ramekins
11. Bake for about 25 minutes until the custard has set
12. Take out and remove ramekins
13. The puddings can be enhanced by the two suggestions above, or with chopped bananas stewed with dessicated coconut<|endoftext|>
