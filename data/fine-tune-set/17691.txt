Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple Cranberry Muffins

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Eggs
• Brown sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Sour cream
• Vanilla
• Apple
• Cranberries
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix flour, baking soda, cinnamon and salt together
2. In a medium size bowl, whisk eggs, sugar, oil, sour cream and vanilla until smooth
3. Stir apple, cranberries, and walnuts into wet mixture
4. Fold wet mixture into dry ingredients
5. I use an ice cream scoop to measure batter into greased muffin cups
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 20 to 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
