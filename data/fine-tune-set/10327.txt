Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Butter
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Chocolate-covered english toffee bars
• Almonds
• Semisweet chocolate morsel

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Spray an 8-inch square baking pan with vegetable cooking spray
3. Mix together flour, baking soda, and salt
4. Beat together butter and sugar at medium speed until light and fluffy
5. Beat in eggs, 1 at a time, beating well after each addition
6. Beat in vanilla
7. Stir flour mixture into butter mixture until just combined
8. Fold in toffee bars, nuts, and chocolate morsels
9. Using a rubber spatula, spread mixture evenly in prepared pan
10. Bake cookies until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean and top is lightly browned, 20 to 25 minutes
11. To prepare topping, immediately sprinkle chocolate morsels over top of warm cookies
12. Return cookies to oven
13. Bake just until chocolate has melted, 2 minutes
14. Spread melted chocolate evenly over cookies
15. Sprinkle with nuts
16. Transfer pan to a wire rack to cool completely
17. Cut into bars<|endoftext|>
