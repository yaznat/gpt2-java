What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Chicken Curry

Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Chili powder
• Curry powder
• Ginger
• Paprika
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Tomatoes
• Cilantro
• Oil
• Bay leaves
• Potato
• Carrot
• Coconut milk
• Sugar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Water

Steps:
1. Place chopped onions into a pan
2. Add in the 2 tbls of oil and cook on medium high heat until onions are transparent
3. Once onions are transparent put in either diced chicken or beef and cook for 6 minutes
4. Mix in the coconut milk and water with the onions and chicken
5. Add the spices and veggies to the milk mixture
6. Simmer for at least 20-30 minutes
7. Stirring occasionally
8. Server over steamed basmati rice<|endoftext|>
