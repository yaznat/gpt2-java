What's a recipe for ice cream?<|endoftext|>Badam Pista Kulfi Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Heavy cream
• Condensed milk
• Bread
• Badam powder
• Pista powder

Steps:
1. Mix all the above ingredients together in a bowl
2. Make sure eveything blends completely together well
3. Then fill this mixture either in an ice-cream tray or in kulfi cones
4. Refrigerate overnight in the frozen section of your refrigerator
5. At the time of serving the kulfi, put the cones under the flowing hot water tap for only 1-2 seconds, and with a slight jerk, remove it in the individual serving plates<|endoftext|>
