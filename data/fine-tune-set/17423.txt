Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Almond extract
• Apricot preserves
• Sliced almonds
• Powdered sugar
• Milk
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Grease and lightly flour a 10-inch bundt pan
3. Set aside
4. In a medium bowl, combine flour, baking powder and salt
5. In a large bowl, beat butter with an electric mixer on medium to high speed for 30 seconds
6. Slowly add granulated sugar, beating until light and fluffy
7. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each
8. Beat in sour cream and almond extract
9. Beat in flour mixture until batter is smooth
10. Spoon half of batter into prepared pan
11. Spoon apricot preserves over batter in pan, making sure preserves do not touch side of pan
12. Sprinkle with almonds
13. Spoon remaining batter over almonds in pan
14. Bake for 55 to 60 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean
15. Cool in pan on a wire rack for 10 minutes
16. Remove from pan
17. Serve warm or cool
18. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and drizzle with powdered sugar icing
19. To prepare icing: in a medium bowl combine sifted powdered sugar, milk and vanilla
20. Stir in enough additional milk, 1 teaspoon at a time, to make an icing that's easy to drizzle<|endoftext|>
