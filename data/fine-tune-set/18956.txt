What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Custard Sauce

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Half-and-half
• Egg yolk
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients, except vanilla, in a heavy pot
2. Cook, over medium heat, stirring constantly, until mixture thickens& will coat the back of a spoon
3. Remove from heat
4. Stir in vanilla& refrigerate in a covered container<|endoftext|>
