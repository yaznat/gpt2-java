Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Caster sugar
• Chocolate
• Cream
• Dark chocolate
• Raspberries
• Chocolate stick

Steps:
1. Beat eggs and sugar in a bowl till its fluffy, pale and increases in volume
2. Put the bowl mixture o'er a pan of simmering water and add milk chocolate in it
3. Mix well
4. Remove from heat
5. Beat the cream until it forms a soft peak
6. Fold it into the egg mixture
7. Add the chopped dark chocolate and mix well
8. Pour the mixture into a tray and freeze it for 4-5 hours or until firm
9. Serve garnished with chocolate sticks and raspberries<|endoftext|>
