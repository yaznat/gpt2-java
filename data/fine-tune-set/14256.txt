Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Cider vinegar
• Honey
• Sea salt
• Miracle whip
• Water
• Garlic powder
• Dill weed
• Parsley
• Onion powder

Steps:
1. Put all ingredients into the blender except for the 2-3 tablespoons water
2. Blend on a slow speed and add the additional water a tablespoon at a time until using a rubber spatula you can get all to blend well until creamy and smooth
3. Put in a covered container or jar and refrigerate for several hours or overnight
4. Store refrigerated for up to 10 days<|endoftext|>
