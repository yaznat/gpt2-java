Give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Bbq D Lamb Meatballs With Plums In Tomato Sauce

Ingredients:
• Ground lamb
• Plum
• Tomatoes
• Onions
• Garlic
• Salt and pepper
• Garlic powder
• Sugar
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Water

Steps:
1. Combine the ground lamb, salt, pepper and garlic powder and make medium sized meatballs
2. Cut the plums in half, discarding the seed
3. Set three plums aside for the sauce
4. On a skewer, "sandwich" the meatballs in between the plums, make three meatball"sandwiches" per skewer
5. Meanwhile, roughly chop the tomatoes, three plums, onions and garlic
6. Saute' the onions and garlic in the olive oil and butter until soft, making sure to use a medim to large saute pan
7. Add tomatoes and plums and cook for 30 minutes with the lid on
8. During this time, keep checking to make sure it doesn't dry up, and add the cup of water as needed
9. Add sugar and salt and pepper to taste
10. The sauce should be the consistency of a chunky marinara sauce
11. Bbq the skewers until cooked through
12. The plums should be soft
13. Remove the skewers to the sauce pan and saute the skewers, turning from time to time, in the sauce for about 5-10 minutes minutes
14. Serve with meat and plums still on the skewers doused in the sauce
15. Eat with crusty bread to sop up the wonderful leftovers!
16. Afiyet olsun!<|endoftext|>
