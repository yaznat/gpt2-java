Can you give me a tea recipe?<|endoftext|>Ants On A Log For A Tea Party

Ingredients:
• Celery
• Cream cheese
• Peanut butter
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Use the inside stalks of celery as they will be more tender and thinner than the outside stalks
2. Wash and dry them
3. Spread either the peanut butter or cream cheese or both in the valley of the celery
4. Then line up a few raisins along the center
5. Cut them to finger food size for a tea, otherwise just eat them<|endoftext|>
