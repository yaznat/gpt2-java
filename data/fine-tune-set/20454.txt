Can you give me a casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon French Toast Casserole In A Mug

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat bread
• Egg
• Egg white
• Skim milk
• Cinnamon
• Sugar-free maple syrup
• Apple

Steps:
1. In a microwave safe mug, place cubed bread and apples
2. Mix together 1 egg, egg white, skim milk, syrup, and cinnamon
3. Pour mixture over bread cubes and apples
4. Microwave for 2 minutes
5. Get a fork and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
