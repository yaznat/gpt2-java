What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Beef Pepper Jack Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Beef
• Creole seasoning
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Feta cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Add the beef, creole seasoning, onion powder, garlic powder and black pepper to a pan and brown the meat until fully cooked
2. Lay out your four tortillas and evenly divide the pepper jack cheese among them
3. Keep the ingredients on one half of the tortilla so that you can easily fold it later
4. Sprinkle one tablespoon of feta on each tortilla, on top of the pepper jack
5. Evenly divide the beef among the four tortillas, on top of the cheeses
6. Spray a frying pan with non-stick cooking spray
7. Fold each tortilla in half and fry on medium-high heat, flipping until both sides are golden brown
8. Cut in half and serve with sour cream<|endoftext|>
