Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Hand Cut Fries With Smoked Aioli Gluten Free

Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Garlic clove
• Paprika
• Fresh lemon juice
• Flat leaf parsley
• Kosher salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Baking potatoes

Steps:
1. In a food processor, puree the mayonnaise with the garlic, smoked paprika and lemon juice
2. Stir in 2 teaspoons of the parsley and season with salt and pepper
3. Scrape the aioli into a small bowl and refrigerate
4. In a large saucepan, heat 1 inch of oil to 250
5. In a large bowl, rinse the potatoes and pat thoroughly dry
6. Working in batches, fry the potatoes until they are almost tender and look dry on the outside, about 6 minutes
7. Transfer to paper towels to drain
8. Increase the oil temperature to 350
9. Cook the fries in batches until golden and crisp, about 3 minutes
10. Transfer to paper towels to drain and immediately season with salt
11. Sprinkle the fries with the remaining 1 tablespoon of parsley and serve with the smoked aioli<|endoftext|>
