Ingredients:
• Dried apricot
• Boiling water
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven to 350
2. Grease a 9-inch loaf pan
3. Cut the apricots into small pieces and places in a bowl
4. Add the boiling water and set aside to cool
5. Sift the flour, baking powder, and salt and set aside
6. In a large bowl, combine the egg, vanilla, sugar and oil
7. Beat until blended
8. Gradually add the apricot mixture, beating constantly
9. Add the flour mixture and beat until smooth
10. Stir in the nuts, then scrape the dough into the prepared pan
11. Bake for 1 hour
12. Let the bread cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then loosen with spatulas and transfer to a rack
13. Cool then wrap in foil and store in the fridge for at least 12 hours before serving<|endoftext|>
