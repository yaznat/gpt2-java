What's a good tea recipe?<|endoftext|>My Way Of Steamed Cabbage

Ingredients:
• Head of cabbage
• Onion
• Fresh mushrooms
• Margarine
• Bacon grease
• Black pepper
• Beau monde seasoning
• Salt
• Seasoning salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Pam cooking spray

Steps:
1. In a large frying pan on medium heat, spray pan with pam
2. Add bacon grease, turn heat down
3. Add butter and saut onion and mushrooms in grease
4. Add rest of seasonings
5. Add shredded cabbage, stir well to blend seasoning
6. Cover with lid and steam on low for 20 minutes or until tender<|endoftext|>
