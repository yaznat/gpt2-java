Ingredients:
• Unsweetened kool-aid powdered drink mix
• Jello gelatin
• Granulated sugar
• Boiling water
• Cold water

Steps:
1. In a 2-quart glass measure, combine the kool-aid, jello powder and sugar
2. Add hot water and stir until completely dissolved
3. Stir in cold water
4. Pour into popsicle molds and freeze<|endoftext|>
