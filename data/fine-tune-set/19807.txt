Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Vegetable oil
• Salt

Steps:
1. Peel the potatoes and slice them 1 / 4 inch thick into either flat rounds or traditional french fry shape
2. Heat vegetble oil in large frying pan until it is good and hot
3. Add potatoes, cooking until they become golden, then flipping them
4. Cook until they are the color you desire
5. Remove and place on a paper towel covered plate or lined bowl
