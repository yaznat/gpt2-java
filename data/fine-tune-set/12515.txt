Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Oil
• Bay leaf
• Onion
• Pepper
• Salt
• Honey
• Carrots
• Celery
• White onions
• Water

Steps:
1. Heat oil in skillet
2. Brown meat on all slides
3. In slow cooker, combine browned beef, bay leaf, chopped onion, pepper, salt, honey, and vegetables
4. Pour water over all
5. Cover and cook on low 8 to 10 hours<|endoftext|>
