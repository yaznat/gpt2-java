Ingredients:
• Creamy peanut butter
• Jam
• Bread

Steps:
1. Remove crusts from bread
2. With a rolling pin or large soup can, completely flatten bread
3. Spread 1 tablespoon of peanut butter and 1 tablespoon of fruit spread on each slice of bread
4. Roll each slice into a tight spiral
5. Cut each spiral into 4 pieces<|endoftext|>
