Ingredients:
• Red onion
• Garlic cloves
• Turkey bacon
• Chicken breast
• Red pepper
• Green pepper
• Seasoning
• Dried chipotle powder
• Fresh black pepper
• Green chili
• Reduced-fat cream cheese
• Barbecue sauce
• Reduced-fat mexican cheese blend
• Fresh cilantro
• Thin pizza crust

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 and heat pizza stone if using one
2. Heat large nonstick pan and saute onions and garlic with bacon
3. Add chicken and saute until chicken is cooked through
4. Add peppers and seasonings
5. Add chilies, cream cheese and bbq sauce reducing heat and stirring until cheese is melted and "sauce" is blended
6. For a crispy crust, pre bake crust for 10 minutes before loading it with toppings
7. Add chicken mixture and top with cheese and cilantro if desired
8. Bake until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
