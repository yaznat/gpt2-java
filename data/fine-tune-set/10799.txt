What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>4Th Of July Cabbage Salad

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Cabbage
• Coleslaw dressing
• Blue cheese
• Cherry tomatoes

Steps:
1. Place bacon in skillet, cook over med-high heat until brown, crumble and set aside
2. In large bowl, combine bacon, cabbage and dressing
3. Mix well
4. Sprinkle with the sliced cherry tomatoes and blue cheese
5. Refrigerate and serve chilled<|endoftext|>
