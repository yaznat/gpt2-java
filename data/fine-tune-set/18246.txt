Please give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Basic Buttermilk Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix together all dry ingredients
2. In a separate bowl, mix together the wet ingredients, including the vegetable oil
3. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients, and pour the wet ingredients into the well
4. Stir with a spoon or whisk just until it is combined
5. There should be lumps and the batter will be fairly thick
6. At this point you can let the batter rest for up to 60 minutes, or cover and refrigerate it overnight
7. For large pancakes use 1 / 4 cup of batter, or use about 1 / 8 cup for little pancakes
8. Cook until the top is bubbling and first side is brown, then flip
9. Don't mash them with the spatula after you flip them, or they will be flattened instead of fluffy
10. When they are cooked through, the top will be a little bouncy if you poke it with your fingers<|endoftext|>
