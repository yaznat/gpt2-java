Give me a tofu recipe.<|endoftext|>Evil Jungle Prince With Tofu

Ingredients:
• Bird chiles
• Lemongrass
• Kaffir lime leaves
• Peanut oil
• Coconut milk
• Tofu
• Fresh basil leaves
• Thai fish sauce
• Cabbage

Steps:
1. Pound the peppers, lemongrass and lime leaves to a paste
2. Heat the oil in a skillet and add the paste
3. Fry until fragrant
4. Add the chicken or tofu and cook until chicken is cooked through
5. Add the basil and fish sauce to taste
6. Serve on thinly shredded cabbage<|endoftext|>
