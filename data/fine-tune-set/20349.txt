Can you give me a casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>25 000 Casserole

Ingredients:
• Beef
• Flour
• Paprika
• Onion
• Cream of chicken soup
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Poultry seasoning
• Celery seed
• Dry onion flakes
• Poppy seed
• Vegetable oil
• Milk
• Sour cream
• Butter
• Breadcrumbs

Steps:
1. Coat the meat with 1 / 2 c of flour and the paprika
2. Brown in vegetable oil
3. Transfer to a casserole dish
4. Add can of onions
5. Combine one can of the chicken soup and one can water
6. Pour over meat and onions
7. Bake at 350 for 45 minutes
8. While the meat is baking-- combine 2 c flour, the baking powder, salt, poultry seasoning, celery seed, onion flakes and poppy seeds
9. Mix well, and add 1 / 4 c oil and 1 c milk
10. Drop tablespoons of dough into breadcrumbs which you have mixed with melted butter
11. Put dumplings on top of the meat
12. Increase heat to 425 and bake for another 20 minutes
13. Make sauce
14. Combine one can of cream of chicken soup and sour cream
15. Heat sauce and serve over meat and dumplings<|endoftext|>
