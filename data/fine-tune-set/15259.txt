Ingredients:
• Pizza crust
• Broccoli
• Boneless chicken breast
• Olive oil
• Oregano
• Basil
• Part-skim ricotta cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Dry mustard
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees fahrenheit
2. Prepare dough
3. While dough is rising or sitting, steam broccoli and cook chicken with olive oil, oregano and basil to taste, until no longer pink
4. Spread the dough into a prepared jelly roll pan
5. Bake for about ten minutes
6. Meanwhile, chop cooled chicken into small chunks and chop steamed broccoli
7. Combine chicken, ricotta cheese, broccoli, 1 / 2 cup mozzarella cheese, 1 / 2 cup cheddar cheese, dry mustard, and crushed red pepper
8. Remove crust from oven
9. Spread with ricotta mixture
10. Top with remaining cheese
11. Cook until cheese is browned<|endoftext|>
