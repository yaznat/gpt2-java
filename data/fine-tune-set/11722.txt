Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Butter
• Cocoa
• Baking soda
• Ale 8 soft drink
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Vanilla
• Mini marshmallows
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Mix flour and sugar in a large bowl-set aside
3. Mix butter, cocoa, and ale 8
4. Bring to a boil, but do not boil
5. Pour hot mixture over flour and sugar
6. Mix well
7. Add buttermilk, eggs, soda, and vanilla
8. Beat until smooth then add marshmallows
9. Bake for 30-35 minutes
10. Topping
11. Mix together 4 oz of ale 8, butter, cocoa
12. Bring to a boil
13. Pour over confectioner's sugar
14. Beat until smooth
