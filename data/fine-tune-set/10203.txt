Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>Best Ever Homemade Chili

Ingredients:
• Pink kidney beans
• Chili hot beans
• Tomato sauce
• Diced tomatoes
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Emeril's original essence
• Tabasco sauce
• Chili powder
• Campbell's tomato soup
• Heinz ketchup
• Sugar
• Ground chuck

Steps:
1. Cook the ground chuck, onions, & pepper until done
2. Add spices to the mixture and mix well
3. Place in a large soup kettle and add the beans, tomatoes and sauce
4. Stir, then add the rest of the ingredients
5. Cook on low to med heat for around 20 minutes, making sure you stir the bottom
6. Serve with mexican cornbread or crackers
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
