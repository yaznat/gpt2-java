Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Lemon juice
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Fresh oregano
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Avocados
• Prosciutto

Steps:
1. Combine oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, and pepper in a container with a tight lid and set aside
2. Cut avocados into halves, remove pit, and peel each half carefully
3. Slice each avocado half into 4 wedges and wrap a proscuitto strip around each in the center
4. Shake the dressing if the ingredients have settled and test for flavor-adjust seasonings if needed
5. Set on a serving platter and drizzle with some of the dressing
6. Serve immediately as avocados brown quickly once cut<|endoftext|>
