What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Cai Bao Filling Vegetable Tofu Bun Filling Sesame Flavored

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Firm tofu
• Fresh spinach leaves
• Mushrooms
• Canola oil
• Sesame oil
• Light soy sauce
• Rice wine vinegar
• Garlic

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a wok over high heat
2. Add onions, garlic, and a splash of the sesame oil and soy sauce
3. Stir fry for 1 3 minutes to bring out flavor
4. Add tofu and stir fry until golden
5. If cubes are too large, break them during frying
6. Add mushrooms and another splash of sesame oil and soy sauce
7. Stir fry for 3 5 minutes
8. Add remaining sesame oil, soy sauce, and the vinegar
9. Add the spinach immediately
10. Stir fry until spinach is well wilted, 4 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
