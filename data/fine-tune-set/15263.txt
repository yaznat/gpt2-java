What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Brown Rice Pizza Wheat Free Recipe

Ingredients:
• Cooked brown rice
• Egg whites
• Mozzarella cheese
• Vegetable oil cooking spray
• Pizza sauce
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Parmesan cheese
• Sliced mushrooms
• Ripe olives
• Red pepper

Steps:
1. Combine cooled rice, egg whites, and 1 / 3 cup mozzarella cheese in a large bowl
2. Press into 12-inch pizza pan sprayed with vegetable cooking spray
3. Bake at 400 degrees for 5 minutes
4. Combine pizza sauce, garlic powder, and black pepper in a small bowl
5. Spread over prepared rice crust
6. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese
7. Layer 1 / 3 cup mozzarella cheese, mushrooms, olives, and red pepper
8. Top with remaining cheese
9. Bake at 400 for 10 minutes
10. Garnish with green peppers<|endoftext|>
