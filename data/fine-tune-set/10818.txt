Give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>7 Layer Salad W O Cheese

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Lettuce
• Fresh spinach
• Green onion
• Frozen peas
• Mushroom
• Sour cream
• Hellmann's mayonnaise
• Hidden valley original ranch dressing

Steps:
1. Fry bacon, drain and crumble
2. Sit aside
3. Mix ingredients for topping
4. Layer all ingredients in order given, in a very large glass bowl, starting with lettuce
5. Cut up lettuce or tear up
6. Wash and tear spinach
7. Chop green onions
8. Pour in thawed frozen peas
9. Pour in sliced, cubed mushrooms
10. Place bacon on very top
11. Pour topping over all
12. Refrigerate overnight
13. Do not mix till ready to serve<|endoftext|>
