Ingredients:
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Lemon juice
• Garlic cloves
• Ground black pepper
• Chicken wings

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees
2. In a large bowl, combine all the ingredients except the chicken wings
3. Mix well
4. Rinse and pat dry the chicken wings and place in the sauce
5. Toss to coat well
6. Place on rimmed baking sheets or cookie sheets that have been coated with nonstick vegetable spray and bake for 45-50 minutes or until done to desired crispness, turning once<|endoftext|>
