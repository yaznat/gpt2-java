What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Easy Vegan Wheat Free Pancakes Waffles

Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Millet
• Water
• Apple
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Rice bran powder
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Honey

Steps:
1. Blend all ingredients in blender until thoroughly mixed
2. Add baking powder at end and blend until just mixed
3. Bake on a hot grill, skillet, or waffle maker<|endoftext|>
