What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Basting Sauce For Grilled Fish

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Garlic powder
• Lemon juice
• Soy sauce
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan combine all ingredients
2. Cook over low heat until the sugar is dissolved
3. Cool
4. Before grilling the fish coat both sides in the sauce
5. Place on grill and continue to baste frequently
6. Grilling time for thicker cuts of fish is about 7-9 minutes<|endoftext|>
