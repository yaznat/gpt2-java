Give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>Apple Onion Rice

Ingredients:
• White rice
• Apple juice
• Water
• Dry onion soup mix
• Margarine

Steps:
1. Wash rice and drain
2. Add apple juice and water in large saucepan
3. Add margarine and dry onion soup mix
4. Bring saucepan to a simmer
5. Add rice
6. Stir
7. Cover
8. Turn heat to low
9. Cook until liquid is absorbed
10. Cooking time is about 15 to 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
