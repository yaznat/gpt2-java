Generate a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Berries With Custard Sauce Light And Easy

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Milk
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Vanilla extract
• Berries

Steps:
1. Beat eggs in a small bowl, set aside
2. In a saucepan, combine sugar, cornstarch and salt
3. Gradually stir in milk until smooth
4. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly
5. Stir a small amount into eggs
6. Return all to pan, stirring constantly
7. Cook and stir for 2 1 / 2 minutes or until mixture reaches 160 degrees
8. Remove from the heat
9. Stir in sour cream and vanilla
10. Set saucepan in ice and stir mixture for 5 minutes
11. Cover and refrigerate until serving
12. Serve over berries -- strawberries, blueberries, raspberries
13. Yield: about 2 cups of sauce
