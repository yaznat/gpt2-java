Ingredients:
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Evaporated milk
• Chocolate milk
• Chocolate syrup
• Toasted sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Whisk first 4 ingredients together in a large bowl until blended
2. Cover and chill for 30 minutes
3. Pour milk mixture into freezer container of a 1 quart ice cream maker and freeze according to manufacturer's directions
4. Remove container with ice cream from ice cream maker and place in freezer for 15 minutes
5. Stir almonds into prepared ice cream
6. Transfer to an airtight container and freeze for 1 1 / 2 hours or until firm<|endoftext|>
