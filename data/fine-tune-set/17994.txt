Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Water
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Eggs
• Extract
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Pecans
• Bourbon
• Light brown sugar
• Milk
• Powdered sugar
• Almond extract

Steps:
1. Brownies:
2. In large saucepan, combine the butter, sugar, and water
3. Cook, over medium heat, until just boiling
4. Add chocolate chips, and stir until melted
5. Remove from heat and allow to cool slightly
6. Stir in the eggs, bourbon, and vanilla, and beat lightly
7. Stir in the flour, baking soda, salt and pecans and mix well
8. Spread the batter into prepared 8x8 baking dish
9. Bake in a preheated 350 degree oven for 20 minutes and cool in the pan
10. Top with praline icing
11. Praline icing:
12. In a medium saucepan, over medium heat, bring the first 3 ingredients to a boil
13. Boil for one minute, whisking constantly
14. Remove from heat and whisk in powdered sugar and almond extract until smooth
15. Add pecans and continue to stir for about 3 minutes or until the mixture begins to thicken
16. Pour onto cooled brownies
17. Makes 1 1 / 2 cups<|endoftext|>
