Please give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>Anisette Cookies

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Orange juice
• Almond extract
• Vanilla extract
• Lemon extract
• Anise flavoring
• All-purpose flour
• White sugar
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
2. In a large bowl, beat eggs
3. Blend in vegetable oil, orange juice, almond extract, vanilla extract, lemon extract, and anise flavoring
4. Sift the flour with the sugar and baking powder
5. Add the flour mixture to the egg mixture
6. The dough will probably be sticky
7. Add additional flour until dough no longer sticks to your hands and can rolled
8. Pull off a piece of dough about the size of a walnut
9. Roll it into a rope and shape it into a knot
10. Place finished knots 2 inches apart on an ungreased cookie sheet
11. Bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 15 minutes
12. Bottoms should slightly brown
13. Cool cookies on a wire rack
14. If desired ice with confectioner's sugar icing<|endoftext|>
