Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Bacon
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Toothpick

Steps:
1. Preheat broiler
2. Pour drained green beans into large bowl
3. Season with salt, pepper and garlic powder
4. Cut each bacon slice into 3 equal portions
5. Wrap 6-8 beans with bacon piece, securing with toothpick
6. Place on broiler pan
7. Sprinkle evenly with some of the brown sugar
8. Broil until sugar melts and bacon browns
9. Turn bundles over and sprinkle with additional brown sugar
10. Broil until brown and bubbly<|endoftext|>
