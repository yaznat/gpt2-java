Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Beef stew meat
• Yellow onions
• Garlic
• Fresh lemon juice
• Brown sugar
• Whole tomatoes
• Beef stock
• Water
• Salt and pepper
• Cabbage
• Raisins

Steps:
1. In a large soup pot over medium heat, let the oil get warm
2. Brown the stew meat on all sides for about 6 minutes
3. Add in the next 8 ingredients
4. Stir to combine
5. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 1 hour or until meat is tender
6. Add in the cabbage and raisins and let simmer, uncovered, for 30 more minutes<|endoftext|>
