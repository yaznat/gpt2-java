Ingredients:
• Pesto sauce
• Balsamic vinegar
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Salt and pepper
• Leg of lamb

Steps:
1. Combine all but the lamb
2. Trim fat from lamb and marinade in above up to 24 hours
3. Foil line a baking pan, place lamb on a rack in pan
4. Roast at 450 f for 20 to 30 minutes until thermometer reads 140 f
5. Tent with foil and let stand 10 minutes
6. Serve with pan juices<|endoftext|>
