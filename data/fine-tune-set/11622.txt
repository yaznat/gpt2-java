Ingredients:
• Bisquick baking mix
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Water
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, mix bisquik, applesauce and water
2. Add cinnamon if desired
3. Be sure to add liquids slowly to adjust for desired thick / thinness
4. Do not make it too thin or pancakes will not set
5. Spray skillet with nonstick spray, set over high heat
6. To test for appropriate heat before cooking: splash a few droplets of water on skillet
7. If water splatters across the skillet, it is ready
8. It is important to get the pan hot before you cook or pancakes won't brown properly
9. When ready, pour in batter, about 3 tbsp full each for smaller pancakes, or according to personal preference
10. Wait about 1 minute, or until bubbles set, before flipping
11. Don't flip too many times or your pancakes will be flat!
12. Serve immediately, or if not, leave to cool on paper towels and freeze for longer storage<|endoftext|>
