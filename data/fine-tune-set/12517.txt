Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Whole kernel corn
• Creamed corn
• Onion
• Chicken broth
• Cream of chicken soup
• Salt and pepper
• Bay leaves
• Basil
• Fresh rosemary
• Butter

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients into crock pot
2. Cook on "low" setting for approximately 6 hours
