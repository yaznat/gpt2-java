Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Fat-free mayonnaise
• Orange
• Dijon mustard
• Garlic
• Pepper
• Sandwich buns
• Catfish
• Salt and pepper
• Lettuce leaf
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Heat a grill pan over medium heat and cook bacon
2. Drain on paper towels
3. Drain excess grease from pan and after salt and peppering fish cook fish in same pan
4. In a small bowl mix together dressing
5. Toast buns and spread mayo mixure on bottom of bun, add lettuce tomato slice, bacon and 1 piece fish top with lettuce and tomato and top bun mayo side down
6. Repeat for rest of the sandwiches<|endoftext|>
