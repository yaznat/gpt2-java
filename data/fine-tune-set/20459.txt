What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Apple Turnip Casserole

Ingredients:
• Rome apples
• Turnip
• Coriander
• Apple juice
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In a 1 1 / 2 quart casserole dish layer the apple slices and the turnip slices
2. Dust with the coriander
3. Pour in the liquid
4. Cover and bake in a 350f degrees oven for 30 minutes
5. Uncover and sprinkle with nuts
6. Bake for another 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
