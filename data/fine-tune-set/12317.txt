Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>A Healthy Apple And Celery Soup

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Celery
• Chicken broth
• Butter
• Onion
• Fresh sage
• Clove
• Ground black pepper
• Mascarpone
• Brandy

Steps:
1. Base -- in a medium sauce pan, melt the butter over medium heat and add the celery, onion and cook 3-4 minutes
2. Add in the apples and broth and cook until tender
3. It will take about 20 minutes on a medium low simmer, uncovered
4. Puree -- i used my immersion blender, but you can you can transfer it to a standard blender and puree until smooth
5. Season -- add in the the sage, and a very small pinch of cloves, go easy ad season with black pepper
6. Cook another 10 minutes
7. Mascarpone -- mix the mascarpone with the brandy, and return to the refrigerator
8. I mixed mine right in the container, save on those dirty dishes
9. Serve -- i like to serve with a fresh small sprig of sage and a dollop of the brandied mascarpone cheese
10. You can serve this hot, which i prefer, or chilled is also very good
11. And don't forget my sweet savory bread sticks<|endoftext|>
