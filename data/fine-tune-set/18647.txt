Ingredients:
• Dried apricot
• Dried cranberries
• Orange liqueur
• French bread
• Apricot jam
• Whipping cream
• Milk
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Salt

Steps:
1. Combine apricots, cranberries and liqueur in small bowl
2. Let stand for 15 minutes
3. Scatter half of apricot mixture with liqueur, on bottom on greased shallow 12 cup capacity ovenproof dish
4. Spread one side of each piece of bread with apricot jam
5. Arrange bread, jam side up, in dish slightly overlapping
6. Scatter remaining apricot mixture over bread
7. Whisk remaining 6 ingredients together in medium sized bowl
8. Pour over bread
9. Let stand for 10 minutes
10. Cover with foil
11. Place dish in roaster
12. Pour enough boiling water to come halfway up side of dish
13. Bake in 325 f oven for 1 1 / 4 hours
14. Remove foil and bake for a further 15 minutes until custard is just set
15. Carefully remove dish from roaster
16. Let stand for 15 minutes before serving
17. Serve warm or cold
18. Serves 4 to 6<|endoftext|>
