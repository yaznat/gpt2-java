Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Friendship Bread And Starter

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Milk
• Flour
• Oil
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Place one cup each sugar, milk, and flour in large glass or plastic bowl
2. Cover lightly with plastic wrap and set on your counter at room temperature
3. Stir with a wooden spoon everyday for 17 days
4. On day 18 do nothing
5. On days 19, 20, and 21, stir
6. On day 22, add 1 cup sugar, 1 cup milk, and 1 cup flour, and stir again
7. On days 23, 24, 25, and 26 stir
8. On day 27, add 1 cup milk, 1 cup flour, and 1 cup sugar and stir
9. It's now ready to use to make the bread
10. Give away two cups of the starter to two friends, use one to make the bread, and keep one to keep the starter going
11. When you give away a cup of the starter, give these directions for keeping it going: do not refrigerate
12. Do not use metal spoon or metal bowl
13. Do not cover tightly, cover loosely with plastic wrap
14. Amish friendship bread: mix oil, eggs, and vanilla with the starter mix
15. In a separate bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, salt, baking soda, vanilla pudding mix, and nuts
16. Add to liquid mixture and stir thoroughly
17. Pour into two large well-greased 9x5 inch loaf pans, or one bundt pan, that have been sprinkled with a mixture of cinnamon, sugar, and flour
18. Sprinkle more cinnamon and sugar on the tops of the unbaked loaves
19. Bake at 325f for one hour or until done<|endoftext|>
