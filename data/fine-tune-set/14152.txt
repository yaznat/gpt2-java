Give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Basil Tofu Ricotta Non Dairy

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Lemon juice
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Fresh black pepper
• Fresh basil leaves
• Olive oil
• Nutritional yeast

Steps:
1. In large bowl, mash tofu with hands until crumbly
2. Add lemon juice, garlic, salt, pepper, and basil
3. Mash with hands until mixture reaches the consistency of ricotta cheese
4. Add olive oil
5. Stir with fork
6. Add nutritional yeast and mix all ingredients well with fork
7. Cover and refrigerate until ready to use<|endoftext|>
