Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Beanie Brownies

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Black beans
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Put entire can of beans in a blender and liquefy
2. Add liquefied beans to dry brownie mix and blend well
3. Pour into a 9 x 13 greased pan
4. Bake at 350f for 30 minutes
5. Sprinkle lightly with powdered sugar<|endoftext|>
