What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Amadeus Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Rye bread
• Roasted red pepper and eggplant spread
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Salt
• Low-sodium turkey breast
• Tomatoes
• Provolone cheese

Steps:
1. Spread both slices of bread with the roasted vegetable spread
2. Sprinkle the spread with the red pepper flakes and salt
3. Layer the turkey, tomatoes, cheese, and the other slice of bread
4. Press gently but do not flatten
5. Cut in half and serve or wrap tightly in plastic wrap, foil, or with parchment paper and a rubber band and refrigerate for 4-8 hours<|endoftext|>
