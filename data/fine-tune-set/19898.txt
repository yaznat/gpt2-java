Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Loaded Potato Fries

Ingredients:
• Waffle-shaped french fries
• Steak seasoning
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onions
• Bacon bits

Steps:
1. Arrange waffle fries in a greased 15 x 10 inch baking pan
2. Bake at 450 for 20-25 minutes or until lightly browned
3. Sprinkle with steak seasoning
4. Toss to coat
5. Top with cheese, onions, and bacon bits
6. Bake 2-3 minutes longer or until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
