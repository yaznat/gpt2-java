What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Beer Marinade For Steak

Ingredients:
• Fresh gingerroot
• Orange marmalade
• Garlic cloves
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Red pepper flakes
• Dry mustard
• Beer

Steps:
1. Mix and pour over steaks in a ziplock bag, pressing bag as you seal it up to remove enough air so that meat stays coated on both sides
2. Refrigerate for 6 hours or overnight
3. Allow steaks to come up to room temperature before grilling<|endoftext|>
