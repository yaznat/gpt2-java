Ingredients:
• Caramel sauce
• Brown sugar cinnamon syrup
• Brewed espresso
• Milk
• Whipped cream
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. In 8 oz
2. Coffee glass, add add sauce, syrup and espresso
3. Stir well
4. Top with steamed milk
5. Stir well
6. Fold in froth
7. Top with whipped cream
8. Sprinkle with cinnamon<|endoftext|>
