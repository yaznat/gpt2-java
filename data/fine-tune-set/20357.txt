Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Refried beans
• Cheese
• Green onion
• Tortilla chips
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat over to 350 degrees
2. Brown ground beef and drain
3. Add the two packets of taco seasoning to the beef
4. Fill each packet with water and add to the meat and seasoning mixture
5. Stir well
6. Let meat simmer about 15 minutes covered or until water has evaporated and mixture is not soupy
7. In casserole dish spread refried beans evenly on bottom of dish, fully covering
8. Sprinkle 1 cup of shredded cheese over refried beans
9. Next pour meat mixture over beans and cheese
10. Spread evenly
11. Cover with remaining cheese and top with green onions
12. Bake in oven about 15 minutes or until cheese is melted
13. Serve with tortilla chips and desired toppings<|endoftext|>
