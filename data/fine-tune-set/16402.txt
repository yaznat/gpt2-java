Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Marinate tenderloins in recipe #70178 as directed or sprinkle tenderloins lightly with seasoned pepper
2. Wrap tenderloins with bacon slices, securing with toothpicks
3. Preheat right-side burners set to medium or use regular grill with coals set to one side
4. With drip-pan place pork loins over indirect heat
5. Or, place on broiling pan and broil in oven
6. Grill covered, turning every so often until bacon is brown and cooked on all sides
7. When bacon is cooked and brown pork tenderloins should be done -- usually in 20 to 30 minutes
8. Recommended internal temperature is 155 dg

10. After meat is done, remove and let rest for 5 minutes
11. Remove toothpicks and cut in 1" to 1 1 / 2" slices and serve, still wrapped in bacon<|endoftext|>
