Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Arroz Con Cebolla Rice With Onions

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• French onion soup
• Long grain rice
• Water
• Onion

Steps:
1. Cut bacon in fine strips and cook
2. Remove from heat as soon as is browned
3. Do not toast
4. In same pan, cook onions
5. Add onion soup
6. Add bacon
7. Add 2 c rice and then add water
8. Cover and cook for 20 minutes until done<|endoftext|>
