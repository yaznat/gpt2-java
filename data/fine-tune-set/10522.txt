Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Aelplermagronen Swiss Pasta

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Salt
• Noodles
• Cheese
• Cream
• Butter
• Onions

Steps:
1. Peel and dice the potatoes
2. Boil in salted water
3. Add the noodles shortly before the potatoes are done
4. The potatoes should be soft
5. Drain and arrange in alternate layers with the cheese in a deep baking dish
6. Pour in the cream and place in the hot oven for a few minutes until the cheese melts
7. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a skillet
8. Slice the onions and saute until they turn light brown
9. Spread over the noodles and potatoes
10. Serve with apple puree or salad<|endoftext|>
