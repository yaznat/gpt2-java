Give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>Aunt Helen S Rice Pilaf

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Angel hair pasta
• Uncle ben's converted brand rice
• Chicken broth
• Salt
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Melt butter over moderate heat
2. Add birds nest angel hair pasta and stir constantly until nicely browned
3. Be careful, as the pasta can burn very quickly
4. Add rice to the pot and blend with pasta
5. Add the chicken broth, salt, and garlic powder
6. Bring to boil
7. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for about 20-25 minutes
8. When cooked, stir and let set for about 5-10 minutes<|endoftext|>
