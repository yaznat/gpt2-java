Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Celery
• Onion
• Rotel tomatoes
• Beef broth
• Stew meat
• Fresh green beans
• Carrots
• Corn
• Lima beans
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Add all ingredients except corn and lima beans to crock pot
2. Stir
3. Add 15 ounces of water
4. Cook on low for 8 hours
5. Add corn and lima beans and heat a few additional minutes
6. Add more water if needed
7. This can be frozen and is terrific reheated as leftovers<|endoftext|>
