What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Fish With Lemon Mushroom Sauce

Ingredients:
• Haddock fillets
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Milk
• Sliced mushrooms
• Onion
• Lemon juice
• Paprika
• Salt
• Oregano
• Pepper
• Bay leaf
• Buttered bread crumb
• Poultry seasoning

Steps:
1. Place frozen fillets in shallow buttered baking dish
2. In saucepan, combine remaining ingredients and simmer 10 minutes
3. Pour over fillets
4. Top with 1 cup buttered breadcrumbs which have been lightly seasoned with poultry seasoning
5. Dot with butter and bake for 45 minutes at 375 degrees
6. This may easily be halved<|endoftext|>
