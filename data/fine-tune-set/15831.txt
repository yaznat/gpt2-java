Give me a burrito recipe.<|endoftext|>Breakfast Burritos Filling

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Garlic clove
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Melt margarine in skillet
2. Brown the potato, onion, pepper, and garlic in the skillet
3. Add the meat and heat until the meat is heated through
4. Mix the eggs with milk or water, and pour over the meat and veggies
5. Cook, stirring, until the eggs are done
6. Spoon into flour tortillas, along with 1 tablespoon of grated cheese per tortilla
7. Serve with sour cream and salsa on the side<|endoftext|>
