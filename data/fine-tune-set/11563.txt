What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>Oh I Love This Cheesecake

Ingredients:
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Butter
• Sugar
• Small curd cottage cheese
• Cream cheese
• Eggs
• Lemon juice
• Vanilla
• Cornstarch
• Flour
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 dg
2. Place jellyroll pan or cookie sheet with sides half filled with hot water on rack underneath center rack in oven
3. Grease or spray bottom and sides of a 10" springform pan with butter or non-stick vegetable spray
4. Make crust by putting graham cracker crumbs and sugar in a small bowl and mixing in melted butter until crumbly
5. Press mixture into bottom and partially up the sides of a greased 10" springform pan
6. Bake on center rack in oven at 325 dg
7. For 10 minutes
8. Set aside to cool
9. Mix together cheeses, sugar, beaten eggs, lemon juice and vanilla
10. Add cornstarch, flour and melted stick of butter
11. Mix until smooth
12. Blend in sour cream and mix well
13. Pour over prepared crust
14. Bake at 325 dg
15. For 1 hour on center rack of oven, centered over water bath underneath
16. After 1 hr
17. , turn off oven and keep cheesecake in oven for 2 hours -- do not open oven door during resting period!
18. Remove from oven and refrigerate, do not worry if cheesecake is a little "jiggly" when removing from oven, because it will firm up during chilling process
19. When chilled, remove from pan just prior to serving
20. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
