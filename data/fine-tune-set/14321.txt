Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Olive oil
• Broth
• Pesto sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Dried oregano
• Dried thyme
• Dried rosemary
• Lemon juice
• Salt
• Pepper
• Feta
• Tzatziki
• Cucumber
• Tomatoes
• Kalamata olive
• Pita bread

Steps:
1. Drain the tofu and press for 30 min by putting between paper towels with a large weight on top
2. Prepare the marinade by mixing oil, broth, pesto, garlic, oregano, thyme, rosemary, lemon and salt and pepper to taste
3. Cut the tofu into thin strips
4. Marinate the tofu for 1hr minimum, tossing a few times to make sure it is evenly distributed but being careful not to break pieces
5. Meanwhile, combine cucumber, tomato, olive and feta in a bowl
6. Take the tofu out of the marinade and fry over very high heat in a non-stick skillet until darkly browned
7. Turn off heat and immediately pour extra marinade over tofu
8. The pan will be very hot and the marinade should mostly cook off immediately,
9. Meanwhile, heat up pitas in oven or microwave
10. Cut in half and fill each one with tofu, tzatziki, and veggies<|endoftext|>
