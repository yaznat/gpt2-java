What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Alfredo Sauce With Pasta

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Heavy cream
• Parmesan cheese
• Salt
• Pepper
• Noodles

Steps:
1. Cook noodles or fettuccine according to package directions
2. Heat butter and cream in saucepan until butter is melted
3. Remove from heat
4. Add 1 cup parmesan cheese, salt and pepper
5. Stir until sauce is blended and fairly smooth
6. Add to drained noodles and toss until they are well coated
7. Sprinkle with remaining cheese
8. Variation: add cooked shrimp, crab or mushrooms<|endoftext|>
