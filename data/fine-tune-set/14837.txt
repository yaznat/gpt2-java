Ingredients:
• Beef
• Cooked rice
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Sugar
• Garlic cloves
• Red chile
• Dried red chilies
• Green onion
• Carrot
• Oil
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Slice beef or chicken breast into thin strips
2. For the marinade
3. Combine soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, one minced clove of garlic, and red chili
4. Marinate meat for several hours
5. Thinly slice green onions and coarsely chop the green tops
6. Separate, as they will go in at different times
7. Grate the carrots
8. Slice the dried chilies lengthwise
9. Scramble the eggs lightly in a bowl
10. Heat the oil in a wok or deep pan
11. Fry the eggs until set, remove and slice into strips
12. Add the garlic and sliced green onions and the sliced red chilies to the hot oil, saut for a minute or so, then add the marinated meat, reserving the marinade
13. If you're cooking chicken, don't reserve the marinade
14. Just add some soy sauce in its place later in the process
15. Stir fry the meat until cooked, and then remove
16. Add the chilled, cooked rice to the hot oil and stir fry until heated thoroughly
17. Add the meat, green onion tops, carrots, egg slices, and some or all of the the reserved marinade
18. Stir fry until all is heated through
19. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
