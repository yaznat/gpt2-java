What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Date Peach Pudding Raw Food

Ingredients:
• Dates
• Water
• Banana
• Peaches
• Vanilla extract
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine dates & water & let set for 15 minutes, then drain & discard the liquid
2. Place all ingredients in the bowl of a blender & process until smooth
3. Spoon into several serving bowls & enjoy!<|endoftext|>
