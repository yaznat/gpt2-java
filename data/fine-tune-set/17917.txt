Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Banana Chocolate Chip Brownies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Chocolate chips
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Banana
• Walnuts
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f butter a 9 inch square pan
2. In a small bowl, stir together flour, baking soda and salt
3. Set aide
4. In a small saucepan, combine butter and brown sugar
5. Bring to a boil over low heat, stirring constantly
6. Immediately remove from heat and stir in 1 cup of the chocolate chips until melted and smooth
7. Blend in vanilla
8. Transfer mixture to a large mixing bowl and cool slightly
9. With wire whisk, beat in eggs one at a time
10. Blend in mashed banana, then flour mixture
11. Fold in remaining 1 / 2 cup chocolate chips and walnuts
12. Spread in pan
13. Bake about 45 minutes
14. Cool completely in pan
15. Lightly sift powdered sugar over top before cutting into bars<|endoftext|>
