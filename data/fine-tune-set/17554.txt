What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Buttered Rhumba Coffee

Ingredients:
• Ground coffee
• Ground nutmeg
• Butter flavor extract
• Rum extract

Steps:
1. Place ground coffee, nutmeg, and extracts in a plastic resealable bag
2. Seal and shake well to blend
3. To prepare, place the coffee mixture in a paper filter and pour 4 cups hot water through the filter
4. Sweeten as you wish<|endoftext|>
