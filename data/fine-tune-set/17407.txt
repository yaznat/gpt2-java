Ingredients:
• Active dry yeast
• Warm water
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Salt
• All-purpose flour
• Apple pie filling
• Egg
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl, dissolve the yeast in the warm water
2. Add butter, sugar, eggs and salt
3. Beating well
4. Stir in the flour and knead until a smooth, satiny dough is formed
5. Cover and refrigerate overnight
6. Cut dough into 3rds
7. Roll each portion out to form a 10x12 sheet
8. Divide sheet of dough into 3 equal strips
9. Spoon about 2 tbsp apple filling down the length of each strip
10. Fold dough over filling to seal filling inside
11. Braid the 3 strips together to make one loaf
12. Repeat for remaining 2 portions of dough
13. Place on greased cookie sheet
14. Cover and let rise about 1 to 1 1 / 2 hours or until puffy and almost doubled
15. Mix egg and milk
16. Brush mixture over loaves
17. Bake at 375 for 20 to 25 minutes or until golden<|endoftext|>
