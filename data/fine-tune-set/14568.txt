Ingredients:
• Water
• White rice
• Tea bag
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Dried basil
• Chili powder
• Garlic powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Soy sauce
• Lemon juice
• Butter

Steps:
1. Boil water, take off heat and add tea bag
2. Let steep
3. Add rice and all other ingredients except butter, bring back to a boil
4. Reduce heat, cover and simmer for 20 minutes
5. Remove from heat, stir in butter until completely melted<|endoftext|>
