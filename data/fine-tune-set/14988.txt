Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Butter
• Cheddar cheese
• Salsa
• Braggs liquid aminos

Steps:
1. Heat the butter or oil in a skillet
2. Add the rice, stir fry it for a time
3. Remove, put in a bowl add the rest of the ingredients, toss and devour!<|endoftext|>
