Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Roasted peanuts
• Salt
• Red bell pepper
• Green onion
• Green peas
• Snow peas
• Cooked long-grain brown rice
• Fresh cilantro
• Pineapple chunks in juice
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Unsalted dry roasted peanuts

Steps:
1. Place tofu between paper towels until barely moist
2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large nonstick skillet or stir-fry pan over medium-high heat
3. Add tofu, and cook 8 minutes or until golden
4. Sprinkle with salt
5. Remove tofu from pan
6. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in pan over medium-high heat
7. Add bell pepper and onions, and saut 2 minutes
8. Add peas, and saut 30 seconds
9. Stir in rice, and cook 2 minutes
10. Add tofu, 2 tablespoons cilantro, and pineapple
11. Cook 1 minute, stirring gently
12. Remove from heat
13. Stir in soy sauce and peanuts
14. Sprinkle with 2 tablespoons cilantro<|endoftext|>
