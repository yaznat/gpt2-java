What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Baked Ziti With Beef And Green Beans

Ingredients:
• Ziti pasta
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Diced tomatoes
• Nonfat beef broth
• Tomato paste
• Oregano
• Garlic cloves
• Frozen green beans
• Fat free mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to pasta directions
2. Omit oil and salt if required for your dietary requirements
3. Cook beef in nonstick skillet until brown, breaking up the meat while cooking
4. Drain meat and run under hot water
5. Wipe skillet with paper towels and put beef back into skillet
6. Add onions to beef and cook until onions are soft about 3-4 minutes
7. Stir as needed
8. Stir in tomatoes, broth, tomato paste, oregano and garlic
9. Simmer for 10 minutes
10. Mix mixture with pasta and green beans and half of the cheese
11. Sprinkle top with remaining cheese and bake uncovered at 350 for 20 to 25 minutes
12. To freeze: undercook pasta slightly
13. Freeze fully assembled
14. Fully thaw dish and let come to almost room temp before putting in 350 oven
15. Cook until heated through
16. Serving is approx 1
