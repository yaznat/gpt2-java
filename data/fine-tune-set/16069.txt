Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Button mushroom
• Jalapeno
• Fresh cilantro leaves
• Salt
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese
• Salsa

Steps:
1. In a medium skillet, heat the 2 tablespoons oil over medium heat
2. Add the mushrooms and chiles, stir well, and cover
3. Continue to stir every minute or so, replacing the cover each time, until the mushrooms have released a lot of juice, 4 to 5 minutes
4. Uncover, and let briskly simmer until all the liquid has evaporated, about 3 minutes
5. Taste and season with salt, usually about 1 / 4 teaspoon
6. Add the cilantro
7. Heat a well-seasoned or nonstick griddle or heavy skillet over medium heat
8. Turn the oven on to its lowest setting
9. One by one, make the quesadillas: lightly brush 1 side of each tortilla with oil, then lay it oiled side down on the hot griddle
10. Spread with about 1 / 4 cup of the cheese, leaving a 1 / 4-inch border all around
11. Spoon about 3 tablespoons of the mushroom mixture down the center of the cheese-covered tortilla
12. When the cheese begins to melt, place another tortilla on top and flip
13. Cook, flipping the quesadilla every minute or so, until the cheese is completely melted and the tortilla crisps, about 3-5 minutes in all
14. As each quesadilla is done, transfer it to a baking sheet and keep in a warm oven<|endoftext|>
