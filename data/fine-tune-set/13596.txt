Ingredients:
• Horseradish
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Garlic clove
• Cayenne
• Salt and pepper
• Salt
• Paprika
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Dried oregano
• Eye of round roast
• Vegetable oil
• Kaiser rolls
• Onion

Steps:
1. Sauce: whisk all ingredients together
2. Beef: combine all seasonings
3. Pat meat dry with paper towels and rub evenly over beef
4. Cover with saran wrap and let stand at room temp for at least 1 hour or refrigerated for up to 24 hours
5. Before cooking, unwrap meat and rub with oil
6. Charcoal grill: open bottom grill vents completely
7. Light a large chimney started filled with charcoal briquettes
8. When coals are hot, pour in an even layer over 1 / 2 grill, leaving other 1 / 2 empty
9. Set cooking grate in place, cover and heat grill until hot
10. Gas grill: turn all burners to high, cover and heat until hot
11. Clean and oil cooking grate
12. Place meat on the hotter part of the grill
13. Cook, , turning often, until evenly blackened on all sides and the center of meat is 95-100 degrees
14. 30 minutes
15. Transfer meat to a cutting board, tent with foil and let rest 30 minutes
16. Cut roast in 1 / 2 lengthwise, then slice against the grain as thinly as possible
17. Divide sliced meat equally among kaiser rolls, top with onion and tiger sauce and serve<|endoftext|>
