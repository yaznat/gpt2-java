Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>High Fiber Bean Tacos Burritos

Ingredients:
• Flax seed whole wheat tortillas
• Low-fat refried beans
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Lettuce
• Salsa
• Fat free sour cream

Steps:
1. Place 1 / 4 cup refried beans on each tortilla
2. Microwave 30 seconds each
3. Arrange 1 / 8 cup cheese on each tortilla and microwave another 30 seconds
4. Layer with lettuce, salsa and sour cream, fold and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
