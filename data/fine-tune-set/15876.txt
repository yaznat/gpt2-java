What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Cheesy Crab Burritos

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Imitation crabmeat
• Flour tortillas
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the cream cheese and cheddar cheese
3. Stir in the crab
4. Spoon down the center of each tortilla
5. Roll up tightly and place on an ungreased baking sheet
6. Bake for 20 minutes or until heated through
7. Serve with salsa<|endoftext|>
