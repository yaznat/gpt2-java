What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Cinnamon

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Boneless beef chuck
• White onion
• Celery ribs
• Green pepper
• Garlic clove
• Tomatoes with juice
• Tomato sauce
• Red wine
• Parsley
• Rosemary
• Thyme
• Oregano
• Cinnamon
• Red pepper flakes
• Carrots

Steps:
1. In a large pot heat oil
2. Add meat and sear on all sides
3. Remove and set aside
4. In pot pour off fat
5. Add onion, celery, green pepper, and garlic
6. Sautee about 3minutes
7. Add tomatoes with juice, tomato sauce, wine, rosemary, thyme, oregano, cinnamon and redpepper flakes
8. Bring to a boil
9. Add meat to simmering sauce, reduce heat and cover
10. Simmer low for 2 hours
11. Add carrots and simmer 30minutes longer
12. Serve<|endoftext|>
