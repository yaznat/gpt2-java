Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Marbled cheddar and swiss cheese
• Mushroom
• Bacon
• Honey mustard dressing

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. On each piece of chicken, carefully layer the mushrooms first and then the bacon
3. Cover and bake for 30-45 minutes or until chicken is cooked through
4. Remove from oven
5. Add cheese and bake for 15 minutes uncovered
6. Serve with a side of honey mustard salad dressing for a real treat!<|endoftext|>
