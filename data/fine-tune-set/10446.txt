Ingredients:

• Sugar
• Margarine
• Egg
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Dark molasses
• Baking soda
• Ginger
• Fresh ginger

Steps:
1. Mix everything and chill the dough until it's easy to handle
2. Roll into 1" balls
3. Roll in sugar
4. Place on ungreased cookie sheets
