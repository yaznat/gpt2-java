Can you give me a beans recipe?<|endoftext|>American Style Baked Beans

Ingredients:
• Baked beans
• Onion
• Molasses
• Coarse grain mustard
• Catsup
• Brown sugar
• Streaky bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 150 degrees celsius
2. Mix all ingredients in a baking dish
3. Bake uncovered for 2 hours, stirring occassionally to stop the top from caramelising too much<|endoftext|>
