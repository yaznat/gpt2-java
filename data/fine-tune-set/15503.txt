What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Basil Mozzarella Burgers

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Fine dry breadcrumbs
• Green bell peppers
• Ketchup
• Prepared horseradish
• Prepared mustard
• Salt and pepper
• Ground beef
• Smoked mozzarella cheese
• Whole wheat hamburger buns
• Fresh basil leaf
• Roasted red pepper
• Olive
• Olive oil
• Dried thyme
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. To make relish: add the relish ingredients to the container of a food processor
2. Cover and pulse until coarsely chopped
3. Cover and chill for 2-3 hours
4. To make burgers: stir together the onion, bread crumbs, and bell pepper in a mixing bowl
5. Add the ketchup, horseradish, mustard, salt and pepper to taste
6. Stir to mix
7. Add the ground beef
8. Stir to mix
9. Shape mixture into 4 patties
10. Grill patties over medium heat for 7 minutes
11. Turn and grill 7-11 more minutes or until done
12. Top patties with a slice of cheese during the last 2 minutes or grilling time
13. Serve patties on toasted whole wheat buns with red sweet pepper relish and fresh basil leaves<|endoftext|>
