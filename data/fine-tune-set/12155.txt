Ingredients:
• Apple butter
• Pumpkin
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground ginger
• Eggs
• Evaporated milk
• Unbaked pie shell

Steps:
1. Combine apple butter, pumpkin, sugar, salt & spices in bowl
2. Mix well
3. Stir in beaten eggs
4. Add milk slowly and mix well
5. Pour into unbaked pie shell
6. Bake in 425 oven 40 minutes or until set<|endoftext|>
