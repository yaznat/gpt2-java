What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Ingredient Pumpkin Spice Cupcakes

Ingredients:
• Duncan hines moist deluxe spice cake mix
• Pumpkin
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl for 2 minutes
3. Contents will be very thick
4. Line muffin tin with paper cups
5. Fill 2 / 3 full with batter
6. You may add a 1 / 4 cup of water to make it easier to stir
7. Bake 22 to 24 minutes
8. Optional: sprinkle tops with cinnamon sugar before placing in oven if you don't use frosting<|endoftext|>
