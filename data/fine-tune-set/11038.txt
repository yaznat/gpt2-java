Give me a sandwich recipe.<|endoftext|>Angelique S Veggie Sandwich For 1

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Mayonnaise
• Smoked gouda cheese
• Avocados
• Tomatoes
• Red onions
• Pepperoncini pepper
• Greens
• Black pepper
• Raspberry vinaigrette dressing

Steps:
1. Decide if you want your bread toasted first
2. Spread one slice of bread with mayonnaise
3. Arrange the greens on top
4. Place the remaining ingredients on the other slice of bread and finish by sprinkling lightly with the raspberry vinaigrette
5. Cut in half and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
