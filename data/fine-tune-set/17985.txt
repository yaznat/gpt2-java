Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Chocolate chips
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Spray a jelly roll pan with no-stick spray
2. Preheat oven to 350
3. Stir together dry ingredients
4. Set aside
5. In large bowl combine brown sugar and oil till combined well
6. Beat in eggs one at a time
7. Add vanilla and blend well
8. Add flour mixture, chips and nuts
9. Mix completely
10. Pour into prepared pan
11. Bake at 350 for 35-40 minutes
12. Test with toothpick
13. Careful not to over bake
14. Check at 35 minutes
15. Cool on wire rack
16. Cut into squares and when cool store airtight<|endoftext|>
