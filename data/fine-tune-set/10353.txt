What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Almond Joy Drop Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Flaked coconut
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 375
2. Grease cookie sheets
3. In a large bowl, cream together the butter, white sugar and brown sugar until smooth
4. Beat in the eggs, one at a time, then stir in the vanilla
5. Combine the flour, baking soda and salt
6. Stir into the creamed mixture until well blended
7. Finally, stir in the chocolate chips, coconut and almonds
8. Drop by heaping teaspoonfuls onto the prepared cookie sheets
9. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes in the preheated oven
10. Allow cookies to cool on baking sheet for 2 minutes before removing to a wire rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
