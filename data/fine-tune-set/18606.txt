Ingredients:
• Angel food cake
• Vanilla instant pudding mix
• Crushed pineapple
• Whipped topping

Steps:
1. Slice cake into 3 rings, set aside
2. Stir dry pudding mix with reserved pineapple juice
3. Fold in pineapple, then whipped topping
4. Frost each ring top as you rebuild cake
5. Frost outside of cake with remaining mixture
6. Chill for 8 hours<|endoftext|>
