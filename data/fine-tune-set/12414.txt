Ingredients:
• Lean bacon
• Celery
• Onion
• Potatoes
• Water
• Evaporated milk
• Worcestershire sauce
• Parsley
• Pepper
• Celery salt
• Bay leaf

Steps:
1. Chop bacon
2. Saute' bacon, celery and onion in a deep sauce pan
3. Add potatoes, water, bay leaf, salt, pepper and celery salt and bring to a boil
4. Reduce heat, cover and let simmer for 15 minutes or until potatoes are tender
5. Mash lightly, leave about 1 / 4 whole
6. Remove bay leaf and add milk, worcestershire sauce and parsley
7. Bring to a boil
8. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
