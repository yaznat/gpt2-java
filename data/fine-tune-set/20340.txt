Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Rice vermicelli
• Green beans
• Carrots
• Zucchini
• Shiitake mushrooms
• Fresh gingerroot
• Napa cabbage
• Scallions
• Fresh bean sprout
• Dark soy sauce
• Rice wine
• Sugar
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Half-fill a preheated wok or deep, heavy-bottom skillet with oil
2. Heat to 350
3. Add the noodles, in batches, and cook for 30 seconds, or until crisp and puffed up
4. Remove with tongs, then drain on paper towels and set aside
5. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil from the wok
6. Heat the remaining oil in the wok over high heat
7. Add the green beans and stir-fry for 2 minutes
8. Add the carrot and zucchini sticks, mushrooms, and ginger and stir-fry for an additional 2 minutes
9. Add the napa cabbage, scallions, and bean sprouts and stir-fry for an additional minute
10. Add the soy sauce, rice wine, and sugar, and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute
11. Add noodles and cilantro and toss well
12. Serve immediately
