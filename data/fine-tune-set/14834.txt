Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Basil Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Garlic
• Red chilies
• Fresh mushrooms
• Onion
• Cooked rice
• Green bell pepper
• Long beans
• Soy sauce
• Basil

Steps:
1. In a wok or skillet, heat oil until hot but not smoking
2. Add garlic and chilis and fry until garlic is light brown
3. Add mushrooms and onions and stir quickly
4. Add cooked rice
5. Stir thoroughly
6. Add long beans, peppers, and soy and stir thoroughly
7. At the very end, stir in basil leaves and immediately turn into a serving bowl<|endoftext|>
