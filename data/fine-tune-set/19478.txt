Ingredients:
• Potato
• Red chili powder
• Chat masala
• Coriander powder
• Onion
• Salt
• Tamarind pulp
• Oil
• Butter
• Mint leaves
• Coriander leaves
• Green chilies

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a pan and add onions fry till they soften do not brown
2. Now add coriander powder, chillies powder and tamrind pulp
3. After this add the potatoes, salt and chaat masala
4. Mix well
5. Add butter
6. Turn off the heat
7. And garnish it with greens that is coriander leaves, mint leaves and green chillies<|endoftext|>
