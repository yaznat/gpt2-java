Ingredients:
• Red onion
• Margarine
• Brown sugar
• Catsup
• Hot water
• Dijon mustard
• Maple syrup
• Fresh lemon juice
• Cooked chicken breasts
• French bread
• Provolone cheese

Steps:
1. Melt margarine over medium heat
2. Separate onion slices into rings and saut until softened
3. Stir in brown sugar and heat and stir until onions are glazed
4. Add catsup, water, mustard, and lemon juice
5. Let simmer for 20 minutes, then stir in diced chicken
6. Cover and set aside
7. Heat oven to 350
8. Cut french bread in half lengthwise and place cut side up on foil lined baking sheet
9. Divide barbecued chicken evenly on bread halves, spreading to edges
10. Place 4 slices provolone evenly on each half
11. Bake for 10 minutes at 350 until cheese is melted
12. Remove from oven and let rest for 5 minutes on wire cooling rack
13. Cut each half into 4 slices and serve<|endoftext|>
