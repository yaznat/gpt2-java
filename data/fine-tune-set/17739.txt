What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cranberry Wheat Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Granulated sugar
• Toasted wheat germ
• Baking powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Fat-free evaporated milk
• Apple juice
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Baking apples
• Dried sweetened cranberries
• Cinnamon sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 f
2. Grease or paper-line 12 muffin cups
3. Combine all-purpose flour, whole-wheat flour, granulated sugar, wheat germ, baking powder and cinnamon in medium bowl
4. Beat evaporated milk, apple juice, vegetable oil and egg in small bowl until blended
5. Add to flour mixture
6. Stir just until moistened
7. Fold in apple and cranberries
8. Spoon batter into prepared muffin cups, filling 3 / 4 full
9. Sprinkle with cinnamon-sugar
10. Bake for 14 to 16 minutes or until wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean
11. Cool in pan on wire rack for 2 minutes
12. Remove to wire rack to cool slightly
13. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
