What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Aussie Lamb Sandwich Souvlaki

Ingredients:
• Lamb steaks
• Teriyaki sauce
• Garlic
• Tabasco sauce
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Ground black pepper
• Lebanese bread
• Mesclun
• Capers
• Sour cream
• Red capsicum

Steps:
1. Place lamb strips, teriyaki sauce, garlic, tabasco sauce, oil and pepper in a small bowl
2. Mix well and marinate 30 minutes or so
3. Cook lamb on hot bbq plate turning occasionally until cooked as desired
4. Place lebanese bread on a plate and top with mesclun mix and capers
5. Put sour cream over top in three dollops and place capsicum strips to the side, followed by the cooked lamb strips over the middle
6. Roll up starting by folding in at the top and bottom a little followed by tightly rolling at the sides
7. Place under a sandwich press for 5 minutes or so to make the bread crispy
8. This is optional but tastes a lot better
9. Enjoy with an ice cold beer in the other hand!<|endoftext|>
