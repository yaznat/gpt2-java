Can you give me a casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Global Gourmet Taco Casserole

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Tomato sauce
• Taco sauce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Tabasco sauce
• Hot chili pepper
• Cornmeal
• Whole kernel corn
• Sliced ripe olives
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375 degrees
2. Brown ground beef and onion over medium heat
3. Drain
4. Stir in tomato sauce, taco or other sauce, salt, pepper, tabasco, chiles, and cornmeal, adjusting for"hot" taste preference and desired thickness
5. Stir in more cornmeal, if desired
6. Stir in corn and olives
7. Transfer mixture to casserole dish
8. Cover and bake for 30 to 40 minutes, or until bubbly and heated through
9. Top with cheese immediately on removing from oven so cheese melts
10. Garnish with corn chips just before serving to keep them crisp
11. Serve with a cool fresh vegetable such as cucumber slices, avocado, or lettuce leaves, if desired<|endoftext|>
