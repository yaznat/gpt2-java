What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Crunch Ice Cream Bowls

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Mini marshmallows
• Chocolate chips
• Peanut butter
• Vanilla
• Corn flakes

Steps:
1. Grease insides of 4 bowls and set aside
2. On medium heat, heat all but cornflakes until melted and smooth
3. Pour over cornflakes and mix until well coated
4. Divide among bowls and press firmly against bottom and sides
5. Cool completely
6. Remove from bowls, place on serving plate and fill with ice cream<|endoftext|>
