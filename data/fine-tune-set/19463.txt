What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Minute Cheese Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Cheddar cheese sauce
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground cayenne pepper
• Scallions
• Red potatoes
• Sharp cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400 degrees
2. Grease a 1 1 / 2 quart shallow casserole
3. Stir cheese sauce, nutmeg and cayenne until blended
4. Reserve 1 t of scallions for garnish
5. Stir remaining scallions and the potatoes into cheese sauce until well mixed
6. Spoon into casserole, sprinkle with cheese and cover with nonstick foil
7. Bake 30 minutes, removing foil after 15 minutes, or until potatoes are tender, mixture is bubbly and top is slightly browned
8. Sprinkle with reserved scallions and let rest 10 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
