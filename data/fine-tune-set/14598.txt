What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>20 Minute Spanish Rice

Ingredients:
• Chicken stock
• Long grain rice
• Taco seasoning
• Salsa
• Corn kernel
• Black beans

Steps:
1. Boil the chicken stock and taco seasoning in a medium pot
2. Once mixture comes to a boil add the rice, salsa, corn and beans (or peas if you prefer
3. Stir a bit
4. Turn heat to low and cover pot
5. Allow to cook on low, undisturbed, for 20 minutes
6. After 20 minutes are up, toss the rice a bit, and serve!<|endoftext|>
