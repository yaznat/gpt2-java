What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Chili Taco Stew

Ingredients:
• Canned chicken
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Rotel
• Chili beans
• Corn
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Salt
• Pace picante sauce
• Chicken broth

Steps:
1. Saute chicken, onion, garlic, and green pepper in cooking spray
2. Add remaining ingredients
3. Heat on stove until warm throughout
4. If desired, top stew with shredded cheese, sour cream, avocados, and crushed up tortilla chips
5. My kids love to eat this as a dip for their chips
6. I serve it with the baked version of tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
