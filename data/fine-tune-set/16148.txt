Give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Dessert Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Granny smith apples
• Dried sweetened cranberries
• Cinnamon sugar
• Lemon juice
• Butter
• 10-inch flour tortillas
• Cream cheese
• Powdered sugar
• Caramel sauce
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, add the first 4 ingredients
2. Toss to combine
3. In a big non-stick skillet, melt 1 tablespoon butter over medium heat
4. Add in apple mixture and saute / stir for 5 minutes or until tender
5. Transfer apple mixture to a plate or bowl and set aside
6. Clean skillet
7. Spread 1 tablespoon butter evenly on 1 side of each tortilla
8. In a small bowl, mix together the cream cheese and powdered sugar until smooth
9. Spread the cream cheese mixture even on the non-buttered side of each tortilla
10. Top with apple mixture
11. Fold each tortilla in half, over apple mixture
12. In a heated skillet over medium heat, cook tortillas for about 2 minutes on each side or until golden brown
13. Drizzle caramel sauce over each quesadilla and sprinkle with pecans<|endoftext|>
