Ingredients:
• Nori
• Shrimp
• Imitation crab sticks
• Carrot
• Cucumber
• Avocado
• Romaine lettuce
• Cooked rice
• Rice vinegar

Steps:
1. Cut sea sticks in half lengthwise
2. Cut cucumber lengthwise into narrow strips
3. Halve avocado, remove pit and slice into strips
4. Remove center ribs and shred lettuce
5. Mix one cup of water with the rice vinegar in a small bowl and set aside to use as hand vinegar, to rinse your hands and keep the rice from sticking to them
6. Place a piece of plastic wrap on a bamboo rolling mat
7. Place a piece of nori, rough side up on the plastic wrap
8. Take a handful of rice and spread it over half of the nori, leaving 1" on the far side of the nori clear
9. Repeat to cover the other half of the seaweed sheet
10. Arrange 5-6 shrimp in a row about 2" from the close edge
11. Add a row of sea sticks, then a row of shredded lettuce
12. On top of the shrimp, place slices of avocado and shredded carrots so the ingredients are piled like logs of wood
13. One third of each of the filling ingredients should be used for each roll
14. Pick up the mat from the near side and roll it up, pressing gently
15. Remove from rolling mat
16. Repeat steps to make two more rolls
17. Cut each roll into 8 slices
18. Serve with soy sauce, wasabi and pickled ginger<|endoftext|>
