What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Banana Pudding Beach Icebox Cake

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Heavy cream
• Pudding mix
• Powdered sugar
• Vanilla wafers
• Bananas
• Cream

Steps:
1. For the banana filling:
2. In a bowl, mix cream cheese, sugar, milk, and banana pudding mix
3. In another bowl mix, heavy cream, confectioners' sugar making whipped cream
4. Combine the 2 bowls, folding them together gently
5. Line the side of 9" springform cake pan with parchment paper
6. Make one layer with this mixture in springform cake pan
7. For the vanilla filling
8. In another bowl, combine cream cheese, sugar, vanilla extract, milk
9. Then in an additional bowl, whip together heavy cream and confectioners' sugar
10. Gently fold the two together
11. And add that mixture as a 2nd layer
12. Dip vanilla wafers in milk and add a 3rd layer with these cookies
13. Spread another layer of the cream cheese, sugar, milk, and banana pudding mix
14. Then add a layer of sliced bananas
15. Spread yet another layer of same pudding mix
16. Then spread a layer of the whipped cream
17. Crush the remaining vanilla wafers into fine pieces and sprinkle over the top of the pie
18. Freeze for 4 hours or more
19. Remove the pie ring carefully
20. Place chocolate sea shells along the outer rim of the cake
21. And insert several paper palm tree umbrellas into the center of the cake<|endoftext|>
