Ingredients:
• Honey
• White onion
• Lime, juice of
• Soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh jalapeno pepper
• Fresh ginger
• Fish steaks

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, combine honey, onion, lime juice, soy sauce, hoisin sauce, garlic, jalapeno, and ginger
2. Stir to mix well
3. Pour over fish and let marinate at least 2 hours in the refrigerator
4. Grill the fish for 5 minutes
5. Turn and cook another 5 minutes or until the flesh is flaky when tested with a fork<|endoftext|>
