Ingredients:
• Vanilla
• Chocolate chips
• Egg whites
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Set oven, yes you still have to turn the oven on, for now, to 400 degrees
2. Cover a cookie sheet with foil
3. Beat egg whites until stiff
4. Add sugar, 1 / 8th at a time, beat until crystals disappear
5. Add vanilla and mix 2 minutes
6. Stir in chocolate chips
7. Drop by teaspoons onto cookie sheet
8. Turn oven off
9. Leave cookies in oven for 2 hours
10. Store in air tight container after cool<|endoftext|>
