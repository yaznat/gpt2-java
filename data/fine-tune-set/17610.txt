What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>20 Minute Huckleberry Muffins

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Huckleberries
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sugar
• Butter
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f grease a standard 12-muffin pan or line with paper cups
2. Thoroughly mix the flour, baking powder, salt and huckleberries together in a large bowl
3. Set aside
4. In another bowl, whisk together eggs, milk or cream, sugar, butter, and vanilla
5. Add the wet ingredients to the dry and mix together with a few light strokes just until just blended
6. Do not overmix
7. The batter should not be smooth
8. Divide the batter among the muffin cups
9. Bake for 15 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in 1 or 2 of the muffins comes out clean
10. Let cool for a few minutes, and serve warm<|endoftext|>
