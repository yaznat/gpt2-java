Give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Faux Egg Salad Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Red onion
• Hellmann's light mayonnaise
• Light soy sauce
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Freeze tofu in its original packaging for 24 hours then thaw
2. Cube or crumble the tofu in medium bowl
3. Add all other ingredients and mix well
4. Soy mayonnaise can be substituted for the hellman's for a vegan salad<|endoftext|>
