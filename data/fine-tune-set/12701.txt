Please give me a recipe for steak.<|endoftext|>All Seasons Grilled Steak

Ingredients:
• Steak seasoning
• Balsamic vinegar
• Olive oil
• Ground mustard
• Boneless sirloin

Steps:
1. In small bowl, mix steak seasoning, vinegar, olive oil and mustard
2. Spread mixture on steak, or place steak in large resealable plastic bag, add seasoning mixture, and turn to coat well
3. Broil or grill over medium-high heat 6 to 8 minutes per side or until desired doneness<|endoftext|>
