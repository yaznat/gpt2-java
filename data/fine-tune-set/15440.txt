Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Bacon
• Onion
• Velveeta cheese
• Pizza crusts
• Garlic powder
• Pepper
• Seasoning salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Cook ground beef and onion together in skillet
3. Drain
4. Add cubed velveeta and cook till melted, then stir in the crumbled bacon
5. Lightly grease a rectangle cookie sheet
6. Press out pizza dough into roughly a 15 x 8 inch rectangle, spread evenly with meat mixture
7. Roll into log, starting at one of the long sides
8. Rearrange if necessary, so that seam side is down
9. Bake 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown
10. Cut diagonally into slices to serve<|endoftext|>
