Ingredients:
• Center-cut bacon
• Onions
• Water
• Mexican hot sauce
• Butter
• Sugar
• Cooking spray
• Whole wheat bread
• Eggs
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Arugula

Steps:
1. Cook bacon in a nonstick skillet over medium heat until crisp, about 8 minutes
2. Remove bacon from pan, reserving drippings
3. Drain on paper towels
4. Add onion, water, and hot sauce to drippings in pan
5. Cover and cook for 3 minutes
6. Stir in butter and sugar
7. Cover and cook for 3 minutes
8. Uncover and cook for 5 minutes or until golden brown, stirring frequently
9. Set aside
10. Keep warm
11. Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
12. Coat pan with cooking spray
13. Place bread in pan, and cook for 3 minutes on each side or until lightly browned
14. Set aside, and keep warm
15. Recoat pan with cooking spray
16. Crack eggs into pan, and cook for 2 minutes
17. Gently turn eggs, one at a time, cook 1 minute or until desired degree of doneness
18. Sprinkle evenly with salt and black pepper
19. Place one bread slice on each of two plates, arrange onion mixture evenly over bread
20. Place 1 egg, 2 bacon slices, and 1 / 2 cup arugula over each serving
21. Top with remaining bread slices
22. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
