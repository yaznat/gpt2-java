Ingredients:
• Vanilla wafers
• Miniature marshmallow
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Butter
• Pecans
• Walnuts
• Raisins
• Maraschino cherry
• Candied fruit
• Crushed pineapple
• Flaked coconut

Steps:
1. Warm the opened can of sweetened condensed milk in a saucepan of boiling water to make it easier to pour
2. Add the milk to the melted butter and marshmallows stirring to combine well
3. Combine the wet mixture with the remaining ingredients
4. Pack into a buttered dish or pan
5. Let set overnight
6. Keep in refrigerator
