Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>18 Carrot Cupcakes

Ingredients:
• Light brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Flour
• Baby carrots
• Unsalted butter
• Confectioners' sugar
• Salt
• Marshmallow cream
• Pure vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350
2. Line two 9-cup muffin pans with baking liners
3. Using an electric mixer, beat the brown sugar and eggs at high speed for 5 minutes
4. Add the oil and 2 tablespoons water
5. Beat at medium speed until blended
6. Mix in the pumpkin pie spice, baking powder and baking soda
7. Mix in the flour at low speed
8. Stir in the carrots
9. Fill each muffin cup halfway with batter and bake until golden, about 20 minutes
10. Let cool
11. Using the electric mixer, beat the butter and confectioners' sugar for 5 minutes
12. Add in the marshmallow cream and vanilla and mix until combined
13. Spread on the cupcakes<|endoftext|>
