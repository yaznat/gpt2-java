Give me a tea recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Mint Tea

Ingredients:
• Tea bags
• Fresh mint leaves
• Fresh lemon juice
• Boiling water
• Unsweetened apple juice
• Lemon slices
• Of fresh mint

Steps:
1. Combine tea bags, mint leaves, and lemon juice in a pitcher
2. Pour in boiling water and let stand 10 minutes
3. Remove and discard tea bags and mint leaves
4. Stir in apple juice, and serve over crushed ice
5. Garnish with lemon slices and mint sprigs<|endoftext|>
