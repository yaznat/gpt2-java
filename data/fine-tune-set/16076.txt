Can you give me a quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Cheeseburger Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Lean ground beef
• Swiss cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Seasoning salt
• Seasoned pepper
• White onion
• Ketchup
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. In a skillet, brown the ground beef, adding seasoned salt and pepper, until completely cooked
2. Drain off the fat and place the meat in a medium bowl
3. Pour in the 2 cheeses and onions
4. Mix thouroughly
5. Place an equal amount of the cheeseburger mixture on each tortilla
6. Fold each tortilla over to create a half circle and press down lightly
7. Coat a skillet or griddle with a little butter or oil and warm to medium heat
8. Lightly brown each quesadilla for 2-3 minutes per side until the cheese melts
9. Remove quesadillas from the pan and cut each into 3 wedges
10. Serve hot with your favorite cheeseburger toppings<|endoftext|>
