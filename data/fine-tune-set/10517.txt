Ingredients:
• Spaghettini
• Scallions
• Garlic cloves
• Cherry tomatoes
• Parmesan cheese
• Fresh basil leaves
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Olive oil
• Fresh mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Cook the pasta in a large pot of boiling salted water until al dente, tender but still firm to the bite, stirring often, about 8 minutes
2. Meanwhile, combine the next 7 ingredients in a food processor
3. Pulse just until the tomatoes are coarsely chopped
4. Drain the pasta, reserving some of the pasta water
5. Toss the pasta with the tomato mixture and fresh mozzarella in a large bowl
6. Add some of the reserved pasta water if the sauce looks dry
7. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
