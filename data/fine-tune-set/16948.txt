What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Blue Cheese Cocktail Crackers

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Butter
• Cayenne pepper
• Blue cheese
• All-purpose flour
• Crisp rice cereal
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. With a mixer on medium speed, cream together cream cheese and butter until light and fluffy
3. Add cayenne, blue cheese, flour, rice cereal, and bacon and beat on medium-low speed until well blended
4. The dough will be stiff
5. Roll 1-tablespoon portions into balls and place slightly apart on baking sheets
6. Flatten each ball with a fork
7. Bake until crackers are golden brown, 12 to 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
