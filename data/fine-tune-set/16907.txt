Ingredients:
• Avocados
• Lemon
• Baby shrimp
• Mayonnaise
• Heavy cream
• Tabasco sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Paprika
• Cucumber

Steps:
1. Halve avocados, remove pits, rub surface with lemon slice
2. Combine mayonnaise, cream, tabasco, worcestershire, salt and pepper
3. Fold in shrimp
4. Spoon into avocados
5. Sprinkle with paprika and chill
6. Garnish with cucumber and lemon slices
7. Serve chilled on bed of lettuce<|endoftext|>
