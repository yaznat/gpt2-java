Ingredients:
• Milk
• Sugar
• Egg yolks
• Cornstarch
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Put the milk over moderate heat in pan of suitable size
2. Combine the egg yolks, sugar and corn starch in a heavy-bottomed pan
3. When the milk boils, turn down the heat
4. Combine the milk with sugar / egg-yolk mixture bit by bit, making sure the mixture is completely dissolved in the milk
5. Now add the vanilla extract or other flavouring
6. Now turn on the heat and keep stirring until the mixture starts to boil again and then turn off the heat
7. I like to use a whisk, which helps to mix everything well and make the custard slightly fluffy
8. If you feel the custard is too fluid even after it has boiled you can consider adding a bit more corn starch, but be very sure to mix it with some milk first, and be sure to not let the custard continue boiling for too long
9. Pour the custard into serving dishes
10. The custard can be served while hot or after letting it cool down<|endoftext|>
