Ingredients:
• Semisweet chocolate
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Flour tortillas
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Sprinkle 1 tablespoon of chocolate, 1 / 2 tablespoon of sugar and 1 / 4 teaspoon cinnamon over half of each tortilla
2. Fold tortillas in half to cover ingredients
3. Set quesadillas in a dry skillet and cook over medium heat until speckled with brown spots on both sides, about 2 minutes per side
4. Sprinkle each with 1 / 2 teaspoon of powdered sugar: serve<|endoftext|>
