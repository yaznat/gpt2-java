What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Awesome Chocolate Chip Streusel Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Vanilla
• Nuts
• Mini chocolate chip
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease a tube pan
3. Mix together all streusel ingredients
4. Set aside
5. Sift together the first 4 ingredients
6. In a bowl cream the butter, sugar and eggs, until light and fluffy
7. Mix together the dry ingredients with the cream mixture alternately with the sour cream
8. Beat well
9. Add vanilla
10. Beat
11. Pour 1 / 2 of batter into prepared pan
12. Sprinkle with 1 / 2 of the streusel mix
13. Add the remaining cake batter over the streusel
14. Sprinkle with remaining streusel mix
15. Bake for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
