Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Sirloin tip roast
• All-purpose flour
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Small onion
• Carrots
• Baby onions
• Fresh garlic
• Beef broth
• Brandy
• Dry red wine
• White button mushrooms
• Dried thyme
• Brown sugar
• Tomato paste
• Salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees
2. In a large dutch oven over medium heat cook the bacon until crisp remove to paper towels
3. Season the flour with seasoning salt and pepper
4. Coat the beef in the flour mixture, then brown well in oil on all sides for about 6 minutes
5. Transfer to a large bowl
6. Add in small onions and carrots to the pot and saute until light brown
7. Add in chopped garlic and saute for 1 minute
8. Remove the carrots and onions to a bowl
9. Add in 1 cup broth and brandy to the pot
10. Boil until reduced to a glaze scraping up the browned bits with a wooden spoon
11. Return the beef and carrots, onions and bacon back to the pot
12. Add in wine, mushrooms, thyme, sugar, tomato paste and 1-1 / 2 cups beef broth
13. Bring to a boil, stirring occasionally
14. Cover the pot and place in oven
15. Cook for about 1-1 / 2 hours or until beef is tender
16. Remove the pot from oven and ladle the liquid from the pot into a medium saucepan
17. Boil the liquid until reduced to about 2-1 / 2 cups, or a little more
18. Season with salt and pepper
19. Pour the liquid back over the beef and veggies
20. Rewarm over low heat before serving
21. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
