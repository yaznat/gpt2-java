Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Bar B Q Beef

Ingredients:
• Boneless chuck roast
• Chili sauce
• Dill pickle

Steps:
1. Put all ingredients into a large roaster and bake, covered @ 250 degrees for 11 hours
2. Do nothing for the first 6 hours
3. After 6 hours, stir every hour to break apart
4. Not necessary to add any other seasonings, not even salt
5. Before serving, mash or break apart with fork
6. Serve on hamburger buns<|endoftext|>
