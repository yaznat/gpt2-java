What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Acapulco Rice

Ingredients:
• Tomato sauce
• Water
• Chili powder
• Minute rice
• Tortilla chips
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Combine tomato sauce water and chili powder in medium saucepan
2. Bring to a full boil
3. Stir in rice
4. Cover remove from heat and let stand for 10 mins
5. Sprinkle with tortilla chips and cheese<|endoftext|>
