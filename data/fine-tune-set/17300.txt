Ingredients:
• Beef tenderloin
• Bacon
• Butter
• Fresh rosemary
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Red wine

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 420 degrees f
2. Wrap each steak with a strip of bacon
3. Put the olive oil in a large pan and heat the pan until you can move the olive oil around easily
4. Put your steaks in the pan and if you hear a sizzling noise that is good if not take the steaks out and heat it longer
5. Cook the steaks for 1 min on each side
6. Place the rosemary sprigs, garlic, and butter in the pan with the steaks
7. Flip steaks and continue to cook them 1 minute on each side until they have cooked a total of 3 minutes on each side
8. Put the steaks in the oven for 8 mins
9. Remove steaks from the oven and let them rest for 2 minutes
10. Remove the rosemary sprigs and the garlic from the pan, leaving all of the liquid
11. Pour red wine into the liquid left over in the pan
12. Cook the juices and red wine over low heat until boiling
13. Add the juice from the meat resting pan into the mix of liquids left over in the pan and it makes an amazing sauce
14. Plate the steak and pour the sauce over it
15. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
