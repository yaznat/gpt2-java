What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>A Very Simple Sweet Potato Or Yam

Ingredients:
• Yams
• Liquid honey
• Butter
• Salt & fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Wash potato thoroughly
2. Cut off any dark marks on the skin and cut about a quarter inch into it completely around it lengthwise
3. Microwave for approximately 10 minutes on high or until fork tender
4. Remove to a microwaveable dish, slice it in half through the cut made earlier, score the inside meat
5. Drizzle the honey over the inside, spread 1 teaspoon on each side and spread 1 teaspoon of butter on each side
6. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
7. Place back in microwave and cook for 30 seconds
8. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
