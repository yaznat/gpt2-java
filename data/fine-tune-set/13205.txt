Give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>10 Out Of 10 Chicken Breast

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Bottled garlic
• Parmesan cheese
• Corn flakes
• Dry ranch dressing mix
• Chicken breast halves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Melt butter with the garlic in microwave or stove top
3. Mix cheese, corn flakes and dressing mix in a flat bottom bowl or in a large plastic bag
4. Dip chicken in butter / garlic mixture
5. Shake or dredge in the crumb mixture
6. Place on a rimmed cookie sheet that is lined with foil and sprayed with oil
7. Drizzle any remaining garlic butter over chicken
8. Bake for 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
