Ingredients:
• Corn
• Cream
• Fresh rosemary
• Butter
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Pre heat oven to 375
2. Cut kernals off of raw, husked corn
3. Scrape husks with back of knife blade to get all the starch! mix all ingredients together except for butter and transfer to small casserole dish
4. Cut butter into small squares and place on top of corn
5. Pop in oven for 20 min or until corn starts to turn brown<|endoftext|>
