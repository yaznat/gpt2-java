Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>Anytime Spinach Casserole

Ingredients:
• Spinach
• Eggs
• Milk
• Cheese
• Crackers
• Butter
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Thaw spinach in microwave or on stove top
2. In a bowl beat 6 eggs and add milk
3. Mix in 1 cup of your favorite cheese, we like co jack, mozzarella or a blend of the two
4. Next place thawed and drained spinach in casserole dish and pour egg / cheese mixture over the top
5. Do a light stir and then sprinkle crushed crackers and top with pats of butter and a light layer of cheese
6. Place in 350f oven until casserole is firm in the center
7. About 35-45 minutes depending on oven
8. Casserole will settle upon cooling
9. Flavor with salt and pepper, a dash of cayenne pepper or hot sauce
10. I have not tried variations of this dish because we like it as is but i think using pepper jack cheese or jack cheese with chilies would taste great
11. Yum!<|endoftext|>
