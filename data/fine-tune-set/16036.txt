Can you give me a quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Bevs Hot And Cheesy Tuna Quesadillas To Die For

Ingredients:
• Tuna in water
• Tomatoes
• Green onions
• Mozzarella cheese
• Dry mustard
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Put the ingredients on 1 / 2 of each tortilla
2. Divide the tuna between the 2 tortillas and sprinkle with the dry mustard
3. Divide the green onions and the tomato slices and put on top of the tuna sprinkle each with 1 / 2 cup cheese
4. Fold each tortilla over and grill on both sides until lightly toasted and crispy<|endoftext|>
