What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Black Bean And Roasted Corn Chipotle Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Yellow onion
• Chipotle chile in adobo
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Chili seasoning mix
• Liquid smoke
• Diced tomatoes
• Rotel
• Frozen corn
• Black beans
• Fresh cilantro leaves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Place corn on a cookie sheet and sprinkle lightly with olive oil
3. Bake until corn starts to brown, about ten minutes
4. In large skillet, over medium-high heat, combine ground turkey chopped onion, garlic, salt, and black pepper
5. Cook until turkey no longer appears pink
6. Add seasonings, chipotle and combine
7. Reduce heat to medium-low and add diced tomatoes, rotel, corn and black beans, stir occasionally
8. Cover and simmer for 35-45 minutes
9. Serve hot and garnish with fresh cilantro
10. For a milder chili only use half of a chipotle
11. This is also great served over rice<|endoftext|>
