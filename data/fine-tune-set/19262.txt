Give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Cinnamon Ice Cream Diet Version For Electric Ice

Ingredients:
• Egg yolk
• Equal sugar substitute
• Cinnamon
• 1% low-fat milk
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Lightly beat egg yolk
2. Add 1 / 2 cup of milk
3. Cook over low heat stirring frequently, until mixture thickens a bit
4. Whisk cooked mixture together with remaining ingredients
5. Cool and chill mixture for a few hours
6. Pour cold mixture into ice cream machine and process according to manufacturer's directions<|endoftext|>
