Give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>Apple Stuffed Pork Roast

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin
• Apples
• Raisins
• Prepared stuffing
• Apple juice
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. With a knife sharpener, poke a hole through the roast to make a tunnel
2. Then take a knife and cut through until the hole is wide enough to stuff
3. In a large bowl, mix together apple juice and brown sugar
4. Place roast in bowl and turn a few times
5. Let sit in fridge fat side up for about an hour
6. In the meantime, peel and dice apples
7. Start making stuffing, adding apples and raisins before adding the stuffing mix so they get soft
8. Let apple stuffing cool while the pork in in the fridge
9. Preheat oven to 350f
10. Line a roasting pan with heavy duty foil
11. Remove pork from fridge and pour some of the marinade into the foil lined pan
12. Just enough to coat the bottom
13. Discard the rest
14. Start stuffing the roast
15. It's easiest to use your hands
16. Make sure you stuff it way down so you don't forget the middle
17. Bake about 20 minutes per pound - 170 degrees using a meat thermometer
18. Remove from oven and let stand 10 minutes
19. Carve 1 1 / 2 inches wide<|endoftext|>
