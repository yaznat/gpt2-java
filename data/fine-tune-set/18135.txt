Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pancake

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Apple

Steps:
1. Combine the eggs, flour, 1 1 / 2 tablespoons sugar, the pinch of salt, the baking powder, and the milk in a blender and blend until smooth
2. Melt 3 tablespoons of the butter and add it, the vanilla, and the 1 / 8 teaspoon nutmeg to the batter and blend until mixed
3. Allow to stand at room temperature for 30 minutes
4. Place the remaining 2 tablespoons butter in your skillet or oven pancake pan and melt while you are preheating the oven to 400 degrees
5. Swirl the melted butter to coat the sides of the pan
6. Combine the 1 / 3 cup sugar with the 1 / 2 teaspoon of cinnamon and the pinch of nutmeg
7. Sprinkle half this sugar mixture over the melted butter in the pan
8. Evenly layer the apple slices on top
9. Sprinkle the rest of the sugar mixture over the apples
10. Bake this for 5 minutes or until the mixture is bubbly
11. Gently pour the batter over the apples
12. Bake for 15 minutes, reduce the oven temperature to 325 degrees and continue to bake for 10 more minutes
13. Serve at once with your choice of toppings<|endoftext|>
