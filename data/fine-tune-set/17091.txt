What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Creole Brandy Milk Punch Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Brandy
• Whole milk
• Powdered sugar
• Ice cubes
• Cracked ice
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. In a cocktail shaker, combine the brandy, milk, and sugar with 3 ice cubes, and shake until frothy, about 1 minute
2. Strain into a double-old fashioned glass with cracked ice
3. Sprinkle with the nutmeg and serve<|endoftext|>
