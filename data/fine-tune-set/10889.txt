Give me a salad recipe.<|endoftext|>After Easter Layered Salad

Ingredients:
• Mixed salad greens
• Plum tomatoes
• Baby dill pickles
• Hard-boiled eggs
• Cooked ham
• Red onion
• Peppercorn ranch dressing

Steps:
1. Place greens in bottom of large salad bowl
2. Layer tomatoes, pickles, eggs, ham and onion over greens in bowl
3. Spread dressing evenly over top of salad
4. Refrigerate 1 hour or until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
