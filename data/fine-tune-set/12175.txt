What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Crumble Pie

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Lemon juice
• Brown sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Butter
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Line 9 inch pie plate
2. Arrange apples in pastry, and sprinkle with lemon juice
3. Make crumble topping by blending sugar, flour, butter, and cinnamon together until fat is well distributed
4. Sprinkle over apples
5. Bake at 425f for 20 minutes
6. Reduce heat to 350f and bake until apples are tender, about 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
