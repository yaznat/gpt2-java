What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Blackened Fish With Salsa Verde Low Carb

Ingredients:
• Catfish fillets
• Cajun seasoning
• Canola oil
• Salsa verde
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Sprinkle fish on both sides with cajun seasoning
2. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium high heat
3. Add half of the fillets and cook until lightly browned, about three minutes
4. Turn and cook until just opaque, two to three minutes longer
5. Transfer to plate and repeat with remaining fillets
6. Meanwhile, combine salsa and cilantro
7. Serve the fish topped with this<|endoftext|>
