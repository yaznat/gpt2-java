Please give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>Aloo Achar Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Potato
• Sesame seeds
• Hot green chili peppers
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Fresh cilantro
• Lemons
• Salt
• Mustard oil
• Fenugreek seeds
• Lettuce leaf

Steps:
1. Boil potatoes until tender
2. Drain and set aside
3. Toast sesame seeds in a hot oven until fragrant and lightly colored
4. Cool then grind in food processor with hot chiles, garlic, ginger and cilantro
5. Add lemon juice and salt
6. Mix sesame seed paste with warm potatoes and toss lightly
7. Saut fenugreek seeds in oil
8. Pour over salad and mix lightly
9. Serve on a bed of lettuce leaves<|endoftext|>
