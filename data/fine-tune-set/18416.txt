Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Waffle Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Unsweetened chocolate square
• Flour

Steps:
1. In a bowl, cream butter and sugar
2. Beat in the egg and vanilla til light
3. Blend in melted chocolate
4. Add flour
5. Mix well
6. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls 1 inch apart on a preheated waffle iron
7. Bake for 1 minute
8. Remove to a wire rack to cool
9. Dust with icing sugar<|endoftext|>
