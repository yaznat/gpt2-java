Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Apple Walnut Pancakes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Egg whites
• Egg
• Milk
• Canola oil
• Apple
• Walnuts
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the flours, brown sugar, baking powder and salt
2. Combine the egg whites, egg, milk and oil
3. Add to dry ingredients just until moistened
4. Fold in apple and walnuts
5. Pour batter by 1 / 4 cupfuls onto a hot griddle coated with cooking spray
6. Turn when bubbles form on top
7. Cook until the second side is golden brown
8. Serve with syrup<|endoftext|>
