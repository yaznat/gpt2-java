Ingredients:
• Butter
• Unsweetened baking chocolate
• Sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Salt
• Flour
• Walnuts
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Pre-heat your oven to 375 degrees
2. Melt butter or margarine and chocolate in medium saucepan over low heat
3. Set aside to cool for five minutes
4. While you're letting the chocolate cool, stir sugar, egg, vanilla and salt together until well blended
5. Stir the cooled chocolate / butter or margarine mixture until nice and smooth
6. Then mix it in with the above sugar, etc
7. Mixture
8. Now stir in the flour and nuts
9. Shape mixture into 1-inch balls and place 2 inches apart on ungreased baking sheet
10. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes
11. While warm, roll in powdered sugar
12. Cool on wire racks
13. Once cool, roll in powdered sugar again!
14. Store in airtight container<|endoftext|>
