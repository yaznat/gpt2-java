Ingredients:
• Flour
• Self-rising flour
• Caster sugar
• Skim milk
• Vanilla essence
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Sift both flours in a bowl, add sugar
2. In a seperate bowl, whisk eggs, milk and vanilla essence
3. Mix wet into dry ingredients til well combined
4. Heat non stick pan and spray a little oil on base
5. Pour some mixture in pan, when small bubbles appear flip over
6. Spread with butter and either maple syrup or cinnamon sugar<|endoftext|>
