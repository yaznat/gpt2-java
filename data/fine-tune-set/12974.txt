Ingredients:
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Almonds
• Light butter
• Kosher salt
• Cod fish fillets
• All-purpose flour
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. In a small bowl, combine bread crumbs, almonds, salt and butter or margarine and mix well,
3. Coat fish with flour, shaking off excess
4. Spread mayonnaise evenly over fillets and cover evenly with the crumbs mixture, lightly pressing into the fish fillets
5. Place them skin down in a baking pan large enough to hold in a single layer,
6. Carefully pour water around the fish to about 1 / 8 inch depth and bake for about 15-20 minutes or until fish flakes when tested with a fork
7. This step helps to ensure that the fish cooks up moist and tender<|endoftext|>
