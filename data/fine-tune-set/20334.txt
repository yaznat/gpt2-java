Ingredients:
• Water
• Salt
• Ground red pepper
• Light coconut milk
• Jasmine rice
• Dark sesame oil
• Flank steak
• Onion
• Red bell pepper
• Bok choy
• Fresh cilantro
• Chili-garlic sauce
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Lime wedges

Steps:
1. To prepare rice, bring first 4 ingredients to a boil in a medium saucepan
2. Stir in rice
3. Cover, reduce heat, and simmer 15 minutes or until liquid is absorbed
4. To prepare stir-fry, heat 1 / 2 teaspoon oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
5. Add half of beef
6. Stir-fry 4 minutes or until done
7. Remove from pan
8. Cover and keep warm
9. Repeat procedure with remaining oil and beef
10. Add onion and bell pepper to pan
11. Stir-fry 5 minutes
12. Add bok choy
13. Stir-fry 3 minutes or until vegetables are tender
14. Add beef, cilantro, chili garlic sauce, 1 / 4 teaspoon salt, and black pepper
15. Cook 2 minutes or until thoroughly heated
16. Serve with rice and lime wedges<|endoftext|>
