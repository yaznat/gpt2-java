Ingredients:
• Baby carrots
• Butter-flavored cooking spray
• Mrs. dash garlic and herb seasoning
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Put baby carrots in medium or large ziploc steamer bag
2. Microwave for 6 minutes
3. Carefully open bag and add the butter sprays, seasoning mix and cheese
4. Close the bag and shake well to combine
5. Serve!<|endoftext|>
