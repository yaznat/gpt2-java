Give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>A Student S Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Bisquick baking mix
• Egg
• Water
• Sugar
• Butter
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. For this recipe, you will need a cup, fork, 1-cup measure, mixing spoon, tablespoon, teaspoon, 9-inch glass pie plate, and a knife
2. First you will want to crack your egg into a cup and beat it
3. In your pie plate, put 2 cups of bisquick, the egg, water, and 1 / 3cup sugar
4. Stir until mixture is smooth
5. With a knife, cut your butter or margarine into small pieces and drop it on to the top of the batter
6. Sprinkle the top with 2 tablespoons sugar and 1 teaspoon cinnamon
7. Cook, uncovered in your microwave for 10 minutes
8. Allow cake to cool for 10 minutes and serve<|endoftext|>
