What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Aunt Penny S Chicken And Stuffing Casserole

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Mushroom soup
• White wine
• Milk
• Pepperidge farm stuffing

Steps:
1. Combine soup, wine, and milk
2. Grease 9x13 pan
3. Place chicken and soup mix in pan
4. Top with stuffing
5. You can freeze at this point
6. Bake 350 degrees for 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
