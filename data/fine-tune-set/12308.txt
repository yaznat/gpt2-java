What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>7 Minute Vegetable Soup

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Garlic
• Tomato sauce
• Tomatoes
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Dried basil

Steps:
1. Spray a medium saucepan or large non-stick skillet with cooking spray and place over medium-high heat
2. Add onion and saute briefly
3. Add garlic and saute until onion and garlic are golden brown
4. Add tomato sauce and tomatoes
5. Chop tomatoes, coarsely with a spatula
6. Add vegetables
7. Season with basil
8. Heat until vegetables are cooked through
9. This soup may also be made in the microwave
10. Serve with a good whole grain bread or crusty rolls<|endoftext|>
