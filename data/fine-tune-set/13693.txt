Please give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>Andalusian Pork

Ingredients:
• Paprika
• Dried oregano
• Dried thyme
• Garlic clove
• Coarse sea salt
• Pork fillets
• Lemons

Steps:
1. Mix the paprika, oregano, thyme garlic and salt with the olive oil to make a thick paste
2. Spread the paste evenly over the pork, cover and marinate in the fridge, ideally overnight
3. Cut the meat into slices on the diagonal
4. Heat a griddle or large heavy frying pan until very hot
5. Cook the pork for 3-4 minutes on each side until cooked through
6. Serve at once with the lemon wedges<|endoftext|>
