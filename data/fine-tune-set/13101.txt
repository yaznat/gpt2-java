What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Beer Batter Fish

Ingredients:
• Beer
• Milk
• Flour
• Baking powder
• White sugar
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Mix beer and milk
2. Whisk in flour
3. Add remaining ingredients
4. Batter fish and put in deep fryer until golden brown
5. If too thick add more milk
6. But remember its supposed to be very thick!<|endoftext|>
