Ingredients:
• Fat free cream cheese
• Smart balance butter spread
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Egg substitute
• Fat-free buttermilk
• Vanilla
• Oat flour
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Blueberries
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 and grease a bundt pan
2. Beat cream cheese and butter together
3. Add sugar and beat until fluffy, then add vanilla
4. Add eggs and egg substitute 1 at a time, then buttermilk
5. Combine dry ingredients, adding nuts and berries to coat well with flour
6. Combine wet and dry ingredients
7. Spoon into the prepared pan and bake 1 hour or until done<|endoftext|>
