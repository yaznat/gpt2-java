What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Apple Bread Pudding Diabetic

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Brown sugar
• Water
• Nonfat milk
• Vanilla bean
• Fruit bread
• Eggs
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 160 degree celsius
2. Grease deep 1
3. 5 litre
4. Peel, core and quarter apples, cut each quarter into 3mm slices
5. Dissolve brown sugar in the water in medium frying pan over low heat, add apples
6. Simmer, uncovered, about 5 minutes or until tender, stirring occasionally
7. Combine milk and vanilla bean in medium saucepan
8. Bring to a boil
9. Remove from heat
10. Stand, covered, 5 minutes
11. Discard vanilla bean
12. Meanwhile cut bread slices into quarters
13. Arrange bread and apple in alternate layers in dish
14. Whisk eggs, cinnamon and nutmeg in medium bowl
15. Gradually whisk hot milk mixture into egg mixture
16. Pour egg mixture carefully over bread and apple
17. Place dish in a large baking dish
18. Add enough boiling water to baking dish to come halfway up side of pudding dish
19. Bake uncovered about 1 hour or until set
20. Serve with low fat ice cream or cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
