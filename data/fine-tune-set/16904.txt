What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Aussie Beachcomber Australian Mai Tai Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Spiced rum
• Cointreau liqueur
• Orgeat syrup
• Agave syrup
• Lime
• Mint
• Pineapple

Steps:
1. Precool a low-ball glass with ice
2. Add all the ingredients into a cocktail shaker including the mint
3. Shake vigorously and pour into the low-ball glass over ice
4. Serve with a cocktail spear of mulled pineapple or fresh pineapple chunks<|endoftext|>
