Give me a recipe for sushi.<|endoftext|>Low Carb Riceless Maki Sushi Rolls

Ingredients:
• Nori
• Carrots
• Zucchini
• Cauliflower
• Toasted sesame oil
• Mirin
• Toasted sesame seeds
• Smoked salmon

Steps:
1. Grate the carrots and zucchini but keep them separated
2. Mix the sesame oil, mirin and sesame seeds and drizzle 1 / 2 over the zucchini and 1 / 2 over the carrots
3. Toss until the veggies are coated
4. Set aside
5. Boil the cauliflower until very, very tender
6. Drain
7. Puree
8. Set aside
9. Place a sheet of nori on bamboo sushi roller mat or piece of plastic wrap
10. Place the grated carrot, zucchini, or pureed cauliflower on about 1 / 2 of the nori sheet
11. Then lay out fillings of choice on top of the vegetables
12. Once filled, roll into a tube, seal edges with a bit of water and then slice each roll into 6 slices
13. Suggestions for combinations: grated carrot w / marinated shiitaki mushrooms, grated zucchini and carrot w / crab and avocado, pureed cauliflower w / salmon, grated carrot w / shrimps, zucchini, avocado and crab, cauliflower puree w / shiitaki mushroom and grated carrot, zucchini w / smoked salmon and green onion<|endoftext|>
