What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken And Broccoli Thai Curry

Ingredients:
• Green curry paste
• Coconut milk
• Fresh basil
• Bamboo shoot
• Fish sauce
• Brown sugar
• Chicken broth
• Chicken breast
• Fresh broccoli florets

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan combine curry paste and coconut milk and heat
2. Just before boiling, reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes
3. Add remaining ingredients and simmer for 10-15 minutes
4. Serve over freshly cooked rice<|endoftext|>
