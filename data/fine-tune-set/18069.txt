Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Mounds

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Light corn syrup
• Egg
• Unsweetened chocolate squares
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl, cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy
2. Add corn syrup and egg
3. Beat well
4. Stir in chocolate and vanilla
5. Combine the flour, baking powder and salt
6. Add to chocolate mixture
7. Beat well
8. Stir in nuts
9. Drop by tablespoonfuls, 2 inches apart onto greased baking sheets
10. Bake at 350 degrees, for 10-12 minutes or until edges are firm
11. Remove to wire racks to cool completely<|endoftext|>
