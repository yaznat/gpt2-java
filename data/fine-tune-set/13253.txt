What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>4 Cheese Chicken And Bacon Tortellini

Ingredients:
• Four cheese pasta sauce
• Cheese tortellini
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Bacon
• Green onion
• Mushroom
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Fry the bacon till crunchy so it can be crumbled into bacon bits
2. Drain of the bacon fat and cook the boneless skinless chicken breasts in the same pan, add seasonings to taste
3. I like to add a bit of garlic powder and paprika
4. When cool, crumble the bacon and cut the chicken into bite size pieces
5. You can put this in the fridge till later in the day or use right away
6. Put on a large pot of water to boil when it has reached the boiling point add the tortellini, while cooking chop up the green onion and fresh mushrooms
7. Once tortellini is cooked drain off the water, add 2 packages 4 cheese pasta sauce, the cooked chicken, bacon bits, green onion and mushrooms, simmer on low heat for approx 20 min or until the sauce is warmed through
8. You will need to stir often so the pasta does not stick to the bottom
9. Serve hot, this recipe will make 4-6 good size portions<|endoftext|>
