Ingredients:
• Small shell pasta
• Asparagus
• Red bell pepper
• Fresh mushrooms
• Parsley
• Slivered almonds
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Anchovy fillets
• Dijon mustard
• Salt and pepper
• Worcestershire sauce
• Egg yolk
• Lemon juice
• White wine vinegar
• Olive oil
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Boil pasta in water until firm-tender drain, and rinse with cold water
2. Set aside
3. Steam asparagus until crisp-tender
4. Drain and plunge into very cold or ice water
5. Drain and pat dry with a paper towel
6. Place all dressing ingredients in a blender, and blend until well mixed
7. Place the veggies and the cooked pasta shells in a large bowl
8. Toss to combine
9. Pour the dressing over
10. Toss well to coat
11. Add in slivered almonds and mix
12. Chill in the fridge for a minimum of 2 hours before serving<|endoftext|>
