Ingredients:
• Pinto beans
• Lean hamburger
• Breakfast sausage
• Yellow onions
• Tomato sauce
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Salt
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Black pepper
• Sugar
• Garlic powder
• Oregano

Steps:
1. Brown hamburger, sausage, and onion together in 6 quart saucepan
2. Add pinto beans, add can of tomato sauce and add enough water to rinse can
3. Add beef bouillon granules
4. If using bouillon cubes, dissolve in 1 / 4 cup water then add to the pan
5. Add salt, black pepper, garlic powder, oregano, cumin, chili powder and sugar
6. Blend thoroughly
7. Bring to boil, reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes
8. Serve<|endoftext|>
