What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Baked Penne With Ground Beef And Tomato Sauce

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Cooked penne
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Oregano
• Dried basil
• Dried chili pepper flakes
• Fresh garlic
• Tomato paste
• Dry red wine
• Plum tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Bay leaf
• Salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. For the sauce
2. Heat oil in a large pot over medium high heat
3. Add in onion, bell pepper, oregano, basil and chili flakes saut for about 5 minutes
4. Add in the tomato paste and stir for about 1 minute
5. Add in all remaining ingredients
6. Bring to a boil and simmer on low heat for about 1-1 / 2 hours
7. Season with salt and pepper then remove the bay leaf
8. At this point you can cool then refrigerate for up to 2 days
9. Set oven to 350 degrees
10. Grease a medium casserole dish
11. In a large skillet cook the ground beef until well browned, breaking up with a spoon until crumbled
12. Drain fat
13. To the skillet add in about 5 cups prepared tomato sauce cook for about 15-20 minutes on medium-low heat
14. Remove from heat and add in 1-1 / 2 cups mozzarella cheese
15. Stir with wooden spoon to combine
16. Add in the cooked penne pasta
17. Toss to combine with sauce and cheese
18. Transfer to baking dish
19. Bake for about 20 minutes
20. Remove from oven and sprinkle with parmesan cheese then about 1 / 2 cup mozzarella cheese, return to oven and cook 5-8 minutes more or until the cheese is melted
21. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
