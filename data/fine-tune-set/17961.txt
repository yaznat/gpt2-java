Ingredients:
• White chocolate chips
• Brown sugar
• Low-fat biscuit mix
• Almonds
• Butter
• Egg substitute
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degree f
2. Grease an 8x8x2-inch baking pan
3. In a large mixing bowl mix white baking pieces, brown sugar, packaged biscuit mix, and almonds
4. Melt butter
5. Add to cookie mix along with egg and vanilla
6. Stir until combined
7. Spread into prepared pan
8. Bake about 25 minutes or until golden brown around edges and center is almost set
9. Cool in pan on a wire rack
10. Cut into bars
11. Gift packaging directions: layer in a 1-quart glass jar or canister the following ingredients: white baking pieces, half the brown sugar, half the biscuit mix, remaining brown sugar, remaining biscuit mix, and almonds
12. Tap jar gently on the counter to settle each layer before adding the next
13. Cover jar and store at room temperature up to 1 month, or attach baking directions and give as a gift<|endoftext|>
