Ingredients:
• Slivered almonds
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Butter
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• Almond flavored liqueur
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Spread almonds on baking sheet, and bake, stirring frequently, until golden brown
3. Remove almonds from pan
4. Set aside and let cool
5. Turn oven up to 375f
6. In a small bowl, sift together flour, baking soda, and salt
7. In a large bowl, beat butter, sugar, and brown sugar at medium speed of electric mixer until light and fluffy
8. Beat in egg until well blended
9. Then beat in liquer
10. Gradually add flour mixture, and beat at low speed until well mixed
11. Stir in chocolate and almonds with a spoon
12. Drop rounded teaspoonfuls of dough 2" apart on an ungreased cookie sheet
13. Bake until edges are golden brown
14. Let cookies cool on cookie sheets for 2 minutes
15. Remove cookies to wire rack and let cool completely<|endoftext|>
