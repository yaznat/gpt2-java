Give me a recipe for pudding.<|endoftext|>Banana Peanut Butter Ice Cream Or Pudding Vegan

Ingredients:
• Rice milk
• White sugar
• Cornstarch
• Peanut butter
• Vanilla extract
• Coconut flavoring
• Bananas

Steps:
1. Bring 3 cups soy or rice milk and the sugar to a simmer over low heat in medium saucepan, stirring frequently
2. While heating this mixture, mix the remaining milk with the cornstarch until all the lumps are dissolved
3. Once mixture on stove comes to a simmer, add in peanut butter
4. Stir until dissolved
5. Then add cornstarch mixture
6. Continue heating mixture over medium heat, stirring constantly, until it thickens
7. Remove from heat
8. Stir in the vanilla and coconut flavorings
9. Transfer to 8 x 8 pan and cool completely
10. If eating as pudding, stir in bananas and enjoy
11. For ice cream, pour into ice cream machine and freeze according to manufacturer's instructions
12. Once ice cream is firming up, pour in bananas and continue freezing
13. For harder ice cream, freeze in the freezer before eating<|endoftext|>
