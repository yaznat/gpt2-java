Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cream cheese
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Milk
• Fresh lemon zest
• Heavy cream
• Graham crackers

Steps:
1. In a bowl, beat the sugar and cream cheese until smooth and creamy
2. Beat in the egg and vanilla
3. Set aside
4. Bring the milk to a boil in a medium saucepan
5. Slowly beat the hot milk into the cheese mixture
6. Pour the entire mixture back into the pan and place over low heat
7. Stir constantly with a whisk or wooden spoon until the custard thickens slightly
8. Remove pan from heat and pour the hot custard through a strainer into a large clean bowl
9. Let the custard cool slightly, then stir in the lemon zest and cream
10. Cover and refrigerate until cold or overnight
11. Stir the chilled custard
12. Freeze in 1 or 2 batches in an ice cream maker according to the manufacturers directions, adding the crumbled graham crackers to the machine when the ice cream is semifrozen
13. Allow the machine to mix in the crackers
14. When finished, the ice cream will be soft but ready to eat
15. For firmer ice cream, transfer mixture to a freezer-safe container and freeze several hours
16. White chocolate cheesecake ice cream: omit the lemon zest
17. Add cup melted white chocolate to the custard along with the vanilla
18. Proceed as directed
19. Oreo cheesecake ice cream: omit the lemon zest
20. Substitute crumbled oreos for the graham crackers
21. Proceed as directed<|endoftext|>
