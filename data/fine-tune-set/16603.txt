Can you give me a smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Rum Raisin Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Raisins
• Dark rum
• Banana
• Nonfat vanilla yogurt
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Plump the raisins by soaking them in the rum for at least 30 minutes, and as much as overnight
2. Put all of the ingredients in a blender or food processor and puree
3. Divide between two tall glasses and serve at once<|endoftext|>
