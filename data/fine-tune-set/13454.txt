What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazing Beef N Peppers

Ingredients:
• Boneless beef sirloin
• Cornstarch
• Beef broth
• Soy sauce
• Garlic powder
• Green peppers
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Slice beef into thin strips
2. Slice peppers into thin strips
3. Stir fry beef in "nonstick" skillet, or wok, until browned and juices evaporate
4. In seperate bowl, mix soy, broth, cornstarch, and garlic
5. Mix constantly
6. Add peppers to wok
7. Add cornstarch mixture
8. Stir--cook until mixture boils and thickens, and peppers become soft
9. Serve over cooked rice<|endoftext|>
