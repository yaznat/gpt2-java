Ingredients:
• Passion fruit
• Peach schnapps
• Vodka
• Cranberry juice
• Ice cube

Steps:
1. Strain passionfruit pulp to remove seeds
2. Place half of each of the schnapps, vodka, cranberry juice, passionfruit juice and ice into a cocktail shaker
3. Cover and shake well
4. Strain mixture and pour into 2 glasses
