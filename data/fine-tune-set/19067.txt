What's a recipe for ice cream?<|endoftext|>Avocado Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Avocados
• Fresh lemon juice
• Whole milk
• Sugar
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Peel and pit the avocados
2. Add the avocados, lemon juice, milk, and sugar to a blender and puree
3. Transfer the mixture to a medium mixing bowl, add the heavy cream and whisk to combine
4. Place the mixture into the refrigerator and chill until it reaches 40 degrees f or below, approximately 4 to 6 hours
5. Process the mixture in an ice cream maker according to manufacturer's directions
6. However, this mixture sets up very fast, so count on it taking only 5 to 10 minutes to process
7. For soft ice cream, serve immediately
8. If desired, place in freezer for 3 to 4 hours for firmer texture<|endoftext|>
