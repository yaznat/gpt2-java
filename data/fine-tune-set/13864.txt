Give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Tis An Irish Lamb Stew

Ingredients:
• Boneless lamb shoulder
• Flour
• Baby potatoes
• Baby carrots
• Parsnips
• Sweet onion
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Pepper
• Lamb broth
• Green peas
• Cabbage
• Fresh thyme
• Fresh parsley
• Whiskey

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f
2. In a large bowl gently toss the lamb chunks with the flour to coat
3. In a large dutch oven with a tight fitting lid, arrange first the lamb, followed by half potatoes, carrots, parsnips, onion and garlic in layers
4. Finish the top layer with the remaining potatoes
5. Season each layer with salt and pepper
6. Add broth, cover pot
7. Cook 2 to 2 1 / 2 hours until meat is tender and sauce thickened
8. Do not allow stew to dry out, adding stock as necessary!
9. The last 15 minutes of cooking, add the green peas and cabbage
10. Watch carefully, being sure that cabbage doesn't cook too long and become slimy
11. Just before serving, garnish top of stew with the thyme and parsley
12. Fyi: for a thicker sauce, in a small bowl combine 2 tablespoons of arrowroot or cornstarch with 2 tablespoons of stock or water
13. Whisk, breaking up any lumps and stir into the casserole, incorporating well<|endoftext|>
