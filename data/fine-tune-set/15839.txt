Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Brunch Egg Burritos

Ingredients:
• Hash browns
• Butter
• Eggs
• Milk
• Green chilies
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Bacon
• Monterey jack cheese
• Salsa
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook the potatoes in 2 tbs butter over medium heat for 6-7 minutes or until golden brown, stirring occasionally
2. In a small bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, chilies, and seasonings
3. In another large skillet, heat remaining butter until hot
4. Add egg mixture
5. Cook and stir over medium heat until eggs are completely set
6. Layer 1 / 3 cup potatoes, about 1 / 2 cup egg mixture, 1 / 4 cup bacon, 1 / 2 cup cheese and 1 / 4 cup salsa off center of each tortilla
7. Fold sides and ends over filling and rool up
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
