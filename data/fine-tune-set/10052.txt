What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>After Thanksgiving Turkey Chili

Ingredients:
• Cooked turkey
• Chili seasoning mix
• Crushed tomatoes
• Chili beans
• Onion
• Sweet pepper
• Olive oil
• Water
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In large boiler, saute onions and peppers in olive oil
2. Add chili seasoning, tomatoes, beans, turkey and water
3. Bring to a slow boil, reduce heat and simmer on very low temperature for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
