Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>All In One Corn And Beef Dish

Ingredients:
• Light olive oil
• Garlic
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Ground sirloin
• No-salt-added diced tomatoes
• Whole kernel corn
• Salt substitute
• Chili powder
• Macaroni

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in dutch oven on medium-high heat
2. Saut the garlic, onion and pepper until the onion is translucent
3. Add the ground beef, brown and crumble
4. Add the tomatoes, corn, salt, and chili powder
5. Bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for about an hour
7. Serve in large warmed soup bowls over hot, cooked macaroni<|endoftext|>
