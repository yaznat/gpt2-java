Can you give me a quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Hawaiian Appetizer Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Cooking spray
• Canadian bacon
• Crushed pineapple
• Mexican blend cheese
• Mango salsa

Steps:
1. Place oven rack to lowest position
2. Heat oven to 400f
3. Spray 1 side of 4 tortillas with cooking spray
4. On large cookie sheet, place tortillas, overlapping in center so tortillas do not hand over edge of sheet
5. Top evenly with bacon, pineapple, cheese and remaining tortillas
6. Spray tops of tortillas with cooking spray
7. Place another cookie sheet on top of tortillas
8. Press down
9. Bake on bottom oven rack 8-10 minutes or until bottom tortillas are golden brown
10. Turn cookie sheets and quesadillas over
11. Bake about 5 minutes longer of until bottoms are golden brown and cheese is melted
12. Cut into 8 small wedges
13. Serve warm with salsa<|endoftext|>
