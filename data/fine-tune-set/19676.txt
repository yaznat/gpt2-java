What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Ancho Chile Fries

Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Olive oil
• Dried ancho chile powder
• Sugar
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
2. Cut potatoes in half lengthwise, then cut into 1 / 4-inch-thick slabs
3. Cut slabs lengthwise into strips, gently pat strips dry with paper toweling and place on a greased foil-lined baking sheet
4. In a small bowl, combine oil, chile powder, sugar, garlic, salt and pepper
5. Pour mixture over the potatoes, and gently toss to combine using your hands
6. Bake about 30 minutes until tender and golden brown, carefully turning once halfway through<|endoftext|>
