What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Bbq Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Mushroom soup
• Catsup
• Worcestershire sauce
• Onion

Steps:
1. Mix the soup, catsup, worcestershire sauce and onions together and pour over the chops
2. Bake 1 hour at 325 degrees or until the chops are tender
3. You may prefer thicker chops- just bake longer<|endoftext|>
