Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Light soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Sesame oil
• Sesame seeds
• Cornstarch
• Gingerroot
• Garlic
• Red pepper flakes
• Boneless chicken
• Firm tofu
• Snow peas
• Spaghetti
• Green onion

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, whisk together first nine ingredients and set aside
2. In large skillet over medium-high heat saute chicken in a small amount of olive oil until meat is cooked through or tofu is lightly browned
3. Remove from skillet and set aside
4. Add snow peas or sugar snap peas to skillet
5. Add soy sauce mixture and bring to a boil, stirring frequently
6. Reduce heat and simmer until sauce thickens, approximately 3 minutes
7. Mix in cooked spaghetti and green onions
8. Top with chicken / tofu and serve immediately
9. Variation: add other vegetables according to the length of time needed to cook until crisp-tender
10. Add first: thinly sliced carrots
11. Next: sliced, peeled broccoli stems, onions, bell pepper strips, asparagus, chopped swiss chard stems, cabbage
12. Next: broccoli florets, along with soy sauce mixture
13. Next: sliced mushrooms and summer squash
14. Last: fresh greens like spinach
15. You can use any combination of the vegetables listed above<|endoftext|>
