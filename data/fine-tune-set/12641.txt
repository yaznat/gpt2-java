What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Cheddar Dumplings

Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Vegetable oil
• Onion salt
• Garlic salt
• Pepper
• Water
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Carrots
• Onion
• Green beans
• Biscuit mix
• Cheddar cheese
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a dutch oven, heat oil and brown meat on all sides
2. Add onion salt, pepper, garlic salt, water and bouillon
3. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer, covered, about 1 hour
4. Add carrots and onion
5. Simmer, covered, until vegetables are tender
6. Stir in green beans
7. For dumplings, combine biscuit mix and cheese
8. Stir in enough milk to form a soft dough
9. Drop by tablespoonful into bubbling stew
10. Cover and simmer 12 minutes or until dumplings are done
11. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
