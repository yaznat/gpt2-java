Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>Anise Rye Bread

Ingredients:
• Water
• Caraway seed
• Anise seed
• Molasses
• Brown sugar
• Shortening
• Yeast
• Flour
• Salt
• Rye flour

Steps:
1. Boil water, caraway and anise seeds for 5 minutes
2. When warm add molasses, brown sugar, and shortening
3. Add yeast
4. Mix in flour and salt
5. Let rise 1-1 / 2 hours in a warm place
6. Add rye flour gradually
7. If sticky add more flour
8. Rye soaks up water slower than regular flour so hold off a scooch
9. Let rise again
10. Form into 2 loaves
11. Let rise
12. Bake at 375 degrees fahrenheit for 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
