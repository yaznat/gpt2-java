Ingredients:
• Pumpkin puree
• Vanilla ice cream
• Milk
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground mace
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. In a blender or food processor, blend the pumpkin, ice cream, milk, cinnamon, nutmeg, and mace in batches
2. Pour into individual cups
3. Top with whipped cream and a sprinkle of nutmeg<|endoftext|>
