Ingredients:
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Evaporated milk
• Vanilla extract
• Water

Steps:
1. Thoroughly combine two cans of milk, use water to rinse out the cans and add vanilla extract
2. Serve with your preferred brew
3. Refrigerate if you drink a lot of coffee in your house or if you have company
4. Or freeze in ice cube containers for individual use<|endoftext|>
