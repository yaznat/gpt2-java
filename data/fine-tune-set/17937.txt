Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Berry Patch Brownie Pizza

Ingredients:
• Fudge brownie mix
• Almonds
• Cream cheese
• Granulated sugar
• Vanilla
• Frozen whipped topping
• Fresh strawberries
• Raspberries
• Fresh blueberries
• Lemon
• Powdered sugar
• Strawberry

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Lightly oil a 14 inch pizza pan and set aside
3. Prepare brownie mix according to package directions
4. Stir in almonds and spread batter onto bottom of prepared pan
5. Bake 18 to 20 minutes or until set
6. Cool completely
7. In a large bowl, combine the cream cheese, sugar and vanilla
8. Mix until smooth
9. Fold in whipped topping
10. Chop enough strawberries to measure 1 / 2 cup and gently stir into cream cheese mixture
11. Spread strawberry mixture evenly onto brownie
12. Slice the remaining strawberries and arrange on brownie
13. Top with raspberries and blueberries
14. Sprinkle with lemon zest
15. Sift powdered sugar over top and garnish with strawberry fan<|endoftext|>
