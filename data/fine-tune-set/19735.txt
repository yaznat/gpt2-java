What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Cajun Fries With Dipping Sauce

Ingredients:
• White potatoes
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Cajun seasoning
• Low-fat sour cream
• Green onion
• Red wine vinegar

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 425
2. Place first 4 ingredients in a ziplock bag
3. Seal and shake to coat potatoes
4. Place potatoes on a baking sheet covered with cooking spray
5. Bake 30 minutes or until tender and golden brown
6. Dressing ~ combine remaining ingredients and serve with fries<|endoftext|>
