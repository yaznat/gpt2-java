What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>George Washington S Rice Waffles

Ingredients:
• Sifted flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Egg yolks
• Milk
• Butter
• Cooked rice
• Egg whites
• Honey
• Maple syrup
• Caraway seed
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Sift the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt
2. Stir in the combined egg yolks, milk and butter
3. Beat just until smooth
4. Fold in rice and egg whites that have been beaten stiff but not dry
5. Bake in hot waffle iron
6. Honey maple syrup:
7. Combine honey, maple syrup, caraway seed, cinnamon and butter
8. Heat to boiling
9. Serve over waffles
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
