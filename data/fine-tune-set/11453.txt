Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Poppy Seed Bread

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Poppy seeds
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Butter flavoring

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl
2. Beat 2 minutes at medium speed
3. Spoon batter into 2 greased and floured loaf pans
4. Bake at 350f for 1 hour or until test done with toothpick
5. Cool in pans for 10 minutes, remove from pans and cool completely<|endoftext|>
