What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Awesome Dill Chicken Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Rotini pasta
• Cooked chicken
• Celery
• Onion
• Frozen peas
• Ranch dressing mix
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• Milk
• Fresh dill
• Garlic salt

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine pasta, chicken, celery, onion and peas and mix together
2. Combine dressing ingredients and whisk until smooth
3. Pour over salad and toss to coat
4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours for flavors to develop<|endoftext|>
