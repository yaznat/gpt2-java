Ingredients:
• Low-carb tortillas
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Butter
• Fat free sour cream
• Sugar-free salsa
• Egg

Steps:
1. Melt one teaspoon of butter in a skillet and add tortilla
2. Place shredded cheese on one half of tortilla and when tortilla softens, fold top half over it
3. Add the other teaspoon of butter to the pan and flip the tortilla with your spatula, just like a grilled cheese
4. When it browns, serve it with sour cream and salsa on top
5. For breakfast, top tortilla with a fried egg and a little more salsa if you like
6. Otherwise just serve it plain<|endoftext|>
