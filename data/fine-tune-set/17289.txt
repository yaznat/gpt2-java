Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Reduced-fat mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Cracked black pepper
• Bread
• Smoked salmon
• English cucumbers

Steps:
1. Combine mayonnaise, lemon juice and pepper in a small bowl
2. Thinly spread on bread and top with salmon, avocado and cucumber
3. Serve with your favorite tea beverage<|endoftext|>
