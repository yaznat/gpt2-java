Give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Appetizer Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Prepared mustard
• Poppy seeds
• Worcestershire sauce
• Onion
• Dinner rolls
• Cooked ham
• Swiss cheese

Steps:
1. Melt butter or margarine and mix with mustard, poppy seeds, worcestershire sauce, and onion
2. Open rolls and spread mixture on both sides
3. Top each roll with ham and cheese
4. Close rolls
5. Heat in 350 oven until cheese is melted
6. Cut sandwiches into small pieces<|endoftext|>
