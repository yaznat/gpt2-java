Ingredients:
• Garbanzo beans
• Garlic
• Of fresh mint
• Lemon zest
• Lemon juice
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Pitted black olives
• Tuna in olive oil
• Artichoke hearts
• Baguette
• Arugula

Steps:
1. To make the garbanzo bean spread: combine all the ingredients in a food processor
2. Pulse until the mixture is smooth
3. Transfer to a small bowl and set aside
4. To make the panini: combine the black olives, olive oil, salt, pepper, tuna, and artichokes in a bowl
5. Lay out the sliced baguettes
6. Spread the garbanzo bean spread on both halves of the baguettes
7. Spoon the tuna mixture over the bean spread
8. Top with the arugula and close the sandwich
9. Wrap 1 end of the sandwich in parchment paper to make it easier to eat
10. Place the sandwiches on a platter and serve<|endoftext|>
