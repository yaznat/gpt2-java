Give me a beef recipe.<|endoftext|>Barbecue Beef Strips

Ingredients:
• Round steaks
• Vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Canola oil
• Garlic salt
• Chili powder
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dry mustard
• Paprika
• Pepper
• Ketchup
• Tomato sauce
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Cut steak into strips
2. Combine next 9 ingredients in a large bowl to make a marinade
3. Add beef strips, stir, cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours or overnight
4. Drain beef strips, reserving marinade
5. In a hot skillet, brown the beef strips for 3-4 minutes
6. Meanwhile, combine ketchup, tomato sauce, and cornstarch with reserved marinade
7. Pour over beef strips and stir
8. Simmer for 15-20 minutes
9. Serve on a bun or over rice<|endoftext|>
