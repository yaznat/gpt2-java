Ingredients:
• Boneless pork roast
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Barbecue sauce
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325f
2. Sprinkle pork with salt and pepper
3. Heat a dutch oven for 1 minute over medium-high heat
4. Brown pork on all sides, about 4 minutes per side
5. Add barbecue sauce to dutch oven
6. Cover and bake 1 hours until pork is fork-tender
7. Transfer pork to cutting board
8. Slice into thin pieces
9. Return to dutch oven and cook for 5 minutes over medium heat
10. Place rolls on serving plate
11. Divide pork mixture and spoon into each roll
12. Serve with coleslaw, if desired<|endoftext|>
