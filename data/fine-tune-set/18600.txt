Give me a recipe for pudding.<|endoftext|>Ambrosia De Laranja Brazilian Orange Pudding

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Orange juice
• Orange zest
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 300f
2. Mix all ingredients well with a spoon and pass through a strainer
3. Pour into an 8 x 6 inch pyrex and bake for 20 minutes or until sides start to brown and become crunchy and top is golden brown<|endoftext|>
