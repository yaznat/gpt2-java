Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Garlic salt
• Oregano leaves
• Kidney beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Low-sodium tomato sauce

Steps:
1. Cook ground beef and onion in large skillet on medium-high heat until beef is no longer pink, stirring occasionally
2. Drain fat, if needed
3. Stir in spices and remaining ingredients
4. Bring to boil
5. Reduce heat to low
6. Cover and simmer 20 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Serve with shredded cheese, sour cream and chopped onion, if desired<|endoftext|>
