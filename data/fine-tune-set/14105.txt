Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Oil
• Onion
• Apricot jam
• Frozen orange juice concentrate
• Water
• Prepared mustard
• Vinegar
• Coriander
• Salt
• Ginger
• Garlic powder
• Cayenne

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Slice the tofu into 1 / 4" slices and lightly fry
3. Arrange the fried slices in a baking dish
4. Saute the chopped onions in the oil until transparent
5. Stir in the rest of the ingredients
6. Pour the sauce over the tofu and bake 10-15 minutes, until bubbling
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
