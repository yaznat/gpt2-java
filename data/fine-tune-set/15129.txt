What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Awesome Bread Machine Pizza Dough

Ingredients:
• Water
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Parmesan cheese
• Cornmeal
• Bread flour
• Dry yeast

Steps:
1. Put this in the bread machine on the manual / dough cycle
2. At the end of the cycle punch down the dough and let rest 5 min
3. Roll out the dough to the shape of your pan, and put in pan, top as desired bake at 425 for at least 15 min
4. Until cheese is well melted, and browned
5. Variation: add 1 to 2 tsp italian seasoning<|endoftext|>
