Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Oil
• Sugar
• Tomato paste
• Green pepper
• Oregano
• Garlic
• Black pepper
• Olive
• Celery
• Parsley
• Coriander
• Thyme leaves
• Salt
• Rice
• Water

Steps:
1. Marinate the chicken for approximately 10 minutes in a bowl containing the green peppers, oregano, garlic, black pepper, olives, celery, parsley, coriander, and thyme
2. In an iron pot, heat 3 tablespoons of oil, reserving 2 tablespoons for later use
3. Add sugar to heated oil and quickly stir
4. When sugar turns dark brown, add the chicken, being careful with splattering oil
5. Stir, cover and let simmer at medium heat for 10 minutes, adding tablespoonfuls of water regularly to prevent it from burning
6. Add the vegetables
7. Add tomato paste and stir to combine
8. Add remaining water and bring to a boil
9. Add salt to taste
10. Add the rice and stir often to avoid excessive sticking
11. Once all the water has evaporated, cover with a tight-fitting lid and simmer over very low heat
12. Wait 15 minutes, uncover and stir, before adding remaining oil
13. Cover and wait another 5 minutes
14. Taste rice for doneness
15. It should be firm but tender inside
16. If necessary, cover and leave another 5 minutes over very low heat<|endoftext|>
