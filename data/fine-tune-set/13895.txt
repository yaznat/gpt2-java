What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Racks Of Lamb With Sesame Sauce

Ingredients:
• Lamb racks
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Sesame seeds
• Sugar
• Light soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Beef stock
• Tahini

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 200c place lamb racks in oven dish and season well
2. Combine all other ingredients and rub onto racks
3. Marinate at least 1 hour in fridge
4. Roast racks until golden brown and cooked to your liking
5. Set racks aside and cover with foil to rest
6. Meanwhile remove fat from oven pan
7. Add stock to deglaze
8. Then add sesame oil and paste
9. Bring to boil stirring until thickened and season
10. Serve racks with the sesame gravy<|endoftext|>
