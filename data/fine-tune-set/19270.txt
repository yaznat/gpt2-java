Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Egg yolks
• Golden caster sugar
• Single cream
• White chocolate
• Creamed coconut
• Double cream
• Coconut rum

Steps:
1. Place the eggs, egg yolks and sugar in a heatproof bowl and beat together until well blended
2. Gently heat the single cream, chocolate and coconut in a saucepan until they melt
3. Once melted continue to heat, stirring constantly until almost boiling
4. Pour the cream mixture onto the egg mixture, stirring vigourously to mix everything thoroughly
5. Place the bowl over a saucepan of gently simmering wter, making sure the base of the bowl does not touch the water
6. Heat the mixture, stirring constantly, until it lightly coats the back of a spoon
7. Turn off the heat, carefully take the bowl off the pan and leave the contents to cool
8. Place the double cream and rum into a seperate bowl and whip until slightly thickened
9. Then fold into the cooled chocolate mixture
10. Freeze in an ice-cream maker, or pour into one large or 2 small freezerproof containers and freeze for 8 hours
11. Unless you want spoons like uri geller's, transfer the container from the freezer to your fridge for 30 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
