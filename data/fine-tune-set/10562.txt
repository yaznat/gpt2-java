What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Andouille Sausage Tomato Pasta

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Red onion
• Garlic clove
• Flour
• Whole milk
• Chicken broth
• Andouille sausages
• Grape tomatoes
• Salt & pepper
• Parmesan cheese
• Balsamic vinegar
• Pesto sauce
• Spaghetti

Steps:
1. Cook onion and garlic in olive oil over medium heat until softened
2. Add flour and cook for about three minutes, stirring constantly
3. Add milk, chicken broth, sausage, grape tomatoes, balsamic vinegar, pesto and salt and pepper
4. Stir constantly until sauce thickens
5. Once sauce thickens, add cheese and stir until melted and heated through
6. Toss with spaghetti and serve with additional parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
