Please give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>All Day Soup

Ingredients:
• Beef flank steak
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Carrots
• Cabbage
• Red potatoes
• Celery ribs
• Diced tomatoes
• Beef broth
• Condensed tomato soup
• Sugar
• Italian seasoning
• Dried parsley flakes

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, brown steak and onion in oil
2. Drain
3. Transfer to a 5 quart slow cooker
4. Stir in remaining ingredients
5. Cover and cook on low for 8-10 hours or until meat is tender<|endoftext|>
