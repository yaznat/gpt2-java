Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>In A Hurry Simple Fried Rice Vegan

Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Frozen peas and carrot
• Veggie crumbles
• Sweet onion
• Canola oil
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Black pepper
• Ginger powder
• Garlic powder
• Chili flakes
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a wok or large skillet on med-high heat, add oil and sautee onion briefly
2. Add the rice, stir, and let set for a couple minutes
3. Part the rice in center and add peas and carrots, then veggie crumbles and let cook for a minute or so, then combine with the rest of the rice
4. Add the splashes of soy sauce and the rest of the ingredients
5. Let the mixture cook for a few more minutes
6. Adjust seasoning to liking<|endoftext|>
