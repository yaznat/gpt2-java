Give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>1 In Canada Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Margarine
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Water
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking soda
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven the 350 degrees f
2. In a mixing bowl, sift together the flours and baking powder
3. Set aside
4. In another mixing bowl, blend together the sugars, margarine, and salt until light and fluffy
5. Add the eggs, water, and vanilla to the margarine / sugar mixture and mix together until well combined
6. Add in the flour mixture to the wet ingredients and blend until combined
7. Scrape down the sides of the bowl and add the chocolate chips
8. Mix until combined
9. Scrape down the sides to the bowl again
10. Using an ice cream scoop, scoop evenly rounded balls of dough and place of cookie sheet about 1 - 2 inches apart to allow for spreading during baking
11. Bake for 10 - 15 minutes or until golden brown on the outside and soft & chewy in the center
12. Serve hot and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
