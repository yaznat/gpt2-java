Generate a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>All American Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat griddle to medium-high heat
2. In large bowl, beat eggs
3. Stir in milk and oil
4. Add flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt
5. Stir just until large lumps disappear
6. For thicker pancakes, add 1 / 4 cup flour
7. For thinner pancakes, add 1 / 4 cup milk
8. Lightly grease heated griddle
9. Pour batter, about 1 / 4 cup at a time onto the prepared griddle
10. Bake until bubbles form and edges start to dry
11. Turn and bake other side
12. Serve hot
13. Great with strawberries and freshly whipped cream
14. On fourth of july or memorial day weekend, add blueberries<|endoftext|>
