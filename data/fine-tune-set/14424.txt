Ingredients:
• Dried navy beans
• Smoked bacon
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Dark brown sugar
• Ketchup
• Maple syrup
• Dark molasses
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Rinse and pick through beans
2. Soak them overnight in large pot of water
3. Place the beans in a colander and rinse well under cold running water
4. Transfer beans to a heavy saucepan
5. Cover with water and bring to a boil
6. Reduce the heat and simmer until tender, 45 minutes to 1 hour
7. Drain, reserving the liquid
8. Preheat the oven to 300 degrees farenheit
9. Heat a 2-quart ovenproof casserole or dutch oven over medium heat
10. Saute the bacon until it is slightly crisp and fat is rendered, 5 minutes
11. Add the onions and garlic, and cook until wilted, 5 to 10 minutes
12. Add the brown sugar and stir over medium-low heat until dissolved, about 5 minutes
13. Stir in the ketchup, syrup, molasses, worcestershire, salt, and pepper
14. Add the drained beans and mix well
15. Cover casserole and transfer to oven
16. Bake, stirring occasionally, for 2 / 12 hours
17. Add 3 / 4 cup of the reserved bean liquid, recover, and bake 30 minutes
18. Then remove the cover and bake until the sauce is thick and syrupy, another 10 to 15 minutes, stirring once
19. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
