What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Cinnamon Waffle Crisps

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Frozen waffles
• Butter-flavored cooking spray

Steps:
1. Combine sugar and cinnamon, mix well
2. Coat waffles with cooking spray and sprinkle each with 1 / 2 tbsp sugar mixture
3. Place on a baking sheet and bake at 450 for 5-7 minutes or until crisp
4. Cut waffles into 4 wedges to serve<|endoftext|>
