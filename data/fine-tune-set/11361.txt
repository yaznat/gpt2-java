Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>3 C Bread

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Carrots
• Flaked coconut
• Candied cherry
• Raisins
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the first six ingredients
2. In a small bowl, combine the eggs, milk and oil
3. Stir into dry ingredients just until moistened
4. Fold in the carrots, coconut, cherries, raisins and pecans
5. Pour into four greased 5-3 / 4-in
6. X 3-in
7. X 2-in
8. Loaf pans
9. Bake at 350 for 40-50 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean
10. Cool for 10 minutes before removing from pans to wire racks
11. Cover and store in the refrigerator
12. Yield: 4 mini loaves<|endoftext|>
