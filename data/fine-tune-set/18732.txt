What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Pecan Bread Pudding

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Bananas
• Sugar
• Heavy cream
• Dark rum
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Brioche bread
• Dark brown sugar
• Salt
• Dark jamaican rum
• All-purpose flour
• Pecan pieces
• Oil
• Butter
• Vanilla ice cream
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat
2. Add bananas and 2 ounces of sugar and cook several minutes, stirring to cook evenly
3. Remove from heat and cool to room temperature
4. Combine the heavy cream, rum, eggs, vanilla and remaining 3 ounces of sugar in a bowl and whisk to blend
5. Add bananas and bread and let this mixture soak for an hour minimum
6. Preheat oven to 300f
7. Line a rectangular terrine mold with plastic wrap and pour banana pudding mixture inside
8. Cover and cook in a water bath until it reaches 175f
9. Remove from oven and cool completely
10. Rum sauce:
11. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium-high heat
12. Add cream, sugar and salt
13. Bring to a boil, stirring until sugar dissolves
14. Remove from heat and stir in rum
15. To assemble and serve:
16. Remove pudding from mold and slice into -inch slices
17. Coat each piece evenly with flour, egg wash, and pecan pieces
18. Fry each piece to order using equal parts oil and butter till golden on each side
19. Sprinkle with a bit of salt and serve with a scoop of ice cream
20. Drizzle with rum sauce and sprinkle with powdered sugar<|endoftext|>
