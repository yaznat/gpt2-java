Ingredients:
• Deep dish pie shells
• Unsalted butter
• Light brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Coffee
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Milk
• Butterscotch chips
• Walnuts
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees
2. While the oven preheats, put your pie shell in the refrigerator to keep it cool
3. Filling:
4. Using an electric mixer, cream the butter on high speed, gradually adding the sugars
5. Beat in the coffee
6. The mixture will remain coarse and clumpy
7. Beat in the eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition
8. Blend in the vanilla
9. Add the flour and salt and blend the filling until evenly mixed
10. Blend in the milk, then stir in the butterscotch chips and nuts
11. Scrape the filling into the chilled pie shell, smoothing with a spoon
12. Bake the pie on the middle oven rack until the center is set, about 50 minutes, turning the pie 180 degrees about midway through the baking
13. When done, the top of the pie will turn dark golden brown
14. Thats fine, and it does not mean it has over-baked
15. Give the pie a slight nudge to check for doneness: the filling should not move in waves, but it may jiggle near the center
16. Transfer the pie to a cooling rack and cool
17. The pie can be eaten slightly warm or at room temperature or it can be chilled for 1 to 2 hours before serving, though it is best not to serve it cold or the butterscotch chips will be hard<|endoftext|>
