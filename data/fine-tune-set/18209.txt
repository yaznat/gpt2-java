Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Milk
• Light brown sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Banana
• Butter
• Pure maple syrup
• Unsalted butter
• Dark rum

Steps:
1. Pancakes:
2. Combine dry ingredients in a large bowl
3. In a separate bowl mix together milk, sugar, 2 t oil, egg, and vanilla
4. Add to the dry ingredients and stir until the mixture is almost smooth
5. Fold the bananas into the batter
6. Let rest, loosely covered, for 20 minutes
7. Combine the melted butter and 1 t oil
8. Put small amount in skillet
9. For each pancake, pour cup batter
10. Cook over medium heat
11. Serve with butter rum sauce
12. Butter rum sauce:
13. Combine all the ingredients into a glass measuring cup
14. Cook on medium power in microwave for 2 minutes
15. Stir and serve hot<|endoftext|>
