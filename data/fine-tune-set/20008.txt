What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Mushroom Omelette For One

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Salt and pepper
• Garlic powder
• Tarragon
• Sweet basil
• Mushrooms

Steps:
1. Set burner to medium to medium high
2. Whisk eggs in a small bowl
3. Whisk in remaining ingredients
4. If burner is on medium, pour the eggs onto the pan first
5. If burner is on medium high, whisk in mushrooms before pouring onto pan
6. Flip omelette constantly so mushrooms don't burn
7. Use your better judgement as to when the omelette is ready
8. My omelette took a little under 2 minutes on medium high<|endoftext|>
