Give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Baby Bear Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Peanut butter
• Honey
• Raisins
• Maraschino cherry

Steps:
1. Mix the peanut butter with honey if desired and spread mixture onto bread
2. Cut the bread into a heart shape, using a cookie cutter
3. Use the raisins on the heart shape to make the eyes
4. Place the cherry on the point of the heart shape to make the nose<|endoftext|>
