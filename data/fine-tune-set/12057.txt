What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>All American Fruit Pie

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Flour
• Nutmeg
• Ginger
• Sour cream
• Fruit salad

Steps:
1. Crust:
2. In a small bowl, blend butter with 1 / 4 cup sugar until creamy
3. Add flour
4. Mix with a fork until well blended
5. Press onto the bottom and up sides of pie pan
6. Pour filling into crust
7. Combine 1 tablespoon sugar and nutmeg
8. Sprinkle over filling
9. Bake at 400f for 20 to 25 minutes until crust is golden brown and filling is slightly firm
10. Cool or chill before serving
11. Filling:
12. In a medium mixing bowl, combine sugar, flour, nutmeg and ginger
13. Blend in sour cream
14. Carefully stir in fruit<|endoftext|>
