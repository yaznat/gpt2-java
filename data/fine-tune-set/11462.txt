Ingredients:
• Active dry yeast
• All-purpose flour
• Unsalted butter
• Sugar
• Fine salt
• Kosher salt
• Garlic powder
• Dried oregano

Steps:
1. Make the dough: place 1 / 4 cup warm water in the bowl of a mixer
2. Sprinkle in the yeast and set aside until foamy, about 5 minutes
3. Add the flour, butter, sugar, fine salt and 1 1 / 4 cups plus 2 tablespoons warm water
4. Mix with the paddle attachment until a slightly sticky dough forms, 5 minutes
5. Knead the dough by hand on a floured surface until very smooth and soft, 3 minutes
6. Roll into a 2-foot-long log
7. Cut into 16 1 1 / 2-inch pieces
8. Knead each piece slightly and shape into a 7-inch long breadstick
9. Arrange 2 inches apart on a parchment-lined baking sheet
10. Cover with a cloth
11. Let rise in a warm spot until almost doubled, about 45 minutes
12. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees f
13. Make the topping: brush the breadsticks with 1 1 / 2 tablespoons of the butter and sprinkle with 1 / 4 teaspoon kosher salt
14. Bake until slightly golden, about 15 minutes
15. Meanwhile, combine the remaining 1 / 4 teaspoon salt with the garlic powder and oregano
16. Brush the warm breadsticks with the remaining 1 1 / 2 tablespoons melted butter and sprinkle with the flavored salt
17. Serve warm with salad<|endoftext|>
