Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Chili Garlic Sauce

Ingredients:
• Cider vinegar
• Honey
• Green onions
• Minced ginger
• Garlic and red chile paste
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients except the sesame oil
2. Stir to mix thoroughly
3. Using a wire whisk, slowly drizzle in sesame oil while whisking sauce to combine sauce can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks
4. Serve at room temperature
5. Whisk again before serving to combine ingredients<|endoftext|>
