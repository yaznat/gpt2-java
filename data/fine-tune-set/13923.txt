What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Basil Lamb Casserole

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Yellow onion
• Ground lamb
• Fresh mint leaves
• Fresh basil leaf
• Fresh parmesan cheese
• Chopped tomatoes
• Macaroni
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a large saut pan, cook onions and ground lamb in olive oil, on medium high heat, stirring frequently, until onions are translucent and meat is cooked through
2. While cooking, season with salt and pepper
3. Set aside
4. Boil a couple of quarts of water in a large saucepan, add a teaspoon of salt
5. Add macaroni, reduce heat to simmer
6. Cook until al dente firm, not soft
7. In a large casserole dish, lay down layers of the meat, tomatoes, macaroni, basil & mint leaves, and parmesan
8. Top with parmesan
9. Cook in oven for 25 to 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
