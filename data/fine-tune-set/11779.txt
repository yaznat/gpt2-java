What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Asparagus Curry

Ingredients:
• Asparagus
• Carrots
• Potatoes
• Chana dal powder
• Dry coconut powder
• Chili powder
• Oil
• Mustard seeds
• Urad dal
• Jeera powder
• Curry leaf
• Coriander
• Salt
• Turmeric

Steps:
1. Chop asparagus, carrots and potato into 1 / 2 inch pieces
2. Take a non-stick pan and put it on medium flame
3. Add oil and other seasonings like mustard seeds and jeera and curry leaves
4. Now add carrots and potatoes first
5. Add turmeric and mix them all properly and close the lid
6. After few minutes stir by adding salt and put the lid on again for a minute
7. Add chopped asparagus and stir them all and close the lid to allow them cook
8. After a few minutes check if all ingredients are cooked
9. Then reduce stove to low and add roasted chana dal powder and dry coconut powder and chili powder
10. Stir them all and close lid for a few minutes and switch off the stove
11. Garnish with chopped coriander
12. Goes well with rice, chapathi, dosa
13. Also can use this in sandwiches and is very healthy<|endoftext|>
