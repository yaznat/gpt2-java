Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Beef chuck
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Smoked paprika
• Salt
• Beef stock
• Tomato paste
• Chipotle chile in adobo
• Onion
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Heat the olive oil in a large, heavy skillet over medium-high heat
2. Meanwhile, combine the chili powder, cumin
3. Smoked paprika and salt in a small bowl
4. Rub the spice mix into the beef, covering each side evenly
5. Once the oil is hot, place the beef in the skillet and sear on each side
6. Do not move the meat while it cooks for 2-3 minutes on each side
7. Remove the beef from the skillet and place in the bottom of a slow cooker
8. Leave the pan on the heat and add in the beef stock to deglaze, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan
9. Add the tomato paste and minced chipotle and whisk into the pan sauce
10. Bring the sauce to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer for a few minutes until the sauce has reduced and thickened slightly
11. Add the diced onion and minced garlic on top of the seared beef in the slow cooker
12. Pour the pan sauce over the onions, garlic and beef
13. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours or high for 3-4 hours until fork tender
14. Remove the beef from the slow cooker and shred with two forks
15. Use a slotted spoon to remove the onions from the slow cooker and mix into the shredded beef<|endoftext|>
