Give me a soup recipe.<|endoftext|>Country Style Onion Soup

Ingredients:
• White onions
• Unsalted butter
• Flour
• Vegetable stock
• Water
• Salt

Steps:
1. Slice onions in half latitudinally, peel, cut off tops
2. Cut in half again, the other way
3. Turn on the flat side and cut each quarter into thirds or fourths, to get fairly uniform, fairly thin half-rings of onion
4. In a sturdy saucepan over medium-high heat, melt butter
5. Watch carefully to make sure it doesn't separate
6. Just before butter melts completely, add onions
7. Cook, stirring fairly frequently, until they are soft, clear, and just on the edge of caramelizing
8. Add flour all at once to the middle of the pan
9. Carefully and evenly work onions and butter into the flour, stirring constantly, until all onions are completely coated with flour-butter paste
10. Cook this mixture, stirring constantly, until it is a medium golden-brown
11. Add stock slowly while stirring constantly to avoid lumps
12. Add water until soup is just a little thinner than desired consistency
13. Salt lightly
14. Cover, and bring just to a boil
15. Reduce heat to medium
16. Simmer, partially covered but vented, about 20 minutes or until soup is desired consistency
17. Taste for seasoning and salt to taste<|endoftext|>
