What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Brazilian Vegetable Curry With Spicy Tomato And Coconut Sauce

Ingredients:
• Butternut squash
• Red onions
• Aubergine
• Red peppers
• Chickpeas
• Garlic cloves
• Gingerroot
• Red chili pepper
• Chopped tomatoes
• Coconut cream
• Fresh coriander
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Toss veg in a roasting tin with 2 tbsp of the the oil
2. Roast at 200c for 40 minutes
3. Meanwhile begin making the sauce
4. Put the chilli, garlic, ginger and onion in a food processor and process to a rough paste
5. Heat the remaining oil and fry the onion paste until the onions have softened
6. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes
7. Add the coconut cream and and cook for another 5 mins, if the sauce becomes too thick, thin with a little water, leave to one side until the veg is ready
8. When the veg is ready add the veg and the chickpeas to the sauce and heat through<|endoftext|>
