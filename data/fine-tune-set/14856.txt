What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Cantonese Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Eggs
• Meat
• Cooked rice
• Green onions
• Bean sprouts
• Low sodium soy sauce

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large skillet or wok
2. Saut onion over medium-high heat for 2 minutes
3. Add garlic and saut for 1 minute
4. Push onions and garlic to side
5. Pour eggs onto bottom of wok
6. Cook and stir eggs for 1-2 minutes until firm
7. Stir onions, garlic, and eggs together
8. Add meat, rice, green onions, and any additional veggies from optional list below to wok
9. Stir and toss until evenly mixed and almost heated through
10. Add bean sprouts and soy sauce to wok
11. Stir until heated through
12. Serve and enjoy!
13. For variety you may add any of the following optional veggies: sliced mushrooms, sliced water chestnuts, thinly sliced celery, snow peas, peas & carrots, chopped green pepper, finely shredded cabbage, or slivered almonds ~ just don't be afraid to experiment and use whatever's in your refrigerator!<|endoftext|>
