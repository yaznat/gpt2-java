Please give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>All Occasion White Bread Bread Machine

Ingredients:
• Water
• Nonfat dry milk powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Bread flour
• Bread machine yeast

Steps:
1. Measure ingredients into baking pan in the order recommended by the manufacturer
2. Inset pan into the oven chamber
3. Select basic cycle, press start
4. When the baking cycle ends, immediately remove the bread from the pan and place it on a rack
5. Let cool to room temperature before slicing<|endoftext|>
