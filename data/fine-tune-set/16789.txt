Give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Ice Creamless Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Chocolate syrup
• Sugar-free vanilla syrup
• Ice cubes
• Milk

Steps:
1. Put ice cubes into blender
2. Pour in chocolate syrup, vanilla syrup and milk
3. Blend until the noise of big chunks knocking together stops
4. Pour into glass and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
