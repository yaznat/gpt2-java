Ingredients:
• Ground coffee
• Tap water
• Coffee
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Milk
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Combine the ground coffee and water and let soak overnight
2. Strain the coffee grounds
3. Combine the blender ingredients in a blender until blended well
4. If you don't use ice in the blender, serve over ice
5. Variations:
6. Substitute an ice cream sandwich or two instead of milk
7. Substitute frozen soy milk for ice and milk
