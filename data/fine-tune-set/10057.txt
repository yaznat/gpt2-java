Ingredients:
• Hot dogs
• Hot dog buns
• Cheddar cheese
• Lettuce
• Roma tomatoes
• Onion
• Fat-free turkey chili

Steps:
1. Cook hot dogs however you like
2. Heat chili in saucepan on stove or in bowl in microwave
3. Chop lettuce, tomatoes and onions and put in separate bowls
4. Let everyone assemble their own
5. My favorite is laying the hot dog bun out on the plate, add the hot dog, cover bun and hot dog with chili, top chili with cheese, top cheese with lettuce, top lettuce with tomatoes, then onions
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
