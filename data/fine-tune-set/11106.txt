Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Awesome Turkey Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Cooked turkey
• Mayonnaise
• Red onion
• Dried dill weed
• Cranberry sauce
• Tomatoes
• Alfalfa sprout
• Whole wheat bread

Steps:
1. In a food processor, shred turkey meat
2. In a medium mixing bowl combine shredded turkey, mayonnaise, red onion, dill, and cranberry sauce
3. Stir together until thoroughly combined
4. On two slices of bread, spread on turkey mixture
5. Add tomato slices and sprouts
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
