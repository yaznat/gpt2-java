What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Bison Steaks With Mushroom Sauce

Ingredients:
• Steaks
• Vegetable oil
• Salt and pepper
• Fresh mushrooms
• Butter
• Garlic clove
• All-purpose flour
• Water
• White wine
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. Season bison steaks with salt and pepper, to taste
2. Heat oil in a skillet over high heat
3. Sear steaks in oil until light brown on each side, about 3-4 minutes per side
4. Bison should never be cooked more than medium because otherwise it will get too tough
5. When bison is done cooking, remove from skillet and keep warm
6. Add mushrooms, butter, and garlic to meat drippings in skillet
7. Cook about 5 minutes at medium high heat
8. Reduce heat to low
9. Sprinkle mushroom mixture with flour and stir well to ensure that all flour is absorbed
10. Add water, white wine, and worcestershire sauce to the skillet
11. Stir well to incorporate liquids into mushroom mixture
12. Top steaks with mushroom sauce and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
