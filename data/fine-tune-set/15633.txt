Give me a recipe for a taco.<|endoftext|>Black Bean Corn Tacos With Radish Salsa Feta

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Black beans
• Corn
• Cilantro
• Salt
• Pepper
• Radishes
• Green onions
• Serrano pepper
• Limes
• Corn tortillas
• Feta cheese

Steps:
1. In a medium pot, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil
2. Add garlic and cook for about 1 to 2 minutes until the garlic is just fragrant barely starting to brown
3. Add the black beans along with the can liquid, corn, 1 tablespoon of cilantro, 1 / 2 teaspoon salt and 1 / 4 teaspoon pepper
4. Bring to a boil, then simmer 15 minutes or until the liquid condenses
5. While the beans & corn cook, prepare the salse: combine 1 tablespoon olive oil, 3 tablespoons cilantro, 1 / 2 teaspoon salt, 1 / 4 teaspoon pepper, radishes, green onions, serrano pepper and lime juice
6. To serve the tacos, place the beans & corn in the middle of a tortilla and top with salsa and feta
7. Sprinkle with additional cilantro, if desired<|endoftext|>
