Ingredients:
• Frozen southern style hash brown potatoes
• Eggs
• Half-and-half cream
• Salt and pepper
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Bulk pork sausage
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees
2. In a large skillet, fry hash browns according to package directions
3. Remove and set aside
4. In a large bowl beat eggs with milk
5. Add chopped onion and green pepper, then season with salt and pepper
6. Pour the mixture into the same skillet
7. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until eggs are set
8. Remove from heat
9. Add the cooked hash browns and sausage
10. Mix gently
11. Place about 3 / 4 cup filling on each tortilla
12. Top with 1 / 4 cup cheese
13. Roll up, and place on a greased baking sheet
14. Bake, uncovered for 15-20 minutes, or until heated through
15. Serve with salsa if desired<|endoftext|>
