Can you give me a coffee recipe?<|endoftext|>Butter Mint Coffee Blend

Ingredients:
• Non-dairy powdered coffee creamer
• Butter mints
• Powdered sugar
• Powdered milk
• Instant coffee granules

Steps:
1. Combine the first 4 ingredients together in medium mixing bowl
2. Divide equally and pour into jars
3. Divide and layer instant coffee on top of each
4. Secure lid and attach instructions
5. Instructions:
6. Place mixture in a bowl and toss lightly
7. Spoon back into jar
8. Combine 1 / 4 cup mix with 2 / 3 cups boiling water
9. Stir until mixture dissolves<|endoftext|>
