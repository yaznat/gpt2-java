Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Brownies With Frosting

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Flour
• Cocoa
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Nuts
• Butter
• Light corn syrup
• Powdered sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Stir oil or melted butter, sugar and vanilla together in large mixer bowl
2. Add eggs, beat until well blended using a spoon
3. Combine flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt
4. Gradually add to egg mixture
5. Add nuts if desired
6. Spread into a greased 9 inch square pan
7. Bake at 350 for 20-25 minutes or until brownie pulls away from the edge of the pan
8. Cool in the pan
9. Frost with creamy brownie frosting if desired
10. Cut into squares
11. Creamy brownie frosting instructions:
12. Cream butter, cocoa, corn syrup or honey and vanilla in small mixer bowl
13. Add powdered sugar and milk
14. Beat to spreading consistency
15. Makes about 1 cup frosting<|endoftext|>
