Give me a stir fry recipe.<|endoftext|>Beef Teriyaki Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Boneless sirloin steaks
• Teriyaki sauce
• Water
• Brown sugar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Fresh garlic
• Vegetable oil
• Carrots
• Celery
• Onion
• Cornstarch
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Partially freeze steak, slice diagonally across grain into 3 quarter inch strips
2. The partial freezing, makes slicing easy, and the diagonal cut makes the strips tender
3. Combine next 5 ingredients in a large bowl
4. Add meat
5. Cover and marinate in the fridge 4 to 6 hours, stirring occasionally
6. Pour 1 tablespoon oil around top of a preheated wok, coating sides, heat medium high for 2 minutes
7. Add vegetables and stir fry 4 minutes
8. Remove from wok
9. Pour 1 tablespoon oil around top of wok
10. Remove steak from marinade, reserving marinade
11. Add steak to wok, stir fry, until browned, do not over cook
12. Combine cornstarch and reserved marinade, stirring well, stir mixture into beef
13. Cook, stirring constantly until thickened
14. Combine veggies and rice, place on a serving dish
15. Spoon meat mixture over rice<|endoftext|>
