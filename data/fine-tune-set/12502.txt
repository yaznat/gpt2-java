Give me a stew recipe.<|endoftext|>All Day Ravioli Stew

Ingredients:
• Carrots
• Onion
• Chicken broth
• Italian-style diced tomatoes
• Cannellini beans
• Dried basil
• Sausage-filled ravioli

Steps:
1. In large crock pot, combine all ingredients except ravioli
2. Mix well
3. Cover
4. Cook on low setting for 6 hours or until vegetables are tender
5. At serving time, increase heat to high setting
6. Add ravioli
7. Cook an additional 8 minutes or until ravioli is tender<|endoftext|>
