Ingredients:
• Nonstick spray coating
• Sweet potatoes
• Margarine
• Seasoning salt
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Spray a 15x10x1-inch baking pan with nonstick coating
2. Scrub potatoes
3. Cut lengthwise into quarters, then cut each quarter into 2 wedges
4. Arrange potatoes in a single layer in pan
5. Combine margarine or butter, salt, and nutmeg
6. Brush onto potatoes
7. Bake in a 450 oven 20 minutes or until brown and tender<|endoftext|>
