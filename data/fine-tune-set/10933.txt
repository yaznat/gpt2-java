Give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>Almost Egg White Salad

Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Egg
• Light mayonnaise
• Mustard
• Relish
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Hard boil eggs, shell
2. Discard 5 yolks, leaving one for flavor
3. Coarsely chop eggs
4. Add in mayo, mustard, relish-and whatever else!
5. I use a pita bread, tomato and lettuce
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
