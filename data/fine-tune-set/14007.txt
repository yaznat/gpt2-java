Ingredients:
• Diced tomatoes
• Sliced mushrooms
• Artichoke hearts
• Black olives
• Capers
• Onion
• White wine
• Heavy cream
• Salt
• Pepper
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Base -- add 2 teaspoons olive oil to your saute pan
2. If using non stick 1 teaspoon is fine
3. Heat to medium / medium high heat
4. Add in the onions and cook 2 minutes
5. Then add in the capers, olives and mushrooms and cook another minute or two to combine all the flavors
6. Sauce -- add the white wine to deglaze the pan and scrape up any bits
7. Cook another minute to reduce the wine
8. Reduce the heat to medium and add in the tomatoes, artichoke hearts and cream, stir well to combine
9. Season with salt and pepper
10. Go easy on the salt
11. Nothing more is needed
12. Simmer -- just simmer on medium / medium low and let the sauce reduce
13. It will take about 10 minutes to slightly thicken
14. Remember this is not a thick sauce or gravy
15. Serve -- spoon the sauce over grilled pork, chicken or lamb
16. This is excellent over rice, pasta, couscous and even butter noodles
17. This is even excellent over a baked potato topped with feta cheese
18. Fresh basil makes a nice garnish if you want
19. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
