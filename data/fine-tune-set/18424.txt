Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Cinnamon Apple Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter
• Apple

Steps:
1. Sift flour, salt, cinnamon, baking powder, and sugar together
2. Separate eggs
3. Beat egg yolks
4. Add milk and melted butter
5. Combine wet and dry ingredients
6. Beat until smooth
7. Add apples
8. Beat egg whites until stiff
9. Fold into batter
10. Bake until done<|endoftext|>
