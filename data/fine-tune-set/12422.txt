What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>Andersen S Split Pea Soup Copycat

Ingredients:
• Ham hocks
• Water
• Split peas
• Celery
• Carrot
• Onion
• Ground thyme
• Cayenne
• Bay leaves
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Add above ingredients in large pot
2. Boil hard for 20 minutes then simmer until peas are tender, about another 25-20 minute
3. Take ham hocks out and set aside to cool
4. Strain soup through fine sieve
5. Take meat off ham hocks and add back to soup
6. Reheat and enjoy
7. Makes 8 bowls of yummy soup<|endoftext|>
