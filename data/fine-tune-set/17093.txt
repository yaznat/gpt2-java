Ingredients:
• Brandy
• Fresh lemon juice
• Maraschino juice
• Cointreau liqueur
• Sugar syrup
• Angostura bitters
• Superfine sugar

Steps:
1. Moisten the rim of a chilled cocktail glass with a lemon wedge, then dip the rim into superfine granulated sugar
2. Shake all ingredients with cracked ice, then strain into the glass
3. Drape a very long spiral of either orange or lemon peel into and hanging out of the glass by about two inches, then serve<|endoftext|>
