Ingredients:
• 1% low-fat milk
• Dry milk
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Ground cardamom
• Preserves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Spray 6 custard cups or ramekins with pam
3. In a saucepan mix together the milk, dry milk and sugar over medium high heat
4. Cook stirring until mixture boils and sugar is dissolved
5. Meanwhile whisk together the eggs in a bowl
6. Remove pan from heat and gradually whisk in eggs, vanilla and the cardamom
7. Divide custard mixture among custard cups which are put in a roasting pan
8. Pour hot water into roasting pan to come halfway up the sides of the custard cups
9. Bake for 20-30 minutes or until knife inserted in center of custard comes out clean
10. Transfer custard cups to a wire rack and cool to room temperature and then chill for at least 4 hours
11. Serve custards topped with preserves<|endoftext|>
