Ingredients:
• Water
• Dried apricot
• Dried apple
• Nuts
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Flour tortillas
• Vegetable oil
• Powdered sugar
• Fresh mint leaves
• Cornstarch
• Evaporated milk
• Egg yolk
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. For chimichangas, combine water, apricots, apples, nuts, sugar and cinnamon in a medium saucepan
2. Bring to boil, reduce heat to medium and cover
3. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 10-15 minutes or until excess moisture is absorbed
4. Cool for 15 minutes
5. For the sauce, combine sugar, cornstarch and cinnamon in medium heavy duty saucepan
6. Gradually stir in the evaporated milk, water and egg yolk
7. Bring to boil over medium heat, stir constantly until mixture is slightly thickened
8. Removed from heat, stir in vanilla
9. Cover and keep warm
10. Place 1 / 4 filling in the center of each tortilla
11. Fold into burritos: secure ends with wooden picks
12. Add vegetable oil to 1 inch depth in medium skillet - heat over high heat for 3-4 minutes
13. Place 2 or 3 chimichangas at a time in oil, fry until golden brown
14. Place on paper towels to dry, remove the wooden picks
15. Serve with cinnamon custard sauce, and garnish with powdered sugar and fresh mint leaves<|endoftext|>
