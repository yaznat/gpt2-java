What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado Cream Cheese Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Whole grain bread
• Avocados
• Sour cream
• Scallions
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Spread a thick layer of softened cream cheese on 3 or 4 slices of bread
2. Peel and slice the avocados and cover the cream cheese with them
3. Spread sour cream over the avocados and sprinkle with the chopped scallions
4. Season with salt & pepper
5. You may also use bean sprouts for extra added crunch<|endoftext|>
