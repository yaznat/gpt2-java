Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Butter
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Grease bottom and 1 / 2 inch up the sides of a 13x9x2-inch baking pan
3. Set aside
4. In a medium bowl, combine flour, brown sugar, and salt
5. Cut in butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs
6. Set aside 1 / 2 cup
7. Stir baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and nutmeg into remaining crumb mixture
8. In another bowl, combine eggs and buttermilk
9. Add egg mixture all at once to flour mixture and mix until just combined
10. Spoon batter into prepared pan
11. Combine reserved crumb mixture and nuts
12. Sprinkle over batter
13. Bake at 350f for 35-40 minutes, or until a wooden toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
14. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
