What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Refried beans
• Flour tortillas
• Mexican blend cheese
• Taco sauce

Steps:
1. Spread beans evenly over 2 tortillas
2. Sprinkle evenly with 3 / 4 c
3. Cheese and drizzle with 1 / 4 c
4. Taco sauce
5. Top with remaining tortillas
6. Microwave 1 quesadilla, covered with paper towel on a microwave safe plate, at high 1 to 1 1 / 2 minutes
7. Repeat procedure with remaining quesadilla
8. Cut each quesadilla into 8 wedges
9. Serve with taco sauce, cheese, and sour cream<|endoftext|>
