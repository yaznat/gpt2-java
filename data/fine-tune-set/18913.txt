Give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Cooked Custard Eggnog

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Eggs
• Salt
• Sugar
• Ground nutmeg
• Vanilla extract
• Whipping cream

Steps:
1. Heat milk in a large saucepan, don't boil
2. Beat eggs and salt in a large bowl
3. Gradually add sugar, mixing well
4. Gradually stir about 1 / 4 of hot milk into egg mixture
5. Add to remaining hot milk, stirring constantly
6. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until misture thickens and reaches 160 degrees
7. Stir in 1 / 4 tsp nutmeg and vanilla
8. Set saucepan in larger pan of ice for 10 minutes to rapidly cool mixture
9. Cover and refrigerate up to 48 hours
10. When ready to serve, fold in whipping cream and sprinkle with nutmeg<|endoftext|>
