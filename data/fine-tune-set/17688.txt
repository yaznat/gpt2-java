Ingredients:
• Yellow cake mix
• Baking powder
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Canola oil
• Eggs
• Cinnamon
• Apples
• Butter
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Grease muffin cups generously or line with paper liners
3. Mix yellow cake mix, baking powder, flour, milk, canola oil, eggs and cinnamon in a large bowl
4. Mix until combined well, scraping the sides of bowl as you go
5. Fold in apples and stir until covered
6. Spoon batter into muffin pan
7. Fill cups 2 / 3 full
8. Bake 15-18 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
9. Remove from oven and cool
10. Mix sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon in a small bowl
11. Dip muffin top in melted butter and then into cinnamon / sugar mixture until lightly coated
12. Shake off excess and serve<|endoftext|>
