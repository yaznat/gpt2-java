What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Hamburger Patties

Ingredients:
• Beef
• Onion
• Salt
• Catsup
• Chili sauce
• Brown sugar
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Mix the meat, onion, and salt together
2. Shape the mixture into 6 patties, each about 3 / 4-inch thick
3. Brown the patties in a large skillet over medium-high heat, turning once
4. Cover and cook over low heat about 10 minutes
5. Drain off the excess fat
6. Mix catsup, chili sauce, brown sugar, and lemon juice
7. Pour sauce over the patties
8. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes, spooning the sauce onto the patties occasionally
9. Serve with the sauce spooned over the patties
10. For once a month cooking--freeze cooked patties in the sauce in a large ziploc bag
11. Label and freeze
12. To serve: dump contents of bag into a casserole dish and bake, covered, for approximately 1 hour at 350 degrees, or until meat is heated through<|endoftext|>
