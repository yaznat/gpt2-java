What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Fried Lamb Chops With Creamy Gravy

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Olive oil
• Whole milk
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Sugar
• Oil
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Lamb chops
• Buttermilk

Steps:
1. For the gravy:
2. In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, add flour and oil
3. Cook over medium heat for about 10 to 12 minutes whisking constantly until a peanut-brown color
4. Whisk in milk, salt, pepper, and sugar
5. Bring to a boil, continuing to whisk
6. Reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes whisking frequently until sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon
7. Make gravy before you fry the chops
8. For the lamb:
9. Add 1-inch of oil in a straight-sided 3-inch deep pan
10. Preheat oil in frying pan to 365 degrees f
11. Mix salt and pepper with flour
12. Dredge the lamb chops in the seasoned flour
13. Dip lamb chops in buttermilk, then back into the flour
14. Slowly add chops to preheated oil, about 4 per batch
15. Cook 2 minutes per side for medium doneness
16. Drain on a rack
17. Serve with gravy<|endoftext|>
