Give me a stew recipe.<|endoftext|>Amager Island Stew Amagergryde

Ingredients:
• Ham
• Water
• Frozen peas and carrots
• New potato
• Butter
• Flour
• Salt & pepper
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Cut the ham or pork into small squares
2. Peel the potatoes and cut them into small squares
3. In a dutch oven, bring the ham and potatoes to a boil and continue cooking until the stew is almost cooked through and then add the peas and carrots
4. Mix the butter and flour and add to the mixture
5. Return to a boil and boil for 5 minute
6. Add salt and pepper
7. Add the parsley on top of the dish<|endoftext|>
