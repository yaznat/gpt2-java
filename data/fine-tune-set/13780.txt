Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin roast
• Apricot jam
• Chili sauce
• Red wine vinegar
• Dijon mustard
• Vegetable oil cooking spray
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325degrees f
2. Spray a shallow roasting pan with veggie oil
3. Place the roast, fat side up, in the pan
4. Mix remaining ingredients
5. Salt& pepper the roast and bake for 1 / 2 hour
6. Remove from oven and smear the jam mixture all over the roast
7. Return roast to the oven, uncovered, and continue baking for apprx 1 hour or until the internal temp reads 160 degrees f
8. Baste a couple of times and watch that the glaze doesn't burn
9. Let the roast rest for 10 minutes, carve& serve<|endoftext|>
