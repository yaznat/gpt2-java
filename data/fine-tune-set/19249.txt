Ingredients:
• Semisweet chocolate
• Sweetened coconut
• Blanched almond
• Butter
• Phyllo pastry
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. In a food processor, chop chocolate fine
2. Add coconut, almonds and cup of butter
3. Blend mixture until smooth
4. Melt remaining butter
5. Working with 1 strip of filo at a time, arrange the strip on a work surface with short side facing you
6. Brush strip lightly with some of the melted butter
7. Fold down top right hand corner of filo to form a triangle
8. Put 1 tablespoon of filling in center and continue folding in same way, to enclose filling completely
9. Brush with some of remaining butter and repeat 11 more times, making 12 filo triangles
10. Preheat oven to 400f and bake triangles for 8-12 minutes, or until golden
11. Serve hot with ice cream<|endoftext|>
