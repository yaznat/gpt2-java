Give me a curry recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Almond Quinoa With Curry Vinaigrette

Ingredients:
• Gala apples
• Quinoa
• Almonds
• Currants
• Olive oil
• Lemon juice
• Honey
• Shallot
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Mix dressing ingredients together: olive oil, lemon juice, minced shallot, curry powder, salt and pepper
2. Taste, adjust as needed for your taste
3. When quinoa has cooled, add all other ingredients
4. Mix with dressing
5. Do not drown with dressing
6. It should be background, not foreground in taste<|endoftext|>
