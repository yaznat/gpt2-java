Give me a recipe for chili.<|endoftext|>Beef Chili For The Pressure Cooker

Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Oil
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Red pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Chipotle chile in adobo
• Chili powder
• Oregano
• Ground cumin
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Chopped tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Beef broth
• Water
• Salt and pepper
• Cheese
• Sour cream
• Jalapeno
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. In 8-quart pressure cooker:
2. Heat oil
3. Brown meat in small batches
4. Add all meat and the remaining ingredients to the cooker
5. Attach lid, bring to 15lbs of pressure
6. Cook for 35 - 40 minutes
7. If it cooks longer, you won't hurt it
8. Enjoy with toppings of choice<|endoftext|>
