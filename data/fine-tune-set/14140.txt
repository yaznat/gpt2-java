What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Balsamic Tofu Vegetable Medley

Ingredients:
• Tofu
• Celery ribs
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red bell pepper
• Asparagus
• Broccoli florets
• Green beans
• Mango
• Teriyaki sauce
• Balsamic vinegar
• Red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Spray a wok, or large pot with cooking spray and heat over medium heat
2. Add tofu and saute for 5 minutes, then remove to a plate
3. Add vegetables, mango, teriyaki sauce, balsamic vinegar and pepper flakes, and saute for 5 minutes
4. Then add tofu back to wok and cook for an additional 3 to 5 minutes
5. Serve on its own, over with brown rice<|endoftext|>
