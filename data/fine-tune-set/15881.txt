Ingredients:
• Chicken bouillon cubes
• Hot water
• Instant rice
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Olive oil
• Taco seasoning
• Crushed tomatoes
• Sour cream
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Dissolve bouillon in water, bring to a boil add instant rice and set aside
2. If using standard rice, add rice to water mixture, bring to a boil, cover, cook on low for 20 minutes
3. Meanwhile, place olive oil in a large dutch oven, cook the onion until caramelized
4. Then add garlic, long enough to let the garlic sweat
5. Add the cut up chicken to the onion garlic mixture, salt and pepper to taste
6. Cook until chicken is completely cooked
7. Add taco seasoning and water, mix well and simmer for another 3 minutes
8. Add crushed tomatoes
9. Add rice mixture
10. Stir in sour cream
11. Place 1 to 1
12. 5 cup measurements into containers
13. Place 1-2 tortillas in wax paper, presteaming or spraying with no-stick spray helps keep them from drying out too much
14. Fold up the wax paper as tight as you can, i seal it like an envelope and tape it shut with masking tape
15. Place on top of rice / chicken mixture, cover container and freeze<|endoftext|>
