What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Bacon Pasta

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Fresh mushrooms
• Garlic cloves
• Penne pasta
• Parmesan cheese
• Heavy cream
• Pepper
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Cook bacon until crisp, drain on paper towel and crumble
2. Reserve 2 tablespoons drippings
3. Saute mushrooms and garlic in bacon drippings until tender
4. Stir in cooked pasta, parmesan cheese, heavy cream and pepper
5. Simmer over low-med heat until sauce is thickened
6. Remove from heat
7. Stir in bacon and green onions
8. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
