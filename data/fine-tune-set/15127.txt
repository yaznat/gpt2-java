What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado Pizza

Ingredients:
• Pizza crusts
• Tomato paste
• Garlic clove
• Green onion
• Mushroom
• Olive
• Cherry tomatoes
• Reduced-fat mozzarella cheese
• Basil leaves
• Avocado

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180f
2. Spread pizza base with tomato paste, garlic and shallots
3. Arrange mushrooms, olive and cherry tomatoes around base
4. Sprinkle with cheese
5. Put in oven and bake for 20 minutes or until everything is golden
6. Remove from oven and serve topped with avocado and basil leaves<|endoftext|>
