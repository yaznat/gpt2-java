Ingredients:
• Onion
• Zucchini
• Red sweet bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Green chili pepper
• Olive oil
• Tofu
• Canned spinach
• Vegan egg replacer powder
• Fresh cilantro
• Ground cumin
• Dried oregano
• Paprika
• Dried parsley
• Turmeric
• Lime wedge
• Soy sauce
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Green onion
• Avocado

Steps:
1. Fry the onion, zucchini, pepper, garlic and chile in olive oil at medium heat until onion is approaching translucence
2. Mash together tofu, spinach, egg replacer, cilantro, dried spices
3. Add the juice from the lime wedges
4. Add tofu mash to the onion-veggie-garlic-chile mix and fry at slightly lower heat, till everything is hot, tender and mixed together
5. Add soy sauce and black pepper as needed
6. Garish, and serve<|endoftext|>
