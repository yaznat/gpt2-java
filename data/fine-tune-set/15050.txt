Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• All-purpose flour
• Fast rising yeast
• Sugar
• Salt
• Water
• Olive oil
• Pizza sauce
• Fresh mozzarella cheese
• Five cheese blend
• Italian herb seasoning

Steps:
1. Mix batter ingredients in a 9-1 / 2-inch deep dish pie plate that has been coated with cooking spray
2. Top dough with the pizza sauce
3. Evenly distribute fresh mozzarella slices on sauce and top with shredded cheese
4. Sprinkle with italian herb seasoning
5. Bake by placing in a cold oven
6. Set temperature to 350f bake for 30 minutes or until done
