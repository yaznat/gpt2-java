Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onions
• Tomato juice
• Whole tomatoes
• Kidney beans
• Chili powder
• Hot sauce
• Baking soda
• Water

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, saute beef and onions together until beef is browned
2. Drain beef and add to a large soup pot
3. Add tomato juice and simmer over medium heat
4. Add tomatoes, crushing between fingers to break apart
5. Add kidney beans, chili powder, and optional hot sauce
6. After mixture comes to a simmer, add baking soda
7. Simmer for about 20-30 minutes, adding water if necessary to thin out the chili
8. Taste for seasoning<|endoftext|>
