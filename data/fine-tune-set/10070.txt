Ingredients:
• Great northern beans
• Chicken breasts
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Red pepper
• Jalapenos
• Garlic cloves
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Dried oregano
• Chicken broth

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in crock pot
2. Cover, cook on low 8 hours or on high 4 hours
3. Ladle into bowls and serve!<|endoftext|>
