Give me a cookie recipe.<|endoftext|>Angel Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Shortening
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Egg
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Cream of tartar
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Cream butter and shortening
2. Blend sugars, add egg
3. Mix flour and baking soda and cream of tartar
4. Add vanilla
5. Drop dough by spoonfuls on cookie sheet
6. Press down
7. Put fork in sugar before pressing
8. Bake at 325 degrees for 10 to 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
