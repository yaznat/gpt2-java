Can you give me a omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Greek Style Omelette Gluten Free

Ingredients:
• Long-grain rice
• Olive oil
• Leeks
• Zucchini
• Garlic cloves
• Of fresh mint
• Dill
• Eggs
• Feta cheese
• Red onion
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 180c grease a 1-litre round ovenproof dish
2. Cook rice in boiling, salted water for 8 minutes
3. Drain and refresh
4. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a saucepan, add leek and cook over low heat for 7-8 minutes or until softened
5. Add zucchini and garlic, cook for 1-2 minutes, then add rice with mint, dill, eggs and feta
6. Season
7. Mix, then pour into dish and bake for 30 minutes or until golden and set
8. Toss onion and tomato in remaining oil and place on top of omelette
9. Serve with a green salad<|endoftext|>
