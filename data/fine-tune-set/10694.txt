Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>Authentic And Easy Pasta Carbonara

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Parmesan cheese
• Eggs
• Lean bacon
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Put large pot on to boil for pasta and cook pasta as directed
2. Cut the bacon into 2" pieces and add to saute pan on medium
3. Grate cheese
4. Break eggs into large bowl and beat until smooth
5. Add grated cheese to eggs and mix until a paste
6. When pasta is "al dente", remove and drain
7. Immediately add pasta to egg / cheese mixture and use tongs to rapidly toss, coating the pasta
8. Add the bacon with about 1 teaspoon of drippings and continue to toss pasta until a creamy sauce evenly coats the pasta
9. Serve and garnish with a sprinkle of cheese, fresh ground salt and pepper
10. Watch your friends and family scrap the bowl!
11. Nice served with a salad and a crisp white wine!<|endoftext|>
