Ingredients:
• Dark brown sugar
• Walnuts
• Cinnamon
• Clove
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Flour
• Raspberries

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees
2. Set oven rack to second-lowest position
3. Grease a 13 x 9-inch baking pan, and line bottom with parchment paper to prevent bottom of cake from over-browning)
4. Mix together all streusel ingredients
5. Set aside
6. With an electric mixer, cream butter and sugar until fluffy, about 4-5 minutes, until no sugar granules remain
7. Mix in eggs and vanilla
8. Beat until blended, about 1-2 minutes, scraping bowl often
9. Stir in the sour cream
10. Beat until well blended, about 1 minute
11. In a small bowl, sift together the flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt
12. Add to the creamed mixture, beating until blended
13. Gently fold in the raspberries or blueberries with a spatula, if you are using fresh berries, careful not to break the berries while mixing in, especially if using fresh raspberries
14. Spoon about two-thirds of the cake batter into the prepared baking pan
15. Sprinkle half of the streusel mixture on top
16. Then dollup the remaining cake batter on top of the streusel mix
17. Then sprinkle the remaining streusel mixture on top
18. Bake for about 1 hour, or until cake tests done<|endoftext|>
