What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>2 Bean Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Cooking oil
• Carrot
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Oregano
• White kidney beans
• Red kidney beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Beef broth
• Green chilies
• Frozen corn
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Cook onions, carrots and garlic in cooking oil until tender-crisp
2. In a large deep skillet, brown meat and drain fat
3. Add cooked vegetable mixture and seasonings to cooked meat
4. Cook and stir 1 minute
5. Add remaining ingredients except for cheese
6. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low and simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Serve topped with cheese<|endoftext|>
