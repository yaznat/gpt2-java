Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Moose Or Venison Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground moose
• Beer
• Onions
• Tomato sauce
• Hot pepper
• Tomato paste
• Vinegar
• Chili powder
• Tabasco sauce
• Bacon
• Cumin
• Red pepper
• Msg
• Garlic cloves
• Oregano
• Worcestershire sauce
• Bacon drippings
• Black pepper
• Kidney beans

Steps:
1. In a large chili pot, combine: tomato sauce, tomato paste, beer, chopped peppers, chili powder, vinegar, tabasco sauce, cumin, crushed red pepper, oregano, worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper
2. Bring to simmer
3. In a large frying pan cook bacon until crisp
4. Add bacon and 2 tb drippings to chili pot
5. Brown moose meat, onions, garlic and msg and add to chili pot
6. Cover pot and simmer for one hour
7. Add beans to chili pot and simmer 1 / 2 hour more
8. If you like your chili thinner add tomato juice<|endoftext|>
