Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Salt
• Mayonnaise
• Green chilies
• Fresh cilantro
• Green onions
• Fresh lime juice
• Fresh garlic clove
• Black pepper
• Maple bacon

Steps:
1. Cook potatoes in boiling water to cover along with 1 tsp of the salt until fork tender, about 15 minutes or so
2. Drain and cool
3. Cut potatoes into bite sized chunks
4. Stir together the mayonnaise, chilies, chopped cilantro, green onions, lime juice, garlic, black pepper, and remaining 1 teaspoons salt in a large bowl
5. Add potatoes and toss to coat
6. Cover and chill at least 4 hours
7. Just before serving, stir in bacon crumbles and garnish with additional fresh cilantro<|endoftext|>
