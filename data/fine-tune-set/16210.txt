Give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Jack Quesadillas With Cranberry Salsa

Ingredients:
• Whole berry cranberry sauce
• Fresh cilantro
• Green onions
• Fresh lime juice
• Ground cumin
• Anjou pear
• Jalapeno pepper
• Green onion
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Cooked turkey
• Nonfat sour cream

Steps:
1. To make the salsa: combine the salsa ingredients in a bowl
2. Cover and chill
3. To make the quesadillas: heat a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
4. Spray pan with nonstick cooking spray
5. Add in sliced onions
6. Stir / saute for 3 minutes or until tender
7. Remove onions from pan
8. Lower heat to medium
9. Sprinkle 2 tablespoons cheese over each of 4 tortillas
10. Top each cheese-covered tortilla with 1 / 4 of the onions, 1 / 2 cup turkey, 2 tablespoons cheese and 1 tortilla
11. Recoat pan with cooking spray
12. Place over medium heat
13. Add 1 quesadilla to pan
14. Cook 2 minutes on each side or until lightly browned and cheese is melted
15. Repeat process with remaining quesadillas
16. Cut each quesadilla into 6 wedges
17. Serve with cranberry salsa and sour cream<|endoftext|>
