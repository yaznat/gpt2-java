Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Ribbon
• Apricot preserves
• Salt

Steps:

2. Add the green beans and carrot ribbons
3. Cover the saucepan and cook 5 minutes, or until the veggies are crisp-tender
4. Drain
5. Meanwhile, in microwave on high power, heat the apricot preserves 30 seconds or until it becomes liquid
6. Place the green beans and carrots in serving bowl
