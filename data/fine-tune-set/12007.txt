Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Turbinado sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Honey
• Water
• Vanilla
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Melt butter in microwave or on stovetop
3. Mix together dry ingredients
4. Add the rest of the ingredients and mix until the dough forms a ball
5. Add a little water if needed
6. Line two jelly roll pans with parchment paper or silpat
7. Put half the dough on each pan and gently flatten out with a rolling pin, covering the pan
8. Gently score the crackers into rectangles with a pizza or pasta cutter
9. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until crisp and just beginning to brown
10. Let cool on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
