Ingredients:
• Hamburger
• Pinto beans
• Cheese
• Onion
• Southwest seasoning
• Jalapeno
• Burrito-size flour tortillas
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Brown hamburger, onion, jalapeo and seasoning
3. Add beans and simmer for 15-20 minutes uncovered
4. Pour off any juice
5. To assemble:
6. Place 2 hefty spoonfuls on one end of a tortilla
7. Cover the mixture with cheese - as much as you want
8. Roll up burrito style
9. Place on a cookie sheet, seam down
10. Make sure the burritos don't touch
11. Place the burritos in the oven and bake for 20 minutes, or until the tortillas are light brown and the cheese is bubbly
12. Serve with your favorite salsa
13. These are good hot or cold!<|endoftext|>
