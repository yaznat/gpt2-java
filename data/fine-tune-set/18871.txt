Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Coconut milk
• Sugar
• Palm sugar
• Pure vanilla extract
• Crushed pineapple

Steps:
1. Pre heat oven to 350 f
2. Place eggs, coconut milk, sugar, palm sugar, vanilla and pineapple together in a blender, and puree until smooth
3. Pour mixture into small greased custard bowls and place on a baking sheet with a rim
4. Pour hot water in baking sheet so the custard bowls have water 1 / 3 of the way up their sides
5. Bake for an hour or until a knife comes out clean
6. Cool completely and refrigerate until ready to serve
7. Unmold onto plates and serve cold - i think it would be pretty to serve with fresh fruit and mint as a garnish<|endoftext|>
