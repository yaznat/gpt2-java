Steps:
1. Make sure all spices & herbs are crumbled
2. Combine 1 cup all-purpose flour, whole wheat flour, undissolved yeast, sugar, dried onions, all spices and herbs and salt in a large bowl
3. Heat water and butter until very warm
4. Stir into flour mixture
5. Beat 2 minutes at medium speed, scraping bowl occasionally
6. Stir in remaining all-purpose flour to make stiff batter
7. Cover and let rest 10 minutes
8. Turn batter in greased 1 1 / 2-quart casserole
9. Smooth top of dough in casserole with floured hands
10. Cover
11. Rise in warm, draft-free place until doubled in size, about 30 minutes
12. Brush beaten egg on loaf
13. Sprinkle with celery seed and dried onion
14. Bake at 375f for 35 minutes or until done
