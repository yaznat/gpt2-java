What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Beefy Burritos

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Chili powder
• Cheddar cheese
• Diced green chilies
• Flour tortillas
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Chunky salsa
• Milk

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef with onion and drain
2. Add chili powder and mix well
3. Spoon 1 / 4-1 / 2 cup beef mix and 1 / 4 cup cheese in center of a tortilla
4. Roll up and arrange in a greased 9x13 pan
5. In a bowl, combine soup, salsa, milk, green chiles and mix well
6. Pour mix over burritos
7. Top with remaining cheese
8. Bake at 350 degrees for 40 minutes<|endoftext|>
