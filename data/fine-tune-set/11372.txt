Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>4 H Applesauce Nut Bread

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Butter
• Sugar
• Applesauce
• Baking powder
• Egg
• Baking soda
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat over to 350 and grease 1 pound loaf pan
2. Mix dry ingredients
3. Beat egg, add melted butter, and applesauce
4. Add to dry ingredients
5. Fold in nuts
6. Pour into loaf pan and bake 1 hour<|endoftext|>
