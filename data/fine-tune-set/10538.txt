What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Algerian Tli Tli B Djedj Pasta With Chicken

Ingredients:
• Whole chicken
• Canned chick-peas
• Ghee
• Black pepper
• Cinnamon
• Tomato puree
• Onions
• Garlic clove
• Ras el hanout spice mix
• Salt
• Eggs
• Orzo pasta
• Chicken stock cube
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Cut chicken up into serving sizes
2. Brown in a large pan with the ghee, finely chopped onions, cinnamon, ras el hanout & pepper
3. Add 1 cup water & cook for 10 minutes
4. Add chick peas & add enough water to cover
5. Tomato puree & the stock cube
6. Cover & cook on medium heat for 1 hour & a half in a pan or 40 mins in the pressure cooker on med-high
7. If cooking in a pan, you may need to add a little extra water
8. Whilst the sauce is cooking, prepare the pasta & eggs
9. Hard boil the eggs, then peel & cut into halves
10. In a bowl, mix the pasta with 1 / 2 cup water & a tiny bit of olive or sunflower oil
11. Place in a steamer & steam for 15 minutes
12. Remove from steamer & separate the pasta with a little water
13. Put the pasta in a large pan & slowly spoon in a little sauce at a time until the pasta is fully cooked & the sauce has been absorbed nicely - the pasta should be moist with a little sauce coating it - not dry
14. Place pasta in a large dish / platter & top with the chicken pieces, egg halves & a some more sauce
15. Serve immediately with a green salad & crusty bread<|endoftext|>
