Give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Spirits

Ingredients:
• Apple juice
• Cinnamon sticks
• Honey
• White rum
• Lemon juice
• Nutmeg
• Allspice

Steps:
1. Pour 1 gallon of apple juice into a large kettle or pot
2. Put in 3 to 8 cinnamon sticks and nutmeg
3. Heat over fire until good and hot add 1 / 2 cup of honey and lemon juice, stir and heat until honey is dissolved
4. Remove from heat and add 1 / 2 fifth of good white rum, stir
5. You can drink it hot or allow it to cool
6. Tastes just like you are eatin apple pie!
7. Beware this drink can be dangerous
8. It will sneak up on ya quick
9. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
