What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>The Ultimate Brownie

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate squares
• Butter
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Salt
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven to 365 degrees f and grease a 9x13 pan
2. Melt the chocolate and butter in pan over low heat and set aside
3. Just make sure it doesn't burn!
4. In a large bowl, beat the eggs, sugar, and vanilla at high speed
5. Doing this for 10 minutes would be ideal but your brownies won't be ruined if you don't
6. Mix in the chocolate / butter mix, flour, and salt until just combined
7. Fold in the nuts
8. Pour into the prepared pan and bake for 32-37 minutes<|endoftext|>
