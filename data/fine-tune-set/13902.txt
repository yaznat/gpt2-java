What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>B B Q Lamb Riblets

Ingredients:
• Lamb rib chops
• Diced tomatoes
• Onion soup mix
• Apple cider vinegar
• Molasses
• Corn oil
• Dry mustard
• Instant minced garlic
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Place riblets in large roasting pan
3. Combine remaining ingredients in saucepan and heat to boiling
4. Simmer 5 minutes
5. Pour sauce over riblets
6. Cook or 1 1 / 2 hours in 350-degree oven<|endoftext|>
