Ingredients:
• Barbecued chicken
• Peanut oil
• Eggs
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red chilies
• Broccoli florets
• Shiitake mushrooms
• Carrot
• Snow peas
• Green onions
• Cooked rice
• Light soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Ketjap manis

Steps:
1. Heat 2 teaspoon of peanut oil in a wok, add half the egg mixture, swirl to make a thin omelette
2. Remove omelette from wok, roll up then cut into thin strips
3. Repeat process using another 2 teaspoons of oil and remaining egg
4. Heat the remaining oil in wok, stir fry onion, garlic and chili, until onion softens, add broccoli, mushrooms and carrot, stir fry 1 miunte
5. Add sauces, snow peas, green onion, chicken and rice, stir-fry until vegetables are just tender and the dish is hot, add omelette, toss gently
6. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
