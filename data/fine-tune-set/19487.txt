Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Butter
• Cheddar cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Bacon
• Chives
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f, and wait for it to reach temperature
2. Wash potatoes and stab them with a fork or knife on one side 2-3 times
3. Place them on middle rack of oven with cuts facing up
4. Bake for 1
5. 5 hours
6. Remove hot potatoes and place them on a microwave safe dish
7. Cut them length wise about an inch and half deep
8. Squeeze ends to open a large hole in top
9. Use fork to mash up the potato flesh and add 2-tablespoons butter to each potato
10. Do not remove the flesh from the potato
11. Divide crumbled bacon into 4 piles and add to potato flesh
12. Mix shredded cheeses together
13. Use a 1 / 4 cup of cheese in each potato opening
14. Microwave potatoes 1-2 minutes, or until cheese melts
15. Top with 1 ounce of sour cream on each potato
16. Top with chives
17. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
