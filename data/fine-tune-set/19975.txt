Give me a omelette recipe.<|endoftext|>Fresh Tasting Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Tomatoes
• Smoked ham
• Button mushrooms
• Butter
• Fresh basil
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In small skillet, melt 1 pat butter
2. Add ham, tomato and mushrooms
3. Cook for about 3 minutes until mushroom softens and season with salt and pepper and basil
4. Crack eggs into a bowl and add a splash of milk
5. Mix well
6. Heat a 10 inch non-stick skillet over medium flame
7. Add 2 pats of butter
8. Add the eggs and spread by
9. Let the eggs set over medium heat
10. After 30 seconds or so, start shaking the pan occasionally so the eggs slide around and don't stick
11. When the eggs are set, add the filling in a line down the middle of the pan
12. Use a plastic spatula to fold a third of the omelet over the filling and remove the pan
13. Season lightly with salt and pepper and garnish with basil
14. Add some toast and you're done<|endoftext|>
