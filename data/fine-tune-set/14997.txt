Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Nyonya Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Long grain rice
• Prawns
• Red chilies
• Shallots
• Garlic cloves
• Sesame oil
• Shrimp paste
• Light soy sauce
• Cooking oil
• Dark soy sauce
• Egg
• Spring onion

Steps:
1. Soak the prawns in water for 30 mins, drain them
2. Chop the prawns
3. Pound the shallots set aside
4. Pound the garlic and set aside
5. Pound the chillies, set aside
6. Heat a deep frying pan and add the sesame oil
7. When the oil is hot fry the shallots and garlic
8. Reduce the heat and add the chillies and prawns, stir 30 seconds
9. Add the shrimp paste, stir till it dissolves
10. Add the cooked rice and the soy sauces, stir to mix the ingredients
11. Set the pan aside
12. In a wok, heat 3 tbsp oil, when hot add the egg and cover it with the rice, cook till the egg is just cooked
13. Fry the whole, mixing the egg into the rice
14. Add the spring onion and season with salt to taste<|endoftext|>
