Ingredients:
• Whole black peppercorns
• Beef flank steak
• Fresh mushrooms
• Green onion
• Water
• Beef bouillon granules
• Plain low-fat yogurt
• All-purpose flour
• Dijon mustard
• Asparagus spears

Steps:
1. Coarsley crack the black peppers
2. Rub half of the peppers into each side of the steak
3. Place steak on the unheated rack of a broiler pan
4. Broil 3 inches from heat for 6 minutes
5. Turn steak over and broil for 6 to 8 minutes more or until steak is to desired doneness
6. Meanwhile, for sauce, in a saucepan combine mushrooms, green onion, water, and bouillon granules
7. Cover and cook over medium heat about 5 minutes or until mushrooms are tender
8. Stir together yogurt, flour, and mustard
9. Stir yogurt mixture into mushroom mixture
10. Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly
11. Then, cook and stir for 1 minute more
12. To serve, thinly slice steak diagonally across the grain
13. Arrange steak slices ad asparagus on dinner plates
14. Serve with sauce<|endoftext|>
