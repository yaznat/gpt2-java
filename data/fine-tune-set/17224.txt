What's a recipe for tea?<|endoftext|>Apple Spiced Honey Iced Tea

Ingredients:
• Water
• Orange pekoe tea bags
• Honey
• Apple juice
• Cinnamon
• Clove
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon sticks
• Ice cube

Steps:
1. Steep tea bags in hot water for 3 - 4 minutes
2. Remove tea bags and stir in honey, juice and spices
3. Mix gently until the honey is dissolved
4. Cool on counter and then chill for at least 4 hours
5. Stir and serve in a tall frosted glasses over ice with a cinnamon stick<|endoftext|>
