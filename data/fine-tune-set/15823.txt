Ingredients:
• Tortilla
• Mashed potatoes
• Cheddar cheese
• Eggs
• Cooked bacon
• Salsa
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Warm a large skillet over medium heat
2. Butter can be added to the skillet to help brown the tortilla and give the outside more flavor and crispness, but i always just warm it in a dry skillet
3. Spread mashed potatoes down the center of tortilla
4. Place the tortilla in warm skillet, mashed potato side up
5. Sprinkle cheese on potatoes
6. Sprinkle bacon pieces on cheese
7. Spread scrambled eggs on top of bacon
8. Top with salsa and sour cream
9. If you like your tortilla soft, slide it out of the pan onto a plate as soon as it is warm
10. Roll and eat
11. If you prefer your tortilla crunchy, continue to cook until it starts to brown, slide out of the pan onto a plate and roll imediatly<|endoftext|>
