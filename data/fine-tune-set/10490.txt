Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>Mexican Pasta

Ingredients:
• Fettuccine pasta
• Cheddar cheese soup
• Milk
• Picante sauce
• Black olives
• Jalapeno

Steps:
1. Boil pasta as directed on package
2. Drain and put in large serving bowl
3. Mix picante sauce, milk, and cheese soup in a smaller bowl until creamy
4. Heat in microwave until warm
5. Mix sauce and pasta together and garnish with olives and jalepenos if desired<|endoftext|>
