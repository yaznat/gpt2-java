Can you give me a pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Vanilla Pudding

Ingredients:
• Almond milk
• Cornstarch
• Agave nectar
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Mix a little of the milk with the cornstarch to form a paste
2. Heat the rest of the milk gently to a simmer, then add in the cornstarch paste, agave and vanilla
3. Stir until thickened and serve with fruit<|endoftext|>
