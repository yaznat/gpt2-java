Ingredients:
• Tilapia fillets
• Onion
• Zucchini
• Sliced mushrooms
• Butter
• Tarragon
• Salt and pepper
• Paprika

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. Spray a nonreactive pan with non-stick cooking spray
3. Add fish filets and sprinkle with salt and pepper
4. Melt butter over medium heat
5. Add onions, mushrooms, zucchini, tarragon, and salt and pepper to taste
6. Sautee for five to seven minutes or until vegetables are tender
7. Top fish with mushroom-zucchini mixture and sprinkle with paprika
8. Bake, covered, for twelve to twenty minutes, depending on thickness of filets
9. Fish will flake easily with a fork when cooked through<|endoftext|>
