Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Lemon juice
• Cornflour
• Sweet chili sauce
• Garlic clove
• Coriander
• Carrot
• Green beans
• Fish fillets

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 200 c with a baking tray in the centre of the oven
2. Measure the soy sauce and lemon juice into a small bowl
3. Add the cornflour and mix until the are no lumps, then stir in the sweet chilli sauce, garlic and chopped coriander
4. Cut the carrot into thin julienne's and top and tail the beans or trim the asparagus spears and cut them diagonally into 5cm pieces
5. Place a 30x40 cm piece of baking paper on a dinner plate and place half the vegetables in the middle
6. Place one serving of the fish on top of the vegetables, then drizzle the stack with half the sauce
7. Fold up the edges of the baking paper sheets and seal loosely with paperclips
8. Repeat the process on another sheet of paper or foil
9. Carefully lift the parcels onto the hot baking tray in the oven, then bake at 200 c for 12 minutes
10. Remove the tray from the oven and allow the parcels to cool for a few minutes before serving with rice or noodles<|endoftext|>
