What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Deviled Lamb Chops Microwave

Ingredients:
• Lamb chops
• Fruit chutney
• Curry powder
• Brown sugar
• Soy sauce
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. Remove fat from chops and place in a single layer in a shallow dish
2. Combine remaining ingredients and pour over lamb
3. Cook for 5 minutes on high or until chops are tender<|endoftext|>
