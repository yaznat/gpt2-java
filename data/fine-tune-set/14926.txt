What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Fried Rice With Cilantro

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic cloves
• Green chilies
• Chicken breasts
• Cooked jasmine rice
• White sugar
• Fish sauce
• Soy sauce
• Green onions
• Basil leaves
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a wok or large skillet, over medium-high heat
2. Fry the garlic until golden, then add the chili pepper and chicken meat, and stir-fry until cooked through
3. When the chicken is cooked, add the rice, sugar, fish sauce, and soy sauce
4. Cook over medium heat, stirring gently
5. When the mixture is well blended, stir in the green onions, basil, and cilantro
6. Cook for 1 more minute, then serve hot<|endoftext|>
