Ingredients:
• Sesame oil
• Fresh garlic
• Dried red pepper flakes
• Broccoli florets
• Chicken broth
• Salt and pepper
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Green onions
• Hoisin sauce
• Oyster sauce
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. In a large skillet heat about 1-1 / 2 tablespoons sesame oil over medium-high heat
2. Add in chopped garlic and dryed chili flakes
3. Saute for about 1 minute
4. Add in broccoli florets and 4 tablespoons chicken broth
5. Cover skillet and cook until the broccoli is just crisp-tender transfer to a bowl
6. Add in 1 tablespoon more sesame oil to the skillet
7. Season the chicken strips with salt and pepper, then add to the hot skillet with green onions
8. Saute until the chicken is cooked through
9. Add in remaining 2 tablespoons chicken broth, hoisin sauce and oyster sauce
10. Add in the broccoli florets back to the skillet
11. Toss to combine until heated through
12. Transfer to a platter or large bowl and sprinkle with sesame seeds
13. Serve with cooked rice
14. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
