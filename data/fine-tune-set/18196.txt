Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Baked Simple German Pancake Auflaufen

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Flour
• Eggs
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Pam cooking spray
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Mix milk and flour
2. Add beaten eggs, a pinch of salt, and vanilla
3. Pour batter into sprayed or well buttered 9"x13" pyrex dish
4. Sprinkle with nutmeg
5. Bake in preheated 425 degree f oven for 15-20 minutes depending on altitude
6. Serve with powdered sugar, lemon wedges, sauted apples, or of course, good ole pure maple syrup
