Can you give me a pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Big John S Bread Pudding With Whisky Sauce

Ingredients:
• French bread
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Butter
• Milk
• Pecans
• Canned pineapple
• Coconut
• Egg yolks
• Powdered sugar
• Canadian rye whisky
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Add sugar, vanilla and spices to eggs and beat
3. Add milk and butter, beat again
4. Stir in pineapple, coconut and pecans
5. Place bread in a large buttered baking pan and pour beaten mixture over top
6. Let stand for 15 minutes
7. Bake until the center becomes firm
8. Sauce: cream butter and sugar over medium heat until all butter is absorbed
9. Remove from heat and blend in egg yolk and vanilla
10. Pour in whiskey while stirring constantly
11. Sauce will thicken as it cools
12. Serve over warm bread pudding<|endoftext|>
