Ingredients:
• Crawfish
• Water
• Onion
• Carrots
• Parsley
• Celery
• Thyme
• Butter
• Flour
• Cracker crumbs
• Milk
• Crawfish meat
• Stock
• Salt and pepper
• Egg

Steps:
1. Soak crawfish in cold water for 30 minutes
2. Wash carefully, using a brush
3. Place in stockpot with water, vegetables and thyme
4. Remove crawfish, reserving stock
5. Remove all meat from head and bodies of crawfish to use in the stuffing
6. Reserve the head shells
7. Drain stock
8. For the stuffing: moisten cracker crumbs with milk
9. Add next 7 ingredients and simmer a few minutes
10. Cool slightly and add egg
11. Fill head shells with the stuffing mixture
12. Dredge with flour and brown in butter
13. Drain on paper towels and keep hot
14. Blend 1 1 / 2 tablespoons butter with 1 tablespoon flour and cook until toasted
15. Add strained stock gradually and cook for about ten minutes, stirring often to prevent lumping
16. Season, add stuffed heads and serve<|endoftext|>
