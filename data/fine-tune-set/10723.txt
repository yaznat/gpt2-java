Ingredients:
• Bow tie pasta
• Bacon
• Cream cheese
• Butter
• Italian seasoning
• Milk
• Parmesan cheese
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Drain
3. Keep warm
4. Cook bacon according to package directions
5. Drain well, and crumble
6. Beat cream cheese, butter, and italian seasoning in a medium-size microwave-safe bowl at low speed with an electric mixer until smooth
7. Gradually add milk, beating until mixture is smooth
8. Microwave cream cheese mixture at high 3 minutes or until thoroughly heated, whisking every 30 seconds
9. Add salt and pepper to taste
10. Pour cream cheese mixture over warm pasta, tossing to coat
11. Top with crumbled bacon and 1 / 2 cup grated parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
