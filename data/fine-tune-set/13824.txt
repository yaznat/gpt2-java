What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Spiced Pork Rissoles

Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Red chilies
• Lemongrass
• Green onions
• Coriander leaves
• Pork mince
• Egg
• Olive oil flavored cooking spray
• Lebanese cucumbers
• Jasmine rice
• Sweet chili sauce

Steps:
1. Combine garlic, ginger, chilli, lemongrass, green onions and coriander in a food processor
2. Process until finely chopped
3. Transfer to a bowl
4. Add mince and egg
5. Season with salt and pepper
6. Using clean hands, mix until well combined
7. Using 2 heaped tablespoons mixture at a time, roll mixture into 16 balls
8. Flatten slightly
9. Place rissoles on a plate
10. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes, if time permits
11. Preheat a greased barbecue plate on medium-low heat
12. Spray both sides rissoles with oil
13. Cook, turning occasionally, for 12 to 15 minutes or until cooked through
14. Using a vegetable peeler, peel long, thin strips from cucumbers
15. Spoon rice into bowls
16. Top with rissoles, cucumber and sweet chilli
17. Serve<|endoftext|>
