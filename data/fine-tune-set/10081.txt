What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Ann S Close To Wendy S Style Chili Recipe

Ingredients:
• Hamburger
• Dark red kidney beans
• Chili beans
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Crushed tomatoes
• Frozen vegetables
• Water
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Bay leaf
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Brown the hamburger and drain the grease
2. Add seasonings and frozen vegetables when frozen vegetables start to heat up
3. Add the rest of the ingredients
4. Simmer the longer the better i like to simmer at least a couple of hours
5. Remove the bay leaf before serving<|endoftext|>
