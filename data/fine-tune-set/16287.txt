Ingredients:
• Marinated artichokes
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Cut your bacon strips in half
2. Wrap a half slice of bacon around each quarter of an artichoke
3. Spear with a toothpick to hold it together
4. Place in a foil-lined jelly roll pan
5. Drizzle a little of the extra artichoke oil on top
6. Bake at 425 for 12-15 minutes
7. Put on a plate and serve<|endoftext|>
