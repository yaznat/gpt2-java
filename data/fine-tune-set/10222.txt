Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>Black And White Bean Chicken Chili

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Flour
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Salt
• Milk
• Chicken broth
• White shoepeg corn
• White beans
• Black beans
• Cooked chicken
• Green chilies
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Heat oil in dutch oven or large saucepan over medium-high heat
2. Add onions and garlic
3. Cook until onions are tender
4. Stir in flour, chili powder, cumin and salt
5. Gradually stir in chicken broth until thickened
6. Add 2 cups of milk and continue stirring
7. Add remaining ingredients except cilantro
8. Stir to combine
9. Bring to a low boil, but watch for scorching of milk
10. Reduce heat
11. Simmer 15 minutes
12. Stir in chopped cilantro
13. Garnish with crushed taco chips, sour cream, fresh cilantro and hot sauce if you like<|endoftext|>
