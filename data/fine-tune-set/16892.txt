What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Ambrosia Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Navel oranges
• Red grapefruit
• Fresh pineapple
• Orange liqueur
• Turbinado sugar
• Sparkling rose wine
• Coconut
• Maraschino cherries

Steps:
1. Toss together oranges, grapefruit, and pineapple
2. Set aside
3. Pour orange liqueur into a shallow saucer
4. Dip rims of 8 glasses in liqueur
5. Dip rims in sugar, and let stand 30 minutes to dry completely
6. Spoon fruit mixture evenly into glasses
7. Add ros wine, filling three-fourths full
8. Top with grated coconut and cherries
9. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
