What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>1 001 Nights Cake

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Baking powder
• Flour
• Honey
• Slivered almonds
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Butter a 8"x8" square glass baking dish
2. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees f
3. Beat the eggs, sugar and vanilla together until the color"lightens" slightly
4. Add melted butter and mix well
5. Combine the flour and baking powder, then add to the egg mixture
6. Mixing gently but thoroughly
7. Pour into the prepared dish and bake 12 minutes
8. Meanwhile, prepare the topping by melting the butter in a small saucepan, then adding the sugar, honey, almonds and cinnamon
9. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly until thickened a bit
10. After the 12 minutes of baking, take cake out of the oven and gently pour the topping onto the cake
11. Return cake to the oven and bake for another 15 to 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
