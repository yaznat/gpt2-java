Give me a recipe for pudding.<|endoftext|>Almond Pudding

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Long grain rice
• Ground cardamom
• Sugar
• Almonds
• Rose water
• Ground nutmeg
• Strawberries

Steps:
1. In a heavy 3 quart saucepan, combine the milk or light cream, rice, and cardamom
2. Bring to boiling
3. Reduce heat
4. Cook, uncovered over low heat, stirring occasionally about 1 hour or until the milk mixture is reduced to about 2 cups
5. Remove from heat
6. Stir in the sugar and chopped almonds or pistachio nuts, continue stirring till the sugar has dissolved
7. Cool for 30 minutes
8. Stir pudding
9. Stir in the rose water, if desired
10. Spoon the mixture into 4 to 6 sherbet or dessert dishes
11. Chill in the refrigerator
12. To serve, sprinkle with ground nutmeg and garnish each serving with 2 whole strawberries<|endoftext|>
