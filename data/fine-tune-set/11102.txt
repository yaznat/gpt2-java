Give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Awesome Garlic Chicken Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Buns
• Garlic powder
• Butter
• Parsley
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce
• Mayonnaise
• Black olives
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to broil
2. Cut the crusty buns in half
3. Place the butter / margarine in a small bowl
4. Mix the garlic powder and parsley into it
5. Spread the garlic butter mixture on the crusty buns
6. Toast the buns under the broiler for a couple of minutes, until golden brown
7. Remove and layer the chicken, tomatoes, lettuce, olives and mayo onto the buns
8. Enjoy while still warm!<|endoftext|>
