What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Arugula Pesto Pasta

Ingredients:
• Arugula
• Capers
• Garlic cloves
• Parmesan cheese
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper
• Spaghetti
• Cherry tomatoes

Steps:
1. Boil spaghetti in water as directed, drain
2. Add arugula, capers, cheese, oil, garlic, salt& pepper in processor, pulse until a paste is formed
3. Add to hot spaghetti, top with tomatoes<|endoftext|>
