What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Anadama Batter Bread

Ingredients:
• Dry yeast
• Water
• Boiling water
• Yellow cornmeal
• Butter
• Molasses
• Salt
• Egg
• All-purpose flour

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, proof the yeast in the warm water
2. In large bowl, combine the boiling water, cornmeal, butter, molasses, and salt
3. Add the egg and 1 1 / 2 cups of the flour
4. Beat until well combined
5. Add the rest of the flour and yeast, and beat again
6. Spoon the dough into a 9-inch bread pan
7. Let rise for about 1 hour
8. Preheat the oven at 375f and bake for 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
