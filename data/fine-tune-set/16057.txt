Please give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>California Roll Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Imitation crabmeat
• Low-fat cream cheese
• English cucumber
• Avocado
• Low-fat mayonnaise
• Sesame oil
• Soy sauce
• Chili sauce

Steps:
1. Combine the last four ingredients in a small bowl and mix well
2. Set aside
3. On each tortilla, spread as much cream cheese as you like
4. Leave about half an inch around the edge to prevent a mess in the pan
5. On four tortillas, layer shredded crabmeat, cucumber, and avocado
6. Top with the four remaining tortillas
7. On an ungreased griddle or skillet, toast the quesadillas for about 90 seconds per side
8. You just want to melt the cream cheese, not heat the avocado and cucumber
9. Cut the quesadillas into wedges, drizzle with spicy mayo, and serve
10. Serves 4 as a meal, 8-10 as an appetizer<|endoftext|>
