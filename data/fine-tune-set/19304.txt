Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Confectioners' sugar
• Heavy whipping cream
• Oreo cookies

Steps:
1. Place cream cheese and confectioners sugar in a mixer with whisk attachment, and beat on medium speed until smooth
2. Reduce to low speed and slowly pour heavy cream into bowl with cream cheese
3. Place mixing bowl in freezer for 45 minutes, stirring occasionally until thick, but still pourable
4. Rough chop 5 oreos
5. Place other 5 oreos in a food processor until fine crumbs
6. Place chopped oreos in molds and pour in cheesecake mix
7. Place in freezer for 3 to 4 hours
8. Unmold popsicles and roll in oreo crumbs<|endoftext|>
