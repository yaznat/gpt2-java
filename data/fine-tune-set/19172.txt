Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Heavy cream
• Pure vanilla extract
• Cinnamon
• Fresh figs
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Dissolve sugar in 1 / 4 cup water in a heavy-bottomed saucepan
2. Place over high heat
3. Wash down sides of pan with pastry brush
4. Cook, without stirring, swirling pan occasionally, until an amber-colored caramel forms, about 8-10 minutes
5. Watch it carefully
6. Remove from heat
7. Carefully stir in cream
8. Reheat until smooth
9. Add vanilla extract and cinnamon
10. Mix well
11. Remove from heat
12. Let stand until cool
13. Scoop ice cream into serving bowls and arrange figs on top
14. Drizzle with caramel sauce<|endoftext|>
