Ingredients:
• Milk
• Cocoa
• Light corn syrup
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Whipping cream
• Amaretto liqueur

Steps:
1. In a saucepan combine 2 cups of milk, cocoa and corn syrup
2. Bring to boil over medium heat, stirring constantly
3. Cool
4. Beat eggs until foamy and gradually beat is sugar
5. Add cocoa mixture
6. Stir in remaining 2 cups of milk, whipping cream and liqueur blending well
7. Chill serval hours or overnight
8. Freeze according to manufacturer's directions<|endoftext|>
