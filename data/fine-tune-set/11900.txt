Can you give me a curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Carrot Curry

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Shallots
• Hot green chili pepper
• Carrot
• Cumin
• Ground coriander
• Ground fennel
• Cayenne pepper
• Turmeric
• Salt
• Coconut milk
• Curry leaves

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan heat oil over medium heat
2. Add shallots and chile, cook stirring for 2 minutes
3. Add carrots, spices and curry leaves and cook another 3-4 minutes
4. Add coconut milk and bring to a simmer
5. Cover, reduce heat to low, and cook 20-25 minutes until carrots are tender<|endoftext|>
