Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Abby S Rice Pudding

Ingredients:
• White rice
• Water
• Whole milk
• Sugar
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan add rice and water
2. Cook until the rice in tender
3. Add milk and simmer 60 min or until the milk is almost cooked out
4. Remove from heat and add sugar and cinnamon to taste
5. Place in fridge for 4 hours<|endoftext|>
