Ingredients:
• Pork sausage
• Onions
• Green bell pepper
• Celery
• Long grain rice
• Sliced almonds
• Soup mix
• Boiling water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f degrees
2. In a dutch oven, over medium-high heat, add sausage, onions, pepper and celery
3. When sausage is browned, stir in rice and almonds
4. In a separate bowl, combine dry soup mix with boiling water
5. Stir well
6. Stir soup mixture into dutch oven and combine
7. Cover casserole and bake in preheated oven for 40 minutes
8. Uncover and bake for an additional 50 minutes
9. Serves a lot!<|endoftext|>
