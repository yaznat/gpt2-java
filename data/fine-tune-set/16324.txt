Ingredients:
• Hot dog
• Bacon
• Hot dog bun

Steps:
1. Wrap the hot dog with the bacon, and place in a hot skillet or you can put it in the oven on 350f
2. Cook until bacon is crispy
3. Remove from skillet
4. Open the bun and place, in the skillet, let it brown, and place hot dog on the bun<|endoftext|>
