Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Dry active yeast
• Water
• Milk
• Margarine

Steps:
1. In a large bowl thoroughly mix 2 cups of flour, sugar, salt and undissolved yeast
2. Combine water, milk and margarine in a sauce pan
3. Heat over low heat until liquids are warm
4. Gradually add to dry mixture and beat 2 minutes with a mixer, at medium speed scrapping the sides of the bowl
5. Stir in enough of the remainig flour to make a soft dough
6. Turn dough out on a lightly foured board and kneed until elastic and smooth
7. About 8 minutes
8. Place in a greased bowl
9. Turn dough over and cover
10. Let rise in a warm place intil doubled
11. About 1 hour
12. Punch down dough
13. Turn onto a lightly floured board
14. Cover and let rest for 15 minutes
15. Divide dough in half
16. Shape each into a loaf
17. Place each half in a greased 8 1 / 3 x 4 1 / 2 x 2 1 / 2 inch loaf pan
18. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled
19. About 1 hour
20. Bake in 400f oven for 25 to 30 minutes
21. Remove from pan and let cool on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
