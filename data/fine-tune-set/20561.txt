Ingredients:
• French bread
• Eggs
• Milk
• Dry mustard
• Onion powder
• Salt
• Worcestershire sauce
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese
• Fresh tomato
• Mushroom

Steps:
1. Butter a 13 x 9 inch casserole dish
2. Arrange bread cubes in casserole dish
3. Sprinkle with shredded cheddar cheese
4. Beat together the eggs, milk, dry mustard, onion powder, salt and worcestershire sauce
5. Pour over the bread and cheese in the casserole dish
6. Sprinkle with crumbled bacon, mushrooms and chopped tomatoes
7. Cover and chill overnight
8. Bake uncovered for 1 hour or until set<|endoftext|>
