What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Beer Marinated Grilled New York Strip Steaks With Pepper Sauce

Ingredients:
• New york strip steaks
• White wine
• Shallot
• Peppercorn blend
• Chicken broth
• Canned beef broth
• Whipping cream
• Salt
• Beer
• Brown sugar
• Lime juice
• Onion
• Garlic
• Worcestershire sauce
• Grainy mustard
• Olive oil
• Fresh ginger
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Place the striploin steaks in a large shallow baking dish and prick holes in the beef all over and both sides using a fork
2. In a bowl whisk together beer, brown sugar, lime juice, onion, garlic, worcestershire sauce, mustard, oil, ginger and cayenne pepper or tabasco
3. Pour the marinade over the beef, turning the beef over to coat well on both sides
4. Cover and refrigerate 24 hours
5. For the peppercorn sauce
6. Bring the wine, shallot and crushed peppercorns to a boil in a medium saucepan
7. Simmer until the mixture is reduced by half
8. Add in the chicken stock and beef broth
9. Boil until reduced to 2 cups
10. Add in whipping cream and cook until the sauce coats the spoon
11. Remove the steaks for the marinade and let sit out for 45 minutes at room temperature before grilling, the longer the better,
12. Prepare the grill to medium-high heat
13. Season the steaks with salt and pepper
14. Grill to desired doneness
15. Transfer the sauce to plates and drizzle with sauce
16. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
