Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Vegetable oil
• Sweet paprika
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Italian seasoning
• Garlic powder
• Fresh parsley
• Fresh parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Scrub potatoes, cut in half, and into 8 wedges per half
2. Preheat oven to 450f
3. Toss all ingredients together except parmesan and parsley, mixing until potatoes are evenly coated
4. Spread potatoes in an even layer on a greased or nonstick-foil lined baking sheet
5. Bake 35-45 minutes or until potates are cooked through and crisp up, stirring potatoes several times during cooking to ensure even browning
6. Toss potatoes with parmesan and parsley when they come out of the oven, and adjust salt if necessary<|endoftext|>
