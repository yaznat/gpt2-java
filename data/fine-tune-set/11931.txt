What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chennai Chicken Curry

Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Oil
• Onion
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic
• Tomatoes
• Turmeric
• Chili powder
• Garam masala
• Limes
• Salt
• Yogurt
• Water
• Coriander leaves

Steps:
1. In a non stick pan heat the oil and fry the onions till light brown in colour
2. Add the ginger, garlic and tomatoes, fry for 2 minutes
3. Add turmeric, chilli and garam masala powders
4. Stir and fry for 2 minutes
5. Remove the pan from the heat and allow the mixture to cool
6. In a blender, blitz the spice mix to a smooth paste, add the yogurt and mix well
7. Place chicken in a non metallic bowl, add the lime juice and give a quick stir
8. Add the spiced paste and make sure that all of the chicken is coated with the mixture
9. Allow mixture to stand for 15 minutes
10. Reheat the pan
11. Add the chicken and marinade to the hot pan and stir well
12. Add the water and mix again
13. At this point taste the gravy and adjust the salt, to your taste
14. Reduce the heat and cover pot, cook 10 minutes add and stir in the coriander leaves and cook for a further 5 minutes
15. Serve with rice or chapati<|endoftext|>
