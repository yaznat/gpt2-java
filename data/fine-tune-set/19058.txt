What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Food Ice Cream Cake

Ingredients:
• Angel food cake
• Vanilla ice cream
• Fresh strawberries
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Cut the cake in half
2. Tear one half into small pieces and set aside
3. Cut the other half into 12-14 thin slices
4. Arrange in the bottom of a waxed paper-lined 13-in
5. X 9-in
6. X 2-in
7. Baking pan, overlapping as needed
8. Spread softened ice cream over cake, pressing down to smooth
9. Gently press the small cake pieces into ice cream
10. Cover and freeze
11. Just before serving, slice strawberries and sweeten to taste
12. Cut dessert into squares and top with strawberries<|endoftext|>
