Give me a recipe for stir fry.<|endoftext|>Basic Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Soya sauce
• Water
• Vegetables

Steps:
1. Cook the chicken if you are using
2. Mix the vegetable oil, soya sauce and water together in a small bowl
3. Place vegetables in a wok and add in sauce and cook until vegetables are tender
4. Then add in the chicken if using and cook until hot<|endoftext|>
