What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Audrey S Salsa Burgers

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Salsa
• Plain breadcrumbs
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Hamburger buns
• Tomatoes
• Avocados
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. In medium mixing bowl, combine ground beef, salsa and bread crumbs
2. Mix well and shape into 12 hamburger patties
3. No bigger than 4 inches in diameter and 1 / 4 inch thick
4. On 6 patties, sprinkle cheese on top of each patty
5. Place the remaining patties on top of the cheese covered patties to form a hamburger
6. Seal ends together by pressing the ends shut
7. Place on grill
8. Grill each side for 11 to 15 minutes or until your liking
9. Place cooked hamburger on bun topped with tomato, avocado, salsa and 1 tablespoon sour cream
10. When doubling recipe, i found i needed to add more bread crumbs so the meat was the right consistency for a hamburger<|endoftext|>
