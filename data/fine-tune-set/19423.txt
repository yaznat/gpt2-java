Ingredients:
• Frozen southern style hash brown potatoes
• Sour cream
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cheddar cheese
• Poppy seed

Steps:
1. In a very large mixing bowl, mix sour cream and soup together
2. Add potatoes
3. Add in 1 cup of shredded cheese
4. Add in poppy seeds
5. Mix all together
6. Pour into 9 x 13 baking pan
7. Top with remaining cheese
8. Bake at 350f for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
