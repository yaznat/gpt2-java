Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Sugar
• Egg substitute
• Flour
• Cocoa
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Mini marshmallows
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Cream margarine and sugar, beat in egg product
3. Combine and thoroughly mix flour, cocoa, baking powder, and salt
4. Beat into creamed mixture
5. Fold in marshmallows and chocolate chips
6. Spread into greased 13x9-inch baking pan
7. Bake 35-40 min, or until firmly set
8. Cool before cutting into bars<|endoftext|>
