Ingredients:
• Pretzels
• Brown sugar
• Pumpkin spice
• Unsalted butter
• Coconut milk
• Low-fat sour cream
• Low-fat milk
• Chocolate flavor instant pudding and pie filling mix
• Coconut flakes
• Mini marshmallows
• Cool whip lite
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Crust:
2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
3. In a food processor pulse the pretzels, brown sugar, spice powder, till fine pieces
4. Add butter and pulse to mix
5. Spray a 9 inch pie pan and spread the crust mixture in pan pressing to the bottom and sides
6. Bake 10 minutes and cool completely
7. Filling:
8. In a mixer beat the coconut milk and sour cream mix in the milk
9. Blend in pudding mix
10. Fold in coconut flakes, marshmallows and whipped cream
11. Pour into pie crust and refrigerate for 3 hours
12. Garnish with whipped cream and coconut flakes<|endoftext|>
