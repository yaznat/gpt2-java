Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Olive oil
• Kosher salt
• Garlic cloves
• Red jalapeno chile
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt
• Greek yogurt
• Mayonnaise
• Chipotle pepper
• Adobo sauce
• Fresh lime juice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
2. Cut potatoes lengthwise into slices that are 1 / 3-inch thick and then cut each slice into 1 / 3-inch thick strips
3. Dry the potatoes between two kitchen towels
4. In a large bowl, combine the potatoes, 3 tablespoons olive oil and 1 teaspoon kosher salt
5. Divide the potatoes between two baking sheets lined with parchment paper, spread into a single layer
6. Bake until the potatoes are golden brown, about 40 minutes, turning the potatoes every 10 minutes, and rotating the racks halfway through baking
7. Heat 1 teaspoon olive in a small nonstick skillet set over medium heat
8. Add garlic and jalapenos and cook until fragrant, about 1 minutes
9. Take care not to burn the garlic
10. Place the cooked potatoes in a large bowl and toss with garlic, peppers, and cilantro
11. Taste and season with additional salt, if necessary
12. To make sauce: in a medium bowl, stir to combine yogurt, mayonnaise, chipotle pepper, adobo sauce and lime juice
13. Serve with fries<|endoftext|>
