Can you give me a milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>South Beach Milkshakes

Ingredients:
• Ice cubes
• Skim milk
• Vanilla extract
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Cocoa
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Crush ice cubes in blender
2. Add remaining ingredients
3. Blend and enjoy!
4. I accidently added cinnamon instead of cocoa and had a yummy surprise, so experiment!<|endoftext|>
