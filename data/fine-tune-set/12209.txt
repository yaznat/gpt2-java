Give me a pie recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Blondies

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Granulated sugar
• Light brown sugar
• Eggs
• Cinnamon
• Vanilla
• Baking powder
• All-purpose flour
• Apples
• White sugar

Steps:
1. Mix ingredients in the order listed batter will be very thick mix until all dry ingredients are incorporated, but try not to over mix, and fold in apples as gently as possible
2. Press batter down into a greased 9 x 13 pan with the back of a large spoon to even out
3. Sprinkle with cinnamon sugar evenly
4. Bake at 350 for 30-35 minutes, until just beginning to brown on top
5. Allow to cool before cutting to prevent crumbling<|endoftext|>
