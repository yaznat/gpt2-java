Ingredients:
• Linguine
• Basil leaves
• Pine nuts
• Avocados
• Fresh lemon juice
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Sun-dried tomato

Steps:
1. In a large pot, bring water to a boil
2. Add pasta and cook to package directions
3. While pasta cooks, in a food processor, blend basil, pine nuts, avocados, lemon juice, garlic, and olive oil
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Drain pasta
6. In a large serving bowl, toss pesto with hot, freshly cooked pasta and garnish each serving with a basil leaf
7. For an extra touch of color and flavor, top pasta with sun-dried tomatoes<|endoftext|>
