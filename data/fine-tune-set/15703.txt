What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Chicken Puffy Tacos

Ingredients:
• Whole chicken
• Yellow onion
• Garlic clove
• Diced green chilies
• Chicken stock
• Smoked paprika
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Mexican oregano
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Soft taco-size flour tortillas
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Place everything except the tortillas and oil in a crockpot or slow cooker and set to high for three hours or until meat is easily removed from the bone
2. Remove chicken and let cool, leaving sauce in the vessel
3. When cool enough to handle, pull the chicken meat from the bone and shred
4. Add shredded meat back into the sauce
5. Discard chicken skin and bones
6. Reduce crockpot or slow cooker to low and simmer meat in sauce for at least one additional hour
7. Meanwhile, pour vegetable oil into a frying pan until 1 / 4" deep
8. Heat oil to 350 degrees or until a flour tortilla "puffs" up and browns quickly
9. Add one flour tortilla at a time into oil and fry one side until lightly browned
10. Using tongs, fold the tortilla over so that the browned side is on the inside
11. Fry the outside until lightly browned
12. Remove from oil, add a pinch of kosher salt and place on a cooling rack
13. Repeat until desired number of puffy taco shells are done<|endoftext|>
