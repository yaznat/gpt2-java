Give me a lamb recipe.<|endoftext|>Curried Lamb And Spinach Burgers

Ingredients:
• Lean ground lamb
• Fresh breadcrumb
• Chutney
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Dry crushed red pepper
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Garlic
• Sandwich buns

Steps:
1. Combine first 8 ingredients in a medium bowl
2. Divide mixture into 4 equal portions
3. Shape into 1 / 2 inch thick patties
4. Broil, grill or pan grill the burgers for about 7 minutes on each side or until done
5. Place patties on rolls, spread with chutney and garnish with lettuce if desired<|endoftext|>
