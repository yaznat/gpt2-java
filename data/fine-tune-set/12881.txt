Can you give me a steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Betty Ford S Chinese Pepper Steak

Ingredients:
• Flank steaks
• Oil
• Green peppers
• Water
• Onion soup mix
• Soy sauce
• Sherry wine
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Cut steak lengthwise in 1 / 2 inch strips
2. Heat oil in lg skillet
3. Fry steak a handful at a time only long enough to change color
4. Remove steak from skillet
5. Saute the green peppers
6. Bring 2 cups water to a boil in dutch oven
7. Add onion soup mix and boil for 5 minutes
8. Add steak and peppers
9. Simmer for 1 / 2 hour
10. Add soy sauce, salt, pepper and wine
11. Add cornstarch mixture and cook until just thickened<|endoftext|>
