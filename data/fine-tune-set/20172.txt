What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef And Pepper Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Red wine vinegar
• Vegetable oil
• Cornstarch
• Garlic
• Beef steaks
• Water
• Water chestnuts
• Green pepper

Steps:
1. Combine soy sauce, vinegar, 3 tsp cornstarch, and garlic
2. Add beef strips
3. Marinate 30 minutes
4. Combine water and remaining 2tsps cornstarch
5. Set aside
6. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a skillet
7. Add beef and marinade
8. Stir fry until almost done
9. Add water chestnuts and green pepper
10. Stir fry until as done as you like
11. Add cornstarch mixture
12. Cook and stir until it boils and thickens, a couple minutes<|endoftext|>
