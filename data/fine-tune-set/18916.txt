Ingredients:
• Whole kernel corn
• Flour
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Fold flour through corn thoroughly
2. Mix sugar, eggs and milk
3. Pour liquid mixture over corn and flour and mix thoroughly
4. Add salt and pepper to taste
5. Bake 1 hour in 375 degree oven
6. Let set for 10 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
