What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Halloumi Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter
• Salt and pepper
• Halloumi cheese

Steps:
1. Break eggs into a bowl
2. Add milk, salt and pepper
3. Beat lightly
4. Add the butter to a preheated frying pan and increase the heat until the butter sizzles
5. Pour in the egg mixture and cook until the top is still liquid
6. Add the grated halloumi and fold over one-third of the omelette
7. Reduce heat to medium and cook 2-3 minutes on each side<|endoftext|>
