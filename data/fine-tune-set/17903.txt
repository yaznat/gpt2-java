What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Austin S Peanut Butter Butterscotch Chip Brownie Cookies

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Crunchy peanut butter
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Butterscotch chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Spray cookie sheet with nonstick cooking spray
3. In a medium bowl, mix the brownie mix, peanut butter, brown sugar, eggs and vanilla with a wooden spoon until well blended
4. Stir in butterscotch baking chips
5. Drop dough by teaspoons, 2 inches apart, onto prepared pan
6. Bake 10-13 minutes
7. Cool 1 minutes and transfer to wire racks to cool completely<|endoftext|>
