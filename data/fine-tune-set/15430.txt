Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Eggs
• Breadcrumbs
• Mayonnaise
• Worcestershire sauce
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Ketchup
• Yellow mustard
• Bacon bits
• Brown sugar
• French-fried onions

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Spray a muffin tin with nonstick cooking spray
3. In a large bowl, combine meat with eggs, bread crumbs, mayonnaise, worcestershire sauce, cheese, 1 / 2 cup ketchup, 1 / 4 cup mustard, bacon bits
4. Mix well
5. Add more bread crumbs if it seems runny
6. Spoon into prepared muffin tin
7. Mix the remaining 1 / 2 cup ketchup, 1 / 4 cup mustard, and brown sugar in a small bowl
8. Spoon and spread over the top of the meatloaf muffins
9. Place muffin tin on a baking sheet, and bake for 40 minutes
10. Five minutes before they are done, add french fried onions, watching to make sure the onions dont burn<|endoftext|>
