Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Classic Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Oil

Steps:
1. Preheat waffle iron
2. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl
3. Whisk milk, eggs and oil in a medium bowl
4. Whisk milk mixture into dry ingredients until smooth
5. Pour one cup of batter into preheated waffle iron
6. Close top and flip waffle maker
7. Bake until brown, about three minutes<|endoftext|>
