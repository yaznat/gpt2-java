Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Bean Cheese Burritos

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Chopped tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Diced green chilies
• Fresh oregano
• Fresh basil
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Vegetarian refried beans
• Black beans
• Monterey jack cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Saute onion in oil, just until softened
2. Add garlic and stir for 30 seconds
3. Add tomatoes, tomato sauce, chilies, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper
4. Bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes
6. Combine the black beans and refried beans in a bowl
7. Combine both types of cheeses in a separate bowl
8. Place 6 tablespoons or 1 / 10th of the beans and about 2 tablespoons of cheese in a tortilla
9. Roll it up and place it in and ungreased 9 x 13 baking dish
10. Repeat with all 10 tortillas
11. Pour the sauce on top and sprinkle with the remaining cheese
12. Bake at 350 degrees for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
