Ingredients:
• White cake mix
• Sugar
• Light sour cream
• Flaked coconut
• Cool whip lite

Steps:
1. Mix sour cream, sugar and coconut well and refrigerate for 2 hours
2. Bake cake according to package instruccions in two 9 inch layers
3. Cool and split in four layers
4. Spread the coconut mixture as filling between the layers, reserving a cup
5. Combine reserved coconut mixture with cool whipped topping blending until smooth and frost the cake
6. Cover the cake and refrigerate for 3 days before serving
7. It is very important to let it set the full time for better flavor<|endoftext|>
