Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Butter flavor shortening
• Cheddar cheese
• Cold water
• Cooking apples
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl stir together the 1 cup flour and 1 / 4 teaspoons salt
2. Using a pastry blender cut in the shortening and cheese
3. Add water a tablespoonful at a time to make crust stirring with a fork to blend
4. Roll out and ease into a 9" pie plate
5. Fold and flute edges
6. Combine apples, sugar, 1 / 4 cup flour, cinnamon and salt
7. Spoon into the pie shell
8. Mix cheddar topping ingredients together until crumbly and sprinkle over the apples
9. Cover edge of pie with foil and bake at 375 for 25 minutes
10. Remove the foil and bake an additional 20-25 minutes or until top is golden and the fruit is tender<|endoftext|>
