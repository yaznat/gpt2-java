Ingredients:
• White fish fillets
• Boiling water
• Chicken bouillon cube
• Scallions
• Ginger
• Cayenne pepper
• Soy sauce
• Rice wine vinegar
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 425f
2. Place fish fillets in a 13 x 9 inch baking dish
3. Dissolve the chicken bouillon cube in one cup boiling water, and set aside
4. Clean the scallions, and cut each in half
5. Slice the green portions, then cut the white portion in matchstick-size pieces
6. Keep these apart, you will be using them separately
7. Slice the ginger into matchstick-size pieces
8. From the one cup bouillon, remove 1 / 4 cup and pour this over the fish
9. Sprinkle the fillets with cayenne pepper, salt and pepper if using, then top with the green portion of the scallions
10. Place, covered, in the oven and bake for 15 minutes, or until it flakes easily with a fork
11. In a small bowl, mix the remaining bouillon with the soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, cornstarch and 2 tbs water
12. Set aside
13. Heat the oil in a small non-stick skillet
14. Add the scallion strips and the ginger and cook until golden
15. Give the soy mixture a good stir, then add to the skillet
16. Heat to boiling, and boil stirring for 1 minute or until the sauce has thickened
17. Remove fish from oven and onto serving plates
18. Serve sauce over the fish<|endoftext|>
