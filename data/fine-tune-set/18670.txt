Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Milk
• Cream
• Egg yolk
• Baileys irish cream
• Chocolate

Steps:
1. Combine sugar & cornstarch in a heavy, medium saucepan
2. Add milk& whisk until dry ingredients are dissolved
3. Whisk in egg yolk & cream
4. Cook, over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture comes to a full boil & thickens
5. Remove from heat & whisk in bailey's
6. Add chocolate & whisk until melted& smooth
7. Pour pudding into ramekins or serving dishes
8. Let cool slightly & cover the tops with plastic wrap to prevent a skin from forming
9. Refrigerate until chilled- a minimum of 2 hours
10. To serve, garnish with chocolate curls or cocoa powder or whipped cream or all three!<|endoftext|>
