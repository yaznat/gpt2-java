Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Egg
• 2% low-fat milk
• Canola oil
• Apples
• Cream cheese
• Maple syrup
• Orange peel
• Ground nutmeg
• Old fashioned oats
• Butter
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt and cinnamon
2. In another bowl, combine the egg, milk and oil
3. Stir into dry ingredients just until moistened
4. Fold in apples
5. Fill greased or paper-lined muffin cups three-fourths full
6. In a small bowl, beat the filling ingredients until smooth
7. Drop by tablespoonfuls into centers of muffins
8. For topping, in a small bowl, combine the flour, oats, brown sugar and cinnamon
9. Cut in butter until crumbly
10. Stir in pecans
11. Sprinkle over filling
12. Bake at 400 for 16-20 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in muffin comes out clean
13. Cool for 5 minutes before removing from pan to wire rack<|endoftext|>
