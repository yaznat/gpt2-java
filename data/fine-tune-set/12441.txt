Please give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>Apple And Squash Soup

Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Sweet onion
• Garlic cloves
• Curry powder
• Ground cumin
• Cayenne pepper
• Butternut squash
• Chicken broth
• Water
• Tart apple
• Salt
• White pepper
• Light cream

Steps:
1. In a large pot over medium heat, heat olive or vegetable oil
2. Add onion and saute until golden brown
3. Add garlic, curry powder, cumin, and cayenne pepper
4. Cook, stirring constantly, 30 seconds
5. Add squash, chicken broth, water, apples and salt and pepper
6. Bring liquid to a boil
7. Reduce heat to low and simmer, covered, 25 minutes or until squash is tender
8. Remove from heat and stir in light cream
9. Puree mixture in a blender or food processor, in batches and return to pot to heat through
10. Serve<|endoftext|>
