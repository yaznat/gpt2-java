What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Chicken Taco Meat

Ingredients:
• Diced tomatoes with basil oregano and garlic
• Green chilies
• Chicken broth
• Onion
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a crock pot
2. Heat on low for 8 hours
3. Serve on tacos with all the fixings<|endoftext|>
