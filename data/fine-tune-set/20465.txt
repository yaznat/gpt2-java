Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Apples N Cheese Casserole

Ingredients:
• Apples for pie
• Butter
• Sugar
• Velveeta cheese
• Flour
• Milk

Steps:
1. Place apples in a greased casserole dish
2. Cream butter, sugar and flour
3. Add grated or cut up cheese to creamed mixture
4. Mix well and spread over apples
5. Pour milk on top
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
