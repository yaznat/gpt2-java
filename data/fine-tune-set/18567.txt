Ingredients:
• Butter
• Self-raising flour
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Salt
• Milk
• Vanilla essence
• Brown sugar
• Desiccated coconut
• Boiling water
• Paper

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine flour, 2 tbsps cocoa, salt and 3 / 4 cup sugar
2. Stir in milk, melted butter and vanilla
3. Spoon batter into a microwave ring mold or a microwave-proof casserole dish
4. Combine brown sugar, 1 tbsp cocoa and coconut and sprinkle over the batter
5. Pour hot water gently over the top
6. Cover with a sheet of baking paper
7. Cook on high for 6 minutes
8. Leave to cool for 4-5 minutes before serving
9. Serve with a little whipped cream or vanilla ice cream, if you like<|endoftext|>
