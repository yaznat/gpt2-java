Give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>Aloha Delight Salad

Ingredients:
• Orange
• Mangoes
• Bananas
• Pineapple chunks
• Unsweetened coconut
• Walnuts
• Dates
• Honey
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Peel, section, and dice orange
2. Peel, pit, and slice mangoes
3. Peel bananas and slice into 1 / 4" rounds
4. Drain pineapple, saving the juice
5. Put all the fruit in a large bowl
6. Add coconut, walnuts, and date pieces
7. With a fork, whip honey, lemon juice, and 1 / 4 cup pineapple juice together in a bowl and drizzle over fruit<|endoftext|>
