What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Banana Tapioca Pudding

Ingredients:
• Skim milk
• Quick-cooking tapioca
• Sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Bananas

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, combine milk, tapioca, sugar and egg: let stand for 5 minutes with no heat
2. Cover and stir over medium heat until mixture comes to a full boil
3. Remove from heat
4. Stir in vanilla and bananas
5. Cool for 20 minutes
6. Serve warm or cold<|endoftext|>
