Can you give me a milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Double Chocolate Marshmallow Milkshakes

Ingredients:
• Heavy cream
• Marshmallow cream
• Vanilla extract
• Whole milk
• Chocolate syrup
• Chocolate ice cream
• Bittersweet chocolate

Steps:
1. Whisk together the heavy cream, marshmallow fluff and vanilla extract until stiff peaks form
2. Set aside
3. In a blender, combine milk and chocolate sauce
4. Add ice cream and blend until smooth
5. Fill 4 glasses one-quarter of the way with the shake
6. Add some marshmallow cream, then more milkshake, then cream, then milkshake
7. Finish with a dollop of marshmallow cream and garnish with grated bittersweet chocolate<|endoftext|>
