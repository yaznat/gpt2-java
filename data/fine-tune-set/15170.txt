What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Basic Pizza Dough Use Now Or Use Later Oamc Too

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Active dry yeast
• Warm water
• Cornmeal

Steps:
1. Mix flour and salt together in a large bowl
2. Dissolve yeast in water and add to bowl
3. Cover and let rest for 5 minutes
4. Sprinkle cornmeal on a lightly oiled or sprayed 12"-14" pizza pan or stone
5. Place dough in middle of pan and using oiled hands pat out dough toward the edges
6. In a hurry? add sauce and toppings as desired and bake at 350 degrees until done- about 25 minutes
7. Or for best results
8. Bake crust for 5 minutes at 350 degrees
9. Remove from oven and top with favorite toppings
10. Return to oven and cook until cheese is browned and bubbly
11. For premade crust-bake at 350 until just done in center
12. Cool completely
13. Wrap well and freeze
14. For wheat crust: decrease white flour to 1 cup and add 1 cup whole wheat flour plus 1 / 2 tsp sugar
