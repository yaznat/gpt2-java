Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Water
• Chicken base
• Canola oil
• Onion
• Tomatoes
• Garlic
• Oregano
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Hot chili pepper
• Corn
• Chicken broth
• White hominy
• Fresh lime juice
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. For chicken:
2. Remove skin and wings from chicken
3. Cover with water and add chicken base
4. Cook chicken over medium heat for approximately 1-1 / 2 hours
5. Remove chicken and let cool
6. Bone chicken and chop meat into small pieces
7. Reserve broth
8. While the chicken is cooling, start the soup:
9. Place canola oil in pan and heat
10. Add onion, tomato, garlic, oregano, chili powder, salt and chilies and saut until onions are translucent
11. Add corn, chicken, reserved broth, additional chicken base and hominy
12. Bring to a boil and cook for 15 minutes
13. Add lime juice and remove from heat
14. Add chopped cilantro
15. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
