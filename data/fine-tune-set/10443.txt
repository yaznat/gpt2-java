What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Ann Romney S M M S Cookies

Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Light brown sugar
• Unsalted butter
• Crunchy peanut butter
• Light corn syrup
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Old fashioned oats
• Baking soda
• Chocolate chips
• M&m'

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325
2. In a large bowl, cream sugars, butter, peanut butter and corn syrup on high speed until well combined
3. Beat in eggs 1 at a time
4. Beat in vanilla extract
5. In a separate bowl, mix together oats and baking soda
6. Stir into peanut butter mixture until combined
7. Mix in chocolate chips and m&m's
8. Using a standard-size ice cream scoop, drop dough onto baking sheets
9. Bake at 325 for 18 minutes or until lightly browned
10. Cool 2 minutes, then transfer cookies to a wire rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
