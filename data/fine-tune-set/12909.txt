Can you give me a steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Blackened Tuna Steaks

Ingredients:
• Salt
• Fresh thyme
• Dried oregano
• Cayenne pepper
• Sweet paprika
• Ground black pepper
• Fennel seed
• Tuna steaks
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 deg
2. Mix first 7 ingredients in small bowl
3. Place steaks on baking sheet, brush on both sides with 1 t
4. Oil
5. Sprinkle top of each with seasoning
6. Heat heavy large skillet over high heat until very hot
7. Add remaining 1 t
8. Oil
9. Swirl to coat
10. Place steaks seasoning side down in skillet
11. Cook until very brown on bottom return steaks, browned side up to baking sheet
12. Place in oven
13. Bake until done<|endoftext|>
