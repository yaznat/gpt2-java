What's a good cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Caribbean Tomato Juice Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened tomato juice
• Celery salt
• Worcestershire sauce
• Lemon juice
• White pepper
• Sugar
• Celery ribs

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients, except the celery ribs, in a jar that has a lid
2. Cover and shake well
3. Pour into glasses and add celery sticks as stirrers<|endoftext|>
