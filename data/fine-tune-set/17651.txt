Ingredients:
• Hash browns
• Butter
• Salt
• Breakfast sausage
• Eggs
• Mexican blend cheese
• Red bell pepper
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Place 12 paper liners in a muffin pan
2. Spray liners with cooking spray
3. In bowl, combine hash browns, butter, salt and pepper
4. Press hash brown mixture into bottom and up sides of the muffin cups
5. Bake at 400 degrees f for 12 minutes or until lightly browned
6. Meanwhile, cook sausage according to package directions
7. Cut into 1 / 2 inch pieces
8. Divide sausage between muffin cups
9. Combine eggs, cheese and bell pepper
10. Pour over sausage
11. Sprinkle with onions
12. Bake for 13-15 minutes or until set<|endoftext|>
