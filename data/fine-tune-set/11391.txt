What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Abby S French Bread Braids

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Dry yeast
• Water
• Shortening
• Egg white

Steps:
1. In a bowl combine 3 cups flour, sugar, salt and yeast, mix well
2. In a small sauce pan heat 2 cups water and shortening until very warm
3. Add warm mixture to flour mixture, blend on low speed until moistened and then for 3 min on medium speed
4. By hand stir in an additional 1 1 / 2 - 2 1 / 4 cups flour to form a stiff dough
5. On a floured surface, knead 1 / 4 - 1 / 2 cup flour until dough is smooth and elastic, about 8 minute
6. Place in a greased bowl, cover loosely with greased plastic wrap and a cloth towel
7. Let rise in a warm place until light and doubled in size, about 45-60 min
8. Grease a large cookie sheet
9. Punch the dough down several times to remove all air bubbles
10. Divide the dough in half and then divide each half into 3 equal parts
11. Roll each part into a 14 inch rope
12. Braid the 3 ropes, sealing the ends
13. Place on greased cookie sheet
14. Repeat with other dough half
15. In a small bowl combine 1 tbsp water and the egg white, beat slightly
16. Carefully brush over the loaves
17. Cover, let rise in a warm place until light and doubled in size, 20-30 minute
18. Heat over to 375
19. Uncover the loaves, brush again with egg white mixture
20. Bake for 25-30 min or until golden brown
21. Remove from oven and cool on wire racks<|endoftext|>
