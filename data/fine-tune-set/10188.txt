Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Beef
• Chili powder
• Kosher salt
• Ground pepper
• Onion
• Anaheim chilies
• Garlic cloves
• Tomatoes
• Flour
• Beef broth
• Red chili sauce

Steps:
1. Cube beef then dust with 2 teaspoons chili powder, salt and pepper and set aside
2. Chop all veggies, keep tomatoes separate and set aside
3. Heat a good heavy pot over medium heat for 3-4 minutes
4. Add oil
5. Add the beef in 3 batches and brown on all sides
6. Add all the veggies but the tomatoes
7. Add the last teaspoon of chili powder and stir
8. Add cup beef broth and reduce heat to a simmer
9. Simmer until most of the liquid is gone
10. Add another cup beef broth, the tomatoes and reduce again but only until there is enough liquid to mix the flour into
11. Mix in the flour well
12. Add the remaining beef broth to make a gravy consistency
13. Add the chili sauce and mix well
14. Add the browned beef, mix well
15. Cover and simmer for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
