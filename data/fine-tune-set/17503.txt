Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Water
• Cream cheese
• Lemon zest
• Fresh blueberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Grease and flour a 13x9x2" pan
3. In a large bowl, cream together 1 / 2 cup softened butter and 1 1 / 4 cup sugar
4. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition
5. In a medium bowl, sift together 2 cups flour, baking powder, and salt
6. In a measuring cup, mix milk and water
7. Alternately add flour mixture with milk mixture, ending with milk
8. Beat in softened cream cheese and 1 t
9. Lemon zest
10. In a small bowl, sprinkle 1 / 4 cup flour over blueberries and toss gently
11. Fold into batter and pour into pan
12. Sift 1 / 4 cup sugar and 1 / 4 cup flour into a small bowl
13. Mix in 1 t
14. Lemon zest
15. Using a pastry cutter, cut in 2 t
16. Butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs
17. Sprinkle over batter
18. Bake for 40-45 minutes
19. Serve warm, heavily dusted with powdered sugar<|endoftext|>
