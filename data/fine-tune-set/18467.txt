What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Easy Berry Maple Pancake Or Waffle Sauce Healthy

Ingredients:
• Frozen raspberries
• Maple syrup
• Fat-free evaporated milk
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Heat first 3 ingredients in a bowl for 45 seconds, or until berries are thawed and have released their juices
2. Lightly mash the berries until the sauce no longer has any big chunks, and mix in flour and sweetener
3. Enjoy over pancakes, waffles, crepes, or whatever you want! great with margarine and a dollop of cool whip<|endoftext|>
