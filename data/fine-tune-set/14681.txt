Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Rice Pudding

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Sour cream
• Milk
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Raisins
• Apple
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Brown sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Combine eggs, sugar, sour cream, milk, cinnamon and salt
3. Stir in raisins, apple and rice
4. Spoon mixture into greased baking dish
5. Combine brown sugar, flour, cinnamon and butter, sprinkle over rice mixture
6. Bake uncovered 45 minutes
7. Serves 8<|endoftext|>
