Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Pork cutlets
• Apricot jam
• Breadcrumbs
• Flat leaf parsley
• Chicken stock
• Corn

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 190c heat oil in a large, non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat
2. Add pork and cook for 1 to 2 minutes on each side or until browned
3. Place, in a single layer, in an ovenproof dish
4. Spread the top of the pork with jam
5. Combine breadcrumbs and parsley in a small bowl
6. Sprinkle over jam
7. Pour stock into base of dish
8. Bake, uncovered, for 8 to 10 minutes or until pork is cooked through and breadcrumbs are light golden
9. Drizzle over dish juices
10. Serve with corn<|endoftext|>
