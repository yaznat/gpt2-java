What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Honey Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• Pie crust
• Sugar
• Cornmeal
• Salt
• Milk
• Honey
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Whisk sugar, cornmeal and salt together
3. Combine remaining ingredients in a large bowl, stirring well
4. Whisk in cornmeal mixture until well blended
5. Pour into pie shell
6. Bake 50 minutes or until set
7. Cool on wire rack<|endoftext|>
