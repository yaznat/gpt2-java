Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Carrots
• Potatoes
• Green bell pepper
• Celery
• Eggplant
• Onions
• Tomatoes
• Catsup
• Sugar
• Basil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Zucchini
• Plain yogurt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Chop all vegetables except zucchini and place in a covered casserole or dutch oven
3. Add the rest of the ingredients and mix well
4. Cover and cook for 1 1 / 2 hours
5. Add the zucchini and mix gently
6. Leave uncovered and cook for 20 - 30 minutes more
7. Serve over rice or with a delicious crusty bread, and top with a dollop of yogurt, if you like
8. Leftovers are even better the next day<|endoftext|>
