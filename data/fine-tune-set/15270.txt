Ingredients:
• Brussels sprouts
• Pizza dough
• Buffalo mozzarella
• Garlic clove
• Pancetta
• Parmigiano-reggiano cheese
• Salt
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 550f with a pizza stone on the lowest rack
2. Cut 1 / 4" off the stem end of each brussels sprout, then cut out the core
3. Discard any bruised or damaged outer leaves, then separate the remaining leaves
4. Sprinkle pizza peel with cornmeal, then form dough into a pizza
5. Tear mozzarella into 10-12 pieces and scatter over dough
6. Distribute brussels sprout leaves over the pizza
7. Scatter garlic and pancetta over dough as well
8. Sprinkle with parmigiano and salt, then drizzle with olive oil
9. Slide pizza onto hot stone
10. Bake 12-14 minutes
11. Check after 7 minutes, turning 180 if baking unevenly<|endoftext|>
