Ingredients:
• Coconut rum
• Pineapple juice
• Orange juice
• Lime juice
• Water
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Mix and chill liquid ingredients in fridge for 4 hours
2. Serve over a quart of vanilla icecream
3. Garnish with a sprig of mint or a cherry / pineapple skewer<|endoftext|>
