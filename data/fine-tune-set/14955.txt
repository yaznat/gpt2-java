Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Hot Spicy Chicken Shrimp Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Carrot
• Cabbage
• Peas
• Cooked chicken
• Cooked rice
• Oil
• Egg
• Green onion
• Chili-garlic sauce
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. On a heated wok, add 1 tablespoon of oil
2. Cook egg and set aside
3. Add remaining 2 tablespoons of oil, stir-fry chicken, cabbage, carrots and peas for 2 minutes
4. Add rice, soy sauce and chili garlic sauce
5. Cook until heated through
6. Toss in cooked egg
7. Serve with chopped green onions<|endoftext|>
