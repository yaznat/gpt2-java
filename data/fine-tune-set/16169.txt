What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Fruit And Cinnamon Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Low-fat flour tortillas
• Fat free cream cheese
• Strawberry
• Sugar
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Spread cream cheese over tortilla
2. Top with strawberries and sugar on one side
3. Sprinkle with a dash of cinnamon and fold in half
4. Cook in a nonstick skillet coated with cooking spray for 2 minutes on each side<|endoftext|>
