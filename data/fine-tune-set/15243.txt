Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Breadmaker Pizza Base Yummiest Ever Found

Ingredients:
• Warm water
• Sugar
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Cornmeal
• Plain flour
• Baking powder
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Granulated yeast

Steps:
1. Mix dough in bread maker to mix and rest etc
2. Then push into 2 large greased pizza trays and sit covered in warm place for a further ten minutes to rise
3. Put your favorite toppings on and cook in hot oven 15 - 20 minutes
4. You can freeze dough although of course i think it is better to use fresh
5. For a simple topping just drizzle olive oil over base
6. Add garlic and chopped tomatoes with some fresh basil and black pepper
7. Don't forget some salt it brings out the flavour of the tomatoes<|endoftext|>
