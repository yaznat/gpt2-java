Give me a stir fry recipe.<|endoftext|>Cherry Chicken Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Chicken breast
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Cherries
• Green onion
• Peanut oil
• Egg white
• Light soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Sesame oil
• Sugar-free black cherry gelatin
• Black coffee
• Black pepper
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Fold together the marinade ingredients and dump into a plastic bag with sliced chicken
2. Marinate chicken for at least five minutes
3. In the meantime, add all secret sauce ingredients to a blender or food processor, and pulse a few times until well-blended
4. It will foam a little bit
5. Heat oil over high heat in a large skillet or wok
6. Stir-fry the meat for 2-3 minutes or until it turns color
7. Don't overcook, as it will be back in the pan again
8. Transfer to a platter
9. Add a little more peanut oil to the wok if it needs it
10. Stir fry water chestnuts and green onions for two minutes
11. Add secret sauce and bring to a boil, then cook for two minutes
12. Return meat, add cherries and stir thoroughly to heat through
13. Remove from heat
14. Garnish with reserved green onion and serve hot over rice<|endoftext|>
