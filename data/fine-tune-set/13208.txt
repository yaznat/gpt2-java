Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Water
• Paprika
• Ground pepper
• Instant rice

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a skillet over medium high heat
2. Add the chicken and cook for 10 minutes or until well browned on both sides
3. Remove chicken from skillet
4. Stir the soup, water, paprika, ground pepper in the skillet and heat to a boil
5. Stir in the rice
6. Reduce the heat to low
7. Return the chicken to the skillet, sprinkle chicken with additional paprika and ground pepper
8. Cover and cook for 5 minutes or until chicken is cooked thoroughly<|endoftext|>
