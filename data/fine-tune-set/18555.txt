Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Lentil Waffles

Ingredients:
• Green gram
• Rava
• Spinach
• Green chilies
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic
• Baking soda
• Oil
• Salt

Steps:
1. Wash, drain lentils, do not discard skins
2. Add them back to lentils
3. Grind lentils, garlic, ginger, garlic to a paste
4. Add all other ingredients and make a thick batter
5. Heat waffle iron, pour 1 ladleful of mixture in centre
6. Close and allow to cook till done
7. Serve hot with green chutney or curds<|endoftext|>
