Ingredients:
• Dried apricot
• Dried prune
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Sour cream
• Light brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Icing sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Butter a 10" tube or bundt pan and dust with flour
3. Coarsely chop apricots and prunes
4. Toss to combine
5. Sift together flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt
6. Set aside
7. Beat butter until fluffy
8. Gradually beat in sugar, then eggs, one at a time
9. Add vanilla
10. Blend in flour mixture, alternately with sour cream, beginning and ending with flour mixture
11. Blend until just smooth
12. Gently fold in prunes and apricots
13. Mix the first four streusel ingredients with a fork until crumbly
14. Turn 1 / 3 of batter into prepared pan, spreading evenly
15. Sprinkle with 1 / 3 of streusel mixture
16. Repeat, layering the remaining batter and streusel twice
17. Bake 55 to 60 minutes, or until cake tester comes out clean
18. Let cool in pan 20 minutes
19. Remove from pan to platter
20. Sift icing sugar over top if desired
21. This cake can be served warm<|endoftext|>
