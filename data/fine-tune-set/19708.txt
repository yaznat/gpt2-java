Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Salt & pepper
• Mrs dash's extra spicy seasoning

Steps:
1. Peel and cut your potatoes into french fry chips
2. Place in cold water until you want to cook them, this removes some of the starch from the potato
3. Heat oven to 375f
4. Place a circle of baker's paper on the cold stone, already set in the handle, the paper just protects the stone and makes for easy cleaning
5. Dry the potatoes on paper towels and sprinkle them with salt pepper and your favorite spice blend arrange them on the baking stone and place in the oven
6. After about 20 minutes take stone out of oven and turn the chips over
7. Return to oven and continue baking for a further 15-20 minutes or until they are brown and puffed<|endoftext|>
