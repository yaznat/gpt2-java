Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Any Kind Muffins Gluten Free

Ingredients:
• Brown rice flour
• Rice flour
• Tapioca starch
• Sweet rice flour
• Sugar
• Gluten free baking powder
• Baking soda
• Guar gum
• Sea salt
• Oil
• Egg
• Fruit

Steps:
1. Additionally you need 1 cup of something wet
2. Preheat oven to 350f
3. The basic procedure for all muffins is mix dry ingredients
4. Then add wet ingredients to dry and beat on med
5. With electric mixer for 3 minutes
6. Should be thick batter
7. Mix in fruit by hand
8. Scoop into lined muffin tins generously
9. Bake in preheated oven for about 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
