What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Brownie Bottoms Crockpot

Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Water
• Cocoa powder
• Brownie mix
• Instant chocolate pudding mix
• Milk chocolate chips
• Eggs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Lightly grease a 4 qt slow cooker with non-stick spray
2. In a small saucepan, combine brown sugar, water, cocoa powder - bring to a boil
3. In a small bowl, combine remaining ingredients with 1 / 2 cup water to form a batter
4. Spread into slow cooker
5. Pour boiling cocoa mixture over batter
6. Cover, cook on high for 1 1 / 2 hours
7. Turn off slow cooker and let stand for 30 minutes
8. Serve warm topped with whipped cream or ice cream<|endoftext|>
