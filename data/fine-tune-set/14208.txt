Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Ketchup
• Whole grain mustard
• Turbinado sugar
• Chipotle chiles in adobo
• Tamari soy sauce
• Cider vinegar
• Cinnamon
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Celery seed

Steps:
1. Slice each block of tofu into 9 slices
2. Place them on a double layer of paper towels or a clean tea towel and place another double layer over them
3. Press firmly with your hands to remove as much moisture as possible
4. Let them sit on the absorbent material while you make the sauce
5. Heat a saucepan and add the onion
6. Cook, stirring, over medium-high heat until onion begins to brown, at least 6 minutes
7. Add the garlic and cook for another minute
8. Add all remaining ingredients and cook, stirring, over medium to low heat, for about 15-20 minutes, until thick and fragrant
9. Preheat oven to 425f place a silicone baking mat on a cookie sheet or oil a long, rectangular baking dish
10. Brush one side of each slice of tofu with a thin layer of sauce and place it on the pan sauce-side down
11. Spread remaining sauce on the tops and sides of the tofu
12. Bake for about 25-30 minutes, until tofu is firm and just beginning to brown at the corners
13. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
