Ingredients:
• Rib eye steaks
• Brown sugar
• Chili powder
• Garlic salt
• Black pepper
• Tomatillos
• Water
• Cream cheese
• Avocado
• Green onion
• Salt
• Green onions
• Jalapeno peppers
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Trim fat from around the edges of beef rib-eye steaks
2. In a small bowl combine the brown sugar, chili powder, garlic salt, and pepper
3. Pat onto both sides of the steaks
4. Let steaks stand at room temperature for 30 minutes
5. Meanwhile for sauce, in a small saucepan combine the quartered tomatillos and water
6. Bring to boiling
7. Reduce heat
8. Cover and simmer for 5 to 7 minutes or until soft
9. Stir the cream cheese into tomatillo mixture until melted
10. Cool mixture slightly
11. In a food processor bowl or blender container combine the tomatillo mixture, the avocado, sliced green onions, and salt
12. Cover and process or blend until sauce mixture is smooth
13. Transfer sauce to a serving bowl
14. Prepare charcoal grill or preheat gas grill
15. Grill steaks on rack of an uncovered grill directly over medium coals to desired doneness, turning halfway through
16. Allow 14 to 18 minutes for medium rare and 18 to 22 minutes for medium
17. If desired, brush the whole green onions and jalapenos lightly with oil
18. Grill jalapenos about 10 minutes or untiil soft and lightly charred, turning occasionally
19. To serve, slice steaks
20. Serve with sauce, grilled jalapenos, and green onions
21. Sprinkle with tomato
22. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
