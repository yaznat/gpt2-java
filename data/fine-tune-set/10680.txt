What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus Pasta Salad With A Creamy Lemon Dressing

Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Asparagus
• Scallion
• Cheddar cheese
• Kosher salt
• Lemon
• Mayonnaise
• Chicken broth
• Lemon juice
• Dijon mustard
• Garlic
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Dressing -- mix all the ingredients and set to the side until the pasta and asparagus are cooked
2. Salad -- in a large pot of salted water, boil the pasta according to directions
3. Approximately 3 minutes before the pasta is done, add the asparagus until is comes out al dente
4. Now drain the pasta and asparagus
5. Rinse under cold water for just a few seconds to stop the cooking process and cool slightly
6. Combine -- now toss the still slightly warm pasta with the dressing
7. Add in the scallions, extra salt and pepper and serve garnished with the cheese fresh lemon slices
8. A nice simple salad!
9. Now this is a very light tasting salad with a very simple dressing
10. It doesn't have bold flavors, but subtle flavors which is a nice compliment to a fresh herb roasted chicken breast or piece of fish
11. This is all about fresh subtle flavors<|endoftext|>
