Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Light brown sugar
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Baking soda
• Unsalted butter
• Peanut butter
• Rolled oats
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• M&m's plain chocolate candy

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Whisk eggs in a large bowl
3. Add sugars, vanilla, baking soda, butter, and peanut butter, one at a time, blending well after each addition
4. Stir in oats, chocolate chips, and m&ms until well blended
5. Form large tablespoons of dough into balls
6. Place on an ungreased baking sheet 4 inches apart
7. Bake 12 -15 minutes
8. Do not overbake
9. Cool on wire racks<|endoftext|>
