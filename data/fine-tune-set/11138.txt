Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onion
• Cheese
• White bread
• Butter
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Place bacon on a baking sheet lined with foil
2. Place in cold oven and set oven to 400f
3. While the bacon is cooking, heat a skillet or small saucepan to medium heat
4. Add butter
5. Once the butter starts to melt, add the onion
6. Cook onion, stirring frequently, about 15-20 minutes or until a caramel color develops and onions are very soft
7. Add more butter as needed
8. After 15 minutes, check on the bacon
9. If it is cooked to your liking, remove the baking sheet from the oven and place bacon on a plate lined with paper towels
10. Set aside
11. 1-2 minutes before the onions have finished cooking, place cheese slices on top of the onions and allow to melt
12. If using, spread mayonnaise on each slice of bread
13. Place bacon on one slice of bread, then top with the onion and cheese
14. Top with other slice of bread to form a sandwich<|endoftext|>
