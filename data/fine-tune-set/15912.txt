Ingredients:
• 12-inch flour tortillas
• Eggs
• Butter
• Chorizo sausage
• Salt and pepper
• White cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Black beans
• Chopped tomato
• Red onions
• Fresh cilantro
• Spicy hot v8
• Garlic
• Green onions
• Salt
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. For black bean salsa: in a bowl, add all salsa ingredients and stir to mix
2. Let rest 30 minutes so that flavors can blend
3. Store covered in the refrigerator
4. For burritos: wrap tortillas in foil and warm in 350 degree oven for about 2 minutes
5. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, melt 1 tablespoon of butter
6. Scramble eggs until desired degree of doneness
7. Remove skillet from heat
8. Add sausage, salt and pepper to taste and stir to combine
9. Spoon 1 / 4 of the egg-sausage mixture into the center of the tortilla
10. Sprinkle with 2 tablespoons cheese
11. Roll tortilla up burrito style
12. Repeat process with remaining tortillas
13. Serve warm with black bean salsa and sour cream if desired<|endoftext|>
