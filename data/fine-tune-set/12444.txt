Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Tart apples
• Potato
• Celery
• Onion
• Ground thyme
• White wine
• Chicken broth
• Cheddar cheese
• Heavy cream
• Milk
• Flour
• Nutmeg
• White pepper

Steps:
1. In large saucepan, heat oil
2. Add apples, potato, celery, onion, and thyme
3. Saute 10 minutes
4. Stir in wine and simmer 2 minutes
5. Add broth and simmer about 40-45 minutes or until everything is very tender
6. Using a blender or food processor, puree soup mixture, but leave just a little bit lumpy
7. Return to saucepan
8. Over very low heat, stir in cheese and cream
9. In a small bowl, whisk together milk and flour
10. Add to soup and stir
11. Add nutmeg, and pepper
12. Cook just until heated through--do not boil or cheese will curdle
13. Serve<|endoftext|>
