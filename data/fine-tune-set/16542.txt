What's a recipe for a smoothie?<|endoftext|>Avocado Pineapple And Apricot Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Pineapple juice
• Apricot yoghurt

Steps:
1. Peel and remove stone from avocado
2. Cut into chunks and place in blender
3. Add pineapple juice and apricot yoghurt
4. Blend until smooth<|endoftext|>
