What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Easy Tofu Lasagne

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Ricotta cheese
• Olive oil
• Oregano
• Basil
• Garlic
• Part-skim mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• No-boil lasagna noodles
• Spaghetti sauce

Steps:
1. Drain tofu and run knife through tofu, still in container, to make chunks
2. In mixing bowl, add tofu chunks, olive oil, cheeses, and spices
3. Use mixer to blend
4. Cover and let sit for 30 minutes to let flavors absorb into the tofu
5. The filling can even be made up to a day ahead and refrigerated
6. Preheat oven according to lasagne package directions
7. Spray a 13x9 pan with cooking spray, pour 1 / 3 - 1 / 2 jar of spaghetti sauce in bottom
8. Layer lasgne noodles according to package instructions and top with 1 / 3 of cheese mixture and 1 / 3-1 / 2 jar of spaghetti sauce
9. Repeat process, ending with noodles on top
10. Pour remaining sauce over noodles, cover with foil, and bake for 50 - 60 minutes
11. Remove foil and top with 1-1 / 2 cups mozzarella for last 5 minutes of baking
12. Let stand 15 minutes before slicing<|endoftext|>
