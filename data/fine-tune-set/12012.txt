Give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>3 Layer Pumpkin Pie Dessert

Ingredients:
• Yellow cake mix
• Butter
• Egg
• Pumpkin pie filling
• Eggs
• Evaporated milk
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 350f
2. Grease or use non-stick cooking spray and prepare 13x9 inches pan
3. Reserve 1 cup dry cake mix for topping
4. Crust - combine remaining cake mix, butter and egg, press into bottom of pan
5. Prepare filling - combine pie mix, 2 eggs and evaporated milk, mix well and pour over crust
6. Topping - combine dry mix, sugar, cinnamon and cut in butter to crumble mixture
7. Sprinkle over filling
8. Bake 350 for 50 minutes or until set
9. Serve with dollip of whip cream<|endoftext|>
