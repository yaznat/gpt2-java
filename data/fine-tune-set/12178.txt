Ingredients:
• Butter
• Apples
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Lemon juice
• Biscuit dough
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. For the filling: add butter to a large saute pan and melt
2. Add apples, sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice and cook over medium heat until the apples are soft, about 15 minutes
3. Remove from heat and cool
4. When the filling is cool, roll the biscuits out on a lightly floured surface so that each biscuit form a 7 to 8-inch circle
5. Place 2 to 3 tablespoons of the filling on 1 / 2 of each circle
6. Brush the edges of the circle with water
7. Fold the circle over the filling to make a half-moon shapes
8. Seal the edges, by pressing the edges with the tines of a fork
9. This is inportant
10. Heat a deep fryer or a deep pot halfway filled with oil to 350
11. Carefully add the pie to the oil, 1 at a time and fry until golden brown, turning the pie as necessary for even browning, about 5 to 8 minutes
12. Drain on paper towels
13. Sprinkle with powdered sugar immediately<|endoftext|>
