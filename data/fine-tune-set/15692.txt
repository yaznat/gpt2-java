Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Black beans
• Corn
• Black olives
• Jalapeno pepper
• Onion
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Chili powder
• Red pepper flakes
• Oregano
• Cumin
• Water
• Salt
• Tomato sauce
• Flour tortilla
• Sour cream
• Salsa
• Cheese
• Guacamole

Steps:
1. Saute onion in large pan till translucent
2. Add garlic and chopped jalapeno, saute briefly, stirring
3. Add chicken, seasonings and remaining ingredients
4. Cook over medium high heat, till ingredients are combined and liquid cooks down - about 5-10 minutes
5. This timing of the cooking is flexible, depending on how much seasoning you add, how much liquid is in your beans, if you add tomato sauce, etc
6. I make it spicy and just wet enough that it's not dried out, but not watery or gloppy
7. Adjust seasonings, salt to taste
8. Serve in flour tortillas with your favorite toppings<|endoftext|>
