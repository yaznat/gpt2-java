Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Kimchi Fried Rice Kimchi Bok Eum Bap

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Kim chee
• Garlic clove
• Rice
• Green onions
• Fish cake
• Sesame seeds
• Soy sauce
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Heat oil in wok or good-sized frying pan
2. Add kim chi and garlic
3. Stir-fry until kim chi is translucent
4. Be careful: it will really spatter when it hits the oil!
5. Add rice, green onions, fish cake or meat, mushrooms
6. Stir-fry until well mixed and heated through
7. Remove from heat
8. Add in sesame seeds, soy sauce, salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
