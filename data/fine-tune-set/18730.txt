Please give me a recipe for pudding.<|endoftext|>Banana Graham Cracker Pudding

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Milk
• Egg yolks
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Bananas
• Graham crackers

Steps:
1. Mix sugar, cornstarch, and salt in saucepan
2. Add milk
3. Cook and stir over medium heat until thick and bubbling
4. Cook and stir 2 minutes more, then remove from heat
5. Add a small amount of hot mixture to beaten egg yolks, then quickly add back to rest of mixture
6. Cook and stir for 2 more minutes, then remove from heat and add butter and vanilla
7. Layer in serving bowl
8. I put 1 / 2 of grahams on bottom, 1 / 2 bananas next, 1 / 2 pudding mixture next
9. Repeat layers and add 1 / 4 cup graham crumbs on top for garnish
10. Makes 4 or 5 servings
11. I usually make this in the morning, because it tastes wonderful after it has cooled all day in refrigerator and all the flavors marry
12. I also double this because there is never enough for all of my family<|endoftext|>
