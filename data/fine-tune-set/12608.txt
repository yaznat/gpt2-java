Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Boneless beef chuck
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Dark beer
• Beef broth
• Parsley sprigs
• Bay leaves
• Vinegar
• Dried thyme
• Sugar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Red potatoes
• Baby carrots
• Frozen peas
• Mushroom
• All-purpose flour
• Water

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large dutch oven over medium heat until hot
2. Add 1 / 4 of the beef cook turning frequently on all sides
3. Remove to plate
4. Repeat until all beef browned
5. Add onions and garlic to pan
6. Cook and stir over low heat until onions are soft and golden or 5-10 minutes
7. Add beef, beer, broth, parsley, bay leaves, vinegar, thyme, sugar, s&p to pan
8. Heat over medium to high heat to boiling
9. Then reduce heat
10. Simmer, covered, stirring occasionally, until beef is tender, about 1 1 / 4hrs
11. Remove and discard parsley, bay leaves and surface fat
12. Add potatoes and carrots to pan
13. Simmer, covered, 15 minutes
14. Add peas and mushrooms
15. Simmer uncovered, until potatoes and carrots are tender or about 10 minutes
16. Mix flour and water in small bowl until smooth
17. Gradually stir into stew
18. Cook and stir over medium low heat until thickened or about 3 minutes<|endoftext|>
