What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>U Wing It Pork Loin Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork loin chops
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Sour cream
• Horseradish
• Grainy mustard
• Ranch dressing
• Dry red wine

Steps:
1. Brown chops in oil in a frying pan
2. Remove to casserole dish
3. Saute onion and garlic over high heat for 2 minutes
4. Add sour cream, horseradish, mustard, dressing and wine
5. Heat for 3 minutes
6. Pour over chops
7. Bake in preheated 350f oven for 1 hour or until done<|endoftext|>
