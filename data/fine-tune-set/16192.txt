Ingredients:
• Red onion
• Oil-cured black olive
• Lemon zest
• Red wine vinegar
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Flour tortillas
• White cheddar cheese
• Feta
• Baby spinach
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. For the relish: mix the onion, olives, zest, vinegar and oil together
2. Season to taste with salt and pepper
3. For the quesadillas: heat your grill to medium
4. Place 8 of the tortillas on a flat work surface
5. Divide the cheeses and spinach among the tortillas and sprinkle with salt and pepper
6. Make 4 quesadillas by stacking the filled tortillas by twos, then placing one of the remaining plain tortillas on top
7. Brush the tops of the quesadillas with olive oil
8. Carefully place them oiled side down on the grill and cook until golden brown on the bottom, about 2 minutes
9. Carefully turn the quesadillas over with a large metal spatula, close the grill cover, and continue cooking for 1 to 2 minutes, until the cheese has melted and the tortillas are crisp
10. Remove the quesadillas from the grill and cut into quarters for serving
11. Top each quarter with a spoonful of relish
12. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
