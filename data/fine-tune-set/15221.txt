Please give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Bliss Chicken Pizza

Ingredients:
• Prepared pizza crust
• Sugar
• Soy sauce
• Garlic
• Jalapeno
• Chicken breasts
• Salt & pepper
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Meat seasoning
• Poultry seasoning
• Onion
• Red pepper
• Canned corn
• Green onion
• Cheese

Steps:
1. In saucepan, add oil, butter, chicken breasts, onion and seasonings
2. Cook until chicken is done, turning once
3. While chicken is cooking, prepare sauce and spoon over pizza crust
4. Next put a thin layer of selected cheese
5. Slice chicken and distribute evenly over crust
6. Add onions, peppers, corn and a little more cheese
7. Bake on foil lined cookie sheet at 300 for 8 minutes
8. Top with green onion
9. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
