Give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Easy Tofu No Meat Balls Vegan And Gluten Free

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Quick oats
• Braggs liquid aminos
• Fresh parsley
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Cornstarch
• Nutritional yeast
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Combine first 6 ingredients in medium mixing bowl and use your hands to mix well
2. Shape into 1 1 / 2" balls, and roll in mixture of corn starch and nutritional yeast
3. Saute balls in oil until browned
4. Add to your favorite pasta dish!<|endoftext|>
