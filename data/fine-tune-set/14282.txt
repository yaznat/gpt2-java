Can you give me a tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Fabulous Tofu With Delicious Nutritional Yeast

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Nutritional yeast
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Salt and pepper
• Tamari

Steps:
1. Heat a big flat pan to medium / medium high heat with olive oil
2. Drain the tofu block and pat dry with a paper towel
3. Cut the tofu widthwise into about 16 thin slices
4. Bread each slice of tofu with nutritional yeast, make sure each piece is really well coated before placing it in the pan
5. Cook the tofu until the edges are golden brown, then flip over and cook the other side the same
6. Salt and pepper to taste while in the pan
7. Serve on toast with tamari or soy sauce for dipping<|endoftext|>
