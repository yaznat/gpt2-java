Ingredients:
• Sushi rice
• Seasoned rice vinegar
• Sesame seeds
• Avocado
• Nori
• Imitation crabmeat
• English cucumber
• Pickled ginger
• Light mayonnaise
• Milk
• Wasabi powder

Steps:
1. In saucepan, combine rice and 1 1 / 4 cups water
2. Bring to boil
3. Cover and reduce heat to low
4. Cook for 25 minutes or until rice is tender
5. With fork, stir in rice vinegar and sesame seeds
6. Meanwhile, line 8-inch round or 8-inch square cake pan with plastic wrap
7. With spatula, press rice firmly and evenly into pan
8. Let cool completely
9. Turn out onto flat serving plate
10. Top with layer of avocado slices
11. Using scissors, cut nori into thin strips
12. Sprinkle over avocado
13. Arrange crabmeat, cucumber and ginger over top
14. Stir together mayonnaise, milk and wasabi powder
15. Drizzle over top
16. Cut into wedges to serve<|endoftext|>
