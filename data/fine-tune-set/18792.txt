Ingredients:
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Bisquick
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Semisweet chocolate morsel

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. In a large bowl mix bisquick and pudding mix
3. Stir in vegetable oil and egg with a wooden spoon until dough forms a ball
4. Shape dough into 1 / 2-inch balls
5. Place onto ungreased cookie sheets
6. Flatten with bottom of a drinking glass
7. Sprinkle with sugar, can use colored sugar for the holidays
8. Bake for 5-8 minutes<|endoftext|>
