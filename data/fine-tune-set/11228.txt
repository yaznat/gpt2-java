Ingredients:
• Nori
• Sushi rice
• Cucumber
• Eel
• Avocados

Steps:
1. Cook the unagi according to the package
2. Wash the cucumber and slice into long thin strips
3. Slice the avocado into very thin slices
4. Slice the unagi into long thin strips
5. Roll the sushi inside-out, using cucumber and unagi as your fillings
6. Place the avocado slices overlapping on the sushi
7. Serve with shoyu, wasabi, and ginger if desired
8. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
