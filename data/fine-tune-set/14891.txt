What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Fried Rice With Veggies

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Soy sauce
• Stir fry vegetables
• Cooked white rice

Steps:
1. Heat wok over medium heat, add oil swirl to coat pan
2. Add veggies, cook stir often for 1-2 minutes
3. Add rice, cook for 10 minutes, stir often until rice is light brown
4. Add soy sauce, stir to coat rice
5. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
