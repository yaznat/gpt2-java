Ingredients:
• Fish fillets
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Garlic clove
• Tomatoes
• Parsley
• Oregano
• Cinnamon
• Salt and pepper
• Red wine

Steps:
1. Rinse fish with cold water
2. Oil baking pan with half the oil
3. Place slices of fish on pan
4. Sauce: heat remaining oil in frying pan, fry onions and garlic on medium heat for 5 minutes
5. Add remaining ingredients, cover and cook for 10 minute
6. Pour sauce over fish
7. Bake 45 minutes at 375 degrees f
8. Serve with a salad and crusty bread<|endoftext|>
