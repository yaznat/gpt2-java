Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Curry powder
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Fresh ground pepper
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Sea salt
• Chicken broth
• Tomato sauce
• Lentils
• Bay leaves
• Plain low-fat yogurt
• Cucumber
• Lemon juice
• Mango chutney
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large pan over medium heat
2. Add onions and cook until sizzling
3. Cover, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 10 to 15 minutes
4. Uncover and cook until liquid is almost absorbed, about 8 minutes
5. Add garlic, curry powder, nutmeg, cinnamon, and pepper
6. Cook until onion starts to turn golden brown and spices are fragrant
7. Add broth, tomato sauce, lentils, and bay leaves
8. Add chicken breasts
9. Cover and simmer, stirring occasionally, until lentils are tender, about 20 minutes
10. Meanwhile, combine yogurt, cucumber, lemon juice, and a pinch of sea salt in a small bowl
11. Cover and chill until ready to serve
12. Remove and discard bay leaves from curry
13. Transfer to serving bowls
14. Serve with a dollop of cucumber yogurt and mango chutney
15. Garnish with cilantro leaves<|endoftext|>
