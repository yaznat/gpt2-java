Ingredients:
• Tart apples
• Lemon, juice of
• White sugar
• Frozen puff pastry
• Butter
• Milk
• Egg yolks
• Vanilla extract
• Orange
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Nutmeg scented orange custard: in small pan, scald milk, remove from heat, set aside
2. Over double broiler beat egg yolks and sugar until thick
3. Add milk in slowly
4. Cook over simmering water, whisking constantly for 10 minutes
5. Remove from heat and add vanilla, orange rind and nutmeg
6. Place a piece of plastic wrap over custard to prevent a skin from forming, cool and chill
7. Custard may be served warm or cold
8. Apple tartlet: preheat oven to 425 degrees
9. Place thinly sliced apples in small bowl and toss with lemon juice and 2 tsps sugar
10. On lightly floured board lay out the puff pastry
11. Cut out 4 circles 5" in diameter
12. Sprinkle the baking sheet with water and place the pastry circles on sheet
13. Brush the circles with butter and sprinkle with the remaining 1 tsp sugar
14. Place the apples over the circle in a pinwheel type pattern, remembering to leave a 1 / 4" border of uncovered pastry all around
15. Drizzle remaining butter over the apples
16. Bake the tarts for 10 minutes
17. Spoon custard sauce onto dessert plate and place a warm tart in the center, spoon a tsp of sauce over apple tart as well<|endoftext|>
