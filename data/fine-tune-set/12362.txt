Give me a soup recipe.<|endoftext|>Albanian White Bean Soup Jani Me Fasule

Ingredients:
• Dry white beans
• Water
• Onion
• Butter
• Tomato paste
• Flat leaf parsley
• Sweet paprika
• Sea salt
• Chili powder

Steps:
1. In a large pot add rinsed presoaked white beans and water
2. Bring to a full boil and cook for 10 minutes
3. In a skillet on medium heat add butter and onion
4. Cook until tender
5. Add to the beans
6. Add the tomato paste, chopped parsley, sweet paprika, and chili powder
7. Mix well
8. Reduce heat to low and cook covered 3 - 4 hours
9. Adding more water if needed
10. Add sea salt, to taste
11. Enjoy especially after a day or two covered in the refrigerator!<|endoftext|>
