Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Swiss Chard Omelette Dinner

Ingredients:
• Swiss chard
• Garlic clove
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Zucchini
• Mushroom
• Eggs
• Cheese
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In skillet, heat oil and garlic on medium heat
2. Add chopped chard
3. I use both the stalks and the leaves
4. Stalks when cooked taste something like celery
5. Leaves taste a lot like spinach
6. Chop and add remaining ingredients in order: onion, zucchini, mushrooms
7. Cover for just a couple minutes
8. When you start to see the mushrooms show signs of cooking, check the chard
9. When leaves are just wilted, put veggies on a plate
10. Return skillet to burner
11. Scramble egg and cheese together
12. I also add a little pepper
13. Place egg on top of veggies and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
