Give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Berries With Custard Sauce Diabetic

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Nonfat milk
• Egg yolks
• Berries

Steps:
1. In a heavy medium saucepan, combine sugar, cornstarch, and salt
2. Stir in milk
3. Cook and stir, over medium heat, until thickened and bubbly
4. Cook and stir for 2 minutes more
5. Remove from heat
6. Gradually whisk about half of the hot mixture into the beaten egg yolks
7. Return all of the egg yolk mixture to the saucepan
8. Cook and stir, over medium heat, just until mixture is bubbly
9. Remove from heat
10. Quickly cool the custard by placing the saucepan into a bowl half-filled with ice water for 3 minutes, stirring constantly
11. Strain mixture through a fine mesh sieve into a medium bowl
12. Stir in sugar substitute and vanilla
13. Cover the surface with plastic wrap
14. Chill for 2 to 24 hours
15. To serve, divide berries among 6 dessert dishes
16. Spoon custard over berries<|endoftext|>
