What's a good tea recipe?<|endoftext|>Autumn Tea

Ingredients:
• Tea bags
• Boiling water
• Apple juice
• Cranberry juice
• Sugar
• Lemon juice
• Pumpkin pie spice

Steps:
1. Place the tea bags in a large heat-proof bowl
2. Add boiling water
3. Cover and steep for 8 minutes
4. Discard tea bags
5. Add the remaining ingredients to tea
6. Stir until sugar is dissolve
7. Serve warm or over ice<|endoftext|>
