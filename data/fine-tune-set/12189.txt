Ingredients:
• Unbaked pie shell
• Granny smith apples
• Pecans
• Cornstarch
• Cinnamon
• Eggs
• Dark brown sugar
• Dark corn syrup
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Pecan halves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Prepare a 9" deep-dish pie plate
3. Set oven rack to lowest position
4. Prepare pie crust
5. Wrap in plastic wrap
6. Refrigerate for at least 20 minutes
7. Roll out the pie dough into a 14" circle
8. Fit into a 9" deep-dish pie pan
9. Trim and flute the edge
10. Refrigerate the pie dough while preparing the pie ingredients
11. In a bowl, combine apples, pecans, cornstarch and cinnamon
12. Spoon into the prepared pie shell
13. Cover completely with foil
14. Bake for 45 minutes
15. Meanwhile, while pie is baking, stir together, eggs, sugar, cornsyrup, butter and vanilla until smooth
16. After baking for 45 mins, remove the foil from the baked pie
17. Slowly pour the sugar mixture over apples
18. Bake, uncovered for 55 mins, or until top is browned, and the filling is set
19. Cool on rack
20. Garnish with pecan halves if desired
21. Serve with whipped cream or icecream<|endoftext|>
