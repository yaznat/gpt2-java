Ingredients:
• Shoulder lamb chops
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Beer
• New potato
• Baby carrots
• White pearl onion
• Lamb stock
• Dark roux
• Fresh parsley leaves

Steps:
1. Season the lamb chops with salt and pepper
2. In a large dutch oven, over medium heat, add the oil
3. When the oil is hot, but not smoking add the chops
4. Sear for 2 to 3 minutes on each side remove the chops from the pan and set aside
5. Add the beer and continue to cook for 1 minute, scraping any brown particles off the bottom of the pan
6. Add the lamb back to the pan
7. In a mixing bowl, toss the vegetables with salt and pepper
8. Add the vegetables to the pan
9. Cover with the stock bring the liquid to a boil, cover and reduce the heat to medium low simmer for about 2 hours or until the lamb falls off the bone
10. Stir in the roux and continue to cook for 10 minutes
11. Stir in the parsley and spoon into serving bowls<|endoftext|>
