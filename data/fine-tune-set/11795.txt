Ingredients:
• Butter
• Curry powder
• Onion
• Raisins
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Short-grain rice
• Chicken broth
• Slivered almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Melt butter in a saucepan that is oven-proof or a 1 1 / 2 casserole pan add onions and raisins
3. Cook stirring constantly until onion is softened and lightly colored
4. Add broth and bring to a boil
5. Add rice and spices and cover
6. Transfer to oven and bake for 50-60 minutes or until liquid is absorbed and rice is tender
7. Remove from oven
8. Stir in nuts and serve<|endoftext|>
