Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>It Smells Like Christmas Pumpkin Bread

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Allspice
• Ground cloves
• Sugar
• Water
• Oil
• Eggs
• Pumpkin
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Grease two 9x5x3-inch loaf pans
2. In a large bowl mix together all of the dry ingredients
3. Blend in the water and oil, and mix in the eggs
4. Blend in the pumpkin
5. Fold in the pecans
6. Divide the batter between the prepared pans
7. Bake at 350f for 1 hour, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
