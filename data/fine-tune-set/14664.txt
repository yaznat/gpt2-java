Ingredients:
• Long grain rice
• Olive oil
• White fish fillets
• Red capsicum
• Spring onions
• Lemon zest
• Salt
• Pepper
• Green beans
• Butter
• Anchovy fillets
• Lemon juice
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Cook rice in a large saucepan of boiling, salted water until tender
2. Rinse under cold water
3. Drain well
4. Lace in a large bowl
5. Heat oil in a large non-stick pan
6. Cook fish both sides
7. Remove and cover to keep warm
8. Clean and oil pan
9. Cook capsicum until soft
10. Add onions and rind, stirring for 1 minute
11. Add mixture to rice
12. Season with salt and pepper
13. To make anchovy butter, melt butter in a frying pan
14. Add anchovies and juice
15. Cook, stirring for 1 minute to break up anchovies
16. Remove from heat
17. Stir in parsley
18. Serve fish over rice with green beans
19. Drizzle with anchovy butter
