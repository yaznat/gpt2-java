Give me a salad recipe.<|endoftext|>Al S Egg Salad

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Mayonnaise
• Dijon mustard
• Dried onion flakes
• Dried dill weed
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Separate the egg yolks from the whites and place in separate bowls
2. Chop the egg whites and set aside
3. Mash the egg yolks thoroughly
4. Add the mayonnaise, mustard, onion, and dillweed
5. Salt and pepper, to taste
6. Blend until smooth
7. Add the chopped egg whites to the yolk mixture
8. Adjust seasonings<|endoftext|>
