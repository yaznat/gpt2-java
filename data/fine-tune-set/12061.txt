What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>All Butter Pie Crust

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• Ice water

Steps:
1. Whisk together the flour and salt
2. Dice the butter into small cubes, or cut it into pats
3. Work it into the flour until it's well-distributed, but not fully incorporated
4. Larger, pea-sized pieces of butter will be scattered throughout the mixture
5. Tossing with a fork or your fingers, drizzle in the ice water
6. Stop adding water when the dough starts to come together, and grab it in your hands
7. If it holds together easily, without crumbling, it's ready
8. If it has dry spots, or pieces break off easily, add a bit more water until it's totally cohesive
9. Gather the dough into a ball, and divide it in half
10. Gently pat / shape each half into a rough disk
11. Roll immediately, if desired
12. For better texture, chill the dough for at least 30 minutes before rolling
13. If dough has been refrigerated longer than 30 minutes, allow it to warm at room temperature for 10 to 15 minutes, until it rolls easily<|endoftext|>
