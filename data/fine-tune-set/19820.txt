Ingredients:
• Miniature marshmallows
• Granulated sugar
• Evaporated milk
• Butter
• Salt
• Milk chocolate chips
• Vanilla
• Toffee pieces

Steps:
1. Line 9 x 9 inch pan with foil, leaving 1 inch overhang on two sides
2. Grease foil with cooking spray
3. Set aside
4. Combine first 5 ingredients in large saucepan
5. Bring to a rolling boil, stirring often
6. Boil, uncovered, for 5 minutes, stirring constantly
7. Remove from heat
8. Add chocolate chips and vanilla
9. Stir until smooth
10. Add toffee bits
11. Stir well
12. Immediately pour into prepared pan
13. Smooth top
14. Let stand until cool
15. Holding foil, remove fudge from pan
16. Discard foil
17. Cuts into 64 pieces
18. Use 4 crushed skor or heath chocolate bars
19. For best results, put unwrapped bars in a resealable freezer bag and freeze for 15 minutes
20. Seal bag
21. Hit with a met mallet or hammer until bars are in small pieces
