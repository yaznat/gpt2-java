Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Plain yogurt
• Honey
• Applesauce
• Cinnamon
• Ground almonds
• Baking soda
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Combine wet ingredients in bowl of food processor
3. Pulse to beat eggs well
4. Add dry ingredients
5. Mix well
6. Fill paper muffin cups almost full
7. Bake on center rack of oven for 25-30 minutes or until lightly golden and toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
8. Remove muffins from pan and cool on rack
9. Store in refrigerator
10. These freeze extremely well
11. Try the following variations in place of the applesauce and cinnamon: 1 banana and 1 teaspoon vanilla, 1 whole orange and 1 teaspoon ginger, 1 whole zucchini or summer squash and 1 teaspoon dried tarragon
12. Pulse fruits into a smooth paste before adding the other wet ingredients<|endoftext|>
