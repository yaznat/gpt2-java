Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Onion
• Frozen french fries
• Frozen peas
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Water
• Ketchup
• Dried parsley
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dried marjoram
• Dry mustard
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Brown the burger and tenderize the onion together in a large skillet
2. Drain
3. In a greased 9x13-inch casserole dish, layer 1 / 2 of the fries, peas and meat mixture
4. Repeat another layer
5. In a separate mixing bowl, combine soup, water, ketchup, parsley, worcestershire sauce, marjoram, dry mustard, salt, and pepper
6. Pour over the top of the casserole
7. Bake, uncovered, at 350f for 40-45 minutes<|endoftext|>
