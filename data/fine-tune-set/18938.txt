Ingredients:
• Cornmeal
• Flour
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Buttermilk
• Egg
• Frozen corn kernels
• Butter
• Whole milk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. In medium bowl, combine cornmeal, flour, brown sugar, salt, baking powder, and baking soda and mix well
3. In small bowl, combine buttermilk, egg and corn and beat until combined
4. Add to flour mixture and stir just until dry ingredients are moistened
5. Place butter in a heavy duty 8-inch square baking pan and melt butter in hot oven for about 5 minutes
6. Remove pan from oven and immediately pour batter over butter
7. Pour whole milk over batter
8. Do not stir
9. Bake for 25-35 minutes or until bread is light golden brown and begins to pull away from sides of pan
10. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
