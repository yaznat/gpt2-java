Give me a recipe for curry.<|endoftext|>Banana Curry Vegetarian

Ingredients:
• Red onions
• Butter
• Curry powder
• Cooking apples
• Red chile
• Apricot jam
• Sultanas
• Wine vinegar
• Salt
• Water
• Bananas
• Rice
• Hard-boiled eggs

Steps:
1. Brown the onion in the butter, then add the curry powder and fry for for a few more minutes
2. Add the remaining ingredients, except for the bananas, cover the pan and simmer for 10 minutes
3. Halve the bananas, and pack them down into the sauce, and continue to simmer with the lid on till be bananas become soft,
4. Serve on a bed of rice, garnished with the hard boild eggs<|endoftext|>
