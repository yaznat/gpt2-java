Please give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Beef Stew Everyday Food

Ingredients:
• Beef chuck
• Tomato paste
• Balsamic vinegar
• All-purpose flour
• Salt and pepper
• Onion
• White potatoes
• Carrot
• Garlic cloves
• Bay leaves
• Water

Steps:
1. Place beef in a 5-quart slow cooker
2. Distribute tomato paste, vinegar, and flour over beef
3. Season generously with salt and pepper
4. Add onions, potatoes, carrots, garlic, and bay leaves
5. Pour in about 3 cups of water or enough to cover the ingredients
6. Cover slow cooker
7. Cook on high until beef is fork-tender, about 5 hours<|endoftext|>
