What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Raisin Oven Pancake

Ingredients:
• Baking apple
• Golden raisin
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Eggs
• Milk
• All-purpose flour
• Butter
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 deg
2. F
3. Spray 9 inch pie plate with nonstick cooking spray
4. Combine apple, raisins, brown sugar and cinnamon in medium bowl
5. Transfer to prepared pie plate
6. Bake, uncovered, 10 - 15 minutes or until apple begins to soften
7. Remove from oven
8. Increase temperature to 450 deg
9. F
10. Meanwhile, whisk eggs, milk, flour and butter in medium bowl until blended
11. Pour batter over apple mixture
12. Bake 15 minutes or until pancake is golden brown
13. Invert onto serving dish
14. Sprinkle with powdered sugar if desired<|endoftext|>
