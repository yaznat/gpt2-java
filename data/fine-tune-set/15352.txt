Please give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Apple And Cheddar Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Red onion
• Cheddar cheese
• Dijon mustard
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Granny smith apple

Steps:
1. Combine beef, onion, cheddar, mustard, salt, pepper and apple in a large bowl and form into four patties
2. Grill over medium heat or broil, flipping once, until done to your taste, 6 to 8 minutes
3. Or, spray a large skillet with cooking spray and cook over medium heat, 6 to 8 minutes<|endoftext|>
