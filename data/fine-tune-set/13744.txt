What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Apple Brown Sugar Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Soy sauce
• Apple juice
• Brown sugar
• Onion powder
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Combine ingredients in a plastic bag or bowl
2. Marinade pork chops in refrigerator
3. Grill over direct medium heat for 10-12 minutes, turning once
4. Serve with rice<|endoftext|>
