Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Cayenne
• Red kidney beans
• Salsa
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Light sour cream
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. In nonstick skillet, cook beef, onion and garlic over medium-high heat, breaking up meat with wooden spoon, until meat is no longer pink, about 5 minutes
2. Add chili powder, oregano, salt and cayenne
3. Cook for 2 minutes
4. In large bowl, mash kidney beans
5. Stir in 1 / 4 cup of the salsa
6. Add beef mixture
7. Stir to blend
8. Place 1 tortilla on rimmed baking sheet
9. Spread one-third of the beef mixture right to edge of tortilla then sprinkle with one-quarter of the cheese
10. Repeat layers twice
11. Top with remaining tortilla and cheese
12. Bake in 450f oven until cheese is melted, 10 minutes
13. Slice into wedges
14. Top with sour cream, lettuce and remaining salsa<|endoftext|>
