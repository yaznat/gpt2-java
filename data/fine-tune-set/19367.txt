Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Sugar
• Milk
• Cinnamon
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine first four ingredients
2. Make a well in the center of the flour mixture
3. In a small mixing bowl, combine oil, egg, sugar and milk
4. Pour milk mixture into center of the well
5. Mix with a spoon just until moistened
6. Spoon batter into greased muffin pan, filling half way
7. Bake at 350f for 20 minutes
8. For topping, mix 1 / 2 cup sugar with the cinnamon
9. Dip tops of muffins in the melted butter, than in the cinnamon topping
10. Cool on a rack or serve warm<|endoftext|>
