Ingredients:
• Watercress
• Alfalfa sprout
• Tomatoes
• Cucumber
• Onion
• Mushroom
• Bell pepper
• Sunflower seeds
• Nonfat cottage cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Olive oil
• Lemon juice
• Tahini
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Prepare dressing and set aside
2. Arrange greens in bowl and top with remaining veggies
3. Top with a dollop of cottage cheese and a sprinkling of cheddar
4. Scatter sunflower seeds
5. Dress salad and enjoy<|endoftext|>
