What's a good cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Bloody Mary Shrimp Cocktail Betty Crocker

Ingredients:
• Medium shrimp
• Tomato juice
• Vodka
• Red pepper sauce
• Sugar
• Celery salt
• Fresh parsley
• Seafood cocktail sauce
• Green olives

Steps:
1. Arrange shrimp in a single layer in shallow rectangular glass or plastic dish so the shrimp rest in the marinate
2. Actually a gallon ziplock bag works fine - but marinate must cool prior to combining
3. Heat tomato juice, vodka, and pepper sauce to boiling in 1-quart saucepan over medium-high heat
4. Stir in sugar
5. Reduce heat
6. Simmer uncovered 5 minutes
7. Stirring occasionally
8. Stir in celery salt and parsley
9. Pour marinate over shrimp, cover, refrigerate, but not more than 3 hours as tomato juice will begin to tough shrimp if left longer
10. Mix cocktail sauce and olives
11. Pour into small serving bowl
12. Remove shrimp from marinade with slotted spoon
13. Arrange on serving platter
14. Serve shrimp with sauce and toothpicks
15. Serve with sauce and toothpicks<|endoftext|>
