Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Confetti Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Garlic cloves
• Gingerroot
• Rice
• Water chestnuts
• Ham
• Carrot
• Frozen peas
• Green onion
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a 12-inch skillet
2. Add eggs
3. Tilt skillet so eggs form about a 6-inch pancake
4. Cook until set spooning oil over eggs where necessary to set edges
5. Remove from skillet with a spatula
6. Cool
7. Cut into strips
8. Add 3 tablespoons oil to skillet
9. Saute garlic and ginger
10. Add rice
11. Stir fry, lifting from from the bottom until grains separate
12. Add water chestnuts, ham, carrots, peas, green onion and soy sauce
13. Continue to lift from bottom while stir-frying
14. Remove from heat
15. Stir in egg strips<|endoftext|>
