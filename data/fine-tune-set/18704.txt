Steps:
1. Prepare pudding as indicated on box
2. Make a layer of the wafer cookies and graham crackers in the bottom
3. Of the bowl or pan, then add some sliced bananas
4. ( if using a see

8. Now continue this

10. At the top do not add cherry juice, instead top with remainin cool whip
