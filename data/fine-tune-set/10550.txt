Give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>Amazing Angel Hair Pasta With Shrimp

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Shrimp
• Garlic
• Heavy cream
• Italian-style diced tomatoes
• White wine
• Fresh mushrooms
• Fresh parsley
• Parmesan cheese
• Dried basil
• Red pepper flakes
• Angel hair pasta

Steps:
1. In a medium skillet over medium high heat, melt butter with the oil, then add the shrimp and garlic
2. Cook uncovered for about 5 minutes stirring occasionally until shrimp are done
3. Remove shrimp to a plate and cover to keep warm
4. Add remaining ingredients to skillet except for 1 / 4 c of parmesan cheese and the pasta
5. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer for about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
6. Cook pasta and drain
7. Toss shrimp and pasta into the skillet with sauce and distribute everything evenly
8. Serve topped with the remaining parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
