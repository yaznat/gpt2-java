What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>Autumn Harvest Stew

Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Parsnips
• Apples
• Onion
• Chicken
• Salt
• Pepper
• Olive oil
• Fresh rosemary
• Fresh sage
• Fresh thyme
• Fresh parsley
• Vegetable broth
• Apple juice

Steps:
1. Dump all veggies, chicken, salt, pepper and olive oil into crockpot and stir
2. Tie herbs together with string and put in the pot
3. Pour in liquids
4. Cook on low for 7-8 hours<|endoftext|>
