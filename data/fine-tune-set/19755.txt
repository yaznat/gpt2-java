What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Cheesy Bacon Fries

Ingredients:
• Frozen french fries
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Cook fries as directed on package
2. Place in oven proof dish
3. Sprinkle with bacon, cheese and onions
4. Place 4-6 inches under preheated broiler for 1-2 minute until cheese melts<|endoftext|>
