Ingredients:
• Chicken thighs
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Reduced-sodium chicken broth
• Black bean garlic sauce
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Sweet red pepper
• Cornstarch
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large skillet
2. Brown chicken thighs and remove to crock pot
3. Add onion and garlic to crock
4. Whisk together chicken broth, black bean sauce and soy sauce
5. Pour over the chicken
6. Cover and cook on low for 4-5 hours
7. Whisk together cornstarch and a small amount of water and pour over chicken
8. Add red pepper slices, stir, cover, and cook another 30 minutes
9. Garnish with cilantro<|endoftext|>
