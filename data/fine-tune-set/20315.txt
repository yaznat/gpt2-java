Give me a stir fry recipe.<|endoftext|>Chinese Beef Stir Fry Over Golden Pancake

Ingredients:
• Dry sherry
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Sirloin
• Chicken broth
• Oyster sauce
• Chili-garlic sauce
• White pepper
• Sesame oil
• Chinese egg noodles
• Onion
• Asparagus

Steps:
1. Marinate beef
2. Stir sherry, soy, cornstarch, garlic and ginger
3. Toss beef to marinate
4. Sauce: stir fry chicken broth, oyster sauce, chili garlic sauce, cornstarch, pepper and sesame oil together
5. Set aside
6. Cook noodles in large pot, according to directions
7. Drain, rinse under cool water, drain again
8. Heat skillet over medium high heat
9. Add 1 t of oil and coat
10. Spread noodles evenly and cook, pressing lightly from time to time to form a cake, until bottom is golden brown, about 5 minutes
11. Turn cake over
12. Drizzle 1 t oil on bottom and cook other side
13. Transfer to plate
14. Heat wok over high heat
15. Add 1 t oil, swirl add meat and stir fry until no longer pink
16. Remove from pan
17. Add onion to wok and stir fry until crisp tender
18. Add asparagus and cook
19. Return meat to wok pour in sauce and bring to boil
20. Cook until slightly thickened
21. Spoon over cake<|endoftext|>
