Give me a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>Amazing Ranch Potato Casserole

Ingredients:
• Sour cream
• Bacon
• Ranch dressing
• Parsley
• Cheddar cheese
• New potatoes
• French's french fried onions
• Butter

Steps:
1. Mix the sour cream, bacon bits, dressing, parsley and cheese together
2. Toss mixture with potatoes and place in a 9 x 13-inch casserole dish
3. Mix all ingredients together for the topping
4. Spread over the potato mixture in casserole dish
5. Bake at 350f for 40 minutes
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
