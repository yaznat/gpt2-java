What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Brownie Biscotti

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Butter
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Brazil nuts

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except the nuts into mixer bowl
2. Blend for 3 to 4 minutes
3. Dough should form a ball
4. Toss in the nuts and combine well
5. Pat into a 14 x 4 inch log on parchment lined cookie sheet
6. Flatten slightly
7. Bake 350 degrees preheated oven for 30 minutes
8. Remove onto a wire rack and cool 12 minutes
9. Slice into slices with a serrated knife
10. Place back on parchment sheet cut side down
11. Return to oven for 8 minutes
12. Turn each slice over
13. Return back to oven 8 minutes
14. Turn off oven, keep door slightly ajar
15. Leave in oven 40 minutes
16. Remove to a wire rack, and cool completely
17. Store airtight in a tin container<|endoftext|>
