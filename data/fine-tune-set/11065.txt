What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Applesauce
• Bread
• Butter
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Evenly spread the applesauce on four slices of bread
2. Top with remaining bread to make 4 sandwiches
3. Spread butter on the outsides of the sandwiches
4. Place sandwiches in a skillet over medium-high heat
5. Cook for 3-4 minutes on each side or until golden brown
6. Mix together the sugar and cinnamon
7. Sprinkle over hot sandwiches
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
