What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>Algerian Basboussa Semolina Cake With Syrup

Ingredients:
• Semolina
• Plain flour
• Baking powder
• Unsweetened dried shredded coconut
• Granulated sugar
• Sunflower oil
• Lemon
• Yogurt
• Eggs
• Vanilla flavoring
• Water
• Orange flower water
• Lemon juice
• Whole almond

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 180c grease a large baking dish
2. In a pan, place the sugar & water from the 'syrup ingredients', bring to the boil then simmer until you have a pale golden colour
3. Remove from the heat & stir in the lemon juice & the orange flower water
4. Set aside to cool, you can also store this syrup in the fridge until you need to use it
5. Place all the wet ingredients in a large bowl & using a hand mixer, mix until creamy
6. Add the dry ingredients a little at a time & mix well
7. Pour into your baking dish & level off the surface with the back of a dessert spoon or palett knife
8. With a small knife, score the surface into equal squares or diamonds & place a blanced almond in the centre of each square / diamond
9. Bake in the oven until golden - this will take approximately 30-40 minutes
10. Remove from the oven & cut through the scored squares / diamonds
11. Pour the syrup over the top & allow the basboussa to soak up the syrup before serving
12. It is best to leave it for at least 2 hours for best results<|endoftext|>
