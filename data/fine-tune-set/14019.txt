Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Crock Pot Lamb Chops

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Dried oregano
• Dried thyme
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Lamb loin chops
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Place onion in a crock pot
2. Combine oregano, thyme, garlic powder, salt, and pepper
3. Rub over the lamb chops
4. Place chops over onion
5. Top with garlic
6. Cover and cook on low for 4 to 6 hours or until the meat is tender<|endoftext|>
