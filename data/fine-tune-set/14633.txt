What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>All Star Breakfast Rice

Ingredients:
• Ground cinnamon
• Brown sugar
• Milk
• Cooked rice
• Fresh blueberries

Steps:
1. Combine rice, milk, brown sugar and cinnamon in small saucepan
2. Cook over medium heat until thick and creamy, about 10 minutes
3. Spoon into serving bowl, let cool 3 minutes
4. Top with fresh berries<|endoftext|>
