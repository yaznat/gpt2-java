Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>100 Honey Whole Wheat Cracked Wheat Bread

Ingredients:
• Warm water
• Fast-rising active dry yeast
• Milk
• Honey
• Oil
• Salt
• Eggs
• Stone ground whole wheat flour
• Cracked wheat

Steps:
1. In the bowl of your electric mixer dissolve yeast in warm water
2. Add milk, honey, oil, salt and eggs
3. Mix until blended
4. Add 2 1 / 2 cups of the flour and beat for 3 to 4 minutes
5. Cover bowl with plastic wrap or a warm towel and allow sponge to rise in a warm place until doubled in bulk, about 30 minutes
6. Mix in remaining flour reserving 1 / 2 c for kneading
7. At this point you may add the softened cracked wheat if desired
8. This may seem a little stickier than you may be used to but as long as your fingers don't stick in the dough you have the right amount of flour
9. Turn out onto floured board or counter top or use the dough hook from your mixer
10. Knead for 8 to 10 minutes until elastic and smooth
11. Put in a large buttered bowl and let rise in warm place until doubled in bulk
12. Punch dough down
13. On lightly floured board or counter top divide into 3 equal portions
14. Shape into loaves and place in buttered loaf pans approximately 8" x 4"
15. Cover and let rise for the third time until doubled in bulk
16. Bake at 375 degrees for 30 - 35 minutes
17. The bread tests done when the crust make a hollow sound when tapped
18. Remove from pans and brush with melted butter
19. Cover with a hot damp towel to soften crust if desired<|endoftext|>
