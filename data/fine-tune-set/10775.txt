What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>1950S Picture Salad Peach And Cottage Cheese

Ingredients:
• Canned peach halves
• Cottage cheese with chives
• Lettuce cups

Steps:
1. Put a lettuce cup on each salad plate
2. This is a leaf of lettuce that is curled to form a cup-like shape
3. Place one peach half in each cup
4. Top with about 1 / 2 cup cottage cheese mixed with chives
5. Pineapple chunks 'n' cherries: arrange these two in lettuce cups
6. If using fresh cherries, it is nice to pit them
7. Apricot 'n' cream cheese: use drained apricot halves, cut side up
8. Top with softened cream cheese and chopped dates
9. Pineapple-banana nut: plae fluted sliced bananas on rained pineapple slices
10. Sprinkle with salted peanuts
11. Molded fruit cocktail: fold well-drained fruit cocktail into slightly thickened fruit jello, using the syrup for part of the liquid used to make the jello
12. Blushing pear: put 2 pear halves together with softened cream cheese
13. Sprinkle paprika on one side to make a blush
14. Top with cream cheese green pepper, and a water cress stem<|endoftext|>
