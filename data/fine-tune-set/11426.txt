Ingredients:
• Italian bread
• Butter
• Poppy seed
• Garlic salt
• Cheddar cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f cut loaf into 10 slices almost to bottom crust
2. Combine butter, poppy seed and garlic salt in small bowl
3. Mix well
4. Brush cut-sides of bread with butter mixture
5. Place 1 slice cheddar and 1 slice monterey jack cheese in each cut
6. Sprinkle with parsley
7. Wrap loaves loosely in heavy-duty aluminum foil
8. Seal top and ends
9. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes or until heated through and cheese is melted
10. Grilling directions: prepare bread as directed above
11. Prepare grill placing coals to one side
12. Heat until coals are ash white
13. Place bread on grill opposite coals
14. Cover
15. Grill until heated through and cheese is melted
