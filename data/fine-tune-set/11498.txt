What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Amy Grants Grandma Grant Banana Nut Bread

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Flour
• Pecans
• Baking soda
• Salt

Steps:
1. Grease 8x4 loaf pan
2. Cream sugar with butter
3. Mix in eggs, bananas, flour, pecans, baking soda and salt
4. Pour into prepared pan
5. Place loaf pan in cold oven
6. Set oven to 300f and bake bread 1 1 / 2 hours or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
7. Cool breead in pan 5 minutes on wire rack, then remove from pan and cool completely, right side up<|endoftext|>
