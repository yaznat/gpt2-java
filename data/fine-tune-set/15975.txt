What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Grilled Burritos

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Red kidney beans
• Instant rice
• Taco seasoning mix
• Refried beans
• Cheese
• Sour cream
• Tortilla
• Taco sauce
• Onion
• Chile
• Tomatoes
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef and drain
2. While browning ground beef, cook instant rice according to box directions
3. Warm refried beans in small saucepan
4. In a frying pan mix ground beef, enough water to just cover bottom of pan and taco seasoning mix
5. Stir well
6. Cook covered for 5 to 10 minutes until meat and sauce are combined
7. Add cooked rice and kidney beans to meat mixture
8. Cook until warmed throughout
9. Warm tortilla shells in microwave for 10-12 seconds
10. Cover center of shell with refried beans
11. Add meat mixture, then cheese, sour cream, taco sauce and whatever other ingredients you prefer
12. Fold over one side of tortilla, then fold in both ends
13. Finally fold over the other side of tortilla
14. Carefully place tortilla folded side down on a warm / hot frying pan or griddle
15. Let brown then turn over and brown other side<|endoftext|>
