Ingredients:
• Navy beans
• Pineapple chunks
• Bacon
• Tomato paste
• Molasses
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Cider vinegar
• Honey
• Pineapple juice
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Cinnamon
• Ground ginger

Steps:
1. Rinse beans and cook overnight on low in a slow cooker
2. Rinse again and place in a large bowl
3. Open pineapple chunks and drain, add to the beans
4. Mix tomato paste, molassas, soy sauce, cider vinegar, honey, pineapple juice, garlic powder, black pepper, cinnamon and ground ginger in a small bowl then add to beans
5. Mix to coat completely
6. Divide the mixture between 2 baking dishes that will fit in your smoker
7. Cut the bacon strips into @3 / 4 inch pieces then layer the top of each baking dish
8. Place in your smoker with the wood chips of your choice at 200 degrees until the bacon is cooked, @6 hours
9. Please
10. If you are using an oven either place a pan of water in the oven or reduce the cooking time, to when the bacon is cooked to a golden brown
11. Mix both batches of beans in a large covered bowl, refrigerate over night, then gently reheat and serve the next day<|endoftext|>
