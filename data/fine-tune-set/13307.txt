Give me a chicken recipe.<|endoftext|>A True Texan S King Ranch Chicken

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Sour cream
• Salsa
• Evaporated milk
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Black olives
• Corn chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Take 1 / 2 the bag of chips and layer them on the bottom of a greased 13" x 9" x 2" casserole dish
3. Then place shredded chicken atop the layer of chips
4. Layer half of the cheese on top of the chicken
5. In a separate bowl, prepare sauce
6. Combine salsa, sour cream, cream of chicken, evaporated milk, and olives and mix well
7. Pour over casserole, then add the remaining cheese on top
8. Cook for 1 hour, or until the top is brown and bubbly
9. Check it when there is about 15 minutes left--you may want to cover with foil for remainder of cook time<|endoftext|>
