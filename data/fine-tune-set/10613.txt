Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Whipping cream
• Milk
• Fresh dill
• Green onion
• Capers
• Lemon peel
• Smoked salmon

Steps:
1. Cook pasta in large pot of boiling salted water until just tender but still firm to bite
2. Drain well
3. Return to same pot
4. Combine cream, milk, dill, onions, capers and lemon peel in heavy small saucepan
5. Bring to boil over medium-high heat
6. Add sauce to pasta
7. Toss to coat
8. Add salmon and toss to combine
9. Season win salt and pepper and serve<|endoftext|>
