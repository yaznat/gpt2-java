Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Bandscakes Dad S Famous Pancakes

Ingredients:
• White flour
• Buckwheat flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Egg whites
• Egg
• Milk
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients in a larger bowl
2. In a separate bowl beat eggs and egg whites with a fork
3. Add milk, and oil to egg mixture and mix together
4. Add egg mixture to the dry ingredients
5. Mix together until blended but do not over mix to avoid making pancakes gummy
6. Cook on skillet over medium heat
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
