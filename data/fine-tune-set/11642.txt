What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Ingredient Dump Cake

Ingredients:
• Crushed pineapple
• Cherry pie filling
• Yellow cake mix
• Butter
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Dump pineapples with juice and cherry pie filling into a 9 x 13 inch pan and smooth out
3. Sprinkle cake mix evenly over fruit
4. Next, sprinkle pecans over all
5. Drizzle butter over the top
6. Bake for an hour
7. Serve in pan<|endoftext|>
