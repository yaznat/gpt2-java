What's a good custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Custard Teacake

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Caster sugar
• Eggs
• Self raising flour
• Custard powder
• Milk
• Vanilla essence

Steps:
1. Grease and line base and side of 23cm deep round cake pan
2. Grease paper well
3. Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy
4. Beat in eggs one at a time, beat until combined
5. Stir in sifted flour and custard powder
6. Spread half the cake mixture into prepared pan, spread with cold custard, spread carefully with remaining cake mixture
7. Bake in moderate oven for about 40 to 45 minutes or until golden brown
8. Cool in pan
9. When cake is cold, carefully removed paper
10. Serve dusted with sifted icing sugar and fruit
11. -custard-blend custard powder and sugar with milk in saucepan
12. Stir constantly over heat until mixture boils and thickens, stir in butter and essence
13. Cool to room temperature<|endoftext|>
