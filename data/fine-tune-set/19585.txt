Can you give me a potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Anita S Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Egg
• Garlic salt
• Green olives
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Boil eggs put eggs in cold water and bring to a boil- once the pot comes to a good boil, turn off heat and leave the pot on the burner - put a lid on pot after you turn off fire
2. Let the pot sit on the burner for 20 minutes and then the eggs should be done
3. Cut cooled potatoes in a big bowl, add the chopped hard boiled eggs, your cut up olives, garlic salt to taste and 2-3 cups of kraft real mayonnaise, mix well
4. Serve immediately or cover and keep in refrigerator until ready<|endoftext|>
