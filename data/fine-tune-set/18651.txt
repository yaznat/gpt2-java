Ingredients:
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Whole wheat bread
• Dried apricot
• Egg beaters egg substitute
• Nonfat milk
• Canned pumpkin
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Ground cinnamon
• Fat-free whipped dessert topping

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degree f
2. Coat a 2-quart square baking dish with nonstick cooking spray
3. In the prepared baking dish, combine bread cubes and dried apricots
4. Set aside
5. In a large bowl, combine milk, pumpkin, egg, sugar, and cinnamon or pumpkin pie spice
6. Slowly pour pumpkin mixture over bread mixture in baking dish
7. Gently press bread mixture down into liquid to moisten
8. Let stand for 15 minutes
9. Bake, uncovered, for 45 to 50 minutes or until a knife inserted in center comes out clean
10. Cool slightly
11. Serve warm
12. If desired, serve with dessert topping
13. Makes 9 servings<|endoftext|>
