What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Almond Coconut Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Unsweetened chocolate squares
• Sugar
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Slivered almonds
• Vanilla extract
• Milk
• Marshmallows
• Flaked coconut
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. In a microwave, melt butter and chocolate
2. Stir until smooth
3. Cool slightly
4. Add sugar
5. Stir in the eggs, flour, almonds and vanilla
6. Transfer to a greased 13-in
7. X 9-in
8. X 2-in
9. Baking pan
10. Bake at 350 for 30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
11. Cool on a wire rack
12. In a large saucepan, combine filling ingredients
13. Bring to a boil
14. Pour over cooled brownies
15. In another saucepan, combine the chocolate chips, sugar, butter and milk
16. Bring to a boil
17. Spoon over the filling
18. Sprinkle with almonds
19. Cover and refrigerate for 2 hours or until set
20. Store in the refrigerator
21. Yield: 4 dozen<|endoftext|>
