Please generate a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>5 Ingredient Baked Potato Skins

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Cream cheese
• Bacon bits
• Cheddar cheese
• Red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. Cut potatoes into quarters lengthwise, scoop out center leaving 1 / 2 inch of flesh, reserve centers for another use
3. Combine cream cheese, cheddar, bacon bits and pepper flakes until blended, mixture should be thick
4. Press onto each skin
5. Bake for 15 minutes, switch to broil and toast tops for another 3 or until lightly browned
6. Cooking time may vary depending on if potatoes have been refrigerated
7. For oamc, tightly wrap in plastic wrap or vacuum seal and freeze, these reheat in 5-7 minutes in a toaster oven, great for hungry teens and superbowl parties!<|endoftext|>
