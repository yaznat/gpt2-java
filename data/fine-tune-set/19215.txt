Ingredients:
• Milk chocolate candy bars
• Burrito-size flour tortillas
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla ice cream
• Chocolate syrup
• Chocolate shavings
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Place 1 candy bar just below center of 1 tortilla
2. Fold bottom of tortilla up and over candy bar just until partially covered
3. Fold left and right sides of tortilla over
4. Roll up, and place, seam side down, on a baking sheet
5. Repeat procedure with remaining candy bars and tortillas
6. Freeze 20 minutes
7. Pour oil to depth of 1 inch into a large skillet
8. Heat to 375 degrees
9. Place 2 tortillas, seam sides down, in hot oil, gently pressing with tongs for a few seconds to seal
10. Fry tortillas 2 to 3 minutes on each side or until golden brown
11. Drain on paper towels<|endoftext|>
