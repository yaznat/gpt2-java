Ingredients:
• Peach
• Sugar-reduced peach jam
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Mix the peach and jam in a small bowl, set aside
2. In a small bowl, beat 1 teaspoon sugar with the egg yolks until pale and creamy
3. In another bowl, beat egg whites until stiff
4. Pour egg yolks over it, fold gently
5. Heat a non-stick 7" skillet, spray with non-stick cooking spray
6. Pour eggs into it
7. Cook for a few minutes until lightly browned, transfer to a broiler and continue cooking until top is browned
8. Watch carefully!
9. Fill with the peach mixture and fold, sprinkle with icing sugar<|endoftext|>
