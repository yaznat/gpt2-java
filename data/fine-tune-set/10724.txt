What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Bacon Broccoli Ranch Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Broccoli
• Crisp bacon
• Hidden valley original ranch dressing
• Black olives
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Fry bacon crisp & crumble
2. You don't want it to still be hot when you add it to pasta salad
3. Boil pasta according to package instructions
4. While pasta is boiling, cut broccoli into bite size florets
5. Rinse pasta in cold water & drain
6. In large mixing bowl combine pasta, broccoli, olives, ranch dressing to taste, crumbled bacon and salt & pepper
7. Chill before serving
8. I like to make it the day before i plan to serve it<|endoftext|>
