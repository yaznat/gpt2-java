Give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>Apple S Restaurant Election Day Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• White sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• White chocolate chips
• Milk chocolate chips
• English toffee bits

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Cream butter, gradually add sugars, beating well
3. Add eggs and vanilla, beat well
4. In separate bowl: combine flour, baking powder, soda, and salt - stir well
5. Add to butter mixture, beat at low speed until blended
6. Stir in toffee bits, chocolate chips
7. Drop dough by heaping tablespoons onto ungreased cookie sheet
8. Bake 10-15 minutes or until cookie is lightly browned
9. Let cool slightly on sheets, then cool completely on wire racks<|endoftext|>
