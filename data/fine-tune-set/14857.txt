Ingredients:
• Soft tofu
• Turmeric
• Onion powder
• Vegetable oil
• Cooked rice
• Cashews
• Yellow onions
• Salt
• Tamari
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Mix tofu, turmeric and onion powder in a bowl
2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a skillet
3. Add the tofu mixture and cook, stirring constantly, about 10 minutes
4. Remove from heat and set aside
5. Place a dry wok over medium heat
6. When it begins to smoke, add the remaining oil, the onions, and the rice
7. Stir fry for 3 minutes
8. Pour in the"sauce" and stir fry until the rice is heated through, about 4 minutes
9. Stir in the cashews and the tofu mixture<|endoftext|>
