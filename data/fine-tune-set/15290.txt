Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Cheeseburger Rice

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Water
• Ketchup
• Mustard
• Minute white rice
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef in large skillet
2. Drain
3. Add water, ketchup and mustard
4. Bring to a boil
5. Stir in 2 cups uncooked minute rice
6. Sprinkle with 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
7. Cover
8. Cook on low heat 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
