Generate a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Lickity Split Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Cooked rice
• Soy sauce
• Black pepper
• Garlic clove
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Green onions
• Egg

Steps:
1. In a large skillet or wok fry the bacon on medium heat until crispy
2. Set aside to cool and drain then cut up into little pieces
3. Save the bacon drippings
4. Increase heat to medium-high
5. Add the cooked rice to the pan and break up any lumps with a fork
6. Fry until the rice is browned, adding soy sauce and black pepper, to taste
7. Stir in the garlic and mixed vegetables
8. Reduce heat to medium and fry rice until the vegetables are brightly colored and heated through
9. Stir in the green onions and fry another minute
10. Remove from heat and add the crumbled bacon and chopped egg<|endoftext|>
