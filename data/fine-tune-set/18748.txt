Ingredients:
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Milk
• Egg yolks
• Vanilla extract
• Vanilla wafers
• Bourbon
• Rum
• Bananas
• English toffee-flavored candy bars
• Whipping cream
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Combine first 3 ingredients in a large heavy saucepan
2. Whisk in milk
3. Bring to a boil over medium heat, whisking constantly
4. Remove from heat
5. Beat egg yolks until thick and pale
6. Gradually stir one-fourth of hot mixture into yolks
7. Add to remaining hot mixture, stirring constantly
8. Cook, stirring constantly, 1 minute
9. Stir in vanilla
10. Layer one-third of wafers in a 16-cup trifle bowl
11. Combine bourbon and rum
12. Brush over wafers
13. Top with one-third of banana
14. Spoon one-third of custard over banana, and sprinkle with 1 / 3 cup crushed candy bar
15. Repeat layers twice
16. Beat cream at medium speed with an electric mixer until foamy
17. Gradually add powdered sugar, beating until soft peaks form
18. Spread whipped cream over trifle, and sprinkle with remaining crushed candy bar
19. Cover and chill 3 hours<|endoftext|>
