Ingredients:
• Cauliflower
• Chicken stock
• Sweet onion
• Butter
• Flour
• Light cream
• Cheddar cheese
• Salt
• Pepper
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Break the cauliflower down into small flowerettes, chop some of the stems up into bite size pieces
2. Set the stock on the stove to boil
3. Add the chopped onions to the broth and season with salt and pepper
4. Let this boil until the onions are transparent
5. Add the cauliflower flowerettes and turn the heat down to a simmer
6. Cover the pot and keep at a simmer
7. Meanwhile, make a roux of the flour and butter over medium-low heat
8. Cook this while whisking until the flour slightly changes color
9. Add the cream slowly, whisking in between each addition so that the lumps come out and the roux stays smooth
10. This mixture should always come back to a boil once the milk has been stirred in well, if it doesn't, turn the heat up!
11. When all the cream has been added and the roux is thick and smooth add it to the soup
12. Stir the soup well to incorporate the cream, and keep stirring until it comes up to a boil
13. Add the cheese, turn the heat down to low, cover and bring to a slow boil
14. Stir frequently so it doesn't burn on the bottom<|endoftext|>
