Give me a tofu recipe.<|endoftext|>Better Than Takeout Sesame Tofu

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Egg
• Cornstarch
• Peanut oil
• Sesame seeds
• Broccolini
• Sugar
• Water
• Rice vinegar
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Chili paste
• Garlic clove

Steps:
1. Cut tofu into 1 / 2 thick slices
2. Place in a 9~13 baking pan lined with a tea towel, top with another towel followed by another baking pan
3. Weight with something heavy, and let press for 20-30 minutes
4. Cut into cubes
5. Coat tofu cubes in egg, then toss in cornstarch until coated and dry
6. Shake off extra cornstarch
7. Combine sugar, cornstarch, water, rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, chili paste, and garlic in a small saucepan
8. Bring to a boil, and boil for 5 minutes, until thick
9. While sauce is cooking, heat oil in large non-stick skillet or wok
10. Carefully place tofu in hot oil, spacing evenly so the pieces donft stick together
11. Cook for 3-5 minutes, or until golden brown on the bottom, and then carefully turn and cook other side
12. Continue to turn until cubes are golden on a few sides
13. Remove to a paper-towel lined plate
14. Wipe out tofu pan and heat a drizzle of oil
15. Saute broccolini, turning once, for about 2-4 minutes or until bright green and tender
16. Remove from heat
17. Return tofu cubes to pan, and pour over sauce
18. Stir until well coated
19. Serve with jasmine rice and sprinkled with lightly toasted sesame seeds<|endoftext|>
