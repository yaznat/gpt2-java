Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Philly Steak Cheese Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Cream cheese
• Onion
• Steak-ums
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, heat the butter on medium-high heat
2. Add onions and cook until tender / translucent
3. Transfer to small bowl
4. Add the cream cheese to the onions in the bowl
5. In the same large skillet, cook the steak-ums (about 3-4 minutes or until cooked through
6. Strain the meat and add to the bowl with the onions and cream cheese
7. Mix well softening the cream cheese so it spreads easily
8. Whip the eggs
9. Wipe out the large skillet after cooking the steak and spray with pam and heat on medium heat
10. Add your eggs and cook until the eggs on the top are almost cooked
11. Add the steak & cheese mixture to one side of the eggs
12. Fold over the side of the eggs on top of the mixture
13. Cook a minute or two and then flip
14. Season with salt and pepper to your desire<|endoftext|>
