Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Aunt Juju S Beef Tips With Caramelized Onions

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Beef tips
• White onions
• Carrots
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Flour
• Cornstarch
• Dried thyme
• Lawry's seasoned salt
• Beer
• Beef broth
• Tomato paste
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Warm the oil in large heavy pot until hot but not smoking
2. Add cornstarch and season salt to large ziplock bag and shake the meat in it
3. Brown meat on all sides for about 5 minutes
4. Transfer browned meat to a plate
5. Add the onions and butter to the pot and stir over high heat until the onions start to soften, about 5 minutes
6. Reduce heat to medium and sprinkle in sugar
7. Continue to cook the onions, stirring occasionally, until nice and golden brown, about 15 minutes
8. Add the flour, thyme, and carrots and raise the heat to high
9. Stir for 1 minute, then pour in beer, letting it come to a vigorous boil
10. Stir in broth and tomato paste and return to the boil
11. Return meat and accumulated juices to the pot
12. Let the liquid come just to a boil, then reduce heat to low
13. Cover and simmer 1 1 / 2 to 2 hours
14. Check on occasion and add beef broth if it is getting too dry
15. Season with salt and pepper to taste
16. We like this over refrigerator bisquits<|endoftext|>
