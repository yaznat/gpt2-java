What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>After Thanksgiving Soup

Ingredients:
• Turkey broth
• Cooked turkey
• Pearl barley
• Onion
• Celery
• Carrots
• Bay leaf
• Dried parsley
• Dried thyme
• Dried marjoram
• Salt
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Slow cooker instructions
2. Combine all of the ingredients into the slow cooker
3. Cook over low heat for 6-8 hours
4. Stove top instructions
5. Combine all of the ingredients into a large soup kettle
6. Simmer at least one hour or until the carrots are tender and the barley soft<|endoftext|>
