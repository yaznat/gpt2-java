Ingredients:
• Chicken tenders
• Onion
• Red pepper
• Olive oil
• Alfredo sauce
• Broccoli florets
• Fresh ground pepper
• Parmesan cheese
• Fettuccine pasta

Steps:
1. Get olive oil hot in 12" frying pan
2. Saute onion and pepper until tender
3. Add chicken and cook until done
4. Turn heat to low and add alfredo sauce
5. Boil broccoli until done
6. Cook pasta according to package directions
7. Once broccoli is done, add to the prepared sauce
8. Once noodles are done, place into bowl and cover with sauce
9. Top with parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
