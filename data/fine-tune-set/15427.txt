Ingredients:
• Macaroni
• Onion
• Ground beef
• Tomato soup
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese
• Salt
• Pepper
• Ketchup

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Cook bacon & drain on paper towels
3. Discard drippings
4. Cook onion in the same pan you cooked the bacon in and cook ground beef until no longer pink
5. Drain pasta and add to the skillet
6. Also add bacon, tomato soup, cheese, and ketchup
7. Mix thoroughly until cheese is melted
8. Add salt & pepper to suite your tastes
9. Serve with salad
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
