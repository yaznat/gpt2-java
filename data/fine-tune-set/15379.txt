Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Vegetable oil
• Soy sauce
• Ketchup
• White vinegar
• Garlic cloves
• Pepper
• Hamburger buns
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Shape meat into six patties and place in a shallow dish
2. In a bowl, whisk together the oil, soy sauce, ketchup, vinegar, garlic and pepper
3. Set aside 1 / 4 cup for basting and refrigerate
4. Pour remaining marinade over the patties
5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours
6. Grill burgers, uncovered, over medium heat for 5-6 minutes on each side until meat juices run clear, basting with reserved marinade
7. Serve on hamburger buns with lettuce leaves and tomato slices if desired<|endoftext|>
