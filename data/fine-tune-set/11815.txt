Give me a recipe for curry.<|endoftext|>Beef Sweet Potato Curry

Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Salt
• Turmeric
• Vegetable oil
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Cumin
• Curry powder
• Coriander
• Paprika
• Cayenne pepper
• Cardamom
• Fresh cilantro
• Sweet potatoes
• Water

Steps:
1. In a bowl, mix beef, salt and turmeric
2. Heat oil in a pot on medium heat
3. Add onion, garlic and ginger and saute 3 minute
4. Add the beef mix and spices and saute 5 minute
5. Add the water, cover and simmer until beef tender
6. Add potatoes and simmer another 20 minute
7. Serve over top of rice or as a stew with nana bread<|endoftext|>
