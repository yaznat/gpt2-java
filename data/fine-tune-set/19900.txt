Give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Low Fat French Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Egg whites
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375-400
2. Depending on how you like your french fries, either wash or peel the potatoes
3. Cut the potatoes into either strips or wedges
4. Place the potato slices / wedges in a bowl or plastic bag and add egg whites
5. Toss to coat
6. Add your seasonings of choice & toss to coat again
7. Spread the fries evenly in a single layer on a large baking sheet
8. Bake the fries for 15-20 minutes, flipping after 10
9. Remove from oven and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
