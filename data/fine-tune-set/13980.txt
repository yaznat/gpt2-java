What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Butterflied Leg Of Lamb Williams Sonoma

Ingredients:
• Cider vinegar
• Sugar
• Fresh mint leaves
• Salt
• Dry red wine
• Olive oil
• Shallots
• Fresh rosemary
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ground pepper
• Leg of lamb

Steps:
1. For the sauce, combine the vinegar and sugar in a small saucepan and bring to a boil, simmering just until the sugar dissolves
2. Remove from the heat and add the mint and salt
3. Set aside to allow the flavors to mellow
4. To prepare the lamb, place it in a glass or porcelain dish or enameled baking pan large enough for it to lie flat
5. Stir together the wine, oil, shallots, rosemary, garlic, salt and pepper
6. Pour over the lamb and marinate for at least 2 hours, or all day if you wish, turning occasionally
7. Prepare grill and position rack 4-6 inches from flame
8. Remove lamb from the marinade and pat dry with paper towels but reserve marinade
9. Place lamb on the rack and grill 35-45 minutes, turning frequently and brushing occasionally with the reserved marinade
10. The meat should remain pink inside
11. Make a cut in the thickest part to check
12. Remove from the grill and let rest 5 minutes, covered loosely with foil
13. Carve across the grain into thin slices and serve with the mint sauce<|endoftext|>
