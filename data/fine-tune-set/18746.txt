Can you give me a pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Pudding Squares

Ingredients:
• Vanilla wafers
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Powdered sugar
• Cool whip
• Bananas
• Milk
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Semi-sweet chocolate baking square

Steps:
1. Mix wafers and butter
2. Press firmly into bottom of 8x8 baking dish
3. Beat cream cheese and powdered sugar together
4. Gently fold in 1-1 / 2 cups of the cool whip
5. Spoon mixture over the crust
6. Carefully spread to cover
7. Cut bananas in half crosswise
8. Cut each piece lengthwise in half
9. Arrange banana pieces over the cheese mixture
10. Pour milk into a large bowl
11. Add pudding mixes
12. Beat 2 minutes
13. Spoon over bananas
14. Spread with remaining whipped topping
15. Sprinkle evenly with chocolate
16. Refrigerate at least 3 hours<|endoftext|>
