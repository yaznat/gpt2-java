Please generate a soup recipe.<|endoftext|>15 Minute White Bean Soup

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Scallions
• Garlic clove
• Dried oregano
• Vegetable broth
• White beans
• Fresh lemon juice
• Salt and pepper
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan, heat oil over medium
2. Add scallions, garlic, and oregano
3. Cook, stirring frequently, until scallions begin to soften, about 3 minutes
4. Stir in broth and beans
5. Cook until heated through, about 4 minutes
6. Using a wooden spoon or potato masher, lightly mash some of the beans to thicken the soup
7. Stir in lemon juice
8. Season with salt and pepper
9. Before serving, sprinkle with parmesan
10. To store, refrigerate in an airtight container, up to three days<|endoftext|>
