Ingredients:
• Rice vinegar
• Sugar
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Cucumber
• Red onion
• Fresh cilantro leaves
• Red chili pepper
• Mixed greens

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, stir vinegar, sugar, salt, and pepper until sugar and salt are dissolved
2. Quarter cucumber lengthwise
3. Remove seeds
4. Cut quarters crossswise into 1 / 8 thick slices
5. Add cucumber, onion, cilantro and chili pepper to vinegar dressing
6. Toss until combined
7. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for 3 hours, stirring occasionally
8. Serve on bed of greens<|endoftext|>
