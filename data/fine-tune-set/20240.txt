What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Chicken Broccoli Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Garlic
• Honey
• Cider vinegar
• Molasses
• Dry sherry
• Cornstarch
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Cooking oil
• Broccoli florets

Steps:
1. In a medium mixing bowl stir together flour, baking powder, 3 / 4-cup water, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, and flattened garlic
2. Let stand for 15 minutes
3. Remove and discard garlic
4. Meanwhile, in a small mixing bowl combine honey, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, vinegar, molasses, 2 tablespoons water, dry sherry, minced garlic, and cornstarch
5. Set aside
6. Rinse chicken
7. Pat dry
8. Cut chicken into 1 1 / 2x1 / 2-inch strips
9. Add to flour batter
10. In a wok or 2-quart saucepan heat 2 inches of oil to 350 degrees
11. Remove chicken from flour batter, allowing excess to drain off
12. Fry chicken strips, a few pieces at a time, in hot oil for 30-60 seconds, or till golden
13. Drain on paper towels
14. Pour 1 tablespoon cooking oil into a large skillet
15. Preheat over medium-high heat
16. Add the broccoli and stir-fry for 4-5 minutes, or till crisp-tender
17. Arrange broccoli around the edge of a serving platter
18. Keep warm
19. Stir honey-soy mixture
20. Add to the skillet
21. Cook and stir till thickened and bubbly
22. Cook and stir for 1 minute more
23. Return cooked chicken to skillet
24. Heat through
25. Pour chicken and sauce into center of broccoli-lined serving platter<|endoftext|>
