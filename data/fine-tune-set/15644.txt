Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Ground pork
• Taco seasoning mix
• Green pepper
• Tomato sauce
• Diced tomatoes
• Bow tie pasta
• Monterey jack cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Brown the ground beef& ground pork
2. Drain
3. Stir in the taco seasoning mix, tomato sauce, diced tomatoes and green pepper
4. Bring to a boil then reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes
5. Cook bow tie pasta according to package directions
6. Drain
7. Combine pasta with 1 cup of the cheese and sour cream
8. Spray a 13x9-inch baking dish with pam cooking spray
9. Pour the pasta into the baking dish
10. Top with meat mixture and sprinkle with the remaining cheese
11. Bake in a 350f oven for 35-40 minutes<|endoftext|>
