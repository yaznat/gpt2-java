Ingredients:
• Dry sherry
• Japanese soy sauce
• Seasoned rice vinegar
• Dijon mustard
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Toasted sesame oil
• Molasses
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Ground star anise
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• Broccoli
• Peanut oil
• Water chestnuts
• Cooked barley

Steps:
1. First make the sauce:
2. In a bowl, combine the sherry, soy sauce, vinegar, mustard, ginger, garlic, sesame oil, molasses and red pepper flakes
3. Slice the chicken into thin strips along the grain and place them in a small bowl
4. Add 3 tablespoons of the sauce and toss the chicken to coat
5. Set aside
6. Cut the broccoli florets from the stalks
7. Reserve the stalks for another purpose
8. Cut the florets into small pieces about 1 1 / 2 inches across the top
9. Heat a large wok or skillet over high heat
10. Add the peanut oil and swirl to coat the surface
11. Add the broccoli and stir-fry for 30 seconds
12. Stir in the chicken, water chestnuts, cooked barley, and remaining sauce
13. Cook uncovered, stirring frequently, until the chicken is cooked 3 to 4 minutes
14. Stir in sesame seeds and scallion greens
15. Add more soy sauce, if needed
16. Chenin blanc is the recommended wine to drink with this dish
17. Option:
18. Vegetarian brown rice and tofu stir-fry:
19. Instead of chicken, use 1 pound asian-style baked tofu, cut into cubes
20. Cook for only 1 minute before adding the sesame seeds<|endoftext|>
