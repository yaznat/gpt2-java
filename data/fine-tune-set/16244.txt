Give me a recipe for a wrap.<|endoftext|>Apple Cranberry Wrap Arounds

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Golden delicious apples
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Apricot preserves
• Dried cranberries
• Large refrigerated biscuits

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat
2. Add apples through cinnamon
3. Cook, stirring frequently, 5 minutes
4. Add apricot preserves and dried cranberries with 1 / 4 cup water
5. Cook, stirring, about 4-5 minutes until apples are tender
6. Remove from heat and let cool
7. Separate biscuit dough into 8 rounds and use a rolling pin to roll each out to a 6-inch circle
8. Mound about 1 / 3-1 / 2 cup apple mixture in center of each circle of dough and wrap the dough around the filling, pressing together the seams to enclose
9. Place wraps on greased baking sheets, seam side down
10. Sprinkle tops with 1 tablespoon sugar
11. Bake at 375 approximately 15 minutes until golden brown<|endoftext|>
