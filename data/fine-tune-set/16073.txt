Give me a quesadilla recipe.<|endoftext|>Cheese Quesadillas With Grilled Peppers

Ingredients:
• Green bell peppers
• Jalapeno
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Monterey jack cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Nonstick cooking spray

Steps:
1. Prepare a medium-hot fire in the grill with a lightly oiled vegetable grill rack in place
2. Toss bell peppers, jalapenos and onions with olive oil
3. Grill vegetable mixture, tossing frequently, until tender and lightly charred
4. Cool down grill to low by spreading coals out or reducing flame
5. Coat one side of 3 of the tortillas with nonstick cooking spray
6. Spread 1 / 3 of the veggies on each tortilla
7. Top tortillas with cheese, place another tortilla on top of each and press firmly together
8. Spray tops of tortillas with nonstick cooking spray
9. Grill each quesadilla until cheese is melted and tortilla is lightly toasted
10. Flip and grill other side for 2 to 3 minutes
11. Remove from grill and keep warm
12. Repeat with remaining quesadillas
13. Cut warm quesadillas into wedges and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
