Give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>24 Hour Lettuce Salad

Ingredients:
• Lettuce
• Frozen peas
• Parmesan cheese
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Sugar
• Celery
• Hellmann's mayonnaise
• Eggs
• Water chestnuts
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Shred lettuce, put in a 9x13 inches pan
2. On top of this sprinkle onions, celery and chestnuts in layers
3. Break frozen peas and sprinkle on top
4. Spread mayonnaise over top like a frosting to the edge of dish, sealing in ingredients
5. Spread on sugar and grated cheese to cover top
6. Cover and refrigerate over night
7. Before serving top with bacon and decorated with egg slices and tomatoes wedges<|endoftext|>
