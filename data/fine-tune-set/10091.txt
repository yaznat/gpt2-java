Ingredients:
• Creamed corn
• Egg
• Cornmeal
• Garlic salt
• Salad oil
• Baking powder
• Diced green chilies
• Longhorn cheese

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients together, except for 2 handfuls of cheese
2. Pour into a greased casserole dish
3. Sprinkle remaining cheese on top
4. Bake at 350 for 40 minutes or so
5. Recipe can easily be doubled, tripled, etc
