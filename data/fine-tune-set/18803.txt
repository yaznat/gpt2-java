Ingredients:
• Acorn squash
• Boursin cheese
• Cream cheese
• Egg

Steps:
1. Cut squash in half lengthwise
2. Microwave squash halves, covered in syran wrap, for 10 minutes
3. With a small spoon, gently scoop out and discard seeds, taking care not to break skin
4. In a food processor or with a mixer beat herb cheese, cream cheese, and egg until smooth
5. Pour filling equally into squash halves
6. Return to microwave, cooking uncovered for 9 minutes
