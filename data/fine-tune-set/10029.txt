Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>3 Cans And A Box Chili Pasta

Ingredients:
• Rotini pasta
• Chili with chicken and beans
• Chili beans
• Diced tomatoes

Steps:
1. Cook the pasta according to the directions on the box
2. Meantime, in a saucepan, combine the chili, beans & tomatoes & heat, but do not boil
3. When pasta is done, serve pasta in one bowl & chili in another bowl<|endoftext|>
