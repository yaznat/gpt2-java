Ingredients:
• Light brown sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Salt
• Flour
• Quick-cooking oatmeal
• Ground cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Boiling water
• Egg white
• Vanilla
• Milk
• Powdered sugar
• Vegetable shortening

Steps:
1. For oatmeal cookie mixture -- cream butter, sugar and eggs
2. Sift together flour, salt and baking powder and add to creamed mixture
3. Add cinnamon and oatmeal
4. Mix well
5. Add soda to hot water and add to batter -- mix well
6. Drop by tblspoon onto greased cookie sheet
7. Bake 19-15 min in 325 oven
8. For filling, combine egg white, vanilla, milk and 1 cup of powdered sugar
9. Cream well
10. Beat in mixing bowl about 10 minutes
11. Add rest of ingredients and beat an additional 2-3 minutes
12. Put filling between 2 cookies
13. Wrap each whoopie pie cookie in plastic wrap<|endoftext|>
