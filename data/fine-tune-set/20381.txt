Please give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>A Different Crock Pot Vegetable Casserole

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Zucchini
• Yellow squash
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Tomatoes
• Brown sugar
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Spray crock pot with cooking spray
2. Layer ingredients in order as listed, except brown sugar and olive oil
3. Sprinkle brown sugar over all the vegetables
4. Drizzle olive oil over all
5. Cook 6 hours on low<|endoftext|>
