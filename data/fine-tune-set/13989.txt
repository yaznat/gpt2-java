Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Lamb
• Garlic cloves
• Scallions
• Curry powder
• Onions
• Fresh ginger
• Scotch bonnet pepper
• Allspice
• Fresh thyme
• Parsley
• Salt
• Pepper
• Lime juice
• Coconut cream
• Sweet basil
• Chicken stock
• Butter

Steps:
1. Place a large stock pot with olive oil over a medium high heat
2. Season the cubed lamb
3. Place in pot
4. Add garlic, scallions and curry powder
5. Mix well
6. Next add onions, ginger, scotch bonnet
7. Add all spice and fresh thyme, add parsley
8. Add salt and pepper
9. Next add lime juice and coconut cream
10. Stir
11. Next evenly add the curry and mix well
12. Let simmer for approximately 3 minutes
13. Place your sweet basil into the pot and stir
14. Add butter and mix until it's melted
15. Add the chicken stock and stir
16. Cover and let simmer for approximately 25-30 minutes
17. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
