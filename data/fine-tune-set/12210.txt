What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Bread

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Buttermilk
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Apples
• Walnuts
• Raisins
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f, grease the bottom and 1 / 2 inch up the sides of an 9x5x3-inch loaf pan
2. Set aside
3. In a large bowl, beat butter with an electric mixer on medium to high speed for 30 seconds, beat in sugar until combined
4. Add buttermilk and baking powder
5. Beat until combined
6. Add eggs and vanilla
7. Beat until combined
8. Add flour, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg
9. Beat until combined them stir in apple, nuts, and raisins
10. Spoon batter into prepared pan
11. Spread evenly and sprinkle streusel-nut topping over batter
12. Bake for 60 to 65 minutes or until a wooden toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean
13. Cool in pan on a wire rack for 10 minutes
14. Remove from pan
15. Cool completely on wire rack
16. Wrap and store overnight before slicing
17. Streusel-nut topping: in a small bowl, combine 1 / 4 cup packed brown sugar and 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
18. Using a pastry blender, cut in 2 tablespoons butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs
19. Stir in 1 / 3 cup chopped walnuts or pecans
20. To make 1 / 4 cup sour milk, place 3 / 4 teaspoon lemon juice or vinegar in a glass measuring cup
21. Add enough milk to make 1 / 4 cup total liquid
22. Stir
23. Let mixture stand for 5 minutes before using<|endoftext|>
