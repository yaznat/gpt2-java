Ingredients:
• Margarine
• White sugar
• Eggs
• Mashed banana
• Milk
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Vanilla
• Sugar
• Shortening

Steps:
1. To make cake: mix margarine, sugar and eggs together
2. In separate bowl mash the bananas and add the milk
3. Once mixed, add the banana mixture to the margarine, sugar and eggs
4. In separate bowl mix the remaining dry cake ingredients
5. Mix the dry and wet cake ingredients together
6. Pour into 9 x 13 inch pan and bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 35 - 40 minutes
7. Cool to room temperature
8. To make icing: mix the flour, milk and sugar in a small sauce pan
9. Cook to boiling stirring continuously
10. Cool to room temperature
11. In a separate bowl whip the margarine, shortening and vanilla
12. Mix the cooled pudding mixture with the whipped marg / shortening mixture until fluffy
13. Top cooled cake with icing and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
