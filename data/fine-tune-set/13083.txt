What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Bb King S Catfish Nuggets Sauce

Ingredients:
• Catfish fillets
• Buttermilk
• Paprika
• Cayenne
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Flour
• Yellow cornmeal
• Ranch dressing
• Hot sauce
• Chili powder
• Lime juice

Steps:
1. Cut the catfish fillets into finger or nuggets
2. Mix together the buttermilk, paprika, cayenne, onion, and garlic powder
3. Mix together the flour and yellow cornmeal
4. Dip the catfish into the buttermilk mixture and then dredge in the flour / cornmeal mixture
5. Deep fry until golden
6. Mix all sauce ingredients together and refrigerate
7. This sauce double very easy
8. Serve sauce with catfish<|endoftext|>
