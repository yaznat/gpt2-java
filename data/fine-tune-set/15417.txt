Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Onion
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Fry bacon until crisp
2. Crumble
3. Combine beef, salt, pepper, garlic powder and onion and shape into 12 large, thin patties
4. Top six patties with 1 tablespoon bacon and 1 tablespoon cheese
5. Place remaining 6 patties on top of cheese / bacon topped patties and press edges to seal
6. Grill or broil for 3-4 minutes per side until done
7. We like ours well done
8. The last 2 minutes of cooking top patties with remaining bacon and cheese
9. Serve on buns<|endoftext|>
