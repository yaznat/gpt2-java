Ingredients:
• Rice vinegar
• Lemon juice
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Ginger juice
• Sesame oil
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. To make ginger juice, peel some fresh ginger and cut into small pieces
2. Put pieces in a garlic press and squeeze
3. Combine all ingredients except mayonnaise in a small bowl and whisk until honey is dissolved
4. Add mayonnaise and whisk again until smooth
5. Good on green salads, cucumbers, and just about anything else!<|endoftext|>
