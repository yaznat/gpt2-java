Ingredients:
• Raisin bran cereal
• Sugar
• Splenda granular
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Eggs
• Egg whites
• Applesauce
• Vegetable oil
• Buttermilk

Steps:
1. Mix all dry ingredients
2. Add eggs, oil, applesauce and buttermilk
3. Put in plastic container and cover
4. Fill muffin tin 2 / 3 full
5. Bake at 375 for 15-20 minute
