Can you give me a wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Avocado Wraps

Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Chipotle chili sauce
• Sour cream
• Cooked bacon
• Flour tortillas
• Lettuce leaves
• Tomatoes
• Avocados

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine the mayonnaise, chipotle sauce and sour cream until smooth
2. Heat bacon according to package directions
3. Spread chipotle mayonnaise over tortillas
4. Layer tortillas with lettuce, tomato, bacon and avocados
5. Roll up tightly<|endoftext|>
