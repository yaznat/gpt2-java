Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Toasted sesame oil
• Lemon juice
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• Ginger powder
• Garlic
• Cooked wild rice
• Cooked chicken
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Onion
• Celery ribs
• Fresh snow peas
• Salt and black pepper
• Chow mein noodles
• Green onion

Steps:
1. For the dressing
2. Whisk all ingredients together until the sugar is completey dissolved
3. Let stand at room temperature for 45 minutes to blend flavors before using
4. For the salad
5. Place the cooked wild rice, cooked chicken or turkey, water chestnuts, chopped onion, celery and snow peas in a large glass salad bowl
6. Toss to combine
7. Pour the dressing over
8. Toss to combine well and chill for about 2 hours
9. Just before serving, toss again and season with salt and black pepper
10. Sprinkle with crispy noodles and green onions
11. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
