Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Dried oregano
• Pizza sauce
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375 degrees
2. Brown ground beef with onion until no longer pink
3. Drain
4. In a large bowl combine ground beef, oregano, pizza sauce and cheese along with any other meats or vegetables you wish to add
5. Mix well
6. Spoon 2 heaping tablespoons of mixture on each bun half and place on cookie sheet
7. Bake for 15 minutes or until cheese is melted
8. Let set for a few minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
