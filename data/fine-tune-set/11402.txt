Steps:
1. Sift dry ingredients
2. Set aside
3. Beat eggs in a large bowl
4. Add oil and sugar
5. Cream well
6. Add 'add ins' and vanilla
7. Add dry ingredients
8. Mix well
9. Add nuts
10. Spoon into 2 well greased loaf pans
11. Bake in preheated oven at 325 degrees for 1 hour
12. Makes 2 large loafs -- but at holiday time, i use the small foil pans for gifts -- usually 5 at a time!<|endoftext|>
