Please give me a recipe for a taco.<|endoftext|>Becca S Taco Soup

Ingredients:
• 85% lean ground beef
• Onion
• Tomato juice
• Whole kernel corn
• Kidney beans
• Tomato sauce
• Taco seasoning mix

Steps:
1. In a large pot over medium high heat, combine the ground beef and the onions
2. Saute for about 5 minutes or until onions are tender
3. Add the tomato juice, corn, beans, tomato sauce and taco seasoning
4. Stir well
5. Heat throughout, but do not boil<|endoftext|>
