What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Barley Baked Pudding

Ingredients:
• Pearl barley
• Walnuts
• Currants
• Maple syrup
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Milk
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Nutmeg
• Clove

Steps:
1. Cook 1 cup pearl barley in enough water
2. Mix cooked barley, currants, walnuts, in 3 quart dish
3. In a bowl combine maple syrup, vanilla, eggs and milk
4. Pour over barley mixture and stir to mix well into barley mixture
5. Bake in 350f for 30-35 minutes<|endoftext|>
