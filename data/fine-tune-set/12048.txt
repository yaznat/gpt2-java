What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Albania Byrek Ose Lakror Leek Pie

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Water
• Butter
• Leeks
• Cottage cheese
• Feta cheese
• Eggs
• Yogurt

Steps:
1. Mix the flour, salt and water together and knead for five minutes
2. Let sit for 10 minutes, then divide in half
3. Roll out one half until thin and then butter
4. Fold the sides toward the center, then butter again
5. Fold the dough in half lengthwise, then butter again
6. Keep going until you have a five-inch square
7. Then butter the top and cover with plastic wrap
8. Refrigerate for 15 minutes
9. Repeat with second ball of dough
10. Grease a standard sized pie-pan
11. After 15 minutes in the fridge, bring the dough out and roll it until it is the size of a pie the pan
12. Lay it on the bottom, leaving about a half inch hanging over the edges
13. Now you are ready to make the filling
14. Wash and thinly slice the leeks
15. Set aside
16. In a large bowl, mix the cottage cheese, feta cheese, eggs, salt, yogurt and melted butter
17. Then add the leeks and mix until everything is well incorporated
18. Pour the filling into the prepared pie plate
19. Roll out the other piece of dough and top the pie with it
20. Crimp the edges or just roll the top up with the dough on the bottom
21. Brush with butter
22. Bake at 400 degrees for 45 minutes, or until top of pie is golden<|endoftext|>
