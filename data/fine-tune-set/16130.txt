Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Corn tortillas
• Cooking spray
• Fresh corn
• Red onion
• Sun-dried tomatoes
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Smoked mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Pour oil into one side of tortilla
2. Place second tortilla over oiled side of first tortilla and rub tortillas together to spread oil evenly over both tortillas
3. Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
4. Coat pan with cooking spray
5. Add corn, cook 4 minutes or until lightly browned, stirring occasionally
6. Place corn in a bowl
7. Add onion, sun-dried tomato, salt and pepper to pan, saute 1 minute
8. Add to the corn mixture
9. Place one tortilla, oiled side down in hot skillet
10. Sprinkle 2 tbs cheese over tortilla, top with corn mixture
11. Sprinkle with remaining cheese, top with remaining tortilla, oiled side up
12. Cook 2 minutes on each side, or until cheese melts and tortilla is crisp
13. Cut into 4 wedges<|endoftext|>
