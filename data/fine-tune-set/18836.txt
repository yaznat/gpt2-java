Ingredients:
• Meat stock
• Leeks
• Celery
• Carrots
• Double cream
• Eggs
• Parsley
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 170 c or
2. Grease the ramakin dishes with margarine and put aside until needed
3. Place the meat stock in a pan and bring to a boil
4. Add the leeks, celery and carrots, reduce heat, cover and simmer for about 3 - 5 minutes
5. Transfer the vegetable mixture to a bowl and cool slightly
6. Mix the cream and eggs together, then add them to the vegetable mixture, season with parsley, nutmeg, salt and pepper
7. Divide the mixture into 6 ramakin dishes and seal with foil
8. To bake: stand the 6 dishes on a cloth in a baking tin
9. Fill the tin with boiling
10. Water to 2 / 3 the height of the dishes and set the tin in the middle of baking sheet
11. Bake for approximately 40 minutes on a low rack in the oven
12. The custard should give slightly when pressed with a finger
13. Remove baking tin, take dishes out of water let stand for 5 minutes, them remove foil and cool slightly
14. When serving carefully loosen custard around sides with the point of a knife
15. Turn out onto plates and garnish with halved bacon slices<|endoftext|>
