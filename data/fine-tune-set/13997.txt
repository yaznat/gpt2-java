Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Slivered almonds
• Scallion
• Cream cheese
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Chicken breast halves
• Vegetable oil
• Dark sweet cherries
• Orange peel
• Cornstarch
• Brandy

Steps:
1. Melt 2 tablespoons of the margarine in a medium-size frying pan
2. Add almonds and scallions and cook about 3 minutes, until scallions are soft and nuts are lightly browned
3. Remove to a small bowl
4. Add cream cheese, 1 / 4 teaspoon salt and 1 / 8 teaspoon pepper
5. Mix until well blended
6. Slit a pocket in each chicken breast
7. Stuff each with a portion of cream cheese mixture and secure with wooden toothpicks
8. Season with additional salt and pepper
9. In a large frying pan, heat remaining 2 tablespoons margarine in the oil
10. Brown chicken on all sides over medium-high heat, about 8 minutes
11. Add cherries with juice and orange peel to chicken and cook, covered, for 10-15 minutes, until cooked through
12. Arrange chicken and cherries on a serving dish and cover with foil to keep warm
13. Bring liquid in pan to a boil and boil until reduced to about 2 / 3 cup
14. Stir dissolved cornstarch into juices in pan
15. Cook, stirring constantly, until sauce boils and thickens
16. Season with salt and pepper to taste
17. Pour half the sauce over chicken
18. Warm brandy in a large ladle or small saucepan, but do not boil
19. Ignite carefully and pour flaming brandy over chicken
20. Pass remaining cherry sauce separately<|endoftext|>
