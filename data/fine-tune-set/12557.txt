Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Bell pepper
• Tomatoes
• Cilantro
• Garlic clove
• Tomato paste
• Bay shrimp
• Lemon juice
• Coconut milk

Steps:
1. Heat oil in skillet, add onion, pepper, tomatoes, cilantro, garlic and tomato paste
2. Cook for 10 minutes
3. Bring sauce to a boil, then add shrimp and lemon juice
4. Reduce heat to medium and cook for 2 minutes
5. Add coconut milk and bring the mixture to a boil
6. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
