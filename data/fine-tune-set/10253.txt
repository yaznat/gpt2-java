Ingredients:
• French vanilla cake mix
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Blend, cake mix, eggs, and oil until moistened
3. Roll into 1" balls
4. Place on ungreased cookie sheet
5. Flatten with a glass or cookie stamp
6. Bake at 375f for 6-8 minutes
7. Allow to cool 1 minute before removing to cooling rack
8. While the cookies are still a little warm, spread on some buttercream frosting
9. Allow the frosting to set up before storing<|endoftext|>
