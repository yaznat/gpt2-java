What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Easy Crispy Oven Fries Low Fat Low Cal

Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Sugar
• Oil
• Paprika
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. Spray a non-stick baking sheet w / non-stick cooking spray
3. In a lg
4. Bowl, combine potatoes, 1 / 4 teaspoons of the salt and all of sugar w / cold water to cover
5. Soak 15 min, drain and blot dry
6. In another lg
7. Bowl, toss potatoes w / oil and paprika
8. Place in a single layer on the baking sheet
9. Bake, turning potatoes over as they brown, until cooked through and crisp
10. Sprinkle w / the remaining 1 / 2 teaspoons of salt<|endoftext|>
