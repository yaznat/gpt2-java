Ingredients:
• Light coconut milk
• Fresh cilantro
• Red curry paste
• Curry powder
• Chili sauce
• Soy sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Extra firm tofu
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Baby spinach
• Red bell pepper
• Red onion
• Cooked brown rice

Steps:
1. Drain tofu and cut into thin slices
2. Heat oil over medium heat
3. Cook tofu in a single layer without stirring about 5 minutes or until slightly browned on bottom
4. Stir gently
5. Then continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until thouroughly cooked, about 5-7 minutes
6. While tofu is cooking, prepare sauce by mixing coconut milk, cilantro, curry paste, curry powder, chili sauce, soy sauce, and garlic
7. Add veggies and sauce and cook, stirring, until veggies are barely wilted, about 1-2 minutes
8. Serve with brown rice and garnish with extra cilantro<|endoftext|>
