Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Butternut squash
• Sweet potatoes
• Potatoes
• Apple
• Mild curry paste
• Turmeric
• Fresh gingerroot
• Bay leaves
• Vegetable stock
• Raisins
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Plain yogurt

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onions for 4-5 minutes until golden
2. Add the garlic, butternut squash, sweet potatoes, potatoes and apple
3. Then stir in the curry paste, turmeric, ginger, bay leaves, stock, raisins and plenty of seasoning
4. Bring to the boil, stir well, then cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are just tender
5. Spoon into bowls and add a spoonful of yogurt and serve over basmati rice<|endoftext|>
