Ingredients:
• Water
• Sugar
• Creamy peanut butter
• Milk

Steps:
1. Boil water and sugar for 3 minutes
2. Remove from heat
3. Stir in peanut butter
4. Then add milk
5. Pour into 8 tupperware popsicle makers
6. Place lids on tightly
7. Freeze for at least three hours<|endoftext|>
