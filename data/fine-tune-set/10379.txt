Give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>Aloha Cookies

Ingredients:
• Light brown sugar
• Butter flavor crisco
• Egg
• Orange juice
• Vanilla extract
• Lemon extract
• Quick oats
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Crushed pineapple in juice
• Flaked coconut
• Macadamia nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f lightly grease / spray cookie sheets
2. Place brown sugar, shortening, egg, orange juice, vanilla extract and lemon extract in large bowl
3. Beat at medium speed of electric mixer until well blended
4. Combine oats, flour, baking soda, salt, nutmeg and cinnamon in a separate bowl
5. Add to shortening mixture
6. Beat at low speed just until blended
7. Stir in pineapple, coconut and chopped nuts
8. Drop dough by rounded tablespoonsful 2-inches apart onto lightly greased cookie sheets
9. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes or until cookies are just lightly browned
10. Do not overbake
11. Cool 2 minutes before removing to cooling racks to cool completely<|endoftext|>
