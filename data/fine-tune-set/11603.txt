Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Oil
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Baking soda
• Salt
• All-purpose flour
• Dark corn syrup
• Pecans
• Mincemeat

Steps:
1. Cream sugar, eggs, and oil
2. Add milk alternately with flour which has been mixed with soda and salt
3. Add syrup, pecans and mincemeat
4. Bake in bundt pan at 350 degrees for about an 1 hour or until an inserted toothpick comes out clean<|endoftext|>
