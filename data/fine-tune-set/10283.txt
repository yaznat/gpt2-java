Give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>5 Star Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Hot water
• Salt
• Chocolate chips
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Cream together butter, both sugars and vanilla
3. Beat in eggs one at a time
4. Dissovle baking soda in hot water
5. Add to batter
6. Add salt
7. Slowly add flour
8. Stir in chocolate chips and nuts
9. Drop onto ungreased cookie sheet, about 1 inch apart
10. Bake for about 10 minutes or until edges are just brown
11. Take out of oven and leave them sit on the pan for about 2 minutes as they will continue to bake a bit outside of the oven<|endoftext|>
