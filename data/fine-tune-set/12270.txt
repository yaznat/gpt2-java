Please give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>10 Minute Tuscan Tomato Soup With Parmesan Toasts

Ingredients:
• Marinara sauce
• Cannellini beans
• Roasted red peppers
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Chicken broth
• Fresh basil
• Fresh ground pepper
• French bread
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Put sauce, beans, peppers, oil and garlic in a blender or food processor
2. Process until smooth
3. Pour into medium saucepan along with the broth
4. Stirring often, bring to a simmer over medium-high heat
5. Remove from heat
6. Stir in basil
7. Spoon into bowls
8. Grind pepper on top
9. For parmesan toasts, heat broiler
10. Line a baking sheet with foil
11. Place 12 thin slices bread on foil
12. Sprinkle each slice with 1 t grated parmesan cheese
13. Broil 2-3 minutes until lightly toasted<|endoftext|>
