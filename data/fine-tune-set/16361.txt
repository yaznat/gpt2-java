Ingredients:
• Pork loin
• Stove top stuffing mix
• Butter
• Water
• Bacon
• Salt & pepper
• Rosemary
• Fresh thyme

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Slice pork loin lengthwise, but not all the way through, so you can still open it up
3. Prepare stuffing and stuff into sliced pork loin
4. Sprinkle top of pork loin with rosemary, thyme, salt & pepper
5. Wrap pork loin with uncooked bacon slices
6. Bake in oven until meat thermometer reaches 155f when inserted to the thickest part of the meat approx one and a half hours
7. Tent with foil for 5-10 minutes and slice<|endoftext|>
