Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Buttermilk Waffle Batter

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Buttermilk
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredidents together
2. Measure milk, oil and egg yolk in a pyrex measuring cup
3. Set aside
4. In another bowl, beat egg white, add sugar, beat until peaks are firm and glossy
5. Beat in vanilla
6. Pour milk mix in with dry ingreds
7. Whisk until just mixed
8. Fold in egg white<|endoftext|>
