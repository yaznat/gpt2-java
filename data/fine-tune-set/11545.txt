Ingredients:
• Pineapple in syrup
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Glace cherries
• Caster sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla essence
• Self-raising flour
• Milk

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven to 180c
2. Pineapple layer:
3. Melt the butter and pour into the base of a 20cm round cake tin
4. Also brush the butter around the sides of the tin
5. Sprinkle the brown sugar over the base of the tin
6. Arrange the pineapple rings in the tin and place a cherry into the centre of each ring
7. Cake batter:
8. Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy
9. Beat in the egg and the vanilla
10. Sift the flour and fold into the mixture, alternating with the milk
11. Pour the cake batter over the pineapple and cherries
12. Bake for 40 to 50 minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the cake comes out clean
13. Remove the cake from the oven and loosen the cake from the sides of the tin
14. Invert the cake onto a serving plate
15. Enjoy either warm or cold<|endoftext|>
