What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Arroz Con Pollo Burritos

Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Long grain white rice
• Taco seasoning
• Water
• Tomato sauce
• Tomatoes
• Green onion
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Salsa
• Guacamole

Steps:
1. In large deep skillet, add chicken, rice, taco spices & seasonings, water and tomato sauce
2. Bring to a boil
3. Cover, reduce heat to low and cook for 20 minutes
4. Stir in tomato and green onion
5. Scoop 1 / 2 cup filling on each tortilla
6. Fold in sides and roll-up to enclose filling
7. Top each burrito with cheese, if desired, and heat in 350f oven until cheese melts, about 5 minutes
8. Garnish each burrito with salsa and guacamole, if desired<|endoftext|>
