What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>General Tsao S Tofu Vegan

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Cornstarch
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Water
• Tamari soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Sriracha sauce
• Broccoli florets

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400
2. Toss tofu cubes with 1 tbsp corn starch and place on a greased baking sheet
3. Bake 20-25 minutes, or until slightly brown
4. While the tofu bakes, cook garlic and ginger in a medium saucepan for about two minutes
5. Add water, vegetable oil, hoisin, rice vinegar, brown sugar and sriracha and cook for about 3 minutes
6. Add corn starch and water mixture slowly, stirring constantly, until sauce reaches desired consistency
7. Toss baked tofu with sauce
8. Serve over brown rice or quinoa with piles of freshly steamed broccoli
9. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
