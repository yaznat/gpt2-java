What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Herb And Macadamia Crusted Fish

Ingredients:
• Fish fillets
• Macadamia nut oil
• Macadamia nuts
• Fresh oregano
• Parsley
• Garlic clove
• Lemon juice
• Lemon zest

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180c and spray a baking dish with cooking spray
2. Place fillets into dish and brush with the macadamia oil
3. In a small bowl combine the crust ingredients and mix to form a thick paste
4. Spread the macadamia crust over the top of the fish fillets
5. Press on evenly using your hands or the back of a soon
6. Bake for approx 10 minutes, or until the fish flakes when tested with a fork
7. The cooking time will very depending on the thickness of the fish
8. Check for doneness after each few minutes<|endoftext|>
