Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Agnes Creamy Rice Pudding

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Water
• Salt
• Butter
• Milk
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Combine rice, water, salt, and butter in a pan
2. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes
3. At the same time, heat the milk to scalding
4. Stir hot milk into rice, add raisins if using, cover, and simmer 15 more minutes
5. Beat eggs and sugar together
6. Add some of the hot mixture to them, stirring constantly
7. Return egg mixture to rice mixture and cook 5 minutes, stirring constantly
8. It should start to bubble
9. Pour into a bowl
10. Add the vanilla
11. Cool<|endoftext|>
