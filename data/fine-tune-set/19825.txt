Give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Garlic Fries The Real Deal

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Baking potatoes
• Cooking spray
• Butter
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh parsley
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. Combine first 3 ingredients in a large zip-top plastic bag, tossing to coat
3. Arrange potatoes in a single layer on a baking sheet coated with cooking spray
4. Bake at 400 for 50 minutes or until potatoes are tender and golden brown, turning after 20 minutes
5. Place butter and garlic in a large nonstick skillet, and cook over low heat 2 minutes, stirring constantly
6. Add potatoes, parsley, and parmesan cheese to pan
7. Toss to coat
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
