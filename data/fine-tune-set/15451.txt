Ingredients:
• Elbow macaroni
• Lean ground beef
• Milk
• Garlic pepper seasoning
• Condensed cheddar cheese soup
• Cheddar cheese cubes
• Cooked bacon
• Plain breadcrumbs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Cook macaroni according to package directions
2. Drain
3. Add beef to a 4-quart saucepan
4. Cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until brown
5. Drain
6. Stir in macaroni, milk, garlic pepper, soup, cheese cubes, and half the bacon
7. Spoon mixture into a 3-quart casserole
8. Bake, uncovered, in a 350 oven for 20 minutes
9. Mix together the remaining bacon, bread crumbs, and butter
10. Sprinkle over casserole
11. Bake uncovered 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
