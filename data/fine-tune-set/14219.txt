Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Carrot
• Dried shiitake mushroom
• Wakame seaweed
• Red bell pepper
• Rice wine vinegar
• Agave nectar
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Sesame oil
• Medium firm tofu

Steps:
1. Cook rice according to package directions
2. Add carrot during last 5 minutes of cooking and mix
3. Meanwhile, soak mushrooms in a bowl with enough warm water to cover and let stand for 20 minutes
4. Drain and chop mushrooms
5. Transfer rice and carrots to a large mixing bowl
6. Add mushrooms, wakame and bell pepper
7. Combine well
8. In a small bowl, whisk together vinegar, agave nectar, soy sauce, ginger and oil
9. Pour sauce over rice mixture and toss to combine
10. Carefully stir in tofu
11. Refrigerate for 2-4 hours for best flavor
12. Can be kept in refrigerator for up to 4 days<|endoftext|>
