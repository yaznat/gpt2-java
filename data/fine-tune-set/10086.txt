Give me a recipe for chili.<|endoftext|>Apocalypse Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Water
• Diced tomatoes
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Paprika
• Dried onion flakes
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Flour
• Salt
• Beef base

Steps:
1. Brown and drain ground beef
2. Add water, tomatoes, cumin, chili powder, paprika, onion flakes, garlic powder and pepper
3. Simmer, covered, for hour stirring occasionally
4. Make a roux from the flour and a bit of water
5. Start with 1 tablespoon and increase to a thick but pourable consistency
6. Add roux to chili
7. Cover and simmer for an additional 20 minutes
8. Chow down, toots!<|endoftext|>
