Give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Hazelnussomeletten

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Milk
• Egg
• Sugar
• Hazelnuts
• Salt
• Butter

Steps:
1. Combine flour and milk
2. Stir in egg yolk
3. Fold in sugar, hazelnuts and stiffly beaten egg white
4. Add salt to taste
5. Melt butter and fry omelet until golden brown on both sides
6. Serve with stewed fruits or berries<|endoftext|>
