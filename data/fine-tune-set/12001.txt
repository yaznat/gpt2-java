Ingredients:
• Frozen hash brown potatoes
• Mozzarella cheese
• Mushrooms
• Frozen broccoli florets
• Eggs
• Milk
• Dried thyme
• Onion powder
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Lightly press hash brown potatoes into greased 9-inch pie plate to form crust
3. Sprinkle with 1 / 2 cup of the cheese
4. Top with mushrooms and broccoli
5. Beat eggs, milk, thyme, onion powder and pepper with wire whisk until well blended
6. Pour over broccoli
7. Sprinkle with remaining 1 / 2 cup cheese
8. Bake 35 minute or until center is set and top is golden brown
9. Creative leftovers:
10. This is great reheated the next day
11. Microwave each slice on high 1 to 2 minute or until heated through
12. Serve for lunch with a side salad<|endoftext|>
