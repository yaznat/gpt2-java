Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Mix For Gifts

Ingredients:
• Nuts
• Chocolate chips
• Coconut
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Flour
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Layer first set of ingredients in order listed in a quart jar, packing well between layers
2. Cover and store in cool, dry place for up to 6 months
3. For brownies: mix eggs, oil, and vanilla
4. Add brownie mix and mix well
5. Spread in greased 8x8 pan
6. Bake at 350 for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
