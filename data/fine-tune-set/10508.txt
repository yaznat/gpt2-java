Ingredients:
• Rotini pasta
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Mozzarella cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Milk
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In a 1 1 / 2 quart casserole dish combine the soup, cheeses, milk and pepper
2. Add pasta and mix well
3. Cover and bake 350' for 35 minutes or until hot<|endoftext|>
