Ingredients:
• Ranch dip mix
• Lean ground beef
• Red onion
• Barbecue sauce
• Hass avocado
• Sourdough bread
• Smoked cheddar cheese
• Romaine lettuce leaves
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Place 1 / 4 of the dry ranch mix in a small bowl
2. Set aside
3. In a medium bowl, mix together the remaining ranch mix, ground beef, onion and barbecue sauce
4. Mix well
5. Form into 4 large flat patties and grill over medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes per side or until cooked through
6. Top each with about 3 tablespoons shredded cheese while still warm
7. Grill bread slices until lightly toasted
8. Add mashed avocado to remaining dry ranch mix and mix well
9. To assemble, top each bread slice with a lettuce leaf, 2 tomato slices and a burger patty
