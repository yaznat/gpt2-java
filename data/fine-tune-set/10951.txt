Ingredients:
• Watermelon
• Raspberries
• Blueberries
• Strawberry
• Seedless grapes
• Sugar
• Water
• Of fresh mint
• Ground ginger
• Fresh mint leaves

Steps:
1. Chunk watermelon, hull and half strawberries and half grapes
2. For syrup: in small saucepan combine sugar, water, mint and ginger
3. Bring to boil
4. Reduce heat and simmer 5 minutes to thicken slightly
5. Set aside to cool
6. Put all the fruit in a large bowl and add syrup mixture
7. Toss to coat
8. Garnish with mint leaves and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
