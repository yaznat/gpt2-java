What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>Allison S Banana Bread

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Mashed bananas
• Plain yogurt
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine flour, baking soda, salt
3. In a separate bowl, beat sugar and butter
4. Add eggs, one at a time
5. Add banana, yogurt, and vanilla
6. Add flour mixture until just moist
7. Pour into greased loaf pan
8. Bake 1 hour
9. Cool at least 10 minutes in pan before removing to rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
