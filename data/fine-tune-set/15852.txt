Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic
• Black olives
• Diced green chilies
• Diced tomatoes
• Taco sauce
• Refried beans
• Tortillas
• Colby cheese
• Jalapeno
• Sour cream
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Salsa
• Guacamole

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. In a large skillet over medium heat, saut the ground beef for 5 minutes, add the onion, garlic and jalapeo saut for 5 more minutes
3. Drain
4. Mix in olives, green chilies, tomatoes, taco sauce and refried beans and stir thoroughly, reduce heat to low and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes
5. In a 4 quart casserole dish, spread a thin layer of meat mixture on the bottom, cover with a layer of tortillas or crumbled chips, add another layer of meat mixture, then a layer of cheese
6. Repeat tortilla, meat and cheese layers
7. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes or until cheese is melted
8. Remove from oven, let sit for 5 minutes
9. Serve with garnish<|endoftext|>
