What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Another Wonderful Potato Salad With Leeks

Ingredients:
• New potatoes
• Leeks
• Celery
• Light mayonnaise
• Fresh parsley
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Lemon juice
• Salt
• Pepper
• Feta cheese
• Black olives

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan of boiling salted water, cover and cook potatoes until tender, about 25-30 minutes
2. With slotted spoon, remove and let cool
3. Peel and cut into 3 / 4 inch cubes
4. Place in a large bowl
5. Meanwhile, blanch leeks and celery in same boiling water for 20 seconds
6. Drain well and add to potatoes
7. In a small bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, parsley, oil, lemon juice, salt and pepper
8. Pour over veggies and mix well
9. Sprinkle with feta cheese and olives<|endoftext|>
