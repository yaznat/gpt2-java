Ingredients:
• Bean curd
• Dried chinese mushrooms
• Oil
• Scallion
• Gingerroot
• Dry sherry
• Dark soy sauce
• Sugar
• Mushroom liquid
• Dark sesame oil

Steps:
1. Quarter your bean curd squares then cut into 2 triangles
2. Turn each on longest side and cut thru to make 3 thin triangles from each
3. Set aside
4. Cover mushrooms with 3 / 4 cup boiling water and soak 1 hour
5. Lightly squeeze to let liquid drain back into bowl [
6. The soaking water not the rinse step] then rinse briefly and squeeze dry
7. Destem and cut cap into 1 / 2 inch wide slices
8. Place scallions, ginger, mushrooms and curd on work tray
9. Measure mushroom liquid if you are short 1 / 2 cup add some stock or water
10. Heat large heavy skillet over high until hot
11. Add oil
12. Swirl
13. Heat for 15 seconds
14. Lower heat to med and place scallions and ginger in and stir rapidly a few times
15. Add the sherry, soy, sugar and mushroom liquid
16. As it sizzles to boil add mushrooms and bean curd
17. Turn gently a few times
18. Adjust heat to maintain gentle simmer
19. Cover and simmer for 10 to 12 minutes
20. Turn contents half way thru
21. You should be minus the liquid or close to it at this point
22. Add the oil and turn once more
23. The serve<|endoftext|>
