What's a good custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Eggnog Custard Easy

Ingredients:
• Eggnog
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Beat eggs using an electric mixer
3. Gradually add eggnog while continuing to beat mixture
4. Pour mixture into buttered custard cups
5. Place custard cups in a pan of hot water about halfway up the side
6. Bake 30 minutes or until knife inserted in the custard comes out clean<|endoftext|>
