Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Spicy Pasta Fagioli

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Tomato sauce
• Water
• Chicken broth
• Diced tomatoes
• Italian seasoning
• Ditalini
• Carrots
• Cannellini beans
• Capicola
• Salt and pepper
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oil in saucepan over medium-high heat
2. Add garlic and cook 1 minute
3. Stir in broth, water, tomato sauce, diced tomatoes and italian seasoning
4. Bring to a boil
5. Add pasta and carrots
6. Simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Stir in beans, capicola, salt and pepper
8. Heat through
9. Serve with grated parmesan cheese and garlic bread<|endoftext|>
