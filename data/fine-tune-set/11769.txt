Ingredients:
• Whole almond
• Quinoa
• Honey
• Shallot
• Curry powder
• Coarse salt
• Fresh lemon juice
• Fresh ground pepper
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Dried currants
• Apple

Steps:
1. Toast almonds until fragrant in a 375 f oven for about seven minutes
2. Rinse quinoa thoroughly in a fine sieve and drain
3. Bring 2 cups of water to a boil in a medium saucepan
4. Add quinoa, return to boil, stir, cover and reduce heat
5. Simmer until quinoa is tender but still chewy, about 15 minutes
6. Fluff quinoa with a fork and let cool
7. For the dressing, whisk together honey, shallot, curry powder, salt and lemon juice in a large bowl
8. Season with pepper
9. Whisking constantly, add olive oil in a slow, steady stream and whisk until emulsified
10. Add quinoa, currants, apple and nuts and toss well<|endoftext|>
