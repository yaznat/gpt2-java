Can you give me a beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Sesame Roasted Green Beans

Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Olive oil
• Sesame seeds
• Sesame oil
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Mix beans with olive oil and place in 13x9 pan
2. Bake at 450 until beans are slightly browned and tender when pierced- about 7 to 10 minutes
3. Stir sesame seeds and sesame oil into beans and continue baking until seeds are golden brown, about 5 minutes longer
4. Pour into a bowl and add soy sauce to taste
5. Mix well and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
