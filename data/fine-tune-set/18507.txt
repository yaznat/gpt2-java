Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Grandma Joyce S Famous Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Eggs
• Shortening

Steps:
1. Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt
2. In a separate bowl, mix together eggs and milk, then stir in melted shortening
3. Pour into dry ingredients and mix well
4. Batter needs to be somewhat thin
5. Add more water if necessary
6. Waffle iron has to be good and hot before pouring in the batter
7. Waffles are done when steam stops
8. Grandma's famous syrup! : in a saucepan, bring to a boil the following ingredients: 2 cups white sugar, 2 / 3 cup water, pinch of salt, 1 tbs
9. White corn syrup
10. Simmer about 5 minutes, then add 1 tbs
11. Maple flavoring<|endoftext|>
