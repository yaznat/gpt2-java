Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Lean ground beef
• Onions
• Whole tomatoes
• Pinto beans
• Picante sauce
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Salt

Steps:
1. Optional toppings: sour cream, shredded cheddar cheese, chopped onion, chopped cilantro
2. In a large saucepan, cook bacon over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until crisp
3. Remove to paper towels with slotted spoon
4. Pour off all but about 1 tablespoon of the drippings
5. In drippings, brown ground beef with onion and green pepper
6. Drain
7. Add reserved bacon and remaining ingredients except optional toppings
8. Mix well
9. Bring to a boil
10. Reduce heat, cover and simmer 20 minutes
11. Ladle into bowls, top as desired, and serve with additional picante sauce<|endoftext|>
