Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Flour
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Apples
• Pecans
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sour cream
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. For apple filling:
2. In a saucepan, melt butter
3. Stir in brown sugar, flour, cinnamon, and nutmeg until smooth
4. Add apples
5. Cook over medium-low hear for 10 minutes or until tender, stirring frequently
6. Remove from heat, stir in nuts and cool
7. For muffins:
8. In a mixing bowl, cream butter and sugar
9. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each
10. Beat in vanilla
11. Combine dry ingredients and mix to distribute
12. Add to creamed mixture alternately with sour cream
13. Spoon batter into greased jumbo muffin cups to 1 / 4 full
14. Spoon apple mixture into the center of each but do not spread the mixture
15. Top with remaining batter
16. In a small bowl combine the 2 tbs
17. Sugar and 1 / 2 tsp cinnamon
18. Sprinkle muffin batter with cinnamon and sugar mix
19. Bake at 350 degrees for 25-27 minutes or until muffins test done
20. Cool 5 minutes before removing from pans to wire racks
21. If using a regular size pan fill muffin cup 1 / 2 full with batter then top with a slightly rounded teaspoons of the apple nut filling and top with remaining batter
