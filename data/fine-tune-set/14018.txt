Ingredients:
• Lamb stew meat
• Onion
• Turnip
• Carrots
• Beef broth
• Dark beer
• Pearl barley
• Fresh ground pepper
• Salt
• Dried thyme
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Place the meat, onion, turnips and carrots in the crock pot
2. Stir in the broth, beer, barley and all seasonings
3. Cover and cook on low for 8-10 hours
4. Stir in the parsley before serving<|endoftext|>
