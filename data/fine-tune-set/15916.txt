What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Cowboy Burritos

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Tortilla
• Mild salsa
• Sweet relish
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Heat the frying pan and while pan is heating, take a small bowl and combine: egg, salsa, relish, barbecue sauce, using a fork or whisk
2. Heat / warm the tortilla any way you wish, or leave it cold
3. Place egg combination in the frying pan and fry until thoroughly cooked
4. After thoroughly cooked, place the egg onto the tortilla and wrap like a burrito<|endoftext|>
