What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Hour Beef Stew

Ingredients:
• Stew meat
• Canned green beans
• Peas
• Mushrooms
• Celery
• Carrots
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Burgundy wine
• Tomato soup
• Salt
• Pepper
• Ground pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 275 degrees
2. In a large bowl add all the ingredients and mix to combine
3. Place mixture in a baking dish, cover and bake for 5 hours
4. Do not uncover during baking time
5. Serve in bread bowls or with hard rolls<|endoftext|>
