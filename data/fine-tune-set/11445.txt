What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>Almond Cracked Wheat Bread Abm

Ingredients:
• Water
• Unsalted butter
• Almond paste
• Granulated sugar
• Salt
• Cracked wheat
• Wheat flour
• Unbleached bread flour
• Nonfat dry milk powder
• Instant yeast

Steps:
1. If you don't have access to prepared almond paste, substitute 1 / 4 cup blanched slivered almonds ground to a paste with 1 tablespoon sugar
2. Place all of the ingredients into the pan of your bread machine, program the machine for basic white bread, and press start
3. Check the dough after 10 to 15 minutes of kneading
4. It should have formed a smooth ball, soft but not sticky
5. If necessary, adjust the dough's consistency with additional flour or water
6. Yield: one 7 to 8-inch loaf<|endoftext|>
