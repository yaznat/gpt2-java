What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Dilly Dumplings

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Lean stewing beef
• Canola oil
• Water
• Potatoes
• Fresh carrots
• Onions
• Celery
• Fresh parsley
• Dried thyme
• Bay leaf
• Baking powder
• Dill weed
• Egg
• Nonfat milk

Steps:
1. In a large resealable plastic bag, combine the flour, 1 / 8 t
2. Salt and pepper
3. Add meat
4. Seal bag and shake to coat
5. In a dutch oven, brown beef in oil in batches
6. Add water, stirring to loosen browned brits from pan
7. Reduce heat
8. Cover and simmer for 1 hour
9. Add the potatoes, carrots, onions, celery, parsley, thyme, bay leaf and remaining salt
10. Bring to a boil
11. Reduce heat
12. Cover and simmer for 45 minutes or until meat and vegetables are tender
13. Discard bay leaf
14. For dumplings, in a bowl, combine the flour, parsley, baking powder, salt, thyme and dill
15. Combine egg, milk and oil
16. Stir in to dry ingredients just until moistened
17. Drop by tablespoonfuls onto simmering stew
18. Cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in a dumpling comes out clean<|endoftext|>
