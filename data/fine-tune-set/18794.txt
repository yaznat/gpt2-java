Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Flour
• Cocoa
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Light brown sugar
• Hot water
• Kirsch
• Cherry pie filling

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Combine 3 / 4 cup granulated sugar, flour, 3 tablespoons cocoa, baking powder and salt in large bowl
3. Add milk, melted butter and vanilla
4. Beat until smooth
5. Spread into 8 or 9 inch square baking pan
6. Stir together remaining 1 / 2 cup granulated sugar, brown sugar and 1 / 4 cup cocoa in small bowl
7. Sprinkle mixture evenly over batter
8. Combine hot water and kirsch, pour it over the batter
9. Do not stir
10. Bake for 40 minutes or until the center is almost set
11. Let rest 15 minutes
12. Spoon into dessert dishes, spooning sauce from the bottom of pan over the top
13. Serve with cherry pie filling<|endoftext|>
