Ingredients:
• Lamb stew meat
• Cooking oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Diced tomatoes
• Pearl barley
• Dry white wine
• Fresh dill
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Roasted sweet red peppers
• Of fresh mint

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large heavy-bottomed skillet, or an electric frying appliance, such as in my case
2. Brown half of the meat in the hot oil and set aside
3. Add remaining meat
4. Add onion when meat is nearly browned
5. After the onion is tender, two minutes or so, add the garlic to the onion / meat mixture and saute for a couple of more minutes
6. In a 3 1 / 2- to 5-quart slow cooker, combine the broth, undrained tomatoes, barley, wine, dried dillweed, salt, and black pepper
7. Add the meat mixture and stir
8. Cover
9. Cook on low-heat setting for 8 to 10 hours or on high-heat setting for 4 to 5 hours
10. Before serving, stir in the roasted peppers and fresh mint
11. If using fresh dill, stir that in as well<|endoftext|>
