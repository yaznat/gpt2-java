Ingredients:
• Linguine
• Shrimp
• Cream cheese
• Green onions
• Dill
• Lemon juice
• Worcestershire sauce
• Artichoke hearts
• Milk

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. Cook linguine
3. Drain
4. Mix cream cheese, green onions, dill, lemon juice, worcestershire sauce, & artichoke hearts
5. Add enough milk needed to make a sauce
6. Mix linguine, shrimp, and sauce together in an 11x7 pan
7. Cover and bake for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
