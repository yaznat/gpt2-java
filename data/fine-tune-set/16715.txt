Give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Bailey S Chocolate Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Fudge sauce
• Milk
• Irish cream
• Chocolate ice cream

Steps:
1. Dip the rim of a glass in the chocolate sauce, allowing it to drip down the side
2. Place the remaining sauce in a blender with the milk, liqueur and 1 scoop of the ice cream and buzz until thick and rich
3. Spoon remaining ice cream into a milkshake glass and pour in the milkshake mix<|endoftext|>
