Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Oil
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Whisk together dry ingredients
2. Combine oil and eggs in a measuring cup, add to dry mixture
3. Pour buttermilk in and whisk till all clumps and lumps are gone
4. Pour 1 / 2 cup of batter onto a hot waffle iron, or 1 1 / 2 cups or ballet on a square iron
5. We like to top with crushed frozen strawberries and home made whipped cream<|endoftext|>
