What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Bars

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Shortening
• Egg
• Milk
• Apples
• Cinnamon
• Powdered sugar
• Butter
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Crust: put egg yolk in a liquid measuring cup and fill to 2 / 3 cup mark with milk
2. Mix with flour, 1 t
3. Sugar, 1 tsp salt, and shortening
4. Divide dough and roll out 1 / 2 to fit a greased and floured jelly roll pan
5. Filling: place sliced apples on rolled dough in pan
6. Pour 1 cup sugar, salt and cinnamon to taste over apples
7. Roll out remaining dough and place over the top
8. Slice with a knife to vent
9. Beat egg white and spread over top crust
10. Bake 45 to 60 minutes at 400f
11. Mix together powdered sugar, butter, dash of salt and 1 teaspoon vanilla
12. Drizzle over top of baked apple bars<|endoftext|>
