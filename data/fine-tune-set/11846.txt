Ingredients:
• Black tea
• Raisins
• Dried apricot
• Canola oil
• Garlic cloves
• Yellow onion
• Ground turmeric
• Ground coriander
• Cayenne pepper
• Ground cumin
• Ground fenugreek
• Ground black pepper
• Ground fennel
• Sea salt
• Apple cider vinegar
• Lean ground beef
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Combine tea, raisins, and apricots in a bowl
3. Let sit for 30 minutes
4. Drain, and reserve 1 / 3 cup soaking liquid
5. Set both aside
6. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat
7. Add garlic and onion
8. Cook until lightly caramelized, about 8 minutes
9. Add turmeric, coriander, cayenne, cumin, fenugreek, pepper, fennel, and salt
10. Cook until fragrant, about 1 minute
11. Add reserved soaked fruit and soaking liquid and vinegar
12. Cook until almost all liquid evaporates, about 2 minutes
13. Remove from heat and transfer to a large bowl
14. Add beef, and mix until evenly combined
15. Transfer meat mixture to a foil-lined baking sheet, and shape into an 8 4 loaf
16. Press almonds over top of meat loaf, and bake until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the center of the meat loaf reads 160f, about 40 minutes<|endoftext|>
