What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Pork Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Pork tenderloin
• Chicken bouillon cube
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Minute rice

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large skillet
2. Add pork
3. Cook and stir 5 minutes
4. Add bouillon cube, water, soy sauce and vegetables
5. Stir
6. Bring to
7. A boil
8. Stir in rice, cover
9. Cook on low heat 5 minutes or until cook through
10. Fluff with fork
11. Can substitute, boneless chicken breast or bonelss pork chop for tenderloin<|endoftext|>
