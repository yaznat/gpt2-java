What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Beef Stroganoff Sauteed Steak In Sour Cream Sauce

Ingredients:
• Boneless sirloin steaks
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic clove
• Mushroom
• Beef consomme
• Ketchup
• Butter
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dry mustard
• Onion soup mix
• Sour cream
• Wide egg noodles

Steps:
1. Trim all fat from steak, and cut across the grain into 1 / 4" wide strips
2. Combine flour with salt & pepper and dredge meat lightly in mixture
3. Heat oil in a large skillet over high heat until very hot, drop in garlic and steak and saute until meat is browned
4. Add mushrooms, broth, ketchup, butter, worcestershire sauce, mustard, and onion soup mix
5. Mix well
6. Cover and cook on low heat for about an hour
7. Just before serving add the sour cream, stirring just until blended
8. Serve over noodles or rice<|endoftext|>
