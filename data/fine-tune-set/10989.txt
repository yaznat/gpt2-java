Please give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>The Other White Meat Chicken Salad Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Red onions
• Carrot
• Lemon juice
• Mayonnaise
• Celery seeds
• Dried cranberries
• Pecans
• Asian pear
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in large bowl, stir til combined
2. Serve immediately or refrigerate for an hour or two to let flavors mingle
3. We especially like it on cracked wheat bread but sourdough or croissants would be delicious as well<|endoftext|>
