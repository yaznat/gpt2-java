Give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>Allison S Creamy Veggie Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Shell pasta
• Cheddar cheese
• Red pepper
• Yellow pepper
• Cucumber
• Tomatoes
• Red onion
• Plain yogurt
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• Salt
• Sugar
• Balsamic vinegar
• Dijon mustard

Steps:
1. Cook pasta as directed on box
2. Combine other ingredients except for vegetables and cheese in a separate bowl
3. When pasta is done, combine mixture with pasta
4. Toss in cheese and vegetables
5. Chill for at least 3 hours<|endoftext|>
