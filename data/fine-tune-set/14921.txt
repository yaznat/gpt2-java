What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Fried Rice My Way

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Sesame oil
• Chili oil
• Bacon
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Red capsicum
• Green capsicum
• Green peas
• Corn kernel
• Eggs
• Chicken stock powder
• Cooked white rice
• Green onion top
• Oyster sauce

Steps:
1. Heat the oils in a wok or frying pan and add the onion, bacon and garlic clove
2. Cook over medium heat until the onion is really well softened and the bacon is cooked through
3. Add the red and green capsicums, peas and corn and cook for 2 or three minutes
4. You want the capsicums brightly coloured and still crisp
5. Push all that to the edge and pour in the egg
6. Gently push the cooked egg to the outside dont beat it around too much as you want it visible in the finished dish!
7. When all the egg is cooked, gently mix all this together, then increase the heat and add the stock powder and rice
8. Get a lifter and turn the rice over
9. When the rice is well heated through, stir though the oyster sauce and the onion tops and serve
10. You can offer soy at the table if you must<|endoftext|>
