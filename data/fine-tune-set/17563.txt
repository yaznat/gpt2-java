Ingredients:
• Butterscotch chips
• Cream cheese
• Milk
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Butter
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Vanilla
• White sugar
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees f
2. Grease a 13" x 9" baking pan
3. In the microwave, melt the butterscotch baking chips
4. In a mixing bowl, combine cream cheese and milk
5. Stir in melted butterscotch chips
6. Set aside
7. Combine flour, sugar, baking soda and baking powder
8. Cut in cold butter, until mixture resembles fine crumbs
9. In another bowl, combine eggs, buttermilk and vanilla
10. Stir into flour mixture just to moisten
11. In prepared baking pan, spread the butterscotch / cream cheese mixture evenly in the bottom of the pan
12. Spoon the buttermilk cake batter over the butterscotch crust
13. Sprinkle with the topping
14. Bake for 40-45 minutes
15. To make the topping: combine the flour and sugar
16. Cut in butter until mixture resembles fine crumbs, stir in pecans
17. Sprinkle over top of cake before baking<|endoftext|>
