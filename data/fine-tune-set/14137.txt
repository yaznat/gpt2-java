Ingredients:
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Low-fat tofu
• Onion
• Nutritional yeast
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees and cook spinach according to package
2. Place cooked spinach in a food processor cup and add the remaining ingredients
3. Blend well
4. Pour mixture into a nonstick pan
5. Bake for 50 minutes
6. Allow to cool, and serve<|endoftext|>
