Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Lean ground chuck
• Fresh white breadcrumbs
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Sweet onion
• Mayonnaise
• Sweet pickle relish
• Dry mustard
• Salt
• Ground pepper
• Egg
• Ketchup

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350
2. In medium skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until it is limp& some fat is rendered, 3-4 minutes
3. Remove bacon from the skillet and reserve it, set aside
4. In large mixing bowl, use your hands to gently but thoroughly mix together: meat, bread crumbs, 1 / 2 cup cheese, onion, mayo, relish, mustard pepper and egg
5. Pat mixture into a shallow 2-quart baking pan
6. Spread top of the loaf with the ketchup then lay bacon strips over the ketchup
7. Bake until loaf is firm& the bacon is crisp, 45 to 50 minutes
8. Sprinkling top with remaining 1 / 2 cup cheese to melt during last 5-10 minutes of baking
9. Let meatloaf stand in the baking dish for 10 minutes, then cut into squares to serve<|endoftext|>
