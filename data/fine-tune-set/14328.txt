What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Put Down Your Fork Tuna And Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Light olive oil
• Red wine vinegar
• Garlic
• Salt
• Fresh basil
• Black pepper
• White kidney beans
• Albacore tuna in water
• Scallions
• Red sweet bell peppers
• Bibb lettuce

Steps:
1. In salad bowl, combine oil, vinegar, garlic, salt, basil and black pepper
2. Add beans, tuna, scallions and sweet pepper and toss
3. Chill several hours for flavors to blend
4. To make a beautiful presentation, serve on a leaf of bibb lettuce<|endoftext|>
