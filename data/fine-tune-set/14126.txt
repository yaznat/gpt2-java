Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper
• Paprika
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Drain tofu in cheesecloth or paper towels
2. Cut into 1 inch slices
3. Heat oven to 400f
4. Coat tofu with olive oil and sprinkle with paprika and salt and pepper
5. Bake until crispy about 35 minutes checking on it and flipping half way through
6. Heat sauce in a separate pan and serve over tofu when done
7. I like to serve this with home made mash potatoes and a green leafy salad yum!<|endoftext|>
