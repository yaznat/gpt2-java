Can you give me a coffee recipe?<|endoftext|>Brewed Orange Cinnamon Coffee

Ingredients:
• Regular grind coffee
• Orange zest
• Vanilla extract
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Blend coffee and dry ingredients in a blender
2. Blend in flavoring and extracts
3. Scrape sides and blend 15 seconds more
4. Makes one 8-cup pot
5. Add sugar / sweetener and / or creamer as desired
6. To give as a gift: place coffee mix in a filter
7. Place filter on a square of plastic wrap
8. Draw together with a ribbon<|endoftext|>
