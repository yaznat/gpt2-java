Ingredients:
• Cointreau liqueur
• Cranberry juice cocktail
• Fresh lime juice
• Superfine sugar
• Champagne

Steps:
1. Stir together cointreau, juices, and sugar and chill, covered, 2 to 6 hours
2. Just before serving, divide among 10 champagne flutes and top off with champagne<|endoftext|>
