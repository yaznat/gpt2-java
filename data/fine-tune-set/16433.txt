What's a recipe for a wrap?<|endoftext|>Banana Peanut Butter And Jelly Wraps

Ingredients:
• Sandwich wrap
• Fresh strawberries
• Banana
• Creamy peanut butter
• Strawberry jam
• Granola cereal

Steps:
1. Lay out the wrap and all the ingredients
2. Spread the peanut butter as the first layer on the wrap, the thickness depending on your preference
3. Leave about a centimeter or two around the outside edges not covered
4. Next spread the jelly on top, leaving spaces to place the strawberries
5. Clean and slice the strawberries and place them in the gaps of jelly
6. Sprinkle the granola over the pb&j layers on the wrap
7. Peel the banana and place it whole near one edge of the wrap
8. Roll the wrap up tightly around the banana and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
