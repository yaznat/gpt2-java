What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Butterfinger Pudding

Ingredients:
• Whole milk
• Instant banana pudding mix
• Butterfinger candy bars
• Frozen whipped topping
• Firm bananas

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, combine milk and pudding mix until thickened and smooth
2. Set aside 1 / 3 cup crushed candy bars for topping
3. Folf in whipped topping, bananas, and remaining crushed candy bars into pudding
4. Spoon into serving dishes
5. Refrigerate until ready to serve
6. Sprinkle with reserved candy bars<|endoftext|>
