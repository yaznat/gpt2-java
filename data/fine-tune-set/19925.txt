Ingredients:
• Butter
• Garlic cloves
• Red swiss chard
• Hot pepper sauce
• Eggs
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Salt
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Melt 1 tbsp of butter in an 8-inch diameter non-stick skillet over medium-low heat
2. Add the garlic and saute until soft
3. Stir in the swiss chard, cover, and cook until tender
4. Stir in the hot sauce
5. Season with salt and pepper
6. Transfer to a small bowl, and wipe the skillet clean
7. Whisk the eggs, 1 / 4 cup cheese, salt, and pepper ina medium bowl to blend
8. Melt 1 / 2 tbsp butter in the skillet over medium-high heat
9. Add half of the egg mixture and cook until the eggs are just set in the centre, tilting the pan and lifting the edge of the omellete with a spatula to let the uncooked portion flow underneath
10. Scatter half of the chard mixture over half of the omelette
11. Sprinkle 1 / 4 cup of cheese over the chard
12. Fold the omelette over the cheese, and slide onto a plate
13. Repeat with the remaining butter, egg mixture, chard, and cheese<|endoftext|>
