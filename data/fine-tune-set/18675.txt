What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Barley Pudding

Ingredients:
• Water
• Barley
• Salt
• Milk
• Heavy whipping cream
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Golden raisin
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, bring water to a boil
2. Stir in barley and salt
3. Reduce heat
4. Simmer, uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Add milk
6. Cook over medium heat for 10 minutes or until barley is almost tender, stirring frequently
7. In a bowl, whisk the cream, sugar, eggs and vanilla
8. Gradually stir into the barley mixture
9. Spoon into eight greased 6 ounce custard cups
10. Sprinkle with raisins and cinnamon
11. Place custard cups in baking pans
12. Fill pan with one inch of boiling water
13. Bake, uncovered, at 350 for 30-35 minutes or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
