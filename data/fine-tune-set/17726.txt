What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Strudel Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Butter
• White sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Apples
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 and grease a 12 cup muffin pan or use muffin liners
2. In a medium bowl, mix flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt and set aside
3. In a large bowl, beat together butter, sugar and eggs until smooth
4. Mix in vanilla
5. Stir in apples, and gradually blend in the flour mixture
6. Spoon the mixture into the prepared muffin pan
7. In a small bowl, mix brown sugar, flour and cinnamon
8. Cut in butter until mixture is like coarse crumbs
9. Sprinkle over tops of mixture in muffin pan
10. Bake 20 minutes in the preheated oven, or until a toothpick inserted in the center of a muffin comes out clean
11. Allow to sit 5 minutes before removing muffins from pan
12. Cool on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
