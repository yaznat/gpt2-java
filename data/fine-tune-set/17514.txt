Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Raw sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Milk
• Blueberries
• White flour
• Rolled oats
• Ground cinnamon
• Pecans
• Butter

Steps:
1. Combine the flours, sugar, baking powder and salt in a bowl
2. Beat the oil, egg and milk and add to dry ingredients
3. Beat just until blended
4. Pour into a greased 9-inch baking pan, then spread the berries over the batter
5. Mix the topping ingredients in a blender or food processor
6. Crumble the mixture over the cake
7. Bake at 375 degrees for 25 to 35 minutes or until center is set<|endoftext|>
