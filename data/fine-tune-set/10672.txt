Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Peanut And Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Spaghetti
• Roasted peanuts
• Fresh orange juice
• Rice wine vinegar
• Teriyaki marinade
• Sesame oil
• Orange rind
• Garlic powder
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Radish
• Carrot
• Green onions
• Fresh cilantro
• Avocado

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Drain and set aside
3. Process 1 / 2 cup peanuts, orange juice and next 6 ingredients in a blender until smooth, stopping to scrape down sides
4. Combine pasta, half of dressing, remaining 1 / 2 cup peanuts, radishes and the next 3 ingredients, gently tossing to coat
5. Cover and chill 30 minutes
6. Serve with remaining dressing and avocado slices<|endoftext|>
