Give me a cake recipe.<|endoftext|>Allegra S New Favorite Cocacola Cake

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Butter
• Cocoa
• Coca-cola
• Mini marshmallows
• Baking soda
• Eggs
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Vanilla
• Pecans
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Sift flour, sugar, soda and salt into a large bowl
2. Heat butter, coke, and butter to boiling point
3. Pour over flour mixture
4. Stir in eggs, buttermilk, vanilla and marshmallows
5. The marshmallows will float and batter will be lumpy
6. Pour into greased and floured 9 x 13 inch pan
7. Bake at 350 degrees for 30 to 35 minutes
8. Cool
9. After cake has cooled, heat butter, coke and cocoa to boiling point
10. Pour over sugar and mix well
11. Add nuts and vanilla
12. Pour over cooled cake<|endoftext|>
