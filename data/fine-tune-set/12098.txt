Ingredients:
• Mandarin oranges in light syrup
• Crushed pineapple in syrup
• Cream cheese
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Frozen whipped topping
• Flaked coconut
• Graham cracker crust
• Maraschino cherries

Steps:
1. Drain oranges and pineapple, reserving 2 tablespoons pineapple syrup
2. Gently press oranges and pineapple between paper towels to remove
3. Excess moisture
4. Beat cream cheese mixture at med-high speed of an electric mixer until
5. Creamy
6. Gradually add reserved pineapple syrup and sweetened condensed
7. Milk, beating until smooth
8. Fold in 1 1 / 2 cups whipped topping
9. Gently
10. Fold in orange-pineapple mixture and coconut
11. Spoon filling into
12. Crust
13. Cover
14. Freeze until firm
15. Let pie stand at room temperature 20 minutes before serving
16. Dollop
17. Remaining 1 cup whipped topping on top of pie
18. Garnish, if desired<|endoftext|>
