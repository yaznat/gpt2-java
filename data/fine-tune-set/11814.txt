Can you give me a curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Bebe S Chicken Curry

Ingredients:
• Onions
• Oil
• Garlic
• Gingerroot
• Curry powder
• Chili powder
• Ground cloves
• Ground cardamom
• Turmeric
• Chicken breasts
• Curry paste
• Water
• Tomatoes
• Coconut milk
• Golden raisin
• Lemon juice
• Salt
• Plain yogurt
• Flaked coconut
• Cucumber
• Onion
• Jalapeno chiles

Steps:
1. Reserve 1 / 2 cup of the chopped onions for the yogurt sauce
2. In a 12 inch heavy skillet, het oil and saute the remaining onion, garlic, ginger, curry powder, chili powder, ground cloves, cardamom and tumeric
3. When onion is soft, add cubed chicken and saute until lightly browned
4. Add curry paste, water and tomatoes
5. Simmer until chicken is done, stirring periodically
6. About 7 to 10 minutes
7. Remove from heat
8. Add coconut milk, raisins, lemon juice and salt to taste
9. To serve reheat curry, but do not boil
10. Spoon over steaming rice on individual plates, garnish each with a slice of banana if desired and sprigs of cilantro
11. Pass the chutney, warm chapatis and yogurt sauce!
12. For the yogurt sauce, combine all ingredients and let stand for several hours<|endoftext|>
