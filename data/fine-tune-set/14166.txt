Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Carrots
• Oil
• Seasoning salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Dried basil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a medium skillet over medium-low heat
2. Add tofu, sprinkle generously with seasoned salt and fry until browned, approximately 5 7 minutes
3. Add vegetables to skillet and continue cooking over medium flame, until hot
4. Garnish with dried basil, hot sauce or cayenne, and add salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
