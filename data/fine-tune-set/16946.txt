Please give me a recipe for a cocktail.<|endoftext|>Bloody Mary Shrimp Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Medium shrimp
• Tomato juice
• Vodka
• Red pepper sauce
• Sugar
• Celery salt
• Fresh parsley
• Seafood cocktail sauce
• Green olives

Steps:
1. Arrange shrimp in single layer in rectangular glass or plastic dish
2. Heat tomato juice, vodka if using and pepper sauce to boiling in 1 quart saucepan over medium high heat
3. Stir in sugar
4. Reduce heat
5. Simmer uncovered 5 minutes, stirring occasionally,
6. Stir in celery salt and parsley
7. Pour over shrimp
8. Cover and refrigerate 2 to 3 hours
9. Mix cocktail sauce and olives
10. Pour into small serving bowl
11. Remove shrimp from marinade with slotted spoon
12. Arrange on serving platter
13. Serve shrimp with sauce and toothpicks
14. A pretty way to serve: place shrimp in an oversized martini glass, 12 to 14 inches in diameter
15. Pour cocktail sauce mixture over shrimp, and garnish like a bloody mary with a skewer of colossal olives, small lime wedges and a celery stick<|endoftext|>
