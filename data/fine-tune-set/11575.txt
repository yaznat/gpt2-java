Steps:

2. Add sugar, flour, and cube of butter into small mixing bowl
3. Cut butter into sugar / flour mixture with pastery cutter
4. Spray bundt pan with pam cooking spray
5. Put butter / flour / sugar mixture into bottom of bundt pan evenly
6. In a seperate large mixing bowl add bananas and mash
7. Add remaining ingredients
8. Using mixer, blend for approximately 1 minute
9. Pour cake mixture into bundt pan on top of first layer
10. Follow the baking directions on the box of cake mix
11. Check for doneness with a toothpick
12. Remove cake from oven after tooth pick comes out clean
13. Let cake stand for 10 to 15 minutes
14. Place cake plate on bottom of bundt pan and carefully flip over
15. Remove bundt pan
