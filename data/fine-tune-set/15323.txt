Ingredients:
• Rajma
• Cilantro
• Onion
• Pomegranate seeds
• Salt
• Oil
• Garlic
• Buns
• Green chili
• Bread
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Mash the boiled rajma with the back of a fork in a large bowl
2. Using your fingers, break the 2 slices of bread and add to the bowl
3. Add garlic, onions, green chillies, salt, cilantro and pommegranate seeds
4. Mix everything well
5. Make small round / slightly big tikkis out of this
6. Shallow fry in oil on either side till brown
7. Drain out excess oil
8. Remove from oil and keep aside
9. Now take the bun
10. Cut it into 2 halves
11. Put one patties on the bun and put a sliced tomato over it
12. Cover with the other slice of bun
13. Likewise, repeat for remaining patties
14. Serve immediately with tomato ketchup<|endoftext|>
