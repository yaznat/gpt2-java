What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Black Bean Butternut Squash Chili

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Celery
• Red pepper
• Butternut squash
• Zucchini
• Black beans
• Crushed tomatoes
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Dried chili
• Salt
• Water

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large saucepan
2. Add onion celery and red pepper
3. Cook over medium heat stirring occasionally until vegetables are soft
4. Add squashes, beans, tomatoes, garlic, seasonings and water
5. Bring to a boil reduce heat and cook uncovered for 30-45 minutes stirring occasionally<|endoftext|>
