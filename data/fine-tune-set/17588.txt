Ingredients:
• Coconut
• Nuts
• Sugar
• Butter
• Bisquick baking mix
• Milk
• Egg
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Powdered instant coffee

Steps:
1. Set oven to 400 degrees
2. Set oven rack to second-lowest position
3. Grease a square 8x8x2-in baking pan
4. Mix coconut, nuts, 1 / 4 cup sugar and 1 tbsp melted butter
5. Set aside
6. Beat remaining ingredients except chocolate chips and coffee, in a large bowl for 30 seconds, scraping bowl
7. Beat on med speed 4 mins, scaping bowl
8. Pour into pan
9. Stir together, melted chocolate and coffee
10. Spoon over batter
11. Lightly swirl chocolate mixture through batter several times with a knife for a marbled design
12. Sprinkle coconut mixture evenly over top
13. Bake for 20-25 mins, or until golden brown, and cake tests done<|endoftext|>
