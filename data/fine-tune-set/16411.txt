Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Heavy whipping cream
• Maple syrup
• Sea scallops
• Dijon mustard
• Salt

Steps:
1. Place bacon in an ungreased 15 x 10 x 1 inch baking pan
2. Bake at 350f degrees for 7-10 minutes or until partially cooked and lightly browned
3. Drain on paper towels
4. Wrap each strip of bacon around a scallop
5. Secure with toothpicks
6. In a saucepan, bring cream to a boil
7. Reduce heat
8. Simmer, uncovered, until cream is reduced to 3 / 4 cup, about 8 minutes
9. Stir in the mustard, syrup, and salt
10. Bring to a boil and boil for 2 minutes
11. Meanwhile, place the scallop on a greased baking sheet
12. Bake at 400f degrees for 8-12 minutes or until firm and opaque
13. Serve with the cream sauce<|endoftext|>
