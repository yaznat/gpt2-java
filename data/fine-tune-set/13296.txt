Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>A Different Chicken Dish

Ingredients:
• Milk
• French style green bean
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Condensed golden mushroom soup
• Chicken breasts
• French-fried onions
• Hormel dried beef
• Garlic powder
• Tony chachere's seasoning
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Cut chicken into strips
3. Sprinkle with seasonings
4. Wrap a slice of beef around each strip and place in a greased casserole dish
5. Top w / any remaining dried beef
6. Mix soup with milk, beat with fork until well blended
7. Stir in 1 / 2 can of fried onions
8. Top evenly over chicken
9. Sprinkle cheese over soup mixture
10. Layer beans over cheese
11. Top with remaining onions
12. Cook at 350 degrees for 35 minutes, covered
13. Remove cover last 10 minutes
14. Let set for 15 minutes
15. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
