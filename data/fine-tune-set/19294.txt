Can you give me a popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Hot Chocolate Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Cream
• Milk
• Chocolate
• Cocoa powder
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In a saucepan combine cream, milk, chocolate pieces, cocoa powder and vanilla
2. Heat over medium flame stirring frequently
3. Once chocolate has melted, turn heat to low and keep on stirring every once in a while for 5 minutes
4. Remove from heat and allow to cool to room temperature
5. Fill into popsicle molds and freeze for at least 4 hours
6. Enjoy
