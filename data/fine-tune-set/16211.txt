What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Jalapeno Popper Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Tortillas
• Bacon
• Jalapenos
• Boursin cheese
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Tortillas -- wrap up the 4 of the tortillas in a damp paper towel and heat on medium heat for about 30 seconds
2. Just until they are slightly warm
3. Filling -- top each of the tortillas with 1 tablespoon of the the herb cheese, , top 1 / 2 of the tortilla with 1 of the minced jalapenos, then 1 / 4 of the bacon, and 1 / 4 cup of the shredded cheese
4. Season with a pinch of both salt and pepper
5. Fold over the side not filled to make a 1 / 2 moon shape
6. Grill -- brush each side of the tortilla, with olive oil and either grill, inside or out or bake
7. They take just a few minutes per side until golden brown and then flip
8. They don't take long so keep an eye on them
9. If you use the oven 400 for about 10-12 minutes on a cookie sheet, middle shelf, uncovered
10. And same thing -- flip half way through the cooking process
11. Serve -- you could serve with sour cream if you want, but for me
12. Nothing is needed
13. Just slice them in wedges and enjoy! cheesy, spicy and creamy<|endoftext|>
