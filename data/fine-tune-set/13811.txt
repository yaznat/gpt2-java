Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin roast
• Chicken broth
• Soy sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Spaghettini
• Snow peas
• Vegetable oil
• Cremini mushroom
• Scallions
• Dry roasted salted peanut

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine the pork, 1 / 2 cup chicken broth, 2 tablespoons soy sauce and half of the garlic
2. In a large pot of boiling, salted water, cook the pasta until al dente
3. Place the snow peas in a colander and drain the pasta over them
4. Meanwhile, in a large, deep skillet, heat 2 tablespoons oil over medium-high heat
5. Add the mushrooms and cook until softened, about 3 minutes
6. Add the scallions and the remaining garlic and cook for 3 minutes
7. Transfer to a small bowl
8. In the skillet, heat the remaining 2 tablespoons oil
9. Add the pork mixture and cook until opaque, about 3 minutes
10. Add the remaining 1 cup chicken broth and 3 tablespoons soy sauce and cook until the liquid is slightly thickened, about 4 minutes
11. Return the mushroom mixture to the skillet and remove from the heat
12. Add the pasta and snow peas and toss
13. Top with the peanuts<|endoftext|>
