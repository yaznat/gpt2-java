Ingredients:
• Coffee
• Heavy cream
• Milk
• Eggs
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Pour milk into a 1 qt saucepan
3. Stir coffee into the milk & slowly bring the mixture to the boiling point
4. Meanwhile, put the eggs & egg yolks in a lrg mixing bowl & beat together thoroughly w / a wire whisk
5. Then beat in the cream & sugar
6. When the milk mixture reaches the boiling point, slowly pour it into the egg mixture, beating vigorously w / the whisk
7. Add the vanilla extract & beat again
8. Pour half of the coffee cream into a 10-in pie dish or 6-cup souffle dish
9. Set the dish in a shallow pan filled w / boiling water to reach halfway up the side of the dish
10. Working quickly, transfer the dish to the preheated oven & pour the rest of the coffee cream into the dish
11. Reduce the oven temp to 325f & bake the coffee cream for 1 1 / 4 hours or till a knife plunged into the center comes out clean & dry
12. Remove the dish from the oven & allow to cool to room temperature
13. Cover & refrigerate for a minimum of 3 hours to overnite
