Ingredients:
• Flour
• Shortening
• Salt
• Ice water
• Apples
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Butter
• Vanilla caramels
• Water

Steps:
1. To make crust:
2. Cut shortening into flour until fine crumbs form
3. Add salt
4. Mix in water
5. Rollout into 2-9 inch crusts
6. Procedure:
7. Combine sliced apples with sugar, flour, cinnamon, and butter
8. Spoon half into pastry lined pie dish and spread evenly
9. Melt caramels with water in top of double boiler
10. Drizzle over apples
11. Spoon remaining apple mixture over caramel layer
12. Place second crust over apples and crimp edges to seal
13. Bake 45 minutes in 425 oven
14. Cover edge of crust with foil if it browns to quickly<|endoftext|>
