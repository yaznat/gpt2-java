Give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Amish Never Fail Pie Crust

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Egg
• Granulated sugar
• Crisco
• Vinegar
• Water

Steps:
1. Blend flour, sugar and salt
2. Cut in crisco to pea sized pieces
3. Mix together egg, water and vinegar
4. Add to flour
5. Mix until moistened and a soft dough forms
6. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before rolling
7. This will keep in the refrigerator for up to a week and will be easy to roll<|endoftext|>
