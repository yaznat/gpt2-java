What's a good smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Blaster Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Milk
• Coconut milk
• Ice cubes
• Pure vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In a blender combine the bananas and milk
2. Next, pour in coconut milk and add ice
3. Add the pure vanilla extract
4. On high speed, blend until smooth
5. Serve over ice in two tall glasses
6. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
