Can you give me a custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Ever Low Fat Custard Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Fat-free sweetened condensed milk
• Fat-free evaporated milk
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Vanilla flavoring
• Skim milk

Steps:
1. Put eggs, sugar, flour, and part of milk in blender
2. Blend well
3. Add remaining ingredients
4. Cook on low heat until the mixture thickens slightly
5. Cool and refrigerate until you are ready to churn<|endoftext|>
