Ingredients:
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Cornstarch
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Canola oil
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Pineapple chunks
• Vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Light soy sauce
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Mix broth with cornstarch
2. Set aside
3. Saute chicken in oil in skillet until browned
4. Then add vegetables and saute for 3 minutes
5. Add broth mixture and remaining ingredients and cook until mixture boild and thickens<|endoftext|>
