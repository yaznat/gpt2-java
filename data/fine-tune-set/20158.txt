Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breasts
• Vegetable oil
• Ground ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Asparagus spears
• Onion
• Mushrooms
• Oyster sauce

Steps:
1. Heat up 2 tbsp oil in a wok, when hot, add chicken, 1 minced clove garlic and tsp ginger
2. Cook through, remove chicken and set aside
3. Clean pan and add 2 tbsp oil and add asparagus, 1 minced clove garlic and tsp ginger, cook for 2 minutes
4. Add onion and mushrooms and cook until veggies are just done
5. Add the oyster sauce
6. Serve<|endoftext|>
