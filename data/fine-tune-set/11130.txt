What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Bacon Horseradish Turkey Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Croissants
• Peach preserves
• Horseradish
• Havarti cheese
• Roasted turkey
• Bacon
• Arugula
• Dijon mustard

Steps:
1. Combine the peach preserves and horseradish
2. Spread on 1 side of 6 sliced croissants
3. Layer with havarti cheese, turkey, bacon, and arugula
4. Spread 1 side of remaining croissant slices with the mustard, and place, mustard side down on the arugula
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
