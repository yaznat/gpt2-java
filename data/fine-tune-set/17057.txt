Ingredients:
• Medium shrimp
• Chili sauce
• Hard-boiled egg
• Fat-free cream cheese
• Celery
• Onions
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Process all ingredients except the parsley in a food processor until they are finely chopped, but not like a paste
2. The mixture should still be somewhat chunky
3. Form the mixture into balls and roll in parsley
4. Place on a nonstick cookie sheet covered with wax paper and refrigerate overnight before serving<|endoftext|>
