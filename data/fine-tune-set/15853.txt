Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Water
• Sliced ripe olives
• Pita breads
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Reduced-fat cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a large nonstick skillet coated with nonstick cooking spray, cook beef over medium heat until no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Stir in burrito seasoning and water
4. Bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat
6. Simmer, uncovered, for 3-5 minutes or until thickened
7. Stir in olives
8. Fill pita bread halves with beef mixture, lettuce, tomato and cheese<|endoftext|>
