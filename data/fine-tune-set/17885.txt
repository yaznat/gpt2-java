Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Butterscotch Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a pan, stir in the sugar
2. Heat it until the sugar has melted
3. Allow it to cool then add the beaten egg, flour, vanilla, salt, and baking powder
4. Bake the dough at 350 degrees for 30 minutes
5. Cut then sprinkle it with confectioners sugar, if you like<|endoftext|>
