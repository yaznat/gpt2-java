What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Almond Tres Leches Muffins

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Almond extract
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Heavy whipping cream
• Milk
• Slivered almonds
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Grease 12 regular-size muffin cups with shortening or line with paper cups
3. In a large bowl beat together butter and sugar on medium speed until smooth
4. Beat in almond extract and eggs
5. With wooden spoon, stir in flour, baking powder, 1 / 3 cup condensed milk, whipping cream
6. Milk and 1 / 2 cup of the almonds just until combined
7. Divide batter evenly among muffin cups sprinkle 1 / 4 cup sliced almonds evenly over batter
8. Bake 15-20 minutes or until light golden brown
9. Cool 10 minutes and drizzle each with 1 tsp of the additional condensed milk
10. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
