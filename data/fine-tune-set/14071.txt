What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy 5 Step Chicken Pork Beef Or Lamb Vindaloo

Ingredients:
• Lean meat
• Onion
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Yellow mustard seeds
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cardamom
• Ground turmeric
• Ground cloves
• Black peppercorns
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Sugar
• White wine vinegar
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Water

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except oil, onion and meat
2. Leave standing while you cook the onions
3. Heat oil in medium to large pot and add onions
4. Stir until onions are soft
5. Add vindaloo mix to pot and cook for 1 to 2 minutes
6. Add meat and mix so that it is covered with the vindaloo mix
7. Add 1 / 2 c water
8. Simmer, covered, over low heat for about 1 1 / 4 hours or until meat is tender<|endoftext|>
