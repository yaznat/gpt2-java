What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Au Gratin Potato Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Mashed potatoes
• Egg
• Chives
• Fresh parsley
• Salt
• Instant minced garlic
• Pepper
• Dried rosemary
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the first eight ingredients
2. Stir in cheese
3. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat
4. Drop batter by 1 / 4 cupfuls into oil
5. Press lightly to flatten
6. Cook in batches for 2-3 minutes on each side or until golden brown, using remaining oil as needed
7. Drain on paper towels<|endoftext|>
