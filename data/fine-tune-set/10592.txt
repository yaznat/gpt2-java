What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Fresh Tomato Sauce

Ingredients:
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Fresh basil
• Red wine vinegar
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Sugar
• Angel hair pasta
• Part-skim mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the tomatoes, onion, basil, vinegar, olive oil, garlic, and sugar
2. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let it stand at room temperature for at least 4 hours
3. When the sauce is ready, cook the pasta according to the package directions, and drain
4. Transfer the pasta to a large serving bowl, and cover with the sauce and cheese
5. Gently toss to mix well<|endoftext|>
