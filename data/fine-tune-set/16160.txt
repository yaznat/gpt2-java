Ingredients:
• Cheddar cheese
• Tortillas
• Spring onions
• Sun-dried tomatoes
• Sweetcorn
• Sunflower oil
• Minced meat

Steps:
1. Sprinkle the cheese onto 4 tortillas, leaving a 2cm border, then scatter the spring onions, tomatoes, optional meat and sweetcorn
2. Put the remaining tortillas on top and press lightly together
3. Grease a non-stick frying pan with a little oil and place one quesadilla in the frying pan and cook for 2-3min over a medium heat-or if botom is golden brown
4. Carefully turn over and cook for a further 2 mins basically till cheese is melted
5. Repeast for the other quesadillas
6. Cut into quarters and serve with homemade sour cream and chive dip<|endoftext|>
