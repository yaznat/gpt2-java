What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Blackened Opelousas Topping For Fish

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Heavy cream
• Cajun seasoning
• Jalapeno powder
• Instant roux mix
• Blackening seasoning
• Black pepper
• Paprika
• Crabmeat
• Oysters
• Crawfish tail meat

Steps:
1. In a medium skillet over medium heat melt butter still it starts to brown
2. Add cream and stir and heat till bubbling
3. Add remaining ingredients stirring in between
4. Reduce heat to simmer uncovered for 30 minutes stirring occasionally
5. This sauce is great over blackened fish with a side of dirty rice<|endoftext|>
