What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>A Simple Greek Salad

Ingredients:
• Tomatoes
• Cucumber
• Red onion
• Capers
• Feta cheese
• Black olives
• Olive oil
• Dried oregano
• Sea salt

Steps:
1. Slice the tomatoes, onion and cucumber
2. Combine
3. Sprinkle over the 1 tablespoon of oregano and pour over the olive oil
4. Add the capers
5. Toss the salad lightly
6. Just prior to serving sprinkle cubed feta cheese and olives over the top of the salad
7. Sprinkle with some extra dried oregano and sea salt if desired
8. You can drizzle over some extra olive oil if desired<|endoftext|>
