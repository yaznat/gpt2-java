What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apricot Mustard Glazed Pork Roast

Ingredients:
• Apricot preserves
• Dijon mustard
• Cider vinegar
• Garlic salt
• Pepper
• Boneless pork loin

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Mix apricot preserves, dijon mustard, vinegar, garlic salt, and pepper
3. Cover roasting pan with foil
4. Place pork roast on rack in pan
5. Pour about 2 / 3 of apricot-mustard mixture over roast
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes
7. Pour remaining apricot-mustard mixture over pork roast
8. Bake another 45 minutes
9. Remove from oven, tent with foil, and allow roast to rest 15 minutes before carving<|endoftext|>
