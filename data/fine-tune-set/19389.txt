Give me a donut recipe.<|endoftext|>Grandma Clarks Fried Donuts

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Liquid shortening
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Milk
• Flour
• Icing
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Mix sugar, spices, salt, and baking powder
2. Add shortening and vanilla
3. Mix
4. Add eggs, mix
5. Add milk and flour
6. Mix
7. Drop into hot oil using donut dropper
8. Fry in oil turning when first crack appears
9. -be careful not to crowd
10. Remove and place on either a drying rack or brown paper bag to drain oil
11. Ice while warm with powdered sugar icing<|endoftext|>
