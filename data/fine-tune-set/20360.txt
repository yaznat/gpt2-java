Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cheddar cheese
• Frozen tater tots

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Brown ground beef until all pink is gone

4. Put ground beef in the bottom of a greased 13x9x2-inch pan
5. Spread cream of mushroom soup on top of ground beef
6. Cover with grated cheese
7. Top with tater tots
