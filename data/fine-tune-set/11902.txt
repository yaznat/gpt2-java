Ingredients:
• Butter
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic clove
• Curry powder
• Chicken broth
• Apricot preserves
• Carrot
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat
2. Add ginger, garlic, and curry and saute 30 seconds
3. Stir in broth and preserves
4. Add carrots, cover and simmer for about 6 minutes or tender
5. Season with salt and pepper
6. Mix in cilantro<|endoftext|>
