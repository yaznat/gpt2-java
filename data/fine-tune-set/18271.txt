What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Ever Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Buttermilk
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Put all dry ingredients in mixing bowl
2. Blend well with wire whip
3. Add remaining ingredients and mix well
4. Add more buttermilk if needed<|endoftext|>
