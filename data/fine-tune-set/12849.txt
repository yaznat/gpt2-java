Ingredients:
• Dried cherries
• Port wine
• Cherry juice
• Beef tenderloin
• Butter-flavored cooking spray
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Olive oil
• Shallots
• Garlic clove
• Low sodium beef broth
• Dried thyme

Steps:
1. Set oven to broil
2. Combine cherries, 1 / 4 cup port, and juice in a small bowl
3. Let stand for 30 minutes for the cherries to absorb most of the liquid
4. Coat beef with cooking spray
5. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
6. Broil 6 inches from heat for 15 minutes
7. Reduce heat to 375 and bake for 30 minutes or until meat thermometer reads 145 degrees
8. Let stand 10 minutes before slicing
9. Heat oil in pan over medium-high heat
10. Add shallot and garlic
11. Saute 1 1 / 2 minutes
12. Stir in cherry mixture, 3 / 4 cup port, broth, and thyme
13. Bring to a boil
14. Cook 10 minutes or until reduced to 1 / 2 cup
15. Pour over steaks
16. Serve immediately
17. Serving size is 1 steak with 2 tbsp sauce<|endoftext|>
