Ingredients:
• Cranberries
• Water
• Sugar
• Egg
• Butter
• Heavy cream
• Pastry for double-crust pie

Steps:
1. Cook cranberries, water & both sugar amts for about 25 minutes
2. Allow mixture to cook, then add beaten egg & butter
3. Roll out pie pastry for bottom crust
4. Place in pie pan & bake at 400 f for 5 minutes
5. Remove from oven & allow to cool
6. Fill crust w / cranberry mixture & top w / lattice crust
7. Return pie to oven and bake for another 35-40 minutes at 350 degrees
8. Serve with cream<|endoftext|>
