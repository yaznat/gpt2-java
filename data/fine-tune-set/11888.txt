What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Captain S Curry

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Chicken pieces
• Onions
• Celery
• Garlic cloves
• Curry powder
• Chili powder
• Ground allspice
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon stick
• Bay leaf
• All-purpose flour
• Condensed chicken broth
• Apples
• Raisins
• Coconut milk

Steps:
1. In a non stick skillet, heat oil over medium-high heat
2. Add chicken in batches, and brown on all sides
3. Transfer browned chicken to slow cooker stoneware
4. Reduce the heat on the frying pan to medium
5. Add to the frying pan the onions and celery and cook, stirring until softened
6. Add garlic, curry powder, chili powder, allspice, nutmeg, cinnamon stick and bay leaf and cook, stirring for 1 minute
7. Sprinkle flour over mixture and cook, stirring for 1 minute
8. Add chicken broth, bring to a boil and cook, stirring, until thickened, stir in apples and raisins
9. Pour the mixture from the frying pan over the chicken in the slow cooker, cover and cook on low for 5 to 6 hours, or on high for 2 to 3 hours, until juices run clear when the chicken is pierced with a fork
10. Stir in coconut milk and cook until heated through
11. Discard cinnamon stick and bay leaf before serving
12. Serve with rice<|endoftext|>
