What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 250 degrees
2. Wash and scrub the potatoes in a sink
3. Cut of any spuds on each potato
4. Cut each potato in 8ths like a circle
5. Spray a pan or plate with non-stick spray
6. Then, put the potatoes on the pan or plate
7. Put the potato slices in the oven for 3 to 6 minutes
8. If they're not cooked enough put them back into the oven for another minute
9. Let them sit out for 3 minutes
10. Then, sprinkle salt on them<|endoftext|>
