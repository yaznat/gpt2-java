Ingredients:
• Lump crabmeat
• Sour cream
• Lime juice
• Green onions
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Queso fresco
• Fresh cilantro
• 8-inch fat-free flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Shred crab meat
2. Mix together with sour cream, lime juice, and green onions in a small bowl
3. Season to taste with salt and pepper
4. Heat griddle or small frying pan with a small amount of oil or cooking spray
5. Take a tortilla, place about 1 / 8 of crab mixture on it and spread evenly
6. Top with 1oz
7. Cheese and sprinkle with some chopped cilantro
8. Place on hot griddle
9. When cheese starts to melt, fold in half
10. Allow to heat through and brown nicely, then remove from heat
11. Repeat with other tortillas
12. Quesadillas can be kept warm on a stoneware plate or baking dish in the oven on warm while you are busy with the others
13. Serve topped with a dallop of sour cream or guacamole, and wedges of lime, if you wish<|endoftext|>
