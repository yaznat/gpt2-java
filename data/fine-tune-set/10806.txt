What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>5 A Day Bulgur Wheat Salad

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Broccoli
• Carrot
• Bell pepper
• Fresh parsley
• Fresh lemon juice
• Olive oil
• Bulgur
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Chickpeas

Steps:
1. Wash and chop fresh onion, garlic, broccoli, carrots, peppers and parsley
2. Heat oil in a large skillet
3. Add onions and garlic and cook until soft, be careful not to burn garlic as it will be bitter
4. Add bulgur and stir to coat
5. Add 2 cups chicken broth to the skillet, bring to a boil
6. Lower the heat, add remaining vegetables and chickpeas
7. Cook for 10 minutes or until the liquid is absorbed
8. Add parsley and lemon juice and stir
9. Refrigerate 30 minutes to let the flavors meld
10. Serve cold<|endoftext|>
