Ingredients:
• Cod
• Dry yeast
• Beer
• Flour
• Salt
• Egg white

Steps:
1. Dissolve 1 package dry yeast in 1 / 2 cup warm beer
2. Add 1 cup flour, and a pinch of salt
3. Stir
4. Add remaining 1 / 2 cup beer and more as needed for "sticky run" dough
5. Cover with a dish towel and let it stand in a warm place for a 1 / 2 hour or more
6. Fold in egg white and use immediately
7. Dry fish fillets with a paper towel before dipping in batter
8. Fry in hot vegetable, peanut, or corn oil
9. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
