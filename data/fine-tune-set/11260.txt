Ingredients:
• Nori
• Cooked sushi rice
• Medium shrimp
• Carrots
• Avocados
• Green onion tops
• Cucumber

Steps:
1. Cut off top quarter of nori sheet along short end
2. Place nori, shiny side down, on a sushi mat covered with plastic wrap, with long end toward you
3. Pat 3 / 4 cup rice over nori with moist hands, leaving a 1-inch border on one long end of the nori
4. Arrange 1 / 4 cup shrimp, 2 carrot strips, 2 slices avocado, 1 green onion top, and 2 cucumber strips along top third of rice-covered nori
5. Lift edge of nori closest to you
6. Fold over filling
7. Lift bottom edge of sushi mat
8. Roll toward top edge, pressing firmly on sushi roll
9. Continue rolling to top edge
10. Press mat to seal sushi roll
11. Let rest, seam side down, for 5 minutes
12. Repeat procedure with remaining ingredients
13. Slice each roll into 8 pieces with a sharp knife<|endoftext|>
