Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Olive oil
• Light brown sugar
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Cut sweet potatoes in wedges or like french fries
3. Place wedges on large baking pan or cookie sheet
4. Pour over olive oil, and toss to mix
5. Now sprinkle over brown sugar, salt, and black pepper
6. Toss to coat wedges
7. Spread out in a single layer
8. Place pan in oven and bake for 15 minutes, then take out and turn wedges over
9. Place back in oven and bake another 15-20 minutes, or until golden brown and crispy on the outside
10. Sprinke with extra kosher salt to taste
11. The cooking time will depend on how big the wedges are
12. Enjoy!
13. Have fun and experiment!<|endoftext|>
