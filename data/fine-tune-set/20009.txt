Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Sweet red pepper
• Sliced mushrooms
• Fresh thyme
• Pepper
• Green onions
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Line bottom of 13 x 9 inch metal cake pan with parchment or waxed paper
2. Grease paper and sides of pan
3. Set aside
4. In bowl, whisk together eggs, milk and salt, pour into prepared pan
5. Bake in 400f degree oven for 10 minutes or until centre is firm when touched
6. Meanwhile, in nonstick frypan, heat oil over medium high heat
7. Cook red pepper, mushrooms, thyme and pepper, stirring often, for 5 minutes or until mushroom liquid has evaporated
8. Stir in onions
9. Invert egg onto work surface
10. Peel off paper
11. Sprinkle with half of the cheese
12. Spread mushroom mixture over top
13. Starting at narrow end, roll up jello roll style
14. Return omelette roll to cake pan
15. Sprinkle with remaining cheese
16. Broil for 3 to 5 minutes or until cheese is bubbly
17. Slice crosswise to serve<|endoftext|>
