Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Apple Orange Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Applesauce
• Eggs
• Orange rind
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Nuts
• Icing sugar

Steps:
1. Melt butter and remove from heat
2. Add brown sugar
3. Mix well
4. Stir in apple sauce, eggs, orange peel and vanilla
5. Sift together flour, baking powder, salt and baking soda
6. Stir into apple sauce mixture
7. Pour into a greased 9 inch x 13 inch pan
8. Bake at 325 f for 30-40 minutes
9. Cut into squares while still warm and roll in icing sugar<|endoftext|>
