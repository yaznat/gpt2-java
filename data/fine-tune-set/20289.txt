Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Peanut butter
• White wine
• Brown sugar
• Ginger
• Hot pepper sauce
• Peanut oil
• Chicken breast
• Carrots
• Red pepper
• Yellow pepper
• Snow peas

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, stir together the soy sauce, peanut butter, white wine, brown sugar, ginger, garlic and hot pepper sauce
2. Set aside
3. In a wok or large skillet heat oil
4. Add chicken and stir fry for about 3 minutes
5. Add carrots and stir fry 2 minutes
6. Add peanut sauce, peppers and snow peas
7. Stir fry until vegetables are tender but still crisp and thicken is cooked through, about 2 minutes<|endoftext|>
