Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Blender Potato Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Onion
• Salt
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Raw potatoes

Steps:
1. Assemble blender
2. Put eggs, onion, salt, flour, baking powder and 1 / 2 c potato cubes into blender container
3. Cover and process at#7 until smooth
4. Add remaining potatoes, cover and process 2 cycles at#9
5. Pour into a hot, well greased griddle
6. Drain on absorbent paper<|endoftext|>
