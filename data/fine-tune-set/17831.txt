What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Bakery Style Upside Down Hawaiian Pineapple Muffins

Ingredients:
• Pineapple tidbits
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Honey
• Flour
• Natural bran
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Egg
• Buttermilk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350f
2. Place oven rack to second-lowest position)
3. Generously grease 12 regular-size muffin tins
4. Divide and place the tidbits in the bottom of each muffin tin in a single layer
5. Top the pineapple with about 1 tablespoon brown sugar, then 1 teaspoon melted butter, then 1 teaspoon honey
6. Set aside tins while preparing the muffin batter
7. To prepare the muffin batter
8. In a large bowl mix together flour with bran, baking powder, baking soda and salt
9. Stir in 3 / 4 cup brown sugar until blended
10. In a medium bowl whisk egg with buttermilk, oil, vanilla and 3 tablespoons honey
11. Pour into the flour mixture and stir just until combined
12. Divide and spoon batter into muffin cups on top of the pineapple mixture
13. Place the muffin tins on baking sheet to catch any spills
14. Bake for about 25 minutes, or until muffins test done
15. Cool muffins in tins for 5 minutes
16. Meanwhile place a large piece of wax paper onto a cooling rack to catch any spills
17. Turn muffins out onto cooling rack upside down and remove from pan / s<|endoftext|>
