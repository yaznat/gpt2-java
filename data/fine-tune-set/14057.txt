What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Delhi Style Lamb With Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Lamb
• Tomatoes
• Cumin
• Coriander
• Turmeric
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt
• Potatoes
• Red lentil
• Water

Steps:
1. Fry onions, garlic, meat
2. Add tomatoes, cumin, coriander, turmeric, cayenne, salt
3. Cook on high for 10-15 mins until oil seems to separate
4. Add potatoes, lentils
5. Cook on low for 70 mins<|endoftext|>
