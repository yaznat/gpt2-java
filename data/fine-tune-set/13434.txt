Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>African Beef

Ingredients:
• Creamy peanut butter
• Beef broth
• Canola oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Green bell pepper
• Carrot
• Beef stew meat
• Salt and pepper
• Diced tomatoes
• Dried thyme
• Bay leaf

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the creamy peanut butter and the beef broth until well blended
2. Set aside
3. Cut stewing beef in 1 1 / 2 inch cubes
4. Season the meat with salt and pepper
5. In a large saucepan or stew pot, heat the canola oil over medium heat
6. Add the onion, garlic, bell pepper, and carrots
7. Saute until the onions are translucent
8. Add the meat and continue to cook, stirring often, until it is browned on all sides
9. Add the peanut butter and broth mixture, tomatoes and liquid, thyme, and bay leaf
10. Stir well and bring to a boil
11. Reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring often for about 1 1 / 2 hour or until the meat is tender
12. Taste and add salt and pepper if desired
13. Remove the bay leaf and discard
14. Serve hot over rice
15. Cook on low for 7 hours if using your slow cooker<|endoftext|>
