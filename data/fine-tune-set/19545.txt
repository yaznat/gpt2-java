Ingredients:
• Russet potato
• Canola oil
• Kosher salt

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degrees
2. Wash potatoes thoroughly with a stiff brush and cold running water
3. Dry, then using a standard fork poke 8 to 12 deep holes all over the potatoes
4. Place in a bowl and coat lightly with oil
5. Sprinkle with salt
6. Place potatoes directly on top rack in middle of oven
7. Place a baking sheet on the lower rack to catch any drippings
8. Bake 1 hour or until skin feels crisp and inside feels soft<|endoftext|>
