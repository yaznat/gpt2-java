What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Peanut butter
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Oatmeal
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Milk chocolate chips
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Peanut butter chips
• Vanilla chip
• Butterscotch chips

Steps:
1. In large bowl, cream first 4 ingredients
2. Add eggs and vanilla
3. In separate bowl, combine dry mixture, except chips
4. Add dry mixture to creamed mixture, blend
5. Add chips to dough
6. Drop by spoonfuls onto greased cookie sheet about 2-inches apart
7. Bake in pre-heated oven at 350f for 10-12 minutes
8. Let set on pan for 1 minute after removing from oven<|endoftext|>
