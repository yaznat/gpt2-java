What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Broccoli Garlic Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Sesame oil
• Fresh broccoli
• Soy sauce
• Green onions
• Brown sugar
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic
• Dried red pepper flakes
• Black pepper
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. In a bowl combine together the soy sauce, green onions, brown sugar, ginger, garlic and red pepper flakes
2. Mix well to combine
3. Heat the sesame oil in a large skillet or wok over medium heat
4. Stir-fry until crisp-tender
5. Add in the soy sauce mixture until thoroughly heated and coated well
6. Season with black pepper if desired
7. Transfer to a bowl and toss with sesame seeds<|endoftext|>
