Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>7 Up Chicken In Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Butter
• 7-up soda
• Seasoning salt
• Parsley flakes

Steps:
1. Place chicken in bottom of crock pot
2. Add butter
3. Mix 7-up with salt and parsley
4. Pour over top
5. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 hours<|endoftext|>
