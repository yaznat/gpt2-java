Ingredients:
• Sirloin steak
• Tomatoes
• Onions
• Green bell pepper
• Pineapple chunks
• Molasses
• Pineapple juice
• Vinegar
• Oil
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Make the marinade
2. Put the molasses, pineapple juice, vinegar, and oil in a bowl and mix well
3. Add garlic, salt, and pepper
4. Cut steak into 1 inch cubes and add the marinade
5. Cover and leave in a cool places for at least one hour
6. Remove the steak and reserve the marinade for basting the kebabs
7. Cut the tomatoes into quarters,
8. Peel the onions and cut them into small chunks
9. Remove the ribs and seed from the green pepper and cut into squares
10. Thread steak, tomatoes, onions, bell peppers, and pineapple chunks alternately on to a 4 long or 8 short skewers
11. Brush skewers with marinade
12. Cook under a hot broiler, or grill outside for approximately 10 minutes, turning frequently and basting often
13. Serve with plain rice, or coconut rice<|endoftext|>
