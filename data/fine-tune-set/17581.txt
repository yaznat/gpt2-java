Please give me a recipe for coffee.<|endoftext|>Canadian Coffee

Ingredients:
• Whipping cream
• Pure maple syrup
• Whiskey
• Black coffee

Steps:
1. For topping whip cream with maple syrup just up until soft mounds
2. Set aside
3. For coffee divide maple syrup and whiskey among 4 warmed heat proof glass mugs or goblets
4. Pour in coffee to 1 inch of top
5. Spoon topping over coffee
6. Serve!<|endoftext|>
