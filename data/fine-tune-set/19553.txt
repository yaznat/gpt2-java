Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Sugar
• Buttermilk
• Butter
• Eggs
• Amaretto
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, cover sweet potatoes with water
2. Boil until tender
3. Drain
4. Remove the skins from the sweet potatoes and arrange them in a greased 2 quart baking dish
5. Process the sugar, buttermilk, butter, eggs, amaretto, vanilla, cinnamon, and baking soda in a blender until smooth
6. Stir in pecans
7. Spoon pecan mixture over sweet potatoes
8. Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
