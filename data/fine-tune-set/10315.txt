Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Unsalted butter
• Light brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Salt
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Chunky peanut butter

Steps:
1. Combine flour and baking soda with wisk
2. Set aside
3. In a large bowl, use an electric mixer to combine the butter, both sugars and salt until smooth and creamy, not fluffy
4. Add the egg, vanilla and peanut butter and mix until thoroughly blended
5. Add the flour mixture and stir with wooden spoon, just until incorporated
6. Wrap and refrigerate the dough for at least 2 hours
7. Using a small ice cream scoop, form dough into balls
8. Place 2-inches apart, flatten slightly, and use a cookie stamp, or tines of a fork to decorate the top of the cookie
9. Bake for 14 to 16 minutes in preheated 325 oven until firm to the touch and golden brown on the bottom
10. Cool cookies completely before stacking and storing
11. May be stored in airtight container for at least 2 weeks
12. Makes 3 - 5 dozen cookies, depending on size of scoop<|endoftext|>
