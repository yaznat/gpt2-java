What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Homebakes Knock Off Beef Stew And Biscuits

Ingredients:
• Beef stew
• Biscuits

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Pour stew into an 8 x 8-inch baking dish, i recommend glass
3. Prepare biscuits according to package
4. Spoon them onto the top of the stew
5. Bake for 30 minutes or until biscuits are browned
6. Let stand for 3-5 minutes
7. Serve and enjoy<|endoftext|>
