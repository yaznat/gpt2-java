What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Barley Pudding

Ingredients:
• Pearl barley
• Apples
• Sugar
• Heavy cream
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Bring a kettle full of water to boil
2. Add the barley and the apples
3. Cook until both begin to soften
4. Drain and blend the mixture in a blender or sieve
5. Cook the mixture after adding the sugar and lemon and boil again
6. Allow to cool and then chill for an hour
7. To serve, add a dollop of cream on top and maybe a dash of cinnamon<|endoftext|>
