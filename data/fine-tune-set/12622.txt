Ingredients:
• Top round steaks
• Salt
• Pepper
• Flour
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Beer
• Red wine vinegar
• Fresh sage
• Butter
• New potatoes
• Cabbage

Steps:
1. In a large skillet over medium high heat heat oil
2. Put 1 / 4 cup flour, salt and pepper in a baggie or pie dish and dredge beef in flour mixture
3. Brown beef cubes in skillet
4. Remove beef from skillet and place in a heat-proof bowl that will fit in steamer
5. Add onions and garlic to skillet, cooking until browned
6. Pour in beer and vinegar, bring to a boil and pour over meat
7. Stir in sage, salt and pepper
8. Cover bowl with foil or a lid and place on steamer rack
9. When steamer water comes to a boil, place rack in steamer and steam for 1 hour
10. If you want you can steam potatoes and cabbage with the meat about 35 minutes before meat is done add potatoes
11. Pour off liquid through a strainer into a saucepan
12. Place saucepan over medium heat and bring to a boil, gradually adding 1 tablespoon flour mixed with 1 tablespoon butter, stirring until sauce thickens
13. Place cabbage wedges in steamer and steam for 6-8 minutes
14. Pour sauce over meat and potatoes<|endoftext|>
