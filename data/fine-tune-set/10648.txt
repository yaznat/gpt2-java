Ingredients:
• Chicken breast tenders
• Artichoke hearts
• Roasted red pepper
• White wine
• American cheese
• White wine worcestershire sauce
• Cream of chicken soup
• Cheddar cheese
• Cooked bow tie pasta

Steps:
1. In a slow cooker, combine chicken tenders, artichoke, roasted peppers, american cheese, worcestershire sauce, white wine, and soup and mix well
2. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours
3. 20 minutes before serving, fold in cheddar cheese, hot pasta, and salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
