Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin
• Apple butter
• Chunky peanut butter
• Orange zest
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. Place roast, fat side up, on a rack in a shallow roasting pan, & place in an unheated oven
2. Turn the oven on to 350 degrees f, & roast the meat, uncovered for 3 hours
3. About 30 minutes before the end of the roasting time, insert a meat thermometer into the center & continue roasting until the thermometer reaches 170 degrees f
4. Meantime, gradually stir the apple butter into the peanut butter, then add the orange zest & juice
5. When the internal temperature of the roast reaches 170 degrees f, brush the apple butter mixture over the entire exposed surface of the roast, then continue cooking for another 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
