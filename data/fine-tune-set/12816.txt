What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Basil Marinated Steak

Ingredients:
• Worcestershire sauce
• Fresh basil
• Red wine vinegar
• Olive oil
• Honey
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Boneless beef top round steaks

Steps:
1. In a large nonmetal dish or resealable food storage plastic bag, combine all ingredients except steak
2. Mix well
3. Add steak and turn to coat
4. Cover dish or seal bag
5. Refrigerate atleast 4 hours or overnight to marinate
6. Heat grill
7. When ready to grill, remove steak from marinade
8. Discard marinade
9. Place steak on gas grill over medium heat
10. Cook 10 to 15 minutes or until desired doneness, turning once or twice
11. To serve, cut steak across grain into slices
12. Place on serving platter<|endoftext|>
