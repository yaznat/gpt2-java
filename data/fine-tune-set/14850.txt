Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Broccoli florets
• Onions
• Reduced-sodium chicken broth
• Cooked brown rice
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Braggs liquid aminos
• Soy sauce
• Oyster sauce
• Rice wine vinegar
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add broccoli and onion
3. Saut for 5 minutes
4. Add 1 / 4 cup broth
5. Cover and cook for 3 minutes
6. Remove broccoli mixture from pan
7. Place pan over medium-high heat
8. Add rice
9. Cook 5 minutes, stirring occasionally
10. Add broccoli mixture, garlic, ginger, pepper, and chicken
11. Toss well
12. Combine remaining 1 / 4 cup broth, soy sauce, oyster sauce, vinegar, and cornstarch
13. Add to pan
14. Bring to a boil
15. Cook 1 minute<|endoftext|>
