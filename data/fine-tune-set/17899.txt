Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Aunt Eileen S Sauce Pan Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Vanilla
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Salt
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f
2. Melt butter and chocolate in pan over low heat, stirring
3. Cool
4. Beat in vanilla and sugar
5. Add eggs one at a time, stir well
6. Sift flour and salt, add to chocolate
7. Gently stir in chocolate chips
8. Pour into 8x8x2 pan
9. Bake 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
