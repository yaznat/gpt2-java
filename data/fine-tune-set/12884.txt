What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Big Island Paniolo Tri Tip Steak

Ingredients:
• Rock salt
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Sesame oil
• Pepper
• Steaks

Steps:
1. Combine salt, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, and pepper in a large bowl
2. Add steak
3. Cover and let marinate for 1 hour
4. Preheat grill to medium
5. Grill steaks to desired degree of doneness, about 4-5 minute per side for medium rare<|endoftext|>
