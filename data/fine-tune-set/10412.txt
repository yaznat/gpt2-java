What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Buttermilk Cookies

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Crisco
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425
2. In a large bowl, cream together sugar and crisco -- beat in the eggs, one at a time, then stir in the vanilla
3. Combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt
4. In seperate bowl -- and then add to the creamed mixture alternately with buttermilk
5. Drop by rounded tspful 2" apart onto greased sheet
6. Bake at 425 for 6-8 minutes
7. Allow cookies to cool 5 minutes before removing to rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
