Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Asparagus spears
• Pesto sauce
• Olive oil
• Italian seasoning
• Parmesan cheese
• Salt and pepper
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 f
2. Line baking sheet with heavy-duty foil
3. Pound out of the chicken to approximately 1 / 2" thickness
4. Place chicken and pesto in medium bowl
5. Toss to coat
6. Cut asparagus to approx size of chicken roll
7. Place olive oil and italian seasoning in a shallow pan or ziplock bag and toss asparagus until coated well
8. Place 3 asparagus spears and top with parmesan cheese
9. Roll chicken around asparagus and wrap each roll with a bacon strip
10. Place, seam side down, in lined pan
11. Bake, uncovered, at 400f for 35-40 minutes or until juices run clear
12. Broil 6 inches from the heat for 3-5 minutes or until bacon is crisped
13. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
