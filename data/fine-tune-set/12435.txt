Give me a soup recipe.<|endoftext|>Antioxidant Soup All Ingredients Are To Be Organic

Ingredients:
• Vegetable stock
• Water
• Garlic cloves
• Green onions
• Raw carrots
• Sweet potatoes
• Purple cabbage
• Green beans
• Sweet corn
• Spinach
• Chopped tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Squash puree
• Pumpkin puree
• Miso
• Hot water
• Fresh oregano
• Fresh thyme

Steps:
1. Put the roasted vegetable stock and water in large pot
2. Add garlic, onions, fresh carrots and sweet potatoes
3. Bring to simmer and cover
4. Cook for 25 minutes
5. Add green beans, sweet potatoes, corn and spinach
6. Cook for 10 minutes
7. Add tomatoes products, squash puree, miso mixture and herbs
8. Simmer for 60 minutes
9. You can add more water if you want a more brothy soup
10. This soup can be frozen in quart jars
11. When i serve the soup, i usually add cup of beans, rice, tofu or quinoa for each 1 cup of soup
12. Whatever is in the fridge works great
13. Great with rustic italian rosemary bread!<|endoftext|>
