Ingredients:
• Boneless beef chuck roast
• Water
• Carrots
• Onion
• Peas
• Potato

Steps:
1. Trim excess fat off roast and cut into 1-1 / 2 inch cubes
2. Fill medium sauce pan with water, add meat, cover pan and simmer over medium / low heat for 40 minutes
3. While the meat is cooking, peel the carrots and slice
4. Peel the potato and cut into 1 / 2 inch cubes
5. Set aside
6. After the meat has been cooking for 40 minutes, add the carrots, onion, peas & potatoes
7. Cover and cook another 15 minutes or until vegetables are soft and meat is longer pink
8. If the water disipates before the vegetables are done, add more as needed
9. For infants:
10. Separate the meat from vegetables
11. Using your food processor, puree the meat first to almost the desired consitency
12. Add the vegetables and puree until smooth or desired consistency
13. Scoop into ice cube tray and freeze
14. Once frozen, pop out and store in sealed plastic bag for up to 3 months
15. For older babies:
16. Cut up meat to bite size pieces and serve veggies as-is
17. Freeze in indivual containers for easy one serving sizes<|endoftext|>
