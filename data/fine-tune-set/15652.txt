Give me a taco recipe.<|endoftext|>Buffalo Chicken Tacos

Ingredients:
• Flat bread
• Chicken tenders
• Buffalo wing sauce
• Yellow onion
• Flour
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Blue cheese
• Ranch dressing
• Cumin

Steps:
1. Slice chicken tenders in half lengthwise
2. Saute in vegetable oil over medium-high heat until browned and cooked through
3. Remove tenders from pan and place into a bowl with 1 cup hotwing sauce
4. Set aside
5. Peel and slice onion into thin circles
6. Dip individual rings into milk, then into flour and set aside
7. Preheat 2 inches vegetable oil in a pan over medium-high heat
8. Fry onion rings until golden browned, then remove to paper towel lined pan to drain well
9. Place flatbread into microwave and warm for 45 sec-1 minute
10. Per taco: use one flatbread, sprinkle crumbled blue cheese into center
11. Add chicken tenders, onion rings
12. Top with ranch sauce
13. Ranch sauce: 1 cup ranch dressing, 1 / 4 tsp cumin, 1 / 2 cup hotwing sauce, combined well<|endoftext|>
