Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Algerian Green Bean And Lamb Tagine

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Boneless lamb shoulder
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Ground cumin
• Cayenne pepper
• Saffron
• Water
• Green beans
• Tomatoes
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large saucepan or soup pot
2. Add the lamb, onion and garlic and saut until lamb is browned
3. Add the salt, pepper, 1 tsp cumin, cayenne and saffron or turmeric and saut a minute more
4. The original recipe calls for the sliced tomatoes to be added with the second onion / parsley / cumin
5. If you would rather do it this way, do not add them in the following step
6. Add water and tomatoes and bring to a boil
7. Reduce heat, cover and simmer for about 45 minutes
8. Add the green beans and simmer for about 10 minutes more, until almost tender
9. Add the sliced onion, tomatoes, parsley and remaining 1 tsp cumin
10. Simmer for about 10 minutes more
11. Serve hot over couscous<|endoftext|>
