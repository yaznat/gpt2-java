Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Cooked chicken
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Heavy cream
• Taco seasoning
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Meanwhile, in large skillet over medium heat, cook chicken and garlic in oil for 4-5 minutes or until heated through
3. Whisk in the cream and taco seasoning until well blended
4. Cook and stir until heated through
5. Stir in parmesan cheese
6. Drain pasta and toss with chicken mixture<|endoftext|>
