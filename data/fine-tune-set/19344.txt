Ingredients:
• Apple cider
• Vegetable oil
• Applesauce
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Ground cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Kosher salt
• Powdered sugar
• Food coloring
• Doughnut
• Licorice
• Sour apple

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees f
2. Lightly grease two 6-count donut pans and set aside
3. In a small saucepan over high heat, bring cider to a boil
4. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until reduced to 1 / 4 cup, about 15 minutes
5. In a large bowl, whisk together the vegetable oil, applesauce, sugar, eggs and vanilla
6. Add the reduced cider and stir to combine
7. In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, cinnamon, baking powder and salt
8. Add dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until just incorporated
9. Transfer batter to a disposable piping bag
10. Pipe batter into donut pans, filling each well nearly to the rim
11. Bake until a toothpick inserted into a donut comes out clean, 12-14 minutes
12. Allow doughnuts to cool in pans for 10 minutes, then remove to a wire cooling rack and allow to cool completely
13. Meanwhile, in a large bowl whisk together powdered sugar and apple cider
14. Tint to desired shade of green using gel paste food coloring
15. Carefully dip the top of each doughnut into the glaze and decorate as desired with sprinkles, disco dust, a candy stem and candy leaves<|endoftext|>
