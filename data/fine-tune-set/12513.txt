Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Anginares Me Araka Artichoke And Pea Stew

Ingredients:
• Artichoke hearts
• Baby peas
• Carrots
• Onions
• Water
• Tomato paste
• Olive oil
• Dill
• Lemon, juice of
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Place the oil, onions and carrots in a large saucepan and cook until the onion is translucent
2. Add 1 1 / 2 cups of water and bring to the boil
3. Cook for 10 minutes
4. Add the artichokes, tomato paste, lemon juice and salt and pepper to taste
5. Cover and cook for 10 minutes more
6. Add the peas and dill, check seasoning
7. Cover and cook a further 10 minutes
8. The stew should have quite a bit of sauce, but not be swimming in it
9. If there's too much liquid, uncover pan, turn up heat slightly and boil to evaporate liquid, about 5 minutes
10. This is best served warm, nearly room-temperature
11. Serve with feta and crusty bread to mop up the juices<|endoftext|>
