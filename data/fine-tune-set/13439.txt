Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Alberta Beef Mushroom And Pepper Pasta

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onions
• Paprika
• Caraway seed
• Round steaks
• Salt and pepper
• Beef stock
• Red wine
• Sour cream
• Flour
• Olive oil
• Portabella mushroom
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Pasta

Steps:
1. Saute bacon until crisp
2. Remove and set aside
3. Add onions and saute for 5 minutes
4. Add paprika and caraway, saute for 1 minute
5. Add beef and toss well, cook 3-5 minutes until beef is browned
6. Season with salt and pepper
7. Add stock and wine
8. Bring to a boil and simmer covered for 30 minutes
9. Remove cover and simmer 30 longer
10. Bring 2 quarts of water to a rolling boil, and cook pasta according to package directions, drain, and set aside keeping warm
11. Whisk sour cream and flour together, add to meat mixture, and set aside keeping warm
12. Saute mushrooms in oil for 3 minutes
13. Add peppers and saute for another 5 minutes
14. Season with salt and pepper
15. Top pasta first with beef mixture, then mushroom / pepper mixture and sprinkle with bacon<|endoftext|>
