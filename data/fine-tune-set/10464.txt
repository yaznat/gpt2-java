Ingredients:
• Apple butter
• Natural-style chunky peanut butter
• Vanilla
• Non-fat powdered milk
• Whole wheat flour
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Prepare cookie sheet with oil
2. Preheat oven to 350
3. In a large bowl, beat apple butter, peanut butter, and vanilla
4. Set aside
5. Stir together in a medium bowl powdered milk, flour, salt, cinnamon and raisins
6. Add dry ingredients to apple butter mixture and mix well
7. Drop by teaspoon onto prepared cookie sheet
8. Flatten with fork dipped in ice water, making a criss-cross pattern on top
9. Bake at 350 for 10 minutes cool
10. Refrigerate until well chilled
11. Serve cold
12. These freeze well<|endoftext|>
