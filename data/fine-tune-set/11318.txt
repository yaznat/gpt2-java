Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Mom S Amazing Banana Bread

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Sugar
• Light brown sugar
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Cardamom
• Allspice
• Nuts
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Beat butter and sugars until smooth and creamy
3. Beat in eggs, one at a time
4. Beat in bananas and vanilla
5. In a separate bowl, mix dry ingredients together
6. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet, until well blended
7. Fold the nuts and sour cream into batter until just blended
8. Do not over-mix
9. Butter a cast iron skillet
10. Pour batter into skillet-bake in oven until toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean-45 minutes to an hour<|endoftext|>
