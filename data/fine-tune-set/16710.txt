What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>A Delectable Cinnamon Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Milk
• Cream
• Cinnamon extract
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in blender and mix
2. Pour milkshake into cup
3. Garnish with whipcream and sprinkle with cinnamon if desired and serve<|endoftext|>
