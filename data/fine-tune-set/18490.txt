Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>German Hazelnut Waffles With Blackberry Cream

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Cornstarch
• Baking powder
• Heavy cream
• Hazelnuts
• Cream cheese
• Blackberry jam
• Confectioners' sugar
• Blackberry

Steps:
1. With an electric mixer, beat the melted butter with the sugar, vanilla sugar and eggs until fluffy
2. In a separate bowl, combine flour, corn starch and baking powder
3. Add the dry ingredients and the cream gradually to the butter-egg-sugar mixture
4. Fold in the ground hazelnuts
5. Prepare waffles in preheated waffle maker
6. Combine the cream cheese, confectioners' sugar and blackberry jam until smooth
7. Serve the waffles with the cream and fresh blackberries
8. Dust with confectioners' sugar<|endoftext|>
