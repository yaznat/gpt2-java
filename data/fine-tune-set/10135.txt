What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Baked Chili Cheese Spread

Ingredients:
• Yellow cornmeal
• Chili powder
• Cream cheese
• Eggs
• Garlic powder
• Ground cumin
• Diced green chilies
• Green onion
• Salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Black olives
• Sour cream
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 f
2. Heavily spray bottom and side of 9-inch springform pan with non stick cooking spray
3. Combine corn meal and chili powder
4. Sprinkle over bottom and side of prepared pan
5. Beat cream cheese, eggs, garlic powder, and cumin in large mixer bowl until smooth
6. Stir in chiles and 1 / 2 cup green onion
7. Pour half of batter into pan
8. Spoon 1 cup salsa over top of batter
9. Sprinkle 1 1 / 2 cups cheddar cheese over salsa
10. Spread remaining batter over top of cheese
11. Bake for 55 to 60 minutes or until edges are set but center still moves slightly
12. Cool completely in pan on wire rack
13. Remove side of springform pan
14. Spread remaining salsa over top
15. Sprinkle with remaining cheese and green onion
16. If desired garnish with black olives and sour cream
17. Serve with tortilla chips, crackers, or sliced baguette<|endoftext|>
