Ingredients:
• Center-cut pork chops
• Bacon
• Red delicious apple
• Onion
• White wine vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Meat tenderizer
• Black pepper
• Ground sage
• Ground celery seed
• Fresh thyme
• Garlic clove

Steps:
1. Wrap your center cut pork chops with bacon and fix into place
2. Line a loaf pan with aluminum foil and grease the bottom with a tiny bit of oil or cooking spray
3. Place your chops into the center of the pan, neither touching the sides, nor one another
4. Directly onto each chop, deposit 1 / 4 teaspoon no msg unseasoned meat tenderizer, flip chop and repeat
5. Both sides are now tenderized and oiled
6. Add thyme, salt, pepper, sage, ground celery seed, and garlic
7. Using a medium grater, grate small onion equally right onto each chop, repeat with small apple
8. Split white wine vinegar and worcestershire sauce evenly, pouring over onion and apple
9. Cover and let marinade in refrigerator overnight if possible
10. 4 hours should do the trick, if you don't have the time
11. Preheat oven to 350f and bake, covered, for 1 1 / 2 hours
12. When done, the apple and onion should still be on top of the pork chop
13. Leave them as garnish and remove to serving plate
14. Serve hot over a bed of pork dressing
15. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
