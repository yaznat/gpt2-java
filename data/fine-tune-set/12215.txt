Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Cake

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Egg
• Salt
• Butter
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Vanilla
• Baking apples
• Pecans
• Golden raisin

Steps:
1. Cream butter, sugar and the egg
2. Mix together the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, salt, nutmeg, vanilla, apples, pecans, and raisins
3. Mix together well with the creamed mixture
4. Pour the batter into a round pie dish and bake at 350 30 to 40 minutes or until the cake is done and a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean, not doughy<|endoftext|>
