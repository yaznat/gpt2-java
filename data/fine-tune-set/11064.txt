Give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Apple Walnut Turkey Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Celery
• Raisins
• Walnuts
• Tart apple
• Deli turkey
• Sourdough bread
• Lettuce leaf

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine the mayonnaise, celery, raisins, and walnuts
2. Stir in apple
3. Season to taste with salt and pepper
4. Set aside
5. Place turkey on four slices of bread
6. Top with apple mixture, lettuce, and remaining bread<|endoftext|>
