Please give me a recipe for steak.<|endoftext|>Basic Steak Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Sirloin steak
• Barbecue sauce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Yellow onion
• Mushroom
• Vegetable oil
• Bread

Steps:
1. Once your stake is grilled, slice it into thin strips
2. Place bbq sauce a little salt and a little pepper in a bowl
3. Add the steak to the bowl and mix the ingredients together until the steak is coated well
4. You may add more sauce if you need to
5. Peel the onion
6. Slice thinly
7. Slice mushrooms into medium sized slices
8. Turn stove to high
9. Place a large skillet on the stove
10. Add mushrooms and onions and a few table spoons of cooking oil
11. Stir while cooking
12. The mushrooms and onions should become soft and slightly brown
13. Add the mushrooms and onions to the open sub
14. Place the strips of steak on top
15. I like to drizzle a little ketchup on top, but thats up to you
16. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
