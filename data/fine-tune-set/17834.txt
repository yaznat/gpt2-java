Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• All-bran cereal
• Skim milk
• Mashed banana
• Egg whites
• Vegetable oil
• Walnuts
• Raisins

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, put the baking powder and flour
2. In a smaller bowl, mix the beaten egg, the all bran, the milk, mashed banana, and the vegetable oil
3. Let this mixture soak for five minutes and then add to first mixture
4. Mix together and then add walnuts and raisins
5. Bake in a preheated 350 degree oven for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
