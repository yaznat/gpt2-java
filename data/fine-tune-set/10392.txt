Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Cream of tartar
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Canola oil
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Egg
• Butterscotch chips
• Walnuts
• Toffee pieces

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350
2. In a bowl, mix together the flour, salt, baking soda and cream of tartar
3. In another bowl with an electric mixer, cream the butter with the both sugars until creamy
4. Add in the 1 cup oil, egg, 1 tsp milk and the extract
5. Beat with mixer until well mixed, and the sugars are dissolved
6. Add in the flour mixture, mix on low speed until well combined
7. With a wooden spoon, mix in the baking chips and the nuts, and the skor toffee bits
8. Drop about 1 tablespoon of dough onto a greased cookie sheet
9. Bake for 7-8 minutes
10. Let cool on sheet for 5 minutes
11. Transfer to a wire rack<|endoftext|>
