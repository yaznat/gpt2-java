What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Al S Oasis Lemon Cream Cheese Pie

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Egg yolks
• Lemon juice
• Salt
• Butter
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Cream cheese
• Instant lemon pudding mix
• 9-inch pie shells
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, combine sugar and cornstarch
2. Gradually stir in water, mixing until smooth
3. Cook and stir over medium-high heat until thickened and clear
4. Quickly stir in egg yolks
5. Bring to a boil
6. Boil for 1 minute stirring constantly
7. Remove from heat
8. Stir in 1 / 3 cup lemon juice, salt and butter
9. Cool for several hours or overnight
10. In a mixing bowl, blend condensed milk and cream cheese until smooth
11. Stir in pudding mix and remaining lemon juice
12. Fold into chilled lemon filling
13. Divide and spoon into baked pie shells
14. Refrigerate for several hours
15. Garnish with whipped cream<|endoftext|>
