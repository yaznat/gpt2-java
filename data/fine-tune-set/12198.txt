What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie A La Mode

Ingredients:
• Apple pie filling
• Graham cracker crust
• Butter pecan ice cream
• Caramel ice cream topping
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Spread half of the pie filling over crust
2. Top with half of the ice cream
3. Cover and freeze for 30 minutes
4. Drizzle with half of the caramel topping
5. Cover and freeze for 30 minutes
6. Top with remaining pie filling
7. Cover and freeze for 30 minutes
8. Top with remaining ice cream
9. Cover and freeze until firm
10. May be frozen for up to 2 months
11. Remove from the freezer about 30 minutes before serving
12. Warm remaining caramel topping
13. Drizzle some on serving plates
14. Top with a slice of pie
15. Drizzle remaining caramel topping over pie and sprinkle with pecans
16. Yield: 6-8 servings<|endoftext|>
