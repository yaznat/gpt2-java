Give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Banquet Burgers

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Water
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Onion
• Dijon mustard
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Pepper
• Lean ground beef
• Cheddar cheese
• Cooked bacon

Steps:
1. In large bowl, beat egg with water
2. Stir in bread crumbs, grated onion, dijon mustard, garlic, salt and pepper
3. Add beef
4. Mix just until combined
5. Shape into four 3 / 4-inch thick patties
6. Place on greased grill over medium heat
7. Close lid and grill, turning once, until digital thermometer inserted sideways into centre reads 160f, about 15 minutes
8. Top each burger with cheese and bacon
9. Grill until cheese melts, about 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
