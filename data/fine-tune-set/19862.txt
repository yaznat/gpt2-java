Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Margarine
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 f
2. Melt margarine / butter over medium heat in a large heavy bottomed fry pan which has a heat resistant handle
3. When butter starts to sizzle add potatoes and let fry for 2 minutes
4. Turn with a spatula and cook for another 2 minutes
5. Continue until all sides of potato cubes are browned
6. Place in preheated oven for 15-20 minutes until potatoes are tender<|endoftext|>
