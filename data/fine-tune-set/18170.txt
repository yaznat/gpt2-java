Ingredients:
• Plain flour
• Brown sugar
• Dried lavender flowers
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Butter
• Cooking spray
• Lavender honey
• Cream

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix together all dry ingredients together
2. Add the milk, eggs and vanilla extract and whisk until well combined
3. Whisk in the melted butter until the batter is thick and very smooth
4. Heat frying pan or griddle over medium heat, and spray with cooking oil spray or grease with a knob of butter
5. Spoon the pancake batter onto the griddle in equal amounts and cook until bubbles come to the surface of the batter and the edges start to curl up
6. Flip pancakes with a spatula, and cook for a few seconds more on the second side
7. Check underneath for a golden brown colour if they are too pale, keep cooking them a bit longer
8. When they are done, remove them from the griddle / frying pan and put on a large heat-proof plate in the oven to keep warm and continue until all the batter is used
9. Makes about a dozen pancakes
10. Top with lavender honey and cream, or butter and lavender honey
11. Great with fruit also!<|endoftext|>
