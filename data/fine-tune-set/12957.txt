What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Awesome Ritz Fish

Ingredients:
• Fish fillets
• Ritz crackers
• Butter

Steps:
1. Wash fish filets and pat dry
2. Lay filets in a 9x13" baking dish
3. Set aside
4. Put ritz crackers in a gallon size baggie and crush the crackers to a fine crumble
5. Add butter to the ritz and mix well
6. Spread the ritz mix over the top of all the filets
7. Bake at 350 degrees for 20 to 30 minutes
8. Test doneness by sticking a fork in the fish and twisting
9. The fish should be flakey and the ritz crackers should be nicely browned
10. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
