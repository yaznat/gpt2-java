What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Bacon And Egg Salad Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Bacon
• Celery
• Mayonnaise
• Salt
• Pepper
• Lettuce leaves
• Tomatoes
• Whole wheat bread

Steps:
1. Place eggs in medium saucepan
2. Cover with cold water
3. Bring to boil
4. Reduce heat
5. Simmer about 15 minutes
6. Meanwhile cook bacon until crisp
7. Drain on paper towel
8. Crumble
9. Immediately drain
10. Run cold water over eggs to stop cooking
11. Peel eggs
12. Chop
13. In medium bowl, combine chopped eggs, celery, mayonnaise, salt and pepper
14. Mix well
15. Stir in bacon
16. Chill to help blend flavors
17. Layer lettuce, egg salad and tomato slices on 4 slices of bread
18. Top with remaining bread slices<|endoftext|>
