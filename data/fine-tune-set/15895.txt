Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Cucumber
• Cheddar cheese
• Ranch dressing
• 7-inch flour tortillas
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Drain canned chicken, put inot bowl and break up with fork
2. Chop cucumber and add to meat
3. Stir in cheese and dressing
4. Mix well
5. Spoon one-forth of the mixture on edge of tortilla, top with one forth of the lettuce
6. Fold the edge nearest the filling over the filling till it is covered
7. Then roll up the tortillia
8. Repeat to make three more burritos<|endoftext|>
