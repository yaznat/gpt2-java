What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Easy Tofu Fillet Sandwich With Tartar Sauce

Ingredients:
• Cornmeal
• Flour
• Breadcrumbs
• Lemon pepper
• Extra firm tofu
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Pickles
• Onions
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. For best results, press tofu and freeze overnight
2. Cut into 1 / 2 inch thick slices
3. Mix cornmeal, flour, bread crumbs, and your seasoning thoroughly in a bowl for breading
4. Coat tofu slices in breading, and fry in 1 / 4 inch of oil in a pan for about 5 minutes on each side
5. Remove from pan and drain on paper towel
6. Make tartar sauce by mixing mayo, pickle, and onion well in a small bowl
7. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper to taste
8. Serve on a bun<|endoftext|>
