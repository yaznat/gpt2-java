Ingredients:
• Sesame oil
• Green onions
• Broccoli
• Mushrooms
• Snow peas
• Bok choy
• Cashews
• Water
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Gingerroot
• Honey
• Cornstarch
• Pepper
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, mix together all ingredients for the sauce
2. Set aside
3. Heat a large fry pan or wok over medium-high heat
4. Add cashews and roast for 2 to 3 minutes
5. Stirring frequently
6. Careful not to burn them
7. Set aside with the green parts of the green onion
8. In the same pan, add the sesame oil and heat over medium-high heat
9. Add the white parts of the green onion, broccoli and mushrooms
10. Saut for about 5 minutes or until broccoli is starting to become tender
11. Add a few tablespoons of water, if needed, to stop browning or burning
12. Add the snow peas and bok choy and saut for 2 minutes
13. Add the sauce and saut for about 3 to 5 minutes or until the sauce thickens
14. Remove from heat and add in the cashews and green parts of the green onion
15. Serve immediately with brown rice<|endoftext|>
