Ingredients:
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Half-and-half
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Fresh blackberries
• Fresh lemon juice
• Of fresh mint

Steps:
1. Combine first 3 ingredients in a large dutch oven
2. Whisk in half-and-half
3. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly, over medium-high heat
4. Slowly whisk about 4 cups of hot mixture into beaten egg
5. Add egg mixture to remaining hot mixture, whisking constantly, until mixture reaches 160
6. Let cool completely, stirring occasionally
7. Stir in vanilla
8. Chill 8 hours or overnight
9. Combine blackberries and 1 1 / 2 cups sugar in a separate bowl
10. Stir well
11. Cover and chill 8 hours, stirring occasionally to dissolve sugar
12. Stir in lemon juice
13. Stir berry mixture into custard mixture
14. Pour into container of a 4-quart ice-cream maker
15. Freeze according to manufacturer's instructions
16. If recommended, pack freezer with additional ice and rock salt, and let stand 1 hour before serving
17. Scoop into serving bowls
18. Garnish, if desired
19. Makes 1 gallon
