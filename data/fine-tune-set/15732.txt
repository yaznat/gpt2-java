Ingredients:
• Chili-flavored ramen noodles
• Chili
• Flour tortilla
• Sour cream
• Pico de gallo
• Lettuce
• Cheese

Steps:
1. In a pan, boil water and cook noodles
2. Drain noodles, and return to pan
3. Open seasoning packets, mix in, open can of chili and mix all ingredients
4. Add whatever you like to your chili ramen noodle soft taco
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
