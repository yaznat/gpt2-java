What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Amazing Tuna Fish Casserole

Ingredients:
• Kraft macaroni and cheese
• Campbell's cream of mushroom soup
• Tuna in water
• Saltine crackers
• Butter

Steps:
1. Prepare macaroni and cheese as directed on the box
2. Drain tuna and add to the mac and cheese
3. Add cream of mushroom soup
4. Place noodle mixture in casserole dish
5. Crumble crackers
6. Cover casserole with crackers
7. Melt butter and drizzle over crackers
8. Bake at 350 for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
