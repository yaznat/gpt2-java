What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Burrito Casserole

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Dry enchilada mix
• Water
• Tomato paste
• Garlic clove
• Pepper
• Salt
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Refried beans
• Salsa
• Sour cream
• Diced green chilies
• Onions
• Guacamole
• Black olives
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, combine sauce mix, water, tomato paste, garlic, pepper & salt
2. Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes
3. In a skillet, brown the beef and drain
4. Stir in 1 / 3 of sauce
5. Spread 1 / 3 of sauce in the bottom of a greased 9 x 13" baking dish
6. Place 3 tortillas over sauce, tearing to fit the bottom of pan
7. Spoon half of the meat mixture over the tortillas, then sprinkle with 1 1 / 2 cups of the cheddar cheese
8. Add 3 more tortillas
9. Spread refried beans over tortillas, then top with remaining meat
10. Sprinkle with 1 1 / 2 cups of the cheddar cheese
11. Layer remaining tortillas, then top with the remaining sauce
12. Sprinkle with remaining cheddar cheese
13. Bake, uncovered, at 350 degrees for 35-40 minutes
14. Let stand 10 minutes before cutting
15. While it is baking, prepare topping ingredients into small containers and arrange on a serving tray
16. Serve casserole with topping tray so guests can choose whatever toppings they desire<|endoftext|>
