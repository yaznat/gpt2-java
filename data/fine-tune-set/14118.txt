What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Style Tofu And Cucumber Noodles

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Gingerroot
• Lime
• Firm tofu
• Medium egg noodles
• Sunflower oil
• Chili
• Cucumber
• Peanuts

Steps:
1. Mix soy, ginger and lime juice in a dish, add tofu
2. Marinate 5 minutes, turn once
3. Boil noodles according to pack instructions, drain, cool
4. Dry tofu on kitchen paper
5. Heat oil in nonstick pan, fry tofu till golden, about 3 minutes each side
6. Cut into cubes
7. To serve mix noodles, chilli, cucumber, tofu and the marinade in a bowl
8. Garnish with lime wedges and peanuts<|endoftext|>
