Ingredients:
• Dried apricots
• Sugar
• Lemon, juice of

Steps:
1. Wash and soak the apricots in warm water for 6-8 hours or overnight remove the seeds with a knife
2. Mash a bit and add sugar boil in water till very soft
3. Add lemon juice and chill
4. Serve as a topping on custard and garnish with the nut from the seed chopped<|endoftext|>
