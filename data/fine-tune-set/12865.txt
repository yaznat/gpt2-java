Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Dijon mustard
• Fresh rosemary
• Worcestershire sauce
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Lager beer
• Steak

Steps:
1. Whisk first 7 ingredients together in a bowl, then whisk in the beer
2. Pour it into a big zip lock bag and add the steaks
3. Marinate - turning occasionally - for 1 day if possible
4. Remove from marinade, shaking off excess and cook on a good hot skillet or bbq
5. Once cooked to your liking, remove from heat and let rest for 5 minutes
6. Slice steaks thinly across the grain, and arrange on a platter or on a good salad
7. Spoon any juices over and serve<|endoftext|>
