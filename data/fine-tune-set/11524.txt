What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Another Banana Bread

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Plain fat-free yogurt
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Bananas
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Spray 2 loaf pans with cooking spray
3. In medium bowl, combine flour, baking powder, cinnamon and cloves
4. Set aside
5. In another bowl, cream together butter and splenda
6. Blend in mashed bananas
7. Add flour mixture to wet mixture, stir together just until combined
8. Spread in pans and level off with knife or spatula
9. Bake for approximately 1 hour or till toothpick inserted in center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
