Ingredients:
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Fresh basil
• Red chilies
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Combine garlic and ginger
2. Wash basil, shake off excess water and pluck leaves from the stalks
3. Remove seeds from chillies and chop roughly
4. Leave seeds in for a hotter paste
5. Mix all ingredients together to make a smooth paste
6. Add lemon juice and mix through
7. Decant and refrigerate
8. Makes about 250g
9. For a more authtentic touch add 1 teaspoon brown sugar and a dash of oyster and fish sauce just before serving<|endoftext|>
