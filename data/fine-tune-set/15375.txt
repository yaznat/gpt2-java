Give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Asian Turkey Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• French's french fried onions
• Egg
• Water chestnut
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Stir-fry sauce
• Cayenne pepper sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Sandwich buns
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. In a large bowl combine the ground turkey, one cup french fried onions, egg, water chestnuts, bread crumbs, stir-fry sauce, franks redhot sauce and the ginger
2. Shape meat mixture into four patties
3. Broil patties six inches away from the heat source or grill over medium coals
4. Cook for 10 minutes or until burgers are no longer pink in the middle, turning only once
5. Serve on buns and top with the remaining onions and lettuce<|endoftext|>
