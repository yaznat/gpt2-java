What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Alfredo Potato Finger Fries

Ingredients:
• Fingerling potatoes
• Butter
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Parmigiano-reggiano cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. In a large pot, cover the fingerling potatoes with cold, salted water and bring up to a boil, cook until just tender, about 8 minutes, then drain
3. While the potatoes cook, melt the butter in a small pot with the garlic and cook over medium-low heat for about 4-5 minutes
4. Pour the melted butter onto a baking sheet then place the potatoes on top, cut side-down
5. Roast until deeply golden brown, about 20 minutes
6. Remove from the oven and flip them over with a spatula
7. Season with some salt and sprinkle with parmigiano and lots of black pepper
8. Return the potatoes to the oven for 5 more minutes until the cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
