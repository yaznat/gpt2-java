What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Berry Bread Pudding With Vanilla Cream Sauce

Ingredients:
• French bread
• Frozen raspberries
• Frozen blackberrie
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sugar
• Butter
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Vanilla extract
• Whipping cream
• All-purpose flour

Steps:
1. Arrange half of bread pieces in a lightly greased 11x7 inch baking dish
2. Arrange frozen berries in a single layer over bread
3. Top with remaining bread pieces
4. Whisk eggs and next 6 ingredients until smooth
5. Slowly pour egg mixture over bread, pressing down with a wooden spoon until bread absorbs mixture
6. Let stand 20 minutes
7. Bake covered at 350 for 30 minutes
8. Uncover, brush evenly with 2 tablespoons melted butter
9. Sprinkle evenly with 2 tablespoons sugar and bake 30 more minutes or until set
10. Remove from oven and let stand 30 minutes
11. Serve with vanilla cream sauce
12. Vanilla cream sauce directions
13. Stir whipping cream, sugar, and flour together in a saucepan
14. Add butter and cook stirring constantly, over medium heat until mixture is slightly thickened
15. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla
16. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
