Ingredients:
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Fresh lemon juice
• Wasabi powder
• Garlic clove
• Firm silken tofu
• Napa cabbage
• Bok choy
• Canola oil
• Shiitake mushrooms
• Toasted sesame oil

Steps:
1. Whisk soy sauce, lemon juice, wasabi powder, and garlic
2. Gently stir in tofu
3. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for 15 min, stirring occasionally
4. Place cabbage and bok choy in a large serving bowl
5. Drain the tofu, reserving the marinade
6. Heat canola oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat
7. Add mushrooms and sesame oil
8. Cook, stirring often, for 2 minute add tofu
9. Cook, stirring often, until the tofu is lightly browned, about 4 minute
10. Spoon tofu mixture over cabbage
11. Add reserved marinade to the skillet and bring to a boil, stirring
12. Pour hot marinade over the salad and toss gently to coat
13. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
