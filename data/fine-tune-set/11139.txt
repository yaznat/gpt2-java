Ingredients:
• Bread
• Eggs
• Cream cheese
• Green onions
• Parsley
• Avocado
• Tomatoes
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Toast your bread in a toaster
2. Mix the cream cheese, green onion, and parsley together
3. Cook the bacon
4. Fry the eggs
5. Spread the cream cheese mixture on the toast
6. Slice the tomato and avocado
7. Layer the tomato, avocado, bacon & egg on the toast
8. Enjoy: )<|endoftext|>
