What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Awesome Barbecued Pork Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Boneless center cut pork loin roast
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Salt and pepper
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Trim all fat from pork, cut in to fit in crock pot
2. Salt and pepper roast on both sides of meat
3. Top with sliced onions and chopped garlic
4. Cook on low for 7 hours
5. Take meat out and shred with a fork, put back in crock pot and put in as much barbecue sauce as you like
6. Cook 1 / 12 hours longer and serve on any type of sandwich roll
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
