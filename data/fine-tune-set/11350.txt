What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>100 Stroke Banana Bread

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Sugar
• Canola oil
• Egg
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Walnuts
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Mash bananas with sugar, oil, and egg
2. Add other ingredients in order listed
3. Stir 100 strokes
4. Bake in oiled loaf pan for 1 hour at 325 degrees<|endoftext|>
