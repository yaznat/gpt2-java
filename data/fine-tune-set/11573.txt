Give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>Almost No Fat Chocolate Cake

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened cocoa
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Egg whites
• Dark brown sugar
• Plain fat-free yogurt
• Vanilla extract
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Spray the bottom and sides of a 10-inch spring-form pan with nonstick cooking spray
3. Dust the pan with 1 tablespoon cocoa and tap out the excess
4. To prepare batter: in a medium bowl, sift together 1 / 3 cup plus 1 tablespoon cocoa, flour, baking power and baking soda
5. Set aside
6. In a large bowl, with an electric mixer, beat egg whites, brown sugar, yogurt and vanilla until blended, about 1 to 2 minutes on medium speed
7. Mix in dry ingredients by hand or on low mixer speed until just moistened
8. Do not over-mix
9. Pour batter into prepared pan and bake for about 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
10. Cool in the pan on a wire rack for 15 minutes
11. Then use a knife to loosen the sides of the cake from the pan
12. Remove the ring and cool completely on a rack
13. Serve the cake on the metal bottom of the pan
14. To prepare the topping: in a small bowl, combine the remaining tablespoon of cocoa with the powdered sugar
15. Place the mixture in a small fine sieve and dust the top of cake before serving<|endoftext|>
