Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Brownie Cheesecake Cherry Bars

Ingredients:
• Fudge brownie mix
• Vegetable oil
• Water
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Egg
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Cream cheese
• Cherry pie filling

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Grease bottom of a 13x9 inch pan
3. Brownie layer: combine brownie mix, oil, water and eggs in a large bowl just until all dry mix is moistened
4. Spread in greased pan
5. Bake for 20 minutes
6. Cheesecake layer: beat vanilla, egg, milk and cream cheese in large bowl until smooth
7. Pour over baked brownie layer
8. Bake until topping is set, about 25 minutes
9. Cool 2 hours
10. Cherry layer: spread pie filling over bars
11. Cut into 1 1 / 2 or 2-inch squares
12. Store in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
