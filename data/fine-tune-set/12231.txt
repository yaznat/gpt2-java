Ingredients:
• Mushrooms
• Margarine
• Garlic cloves
• Dry-roasted cashews
• Water
• Vegetable bouillon cubes
• Parsley
• Chili powder
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. In a 4 quart pot, saut the mushrooms in butter till tender
2. Add garlic
3. Saut on low heat 5 minutes
4. In a blender, pure the cashews with the water until very smooth
5. Add to the mushrooms
6. Add the bouillon and cook on low heat, stirring frequently, until the soup begins to thicken
7. Add parsley and nutmeg
8. Serve<|endoftext|>
