Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Another Eggplant Stew

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Eggplant
• Baby carrots
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Diced tomatoes
• Bay leaf
• Thyme
• Rosemary
• Salt
• Paprika

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large skillet, and saute onions for 2 minutes
2. Add eggplant and carrots and saute for 5 more minutes, stirring frequently
3. Add remaining ingredients and cook, covered, on medium-low heat for 5 minutes, stirring often
4. Vegetables should be tender, but not mushy<|endoftext|>
