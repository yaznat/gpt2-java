What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Pie Bars

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Icing sugar
• Flour
• White sugar
• Eggs
• Almond extract
• Light corn syrup
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Blend together the ingredients for the base
2. Use enough flour so the mixture is crumbly but not dusty
3. Press into 9x13 pan and bake at 350 degrees for 10-15 minutes until golden
4. Beat together the ingredients for the topping and spread evenly over the base
5. Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes until deeply golden and set
6. Chill completely before cutting
7. This keeps best in the refrigerator<|endoftext|>
