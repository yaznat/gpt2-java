Give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>Ali S Chicken And Sausage Gumbo

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Smoked sausage
• Zucchini
• Carrots
• Green pepper
• Celery
• Crushed tomatoes
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Chicken broth
• Cajun spices
• Marjoram
• Basil

Steps:
1. Cook chicken and dice
2. Slice the smoked sausage into medium thick quarters, refrigerate
3. Refrigerate zucchini
4. Combine the rest of the ingredients into crock pot, set on low for six hours
5. After six hours, add smoked sausage and zucchini
6. Continue cooking for another two hours
7. Serve over hot rice in bowls
8. Chopped fresh cucumber can be added to individual servings to combat the heat, if desired<|endoftext|>
