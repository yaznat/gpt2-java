Ingredients:
• Paprika
• Black peppercorns
• Cumin
• Coriander seed
• Cassia
• Clove
• Green cardamom seeds
• Nutmeg
• Sirloin steaks

Steps:
1. Place all spices in mortar and pestle and pound until fine powder
2. Combine oil and 2 tablespoons spice rub in a bowl
3. Brush over both sides of steaks
4. Cook on preheated flat plate on your barbecue for 2 minutes each side or until cooked to your liking<|endoftext|>
