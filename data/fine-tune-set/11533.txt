Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• Brown sugar
• Hot water

Steps:
1. Combine flour, sugar, 2 t cocoa, baking powder, and salt
2. Stir in milk, oil, and vanilla until smooth
3. Spread in an ungreased 9" square pan
4. Combine brown sugar and remaining 4 t cocoa
5. Sprinkle over batter
6. Pour hot water over all
7. Do not stir
8. Bake@ 350 for 35-40 min
9. Serve warm or cold with vanilla ice cream
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
