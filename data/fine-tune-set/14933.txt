Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Fried Rice With Smoked Ham 9 Ww Pts

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Egg
• Onions
• Garlic
• Smoked ham
• Brown basmati rice
• Frozen green pea
• Baby corn
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Dark sesame oil
• Salt
• White pepper
• Fresh cilantro stem

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tbsp of oil in a stir fry pan or wok over medium heat
2. Add egg, stir fry 30 seconds or until egg is done
3. Remove egg mixture from skillet
4. Add 1 tbsp oil to pan, add onion, stir fry 3 minutes
5. Add garlic, stir fry 1 minute
6. Increase heat to medium high, add ham and stir fry 3 minutes
7. Add rice, peas, and corn stir fry 3 minutes
8. Add egg mixture, soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, and pepper, stir fry 1 minute or until thoroughly heated
9. Garnish with cilantro
10. Serving size: 1 1 / 2 cups<|endoftext|>
