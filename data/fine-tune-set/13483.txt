Please give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Apple Wine Soup With Roast Beef

Ingredients:
• Beef stock
• Carrots
• Onion
• Bay leaf
• Cloves
• Wine
• Butter
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Apple
• Roast beef

Steps:
1. Spike the onion with the bay leaf and cloves
2. Bring stock to boil, reduce heat and add carrots and spiked onion
3. Simmer for about 5 minutes
4. Add apple wine and simmer
5. Blend softened butter and flour with a fork, stir gradually into soup
6. Simmer for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Remove onion and season to taste with salt and pepper
8. Add apple slices and cook for a few minutes more
9. Transfer to deep plates / soup bowls
10. Roll sliced beef into garnish for top of soup and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
