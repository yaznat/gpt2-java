Give me a sushi recipe.<|endoftext|>Tampa Sushi Rolls

Ingredients:
• Sushi rice
• Grouper
• Tempura batter
• Nori
• Onions
• Mayonnaise
• Toasted sesame seeds
• Oil

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a deep fryer to 350 degrees
2. Dredge grouper in tempura flour then dip in tempura batter and deep fry for 1 1 / 2 mins, set aside
3. Spread sushi rice out on bamboo mat about 8"x 8" square
4. Toast nori over an open flame, just til crispy, lay sheet over rice
5. Spread mayo on bottom edge of nori sheet, add grouper and onion slivers
6. Start at bottom and roll, rice should be on the outside if done right
7. Cut into 8 pieces, lay flat and sprinkle sesame seeds on top
8. Serve with japanese soy sauce, wasabi and pickled ginger
9. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
