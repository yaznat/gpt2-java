Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Blue Ribbon Cheese Cake Brownies

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Sugar
• Flour
• Milk chocolate chips
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Dark brown sugar
• Eggs
• Salt

Steps:
1. For cheese cake topping:
2. In a small bowl, stir together all the ingredients for topping and set aside
3. For brownie:
4. Preheat oven to 350f
5. Grease a 9x13-inch glass pan
6. In a small bowl, melt the chocolate chips with the olive oil in the microwave, set aside
7. In a medium mixing bowl, beat the butter and sugar until light and fluffy
8. Slowly beat in chocolate
9. Add vanilla and 1 egg at a time until combined
10. Add flour and beat until just combined, do not over mix
11. Pour brownie mix into pan and spread out evenly
12. Spoon cheese cake topping over and spread out
13. With a knife, make little swirls in the batters, not to really combined them, but to just make swirly patterns on the top
14. Bake for 20-30 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out with crumbs on it<|endoftext|>
