Ingredients:
• Onion
• Slivered almonds
• Butter
• Chicken broth
• Instant rice

Steps:
1. Saute the onions in the butter until the onion is translucent, apprx 7 minutes add broth
2. Bring to a boil
3. Stir in instant rice, cover and let stand 5-8 minutes
4. Stir in the browned almonds& serve<|endoftext|>
