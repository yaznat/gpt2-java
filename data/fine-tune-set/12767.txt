Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Fresh parsley
• Garlic
• Tomato sauce
• Halibut steaks
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375-degrees f
2. Spray 9x13-inch baking dish with non stick cooking spray
3. Heat oil in skillet over medium heat
4. Add onions and cook for 8- 10 minutes
5. Add green onions, parsley, garlic and tomato sauce
6. Continue cooking over medium low for 5 minutes
7. Season fish with salt and pepper
8. Place in prepared pan
9. Pour sauce over fish
10. Bake for 20-30 minutes or until fish is cooked through<|endoftext|>
