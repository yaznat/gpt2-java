Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Chapli Kebab Spiced Lamb Patties

Ingredients:
• Scallions
• Onion
• Tomatoes
• Ground lamb
• Coriander
• Pomegranate molasses
• Red pepper flakes
• Pepper
• Salt
• Cilantro
• Green chilies
• Garlic cloves
• Flour
• Eggs
• Canola oil
• Pomegranate seeds

Steps:
1. In the center of a large kitchen towel combine first 3 ingredients
2. Squeeze gently over the sink to remove excess water and then place vegetables in a large bowl
3. Stir in next 12 ingredients
4. Mix well, cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours
5. Form meat into 12 patties
6. Slice tomatoes into 12 slices and then press 1 tomato slice into the top of each patty
7. Heat the oven to broil
8. In and oven-safe skillet over medium high heat heat 1 tablespoon of the oil until hot
9. Working in batches, slice patties into pan with tomato side up and cook for 2 minutes and then flip patties and place skillet under broiler for 4-5 minutes or until cooked through
10. Transfer patties to a paper towel lined plate
11. Cover with foil and keep warm while cooking remaining patties, using additional oil per batch if needed
12. To serve, place kebabs on a platter and sprinkle with additional cilantro and pomegranate seeds, if desired<|endoftext|>
