Ingredients:
• Crescent rolls
• Corn chips
• Ground beef
• Tomato paste
• Water
• Taco seasoning
• Sour cream
• Monterey jack and cheddar cheese blend

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. In a large skillet cook ground beef until no pink in meat
3. Add tomato paste and use the 6 oz
4. Can to add 1 1 / 2 cans water, also add taco seasoning and simmer on low, uncovered for 5 minutes
5. Grease pan, roll crescent rolls into 9x13 pan up the edges
6. Seal seams
7. It will be long one continuous piece in the pan
8. Spread one cup of the crushed corn tortilla chips on top of the crescent rolls
9. Then spread meat, add sour cream on top of meat, then cheese
10. Last, spread remaining cup of chips on top
11. Bake uncovered for 25-30 minutes, crust will be lightly browned
12. I leave sour cream and cheese off a small portion for my son who does not like cheese or creamy
13. Can make vegetarian by using veggie crumble instead of ground beef<|endoftext|>
