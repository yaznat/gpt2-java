What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Lamb Meatball And Bean Crock Pot Soup

Ingredients:
• Ground lamb
• Salt
• Ground cumin
• Garlic clove
• Onion
• Chicken broth
• Low-sodium tomatoes
• Garbanzo beans
• Frozen chopped broccoli
• Dried thyme leaves
• Salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. In medium bowl, combine lamb, onion, garlic, cumin, and salt
2. Mix lightly
3. Shape into 1-inch balls
4. Brown meatballs in large skillet over medium-high heat, turning occasionally
5. Place broth, tomatoes, beans, broccoli, thyme, and meatballs in slow cooker
6. Cook on low for 4-5 hours
7. Season with salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
