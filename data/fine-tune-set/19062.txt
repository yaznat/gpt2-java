What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Vice Cream

Ingredients:
• Apple juice
• Raw cashews
• Maple syrup
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Blend all ingredients in the blender for at least 1-2 minutes
2. You need to get the mixture very smooth
3. Place the blender in the freezer for an hour, or the fridge for a couple of hours or overnight, till well chilled
4. Pour the mixture into an ice cream maker and process according to directions till it reaches a very thick but soft-serve consistency
5. Serve immediately, or store in the freezer for later use
6. You will need to let any leftovers thaw for ten minutes or so before serving<|endoftext|>
