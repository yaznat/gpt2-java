What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>16 Hour Pulled Pork

Ingredients:
• Pork butt
• Onions
• Ginger ale
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Slice one onion and cover the bottom of the crockpot with it
2. Put the roast on top of the onions
3. Slice the other onion and put it on top of the roast
4. Pour the ginger ale inches cover and cook on low for 12 hours
5. Remove the meat, strain and save the onions, discard all liquid
6. With two forks, shred the meat, discarding any remaining fat, bones or skin
7. Most of the fat will have melted away
8. Return the shredded meat and the onions to the crockpot, stir in the barbecue sauce
9. Continue to cook for another 4 hours on low<|endoftext|>
