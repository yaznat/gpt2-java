Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Apple juice
• Oil
• Amaranth flour
• Tapioca flour
• Arrowroot
• Cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Sea salt

Steps:
1. Beat egg, add juice or milk, and oil
2. Mix dry ingredients together in separate bowl
3. Add to egg mixture a little at a time, mixing well after each addition
4. Heat griddle or shallow pan to medium heat and cook until golden brown on both sides
5. I often add extra cinnamon after i pour them in the pan<|endoftext|>
