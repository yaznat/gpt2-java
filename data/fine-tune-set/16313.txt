Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Pitted dates
• Parmigiano-reggiano cheese

Steps:
1. Cut bacon in half crosswise
2. Cook bacon in a skillet to get most of fat out, then drain on paper towel
3. Cut cheese in small rectangles to slide into dates
4. Slide cheese into open end of pitted dates
5. Wrap cheese filled date in 1 / 2 piece of bacon and secure with a toothpick
6. Cook at 400 for 15 minutes until bacon is crisp<|endoftext|>
