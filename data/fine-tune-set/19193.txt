Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Cherry And Dark Chocolate Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Heavy cream
• Egg
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Fresh cherries
• Dark chocolate

Steps:
1. Whisk egg and yolks until foamy
2. Whisk in sugar slowly
3. Add cream, vanilla and almond extract
4. Pour into ice cream maker and freeze to instructions
5. Add chocolate and cherries in the last 2 minutes
6. Freeze to harden or eat right from the machine, my favorite way!<|endoftext|>
