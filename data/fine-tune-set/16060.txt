What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Campbell S Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Campbell's cheddar cheese soup
• Salsa
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425
2. Cook chicken until no longer pink, add soup and salsa and heat through
3. Spread about 1 / 3 cup soup mixture on half of each tortilla to within 1 / 2" of edge
4. Moisten edge with water
5. Fold over and seal
6. Place on 2 baking sheets
7. Bake 5 minute or until hot<|endoftext|>
