Ingredients:
• Mangoes
• Oranges
• Vanilla bean
• Strawberries
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Peel mangoes and remove as much flesh around pit as you can
2. Peel oranges
3. If you have a good blender, don't worry about the orange seeds, they'll get blended up just fine, as well as the vanilla bean
4. Put all ingredients in blender, blend to desired consistency and consume!
5. Makes 3 large servings<|endoftext|>
