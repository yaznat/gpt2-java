Please give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>African Rice Balls

Ingredients:
• Basmati rice
• Water

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, bring the water to a boil and add rice
2. Simmer for 10 minutes or until the rice is really soft and has absorbed all the water
3. With a wooden spoon, mash the rice until it sticks together
4. Divide the rice into four equal portions then, using a clean cloth to protect your hands, or two large serving spoons, mould each portion into a ball<|endoftext|>
