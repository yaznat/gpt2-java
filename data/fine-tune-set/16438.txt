What's a recipe for a wrap?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Chicken Wraps

Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Bacon
• Flour tortillas
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Coleslaw

Steps:
1. In 1-quart saucepan, cook barbecue sauce with shredded chicken and bacon over medium heat for 5 to 10 minutes or until thoroughly heated, stirring occasionally
2. Spoon 1 / 2 cup chicken mixture down center of each warm tortilla
3. Top each with cheese
4. Spoon coleslaw down sides of each
5. Fold up bottom of each tortilla
6. Fold in sides
7. If desired, enclose bottom of wraps in foil or waxed paper
