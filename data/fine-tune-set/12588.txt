Please give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Beaufort Stew Low Country Boil

Ingredients:
• Kielbasa
• Baby sweet corn cobs
• Shrimp
• Tiny new potatoes
• Old bay seasoning
• Seafood cocktail sauce
• Butter

Steps:
1. Bring a large stock pot of water to a boil
2. Add old bay, potatoes and sausage
3. Cook 15 minutes maintaining the boil
4. Add corn and shrimp, returning liquid to a boil
5. Cook for 5 minutes
6. Dump into large colander to drain
7. Transfer to large serving platter and serve with plenty of napkins and condiments<|endoftext|>
