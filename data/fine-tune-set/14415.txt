What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Al Dente Green Beans In Soy Garlic Sauce

Ingredients:
• Fresh green beans
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic
• Soy sauce
• Balsamic vinegar
• Greek salad dressing
• Sugar
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a 2 - 3 quart sauce pan, cook the green beans in well salted water until a little past crisp
2. We like them al dente, not mushy and not crispy
3. Drain and let rest in colander a minute
4. In the saucepan, add the olive oil and garlic and sautee one to two minutes
5. Add remaining ingredients and heat throughly
6. Toss with green beans
7. These are definitely better the next day after marinating so you might do well to make tons and eat the majority the next day<|endoftext|>
