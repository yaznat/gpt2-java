What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Jiffy Deluxe Pizza Sticks

Ingredients:
• Pizza crust mix
• Garlic powder
• Tap water
• Pepperoni slices
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Margarine

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees, grease one baking sheet
2. Mix pizza crust mix, garlic powder, pepperoni and mozzarella cheese
3. Add hot water and mix well
4. Spread parmesan cheese onto greased baking sheet
5. Drop dough onto parmesan cheese and knead dough into cheese to reduce stickiness
6. Roll dough out on same pan, to 1 / 2 inch thickness
7. Using a pizza slicer, cut dough into 1 inch strips, but don't separate
8. Bake 15-18 minutes and remove from oven
9. Mix topping ingredients and spread over hot breadsticks<|endoftext|>
