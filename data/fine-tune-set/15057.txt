Generate a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>3 C S And A P Pizza

Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• Prosciutto
• Roasted red pepper
• Alfredo sauce
• Pizza sauce
• Fresh basil
• Mozzarella cheese
• Provolone cheese
• Gouda cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Press pizza dough into 15x10 inch rimmed baking pan
3. When is oven is ready, cook pizza dough for 5 minutes
4. When done, let rest for 2 minutes or so
5. Meanwhile, lay proscuitto on a foil-lined baking sheet and put on lower rack in 400 degree oven
6. Leave in oven for 5-7 minutes, until crispy
7. When done, remove from oven and chop or crumble
8. Combine alfredo and pizza sauces and spread evenly over pizza crust to the edges
9. Chiffonade the basil and spread over sauce
10. Spread proscuitto over pizza, then diced pepper
11. Evenly distribute shredded cheeses over all
12. Put back in oven and cook for 10-15 minutes until browned and bubbly
13. Let rest for a couple of minutes before slicing into 8 slices<|endoftext|>
