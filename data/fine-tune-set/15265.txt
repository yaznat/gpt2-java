Please give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Brownie Pizza

Ingredients:
• Mini chocolate chip
• Flaked coconut
• Pecans
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Reduced fat margarine
• Fat-free sweetened condensed milk
• Baking mix
• Egg substitute
• Vanilla extract
• Confectioners' sugar
• Creamy peanut butter
• Nonfat milk

Steps:
1. Coat a 12-inch pizza pan with nonstick cooking spray
2. Set aside
3. In a small bowl, combine chocolate chips, coconut and nuts
4. Set aside
5. In a saucepan, melt the chocolate chips in the margarine over medium-low to low heat
6. Cool slightly
7. Meanwhile, in a large bowl, combine the milk, baking mix, egg substitute and 1 teaspoon of the vanilla extract
8. Fold in the chocolate mixture
9. Blend until smooth
10. Spread mixture onto prepared pan
11. Bake at 375 for 20-25 minutes until center is set
12. Allow to cool completely on wire rack
13. In a small bowl, beat sugar, peanut butter, remaining vanilla extract and milk
14. Spread over cooled brownie crust
15. Top with chocolate chips, coconut and pecans
16. Slice into wedges<|endoftext|>
