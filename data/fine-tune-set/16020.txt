Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Red onion
• Chicken breasts
• Barbecue sauce
• Mexican blend cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Mix chicken and bbq sauce
3. Layer all ingredients over tortilla top with other tortilla
4. Place in oven for 7 minutes or until cheese is melted
5. Serve with sour cream, salsa and guacamole<|endoftext|>
