Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Leeks
• Garlic cloves
• Chicken stock
• Butter beans
• Self-raising flour
• Fennel seeds
• Olive oil
• Warm water

Steps:
1. Heat a large heavy based saucepan over a medium heat
2. Add bacon, leek and garlic and stir to combine
3. Cover and cook over a low heat for 8 - 10 minutes
4. Add bacon bones and stock to the pan
5. Bring slowly to the boil, reduce heat and simmer covered for 1 hour
6. Remove bones, scrape meat from them and return meat to the pan
7. Add beans, leave soup and prepard dumplings
8. To make dumplings:
9. Place flour in a medium bowl
10. Stir through fennel seeds
11. Make a well in the centre
12. Add oil and water, then stir with a table knife until a soft dough is formed
13. Place large spoonfuls of dough on top of the simmering soup
14. Cover and cook for 10 minutes or until dumplings are well risen and cooked through, then serve immediately<|endoftext|>
