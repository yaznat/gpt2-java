Ingredients:
• Onion
• Butter
• Chicken stock
• Potatoes
• Fiddleheads
• Bay leaf
• Salt and pepper
• Milk
• Yogurt
• Lemon rind

Steps:
1. In a large heavy bottom saucepan, saut onion in butter or oil until soft but not brown, about 5 minutes
2. Add potatoes, fiddleheads, bay leaf, and enough chicken stock to make the vegetables just swim
3. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and barely simmer, partially covered, for one hour
4. Remove from heat and cool slightly
5. Remove bay leaf
6. In a blender, process mixture in batches
7. Return mixture to saucepan and slowly bring to a simmer, stirring frequently
8. Add milk, a little at a time, until it reaches the consistency you like
9. Add salt and pepper to taste
10. Serve in heated soup bowls, garnish as desired<|endoftext|>
