What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Floridanative S Pinto Bean Burritos

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Pinto beans
• Green chilies
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Low-fat cheese
• Salsa
• Tortillas
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. In a large sauce pan over medium heat, saute onion and garlic in oil until soft
3. Add beans, chilies and seasonings
4. Mix and mash the beans somewhat to the desired consistency
5. Remove from heat
6. Spread beans, cheese and salsa in the middle of each tortilla
7. Roll up tortillas and place in 7 x 11 or 9 x 13 ovenproof casserole dish, lightly sprayed with oil
8. Cover with foil and bake at 350f for 15 minutes
9. Serve<|endoftext|>
