What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Cabo Wabo Fish Tacos

Ingredients:
• Fresh lime juice
• Tequila
• Mexican oregano
• Fresh garlic
• Onion powder
• Ground cumin
• Fresh cilantro
• Yellowfin tuna fillet
• Corn tortillas
• Cabbage
• Lime
• Chipotle chiles
• Feta

Steps:
1. Mix together lime juice, tequila, mexican oregano, minced garlic, onion powder, ground cumin, and 1 tablespoon of fresh cilantro in a small bowl
2. Place tuna fillet in a ziploc bag and pour marinade over fish
3. Remove excess air from bag and seal
4. Refrigerate for one hour, while you prepare your grill
5. Prepare grill by spraying grill surface with non-stick cooking spray before applying heat
6. Over medium-hot grill cook tuna, turning once, until cooked through
7. Set aside
8. Briefly heat corn tortillas carefully on grill
9. Do not overcook, or they will get hard
10. Break tuna into small chunks or shredded pieces and place about 1 1 / 2 ounces on a double layer of corn tortilla for each taco
11. Add shredded cabbage and chopped cilantro and squeeze a wedge of lime over the taco contents
12. If you wish a spicier taste, add the chilies
13. Serve<|endoftext|>
