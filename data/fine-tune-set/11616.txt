What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>22 Minute Cake

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Butter
• Water
• Shortening
• Salt
• Cocoa
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Baking soda
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon
• Milk
• Powdered sugar
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Do not use a mixer
2. Combine flour and sugar in a large bowl
3. In saucepan combine butter, water, shortening, cocoa and salt
4. Bring to a boil
5. Pour over flour mixture
6. Combine buttermilk, eggs, soda, vanilla and cinnamon
7. Add to the other mixture and mix all together
8. Pour batter into a 9 x 12 greased pan
9. Bake 22 minutes at 400 degrees fahrenheit
10. When cake has baked 18 minutes, start combining in large bowl the 1 / 2 cup butter, 4 tablespoons cocoa, and 1 / 3 cup milk
11. Bring to a boil and while boiling stir in 1 pound powdered sugar and 1 cup chopped nuts
12. Do not remove from heat while stirring in sugar
13. Pour over hot cake as soon as it is removed from oven<|endoftext|>
