Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Wasabi powder
• Garlic powder
• Tuna steaks
• Green beans
• Sesame oil
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Mix soy sauce, wasabi powder and garlic powder in 8x8x2-inch glass dish
2. Add tuna
3. Turn several times to coat with marinade
4. Let stand 15 minutes
5. Meanwhile, cook beans in large pot of boiling salted water until crisp-tender, about 4 minutes
6. Drain well
7. Heat sesame oil in heavy large skillet over medium-high heat
8. Remove tuna from marinade
9. Reserve marinade
10. Sprinkle tuna on all sides with pepper
11. Add tuna to skillet and cook until just opaque in center, about 3 minutes per side
12. Transfer tuna to plates
13. Add green beans, sugar and reserved marinade to skillet
14. Cook until sauce is reduced enough to coat beans, stirring occasionally, about 4 minutes
15. Using tongs, place beans alongside tuna and serve<|endoftext|>
