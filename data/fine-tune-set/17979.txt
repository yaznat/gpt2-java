Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Blond Brownies

Ingredients:
• Light brown sugar
• Self rising flour
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• Semisweet chocolate morsel
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Spray 9 x 13 pan with cooking spray
2. Preheat oven to 350f
3. In a large bowl mix all ingredients together
4. Batter will be stiff
5. Spread in prepared pan
6. Bake 30 minutes
7. Cool and cut into 1-inch squares<|endoftext|>
