Give me a soup recipe.<|endoftext|>Alotizzip Eggplant Soup

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Eggplant
• Yellow onion
• Celery ribs
• Carrots
• Elbow macaroni
• Parsley
• Bay leaf
• Basil
• Fresh garlic
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Place ground beef in soup pot, add garlic and onion, and cook until beef is browned
2. Chop remaining vegetables to desired size while waiting for meat to cook
3. Spoon meat fat out of pot if necessary and add diced tomatoes and tomato sauce
4. Add veggies and all seasonings
5. Add just enough water to cover everything in the pot
6. Simmer, don't boil, 'til carrots are tender but still a little crisp
7. Add noodles and continue to simmer 'til they're cooked
8. Remove bay leaf
9. Serve with romono cheese if desired<|endoftext|>
