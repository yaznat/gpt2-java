Please give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Apple Custard Bars

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Butter
• Tart apples
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Evaporated milk
• Eggs

Steps:
1. For crust:
2. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
3. Combine flour and salt
4. Cut in butter until crumbly
5. Press into bottom and up sides of a 13"x9"x2" cake pan
6. Put in apples
7. Combine 2 / 3 cup sugar with cinnamon
8. Sprinkle over apples
9. Bake for 20 minute
10. Remove from oven
11. For custard:
12. Whisk milk, eggs, and rest of sugar until smooth
13. Pour over apples
14. Bake 25-30 minutes longer or until knife in center comes out clean
15. If you're worried the crust will be too browned, whatever is showing up above the custard, just put foil all the way around the edges<|endoftext|>
