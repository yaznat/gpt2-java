Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>Alice S Pistachio Streusel Cake No Frost

Ingredients:
• Yellow cake mix
• Instant pistachio pudding mix
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Vegetable oil
• Cinnamon
• Sugar
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Make cake batter by beating together cake mix, pudding, eggs, sour cream and oil
2. In ungreased bundt cake pan, pour in 1 / 3 of the batter, then layer 1 / 2 of the nut mixture, another 1 / 3 batter, remaining nut mixture, then rest of batter
3. After cake is assembled, place in oven, turn on oven to 350 dg
4. And bake for 50-60 minutes until cake is done and knife inserted in cake comes out clean
5. Serve and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
