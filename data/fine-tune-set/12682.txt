Ingredients:
• Skirt steak
• Sweet onion
• Celery ribs
• Green onions
• Garlic cloves
• Canola oil
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Szechuan sauce
• Hot chili sauce
• Sake
• Hoisin sauce
• Sesame oil
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Partially freeze beef and slice thinly against the grain
2. Toss with corn starch, add soy, and marinate for 15 to 30 minutes
3. Thinly slice onion, dice celery and mince garlic
4. Combine all ingredients for sauce
5. Heat wok over high heat until almost smoking
6. Add pepper flakes if desired and oil
7. Cook beef at a time until meat is medium, remove and set aside
8. Add onion, celery to hot wok and stir-fry for 30 seconds, then add garlic and beef
9. Toss together
10. Add sauce and cook for 1 2 minutes
11. Garnish with chopped green onion<|endoftext|>
