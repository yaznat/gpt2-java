Ingredients:
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Unsalted butter
• Baking powder
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Milk
• Frozen blackberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Butter an 8-inch square pan
3. Set aside
4. For the topping, in a large bowl, stir together the flour, sugar, and cinnamon
5. Using a pastry cutter or your finger, cut in the butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs
6. Refrigerate until ready to use
7. For the cake
8. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, ginger and salt
9. In a large mixing bowl, cream the butter with an electric mixer on medium speed
10. Add the sugar
11. Beat until light and fluffy
12. Beat in the egg and vanilla
13. Alternately beat in the flour mixture and the milk until just combined
14. Spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan
15. Scatter the blackberries over the batter
16. Sprinkle with the streusel topping
17. Bake for 45 to 60 minuets or until a toothpick inserted in the center of the cake comes out clean
18. Transfer to wire rack to cool slightly before serving<|endoftext|>
