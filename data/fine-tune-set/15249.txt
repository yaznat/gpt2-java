What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Breakfast Pizza From Smitten Kitchen

Ingredients:
• Prepared pizza crust
• Kosher salt
• Bacon
• Parmesan cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Eggs
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Flat leaf parsley
• Chives
• Scallion
• Shallot

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 500 degrees 30 mins before you are ready to bake the pizza
2. Fry the bacon in a large saute pan over medium heat until crisp
3. Cool on a paper-towel-lined plate
4. Roughly chop
5. Sprinkle the dough with half of the parmesan, mozzarella and bacon
6. Make divots in the toppings and crack 3 eggs into the divots and season with salt and pepper
7. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the egg yolks are cooked and cheese is melted
8. Remove from oven and sprinkle parsley, chives, scallions and shallot on top
9. Let cool for 2 minutes, slice and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
