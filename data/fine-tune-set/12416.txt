What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Style Chicken And Corn Soup Ww Core

Ingredients:
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Skinless chicken breasts
• Onion
• Carrot
• Celery
• Saffron
• Frozen corn
• Whole wheat egg noodles
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Add the broth, chicken, onion, chopped celery and saffron to a large saucepan
2. Bring to a boil
3. Cover and reduce to a simmer for 30 minutes
4. During simmering time, you may want to gently skim any foam that comes to the top of the broth
5. Take the chicken from the pan and reserve to the side while it cools
6. Strain broth removing all bits
7. Return to the sauce pan
8. Take the now cooked chicken, remove the bones and dice into small inch cubes
9. Add to the broth along with the cooked noodles, corn, celery and parsley
10. Heat the soup until all ingredients are hot
11. Serve<|endoftext|>
