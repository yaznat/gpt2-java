What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Baked Chili Red Chicken Strips

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Paprika
• Chili peppers
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Milk
• Pam cooking spray

Steps:
1. Cut chicken strips across the grain in thin slices
2. Set aside
3. In medium mixing bowl, add eggs, oil, chili powder, ground cumin, salt and paprika
4. Add optional diced peppers if desired
5. Whisk until mixed
6. In separate bowl mix flour and baking powder
7. Add into egg mixture and add milk
8. Mix with spoon
9. Batter will be sticky and texture like peanut butter
10. Preheat oven 400
11. Spray pam on baking pan
12. Coat each slice or strip with batter
13. Place on pan
14. Bake 12 minutes
15. Take out of oven
16. Turn the strips over
17. Bake 5 more minutes
18. They will be flat on one side
19. Serve with puffy side up<|endoftext|>
