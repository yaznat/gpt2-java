Ingredients:
• Unbleached flour
• Cornstarch
• Unsalted butter
• Confectioners' sugar
• Fresh lime juice
• Lime zest
• Lime oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. In a medium bowl combine the flour and cornstarch and blend well
3. Set aside
4. In a larger mixing bowl, using electric mixer, beat the butter and 1 / 2 cup confectioner's sugar until light and fluffy
5. Mix in the fresh lime juice, lime zest, and lime oil if using
6. Beat in the flour mixture until smooth
7. Refrigerate cookie dough until just firm, approximately 45 minutes
8. Line two aluminum baking sheets with parchment paper
9. Butter a measuring spoon and measure out a scant 1 tablespoon of the dough
10. Form into balls and place on the prepared baking sheets
11. Space the cookies 1" apart
12. Bake cookies for about 23 minutes or until browned on bottom and golden brown on top
13. Transfer the baking sheets to counter, and immediately sift with the remaining confectioner's sugar
14. Cool the cookies completely on baking sheet
