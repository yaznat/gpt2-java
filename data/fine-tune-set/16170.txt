Can you give me a quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Fruit Filled Dessert Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Butter
• Confectioners' sugar
• Comstock brand pie filling
• Low-fat cream cheese

Steps:
1. Lightly brush one side of a tortilla with butter
2. Spread the tortilla with a thin layer of cream cheese, if desired
3. Spread about 1 / 4 cup pie filling on one-half of the tortilla, then fold the tortilla in half, pressing slightly to seal the edges
4. In a large skillet on medium-low heat, cook the tortilla until lightly browned
5. Turn and brown the other side
6. To serve, dust with confectioners' sugar or cinnamon-sugar
7. Each quesadilla may be served whole or cut into 3-4 wedges before serving
8. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
