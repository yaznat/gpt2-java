Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Bread Ii

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Oil
• Active dry yeast
• Sugar
• Salt
• Warm water

Steps:
1. Proof the yeast in 3 / 4 cup warm water and 1 / 4 cup sugar
2. Add the rest of the ingredients once the yeast is lively
3. Mix well
4. Turn the batter out on a floured surface and knead thoroughly, about 10 minutes
5. Coat a bowl with oil or butter
6. Put the dough in the oiled bowl and allow it to rise until it has doubled in bulk
7. Punch the dough down and shape into bread pan
8. Allow it to rise again
9. The second rise usually only takes about 1 hour
10. Bake for 25 - 30 minutes in a 350f oven<|endoftext|>
