What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Amaretto Florentine Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Quick oats
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Almonds
• Corn syrup
• Amaretto
• Salt
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f foil-line cookie sheets
2. In medium saucepan, melt butter
3. Remove from heat
4. Stir in quick oats, sugar, flour, almonds, corn syrup, celsius amaretto and salt
5. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls 3 inches apart onto prepared cookie sheets
6. Spread each to make thin
7. Bake 8-11 minutes, until golden
8. Cool completely on cookie sheets
9. Peel off foil
10. Spread filling on flat side of the cookies
11. Top with remaining cookies
12. Makes 2-1 / 2 dozen
13. Filling
14. In medium heavy gauge saucepan over low heat, melt butter with celsius amaretto di amore
15. Remove from heat
16. Stir in chocolate chips
17. Cool to room temperature<|endoftext|>
