What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Chilli Tofu And Noodle Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Hokkien noodles
• Olive oil flavored cooking spray
• Firm tofu
• Minced ginger
• Garlic
• Onion
• Red capsicum
• Zucchini
• Carrot
• Baby corn
• Broccolini
• Soy sauce
• Chili sauce
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Place noodles in a large heatproof bowl
2. Cover with boiling water and leave to stand 2 minutes, using a wooden spoon to gently separate
3. Spray a non-stick wok or frying pan with oil
4. Cook tofu in 2 or 3 batches over high heat until browned
5. Set aside
6. Reheat wok
7. Add ginger, garlic, and onion
8. Stir-fry for 2 minutes until soft
9. Add remaining vegetables and stir-fry for 3 minutes or until tender but still crisp
10. Add drained noodles, tofu, and combined sauces to wok
11. Stir-fry for 2 minutes or until heated through
12. Toss with sesame seeds and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
