Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>7 Layer Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Shredded coconut
• Butterscotch chips
• Chocolate chips
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Melt 1 stick butter in a jelly roll pan
2. Spread in pan - 1 cup of graham cracker crumbs
3. 1 cup of shredded coconut
4. 6 oz of butterscotch chips
5. 6 oz
6. Of chocolate chips
7. Drizzle one can of sweet condensed milk on top
8. Add 1 cup of chopped walnuts on top
9. Bake at 350f for 30 minutes
10. Remove and let cool and then cut into squares<|endoftext|>
