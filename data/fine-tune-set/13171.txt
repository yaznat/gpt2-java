Ingredients:
• Chicken pieces
• Orange marmalade
• Barbecue sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Skin chicken, if desired
2. Arrange chicken on a foil-lined 13x9x2-inch baking pan
3. Set aside
4. In a small bowl, combine marmalade, barbecue sauce, worcestershire sauce, and lemon juice
5. Pour over chicken
6. Bake, uncovered, in a 350 degree oven for 45 minutes, spooning sauce over chicken occasionally
7. Increase oven temperature to 400 degrees
8. Bake for 15 minutes more or until chicken is tender and no longer pink<|endoftext|>
