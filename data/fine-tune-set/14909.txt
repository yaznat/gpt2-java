What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Fran S Fab Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Long grain white rice
• Green onion
• Eggs
• Ham
• Garlic
• Soy sauce
• Butter
• Salt and pepper
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Put soy sauce and garlic in a frying pan, fry for approximately 1 minute
2. Add rice and cook until the liquid cooks inches
3. Add the rest of the ingredients and cook until the liquid is dissipated<|endoftext|>
