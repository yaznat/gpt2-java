What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Beef N Chili Beans

Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Brown sugar
• Ground mustard
• Salt
• Paprika
• Chili powder
• Pepper
• Onion
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Ranch style beans
• Whole kernel corn

Steps:
1. Place the beef in a 3-quart slow cooker
2. Combine the brown sugar, mustard, salt, paprika, chili powder and pepper
3. Sprinkle over beef and toss to coat
4. Top with onion, tomatoes, beans, and corn
5. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours or until meat is tender<|endoftext|>
