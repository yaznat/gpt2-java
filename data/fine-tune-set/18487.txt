Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Fruit Loop Waffles

Ingredients:
• Fruit loops cereal
• All-purpose flour
• Cornmeal
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Whole milk
• Unsalted butter
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Place all of your cereal into a food processor and pulse until they are coarse crumbs
2. Whisk together flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder and salt in a bowl and set aside
3. Beat the eggs, milk and butter together in a large bowl
4. Gently add dry ingredients into the wet and mix until just combined
5. Fold in the cereal crumbs
6. Pour the appropriate amount of batter into your waffle maker and cook until golden
7. For the glaze:
8. In a bowl, whisk together milk and powdered sugar until smooth
9. Plate the waffles with the glaze on top and some extra cereal as a garnish<|endoftext|>
