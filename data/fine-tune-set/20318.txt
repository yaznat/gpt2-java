Give me a recipe for stir fry.<|endoftext|>Chinese Chicken Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken
• Pineapple chunks
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Garlic
• Cornstarch
• Chicken broth
• Hoisin sauce
• Sweet red peppers
• Green peppers
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In resealable bag, put the chicken pieces, pineapple and juice, soy sauce, sugar, garlic, cornstarch, chicken broth and hoisin sauce
2. Shake well
3. Refrigerate for 25 minutes
4. Coat a wok with cooking spray and set to med hi heat
5. Remove the chicken and pineapple from bag with slotted spoon,
6. When wok is hot, add the chicken and pineapple
7. Cook and stir for 2 minutes
8. Add the peppers
9. Cook and stir for 3 minutes
10. Add the marinade
11. Bring to a boil
12. Cook and stir for 5 minutes
13. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
