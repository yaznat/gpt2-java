Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Corn oil
• Diced onion
• Chicken breast halves
• Tomatoes
• Cooked rice
• Powdered cumin
• Chili powder
• Bay leaf
• Oregano
• Monterey jack cheese
• Mild salsa
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a skillet
2. Saut onion, spices, oregano and bay leaf
3. Add the chicken, tomatoes, heat throughout
4. Add the rice to the chicken mixture, stir to combine and cover
5. Heat about 5 minutes or until heated throughout, don't let it stick
6. Remove from heat
7. Warm tortillas according to package
8. Add chicken and rice mixture to the center of the tortilla
9. Sprinkle with cheese and add sour cream and a little mild salsa
10. Fold the tortilla at the top and bottom then fold over one side and roll towards the other side<|endoftext|>
