What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Bangkok Beef Salad

Ingredients:
• Long grain rice
• Lean ground beef
• Shallots
• Garlic
• Lemon juice
• Fish sauce
• Soy sauce
• Fresh mint leaves
• Sugar
• Cayenne pepper
• Romaine lettuce leaf
• Bean sprouts
• Green bell pepper
• Thai red chili peppers

Steps:
1. Heat a heavy skillet over high heat
2. Add raw rice
3. Cook, stirring, and shaking pan, for 3 minutes, or until golden
4. Cool rice slightly
5. Blend to a coarse powder in a blender or spice mill
6. Stir-fry meat, shallots, and garlic till meat is browned
7. Drain off all excess fat
8. Stir in rice powder, lemon juice, fish sauce, soy sauce, mint, sugar, and cayenne pepper
9. Mound meat in the center of serving tray
10. Arrange romaine leaves and bean sprouts on either side of meat
11. Garnish with bell peppers, mint sprigs, and chili slivers
12. Serve with additional soy sauce or fish sauce, if desired
13. To eat, use romaine leaves to scoop up the meat, top with sprouts, peppers, mint, and any extra sauce<|endoftext|>
