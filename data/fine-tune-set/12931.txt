Ingredients:
• Salt-free seasoned dried breadcrumbs
• Butter
• Lemon, juice of
• Dried chives
• Scrod fillets
• Pam cooking spray

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Melt butter or margarine
3. Combine breadcrumbs, butter, lemon juice and chive in a bowl
4. Set aside
5. If you can let the mixture stay overnight in the fridge the flavor is better
6. Just warm it in the microwave a little to loosen it up
7. Place fish on individual foil sheets sprayed with pam
8. Top with the bread crumb mixture firmly packed on fish
9. Wrap foil loosely around fish, leaving a small hole in the top for steam to escape
10. Cook at 350 degrees for 15 to 20 minutes or to desired doneness<|endoftext|>
