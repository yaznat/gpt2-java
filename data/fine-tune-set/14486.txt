Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Sesame oil
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Fresh green beans
• Red onion
• Fresh mushrooms

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the first five ingredients
2. Add the beans, onion and mushrooms
3. Toss to coat
4. Place half of the vegetables on a double thickness of heavy-duty foil
5. Fold foil around vegetables and seal tightly
6. Repeat with remaining vegetables
7. Grill, covered, over medium heat for 18-22 minutes or until beans are tender, turning packets over several times to get an even coating of the sauce on all the vegetables
8. Open foil carefully to allow steam to escape<|endoftext|>
