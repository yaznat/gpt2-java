What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>My Thai Chicken Soup

Ingredients:
• Chicken stock
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Ground coriander
• Limes, juice and zest of
• Lemons, juice and zest of
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Portabella mushrooms
• Thai fish sauce
• Cherry tomatoes
• Birds eye chiles
• Baby spinach
• Fresh basil
• Lime wedges

Steps:
1. Place the stock in a saucepan
2. Add the ginger, garlic, coriander, lime zest and juice and bring to a boil over medium heat
3. Add the diced chicken, mushrooms and fish sauce
4. Simmer slowly, uncovered, for 10 minutes
5. Check and adjust seasoning
6. Add the tomatoes and chiles and cook for 5 more minutes
7. Stir in the baby spinach and cook 1 minute longer
8. Discard ginger
9. Serve, topped with shredded basil
10. Garnish with fresh lime wedges in case you want to squeeze some into your soup
11. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
