Give me a pie recipe.<|endoftext|>Aida S Single Pie Crust

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Butter
• Shortening
• Ice water

Steps:
1. In a large bowl mix together the flour, sugar, and salt
2. Cut in the butter until there are large crumbles
3. Cut in the shortening until incorporated
4. The crumbles do not have to be uniform in size
5. Add the ice water by tablespoon just until the dough can be shaped into a ball
6. Form the dough into a disk, wrap in plastic wrap and let in rest in the refrigerator
7. Roll to the desired size on a floured surface, place in pie plate, and pierce the bottom with fork tines
8. While preheating the oven, put a cookie sheet in the oven
9. Place the pie plate on this hot surface while baking to ensure a flaky bottom crust<|endoftext|>
