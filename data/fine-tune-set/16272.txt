Ingredients:
• Asparagus
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Bacon
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cut 1 / 2 inch to 1 inch off the end of the asparagus
2. The asparagus should be at least 4 to 5 inches long
3. Toss the asparagus with olive oil- just enough to lightly coat and add a touch of salt and pepper
4. Separate into bundles and starting at bottom of asparagus bundle, wrap with 1 slice of uncooked bacon
5. Repeat with the rest
6. Place on broiling pan and bake at 500 degrees for 5 minutes, turn them over and bake another 5 minutes
7. Reduce oven to 350 degrees and bake for 15- 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
