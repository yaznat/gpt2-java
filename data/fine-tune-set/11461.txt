Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Light olive oil
• Rosemary
• Butter
• Flour
• Skim milk
• Eggs
• Fresh mozzarella cheese
• Ricotta cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Dijon mustard
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. Toss potatoes with olive oil, rosemary, 1 tsp kosher salt and pepper
3. Roast potatoes on a jellyroll pan at 450 for 20 minutes until golden and crispy
4. While potatoes are roasting, melt butter in small saucepan or pot
5. Add flour to melted butter, stirring to make roux
6. A little at a time, stir in milk
7. Heat on medium until sauce thickens
8. Remove from heat
9. Add parmesan and durkees or mustard
10. Whisk eggs in bowl and add small amount of heated milk mixture to temper
11. Slowly combine tempered eggs and remaining milk mixture and whisk until smooth
12. Season well with 1 tsp kosher salt and black pepper
13. Spray loaf pan with cooking spray and place roasted potatoes in the bottom of the pan
14. Top with mozzarella and ricotta cheese
15. Pour eggs over potatoes but do not overfill
16. Bake at 375 for 25-35 minutes until puffed and lightly browned
17. Eggs will fall once removed from oven
