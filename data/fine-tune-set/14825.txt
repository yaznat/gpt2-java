Ingredients:
• Onion
• Carrot
• Frozen peas
• Garlic
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Eggs
• Soy sauce
• Butter
• Bacon
• Accent seasoning
• Black pepper
• Tomatoes
• Fish sauce

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, saute the bacon, garlic, carrot, peas and onion in butter until bacon is no longer pink
2. Remove from skillet and set aside
3. In same skillet, cook and stir egg over medium heat until completely set
4. Stir in the rice, soy sauce, accent seasoning, pepper, and bacon mixture
5. Heat through
6. Serve!
7. Tomatoes in fish sauce:
8. In a bowl, combine tomatoes, onions and fish sauce
9. Serve with spam or smoked milkfish and fried rice
10. Yummy!<|endoftext|>
