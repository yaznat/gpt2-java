Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Actual Panera Bread Recipe For Hearty Cheese Soup In A Sourdough

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Carrots
• Celery
• Green bell pepper
• Onion
• Mixed mushrooms
• Cooked ham
• All-purpose flour
• Cornstarch
• Chicken broth
• Milk
• Paprika
• Cayenne pepper
• Ground mustard
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Bread bowls

Steps:
1. In a large, heavy stockpot, melt butter or margarine
2. Add chopped vegetables
3. Add ham or bacon
4. Cook over medium heat until vegetables are slightly tender
5. Stir in flour
6. Stir in cornstarch
7. Cook, stirring constantly
8. Add broth and continue stirring until slightly thickened
9. Add milk
10. Add spices
11. Add cheese gradually, stirring until cheese is melted
12. To avoid curdling, do not allow soup to boil after cheese is added
13. Season to taste with salt and pepper
14. Serve piping hot in the hollowed out sourdough bread bowls<|endoftext|>
