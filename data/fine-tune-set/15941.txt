Ingredients:
• Onion
• Lean beef chuck roast
• Water
• Taco seasoning mix
• Flour tortillas
• Fat-free cheddar cheese
• Tomatoes
• Iceberg lettuce
• Fat free sour cream

Steps:
1. Place onion rings in bottom of crockpot
2. Place meat on top
3. Add water
4. Sprinkle one package of seasoning on top
5. Cook on low for 8 hours
6. Shred roast with forks
7. Add second package of seasoning and stir well
8. Turn on high for 1 / 2 hour
9. Assemble burritos using tortillas, cheese, tomatoes, lettuce, and sour cream<|endoftext|>
