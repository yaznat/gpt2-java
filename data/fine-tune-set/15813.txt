What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Black Bean Burrito Filling Or Side Dish

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Jalapeno chile
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Black beans
• Cilantro leaves
• Ground cumin
• Cayenne pepper sauce
• Coarse salt

Steps:
1. In a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat, add olive oil, jalapeno, garlic and onion
2. Saut for 2 or 3 minutes
3. Add beans and stir in seasonings and cayenne pepper sauce
4. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 10-15 minutes<|endoftext|>
