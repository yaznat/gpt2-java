Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Carrots
• Celery rib
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Curry powder
• Cinnamon
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cream of celery soup
• Water
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Brown meat and onion, drain
2. Add celery, carrot, garlic and ginger, cook 1-2 minutes until fragrant
3. Add cinnamon and curry powder, stir well
4. Add soup and water and hot sauce if desired
5. Simmer 20-30 minutes, serve over hot rice or noodles<|endoftext|>
