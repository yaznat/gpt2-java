Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Dried chili
• Szechuan peppercorns
• Fennel seed
• Fine salt
• Rice wine

Steps:
1. Press the bean curd for 2 hours
2. Dry with towels and cut into 9 pieces
3. Use a very thin 5 to 6 inch bamboo or wooden skewer thread 4 small squares of curd onto each
4. Leave space between each
5. D place in covered steamer pot in well aired room for 2 to 4 days
6. {trust me you want it well aired
7. } it will develop a orangish yellow mold and have a slippery appearance and strong odor
8. Grind the crushed peppers, peppercorns and fennel
9. Add salt and mix well then pour into a dish
10. Carefully take a skewer and sprinkle seasoning mix over all of the fermented curd
11. Repeat until done
12. Put into a wide-mouth jar and pull out skewers carefully
13. Pour wine to cover and sprinkle salt mix on top
14. Cover in keep in refrigerator
15. It will be ready to use in a week and will keep indefinitely
16. Use small portions
17. If wine is too strong use salt dissolved in water
18. It may be made without the spices and just the salt<|endoftext|>
