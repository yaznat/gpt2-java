Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Round roast
• Onions
• Water
• Fresh green beans
• Allspice
• Ground black pepper
• Salt
• Canned tomatoes
• Tomato sauce

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat
2. Brown the beef in the hot oil for 10 minutes
3. Stir in the onions and 1 / 2 cup water
4. Cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Mix the green beans, allspice, pepper, and salt into the mixture
6. Cover and simmer another 15 minutes
7. Add the tomatoes and tomato sauce
8. Cover with water
9. Cover and simmer another 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
