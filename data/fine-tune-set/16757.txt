Ingredients:
• Orange sherbet
• Vanilla ice cream
• Milk
• Whipped topping
• Orange food coloring
• Decorator sugar
• Candy orange slices

Steps:
1. In blender process sherbert, ice cream and milk until smooth
2. Seperately, combine 1 / 2 c whipped topping and orange food coloring
3. Place orange topping and the other 1 / 2 c whipped topping in pastry bag fitted with large star
4. Pipe into milkshakes
5. If desired, garnish with orange decorating sugar and orange jelly candy<|endoftext|>
