Give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Corn Custard

Ingredients:
• Creamed corn
• Eggs
• Cheddar cheese
• Green bell peppers
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Nutmeg
• Half-and-half cream

Steps:
1. Stir corn, eggs, cheese and bell pepper together
2. Combine flour, sugar and spices
3. Add to corn mixture
4. Stir in half and half
5. Spoon into a greased 1 1 / 2 qt baking dish
6. Set baking dish in a deep pan and add 1 inch hot water to outer pan
7. Bake in a preheated 325 degree oven for 1 hour, 20 minutes or until a knife inserted in the middle comes out clean<|endoftext|>
