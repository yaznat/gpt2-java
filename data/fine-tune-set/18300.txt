Ingredients:
• Canned black beans
• Egg whites
• Roasted garlic
• Ground cumin
• Zucchini
• Whole wheat breadcrumbs
• Olive oil
• Salsa
• Reduced-fat sour cream

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, mash beans, egg whites, garlic, and cumin to a coarse texture
2. Stir in the zucchini and bread crumbs
3. Heat large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
4. Add 1 teaspoon of oil
5. Using 1 / 2 of the bean mixture, drop 4 mounds into the pan and flatten them to about 3 1 / 2-inch rounds
6. Cook for 4 minutes, turning halfway during cooking
7. Repeat with remaining mixture
8. Serve with salsa and sour cream<|endoftext|>
