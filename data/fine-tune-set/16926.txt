What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Bayou Self Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Spiced rum
• Butterscotch schnapps
• Pineapple juice
• Grenadine

Steps:
1. Pour captain morgan spiced rum, butterscotch schnapps and pineapple juice into a cocktail shaker half-filled with ice cubes
2. Shake well, and strain into a highball glass 3 / 4 filled with ice
3. Top with grenadine, and garnish with a maraschino cherry and a slice of pineapple<|endoftext|>
