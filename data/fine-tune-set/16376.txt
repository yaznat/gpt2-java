Ingredients:
• Shortcut bacon
• Eggs
• Saltine
• Onions
• Salt
• Pepper
• Ground beef
• Ketchup
• Brown sugar
• Ground mustard

Steps:
1. Combine eggs, saltines, onion, salt and pepper
2. Mix beef into above mixture and mix well
3. Shape into six patties
4. Wrap bacon strips around patties and secure with toothpicks
5. Broil 3-4 inches from the heat for 6 minutes
6. In a bowl, combine the ketchup, brown sugar and mustard
7. Turn patties and top with ketchup mixture
8. Broil 6-7 minutes longer or until meat is no longer pink and bacon is crisp
9. Remove toothpicks before serving<|endoftext|>
