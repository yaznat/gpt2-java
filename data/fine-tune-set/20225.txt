Ingredients:
• Butter
• Garlic
• Dried basil
• Carrot
• Zucchini
• Summer squash
• Leeks
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Place butter or cooking oil in a wok or large skillet
2. Preheat over medium-high heat
3. Stir-fry garlic and basil, oregano or italian seasoning in hot cooking oil or butter for 15 seconds
4. Add carrot and stir-fry for 1 minute
5. Add zucchini and / or yellow squash and stir-fry for 2 1 / 2 minutes
6. Add leeks or green onions and stir-fry about 1 1 / 2 minutes more or until vegetables are crisp-tender
7. Option: sprinkle with parmesan or romano cheese
8. Toss gently
9. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
