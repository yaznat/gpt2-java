What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Rice Burritos

Ingredients:
• Garlic clove
• Butter
• Cooked chicken
• Chili powder
• Chicken broth
• Picante sauce
• Long grain rice
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. In a skillet, saute garlic in butter until tender
2. Stir in chicken, chili powder, 1 / 4 cup broth and 3 / 4 cup of picante sauce
3. Heat through
4. Set aside
5. In a medium saucepan, bring rice and remaining picante sauce
6. Cover and simmer 5-10 minutes or until rice is tender
7. Stir into chicken mixture
8. Add 2 cups of cheese
9. Spoon 1 cup filling, off center on each tortilla
10. Fold sides and ends over filling, then roll up
11. Arrange burritos in two ungreased 13 x 9 inch baking dishes
12. Sprinkle with the remaining cheese
13. Cover and bake at 375 for 10-15 minutes or until heated through
14. Garnish with picante sauce and cheese<|endoftext|>
