What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Amaretto Chunk Cookies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Amaretto liqueur
• Almond extract
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Sweetened flaked coconut
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Combine four, baking soda, baking powder and salt
3. Set aside
4. In a large bowl, with mixer at medium speed, beat butter and sugars until creamy
5. Beat in eggs, amaretto and extract
6. Reduce speed to low
7. Beat in flour mixture just until blended
8. Stir in chocolate chips, coconut and almonds
9. Drop dough by heaping measuring spoons on large, ungreased cookie sheets
10. Bake 10-12 minutes or until golden around edges<|endoftext|>
