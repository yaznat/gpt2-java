Can you give me a potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Armenian Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Salsa
• Parsley
• Fresh lemon juice
• Olive oil
• Salt

Steps:
1. Steam the potatoes for about 30 minutes
2. Allow to cool, then
3. Peel off the skin
4. Cut potatoes into a 1 inch uneven dice shape
5. Chop the parsley and mix with the other salsa ingredients
6. For a spicier salsa, substitute 1 / 2 of the parsley with watercress
7. Mix the potatoes with the salsa<|endoftext|>
