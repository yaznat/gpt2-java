Give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Cheesy Ham Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Monterey jack cheese
• Tortillas
• Deli ham
• Tomatoes
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Sprinkle cheese onto one half of each tortilla
2. Top with ham, tomato and green onion tops
3. Fold each tortilla in half, pressing firmly together
4. In a 10-inch skillet, cook quesadillas, two at a time, over medium heat for 4 minutes, or until lightly browned, turning once
5. Repeat with remaining quesadillas
6. Cut each quesadilla into wedges and serve<|endoftext|>
