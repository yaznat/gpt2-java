Ingredients:
• Butter
• Orange liqueur
• Orange rind
• Pecan halves
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 300
2. In a large saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat
3. Add in liqueur and orange zest
4. Stir until blended
5. Remove the pan from heat, add in the pecans, and toss to coat well
6. Spread the nuts evenly on a large baking sheet and toast in the oven, uncovered, until golden brown, about 30 minutes, tossing the nuts from time to time
7. Drain on paper towels, sprinkle salt on top
8. Let cool completely, and serve in crystal bowls with cocktails<|endoftext|>
