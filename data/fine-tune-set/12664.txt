Ingredients:
• Cube steaks
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Flour
• Vegetable oil
• Reduced-sodium beef broth
• Vegetable soup
• Frozen puff pastry

Steps:
1. Sprinkle beef with salt and pepper
2. Toss beef with flour in a large bowl to coat
3. Heat oil in a large dutch oven over medium-high heat
4. Add 1 / 3 of the beef to the hot oil and sear until browned on all sides, about 5 minutes
5. Using a slotted spoon, transfer beef to a clean bowl
6. Repeat with remaining beef in 2 more batches
7. Add beef broth and bring to a simmer, scraping with a wooden spoon to loosen brown bits on bottom
8. Return all beef and any accumulated juices to pot
9. Add soup and simmer, uncovered, until liquid thickens slightly and beef is tender, about 15 minutes
10. Divide stew equally among 4 oven-proof mugs
11. On a floured surface, cut puff pastry into 4, 4-inch rounds
12. Moisten rims of mugs with water then gently adhere puff pastry to each mug
13. Transfer to a baking sheet and bake until golden brown, about 12 minutes<|endoftext|>
