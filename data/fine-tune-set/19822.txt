Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Canola oil
• Baking potatoes
• Salt
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 450f
2. Heat the garlic and oil in a small saucepan over medium heat for 2 minutes
3. Strain the garlic from the oil with a small mesh strainer, reserving the infused oil and setting the garlic aside in a dish
4. Cut the potatoes into 1 / 4-inch sticks
5. In a large bowl, toss the oil, potatoes and 1 / 2 teaspoon of salt
6. Spray a baking sheet with cooking spray and spread the potatoes onto it in a single layer
7. Bake until golden and crisp, about 35 minutes
8. Remove potatoes from the tray with a metal spatula
9. Toss with parsley, reserved garlic, and additional salt, to taste<|endoftext|>
