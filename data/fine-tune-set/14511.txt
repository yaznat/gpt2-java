Ingredients:
• Fresh green beans
• Yellow onion
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Salt
• Black pepper
• White sugar
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. In a stock pot on medium high heat, melt butter
2. Add chopped onion and saute 3 minutes
3. Add green beans and continue to saute another 3 minutes
4. Stir in salt, pepper, and sugar
5. Pour in chicken stock, and cover pot
6. Cook on medium low 35-40 minutes<|endoftext|>
