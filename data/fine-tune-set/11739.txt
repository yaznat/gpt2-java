What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>Almas Pite Hungarian Apple Cake

Ingredients:
• Self raising flour
• Dry yeast
• Sugar
• Butter
• Egg yolks
• Sour cream
• Red delicious apples
• Walnuts
• Caster sugar
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. To make the dough:
2. In a food processor, process butter, egg yolks and sour cream or cream
3. Add the flour, dry yeast and sugar
4. Process until dough is light and does not stick to the sides of the processor
5. If the dough is too moist, add a bit more flour, if too dry, add a bit of milk
6. Cover and put in the fridge overnight
7. Take the dough out of the fridge about 1-2 hours before you bake it, so the dough can soften a little
8. Preheat oven to 200 degrees celsius and greas a rectangular baking dish
9. Cut the dough in two, and roll out to the size of your baking dish and place one part inches
10. Sprinkle with walnuts
11. Grate the apples and squeeze all the juice out
12. Put apples on top of walnuts
13. Sprinkle with caster sugar and cinnamon
14. Put the other half of the dough on top
15. Brush with a beaten egg and prick with a fork in a few places to allow steam to escape
16. Bake for around 35-40 minutes
17. Cool in baking dish
18. Great served with cream!<|endoftext|>
