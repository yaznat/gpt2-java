Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Butternut Squash

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Stewing beef
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Butternut squash
• No-salt-added diced tomatoes
• No-salt-added tomato sauce
• Low sodium beef broth
• Ground cumin
• Cinnamon
• Red pepper flakes
• Whole wheat couscous
• Sliced almonds
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a 4-qt saucepan over medium-high heat
2. Add beef and cook until browned on all sides, about 5 minutes
3. Transfer meat to a plate, leaving juices in saucepan
4. Add onion
5. Cook, stirring, until translucent, about 6 minutes
6. Add garlic and ginger
7. Cook, stirring, 1 minute more
8. Return beef to pot
9. Stir in squash, tomatoes, sauce, broth, cumin, cinnamon and pepper flakes
10. Bring to a boil
11. Reduce heat to low
12. Cover
13. Simmer until beef is tender, 30 to 35 minutes
14. Divide couscous and stew among 4 bowls
15. Sprinkle with almonds and parsley<|endoftext|>
