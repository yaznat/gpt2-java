Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour
• Taco seasoning mix
• Dried oregano
• Cumin seeds
• Tortilla chips
• Corn chips
• Pita bread
• Ripe olives
• Sausage
• Red pepper
• Green pepper
• Tortilla

Steps:
1. Toss together cheese and flour
2. Heat broth in a 1 1 / 2 quart saucepan, until bubbles rise
3. Turn heat to med-low, and add cheese and flour blend, 1 / 2 cup at a time, stirring until cheese is melted after each addition
4. Stir in taco mix, oregano, and crushed cumin seed
5. Transfer to a fondue pot, and serve immediately, along with all your dippers<|endoftext|>
