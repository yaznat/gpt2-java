Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Cornmeal Sourdough Waffles

Ingredients:
• Sourdough starter
• Buttermilk
• Sugar
• White flour
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Eggs
• Yellow cornmeal
• Butter
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Before going to bed, put 1 / 2 cup starter in a large mixing bowl, stir in 2 cups buttermilk, sugar, white and wheat flour
2. Mix well and cover with cloth
3. Next morning, while waffle iron is preheating, beat egg whites until stiff
4. Lightly beat egg yolks and add to starter mixture, stir in cornmeal, butter, soda, salt, vanilla and cinnamon if using
5. Add additional buttermilk if batter seems to stiff
6. Fold in beaten egg whites and bake in waffle iron as per manufactures directions
7. Place on wire racks to cool completely before packaging and freezing
8. To reheat, toast in toaster oven to desired degree of crispness
