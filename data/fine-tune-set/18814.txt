Give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Apple Custard Bread Pudding Cups

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Apricot jam
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla essence
• Brown sugar
• Apple
• Walnuts
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Heat milk in a small saucepan until almost boiling
2. Add sugar and vanilla, stir to dissolve
3. Take off the heat
4. Lightly spray 2 individual ramekin dishes with oil
5. Cut the bread using a pastry / scone cutter into rounds the same size as the ramekin dishes
6. Spread the tops of each bread piece with jam
7. Place one piece of bread on the bottom of each ramekin, jam side up
8. Put some grated apple on top, sprinkle with a little of the nuts and some nutmeg
9. Whisk the eggs into the warm milk mixture
10. Put a few spoonfuls on top of the apple mixture, then place another piece of bread on top, jam side up
11. Continue the layer again- apple, nuts, nutmeg and custard mixture
12. You may have to make more depending on how big your ramekins are and how big the apple was
13. Finally, top with one more piece of bread
14. Sprinkle the tops with nutmeg and sugar
15. Pour over the remaining custard mix
16. Place in the fridge and let rest for at least an hour
17. Preheat oven to 350f
18. Bake for 20 minutes or so until the custard is cooked through<|endoftext|>
