What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Beef Noodle Soup

Ingredients:
• Boneless beef sirloin
• Soy sauce
• Beef stock
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic clove
• Rice vermicelli
• Carrots
• Napa cabbage
• Bean sprouts
• Fresh cilantro
• Green onions
• Sesame seed oil

Steps:
1. Cut beef into very thin strips
2. In a bowl, combine beef with soy sauce
3. In a large saucepan over high heat, bring stock, ginger and garlic to a boil
4. Add vermicelli
5. Boil for 2 minutes or until softened
6. Add beef, carrots and cabbage
7. Return to a boil
8. Cook for another 2 minutes
9. Add bean sprouts and cook for 1 minute or until heated through
10. Ladle into bowls
11. Garnish with cilantro and chopped green onions
12. Drizzle with sesame oil, if desired<|endoftext|>
