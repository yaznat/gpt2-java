What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Aglio Olio E Peperoncino Garlic Oil And Chilli Pasta Sauce

Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Red chile
• Garlic clove
• Cooked spaghetti
• Flat leaf parsley
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-low heat
2. Add the chili and garlic, and gently fry for about 1 minute, until the garlic is just beginning to colour
3. Take care not to let it burn
4. Add the cooked pasta and the parsley, and season to taste
5. Toss well and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
