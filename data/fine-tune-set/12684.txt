Give me a recipe for steak.<|endoftext|>4 2 Round Steak Bake

Ingredients:
• Cooking oil
• Round steaks
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Onion
• Tomato soup
• Water
• Salt
• Pepper
• Gravy master

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 300f
2. Heat cooking oil in a frying pan
3. Add steak cut in serving size pieces
4. Brown both sides
5. Transfer to ungreased 2-quart casserole
6. Add potatoes, carrots, and onion
7. Combine soup, water, salt, pepper, and gravy master
8. Pour over ingredients in casserole
9. Cover the casserole
10. Bake for 2 1 / 2 to 3 hours
11. Until beef is tender
12. Variation:
13. Sprinkle a drained can of string beans over all, cover and bake an extra 10 minutes to heat them through<|endoftext|>
