Ingredients:
• Egg noodles
• Bacon
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Salt
• Dried marjoram
• Dried thyme
• Pepper
• Stewed tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Cook noodles according to package directions
2. Drain
3. In a large skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until crisp
4. Remove to paper towels to drain, reserving 2 tablespoons drippings
5. In the drippings, saute green pepper, onion, salt, marjoram, thyme and pepper for 5 minutes
6. Stir in tomatoes
7. Bring to a boil
8. Reduce heat
9. Simmer, uncovered, for 10 minutes
10. Stir in the noodles
11. Add half of the bacon
12. Transfer to a greased 2-qt
13. Baking dish
14. Top with cheese and remaining bacon
15. Bake, uncovered, at 350 for 10-15 minutes or until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
