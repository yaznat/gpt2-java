What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Chuck Steak W Onions Peppers

Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• Salt
• Boneless beef chuck steak
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Mushroom
• Nonfat beef broth
• Water
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Season meat with salt
2. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat
3. Coat with spray oil and add beef
4. Brown beef on both sides
5. Remove beef from skillet and add onions and peppers
6. Saute onions and peppers about 3 minutes
7. Add beef broth to skillet
8. Return beef to skillet and bring to a boil
9. Reduce heat, cover and simmer for about 45 minutes or until beef is tender
10. Remove beef to a serving platter and keep warm
11. Mix together water and cornstarch and add to skillet
12. Stir constantly until gravy is thickened
13. Pour gravy over meat and serve<|endoftext|>
