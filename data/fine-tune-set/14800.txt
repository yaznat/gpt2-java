What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Awesome Crunchy Lemon Rice

Ingredients:
• White rice
• Water
• Chicken bouillon cubes
• Red pepper sauce
• Water chestnuts
• Green onions
• Lemon zest

Steps:
1. In a medium sized sauce pan, bring rice, water, bouillon cubes and pepper sauce to a boil
2. Stir once or twice, reduce heat, cover and simmer for 15 minutes
3. Remove from heat and stir in water chestnuts, green onions and lemon zest
4. Recover and let stand for 10 minutes
5. Fluff with a fork before serving<|endoftext|>
