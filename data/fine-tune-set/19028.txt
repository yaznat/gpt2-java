What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Hot Custard And Bananas

Ingredients:
• Custard
• Bananas

Steps:
1. Gently heat the custard over a low heat
2. While that's heating, peel the bananas and chop up
3. Pour the custard equally into the pudding bowls
4. Place banana on top
5. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
