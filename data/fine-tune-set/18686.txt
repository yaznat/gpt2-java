Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Lemons, zest of
• Lemon juice
• Eggs
• 2% low-fat milk

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine flour, sugar and salt
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together lemon / lime zest and juice, egg yolks and milk, whisk into flour mixture
3. In a separate bowl, beat egg whites until stiff, glossy peaks form, fold into lemon / lime mixture
4. Pour batter into greased 8 inch square baking dish
5. Place in larger pan and add hot water to come halfway up sides of baking dish
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 45 to 50 minutes or until set and lightly browned<|endoftext|>
