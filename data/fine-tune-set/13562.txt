Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onions
• Beef consomme
• Dijon-style mustard
• Catsup
• Water
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Prepare gravy by cooking bacon in a sauce pan until partially done
2. Add onions and saute for 5 miuntes
3. Pour in the beef consumne'
4. Bring to a boil
5. Mix water and corn starch together
6. Add corn starch mixture to sauce pan
7. Cook till mixture has thicken
8. Lower heat
9. Add mustard, salt and pepper to taste
10. Stir in catsup
11. Serve over mashed potatoes, or noodles<|endoftext|>
