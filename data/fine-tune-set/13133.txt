Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Boston Bluefish With Spinach And Soup

Ingredients:
• Frozen spinach
• Bluefish fillets
• Condensed cream of celery soup
• Water
• Flour
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Spray a 1-1 / 2l to 2l dish with non stick spray
2. Preheat oven to 350f
3. Spread thawed and squeezed dry spinach over bottom of dish
4. Place thawed fillets over spinach and sprinkle with salt and pepper
5. In a bowl mix the flour and water, add the soup and mix well
6. Spoon over fillets and top with shredded cheese
7. Bake 30-35 minutes<|endoftext|>
