What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>No Milk Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Water
• Banana
• Honey
• Almonds
• Ice

Steps:
1. Put banana, honey and almonds in blender
2. Blend till banana is mushed and almonds are ground
3. Add water
4. Blend for about 1 minute
5. Pour into 2 glasses
6. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
