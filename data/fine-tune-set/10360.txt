Give me a cookie recipe.<|endoftext|>Almond Roca Cookies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Toffee pieces
• Almonds
• Milk chocolate
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 300f
2. In a medium size mixing bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda and salt
3. Set aside
4. In bowl of an electric mixer, blend both sugars together at medium speed
5. Add butter and mix to form a grainy paste
6. Add eggs and vanilla and mix at medium speed until it's light and fluffy
7. At low speed, slowly add flour mixture and then toffee bits
8. Mix until it's just blended
9. Don't over-mix
10. Place the ground almonds in a small bowl
11. Using your hands, roll balls of dough into 1 to 1 1 / 2-inch balls, then roll in the ground almonds
12. Place on cookie sheets several inches apart
13. Bake about 22 minutes and transfer cookies to a cooling rack
14. Melt the chocolate with the vegetable oil in a double boiler or in a bowl set over a pan of simmering water
15. Drizzle melted chocolate over the cooled cookies
16. Place cookies on a cookie sheet and place in freezer or refrigerator until chocolate is firmly set<|endoftext|>
