Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>Amber S Favorite Amish Casserole

Ingredients:
• Broad egg noodles
• Hamburger
• Onion
• Brown sugar
• Tomato soup
• Cheddar cheese soup
• Cream of chicken soup
• Milk
• Cheese slice

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef with onions
2. Drain
3. While hamburger is cooking, prepare noodles according to package
4. Drain
5. Mix tomato soup and brown sugar into hamburger
6. Mix cream of chicken and cheddar cheese soups, and milk into noodles
7. Put noodle mixture into a 9x13-inch pan or casserole dish, top with cheese, then the hamburger mixture
8. Bake, covered, for 40-45 minutes at 350f<|endoftext|>
