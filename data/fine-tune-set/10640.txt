Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>Apples And Ground Beef Pasta

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Apples
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Diced tomatoes
• Cumin
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Salt
• Pepper
• Sugar
• Whole wheat pasta

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. While pasta cooks, crumble beef into frying pan and brown with onions and garlic
3. When pasta is done, rinse with cold water to stop the cooking and set aside
4. Drain fat from the pan of ground beef
5. Place contents of cans of tomatoes into pan, along with apples and spices
6. Allow to simmer for about 20 minutes, or until everything becomes saucy and the apple pieces become tender
7. Serve sauce over pasta
