Ingredients:
• Pork sirloin roast
• Southwest seasoning
• Chunky salsa
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Fresh cilantro leaves

Steps:
1. Rub southwest seasoning on both sides of pork and place in slow cooker
2. Pour 2 cups salsa over pork, cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 hours
3. Using two forks, shred pork
4. Meanwhile, wrap tortillas in aluminum foil and heat in a 350-degree oven for 20 minutes or until heated through
5. Spoon pork mixture onto center of each tortilla and top with additional salsa, cheese and cilantro
6. Broil 1 minute or until cheese is bubbly
7. Roll up like a burrito to eat
8. Serve with black beans and spanish rice<|endoftext|>
