What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Bacon Turkey Bravo Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Ketchup
• Lemon juice
• Dried mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Tabasco sauce
• Bread
• Romaine lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Smoked turkey breast
• Smoked gouda cheese
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Dressing:
2. Combine all the ingredients in a small bowl and stir well
3. Refrigerate
4. This makes 1 3 / 4 cups, and will keep a long time in the fridge
5. Sandwich:
6. Spread 2 tbsp of the dressing on one of the bread slices
7. On top of one slice of bread, layer lettuce, tomato, turkey, gouda, and bacon
8. Top with the second slice of bread and serve<|endoftext|>
