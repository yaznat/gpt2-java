What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Asian Pork And Pineapple Salad

Ingredients:
• Pineapple chunks in juice
• Fresh lime juice
• Fish sauce
• Sugar
• Garlic
• Red onion
• Fresh basil
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Pickled jalapeno pepper
• Fresh ginger
• Pork tenderloin
• Vegetable oil
• Arugula

Steps:
1. Drain pineapple, reserving 1 tablespoon juice
2. Discard remaining pineapple juice
3. Set pineapple aside
4. Combine reserved pineapple juice, lime juice, fish sauce, sugar, and garlic
5. Place reserved pineapple chunks, onion, and the next 4 ingredients in a large bowl
6. Drizzle 2 tablespoons lime juice mixture over onion mixture
7. Toss gently
8. Combine remaining lime juice mixture with pork, stirring well
9. Heat the oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
10. Add pork, and saut 3 minutes or until done
11. Add pork to onion mixture
12. Toss well
13. Serve over arugula<|endoftext|>
