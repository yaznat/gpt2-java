Please give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Aunty Diane S Yummy Burgers

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Ground pork
• Onion
• Garlic powder
• Rolled oats
• Salt & pepper
• Egg
• Cold water

Steps:
1. Mix first 6 ingredients together
2. Then add the eggs and cold water
3. Get your hands in there and mix it real good
4. Roll into balls then flatten into patties
5. Bbq for 10-15 minutes
6. Be sure not to press down and squeeze them while they are cooking
7. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
