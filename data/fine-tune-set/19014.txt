Can you give me a custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Grandma S Egg Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• Unbaked pie shells
• Eggs
• White sugar
• Salt
• Vanilla extract
• Egg white
• Whole milk
• Ground nutmeg
• Yellow food coloring

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Mix together eggs, sugar, salt, and vanilla
3. Stir well
4. Blend in the scalded milk
5. For more yellow color, add a few drops yellow food coloring
6. Brush inside bottom and sides of pie crust with egg white to help prevent a soggy crust
7. Pour custard mixture into piecrust
8. Sprinkle with nutmeg
9. Bake for 40 to 50 minutes or until a knife inserted near center comes out clean
10. Cool on rack<|endoftext|>
