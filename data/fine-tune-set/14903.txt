Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Excellent Egg And Ginger Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Scallions
• Garlic
• Fresh ginger
• Egg
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Frozen peas
• Soy sauce
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a wok and cook the scallions, garlic and ginger for 2 minutes
2. Crack in the egg and scramble with a chopstick for a couple of minutes until just set
3. Stir in the rice and peas and continue to cook for a further 4 minutes until piping hot
4. Season with soy sauce and a few twists of black pepper and serve<|endoftext|>
