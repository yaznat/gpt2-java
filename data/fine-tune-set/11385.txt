What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>A Little Different Broccoli Cornbread

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Jiffy corn muffin mix
• Frozen chopped broccoli
• Onion
• Ricotta cheese
• Eggs
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Place stick of butter or margarine in casserole dish
3. Heat in oven just until melted
4. Remove dish from oven and carefully tilt so that sides are coated
5. In a large mixing bowl, combine cornbread mix, broccoli, onion, ricotta and eggs
6. Stir well to mix
7. Add approximately 1 / 2 cup grated cheese, stirring well again
8. Pour mixture into buttered baking dish, using a spatula to distributed evenly
9. Bake for 30 minutes
10. Remove from oven and sprinkle with additional cheese, to taste
11. Return to oven for 10-15 minutes, until cornbread is done and cheese has melted
12. Cut into squares and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
