Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sliced mushrooms
• Eggs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Baking powder

• Cheese

Steps:
1. Heat large electric frying pan to 300f fry mushrooms in butter
2. Meanwhile separate eggs whites and yolks into 2 different bowls
3. Beat egg whites until stiff
4. Set aside
5. Beat egg yolks, salt, pepper, flour, baking powder and milk
6. Fold in egg whites into yolk mixture
7. Gently pour over mushrooms
8. Seal tightly with lid and do not lift lid
9. Cook for 10 to 15 minutes
10. Sprinkle grated cheese over omelette
