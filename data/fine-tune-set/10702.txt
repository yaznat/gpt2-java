What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado Lime Pasta Sauce

Ingredients:
• Lime
• Avocado
• Sour cream
• Chili
• Parsley
• Salt
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Rinse lime under hot water, dry and finely grate the zest
2. Squeeze the juice from the lime, set aside
3. Peel the avocado and mash it with the lime juice by using a fork
4. Add sour cream, chili, parsley, lime zest and season to taste with salt and sugar
5. Serve with penne or linguine pasta<|endoftext|>
