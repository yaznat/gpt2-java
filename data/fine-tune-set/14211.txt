Ingredients:
• Lowfat silken tofu
• Natural-style peanut butter
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Amaretto
• Dutch-processed cocoa powder
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Blend everything at high speed, scraping down the sides
2. Try not to eat right away
3. Best when chilled for at least an hour<|endoftext|>
