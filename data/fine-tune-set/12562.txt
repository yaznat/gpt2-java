Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Onions
• All-purpose flour
• Beef broth
• Red wine vinegar
• Dry thyme leaves
• Dried rosemary
• Pepper
• Carrots
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a shallow 3 to 4 quart casserole, combine beef and onions
2. Bake, uncovered, in a 450 degree fahrenheit oven, stirring occasionally, until beef and onions are browned
3. Remove from oven
4. Sprinkle with flour and stir gently to coat
5. Reduce oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
6. In a 1-1 / 2 quart pan, combine beef broth, red wine vinegar, thyme, rosemary and pepper
7. Bring to a boil
8. Add to beef mixture, stirring to loosen any browned bits
9. Distribute carrots over beef
10. Cover tightly and bake until beef is very tender when pierced
11. Season with salt<|endoftext|>
