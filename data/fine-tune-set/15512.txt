What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Cheeseburger Rice

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Barbecue sauce
• Mustard
• Dried onion
• Pepper
• Cooked rice
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef, drain
2. Add barbecue sauce, mustard, onion, and pepper
3. Bring to a boil
4. Stir in the rice
5. Sprinkle with cheese
6. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
