Ingredients:
• Turnips
• Olive oil
• Kosher salt
• Chili powder

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 425f cut turnips into 2 x 1 / 2-inch sticks
2. Place on a foil-lined jelly-roll pan
3. Drizzle with oil and sprinkle with salt and chili powder
4. Toss with your hands to coat
5. Spread out in a single layer
6. Roast fries 30 minutes, turning halfway through cooking time for even browning
7. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
