Give me a beans recipe.<|endoftext|>3 Bean Salad With Marinated Sweet Onions And Roasted Peppers

Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Olive oil
• Cilantro
• Thyme
• Basil
• Ground cumin
• Honey mustard
• Garlic clove
• Black beans
• Pinto beans
• Navy beans
• Roasted sweet peppers
• Sweet onion
• Cider vinegar
• Sugar
• Salt

Steps:
1. Salad dressing: in a mixing bowl, combine all ingredients
2. Process in food processor until smooth
3. Makes 2 / 3 cup
4. Salad: drain beans in colander
5. Place in large bowl with peppers
6. Add 1 / 2 cup of prepared salad dressing and toss gently
7. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour, preferably overnight
8. Reserve and refrigerate remaining salad dressing
9. Onion garnish: in another bowl, toss the onion with the vinegar sugar and 1 / 2 teaspoon salt
10. Cover and refrigerate
11. At serving time, adjust seasoning on beans
12. Add remaining salad dressing, if desired
13. Garnish with sliced onion mixture<|endoftext|>
