Ingredients:
• Center-cut bacon
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Ground beef
• Garlic cloves
• Worcestershire sauce
• Ground mustard
• Potatoes
• Broccoli cheese soup
• Milk
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Low-fat sour cream
• Cheddar french-fried onions
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. In a large skillet, cook bacon until crisp
3. While bacon is cooking chop and peel potatoes
4. Boil in salted water until tender, but not overdone
5. Drain grease from bacon skillet and add onion, green pepper, garlic, ground mustard, and worcestershire, ground beef and salt / pepper to taste
6. Cook until beef is no longer pink
7. In a medium bowl combine soup, milk, cheese, and sour cream
8. In a large bowl combine drained potatoes, crumbled bacon, drained hamburger, and soup mixture
9. Add more salt and pepper if desired
10. Grease a 9 by 13 glass pan with non stick spray
11. Add potato and hamburger mixture
12. Top with french fried onions
13. Bake 30 minutes at 350<|endoftext|>
