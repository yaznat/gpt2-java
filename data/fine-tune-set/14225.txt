What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Corn Tofu Soup Chinese Style

Ingredients:
• Creamed corn
• Water
• Tofu
• Eggs
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Mix the cream corn, tofu and water together and bring to a boil, stir constantly simmer under low heat, add the egg and stir briefly
2. If you want a thicker soup, stir in some cornflour paste
3. Stir constantly and simmer under low heat for 1 minute<|endoftext|>
