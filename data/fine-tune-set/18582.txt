What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Algerian Date Pudding

Ingredients:
• Dates
• Sugar
• Almonds
• Unsalted dry roasted peanuts
• Egg whites

Steps:
1. Mix together dates, sugar, almonds and peanuts
2. Fold mixture into egg whiles and place in well-buttered baking dish
3. Cook in preheated 325f oven for 35 to 40 min, or until a straw inserted in centre comes out clean
4. Serve hot, tepid or cool<|endoftext|>
