Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Boiled Cookies 2 Ww Points

Ingredients:
• Light butter
• 1% low-fat milk
• Sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa
• Reduced-fat peanut butter
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Quick-cooking oats
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan, over medium heat, mix the butter, milk, sugar, and cocoa
2. Bring to a boil and cook for 1 minute
3. Remove from heat and stir in the peanut butter, vanilla, and salt
4. Mix in the oats and pecans
5. Using a small cookie scoop or teaspoon, drop onto waxed paper and allow cookies to stand 1 hours
6. Store in an airtight container<|endoftext|>
