Give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Banana Split Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Bing cherry
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Break bananas into chunks and place in food processor
2. Add enough water for the food processor to turn bananas into "ice cream"
3. Spoon into bowl, add cherries and almonds, and mix
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
