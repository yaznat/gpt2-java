Ingredients:
• Breadcrumbs
• Milk
• Ground beef
• Salt
• Accent seasoning
• Onion
• Flour
• Margarine
• Molasses
• Vinegar
• Catsup
• Tabasco sauce
• Marjoram

Steps:
1. Mix together bread crumbs, salt, accent, milk and minced onion
2. Form into tiny meatballs
3. Roll in flour and brown in margarine / butter
4. Combine remaining ingredients and pour over meat
5. Simmer 10 minutes, stirring several times until meatballs are glazed<|endoftext|>
