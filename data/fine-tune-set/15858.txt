What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Burritos

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Rice
• Black beans
• Sirloin steaks
• Taco seasoning
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Salsa
• Sour cream
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Prepare: if making steak or chicken, marinate meat in a ziplock bag overnight in the refrigerator
2. Follow the directions on the packet
3. Cook rice - i use parboiled rice which takes 20 minutes to prepare
4. Follow directions on the bag
5. While rice is cooking, warm up canned beans
6. If using steak or chicken, cut up steak or chicken into bite size pieces or strips
7. While rice and beans are cooking, cook the meat
8. If using ground beef, follow the directions on the taco seasoning packet
9. If cooking steak or chicken, put the steak or chicken in a cast iron skillet and cook over medium heat for 10 minutes or until cooked
10. Assemble: heat wraps in microwave until warm
11. Put some rice, beans, meat, salsa, shredded cheese in a wrap and wrap it up
12. Put a small amount of olive oil in a clean cast iron skillet and heat to medium
13. Put burrito face down in the skillet and heat for 3 minutes, turn and heat for another 3 minutes
14. Top burrito with sour cream<|endoftext|>
