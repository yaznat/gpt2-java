Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sweet potatoes
• Sugar
• Butter
• Heavy cream
• Vanilla
• Nutmeg
• Allspice
• Light brown sugar
• Flour
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Beat eggs, stir in potatoes until blended
2. Add remaining ingredients and put in buttered dish
3. Topping:
4. Mix brown sugar, flour and nuts together
5. Work butter iinto mixture with hands until well blended
6. Sprinkle on top of potatoes and bake at 350 for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
