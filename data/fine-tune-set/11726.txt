What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>Alice S Cheesecake

Ingredients:
• Graham crackers
• Butter
• Egg yolks
• Gelatin
• Egg whites
• Crushed pineapple
• Milk
• Real vanilla
• White sugar
• Cream cheese
• Whipped cream
• Water
• Maraschino cherry

Steps:
1. Mix graham cracker crumbs with butter
2. Press into a spring form pan
3. Mix egg yolks with milk
4. Slowly add sugar
5. Cook in a double boiler for 15 minutes
6. Mix gelatin with water, let stand until dissolved
7. Add to hot mixture and cool
8. Cream cheese until smooth
9. Add egg mixture to the creamed cheese
10. Stiffly beat egg whites
11. Fold into mixture
12. Add whipped cream and vanilla
13. Add drained pineapple and cherries
14. Pour into spring form pan
15. Chill for 4 hours
16. Sprinkle graham cracker mixture on top<|endoftext|>
