Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Whole tomatoes
• Great northern beans
• Black beans
• Dark red kidney beans
• White kidney beans
• Chili beans
• Ketchup
• Bay leaves
• Chili powder
• Balsamic vinegar
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef and onion in a large non-stick skillet until beef is no longer pink and onion is tender
2. Drain off fat and transfer ground beef mixture into a large dutch oven
3. Add tomatoes and remaining ingredients
4. Bring to a boil, cover and reduce heat
5. Simmer 1 hour
6. Remove bay leaves and serve
