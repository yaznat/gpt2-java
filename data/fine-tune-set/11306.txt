Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Grilled Ranch Bread

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Dry ranch dressing mix
• French bread

Steps:
1. Cream the butter with the dressing mix
2. Spread evenly on the bread halves
3. Place under the broiler for 2-3 minutes, until golden and the butter begins to seep and bubble
4. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
