What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Curry Lamb Chicken Pork Or Shrimp

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Butter
• Onion
• Curry powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Dry mustard
• Dried ginger
• Milk
• Pork

Steps:
1. Melt butter or margarine with onions in the top of a double boiler, or in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring occasionally until onions are translucent
2. Combine flour, curry powder, sugar, salt, dry mustard and dry ginger
3. Add to butter & onion mixture
4. Stir until blended
5. Slowly whisk in the milk, stirring until smooth
6. Cook until mixture has thickened, about 5-10 minutes
7. Stir in the chopped meat
8. Heat through
9. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
