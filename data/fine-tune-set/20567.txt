What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Potato Carrot Onion Casserole

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Carrots
• Chopped pimiento
• Bacon
• White onion
• Mayonnaise
• Margarine
• Sour cream
• Celery seed
• Bon appetit seasoning mix
• Yellow mustard
• Salt
• White pepper

Steps:
1. In large pot, put potato chunks in 2 qts of water and bring to boil, cover and cook about 10 minutes
2. Drain
3. In frying pan, cook bacon until slightly crisp
4. Take out of pan to cool
5. Add diced onions and boiled potatoes, cook until slightly browned
6. Use 1 tblsp of oil or bacon grease to coat a 4 qt casserole dish
7. Add onions and potatoes
8. Cut cooked bacon into pieces
9. Preheat oven 350
10. In mixing bowl, add bacon, grated carrots
11. Pimentos, mayonnaise, margarine, sour cream, celery seed, bonapetite seasoning, yellow mustard, salt and pepper
12. Mix well
13. If you don't have that seasoning, use 1 clove crushed garlic or 1 tsp minced fresh garlic in a jar, the salt and white pepper
14. Caution: use mustard only if you love the flavor of mustard because it will really flavor when combined with the mayonnaise
15. Pour over potatoes and onions
16. Cover the casserole
17. Cook one hour
18. If refrigerated allow an extra 10 minutes cooking time
19. After cooking let set 10 minutes
20. Addition: serve a bowl of grated cheese on the side to sprinkle on top of each serving if desired<|endoftext|>
