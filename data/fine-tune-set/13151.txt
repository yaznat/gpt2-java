Ingredients:
• Butter
• Lemon juice
• Cayenne pepper
• Dill
• White fish fillets
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Prepare at least several hours before broiling fish
2. Make dill butter by combining first four ingredients and rolling up in plastic wrap or foil
3. When ready to cook, preheat broiler and spray broiler pan with vegetable oil, or coat with butter
4. Rinse fish and dry thoroughly
5. Season with salt and pepper
6. Place on broiler pan
7. Sprinkle fish with lemon juice
8. Place several generous pats of dill butter on fish
9. Broil on both sides several minutes until fish is opaque and flakes easily with a fork
10. Don't overcook<|endoftext|>
