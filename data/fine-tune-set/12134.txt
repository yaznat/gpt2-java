Ingredients:
• Lemon jell-o gelatin
• Water
• Plain fat-free yogurt
• Instant lemon pudding
• Fat-free cool whip
• Shortbread pie crusts

Steps:
1. In a large bowl dissolve lemon jell-o in 1 / 2 cup of boiling water, stirring for about 2 minutes, until fully dissolved
2. Hand whisk yogurt into the jell-o until well blended
3. Add lemon pudding quickly
4. Whisk to blend
5. Fold in half of the cool whip topping
6. Pour into the pie shell
7. Chill 30 minutes
8. Top with remaining cool whip topping and enjoy<|endoftext|>
