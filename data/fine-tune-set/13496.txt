Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Boneless beef cube
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Butternut squash
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Beef broth
• Ground cumin
• Cinnamon
• Red pepper flakes
• Couscous
• Sliced almonds
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large saucepan over med-high heat
2. Add beef and cook until brown on all sides
3. Transfer meat to plate, leaving juices in saucepan
4. Add onion
5. Cook, stirring, until translucent
6. Add garlic & ginger cook 1 more minute
7. Return beef to pot
8. Stir in squash, tomatoes, tomato sauce, broth, cumin, cinnamon, and pepper flakes
9. Bring to a boil
10. Reduce heat to low
11. Cover and simmer until beef is tender
12. Divide cous cous and stew among 4 bowls
13. Top with almonds and parsley
14. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
