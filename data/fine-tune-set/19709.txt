Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Balthazar S French Fries

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Peanut oil
• Fine sea salt

Steps:
1. Peel potatoes, and cut into 4-by-1 / 4-inch matchsticks
2. Place in a large bowl, and add enough water to cover by 1 inch
3. Transfer to the refrigerator for 12 to 24 hours
4. Drain potatoes very well 20 minutes before frying
5. In a medium stockpot, heat oil over medium-high heat to 325 degrees
6. Working in batches, add the potatoes to the oil
7. Blanch for 3 minutes
8. Use a slotted spoon or a skimmer to transfer potatoes to a baking sheet
9. Allow the oil to return to 325 degrees between each batch
10. Heat the oil to 375 degrees
11. Working in batches, add the blanched potatoes to the oil, and cook until golden brown, about 3 1 / 2 minutes
12. Use a slotted spoon to transfer potatoes to a paper towel lined baking sheet to drain
13. Allow the oil to return to 375 degrees between each batch
14. Sprinkle with salt, and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
