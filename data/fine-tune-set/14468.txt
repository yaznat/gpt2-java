Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Cooked white rice
• Sofrito sauce
• Salt and pepper
• Cumin
• Oregano
• Apple cider vinegar
• Garlic clove
• Beer
• Tomato sauce

Steps:
1. In a pot, add olive oil and saut garlic and sofrito for a minute
2. Add salt, pepper, cumin and oregano to sofrito and mix
3. Add beans and mix well
4. Add tomato sauce and beer or broth and mix
5. After 5 minutes, add apple cider vinegar and stir
6. Add 2-3 cups of already cooked white rice and mix well
7. Cook until all is absorbed
8. Let sit for 10-15 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
