Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Brownie Alpine Biscotti

Ingredients:
• Fudge brownie mix
• Ground almonds
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Egg
• Egg whites
• Almond extract
• Sliced almonds
• White baking chocolate

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the brownie mix, ground almonds, flour and baking powder
2. In a small bowl, whisk egg, egg whites and extract
3. Add to brownie mixture
4. Stir until combined
5. Divide dough into thirds
6. On a greased baking sheet, shape each portion into a 7 inch
7. X 3-1 / 2 inch rectangle
8. Bake at 350 for 24 minutes
9. Remove from the oven
10. Cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes
11. Move to a cutting board and cut diagonally with a serrated knife into 3 / 4 inch slices
12. Place cut side down on greased baking sheets
13. Bake 12-14 minutes longer or until firm
14. Cool on wire racks
15. If desired, sprinkle with sliced almonds and drizzle with white or dark chocolate
16. Let stand until chocolate is completely set
17. Store in an airtight container<|endoftext|>
