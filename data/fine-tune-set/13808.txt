What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Asian Pork Patties

Ingredients:
• Ground pork
• Carrot
• Green beans
• Red onion
• Ground coriander
• Fish sauce
• Egg
• Peanut oil
• Green chili
• Lime, juice of
• Caster sugar
• Butter lettuce

Steps:
1. Place the ground pork / pork mince, carrot, beans, onion, coriander and 1 tablespoon of the fish sauce and optional egg in a bowl
2. Use your hands to mix until all the ingredients are well-combined
3. Divide the mixture into 12 portions and use your hands to shape each into 2cm-thick / 3 / 4", round 5cm / 2" patties
4. Heat the oil in a large pan, preferably non-stick, over a medium-high heat
5. Add half the patties and cook for 3 minutes on each side or until they are golden-brown
6. Transfer the cooked patties to a plate, cover loosely with foil and set aside
7. Repeat with the remaining patties
8. Meanwhile, place the chilli, lime juice, sugar, extra oil and the remaining fish sauce in a small bowl
9. Stir to combine and set aside
10. Place lettuce leaves on the serving plates
11. Top with the patties and drizzle with a little of the lime mixture
12. Serve with the remaining lime mixture<|endoftext|>
