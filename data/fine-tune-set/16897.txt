Give me a cocktail recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Apple juice
• Apple cider
• Sugar
• Cinnamon sticks
• Whole cloves
• Everclear alcohol

Steps:
1. Combine apple juice and cider and bring to a near boil
2. Remove from heat and add sugar, cloves, and cinnamon sticks then stir
3. Let mixture cool to at least room temperature and strain / remove cloves and cinnamon sticks
4. Add half gallon of everclear
5. Bottle it up
6. Enjoy warm or on the rocks<|endoftext|>
