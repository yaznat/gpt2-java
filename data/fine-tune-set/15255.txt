What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Bree S Vegetarian Pizza

Ingredients:
• Boboli pizza crust
• Onion
• Bell pepper
• Shiitake mushroom
• Black olives
• Cheese
• Tomato sauce

Steps:
1. Make sure all veggies are roughly chopped and cheese is shredded finely
2. Take pizza crust and add the sauce
3. Cover with finely grated cheese, or for a vegan option try vegan shreds by galaxy brands or for a cheesy soy alternative use crumbled tofu with nutritional yeast flakes
4. Add all of the veggies to pizza
5. Bake in a preheated 400 oven for 30 minutes or until crust is brown and cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
