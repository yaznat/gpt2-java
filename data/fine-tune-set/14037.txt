Give me a lamb recipe.<|endoftext|>Cumin Lamb Sweet Pepper Onion Kebabs

Ingredients:
• Boneless lamb
• Lemon juice
• Olive oil
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Cumin
• Liquid honey
• Yellow sweet pepper
• Red onion
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Soak skewers
2. Cut lamb into bite size pieces
3. In a large bowl whisk 1 / 4 cup lemon juice, 2 tablespoons oil, 2 tablespoons ginger, 2 cloves minced garlic, 2 teaspoons cumin, 2 teaspoons honey
4. Add lamb, stir to coat, cover and chill 4 hours or overnight
5. Stir once
6. Oil grill and preheat bbq to med-high
7. Slice pepper and onion into chunks and thread onto skewers with lamb
8. Sprinkle with salt & pepper
9. In a small bowl, whisk leftover marinade ingredients and set aside for basting
10. Grill kebabs 7-10 minutes, turning occasionally
11. Serve with pita, greek salad and tzatziki -- yummmm!<|endoftext|>
