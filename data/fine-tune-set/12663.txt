Ingredients:
• Dried porcini mushrooms
• Hot water
• Olive oil
• Stewing beef
• Beef bones with marrow
• Onions
• Dry red wine
• Bay leaves
• Cloves
• Salt
• Tomato paste
• Chicken broth
• Gnocchi

Steps:
1. Pour cup hot water over dried mushrooms in small bowl
2. Let stand for an hour
3. Drain
4. Reserving liquid
5. Strain liquid through a fine sieve lined with damp kitchen towel
6. Combine liquid and mushrooms
7. Heat oil in large dutch oven over high heat
8. Add beef and bones in batches and brown well, turning frequently
9. Transfer beef and bones to large bowl with a slotted spoon
10. Add onions to pot
11. Reduce heat to medium and saute until transparent, about 10 minutes
12. Return beef and bones and any juices that have accumulated to pot
13. Add wine, bay leaves, cloves and salt
14. Boil until wine is reduced by half, about 10 minutes
15. Mix in tomato paste and mushrooms with their liquid
16. Simmer 5 minutes
17. Add chicken broth
18. Bring mixture to boil
19. Reduce heat
20. Cover and simmer gently until meat is tender, stirring occasionally, about 2 hours
21. Uncover and simmer until sauce is thick and chunky, about 25 minutes longer
22. Remove cloves and bay leaves
23. Serve with potato gnocchi and a green salad<|endoftext|>
