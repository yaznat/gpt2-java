Ingredients:
• Oil
• Boneless chicken breast
• Broccoli floret
• Snow peas
• Carrot
• Red peppers
• Onion soup mix
• Cornstarch
• Ginger
• Water
• Soy sauce
• White vinegar
• Rice

Steps:
1. In large skillet, heat oil and cook chicken with vegetables over medium- high heat, stirring constantly
2. 10 minutes or until chicken is golden and vegetables are crisp-tender
3. Thoroughly blend golden onion recipe soup mix, cornstarch, ginger, water, soy sauce and vinegar
4. Stir into chicken mixture
5. Bring to a boil, then simmer uncovered t minutes or until sauce is thickened
6. Serve over hot rice and garnish, if desired, with sliced green onion and toasted sesame seeds
7. Microwave directions:
8. Omit oil and degrease ginger to 1 / 4 t
9. In 2-quart casserole, heat chicken, uncovered, at high 4 minutes or until almost done
10. Remove chicken and drain
11. Add vegetables to casserole and heat uncovered 5 minutes at high
12. Thoroughly blend golden onion soup mix, cornstarch, ginger, water, soy sauce and vinegar
13. Stir into vegetables
14. Heat uncovered 5 minutes on high or until sauce is thickened, stirring once
15. Return chicken to casserole and heat 1 minute or until heated through
16. Let stand covered 5 minutes
17. Serve and garnish as above<|endoftext|>
