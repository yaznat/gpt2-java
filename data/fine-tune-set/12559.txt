Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Baja Seafood Stew

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Green chili
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Dry white wine
• Orange peel
• Orange juice
• Sugar
• Fresh cilantro
• Dried basil leaves
• Salt
• Pepper
• Dried oregano leaves
• Whole tomatoes
• Soft-shell clams
• Raw shrimp
• Cod
• Frozen crabmeat

Steps:
1. Cook & stir onion, chiles and garlic in oil in 6-quart dutch oven until onion is tender
2. Stir in remaining ingredients except for seafood
3. Heat to boiling, reduce heat & simmer uncovered 15 minutes
4. Add clams
5. Cover & simmer until clams open, 5-10 minutes
6. Carefully stir in shrimp, cod and crabmeat
7. Heat to boiling, reduce heat, cover & simmer until shrimp are pink and cod flakes easily with fork, about 4-5 minutes<|endoftext|>
