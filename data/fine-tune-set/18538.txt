Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Honeyed Buckwheat Pancakes Or Waffles

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Buckwheat flour
• Whole wheat flour
• 2% low-fat milk
• Vegetable oil
• Honey
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Honey crunch wheat germ

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients except baking powder and wheat germ, just until batter is smooth
2. Gently fold in baking powder with a spatula
3. Spoon batter onto griddle or belgium waffler
4. Sprinkle honey crunch wheat germ lightly on top of each pancake or waffle before closing waffle iron
5. Cook until lightly browned around edges
6. Top with desired toppings- butter, syrup, powdered sugar, whipped cream or?
7. Makes 8 pancakes / 3 large waffles<|endoftext|>
