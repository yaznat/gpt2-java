What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Better Than Any Restaurant Steak

Ingredients:
• Filet mignon steaks
• Garlic cloves
• Oregano
• Mccormick's montreal low-sodium steak seasoning
• Maggi seasoning

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a plastic ziploc bag
2. Marinate for about 20 to 30 minutes in the refrigerator
3. Take it out of refrigerator 5 to 10 min before cooking time
4. Grill or broil to desired temperature<|endoftext|>
