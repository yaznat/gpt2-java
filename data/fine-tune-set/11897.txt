Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Carrots
• Butternut squash
• Salt
• Curry powder
• Dry white wine
• Chicken broth
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat
2. Add garlic and onions and stir to coat
3. Add carrots and cook for 3 minutes, or until they start to get soft
4. Sprinkle in a little salt
5. Add squash
6. Stir and cook for a few minutes, until squash is soft and tender
7. After a few minutes, add salt to taste and the curry powder
8. Pour in wine
9. Let evaporate for about 30 seconds, then pour in chicken broth
10. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover pot, and cook for 1 hour
11. After 1 hour, taste and adjust seasonings
12. Turn off heat
13. Puree soup using an immersion blender, or in batches in a regular blender
14. Return to soup pot and bring to a simmer
15. Serve hot
16. If youre feeling naughty, drizzle heavy cream or coconut milk over the top<|endoftext|>
