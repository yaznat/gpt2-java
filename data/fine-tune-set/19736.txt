Ingredients:
• White potatoes
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Cayenne pepper sauce
• Ground thyme
• Sweet paprika
• Ground cumin
• Salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 475 degrees f
2. Cut potatoes into 1 / 3 inch wedges
3. In a large bowl, combine oil, hot sauce, thyme, paprika, and cumin
4. Toss potatoes in dressing and spread out in a single layer on a nonstick cookie sheet
5. Season wedges with salt and pepper and roast for 30 minutes, turning once<|endoftext|>
