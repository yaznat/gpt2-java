Give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>6 Week Bran Muffins Auntie Anne S Muffins

Ingredients:
• Bran buds
• Boiling water
• Oil
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Butter
• Cinnamon sugar

Steps:
1. Pour boiling water over 1 1 / 2 coups of the bran buds
2. Let stand while mixing other ingredients
3. In a separate bowl, combine rest of dry bran buds, flour, soda, and salt
4. In another separate bowl, cream sugar and oil
5. Add eggs to this mixture
6. Add buttermilk alternately with dry ingredients
7. Add the 1 1 / 2 cups soaked bran buds and mix well
8. Store batter in fridge for up to 6 weeks
9. Bake as needed in 400f oven
10. Bake regular size for 15-20 minutes and mini muffins for 8-10 minutes
11. Optional:
12. Dip tops in melted butter and then cinnamon sugar
13. If you are really trying to be healthy, skip the butter and just dip the hot fresh baked muffins in cinnamon sugar and it will stick<|endoftext|>
