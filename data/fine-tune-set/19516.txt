Give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>All American Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• White vinegar
• Dijon mustard
• Canola oil
• Sweet onion
• Celery
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Cut unpeeled potatoes into 1 / 2 or 3 / 4 inch pieces
2. Place in saucepan
3. Cover with cold water
4. Simmer 10 minutes or until cooked through
5. Remove 2 tbs cooking water from saucepan
6. Add to serving bowl
7. Mix with vinegar, mustard and oil
8. Stir in onion and celery
9. Drain potatoes
10. Add to bowl
11. Add salt and pepper to taste
12. Toss well to coat potatoes<|endoftext|>
