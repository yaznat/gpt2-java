What's a good sushi recipe?<|endoftext|>Sushi Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Salmon
• Cream cheese
• Avocado
• Lemon juice
• Wasabi powder
• Soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Sesame seeds
• Rice cakes
• Pickled ginger

Steps:
1. Flake salmon or tuna with lemon juice in a medium-sized bowl
2. Add cream cheese and stir until the mixture becomes paste-like
3. Stir in wasabi powder and soy sauce
4. Stir until well combined
5. Spread a generous heaping of mixture on each rice cake
6. Slice avocados on top
7. Sprinkle with sesame seeds
8. Drizzle with rice vinegar and a touch of extra soy sauce
9. If you have it, a little pickled ginger with each bite gives the total sushi effect!<|endoftext|>
