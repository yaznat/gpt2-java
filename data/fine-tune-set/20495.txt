Ingredients:
• Asparagus
• Penne
• Salt
• Butter
• Flour
• Milk
• Mascarpone cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Nutmeg
• Cayenne
• Breadcrumbs
• Pine nuts

Steps:
1. Bring a large pot of water to boil
2. Add asparagus and cook 5 minutes
3. Remove and add penne and cook until al dente, about 10-15 minutes
4. Make white sauce with butter, flour and milk
5. Remove from heat and stir in mascarpone and 1 / 2 cup parmesan
6. Season with nutmeg, cayenne, salt and pepper
7. Combine butter and breadcrumbs, stir in cheese
8. Stir together pasta, cheese sauce, nuts and mix well
9. Pour into 9x13 pan prepared with cooking spray and top with breadcrumb mixture
10. Bake at 350f uncovered 30 minutes, longer if refrigerated<|endoftext|>
