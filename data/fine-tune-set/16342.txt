Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Salt and pepper
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Rinse potatoes under cool water
2. Scrub with a vegetable brush
3. Pat dry with paper towels
4. Cut potatoes in half lengthwise
5. Place thin onion slices between potatoes
6. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
7. Top with the other potato half
8. Wrap two slices of bacon around each potato
9. Secure with several toothpicks
10. Place in a greased baking pan
11. Bake 1 hour and 20 minutes
12. In a 325 degree oven<|endoftext|>
