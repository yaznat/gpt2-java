Ingredients:
• Avocados
• Plum tomatoes
• Silken tofu
• Lime juice
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Jalapeno
• Water
• Olive oil
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place the avocado, tomatoes, tofu, lime, garlic, onion, jalapeno, herbs into food processor with metal blade and blend till pureed
2. Combine the water to processor while machine is still running, follow with the oil salt and pepper<|endoftext|>
