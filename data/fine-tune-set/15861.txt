Ingredients:
• Rump roast
• Taco seasoning
• Diced tomatoes
• Green chilies
• Onion
• Bell pepper
• Flour tortilla
• Guacamole
• Sour cream
• Salsa
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Place first 6 ingredients in the crock pot
2. Cook on low for about 8 hours
3. Remove the roast from the crock pot & shred using 2 forks
4. Put the shredded roast in a serving bowl and add enough of the remaining crock pot sauce to moisten
5. Serve burrito-style with flour tortillas, guacamole, cheese, sour cream, and salsa<|endoftext|>
