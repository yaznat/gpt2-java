Ingredients:
• Red kidney beans
• Green bell pepper
• Onion
• Celery ribs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Thyme
• Seasoning salt
• Creole seasoning
• Garlic cloves
• Water
• Smoked sausage
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Soak kidney beans overnight or rinse beans and add to pot, cover with 8 cups water and bring to boil
2. Boil for 2 minutes, then turn off heat and let sit, covered for one hour
3. Pour out water and add 8 cups fresh water
4. Chop onion, bell pepper, celery and garlic
5. Add to beans
6. Add salt, pepper, thyme, seasoned salt, and creole seasoning
7. Cover and bring to boil
8. Reduce heat to medium low and cook for 2 1 / 2 hours, stirring occasionally
9. When you stir, be sure to mash the beans against the sides of the pot
10. This creates a creamier texture
11. Slice the sausage into 1 / 4 inch pieces and add to beans
12. Cook for an additional 30 minutes
13. Serve red beans over hot, steaming rice
14. Serve jiffy cornbread on side (add 1 stick of butter and an extra egg to the recipe
