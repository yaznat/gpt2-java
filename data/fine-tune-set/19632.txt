Ingredients:
• Gruyere cheese
• Half-and-half cream
• Yukon gold potatoes
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Salt
• Dried thyme
• Ground pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat over to 375 degrees
2. In medium-large pan, add half & half, thyme, salt and pepper
3. Bring to a simmer
4. Add sliced potatoes and simmer 10 more minutes
5. Add artichokes, bring back to a simmer, and pour entire mixture into 9x13 baking dish
6. Top evenly with cheese
7. Bake 45-55 minutes until top is golden and gratin is bubbling
8. Let stand 10-15 minutes to set
9. Serve<|endoftext|>
