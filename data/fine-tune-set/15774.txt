Ingredients:
• Butter
• Taco seasoning mix
• Sirloin tip steaks
• Refried beans
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onions
• Enchilada sauce
• Mexican blend cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400f in 10-inch skillet, melt butter over medium heat
2. Stir in taco seasoning mix
3. Add beef strips
4. Cook 5 to 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until desired doneness
5. Drain
6. Meanwhile, place refried beans in microwavable dish
7. Microwave uncovered on high 2 minutes, stirring once or twice
8. Spread each tortilla with refried beans to within 1 / 4 inch of edge
9. Top each with beef, cheddar cheese and onions
10. Roll up, folding in sides
11. In ungreased 13x9-inch glass baking dish, place burritos with seam sides down
12. Pour enchilada sauce over burritos
13. Sprinkle with mexican cheese blend
14. Bake uncovered 7 to 12 minutes or until burritos are thoroughly heated and cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
