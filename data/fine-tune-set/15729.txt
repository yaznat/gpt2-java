Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Chickpeas
• Cilantro
• Fresh lime juice
• Garlic clove
• Taco shells
• Lettuce
• Salsa
• Tofutti sour cream

Steps:
1. Place avocado in small bowl, and mash with fork
2. Stir in chickpeas, cilantro, lime juice, and garlic
3. Season with salt and pepper
4. Set taco shells in napkin-lined basket
5. Place salad greens, salsa, and sour cream in separate bowls, and set out alongside chickpea-avocado mash
6. To serve, let each person build their own taco by filling each shell with 1 / 4 cup chickpea-avocado mash, some greens, salsa, and sour cream<|endoftext|>
