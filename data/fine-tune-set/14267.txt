Give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Easy Baked Tofu Cubes

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Soy sauce
• Cooking sherry
• Lime juice
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Prepare the tofu by pressing between 2 plates and something heavy
2. It needs to be pressed for at least 30minutes, draining the water halfway through
3. Whisk the marinade ingredients and cube the tofu in half inch chunks
4. Let the tofu soak up the marinade for as long as you like
5. Bake with the marinade in a dish at 375 degrees for 30-45 minutes
6. Flip the pieces halfway through baking them
7. Serving ideas: in fried rice, a basic salad, pita pocket w / veggies, w / cooked broccoli<|endoftext|>
