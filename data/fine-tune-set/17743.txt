Give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple Pecan Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Canola oil
• Buttermilk
• Molasses
• Honey
• Wheat bran
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Rolled oats
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Nutmeg
• Apple
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f and grease or line a 12-cup muffin tin
2. In a large bowl, beat together the eggs, oil, buttermilk / yogurt, molasses, and honey
3. Stir in the wheat bran
4. In medium bowl, mix together the pastry flour, oats, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, ginger, and nutmeg
5. Stir the flour mixture into the bran mixture, being careful not to overmix
6. Fold in the apple and pecans
7. Spoon the batter into muffin tin and bake 20 to 25 minutes, or until toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean<|endoftext|>
