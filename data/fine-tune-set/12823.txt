Can you give me a steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Pork Steaks

Ingredients:
• Pork steaks
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Dried chipotle powder
• Paprika
• Brown sugar
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Rub steaks on both sides- about 1 tsp per side
2. Rest in fridge for 30 minutes prepare smoker with 1 / 2 mesquite and 1 / 2 hickory wood chunks and smoke meat for at least 1 hour at a temperature of about 250
3. Turn once or twice
4. Add more wood if necessary
5. Remove steaks from smoker to a roasting pan
6. Brush on 1 / 2 of the bbq sauce
7. Cover with foil and cook in oven for 90 minutes@300
8. Remove from oven a brush on a light coating of more sauce
9. Cook on a medium-heat charcoal or gas bbq, turning, adding more sauce a few times, enough to get a nice caramailzed, dark crust, usually about 10-15 minutes
10. Eat, enjoy<|endoftext|>
