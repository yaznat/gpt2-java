Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Basic Pancake Recipe

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Unsalted butter
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl
2. In another bowl, whisk the milk, butter, eggs, and vanilla
3. Pour the mixture into the large bowl and whisk just enough to combine thoroughly
4. Heat the griddle until a few drops of water tossed onto the surface runs around frantically
5. Melt a pad of butter onto the griddle and, using a paper towel, rub the butter around until it evenly coats the pan and lubricates it
6. Scoop 1 / 3 cups of batter onto the griddle to make a pancake
7. Cook the first side until bubbles cover the pancake surface and begin to pop, then flip the pancake over
8. Once the second side has lightly browned, remove and serve<|endoftext|>
