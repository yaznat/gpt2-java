What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Amaretto Iced Coffee

Ingredients:
• Strong coffee
• Vanilla ice cream
• Milk
• Amaretto
• Brandy
• Vanilla extract
• Ice
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Combine everything except ice in a blender and whip until smooth
2. Fill glasses half full with crushed ice or a few ice cubes, and fill the rest with coffee mixture
3. Sprinkle with some cinnamon or cacao
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
