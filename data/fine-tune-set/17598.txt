What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>It Still Feels Like Winter Vegan Oatmeal Muffins

Ingredients:
• Rolled oats
• Walnuts
• Water
• Apple cider vinegar
• Applesauce
• Brown sugar
• Vegan margarine
• Whole wheat flour
• Sea salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Put the water, vinegar and salt in a blender
2. Add 1 / 4 cup of the walnuts
3. Blend this mixture on high for about a minute
4. This is your nice, slightly nutty tasting "buttermilk" for your muffins
5. Pour your walnut "milk" into a large bowl
6. Add the oats and stir to blend
7. Let set about 10 minutes
8. Add the applesauce, brown sugar and margarine to the oatmeal mixture
9. Mix well
10. In a medium sized bowl, mix the flour, baking soda and baking powder together
11. Add these dry ingredients to the wet oatmeal mixture
12. Mix until well blended
13. Stir in the raisins and remaining walnuts
14. Spoon mixture into muffin tins
15. Bake at 350 for 15 to 20 minutes
16. Cool and serve!<|endoftext|>
