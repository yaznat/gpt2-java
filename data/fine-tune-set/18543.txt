What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Japanese Waffles Dora Yaki

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Light corn syrup
• Mirin
• Soy sauce
• Baking soda
• Water
• Cake flour
• Bean paste

Steps:
1. Break the eggs into a bowl, add the sugar and beat over warm water
2. Add the corn syrup, mirin and soy sauce and beat until light and fluffy
3. Add the soda mixed with water
4. Fold the flour in, alternating with the water
5. Whisk until it is the consisteny of a thick custard sauce
6. Cover and let stand 10 minutes
7. Heat a heavy iron griddle or frying pan
8. Pour on 1 tbsp salad oil and coat the pan well
9. Wipe the griddle with a paper towel
10. Pour about 3 tbsp of the batter onto the griddle and let the batter spread gradually
11. Cook over a medium-low heat until bubbles appear
12. Turn it over with a spatula and cook the other side
13. The batter browns quickly
14. When done, cool on a cake rack
15. When cool, put 2 waffles together with bean paste filling
16. You can keep these in a plastic bag in the fridge for several days
17. You can also fill them with a cream cheese and sugar mixture<|endoftext|>
