Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Pork & beans
• Lima beans
• Kidney beans
• Barbecue sauce
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Mustard
• Ketchup
• Molasses
• Salt
• Chili powder

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook bacon, beef and onion
2. Transfer into a 2 1 / 2 quart casserole dish
3. , add all of the beans and mix well
4. In a small bowl combine remaining ingredients
5. Stir into beef and bean mixture
6. Cover and bake for 45 minutes
7. Uncover and bake 15 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
