Ingredients:
• Round steaks
• Salt and pepper
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Dry onion soup mix

Steps:
1. Place steak in a greased 9x13 baking dish
2. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
3. Pour mushroom soup and 1 / 2 c water over steak and sprinkle with onion soup mix
4. Cover and bake at 325 for 2 hours<|endoftext|>
