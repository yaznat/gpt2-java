Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• White sugar
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Set oven to 375 degrees
2. Grease and flour a 9-in square baking pan, or line bottom with wax paper, and grease
3. In a large bowl, combine the oil, applesauce and cocoa
4. Add sugar, stir with a wooden spoon, until dissolved
5. Add the eggs and vanilla, beat with spoon, until combined
6. Sift all dry ingredients in a bowl
7. Stir into the applesauce / egg mixture
8. Pour the batter into a greased and floured baking pan
9. Sprinkle the chopped nuts on top of the batter
10. Bake for 20-30 minutes, or until top is set, but edges are not dried out<|endoftext|>
