Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Another Easy Pizza Sauce

Ingredients:
• Whole tomatoes
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Red pepper flakes
• Dried oregano
• Dried basil

Steps:
1. Crush the tomatoes well
2. Do not drain
3. Stir in the rest of the ingredients
4. Sauce can be made a day in advance and refrigerated
5. I usually make it in the morning and let it sit
6. This is enough sauce for 3-4 pizzas, depending on how saucy you like it!<|endoftext|>
