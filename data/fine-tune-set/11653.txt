What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>6 Minute Literal Cup Cake

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Cocoa powder
• Sugar
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• Egg

Steps:
1. Combine dry ingredients in a large microwaveable mug
2. Stir in the egg
3. Add oil, vanilla extract and milk and mix well taking care to ensure any dry ingredients aren't sticking to the sides and bottom of the mug
4. Microwave on medium / high for 3 minutes
5. Allow to cool and then enjoy on its own or with a bit of whipped cream or ice cream!<|endoftext|>
