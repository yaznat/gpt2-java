Please give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Armenian Dinner Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Paprika
• Ground allspice
• Salt and pepper
• Ground beef
• Tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Green pepper
• Fresh parsley
• Flour tortillas
• Cucumber
• Feta cheese

Steps:
1. In a nonstick skillet, heat oil over med-high heat
2. Cook onion and garlic, stirring often, for 3 minutes
3. Add paprika, allspice, salt and pepper
4. Cook, stirring, for 1 minute
5. Add beef
6. Cook, breaking up meat, for 5 minutes or until no longer pink
7. Drain off any fat
8. Add tomatoes and tomato paste, breaking up tomatoes with a fork
9. Bring to boil
10. Reduce heat to medium
11. Add green pepper and parsley
12. Cook, stirring often, for 10 to 15 minutes or until most of the liquid has evaporated
13. Divide beef mixture among tortillas
14. Fold tortillas in half to form pockets
15. Sprinkle beef filling with cucumber, and feta cheese<|endoftext|>
