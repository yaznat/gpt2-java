Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>All American Burgers

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Ketchup
• Seasoning
• Worcestershire sauce
• American cheese
• Hamburger buns
• Cooked bacon

Steps:
1. Mix ground beef, ketchup, hamburger seasoning and worcestershire sauce in bowl
2. Shape into 4 patties
3. Grill over medium heat 4 to 6 minutes per side or until burgers are cooked through
4. Add cheese slices to burgers 1 minute before cooking is completed
5. Toast rolls on the grill, open-side down, about 30 seconds
6. Serve burgers with bacon slices on toasted rolls<|endoftext|>
