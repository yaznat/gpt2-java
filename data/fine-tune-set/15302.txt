Please give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>A W Papa Burger

Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Hamburger bun with sesame seeds
• Mayonnaise
• Hamburger relish
• Iceberg lettuce
• Pickles
• American cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Mix the mayonnaise with the relish to make the "secret sauce"
2. Set aside for now
3. On waxed paper, form the 1 / 5 pound beef patty
4. It should be about 1 / 4 inch thick and 5 inches in diameter
5. Freeze at least an hour
6. Fire up the indoor electric grill, or outdoor barbecue
7. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for pre-heating
8. Toast the buns by laying them face down on the grill for 30-60 seconds
9. Cook the frozen beef patty about 2 mnutes per side, or 3 minutes per side
10. Salt and pepper liberally on both sides
11. Dress your toasted top buns in the following order:
12. Special sauce pickles lettuce cheese
13. Place the cooked beef patty on top of the dressed bun, and add the bottom
14. Microwave about 10 seconds to warm the bun, and serve
15. Or -- wrap in a 12"x12" sheet of foil, and warm in your oven 2-3 minutes--then serve
16. The super papa burger
17. Proceed as above, except cook two beef patties per burger instead of one, placing the cheese in between the patties<|endoftext|>
