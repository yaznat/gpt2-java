Please give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>50 Dollar Salad

Ingredients:
• Lime jell-o gelatin
• Boiling water
• Crushed pineapple
• Pear
• Pecans
• Marshmallows
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Vinegar
• Whipping cream

Steps:
1. Mix jell-o and boiling water until thoroughly dissolved
2. Chill until slightly thickened
3. Whip it until light and frothy
4. Fold in the fruit, nuts, and marshmallows
5. Cook the yolks, sugar and vinegar until thick
6. Cool and add to whipped jell-o mixture
7. Whip 1 / 2 pint whipping cream to soft peaks and fold into jell-o mixture
8. Pour into pan and chill overnight
9. Serve cold<|endoftext|>
