Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Walnuts
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease a 9" x 13" pan
3. Sift flour, baking powder and salt together, set aside
4. Mix brown sugar and butter, add eggs one at a time
5. Add the flour mixture, nuts, and chocolate chips
6. Spread in the pan
7. Bake 25 to 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
