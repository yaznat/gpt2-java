Ingredients:
• Baking mix
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter
• Apple

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, comine the biscuit mix and spices
2. Combine the eggs and milk in a separate bowl
3. Add the egg mixture to the dry mixture and stir until smooth
4. Stir in butter and apple
5. Bake in a preheated waffle iron until golden brown<|endoftext|>
