Ingredients:
• Endive
• Iceberg lettuce
• Spinach
• Romaine lettuce
• Kirby cucumbers
• Green onion
• Frozen peas
• Alfalfa sprout
• Green olives
• Italian parsley
• Olive oil
• Red wine vinegar
• Salt
• Cracked pepper
• Sugar
• Oregano
• Garlic clove

Steps:
1. Wash and dry a large salad bowl and rub with one clove garlic
2. Wash greens and dry well, place in bowl
3. Wash cucumbers, onions and rinse sprouts and parsley
4. Defrost frozen peas and dry
5. Chop, dice or slice, your preference
6. Place greens in bowl
7. Add cucumber, peas and onions
8. Toss lightly
9. Top with sprouts and green olives
10. Mix the dressing, oil, wine, salt, black pepper, sugar, oregano
11. Mix with whisk and pour over salad when ready to serve
12. Serve with loaf of hot italian bread
13. Very good and healthy for the new year
14. You can transform this salad into a chef salad very easily with mild cheese provolone and your choice of cooked meat
15. Bite sized pieces of chicken or shrimp
