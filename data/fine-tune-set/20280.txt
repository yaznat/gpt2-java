Ingredients:
• Star fruit
• Chicken breast
• Sherry wine
• Brown sugar
• Gingerroot
• Soy sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Cornstarch
• Vegetable oil
• Red bell peppers
• Onion
• Dark sesame oil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Cut 1 star fruit in half crosswise and squeeze 2 tablespoons juice, using a citrus reamer or juicer
2. Set juice aside
3. Or just whiz it in a food processor or blender on steroids
4. Dice star fruit halves
5. Set aside
6. Slice remaining 3 star fruit crosswise and set aside
7. Combine 2 tablespoons star fruit juice, chicken, sherry, brown sugar, ginger root, soy sauce and garlic in a bowl and stir well
8. Cover and chill 30 minutes
9. Drain chicken, reserving marinade
10. Sprinkle chicken with cornstarch
11. Toss well to coat
12. Heat vegetable oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium high heat
13. Add chicken and star fruit
14. Stir fry 1 minute
15. Add bell pepper and onion and stir fry 3 minutes or until chicken is done
16. Add reserved marinade, sesame oil, salt and pepper
17. Stir fry 1 minute
18. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
