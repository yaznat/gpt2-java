What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Almond Lace Cookies

Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Flour
• Sliced almonds
• Egg whites
• Butter
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In a large bowl mix together the sugar, flour and almonds
2. Stir in the egg white, butter and vanilla until blended
3. Drop dough by tablespoonfuls 3" apart on parchment lined baking sheets
4. Bake in a preheated oven for 10-12 minutes
5. Let cool on the baking sheets and then remove from paper
6. Store in an airtight container<|endoftext|>
