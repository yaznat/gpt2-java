Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Eggs
• Heavy cream
• Apple cider
• Light brown sugar
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Cinnamon raisin bread
• Granny smith apple
• Dried apple
• Sugar
• Hard alcoholic cider
• Egg yolks

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees f
2. Grease a 10 by 14-inch baking dish with the butter and set aside
3. In a large bowl, beat the eggs
4. Add the cream, cider, sugar, melted butter, salt, and cinnamon, and whisk to combine
5. Add the bread, fresh and dried apples, and stir to combine
6. Pour into the prepared dish, cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate until the bread is well saturated, up to 1 hour
7. )
8. Bake until the top is golden brown and the center is firm, about 1 hour
9. Remove from the oven and let sit on a wire rack for 15 minutes
10. Cut into pieces and drizzle with the hard cider sauce
11. Serve immediately
12. Hard cider sauce:
13. In the top of a double boiler, melt the butter over simmering water
14. Add the sugar and whisk to combine, whisking for 1 minute
15. Add the cider and whisk until the sugar is dissolved, 2 minutes
16. Remove from the heat and add the egg yolks, 1 at a time, whisking constantly
17. Return to the heat and continue whisking until the sauce is pale and slightly thickened, 5 minutes
18. Remove from the heat
19. Spoon the sauce over the pudding and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
