Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Beef For The Gang

Ingredients:
• Beef
• Onion
• Catsup
• Pepper
• Vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Place meat in a sauce pan
2. Mix the rest of ingredients
3. Add to meat
4. Add 1 / 2c water or less to almost cover the meat
5. Simmer until onions are done
6. You can either simmer off the water or thicken with flour if it's to runny
7. Serve on buns<|endoftext|>
