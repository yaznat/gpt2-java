What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Brownie Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-bran cereal
• Water
• Low-fat brownie mix
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Soak all bran in water for a couple of minutes
2. Add baking powder to cereal and stir
3. Add brownie mix and stir to combine
4. Spoon mix into medium sized muffin tins lined with muffin liners or sprayed with nonstick cooking spray
5. Eacg muffin cup should be 3 / 4 full
6. Bake in oven @ 350 for about 20-25 minutes<|endoftext|>
