Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Buttermilk Pancakes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• White sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Milk
• Eggs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Directions:
2. In a large bowl, combine flour, baking powder and baking soda
3. Stir well
4. In a separate bowl, beat together buttermilk, milk, eggs and melted butter
5. Keep the two mixtures separate until you are ready to cook
6. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium high heat
7. You can flick water across the surface and if it beads up and sizzles, it's ready!
8. Pour the wet mixture into the dry mixture, using a wooden spoon or fork to blend
9. Stir until it's just blended together
10. Do not over stir!
11. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1 / 2 cup for each pancake
12. Brown on both sides and serve hot
13. Freeze the leftovers
14. From freezer, just reheat in microwave<|endoftext|>
