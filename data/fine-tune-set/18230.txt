Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Banana Walnut Vegan Yummy Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Soymilk
• Vinegar
• Canola oil
• Vanilla
• Bananas
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Sift together first 5 ingredients
2. In a separate bowl combine soy milk and vinegar
3. Let sit for five minutes
4. Mix bananas and other wet ingredients until pretty smooth
5. Poor wet into dry -- but don't overmix! fold in the walnuts
6. Brush a large nonstick skillet with canola oil and heat over moderate heat
7. Pour about 1 / 4 cup batter per pancake into hot skillet and cook until bubbles appear on surface and undersides are golden brown, 1 to 2 minutes
8. [you should get nine pancakes, allow three per serving]
9. Flip pancakes with a spatula till cooked through, 1 to 2 minutes more
10. Transfer to a large plate and loosely cover with foil to keep warm
11. Serve with sliced bananas and maple syrup<|endoftext|>
