Ingredients:
• Fresh green beans
• Bacon
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Garlic salt
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Place beans in a large saucepan and cover with water
2. Bring to a boil
3. Cook, uncovered, for 8 minutes or until crisp-tender
4. Meanwhile, in a skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until cooked but not crisp, about 3 minutes
5. Remove to paper towels
6. Drain beans
7. Place about 12 beans on each bacon strip
8. Wrap bacon around beans and secure with a toothpick
9. Place on an ungreased baking sheet
10. In a small bowl, combine the butter, brown sugar, garlic salt and soy sauce
11. Brush over bundles
12. Bake at 400 for 10-15 minutes or until bacon is crisp<|endoftext|>
