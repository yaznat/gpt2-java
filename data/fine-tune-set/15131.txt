Ingredients:
• Low-fat sour cream
• Part-skim ricotta cheese
• Reduced-fat mozzarella cheese
• Herb with garlic soup mix
• Red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients well
2. Place in a casserole dish and bake at 350 for 25 - 30 minutes
3. Serve with sliced italian bread or soft italian breadsticks<|endoftext|>
