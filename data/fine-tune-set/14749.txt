Ingredients:
• Uncle ben's converted brand rice
• Chicken stock
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Green onion
• Flat leaf parsley
• Plum tomato
• Parmesan cheese
• Mayonnaise
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Cook rice in the chicken stock according to directions on box & cool
2. Chop artichoke hearts to bite size
3. Add artichokes, green onions, parsley, tomatoes and parmesan to rice
4. Mix together mayonnaise & marinade and blend into rice mixture
5. Season w / salt & pepper<|endoftext|>
