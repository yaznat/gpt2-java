Please give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Australian Ground Beef Rarebit

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Ground beef
• Salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Beer
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• English muffin

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in a deep skillet over medium heat
2. Add onions and peppers and saute 5 minutes
3. Add meat and cook until browned, stirring almost constantly
4. Add salt, cayenne and beer
5. Add cheese, stirring until melted
6. Serve over toasted english muffins<|endoftext|>
