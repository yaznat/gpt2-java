What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Almond Stuffed Tofu Cubes

Ingredients:
• Slivered almonds
• Firm tofu
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Water
• Egg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350-degrees
2. Oven toast almonds on shallow baking sheet 8- 10 minutes, until golden brown
3. Press an almond sliver through the center of each tofu cube
4. Place cubes between paper towels to drain and set aside
5. In a medium bowl, mix together bread crumbs, parmesan cheese, garlic, salt and pepper
6. Using a cocktail pick, spear cubes, dip into egg mixture, then into crumbs, covering completely
7. Place on a baking sheet and bake 25 minutes, turning once, until golden brown
8. Tofu cubes may be made ahead and reheated for about 6 minutes in a 350-degree oven<|endoftext|>
