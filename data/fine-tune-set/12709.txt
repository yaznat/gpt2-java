Give me a recipe for steak.<|endoftext|>Amazing Hamburger Steak With Gravy

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Dry steak seasoning
• Garlic clove
• Oatmeal
• Milk
• Eggs
• Salt and pepper
• Red wine
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Mix the hamburger steak ingredients thoroughly
2. Let rest 5 minutes
3. Form meat into 6-8, oval patties
4. Heat a little olive oil in a large skillet on medium-high
5. Cook hamburger steaks over medium-high heat so that they are well-browned and cooked through
6. There will probably be 2 or 3 batches depending on skillet size
7. Pour off excess grease
8. Deglaze pan with the wine -- scraping up the good, brown bits
9. Shake milk and cornstarch together well and add to pan
10. Turn heat down
11. Bring gravy to a simmer, stirring constantly
12. Serve with rice, mashed potatoes, bread, or anything else that tastes good under gravy
13. If you do not have genuine stock a good soup base will give better results than bullion cubes
14. Or use the dripping from roasts or pot roasts, thinned down with up to 50% water<|endoftext|>
