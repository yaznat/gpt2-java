What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Blender Waffles Or Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Margarine

Steps:
1. Put all the ingredients in the blender and blend on a medium setting for 30 seconds
2. Scrape sides of blender if necessary and blend another 30 seconds
3. When batter is smooth, pour by 1 / 4 cup measure onto hot griddle or waffle iron
4. Can be stored in refrigerator for 4-5 days
5. Yogurt can be used with or instead of the buttermilk<|endoftext|>
