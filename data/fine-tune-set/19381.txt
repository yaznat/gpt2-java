Ingredients:
• Quick-rising yeast
• Tap water
• Milk
• Sugar
• Salt
• Egg
• Shortening
• All-purpose flour
• Vegetable shortening
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Confectioners' sugar
• Evaporated milk

Steps:
1. Dissolve yeast in warm water in mixing bowl
2. Add milk, sugar, salt, egg, shortening and 1 cup of flour
3. Beat on low speed scraping bowl constantly, 30 seconds
4. Beat on medium speed, scraping bowl occasionally, 2 minutes
5. Stir in remaining flour until smooth
6. Cover and let rise in warm place until double, approximately 50-60 minutes
7. Turn dough onto floured surface
8. Roll dough 1 / 2-inch thick
9. Cut with round cookie cutter
10. Cover and let rise on floured baking sheets until double, 30-40 minutes
11. Heat vegetable oil in deep fryer or dutch oven to 350 degrees f
12. Slide doughnuts into hot oil
13. Turn doughnuts as they turn golden brown, about one minute on each side
14. Remove carefully from oil and drain
15. When cool, make small hole to insert vanilla filling
16. Take a sharp narrow knife and carefully make a large cavity inside of the doughnut to hold the filling
17. Fill the doughnuts generously and dust heavily with confectioners' sugar
18. Vanilla filling: cream butter and shortening, gradually add sugar 1 / 2 cup at a time
19. Add milk and vanilla, beat until light and fluffy<|endoftext|>
