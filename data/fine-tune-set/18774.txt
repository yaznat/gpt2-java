Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Buttermilk
• Fresh lemon zest
• Lemon juice
• Butter
• Eggs
• Blueberries
• Cooking spray
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Combine flour, 2 / 3 c sugar, salt and nutmeg in a large bowl
2. Add buttermilk, lemon rind, lemon juice, butter, and egg yolks stirring with whisk til smooth
3. Beat egg whites at high speed til foamy, add 1 / 4 c sugar 1 tablespoon at a time, beating til stiff peaks form
4. Gently stir 1 / 4 egg white mixture into buttermilk mixture, then gently fold in remaining egg white mixture
5. Fold in berries
6. Pour batter into sprayed 8 inch square baking pan
7. Place in larger baking pan add hot water to larger pan to depth of 1 inch
8. Bake 350 degrees f for 35 minutes or til cake springs back when lightly touched in the center
9. Let cool
10. Serve warm, shift powdered sugar over top<|endoftext|>
