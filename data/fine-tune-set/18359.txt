What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Belgian Waffle Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Eggs
• Flour

Steps:
1. Preheat waffle maker
2. Place butter and sugar in a large mixing bowl
3. Cream together on med-low
4. Add vanilla and salt, increase speed to medium
5. Add eggs and mix until smooth
6. Decrease speed to low, add flour and mix well
7. Divide batter into 4 3 / 4 cup portions
8. Using one portion, drop batter on each corner of the grids
9. Close waffle maker and bake until steam no longer escapes
10. Carefully remove waffle from maker and allow to cool
11. Serve plain or with your favorite fruit filling and whipped cream<|endoftext|>
