Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Cornstarch
• Ground cinnamon
• Brown sugar
• Apples
• Dried cranberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Place the tenderloin in a roasting pan or casserole dish
3. Combine the remaining ingredients together and stir
4. Spoon the apple mixture around the tenderloin, cover and bake for 30 minutes
5. Remove the lid and spoon the apple mix over top of the tenderloin
6. Place back in the oven and bake 15 to 20 minutes or until tenderloin registers at least 155 to 160 degrees on a meat thermometer<|endoftext|>
