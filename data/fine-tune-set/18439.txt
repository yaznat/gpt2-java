Ingredients:
• Almond milk
• Apple cider vinegar
• Cornmeal
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Preheat waffle iron
2. Measure milk in large measuring cup, add vinegar and set aside to curdle
3. In a large mixing bowl, mix cornmeal, flour, baking powder, salt and sugar
4. Make well in center and add milk mixture and oil
5. Mix until smooth
6. Cook waffles according to waffle iron directions!<|endoftext|>
