Give me a pork recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Braised Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork chops
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Olive oil
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Apple juice
• Dried tart cherry

Steps:
1. In a heavy skillet, saute onion and garlic in olive oil until just about ready to carmelize
2. Remove vegetables from skillet and set aside
3. Season chops with salt and pepper
4. Dredge with flour on both sides
5. Brown chops for about five minutes on each side
6. After browning both sides, pour apple juice over chops
7. Let the chops cook in the juice for another five minutes over med-low heat
8. Do not cover
9. Remove chops and add onion and garlic back into the pan
10. Reduce sauce slightly, adding dried cherries in last 2-3 minutes of cooking
11. To serve, plate chops with wild rice and spoon a bit of the sauce over each chop
12. Depending on thickness of the chops, you may need to adjust cooking time slightly<|endoftext|>
