Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Bacon
• Blue cheese

Steps:
1. Mix the meat with the worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper and form into 8 patties
2. Combine the bacon and blue cheese and divide between half the patties
3. Place the remaining patties on top and press the edges to seal
4. Grill over hot coals, pan fry, or broil until cooked through, turning once halfway through cooking<|endoftext|>
