Ingredients:
• Boneless leg of lamb
• Garlic cloves
• Fennel seeds
• Cumin seeds
• Coriander seeds
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Lemon wedge

Steps:
1. Rub the lamb with the cut side of the garlic cloves
2. Discard garlic
3. In a mortar with pestle, crush fennel, cumin, and coriander seeds
4. In a small bowl combine crushed seeds with olive oil and salt using your hands rub the lamb with the crushed seed mixture place lamb on bbq over medium heat
5. Cook 15-20 minutes turning occasionally
6. Or cook until it reaches your preferred doneness
7. Thickness of butterflied lamb will vary throughout: cut off sections of lamb as they are cooked to your liking and keep warm
8. If desired serve with lemon wedges<|endoftext|>
