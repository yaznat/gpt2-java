What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Almond Crescent Cookies

Ingredients:
• Almonds
• Confectioners' sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Almond extract
• Egg
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Put ground nuts, sugar, 1 cup flour, almond extract, egg, cinnamon, and butter in mixing bowl and mix well
3. Knead mixture into a dough
4. Pour seeds into pie pan
5. Pinch off walnut size pieces of dough
6. Roll dough pieces in seeds to coat, and then, between the palms of your hands, roll pieces into about 2 inch long cylindrical shapes, thicker in the middle, tapering at the ends
7. Place side by side onto lightly greased cookie sheet, curving slightly into crescents
8. Bake in oven for about 30 minutes or until golden brown
