Give me a pizza recipe.<|endoftext|>A Different Pizza

Ingredients:
• Sausage
• Onion
• Flour
• Italian seasoning
• Salt
• Pepper
• Milk
• Eggs
• Pizza sauce
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Brown sausage or hamburger, with one medium onion, set aside
2. Mix flour, italian seasoning, dash salt and pepper, milk, and eggs
3. Pour batter into a 12" round pizza pan sprayed with pam
4. Top with the browned meat and onion
5. Bake at 425 for 20-25 min
6. Take out of oven,
7. Drizzle one can of pizza sauce over this
8. Sprinkle 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese on top
9. Bake for another 12 to 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
