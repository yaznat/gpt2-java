Ingredients:
• Sushi rice
• Carrot
• Cream cheese

Steps:
1. Lay the rice on a bamboo mat, in a rectangle shape
2. Spread half the cream cheese on the top half of the rice
3. Top with half the carrot
4. Roll to form a log
5. Refrigerate for at least 20 minutes, then cut into 6 slices<|endoftext|>
