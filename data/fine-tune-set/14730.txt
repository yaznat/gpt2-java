Give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>Arroz Con Leche Rice With Milk

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Evaporated milk
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Stovetop: place all ingredients in a pot, stir together and cook on medium low for about five minutes stirring occasionally as so it does not scorch
2. This should be somewhat soupy
3. Warms up nicely
4. Microwave: same as above, but in microwave safe bowl, microwave for about 2 minutes stirring occasionally so it doesn't boil over
5. You can add whatever fruits that you want to it, dried fruits are great<|endoftext|>
