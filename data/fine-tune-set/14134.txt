• Prepared mustard

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f / 200c
2. Chop the cauliflower florets and pour into a non-stick casserole dish
3. Mix together the marinade and pour over the tofu
4. Mix well
5. Pour tofu over the chopped cauliflower
6. Bake about 15 minutes
7. Cheese sauce: blend all ingredients until smooth
8. Transfer to a small saucepan
9. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thick
