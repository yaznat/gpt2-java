Give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple Lavender Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Lavender flowers
• Butter
• Egg
• Apple

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Coat 12 muffin tins with cooking spray or line with muffin cups
3. In large bowl, sift together flour
4. Sugar
5. Baking powder and salt
6. Make a well in center
7. In separate bowl, whisk together milk, lavender
8. Butter and egg
9. Add milk mixture to flour mixture and stir by hand just until batter is evenly moistened
10. Fold in 1 / 2 cup of the apples
11. Fill prepared muffin tins about three-quarters full
12. Gently tap filled tins to release any air bubbles
13. Sprinkle remaining diced apple over muffins
14. Bake until skewer inserted into center of a muffin comes our clean, 25 to 30 minutes
15. Cool muffins in pan about 10 minutes, then transfer to cooling rack<|endoftext|>
