Ingredients:
• White chocolate baking squares
• Unsalted butter
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Dried apricot
• Sliced almonds
• Flaked coconut

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f, then grease a 9-inch square baking pan
2. In a saucepan, melt chocolate & butter over low heat, stirring constantly, until all the chocolate is melted
3. Remove from heat & stir in brown sugar, eggs & vanilla until blended, then set aside
4. In a bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder & salt, then stir in chocolate mixture
5. In another bowl, combine apricots, almonds & coconut, then stir half of this apricot mixture into the batter
6. Pour batter into the prepared baking pan, & sprinkle remaining apricot mixture on top
7. Bake for 25 minutes or until golden brown
8. Remove pan to wire rack & let cool before slicing<|endoftext|>
