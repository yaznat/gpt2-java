Can you give me a tea recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Tea Hot Toddy

Ingredients:
• Apple juice
• Brewed black tea
• Calvados
• Cinnamon sticks

Steps:
1. Boil apple juice in a 4-5 quart heavy pot over high heat until syrupy and reduced to 1 1 / 2 cups, about 50 minutes
2. Combine apple syrup, tea and calvados in a 2 quart saucepan and heat over moderate heat until hot
3. Divide toddy among 8 small cups and serve with a cinnamon stick in each<|endoftext|>
