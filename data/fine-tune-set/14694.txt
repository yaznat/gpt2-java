What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Arabic Rice

Ingredients:
• Basmati rice
• Vermicelli
• Hot chicken broth
• Salt
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Saute vermicelli in oil on low heat, stirring constantly until brown
2. Add rice, salt, mix, add hot chicken broth or hot water, stir once, bring to boil
3. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes or until rice is tender and broth is absorbed
4. Ready to serve
5. Excellent with any stew recipe or any main dish<|endoftext|>
