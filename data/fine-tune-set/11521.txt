Give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>Ann S Heavenly Whole Wheat Oatmeal Bread

Ingredients:
• Warm water
• Dry yeast
• Hot water
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Oil
• White flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Oatmeal

Steps:
1. Soften yeast in warm water
2. Combine hot water, brown sugar or honey, salt, and oil or melted butter
3. Cool until lukewarm, then add softened yeast
4. Pour into a large pan
5. Add the flours and oatmeal, stirring well
6. Turn out and knead lightly
7. Dough will be sticky
8. Let dough rise in warm place for 45 minutes to an hour
9. Punch down
10. Grease 2 large loaf pans well
11. Divide and shape dough into two loaves and put into pans
12. Let rise until light, about 30 to 45 minutes
13. Bake at 375 degrees f for 35 to 40 minutes
14. Let cool on racks<|endoftext|>
