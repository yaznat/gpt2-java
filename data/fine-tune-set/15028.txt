What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Shiitake And Sweet Pepper Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Shiitake mushrooms
• Shallots
• Garlic
• Red bell peppers
• Yellow bell peppers
• Green bell peppers
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Light soy sauce
• Green onions
• Sugar
• Rice vinegar
• Sesame oil
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat oil in rondo or large braising pan until hot
2. Add mushrooms, shallots, garlic and bell peppers to pan
3. Saute until mushrooms are softened
4. Add rice to pan
5. Cook and stir 2 to 3 minutes or until rice is hot
6. Stir in soy sauce, green onions, sugar and rice vinegar
7. Cook, stirring occasionally, about 5 minutes
8. Stir in sesame oil and season with salt
9. Spread rice mixture on sheet pan to cool
10. Cover and refrigerate until ready to use<|endoftext|>
