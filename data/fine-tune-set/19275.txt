Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Almonds
• Walnuts
• Carob powder
• Dried coconut

Steps:
1. Place all of the ingredients on seperate plates and then roll one banana at a time through each of the ingredients
2. Press the nuts in a bit so they stick
3. Once each banana is loaded with goodies place on a piece of wax paper and freeze
