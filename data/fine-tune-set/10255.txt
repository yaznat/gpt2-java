Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Light corn syrup
• Creamy peanut butter
• Corn flakes

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, mix sugar and corn syrup and bring to a boil, stirring frequently
2. Boil for 1 minute
3. Remove from heat and blend in peanut butter
4. Add cornflakes, stirring gently to coat
5. Working quickly, drop mixture in 1 / 4 cup lumps, 2" apart onto countertop lined with waxed paper
6. Using fingers, shape each cookie into rounded mound as necessary
7. Let stand at room temp for 20-30 minutes to set
8. Store in airtight container at room temperature<|endoftext|>
