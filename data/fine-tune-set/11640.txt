Ingredients:
• Flour
• Butter
• Powdered sugar
• Cream cheese
• Cool whip
• Fresh blueberries
• Strawberry
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Mix with a pastry blender flour, butter and powdered sugar
3. Push dough evenly onto a baking sheet
4. Bake about 10 minutes or until golden brown
5. Mix powdered sugar, softened cream cheese and cool whip with a hand mixer until creamy and well blended
6. Spread mixture over crust after it has cooled
7. Reserve a small amount for "stars

9. Finish with arranging blueberries and strawberries into american flag
