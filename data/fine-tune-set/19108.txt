Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Ben Jerry S Chocolate Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Milk
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Heavy cream
• Vanilla
• Salt

Steps:
1. Gradually whisk chocolate into the milk and heat, stirring constantly, until smooth
2. Remove from the heat and let cool
3. Whisk the eggs in a mixing bowl until light anf fluffy, 1-2 minutes
4. Whisk in the sugar, a little at a time, then continue whisking until completely blended, about 1 minute more
5. Add the cream, vanilla and salt and whisk to blend
6. Pour the chocolate mixture into the cream mixture and blend
7. Cover and refrigerate until cold, about 1-3 hours, depending on your refrigerator
8. Transfer the mixture to an ice cream maker and freeze following the manufacturer's instructions<|endoftext|>
