Ingredients:
• Granny smith apple
• Pancake mix
• Cinnamon
• Egg
• Oil
• Milk

Steps:
1. Peel, core and slice apples using apple / peeler / corer / slicer
2. Or core the apple, peel it, and cut it into thin rings
3. Set aside
4. Combine remaining ingredients
5. Heat lightly greased griddle over medium heat
6. Place an apple ring on the griddle
7. Slowly pour 1 / 4 cup batter over apple ring, starting in center
8. Use just enough batter to cover each ring, less then 1 / 4 cup
9. Repeat with additional apple slices
10. Cook until bubbles begin to appear
11. Turn, cook other side until lightly browned
12. If any apple juice stays on griddle, wipe it off with lightly oiled paper
13. Towel
14. Serve pancakes with butter and maple flavored syrup<|endoftext|>
