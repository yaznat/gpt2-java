What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Cheesy Jack Tacos

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Campbell's cream of chicken soup
• Water
• Taco seasoning mix
• Taco shells
• Monterey jack cheese
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Cook the beef in a 10 -inch skillet over medium-high heat until well browned, stirring often to separate meat
2. Pour off any fat
3. Stir the soup, water and taco seasoning mix in the skillet and cook until the mixture is hot and bubbling
4. Spoon about 1 / 3 cup beef mixture into the taco shells
5. Top with the cheese and lettuce<|endoftext|>
