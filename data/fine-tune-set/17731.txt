Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple Yogurt Muffins

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Butter
• Ground cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Nonfat vanilla yogurt
• Egg
• Vegetable oil
• Low-fat milk
• Apple
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Place baking cups in a muffin pan
3. For topping: combine topping ingredients until crumbly
4. Set aside
5. For muffins: combine flour, sugar, baking powder, salt and cinnamon in large bowl
6. Beat together yogurt, egg, oil and milk in small bowl
7. Add to flour mixture
8. Stir just until dry ingredients are moistened
9. Gently stir in apples and raisins
10. Spoon batter into bake cups, filling even with top of bake cups
11. Sprinkle topping over each muffin
12. Bake 23 to 25 minutes or until golden brown
13. Cool in pan<|endoftext|>
