Ingredients:
• Tomatoes
• Green onion
• Pickled jalapeno peppers
• Fat free tortillas
• Reduced-fat cheddar cheese
• No-salt-added salsa

Steps:
1. Divide first three ingredients evenly among tortillas, arranging just off center of each
2. Top each evenly with cheese
3. Coat a medium sized nonstick skillet with vegetable cooking spray and place over medium heat until hot
4. Place one tortilla in skillet
5. Cooke 1 minute or until bottom of tortilla is golden
6. Fold tortilla in half
7. Cook 30 seconds or until cheese melts
8. Repeat for all quesadillas
9. Cut each quesadilla into 4 wedges
10. Top wedges with salsa<|endoftext|>
