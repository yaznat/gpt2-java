Ingredients:
• Ground cumin
• Garlic cloves
• Paprika
• Whole black peppercorn
• Pork tenderloin
• White pearl onions
• Cardamom seed
• Bay leaf
• Zucchini
• Olive oil
• Fresh figs

Steps:
1. In small bowl combine cumin, garlic, paprika, pepper, and 1 / 4 teaspoon of salt
2. Cut tenderloin crosswise into -inch thick slices
3. Rub the cumin mixture over surfaces of tenderloin and set aside
4. In large nonstick skillet, combine onion, cardamom, bay leaf, 1 / 2 teaspoon of salt and 1 / 2 cup of water
5. Bring to boil over high heat
6. Reduce heat
7. Cover and cook for 5 minutes
8. Add zucchini and cover and cook 3 to 4 minutes or until zucchini and onion are just tender
9. Transfer mixture to a bowl and keep warm
10. Wipe skillet clean
11. In skillet, heat oil over medium high heat
12. Add tenderloin slices
13. Cook for 6 minutes or until pork is just slightly pink in center, turning once
14. Remove from skillet and keep warm
15. Place figs in skillet cook for about 1 minute or until just heated through set aside
16. To serve, arrange pork on 4 plates
17. Serve zucchini and onion mixture with pork and figs<|endoftext|>
