Ingredients:
• Lean hamburger
• Hormel chili without beans
• Taco seasoning
• Ritz crackers
• Egg
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees
2. Combine the hamburger and crushed crackers in a large bowl
3. Next, add the chili and egg
4. I find that mixing with a fork is best
5. Now add the chopped onion and green pepper, salt, and pepper
6. Mix well
7. Form into 1
8. 5 to 2 inch balls and place them into two 9 x 13 baking dishes sprayed well with a non-stick coating
9. There will 12 in one dish and approximately 5 in the other
10. Place in the preheated oven
11. Cook for 55 minutes
12. Carefully remove from the oven and top each of the meatballs with the shredded cheese mixture
13. Return to the oven for 5 minutes to melt the cheese
14. Remove from the oven and cool for 5 minutes
15. We enjoyed these best when served with tortilla chips, salsa, and jalepenos<|endoftext|>
