Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus Nut Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Asparagus spears
• Olive oil
• Red sweet bell pepper
• Walnuts
• Ground ginger
• Garlic
• Red pepper flakes
• Water
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Sugar
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a skillet or wok, stir-fry asparagus in oil until crisp-tender
2. Remove and keep warm
3. In the same skillet, stir-fry red pepper, walnuts, ginger, garlic, and pepper flakes for 2 minutes or until red pepper is crisp-tender
4. Stir in water, soy sauce, sugar, and salt
5. Heat through
6. Add asparagus
7. Stir to coat<|endoftext|>
