What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Florentine Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Fresh linguine
• Pesto sauce
• Lemon juice
• Fresh spinach
• Tomatoes
• Red onion
• Pine nuts
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook linguine according to package directions
2. Drain and cool under cold running water
3. Drain thoroughly
4. In a large mixing bowl, combine pesto sauce and lemon juice
5. Add linguine
6. Toss well
7. Add spinach, tomato, and onion
8. Toss until coated well
9. Spoon onto individual plates or a large platter
10. Sprinkle w / pine nuts and top w / grated parmesan cheese
11. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
