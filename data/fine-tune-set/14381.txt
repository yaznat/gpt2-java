Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Baked beans
• Tomato juice
• V8 vegetable juice
• Chili seasoning mix

Steps:
1. In a dutch oven, cook beef over medium heat until no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Stir in the remaining ingredients
4. Bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat
6. Simmer, uncovered for 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
