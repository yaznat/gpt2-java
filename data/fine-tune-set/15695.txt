Ingredients:
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Cooking oil
• Taco shells
• Romaine lettuce
• Monterey jack cheese
• Kiwi fruits
• Tomatoes
• Lime juice

Steps:
1. Combine cumin, salt, and red pepper in a small bowl
2. Brush chicken breasts with oil
3. Rub brushed chicken breasts with cumin mixture
4. Place each breast half between 2 pieces of plastic wrap
5. Gently pound with a meat mallet to 1 / 2 inch thickness
6. Place chicken on the lightly oiled rack of an uncovered grill directly over medium-high heat
7. Grill for 6 to 8 minutes or until an instant-read thermometer registers 170 degree f and juices run clear, turning once
8. Remove chicken
9. Set aside until cool enough to handle
10. Meanwhile, if using purchased taco shells heat shells according to the package directions
11. Cut chicken into thin strips and arrange in taco shells
12. Top with lettuce and cheese
13. Toss together chopped kiwifruit, tomato, and lime or lemon juice in a small bowl
14. Sprinkle atop tacos
15. Makes 6 tacos
