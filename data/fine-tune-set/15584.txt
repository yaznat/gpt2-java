Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning mix
• Bisquick baking mix
• Cold water
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Lettuce
• Chopped tomato

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 450
2. Grease square pan, 8x8x2 inches
3. Cook beef as directed on envelope of taco seasoning mix
4. Mix baking mix and cold water till smooth
5. Spread in pan
6. Spread beef mixture over dough
7. Bake uncovered 25 to 30 minutes or until edges are golden brown and toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
8. Immediately sprinkle with cheese
9. Let stand 1-2 minutes or until cheese is melted
10. Serve with sour cream, lettuce and tomatoes<|endoftext|>
