What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Blender Cheese Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Low fat cottage cheese
• Skim milk
• Vanilla extract
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Combine eggs, cottage cheese, milk and vanilla in blender container
2. Cover
3. Blend until smooth
4. Add flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt
5. Blend on low speed just until mixed
6. Spray nonstick skillet with cooking spray
7. Heat over moderate heat
8. Spoon the batter into hot skillet to make 4-inch pancakes
9. Cook over low heat until bubbles form on surface
10. Turn, cook other side until bottom is golden brown<|endoftext|>
