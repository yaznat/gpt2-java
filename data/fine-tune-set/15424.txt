Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Hot water
• Milk
• Hamburger helper cheeseburger macaroni
• Barbecue sauce
• Cheddar cheese
• Bacon

Steps:
1. In 10-inch skillet, cook beef and onion over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until beef is brown
2. Drain
3. Stir in hot water, milk, uncooked pasta, sauce mix and barbecue sauce
4. Heat to boiling, stirring occasionally
5. Reduce heat
6. Cover and simmer 13-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until pasta is tender
7. Remove from heat and uncover
8. Sprinkle with cheese and bacon
9. Sprinkle with additional chopped onion if desired
