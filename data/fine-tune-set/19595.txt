Ingredients:
• Frozen hash brown potatoes
• Butter
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Onion
• Salt
• Pepper
• Corn flakes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. In a large bowl, combine hash browns, 1 / 2 cup melted butter, cheddar cheese soup, sour cream, chopped onion, cheddar cheese, salt and pepper
3. Place mixture in a 3 quart casserole dish
4. Mix crushed corn flakes and 1 / 4 cup melted butter together and sprinkle the mixture over the top of the casserole
5. Baked covered in preheated oven for 45 minutes
6. Can be made in crockpot
7. Cook on high for 3 - 4 hours or on low for 7 - 8 hours<|endoftext|>
