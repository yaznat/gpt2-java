What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Banana Egg Rolls With Custard Sauce

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Egg roll wraps
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Crystallized ginger
• Cooking oil
• Egg
• Milk
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Peel the bananas and quarter lengthwise
2. Lay egg roll wrappers on a flat surface with the point facing you
3. Arrange 2 of the banana quarters on center of wrapper
4. Sprinkle with 1 / 2 tablespoon chocolate and / or crystallized ginger
5. Fold and seal as directed on egg roll package
6. In a deep skillet, pour the oil 2 inches deep
7. Heat to 365f deep-fry one at a time, for 1 to 1 1 / 2 minutes or till very golden brown
8. Drain on paper towels
9. Keep warm in a 250f oven till all are fried
10. Serve warm with custard sauce
11. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
