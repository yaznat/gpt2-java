Give me a stew recipe.<|endoftext|>Beef Stew Bake

Ingredients:
• Stewing beef
• Shortening
• Mushroom beef gravy
• Tomato juice
• Dry onion soup mix
• Prepared horseradish
• Potatoes

Steps:
1. In heavy skillet, brown stew meat, in 2 tbsp hot shortening
2. Drain off excess fat
3. Add mushroom gravy, tomato juice, dry onion soup mix and horseradish
4. Simmer, uncovered, for 5 minutes
5. Place 4 medium potatoes, pared and quartered in the bottom of a 2-quart casserole
6. Top with meat mixture
7. Bake, covered, at 350 for 1 1 / 2 hours or til meat and potatoes are tender, stirring once or twice during baking<|endoftext|>
