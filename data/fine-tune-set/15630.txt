Ingredients:
• Taco sauce
• Refrigerated buttermilk biscuits
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Sliced ripe olives
• Lean ground beef
• Red bell pepper
• Green pepper
• Mushroom stems and pieces

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400 degrees
2. Lightly grease 13 x 9 baking dish
3. Spread taco sauce evenly over bottom of greased dish
4. Separate dough into 10 biscuits
5. Cut each biscuit into 4 pieces
6. Place biscuit pieces in taco sauce, turn to coat
7. Sprinkle biscuits with l / 2 cup to 1 cup of each of the cheeses and olives
8. Mix gently
9. Bake 15 to 18 minutes or until bubbly
10. Meanwhile, in large skillet, combine ground beef, peppers and mushrooms
11. Cook until beef is thoroughly cooked, drain
12. Once baked, sprinkle remaining cheeses over mixture in casserole
13. Top with ground beef mixture
14. Bake additional 5 to 7 minutes or until mixture is bubbies vigorously around edges<|endoftext|>
