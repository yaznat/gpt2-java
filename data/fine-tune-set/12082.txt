Ingredients:
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Raspberry liqueur
• Vanilla extract
• Silken tofu
• Agave nectar
• Chocolate wafer pie crust

Steps:
1. Fill a 4-quart saucepan with enough water to come 1 inch up the sides
2. Bring to a simmer over medium heat
3. Add chocolate chips and liqueur to a medium metal mixing bowl and set the bowl over the simmering water
4. Stir with a rubber or silicone spatula until melted
5. Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla extract
6. Combine the tofu, chocolate mixture, and honey in a blender or food processor and spin until smooth
7. Pour the filling evenly over the crust, smoothing the top as necessary
