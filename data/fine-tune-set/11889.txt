Ingredients:
• Cream of coconut
• Fresh lime juice
• Green onions
• Curry powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt

Steps:
1. Whisk cream of coconut and fresh lime juice in small bowl until smooth
2. Stir in green onions, curry powder, cayenne pepper and salt
3. Brush half of sauce over chicken or seafood before and during grilling
