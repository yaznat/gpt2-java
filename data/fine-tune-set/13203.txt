Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Sliced mushrooms
• Mexican blend cheese
• Green onion
• Bacon bits

Steps:
1. Flatten chicken to 1 / 4 inch thickness
2. Sprinkle with garlic powder, and salt
3. In a large nonstick skillet over medium, heat brown chicken in oil for 4 minutes
4. Turn
5. Top with mushrooms, cheese, green onion, and bacon
6. Cover and cook until chicken juices run clear and cheese is melted, about 4 minutes<|endoftext|>
