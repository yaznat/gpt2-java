Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Bbq Pork Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Oyster sauce
• Ginger
• Garlic
• Sesame oil
• Pork tenderloin
• Sweet and sour sauce
• Canola oil
• Red bell pepper
• Yellow bell pepper
• Red onion
• Snap peas
• Carrot
• Short-grain white rice
• Eggs
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Pork: in medium bowl, add soy and oyster sauce, ginger and garlic, and sesame oil
2. Add pork and let marinate for 1 hour in the refrigerator
3. Heat grill to medium-high, and grill pork on both sides until done
4. Heat remaining marinade to 165 degrees f and use to baste meat as it cooks
5. When meat is almost cooked through, glaze both sides with sweet and sour sauce and let finish cooking while sauce caramelizes on meat
6. Remove from grill, allow to stand for 10 minutes, and cut into 1 / 2-inch pieces
7. Rice: while pork is standing, heat oil in wok or large skillet on high heat until oil almost begins to smoke
8. Add ginger, as well as all vegetables except garlic
9. Cook for 3 minutes, or until vegetables are cooked, then add garlic
10. Before garlic browns, add rice and mix rapidly so rice does not stick to sides of wok or pan
11. Once entire mixture is thoroughly combined, pour beaten eggs over rice and vegetables, and toss again rapidly until all egg is cooked
12. Add the soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil
13. Mix thoroughly and then add pork<|endoftext|>
