Ingredients:
• Seedless watermelon
• Ice
• Lime
• Condensed milk

Steps:
1. Combine watermelon, ice, and lime juice, blending until smooth
2. To sweeten, add condensed milk to taste, while blending
3. Pour and serve!<|endoftext|>
