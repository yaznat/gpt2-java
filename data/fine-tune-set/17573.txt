Ingredients:
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Coffee
• Ice

Steps:
1. Make your coffee
2. I use a traditional vietnamese coffee filter, but you do not have to if you don't have one
3. Prepare a cup with about 3 - 4 tablespoons of the sweetened condensed milk
4. Stir together coffee and condensed milk
5. Pour over ice<|endoftext|>
