Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Broiled Fish Steaks With Tomato Glaze

Ingredients:
• Tuna steaks
• Oil
• Salt and pepper
• Diced tomato
• Garlic
• Lemon wedge

Steps:
1. Preheat the broiler
2. Brush a baking sheet with oil
3. Lay the fish on the baking sheet in a single layer
4. Brush with 1 t of oil and season with salt and pepper
5. Combine the tomatoes and garlic in a small bowl
6. Stir in the remaining t oil
7. Season with salt and pepper
8. Broil the fish for 4 minutes
9. Turn over with a spatula, top with the tomato mixture, and continue to broil for another 4 to 5 minutes
10. Check doneness by inserting a knife between layers of the flesh
11. The meat should be just translucent
12. Spoon pan juices back over the fish and serve with lemon wedges<|endoftext|>
