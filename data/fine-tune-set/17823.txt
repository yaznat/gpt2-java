What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Baileys Cream Cheese And Chocolate Muffins

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Baileys irish cream
• Egg
• Milk
• Water
• Butter
• Chocolate
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cocoa
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Beat cream cheese until smooth, add baileys and beat again
2. In separate bowl whisk egg, water and butter, then add chocolate
3. Sift remaining dry ingredients into egg mixture and stir until just combined
4. One third fill well-greased muffins pans with muffin mixture, make a hollow in centre, and fill with 1 teaspoon cream cheese mixture
5. Top up to three quarters full with reamaining muffin mixture
6. Bake 190 c for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
