Ingredients:
• Hoisin sauce
• Water
• Rice vinegar
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Dark sesame oil
• Nutmeg
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Garlic cloves
• Flank steak
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Combine first 9 ingredients in a small bowl
2. Reserve 1 / 4 cup hoisin mixture
3. Spoon remaining mixture into a large zip-top plastic bag
4. Add steak to bag
5. Seal and marinate in refrigerator 24 hours, turning bag occasionally
6. Remove steak from bag
7. Discard marinade
8. Prepare grill, grill pan or broiler to medium-high heat
9. Place steak on rack coated with cooking spray
10. Grill 4 minutes on each side or until desired degree of doneness
11. Let stand 5 minutes
12. Cut steak diagonally across the grain into thin slices
13. Serve with reserved 1 / 4 cup sauce<|endoftext|>
