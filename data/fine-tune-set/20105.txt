What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>5 Tablespoon Shrimp Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Cooked shrimp
• Orange juice
• Fresh lime juice
• Canola oil
• Szechuan sauce
• Orange marmalade

Steps:
1. Mix all the tbsp marinade ingredients together in a little bowl
2. Heat a skillet over medium heat for a little bit then add the marinade to the skillet
3. When the marinade starts to sizzle, pour in the shrimp
4. Saute for 2 minutes then serve!<|endoftext|>
