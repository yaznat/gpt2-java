Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Aussie Burgers

Ingredients:
• Kaiser rolls
• Eggs
• Bacon
• Ground beef
• Cheese
• Lettuce
• Pineapple rings
• Onions
• Tomatoes
• Beetroots
• Ketchup

Steps:
1. Slice kaiser rolls and set aside
2. Divide ground beef into 2 equal portions and shape into patties
3. Fry or grill until desired doneness
4. Place cooked patties on the kaiser roll
5. Stack all the other ingredients on top of patty in any order and top with ketchup<|endoftext|>
