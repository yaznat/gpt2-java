Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate squares
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Vanilla
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Melt chocolate and butter in heavy pot
2. Let cool slightly, then add sugar and mix well
3. Add eggs, mix well, then beat in flour
4. Add vanilla
5. Add nuts or raisins, if desired
6. Stir well
7. Bake in 9 1 / 2 x 13 inch greased pan for 20-25 minutes at 375 degrees
8. Do not overbake<|endoftext|>
