What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Apple Blush Pie

Ingredients:
• Double crust pie crust
• Apples
• Crushed pineapple
• Cinnamon
• Lemon juice
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Butter

Steps:
1. Prepare pie dough or use store bought
2. Line a pie plate with the botton crust
3. Peel and slice the apples and place in a large bowl
4. Add pineapple, cinnamon candies, lemon juice, sugar, flour, and salt
5. Mix well
6. Pour into pie shell and dot with butter
7. Roll out top crust and place on top
8. Pinch edges well and cut a few slits of the top to vent steam
9. Place on a baking sheet to catch drips
10. Bake at 350 for approx 45 minutes or until apples seem tender when a knife is inserted thru a vent hole<|endoftext|>
