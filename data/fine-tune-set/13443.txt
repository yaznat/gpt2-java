Please give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>All Beef Swedish Meatballs

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onions
• Butter
• Eggs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Ground allspice
• Ground nutmeg
• Plain breadcrumbs
• Flour
• Milk
• Water
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Wide egg noodles

Steps:
1. Dice onions, put 1 / 2 stick butter in fry pan
2. Mix eggs, salt, pepper, allspice, nutmeg, and breadcrumbs
3. Should form a paste
4. Mix paste, ground beef, and sauteed onions
5. Make balls, place on baking sheet, put in 350 degree oven,
6. For about 50 minutes
7. Now the sauce:
8. Put 3 sticks butter in large pot, melt, over medium heat
9. Add 1 cup flour
10. Mix and heat up
11. Slowly add and constant stirring 6 cups milk
12. Add 1 cup boiling beef broth
13. Keep stirring
14. Let the gravy come to a boil
15. Keep stirring
16. Take gravy off heat
17. Cook the egg noodles it takes about 10 minutes for them<|endoftext|>
