Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Brown Sugar Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Egg yolks
• Light brown sugar
• Heavy cream
• Half-and-half
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. In heavy saucepan, whisk egg yolks and sugar till thick
2. In another saucepan, bring cream and half and half just to a boil over medium heat
3. Gradually whisk the hot cream into the egg mixture in a thin stream
4. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, till custard is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon
5. Do not boil!
6. Strain into a bowl
7. Add vanilla and let cool to room temperature
8. Cover and refrigerate over-night
9. Pour into freezer and follow directions for your freezer<|endoftext|>
