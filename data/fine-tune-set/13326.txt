What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>Acadia S Crock Pot Chicken Cacciatore

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Crushed tomatoes
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Sweet onion
• Carrot
• Orange bell pepper
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Italian spices
• Basil
• Parsley
• Sugar

Steps:
1. In 3-quart crock-pot add in this order: chicken, tomatoes, olive oil, onion, carrot, bell pepper, black pepper, salt, italian spices, basil, parsley and sugar
2. Place lid on, cook on low for 6-8 hours<|endoftext|>
