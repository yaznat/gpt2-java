Ingredients:
• Lemon, zest of
• Almond meal
• All-purpose flour
• Oat flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Figs
• Egg
• Honey
• Skim milk
• Almond oil
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f combine first 9 ingredients in a bowl
2. Puree figs in a food processor until smooth and scrape into a small saucepan
3. Place over low to medium heat, stirring constantly, until puree gently boils, about 5 to 10 minutes
4. Let cool, then refrigerate
5. Whisk egg, honey, milk and oil in a bowl
6. Incorporate into flour mixture and blend on low with a hand mixer
7. Line a muffin tin with paper cups
8. Fill each halfway with batter
9. Add 1 tbsp fig puree to center of each cup, then cover with a small spoonful of batter
10. Sprinkle almonds on top
11. Bake until golden brown, 12 to 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
