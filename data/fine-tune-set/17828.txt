What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Baker S Chocolatey Banana Muffins

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Baker's semi-sweet chocolate
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. In a mixing bowl, whisk together oil, sugar and eggs
3. Stir in bananas and chocolate chips
4. Combine flour, soda, salt and cinnamon
5. Stir into mixture just to moisten
6. Spoon into 24 greased muffin cups
7. Bake 15- 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
