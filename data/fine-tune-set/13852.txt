Give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>Awesome Bbq Pulled Pork No Smoker Or Barbecue Needed

Ingredients:
• Boston butt
• Cider vinegar
• Liquid smoke
• Barbecue seasoning
• Barbecue sauce
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Trim pork roast of as much visible fat as possible
2. Wet the roast all over with half of the vinegar
3. Rub all the seasonings into the meat
4. Place into crock pot, set on low and cook for at least 5 hours, preferably 8
5. When its falling off the bone, pour off most of the liquid in the bottom of the pot
6. Pull out the bone, take off any remaining chunks of fat that you can see and add the other half of the vinegar
7. Pull pork into chunks, but dont completely shred at this time, itll fall apart more with the addition of the barbecue sauces
8. Add equal the amounts of barbecue sauces and the brown sugar, and fold into the shredded meat to combine
9. I like to leave some larger pieces<|endoftext|>
