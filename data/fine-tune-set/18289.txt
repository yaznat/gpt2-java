Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Yellow cake mix
• Candy sprinkles
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Heat griddle and grease
2. Stir together bisquick, cake mix, sprinkles, milk, and vanilla and eggsm until blended
3. Pour slightly less than 1 / 4 cup onto hot griddle
4. Cook until edges are dry
5. Turn
6. Cook until golden brown
7. Stake on plates
8. Glaze:
9. In small bowl, whisk powdered sugrar, milk and vanilla until smooth
10. Top each stack with glaze and more sprinkles<|endoftext|>
