What's a good custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Grandma S Coconut Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Salt
• Sugar
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Ground nutmeg
• Flaked coconut
• Pastry crust

Steps:
1. Combine eggs, salt and sugar
2. Add milk and vanilla slowly
3. Line pie pan with prepared pastry and pour in filling
4. Sprinkle with nutmeg and coconut
5. Bake at 450 degrees for 10 minutes, then reduce heat to 325 degrees and bake 30-40 minutes or until knife inserted in center of pie comes out clean<|endoftext|>
