Ingredients:
• Crushed tomatoes
• Salsa
• Black beans
• Red kidney beans
• Pinto beans
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Cheese
• Sour cream
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Rinse and drain all the beans-- but don't rinse / drain the tomatoes
2. Combine tomatoes, salsa, beans, and spices in a large saucepan, mix well
3. Bring to boil
4. Reduce heat to medium-low, simmer 10 minutes stirring occasionally
5. Serve topped with the topping, or with your own if you like<|endoftext|>
