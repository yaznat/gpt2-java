Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Shrimp And Vegetable Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Salt
• Water
• Sugar snap peas
• Chicken stock
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Canola oil
• Small shrimp
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Shiitake mushrooms
• Celery
• Carrot
• Red bell pepper
• Black pepper
• Bean sprouts
• Scallions
• Watercress leaf

Steps:
1. Bring rice, salt and water to a boil, cover and simmer until water is absorbed and rice is tender, about 30 minutes
2. Spread rice in a single layer over a parchment-lined baking sheet
3. Set aside for 3-4 hours
4. Blanch peas in boiling water for 1 minute, drain and set aside
5. Combine chicken stock and soy sauce and set aside
6. Heat 1 / 2 tsp oil in a wok over high heat
7. Add shrimp and cook until opaque, 2-3 minutes
8. Set aside
9. Add remaining 1 / 2 tsp oil to wok
10. Stir-fry onion and garlic 2 minutes
11. Add 1 t stock, ginger, mushrooms, celery, carrot, bell pepper and black pepper
12. Stir-fry 3 minutes
13. Add 1 t stock, sprouts and shrimp
14. Stir-fry 1 minute
15. Add rice and remaining 2 t stock
16. Stir-fry 2 minutes
17. Stir in scallions and peas
18. Stir-fry 1 minute
19. Stir in watercress and serve<|endoftext|>
