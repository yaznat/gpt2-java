Ingredients:
• Boneless beef roast
• Water
• Seasoning salt
• Soy sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic powder
• Tart apple
• Onion
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Brown roast on all sides in oil, in skillet, and transfer to a slow cooker
2. Add 1 cup of water to skillet to loosen browned bits, and pour over roast
3. Sprinkle with seasoned salt, soy and worcestershire sauces, and garlic powder
4. Top with apple and onion
5. Cover, and cook on low 5-6 hours
6. Remove roast, and onion, discard apple, and let stand 15 minutes
7. To make the gravy, pour juices from roast into a saucepan and simmer until reduced to 2 cups
8. Combine cornstarch and water until smooth in a small bowl, and stir in beef broth
9. Bring to a boil, continue cooking for 2 minutes until thickened
10. Slice roast, and serve with gravy<|endoftext|>
