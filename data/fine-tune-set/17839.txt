Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking cocoa
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Miniature peppermint patties

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease 13 x 9 baking pan
3. In a large bowl, whisk together butter, sugar and vanilla
4. Add eggs and stir until well combined
5. Combine dry ingredients and blend well with wet ingredients
6. Reserve 2 cups of batter and set aside
7. Spread remaining batter in prepared pan
8. Arrange peppermint patties in a single layer over batter, about 1 / 2 inch apart
9. Carefully spread reserved 2 cups of batter on top
10. Bake for 50- 55 minutes or until brownies begin to pull away from sides of pan
11. Cool completely on wire rack and cut into squares<|endoftext|>
