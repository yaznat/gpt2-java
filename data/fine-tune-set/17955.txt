What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Better Than Sex Brownies

Ingredients:
• German chocolate cake mix
• Evaporated milk
• Caramels
• Butter
• Chocolate chips
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Mix together: cake mix, butter and 1 / 3 cup milk
2. Press 1 / 2 of mix into a 9 x 13-in
3. Greased pan
4. Bake at 350 degrees for 6 minutes
5. Sprinkle on pecans
6. Melt together caramels and rest of milk, pour over pecans
7. Sprinkle on chocolate chips
8. Pour on remaining cake mix
9. Bake at 350 degrees for 20 minutes
10. Refrigerate for 3 hours<|endoftext|>
