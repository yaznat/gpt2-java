Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Mix Amazing Every Single Dang Time

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa powder

Steps:
1. To make the mixing as painless as possible, i often take 1 cup of sugar and blend it with the baking powder, salt, and cocoa powder
2. This breaks up all the little clumps before you tackle the monster-big bowl of dry ingredients
3. I've found a whisk to be most helpful for this task
4. Once you've got a clump-free mixture, mix in the rest of the flour and sugar, being very careful that no pockets of un-mixedness remain
5. I put mine in quart-sized ziploc bags, 2 1 / 4 cups per bag
6. Label each bag with the recipe which is as follows:
7. 1 bag of brownie mix, 2 eggs, 1 teaspoon vanilla, and 1 / 4 cup melted butter or margarine
8. Blend well
9. Add 1 / 2 cup chopped walnuts if desired
10. Pour mixture into a cooking-sprayed 8x8" inch pan
11. Bake at 350f for 30-35 minutes
12. For a very fudgey brownie, turn the heat down to 325f and cook for 35 minutes, until a toothpick tests clean
13. Be sure to refrigerate extra-fudgy brownies
14. To serve, i cut 1 / 4" off of each side and remove the "brownie sticks" before cutting and removing individual pieces
15. My family loves the brownie sticks, and the serving pieces are much more clean
16. Store them covered
17. These brownies never need frosting and make a great source for emergency chocolate<|endoftext|>
