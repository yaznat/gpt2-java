Ingredients:
• Dried hibiscus flowers
• Cold water
• Granulated sugar

Steps:
1. Put flowers and 3 cups water in a saucepan and bring to a boil
2. Continue boiling approximately 5 minutes
3. Remove from heat and add remaining cup of water and sugar
4. Set aside at least 4 hours
5. Strain into a pitcher and refrigerate
6. Serve well-chilled<|endoftext|>
