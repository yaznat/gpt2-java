What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Grilled Fresh Fruit Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Light cream cheese
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Flour tortillas
• Red apple
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat lightly sprayed electric grill to medium low
2. Combine cream cheese, brown sugar and vanilla in small bowl until smooth
3. Divide and spread mixture over 2 tortillas
4. Sprinkle apple over remaining 2 tortillas
5. Sprinkle apple with cinnamon
6. Cover with first 2 tortillas
7. Slide, apple side down, onto grill
8. Cook for 1 1 / 2 to 2 minutes
9. Carefully turn over
10. Cook for 1 1 / 2 to 2 minutes until crispy and browed
11. Let stand for 1 minute
12. Cuts into 4 wedges each, for a total of 8
