Ingredients:
• Low fat cottage cheese
• Eggs
• 2% cheddar cheese
• Fresh herbs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 f
2. Place cottage cheese and eggs in a blender and run on medium speed for 1 minute or until fully combined
3. Add half of the cheddar cheese and blend for 10 seconds
4. Pour mixture into a bowl and fold in the remaining cheese and herbs or spinach with a spoon
5. Pour into four ramekins
6. Place on cookie sheet and bake 40 to 45 minutes, until puffy and starting to brown
7. Serve warm in ramekins<|endoftext|>
