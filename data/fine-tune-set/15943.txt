Ingredients:
• Chili with beans
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onion
• Sour cream
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Spoon chili and cheese onto flour tortilla and roll up
2. You can put a toothpick or two to in hold it in place
3. Place on microwavable plate and cook for about 2 minutes
4. Top with sour cream and green onions
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
