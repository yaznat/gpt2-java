Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Betty Crocker Fudge Brownies Homemade

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Flour
• Cocoa
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Oil
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Mix all these ingredients together with a whisk and dump them into a ziploc to save
2. Should be good in the pantry for 6 months
3. When making the brownies, preheat oven to 350f
4. Add eggs, oil and vanilla and mix with a fork til just blended
5. Do not overmix
6. Pour into a silicone or buttered 9x9 pan
7. Bake for 20-25 minutes<|endoftext|>
