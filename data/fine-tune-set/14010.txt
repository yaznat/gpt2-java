What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Croatian Lamb Tingul

Ingredients:
• Cooked lamb
• Fat
• Onions
• Carrots
• Celery root
• Garlic cloves
• Bacon
• Rosemary
• Bay leaf
• Thyme
• White wine
• Dried plums

Steps:
1. Put onion, carrots and celery on prepared meat fat and saut until it become soft
2. Add garlic and bacon
3. Than add the meat with rosemary and wild thyme, plumes and bay leaf
4. Cook shortly, about 10 minute
5. Add wine
6. (add blueberry jam only if you don't have plums
7. Cook for 5 min more
8. Serve with home-made potato gnocchi
9. You can also use any kind of game, turkey, duck, or goose<|endoftext|>
