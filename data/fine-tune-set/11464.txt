Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Ground cinnamon
• Nuts
• Eggs
• Canola oil
• Bananas
• Crushed pineapple
• Vanilla extract
• Honey
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Your will need two loaf pans
2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
3. Spray two loaf pans with bakers joy and set aside
4. In a medium bowl, combine the first 5 ingredients and stir with a wire wh
5. Isk to blend well
6. Stir in nuts to combine and set aside
7. In another bowl, combine the eggs, oil, mashed bananas, pineapple, vanilla and honey stir lightly just to mix
8. Add the wet ingredients to the dry and mix only enough to moisten the dry
9. Do not overmix or your bread will be tough and heavy
10. Its ok to have some lumps
11. Spoon the dough into the two pans, making sure to divide it equally between the two
12. Bake for about 1 hour to 1 hour 10 minutes
13. A good rule of thumb is to check after about 50 minutes
14. Insert a toothpick or bamboo skewer into the center to test for doneness
15. This bread will crack--don't worry
16. Once done, remove to wire racks and cool for about 10-12 minutes in pans
17. Turn the bread out carefully onto the racks
18. Allow it to sit for about 15 minutes upside down, then carefully turn right side up to finish cooling
19. As with any bread, slice with a serrated knife and enjoy
