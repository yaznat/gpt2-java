What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pecan Bread Pudding

Ingredients:
• Cinnamon rolls
• Pecans
• Apple
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Sugar
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Coat an 8 or 9-inch cake pan with nonstick spray
2. Place the bread cubes in the pan and toss in the pecans and apples
3. Blend the eggs, milk and sugar in a medium bowl
4. Add vanilla
5. Pour the mixture over the bread and let stand for about 20 minutes
6. Bake in a 350f oven for about 45 minutes, until golden brown
7. Cut into squares to serve
8. Serve with warm maple syrup<|endoftext|>
