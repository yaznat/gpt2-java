Ingredients:
• Frozen tater tots
• Lean bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Cut bacon strips in half making 2 equal size pieces, about 4 inches each
3. Place a tater tot on the end of the half-strip of bacon and roll it around the tot, slightly overlapping by 1 / 2 inch
4. Cut off any excess bacon
5. Pierce with a toothpick and place bacon wrapped tot on a baking sheet
6. It helps if the tater tots are thawed out a bit
7. Bake until the bacon is crispy, about 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
