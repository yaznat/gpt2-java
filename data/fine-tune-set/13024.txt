Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Eggplant
• Zucchini
• Red bell pepper
• Onion
• Dried oregano
• Tomatoes
• Red wine vinegar
• Salt and pepper
• Orange roughy

Steps:
1. In a large pot, heat oil over medium-high heat
2. Add the chopped garlic to the pot and saute for 30 seconds
3. Add in the next 5 ingredients
4. Stir to combine
5. Cover the pot and cook vegetables about 10 minutes or until they begin to get soft, stirring occasionally
6. Add in the tomatoes and vinegar
7. Stir to mix
8. Lower heat to medium
9. Cover and cook for about 25 minutes or until vegetables are very tender
10. Season with salt and pepper to taste
11. Meanwhile, sprinkle fish fillets with salt and pepper
12. Place fillets in a lightly oiled 13x9 inch glass baking dish
13. Spoon vegetable mixture over fish
14. Bake, uncovered, at 375 degrees for about 20 minutes or until fish is just opaque in the center<|endoftext|>
