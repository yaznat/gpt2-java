Ingredients:
• Butter
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Ascorbic acid
• Almond flour
• Eggs
• Almond milk
• Vanilla extract
• Stevia powder
• Lemon rind

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f melt butter or oil in a 9-inch pie dish in the oven while it preheats
2. In a medium bowl, mix the dry ingredients
3. Use 2 of the tiny scoops of kal brand stevia extract powder or another sweetener in the equivalent of 2 teaspoons of sugar
4. Only use salt if you are using unsalted butter or coconut oil
5. Salted butter will give enough saltiness, so do not add additional salt
6. In a small-medium bowl, wisk the eggs and mix in the milk substitute, and other flavorings
7. Remove the pie plate from the oven and
8. Quickly pour the liquid into the dry mix and wisk thoroughly but quickly
9. Immediately pour into the hot greased pie dish and put into the hot oven
10. Bake about 12 minutes or until set in the middle
11. You might test with a dry knife similar to checking a pumpkin pie, when the knife removed comes out a little wet but no batter sticking to it<|endoftext|>
