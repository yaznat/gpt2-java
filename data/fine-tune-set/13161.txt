Give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>1 00 Tangy Chicken Recipe

Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Cider vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Hot red pepper sauce
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Combine vinegar, worcestershire, chili powder, salt, pepper, and hot pepper sauce in a small bowl
2. You'll have mixed all ingredients except bbq sauce
3. Place chicken pieces in a large plastic bag and pour mixture over chicken
4. Marinate in refrigerator for at least 4 hours, turning several times
5. Spray grill with non stick cooking spray
6. Grill chicken pieces over medium heat in covered grill for 30-45 minutes
7. Turn occasionally while grilling
8. Baste with your favorite bbq sauce for the last 10 minutes of grilling time<|endoftext|>
