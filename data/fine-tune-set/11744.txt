What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>Almond Butter Cake With Crunchy Almond Topping

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Sweet creamy butter
• Eggs
• Almond extract
• Vanilla extract
• Salt
• All-purpose flour
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees f
2. Grease a 9-inch cake pan
3. In a large bowl, stir together sugar and butter until blended
4. Stir in eggs, one at a time, mixing well
5. Stir in almond extract, vanilla, and salt
6. Stir in flour until it is mixed in well
7. Pour the batter into cake pan
8. Smooth the top
9. Sprinkle sugar over the top
10. Sprinkle with almonds
11. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes or until top is golden
12. Using a toothpick won't work with this cake!
13. Cut into wedges and serve warm, chilled, or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
