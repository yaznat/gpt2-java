What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Of Her Eye Pie

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Dried mustard
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• Shortening
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Ice water
• Tart apples
• Cornstarch
• Cinnamon
• Lemon
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. For pastry: combine flour, sugar, mustard and salt
2. Cut in butter and shortening with a pastry blender
3. Stir in cheese
4. Sprinkle in water, 2 tablespoons at a time, tossing with a fork until dough can be gathered in a ball
5. Knead, divide into slightly unequal halves, and chill 45 minutes
6. For filling: cut apples into chunks
7. Toss with butter and remaining ingredients
8. Roll out smaller portion of dough and fit into a 9-inch pie pan
9. Fill with apples
10. Roll out remaining dough and fit on top
11. Sprinkle with topping, cut slits in top
12. Bake at 350f degrees for 1 1 / 4 hours<|endoftext|>
