Please give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>7 Layer Ground Beef Casserole

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Canned whole kernel corn
• Tomato sauce
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Ground beef
• Bacon
• Water
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. In a 2-quart casserole, layer rice, then corn
3. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
4. Pour 1 can tomato sauce mixed with 1 / 2 cup water over first two layers
5. Next, layer chopped onion, green pepper, then ground beef, seasoned with salt and pepper
6. Pour second can of tomato sauce mixed with 1 / 2 cup water over all
7. Cover meat with 4 strips bacon, cut in half
8. Cover dish and bake at 350 degrees for 1 hour
9. Uncover and bake about 30 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
