Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Kosher salt
• Granola cereal
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Bananas
• Butter
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, add the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and granola
2. Stir to combine, in another bowl, beat the eggs with the buttermilk
3. Pour the egg mixture over the flour mixture
4. Stir just until dry ingredients are moistened
5. Fold in bananas
6. Gently stir in the melted butter
7. Spray a large griddle or skillet with nonstick cooking spray
8. Over medium heat, spoon 1 / 4 c
9. Batter
10. Cook until bubbles form on top and edges start to dry
11. Flip pancake over and cook until golden brown
12. Repeat with the remaining batter
13. Serve with warm maple syrup<|endoftext|>
