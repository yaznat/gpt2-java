What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Broiled Indian Spiced Fish

Ingredients:
• Ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Garam masala
• Ground cumin
• Ground turmeric
• Mustard powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Fresh lemon juice
• Coarse salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Unsalted butter
• Fish fillets

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine ginger, garlic, garam masala, cumin, turmeric, mustard powder, cayenne pepper, lemon juice, 3 / 4 teaspoon salt, and a pinch of black pepper
2. Stir in about 1 tablespoon warm water to form a very thick paste
3. Brush a baking sheet or broiler pan with 1 tablespoon butter
4. Place fish on top
5. Cover fish on both sides with marinade
6. Set aside for at least 15 minutes but no more than 30 minutes
7. Preheat broiler
8. Drizzle 2 tablespoons butter over fish
9. Broil until nicely browned, 5 to 6 minutes
10. Turn over steaks, and drizzle with remaining 2 tablespoons butter
11. Broil until browned and cooked through
12. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
