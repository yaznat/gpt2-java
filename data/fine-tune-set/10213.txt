Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Bewitching Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Sweet red pepper
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Tomato juice
• Hot chili beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Condensed beef broth
• Tomato paste
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Sugar
• Pepper
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. In a dutch oven, cook beef, peppers, onion and garlic over medium heat until meat is no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Stir in tomato juice, beans, tomatoes, broth, tomato paste and seasonings
4. Bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat
6. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes
7. Serve in cauldron bread bowls recipe #465277 if desired
8. Garnish with sour cream<|endoftext|>
