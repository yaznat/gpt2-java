Give me a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>Au Gratin And Scalloped Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Au gratin potatoes
• Milk
• Butter
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Place both boxes of potatoes in slow cooker, sprayed with vegetable cooking spray
2. In a large bowl, combine butter, milk, and 4 1 / 4 cups of water and pour over potatoes
3. Cover and cook on low for 4-5 hours
4. When ready to serve, sprinkle crumbled bacon over top of potatoes<|endoftext|>
