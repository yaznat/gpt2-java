Ingredients:
• Bell peppers
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Onion
• Ground beef
• Frozen corn
• Ground ancho chili pepper
• Corn tortillas
• Avocado
• Cheddar cheese
• Fresh cilantro
• Lime wedge

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees f
2. On a rimmed baking sheet, toss the bell peppers with garlic and 1 tablespoon of the oil, and season with salt and pepper
3. Roast peppers until tender
4. Meanwhile, heat remaining oil in a skillet over medium high heat
5. Cook onion, seasoned with a little salt, until it begins to brown, about 3 to 5 minutes
6. Stir in beef, corn, chili powder and cook, stirring occasionally, until beef is browned, 6 to 8 minutes more
7. Divide beef and corn mixture among tortillas and top with desired serving ingredients
8. Serve with roasted peppers and lime wedges<|endoftext|>
