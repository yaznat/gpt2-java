Give me a burrito recipe.<|endoftext|>Firestorm Burritos

Ingredients:
• Sour cream
• Seasoning
• Fresh cilantro
• Green pepper
• Red pepper
• Tomatoes
• Green onion
• Red onion
• Salsa
• Bratwursts
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Mix 1st 9 ingredients & set aside
2. Prepare bratwurst per pkg directions & set aside, but keep warm
3. Heat flour tortillas, immediately sprinkle 1 oz of shredded cheese down the center of ea tortilla & top ea tortilla w / a warm brat
4. Generously spoon the sauce / veggie mixture down ea side of the brat & roll tortilla to enclose the contents
5. To serve: if using 10-12 inch tortillas, you will likely have enough tortilla to fold over 1 or both ends & eat by hand
6. If using 8 in tortillas, just roll it up, plan to use a knife & fork & serve w / any remaining sauce
7. When you taste the sauce, you will want more of it anyway!
8. Time did include 15 min for veggie prep & 15 min for assembly<|endoftext|>
