Can you give me a curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Curry With Fruit

Ingredients:
• Boneless beef chuck roast
• Onion
• Vegetable oil
• Pineapple chunks
• Orange juice
• Quick-cooking tapioca
• Brown sugar
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Dried fig
• Currants
• Apricot halves in light syrup
• Couscous
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Trim all the fat you can from the meat
2. Cut roast into 3 / 4-inch cubes
3. In a big skillet, heat oil
4. Cook meat and onion until meat is browned, stirring frequently
5. Drain off fat
6. In a 4-quart slow cooker, add the undrained pineapple, orange juice, tapioca, brown sugar, curry powder, salt, and pepper
7. Stir to mix it all up
8. Add the meat and onion
9. Cover and cook on low for 7-9 hours
10. Add in the fruit
11. Cover and cook on high for 30 minutes or until heated well
12. Season more to taste if needed
13. Serve over couscous
14. Sprinkle cilantro over the top<|endoftext|>
