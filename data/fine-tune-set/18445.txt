What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Cottage Cheese Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• Cottage cheese

Steps:
1. Sift flour and salt together, set aside
2. Beat eggs, milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla together
3. Add cottage cheese and beat until smooth
4. Combine with flour and cook as usual in your waffle iron<|endoftext|>
