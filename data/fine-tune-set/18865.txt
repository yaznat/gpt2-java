Ingredients:
• Blueberries
• Granulated sugar
• Whipping cream
• Vanilla
• Liquid honey
• White bread
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter

Steps:
1. Mix sugar and blue berries until sugar has melted
2. Whip cream and add vanilla and honey
3. Dip bread slices in sugar and cinnamon
4. Fry in butter in a skillet
5. Place one slice on each plate
6. Divide whipped cream mixture among slices
7. Top with blueberries<|endoftext|>
