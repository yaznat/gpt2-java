What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Audrey S Banana Milkshakes

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Banana
• Pineapple juice
• Vanilla ice cream
• Cinnamon
• Mint extract

Steps:
1. Place first three ingredients in blender, and blend for 15 seconds on medium speed or until banana is smooth
2. Add ice cream, cinnamon and mint extract and blend for another 20-30 seconds
3. Pour into tall glass, drink and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
