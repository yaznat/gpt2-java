Ingredients:
• Oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Red bell pepper
• Yellow bell pepper
• Tomato puree
• Cannellini beans
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a pot
2. Sautee onion, garlic and bell peppers until tender
3. Add tomato puree and beans
4. Season to taste
5. Bring to a boil and let cook for about 15 minutes
6. Everything should be cooked through and nicely warmed
7. Serve with a nice bread on the side<|endoftext|>
