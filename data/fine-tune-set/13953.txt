Give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Braised Lamb Chops

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Salt & pepper
• Butter
• Lamb chops
• Plain flour
• Olive oil
• Fresh rosemary needles
• Button mushrooms
• Onions
• Chicken stock
• Frozen peas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 170c
2. Toss potatoes with seasonings and melted butter
3. Then lay in a heavy-bottomed casserole dish
4. Lightly flour and season chops
5. Seal in a thin layer of hot oil, in 2-3 lots
6. Then place on top of the potatoes together with rosemary
7. Add more oil to the pan, if needed, and gently saute mushrooms and onion until tender
8. Then put on top of lamb, together with 1 cup stock
9. Cover with foil and the lid and cook in the oven for 45 minutes then add frozen peas, toss well and cook for another 15 mins, uncovered<|endoftext|>
