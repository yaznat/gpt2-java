Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Onion
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Swiss cheese
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Onions
• Baby spinach
• Distilled white vinegar
• English muffin
• Eggs
• Prosciutto
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan, melt 1 tablespoon butter over medium heat
2. Add 1 tablespoon onion and cook, stirring, until softened, about 3 minutes
3. Whisk in the flour and stir for 1 minute
4. Whisk in the milk and simmer for 2 minutes, then whisk in the cheese and season to taste with salt and pepper
5. Cover the cheese sauce and set aside
6. Melt the remaining 1 tablespoon butter in a medium skillet over medium heat
7. Add the remaining onion and cook, stirring, until golden, about 7 minutes
8. Add the spinach and cook until soft, 3 to 4 minutes
9. Season to taste with salt and pepper
10. Fill a small nonreactive skillet with 1 inch of water
11. Add the vinegar and a pinch of salt and bring to a boil
12. Meanwhile, toast the english muffin
13. Crack the eggs into saucers, then slide them into the boiling water and simmer until desired doneness, about 3 minutes for lightly poached
14. Transfer to a plate using a slotted spoon
15. Re-warm the cheese sauce over low heat
16. Top each english muffin half with 1 prosciutto slice, 1 tomato slice, half the spinach, some cheese sauce, another prosciutto slice, a poached egg and more cheese sauce<|endoftext|>
