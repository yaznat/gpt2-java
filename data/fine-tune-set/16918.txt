What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Banana Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Fruit cocktail
• Bananas
• Grand marnier
• Orange juice
• Vanilla extract
• Papaya
• Fresh cream

Steps:
1. Combine drained fruit cocktail + banana slices & set aside
2. Use a blender or hand-held whipper / blender tool to combine the remaining ingredients
3. Spoon fruit / banana mix into 6 cocktail glasses & pour pure sauce over fruit
4. Garnish w / reserved cherries & mint leaves<|endoftext|>
