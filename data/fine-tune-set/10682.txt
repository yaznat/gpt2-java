Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Chicken broth
• Asparagus
• Parmesan cheese
• Olive oil
• Ground sage
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Put broth in pan and bring to boil
2. Reduce heat to medium high
3. Add asparagus and cook about 8 t0 10 minutes
4. Stir in half of parmesan cheese
5. Meanwhile, cook oil in medium fry pan, add sage
6. Heat 2 to 3 minutes
7. Add oil, sage and pasta to chicken broth
8. Garnish with sprigs of sage and parmesan cheese
9. Add pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
