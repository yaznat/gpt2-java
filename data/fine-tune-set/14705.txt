Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Armenian Rice Pilaf

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Vermicelli
• Rice
• Chicken broth

Steps:
1. Heat butter in 2qt saucepan until frothy
2. Add vermicelli
3. Saute until golden, dont burn
4. Add rice, stir
5. Add chicken broth, stir to mix
6. Cover and cook for 17 minutes on simmer<|endoftext|>
