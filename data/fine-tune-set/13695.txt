Generate a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>Anne Byrn S Oven Baked Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients:
• Honey
• Ketchup
• Butter
• Light brown sugar
• Fresh lemon juice
• Garlic powder
• Pork tenderloin
• Salt and pepper
• Dijon mustard

Steps:
1. Place rack in center of oven
2. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
3. Place honey, ketchup, butter, brown sugar, lemon juice and garlic powder in a small saucepan
4. Simmer about 5 minutes
5. Keep heat low
6. Remove from heat
7. Rinse pork and dry with paper towels
8. Place in a large roasting pan or 2 9x13 glass dishes
9. Sprinkle with salt and pepper and additional garlic powder
10. Generously brush tops of meat with mustard
11. Bake uncovered 15 minutes and then brush with honey glaze
12. Continue baking 2 hours, brushing every 15 minutes
13. Let meat rest 20 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
