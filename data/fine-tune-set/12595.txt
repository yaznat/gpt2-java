Ingredients:
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Tomato sauce
• Burgundy wine
• Cooked beef
• Carrots
• Dried onion flakes
• Sugar
• Salt
• Dried thyme
• Garlic salt
• Cinnamon
• Cooked noodles

Steps:
1. In saucepan melt butter
2. Blend in flour and stir until smooth
3. Stir in tomato sauce and wine
4. Cook and stir until bubbly
5. Transfer to crock pot
6. Stir in beef and remaining ingredients except noodles
7. Cover and cook on low 4-6 hours
8. Serve over hot cooked noodles<|endoftext|>
