Ingredients:
• Beef flank steak
• Barbecue sauce
• Soy sauce
• Green onion
• Sesame seeds
• Garlic clove
• Ground ginger

Steps:
1. Score steak on both sides
2. Place in shallow glass dish or large resealable plastic bag
3. Mix remaining ingredients
4. Pour over steak
5. Cover dish or seal bag
6. Marinate in refrigerator at least 2 hours or overnight
7. Remove steak from marinade
8. Discard marinade
9. Preheat grill to medium-high heat
10. Grill steak 15 to 20 minutes or until cooked through, turning every 8 minutes
11. To serve, cut steak across the grain into thin slices<|endoftext|>
