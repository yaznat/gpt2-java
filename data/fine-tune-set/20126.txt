What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Asian Shrimp Mushroom Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Baby portabella mushrooms
• Green onion
• Red bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Shrimp
• Creole seasoning
• White wine
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Dried basil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Place shrimp in bowl and mix well to coat with creole seasoning
2. Heat two teaspoons of oil in large skillet on medium high heat
3. Add mushrooms, scallions, pepper, and garlic
4. Saute 5 to 7 minutes, until tender
5. Add shrimp and rest of the oil
6. Add wine, red pepper, and basil
7. Cook until shrimp is cooked, stirring constantly
8. Serve over brown rice and enjoy<|endoftext|>
