Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Water
• Barbecue sauce
• Prepared mustard
• Dried onion flakes
• Pepper
• Instant rice
• Cheddar cheese
• Dill pickle
• Bacon

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan over medium heat, cook the beef until no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Add water, barbecue sauce, mustard, onion and pepper
4. Bring to a boil
5. Stir in the rice
6. Sprinkle with cheese
7. Reduce heat
8. Cover and simmer for 5 minutes
9. Sprinkle with pickles and bacon<|endoftext|>
