Ingredients:
• Broccoli stems
• White fish fillet
• Cream of broccoli soup
• Skim milk
• Cheese
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Margarine
• Paprika

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. In 2 quart shallow baking dish, arrange broccoli
3. Top with fish
4. In small bowl mix soup and milk and pour over fish
5. Sprinkle cheese over soup mixture
6. Mix bread crumbs, margarine and paprika and sprinkle over cheese
7. Bake for 20 minutes or until fish flakes easily when tested with fork<|endoftext|>
