Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple Cider Strusel Muffins

Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Egg
• Apple cider
• Oil

Steps:
1. For streusel: stir brown sugar, flour, and cinnamon in small bowl
2. Cut in butter with a pastry blender
3. Set aside
4. For muffins: lightly oil twelve 2 1 / 2-inch muffin cups or line with paper baking cups
5. Set aside
6. In a medium mixing bowl stir together flour, granulated sugar, baking powder, and salt
7. Make a well in the center
8. In a small mixing bowl combine egg, apple cider, and oil
9. Add all at once to flour mixture
10. Stir just till moistened
11. Spoon about 1 tablespoon of the batter into each prepared muffin cup
12. Sprinkle 1 teaspoon of the brown sugar mixture over each
13. Fill with remaining batter
14. Sprinkle tops of muffins with remaining streusel
15. Bake in a preheated 400 f oven until golden brown, about 20 minutes
16. Remove muffins from pans
17. Cool slightly on racks
18. Makes 12 muffins<|endoftext|>
