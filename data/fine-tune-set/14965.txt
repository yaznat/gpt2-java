Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Green onion
• Jalapeno
• Cooked jasmine rice
• Sugar
• Soy sauce
• Fish sauce
• Cooked shrimp
• Fresh cilantro
• Fresh basil leaf
• Toasted sesame oil

Steps:
1. Pour olive oil into a wok or large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add garlic, onions and chiles and saut until wilted, about 3 minutes
3. Add rice, sugar, soy sauce and fish sauce
4. Reduce heat to medium and saut until ingredients are well coated and hot, 3 to 4 minutes
5. Add shrimp, cilantro, basil, and sesame oil and saut until shrimp are hot to touch, about 3 minutes<|endoftext|>
