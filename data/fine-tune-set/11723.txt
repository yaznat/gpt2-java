What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>Alex S Lemon Cream Cheese Butter Cake

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Butter
• Lemon rind
• Caster sugar
• Eggs
• Self rising flour
• Icing sugar
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Beat cream cheese, butter and lemon rind together until smooth,
2. Add sugar and beat until light and fluffy
3. Beat in eggs one at a time,
4. Stir in flour in two batches,
5. Beat lightly ulntil smooth
6. Spoon into well greased 20cm ring or baba tin
7. Bake in moderate oven for 30 minutes,
8. Reduce heat to moderatle slow and bake for a further 30 minutes
9. Let stand in tin 10 minutes, then turn onto a wire rack
10. When cold top with lemon glaze
11. Lemon glaze:
12. Sift icing sugar into bowl,
13. Beat in enough lemon juice to give a less than pouring consistency
14. Cake will freeze well for up to 2 month
15. Freeze without glaze and top with glaze several hours before serving<|endoftext|>
