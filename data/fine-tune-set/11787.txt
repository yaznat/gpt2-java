Ingredients:
• Meat
• Corn oil
• Onion
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato puree
• Fresh lemon juice
• Ghee
• Dried red chilies
• Black pepper
• Chili powder
• Cumin seed
• Fenugreek seeds
• Turmeric
• Cardamoms
• Garam masala
• Dried fenugreek leaves

Steps:
1. Cut meat into cubes and fry in oil to seal
2. Remove with slotted spoon and leave to one side to drain
3. Fry the onion in the same oil until golden then add spices 1, cooking for a further 5 minutes
4. Add the tinned tomatoes and puree and mix well
5. Cook for 10 minutes or so
6. Add the meat and put the the lot into a casserole dish in a pre-heated oven at 200c and cook for 45 - 60 minutes
7. Stir half-way through cooking time
8. Add the lemon or lime juice and spices 2 and simmer for another 10 minutes
9. Add a little water at any stage if it gets too dry
10. Add a knob of ghee just before serving<|endoftext|>
