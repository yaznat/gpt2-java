Give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Au Jus Onion Burgers

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Garlic clove
• Worcestershire sauce
• Pepper
• Wasabi
• Onion
• French onion soup
• Cheese
• Rolls

Steps:
1. Mix the beef with the worchestershire sauce, garlic, wasabi & pepper
2. Shape into 4 patties to match the shape and size of your rolls
3. Cook the patties in a skillet
4. Have heat on high, brown one side of the patties, turn heat to medium, flip the patties, add the onion and cover the pan
5. Contimue cooking until the patty is nicely browned and the onions are golden, stir the onions a few times to prevent burning while cooking
6. Pour in the onion soup, turn heat to high, bring to a simmer and cook covered a further 5 minutes
7. Meanwhile toast the inside of your bun, top with cheese
8. Place meat and onions on the bun
9. Serve with the" au jus" in individual cups large enough to dip the burger
10. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
