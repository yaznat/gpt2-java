Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Homogenized milk
• Vanilla extract
• Peppermint extract

Steps:
1. Blend icecream, milk, and extracts into blender
2. As soon as it turns into one liquid, stop blending
3. Optional: garnish with either crushed candycane, mint leaves, or vanilla bean<|endoftext|>
