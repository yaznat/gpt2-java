Ingredients:
• Whole wheat sandwich wrap
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce
• Extra firm tofu
• Ranch dressing
• Cheddar cheese
• Buffalo
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Cut the tofu into cubes and fry it until the outside is firm and crispy
2. Add the buffalo sauce to the tofu and fry for about a minute more
3. Lay out the wrap and spread the ranch or bleu cheese dressing in a circle in the middle
4. Place the lettuce and tomato in the center of the wrap
5. Put the buffalo tofu on top of the veggies
6. Sprinkle the cheese on top of the hot tofu
7. Add hot sauce if you like it spicy
8. Wrap it up and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
