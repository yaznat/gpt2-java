Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Sour cream
• Diced tomatoes with chiles and cilantro
• Sweet corn
• Chicken breasts
• Mexican rice
• Fresh cilantro
• Cumin
• Adobo seasoning
• Paprika
• Taco seasoning
• Salt
• Pepper
• Guacamole seasoning mix
• Avocado
• Lettuce
• Cheddar cheese
• Extra virgin olive oil

Steps:
1. Cut up chicken and add cumin, taco seasoning salt + pepper, paprika, and adobo, set to the side
2. Cook rice according to the directions on the box
3. In a large saute or frying pan on med-high heat, estimate and add 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
4. Add chicken
5. While chicken is cooking, peel avacados and make guacamole according to package directions
6. Refrigerate
7. Open cans of black beans, corn, diced tomatoes with chiles and cilantro and put in small bowls
8. If you'd like, you can warm, the can of beans and corn
9. Don't warm the can of diced tomatoes, unless you like warm salsa / tomato mixture
10. Once chicken and rice is done, you can start plating
11. Letting everyone add the toppings of their choice is part of the fun of the meal!
