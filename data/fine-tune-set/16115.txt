Ingredients:
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Goat cheese
• Chorizo sausage
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Brown and break up chorizo in skillet on medium heat
2. Drain, set aside, and wipe out skillet for the quesadillas
3. Use 1 / 2 tablespoon of oil for each quesadilla
4. Heat 1 / 2 tablespoon of oil in skillet to medium or medium-high, depending on your burner
5. When the oil is glistening, it's ready
6. Don't wait until it smokes
7. Place a tortilla in the oil
8. Spread half with softened goat cheese and sprinkle chorizo to your liking and fold in half
9. Let brown for a minute or so, peeking often so as to not burn it
10. Carefully flip it over and brown the other side
11. Repeat with the other 3 tortillas and the rest of the cheese and sausage
12. Salud!<|endoftext|>
