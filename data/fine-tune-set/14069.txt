Ingredients:
• Ground lamb
• Scallion
• Garlic cloves
• Pistachios
• Dried mint
• Turmeric
• Ground allspice
• Salt
• Cayenne
• Egg
• Plain yogurt

Steps:
1. Combine the lamb with all the other ingredients
2. Form 4 large patties
3. Cook the patties as you would a hamburger
4. They should not be cooked rare, but a barely pink center is okay<|endoftext|>
