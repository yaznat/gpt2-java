Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Cocoa powder
• Walnuts
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Cream together margarine and sugar
2. Add eggs and vanilla, mix well
3. Sift together flour, salt and baking powder- add to creamed mixture
4. Blend in cocoa powder and add chopped nuts
5. Spread mixture into greased 9-inch square pan
6. Bake for 35 minutes at 350
7. When cool, top with dusting of confectioners sugar<|endoftext|>
