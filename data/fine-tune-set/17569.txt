Ingredients:
• Water
• Piloncillo
• Cinnamon sticks
• Anise seed
• Whole cloves
• Instant coffee
• Evaporated milk

Steps:
1. Combine water, piloncillo, cinnamon sticks, anise and cloves in medium saucepan
2. Heat over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until boiling
3. Reduce heat to low
4. Cover
5. Cook for 15 minutes
6. Remove from heat
7. Stir in coffee granules
8. Cover
9. Steep for 5 minutes
10. Heat evaporated milk in small saucepan over medium-high heat
11. Whisk constantly until milk is frothy and just comes to a boil
12. Remove from heat
13. Strain coffee through a fine sieve into individual serving cups
14. Serve immediately with frothed milk on the side<|endoftext|>
