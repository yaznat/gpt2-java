Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Bed And Breakfast Buckwheat Waffles

Ingredients:
• Buckwheat flour
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Butter

Steps:
1. Pre heat waffle iron
2. Combine all dry ingredients in a large bowl
3. Beat eggs, buttermilk, and butter till well combined
4. Add to the dry ingredients just till blended
5. Cook in a pan sprayed waffle iron till crisp and brown
6. Serve w / warm maple syrup and fresh strawberries<|endoftext|>
