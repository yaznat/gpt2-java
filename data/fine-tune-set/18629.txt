Ingredients:
• Gingerbread
• Mcintosh apple
• Currants
• Milk
• Cream
• Vanilla bean
• Eggs
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cut the gingerbread into cubes
2. Place in a generously buttered 8 x 8 x 2" pan
3. Top with the apple and currants
4. Set aside
5. Scald the milk, cream, and vanilla bean
6. Meanwhile, in a bowl, combine the eggs, yolks, sugar, cinnamon, and salt
7. Hand whip until mixed
8. Temper the egg mixture with the milk mixture
9. Strain through a sieve into your prepared pan
10. Press the gingerbread down to ensure complete coverage with the liquid
11. Let sit for 30 minutes or overnight
12. Preheat oven to 350f
13. Cover the pan with foil
14. Bake in a water bath for approximately 1 1 / 2 hours or until done
15. Remove the foil during the last 20 minutes of baking to brown the top
16. Baking times may vary with your oven
17. Cut into squares and serve hot
18. Serves 6 to 9<|endoftext|>
