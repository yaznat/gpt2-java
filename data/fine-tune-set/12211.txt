Ingredients:
• Unbleached all-purpose flour
• Old fashioned oats
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Egg
• Egg white
• Brown sugar
• Honey
• Vegetable oil
• Buttermilk
• Apples

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Whisk flour, oats, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt
3. Set aside
4. Beat the egg, egg white, brown sugar, honey, and oil with an electric mixer set at medium until smooth
5. Add the buttermilk and beat again
6. Stir the flour and apples into the batter by hand just until combined
7. Scrape batter into a 9x5-inch loaf pan coated with a non-stick cooking spray
8. Bake for 50-60 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean
9. Cool the bread in the pan set on a wire rack for about 10 minutes
10. Turn our onto a wire rack and cool completely<|endoftext|>
