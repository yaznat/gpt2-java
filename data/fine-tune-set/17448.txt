Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Banana
• Granulated sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• Ground nutmeg
• Egg
• Dark brown sugar
• Water
• Butter
• Macadamia nuts
• Sweetened flaked coconut

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Coat a 9-inch round cake pan with cooking spray
3. Line bottom of pan with wax paper
4. Coat wax paper with cooking spray
5. Lightly spoon flour into dry measuring cups
6. Level with a knife
7. Combine flour, salt, baking powder, and baking soda in a bowl, stirring with a whisk
8. Combine banana and next 5 ingredients in a bowl
9. Beat with a mixer at medium speed for 1 minute or until well blended
10. Add flour mixture to banana mixture, and beat until blended
11. Pour batter into prepared pan
12. Bake at 350 for 30 minutes or until a wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean
13. Cool in pan 10 minutes on a wire rack
14. Remove from pan
15. Carefully peel off wax paper
16. Combine brown sugar, water, and butter in a small saucepan
17. Bring to a boil
18. Cook 1 minute, stirring constantly
19. Remove from heat
20. Stir in nuts and coconut
21. Spread over cake
22. Serve cake warm<|endoftext|>
