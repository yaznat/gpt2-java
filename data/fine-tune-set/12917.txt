Ingredients:
• Fish fillets
• Onion
• Garlic
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Red chili powder
• Turmeric powder
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Heat the oil preferably in cast iron skillet and add onions and garlic
2. Wait till they begin to turn brown
3. Add tomatoes and all the spices
4. Saute gently on medium heat till the oil comes out on the sides and curry is bright red
5. Add fillet cubes and saute gently till pieces are well coated
6. Add around 1 / 2 cup water and cook covered on medium heat for 10 minutes
7. Fragrant, no fuss fish curry is ready!<|endoftext|>
