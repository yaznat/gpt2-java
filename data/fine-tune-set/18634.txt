Ingredients:
• Apples
• Sugar
• Egg
• Flour
• Cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Walnuts
• Raisins
• Vanilla
• Brown sugar
• White sugar
• Water
• Butter

Steps:
1. Mix apples and 1 cup sugar together and let stand until sugar is dissolved
2. Add eggs and mix well
3. Sift together the 1 cup flour, cinnamon, and baking soda
4. Stir into apple mixture
5. Add 1 cup walnuts, 3 / 4 cup raisins, and 1 teaspoon vanilla
6. Pour into a greased 8x8 pan and bake at 375 degrees for 40-45 minutes
7. While cake is baking, mix 1 / 2 cup brown sugar, 1 / 2 cup sugar, 1 cup water, and 2 tablespoons flour in a small saucepan
8. Cook over medium heat until slightly thick and clear
9. Add butter, 1 teaspoon vanilla, 1 / 2 cup walnuts, and 1 / 2 cup raisins and stir until butter is melted
10. Pour topping over hot baked cake while still in pan<|endoftext|>
