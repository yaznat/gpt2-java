Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Sesame oil
• Canola oil
• Oyster sauce
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Chicken stock
• Garlic clove
• Ginger
• Broccoli florets
• Fresh shiitake mushrooms
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Stir fry sesame oil, canola oil, garlic and ginger for 2 minutes in wok
2. Add the cubed tofu and 2 tablespoons of oyster sauce and stir fry for another 5 minutes, then remove this 'tofu mix' from wok
3. Combine soy sauce, corn starch, remaining oyster sauce and chicken stock
4. Bring to a boil
5. Add broccoli florets and shitake mushrooms and simmer for 4 minutes
6. Add the 'tofu mix' back into the wok
7. Stir and simmer for 1 minute
8. Sprinkle with chopped green onions
9. Serve over noodles or rice<|endoftext|>
