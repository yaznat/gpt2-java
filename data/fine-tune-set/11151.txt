Ingredients:
• Bisquick baking mix
• Milk
• Egg
• Deli turkey
• Cheddar cheese
• Cooked bacon

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400 degrees
2. Spray an 8 inch square baking dish with nonstick cooking spray
3. Stir bisquick, milk and egg until blended
4. Spread half of the batter in the dish
5. Top with turkey, 1 cup of cheese and bacon
6. Spread remaining batter over bacon
7. Bake uncovered 29 minutes or until golden brown and set
8. Sprinkle with remaining cheese
9. Bake 3 more minutes until cheese is melted
10. Let stand 5 minutes before cutting<|endoftext|>
