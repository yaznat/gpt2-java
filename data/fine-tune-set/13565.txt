Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onion
• Ground round
• Pork and beans
• Molasses
• Ketchup
• Dry mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt

Steps:
1. Fry the bacon until crisp
2. Remove from the pan and saut the onion and ground beef in the bacon drippings
3. Drain if there is too much liquid
4. Combine all ingredients and place in a casserole dish
5. Bake uncovered in a 350f oven for 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
