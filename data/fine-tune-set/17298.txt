Ingredients:
• Round steaks
• Meat tenderizer
• Bell pepper
• Salt water
• Bacon
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Vinegar
• Salt
• Bay leaves
• Tomato sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375-degrees f
2. Cut pocket in steak for stuffing and sprinkle with tenderizer
3. Cook bell pepper in boiling water for 5 minutes
4. Drain
5. Reserve
6. In a heavy skillet over medium heat cook bacon until limp
7. Drain drippings and reserve
8. Stuff steak with bacon and peppers and skewer shut
9. Brown steak in reserved bacon drippings over medium heat
10. In a seperate skillet melt butter and brown sugar together, stirring until bubblling
11. Add vinegar, salt, bay leaves and tomato sauce and stir to combine
12. Place steak in a 9x13-inch baking dish and pour sauce over meat
13. Bake for 1 3 / 4-2 hours or until meat is tender
14. Slice across grain to serve<|endoftext|>
