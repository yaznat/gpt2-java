Ingredients:
• Almond butter
• Sucanat
• Egg
• Baking soda
• Sea salt
• Dark chocolate

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f in a medium bowl, stir together first 5 ingredients until blended
2. Stir in chocolate
3. Drop dough by rounded tablespoonfuls onto parchment-lined baking sheets
4. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes or until lightly browned
5. Let cool on baking sheets for 5 minutes
6. Remove to a wire rack and let cool for 15 more minutes<|endoftext|>
