What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Breasts With Lime Curry Sauce

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Garlic powder
• Onion
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Flour
• Fresh lime juice
• Light cream
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, melt 2 tablespoons of butter over medium-high heat
2. Add chicken, sprinkle with garlic powder and cook, turning once, until golden, 3-4 minutes per side
3. Remove to a plate and set aside
4. In the same skillet, melt remaining 2 tablespoons of butter
5. Add onion, curry powder, salt and pepper
6. Cook, stirring, 3 minutes, or until onions are tender
7. Stir in flour
8. Cook, stirring, 1 minute
9. Stir in lime juice and light cream
10. Heat to boiling, stirring occasionally
11. Reduce heat to low and simmer 7-8 minutes, or until sauce has thickened
12. Remove from heat
13. Spoon sauce over chicken breasts and top with a sprinkling of tomatoes and lime slices
14. Serve with hot cooked rice<|endoftext|>
