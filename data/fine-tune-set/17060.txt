Ingredients:
• Blueberries
• Strawberry
• Raspberries
• Ice cube
• Unsweetened pineapple juice
• Vodka
• Sugar
• Of fresh mint
• Mint sprig

Steps:
1. Blend berries, ice, juice, vodka, sugar and mint about 2 minutes, or until smooth
2. Some seeds will remain
3. Transfer to large glasses and garnish with mint springs
