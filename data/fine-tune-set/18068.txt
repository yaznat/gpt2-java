Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Mix S Mores

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Flour
• Brownie mix
• Eggs
• Milk
• Miniature marshmallows
• Graham crackers

Steps:
1. Position the rack in the lower third of the oven
2. Preheat oven to 350f
3. Butter and flour 9x13 inch pan
4. Place brownie mix, beaten eggs, melted butter, and milk in a large bowl
5. Stir with a wooden spoon until smooth
6. Pour into prepared pan
7. Bake for 25 minutes or until toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs attached
8. Place pan on a wire rack
9. Maintain oven temperature
10. The moment the pan is out of the oven sprinkle the mini-marshmallows over the top
11. Then cover with graham crackers, breaking some as necessary to cover the entire pan
12. Place pan back in oven and bake for 2 minutes, to melt the marshmallows
13. Remove pan from oven and gently press the graham crackers down into the melted topping-be careful
14. It's very hot
15. Cool the pan completely on a wire rack
16. Then turn it over to unmold the brownies onto a cutting board
17. Place a wire rack on the top and invert once again
18. Cut into 24 pieces<|endoftext|>
