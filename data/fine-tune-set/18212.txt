Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Banana Half Wheat Pancakes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Wheat flour
• Dry buttermilk
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Egg
• Water
• Banana
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Preheat your griddle
2. I get perfect results with my electric griddle set between 350 and 400 degrees
3. Mix all dry ingredients in a medium bowl
4. Smash the banana roughly with a fork on a small plate then add to the dry ingredients
5. Add remaining wet ingredients and stir until just blended -- don't over-stir!
6. I use a 1 / 4 measuring cup to pour the batter onto my griddle
7. Cook until bubbles forming on the top surface stay open when they pop, then flip and cook until bottom is lightly browned
8. These are perfect with just a little syrup, but they're insane garnished with whipped cream and sliced strawberries<|endoftext|>
