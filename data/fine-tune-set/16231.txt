What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Alaska Salmon And Chipotle Wrap

Ingredients:
• Fresh lime juice
• Chipotle chile in adobo
• Fresh cilantro
• Red bell peppers
• Red onion
• Garlic
• Salt
• Pepper
• Salmon
• Light sour cream
• Adobo sauce
• Flour tortillas
• Lettuce leaves

Steps:
1. In bowl, mix lime juice, chiles, cilantro, bell pepper, red onion, garlic, salt, and pepper
2. Gently stir in salmon until blended
3. In a small bowl, blend sour cream and adobo sauce
4. Spread 1 / 4 mixture over each tortilla to within 1 inch of edge
5. Spread 2 / 3 cup salmon mixture over cream cheese / sour cream
6. Top with 1 / 4 of lettuce and roll up burrito-style
7. Repeat for remaining tortillas
8. For appetizers, cut each wrap into thirds
9. As a meal, cut each wrap in half
10. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
