What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Brownie Kiss Cupcakes

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Flour
• Baking cocoa
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk chocolate kisses

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, cream butter and sugar
2. Add eggs and vanilla
3. Mix well
4. Combine next 4 ingredients
5. Add to creamed mixture
6. Mix well
7. Fill paper lined muffin cups 2 / 3 full
8. Place an overturned chocolate kiss in the center of each
9. Bake at 350 degrees for 20-25 minutes or until top springs back when lightly touched<|endoftext|>
