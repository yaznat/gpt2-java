Ingredients:
• Butter
• Chicken breast fillets
• Pancetta
• Parmesan cheese
• Nutmeg
• Egg
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Heat butter in a frying pan, add chicken until golden brown, then drain
2. Process this in food processor until finely chopped
3. Transfer to bowl and add cheese, nutmeg, egg and salt and pepper to taste
4. Fill pasta and seal edges with egg whites<|endoftext|>
