What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Baby S Southern Banana Pudding

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Vanilla wafers
• Bananas
• Butter
• Cream of tartar

Steps:
1. Cream egg yolks, butter, flour, sugar, salt and flavoring well over very low heat on the stovetop
2. Gradually add milk
3. Cook at medium temperature on stove until "thick enough"
4. Stir frequently
5. Make layer of bananas and wafers in bottom and up sides of an oven proof bowl or dish
6. Pour half of thickened pudding mixture over this
7. Make a second layer of bananas and wafers
8. Pour remaining pudding over this
9. Whisk 2 egg whites at high speed until frothy
10. Add cream of tartar
11. Add sugar 1 tablespoon at a time
12. Beat until peaks form
13. Spread on top of pudding mixture making peaks
14. Bake at 400 for 8-10 minutes
15. Serve at room temperature or chilled<|endoftext|>
