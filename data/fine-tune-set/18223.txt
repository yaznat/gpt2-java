Ingredients:
• Pecans
• Whole wheat flour
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground flax seeds
• Eggs
• Unsalted butter
• 2% low-fat milk
• Buttermilk
• Pure vanilla extract
• Bananas

Steps:
1. Finely grind 1 / 2 c of the pecans in a food processor, or chop them into a fine powder
2. Coarsely chop the remaining 1 / 4 c pecans and reserve
3. Transfer the ground pecans to a large bowl
4. Add the flours, sugar, baking powder, salt and flaxseed and whisk until combined
5. In another bowl, whisk the eggs, butter, milk, buttermilk and vanilla until frothy
6. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined
7. Fold the reserved 1 / 4 c of chopped pecans into the batter
8. Heat a large skillet over medium heat
9. Brush the the skillet with butter
10. Keep the surface buttered well throughout cooking to prevent the bananas from sticking
11. Pour 1 / 4 c of the batter onto the skillet for each pancake
12. Lay about 5 banana slices onto each onto each pancake and cook until small bubbles form and pop on the surface and the bottom is golden brown
13. Flip the pancakes and cook until golden brown, 6-8 minutes
14. Garnish with extra bananas and pecans and drizzle with syrup and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
