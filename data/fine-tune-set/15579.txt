Ingredients:
• Italian sausage
• Refried beans
• Mild salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Black olives
• Green onion
• Sour cream
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Cook sausage over medium heat, breaking up the meat with the edge of a spoon, drain
3. Spread the beans over the bottom of a 2 1 / 2 quart shallow baking dish and top with sausage
4. Pour salsa over sausage, add cheeses, sprinkle with olives and green onions
5. Bake uncovered 25-30 minutes, top with sour cream and serve with chips<|endoftext|>
