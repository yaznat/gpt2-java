Give me a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>A Dilly Potato Salad No Mayo

Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Fresh green beans
• Sliced ripe olives
• Celery
• Onion
• Vegetable oil
• White wine vinegar
• Seasoning salt
• Dill weed
• Dry mustard

Steps:
1. Place potatoes large saucepan
2. Add water to cover
3. Bring to boil
4. Cook until potatoes are just tender
5. Add beans
6. Cook 2 minutes
7. Drain
8. Cool slightly
9. Mix potatoes, beans, olives, celery and onion in large bowl
10. Whisk remaining ingredients in small bowl
11. Add to vegetables
12. Toss to coat
13. Refrigerate at least 4 hours or until ready to serve
14. Stir before serving<|endoftext|>
