Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>Fool The Meat Eaters Chili

Ingredients:
• Vegetarian ground beef
• Garlic
• Onion
• Jalapenos
• Green pepper
• Celery
• Kidney beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Chili powder
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Rehydrate tvp if needed
2. Spray or oil a large pot
3. Chop the onion, hot peppers, and garlic and add to the pot
4. Chop remaining vegetables, add to pot and brown lightly
5. Add remaining ingredients
6. Stir well and simmer at least 15 minutes
7. Taste adjust spices to taste
8. Simmer an additional 15 minutes or so
9. Serve with garlic bread, toast, or on rice<|endoftext|>
