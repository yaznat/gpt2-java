Give me a chicken recipe.<|endoftext|>Not Your Every Day Chicken Salad Chicken Salad

Ingredients:
• Light mayonnaise
• Cider vinegar
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh dill
• Skinless chicken breast half
• Feta cheese
• Red bell pepper

Steps:
1. Blend mayonnaise, vinegar, garlic and dill
2. Refrigerate for several hours or overnight
3. Stir together chicken, peppers and cheese and blend with the chilled dressing
4. Serve!<|endoftext|>
