Ingredients:
• Butterflied leg of lamb
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Dried marjoram
• Dried thyme
• Rosemary
• Salt & pepper
• Wine
• Water

Steps:
1. Place the lamb fat side down on a plate or pyrex dish
2. Mix garlic with the oil
3. Salt & pepper
4. Work the mixture into the folds covering the lamb surface completely
5. Grind the three herbs together and spread over underside of the lamb
6. Rub it into the lamb well
7. To bbq: place meat skin side down on an oiled grill
8. Close the cover and cook over medium high heat for approx 45 minutes
9. Watch for flame ups, but do not flip the roast
10. To roast: pour the wine and water into the roasting pan and bake in 375f oven for 1 hours or until the internal temperature reaches 160f
11. Let stand 5 minutes before carving
12. Check internal temperature: 160f is medium rare - just pink<|endoftext|>
