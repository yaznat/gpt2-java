Please generate a tea recipe.<|endoftext|>Another Holiday Spiced Tea

Ingredients:
• Tea bags
• Water
• Whole cloves
• Sugar
• Lemon juice
• Frozen orange juice concentrate

Steps:
1. Brew the tea in 1 / 2 gallon of the water, and set aside
2. Brew the cloves separately in 2 cups of the water until the water is amber-colored and has a strong clove scent
3. This brewing process will take longer than the tea
4. Strain the clove water
5. Stir the sugar into the tea until it dissolves
6. Add the orange juice, lemon juice, and clove water
7. Pour all into a gallon jug
8. Add enough additional water to fill the jug<|endoftext|>
