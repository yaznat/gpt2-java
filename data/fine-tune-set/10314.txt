Ingredients:
• Plain flour
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Sunflower oil
• Lemons, zest of
• Egg
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, beat the eggs & the oil
2. Add the baking powder, & slowly add the flour, mixing the ingredients with your hands until a dough forms
3. You will probably not need all of the flour
4. Once you have a firm dough, you can shape your helouwa
5. To shape, you have 2 choices: you can roll out to 5mm thickness & cut out with your favourite cookie cutters or you can roll into a 1cm thick 'sausage' & make rings & straight lengths them decorate with a 'nakkach' or fork if you don't have one
6. If making the sesame version, once you have cut out all your cookies, brush a little beaten egg over the top of the cookies & sprinkle with the sesame seeds
7. Place on a tray lined with aluminium foil & bake for 25-28 minutes
8. The cookies should be pale but hard when tapped
9. Allow to cool then store in an airtight container for upto 2 weeks<|endoftext|>
