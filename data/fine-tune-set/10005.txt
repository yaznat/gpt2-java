What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>The Works Crock Pot Chili

Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• White onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red pepper
• Chili seasoning mix
• Black beans
• Kidney beans
• Chili beans with chipotle pepper
• Beef broth
• Diced tomatoes
• Chilies
• Tomato sauce

Steps:
1. Cook ground beef, garlic, onion, and red pepper over medium high heat
2. Drain excess fat and put in crockpot
3. Combine all remaining ingredients in crockpot and stir well
4. Cook on low for 6-8 hours<|endoftext|>
