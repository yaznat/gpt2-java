Ingredients:
• Beef flank steak
• Barbecue sauce
• Red pepper
• Yellow pepper
• Mayonnaise
• French baguettes
• Lettuce leaves

Steps:
1. Preheat greased grill to medium-high heat
2. Grill steak 8 to 10 mins on each side or until medium doneness
3. Grill peppers 2 to 3 mins on each side or until crisp-tender, brushing both with barbecue sauce for the last few minutes
4. Thinly slice steak and peppers
5. Spread baguettes with mayo and fill with lettuce, steak and peppers<|endoftext|>
