What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Argentina Skirt Steak Matambre

Ingredients:
• Skirt steaks
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Red wine vinegar
• Oregano
• Red pepper flakes
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Bay leaves
• Hot pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Arrange the steaks in a nonreactive baking dish and set aside while you prepare the marinade
2. Combine the bell pepper, garlic, oil, vinegar, oregano, pepper flakes, salt, and pepper in a small bowl and stir to mix well
3. Pour over the steaks in the baking dish and toss well to coat
4. Add the bay leaves, cover, and let marinate in the refrigerator, for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight
5. Preheat the grill to high
6. Combine the ingredients for the spice mixture in a small bowl
7. When ready to cook, oil the grill grate
8. Drain the steaks and place on the hot grate
9. Sprinkle the steaks with half the spice mixture and grill, turning with tongs, until medium to medium-well done, about 4 minutes per side, sprinkling with the remaining spice mixture after turning
10. Transfer the steaks to a cutting board and let stand about 3 minutes, then cut into 1-inch squares and serve on toothpicks
11. Serves 6 as an appetizer or 4 as a main dish<|endoftext|>
