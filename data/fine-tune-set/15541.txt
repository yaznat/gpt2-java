Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Dried ancho chile powder
• Garlic salt
• Ground cumin
• Cooking spray
• Lime zest
• Fresh lime juice
• Sour cream
• Milk
• Avocado
• Coleslaw
• Green onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Canola oil
• Salt
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. Heat a large skillet over high heat
2. Sprinkle chicken evenly with chile powder, garlic salt, and cumin
3. Coat pan with cooking spray
4. Add chicken to pan
5. Cook 4 - 5 minutes, stirring frequently
6. Remove chicken from pan
7. Combine zest, 1 tablespoon juice and sour cream, milk and avocado in a blender or food processor
8. Process until smooth
9. Combine remaining 1 tablespoon lime juice, coleslaw, green onions, cilantro, oil and salt, tossing to coat
10. Heat tortillas according to directions
11. Divide chicken mixture evenly among tortillas
12. Top each tortilla with about 1 tablespoon avocado mixture and 1 / 4 cup slaw mixture<|endoftext|>
