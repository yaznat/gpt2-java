What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Baked Crab Quesadillas Appetizers

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Jalapeno peppers
• Garlic clove
• Lump crabmeat
• Mayonnaise
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt
• Flour tortillas
• Jalapeno jack cheese

Steps:
1. Combine butter and oil
2. Set aside
3. Saute onion, peppers, and garlic in 2 tbsp reserved butter mixture in a medium saucepan over medium heat, stirring constantly until tender
4. Remove from heat
5. Gently stir in crabmeat and next 3 ingredients
6. Place on tortillas on baking sheets
7. Brush 1 side of each tortilla with remaining butter mixture
8. Turn tortillas over
9. Spread crabmeat mixture evenly over half of each tortilla, and sprinkle with cheese
10. Fold tortillas in half
11. Bake at 475 degrees for 4 minutes or until golden
12. Cut each tortilla into thirds
13. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
