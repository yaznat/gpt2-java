Ingredients:
• Steak-style french fries
• Cheez whiz
• Gravy
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 450 degrees f
2. Arrange steak fries in a single layer in a greased 15-in
3. X 10-in
4. Baking sheet
5. Salt and pepper as desired
6. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until lightly browned
7. About 5 minutes before the fries are finished baking, pour the gravy in a small pot and warm on the stove over medium heat, stirring frequently
8. Empty the cheese sauce in a microwave-safe bowl and cover with a sheet of paper towel
9. Heat the sauce on high for 30 seconds, then stop and stir the sauce
10. Repeat microwaving the cheese sauce on high for 30-second intervals for about 2 to 3 total minutes
11. Take fries out of the oven and place on a large platter
12. Pour gravy over the fries
13. Then pour on the cheese sauce
14. Add as much cheese and gravy as you want -- some like their disco fries drier
