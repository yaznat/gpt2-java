Give me a pizza recipe.<|endoftext|>Basic Pizza Dough Italian

Ingredients:
• Dried yeast
• Water
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Place the yeast into a small bowl and sprinkle with the sugar
2. Add 125 ml of lukewarm water, then stir to dissolve the yeast and sugar
3. Cover with a clean tea towel and prove in a warm spot for 30 minutes
4. Sift the flour into a large bowl
5. Make a hollow in the centre and pour the yeast mixture, salt, olive oil and 5-7 tbsp of water into it
6. Knead everything into a smooth silky dough, shape into a ball
7. Dust the ball with a little flour, cover and set for an hour or until the dough doubles in volume<|endoftext|>
