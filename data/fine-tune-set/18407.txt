Ingredients:
• Butter
• Bisquick baking mix
• Pepper
• Milk
• Ham
• Dried parsley
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat butter over low heat in a 2 quart saucepan until melted
2. Stir in 1 / 4 cup baking mix and the pepper
3. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until mixture is smooth and bubbly
4. Remove from heat and stir in 2 cups milk
5. Heat to boiling, stirring constantly
6. Boil and stir 1 minute
7. Stir in ham and parseley, heat through
8. Keep warm over low heat
9. Beat 2 cups baking mix, 1 1 / 3 cup cups milk, the oil and egg with wire whisk until well blended
10. Fold in the cheese
11. Pour 1 cup batter onto center of hot waffle iron
12. Bake until steaming slows
13. Remove waffle carefully
14. Serve with hot ham sauce over waffles<|endoftext|>
