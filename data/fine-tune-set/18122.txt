Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Apple Cider Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Oat flour
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Eggs
• Apple cider
• Canola oil
• Apples

Steps:
1. Combine flours, baking powder, sugar, and salt
2. Combine eggs, apple cider, oil, and apples then mix with dry ingredients just until moistened
3. Spray hot griddle with cooking spray, ladle 1 / 4 cup batter onto griddle, cook until bubbles form on top, flip, continue cooking until golden
4. Serve with cardamom cider syrup<|endoftext|>
