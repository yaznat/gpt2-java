Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Butter
• Water
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Cocoa
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease a 9x13 pan
3. Mix together sugar, butter, and water
4. Beat until smooth and creamy
5. Add eggs and vanilla to sugar mixture
6. Beat in completely
7. Mix dry ingredients in a separate bowl until evenly combined
8. Add dry ingredients to sugar mixture, mix thoroughly
9. Pour batter into the 9x13 pan and spread well
10. Bake 18-25 minutes, until a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
