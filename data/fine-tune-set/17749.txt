What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Banana Muffins

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Applesauce
• Eggs
• Milk
• Brown sugar
• Artificial sweetener
• Whole wheat flour
• Unbleached flour
• Kosher salt
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Allspice
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Mash banana in a large bowl
3. Add and mix in the applesauce, eggs, milk, brown sugar and sugar substitute
4. Measure and add all the remaining dry ingredients
5. Mix just until all dry ingredients are incorporated, scraping sides and bottom of bowl as needed
6. Fill prepared muffin cups about 2 / 3 full
7. Bake regular muffins about 22 minutes, mini muffins for about 18 minutes<|endoftext|>
