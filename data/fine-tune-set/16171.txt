Ingredients:
• 10-inch flour tortilla
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Jelly
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Lightly brush one side of tortilla with butter or margarine
2. Spread other side of tortilla with a layer of cream cheese
3. Spread 1 / 3 c
4. Jelly or preserves on one half of the tortilla, over cream cheese
5. Fold the tortilla in half, pressing slightly to seal edges
6. In a large skillet on medium-low heat, cook tortilla on each side until lightly browned to serve, dust with confectioner's sugar or cinnamon sugar
7. Each quesedilla may be served whole or cut into 2 to 4 wedges before serving
