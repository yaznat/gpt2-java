Ingredients:
• Water
• Sugar
• Butter
• Dried chili pepper flakes
• Pecans
• Baking sheet

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 200c / 400f
2. Combine and simmer 1st 3 ingredients in a heavy bottom saucepan about 10 mins to make syrup
3. Add chili flakes and reduce the syrup until it starst to form a soft ball
4. Add pecans and quickly mix to cover
5. Pour on a baking sheet and pull apart pecans from each other using either a fork / spoon
6. Place in oven for about 30 mins or until it becomes dry and crispy<|endoftext|>
