Ingredients:
• Peaches
• Fresh goat cheese
• Honey
• Naan bread
• Olive oil
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Prepare the baking sheet by lining it with aluminum foil and spray cooking oil
2. 2)place the naans adjacent to each other on the baking tray
3. 3)place 4-5 slices of peach on each of these naans
4. 4)drizzle some olive oil on top and sprinkle some black pepper
5. 5)bake these naans in a preheated 350-degree oven for 12-15 minutes, or un till they form crispy bottoms
6. 6)take the baking tray out of the oven
7. 7)spread crumbles of goat cheese on each of these naans and drizzle warm honey, 1 tbsp on each of them
8. 8)place the baking tray back in to the oven and bake for 1-2 minutes on 350-degree until the cheese just starts melting
9. Do not overdo this
10. 9)switch off the oven and remove the baking tray
11. Cut each naan in to 4 equal pieces and serve hot to enjoy it with your loved ones!<|endoftext|>
