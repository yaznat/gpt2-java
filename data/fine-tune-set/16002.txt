Ingredients:
• Thai sweet chili sauce
• Dark rum
• Fresh ginger
• Peach
• Fresh nutmeg
• Lime juice
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Fresh asparagus
• Salt
• Pepper
• Crepes
• Monterey jack cheese
• Chicken breast

Steps:
1. For the sweet rum dipping sauce:
2. Place all ingredients in a small food processing bowl
3. Pulse until peaches are still somewhat chunky
4. Grate some nutmeg over and stir to combine
5. Set aside
6. For the crepes:
7. Place the garlic and the olive oil in a small skillet, saut for 2 minutes
8. Don't let it burn!
9. Add the shaved asparagus and saut for 2-3 minutes, until it turns bright green
10. Season with salt and pepper to taste
11. Remove from pan and set aside
12. In an 8 inch skillet, heat the crepes
13. Stack on a piece of parchment paper until all are heated through
14. Place a couple of spoonfuls of the asparagus on the bottom half of a crepe
15. Place sliced brie over the asparagus, or if using mozzarella, sprinkle it over the asparagus
16. Then top with thinly sliced chicken
17. Fold the empty half over the filling
18. Then fold it in half again so it ends up being a quarter circle
19. Lightly grease the skillet and heat over a medium flame
20. When hot, cook each crepe until both sides are golden brown and the cheese has melted
21. Serve with a side of the sweet rum dipping sauce
22. Serving size is 1 as an appetizer, or two as a main course<|endoftext|>
