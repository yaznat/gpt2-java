What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Bacon Cheese Waffles

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Milk
• Sour cream
• Butter
• Pancake mix
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Beat egg, add milk, sour cream and butter
2. Stir in pancake mix
3. Mix well
4. Fold in bacon and cheese
5. Bake in preheated waffle iron<|endoftext|>
