What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Raisin Muffins

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Oil
• Applesauce
• Flour
• Cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Golden seedless raisins
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. With mixer beat eggs slightly
3. Add sugar, oil and applesauce
4. Beat thoroughly
5. In a separate bowl, mix dry ingredients together
6. Add dry to the applesauce mixture
7. Mix well
8. Stir in raisins
9. Fill greased muffin tins 2 / 3 full and sprinkle each muffin with a bit of brown sugar
10. Bake for 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
