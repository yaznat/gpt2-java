Can you give me a casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>7 Layer Ham Casserole

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Ham
• Frozen broccoli
• Onion
• Milk
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In 2 quart casserole dish layer ham, rice, broccoli and onion
2. Mix soup, milk and cheese in bowl, pour over layers
3. Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes, or until hot and bubbly
4. I sometimes add buttered bread crumbs to the top before baking<|endoftext|>
