Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Reduced-fat italian salad dressing
• Coating mix
• White fish fillet
• Reduced-fat mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Cut each potato lengthwise into 8 wedges
3. Place in a large bowl
4. Add italian dressing
5. Toss to coat
6. Add 1 / 4 cup of the coating mix
7. Toss to coat
8. Arrange on baking sheet sprayed with cooking spray
9. Bake 20 minutes
10. Meanwhile, spread remaining coating mix on a large plate
11. Spread tops of fish fillets evenly with half the mayonnaise
12. Press, mayo-sides down, into coating mix on plate
13. Repeat with remaining mayonnaise and coating mix on plate to coat remaining sides of fish fillets
14. Place on broiler pan
15. Bake potatoes and fish 15 minutes or until fish flakes easily with a fork and potatoes are tender<|endoftext|>
