Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Baking mix
• Refried beans
• Green chilies
• Water
• Salsa
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Prepare the ground beef and taco seasoning mix according to package directions
3. While meat is browning, mix baking mix, beans, green chilies and water
4. Spread bean mixture in the bottom of a 2-quart casserole dish
5. Top with meat, salsa, then cheese
6. Bake for 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
