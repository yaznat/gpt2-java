Please give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Fried Bean Curd Tofu With Soy Sauce By Sy

Ingredients:
• Tofu
• Peanut oil
• Scallions
• Hot green peppers
• Sesame seeds
• Soup stock
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Place oil in a pan and use low to medium heat
2. Fry half the bean curd until golden brown, turn as needed and then remove to a serving plate
3. Fry and remove the balance of the bean curd
4. Place the sliced scallions and peppers on top of the fried bean curd
5. In the wok with a little oil remaining, add sesame seeds fry briefly
6. Then add the stock, soy sauce, sesame oil
7. Stir once until heated and then pour over the tofu in the serving plate
8. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
