Can you give me a potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Chive Goat Cheese Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Garlic cloves
• Sour cream
• Soft fresh goat cheese
• Butter
• Salt
• Pepper
• Fresh chives

Steps:
1. Peel potatoes and cut in half crosswise
2. In a large pot of boiling salted water, cover and cook potatoes and garlic until tender, 25-30 minutes
3. Drain and return to pot
4. Dry over low heat, shaking pan, about 1 minute
5. Press through ricer - you could mash but it will not be as smooth or creamy
6. Optional - warm goat cheese, and sour cream in the microwave on low for 2 minutes
7. Mix sour cream, goat cheese, butter, salt and pepper into potatoes and stir smooth
8. Add chives if using
9. Put into a casserole dish or 13x9 baking dish
10. If making ahead - up to 2 days - cool and refrigerate
11. Cover and bake at 400f for 30 minutes, or microwave on high for 10-12 minutes
12. If cold add 10 minutes to baking time in the oven<|endoftext|>
