Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Butter
• Milk
• Eggs
• Bacon
• Shredded cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 425
2. Start frying bacon or sausage
3. Combine first 5 ingredients, then cut in butter
4. Make a well in the center and pour in milk
5. Stir until well combined and moist
6. Turn dough out onto floured surface and knead until dough holds together well and is pliable
7. Press into bottom of a greased 9 x 13 pan
8. Beat the eggs with a splash of milk and pour over biscuit crust
9. Sprinkle crumbled bacon or sausage over the egg mixture and top with cheese
10. Bake for 16 - 20 minutes, or until egg is cooked through<|endoftext|>
