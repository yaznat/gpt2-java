Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Dark rum

Steps:
1. Take 2 ripe, but not soft bananas and cut in half, then split lengthwise
2. Melt a few tablespoons butter over medium heat
3. Add 2-3 tablespoons brown sugar and stir
4. Add bananas
5. Cook until fairly warm and starting to soften
6. Flip bananas and allow to cook an additional 30 seconds
7. Add rum to taste, and swirl in pan a bit
8. Allow to thicken slightly, remove from heat and serve<|endoftext|>
