Please give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Bean And Vegetable Stew Vegan

Ingredients:
• Beans
• Vegetable juice
• Dry white wine
• Soy sauce
• Apples
• Vegetable stock
• Celery
• Parsnip
• Carrot
• Mushroom
• Onion
• Basil
• Parsley
• Bay leaf
• Garlic
• Black pepper
• Rice

Steps:
1. Sort and rinse beans, then soak overnight in water
2. Drain beans and place in crockpot
3. Add vegetable juice, wine, soy sauce, and apple or pineapple juice
4. Cover with vegetable stock or water
5. The amount added depends on whether you prefer a soup or a stew
6. The juice adds just a tad of sweetness and the soy sauce adds depth and the tang of salt
7. Cook at high for 2 hours
8. Add vegetables, herbs, and spices, and cook for 5-6 hours at low until carrots and parsnips are tender
9. When tender, add rice or pasta and cook for one additional hour<|endoftext|>
