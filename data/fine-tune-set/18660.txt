Ingredients:
• Butter
• Dates
• Water
• Baking soda
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Vanilla extract
• Baking powder
• Heavy cream
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Butter a 9" round or square baking pan with 1 tbls butter
2. Place dates in saucepan and cover with water
3. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer for 3 minutes
4. Add baking soda, set aside
5. In a bowl, cream 1 / 2 cup butter, the 5 tbls granualted sugar and the eggs, adding one at a time
6. Mix well after each egg addition
7. Gently fold in flour, salt and 1 / 2 tsp vanilla
8. Slowly stir in baking powder and 1 / 2 cup of the date cooking liquid until cake mixture resembles thick pancake batter
9. Discard remaining cooking liquid
10. Stir in dates
11. Pour into prepared pan and bake at 350 for 30-40 minutes or until cooked in center
12. Make a sauce by combining 1 / 2 cup butter, 1 / 4 cup heavy cream, brown sugar and 1 / 2 tsp vanilla in a medium saucepan
13. Bring to a boil
14. Reduce heat and simmer for 3 minutes
15. Drizzle some of the sauce over the cake as it is cooling
16. Serve the remainder of sauce seperately
17. Garnish with whipped cream
18. I sometimes use cool whip
