Ingredients:
• Ground buffalo meat
• Yellow onion
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic
• Mushroom
• Kidney beans
• Whole canned tomatoes
• Crushed tomatoes
• Celery
• Chili powder
• Dry white wine
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In a large cooking pot, sautee meat, onion, pepper, garlic and mushrooms
2. Once meat is browned, lower to medium-low heat and add tomatoes, then the rest of the ingredients
3. Stir about every 3-5 minutes, making sure to not let any of the ingredients sit at the bottom of the pot
4. After about 1 1 / 2- 2 hours chili should be ready to serve<|endoftext|>
