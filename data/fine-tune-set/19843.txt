Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Salt
• Sourdough bread
• Fig preserves
• Brie cheese
• Prosciutto
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 500 degrees
2. Line a large baking sheet with foil
3. Scrub the sweet potatoes and cut into fries 1 / 2-inch thick
4. Toss on the baking sheet with the olive oil and season lightly with salt
5. Spread in a single layer and bake for 10 minutes
6. Flip with a spatula, then bake until the ends start to crisp about 8 to 10 minutes
7. Heat a nonstick griddle or large skillet over medium heat
8. Lay the bread slices in pairs on a work surface
9. Spread the fig preserves equally onto 4 slices of bread
10. Lay 2 slices of cheese over the preserves
11. Arrange the prosciutto on top, folding evenly with the edges of the bread
12. Cover the remaining bread slices and spread half the butter on top
13. Invert the sandwiches and spread the remaining butter on the other side
14. Put sandwiches in hot pan, cook turning once or twice until both sides are golden and crisp and the cheese is melted
15. Cut the sandwiches in half and serve with sweet potato fries<|endoftext|>
