Give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Banana Caramel Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Caramel sauce
• Banana
• Vanilla ice cream
• Milk
• Whipping cream
• Sugar
• Lemon juice
• Water
• Vanilla extract
• Kosher salt
• Dark rum

Steps:
1. To make caramel sauce:
2. Bring cream to just under a boil over medium heat in a medium saucepan
3. Combine sugar, lemon juice, and 3 tablespoons water into a separate saucepan
4. Place over medium heat, and, without stirring, bring to a simmer
5. Continue to cook while swirling the pan around until it becomes a deep caramel color, about 6 to 8 minutes let the color be your guide
6. Slowly add hot cream, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon
7. Cook 1 minute to dissolve caramel
8. Remove from heat
9. Stir in vanilla, kosher salt, and rum
10. Let cool
11. Store refrigerated in an airtight container up to 5 days
12. Bring to room temperature before using
13. To make milkshake:
14. Process 1 / 2 cup caramel sauce and all ingredients in a blender until smooth
15. Serve in individual glasses, and top with additional caramel sauce, if desired<|endoftext|>
