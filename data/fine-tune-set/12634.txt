Ingredients:
• Beef roast
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Thyme
• Red wine
• Low sodium beef broth
• Hoisin sauce
• Bay leaf
• Carrots
• Red potatoes
• Butter beans
• Green beans
• Frozen corn
• Frozen green pea
• Diced tomatoes
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Dry meat chunks and season with salt and pepper
2. Brown in olive oil, in 2 batches, remove from pot and set aside
3. Add onion, garlic and thyme to pot and lightly brown
4. Deglaze pot with red wine
5. To pot, add beef broth, hoisin or soy sauce and bay leaf
6. Cover pot and simmer about 2 hours or until meat is fork tender
7. Remove 1 / 4 cup of the broth and set aside
8. Add potatoes, carrots, butter beans, green beans, corn, peas and tomatoes if using
9. Simmer until vegetables are tender, about 30 minutes
10. Add cornstarch to reserved broth, mix with a fork an add to pot, stirring until thickened
11. Adjust seasonings to taste<|endoftext|>
