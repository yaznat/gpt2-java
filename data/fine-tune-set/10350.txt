Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Almond extract
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Powdered sugar
• Water
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400
2. Combine butter, sugar and almond extract in large bowl
3. Beat at medium speed, scraping bowl often, until creamy
4. Reduce speed to low
5. Add all remaining cookie ingredients
6. Beat until well mixed
7. Roll dough into 1 inch balls
8. Place 2 inches apart on ungreased cookie sheet
9. Flatten balls to 1 / 4 inch thickness with bottom of buttered glass dipped in sugar
10. Bake for 7-9 minutes or until edges are very lightly browned
11. Cool 1 minute
12. Remove from sheets
13. Cool completely
14. Stir together all glaze ingredients with wire whisk in small bowl
15. Decorate cooled cookies with glaze and sliced almonds
16. Glaze sets up quickly
17. Frost and decorate a few cookies at a time<|endoftext|>
