Ingredients:
• Sliced apples
• Lemon juice
• Sugar
• Self rising flour
• Cinnamon
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• Shortening
• Water

Steps:
1. Combine 1 cup flour, 1 tbsp sugar, egg, shortening, and water
2. Beat 2 minutes at medium speed
3. Pour batter into 9x1 1 / 4 inch pan
4. Combine pie filling: apples, lemon juice, sugar, flour, and cinnamon
5. Pour into center of batter
6. Do not stir!
7. Sprinkle with brown sugar
8. Bake at 425f for 35-40 minutes
9. This pie will make its own crust<|endoftext|>
