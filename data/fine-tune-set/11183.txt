Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Nutella
• Bread

Steps:
1. Add butter to one side of each slice of bread
2. Fry butter side down
3. Remove bread when golden brown
4. Spread on nutella on the non-fried side
5. Add sliced bananas
6. Fold and eat<|endoftext|>
