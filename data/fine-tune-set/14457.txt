Can you give me a beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Orchard Bean Bake

Ingredients:
• Pork sausage
• Baked beans
• Tomato soup
• Pepper
• Apple
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. Core the apple and slice thinly
3. Brown the sausage in a skillet
4. Drain well
5. Stir in beans, soup, and pepper
6. Pour into a greased 1 1 / 2 quart casserole dish
7. Arrange apple slices over casserole
8. Sprinkle brown sugar over apples
9. Bake uncovered at 450 degrees for 25 minutes
10. Let stand 5-10 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
