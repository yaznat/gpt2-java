What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Baked Chutney Lamb Chops

Ingredients:
• Fruit chutney
• Dijon mustard
• Lamb chops
• Potatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 220 c and line a baking tray with baking paper
2. Combine chuntey and mustard in a large bowl
3. Add chops and potato and evenly coat with mixture
4. Place on prepared baking tray and bake for 20-25 minutes or until lamb is cooed an browned and the potatoes golden<|endoftext|>
