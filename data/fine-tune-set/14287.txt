Ingredients:
• Tofu
• Vegetable oil
• Tamari soy sauce
• Water
• Vegetarian worcestershire sauce
• Ground allspice
• Onion
• Savoy cabbage
• Tomato paste
• Vinegar
• Dried dill
• Salt
• Sweet hungarian paprika
• Black pepper
• Cooked rice
• Dill pickle
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. To prepare the tofu:
2. Preheat the oven to 375f combine the marinade ingredients in a 9-inch baking dish
3. Cut the tofu into cubes or triangles
4. Place the tofu in the baking dish with the marinade
5. Bake, turning the tofu two or three times during the baking
6. Bake for 35-45 minutes
7. To prepare the cabbage:
8. While the tofu is baking, saute the onion in the oil in a large heavy skillet
9. When the onion is almost translucent, add the cabbage
10. Stirring occasionally, saute until the cabbage is somewhat reduced, about 5 minutes, but don't let the onion brown
11. Combine the sauce ingredients and pour the over the cabbage, stirring to coat
12. Remove from the heat
13. Cover the skillet with a lid or foil, and bake in a 375 f oven for 30 minutes
14. Serve the cabbage over rice or mashed potatoes
15. Top with minced pickle and baked tofu
16. Add a spoonful of sour cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
