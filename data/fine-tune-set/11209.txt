Ingredients:
• Sourdough bread
• Skinless chicken breast half
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Barbecue sauce
• Guacamole
• Sour cream
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Cut a pocket in chicken breast half
2. Fill with cheese
3. Secure with metal or soaked wooden skewers
4. Grill chicken, covered, over medium heat for 6-8 minutes on each side until juices run clear to desired doneness
5. Toast bread, place sliced tomato on bread, lettuce and chicken
6. Spread sour cream / guacamole mixture on top of bun and place on top of sandwich<|endoftext|>
