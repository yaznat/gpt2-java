Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Nut Cookies

Ingredients:
• Light brown sugar
• Shortening
• Egg
• Flour
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Apple
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Cream together sugar and shortening
2. Beat in egg
3. Sift together dry ingredients, and add to mixture
4. Beat until well blended
5. Stir in apples and nuts
6. Drop in the shape of a ball on a greased cookie sheet
7. Bake at 375, for 12-15 minutes<|endoftext|>
