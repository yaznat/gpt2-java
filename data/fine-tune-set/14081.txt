Ingredients:
• Eggplants
• Ground lamb
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Red bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Tomatoes
• Dried oregano
• Dried thyme leaves
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Long-grain white rice
• Beef broth
• Salt and pepper
• Feta cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f place eggplant halves, cut side down, in a roasting pan
2. Add 1 / 2" of water to pan and bake 20-25 minutes, until tender
3. Reduce oven temp to 400f
4. While eggplant in roasting, heat a large dutch oven over med-high heat
5. Add ground lamb and cook, breaking up meat with a wooden spoon, until browned, 3-5 minutes
6. Transfer to colander to drain off fat
7. Set aside
8. Add oil to the dutch oven and heat over med heat
9. Add onions and cook until golden, about 5 minutes
10. Add red peppers and garlic and cook for 2 minutes more
11. Stir in tomatoes and their juice, oregano, thyme, cinnamon and cloves
12. Simmer, breaking up the tomatoes a bit with the spoon, until mixture has thickened slightly, 3-5 minutes
13. Scoop out eggplant flesh and chop coarsely
14. Stir rice, beef broth, chopped eggplant and lamb into tomato mixture
15. Bring to a simmer, then cover the pan and place in oven
16. Bake 30-35 minutes, or until rice is tender and liquid has been absorbed
17. Season with salt and pepper
18. Dot with feta cheese and serve<|endoftext|>
