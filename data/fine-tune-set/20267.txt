Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• Fat free chicken broth
• Garlic cloves
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Red onion
• White mushrooms
• Red pepper
• Green pepper
• Baby carrots
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Baby bok choy
• Stir-fry sauce
• Water
• Minute rice
• Peanuts
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Spray wok or large skillet, add broth and crushed garlic, heat for 1-2 minutes
2. Add chicken breast pieces and cook on medium heat for 7 minutes
3. Add onion, mushrooms, peppers, carrots, water chestnuts, bok choy, stir-fry sauce and water
4. Allow to cook covered over medium heat for 10 minutes
5. Prepare rice according to directions
6. Add peanuts to stir-fry mixture and cook 2-3 more minutes
7. Serve immediately over rice<|endoftext|>
