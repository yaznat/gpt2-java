Give me a burrito recipe.<|endoftext|>Chicken And Black Bean Burritos

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Pepper
• Chicken breasts
• Jalapeno pepper
• Sweet red pepper
• Black beans
• Salsa
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. In skillet, heat oil over medium-high heat
2. Fry onion, garlic, salt, and pepper until softened, about 3 minute
3. Add chicken, jalapeno, red pepper, beans and salsa
4. Cook, stirring, until chicken is no longer pink inside, about 10 minute
5. Let cool
6. Spoon about 3 / 4 cup of the filling down centre of each tortilla
7. Sprinkle each with 1 / 4 cup of the cheese
8. Fold in bottom edge, then sides
9. Roll up
10. Place, seam down, in greased 13x9 inch glass backing dish, sprinkle remaining cheese over top
11. Back in 400 degree oven until golden and hot, and cheese is melted, about 15 minute
12. Serve with sour cream
13. If baking from frozen, loosely cover dish with foil, and bake in a 400 degree oven until golden and hot, about 25-30 minute
14. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
