Can you give me a casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Almost One Pot Chicken Casserole

Ingredients:
• Whole chickens
• Flour
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic cloves
• White pearl onions
• Button mushrooms
• Fennel
• Chicken stock
• Fresh tomato
• Italian seasoning
• Bacon
• White potatoes
• Broccoli florets

Steps:
1. Put flour, salt, pepper and chicken in a plastic bag
2. Shake well to coat all chicken pieces
3. Heat oil in a large frying pan or dutch oven
4. Fry chicken uncovered for 4-5 minutes each side
5. Remove and set aside
6. In the same pan, add garlic, onions, mushrooms and fennel, and cook for about seven minutes or until these ingredients are softened
7. Add the chicken stock, chopped tomato, italian seasonings, fennel seeds and chicken pieces
8. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes
9. While the above items are cooking, and in a separate pan, cook the diced bacon
10. Drain on paper towels and set aside
11. Reserve bacon fat
12. Cook diced potatoes in the microwave for about five minutes
13. Then saute until tender in the pan with the bacon fat
14. Cook broccoli in the microwave for 3-4 minutes and set aside
15. Use the microwave dish you used for the potatoes
16. Add broccoli and bacon to the main pan
17. Cook for 3-5 minutes to heat through
18. Serve over rice or mashed potatoes<|endoftext|>
