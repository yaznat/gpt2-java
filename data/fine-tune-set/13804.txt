Ingredients:
• Black bean garlic sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Soy sauce
• Oriental sesame oil
• Fresh lime juice
• Fresh ginger
• Boneless center cut pork chops
• Baby bok choy
• Fresh cilantro
• Lime wedges

Steps:
1. Whisk together black bean sauce, garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, lime juice, and ginger in shallow dish
2. Set 2 tablespoons marinade aside
3. Add pork to remaining marinade
4. Let stand 20 minutes
5. Remove pork from marinade
6. Brush cut side of bok choy with reserved 2 tablespoons marinade
7. Place pork on 4" rack and cook on hi for about 8~10 minutes per side
8. Grill bok choy until softened, about 8~10 minutes total
9. Divide pork and bok choy among 4 plates
10. Sprinkle with cilantro, garnish with lime wedges, and serve<|endoftext|>
