Give me a recipe for a wrap.<|endoftext|>Asian Chickpea Lettuce Wraps

Ingredients:
• Chickpeas
• Chili sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Olive oil
• Rice vinegar
• Sugar
• Red pepper flakes
• Hot sauce
• Basil
• Lettuce leaves

Steps:
1. Drain and rinse both cans of chickpeas
2. Place chickpeas in a food processor and puree in 5-10 second increments
3. You do not want to turn the chickpeas into a mash, you simply want to grind them into pieces
4. Add 1 tbsp of olive oil to a pan and heat
5. Add the chickpeas and let cook over medium heat for 3-4 minutes, continuously stirring so as not to burn
6. Add remaining ingredients, except basil, and stir
7. Cook over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring occassionally
8. If using basil leaves, add them and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring the basil in and allowing it to wilt
9. Wash lettuce leaves and pat dry
10. Spoon a healthy serving of chickpea mixture onto lettuce wraps and serve immediately
11. Drizzle with sirachi sauce if desired<|endoftext|>
