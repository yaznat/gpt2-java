Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Black beans
• Chicken broth
• Salt

Steps:
1. Season the ground beef with cumin, chili powder and salt
2. Brown the beef and drain it
3. Add the remaining ingredients and simmer until the liquid is gone, about 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
