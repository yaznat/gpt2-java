Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Garlic salt
• Garlic cloves
• Vegetable oil
• Yellow onion
• Ground ginger
• Green pepper
• Tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Thyme
• Red pepper
• White rice
• Water
• Poultry seasoning

Steps:
1. In a 10 inch skilled saute chicken which has been skinned, deboned, and cut-up
2. With garlic salt and 1 / 2 cup oil until lightly browned
3. Set aside
4. In a 4 quart kettle saute chopped yellow onions, ginger and green pepper in 1 / 4 cup oil until onions are soft
5. Add tomatoes and simmer 5 minutes
6. Add salt, black pepper thyme, tomato paste and simmer 10 minutes
7. Add the cooked meat and crushed red pepper
8. Simmer 20 minutes
9. In a 2 quart saucepan cook rice, chicken stock and onions until tender
10. Serve the rice on a platter arranging the meat / sauce in the center
