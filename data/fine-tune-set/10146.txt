Ingredients:
• Beets
• Olive oil
• Balsamic vinegar
• Garlic cloves
• Thyme
• Couscous
• Vegetable broth
• Salt and pepper
• Pine nuts
• Lemon
• Lemon juice
• Fresh thyme leaves
• Red chili pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 425f peel the beets, cut into 1 / 2in cubes and put in a roasting tin with the oil, vinegar, garlic and thyme
2. Mix well, season and cover the tin with foil
3. Roast for 45 minutes or until tender
4. Set aside to cool
5. Place the couscous in a bowl, pour over the broth, cover and stand for 5 minutes
6. Fluff the grains up with a fork
7. Whisk together the dressing ingredients
8. Stir the roasted beets and any roasting juices, the toasted pine nuts and the dressing into the couscous and stand for 15 minutes to allow the colour to develop
9. Serve garnished with thyme leaves<|endoftext|>
