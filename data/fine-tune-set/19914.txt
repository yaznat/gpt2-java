What's a recipe for an omelette?<|endoftext|>Baked Omelette

Ingredients:
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic clove
• Milk
• Eggs
• Cooked ham
• Cheddar cheese
• Mushroom

Steps:
1. Cream sauce: melt butter in large dutch oven
2. Mix in flour, salt, pepper and garlic
3. Stir in first amount of milk until it boils and thickens
4. Whisk in second amount of milk
5. Mixture can be refrigerated at this point until the next day
6. Beat eggs in small bowl until smooth
7. Stir into cream sauce
8. Add ham, cheese and mushrooms
9. Mix well
10. Pour into greased 8x8 pan
11. Bake in 350 f oven for about 1 1 / 2 hours
12. An inserted knife should come out clean
13. Let stand 10 minutes before cutting<|endoftext|>
