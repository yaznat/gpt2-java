Please give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Bean And Cheese Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Refried beans
• Monterey jack cheese
• Corn
• Salsa
• Flour tortillas
• Sour cream
• Avocado
• Diced tomato

Steps:
1. Heat refried beans, corn, and salsa in a small saucepan- stir to combine well
2. Heat a skillet over medium heat
3. Warm each tortilla for just a minute in the skillet
4. Spread the corn, bean and salsa mix on 2 tortillas
5. Add diced tomato and avocado if you like
6. Sprinkle generously with cheese
7. Top with the extra tortillas
8. Heat about 3 minutes, flip, heat until all hot and melted
9. Use a pizza cutter to cut into 8 wedges
10. Garnish with sour cream and salsa<|endoftext|>
