What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>24 Hour Salad

Ingredients:
• Iceberg lettuce
• Cauliflower
• Bacon
• White onion
• White sugar
• Mayonnaise
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook bacon, crumble and set aside
2. Place the following items in order listed into a big bowl: lettuce, cauliflower, onion, crumbled bacon sprinkle all sugar on top of salad
3. Mix mayo and parmesan cheese- spread on top of salad
4. Cover with plastic wrap then aluminum foil
5. Refrigerate overnite
6. Drain water from salad, mix and serve<|endoftext|>
