Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Amy S Beef Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Beef broth
• Soy sauce
• Dry sherry
• Sugar
• Boneless beef sirloin
• Salad oil
• Garlic clove
• Fresh gingerroot
• Green onions
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Water chestnuts
• Chow mein noodles
• Baby corn
• Broccoli

Steps:
1. Combine broth, soy, sherry and sugar, set aside
2. Cut beef with the grain into strips
3. Place wok over med-high heat
4. Add 1 tbsp of oil
5. Add garlic and ginger
6. Stir-fry for about 30 seconds
7. Turn heat to high
8. Add half the beef and onion and stir-fry until meat is lightly browned
9. Remove from wok
10. Repeat using remaining beef and onion
11. Return cooked meat to wok
12. Pour in broth mixture
13. Cover and simmer for 3 minutes
14. Mix cornstarch and water and gradually stir into wok
15. Cook until slightly thickened, stirring to coat meat
16. Add water chestnuts, baby corn, and broccoli
17. Serve with steamed white rice and sprinkle the noodles on top<|endoftext|>
