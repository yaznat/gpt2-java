Can you give me a tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Fried Tofu In Curry Sauce

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Green pepper
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Curry powder
• Tomato sauce
• Coconut milk
• Whole cloves
• Cardamom
• Cinnamon stick
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and saut tofu pieces until crispy and browned
2. Remove tofu from skillet and set aside
3. Saut onion and green pepper until onion is translucent then add ginger and garlic
4. Cook for 2 to 3 min until fragrant, then stir in the curry powder
5. Return tofu to skillet and add tomato sauce, coconut milk, cloves, cardamom and cinnamon stick
6. Season with salt and pepper
7. And stir all together
8. Reduce heat to low and simmer for about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally<|endoftext|>
