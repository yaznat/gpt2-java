Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Butterfinger Ice Cream Cake

Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Chocolate ice cream
• Cream
• Butterfinger bar

Steps:
1. Break up bars
2. Cut ice cream into slabs
3. Layer 1 white, then butterfinger, chocolate, then butterfinger, then white again
4. Top with cream and butterfinger
5. Put back into freezer until needed<|endoftext|>
