What's a recipe for tea?<|endoftext|>5 Spice Tea

Ingredients:
• Green tea leaves
• Water
• Milk
• Chinese five spice powder

Steps:
1. Bring water to a boil in saucepan
2. Add tea leaves and simmer for 10 minutes
3. Place milk in microwavable container and microwave until steaming
4. Add milk to tea mixture, and stir in 5-spice powder
5. Simmer 1 minute
6. Strain tea into mugs, or refrigerate for iced tea
7. Sweeten to taste, with sweetener of choice<|endoftext|>
