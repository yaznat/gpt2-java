Ingredients:
• Marinara sauce
• Whole grain english muffins
• Arugula leaf
• Tomatoes
• Turkey bacon
• Part-skim mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat broiler
2. Spread marinara sauce evenly over english muffin halves
3. Top with arugula, tomato, and bacon
4. Sprinkle with cheese
5. Broil 3 to 4 inches from the heat for 1 to 2 minutes or until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
