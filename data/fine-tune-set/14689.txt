Ingredients:
• Short-grain rice
• Honey
• Cinnamon
• Dried apricots
• Preserved gingerroot

Steps:
1. Put the rice in a saucepan and add just enough cold water to cover
2. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, cover and cook for 15 minutes or until the water has been absorbed and the rice is done
3. Stir the honey and cinnamon in the rice
4. Lightly grease 4 ramekin dishes
5. Put the apricots and ginger in a food processor and process to make a paste divide the paste into 4 equal portions and shape each into a flat round to fit into the circumference of the ramekin dishes
6. Divide half of the rice among the ramekins and place the apricot paste on top
7. Cover the apricot mixture with the remaining rice
8. Carefully cover the ramekins in a steamer and steam for 30 minutues or unitl set
9. Remove the ramkins from the steamer and let stand for 5 minutes
10. Turn the puddings out onto warm serving plates and drizzle with additional honey
11. Serve immediatley or serve cold with ice cream on the side
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
