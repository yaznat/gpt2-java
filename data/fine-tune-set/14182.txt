Ingredients:
• Onion powder
• Salt
• Dried oregano
• Dried basil
• Dried thyme
• Black pepper
• Chili powder
• Celery seed
• Paprika
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic
• Red pepper
• Celery
• Tomato sauce
• Bay leaf
• Extra firm tofu
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Mix the first 9 ingredients to make a spice mix
2. Set aside
3. Heat butter and olive oil in a large pot
4. Add onion and saute until starting to brown
5. Add garlic and 3 tbsp of the spice mix
6. Stir and cook a couple minutes
7. Add pepper and celery and cook a couple more minutes
8. Add tomato sauce, bay leaf and tofu
9. Mix well
10. Bring to a boil and then simmer 10 minute
11. Add hot sauce to taste<|endoftext|>
