What's a recipe for a smoothie?<|endoftext|>Banana Cream Smoothies

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Unsweetened crushed canned pineapple
• Frozen unsweetened raspberries
• Plain fat-free yogurt

Steps:
1. In a blender, combine all ingredients until smooth
2. Stir if necessary
3. Pour into chilled glasses & serve immediately<|endoftext|>
