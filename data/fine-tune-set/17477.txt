Give me a coffee recipe.<|endoftext|>Berry Wheat Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Raspberries
• Water
• Splenda granular
• Cornstarch
• Flour
• Wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Butter
• Baby food
• Egg whites
• Skim milk
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees and grease 8x8 baking pan
2. Filling: combine berries and water in medium saucepan
3. Bring to boiling
4. Combine splenda and cornstarch
5. Stir into fruit
6. Cooke and stir over medium heat until thick and bubbly
7. Cook and stire 2 minutes more
8. Set filling aside
9. Cake: in a medium bowl, combine flours, splenda, baking powder, and baking soda
10. Cut in butter until mixture resembles course crumbs
11. Add baby food and use pastry cutter to cut it inches mixture should still resemble course crumbs
12. Make a well in the center
13. Set aside
14. Combine milk, egg whites and vanilla
15. Add all at once to flour mixture and stir until just combined
16. This will be lumpy!
17. Spread slightly more than half of mixture into an 8x8 baking pan
18. Spread filling over batter
19. Drop remaining batter in small mounds over filling
20. For topping: mix flour and splenda
21. Cut in butter until mixture resembles course crumbs and sprinkle coffee cake
22. Bake at 350 degrees for 40-45 minutes or until golden
23. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
