What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Minute Double Layer Chocolate Pie

Ingredients:
• Graham cracker pie crust
• Milk
• Chocolate flavor instant pudding and pie filling mix
• Frozen non-dairy topping

Steps:
1. Beat milk, pudding and 1 / 2 of the whipped topping in medium bowl with wire whisk 1 minute
2. Spread in crust
3. Spread remaining whipped topping over pudding layer in crust
4. Enjoy immediately or refrigerate until ready to serve
5. Garnish as desired<|endoftext|>
