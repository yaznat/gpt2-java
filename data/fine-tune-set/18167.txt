What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Ataif Atayif Bil Ishta Arab Pancakes Filled With Cream

Ingredients:
• Plain flour
• Dried yeast
• Sugar
• Skim milk
• Water
• Cornflour
• Granulated sugar
• Double cream
• Rose water
• Vanilla essence
• Pistachios

Steps:
1. To make the filling:
2. Mix corn flour gradually with milk and sugar then bring it to boil in a medium sized, heavy bottomed pan
3. Stir continually until the mixture thickens, add the remaining ingredients & remove from heat
4. Spoon mixture into a bowl & refrigerate until cold
5. To make the dough:
6. Mix all the ingredients and allow to sit for 30-40 minutes
7. Ladle approximately 2 tablespoons of the mixture at a time onto a lightly greased, hot frying pan / skillet & cook until full of bubbles - only cook one side
8. After cooking all of the pancakes, let them cool a little
9. To assemble, gently fold in half & pinch the bottom half edge together - so that you have an opening to place the cream but the rest is sealed
10. Using a piping bag, fill each pancake with the cooled cream, before dipping the open ends in the chopped pistachios<|endoftext|>
