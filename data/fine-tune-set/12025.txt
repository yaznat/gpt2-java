Ingredients:
• Milk
• Chocolate instant pudding
• Whipped topping
• Graham cracker pie crust

Steps:
1. In large bowl beat milk and pudding mix with wire whisk for 1 minute
2. Whisk in half of whipped topping
3. Carefully spread in crust
4. Spread remaining whipped topping over top
5. Garnish as desired
6. Enjoy immediately or refrigerate until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
