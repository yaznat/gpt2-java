Can you give me a tea recipe?<|endoftext|>April S Russian Tea

Ingredients:
• Tea bags
• Water
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Pineapple juice
• Frozen orange juice
• Lemon juice
• Splenda brown sugar blend

Steps:
1. In a pot or stock pot, boil water and tea bags for 5 minutes
2. Remove tea bags and add spices, juice, and sugar
3. Simmer for 15 minutes
4. Remove and drink or refrigerate
5. Heat in microwave to desired temperature or drink cold<|endoftext|>
