What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>All Saints Summer Supper Maryland Crab Cakes

Ingredients:
• Crabmeat
• Egg
• Worcestershire sauce
• Mustard powder
• Dried parsley
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Old bay seasoning
• Breadcrumbs
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Pick through your crab
2. There are always shells missed at the plant
3. Mix all ingredients except the breadcrumbs
4. You can beat the egg before adding, but i scramble it a bit in the bowl then use my hands to get a consistent mix
5. Add the breadcrumbs a bit at a time
6. The goal is to have just enough to bind the mix into patties, but not too much!
7. Gently form the mixture into patties, about 31 / 2 inches in diameter, and 3 / 4 inch thick
8. At this stage, you can refrigerate them
9. Doing so allows a little bit of flavor blending to occur
10. Get a cast iron skillet hot, and melt some olive oil, then butter in the pan
11. Saute over medium heat, flipping once, so that both side are a golden brown
12. Optional step: after you flip once, add some red wine to the pan, it adds a light glaze to the cakes, and a nice flavor!
13. Serve with tartar sauce, as entree, appetizer, or sandwich<|endoftext|>
