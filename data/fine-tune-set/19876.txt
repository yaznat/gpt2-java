What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Italian Baked French Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Light italian dressing
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Wash potatoes and cut into thin wedges
3. Put into a bowl and stir in italian dressing
4. Coat a 9x13 pan with the olive oil and spread potatoes on top
5. Cook 45-60 minutes or until golden brown<|endoftext|>
