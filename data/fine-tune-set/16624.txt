Ingredients:
• Banana
• Unsweetened orange juice
• Nectarine
• Milk
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. In a blender container, combine banana and orange juice
2. Blend until smooth
3. Add cut up nectarine and ice cubes
4. Blend until smooth
5. With blender running, add milk
6. Blend until smooth and frothy<|endoftext|>
