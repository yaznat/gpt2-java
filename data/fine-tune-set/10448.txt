Ingredients:
• Butter
• White sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Powdered sugar
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. In a medium mixing bowl, cream together the butter and sugar
2. Beat in the egg and vanilla
3. In a small bowl, sift together the flour, salt and baking powder
4. Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture
5. Mix well
6. Divide dough in half
7. Flatten halves into discs
8. Wrap in plastic wrap and freeze 20 minutes or longer
9. Remove from freezer and let stand 5 - 10 minutes
10. Place one disk on a lightly floured surface, roll 1 / 8 inch thick
11. Cut with your favorite cookie cutter shapes
12. Place on a greased or parchment lined baking sheet
13. Bake at 325f for 12-14 minutes
14. Allow to cool for a few minutes on the cookie sheet and then remove to cookie racks
15. In a small bowl, stir together the powdered sugar and lemon juice, adding only enough glaze to form a slow sticky stream when you dip into the glaze and let it flow back into the bowl
16. Spread glaze over top of each cookie
17. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
