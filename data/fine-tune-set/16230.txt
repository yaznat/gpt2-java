Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Chili sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dry sherry
• Chili powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Chopped tomato
• Green bell pepper
• Red onions
• Sea salt
• Flour tortillas
• Alfalfa sprout

Steps:
1. Combine the mayonnaise, chili sauce, worcestershire sauce, shery, chili powder, and cayenne if using, in a medium bowl
2. Add the cheese, tomato, bell pepper, and onion
3. Stir until well incorported
4. Season to taste with sea salt
5. Divide the cheese mixture evenly among the tortillas, top with the sprouts, and wrap
6. Enjoy right away
7. How to wrap:
8. If tortilla is stiff, warm slightly to make more flexible
9. Fold the right and left edges of the tortilla over the filling, toward the center
10. Fold the bottom edge of the tortilla toward the center and gently roll until the tortilla is completely wrapped around the filling<|endoftext|>
