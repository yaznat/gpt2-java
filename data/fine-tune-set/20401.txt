Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Alea S Meat Lover S Hashbrown Casserole

Ingredients:
• Hash brown potatoes
• Eggs
• Cottage cheese
• Green onions
• Cheddar cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Ham
• Garlic powder
• Basil
• Seasoning salt
• Pepper
• Bacon
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f grease a 13x9-inch pan
2. In a medium bowl, beat eggs
3. Add cottage cheese and spices to eggs and stir to combine
4. In a large bowl, combine hash browns, 1 cup cheddar cheese, 1 cup mozzarella cheese, ham or sausage, and onions
5. Pour egg mixture over the hash brown mixture and stir to combine
6. Spoon hash brown mixture into the greased casserole
7. Sprinkle remaining cheese, bacon, and parsley over the top
8. Bake at 350f for 1 hour or until an inserted knife comes out clean
9. Let sit for 5-10 minutes to set before serving

11. I serve it with fresh fruit
