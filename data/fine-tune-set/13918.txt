What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Kiwi Lamb Kabobs

Ingredients:
• Leg of lamb
• Kiwi fruit
• Garlic cloves
• Dark soy sauce
• Dry sherry
• Olive oil
• Sugar
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Halve the kiwi and scoop the flesh into a small bowl
2. Mash well with a fork until smooth
3. Add the mashed kiwi and remaining ingredients in a large ziptop bag to marinate
4. Leave at room temperature for about 20 min, no longer than 1 hour
5. Drain the lamb and thread onto skewers that have been soaking in water
6. Grill on high heat for 5-7 min, turning skewers to brown the lamb on all sides
7. Do not over cook<|endoftext|>
