Ingredients:
• Asparagus spear
• Chicken broth
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Cooking oil
• Gingerroot
• Green onions
• Fresh mushrooms
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Snap off and discard woody bases from asparagus
2. Bias slice into 1 inch pieces
3. For sauce, combine chicken broth, soy sauce and cornstarch
4. Set aside
5. Preheat a wok or large skillet over high heat
6. Add oil
7. Stir fry the gingerroot in hot oil for 30 seconds
8. Add asparagus and green onions
9. Stir fry for 3 minutes
10. Add mushrooms
11. Stir fry about 1 minute more until vegetables are crisp tender
12. Push vegetables from center of wok
13. Stir sauce
14. Add to center of wok
15. Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly
16. Add tomatoes
17. Heat through
18. Serve at once
19. Serve with rice if desired<|endoftext|>
