Ingredients:
• Fleischmann's active dry yeast
• Warm water
• Salt
• Sugar
• Mashed potatoes
• Butter
• Milk
• Flour

Steps:
1. Dissolve 2 pkgs active dry yeast in warm water, add salt and 2t sugar
2. Heat until warm: mashed potatoes, butter, 1cup sugar and milk
3. To the potato mixture, add yeast mixture, then mix in flour
4. Mix well and turn out on floured board and kneed for 10 minutes
5. Let rise for 1 hour in greased bowl
6. If you havent killed the yeast it will double in size
7. Wait an hour and punch down and make round rolls
8. Let rise another hr and bake in oven for 20 minutes at 350 degrees
9. Sometimes longer
10. Then sit back and watch brains fall out
11. Delicioso!<|endoftext|>
