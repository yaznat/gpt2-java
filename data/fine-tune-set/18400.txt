Ingredients:
• Butter
• Apple
• Cinnamon
• Brown sugar
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Milk
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Melt butter in an 8" skillet over moderate heat
2. Add apples and cook, stirring frequently, 2 to 3 minutes
3. Stir in cinnamon and sugar and reduce heat to low
4. Cook covered 2 to 3 minutes or until tender and golden brown
5. Cool slightly
6. Sift together dry ingredients
7. Beat egg yolks with sour cream, milk, and melted butter in a separate bowl until blended
8. Stir into dry ingredients until well blended
9. Stir in apples and pecans
10. Beat egg whites until stiff moist peaks form
11. Stir in about 3 tablespoons of the beaten whites into the batter, and gently fold in the remaining egg whites until just combined
12. Pour or ladle batter over center two thirds of hot waffle iron
13. Close lid and cook about 4 minutes, until steam no longer emerges from the crack
14. Remove waffle and keep warm in a warm oven while you cook the rest<|endoftext|>
