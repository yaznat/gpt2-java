Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Best Ever Bourbon Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Water
• Semisweet chocolate piece
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Pecans
• Bourbon
• Powdered sugar
• Milk
• Semisweet chocolate

Steps:
1. Grease an 8x8x2-inch baking pan
2. Set aside
3. Heat oven to 350 degrees
4. In a medium saucepan, combine the 1 / 3 cup butter, sugar and water
5. Cook and stir over medium heat just until boiling, remove from heat, add the 1 cup of chocolate pieces
6. Stir until chocolate is melted
7. Stir in eggs and vanilla, beating lightly with a spoon until combined
8. Stir in flour, baking soda and salt
9. Stir in pecans
10. Spread batter into prepared pan
11. Bake at 350 degrees about 20 minutes or until edges are set and begin to pull away from the sides of pan
12. Using a fork, prick the warm brownies several times, drizzle bourbon evenly over brownies
13. Cool in pan on wire rack
14. For frosting
15. In a small mixing bowl beat the 3 tablespoons butter with an electric mixer on medium to high speed for about 30 seconds
16. Gradually add powdered sugar, beating well, slowly beat in 2 teaspoons of the milk and the vanilla
17. If necessary, beat in remaining milk to reach spreading consistency, spread over brownies
18. Melt chocolate
19. Drizzle melted chocolate over frosting<|endoftext|>
