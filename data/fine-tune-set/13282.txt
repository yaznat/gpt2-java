Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>6 Step Chicken Tikka

Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Red food coloring
• Cumin powder
• Low-fat plain yogurt
• Green chili
• Ginger-garlic paste
• Limes
• Salt
• Oil

Steps:
1. Cut the chicken and make deep cuts on the pieces
2. Mix all the ingredients
3. Marinate the chicken for a minimum of 2 hours
4. Deep fry
5. Drain on paper towels
6. Serve<|endoftext|>
