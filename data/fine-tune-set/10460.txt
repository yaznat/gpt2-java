Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Honey
• Egg
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Ground coriander
• Ground aniseed
• Nutmeg
• Kirsch
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Milk

Steps:
1. Beat sugar, honey, egg, spices and kirsch until smooth and well blended
2. Sift the flour with the baking powder
3. Stir it into the egg mixture until a stiff dough forms
4. Knead lightly on a floured board
5. Cover the dough and let it rest one hour
6. Roll dough to 1 / 2-inch thickness
7. Cut into 3-inch rounds
8. Brush with a little additional milk
9. Bake at 325 for 15 - 20 minutes or until lightly browned<|endoftext|>
