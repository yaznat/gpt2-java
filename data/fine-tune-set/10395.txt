Ingredients:
• Shortening
• Brown sugar
• White sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Water
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Cream together the first four ingredients
3. Add the next five do not add the chocolate chips! and mix
4. Spray a 13 by 9 pan
5. Push mixture into the bottom of the pan and pour the chocolate-chips over it entirely
6. Place the pan into the oven for five minutes, then take the bars out and the chips should be soft
7. Swirl the chips into the dough so you cannot tell they were ever chips
8. Place the pan back into the oven for seven minutes<|endoftext|>
