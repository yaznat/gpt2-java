Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless salmon fillets
• Bacon
• Salt and pepper
• Hoisin sauce
• Maple syrup
• Soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Ketchup
• Garlic
• Ginger

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f spray a broiler pan with cooking spray
2. Wash salmon under cold water and pat dry with paper towel
3. Wrap each filet with 2 strips of bacon and secure with toothpicks
4. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
5. Combine hoisin sauce, maple syrup, soy sauce, rice vinegar, ketchup, garlic, and ginger together in a small saucepan
6. Turn heat to medium to warm, stirring occasionally
7. Place salmon on broiler pan and cover with half the prepared sauce
8. Place in preheated oven and set timer for 10 minutes
9. Place salmon on rice or noodles and pour remaining sauce over top<|endoftext|>
