Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Cornmeal
• Salt
• Pepper
• Beer
• Fish fillets
• Oil

Steps:
1. Heat oil to 375 degrees f
2. Combine dry ingredients
3. Add beer to get a sticky consistency for dipping
4. Salt fish
5. Dip in batter
6. Deep fry until fish is golden brown<|endoftext|>
