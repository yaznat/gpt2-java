Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Cocoa
• Salt
• Self-rising flour
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
2. Grease and flour a 9 x 9-inch pan
3. In a bowl, beat together the butter and sugar
4. Add eggs and vanilla
5. Mix well
6. Combine the cocoa, salt, and flour together
7. Stir into the sugar mixture
8. Stir in the walnuts
9. Spread evenly into prepared pan
10. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes or until firm<|endoftext|>
