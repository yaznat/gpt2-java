Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onions
• Parmesan cheese
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Bacon
• Butter
• Chives
• Seasoning salt
• Pepper
• Flour
• Milk

Steps:
1. Grease 2 1 / 2 qt casserole, layer 1 / 2 potatoes, 1 / 2 onions and 1 / 2 bacon
2. Sprinkle with 1 tbl flour and 1 / 2 salt and pepper
3. Then sprinkle 1 / 2 of each cheese on top
4. Repeat layers, ending with cheese on top
5. Sprinkle chives on top and pour on 1 cup milk
6. Cover casserole dish and bake at 375 degrees for 1 to 1 1 / 2 hours, or until potatoes are tender<|endoftext|>
