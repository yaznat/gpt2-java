Ingredients:
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Pork butt
• Oil
• Whole cloves
• Bay leaves
• Tortillas
• Diced onion
• Cilantro leaf
• Lime wedge

Steps:
1. Mix cumin, chile powder, cayenne, 1 t salt, pepper, and garllic powder in a 9 by 13inch baking dish
2. Toss the meat with the spice mixture to coat competely
3. Stir in the oil, cloves, and bay leaves
4. Cover with foil and refrigerate for at least 6 hours or overnight
5. Remove the meat from the refrigerator and let sit at room temperature for 30 minutes
6. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees
7. Bake the meat, covered, for 1 hour or until it is tender
8. Allow the meat to cool slighty, then remove it from the liquid with a slotted spoon and chop into bite-size pieces
9. Spoon off some of the oil floating on top of the cooking liquid, and reserve
10. Heat a griddle or skillet over medium-heat
11. Spoon a bit of the reserved oil onto the griddle, add some of the meat, season with salt, and cook, tossing, until hot and starting to crisp
12. To serve, overlap 2 tortillas on each plate, top with 1 / 2 cup meat, some onions, and a sprinkle of chopped cilatro
13. Squeeze lime juice over all<|endoftext|>
