What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Belgian Style Yeast Waffles From Kaf

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Butter
• Maple syrup
• Salt
• Vanilla extract
• Eggs
• Unbleached all-purpose flour
• Instant yeast

Steps:
1. Combine all of the ingredients in a large bowl, leaving room for expansion
2. The mixture will bubble and grow
3. Stir to combine
4. It's ok if the mixture isn't perfectly smooth
5. Cover with plastic wrap, and let rest at room temperature for 1 hour
6. The mixture will begin to bubble
7. You can cook the waffles at this point, or refrigerate the batter overnight to cook waffles the next day
8. Preheat your waffle iron
9. Spray with non-stick vegetable oil spray, and pour 2 / 3 to 3 / 4 cup batter onto the center of the iron
10. Close the lid and bake for the recommended amount of time, until the waffle is golden brown
11. It takes us 5 to 6 minutes, using our 7" belgian-style waffle iron
12. Serve immediately, or keep warm in a 200f oven
13. Serve with berries and whipped cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
