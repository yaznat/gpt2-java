Ingredients:
• Bacon
• All-purpose flour
• Cornmeal
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Egg
• Milk
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Line a dozen muffin cups with paper liners and set aside
3. Cook bacon in large skillet or microwave until crisp
4. Transfer to paper towels to drain
5. Reserve 2 tablespoons bacon fat from pan
6. Whisk together flour, cornmeal, baking powder, salt, and pepper in a large bowl
7. Then stir cheese into mixture
8. In a small bowl, beat together egg and milk
9. Stir into dry mixture
10. Crumble bacon
11. Add bacon, reserved bacon drippings and melted butter to batter
12. Fold until blended
13. Spoon batter into prepared muffin pan
14. Bake about 20 minutes
15. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
