Ingredients:
• Baby spinach
• White fish fillets
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Crushed tomatoes
• Reduced-fat cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Rinse spinach and place at the bottom of a glass baking dish
3. Season fish fillets with freshly ground black pepper
4. Lay fish on top of spinach
5. Pour canned tomatoes on top and sprinkle with cheese
6. Bake for 15-18 minutes<|endoftext|>
