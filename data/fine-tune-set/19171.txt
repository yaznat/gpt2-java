Ingredients:
• Butter
• Almonds
• Light corn syrup
• Brown sugar
• Water
• Crisp rice cereal
• Ice cream

Steps:
1. In a medium skillet melt the butter
2. Add the almonds, cook and stir over medium-low hear for 5 minutes or until the almonds brown
3. Add the corn syrup, brown sugar, and water to the skillet
4. Cook and stir until bubbly and the brown sugar is dissolved, about 4 minutes
5. Stir in the rice cereal and serve immediately over ice cream<|endoftext|>
