Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Alsatian Sauerkraut Potato Soup

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Pork chop
• Dried sage
• Chicken broth
• Cold water
• Sauerkraut
• Red potatoes
• Red kidney beans
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Fresh parsley
• Rye bread

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large saucdepan or dutch oven over medium heat
2. Add onion and garlic
3. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened but not browned, about 8 minutes
4. Add pork and sage and cook 2-3 minutes longer
5. Pour in broth and the water
6. Heat to boiling, skimming surface as needed
7. Reduce heat to simmer, partially covered, 10 minutes
8. Stir in sauerkraut, potatoes, and beans
9. Season with salt
10. Simmer, partially covered, 10 minutes longer
11. When potatoes soften, mash some against side of pan to thicken soup slightly
12. Season with pepper and stir in parsley
13. Serve hot with rye bread<|endoftext|>
