Ingredients:
• Cactus pieces
• Cornstarch
• Achiote paste
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Egg white
• Ice water
• Peanut oil

Steps:
1. Remove thorns and eyes from cactus, and trim off the edges of pads
2. Blanch cactus for 2 minutes in a covered pan with enough boiling salted water to cover
3. Drain
4. Cut cactus in 3- to 4-inch strips similar to french fries
5. Set aside
6. Combine cornstarch and achiote paste in a blender or food processor, and pulse until smooth
7. In a medium-size bowl, combine flour and baking powder, add cornstarch mixture, and blend well
8. In another bowl, beat egg white to stiff meringue
9. Fold into the flour mixture, alternating with enough ice water to make batter the consistency of unbeaten egg white
10. Chill
11. Dredge cactus strips in batter, and fry in deep hot oil until crisp and golden, approximately 3 minutes
12. Drain well and serve<|endoftext|>
