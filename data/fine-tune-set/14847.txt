Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Big John S Thai Crab Fried Rice Khao Phad Pu

Ingredients:
• Jasmine rice
• Crabmeat
• Cooking oil
• Soy sauce
• Eggs
• Green onions
• Sesame oil
• Garlic cloves
• Black pepper
• Sugar
• Thai fish sauce

Steps:
1. Combine sugar, pepper, soy & fish sauces then set aside
2. Warm oils in a large frying pan over medium heat
3. Lightly saute garlic and green onion
4. Add rice and mix thoroughly to separate
5. Add egg, mix until it's cooked and evenly dispersed
6. Mix in crab meat, making sure it is broken up and mixed well
7. Add sauce combination and mix well
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
