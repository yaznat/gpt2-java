Ingredients:
• Walnut pieces
• Arugula
• Gorgonzola
• Granny smith apple
• Walnut oil
• Apple cider vinegar
• Shallots
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Calvados

Steps:
1. For the apple cider dressing:
2. Combine oil, vinegar, shallots, garlic and salt and pepper to taste in jar or bowl
3. Add calvados and shake in jar or whisk well in bowl
4. For the salad:
5. Toast walnuts on baking sheet at 350 degrees until lightly browned, about 10 minutes
6. Combine walnuts, arugula, cheese and apple slices in large salad bowl
7. Toss with dressing and serve<|endoftext|>
