Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Sliced almonds
• Unsalted butter
• Vanilla extract
• Egg
• Flour
• Confectioners' sugar
• Salt
• Colored crystal sugar

Steps:
1. Pulse the granulated sugar and almonds in a food processor until finely ground
2. Cream the butter and sugar mixture until light and fluffy
3. Add the egg and beat to combine
4. Add the flour, confectioner's sugar and salt and mix just until a dough forms
5. Divide the dough in half
6. Wrap each piece in plastic wrap and flatten into a disk
7. Refrigerate the disks at least 4 hours or overnight
8. Preheat oven to 350f line two large baking sheets with parchment paper
9. Working with one disk at a time, roll the dough out between two 2 sheets of lightly floured waxed paper to the thickness of 1 / 8-inch
10. Using a 2 1 / 4-inch cookie cutter of any shape, cut out the cookies, rerolling the scraps to make a total of 60 cookies
11. Place on cookie sheets, one inch apart
12. Sprinkling with colored sugar if using
13. Bake 1 sheet at a time, until the cookies are light on top but golden on the bottom, 10-11 minutes
14. Cool completely on a rack<|endoftext|>
