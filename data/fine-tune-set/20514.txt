Ingredients:
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• White sugar
• Onion
• Sour cream
• French style green beans
• Cheddar cheese
• French-fried onions
• Water chestnuts
• Pecans
• Mushrooms

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Melt 2 t butter in large skillet over medium heat
3. Stir in flour until smooth and cook for one minute
4. Stir in salt, sugar, onion, water chestnuts, pecans, mushrooms and sour cream
5. Add green beans and stir until coated
6. Transfer mixture to a 2 1 / 2 quart casserole dish
7. Spread cheese over top
8. In a small bowl toss taste toppers and butter and sprinkle over the cheese
9. Bake for 30 minutes or until top is golden brown and cheese is bubbly<|endoftext|>
