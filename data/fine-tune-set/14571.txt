Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Near East Rice Pilaf Low Fat

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Orzo pasta
• Long-grain rice
• Water
• Chicken soup powder
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Turmeric

Steps:
1. In a non-stick pot, heat oil
2. Add orzo and sautee until browned
3. Add the rice
4. To the 3 cups of boiled water, add the chicken soup mix, garlic powder, turmeric, black pepper, and turmeric
5. Add seasoned water mixture to the pot, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes until all water has evaporated
6. Don't open lid
7. Remove from heat after 20 minutes and allow to rest 10minutes
8. Fluff with fork<|endoftext|>
