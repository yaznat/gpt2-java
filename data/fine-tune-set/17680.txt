What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Apple Cheddar Muffins

Ingredients:
• Egg
• 2% low-fat milk
• Vegetable oil
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine egg, milk, oil, applesauce and sugar
2. Sift flour, baking powder, salt and cinnamon together
3. Add all at once with cheddar cheese to egg mixture
4. Stir only until flour mixture is moistened
5. Divide batter among 12 greased muffin cups
6. Bake at 400 f for about 20 minutes or until wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean
7. Let cool in muffin pan 5 minutes
8. Run knife around outer edges of each muffin
9. Turn out onto rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
