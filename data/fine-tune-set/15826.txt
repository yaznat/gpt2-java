What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Breakfast Burrito Sandwich Maker

Ingredients:
• Flour tortilla
• Egg
• Cheese
• Bell pepper
• Onion
• Salsa
• Potato

Steps:
1. Take small soft shell burrito and fill with scrabbled egg, shredded cheese, chopped peppers& onions, , use what ever you like even shredded potatoes in the dairy section
2. Now place in the iron and fold over the sides and then the ends and place the top on
3. Cook to your liking and you will notice that the pocket stays sealed<|endoftext|>
