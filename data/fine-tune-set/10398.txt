What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazing Huge Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Rolled oats
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Vanilla
• Lemon juice
• Eggs
• Semisweet chocolate chunks

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Grind oats in food processor or small electric chopper until fine
3. Combine together
4. The ground oats, flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon in a medium bowl
5. Cream together
6. Butter, sugars, vanilla and lemon juice in another medium bowl with an electric mixer
7. Gradually add the eggs one at a time until smooth
8. Mix in the dry ingredients into the wet and mix well
9. Add the chocolate chunks by hand
10. Drop by 1 / 4 cup measures about 2-inches
11. Apart and bake for 16-18 minutes until cookies are light brown
12. Cool on cookie sheets so cookies will still cook on carry-over baking<|endoftext|>
