Ingredients:
• 98% fat-free cream of chicken soup
• Skim milk
• Lean ground meat
• Russet potato
• Garlic salt
• Garlic pepper seasoning

Steps:
1. 1
2. If not using leftover potato, cube a russet potato and boil in water until tender
3. Drain and add in garlic pepper and garlic salt
4. 2
5. Warm cream of chicken soup in microwave or in saucepan on stove
6. 3
7. Add in potatoes and leftover taco meat, and milk, if using
8. For thicker soup, do not use milk, or use less
9. 4
10. Allow to simmer 2-3 minutes
11. 5
12. Serve immediately
13. 6
14. Feel free to add in any leftover vegetables on hand as well --<|endoftext|>
