Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Garlic cloves
• Soy yogurt
• Turmeric
• Onions
• Chicken stock
• Cardamom pods
• Cinnamon stick
• Black peppercorns
• Cloves
• Coriander seed
• Fresh ginger
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Fresh coriander

Steps:
1. Slice the chicken and place in a bowl
2. Peel and crush the garlic, mix with the soya yogurt and turmeric and pour over the chicken
3. Cover and leave to marinate in the fridge for at least 4 hours or overnight if possible
4. Peel and slice the onions and place in a pan with the stock
5. Cover the pan, bring to the boil and cook for 10 minutes
6. Reduce the heat, uncover and cook gently for 20 minutes until the onions are tender and golden
7. Finely chop ginger and crush coriander seeds
8. Add all the spices to the pan and cook for 3-4 minutes
9. Then add the chicken and soya yogurt marinade
10. Cover and simmer very gently over a low heat for 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked
11. Season with salt and pepper
12. Serve immediately with freshly chopped coriander<|endoftext|>
