Ingredients:
• Milk
• Instant pudding mix
• Banana
• Cake
• Milk chocolate candy bar
• Candy sprinkles

Steps:
1. Stir milk and pudding mix in medium bowl with large spoon just until pudding mix is moistened
2. Immediately add bananas and cake crumbs
3. Mix well
4. To portion cake balls use cookie scoop to portion the cake mixture for each ball
5. Then, roll the cake balls between the palms of your hands to form smooth round balls
6. Shape into 36 balls
7. Place on waxed paper-covered rimmed baking sheet
8. Freeze 1 hour
9. Melt chocolate in medium microwaveable bowl on high 1-1 / 2 min
10. , stirring after 1 minute
11. To easily coat the cake balls remove inner tines of disposable fork
12. Set aside
13. Add cake balls, in batches, to bowl of melted chocolate
14. Use spoon or rubber scraper to evenly coat balls with chocolate
15. Remove balls with prepared fork
16. If you are using lollipop or popsicle stick, just skewer each pop before dipping
17. Dip balls in chocolate
18. Return to baking sheet
19. Top immediately with sprinkles or whatever you are using
20. Let stand until coating is firm<|endoftext|>
