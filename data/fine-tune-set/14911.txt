Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Fried Rice Chow Fun

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Meat
• Green onions
• Fresh bean sprout
• Mushrooms
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Cook bacon til lightly browned but not crunchy and set aside
2. Add beaten eggs to bacon drippings and scramble
3. Remove and chop very fine
4. Add cooked rice and fry for approximately 5 minutes stirring constantly then add remaining ingredients
5. Mix well and continue cooking for 10 minutes longer
6. Serve piping hot<|endoftext|>
