Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Alisa S Amazing Frosted Brownies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Eggs
• Margarine
• Milk
• Powdered sugar
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Mix together brownie ingredients
2. Pour in greased and floured 9x13 pan
3. Bake at 350 degrees for 20-30 minutes
4. Cool and frost
5. Combine frosting ingredients
6. Feel free to decrease margarine to 3 / 4 of the stick
7. Add more powdered sugar if needed to make desired spreadable consistency<|endoftext|>
