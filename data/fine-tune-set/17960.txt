What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Big Batch Brownies

Ingredients:
• Bittersweet chocolate
• Butter
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Nuts
• Semisweet chocolate
• Powdered sugar
• Egg

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. Melt chocolate and butter on low heat
3. Cool
4. Sift flour, baking powder and salt together - set aside
5. In large bowl beat the eggs well, add the sugar gradually, beating until thick
6. Add the vanilla and melted chocolate / butter to the eggs, blending well
7. Stir in the dry ingredients and the nuts
8. Pour batter into a greased 11 x 15 sheet pan
9. Bake for 30 - 35 minutes until it tests done
10. To make glaze melt butter and chocolate together in saucepan, remove from heat and add the powdered sugar and the egg stirring constantly
11. Pour the glaze over the warm brownies in pan, and spread evenly<|endoftext|>
