What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Green Chili Beef Burritos

Ingredients:
• Boneless beef roast
• Green chilies
• Onion
• Jalapeno peppers
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Sodium-free seasoning
• Reduced-sodium beef broth
• Fat free tortillas
• Chopped tomato
• Lettuce
• Reduced-fat cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Trim fat from roasts
2. Cut meat into large chunks
3. Place in a 5-qt slow cooker
4. Top with the chilies, onion, jalapenos, garlic, chili powder, cumin and seasoning blend if desired
5. Pour broth over all
6. Cover and cook on low for 8-9 hours
7. Remove beef
8. Cool slightly
9. Shred with two forks
10. Cool cooking liquid slightly
11. Skim fat
12. In a blender, cover and process cooking liquid in small batches until smooth
13. Return liquid and beef to slow cooker
14. Heat through
15. Place 1 / 3 cup beef mixture on each tortilla
16. Top with tomatoes, lettuce and cheese if desired
17. Fold in ends and sides<|endoftext|>
