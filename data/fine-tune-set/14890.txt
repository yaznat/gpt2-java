Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Fried Rice For Two

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Sesame oil
• Egg
• Soy sauce
• Stir fry vegetables
• Cooked rice
• Light soy sauce

Steps:
1. 1 tbsp oil in a skillet and stir fry onion until it is golden but not burnt
2. Put onions to the side of the skillet
3. Add eggs to skillet and fry until fluffy, remove eggs from skillet
4. Add the extra oil to skillet then add veggies and rice, tossing to get everything mixed
5. Add egg back to skillet then sprinkle the rice with soy sauce and enjoy<|endoftext|>
