What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Casserole

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Pepper
• Salt
• Refrigerated buttermilk biscuits
• Cooked bacon
• Cheddar cheese
• Green pepper
• Onion

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine sliced peppers, onion, cheese and bacon in a mixing bowl
3. Add milk, salt and pepper
4. Mix gently
5. Pour into a 9inch greased casserole dish
6. Bake for 35 minutes
7. Remove from oven and top with biscuits
8. Bake for an additional 11-13 minutes or until biscuits are done<|endoftext|>
