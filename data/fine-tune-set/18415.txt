What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Waffle Cones Or Bowls

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• Unsalted butter
• Pure vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Cocoa powder
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Beat eggs add in the sugar gradually, beating well until smooth
2. Add the melted cooled butter and vanilla
3. Sift the measured flour, cocoa powder and powder together and blend into the egg mixture well, making a sticky drop by spoon batter
4. Drop a heaping tablespoon onto preheated waffle cone maker and close lid and bake until desired colour
5. Remove and form into cones or bowls, or trim for squares
6. Hints: for cones dip the crisp cooled cone into melted chocolate and roll into nuts or sprinkles
7. For bowls let sit on inverted custard cups pressing around to shape let cool about 30 seconds the remove to completely cool, brush inside bottom with melted chocolate and then nuts or sprinkles, allow chocolate then to harden
8. For squares i trim with a pizza cutter and use for bars
9. You can also brush with melted chocolate and let harden before storing away
10. I also use the squares for some of my baking cakes or kit kat bars to make a delicious square using a few of these as an added layer, gives crunch
11. Sometimes i also add chopped fine walnuts or pecans to the melted chocolate and let harden then use in a layer cake
