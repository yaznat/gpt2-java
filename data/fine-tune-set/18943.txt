Ingredients:
• Unbaked pie shells
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• Mincemeat
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. In medium bowl, slightly whisk eggs
3. In another bowl, whisk together sugar, flour & salt
4. Slowly add dry ingredients to eggs, mixing well
5. Add melted butter, mincemeat & nuts, mixing well
6. Pour into pie shell & bake 15 minutes
7. Reduce oven temperature to 325 degrees f & bake an additional 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
