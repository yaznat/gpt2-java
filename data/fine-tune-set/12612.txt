What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew

Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Onion
• Celery
• Carrots
• Potatoes
• Water
• Salt and pepper
• Paprika
• Dried basil
• Dried parsley flakes
• Worcestershire sauce
• Soya sauce
• Cornstarch
• Cold water

Steps:
1. Place all chopped vegetables and cubed beef into your crockpot and add enough water to sufficiently cover the veggies to cook for at least 5 hrs slow cook or 1 hr and 30 mins high temp cooking
2. No need to cook beef before hand-- as it cooks with the veggies you get its beef stock which adds to the flavor
3. Add all seasonings and stir thoroughly
4. Turn your crock pot on to high and cover
5. If cooking over the stove follow all the steps and boil on med-high stirring occasionally for 35 minutes
6. And simmered covered also stirring occasionally for 45 minutes
7. When your potatoes are done in both cooking methods proceed to the next step
8. Add cornstarch mixed in water gradually 1 / 2 cup at a time, stirring it in thoroughly each time checking its texture, until the stew is as thick as you like it
9. You might have to add more cornstarch
10. Follow directions on the box
11. Serving suggestions: serve over vol-au-vent pastry shells, alone as a stew or enveloped into a pie for a beef pot pie
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
