Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Margarine
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Apples
• Kellogg's all-bran cereal

Steps:
1. Stir together flour, soda, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg
2. Set aside
3. In medium mixing bowl, beat together the 1 / 2 cup margarine and the 1 cup sugar until light and fluffy
4. Add eggs
5. Beat well
6. Stir in apples, all-bran buds cereal and flour mixture
7. Stir until thoroughly combined
8. Spread evenly in greased 9 x 9 x 2-inch baking pan
9. Bake at 350 f about 1 hour or until cake begins to pull away from sides of pan
10. Cool completely
11. Frost or dust with powdered sugar<|endoftext|>
