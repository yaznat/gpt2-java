Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Yellow onion
• Olive oil
• Paprika
• Garlic powder
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 f
2. Combine all ingredients in a plastic bag
3. Shake until well mixed
4. Empty into a baking dish and cook for 30-40 minutes depending on how crispy you like your potatoes<|endoftext|>
