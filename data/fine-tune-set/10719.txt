Ingredients:
• Farfalle pasta
• Bacon
• Zucchini
• Fresh corn kernels
• Heavy cream
• Basil
• Pine nuts
• Salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. Boil pasta in a large pot of salted water
2. Drain pasta, reserving 1 cup pasta water
3. Cook bacon in a large skillet until crisp
4. Transfer bacon to plate and reserve 1 tablespoon drippings
5. Saute zucchini and corn in the drippings in the same skillet until tender, 5-6 minutes
6. Add pasta to corn and zucchini in skillet
7. Stir in bacon, cream, basil and pine nuts
8. Add reserved pasta water, as needed to loosen sauce
9. Season with salt and pepper<|endoftext|>
