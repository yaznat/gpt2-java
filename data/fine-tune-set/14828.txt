What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Pork Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Barbecued pork
• Eggs
• Oil
• Green onions
• Rice
• Salt
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Beat eggs lightly
2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat
3. Add the green onions and eggs, and stir for 10 to 20 seconds
4. Add rice and the diced pork
5. Stir briskly for 2 minutes
6. Season with salt and soy sauce
7. Mix well and serve<|endoftext|>
