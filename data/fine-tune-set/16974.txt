Ingredients:
• Lime
• Sugar
• Vodka
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Wash the lime and cut into fourths
2. Put lime in a glass and add the sugar
3. Crush lime with the sugar well
4. Add the shot of vodka and the ice cubes
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
