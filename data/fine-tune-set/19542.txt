Please give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>Alpine Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Cream of chicken soup
• Sour cream
• Green onion
• Celery
• Cheddar cheese
• Garlic salt
• Pepper
• Potatoes
• Potato chips

Steps:
1. In saucepan, cook and stir butter and soup until smooth
2. Remove from heat
3. Add sour cream
4. Add onions, celery, and cheese
5. Mix well
6. Add salt, pepper and diced potatoes
7. Mix well
8. Pour into 9x13 baking pan
9. Top with potato chips
10. Bake at 350 for 45-55 minutes<|endoftext|>
