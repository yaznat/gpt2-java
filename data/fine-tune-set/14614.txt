What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Addicting Wild Rice Asparagus Salad

Ingredients:
• Long grain and wild rice blend
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Asparagus
• Portabella mushrooms
• Chicken broth
• Olive oil
• Red wine vinegar
• Garlic salt
• Pine nuts
• Craisins

Steps:
1. 1
2. Cook rice according to directions
3. About 2 minutes before rice is finished, add craisins and simmer until rice is cooked
4. 2
5. In a frying pan, add 1 can of broth, garlic, and onion
6. Simmer for about 2-3 minutes and then add the asparagus until cooked to your liking
7. Add mushrooms and cook for about 5 more minutes, more or less
8. 3
9. Drain any remaining broth and mix the onion mix with the rice and craisins
10. 4
11. Add vinegar & olive oil, mix well
12. Then add garlic salt and mix
13. 4
14. If using nuts, place then under a the broiler for a minute or two and then add them to the salad
15. 5
16. This dish can be served hot and is also quite tasty cold for a quick leftover dish!<|endoftext|>
