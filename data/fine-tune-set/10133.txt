Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Vegetarian Chili Spaghetti

Ingredients:
• Spaghetti
• Fat free spaghetti sauce
• Vegetarian chili
• Mushrooms
• Chili powder
• Garlic powder
• Reduced-fat sharp cheddar cheese
• Onion

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package
2. Drain
3. While pasta cooks, mix sauce and next 4 ingredients in a medium saucepan
4. Bring to a boil
5. Cover, reduce heat, simmer 10 minutes
6. Coat a 13 x 9 baking dish with spray
7. Add pasta and top with sauce
8. Bake at 375f for 25 minutes
9. Remove from oven and sprinkle with cheese
10. Top with onions if desired<|endoftext|>
