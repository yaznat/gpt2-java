Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Condensed mushroom soup
• Cheddar cheese soup
• Frozen french fries

Steps:
1. Brown the beef in a skillet
2. Stir in soups
3. Pour in a greased 13x9 inch pan
4. Arrange fries on top
5. Bake uncovered at 350 degrees for 50-55 minutes or until fries are golden brown<|endoftext|>
