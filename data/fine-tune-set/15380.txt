What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Style Turkey Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Garlic cloves
• Onions
• Green peppers
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Fresh gingerroot
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Mixed-grain hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Combine turkey, garlic, onion, green pepper, soy sauce, ginger, salt and pepper in a large bowl
2. Divide turkey mixture into 4 portions and shape into patties
3. Grill over medium-high heat 6 to 8 minutes on each side or until done [170f]
4. Serve on buns<|endoftext|>
