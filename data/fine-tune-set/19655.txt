Please give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>Au Gratin Crock Pot Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Yukon gold potatoes
• Onion
• Velveeta cheese
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Condensed cheddar cheese soup
• Milk
• Margarine
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Place sliced and peeled potatoes in a crock pot
2. Add margarine and toss carefully
3. Add cream of chicken soup
4. Add cheddar cheese soup
5. Stir in milk with soups and potatoes
6. Stir in onion, using more or less to personal taste
7. Add salt, pepper and garlic powder or more or less to taste
8. Top with cubed velveeta cheese
9. Cover crock pot and cook on high for 4 hours or until potatoes are tender
10. Your cook time may be more or less according to how thin you've sliced your potatoes
11. Mine are usually pretty thin<|endoftext|>
