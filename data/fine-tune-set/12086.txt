What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Amaretto Coconut Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Sugar
• Shredded coconut
• Eggs
• Heavy cream
• Unflavored gelatin
• Water
• Amaretto liqueur
• Almond extract
• Strawberries
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 300 degrees
2. Grease bottom of 10 inch springform pan
3. Combine crumbs, sugar and butter and press evenly on bottom of pan to form crust
4. Set aside
5. Evenly distribute coconut on baking sheet
6. Toast in oven until golden brown, stirring frequently, for about 15 minutes
7. Set aside
8. Using electric mixer at medium speed, beat sugar and eggs in large bowl until fluffy
9. Sprinkle gelatin over cold water in heatproof measuring cup or bowl and let stand about 5 minutes to soften
10. Set cup in a small pan of hot water over direct heat, stirring until gelatin is completely dissolved
11. Fold gelatin, amaretto, and almond extract into egg mixture
12. Let stand until slightly thickened
13. Beat whipping cream until soft peaks form
14. Fold coconut into thickened egg mixture
15. Fold in whipping cream and blend thoroughly
16. Pour mixture over crust in springform pan and chill thoroughly, at least six hours
17. Loosen sides before serving and sprinkle with reserved coconut
18. Whip remaining cream with sugar
19. Garnish each slice with a dollop of whipped cream and a strawberry<|endoftext|>
