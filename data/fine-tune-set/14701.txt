Ingredients:
• White rice
• Salt and pepper
• Carrot
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Red wine vinegar
• Dried oregano
• Ear of corn
• Frozen peas
• Celery
• Celery leaves
• Red onion

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan, bring the rice, 1 cup water and 1 / 4 teaspoon salt to a boil
2. Lower the heat, cover and simmer until the water is absorbed, about 17 minutes
3. Fluff with a fork and let cool
4. Meanwhile, fill another saucepan with salted water
5. Add the carrot, bring to a boil and cook for 2 minutes
6. Using a slotted spoon, transfer to a bowl
7. Toss with 2 tablespoon olive oil, the vinegar and oregano
8. Season with salt and pepper
9. In the same saucepan, blanch the corn in the boiling water for 3 minutes
10. Transfer to the bowl with the carrot
11. Add the peas to the boiling water and cook for 1 minute
12. Transfer to the bowl
13. Add the rice, cubed celery, chopped celery leaves and onion to the bowl, season with salt and pepper, drizzle with the remaining 2 tablespoon olive oil and toss<|endoftext|>
