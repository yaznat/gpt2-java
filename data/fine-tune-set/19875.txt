Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Garam masala
• Ground coriander
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Peel the sweet potatoes
2. If they are average size, cut them in half lengthwise
3. Lay each half flat on the cutting board and cut it into four slices
4. These should be between 1 / 4 and 1 / 2-inch slabs
5. Put the sweet potatoes in a large mixing bowl
6. Sprinkle on the seasonings and then pour on the olive oil
7. Use your clean hands to toss the potatoes with the other ingredients until all is evenly distributed
8. Set aside and allow the potatoes to marinate for 1 / 2-hour or more, depending upon the timing of your meal
9. When you are ready to cook, spray a shallow sheet pan with a light coat of oil to prevent sticking and then pour out the potatoes, distributing them evenly and avoiding any stacking
10. Place the pan on the middle rack of an oven preheated to 400 degrees fahrenheit
11. Bake for about 30 minutes or until the fries are looking crisp
12. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
