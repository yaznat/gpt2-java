Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin chops
• Brown sugar
• Celery salt
• Chili powder
• Onion flakes
• Tomato soup
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. Trim fat off pork
2. Cut into bite sized cubes
3. I always season my pork with salt, pepper and garlic powder to taste
4. Put celery salt, brown sugar, chili powder and onion flakes in a big ziploc baggie
5. Add pork
6. Shake well to coat
7. In a casserole or oven proof dish add the soup and vinegar
8. Stir
9. Add contents of baggie to dish
10. Stir
11. Cover and bake 1 1 / 2 hours @350
12. Baking time is alot -- it was very cooked so check after 1 hour
13. Serve over rice
14. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
