What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>Amazing Bread Stuffing

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Salt
• Bread
• Celery
• Poultry seasoning
• Parsley
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Melt butter in deep kettle
2. Add onions and celery, simmer until slightly soft
3. Add rest of ingredients to the kettle and toss with forks until bread cubes are well coated
4. Transfer to a covered baking dish and bake for 30 minutes at 325 degrees<|endoftext|>
