What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Authentic Spicy Chicken Tacos

Ingredients:
• Corn tortillas
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Salt
• Canola oil
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Jalapeno pepper
• Ground cumin
• Hot salsa
• Fresh cilantro
• Scallion
• Queso fresco
• Fresh tomato
• Reduced-fat sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 300f wrap tortillas in foil and bake until heated through, 10 to 15 minutes
2. Meanwhile, season chicken with salt
3. Heat 1 teaspoon oil in a large heavy skillet over high heat until very hot
4. Add chicken and cook, stirring until browned on all sides, about 6 minutes
5. Transfer to a bowl
6. Reduce heat to medium and add the remaining 1 teaspoon oil to skillet
7. Add onion and cook, stirring, until they start to brown around the edges, 3 to 5 minutes
8. Add bell pepper, garlic, jalapeo and cumin
9. Cook, stirring, until peppers are bright green but still crisp, 2 to 3 minutes more
10. Stir in salsa and reserved chicken
11. Cook, stirring, until chicken is heated through, about 2 minutes
12. Remove from heat and stir in cilantro
13. Spoon into warmed tortillas and garnish with scallions, queso fresco, tomatoes and sour cream<|endoftext|>
