Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Onion
• Black beans
• Ground cumin
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Soy cheese

Steps:
1. Saute sweet potatoes and onion in large skillet with 1 tablespoon oil just until tender
2. Add water or apple juice as needed to prevent sticking
3. Add black beans, spices and salt and cook until heated through
4. Divide bean mixture and cheese among tortillas and roll up
5. Place in 9 x 13 inch baking pan
6. Lightly spray with olive oil if desired
7. Cover pan with foil and bake at 350f for 20-25 minutes
8. Garnish with sour cream, salsa and fresh cilantro or wrap up for a to-go meal<|endoftext|>
