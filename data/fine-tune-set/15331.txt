What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Almost White Castle Hamburgers

Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Onion soup mix
• Egg
• Pepper
• Water
• Breadcrumbs
• Dinner rolls
• American cheese
• Pickle
• Mustard
• Ketchup

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Hamburgers: mix first 6 ingredients and press into an ungreased 10 by 15 inch jelly roll pan
3. Prick with a fork
4. Bake for 10 minutes
5. Drain off juices
6. Cool
7. Cut into 24 squares
8. Place squares on dinner rolls
9. Combine mustard and ketchup and spread on rolls
10. Top with pickles and cheese, if desired
11. This makes 12 servings of 2 hamburgers each<|endoftext|>
