Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Batter Fried Catfish Nuggets

Ingredients:
• Catfish nuggets
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Milk
• Egg
• Butter
• Oil

Steps:
1. Wash catfish and pat dry with paper towels
2. Cut fish into 1 inch cubes
3. Place on paper towels and set aside while doing next step
4. Combine: flour, salt and baking powder in a bowl
5. Add milk, egg and butter
6. Beat until smooth
7. Heat 1 inch or more of oil in a skillet to 365 degrees on medium heat
8. Dip one fish cube at a time into batter, coating all sides
9. Place as many in the oil as you can fit without crowding
10. Fry until golden brown
11. Drain on paper towels and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
