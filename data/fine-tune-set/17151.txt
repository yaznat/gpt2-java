Ingredients:
• A. 1. original sauce
• Grey poupon dijon mustard
• Honey
• Soy sauce
• Boneless beef steaks

Steps:
1. Mix steak sauce, mustard, honey and soy sauce
2. Place steak in nonmetal dish or plastic bag
3. Add steak sauce mixture, turning to coat both sides
4. Cover
5. Refrigerate several hours or overnight, turning occasionally
6. Remove steak from marinade, discard marinade
7. Grill or broil steak until desired doneness<|endoftext|>
