What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>Ambrosia Waldorf Salad

Ingredients:
• Fresh cranberries
• Sugar
• Miniature marshmallows
• Apples
• Green seedless grape
• Pecans
• Pineapple tidbits
• Whipping cream
• Shredded coconut

Steps:
1. Combine cranberries and sugar
2. In a large bowl, combine the marshmallows, apples, grapes, pecans and pineapple
3. Add cranberries and mix well
4. Fold in whipped cream
5. Cover and chill
6. Sprinkle wih coconut before serving<|endoftext|>
