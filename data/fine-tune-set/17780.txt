Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Arctic Garden S Blueberry Muffins

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Oats
• Brown sugar
• Frozen blueberries
• Egg
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Orange juice
• Vegetable oil
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 210 c, and grease muffin pan
2. Combine flour, baking powder, oats, brown sugar, and blueberries
3. In a small bowl combine egg, applesauce, orange juice, vegetable oil and maple syrup
4. Pour all at once the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients, stirring just until moistened
5. Fill the greased muffin pans until 2 / 3 full, and bake for 25 minutes
6. Let cool for 2 minutes before removing<|endoftext|>
