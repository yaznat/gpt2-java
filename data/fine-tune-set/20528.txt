Ingredients:
• Baking potato
• Sweet potato
• Red pepper
• Red onion
• Zucchini
• Tomatoes
• Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper
• Extra virgin olive oil

Steps:
1. Spray a rectangular 9x13 pan with cooking spray
2. Arrange all potato slices on the bottom
3. Sprinkle with 1 / 3 of salt, pepper, crumbs and a drizzle of oil
4. Layer the red pepper, onion and zucchini strips next
5. Repeat 1 / 3 of s&p, crumbs, oil
6. Top with tomato slices and the remaining oil, s&p, crumbs
7. Sprinkle on the cheese
8. Bake uncovered in a 350f oven for 1 hour
9. Let stand 10 minutes before slicing
10. Yummy!<|endoftext|>
