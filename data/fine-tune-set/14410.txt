Please give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>African Beans And Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Dried pinto beans
• Potatoes
• Celery
• Salt
• Sweet onion

Steps:
1. Cook beans until tender
2. Add potatoes and celery to beans and cook until potatoes are tender
3. I cut mine into large cubes and it took about 4 minutes
4. Salt to taste
5. Saute onion until browned
6. Mix in with beans and potatoes<|endoftext|>
