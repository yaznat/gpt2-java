Please give me a recipe for fish.<|endoftext|>Blackened Fish

Ingredients:
• Red snapper fillets
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Dried thyme
• Dried parsley flakes
• Dried basil
• Cayenne pepper
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Combine all spices / dry ingredients in a bowl and mix well
2. Spread the spice mixture onto a flat plate
3. Press the fish fillets firmly into the spices, coating both sides
4. Heat the olive oil until almost smoking in a large heavy skillet
5. Cook the fish about 2 1 / 2 minutes on each side for fillets less than 3 / 4 inch thick
6. Increase time slightly for thicker fillets<|endoftext|>
