Give me a smoothie recipe.<|endoftext|>Alice S Basic Fruit Smoothies

Ingredients:
• Frozen unsweetened strawberries
• Unsweetened frozen blueberries
• Low-fat vanilla yogurt
• Reduced-fat milk
• Splenda granular
• Pure vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In a blender, layer 1 cp strawberries, 1 / 2 cp blueberries, 1 cp yogurt, approx 1 / 2 cup milk, or more for thinner smoothie, and vanilla extract
2. Start out by mixing it til blended, then set on puree for one minute, longer for more processed smoothies
3. This also freezes very well
4. I'll make a double batch and put 1 gallon away in the freezer, so i don't have to make it so often<|endoftext|>
