Give me a pork recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple And Onion Smothered Pork Chops With Garam Masala

Ingredients:
• Pork loin chops
• Garam masala
• Olive oil
• Yellow onion
• Tart apple
• Juniper berries
• Salt
• Marsala wine
• Salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. Rub garam masala evenly on both sides of each pork chop and allow to sit about fifteen minutes at room temperature
2. Heat olive oil in a heavy bottomed cast iron skillet over medium heat
3. When it is quite hot, add the pork chops
4. Allow them to sit undisturbed until browned on the bottom, then turn over
5. When brown on the second side, add onions and apples to the pan, along with crushed juniper berries
6. Sprinkle apples and onions with salt
7. Cook, stirring now and then, until the onions and apples are golden brown
8. Deglaze the pan with wine, stirring
9. Allow the alcohol to boil off, then turn heat down to low, and cover the pan
10. Cook until sauce reduces to a reddish brown glaze, and the apples and onions cling to the chops<|endoftext|>
