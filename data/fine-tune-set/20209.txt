What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Broiled Halibut With Thai Stir Fry Vegetables

Ingredients:
• Halibut fillet
• Rice vinegar
• Honey
• Garlic cloves
• Red pepper flakes
• Canola oil
• Red pepper
• Zucchini
• Mushroom
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Preheat broiler
2. Combine vinegar, honey, garlic and red pepper flakes
3. Place fish in a glass dish or plastic bag, spoon 3 tablespoons vinegar mixture over fish
4. Seal bag or cover dish, turn to coat well and marinate 15 minutes
5. Reserve remaining vinegar mixture
6. Spray broiler pan with nonstick cooking spray
7. Drain fish and place on broiler pan
8. Broil 5 minutes, turn and broil 3 minutes or just until fish is done and flakes easily
9. Meanwhile, heat oil in wok or large skillet over medium-high heat
10. Add vegetables and stir-fry 2 minutes
11. Pour reserved vinegar mixture over vegetables and heat to boil
12. Boil 30 seconds, stirring constantly
13. Arrange vegetables and sauce on serving plate
14. Top with fish, then spoon sauce over top of fish
15. Sprinkle with cilantro, if desired<|endoftext|>
