Ingredients:
• Clarified butter
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Butter
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Tart apples
• Flour
• Curry powder
• Cardamom
• Coriander
• Salt
• Pepper
• Chicken stock
• Lime rind
• Lime juice
• Mango chutney

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 200
2. Heat the clarified butter in a large pan and saut chicken breasts on both sides till browned
3. Cover the pan
4. Heat the chicken breasts over low heat for five minutes
5. Transfer to a platter
6. Keep warm in the oven while you're making the sauce
7. To make the sauce, melt the butter in the saut pan
8. Add the onion and saut over low heat till transparent
9. Add the garlic and apples and saut for 2 minutes
10. Add the flour, curry powder, cardamom, coriander, salt and pepper
11. Stir to mix well
12. Add the chicken stock, lime rind, lime juice and chutney
13. Bring sauce to a boil, stirring constantly
14. Pour around chicken and serve
15. It's great served over rice<|endoftext|>
