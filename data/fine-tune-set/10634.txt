Ingredients:
• Bow tie pasta
• Tomatoes
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Black olives
• Artichokes
• Chicken
• Feta cheese
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Slice tomatoes into thin wedges
3. Place tomatoes on cookie sheet
4. Pour olive oil over tomatoes, and sprinkle with salt, pepper and garlic
5. Roast tomatoes until pasta is cooked
6. Prepare pasta according to directions on box, drain
7. Stir in butter, until butter is melted
8. Stir in feta cheese
9. Add tomatoes from cookie sheet
10. Add the rest of the ingredients
11. Serve and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
