What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Jello Magic Milkshakes

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Gelatin
• Ice cream

Steps:
1. Place milk, jello, and ice cream in large pitcher with tight fitting lid
2. Cover
3. Shake vigorously one minute or until well blended
4. Pour into four glasses
5. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
