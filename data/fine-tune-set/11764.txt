Ingredients:
• Panir
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Cashews
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Turmeric
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Ginger-garlic paste
• Cooking oil
• Heavy cream
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. In a blender, make a paste of the onion and tomato
2. Add the ginger garlic paste
3. Heat the oil in a skillet, then add the tomato mixture, and cook until it starts to separate
4. Add salt, chili powder, turmeric, cinnamon and cloves, cook for 1 - 2 minutes
5. Add 1 / 2 cup water and bring to a simmer
6. In a blender, process the cashews and cream to make a paste
7. Add the mixture in the skillet and cook for another 1 - 2 minutes
8. Cut paneer into small pieces, and add to the curry mixture, let simmer for 5 more minutes
9. Garnish with cilantro<|endoftext|>
