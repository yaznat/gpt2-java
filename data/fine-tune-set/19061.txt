Give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Apple Nut Crunch With Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Vanilla
• White sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Apple
• Walnuts
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Beat the egg and vanilla in a medium mixing bowl
3. Combine sugar, flour, powder and cinnamon
4. Add to egg gradually and beat until smooth
5. Fold in apple and nuts
6. Spoon into an 8 inch greased pan
7. Baked for 25 minutes or until center tests done
8. Cool until still slightly warm
9. Cut into 9 squares and top each piece with a 1 / 4 cup of ice cream<|endoftext|>
