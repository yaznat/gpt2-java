What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Baked Catfish

Ingredients:
• Ketchup
• Butter
• Balsamic vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dijon mustard
• Caribbean jerk seasoning
• Garlic
• Catfish fillets
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Add the first 7 ingredients to a saucepan
2. Over medium-low heat, cook mixture for 10 minutes, stirring frequently
3. Sprinkle catfish with pepper
4. Place fillets on a greased foil-lined broiler pan
5. Pour sauce over catfish
6. Bake at 400 degrees for 10-12 minutes or until fish flakes<|endoftext|>
