Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Sesame oil
• Apricot preserves
• Tamari soy sauce
• Pickled ginger

Steps:
1. Slice the pork in about 1 / 2 thick slices and add salt and pepper to both sides for seasoning
2. Chop the ginger into small pieces
3. Heat sesame oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
4. Add pork and sear 2 minutes per side
5. Add apricot preserves, tamari sauce, ginger and bring to a simmer
6. Simmer 5 minutes, or until pork cooked through and sauce reduces
7. Once done, serve over rice<|endoftext|>
