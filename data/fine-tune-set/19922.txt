Ingredients:
• Cabbage
• Carrot
• Onion
• Chicken bouillon cube
• Cornflour
• Soy sauce
• Bacon
• Red capsicum
• Garlic
• Cheese
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Quantities depend on how many you are feeding
2. In a little butter, fry the following until limp
3. Finely shredded cabbage
4. Finely sliced onions
5. Thin strips of carrot
6. Take out and keep warm
7. Beat two eggs with a crushed chicken cube and a dash of soy sauce
8. In a small pot combine one cup of water, a chicken cube, dash soy sauce, a heaped teaspoon cornflour, salt and pepper, and bring to the boil while stirring
9. Let simmer 2 minutes
10. Pour omelette mixture into greased hot frypan, sprinkle over cabbage mixture, cover and cook over low heat till done
11. Fold over and serve on warm plates, pour over sauce
12. Optional
13. Add a little garlic, capsicum, or bacon to the veg
14. Sprinkle a little cheese over the filling and finish under the grill<|endoftext|>
