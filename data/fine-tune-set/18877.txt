Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Salt
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Flaked coconut
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees f
2. Lightly grease a 1 quart baking dish or 4 ramekins
3. Fill a 9x13 pan with 1 inch of hot water and set it in the oven
4. To caramelize the sugar: melt the sugar in a heavy bottomed skillet without stirring
5. Swirl the sugar in the pan as it melts till caramel colored
6. Pour about 1 tablespoons caramelized sugar into each ramekin, or all into baking dish and quickly swirl around to coat bottom and partly up sides
7. The caramel will harden, but the custard will soften it as it bakes
8. Beat the eggs together, then add the sugar and salt
9. Slowly stir in the hot milk, stirring constantly
10. Add the vanilla and coconut, if using
11. Strain into the baking dish
12. I usually just pour it all inches
13. Sprinkle the top with nutmeg
14. Put into the pan with hot water
15. Bake for 55-60 minutes, until a knife comes out clean<|endoftext|>
