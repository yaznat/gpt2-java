Ingredients:
• Whole chicken breasts
• Cream of chicken soup
• Dry sherry
• Butter
• Green onions
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Cut chicken into small piece and place in crock pot
2. Add all remaining ingredients except rice
3. Cover, cook high 2 1 / 2 to 4 hours
4. Serve over hot rice<|endoftext|>
