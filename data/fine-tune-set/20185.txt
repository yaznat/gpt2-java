Ingredients:
• Beef
• Soy sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh cracked pepper
• Onion
• Red bell pepper
• Broccoli

Steps:
1. Marinate the beef in the soy sauce, garlic, and pepper for at least 30 minutes
2. Saute the beef in 2 tablespoons oil for 1 minute on each side
3. Remove from pan
4. Add in remaining ingredients and any remaining marinade and saute for 3-4 minutes or until desired doneness
5. Add in beef and serve over rice<|endoftext|>
