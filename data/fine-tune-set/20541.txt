What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Babzy S Breakfast Casserole

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Milk
• Hash browns
• Green onion
• Cheddar cheese
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. If using bacon, cut into 1 inch pieces and cook until crisp
2. Drain and set aside
3. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
4. Lightly grease a large casserole dish
5. In a large bowl, beat together the eggs and milk and seasonings
6. Stir in the bacon, hash browns, green onions, and 1
7. 5 cups of the shredded cheddar cheese
8. Pour into the prepared baking dish
9. Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes, or until egg is set
10. Sprinkle the remaining cheddar cheese on top, and then broil for 3 to 4 minutes, or until the cheese is melted
11. Remove from oven, and let sit 5 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
