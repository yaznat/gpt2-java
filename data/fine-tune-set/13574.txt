Ingredients:
• Beef brisket
• Onions
• Red wine
• White vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Soy sauce
• Paprika
• Garlic cloves
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Arrange onion slices in bottom of roasting pan, then place the brisket on top
2. Combine all other ingredients and pour over the meat
3. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 3-4 hours at 350 degree f
4. Until meat is fork tender
5. Juice can be thickened if you like
6. Remove meat from oven and let sit for 10-15 minutes
7. Cut in thin slices across the grain
8. Serve with noodles or rice<|endoftext|>
