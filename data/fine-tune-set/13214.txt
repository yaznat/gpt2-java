Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Stir Fried Chicken Asparagus

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Asparagus
• Chicken broth
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Chili flakes
• Salt
• White pepper

Steps:
1. Cut onion and press garlic and let both sit for at least 5 minutes to bring out their hidden health benefits
2. Heat 1 tbs broth in a stainless steel wok or 12-inch skillet
3. Healthy stir fry onion and asparagus in broth for about 3 minutes over medium high heat, stirring constantly
4. Add ginger, garlic, chicken, and continue to stir-fry for another 3-4 minutes
5. Add soy sauce, vinegar and red chili flakes
6. Stir together and cover
7. Cook for another 2-3 minutes
8. This may have to cook for an extra couple minutes if the asparagus is thick
9. Season with salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
