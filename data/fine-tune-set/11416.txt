Ingredients:
• Dark brown sugar
• Butter
• Molasses
• Egg
• Unbleached flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Ground ginger
• Cinnamon
• Hot water
• Sourdough starter
• Blueberries
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Lightly butter and flour a 8"x8" baking pan
3. Cream together the brown sugar and butter
4. Then add molasses and egg, beating continuously
5. Set aside
6. In a separate bowl, sift dry ingredients together and blend into hot water
7. Then beat this mixture into creamed mixture
8. Gradually add the sourdough starter slowly, mixing carefully to maintain a bubbly batter
9. Gently fold in the blueberries
10. Pour into prepared baking pan and bake at 375 degrees f for about 30 minutes or until done
11. Serve warm with good quality vanilla ice cream or freshly whipped cream
12. Makes 6 to 8 servings<|endoftext|>
