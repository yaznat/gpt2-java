What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>5 Minute General Tsos Chicken

Ingredients:
• Chicken tenders
• Broccoli
• Kosher salt
• Cornstarch
• Rice wine vinegar
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Red chili pepper flakes
• Sesame oil
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• White rice
• Scallion
• Toasted sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees f
2. Place chicken tenders on a rimmed baking sheet and transfer to oven
3. Bake until very crispy and golden, about 10 minutes beyond recommended bake time on the package
4. Reduce heat to 200 degrees f to keep tenders warm until ready to serve
5. Meanwhile, fill a medium pot fit with a steamer insert with 1-inch of water
6. Place over medium-high heat and when water boils, add broccoli and season with salt
7. Cover, and steam until broccoli is tender, about 8 minutes
8. Keep warm
9. Place cornstarch in a medium bowl
10. Add rice wine vinegar, soy sauce, honey, chile flakes, and 3 tablespoons water and whisk to combine
11. Set aside
12. Place a small skillet over medium heat and add sesame oil
13. When shimmering, add garlic and ginger and saute until softened, about 2 minutes
14. Add cornstarch mixture and cook, whisking constantly, until mixture comes to a boil and thickens, about 1-2 minutes
15. Remove from heat and keep warm
16. Remove chicken tenders from oven and transfer to a large mixing bowl
17. Add the broccoli, sauce, and toss to coat
18. Serve with white rice on the side, topped with scallions and toasted sesame seeds<|endoftext|>
