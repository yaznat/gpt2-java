Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onion
• Celery
• Lean ground beef
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dark brown sugar
• Ketchup
• Kidney beans
• Lima beans
• Pork and beans

Steps:
1. Saute bacon, onion, celery and ground beef
2. Drain off grease and add rest of ingredients
3. Pour into large casserole dish
4. Bake at 350 degrees for 1 hour, uncovered<|endoftext|>
