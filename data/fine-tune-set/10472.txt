Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Oatmeal Cookies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Quick oats
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Lemons, zest of
• Dark brown sugar
• Salted butter
• Egg
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Honey
• Apple
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 300 degrees f
2. In a bowl, combine the flour, oats, salt, baking soda, cinnamon, cloves and lemon zest
3. Mix well with a wire whisk and then set it aside
4. Cream the butter and sugar together in a large bowl with an electric mixer
5. Add the egg, applesauce and honey, and beat at medium speed until smooth
6. Add the flour mixture, fresh apple and raisings
7. Blend at a low speed until just combined
8. Don't overmix it, and don't worry, it's supposed to be a soft dough
9. Drop the dough by rounded tablespoons on to ungreased cookie sheets
10. The cookies should be spaced about 1 inches apart
11. Bake for 18-22 minutes, or until the bottoms are golden<|endoftext|>
