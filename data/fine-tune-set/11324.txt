Ingredients:
• Yeast
• Oats
• Oat flour
• Rice flour
• Cornstarch
• Tapioca flour
• Sorghum flour
• Xanthan gum
• Salt
• Egg
• Honey
• Canola oil
• Apple cider vinegar
• Warm water

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, sift together the dry ingredients
2. In a separate bowl, mix together the liquid ingredients
3. Gradually beat in the dry ingredients
4. Beat on high for 2-3 minutes
5. Pour batter into a greased and floured 9x5 bread pan
6. Grease one side of a piece of plastic wrap and cover the bread with this side down
7. Place in a warm area and let rise for 30-50 minutes or doubled in size
8. Preheat oven to 350 deg f
9. Remove plastic wrap and place in oven
10. Bake for 30-40 minutes or until internal temperature reaches 200f
11. After first 10 minutes, cover with foil to keep crust from getting too dark<|endoftext|>
