Give me a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>Amish Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Hard-boiled eggs
• Onion
• Celery
• Salad dressing
• Sugar
• Prepared mustard
• Vinegar
• Salt
• Milk

Steps:
1. Chop potatoes, eggs, onion and celery finely
2. Place in bowl
3. Combine dressing ingredients in another bowl
4. Mix well
5. Pour in parts and mix with potato mixture until desired wetness is received
6. Mix well to blend
7. Chill until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
