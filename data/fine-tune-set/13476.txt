Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Tomato sauce
• Onion
• Dried parsley flakes
• Dried oregano
• Basil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Cottage cheese
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Brown beef in skillet
2. Drain
3. Stir in tomato sauce, onion, herbs, 1 / 2 teaspoon salt and pepper
4. Simmer uncovered for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Combine spinach with cottage cheese and remaining 1 / 4 teaspoon salt
6. Spoon spinach around edge of 9x13 inch baking dish
7. Pour beef mixture into center
8. Sprinkle cheese over meat
9. Bake at 375 degrees for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
