What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>6 Can Crock Pot Chili

Ingredients:
• Cornmeal
• Paprika
• Barbecue sauce
• Whole tomatoes
• Chili without beans
• Pinto beans
• Chili beans
• Condensed french onion soup

Steps:
1. In 3-1 / 2 to 4 qt crock pot, combine all ingredients
2. Mix well
3. Cover and cook on low for 8 to 9 hours
4. For hotter chili flavor, 2 ts chili powder can be added<|endoftext|>
