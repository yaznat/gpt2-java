Give me a recipe for a cocktail.<|endoftext|>Caesar Cocktail Lunch

Ingredients:
• Frank's red hot sauce
• Lemon wedges
• Vodka
• Celery salt
• Lemon juice
• Ice cube
• Clamato juice
• Grape tomatoes
• Pickled onions
• Garlic sausage
• Jalapeno pepper
• Celery rib

Steps:
1. Run lemon around the rim of a 8-10 oz stemmed glass
2. Crust glass rim with celery salt
3. Add hot sauce to glass, then ice
4. Add vodka and lemon juice
5. Salt briefly with celery salt
6. Pour in 1 / 2 of the clamato juice
7. Add more celery salt
8. Add remainder of clamato juice
9. Add more celery salt
10. Add lemon or lime wedge
11. Stir with celery rib, and stire briefly to mix
12. Assemble remaining ingredients on a small bamboo skewer, and hang above the glass rim as serving<|endoftext|>
