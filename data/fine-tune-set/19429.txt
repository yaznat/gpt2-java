Ingredients:
• Hash brown potatoes
• Onion
• Eggs
• Italian style breadcrumbs
• Ketchup
• Salt
• Pepper
• Lean ground beef

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375 degrees
2. In large bowl, combine potatoes, onion, eggs, bread crumbs, 1 / 3 cup of the ketchup, salt and pepper
3. Mix well
4. Add ground beef
5. Mix lightly but thoroughly
6. On rack set over broiler pan, shape beef mixture in to 8 x 4-inch loaf
7. Spread remaining 1 / 3 cup ketchup over top of loaf
8. Bake 1 1 / 4 hours or until center is no longer pink and of medium doneness
9. Let stand 5 minutes
10. Cut into slices<|endoftext|>
