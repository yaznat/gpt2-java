What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Avocado Fries With Chipotle Remoulade

Ingredients:
• Chipotle chiles in adobo
• Lime juice
• Mayonnaise
• Cilantro
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Lime, juice of
• Water
• All-purpose flour
• Panko breadcrumbs
• Avocado
• Lime wedge

Steps:
1. Place chipotles with adobo sauce and lime juice in a blender and puree until smooth
2. Set aside
3. Place mayonnaise in a medium bowl
4. Whisk in 1 / 3 of chipotle mixture at a time, tasting between each addition for spiciness
5. Add cilantro and season with salt and pepper
6. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate until ready to serve
7. Heat vegetable oil in a large pot over high heat until instant read thermometer reaches 350 degrees
8. While oil is heating up, place all-purpose flour, lime juice mixed with 2 cups water and panko breadcrumbs in 3 separate shallow dishes
9. Cut avocado in quarters lengthwise
10. Remove wedges from pit and peel off skin
11. Cut each quarter in half so you have 8 wedges total
12. Season with salt and pepper
13. Dredge avocado wedges in flour, then dip into lime water, then dredge through breadcrumbs
14. Make sure each slice is completely coated
15. Once oil has reached 350 degrees, fry breaded slices for about 3 minutes, or until lightly browned
16. Remove avocados from oil using a slotted spoon and place on a paper towel-lined plate to drain
17. Serve with lime wedges and chipotle remoulade<|endoftext|>
