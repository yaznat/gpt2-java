Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Soy sauce
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Honey
• Olive oil
• Ground black pepper
• Worcestershire sauce
• Onion powder
• Salt
• Liquid smoke flavoring
• Cayenne pepper
• Rib eye steak

Steps:
1. In medium bowl, mix all ingredients except steaks
2. Place steaks in a shallow glass dish or ziplock bag with the marinade, turn to coat
3. Cover, and marinate in the refrigerator 1 to 2 days
4. Preheat grill for medium-high to high heat
5. Grill steaks 7 minutes per side, or to desired doneness
6. Discard leftover marinade<|endoftext|>
