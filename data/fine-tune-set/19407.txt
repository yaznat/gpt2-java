What's a good donut recipe?<|endoftext|>Quick And Easy Donuts

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Yogurt
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Powdered sugar
• Oil

Steps:
1. Heat the oil to 375 degrees
2. Start by combining the two eggs and sugar
3. Wisk until the egg mixture is pail and fluffy
4. Add the yogurt, butter and vanilla, wisk briefly
5. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, salt, baking powder and soda
6. Add the dry ingredients to the wet
7. Stir just until ingredients are combined, if you over mix the dough will become tough
8. Roll the dough to 1 / 4 inches thick
9. Cut doughnuts as close together as possible
10. Repeat with the remaining dough
11. Fry on each side until golden brown
12. Drain the doughnuts on a paper towel<|endoftext|>
