Ingredients:
• Chipotle chiles
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Water
• Salt
• Honey
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan, bring chiles, tomato, water, onion, garlic and salt to a boil and reduce to a simmer
2. Cook, slowly, covered for 15 minutes
3. Puree mixture in a blender until smooth
4. Stir in the honey and vinegar
5. Serve at room temperature as a dipping sauce with shrimp<|endoftext|>
