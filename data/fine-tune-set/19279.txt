Ingredients:
• Trix cereal
• Almond milk
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Pour the cereal into a large bowl and add the milk
2. Let this mixture sit for 10-15 minutes
3. Melt two scoops of vanilla ice cream in a bowl until liquid
4. Combine the cereal / milk mixture and the melted vanilla ice cream
5. Pour the mixture into popsicle molds and put them in the freezer for 45 minutes
6. Then remove, add popsicle sticks and freeze for another four hours<|endoftext|>
