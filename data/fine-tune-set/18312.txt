Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Blintz Pancakes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Reduced-fat sour cream
• Low fat cottage cheese
• Eggs
• Strawberry flavored syrup
• Fresh strawberries

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar and salt
2. Stir in the sour cream, cottage cheese and eggs until blended
3. Pour batter by 1 / 4 cupful onto a greased hot griddle in batches
4. Turn when bubbles form on top
5. Cook until the second side is golden brown
6. Serve with syrup and strawberries if desired<|endoftext|>
