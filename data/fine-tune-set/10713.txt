Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Coarse salt
• Black pepper
• Red pepper flakes
• Tabasco sauce
• Tomatoes
• Bacon
• Parsley
• Garlic cloves
• Lemon
• Pasta

Steps:
1. Put water on to boil in pot with table salt,
2. Put diced tomatoes in a bowl
3. Squeeze lemon over top
4. Add in coarse ground salt, black pepper, red pepper flakes, and parsley
5. Stir up to mix flavors
6. Heat olive oil on medium / high for a couple minute on a pan
7. Once pan is hot, adjust temperature down to medium and add garlic and bacon pieces
8. Fry for about 3 minutes
9. Pour tomato mixture into pan
10. Add in 1 tsp tabasco sauce & stir thoroughly
11. Cover and turn temperature down to medium / low
12. Let simmer for approximately 6-8 minutes
13. Add pasta to boiling water
14. Cook until al dente
15. Drain
16. Put in serving bowl
17. Add sauce to pasta
18. Stir and serve!<|endoftext|>
