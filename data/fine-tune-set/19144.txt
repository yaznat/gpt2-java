Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Brownie Ice Cream Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• Light corn syrup
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Salt
• Walnuts
• Ice cream

Steps:
1. Place rack in center of oven and preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Line an 11- x 17-inch jelly roll pan with foil and lightly butter and flour
3. Melt the butter
4. Add chocolate and let stand 1 minute
5. Then whisk smooth
6. Let stand 10 minutes, until tepid
7. In medium bowl, at high speed, beat eggs and sugar 2-3 minutes
8. Beat in the corn syrup, vanilla and cooled chocolate mixture
9. Using a wooden spoon, stir in flour, salt and walnuts
10. Spread batter in prepared pan
11. Bake 12-15 minutes
12. Cool completely
13. Run a knife around inside edges of pan to release
14. Place a baking sheet on top of brownie and invert
15. Carefully peel off foil
16. Cut brownie into two 11- x 8-1 / 2 inch rectangles
17. Line jelly roll pan with plastic wrap
18. Place one rectangle on wrap, crust side up
19. Using metal cake spatula, spread ice cream evenly over rectangle to cover
20. Place other rectangle on ice cream, crust side down
21. Wrap lightly in plastic wrap, pressing ice cream on sides to compact
22. Freeze overnight
23. Unwrap and, using a sharp knife, cut into 16 rectangles
24. Wrap each tightly in plastic wrap
25. Can be prepared up to 2 weeks ahead, wrapped tightly and frozen<|endoftext|>
