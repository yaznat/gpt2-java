What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Becky S Gourmet Southwestern Taco Salad Or Nachos With Steak

Ingredients:
• Beef sirloin
• Fresh mushrooms
• White onion
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Garlic powder
• Garlic paste
• Ground coriander
• Salt & pepper
• Mixed salad greens
• Dark red kidney beans
• Black olives
• Green onion
• Chili pepper
• Avocado
• Cucumber
• Diced tomato
• Corn
• Lime wedge
• Fresh cilantro
• Sour cream
• Baked corn tortilla chips
• Chili powder
• Refried beans
• Shredded cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large skillet or wok
2. Add onion and mushrooms
3. Cook on medium-high heat for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally
4. Add beef strips and seasonings to taste
5. Stir well to coat
6. Cook @ 10 minutes longer, stirring occasionally, until desired doneness
7. For taco salad:
8. Serve warm meat atop beds of mixed lettuce, tomatoes, kidney beans, corn, sliced black olives, green onions, avocado slices, squeeze of fresh lime juice, and crumbled chips
9. For nachos:
10. Alternatively, layer meat and your favorite toppings between layers of tortilla chips, top with shredded cheese, and pop into the microwave for a minute, just until cheese is melted
11. Top with a dollop of mexican sour cream or ranch dressing, a dash of chili powder, and a squeeze of fresh lime<|endoftext|>
