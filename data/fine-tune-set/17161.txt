Ingredients:
• Whole milk
• Green cardamom pods
• Tea bags
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Gently 'bruise' the cardamon by smacking with a rolling to open the husk up a little
2. Place the cardamon, milk & tea bags in a pan & slowly simmer for 5 minutes
3. Remove from heat, mix in sugar & pour into cups to serve<|endoftext|>
