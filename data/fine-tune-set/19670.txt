Please give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Ali Baba S Oven Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Garlic powder
• Ground cumin
• Ground fenugreek
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cayenne pepper
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Fresh lemon juice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Lightly oil a large baking pan
3. Mix the spices, oil and lemon juice together in a small bowl
4. Toss the potatoes with this mixture until evenly coated
5. Spread the potatoes onto the baking sheet and bake in the preheated oven for 30-35 minutes or until nicely browned and crispy, turning once halfway during cooking<|endoftext|>
