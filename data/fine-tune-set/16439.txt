Ingredients:
• Lemon juice
• Teriyaki marinade
• White pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt
• Medium shrimp
• Bacon

Steps:
1. In small bowl stir together lemon juice, teriyaki marinade, white pepper, cayenne pepper, and salt
2. Place shrimp in plastic bag set in shallow pan
3. Pour lemon juice mixture over shrimp
4. Seal bag and marinate in refrigerator for 1 hour
5. Preheat grill
6. Cut bacon slices into thirds
7. In a skillet cook bacon, 1 / 4 at a time
8. Just 3 to 5 min
9. Or until bacon starts to brown on edges, turning once
10. Drain on paper towels
11. Drain shrimp
12. Discard marinade
13. Wrap each shrimp with a piece of bacon
14. Thread bacon-wrapped shrimp on 8 small metal skewers
15. Grill on the rack of an uncovered grill directly over medium hot coals for 8-10min
16. Or until shrimp turns opaque, turning once<|endoftext|>
