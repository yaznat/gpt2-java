Ingredients:
• Butternut squash
• Red bell pepper
• Onion
• Chicken broth
• Olive oil
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. Saut onion with olive oil in a pan over low heat for 1 to 2 minutes, until translucent
2. Add bell pepper and cook for 1 minute
3. Add squash and stir
4. Raise heat to medium and add all spices
5. Mix well
6. Cook for about 7 minutes, until squash starts to soften
7. Raise heat to high and add broth
8. Bring to a boil
9. After 30 seconds of boiling, reduce heat to low
10. Simmer until broth evaporates, stirring often
11. Cover pan and continue to cook for 3 minutes, or until squash is tender
12. Eat up!<|endoftext|>
