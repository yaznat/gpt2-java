Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Ever Homemade Pancakes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Milk
• Eggs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Combine flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar in a large bowl and stir well
2. Combine milk, eggs, and butter in a small bowl and stir well
3. Add to dry ingredients, stirring just until blended
4. For each pancake, pour abour 1 / 4 cup batter onto a hot, lightly greased griddle
5. Turn pancakes when tops are covered with bubbles and edges look cooked<|endoftext|>
