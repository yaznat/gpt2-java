Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>A Winning Banana Nut Bread

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Lemon juice
• Cake flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Peanuts
• Eggs
• Butter
• Lemon rind

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 180c / 350f grease a 23x13x7 cm loaf tin
2. Peel and mash the bananas and mix them with the lemon juice
3. Sift together the cake flour, baking powder and salt, in a fairly large mixing bowl
4. Add the sugar and nuts and mix
5. Beat the egg yolks and add them to the flour mixture with the butter or margarine
6. Mix lightly
7. Add the banana pulp and lemon rind and mix well
8. Beat the egg whites until just stiff, and fold them lightly into the batter with a metal spoon
9. Spoon the batter into the tin, level it off, and sprinkle more nuts on top if desired
10. Bake on the middle shelf of the oven for 60 minutes
11. Test with a skewer to see if done: if the skewer comes out clean, the loaf is ready
12. If it does not, bake for a further 10 to 15 minutes
13. Allow the loaf to cool slightly in the tin
14. Turn it out onto a wire rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
