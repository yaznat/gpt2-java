Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Chicken broth
• Fat-free refried beans
• Taco seasoning
• Black beans
• Burrito-size flour tortilla
• Spanish rice
• Fat free sour cream

Steps:
1. Place chicken breasts in crock pot with chicken broth and cook on high for 3-4 hours or on low for 5-6 hours
2. They're done when you can shred them easily
3. Meanwhile, make a spanish rice
4. I use rice-a-roni
5. Shred chicken and save 1 1 / 2 cups of chicken broth in the crockpot
6. If you need more, just add water
7. Add shredded chicken back to broth
8. Add packet of burrito seasoning and can of refried beans to chicken mixture and stir
9. Heat through
10. Heat tortillas according to directions
11. Place some spanish rice on tortilla
12. Add some chicken over the rice, a scoop of black beans and some sour cream to taste
13. Roll burrito and enjoy! i use 2 tortillas so it doesn't break because i like to pick it up
14. My husband pours some burrito sauce from the crock pot over the top and eats with a fork<|endoftext|>
