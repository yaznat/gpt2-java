What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Becki S Pumpkin Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Nutmeg
• Eggs
• Oil
• Water
• Buttermilk
• Canned pumpkin

Steps:
1. Mix the first 6 ingredients
2. In a seperate bowl, beat together the eggs, oil & water
3. Stir in buttermilk and canned pumpkin
4. Mix together dry & liquid ingredients
5. Pour onto hot griddle & cook until done
6. Sprinkle with confectioners sugar if desired<|endoftext|>
