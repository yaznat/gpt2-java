Ingredients:
• Catfish fillets
• Butter
• Garlic
• Breadcrumbs
• Seasoning salt

Steps:
1. Preheat over to 375f
2. Dredge filets in butter
3. Dredge filets in bread crumbs
4. Dredge filets in minced garlic
5. Sprinkle with seasoning salt
6. Spay small casserole dish add filets and bake covered with foil for 25 minutes
7. Uncover and broil for 3 or more minutes, depending on if ya want them crispier
8. Good served with rice and veggie of your choice<|endoftext|>
