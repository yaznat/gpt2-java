Ingredients:
• Non-dairy coffee creamer
• Dutch process cocoa
• Confectioners' sugar
• Peppermint extract

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a container with a tight-fitting lid
2. Shake well to blend
3. Store in airtight container
4. To make bavarian mint coffee: in a mug, combine 2 tbsp of creamer with 6 oz coffee<|endoftext|>
