What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Authentic Style Chicken Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Crushed tomatoes
• Chicken broth
• Salsa
• Canned jalapeno slices
• Cumin
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Flour tortillas
• American cheese
• Butter

Steps:
1. Add tomatoes, broth, salsa, jalapeos, cumin, garlic, and salt to a medium size sauce pan and bring to a boil
2. Chop chicken into small bite size pieces and add to boiling sauce
3. Turn heat immediately down to low- medium and simmer until chicken is cooked through
4. You can simmer for longer if you want more flavor infused into the meat
5. Heat 1 / 2 tablespoon of butter into a pan on medium low heat
6. Place cheese slice into center of flour tortilla
7. With a slotted spoon pick up chicken pieces and allow the juice to drain out
8. Place in center of tortilla and fold close
9. Cook tortillas about a minute on each side until golden brown and serve
10. Goes well with a guacamole salad<|endoftext|>
