Give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Apple Pie With Cheese Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Cinnamon raisin bread
• American cheese
• Apple
• Butter

Steps:
1. Top 1 of the bread slices with singles and apple slices
2. Cover with remaining bread slice
3. Spread outside of sandwich with butter
4. Cook in skillet on medium heat 2 minute on each side or until golden brown on both sides<|endoftext|>
