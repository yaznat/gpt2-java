What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>Absolutely Apple Cake

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Oil
• Sugar
• Self-rising flour
• Apples
• Coconut
• Walnuts
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Blend eggs, oil and sugar until creamy
2. Add flour a little at a time
3. Blend well
4. Batter will be stiff
5. Fold in apples, coconut and nuts
6. Pour into a greased and floured bundt or tube pan
7. Bake at 350f for 1 hour
8. Let cake cool in pan for 30 minutes and then remove to serving plate
9. Mix together the butter, brown sugar and milk in a saucepan
10. Boil for 3 minutes
11. Pour over warm cake<|endoftext|>
