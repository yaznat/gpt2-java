Can you give me a potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Skordy New Potatoes W Rosemary Lemon Olive Oi

Ingredients:
• New potatoes
• Lemon, juice of
• Lemon, zest of
• Rosemary
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to gas mark 8
2. Cut your potatoes into 1 inch chunks
3. Toss in a bowl the potatoes in the oil with the rosemary and lemon juice and lemon zest and garlic
4. Chuck the lot onto a baking tray not forgetting to scrape herbs and oil from the bowl
5. Bake for twenty minutes while listening to funky tunes and enjoying the yumminess that is to come
6. Check that potatoes are firm yet "give" with a fork
7. Squish them a little bit with a fork and put back in oven for another 10 minutes on gas mark 3
8. Listen to more tunes
9. Serve with a squirt of fresh lemon and with greek or curry or vegan stuff or just eat out of pan
10. I suggest tatskiki
11. You can pulverize this into a dip using more olive oil and lemon juice or eat it chunky
12. I like chunks
13. I reckon this would make an ace potato salad base
