Give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Barbecued Spoonburgers

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Celery
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic clove
• Worcestershire sauce
• Ketchup
• Paprika
• Pepper
• Tomato paste
• Water
• Red wine vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Dry mustard

Steps:
1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet
2. Drain off the fat
3. Add all of the ingredients to the slow cooker
4. Stir well
5. Cook on low for 6-8 hours or on high for 3-4
6. Serve hot over hamburger buns<|endoftext|>
