Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Caramel Ice Cream Sauce

Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Cornstarch
• Half-and-half cream
• Water
• Light corn syrup
• Butter
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Combine brown sugar and cornstarch in a saucepan, stir in the cream, water and cornsyrup until smooth
2. Bring to a boil
3. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened
4. Remove from heat and stir in the butter and vanilla until butter is melted
5. Serve hot or cold over ice cream
6. Refrigerate leftovers<|endoftext|>
