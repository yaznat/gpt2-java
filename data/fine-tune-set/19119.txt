Give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Black Forest Ice Cream Shake

Ingredients:
• Chocolate ice cream
• Frozen cherries
• Milk
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Place the pint of chocolate ice cream on the counter to soften
2. Place the softened ice cream, cherries, milk, and chocolate chips in a blender
3. Blend on high speed until very smooth<|endoftext|>
