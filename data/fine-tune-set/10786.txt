Give me a salad recipe.<|endoftext|>3 Fruit Salad

Ingredients:
• Oranges
• Apples
• Bananas
• Walnuts
• Salad dressing

Steps:
1. Peel and chop oranges into bite size pieces
2. Core and chop apples into bite size pieces
3. Peel and chop bananas into bite size pieces
4. Add walnuts and salad dressing
5. Some people like more salad dressing, some less
6. You judge
7. Stir
8. Chill
9. If you have less people, just use less fruit
10. This is good in the summer time too<|endoftext|>
