Ingredients:
• Onion
• Tomato paste
• Roasted cashew nuts
• Garam masala
• Garlic cloves
• Lemon juice
• Lemon zest
• Turmeric
• Salt
• Yogurt
• Vegetable oil
• Dried apricots
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• Chicken stock
• Fresh coriander leaves

Steps:
1. Spice paste:
2. In a food processor, combine ingredients and process until smoooth
3. Transfer to a small bowl
4. Curry:
5. In a wok or large skillet, heat oil over medium heat and fry spice mixture until fragrant
6. Add apricots and chicken: cook for 1 minute
7. Stir in stock, cover and simmer until tender
8. Spoon into serving bowls and garnish with the extra cashew nuts and coriander
9. Serve with steamed rice<|endoftext|>
