Ingredients:
• Granny smith apples
• Dried cherries
• Walnuts
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in medium or large ziploc brand zip 'n steam bag
2. Seal bag and shake gently to combine ingredients
3. Pat ingredients into single layer
4. Place bag in microwave
5. Microwave on full power for 6 minutes or until apples are fork-tender
6. Allow bag to stand for 1 minute before handling
7. Carefully open bag and serve
8. Serve over vanilla frozen yogurt or angel food cake if desired<|endoftext|>
