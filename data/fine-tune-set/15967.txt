What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Fuzzyhead S Favorite Saucy Burritos

Ingredients:
• Burrito-size flour tortillas
• Refried beans
• Soy chorizo
• Green enchilada sauce
• Cheddar cheese
• Tomatoes
• Green onion
• Sour cream
• Black olives

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Lightly grease a 9" x 13" baking pan
3. In a large, deep skillet, heat soyrizo, refried beans and one can of enchilada sauce over medium-high heat until thoroughly blended and heated through
4. To assemble, divide mixture evenly among 8 tortillas
5. Sprinkle 1-2 tablespoons shredded cheese on top of each, and roll up, tucking in the ends if you wish
6. Place rolled burritos seam-side down in the baking dish
7. Pour remaining can of enchilada sauce over burritos
8. Top with remaining shredded cheese
9. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes or until sauce is hot and bubbly
10. Serve with optional toppings
11. If desired, you can also bake them a bit longer uncovered to let the cheese brown, but we can never wait that long!
12. These can be refrigerated or frozen before baking to serve at a later date<|endoftext|>
