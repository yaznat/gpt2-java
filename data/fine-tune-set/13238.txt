Please generate a chicken recipe.<|endoftext|>3 Cheese Chicken Penne

Ingredients:
• Fresh basil leaf
• Roma tomatoes
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Chicken breast
• Lemon juice
• Italian spices
• Button mushrooms
• Purple onion
• Roasted garlic alfredo sauce
• Provolone cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Penne pasta

Steps:
1. 2t lemon juice, 1 t olive oil and italian seasonings together, pour over chicken and place in a plastic bag or a marinator container
2. Let marinate for 30 minutes to a couple hours
3. Start bruscetta mixture too
4. Just mix altogether and cover in the fridge until dinner is ready to serve
5. Heat grill and cook chicken on a medium heat until chicken is completely done
6. Start to cook your pasta on stove according to package directions
7. Meanwhile heat 2 tsp olive oil over med-high heat in a heavy skillet and cook mushrooms and onions until tender
8. Turn off skillet
9. Empty jar of alfredo sauce into skillet and add drained cooked pasta
10. Chicken is done
11. Slice thick or thin and add to the skillet mix altogether
12. Heat contents of skillet up until just about bubbly then add the cheeses and mix well until combined and melted
13. Serve in pasta dishes and top with bruscetta mix
14. Along with some garlic bread or sticks this is a great meal!<|endoftext|>
