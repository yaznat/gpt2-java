Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Asa Ransom House Brownie Mania

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Unsweetened cocoa
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Vanilla
• Pecans
• White chocolate chips
• Butterscotch chips
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f and butter a 9 x 13" pan
2. Combine butter, sugar and eggs
3. Beat well
4. Add cocoa, flour, baking powder and vanilla
5. Mix well
6. Stir in pecans and chips
7. Spread batter in prepared pan and bake for 40 minutes or until center is set
8. Cool completely and cut into squares for serving<|endoftext|>
