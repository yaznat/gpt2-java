• White flour

Steps:

2. Combine yogurt with the oil, pour over the flour mixture, stirring it with a fork- be gentle
3. On a well floured surface knead the mixture
4. Ightly into a ball cover and let stand while you prepare the topping
5. Roll out the dough to 1 / 4" thickness
6. Transfer to a 12" greased pizza pan
7. Sprinkle 1 1 / 2 cups of the cheese right on the dough and right to the edge
8. Top with tomatoes, artichokes and olives
9. Sprinkle remaining cheese on top
10. Have bbq medium hot and place pan 5-6" above the heat, close cover
