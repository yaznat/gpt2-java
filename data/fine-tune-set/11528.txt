Ingredients:
• Yellow cake mix
• Vanilla instant pudding mix
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Eggs
• Oil
• Water
• Crushed pineapple
• Carrot
• Pecans
• Coconut

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Mix together the cake mix, pudding, nutmeg and cinnamon
3. Add eggs, oil and water to this mixture
4. Mix on medium speed for 1 minute
5. Add the pineapple, carrots, pecans and coconut
6. Blend well
7. Pour into a greased 9 inch round cake pan
8. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, until tooth pick inserted in the center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
