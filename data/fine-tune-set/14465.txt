What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>Arizona Chuck Wagon Beans

Ingredients:
• Dried navy beans
• Water
• Salt pork
• Onion
• Garlic
• Green bell pepper
• Chuck steaks
• Salt
• Oregano
• Red pepper
• Ground cumin
• Tomato sauce

Steps:
1. Pick over beans and rinse well
2. Combine beans and water in a large kettle
3. Bring to boiling
4. Cover
5. Cook 2 minutes
6. Remove from heat and let stand 1 hour then pour into crockpot
7. Brown salt pork in a large skillet
8. Remove with a slotted spoon to crockpot
9. Saut onion, garlic and green pepper in pan drippings
10. Remove with slotted spoon to crockpot
11. Brown beef, a few pieces at a time in pan drippings
12. Remove to crockpot with slotted spoon
13. Stir in salt, oregano, red pepper, cumin and tomato sauce
14. Add more water, if necessary to bring liquid level above beans
15. Cook on low for 10 hours or on high for 6 hours, or until beans are tender<|endoftext|>
