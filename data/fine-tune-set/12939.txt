Ingredients:
• Mahi mahi
• Olive oil
• Lemon, juice of
• Italian style breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Dried dill weed
• Dried rosemary
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Whisk together olive oil and lemon juice in bowl big enough to dip fish into
3. Mix breadcrumbs, parmesan, dill, rosemary and salt and pepper in another bowl of similar size
4. Dip each filet in olive oil mixture, then coat with breadcrumb mixture and place in 9x13 inch glass dish
5. Top each filet with 2-3 dollops butter
6. Bake for about 15 min or until fish flakes easily with fork<|endoftext|>
