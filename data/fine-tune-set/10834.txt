Please give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>A Different Layered Salad

Ingredients:
• Light mayonnaise
• Light sour cream
• Lemon juice
• Garlic cloves
• Dry mustard
• Salt
• Pepper
• Sugar
• Baby spinach
• Roasted deli chicken
• Red pepper
• Eggs
• Head iceberg lettuce
• Carrots
• Cheddar cheese
• Pecan pieces
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Use a 4-5 quart serving bowl, preferably clear glass so layers are visible
2. Whisk all dressing ingredients in a small bowl until well combined
3. Refrigerate until ready to use
4. Spread spinach over bottom of serving bowl
5. Top with chicken
6. Place a row of red pepper strips around the sides of the bowl and scatter remaining strips in the middle
7. Place egg wedges against side of bowl, the rest in the middle
8. Top with even layers of lettuce and carrots
9. Spread dressing over top to the edges of bowl to seal
10. Refrigerate two hours, or cover airtight and refrigerate overnight
11. Just before serving sprinkle with cheese, pecans and bacon
12. I prefer to toss it as well, but some people just spoon through the layers<|endoftext|>
