Ingredients:
• Margarine
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Oatmeal
• Nuts
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients and form into several rolls
2. Wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate 1-2 hours or wrap in ovenwrap of aluminum foil and label and freeze
3. When ready to bake
4. Slice into 1 / 4" slices
5. Place 2" apart on greased cookie sheet and bake 15 minutes at 350f
6. Glaze tops when warm
7. To prepare glaze
8. Mix powdered sugar and hot water to make a thin glaze
