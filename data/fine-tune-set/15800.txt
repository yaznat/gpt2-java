Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• White onion
• Cooked rice
• Condensed cheddar cheese soup
• Taco seasoning
• Burrito-size flour tortillas
• Salsa
• Black beans
• Corn
• Cheddar cheese
• Enchilada sauce
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. In a large skillet, brown ground beef with onion
3. Drain
4. Return beef & onions to hot skillet off of heat and stir in rice, undiluted soup and taco seasoning
5. Mix together well
6. Lightly spray a lasagna pan / 13" x 9" pan with nonstick cooking spray
7. Line bottom of pan with 4 of the tortillas
8. Evenly cover tortillas with half of the meat / rice mixture, then 1 / 2 of the salsa, then sprinkle with half of the corn, half of the black beans and 1 cup of cheese
9. Cover again with 4 more of the tortillas and repeat these layers again
10. Finally, top with the last 4 remaining tortillas
11. Pour entire can of enchilada sauce over top
12. Sprinkle with remaining 2 cups cheese and cilantro
13. Bake at 350 for about 25 minutes
14. Serve with a dallop of sour cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
