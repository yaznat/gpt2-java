Ingredients:
• Butter
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Sugar
• Artificial sweetener
• Salt
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Bananas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease a 9"x5" loaf pan
3. In a mixing bowl, combine butter, apple sauce and sugar / sweetener
4. Mix well
5. Add salt, eggs, vanilla, flour, baking soda, baking powder, and bananas
6. Mix well
7. Pour the batter into prepared pan and bake for 50 minutes<|endoftext|>
