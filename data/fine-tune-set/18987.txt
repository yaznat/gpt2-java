Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Coconut extract
• Eggs
• Milk
• Mochiko sweet rice flour
• Baking powder
• Coconut flakes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease a 9x13-inch baking pan, set aside
3. In a large bowl, cream the butter and sugar
4. Add the eggs, 1 at a time
5. Beating well after each addition
6. Add the remaining ingredients until blended well
7. Pour into prepared pan
8. Top with coconut flakes and bake for 1 hour and 15-20 minutes
9. Cool completely and then chill until ready to serve
10. Slice into equal squares<|endoftext|>
