What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Crunch Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Ground allspice
• Ground nutmeg
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Unsalted butter
• Tart apple
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. For muffins: in a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, baking soda, salt, allspice, and nutmeg
2. In another bowl, beat the eggs, sour cream and butter together
3. Stir the egg mixture into the dry ingredients, just until moistened
4. Fold in the diced apple into the batter
5. Fill greased or lined muffin cups one-third full
6. For topping: in a bowl, combine the nuts, flour, sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg
7. Cut in the butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs
8. Sprinkle about two-thirds of the topping over the batter in the cups
9. Top with remaining batter and then sprinkle the remainder of the topping mix over each muffin
10. Bake at 375f for 20-25 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean
11. Cool for 5 minutes before removing from pans to wire racks to cool completely<|endoftext|>
