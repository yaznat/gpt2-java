What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Bacon Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Toast
• Bacon
• American cheese
• Canned jalapeno slices
• Red hot sauce
• Parmesan cheese
• Pepper
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Fry 8 strips bacon - soft
2. During frying put on generous portion of garlic powder and pepper
3. Toast bottoms of bread
4. Place bacon on bread
5. Tear cheese into 3 peices per sandwich
6. Place on sandwich
7. Toast tops of bread
8. Microwave bottoms with bacon and cheese for 50 seconds
9. Add jalepeno pepper slices
10. Place small to moderate amount of red hot on top
11. Sprinkle parmesan cheese on bacon and cheese
12. Place top peice of bread on sandwich
13. Now you have a sandwich
14. Eat<|endoftext|>
