Ingredients:
• Chicken breast
• Flour tortillas
• Lime
• Garlic
• Onion powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cheddar cheese
• Butter

Steps:
1. Put cubed chicken in heated frying pan sprayed with pam
2. Sprinkle with all of the seasons
3. Let cook all the way through before flipping
4. Pour lime juice over and cover turning off heat
5. Meanwhile heat up clean pan on med low
6. Lightly butter one side of tortilla cook till brown and crispy
7. Repeat with second
8. On third put chicken on tortilla while browning add cheese
9. Place already brown tortilla on top set aside on plate
10. Repeat
11. Use a pizza cutter to cut each into four wedges
12. Again this is just a base you can add what ever ingredients you like<|endoftext|>
