Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breast
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Ginger
• Eggs
• Canola oil
• Green pepper
• Red pepper
• Onion
• Oil
• Cider vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Ketchup
• Cornstarch
• Mandarin oranges

Steps:
1. Cut up chicken into 1 inch pieces
2. Combine flour, salt, pepper and ginger in a bowl
3. Dredge chicken in egg and then seasoned flour
4. In a large frying pan or wok heat canola oil and fry chicken until golden brown
5. Set chicken aside
6. In another large pan, heat 1 tbsp of oil
7. Sautee onion and peppers until slightly softened
8. Add ketchup, vinegar, brown sugar and salt
9. Blend cornstarch and mandarin orange syrup
10. Stir into sauce
11. Cook and stir until thickened
12. Add chicken to sauce
13. Stir and heat through
14. Add mandarin oranges<|endoftext|>
