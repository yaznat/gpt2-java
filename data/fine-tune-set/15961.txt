Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Whole kernel corn
• Black beans
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Diced green chilies
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Paprika
• Pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Reduced-fat cream cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Sour cream
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Lettuce
• Chopped tomato

Steps:
1. Use nonstick spray to spray the interior of 5-6 qt crockpot
2. If doing, season salt and pepper each side of the chicken
3. Lay raw chicken in bottom of crock
4. In medium size bowl, stir together the corn, beans, tomatoes, green chilis and spices
5. Pour over chicken
6. Cook on low 4 to 5 hours until chicken is tender and no longer pink
7. Pull out chicken and shred using 2 forks
8. Return chicken to crock pot and stir to combine
9. Place 8 oz of cream cheese on top of mixture and let melt about 15 minutes
10. Then stir to completely combine
11. Let the whole mixture set for another 15 minutes before serving to let the flavors meld
12. Warm tortilla
13. Add about 1 / 2 cup of chicken mixture and roll / fold up burrito
14. Top with your favorite toppings<|endoftext|>
