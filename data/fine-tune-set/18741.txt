Can you give me a pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Pudding Dessert

Ingredients:
• Vanilla instant pudding mix
• Cold water
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Whipped topping
• Vanilla wafers
• Firm bananas

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine pudding mix, water and milk
2. Beat on low speed for 2 minutes
3. Chill for 5 minutes
4. Fold in the whipped topping
5. In individual dessert dishes, layer wafers, pudding, bananas and more pudding
6. Top each with a wafer
7. Chill until serving<|endoftext|>
