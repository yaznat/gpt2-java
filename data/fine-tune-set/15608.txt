What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef For Tacos

Ingredients:
• Sirloin
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Water

Steps:
1. Place sliced beef strips in medium saucepan
2. Cut the onion and garlic in quarters
3. Add to the beef in the saucepan
4. Add salt and the water
5. Bring to a boil over medium heat
6. Simmer, uncovered, slowly for 1 - 1 1 / 2 hours until the beef is very tender
7. Serve beef with warm soft tortillas and aztec guacamole<|endoftext|>
