Ingredients:
• Almond paste
• Sugar
• Egg whites
• Almond extract
• Bitter almond oil
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 325f
2. Lightly grease two baking sheets
3. Blend the almond paste and sugar until the mixture forms fine crumbs
4. This is best done in a stand mixer
5. Add the egg whites gradually, while mixing, to make a smooth paste
6. Stir in the flavorings
7. Scoop the dough by heaping tablespoons onto the prepared pans
8. Sprinkle the cookies heavily with confectioners' sugar, then use three fingers to press an indentation into the center of each cookie
9. Bake the cookies for 20 to 25 minutes, until they're brown around the edges
10. Remove them from the oven, and let them cool right on the pan<|endoftext|>
