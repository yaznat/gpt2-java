What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>3 Step Pork Chops W Mushroom Gravy

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Oil
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Milk
• Water
• Garlic powder
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Brown pork chops in oil then place in large baking dish or roaster
2. In large bowl, mix remaining ingredients and pour over pork chops
3. Cover and bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 1 to 1 1 / 2 hrs until tender<|endoftext|>
