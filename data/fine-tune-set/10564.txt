What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Tomatoes
• Chicken stock
• Fresh parmesan cheese
• Pepper
• Basil

Steps:
1. Begin boiling water for the pasta
2. In large saucepan, add olive oil and heat
3. Add garlic and saute
4. In blender, chop tomatoes to desired consistancy and add to saute pan
5. Add pepper and basil
6. Allow 5 minutes for the tomatoes to cook
7. In the meantime, start cooking the pasta
8. Add chicken stock and cook a few minutes longer
9. When the pasta is ready, drain and add to tomato sauce
10. Serve with fresh grated parmesan
11. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
