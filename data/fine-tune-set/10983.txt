Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Almost Grilled Cheese Sandwich

Ingredients:
• White bread
• Processed cheese
• Butter

Steps:
1. Toast the bread slices
2. Spread one side of each bread slice very lightly with butter
3. Put cheese slice between buttered sides of bread
4. Heat in the microwave until cheese starts to melt
5. In my oven this takes no more than 15 to 20 seconds<|endoftext|>
