Ingredients:
• Gruyere cheese
• Chives
• Eggs
• Whole milk
• Cream cheese
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f with rack in middle
2. Butter a 2-quart shallow baking dish
3. Sprinkle gruyre and chives evenly in dish
4. Blend eggs, milk, cream cheese, and nutmeg in a blender with 1 / 2 teaspoon pepper and 1 / 4 teaspoon salt until smooth
5. Pour egg mixture over gruyre and chives in dish and bake until puffed, set, and golden, 35 to 45 minutes
6. Serve warm or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
