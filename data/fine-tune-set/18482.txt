What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Fluffy Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Light corn syrup
• Water
• Evaporated milk
• Vanilla extract
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt
2. Combine the egg yolks, milk and oil
3. Stir into dry ingredients just until moistened
4. In a small mixing bowl, beat egg whites until stiff peaks form
5. Fold into batter
6. Bake in a preheated waffle iron according to manufacturer's directions
7. Meanwhile, for syrup, combine sugar, corn syrup and water in a saucepan
8. Bring to a boil over medium heat
9. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened
10. Remove from the heat
11. Stir in the milk, vanilla and cinnamon
12. Serve with waffles<|endoftext|>
