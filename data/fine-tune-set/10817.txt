Give me a salad recipe.<|endoftext|>7 Layer Salad For One

Ingredients:
• Lettuce
• Celery
• Green pepper
• Green onion
• Frozen peas
• Light mayonnaise
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Low-fat cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Layer the lettuce, celery, onions, pepper and peas
2. Spread mayonnaise on top
3. Sprinkle with splenda, then top with cheese
4. Cover tightly and refrigerate at least 4 hours and up to 24 hours
5. Lawl count: 2 veg, 1 fat, 1 / 2 dairy<|endoftext|>
