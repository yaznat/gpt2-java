Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Margarine
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sour cream
• Egg
• Almond extract
• Cream cheese
• Raspberry preserves
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease and flour bottom and sides of 9 or 10 inch springform pan
3. Combine flour and 3 / 4 cup sugar
4. Add the butter to the sugar-flour mixture and cut with pastry blender or fork until it resembles coarse crumbs
5. Reserve 1 cup of crumb mixture
6. To remaining mixture, add baking powder, soda, salt, sour cream, one egg and almond extract
7. Blend well
8. Spread batter over bottom and up sides of pan 2 inches
9. Combine cream cheese, 1 / 4 cup sugar and 1 egg
10. Blend well
11. Pour over batter in pan
12. Carefully spread preserves over all
13. Combine reserved crumbs and sliced almonds
14. Sprinkle over top
15. Bake 55 minutes or until filling is set
16. Cool 15 minutes
17. Remove sides of pan
18. Serve warm or cool
19. Refrigerate leftovers<|endoftext|>
