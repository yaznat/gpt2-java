What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>Amish Chicken Noodle Soup

Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Noodles
• Cream of chicken soup
• Cream of celery soup
• Canned chicken breast meat
• Velveeta cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Bring broth or water to a boil add noodles and cook according to package directions, or til bite tender
2. Do not drain
3. Reduce heat to medium low
4. Add soups and stir well
5. Add chicken and cubed velveeta
6. When cheese melts taste and add salt and pepper
7. Serve<|endoftext|>
