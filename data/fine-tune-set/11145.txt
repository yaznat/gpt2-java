Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Poultry seasoning
• Kaiser rolls
• Bacon
• Provolone cheese
• Romaine leaf
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Flatten chicken and sprinkle with poultry seasoning
2. In a non-stick skillet, cook chicken for 4-5 minutes on each side or until juices run clear
3. On roll, layer the bacon, chicken and cheese
4. Broil 4-6 inches from the heat for 1-2 minutes or until cheese is melted
5. Top with romaine and mayo
6. Add additional topping to your liking<|endoftext|>
