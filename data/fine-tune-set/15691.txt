Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Water
• Taco seasoning
• Salsa
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Light sour cream
• Tortillas
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Spray a large frying with cooking spray then cook the chicken until no longer pink
2. Mix in the water, taco seasoning, & salsa, simmer for 5 minutes
3. Add in the cheese then stir until melted
4. Turn off the heat & stir in the sour cream
5. Spoon the chicken onto the tortilla shells, top with the tomato & lettuce, if using<|endoftext|>
