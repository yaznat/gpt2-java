Give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Apple N Prosciutto Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Walnuts
• Parmesan cheese
• Fresh rosemary
• Focaccia bread
• Prosciutto
• Apple
• Brie cheese

Steps:
1. In a blender, combine the oil, walnuts, parmesan cheese and rosemary
2. Cover and process until blended and nuts are finely chopped
3. With a bread knife, split focaccia into 2 horizontal layers
4. Spread rosemary mixture over cut sides of bread
5. On bottom of bread, layer prosciutto, apple, and brie
6. Replace bread top
7. Cut into quarters
8. Cook on indoor grill for 2-3 minutes or until bread is browned and cheese is melted
9. To serve, cut each wedge in half<|endoftext|>
