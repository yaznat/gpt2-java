What's a good popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Creamy Cherry Kefir Popsicles Or Smoothie Vegan Friendly

Ingredients:
• Cherries
• Kefir
• Agave
• Almond extract

Steps:
1. Place cherries and kefir into a food processor and process until cherries break down and kefir turns pink
2. Add sweetener if desired, the extract of choice, and a pinch of sea salt
3. Pulse until blended
4. Pour mixture into eight 3-ounce pop molds and freeze completely, approximately 3 hours<|endoftext|>
