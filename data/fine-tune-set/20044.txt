Can you give me a omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Persian Herb Omelette Kuku

Ingredients:
• Leeks
• Flat leaf parsley
• Cilantro
• Scallions
• Dill
• Baby spinach
• Eggs
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cumin
• Turmeric
• Ground coriander
• Coriander seed

Steps:
1. Clean and dry all the fresh herbs well
2. Chop up all the herbs (this recipe makes enough to freeze a lot of the herbs in a large tupperware for future use
3. In a medium size mixing bowl combine herbs - fill bowl almost halfway
4. Add a few shakes of each spice (be careful not to overdo the cumin
5. I go heavy on the coriander
6. Gently mix in 2 eggs with the herbs
7. Add olive oil to the bottom of a large teflon frying pan
8. Put mixture of herbs and eggs into the pan and flatten to an omelette shape
9. I like to make it very thin so it cooks very well throughout
10. Cook until brown and flip - i usually slide it onto a plate and then add a little more oil to the pan and flip the omelette from the plate to the pan to finish browning the other side
11. When browned slide kuku onto a plate add some salt if preferred and serve with pita bread
12. Good with plain yogurt too
13. Healthy and delish! enjoy! my friends love when i make this for them<|endoftext|>
