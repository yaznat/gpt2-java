Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>100 Parmesan Chicken

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Butter
• Parmesan cheese
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Dried oregano
• Parsley flakes
• Paprika
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Spray 13x9-inch baking pan with no stick cooking spray
3. Melt butter in a shallow dish
4. Mix remaining ingredients in another shallow dish
5. Dip chicken in melted butter or margarine
6. Then coat chicken with dry ingredients
7. Place in 13x9-inch baking dish
8. Bake 20-25 minutes or until juices of chicken run clear<|endoftext|>
