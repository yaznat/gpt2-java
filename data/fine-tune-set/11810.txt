Give me a curry recipe.<|endoftext|>Basic Curry

Ingredients:
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Tofu
• Curry powder
• Lemon
• Coconut milk
• Vegetable stock
• Vegetable bouillon cube

Steps:
1. Saute the onions for a few minutes until lightly browned
2. Add the garlic and ginger and fry for a few more minutes
3. Add the curry powder
4. Add the lemon juice with the stock and coconut milk
5. Add the tofu
6. Bring to the boil and then simmer gently uncovered for about 30 minutes or until the sauce has thickened
7. Serve over basmati rice<|endoftext|>
