What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Carnation Milk Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Evaporated milk
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Gelatin

Steps:
1. Beat salt, vanilla and milk at medium to fast speed until ingredients triple in bulk
2. Gradually while still beating add sugar followed by gelatine
3. Place in container and freeze for several hours stirring occasionally<|endoftext|>
