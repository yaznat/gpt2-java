Please give me a recipe for curry.<|endoftext|>Beginner S Curry

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Curry powder
• Unsweetened coconut milk
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Chicken breast
• Baby spinach
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, heat vegetable oil over medium heat
2. Add onion, garlic and salt
3. Cook, stirring, until softened, 7 minutes
4. Add curry powder and cook, stirring, 1 minute
5. Stir in coconut milk, tomatoes, and tomato paste
6. Cook, stirring occasionally, until thickened, 10 minutes
7. Add chicken and simmer until cooked through, 6 minutes
8. Turn off heat
9. Add the spinach and stir until wilted, 1 minute
10. Serve hot over rice<|endoftext|>
