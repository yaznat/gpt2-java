What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Aebleskivers Danish Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Low-fat buttermilk
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Ground cardamom
• Vanilla extract
• Unsalted butter
• Vegetable oil
• Powdered sugar
• Apple syrup

Steps:
1. Beat the 2 egg whites in medium-sized bowl until stiff, but not dry
2. Reserve
3. Combine 2 egg yolks, buttermilk, flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cardamom, vanilla, and melted butter in a different bowl, mix until smooth
4. Fold in the beaten egg whites
5. Pour about 3 / 4 teaspoon oil into each round of an aebleskiver pan and heat over medium-high heat on top of stove
6. Add a heaping tablespoon of the batter, or enough to fill each round about three quarters full
7. When bubbly around the edges, turn each round upside down with chopsticks or a fork
8. Continue cooking, turning frequently, until rounds are golden on all sides and done in the middle, about 5 minutes
9. Remove each cake as it is done and drain on paper towels
10. Keep warm and in an oven at 200 degrees until all the rounds are cooked
11. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and serve with apple syrup<|endoftext|>
