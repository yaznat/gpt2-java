Give me a recipe for pudding.<|endoftext|>Best Homemade Chocolate Pudding

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Unsweetened chocolate squares
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Egg yolks
• Vanilla
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. In small saucepan, melt chocolate in milk over medium heat to scalding
2. Do not boil
3. In a medium saucepan, combine sugar, corn starch, and salt
4. Gradually pour in hot milk mixture, stirring constantly
5. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until mixture boils
6. It's good exercise for the arms
7. Works off calories before eating!
8. After it thickens, stir two minutes longer
9. Remove from heat
10. Beat egg yolks in a small bowl
11. Add 1 tablespoon of hot mixture in yolks, stirring constantly so lumps do not form
12. Add 1 tablespoon more pudding, stirring
13. Stir yolks into the hot pudding and cook for two minutes, boiling gently, and of course, stirring
14. Remove from heat
15. Add vanilla and butter
16. Dip into bowls while still warm<|endoftext|>
