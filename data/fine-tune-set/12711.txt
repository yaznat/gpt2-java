What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>American Sirloin Steaks

Ingredients:
• Worcestershire sauce
• Vinegar
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Fresh black pepper
• Sirloin steaks

Steps:
1. In a not too deep glass dish, mix worcestershire sauce, vinegar, garlic, salt and pepper
2. Add steaks and flip them to coat well
3. Cover the dish with a plastic wrap and let marinate in the fridge 45 minutes to 1 hour
4. Set the barbecue to medium-high heat
5. Put steaks on a greased grill of the barbecue and cook about 3 minutes on each side for a rare steak or until desired cooking
6. Put steaks on a cutting board and cover with foil paper, without stretching
7. Let rest for 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
