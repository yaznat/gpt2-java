Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Oil
• Garlic
• Onion
• Soy sauce
• Eggs
• Ham
• Teriyaki sauce
• Peas

Steps:
1. In a wok or frying pan heat 2 tbsp oil
2. Add onions and garlic
3. Saut until onions are tender
4. Add rice, soy sauce, eggs, teriyaki sauce and ham
5. Let sit 10-15 minutes stirring occasionally
6. The less you stir it the more crunchiness there will be
7. Add peas, cover and let stand 5 minutes
8. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds<|endoftext|>
