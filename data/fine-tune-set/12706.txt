Can you give me a steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Amarillo Grilled Steak

Ingredients:
• Top sirloin steaks
• Vegetable oil
• Dried oregano leaves
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Ground pepper
• Orange juice
• Cider vinegar

Steps:
1. In a bowl combine all ingredients except steak
2. Put steak into a shallow dish and pour marinade over steak, turning to coat both sides
3. Cover and refrigerate overnight
4. Remove steak, discard marinade, and grill or broil until desired doneness<|endoftext|>
