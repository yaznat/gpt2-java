What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>300 Lb Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Bacon
• Mushroom
• Cheddar cheese
• Butter
• Milk
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Peel and cut potatoes and boil until tender
2. Cut up bacon into bite sized bits and fry in skillet
3. Remove bacon & set aside when it is slightly crisp
4. Saute onions in bacon fat until translucent
5. Remove & set aside
6. Saute mushrooms until slightly golden
7. Remove & set aside
8. When potatoes are tender, drain and mash
9. Add butter, milk and sour cream to mash in with potatoes
10. Add in all sauted ingredients and the cheese
11. Stir into mashed potatoes then transfer to a casserole dish
12. Bake for 45 minutes at 350 degrees
13. Throw away any diet plans and serve<|endoftext|>
