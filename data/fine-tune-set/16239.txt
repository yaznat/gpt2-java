Ingredients:
• Mild cheddar cheese
• Apple
• Low-fat plain yogurt
• Walnut pieces
• Grainy mustard
• Whole wheat tortillas

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine cheese, apple, yogurt, walnuts and mustard
2. Spoon 1 / 4 cup of mixture into the centre of each tortilla
3. Roll up and serve chilled or warm
4. To warm, place 1 tortilla on a microwave-safe plate, lined with paper towel, and heat on high power for 30 seconds<|endoftext|>
