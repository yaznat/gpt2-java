Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>30 Minute Pasta Sauce

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Red pepper flakes
• Fennel seed
• Garlic cloves
• Tomatoes
• Dried basil
• Dried marjoram
• Dried oregano
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a large skillet
2. Add the pepper, fennel and garlic and saute a couple of minutes just until the garlic is golden
3. Add the tomatoes with their juice, the herbs and salt
4. Stir to combine, bring to a good simmer, turn the heat to low and let cook, uncovered, for about 20 minutes
5. Mix with cooked pasta and serve
6. The same with the other ingredients- add the herbs you want in the amounts you want
7. You can saute 1 / 2 of an onion with the garlic if you like it<|endoftext|>
