Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Mushrooms
• Diced tomatoes
• Black beans
• Dry basil
• Salt and pepper
• Ziti pasta
• Lemon juice
• Parmesan cheese
• Scallions

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Drain and set aside in large serving bowl
3. While pasta water is heating and pasta is cooking, heat oil over medium-high heat in large non-stick skillet
4. Add mushrooms and saute til lightly browned
5. Add undrained tomatoes, beans, basil, salt and pepper
6. Mix gently
7. Cover and simmer until pasta is done cooking
8. Pour tomato and bean mixture over pasta in serving dish
9. Top with scallions, lemon juice and parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
