Ingredients:
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Butter
• Lean ground turkey
• Taco seasoning mix
• Water
• Mangoes
• Red bell pepper
• Green onions
• Fresh cilantro
• Brown sugar
• Lime zest
• Fresh spinach leaves

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 425 fahrenheit
2. Invert four 10oz custard cups onto 15x10x1" baking pan
3. Brush outside of cupse and one side of each tortilla with butter
4. Place tortillas, butter side up, over cups
5. Bake for 4-6 minutes or until lightly browned
6. In medium skillet, brown turkey until no longer pink
7. Add taco seasoning mix and water
8. Cook over low heat 3-5 minutes until thoroughly heated
9. To make salsa, in medium bowl stir together mango, red pepper, green onion, cilantro brown sugar and lime peel
10. Place taco shells on serving plates
11. Divide spinach leaves evenly into shells
12. Top with cooked turkey and salsa<|endoftext|>
