What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Chicken And Broccoli Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Dry sherry
• Rice wine vinegar
• Hoisin sauce
• Brown sugar
• Green onion
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Vegetable oil
• Broccoli florets
• Fresh mushrooms
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Fried chinese noodles

Steps:
1. Combine first 6 ingredients in a medium bowl, stirring well
2. Set aside
3. Cut chicken into 2-inch strips
4. Add chicken to marinade, toss to coat
5. Refrigerate in tightly covered container 8-24 hours
6. Pour oil around top of a preheated wok or skillet, coating sides
7. Heat on medium-high for 1 minute
8. Remove chicken from marinade
9. Place marinade in a small saucepan
10. Bring to a boil, and cook 5 minutes
11. Set aside
12. Add chicken to wok
13. Stir fry 2-3 minutes
14. Remove chicken, and set aside
15. Add broccoli to wok, and stir fry 2 minutes
16. Add mushrooms
17. Stir-fry 2 minutes or until crisp-tender
18. Return chicken to wok
19. Add marinade, stirring well
20. Combine cornstarch and water
21. Add to wok
22. Cook stirring constantly, until thickened
23. Serve chicken mixture over fried chinese noodles or hot rice<|endoftext|>
