What's a good popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Frozen Banana Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Wooden popsicle sticks
• Peanuts
• Chocolate syrup

Steps:
1. Peel bananas, cut in half and insert popsicle sticks into each half
2. Cover each banana in hard chocolate sauce
3. Roll each banana in chopped peanuts and freeze for 3 hours<|endoftext|>
