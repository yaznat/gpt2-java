Can you give me a taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef And Bean Taco Skillet

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning mix
• Water
• Corn
• Ranch style beans
• Diced green chilies
• Instant rice
• Cheddar cheese
• Tortilla chips
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Brown meat in a large skillet
2. Drain
3. Add taco seasoning, water, corn, beans and chiles to the skillet
4. Season to taste with salt
5. Bring to a boil
6. Stir in rice
7. Sprinkle with cheese
8. Cover and cook on low heat 5 minutes
9. Serve with crushed tortilla chips, sour cream and salsa, if desired<|endoftext|>
