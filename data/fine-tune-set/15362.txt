Ingredients:
• Ground cumin
• Fresh cilantro leaves
• Fresh italian parsley
• White wine vinegar
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Sea salt
• Canola oil
• Lean ground beef
• Salt
• Ground red pepper
• Hamburger buns
• Tomatoes
• Onion

Steps:
1. Cover the bottom of a small skillet with the cumin
2. Cook and stir over low heat for 2 minutes or until lightly toasted
3. Remove from heat
4. For chimichurri, combine the cumin, cilantro, parsley, vinegar, red pepper, and sea salt or coarse salt in a blender container
5. Cover and blend on lowest speed, slowly adding the oil
6. Combine ground beef, the 1 / 4 teaspoon salt, and the ground red pepper in a large mixing bowl
7. Shape into 12 patties 3-1 / 2 inches in diameter
8. Spoon about 1 tablespoon of the chimichurri mixture in the center of each of 6 of the patties
9. Place remaining patties on top and seal edges
10. Place burger patties on the lightly oiled rack of the grill directly over medium heat
11. Grill for 18 to 22 minutes or until an instant-read thermometer inserted in centers registers 160 degree f, turning once halfway through grilling
12. Brush rolls with some of the chimichurri sauce
13. Top with burgers, remaining sauce, and tomatoes and onions<|endoftext|>
