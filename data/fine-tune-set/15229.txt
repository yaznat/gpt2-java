Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Boboli Pizza Crust

Ingredients:
• Dry yeast
• Sugar
• Warm water
• Water
• Olive oil
• Flour
• Salt
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper
• Thyme

Steps:
1. In a bowl, dissolve the dry yeast in 1 / 4 cup warm water with 1 teaspoon sugar
2. Let sit for 5-7 minutes, or until foamy
3. Add in the rest of the ingredients
4. Mix well
5. Turn out on board and knead for 10 minutes
6. Place back in bowl, covered with a damp towel, and let rise for 34-40 minutes
7. Divide dough into 3 parts, and place in 3 olive oil-coated pizza pie pans
8. Dimple dough with fingers
9. On top of dough, sprinkle a very small amount of the following ingredients: kosher salt, fresh ground black pepper, chopped rosemary or thyme
10. Let dough rise 50-60 minutes
11. Bake at 350 degrees for 25 minutes
12. The baked crusts can be frozen and reheated at a later time<|endoftext|>
