Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Toasted almond
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Have ready 12 greased or paper-lined standard-size muffin cups
3. In a bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt
4. Add the almonds
5. In another bowl, combine the milk, oil, and egg
6. Add to the flour mixture just until moist
7. In a small bowl, combine 1 / 2 the batter and the cocoa powder
8. Spoon the plain batter evenly into one side of the muffin cups
9. Add the chocolate batter to the other side
10. Gently marbelize the batters
11. Sprinkle with additional almonds
12. Bake for 20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
13. Serve warm, dusted with powdered sugar<|endoftext|>
