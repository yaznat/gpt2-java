What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Beef Stroganoff Round Steak

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Bacon
• Round steak
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Sour cream
• Wide egg noodles
• Paprika
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In large pot, prepare egg noodles according to directions of package
2. Heat large skillet over medium heat
3. Add bacon and cook until lightly crispy
4. Add garlic and onion
5. Saute for 2 minutes
6. Add round steak, paprika, salt, pepper
7. Cook until meat is lightly brown on the outside
8. Do not over- cook because it could make the meat tough
9. Add cream of mushroom soup and 1 can of water
10. Mix well, bring to a boil
11. Once boiling, reduce heat to low, cover and simmer for 30 minutes
12. Remove from heat and stir in sour cream
13. Pour mixture over cooked egg noodles and serve while hot
14. I like mine with a lot of pepper<|endoftext|>
