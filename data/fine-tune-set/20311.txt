Ingredients:
• Red chilies
• Gingerroot
• Onion
• Shrimp paste
• Brown sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Beef
• Scallions
• Green beans

Steps:
1. Place first 6 ingredients into a food processor and process until finely chopped
2. Over medium-high, heat a non-stick frying pan then add the chilli paste
3. Cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring, until the mix is thick and fragrant
4. Depending on the water content of the ingredients this may take even longer
5. Now add the beef and cook for 3 more minutes, stirring the whole time
6. Add the scallions and beans
7. Cover
8. Cook for another 3 minutes, or until beef & vegetables are tender
9. Serve with steamed rice<|endoftext|>
