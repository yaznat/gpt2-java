What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>Ambrosia Salad

Ingredients:
• Mandarin oranges
• Pineapple chunks
• Miniature marshmallows
• Coconut
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. I chill all ingredients before mixing
2. Combine the fruit in a pretty glass bowl, then add the coconut and the sour cream
3. Finally add the marshmallows
4. I have also added walnuts, kiwi fruit, apples, raisins and sometimes mix the sour cream with cool whip
5. I kind of play around with it once in a while just for variety<|endoftext|>
