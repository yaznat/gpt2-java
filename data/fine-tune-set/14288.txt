Please give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Fresh Greens And Spicy Tofu Bento Bowl

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Chili-garlic sauce
• Dark sesame oil
• Tofu
• Green onion
• Nonfat yogurt
• Lemon juice
• Cooked rice
• Mixed baby greens
• Carrots
• Cucumber
• Avocado
• Toasted sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Whisk together soy sauce, chile-garlic sauce, and sesame oil in bowl
2. Heat skillet over medium heat
3. Dip tofu in soy sauce mixture
4. Cook 10 minutes, or until browned
5. Cool 10 minutes
6. Add green onions, yogurt, and lemon juice to remaining chile mixture
7. Toss with tofu
8. Mound rice in serving bowls
9. Top with greens
10. Shave carrot strips over top with vegetable peeler
11. Top with tofu, cucumber, and avocado
12. Sprinkle with sesame seeds
13. Serve with chile-soy sauce mixture<|endoftext|>
