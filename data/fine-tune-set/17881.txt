What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazingly Easy Brownies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Salt
• Egg whites
• Granulated sugar
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Canola oil
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Spray 8" square baking pan with cooking spray and set aside
3. In medium bowl, combine flour, cocoa and salt
4. Mix well
5. In large bowl, whisk together egg whites, sugar, applesauce, oil and vanilla
6. Stir in flour mixture until just blended
7. Pour batter into prepared pan
8. Bake until just set and a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean, about 20-25 min
9. Place pan on wire rack and cool at least 15 min
10. Cut brownies into squares<|endoftext|>
