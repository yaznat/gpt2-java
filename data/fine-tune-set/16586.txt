Ingredients:
• Low-fat vanilla frozen yogurt
• Nonfat milk
• Banana
• Instant coffee granules
• Ice cube

Steps:
1. In a blender, combine all ingredients
2. Cover and process for 45 to 60 seconds or until frothy
3. Pour into glasses
4. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
