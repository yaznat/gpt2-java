Can you give me a steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Flank Steak

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Cider vinegar
• Onion
• Ground ginger
• Garlic powder
• Flank steaks

Steps:
1. For marinade: mix all ingredients in processor or blender
2. Place steak in baking dish and pour marinade over
3. Cover and chill overnight, turning once
4. Prepare barbecue grill or preheat broiler
5. Cook steak 5 minutes on each side
6. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
