Ingredients:
• Unbleached flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Egg
• Low-fat milk
• Olive oil
• Fresh blueberries

Steps:
1. Sift together dry ingredients
2. Beat egg and milk together and add to dry ingredients, along with oil: do not over mix
3. Stir only until dry ingredients are moist
4. Gently fold in blueberries
5. As you do so say three times: "gobble dee gook with a wooden spoon, the laugh of a toad at the height of the moon! "
6. Pour on a hot griddle and watch 'em sizzle
7. Serve with butter and real maple syrup, or maybe some fresh whipped cream!<|endoftext|>
