Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Fabulous Wet Burritos

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Cumin
• Salt
• Pepper
• Green chili peppers
• Refried beans
• Chili without beans
• Condensed tomato soup
• Enchilada sauce
• Flour tortillas
• Lettuce
• Chopped tomato
• Mexican blend cheese
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Crumble ground beef into a skillet over medium-high heat
2. Cook and stir until evenly browned
3. Add onion, and cook until translucent
4. Drain grease, and season with garlic, cumin, salt and pepper
5. Stir in the green chilies and refried beans until well blended
6. Turn off heat, but keep warm
7. In a saucepan, combine the chili without beans, tomato soup and enchilada sauce
8. Mix well, and cook over medium heat until heated through
9. Turn off heat and keep warm
10. Place a warmed tortilla on a plate, and spoon a generous 1 / 2 cup of the ground beef mixture onto the center
11. Top with lettuce and tomato to your liking
12. Roll up tortilla over the filling
13. Spoon a generous amount of the sauce over the top, and sprinkle with cheese and green onions
14. Heat in the microwave for 30 seconds, or until cheese is melted
15. Repeat with remaining tortillas<|endoftext|>
