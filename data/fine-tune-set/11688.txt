Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Cream cheese
• Flour
• Butter flavor extract

Steps:
1. Cream butter, sugar, eggs, cream cheese and vanilla or butter extract together
2. Beat in flour
3. Bake @ 325 degrees in a greased and floured loaf pan for 90 minutes<|endoftext|>
