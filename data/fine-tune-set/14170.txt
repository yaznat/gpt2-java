Ingredients:
• Frozen broccoli florets
• Tofu
• Flour
• Skim milk
• Margarine
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Prepared mustard
• Onion flakes
• Sherry wine
• Salt and pepper
• Wheat germ
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Lightly spray a 9-inch pie pan with nonstick cooking spray
3. Cook broccoli according to package directions
4. Drain well
5. Cut into 2- to 3-inch pieces and arrange in prepared pan
6. Place tofu between towels and gently squeeze out the excess water
7. Place tofu on top of broccoli
8. Place flour in a small bowl and add a few tablespoons of milk, stirring to form a paste
9. Gradually add remaining milk, stirring
10. Melt margarine in a small saucepan over medium heat
11. Add milk mixture
12. Cook, stirring constantly, until mixture comes to a full boil
13. Remove from heat and stir in cheddar cheese, mustard, onion flakes, sherry, salt and pepper
14. Spoon sauce evenly over tofu and broccoli
15. Combine wheat germ and parmesan cheese and sprinkle over top
16. Bake, uncovered, 25 minutes
17. Let stand 5 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
