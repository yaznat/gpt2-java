Ingredients:
• Light molasses
• Butter
• Baking soda
• Milk
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Ground ginger
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Boiling water
• White sugar
• Cornstarch
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In small saucepan, heat molasses and 1 / 2 cup butter, let cool and stir in baking soda, milk and egg
2. In large bowl, sift flour, ginger, cinnamon & salt
3. Pour molasses and mix until smooth
4. Pour waffle mix onto hot waffle iron and cook until golden brown
5. Chocolate sauce
6. Combine water, 1 cup sugar, cocoa powder, cornstarch, 1 teaspoon salt
7. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until boil
8. Remove from heat and add vanilla and 2 tbs of butter
9. Stir until smooth<|endoftext|>
