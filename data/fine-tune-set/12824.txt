Give me a recipe for steak.<|endoftext|>Bbq Spicy Sirloin Steaks

Ingredients:
• Sirloin tip steaks
• Mustard powder
• Oregano
• Thyme
• Garlic
• Light soy sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Lite olive oil
• Lemon pepper
• Fresh lemon rind
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Sprinkle both sides of the steaks with mustard powder
2. Mix remaining ingredients and stir well
3. Put steaks in a shallow pyrex dish
4. Spoon half the marinade on top side of the steaks, turn steaks over and spoon remaining marinade on them
5. Refrigerate covered for at least 4 hours or overnight
6. Preheat bbq to medium-high
7. Remove steaks from marinade
8. Cook about 4 minutes on each side for medium rare
9. Put steaks on a warm platter cover with tinfoil and let them rest for 10 min in a warm place<|endoftext|>
