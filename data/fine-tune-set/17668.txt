What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple And Carrot Muffins

Ingredients:
• Walnuts
• Carrot
• Apple
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Coconut
• Egg
• Canola oil
• Vanilla
• Milk

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 350
2. Grease bottom and 1 / 4 up of muffin pan
3. In a medium bowl mix all the dry ingredients
4. Add the nuts, set aside
5. In a small bowl
6. Mix the egg, oil and vanilla
7. Add egg mixture to the dry ingredients, along with the apple and carrot
8. Stir just until blended
9. If too thick, add a little milk
10. Bake on center rack for 20-25 minutes
11. Remove to cooling rack<|endoftext|>
