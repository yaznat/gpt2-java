What's a recipe for donuts?<|endoftext|>Nutella Doughnut Or Donut Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Doughnut
• Chocolate hazelnut spread

Steps:
1. Using a serrated bread knife, cut the doughnut in half horizontally
2. Spread nutella on both cut sides
3. You can use more or less than the two tablespoons
4. Top with other half of doughnut
5. Enjoy with a tall cold glass of milk
6. You can add sliced strawberries or bananas to the sandwich
7. This makes it a little healthier, but at this point, who's counting?<|endoftext|>
