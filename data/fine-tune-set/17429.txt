Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Yellow cake mix
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Eggs
• Oil
• Water
• Vanilla
• Pecans
• Powdered sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a small bowl combine sugar and cinnamon
2. Set aside
3. Combine pudding and cake mix with eggs, oil, water and vanilla
4. Mix well
5. Grease and flour 9x13 inch pan
6. Sprinkle half of the nuts on the bottom of the pan
7. Spread half of the batter over the nuts
8. Sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon mixture
9. Add rest of batter
10. Sprinkle top with nuts
11. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 30-35 minutes, or until a wooden tooth pick comes out clean
12. Mix powdered sugar and milk
13. Drizzle glaze over cake whil hot<|endoftext|>
