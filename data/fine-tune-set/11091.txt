What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Avocados
• Salt and pepper
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Whole wheat bread

Steps:
1. Cook bacon until crisp, drain on paper towels
2. Gently toss avocados with salt and pepper
3. Combine mayonnaise and lemon juice and spread over toast
4. Top with avocado and bacon and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
