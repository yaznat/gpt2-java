Ingredients:
• Smoked beef sausage
• Eggs
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Bell pepper
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Flour tortillas
• Green chili sauce
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Pan fry the diced potatoes, salt, pepper and garlic powder in a little shortening, vegetable oil or rendered bacon fat until browned and nearly done
2. Add onions and peppers and continue to cook until potatoes are done and onions and peppers are tender
3. Remove from heat and stir in the meat and scrambled eggs
4. Cut the tortillas in half
5. Lay them in a 9 x 13 rectangular pan, placing the cut edges square to the side of the pan, over lapping as needed
6. Spread a 1 / 3 of the meat, potato and egg mixture on top of tortillas
7. Pour 1 / 2 cup of the sauce over the meat, potatoes and egg
8. Sprinkle on 1 / 2 cup of the cheese
9. Repeat layer once more
10. Add one more layer by adding tortillas and meat, potatoes and egg
11. Top with another layer of tortillas
12. Pour last cup of sauce over the tortilla covered casserole
13. Sprinkle with remaining cheese
14. Bake in 350 degree oven until hot and the cheese is melted
15. Top with lettuce and tomatoes if desired
16. Serve with sour cream<|endoftext|>
