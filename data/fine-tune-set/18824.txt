Ingredients:
• Butter
• Apple
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Salt
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add apple slices and saute for 1 minute
3. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover and cook 1 minute longer
4. Reduce heat to low, uncover and let apples cook until light brown
5. Set aside
6. Preheat oven to 325-degrees f
7. Butter 6 custard cups
8. In a bowl, whisk eggs until thick
9. Add sugar and salt and beat well
10. Blend in milk and vanilla
11. Pour custard into prepared custard cups
12. Set custard cups into large shallow baking pan
13. Add enough boiling water to pan so water comes up halfway up sides of cups
14. Bake for 1 hour or until a knife inserted in center of custard comes clean
15. Remove cups from water and let cool to room temperature
16. Top each custard with 4 overlapping apple slices
17. Refrigerate until cold or overnight
18. Sprinkle with nutmeg before serving<|endoftext|>
