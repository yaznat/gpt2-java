Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Harvest Pumpkin Spice Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Brown sugar
• Pecans
• Baking powder
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Salt
• Egg
• Milk
• Canned pumpkin
• Butter
• Pure maple syrup

Steps:
1. Preheat a waffle iron
2. In a small mixing bowl stir together flour, brown sugar, pecans, baking powder, pumpkin pie spice, and salt, set aside
3. In a medium mixing bowl whisk together egg, milk, pumpkin, and butter
4. Stir in flour mixture
5. Bake in a waffle iron according to manufactuer's directions until golden brown
6. Serve waffles immediatly with butter and maple syrup<|endoftext|>
