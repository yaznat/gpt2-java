Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazing Vegan Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Margarine
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Semisweet vegan chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven t 375
2. Mix all of the dry ingredients, excluding the chocolate chips
3. Stir in the wet ingredients
4. Add the chocolate chips
5. If the batter is too dry, add 1tbs of soymilk
6. Bake tablespoon-sized cookies on a greased cookie sheet for 7-10 minutes
7. Be sure to place the cookies about 2 apart, because they expand as they bake<|endoftext|>
