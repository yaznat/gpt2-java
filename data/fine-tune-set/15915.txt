Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Red bell pepper
• Onion
• Sliced mushrooms
• Cooked chicken
• Salsa
• Tortillas
• Sour cream
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Cut the bell pepper into thin strips
2. Slice the onion into strips as well
3. Heat the oil to medium-high in a largish pan or wok, and toss in the pepper, onion, and mushrooms
4. Fry until the onion begins colour through
5. Toss the chicken and stir it around until it's well mixed with the other ingredients
6. Then dump in the jar of salsa
7. Stir it all around again, put a lid on it, and turn the heat down to medium low
8. Leave it for about 5 minutes, stirring once or twice
9. Take the lid off
10. If it's really saucy, leave the lid off for a couple of minutes to let the excess moisture dissipate
11. Prepare the burritos by putting about a 1 / 2 cup portion of the meat mixture in the upper centre of a tortilla
12. Top with shredded cheese and a dollop of sour cream
13. Fold over left side of the tortilla over the filling, fold up the bottom, then roll towards the unfolded side<|endoftext|>
