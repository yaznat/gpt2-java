Ingredients:
• Banana
• Fat free sour cream
• Reduced-fat peanut butter
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Place sliced banana in freezer for 10 minutes or until slightly firm
2. Process all ingredients in a blender until smooth, stopping to scrape down sides
3. Serve immediately
4. Makes 2, 8 oz servings or 1, 16 oz drink<|endoftext|>
