Give me a pudding recipe.<|endoftext|>Berry Yummy Bread Pudding

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Eggs
• Fresh strawberries
• Fresh blueberries
• Fresh raspberry
• Fresh blackberries
• Milk
• Cinnamon
• Vanilla
• Sugar
• Salt
• Brown sugar
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Grease a 9x13 baking pan
3. Mix egg, milk, cinnamon, vanilla, sugar, and salt
4. Add bread and mix
5. Add berries
6. Pour in pan
7. Sprinkle with brown sugar
8. Melt butter and drizzle on top
9. Bake for 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
