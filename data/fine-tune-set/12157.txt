What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cake Jewish Pie

Ingredients:
• Plain flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Yeast
• Egg
• Butter
• Apples
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. In a bowl, mix well all the ingredient until it becomes similar to a dough
2. Butter a tin and cover the bottom with the apples
3. Spoon the dough on the top of the apples so it is all covered
4. Dust some cinnamon powder all over the top and cook for 40 min at 180c<|endoftext|>
