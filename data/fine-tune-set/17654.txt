What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Amy S Low Fat Blueberry Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• Wheat bran
• Skim milk
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Dark brown sugar
• Flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Frozen blueberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. In sperate bowl combine wheat bran and skim milk
3. Let sit 10 minutes while you prepare other ingredients
4. In large bowl, combine applesauce, egg, vanilla and brown sugar
5. In another small bowl, add flour, baking soda and powder, salt and cinnamon to taste
6. I usually add about a teaspoon or more
7. Add wheat bran mix to applesauce mix and stir well
8. Gradually add flour mix
9. Stir well
10. Add blueberries and stir gently
11. Scoop about 1 / 4 cup of mixture into a 12 muffin tin sprayed with baking spray
12. Bake for about 23 minutes or until center of muffin springs back when touched<|endoftext|>
