Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Almond-stuffed green olives

Steps:
1. With the rack in the middle position, preheat the oven to 200c
2. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper
3. On a cutting board, cut each bacon slice crosswise into 3 equal pieces
4. Roll an olive in each bacon piece, securing with a toothpick
5. Place on the baking sheet
6. Bake until the bacon is nicely browned and crisp 30 to 35 minutes
7. Drain on paper towels
8. Serve in a large bowl
9. If they are too salty, soak them in cold water for up to 24 hours<|endoftext|>
