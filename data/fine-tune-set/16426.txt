What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Chicken Veggie Cheese Wraps

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Broccoli
• Sun-dried tomatoes
• Baby spinach leaves
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Cottage cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Blue cheese
• Italian seasoning
• Black pepper
• Garlic salt
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat
3. Add broccoli and sun-dried tomatoes, and saute for 5 minutes
4. Add the baby spinach, and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes
5. Remove pan from heat
6. Add rest of ingredients, except for the tortillas and rest of oil, and stir to combine well
7. Place about 1-1 / 3 cups of the chicken mixture in each tortilla
8. Roll into a wrap / burrito, and place on a sprayed baking pan seams side down
9. Brush the wraps with the remaining olive oil
10. Bake at 350f for 25 minutes, or until the tortillas are lightly browned and crispy
11. Let stand for at least 10 minutes, because filling will be hot!<|endoftext|>
