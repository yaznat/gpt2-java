Ingredients:
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Vegan sugar
• Margarine
• Soft silken tofu
• Vegan cream cheese
• Frozen pineapple concentrate
• Oil
• Lemon juice
• Vanilla extract
• Maple syrup
• Coriander
• Cornstarch
• Water

Steps:
1. For crust:
2. Melt margarine and blend well with graham cracker cumbs and sweetener
3. Press well into bottom of spring-form pan or round cake pan
4. Bake at 350f for 10 minutes
5. Let cool
6. Filling:
7. Mash tofu with vegan cream cheese
8. Add all other ingredients to tofu mixture and whip with hand mixer until well blended and very creamy
9. Try to get all tofu lumps out
10. Pour into crust and bake at 350f for 45-50 minutes
11. Turn off oven and leave in for 15 minutes more to help set center
12. When done edges should be golden and starting to pull away from sides of pan, center should be soft set
13. Cool about an hour then refrigerate another 3 hours to set center
14. Keep refrigerated
15. Helpful hints: draining the tofu well is important
16. Too much liquid and the center won't set well
17. After slicing the cake place a paper towel in the cut out section before returning it to the frige
18. This will absorb any fluid that will condense on the plate or in the pan and keep the bottom of the cake from getting too soggy<|endoftext|>
