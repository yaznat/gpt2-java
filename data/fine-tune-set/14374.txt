Ingredients:
• Onions
• Carrots
• Celery
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Chicken broth
• Cannellini beans
• Dried sage
• Salt and pepper
• Crouton
• Bacon bits

Steps:
1. Saute onion, carrot, celery, and garlic in the olive oil in a large soup pot for about 10 minutes
2. Add broth, beans, and sage
3. Simmer 1 hour
4. Add salt and coarse ground pepper
5. Puree soup with immersion blender
6. Serve with croutons and / or bacon bits<|endoftext|>
