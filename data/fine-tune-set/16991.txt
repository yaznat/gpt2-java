Can you give me a cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Cat S Meow Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Kahlua
• Stolichnaya vodka
• Whipping cream
• Ice
• Chocolate syrup
• Maraschino cherry

Steps:
1. Pour kahlua, vodka, cream and ice into a blender
2. Blend until thick
3. Pour into a martini glass
4. Place a half of cherry in the center for the nose
5. For whiskers, use chocolate or caramel syrup<|endoftext|>
