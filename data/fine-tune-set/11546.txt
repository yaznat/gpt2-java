What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>Ma S Oatmeal Cake And Icing

Ingredients:
• Boiling water
• Quick oats
• Margarine
• Brown sugar
• Flour
• Eggs
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Baking soda
• Shredded coconut
• Vanilla
• Cream
• Nuts

Steps:
1. In a small bowl combine quick oats, margarine, and boiling water
2. Cover and allow margarine to melt, and mixture to cool a bit
3. Mix remaining cake ingredients and mix well
4. Spread into 9x13 greased and lightly floured cake pan
5. Bake at 350f for approximately 35 minutes or until done
6. For icing: mix icing ingredients until well blended
7. Spread over cooled cake and put under broiler until coconut browns<|endoftext|>
