Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Brownie Crunch

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Marshmallow creme
• Creamy peanut butter
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Rice krispies

Steps:
1. Bake brownies in a 9x13 pan
2. Cool brownies
3. Spread marshmallow creme over brownies
4. In saucepan, melt peanut butter and chocolate chips over low heat
5. Mix in cereal and spread over marshmallow layer
6. Chill in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
