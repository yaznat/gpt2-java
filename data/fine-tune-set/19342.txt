Generate a recipe for donuts.<|endoftext|>Aunt Evelyn S Easiest Donuts

Ingredients:
• Refrigerated biscuits
• Oil
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Heat oil in small pan to 350f
2. Cut biscuits with pancake syrup lid or other small circumference cup to make the holes
3. Fry donuts 3 at a time with their holes, turning when golden
4. Sift powdered sugar on top when they come out of the pan or shake in a paper bag to coat
5. Of course, this recipe may be doubled and tripled!<|endoftext|>
