What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Broccoli Brownies

Ingredients:
• Semisweet chocolate
• Puree
• Brown sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Butter
• Pure vanilla extract
• Egg whites
• Unbleached all-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Mix ingredients together
3. Put in 8x8 pan
4. Cook for 35-40 minutes
5. Cut and eat!<|endoftext|>
