Please give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Fried Tofu And Greens

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Gluten-free tamari
• Garlic powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Swiss chard
• Salt

Steps:
1. To make marinade, mix tamari, garlic powder, and cayenne in a plate
2. Slice tofu into medallions, and place in marinade
3. Let sit, turning occasionally, for a minimum of twenty minutes, and as long as you like,
4. Heat oil in pan over med-high heat
5. Carefully place marinated tofu in pan, and fry until golden, crispy edges form
6. Remove tofu
7. Pour off excess oil if you feel there's too much left in the pan
8. Remove to plate to rest, and drizzle with remaining marinade for extra spice, if desired
9. Add garlic to pan and fry until it just begins to turn golden
10. Quickly add chard and salt
11. It might be a good idea to chop the stems a little smaller than the leaves, so the cooking time is similar
12. Cook until stems are tender, about 5-10 minutes
13. Place tofu on plate and top with greens<|endoftext|>
