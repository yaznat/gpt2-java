Ingredients:
• Onion
• Yellow sweet pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Chicken broth
• Black beans
• Cooked turkey
• Solid pack pumpkin
• Tomatoes
• Dried parsley flakes
• Chili powder
• Dried oregano
• Ground cumin
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, saute the onion, yellow pepper, and garlic in oil until tender
2. Transfer to a 5 quart slow cooker
3. Stir in the remaining ingredients
4. Cover and cook on low for 4-5 hours or until heated through<|endoftext|>
