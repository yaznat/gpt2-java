Ingredients:
• Barbecued pork
• Barbecue sauce
• Fresh cilantro
• Green onions
• Flour tortillas
• Four-cheese mexican blend cheese
• Butter
• Sour cream
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Stir together first 4 ingredients
2. Spoon pork mixture evenly on 1 side of each tortilla
3. Sprinkle with cheese
4. Fold tortillas in half, pressing gently to seal
5. Spread butter on both sides of quesadillas
6. Heat a large nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium heat, and cook quesadillas 2 to 3 minutes on each side or until browned
7. Cut each quesadilla in half for main-dish servings or in quarters for appetizer servings
8. Serve with desired toppings
