Ingredients:
• Frozen french fries
• Cheese
• Garlic

Steps:
1. Put frozen chips on tray sprinkle with the garlic and herbs over chips
2. Put in oven to cook for around half an hour
3. When nearly done add grated cheese
4. Sprinkle around the fries
5. Put into the oven to melt the cheese<|endoftext|>
