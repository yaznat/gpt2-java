Please give me a recipe for a taco.<|endoftext|>Bean And Rice Tacos

Ingredients:
• Cooked brown rice
• Black beans
• Picante salsa
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh jalapeno peppers
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, saut the veggies in 2-3 tbs of cooking oil
2. I used grapeseed oil
3. Add remaining ingredients except for the tortillas
4. Bring to boil and reduce heat to simmer
5. Simmer uncovered for 20 minutes or until no longer runny
6. Meanwhile fry your tortillas in oil until soft
7. Fold and lightly sprinkle with salt
8. Place about 1 / 4 cup of bean mixture and top with your choice of toppings: shredded cabbage, cheese, avocado, cilantro, sour cream, etc<|endoftext|>
