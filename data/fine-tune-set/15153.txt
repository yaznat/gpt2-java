Ingredients:
• Prepared pizza crust
• Potatoes
• Margarine
• Garlic powder
• Dried italian seasoning
• Sour cream
• Bacon
• Green onion
• Mozzarella cheese
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. -lightly spray 14inch
2. Pizza pan
3. Place dough on pan
4. Bake at 400 for 6min
5. Or just until crust begins to brown
6. -when potatoes are cool enough cut them into 1 / 2 inch
7. Slices
8. In bowl combine margarine, garlic powder, and dried italian seasoning
9. Add potatoes and gently toss to coat
10. Spread sour cream over pizza crust
11. Top with potato mixture, then bacon, onions and both cheeses
12. -bake at 400 degrees for 17-20min
13. Or until cheese begins to brown and bubble
14. Let stand before cutting
15. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
