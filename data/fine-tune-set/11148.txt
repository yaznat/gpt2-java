Ingredients:
• Whole wheat bread
• Low-fat cream cheese
• Avocado
• Taco sauce
• Lemon juice
• Salt

Steps:
1. Spread cream cheese on both pieces of bread
2. Put avocado on top of cream cheese on one piece of bread
3. Squeeze lemon over avocado, sprinkle salt, and then drizzle with taco sauce
4. Put other piece of bread on top
5. Optional: make a bunch, cut into squares or other shapes and serve as finger sandwiches
6. Fun for cinco de mayo!<|endoftext|>
