Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Light brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Golden syrup
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Instant malted milk powder
• Instant espresso powder
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Bittersweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Place a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to 375f line cookie sheets with parchment
2. In a large bowl, cream together the butter, brown sugar, granulated sugar and golden syrup
3. Beat in the egg, vanilla, malted milk powder and espresso powder
4. Add the flour, salt, baking powder and baking soda
5. Stir until thoroughly combined
6. Stir in the chocolate chips
7. Drop the dough by rounded tablespoons 3 inches apart onto prepared cookie sheets
8. Bake for 10 to 11 minutes, until golden brown at the edges
9. The center will appear slightly undercooked
10. Transfer entire sheet of baked cookies to a wire cooling rack
11. Cool for at least 10 minutes before removing the cookies from the parchment<|endoftext|>
