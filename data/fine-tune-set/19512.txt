Can you give me a potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Alicia S Potato Soup 2 Ww Points Per Serving

Ingredients:
• Water
• Potatoes
• Celery
• Milk
• Salt
• White pepper
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Heat 1 1 / 2 cups water to boiling in 3-quart saucepan
2. Add potatoes and celery
3. Cover and heat to boiling
4. Reduce heat
5. Simmer about 20 minutes or until potatoes are tender
6. Drain liquid into blender
7. Remove 3 cups cooked vegetables from saucepan
8. Place in blender with liquid
9. Add 1 / 4 cup water
10. Cover and blend about 1 minute until smooth
11. Stir pureed vegetables back into saucepan
12. Stir in remaining ingredients
13. Heat over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until hot<|endoftext|>
