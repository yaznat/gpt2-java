Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Berrylicious Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Sugar
• Salt
• Half-and-half
• Vanilla extract
• Whipping cream
• Raspberries
• Blackberry

Steps:
1. Scald milk until bubbles form around edge
2. Remove from heat
3. Add sugar and salt, stir until dissolved
4. Stir in half and half, vanilla, whipping cream
5. Cover and refrigerate 30 minutes
6. Prior to freezing add berries
7. Freeze according to your machines directions<|endoftext|>
