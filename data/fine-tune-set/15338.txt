Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>America S Test Kitchen Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• White bread
• Worcestershire sauce
• Sea salt
• Ground pepper
• Garlic powder
• Milk

Steps:
1. Crumble bread into mixing bowl
2. Add milk 1 / 2 tbsp at a time and mash with fork until a paste-type mixture is formed
3. This may require an adjustment to the amount of milk, we mostly just eyeball it
4. Once paste is formed, add remaining ingredients and mix thoroughly
5. Form into 1 / 4 lb patties and grill or pan-fry
6. Top with cheese if desired
7. 1 tbsp worchestershire sauce can be replaced by 2 tbsp a1 steak sauce<|endoftext|>
