Please give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Another Taco Casserole

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Tomato sauce
• Mexican-style corn
• Cheese
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Brown beef in a large skillet and drain
3. Stir in taco mix, tomato sauce, and corn
4. Simmer about 5 minutes
5. Spoon mixture into a 2-quart casserole dish
6. Top with the cheese and tortilla chips
7. Bake about 5 or 10 minutes, or until cheese is melted
8. Serve with your favorite garnishes, such as chopped green onions, sour cream, etc<|endoftext|>
