What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>1 Minute Chili Cheese Burritos

Ingredients:
• Fat-free refried beans
• Hot taco sauce
• Taco cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Take one flour tortilla, put it on a microwaveable plate and spread a layer of refried beans in the middle of the tortilla leaving lots of room free on the sides for easy rolling
2. I put on about 2t, my husband maybe 3t of beans
3. Pour a strip of taco sauce long ways down the beans, about 1-2t
4. Grab a handful of cheese and put on top of the beans and sauce, about 1 / 4 cup
5. Microwave 30 seconds on high
6. Roll it up like a burrito and microwave 20-30 seconds more
7. Repeat for remaining tortillas, or for however many you can eat
8. All ingredients can stay in fridge for later
9. Enjoy while piping hot!<|endoftext|>
