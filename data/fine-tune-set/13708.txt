Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Salt
• Apple juice
• Apple butter
• Light brown sugar
• Water
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves

Steps:
1. Using an injection needle, inject apple juice into the the roast in mul
2. Sprinkle with salt
3. Place tenderloin in a 11x7-inch baking dish
4. Add remaining apple juice and let stand 30 minutes or chill 2 hours
5. Bake in preheated 350 degree oven for 15 minutes
6. Drain
7. Stir together apple butter and remaining 4 ingredients
8. Spread or brush over roast
9. Bake an additional 15-20 minutes or until thermometer inserted in thickest part registers 145 degrees
10. Let stand 15 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
