What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>4 Ingredient Sugar Cookies

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Confectioners' sugar
• Flour
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Cream together the margarine and sugar
2. Stir in vanilla and flour
3. Roll small walnut-sized pieces into balls and flatten with a fork on a cookie sheet
4. Bake at 350 until brown<|endoftext|>
