Give me a chicken recipe.<|endoftext|>Rainbow Chicken Strips

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Goldfish crackers
• Panko breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Cut chicken breasts into strips
2. Mix bread crumbs, goldfish crackers and parmesan in a large plastic bag and crush until coarse
3. Coat chicken with olive oil and roll
4. Bake at 350 for about 40 minutes<|endoftext|>
