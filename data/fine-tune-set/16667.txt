What's a recipe for a smoothie?<|endoftext|>Berry Medley Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Frozen mixed berries
• Pineapple juice
• Low-fat vanilla yogurt
• Banana
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Add the crushed ice and berries to the blender or food processor and pulse a couple of times
2. Add banana, yogurt and pineapple juice
3. Cover and blend until smooth
4. Pour into tall glasses
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
