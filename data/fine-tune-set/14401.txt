Ingredients:
• Adzuki beans
• Chia seeds
• Coconut sugar crystals
• Cocoa powder
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Vanilla extract
• Salt
• Walnuts
• Oil
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Soak adzuki beans for at least 2 hours or up to overnight
2. Drain water and replace with fresh water
3. Place on stove and bring to a boil
4. Turn off the heat and drain water
5. Add water that is 3 times the amount of beans
6. Heat and bring to a boil again
7. Reduce heat, place a lid, slightly tilted, and cook until beans are tender for about 45 60 minutes
8. If you have a pressure cooker, cook for 10 12 minutes under pressure
9. Drain water through a colander
10. Preheat the oven to 350f / 180c degrees
11. In a small bowl, place chia seeds and 6 tbsp of water
12. Leave it for at least 15 minutes
13. This chia seed gel can be used in place of eggs
14. In a food processor, place the adzuki beans, chia seed gel, sugar, cocoa powder, apple sauce, vanilla and salt and mix well
15. Transfer the brownie mixture to a bowl
16. Mix in walnuts
17. Transfer to an oiled 8-inch square pan and bake for 45 minutes
18. Cool the brownies
19. Dust powdered sugar on top and slice
20. Infuse love and serve!<|endoftext|>
