What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Ataif Arab Filled Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Active dry yeast
• Water
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Lemon juice
• Rose water
• Vegetable oil
• Walnuts
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Dissolve yeast in sugar and 1 / 2 cup of water
2. Let it froth for 10 minutes
3. Put the flour in a large bowl
4. Add the yeast mixture and remaining water gradually
5. Beat vigorously into a creamy lump-free batter
6. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and leave in a warm place for an hour
7. To make the syrup, bring the water to a boil with sugar and lemon juice
8. Simmer for 10 minutes
9. Stir in the rose water and simmer for a few more seconds
10. Allow to cool
11. Heat an oiled skillet until it is very hot
12. Reduce the heat to medium
13. Pour a half ladle of batter into the skillet, allowing it to spread
14. Fry only one side of the pancake
15. When little holes appear, lift them out and set them on a platter to be stuffed
16. Put a heaping tablespoon of stuffing in the middle of each pancake
17. Fold in half and pinch the edges tightly to allow no filling to escape
18. Deep fry a few at a time in medium hot oil about 1 inch deep
19. Remove when they turn pale brown
20. Drain on paper towels
21. Dip, while hot, in the syrup
22. Pour the remaining syrup over the top of all of them and serve either hot or cold<|endoftext|>
