Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Banana Pecan Whole Wheat Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Whole wheat flour
• Milk
• Butter
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Banana
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Beat egg until it's fluffy
2. Stir in milk and butter
3. Stir in flour, sugar, baking powder and salt
4. Melt butter in frying pan at high heat
5. Pour batter into frying pan, reduce heat to medium
6. Wait about 20 seconds, then lay slices of banana on pancake
7. Then sprinkle pecans into them
8. Flip it over to cook the other side
9. Repeat until batter is finished<|endoftext|>
