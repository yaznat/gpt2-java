What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Au Gratin Garlic Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Condensed cheddar cheese soup
• Cream cheese
• Garlic clove
• Ground nutmeg
• Pepper
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Paprika

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, heat milk over medium heat until bubbles form around side of saucepan
2. Remove from the heat
3. Add the soup, cream cheese, garlic, nutmeg and pepper
4. Stir until smooth
5. Place the potatoes and onion in a 3-quart slow cooker
6. Pour the milk mixture over the potato mixture
7. Mix well
8. Cover and cook on low for 6-7 hours or until potatoes are tender
9. Sprinkle with paprika if desired<|endoftext|>
