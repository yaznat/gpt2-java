Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Apple
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Sift together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt
2. Set aside
3. In a small bowl, beat egg whites until stiff
4. Set aside
5. In a meduim bowl, beat egg yolks well
6. Stir in milk
7. Add sifted dry ingredients to egg yolks and mix until moistened
8. Add vegetable oil
9. Fold in egg whites
10. Stir cinnamon into mixture
11. Add apples
12. Blend lightly into mixture
13. Bake in preheated waffle maker for at least 4-5 minutes, until crisp and brown<|endoftext|>
