Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Kidney beans
• Sweet corn
• Olive oil
• Roma tomatoes
• Garlic cloves
• Cooking onion
• Fresh jalapenos
• Lime, juice of
• Fresh cilantro
• Water
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a big pot
2. Add garlic, onion, and jalepeno
3. Stir until onion is soft and clear
4. Add the kidney beans and 1 / 2 the water, still stirring
5. Mash the beans up a little with a fork or a poato masher
6. Add the black beans and stir them inches if you want you can mash them up a little also
7. Keep stirring
8. Once this starts to simmer, and the corn, tomatoes, and the retained tomato juices
9. Add the rest of the water
10. If you are going to let this simmer for a long time while you clean up or make salsa, or if your tomatos weren't very juicy, you will need to add an extra cup of water
11. Add the cilantro, lime juice, and salt to taste
12. Let this simmer until it is the consistancy you want
13. It will get better with longer simmering, as long as you stir occasionally and keep it a little bit wet
14. I recomend about a 1 / 2hr, but 5 min works fine too
15. Serve with brown rice, nachos, salsa, sour cream, guacomole -- yum<|endoftext|>
