Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Cooked bacon
• Fresh mushrooms
• Hamburger buns
• Ranch salad dressing

Steps:
1. Combine ground beef, cheddar cheese, and 4 crumbled slices of the bacon into a bowl
2. Mix together lightly
3. Form the meat mixture into balls and gently flatten into patties of desired thickness
4. Lightly grease a frying pan with olive oil and place the patties into the pan
5. Cook both sides until the patties are golden brown
6. Take the cooked patties out of the pan and place on a plate with paper towels to drain the grease
7. Place the chopped mushrooms into the warm pan and fry them until they are golden brown
8. Toast your hamburger buns on medium heat in the toaster oven or place on grill
9. Then, spread ranch dressing on one side of the bun
10. Take the drained patties and place on the bun
11. Place 2 slices of the cooked bacon on top of the burger, along with the fried mushrooms
12. You can also top with additional cheese if desired<|endoftext|>
