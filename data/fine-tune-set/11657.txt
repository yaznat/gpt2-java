Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>7 Up Cake

Ingredients:
• Lemon supreme cake mix
• Pineapple instant pudding
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Carbonated lemon-lime beverage
• Margarine
• Sugar
• Crushed pineapple
• Flour
• Angel flake coconut

Steps:
1. Empty lemon supreme cake mix and instant pineapple pudding into a large mixing bowl
2. Add oil and eggs
3. Beat about 2 minutes
4. Add 7-up and mix until well blended
5. Pour into a greased and floured cake sheet pan
6. Preheat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
7. Bake 35 to minutes or until done
8. While still hot spread with icing
9. Icing:
10. Cook margarine, sugar, pineapple, flour and eggs until thick
11. Add coconut and blend well
12. Spread over cake while both are still warm<|endoftext|>
