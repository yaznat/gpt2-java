Ingredients:
• Quick oats
• Whole wheat flour
• Unbleached flour
• Sucanat
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ginger
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Clove
• Low-fat milk
• Eggs
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Olive oil
• Dried currant

Steps:
1. Stir together dry ingredients
2. Make well in center
3. Add milk, eggs, applesauce, oil, and currants and stir to combine
4. Spoon into greased muffin cups and bake at 375 degrees for 15-25 minutes, until muffins test done
5. Remove from pan and cool on rack<|endoftext|>
