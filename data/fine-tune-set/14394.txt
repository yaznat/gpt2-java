Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Green peppers
• Water
• Olive oil
• Black pepper
• Onion
• Oregano
• Garlic cloves
• Bay leaf
• Sugar
• Salt
• Vinegar
• Dry wine

Steps:
1. Wash beans and let them soak in water and half of the green pepper over night at room temperature
2. Once beans have swollen, simmer on stove until they become soft
3. Heat olive oil in pan and saut onion, garlic, and the rest of the green pepper
4. Add 1 cup of the coked beans to pan and mash well
5. Return entire mixture to pot with beans
6. Add salt, pepper, oregano, bay leaf, and sugar
7. Cover and return to boil
8. Simmer for 1 hour longer
9. Add vinegar and dry wine, cover and simmer for one hour longer
10. If beans are still too wet after this time, cook uncovered until they thicken
11. Remove bay leaf and top with olive oil prior to serving<|endoftext|>
