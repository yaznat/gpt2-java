Ingredients:
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Olive oil
• Cooked chicken
• Taco seasoning mix
• Diced tomatoes
• Black beans
• Whole kernel corn
• Diced green chilies
• Chicken broth
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Cook onion and garlic in oil in large saucepan until tender
2. Add cooked chicken, taco seasoning, tomatoes, beans, corn and chilies
3. Blend broth and cornstarch
4. Add to saucepan
5. Bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat and simmer 15 minutes
7. Stirring occasionally
8. Serve with cornbread for a hearty meal<|endoftext|>
