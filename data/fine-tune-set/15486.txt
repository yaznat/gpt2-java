Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Ground pork
• Apple
• Cajun seasoning
• Onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Corn oil
• Canadian bacon
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cook the sweet potato in a pan of lightly salted boiling water for 15-20 minutes, or until soft when pierced with a fork
2. Drain well, then mash and set aside
3. Place the ground pork in a bowl, add the mashed potato, grated apple, and cajun seasoning
4. Grate 1 of the onions and add to the pork mixture with salt and pepper to taste and the chopped cilantro
5. Mix together, then shape into 4-6 equal-size burgers
6. Cover and let chill for 1 hour
7. Slice the remaining onions
8. Heat 1 tablespoon of the oil in a skillet
9. Add the onions and cook over low heat for 10-12 minutes, stirring until soft
10. Remove the skillet from the heat and set aside
11. Wrap each burger in 2 slices of bacon
12. Preheat the barbecue
13. Lightly brush the burgers with oil, and cook over hot coals for 5-6 minutes on each side, or until cooked to personal preference
14. Serve with the fried onions<|endoftext|>
