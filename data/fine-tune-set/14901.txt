What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Egyptian Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Short-grain rice
• Oil
• Water
• Salt

Steps:
1. Rinse the rice under running water thoroughly and set aside in a colander to drain
2. Heat the oil in a heavy saucepan
3. When the oil is hot, add the rice and fry it until each grain is coated with oil
4. Add the water and salt to taste and stir
5. Cook, uncovered, over moderate heat until the water is absorbed
6. Cover, reduce heat to low, and cook for 30 minutes more
7. Spoon the top layer of rice into a bowl
8. Remove the browned layer with a spatula, break into pieces and serve alongside the rice<|endoftext|>
