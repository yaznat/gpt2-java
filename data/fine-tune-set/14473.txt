Ingredients:
• Garbanzo beans
• Artichoke hearts
• Italian salad dressing mix
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Fresh lemon juice
• Bread round

Steps:
1. In the work bowl of a food processor, combine beans and artichoke hearts
2. Pulse until finely chopped
3. Add italian dressing mix
4. Pulse to combine
5. With the food processor running, add olive oil in a slow, steady stream
6. Add lemon juice and pulse until desired consistency is reached
7. Serve with french bread rounds, cherry tomatoes, carrot sticks and asparagus spears, if desired
8. Or you can just serve with pita chips or crackers<|endoftext|>
