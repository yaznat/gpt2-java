Ingredients:
• Sushi rice
• Carrots
• Celery
• Mango
• Cucumber
• Rice vinegar
• Sugar
• Salt
• Light soy sauce
• Wasabi
• Fresh seaweed

Steps:
1. In a pot with a cover, bring 1 1 / 2 cups of water to a boil
2. Add 1 cup of sushi rice
3. Turn heat down to low, cover, and cook for 18 minutes
4. Turn off the burner, and let the rice steam in the covered pot for 5 minutes
5. Remove the cover and stir the rice vinegar, sugar and salt into the rice
6. Flake the rice with a fork, and let cool
7. Cut mango, cucumber, and celery into small squares
8. Grate carrot
9. Stir the vegetables into the cooled rice
10. In a small bowl, mix the soy sauce with the wasabi
11. Then stir the soy and wasabi mixture into the rice
12. Tear sushi seaweed paper into small pieces and sprinkle on top of the salad<|endoftext|>
