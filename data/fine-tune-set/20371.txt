What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>5 Can Casserole

Ingredients:
• Chow mein noodles
• Chicken breasts
• Evaporated milk
• Cream of chicken soup
• Chicken and rice soup
• Onion
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Drain canned chicken, then place in 1 1 / 2 quart casserole dish and break apart with a fork
2. Add the rest of the ingredients and mix together well
3. Bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 25 minutes or so until lightly browned<|endoftext|>
