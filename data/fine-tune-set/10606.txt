What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Roasted Garlic

Ingredients:
• Heads of garlic
• Cooking spray
• Dijon mustard
• Lemon juice
• Ground black pepper
• Zucchini
• Fresh mushrooms
• Olive oil
• Water
• Tomatoes
• Angel hair pasta

Steps:
1. Lightly spray each head of garlic with cooking spray
2. Wrap each garlic head separately in foil
3. Place garlic in a small baking pan
4. Bake at 400 for 45 minutes
5. Cool 10 minutes
6. Separate garlic cloves
7. Squeeze cloves to extract the pulp
8. Discard peels
9. Place garlic pulp, mustard, lemon juice and pepper in food processor or blender container
10. Cover
11. Process until pureed
12. Set aside
13. Cook and stir zucchini and mushrooms in hot oil in a large skillet on medium-high heat for 3 minutes or until zucchini is tender-crisp
14. Add the pureed garlic mixture, water and tomato
15. Mix well
16. Reduce heat to low
17. Cook until thoroughly heated, stirring frequently
18. Toss the vegetable mixture with hot cooked pasta, and serve while still warm<|endoftext|>
