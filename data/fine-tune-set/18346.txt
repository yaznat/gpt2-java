Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Poppy Seed Waffles

Ingredients:
• Unbleached flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Poppy seeds
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Banana
• Eggs
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Preheat the waffle iron
2. In a large bowl, stir together both flours, the poppy seeds, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt
3. In a medium bowl, stir together the buttermilk, banana, egg yolks, butter,
4. And vanilla extract
5. In a clean bowl, using clean beaters, beat the egg whites until stiff
6. Stir the liquid ingredients into the dry ingredients until just combined
7. Fold in the egg whites carefully, gently mix in nuts if desired
8. Grease the waffle iron and spread batter onto it
9. Cook until the iron no longer steams, about 5 minutes
10. Add favorite toppings
11. I sprinkled on powdered sugar and drizzeld strawberry syrup on the waffle
12. Serve with nuts on top, too<|endoftext|>
