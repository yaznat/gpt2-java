What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>Healthy 5 Minute Mug Cake

Ingredients:
• White flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Dark unsweetened cocoa
• Raw sugar
• Egg
• Canola oil
• Applesauce
• Dark chocolate chips
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients well in a large mug
2. Add all wet ingredients to mug and mix until batter is smooth
3. Place in microwave for approximately 3 minutes on high
4. Carefully remove mug and allow 2 minutes for the cake to cool, then flip over onto a plate and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
