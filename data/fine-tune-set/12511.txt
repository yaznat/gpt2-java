Give me a stew recipe.<|endoftext|>Amish Country Stew

Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Celery ribs
• Onions
• Tomatoes
• Water
• Minute tapioca
• Worcestershire sauce
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Ground allspice
• Dried marjoram
• Dried thyme
• Bay leaf
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Add all ingredients to slow cooker, except parsley
2. Stir to combine
3. Cook on low for 5-6 hours, without stirring
4. Add parsley just before serving
5. This can be made in the oven:
6. Preheat oven to 300
7. Combine all ingredients in a roasting pan, except parsley
8. Bake covered without stirring for 5 hours
9. Add parsley
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
