What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew Stove Top

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Stewing beef
• Onion
• Bacon grease
• Diced tomatoes with basil and oregano
• Reduced-sodium beef broth
• V8 vegetable juice
• Red wine
• Red wine vinegar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Oregano
• Basil
• Red potatoes
• Carrots
• Frozen peas
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large pot
2. Saute garlic and beef until beef is browned on all sides and some of the liquid, if any, has cooked down
3. Add onions and cook until translucent
4. You can add a little bacon grease or cooked crumbled bacon at this point if you like, for extra flavor
5. Add tomatoes, beef broth, v-8 juice, wine, red wine vinegar and seasonings
6. Cover and simmer 1 hour
7. Add potatoes, peas and carrots and simmer 1 / 2 hour to 1 hour or until vegetables are tender but not mushy
8. In a small cup dissolve cornstarch in a little cold water and stir into stew to thicken
9. Taste and reseason as necessary
10. Serve with biscuits or crusty homemade bread<|endoftext|>
