Ingredients:
• Chocolate ice cream
• Milk
• Chocolate syrup
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Place the ice cream into the blender
2. Pour in 1 / 2 cup milk
3. Pour in the chocolate syrup
4. Blend until smooth
5. Pour into a large tall glass
6. Top with canned whipped cream
7. Insert a long thick straw and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
