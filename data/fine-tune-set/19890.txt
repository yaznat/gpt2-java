Ingredients:
• Yams
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Red pepper flakes
• Lemon zest
• Fresh lemon juice
• Dried basil

Steps:
1. Put the oven rack in the middle position and preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Toss the yam fries with 1 tbsp of the olives oil, 1 / 4 tsp of the salt and the garlic
3. Arrange in one layer on a baking sheet and roast them for 20 minutes
4. Meanwhile whisk together the other tbsp of olive oil, the lemon zest, lemon juice, basil and remaning 1 / 4 tsp salt -- and red pepper flakes if using
5. Remove the yam fries from the oven, drizzle over the olive oil / lemon mixture then flip the fries over
6. You want to flip the fries at this point to help them crisp up as well as distribute the lemon / basil seasonings
7. Return to oven and cook for about another 20 minutes until done
8. I like mine to crisp up a bit so i like to turn on the broiler for the last few minutes
9. Spinkle with a little extra salt if you like<|endoftext|>
