Ingredients:
• Baked beans
• Fresh onion
• Prepared mustard
• Apple butter
• Bacon
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Combine all ingredients in a bowl
3. Mix well
4. Pour mixture into a casserole dish and bake at 375 for 30-35 minutes<|endoftext|>
