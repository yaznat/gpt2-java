What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Oat Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Butter
• Lemon juice
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Oat flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Stir together mashed bananas, lemon juice and sugar in a medium bowl
2. Beat in eggs
3. Mix remaining dry ingredients in a seperate small bowl and create a well in the center
4. Pur in the wet ingredients
5. Stir the batter until thoroughly moistened
6. Let sit for 10 minutes before using
7. Heat a nonstick griddle
8. Surface of pan is hot enough when a drop of water sputters accross it
9. Spoon batter on hot surface
10. Sprinkle pancakes with walnuts if desired
11. Let the pancakes cook 3-4 minutes on the first side or until bubbles begin to form around the edges of the cakes
12. Adjust heat as necessary
13. Flip and let them finish on the other side
14. They should be golden brown on both sides
15. Serve immediately or keep warm on a 200 degree oven<|endoftext|>
