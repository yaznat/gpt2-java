Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Poppy Seed Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Poppy seed
• Egg substitute
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Vanilla
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Combine applesauce, egg substitute, oil, and vanilla in small bowl
3. Mix well
4. In a separate bowl mix flours, brown sugar, baking powder, salt, cinnamon and poppy seeds
5. Add wet ingredients to dry ones
6. Stirring until just moistened
7. Spoon batter into greased muffin tins, filling 2 / 3 full
8. Bake at 400f for 12-15 minute
9. Serve with apple butter or your favorite homemade jam!<|endoftext|>
