Ingredients:
• Orange roughy fillets
• Asparagus spears
• Salt
• Pepper
• Green onions
• White wine
• Lemon juice
• Cornstarch
• Dried basil
• Dried thyme

Steps:
1. Wrap each fillet around 5 asparagus spears
2. Secure with toothpicks
3. Place in 13x9 inch baking dish coated with non-stick cooking spray
4. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
5. Cover and bake at 350 degrees for 15 minutes
6. Uncover and sprinkle with green onions
7. Bake, uncovered, for 12-15 minutes longer or until fish flakes easily with a fork and asparagus is crisp-tender
8. Meanwhile, in a small saucepan, combine the sauce ingredients until blended
9. Bring to a boil
10. Cook and stir for 1-2 minutes or until thickened
11. Discard toothpicks from fish
12. Serve with sauce<|endoftext|>
