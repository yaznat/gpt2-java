Ingredients:
• Small shell pasta
• Mayonnaise
• Chunky salsa
• Fresh cilantro
• Green onions
• Corn
• Black olives
• Red pepper
• Onion salt
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directons, drain and rinse with cold water, drain again
2. In a large bowl combine remaining ingredients and mix well
3. Add shells, toss and chill<|endoftext|>
