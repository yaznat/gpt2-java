What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Artichoke And Garbanzo Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Garlic clove
• Lemon juice
• Oregano
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Garbanzo beans
• Pimento stuffed olive

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix together garlic, lemon juice, oregano and pepper
2. Add other ingredients
3. Chill for several hours
4. Serve at room temperature<|endoftext|>
