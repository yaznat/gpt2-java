Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Nut Sandwich

Ingredients:
• White bread
• Creamy peanut butter
• Barbecue sauce
• Cooked bacon
• Banana

Steps:
1. Spread peanut butter on one slice of bread
2. Top with crumbled bacon followed by the sliced bananas
3. Spread bbq sauce on second slice of bread and put sauce side down to complete sandwich
4. Cut in half and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
