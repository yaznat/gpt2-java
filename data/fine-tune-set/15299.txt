Ingredients:
• Sweet onions
• Dill pickle
• Mayonnaise
• Lettuce
• Ground beef
• Cajun seasoning
• Pepper
• Tomatoes
• Sliced mushrooms
• Butter
• Cheese
• Eggs
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Mix together your sweet onion, dill pickle, mayonnaise and finely chopped lettuce and set aside
2. Fry your eggs until no longer runny and set aside
3. You do want the whole floozie experience don't you?
4. Mix together your ground beef, cajun seasoning and pepper, forming into 8 burgers
5. Fry your burgers until they are no longer pink inside
6. Set aside and place a slice of tomato on top
7. Saute your mushrooms in butter and portion out onto your burgers and immediately top with cheese and your fried egg
8. Top with the slaw toppings then declare yourself a floozie remember, this is a toplessummmopen faced burger
