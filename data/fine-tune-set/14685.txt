Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Fresh cranberries
• Apples
• Apple juice
• Cinnamon
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Place rice, cranberries, apples, juice, and cinnamon in rice cooker
2. Turn on and wait until cooker shuts itself off, stirring occasionally
