Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Fried Rice With Bacon Oriental Style

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt & pepper
• Olive oil
• Minced garlic cloves
• Green onions
• Carrot
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Chicken bouillon granule
• Water
• Cooked rice
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Trim most of the fat from bacon & chop meat into bite-sized pieces
2. Fry in large skillet until almost crisp
3. With slotted spoon, remove to a plate lined with paper towels to drain
4. Set aside
5. Leave 1 teaspoon of the bacon grease in pan but wipe out any more than that & reduce heat to medium low
6. Lightly beat eggs, milk, salt & pepper together, then add to hot pan & scramble
7. Breaking up into small, 1 / 2 inch or so, sized pieces & cook til dried but not browned
8. Remove to small bowl & set aside
9. To pan, add olive oil, garlic, 5 of the sliced onions, shredded carrot & frozen veggies
10. Stir fry for about 2 minutes
11. Sprinkle bouillon over veggies then add the 2 tablespoons water & stir to dissolve
12. To pan, add rice, soy sauce & sesame oil
13. Stir fry for about 3 minutes or until rice is evenly coated with the soy sauce
14. Stir in bacon pieces
15. Gently stir in scrambled egg
16. Garnish with last sliced green onion
17. Serve with additional soy sauce, sesame oil or chili oil<|endoftext|>
