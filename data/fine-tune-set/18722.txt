What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Coconut Chia Pudding

Ingredients:
• Skim milk
• Chia seeds
• Banana
• Unsweetened dried shredded coconut
• Vanilla extract
• Agave syrup
• Cinnamon
• Berries
• Mint leaf

Steps:
1. Place milk and chia seeds in a saucepan and heat over medium heat to a low simmer, stirring occasionally with a whisk
2. When the mixture begins to thicken, stir in all the remaining ingredients except for the garnishes and simmer for 5 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally
3. Divide into four serving dishes and cool for at least an hour in the refrigerator
4. Garnish just before serving<|endoftext|>
