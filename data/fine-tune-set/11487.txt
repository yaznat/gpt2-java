Give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>Amish Chicken Cornbread Stuffing For The Crock Pot Or Oven

Ingredients:
• Cornbread
• Bread
• Ritz crackers
• Butter
• Onion
• Celery
• Seasoning
• Salt and pepper
• Chicken broth
• Eggs
• Milk
• Baking powder
• Cooked chicken
• Dried cranberries

Steps:
1. If you're using a whole chicken cook the chicken for an hour in water to cover seasoned with your favorite seasonings
2. Remove chicken from the broth and retain both
3. Allow to cool and remove the chicken from the bones and dice
4. Put the cornbread, bread, and crackers in a large bowl
5. Saute the onions and celery in the butter
6. Pour this into the bread& cracker crumbs, add the seasoning and mix well
7. Pour enough broth into the mixture to just moisten
8. Add the eggs, milk and enough broth to make a very slushy mixture
9. Add the chicken and mix well
10. Pack this tightly into a crock pot or baking dish large enough to hold the mixture
11. For the crock pot: cover and cook on high for 4 hours
12. Reduce heat to low and cook 4 to 8 hours longer
13. For oven: cover with lid or tin foil and bake at 350 for an hour
14. Uncover and bake another 15 to 30 minutes until baked through and top is lightly browned<|endoftext|>
