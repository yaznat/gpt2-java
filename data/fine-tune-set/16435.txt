Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Peanut butter
• Banana
• Peanuts
• Mini chocolate chip

Steps:
1. Spread tortilla with peanutbutter
2. Chop peanuts
3. Sprinkle with chopped nuts and chocolate chips
4. Peel banana
5. Place on prepared tortilla
6. Roll
