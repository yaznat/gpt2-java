What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Chicken

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Carrot
• Frozen broccoli
• Garlic cloves
• Angel hair pasta
• Chicken broth
• Dried basil
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium skillet
2. Add chicken
3. Cook and stir until chicken is cooked, about 5 minutes
4. Remove from skillet
5. Drain on paper towels
6. Preheat remaining oil in same skillet
7. Add carrot, broccoli, and garlic to skillet
8. Cook and stir for 2 minutes
9. Return chicken to skillet
10. Add chicken broth, basil, and parmesan cheese
11. Simmer for 4 minutes
12. Place pasta in a large serving bowl
13. Top with chicken and vegetable mixture
14. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
