What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Blt Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Cheese
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Fry bacon and discard fat
2. Lay bacon strips on a paper towel to drain
3. Rinse the excess fat from pan, and coat the pan with cooking spray
4. Over medium heat, lay 1 trtilla in pan, and top with 2 tomato slices, 2 strips of bacon, 1 slice cheese, shredded lettuce, & 1 slice cheese
5. Lay 2nd tortilla on top
6. Cook for 2 minutes on each side, pressing tortilla with a spatula
7. Serve with salsa and sour cream, if desired
8. You may also leave the lettuce out and serve it on the side, as it can get a little wilted if it gets hot in the pan<|endoftext|>
