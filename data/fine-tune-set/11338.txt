What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>100 Semolina Bread

Ingredients:
• Active dry yeast
• Warm water
• Sugar
• Semolina
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Salt

Steps:
1. Mix the salt into the warm water and pour into the bread machine pan
2. Next, add the olive oil, flour, sugar and the yeast last
3. If necessary, add 1 / 2 teaspoon ascorbic acid to help the yeast feed
4. Set the bread machine to a 2 lb size and white light, white medium, white dark, whole wheat or french settings
5. Push start and come back in 3 1 / 3 to 4 hours
