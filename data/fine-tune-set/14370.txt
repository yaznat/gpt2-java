What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Cut green beans
• Dijon mustard
• Sugar
• Red wine vinegar
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Kidney beans
• Garbanzo beans
• Dried parsley
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In medium bowl combine mustard, vinegar and sugar
2. Whisk in olive oil
3. Add kidney beans, chick peas, and green beans
4. Add parsley, salt and pepper
5. Toss throughly
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
