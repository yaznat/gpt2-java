Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Chunky salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In large skillet, brown ground beef over medium heat 8-10 minutes or until no longer pink, stirring occasionally
2. Pour off drippings
3. Season beef with chili powder, cumin, 1 / 2 teaspoon salt and 1 / 4 teaspoon pepper
4. Stir in spinach and salsa
5. Heat through
6. Remove from heat
7. Stir in cheese
8. To serve, spoon approx 1 / 2 cup beef mixture in center of each tortilla
9. Fold bottom edge up over filling
10. Fold sides to center overlapping edges<|endoftext|>
