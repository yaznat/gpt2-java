Ingredients:
• Steak
• Bisquick
• Salt
• Worcestershire sauce
• Cornstarch
• Garlic salt
• Milk

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients adding just enough milk to make the consistency of pancake batter
2. Place in gallon size ziplock with steak pieces
3. Marinade in fridge for no less than 2 hours
4. Deep fry till golden brown<|endoftext|>
