What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Acorn Squash And Arugula Pizza

Ingredients:
• Acorn squash
• Red pepper flakes
• Olive oil
• Maple syrup
• Salt and pepper
• Gorgonzola
• Mozzarella cheese
• Arugula
• Oil
• Cracked pepper

Steps:
1. Cut squash into slices
2. Make acorn mix toss with acorn squash
3. Put flat onto baking sheet cook on 375f for 25 minute
4. Take out cool
5. Take non cooked dough roll out place in oven for 15 minutes
6. Pull out and top with both cheeses and stick back in oven for 10 minutes
7. Take out top with peeled squash and arugula
8. Drizzle oil and finish with dash of cracked pepper
9. Cut into about 8 slices and enjoy<|endoftext|>
