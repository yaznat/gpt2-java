Ingredients:
• Sweet potato
• Olive oil
• Sea salt
• Chili
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425
2. In a medium bowl, toss sweet potatoes with olive oil, salt, garlic powder and chipotle chile powder
3. Spread potatoes on a baking sheet
4. Avoid crowding so potatoes get crisp
5. Bake 15 minutes
6. Turn and bake an additional 10-15 minutes
7. Ovens may vary so keep an eye on them and be sure to cut all the potatoes the same size to ensure even cooking<|endoftext|>
