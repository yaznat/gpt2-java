Ingredients:
• Vanilla cake mix
• Ground almonds
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Milk
• Sugar
• Sliced almonds
• Whipping cream
• Pure vanilla extract
• Semisweet chocolate
• Butter

Steps:
1. Grease and flour three 9" round cake pans
2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
3. Cake:
4. Pour cake mix and ground almonds into a large bowl, add milk, oil and egg yolks
5. Beat on low speed until blended, then beat on medium speed for 2 minutes
6. Divide batter between the three prepared pans
7. Set aside
8. In a bowl, whisk egg whites until foamy, continue whisking gradually adding 1 cup of sugar until stiff peaks form
9. Gently fold in sliced almonds
10. Spread mixture evenly over the cake batter in pans
11. Bake as directed on package, approximately 25 - 30 minutes or until toothpick inserted in centre comes out clean
12. Cool 20 minutes, remove from pans
13. Cool completely before you put the filling between the layers
14. Filling and decoration:
15. Whip cream, sugar and vanilla until soft peaks form
16. Place one cake layer on serving plate
17. Spread one half of the cream on cake layer
18. Put second layer on top, repeat the process and place third layer on top
19. Melt chocolate over low heat and stir in butter
20. Drizzle cake with chocolate mixture
21. Chill before serving
22. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
