What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>California Burritos

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Sour cream
• Jalapeno pepper
• Scallion
• Olive
• Garlic
• Tortillas

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients into a paste
2. Spread paste thinly on tortillas all the way to the ends
3. Roll burritos tightly
4. Refrigerate over night
5. Cut burritos into 1 / 4 in slices and serve with salsa<|endoftext|>
