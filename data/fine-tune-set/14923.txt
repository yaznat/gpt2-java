Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Fried Rice W Chicken

Ingredients:
• White rice
• Boneless chicken
• Eggs
• Green onion
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Cook rice and leave sit to the side
2. Cut green onions into about 1 / 4 inch stalks to make up a half cup
3. Scramble eggs and leave to side
4. Cut chicken up into chunks as large or small as you like and brown them in wok
5. I prefer to use a light marinade, but cooking in oil will be fine too
6. Leave to side
7. Heat up wok and lightly coat entire wok with sesame oil
8. You can substitute vegetable oil also, but sesame oil gives it a really good flavor
9. Throw in green onions and lightly brown them making sure not to burn them
10. Once cooked, throw in rice followed by chicken and then eggs
11. Toss around to mix them all together and add small amounts of garlic salt and pepper to season
12. Add soy sauce to your liking
13. Once you put in the ingredients, this shouldn't take long to cook
14. If you leave it on for a long time, it will burn
15. I suggest using a low flame for cooking<|endoftext|>
