Ingredients:
• Vegetable stock
• Vegetable oil
• Yellow onion
• Salt
• Minced ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Ground cumin
• Ground coriander
• Cayenne pepper
• Paprika
• Red bell pepper
• Yams
• Pineapple
• Tomatoes
• Smooth peanut butter
• Fresh cilantro
• Limes, juice of
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Heat stock
2. I actually only use about 4 cups of stock, because i like my soup thick
3. In a big soup pot, heat oil
4. Saute onions with a pinch of salt for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Add garlic, ginger and spices and saut until everything's soft
6. Stir in red peppers and yams, cooking them until they start to stick to the bottom of the pot
7. Add stock, bring to a boil, and reduce to a simmer
8. Cover and simmer until yams are tender
9. Add pineapple with juice, tomatoes and peanut butter and simmer 30 minutes
10. Add stock if needed
11. Pure the soup until smooth
12. I just use my blender
13. Season to taste with more salt, pineapple juice and / or hot sauce
14. Just before serving, add chopped cilantro and fresh lime juice<|endoftext|>
