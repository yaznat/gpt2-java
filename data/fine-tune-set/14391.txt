What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>A Side Of Black Beans And Corn

Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Corn
• Ground cumin
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Taco seasoning
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Heat oil in pan on medium heat
2. Add onions, cumin powder, cooking til onions are soft
3. Add beans, corn, taco seasoning
4. Stir, lower heat to medium-low and cook til warm, mixed, stirring constantly so beans don't stick
5. Add hot sauce, stir and heat for another minute<|endoftext|>
