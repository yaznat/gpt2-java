Ingredients:
• Oil
• Onion
• Garlic
• Salt
• Ground beef
• Tomatoes
• Macaroni and cheese mix
• Milk
• Butter
• American cheese

Steps:
1. Start water boiling for pasta
2. Heat oil or butter in skillet
3. Add onions and garlic and saute
4. When wilted, add beef and seasonings
5. Cook pasta according to package directions
6. Drain and prepare with milk, butter and cheese sauce packet
7. Set aside over low heat
8. When beef is only slightly pink, add tomatoes
9. Continue cooking till meat is done
10. Stir beef mixture into mac and cheese, add extra cheese and stir until melted<|endoftext|>
