Ingredients:
• Frying chicken
• Olive oil
• Thyme
• Savory
• Basil
• Parsley
• Cherry soda
• Sherry wine

Steps:
1. Preheat grill on high
2. Remove parts from breast cavity and discard
3. Pour soda out of can into a glass
4. Then poor 1 / 2 cup of soda along with sherry wine back into the can
5. Mix olive oil, thyme, savory, basil and parsley together and rub all over chicken
6. Place the chicken on the soda can so the can is in the breast cavity right side up
7. Place the chicken & soda can in a foil pan inside the grill
8. Reduce the heat to medium and cook the chicken for 1-1 / 2 hours or until when inserting a thermometer in to the thigh meat, it reads 165 degrees f
9. Remove from grill, plus remove soda can and allow the chicken to rest for 15-20 minutes
10. The juices will be red from the cherry soda<|endoftext|>
