Ingredients:
• Honeydew melon
• Cantaloupe
• Jicama
• Honey
• Powdered ginger
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Put melons on a plate to collect juice, cut melons in 1 / 2 lengthwise
2. Clean and cut off rind of melons
3. While the melon is still on the plate, cut thin slices lengthwise so the slices are long
4. Leave the melon on the plate until ready to assemble your "sushi"
5. On a cutting board, cut thin, long julienne sticks of jicama
6. Cut the sticks 1 / 2" to 1" longer than the widest of your melon strips
7. Pour the melon juice into a small bowl and mix in the honey and ginger
8. Lie strips on cutting board, one at a time
9. Place 3-4 sticks of jicama on melon flush with one side of the melon - -this will allow a flat side when the rolled "sushi" sits up
10. Roll the melon slice around the jicama and sit up on its side
11. Trim any jicama that might hang over so it sits flat
12. Drizzle the honey sauce over the "sushi"
13. Sprinkle sesame seeds lightly over the fruit
14. You may toast the seeds if you wish a nuttier flavor<|endoftext|>
