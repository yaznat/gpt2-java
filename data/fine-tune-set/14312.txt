Can you give me a tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Gingered Garlic Tofu

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Firm tofu
• Brown sugar
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger

Steps:
1. Heat about 12mm / 1 / 2 inch of oil in saucepan
2. Add the tofu and fry until golden brown on all sides
3. Pour off the excess oil
4. Add the sugar, garlic, ginger, salt and pepper and continue to cook, turning from time to time, until the tofu well coated and sugar is just beginning to get sticky
5. Serve hot over rice, or noodles<|endoftext|>
