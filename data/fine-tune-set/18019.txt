Ingredients:
• Butter
• Cocoa
• Applesauce
• Egg whites
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking soda

Steps:
1. Melt butter and cool
2. Beat the eggs, sugar and applesauce together
3. Add flour, salt, cocoa and soda
4. Add cool butter, then vanilla
5. Throw in the nuts, if you want to
6. You can add all types of other things, like chopped white chocolate, raisins, chopped up candy bars, etc
7. Bake at 350 for 20 minutes in a 9x13
8. Dont over bake
9. Dont forget to lick your fingers and the spoon!<|endoftext|>
