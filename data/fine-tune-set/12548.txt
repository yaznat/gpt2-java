Ingredients:
• Mixed vegetables
• Vegetable oil
• Mustard seeds
• Curry leaves
• Frozen coconut
• Hot green chili pepper
• Cumin seed
• Salt
• Yogurt
• Turmeric

Steps:
1. In a small amount of water, cook vegetables until almost done
2. Grind together the coconut, chili, and cumin seeds using just a little water to accomplish this
3. You should end up with a paste
4. Add yogurt, tumeric and salt to the spice paste and mix well
5. Turn heat on the vegetables to low and add the yogurt mix to the vegetables
6. A low heat keeps the yogurt from curdling
7. If it curdles, it's ok
8. You can still eat it
9. It just won't look as nice
10. Heat the vegetable or coconut oil in a small pan
11. When hot add the mustard seeds
12. Cook until they pop
13. Add mustard seeds and the oil they were cooked in as well as the curry leaves to the vegetables
14. Serve over rice
15. If you have them, toss some spicy banana chips on top<|endoftext|>
