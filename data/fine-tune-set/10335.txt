Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground almonds
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350c
2. Mix butter, eggs, sugar & vanilla
3. Mix together flour, salt, baking soda, baking powder in a separate bowl
4. Combine wet and dry ingredients
5. Stir in chocolate chips and almonds
6. Drop golf ball sized dough onto ungreased cookie sheet
7. Bake 10 -12 minutes until just edges are light brown
8. They may not look done, but the hot sugar will bake them more as they cool<|endoftext|>
