Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable shortening
• Powdered sugar
• Frozen sliced strawberries in syrup

Steps:
1. Melt shortening over medium heat in a large non-stick cooking pan or crepe pan
2. While shortening melts, combine and whisk other ingredients
3. Make sure no lumps remain
4. Add melted shortening and mix well
5. Pour just enough to coat bottom of pan
6. Thin is good with crepes
7. Flip when the crepe slides easily around the pan and has a golden color
8. Place on plate, add strawberries and roll crepe
9. Sprinkle with powdered sugar<|endoftext|>
