Ingredients:
• Broccoli florets
• Carrots
• Soba noodles
• Olive oil
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Toasted sesame oil
• Scallions
• Chicken broth
• Cashews

Steps:
1. Bring large pot of water to a boil
2. Add broccoli, carrots, and noodles
3. Cover and cook until vegetables are crisp-tender, 3 to 5 minutes
4. Drain, transfer to medium bowl, and cover loosely with wax paper
5. Heat olive oil, ginger, and garlic in wok or large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
6. Cook, stirring, until garlic starts to turn golden, 1 to 2 minutes
7. Add chicken, soy sauce, and sesame oil
8. Stir-fry 4 to 5 minutes, or until cooked through
9. Add scallions and stir-fry 1 minute
10. Stir in noodles, veggies, and broth and heat through
11. Sprinkle each portion with 2 tablespoons of the cashews
