Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Ground chicken
• Salt
• Ground cumin
• Taco shells
• Chorizo sausages
• Scallions
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Tomatoes
• Iceberg lettuce
• Cheddar cheese
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350f
2. Heat oil in a nonstick frying pan
3. Add the chicken, salt and cumin and fry over a medium heat until the chicken is cooked through, 5 to 8 minutes
4. Stir frequently to prevent large lumps from forming
5. Meanwhile, arrange the taco shells in one layer on a large baking sheet and heat in the oven for about 8 to 10 minutes, or according to package directions
6. Add the chorizo and the scallions to the chicken mixture and stir to combine
7. Cook until just warmed through, stirring occasionally
8. Add salt and freshly ground black pepper if desired, to taste
9. To assemble the tacos, place 1 to 2 spoonfuls of the chicken mixture in the bottom of a warm taco shell
10. Top with a generous sprinkle of chopped tomato, shredded iceberg and grated cheddar cheese
11. Serve with tomato salsa and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
