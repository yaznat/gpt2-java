Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Banana Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• Egg
• Bananas
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Combine first 5 ingredients in a large bowl
2. Stir well
3. Combine buttermilk, oil, vanilla and egg in a small bowl
4. Mix well
5. Add to flour mixture, stirring until smooth
6. Fold in banana and bacon
7. Spoon about 1 / 3 c batter for each pancake onto hot greased nonstick griddle
8. Turn pancakes when tops are covered with bubbles and edges look cooked<|endoftext|>
