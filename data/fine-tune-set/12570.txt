Ingredients:
• Cooking oil
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Crushed tomatoes
• Vegetable broth
• Water
• Cannellini beans
• Oregano
• Barley
• Broccoli
• Carrots
• Red wine
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Saut the garlic and onion in the oil in a large soup pot
2. Cook for several minutes, and then add the tomatoes, vegetable broth, water, beans and oregano
3. Bring to a boil, add the barley, then reduce heat and simmer for 25 minutes or until the barley is almost cooked
4. Add the broccoli, carrots, salt and pepper
5. Simmer for an additional 15 minutes, or until the veggies are just tender
6. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
