Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Water
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Dried cranberries
• Pecans
• Tart apples
• Orange, juice and zest of
• Apple cider
• Flour
• Fresh breadcrumbs
• Salt
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Butter a stainless steel or ovenproof china bowl about 6 cups in size
3. Combine sugar and water in heavy pot on medium heat
4. Heat until sugar has melted, bring to a boil and boil for 5 to 7 minutes or until caramel turns a golden colour
5. Pour into prepared bowl, swirling to coat
6. Reserve
7. Cream together butter and brown sugar until light and fluffy
8. Beat in eggs one at a time
9. Stir in cranberries, pecans, apples, orange zest and juice and apple cider
10. In a separate bowl, combine flour, breadcrumbs, salt and cinnamon
11. Stir half of flour mixture into apple mixture until well blended then blend in remaining mixture
12. Turn into the prepared bowl and cover with foil
13. Place in roasting pan
14. Pour hot water reaching halfway up sides of the bowl
15. Bake in centre of oven for 45 minutes
16. Remove foil and bake another 45 minutes or until a cake tester comes out clean
17. Let cool slightly and turn out onto a serving plate
18. Serve warm or cold<|endoftext|>
