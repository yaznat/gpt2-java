Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Bean And Sweet Potato Burritos Light

Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Onion
• Sweet potatoes
• Chipotle peppers
• Garlic cloves
• Ground cumin
• Light vegetable oil cooking spray
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Peel the sweet potatoes with a vegetable peeler and chop them into 1 / 2 inch cubes
2. Place the sweet potatoes in a glass casserole dish with a lid and 2 tablespoons of water
3. Microwave on high with the lid on for 7 minutes or until the sweet potatoes are soft
4. Chop the onion and mince the garlic
5. Drain the can of black beans, rinsing once
6. Spray a non-stick frying pan with oil and put on medium heat
7. Add all the ingredients except the tortillas
8. Cook 2-3 minutes, stirring
9. Once the ingredients are heated through, mash with a fork until you get the consistency you want
10. You don't have to mash it all if you like chunky burritos
11. Fill the tortillas, wrap as burritos and serve<|endoftext|>
