Ingredients:
• Bananas
• All-bran cereal
• Milk
• Eggs
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. Puree bananas in blender
2. In small bowl, mix bran and milk to soften slightly
3. Add cereal mix to blender along with eggs, sugar, butter and vanilla
4. Blend and stir until well mixed
5. In large bowl, combine remaining ingredients
6. Pour in banana mixture
7. Add blueberries
8. Stir until just blended
9. Pour into 48 greased mini muffin tins
10. Bake at 350 for 15 to 20 minutes
11. Can also make 12 regular sized muffins bake for 25 to 30 minutes
12. Remove from pan, and watch them go!<|endoftext|>
