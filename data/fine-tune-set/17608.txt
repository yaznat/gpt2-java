Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>2 Ingredient Pumpkin Muffins

Ingredients:
• Spice cake mix
• Pumpkin

Steps:
1. Simply mix the pumpkin and the spice cake mix together with electric mixer until well combined
2. Spoon into paper lined small muffin / cupcake tins, 2 / 3 full
3. Bake at 350 18-22 minutes or until a knife comes out clean<|endoftext|>
