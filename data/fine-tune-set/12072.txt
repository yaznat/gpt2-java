Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Dark corn syrup
• Butter
• Lemon juice
• Lemon rind
• Pears
• Unbaked 9-inch pie shell
• Unbleached flour
• Brown sugar
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Combine the cornstarch, ginger and salt in a large bowl
2. Add the corn syrup, melted butter, lemon juice and lemon rind, stirring until smooth
3. Add the pears and toss until well coated with the corn syrup mixture
4. Arrange the mixture into the unbaked pie shell
5. Prepare the almond topping: combine the flour, brown sugar, and ginger in a bowl
6. Cut in the butter, using a pastry blender, until crumbly
7. Stir in the almonds
8. Sprinkle over the pears
9. Bake in a preheated oven at 400 degrees fahrenheit for 15 minutes, then reduce the heat to 350 degrees and bake an additional 60 minutes or until the topping and crust are golden brown
10. Cool on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
