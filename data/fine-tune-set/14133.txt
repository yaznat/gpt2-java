Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Peanut butter
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Boiling water
• Garlic cloves
• Vinegar
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Fresh ginger
• Maple syrup
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Press as much moisture out of the block of tofu as you can
2. Mix all other ingredients in a shallow baking dish
3. If your marinade ingredients are not blending well together, try warming them just a little in the microwave
4. This will soften the peanut butter a bit and they should all blend well together
5. Cut tofu into approx 1 / 4 inch slices
6. Lay the tofu slices into the marinade and carefully turn to coat all pieces evenly
7. Place into fridge to marinade for at least 1 hour
8. Bake at 180c for 25-30 minutes<|endoftext|>
