Please give me a recipe for donuts.<|endoftext|>Incredible Easy Raised Donuts

Ingredients:
• Fast rising yeast
• Water
• Sugar
• Egg
• Cinnamon
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Flour
• Cooking oil

Steps:
1. Mix yeast, water and sugar
2. Wait until bubbles form in mixture
3. Add egg, butter, cinnamon, vanilla, salt and flour
4. Mix to form dough
5. Knead dough for 1 minute
6. Roll out and cut out donuts
7. Allow to rise for 30 minutes on floured surface
8. Fry in oil until golden brown
9. Oil should be approximately 390f
10. Coat in cinnamon & sugar mixture or your favorite topping
11. These freeze very well after they have fully cooled, if there are any left<|endoftext|>
