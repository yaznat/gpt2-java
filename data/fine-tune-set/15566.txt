Can you give me a taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Egg Cheese Tacos

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt
• Bacon
• Cheese
• Salsa
• Sour cream
• Taco shell

Steps:
1. Cover a large baking sheet with sides with foil & lay out sliced bacon strips
2. Put into a cold oven & turn the heat to 400f cook about 20 minutes
3. Be careful not to overcook / overcrisp
4. Heat skillet
5. Melt butter in skillet
6. Saute onion over medium low heat until soft, brown & caramelized
7. Do not skimp on this step
8. Beat eggs with milk
9. Add salt
10. Add to onions & cook until set, occasionally scraping up from the bottom
11. Heat taco shells
12. Assemble by adding scrambled eggs to shells, top with cheese, bacon, salsa, sour cream as desired<|endoftext|>
