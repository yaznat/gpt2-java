Ingredients:
• Sushi rice
• Nori
• Dill pickle
• Smooth peanut butter

Steps:
1. Prepare sushi rice according to your favourite recipe
2. Cut the dill pickle into 4-8 wedges, depending on personal preference and the size of the pickle
3. Place one sheet of nori on a bamboo sushi rolling mat
4. Spread a thin layer of rice on the nori
5. Spread a line of peanut butter along the rice, and arrange a singe row of pickle wedges on top of the peanut butter
6. Roll, cut, and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
