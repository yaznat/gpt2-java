Ingredients:
• Almonds
• Brown sugar
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Cocoa powder
• Instant coffee powder
• Dried apricot
• Cinnamon
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Plain yogurt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Generously butter 10-inch bundt pan or 13 x 9-inch baking pan
3. Combine nuts, brown sugar, morsels, cocoa, coffee granules, apricots and cinnamon in medium bowl
4. Set aside
5. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt in medium bowl
6. Set aside
7. Beat butter in large bowl with an electric mixer until light and fluffy
8. Add granulated sugar and vanilla extract
9. Beat for 2 minutes
10. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition, until mixture is completely smooth
11. Alternately add flour mixture and yogurt
12. Beginning and ending with flour mixture
13. Mix until smooth
14. Pour half the batter into prepared pan
15. Spread half the nut mixture on top
16. Repeat layers
17. Bake for 1 hour or until wooden pick inserted near center comes out almost clean
18. Cool in pan on wire rack for 10 minutes before carefully turning cake out onto rack
19. Serve warm with powdered sugar sifted lightly over the top, if desired<|endoftext|>
