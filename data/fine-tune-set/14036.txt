Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Cumin seeds
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Chili flakes
• Balsamic vinegar
• Tomato puree
• Red onions

Steps:
1. Add the chicken to a non-metallic dish- set aside
2. Mix together all remaining ingredients, except for the onions
3. Pour the marinate over the chicken, toss well to coat
4. Marinate for 2 hours in the fridge
5. Thread the chicken onto skewers, alternating with the red onions
6. Grill or bbq for 10-15 minutes, turning frequently
7. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for about 20 minutes prior to using
8. This should stop them from buring during the cooking stage<|endoftext|>
