What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Chili N Cheddar Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Baking potatoes
• Chili powder
• Chili beans
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onion
• Salt

Steps:
1. Pierce potatoes with fork
2. Microwave on high 11-13 minutes or until tender
3. Rotating 1 / 4 turn after 6 minutes
4. Let stand 5 minutes
5. Meanwhile in large skillet, brown ground beef over medium-high heat 6-8 minutes or until no longer pink, breaking up into 3 / 4 inch crumbles
6. Pour off drippings
7. Stir in chili powder, 1 / 2 teaspoon salt, beans and 1 cup cheese
8. Cook and stir until beated through
9. Cut a slit lengthwise in potatoes
10. Squeeze ends toward center to fluff
11. Season potatoes with salt and pepper if desired
12. Spoon equal amounts of beef mixture over each potato
13. Top with remaining cheese and green onions
14. You can also add sour cream on top<|endoftext|>
