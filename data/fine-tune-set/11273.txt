Please give me a recipe for sushi.<|endoftext|>Sushi Love Vegan

Ingredients:
• Sushi rice
• Water
• Rice vinegar
• Sugar
• Nori
• Avocado
• Carrot
• Green onion
• Firm tofu
• Vegan cream cheese
• Black sesame seed
• Soy sauce
• Pickled ginger
• Wasabi
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. You will also need: bamboo rolling mat
2. Saran wrap for inside out roll
3. Serrated knife
4. In a pot, bring sushi rice and water to a boil then lower heat and cook for about 25 minute
5. Transfer rice to a glass bowl
6. Add vinegar and sugar and let cool down to room temperature
7. Keep your hands wet with water vinegar mixture to keep rice from sticking to your hands
8. Place 1 nori sheet on bamboo and spread out about 1 handful sushi rice, covering 3 / 4 of the nori sheet, leaving an ucovered"tab"
9. Uncovered"tab" should be away from you
10. Place fillings in any order and combination you prefer
11. Gently roll sushi
12. Slice roll into 8 pieces using a serrated knife
13. For an inside-out roll do step 1-5
14. Place saran wrap over rice and flip over with the uncover"tab" towards you
15. Place fillings in any order and combination you prefer
16. Gently roll sushi making sure you don't get the saran wrap caught in your roll, it's a bit tricky
17. Sprinkle black sesame seeds
18. Slice roll into 8 pieces using a serrated knife<|endoftext|>
