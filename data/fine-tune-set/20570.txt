What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Apple And Cheese Casserole

Ingredients:
• Frozen harvest apples
• Sugar
• Flour
• Cheddar cheese
• Butter
• Vanilla wafer
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. Mix sugar and flour in a small bowl
2. Slowly stir in orange juice
3. Mix melted butter and vanilla wafer crumbs in a separate small bowl
4. Place apples in a greased 1 quart baking dish
5. Layer sugar mixture, cheese and vanilla wafer crumb mixture over apples
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 20-30 minutes or until top is browned<|endoftext|>
