What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Basil Garlic Oven Fries

Ingredients:
• Floury potatoes
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh basil
• Lemon, juice of
• Black peppercorns
• Salt
• Virgin olive oil
• Oil

Steps:
1. Place garlic, salt, peppercorns and lemon juice in pestle and mortar and blend well into a paste, pepper should be like cracked pepper
2. Blend olive oil and basil well in a liquidiser
3. Mix paste and basil oil together in a large bowl
4. Add potatoes to this mix, turn until well coated and leave in mixture until needed
5. When ready to cook, grease a heavy baking tray with olive oil and
6. Cook in preheated oven mark 180 degrees c for approx 50 mins or until cooked through and edges crispy<|endoftext|>
