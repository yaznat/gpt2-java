Ingredients:
• Shrimp
• Bacon
• Rum

Steps:
1. Peel and deveine the shrimp
2. Warm the rum in a small saucepan over low heat
3. Wrap each shrimp in a narrow strip of bacon and fasten with a toothpick
4. Place shrimp in a shallow pan and bake at 450 degrees for about 10 minutes, turning often, until bacon is crispy
5. Put the shrimp in a chafing dish or ceramic dish
6. Pour rum over shrimp and ignite
7. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
