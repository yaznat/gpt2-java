What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Cheesy Taco Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Velveeta cheese
• Salsa
• Red beans
• Stewed tomatoes
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Chili powder
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan or dutch oven, cook beef and onion over medium heat until meat is no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Stir in the cheese, salsa, beans, tomatoes and chili powder
4. Cook for 10 minutes or until cheese is melted
5. Remove from the heat
6. Stir in sour cream
7. Yield: 10 servings<|endoftext|>
