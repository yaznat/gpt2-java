Ingredients:
• Fusilli
• Frozen baby peas
• Black olives
• Genoa salami
• Green onion
• Celery
• Fresh parsley
• Italian salad dressing mix
• Mayonnaise
• Sour cream
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a large pot of salted boiling water, cook pasta until al dente, rinse under cold water and drain
2. In a medium bowl combine mayonnaise, sour cream, milk and italian dressing mix
3. Whisk together until smooth, set aside
4. In a large salad bowl combine cooked and cooled pasta, peas, olives, salami, green onions, celery and parsley
5. Mix in dressing last, reserving 1 / 2 cup
6. Let sit over night in fridge
7. Stir before serving
8. Add extra dressing if pasta appears dry<|endoftext|>
