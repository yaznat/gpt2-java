Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Apple Nut Pancakes Vegan

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• White sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Allspice
• Vanilla extract
• Apple cider
• Soymilk
• Pecans
• Granny smith apple

Steps:
1. Stir together dry ingredients in a large bowl
2. Make a well in the center and add vanilla, cider, and soy milk, stirring just until blended
3. Stir in nuts and apples
4. Heat a large skillet over medium heat and add oil or cooking spray
5. When hot, pour 1 / 3 cup batter on skillet
6. Cook until bubble appear and then flip
7. Serve warm with maple syrup or honey<|endoftext|>
