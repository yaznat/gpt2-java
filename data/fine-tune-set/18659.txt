Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Evaporated milk
• Water
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Cranberries
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. To make the pudding: preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. In a medium bowl, cream butter and sugar
3. Add evaporated milk and water
4. Using a spoon, stir in flour, salt, baking soda, and cranberries
5. Line 8 sections of muffin pan with paper liners
6. Spoon batter into muffin pan
7. Bake at 325 degrees for 30 minutes
8. To make the butter sauce: put butter, sugar, evaporated milk, and water in a medium sized sauce pan
9. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly, about 2 minutes
10. Remove pan from heat
11. Add vanilla
12. Pour warm sauce over warm muffins<|endoftext|>
