Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt
• Pepper
• Frozen hash browns
• Ham
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Beat eggs in a bowl
2. Add milk salt and pepper
3. Stir in remaining ingredients till everything is moistened
4. Pour into greased 9x9-inch baking dish
5. Bake at 350 for 45 minutes or until set
6. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
