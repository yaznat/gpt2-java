Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Better Than Cash Brownies

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Cocoa
• Instant coffee
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Pure vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Lightly grease an eight-inch baking pan and set aside
3. Place the butter chunks in a glass mixing bowl and microwave on medium power until melted
4. Put the cocoa, salt and instant coffee into a sieve and sift mixture into the melted butter -- you may need to use your fingers to force the coffee granules through the sieve -- and whisk until smooth
5. Next, add in the sugar and whisk well
6. Then add the eggs, one at a time, whisking after each addition just until blended
7. Whisk in vanilla
8. Using a sieve, sift flour over chocolate mixture, then stir with a wooden spoon just to incorporate flour
9. Batter will be thick
10. Spread batter into prepared pan, smoothing top, and scatter a topping of your choice, if desired, over top
11. Twenty pecan halves or walnut halves, evenly spaced out, looks great and suits those who don't like nuts in brownies, as they can easily be picked off
12. Bake in preheated oven for 30-32 minutes
13. A tester should come out with some crumbs on it
14. Do not overbake or brownies will not be fudgey
15. Let cool, in pan, then cut into small squares
16. Expect these brownies to not cut cleanly, some bits will cling to the knife<|endoftext|>
