What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Sesame Noodle Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Red wine vinegar
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Low-sugar low-sodium ketchup
• Orange juice
• Clear honey
• Cornflour
• Canola oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Spring onions
• Baby corn
• Carrot
• Red pepper
• Christophene
• Chow mein noodles
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Mix vinegar, soy sauce, tomato ketchup, orange juice and honey together
2. Add the cornflour and stir until well combined
3. Heat the oil in a non-stick frying pan and stir fry the chicken strips for 3-4 minutes
4. Add the vegetables and stir fry for 4-5 minutes
5. Add the cornflour mixture and bring to a boil
6. Meanwhile prepare the noodles according to the packet instructions, drain and add to the pan along with the sesame seeds
7. Mix well<|endoftext|>
