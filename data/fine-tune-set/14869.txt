What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Chinese Fried Rice The Real Deal

Ingredients:
• Jasmine rice
• Wok oil
• Ginger
• White onions
• Chicken stock
• Sugar
• Sesame oil
• Eggs
• Frozen peas and carrot
• Salt
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Prepare the jasmine rice
2. Combine 4 cups of jasmine rice and 4 cups of water in a rice cooker
3. Once the rice is cooled place in the refrigerator until chilled
4. Scramble eggs in seperate pan using whatever oil you normally cook eggs with
5. Make sure to fold the eggs over, so they can be cut into thin strips
6. Heat wok and 3-4 tablespoons of wok oil to medium heat
7. Saute the white onion until soft and partially transparent along with 2 tablespoons of soy sauce, frozen peas and / or carrots
8. If you opted for green onions skip this step and saute the peas / carrots in wok oil
9. Add the rice making sure to seperate the grains and mix with the vegetables
10. Add another 3 tablespoons of wok oil, sesame oil, egg strips, ginger, chicken stock, and sugar
11. Raise the heat to high add soy sauce to desired darkness / taste and stir-fry
12. It is crucial for the stove to be as hot as possible
13. Basically you are going to stir-fry till all the liquids are dissolved and the fried rice has reached the dryness desired
14. While cooling salt to taste in order to enhance the flavor<|endoftext|>
