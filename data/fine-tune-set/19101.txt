What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Honey And Walnut Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Honey
• Egg yolks
• Whipping cream
• Bananas
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Combine milk and honey in medium saucepan
2. Bring almost to simmer, stirring occasionally
3. Beat yolks until pale yellow
4. Gradually beat in hot milk
5. Set bowl into a larger bowl filled with ice
6. Cool custard completely, stirring occasionally
7. Fold in whipped cream and bananas
8. Process according to ice cream makers manufacturers instructions, until half set
9. Add walnuts and process until set
10. Let ripen in freezer several hours before serving<|endoftext|>
