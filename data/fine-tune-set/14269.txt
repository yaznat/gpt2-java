Can you give me a tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Chocolate Fudge Tofu Pie

Ingredients:
• Firm silken tofu
• Honey
• Vanilla
• Cocoa powder
• Chocolate graham wafer pie crust

Steps:
1. Drain as much liquid as possible from tofu by putting paper towels on top& bottom of tofu, wrapping a clean dish towel around that, and setting a weight on top of it[a large pan usually works well]
2. Drain for 30 minutes
3. In food processor[or blender], whip tofu until smooth, for several minutes
4. Add honey, cocoa powder, & vanilla
5. Blend for at least 1 minute
6. Be sure to use spatula on sides
7. Pour into pie crust& chill for 2 hours
8. Garnish with bananas or raspberries, if desired<|endoftext|>
