Ingredients:
• Reduced-fat vanilla wafers
• Bananas
• Fat free cream cheese
• Fat-free sweetened condensed milk
• Sugar free fat free french vanilla pudding and pie filling mix
• 1% low-fat milk
• Fat-free whipped topping

Steps:
1. Line a 9 x 13 glass dish with nilla wafers
2. Top them with 3 / 4 the sliced bananas
3. In a bowl mix the pudding and milk with a mixer until smooth and creamy, but not too thick, to taste
4. In a separate bowl, mix cream cheese and condensed milk with mixer until smooth
5. Fold whipped topping into pudding mixture
6. Mix cream cheese mixture into pudding mixture
7. If you left any bananas, mix them into the final mixture and pour over the bananas in the dish
8. Refrigerate overnight for best results<|endoftext|>
