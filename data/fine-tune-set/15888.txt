Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breast halves
• Pasta sauce
• Canned black beans
• Salsa
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. In one small bowl, toss chicken strips with pasta sauce and let marinate 10 minute
2. In another small bowl, combine beans and salsa
3. Place half chicken on half of one tortilla
4. Spread half the beans and salsa over chicken
5. Sprinkle with half the cheese
6. Repeat with second tortilla
7. Roll tortillas up and place, seam side down, in a baking dish
8. Bake at 375f 5 minutes
9. Top with a dollop of sour cream and serve<|endoftext|>
