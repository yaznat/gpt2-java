Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Rice Pudding For A Rice Cooker

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Short-grain white rice
• Water
• Salt
• Raisins
• Nutmeg
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Butter
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Put the rice, salt, 3 cups of boiling water, raisins, apples, and nutmeg
2. In the rice maker
3. Tell the rice cooker to 'cook'
4. When the rice cooker says it is done or switches to 'warm', stir in the condensed milk, vanilla, and butter
5. Tell the rice cooker to 'cook' again
6. When it switches to 'warm' again or says it is done cooking, evaluate
7. If there is too much liquid in your rice pudding for your preference, you can tell the cooker to 'cook' again
8. Garnish with cinnamon<|endoftext|>
