Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Steak fillets
• Red capsicums
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red chilies
• Balsamic vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Corn cobs
• Red potatoes
• Snow peas
• Asparagus spears
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Roast capsicum skin side up, under grill, until skin blisters and blackens
2. Stand for 5 min's to cool, peel away skin and slice thinly
3. Heat a little olive oil in a pan, cook onion and garlic, stirring until soft
4. Add chillies, vinegar, sugar and capsicum, cook, stirring about 8 min's
5. Meanwhile cook steaks, on an oiled grill plate, or grill or bbq, until browned on both sides and cooked as desired
6. Boil, steam or microwave vegetables, until just tender
7. You can either do the same with potato and just boil or do as i did, which was to only par boil them, drain, sprinkle with paprika and garlic powder and finally roast in pre-heated oven of 180 degrees celsius, until browned and crisp, so take your pick
8. To serve: place vegetables on plate, place steaks on veg and top each steak with capsicum relish, sprinkle with parsley<|endoftext|>
