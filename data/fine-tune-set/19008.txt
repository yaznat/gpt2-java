Please give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Grammie Bea S Old Fashioned Cornflake Custard Pudding

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Sugar
• Corn flakes

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients in a glass baking dish
2. Preheat oven at 375, and then turn down to 350 when you put the casserole dish in the oven
3. Cook 25-35 minutes or until top is golden brown
4. Serve hot or cold with whipped cream<|endoftext|>
