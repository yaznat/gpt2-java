Ingredients:
• Corned beef
• Water
• Apple juice
• Soy sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Vinegar
• Ground mustard
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Place whole pieces of corned beef in steam-jacketed kettle or stock pot
2. Cover with water
3. Bring to a boil
4. Cover, reduce heat and simmer about 5 hours
5. Internal temperature of corned beef should read 180 to 200 degrees f
6. During cooking period, remove scum that rises to the surface
7. Remove cooked corned beef from juice and place in roasting pans
8. Combine apple juice, soy sauce, worcestershire sauce, vinegar, mustard and brown sugar
9. Pour equal amounts over meat in roasting pans
10. Place in 350f oven and bake 1 hour, basting every 15 minutes
11. After 3 hours cooking, test each piece of meat with a fork to determine the tenderness
