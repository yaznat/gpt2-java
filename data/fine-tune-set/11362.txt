Give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>3 Cheese Pepper Bread Bread Machine

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Egg
• Buttermilk
• Bread flour
• Salt
• Fast rising yeast
• Sugar
• Habanero pepper cheese
• Fresh chives
• Chili pepper flakes
• Parmesan-romano cheese mix

Steps:
1. Place wet ingredients in bread machine cover with flour
2. In one corner place salt the other sugar and then the yeast in an other
3. Set to dough cycle
4. Add the cheese and chives at beep cycle
5. On a flour surface punch down dough and form into a mound
6. Brush with egg wash
7. Sprinkle with pepper flakes and grated cheese
8. Score top and cover with oiled plastic wrap
9. Let rise for 30 minutes
10. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
11. Bake for 30 minutes
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
