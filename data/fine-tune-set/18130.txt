What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Apple Crisp Oven Pancake

Ingredients:
• Oats
• Bisquick
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Cinnamon
• Milk
• Chunky applesauce
• Vanilla
• Egg

Steps:
1. Mix oats, 1 / 4 cup bisquick, brown sugar, cinnamon, and butter
2. Set aside
3. Mix remaining ingredients until well blended
4. Pour into greased jelly roll pan
5. Spread batter to edges
6. Sprinkle oats mixture over batter
7. Bake at 425f for 14-16 minutes or until light golden brown<|endoftext|>
