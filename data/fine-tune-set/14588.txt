Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Chili And Rice Wraps

Ingredients:
• Chili with beans
• Water
• White minute rice
• Flour tortillas
• Cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Bring chili and water to boil in medium saucepan
2. Stir in rice
3. Cover
4. Cook over low heat for 5 minutes
5. Spoon approx 1 / 2 cup chili mixture onto each tortilla
6. Top with cheese and sour cream, and fold up<|endoftext|>
