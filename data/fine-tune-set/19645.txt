Can you give me a potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus And Potato Tart Jamie Oliver

Ingredients:
• Fresh asparagus
• Phyllo pastry sheets
• Butter
• Potato
• White cheddar cheese
• Lancashire cheese
• Nutmeg
• Heavy cream
• Eggs
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Also, set a large pot of salted water to boil
3. Trim asparagus spears to fit into a rectangular tart pan
4. Toss trimmed asparagus into boiling salted water for about 2 minutes to blanch
5. Boil potatoes for 12 minutes in salted water
6. Drain
7. While potatoes are boiling, layer phyllo into tart pan, leaving about 1" of dough overhanging the edge of the pan
8. Butter each sheet as it goes into the pan
9. Mash potatoes with cheddar and lancashire cheeses
10. Add salt and pepper to taste
11. Add nutmeg and cream
12. Add eggs and mix well
13. Pour into tart pan
14. Lay asparagus spears on top, alternating directions of
15. Brush top lightly with any remaining melted butter
16. Crimp overhanging phyllo up along edge of tart pan
17. Put pan on the lowest shelf of the oven for 15-20 minutes
18. Remove pan from oven and allow it to set for at least 15 minutes
19. Serve with a light salad of fresh spring greens<|endoftext|>
