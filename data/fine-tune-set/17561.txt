Ingredients:
• Rhubarb
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Unbleached flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Buttermilk
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Place rhubarb in a bowl and sprinkle with sugar
3. Let stand while preparing the batter
4. Spray an 8-inch baking pan with nonstick spray
5. Cream together the butter and sugar in an electric mixer, until fluffy
6. Add the eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition
7. Stir in the vanilla
8. In another bowl, mix together the dry ingredients until uniformly blended
9. Add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture, alternating with the buttermilk
10. Stir thoroughly, but minimally, until combined, don't overmix
11. Fold in the rhubarb and transfer the batter to the prepared pan
12. The batter will be stiff and you'll have to work with it a little, spreading it in the pan
13. Don't worry if it doesn't look completely uniform in the pan
14. Mix together the brown sugar and cinnamon and sprinkle on top of the batter
15. Bake 25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted all the way into the center comes out clean
16. Serve warm or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
