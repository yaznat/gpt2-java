Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Backpacker S Beef Jerky

Ingredients:
• Beef
• Black pepper
• Steak spice
• Chili powder
• Brown sugar
• Garlic
• Worcestershire sauce
• Soya sauce
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients into a large ziploc bag, shake well to mix dry spices into liquids
2. Add beef strips
3. Refrigerate for a minimum of 2hrs but best if left for up to 12hrs
4. Pat dry with cloth or paper towel and place into dehydrator for roughly 8-12hrs<|endoftext|>
