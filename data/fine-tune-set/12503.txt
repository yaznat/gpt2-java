Give me a stew recipe.<|endoftext|>All Pumpkins Night Stew

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Garlic clove
• Onions
• Beef stew meat
• Salt
• Pepper
• Caraway seeds
• All-purpose flour
• Beef broth
• Bay leaves
• White sugar
• Nutmeg
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tablespoon canola oil in a dutch oven over medium- high heat
2. Stir in garlic and onion, and cook until softened and translucent
3. Remove from pan
4. Pour 2 tablespoons canola oil into dutch oven
5. While this heats, mix the beef with salt and pepper
6. Sear the beef in batches until browned all over, about 5 minutes
7. Stir in caraway seeds and flour, cook until flour is incorporated and the caraway seed is fragrant, about 1 minute
8. Pour in beef broth
9. Add bay leaves, sugar, nutmeg, and onion mixture
10. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer until the meat is tender, about 2 hours
11. Stir in the potatoes and carrots
12. Continue cooking until the vegetables are tender, about 30 minutes
13. Stir together the cornstarch and water
14. Stir into the stew along with the parsley
15. Simmer until thickened, about 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
