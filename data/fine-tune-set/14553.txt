Ingredients:
• Hamburger
• Bacon
• Onion
• Liquid smoke
• White vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Pork and beans
• Red kidney beans
• Small white beans
• Ketchup
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Brown hamburger and onion
2. Drain
3. Dice bacon and brown
4. Drain
5. Meanwhile, mix remaining ingredients in a very large bowl
6. Stir in bacon and hamburger and mix well
7. Pour into an 11x15 pan
8. Cover with foil and bake at 325 for 2 hours<|endoftext|>
