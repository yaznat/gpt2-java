Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Olive oil
• Jamaican jerk spice
• Mango
• Red bell pepper
• Orange juice
• Cornstarch
• Hot pepper sauce
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Trim fat from meat and cut into thin strips
2. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok until very hot
3. Add pork and seasoning blend
4. Stir-fry 2 minutes
5. Add mango and bell pepper
6. Stir-fry 2 minutes or just until pork loses its pink color
7. Mix together orange juice, cornstarch and pepper sauce until smooth
8. Add to pork mixture in the skillet or wok
9. Cook and stir 2 minutes or until sauce is clear and thick
10. Stir in green onions<|endoftext|>
