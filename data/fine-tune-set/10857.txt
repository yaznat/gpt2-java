What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>A Summer Chopped Salad

Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Radishes
• Agave nectar
• English cucumber
• Sweet corn
• Avocado
• Baby arugula
• Shallot
• Fresh basil
• Fresh oregano
• Fresh lemon juice
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Sea salt
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Blanch the green beans in boiling water for 30-60 seconds, then chill in an ice water bath
2. In the same boiling water, blanch the radishes 20 seconds, then chill in an ice water bath sweetened with a touch of agave nectar
3. Place all of the ingredients except for the vinaigrette and lemon juice in a large bowl
4. Drizzle with the vinaigrette and toss to coat
5. Sprinkle the lemon juice on top just before serving
6. Enjoy!
7. Vinaigrette:
8. Place the vinegar and agave nectar in a small bowl, then whisk constantly while slowly pouring in the oil in a thin stream
9. Season with salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
