Ingredients:
• Baked beans
• Fennel seeds
• Fresh coriander
• Butter
• Cayenne pepper
• Turmeric
• Tandoori masala
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Gingerroot
• Whole green chilies

Steps:
1. Heat the oil / butter / ghee
2. Once hot, chop and add the garlic, ginger, onions and green chillis
3. Stir fry for around 2 minutes on medium heat
4. Do not let it stick to the pan, add a little water if needed
5. Add fennel seeds stir fry for a further 5 minutes or until the onion has turned a light shade of brown
6. When the oil seems to be seperating from the onion mixture around the edges, add the cayenne pepper, turmeric powder, and tandoori masala
7. Stir fry for a further 5 minutes
8. Before adding the baked beans, just double check that any water that you may have added has evaporated
9. If so, then add the baked beans and let it simmer for a further ten minutes
10. Just make sure you stir now and then so it doesnt stick together at the bottom
11. Chop and stir in the coriander, and serve<|endoftext|>
