Give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Artichokes And Mustard Sauce

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Artichoke hearts
• Olive oil
• Red pepper
• Garlic clove
• Dijon mustard
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook pasta as directed on package
2. Drain artichokes, reserving marinade
3. Chop artichokes and set aside
4. Heat oil in skillet
5. Add peppers and garlic
6. Cook until peppers are crisp-tender
7. Add artichokes, the reserved artichoke marinade and the mustard
8. Stir until well blended
9. Drain pasta and place in a large bowl
10. Add artichoke mixture and toss to coat
11. Sprinkle with grated parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
