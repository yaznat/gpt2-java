Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Peanut Butter Layered Brownies

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Walnuts
• Peanut butter
• Powdered sugar
• Semisweet chocolate
• Milk

Steps:
1. Brownie layer
2. Preheat oven to 350f
3. Microwave unsweetened chocolate with 3 / 4 butter on high for 1 1 / 2 minutes
4. Stir till chocolate is completely melted
5. Stir granulated sugar into melted chocolate
6. Mix in eggs and 1 1 / 2 teaspoon vanilla
7. Blend well
8. Stir in flour and walnuts
9. Spread into 13x9-inch greased pan
10. Bake 30 to 35 minutes
11. Do not over bake
12. Cool
13. Peanut butter layer:
14. Mix peanut butter, 3 tablespoons butter, powdered sugar and 1 / 2 teaspoon vanilla in another bowl
15. Blend till smooth, adding up to 1 / 4 cup of milk to make it a nice spreading consistency
16. Spread over the cooled brownies
17. Top layer:
18. Microwave semisweet chocolate for 1 1 / 2 minutes with the 1 / 4 cup butter
19. Stir till smooth
20. Spread over peanut butter layer
21. Cool until set<|endoftext|>
