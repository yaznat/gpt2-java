Ingredients:
• Beer
• Shortening
• Sugar
• Salt
• White flour
• Dry yeast

Steps:
1. Place the 6 ingredients in order listed into the bread pan
2. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for using the dough setting
3. Remove dough from bread pan, cover with clean tea towel and let rest 5 minutes
4. Roll and shape dough into a round<|endoftext|>
