What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Healthy Ground Tukey And Bean Make Ahead Burritos

Ingredients:
• Dry pinto beans
• Brown rice
• Ground turkey
• Salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Water
• Olive oil
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Hot pepper

Steps:
1. Heat 10 cups of water to boiling in a large pot
2. Rinse the dry beans and pick out any stones
3. When the water boils, blanch the beans for 2-3 minutes, then remove from heat and cover
4. Hot soak for 4-16 hours
5. Drain the water from the beans and rinse
6. Pour into a crock pot and add enough water to cover and the salt
7. Cook on low for for 8-12 hours
8. When the beans are tender, freeze about half for a future batch of burritos or re-fried beans
9. Cook the 2 cups of rice as per the directions on the bag of rice
10. In a large skillet add the olive oil and cook the ground turkey
11. When the meat is no longer pink, add the beans and garlic
12. Using a potato masher, mash the beans
13. It will take some muscle to get it all mashed together
14. Add the salsa and the hot peppers
15. Cook until the water is reduced and the beans and meat thicken
16. Assemble the burritos with a scoop of bean-meat mix, scoop of rice and a sprinkle of cheese in the center of the tortilla
17. Fold side, bottom, side, then top
18. Wrap in wax paper and freeze in a zip lock bag
19. Or you can put 3 or 4 into lunch containers and freeze
20. To reheat: microwave 1 minute each side and / or toast in an oven or toaster oven 350 for about 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
