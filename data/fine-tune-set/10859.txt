Ingredients:
• Rice wine vinegar
• Superfine sugar
• Fresh dill
• Cucumbers
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan on medium heat, warm the vinegar and sugar together until the sugar dissolves
2. Set liquid aside to cool
3. Chop the dill
4. Slice the cucumber very thinly and arrange in layers on a shallow, non-reactive platter with sloped edges
5. Sprinkle with salt and pepper and garnish with the fresh dill
6. Pour the dressing over the cucumbers
7. Let marinate at least 1 hour or overnight
