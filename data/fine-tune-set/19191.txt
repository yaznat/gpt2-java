Give me a ice cream recipe.<|endoftext|>Cereal Ice Cream Dessert Squares

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Corn chex
• Brown sugar
• Coconut
• Pecans
• Ice cream

Steps:
1. Mix together the above ingredients except the ice cream
2. Reserve 1 1 / 2 cups of mixture for topping
3. Pat the remaining mixture into bottom of a greased 9x13" pan
4. Spread 1 / 2 gallon of softened ice cream on the cereal mixture in pan
5. Sprinkle the 1 1 / 2 cups reserved cereal mixture on top of ice cream
6. Cover and freeze several hours, or overnight
7. Cut into squares<|endoftext|>
