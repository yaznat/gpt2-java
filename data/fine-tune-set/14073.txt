Ingredients:
• Lamb loin chops
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Coarse salt
• Red currant jam
• Balsamic vinegar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Spray a 1
3. 5-qt
4. Casserole dish with cooking spray
5. Salt and pepper one side of chops, to taste
6. Place chops in prepared dish, seasoned side down and with the thick rib toward the center
7. Season the other side of the chops to taste
8. Spread about 1 1 / 2 tsp of jam on each chop
9. Drizzle with balsamic vinegar
10. Bake, uncovered, to desired doneness, about 25 minutes for rare
11. Add additional time if you like your chops cooked more
12. Let rest for about 10 minutes before serving
13. Spoon vinegar & juices from pan over chops when serving<|endoftext|>
