Please give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Deliciously Easy Lamb Casserole

Ingredients:
• Lamb
• Lemons
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Bacon
• Rosemary
• Mixed spice

Steps:
1. Brown lamb in a frypan, toss in your crockpot with all the other ingredients
2. Cook on high for two hours and then turn down to low for a further two hours
3. Serve with steamed vegies of your choice
4. Delicious for cold winter evenings!
