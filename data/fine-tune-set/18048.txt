Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Brownie Fudge Cake Light

Ingredients:
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Water
• Granulated sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Egg
• Egg whites
• Low-fat sour cream
• Corn syrup
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Icing sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350f spray a 9-inch square baking pan with vegetable spray
2. Make cake: in small microwavable bowl, combine the chocolate chips and water
3. Cook on high for 30 seconds or just until the chips beging to melt
4. Stir the mixture until it is smooth
5. In a large bowl and using a whisk or electrical mixer, beat sugar, cocoa, egg, egg whites, sour cream, corn syrup, oil, vanilla and melted cholate
6. In another bowl, stir together the flour and baking powder
7. With a wooden spoon, stir the dry ingredients into the sour cream mixture just until everything is combined
8. Pour the mixture into a prepared pan
9. Place the pan in the centre of the oven and bake for 30 to 35 minutes or until a tester comes out dry
10. Let the pan cool on a wire rack
11. Make icing: in a bowl and using a whisk or electric mixer, beat icing sugar, cocoa and water until the mixture is smooth
12. Spread it over the cake<|endoftext|>
