Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>Asiago Pasta

Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Heavy cream
• Chicken bouillon cube
• Asiago cheese
• Cornstarch
• Butter
• Prosciutto
• Tomatoes
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Sliced mushrooms
• Parsley flakes

Steps:
1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil
2. Cook pasta for 8 to 10 minutes, or until al dente
3. Drain, and set aside
4. In a medium saucepan, bring 2 cups cream to a simmer, stirring often
5. Whisk in bouillon and cheese until well blended and bouillon has dissolved completely
6. Dissolve cornstarch in 2 tablespoons water, and whisk into mixture
7. Cook and stir till the cream thickens, then remove from heat and set aside
8. Melt butter in a medium skillet over medium high heat
9. Stir in prosciutto, garlic, and mushrooms and cook until mushrooms are tender, about 3 minutes
10. Add remaining 1 / 4 cup cream and parsley flakes
11. Heat through
12. To serve, place pasta in a large mixing or serving bowl
13. Add the mushroom mixture and pour in cream sauce
14. Toss well, and serve<|endoftext|>
