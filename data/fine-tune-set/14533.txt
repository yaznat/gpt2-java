What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Awesome Green Beans

Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Bacon
• Onion
• Apple cider vinegar

Steps:
1. Put a medium skillet on stove and heat to medium
2. Add the bacon and cook till crisp, then remove to paper towel, keep bacon fat in skillet
3. Add onions and saute till lightly brown
4. Add green beans
5. Add vinegar and cook on medium- low until liquid is evaporated
6. Taste the beans and add salt and pepper if needed, but remember the bacon is salty!
7. Then serve with bacon sprinkled on top<|endoftext|>
