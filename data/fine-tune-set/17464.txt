What's a good coffee recipe?<|endoftext|>Basic Iced Coffee

Ingredients:
• Regular grind coffee
• Water
• Milk
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Make coffee in your coffee pot with coffee & water listed under coffee cubes, and allow to cool
2. Pour into an ice tray and freeze
3. Make additional coffee
4. Add milk and sugar to hot coffee, if desired
5. You can throw everything in the blender at this point if you like a smooth consistency like i do, or you can just put the cubes in a tall glass and pour in the freshly brewed coffee<|endoftext|>
