Ingredients:
• Medium shrimp
• Olive oil
• Hidden valley original ranch seasoning mix
• Corn
• Salsa
• Greek yogurt
• Chipotle chiles in adobo
• Fresh lemon juice
• Fresh chives
• Corn tortillas
• Avocado
• Fresh cilantro
• Limes

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine the shrimp, olive oil, and hidden valley ranch seasoning mix
2. Mix well
3. Allow to marinate for 15 minutes
4. Heat your grill to high heat
5. Grill the corn, for 10 minutes, until the husks are browned on all sides
6. Carefully peel off the husks and silk
7. Cut the corn from the cob and place in a medium bowl
8. Add the pico de gallo to the grilled corn kernels and mix well
9. Set aside
10. In a small bowl, combine the greek yogurt, chipotle chiles, lemon juice, and chives
11. Mix well
12. Heat a large nonstick saute pan over medium-high heat
13. Add the marinated shrimp and saute for 2 - 3 minutes, until cooked through
14. Assemble the tacos
15. Top each tortilla with the sauteed shrimp, grilled corn salsa, chipotle yogurt sauce, diced avocado, and garnish with cilantro
16. Serve with lime wedges on the side
17. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
