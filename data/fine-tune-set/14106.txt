Can you give me a tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Arroz Con Tofu

Ingredients:
• Tofu
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Thyme
• Nutmeg
• Bay leaf
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt and pepper
• Vegetable broth
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Brown tofu in skillet in hot oil on medium heat
2. Remove tofu to warm platter and add onion and spices
3. Sautee until golden
4. Return tofu to skillet and add broth
5. Simmer about 20 minutes
6. Remove tofu to platter with warm rice
7. Add to skillet 3 tablespoons corn starch and whisk
8. Boil and stir adding corn starch as needed until thick gravy is formed
9. Spoon over tofu and rice<|endoftext|>
