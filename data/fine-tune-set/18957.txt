Can you give me a custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Custard Squares

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Egg
• Baking powder
• Flour
• Boiling water
• Custard powder

Steps:
1. Mix the ingredients for the bottom layer together and press into 9x13 pan
2. Bake at 325 for 15-20 minutes
3. Mix custard in a little cold water before adding to boiling water
4. Boil til thickens
5. Spread over the base
6. Top with whipping cream
7. Sprinkle coconut over top<|endoftext|>
