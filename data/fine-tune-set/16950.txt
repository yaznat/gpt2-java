What's a good cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Blue Hawaii Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Light rum
• Vodka
• Blue curacao
• Pineapple juice
• Prepared sweet-and-sour mix

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients and mix well
2. If using ice, mix the ingredients in a blender
3. Serve in a tall glass
4. Garnish with a slice of pineapple and a cherry<|endoftext|>
