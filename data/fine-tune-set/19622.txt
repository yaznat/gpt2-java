Give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>Arin S Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Canned whole potatoes
• Lemon pepper
• Salt
• Season salt
• Oregano
• Butter

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a small saucepan
2. Heat on medium low
3. Drain potatoes and add to pan
4. Add seasonings and heat while stirring
5. Cook about 15 minutes or until heated through<|endoftext|>
