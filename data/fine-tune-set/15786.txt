Give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Bean Burritos Simple For Camping

Ingredients:
• Refried beans
• Burrito-size flour tortillas
• Onion
• Cheddar cheese
• Pickled jalapeno pepper
• Picante salsa

Steps:
1. Heat beans in microwave microwave or warm tortillas until soft
2. Stuff tortillas with beans and onion, add jalepeno and cheddar or squirt cheese
3. Roll up and microwave until heated thru
4. Serve with chips and small jar of salsa<|endoftext|>
