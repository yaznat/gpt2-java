Can you give me a tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• No-added-salt tomato paste
• Blackstrap molasses
• Water
• Tamari
• Balsamic vinegar
• Olive oil
• Dry mustard
• Onion powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Cayenne
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Rinse and pat the tofu dry, then slice it horizontally into 5 long rectangular slabs
2. Wrap in a dishtowel and press gently all over to remove excess moisture
3. Stack up the tofu slabs again and slice them in half diagonally so you now have 10 triangular slabs
4. Mix all other ingredients together and pour into a 9-by-13 baking dish
5. Spread the tofu slabs in the dish and turn them carefully, using a basting brush to make sure all sides are coated with sauce
6. Let rest for 15 minutes
7. Bake for 20 minutes at 350f, turning halfway through
8. If grilling, it's basically the same as anything else, but you have to be more careful about burning tofu than meat! just grill till it reaches your desired done-ness, basting occasionally with leftover sauce
9. Serve with baked potatoes, fries, steamed / grilled veg, salad -- endless possibilities! leftovers taste great crumbled in a sandwich with a little mayonnaise!<|endoftext|>
