Can you give me a taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Tacos

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Kidney bean
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Onion
• Green chili pepper
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Sour cream
• Salsa
• Chopped tomato
• Lettuce
• Mexican blend cheese

Steps:
1. Brown beef in pan with onion, peppers, and kidney beans
2. Add taco seasoning and follow instructions to make taco filling
3. In a seperate bowl mix soup, sour cream and salsa together
4. Spread 1 / 2 cup of soup mixture in a 13x9 baking dish
5. To make tacos spread a small amount of the soup mixture on tortilla, fill with meat mixture and top with cheese, roll and place seam side down baking dish top with remaining soup mixture and cheese
6. Cover with foil
7. Bake in 375 degree oven for 15-20 minutes
8. Serve with additional salsa, sour cream, tomatos and shredded lettuce<|endoftext|>
