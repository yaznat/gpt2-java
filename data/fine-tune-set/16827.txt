What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Peanut Butter Chocolate Banana Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Peanut butter
• Nutella
• Banana
• Milk
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Blend milk, banana and ice, then add peanut butter and nutella, to prevent it from sticking in the blender
2. If it's too thin for you, add some peanut butter
3. If it's too thick, add some milk<|endoftext|>
