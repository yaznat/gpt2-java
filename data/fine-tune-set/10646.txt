What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Artichoke And Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Artichoke hearts
• Olive oil
• Water
• Lemon juice
• Dried basil
• Dried oregano
• Black pepper
• Garlic
• Radiatore
• Spinach
• Tomatoes
• Feta cheese

Steps:
1. Combined 2 artichoke hearts, olive oil, and the next six ingredients in a blender or food processor, and process until mixture is smooth
2. Chop the remaining artichoke hearts
3. Combine the chopped artichokes, pasta, spinach and chopped tomato in a large bowl
4. Pour pureed artichoked mixture over pasta mixture, and toss well to coat
5. Cover and chill 2 hours
6. Sprinkle with feta cheese
7. Yields six servings<|endoftext|>
