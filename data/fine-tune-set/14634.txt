Ingredients:
• Onions
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Cinnamon
• Star anise
• Ground coriander
• Converted rice
• Chicken broth
• Cooked chicken
• Roasted pistachios
• Toasted almond
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. In a large skillet over medium heat, brown the onions in the oil
2. Season with salt and pepper
3. Add the garlic, spices and rice
4. Continue cooking for 1 minute, stirring to coat the rice with the oil
5. Add the broth and bring to a boil
6. Cover, reduce the heat and simmer gently for about 25 minutes
7. Add the chicken, pistachios and almonds
8. Continue cooking until the chicken is heated through
9. Adjust the seasoning
10. Serve garnished with cilantro leaves or parsley<|endoftext|>
