Ingredients:
• Whole boneless skinless chicken breast
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Onion
• Curry powder
• Gingerroot
• Garlic
• Ground cumin
• Chicken stock cubes
• Water
• Coconut milk
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. In a large pot add water, chicken stock cubes, salt pepper
2. Bring to a boil
3. Add chicken, reduce to medium heat
4. Let simmer about 10 minutes
5. Add potatoes, carrots, onions
6. Let simmer about 20 minutes
7. Add curry powder, ginger, garlic, salt & pepper
8. Reduce to low heat
9. Let simmer about 10 minutes
10. Once the potatoes are fully tender, remove from heat and add coconut milk
11. Puree with hand blender
12. Serve with basmati rice and enjoy!
13. Try not to substitute the fresh ginger or garlic for powdered or paste
14. This will really alter the overall taste
