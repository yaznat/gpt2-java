Please give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Baked Rice Cream

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Rice
• Sugar
• Salt
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in an oven proof dish stir well, until sugar dissolves bake in a very slow oven 225 degrees for about 3 hours
2. Stir the mixture every 1 / 2 hour until the rice is completely dissolved
3. Cool completely and refrigerate until well chilled
4. Serve very cold with cream<|endoftext|>
