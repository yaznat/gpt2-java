Please give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Banana Ice Cream Weight Watchers

Ingredients:
• Evaporated skim milk
• Egg substitute
• Bananas
• Vanilla
• Sugar substitute
• Low-fat milk

Steps:
1. In an ice cream maker combine evaporated skim milk, egg substitute, bananas, vanilla, and sugar substitute
2. Add low-fat milk to bring to fill line
3. Process and freeze according to manufacturer's directions<|endoftext|>
