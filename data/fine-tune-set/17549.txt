What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Butter Topping For Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Salt

Steps:
1. You'll need a coffee cake dough recipe #40265
2. Melt butter, add sugar, beaten eggs, sifted dry ingredients and vanilla
3. Spread on dough after it has risen
4. Bake at 375 for 40-45 minutes or until done
5. Sprinkle top with powdered sugar<|endoftext|>
