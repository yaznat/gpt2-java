What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Cathy S Taco Dip

Ingredients:
• Sour cream
• Cream cheese
• Lettuce
• Diced tomato
• Cheddar cheese
• Taco seasoning

Steps:
1. Allow cream cheese to warm and soften
2. Mix sour cream, cream cheese and taco seasoning in a bowl
3. Mix until smooth
4. Spread the mixture evenly across a 14" or 16" platter, or a cookie sheet works well for this, too
5. Sprinkle lettuce and tomatoes evenly on top of the mixture
6. Sprinkle the cheddar cheese on top of that
7. Serve with your favorite taco chips<|endoftext|>
