Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Apple Waffle Grills

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Frozen waffles
• American cheese
• Granny smith apple

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, melt butter over medium heat
2. Add two waffles
3. Top each with one cheese slice, apple slices and remaining cheese and waffles
4. Cook until waffles are lightly toasted on both sides and cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
