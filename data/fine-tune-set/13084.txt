What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Grilled Fish In Teriyaki Marinade

Ingredients:
• Sherry wine
• Soy sauce
• Dry onion soup mix
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Blend together the above ingredients
2. Marinate the fish for 10-20 minutes depending upon thickness of the fish
3. Marinating for a shorter time period is preferable
4. Cook on grill or in broiler until fish flakes with fork<|endoftext|>
