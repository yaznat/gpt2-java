What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew Pot

Ingredients:
• Stew meat
• Potatoes
• Turnips
• Onions
• Carrots
• Celery ribs
• Tomato sauce
• Fresh tomato
• Frozen peas
• Water
• Allspice
• Celery salt
• Pepper
• Paprika
• Garlic
• Cayenne pepper
• Bay leaf powder

Steps:
1. In very large pot, place stew meat& cover with cold water
2. Bring to a slow boil
3. Skim top often until broth is clear of"junk"
4. When broth has cleared, stir in all seasonings, tomato sauce& prepared vegetables
5. Bring to a boil, stir& turn heat to lo-med
6. Stew will make its own thickening
7. Simmer 3-5hours
8. Stir occasionally
9. Stir in peas about 1 / 2hr before serving
10. Adjust seasoning to taste<|endoftext|>
