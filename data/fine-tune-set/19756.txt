Ingredients:
• Beef hot dogs
• Idaho potatoes
• Hot dog buns
• Onion
• Fresh tomato
• Yellow mustard
• Sweet pickle relish
• Pickle spears
• Hot pepper
• Celery salt

Steps:
1. Preheat the fryer
2. Fill a stock pot 3 / 4 of the way full with water and bring to a boil
3. Add the hot dogs, reduce the heat to a simmer and cover
4. Cook until the hot dogs are plump, about 6 to 8 minutes
5. Fry the potatoes until golden brown, about 4 to 6 minutes, stirring occasionally for even browning
6. Remove and drain on paper towels
7. Season with salt and pepper
8. Place the hot dogs in the buns
9. Start topping the hot dogs with 2 tablespoons of each: onions, tomatoes, mustard and relish
10. Top with sport peppers to taste and sprinkle a little celery salt on top
11. Place each hot dog in the center of a piece of deli wrap
12. Place the fries next to the hot dog
13. Wrap the dog and the fries up tightly and serve
14. Game time!<|endoftext|>
