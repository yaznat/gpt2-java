Please give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Asian Beef Skewers

Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Sesame oil
• Sugar
• Sirloin tip steak
• Green onions

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix garlic, ginger, soy sauce, vinegar, oil and sugar
2. Remove the fat from the steak and cut the meat in dices of 1 inch
3. Add beef to the marinade and let rest for 10 minutes
4. On metal skewers or presoaked bamboo skewers, thread beef cubes by alternating with green onion
5. Set the barbecue to medium high
6. Brush the beef skewers with the marinade and put on a oiled grill of the barbecue
7. Close cover and cook about 10 minutes or until beef is grilled but still pink inside<|endoftext|>
