Ingredients:
• Light brown sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Shortening
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Strong coffee
• Baking soda

Steps:
1. Mix the sugar, flour and shortening until lumpy
2. Do not mix until creamy
3. Reserve 1 cup to use as topping
4. Dissolve the baking soda in the hot coffee and add to the flour mixture
5. Add the egg and vanilla
6. Spread in a 9"x12"x2" pan and sprinkle on the reserved topping
7. Bake at 325f degrees for approximately 30 minutes
8. Sprinkle with powdered sugar before serving<|endoftext|>
