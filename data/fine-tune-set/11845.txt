What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Bo Kaap Cape Malay Kerrie South African Cape Malay Curry

Ingredients:
• Mutton shoulder
• Oil
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh gingerroot
• Curry powder
• Ground coriander
• Ground cumin
• Turmeric
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Cinnamon stick
• Cloves
• Bay leaves
• Carrots
• Dried apricots
• Bananas
• Tomato paste
• Wine vinegar
• Meat stock
• Apricot jam
• Natural yoghurt

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a large pot or saucepan
2. Over high heat, fry the onions and garlic, stirring continuously
3. Add the ginger, curry powder and all the spices and continue stirring for a minute
4. Season to taste with pepper and add the salt
5. Reduce heat slightly
6. Add the meat and fry it until it is browned on all sides
7. Add the vinegar and stock, plus all the other remaining ingredients
8. Cover
9. Reduce heat
10. Simmer over a low heat, stirring occasionally, until everything is tender, approximately 1 1 / 2 hours for lamb and 2 hours for mutton, maybe a little longer
11. Stir in the apricot jam and the yoghurt a few minutes before serving
12. Serve this curry with yellow rice and a variety of sambals and atjars<|endoftext|>
