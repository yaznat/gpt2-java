Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Blond Brownies With Br Sugar Frosting

Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Vanilla
• Butter
• Eggs
• Flour
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 --
2. Beat sugars, butter, vanilla and eggs in a large bowl on medium speed of mixer till light and fluffy --
3. Stir in flour, baking powder, and salt --
4. Spread in an ungreased 13 x 9" pan
5. And bake 20 -23 minutes or golden brown and toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean
6. Do not overbake
7. Cool completely --
8. To prepare brown sugar frosting:
9. Melt butter in a 2 quart pan over low heat, then stir in brown sugar and cook over low heat 2 minutes -- stirring constantly
10. Stir in milk, and cook until mixture comes t a rolling boil --
11. Remove from heat and gradually stir in powdered sugar and vanilla
12. Mixing well after each addition with a spoon
13. Until smooth and spreadable
14. If necessary add more milk, a few drops at a time
15. Sprinkle with chopped pecans after frosting<|endoftext|>
