Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Low-calorie barbecue sauce
• Pizza dough
• Lowfat mozzarella cheese
• Red onion
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Pre heat broiler and spray a cookie sheet with pam and scatter chicken cubes on the greased sheet and broil until cooked fully
2. Place chicken in fridge until chilled then toss in 2 tbs bbq sauce
3. To make the pizzas place a pizza stone in the center of the oven and preheat to 500 degrees f for one hour before cooking pizzas
4. Spoon 1 / 4 cup low cal bbq sauce evenly over each prepared dough
5. Sprinkle the dough with 3 / 4 cup shredded mozzarella
6. Distribute half the chicken pieces evenly over the cheese
7. Place approximately 18 to 20 pieces of red onion over the surface
8. Sprinkle an additional 1 / 4 cup mozzarella over the top of the pizza<|endoftext|>
