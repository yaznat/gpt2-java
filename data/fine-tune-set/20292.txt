What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Chicken Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Sake
• Honey
• Chili paste with garlic
• Dark sesame oil
• Chicken
• Peanut oil
• Green onion
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Red bell pepper
• Carrot
• Snow peas
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Combine the broth, 1 1 / 2 tbsp cornstarch, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp sake, 2 tsp honey, chile paste, and sesame oil, stirring with a whisk
2. Set aside
3. Combine 1 1 / 2 tsp cornstarch, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sake, and 2 tsp honey in a large bowl, stirring with a whisk
4. Add chicken pieces and toss to gently coat
5. Heat 1 1 / 2 tbsp peanut oil in a wok or pan over medium-high heat
6. Add chicken mixture and saute until cooked through
7. Once chicken is cooked remove from pan and wipe clean with a paper towel
8. Add 1 tbsp peanut oil to pan
9. Stir in onions, ginger, and garlic and saute 30 seconds
10. Add bell pepper and carrot
11. Saute 2 minutes
12. Add snow peas
13. Saute 1 minute
14. Stir in chicken and broth mixture
15. Bring to a boil
16. Cook mixture 1 minute or until it thickens, stirring constantly
17. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
