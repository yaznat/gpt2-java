Give me a pizza recipe.<|endoftext|>Bld Carbonara Pizza Rachael Ray

Ingredients:
• Cornmeal
• Pizza dough
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Pancetta
• Ricotta cheese
• Pecorino romano cheese
• Egg yolks
• Garlic clove
• Pepper
• Provolone cheese
• Flat leaf parsley

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 450
2. Sprinkle a little cornmeal on a pizza pan or baking sheet
3. Place the pizza dough on the pan and stretch into a large round or rectangle
4. Drizzle the dough with evoo and partially bake until puffed and golden, about 8 minutes
5. In a small skillet, heat a drizzle of evoo over medium heat
6. Add the pancetta and cook, stirring often, until crisp, about 5 minutes
7. Transfer to a bowl and let cool
8. Add the ricotta, pecorino romano, egg yolks and garlic to the pancetta
9. Season with pepper and stir to combine
10. Spread the ricotta mixture over the partially baked pizza dough, leaving a 1 / 2-inch border all around
11. Top with the provolone
12. Bake until the crust and topping are golden and cooked through, about 12 minutes
13. Scatter the parsley on top and cut into wedges<|endoftext|>
