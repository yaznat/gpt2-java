Ingredients:
• Orange juice
• Banana
• Blueberries
• Raspberries
• Strawberry

Steps:
1. Whatever else you do, don't use all frozen fruit! place all ingredients in a blender & mix by using on / off pluse function until ingredients are mostly blended
2. Continue mixing, gradually increasing the speed, until the mixture is smooth
3. Pout into 2 glasses & enjoy!<|endoftext|>
