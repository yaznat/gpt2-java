Please give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Magic Bullet Six Second Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Vanilla ice cream
• Chocolate syrup

Steps:
1. Add all ingredients, in the order they appear, to the tall cup
2. Mix until smooth
3. Enjoy!
4. --------
5. If you want to thin your shake, add more milk and blend a little more
6. If you want to make a large batch, double or triple the ingredients and use the magic bullet blender
7. Blueberry - leave out the chocolate and add a handfull of fresh or frozen blueberries
8. Strawberry - add a handful of fresh or frozen strawberries
9. Chocolate monster - use chocolate ice cream and add your favorite bite-size candy bar
10. Mimi's chocolate chocolate mint - add 1 / 4 of a chocolate bar and two starlight mint candies
11. Bullet blizzard - add tiny colored chocolate candies to the milkshake recipe
12. North pole - add a mint patty to the regular milkshake recipe<|endoftext|>
