Please give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Homemade Ice Cream Custard Based

Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Eggs
• Whole milk
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Mix sugar and flour
2. Stir in eggs
3. Set aside
4. Heat milk but do not boil
5. Pour hot milk into egg mixture
6. Stir to mix and return to medium heat
7. Stir constantly with a wooden spoon until mixture thickens
8. Remove from heat
9. Stir in vanilla
10. This completes the custard
11. Leave plain for vanilla ice cream or stir in 3 cups mashed fruit of your choice
12. Pour custard into ice cream maker canister and follow manufacturer's instructions for making the ice cream<|endoftext|>
