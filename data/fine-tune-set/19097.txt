Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Orange Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Fresh lemon juice
• Salt
• Sugar
• Fresh orange juice
• Confectioners' sugar
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Peel and mash bananas
2. Combine with lemon juice, salt, sugar and fresh orange juice
3. Fold confectioners' sugar into lightly whipped heavy cream
4. Add to banana mixture
5. Churn-freeze<|endoftext|>
