Ingredients:
• Green bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Yellow bell pepper
• Red onion
• Fresh garlic
• Salt and pepper
• Butter
• Cumin
• Green chilies
• Cream cheese
• Garlic
• Cheddar cheese
• Tortillas
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. In a large skillet melt butter, add in the sliced bell peppers, garlic, onions and cumin
2. Saute the veggies until soft, season with salt and pepper
3. Drain the bell pepper mixture over a colinder to release any excess juices
4. Set aside
5. In a small bowl combine the green chilies with a little of their juice, cream cheese and 1 teaspoon fresh garlic mix well to combine, then add in the cheddar cheese, mix to combine
6. Assemble the quesadillas as follows
7. Spread about 3-4 tablespoons of the cheese mixture on 1 / 2 of each tortilla
8. Top with sauteed pepper mixture
9. Fold over
10. Lightly butter the tortilla and then sprinkle with cumin or chili powder
11. Place the tortilla halves on a lightly greased baking sheet
12. Set oven to 400 degrees,
13. Bake for about 10-15 minutes or until bubbly and lightly browned
14. Cut the tortillas into thirds or fourths
15. In a small bowl mix the sour cream with salsa, and serve as a dip with the sliced quesadillas
16. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
