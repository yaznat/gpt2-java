Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Chuck Stew

Ingredients:
• Beef chuck
• Flour
• Olive oil
• Unsalted butter
• Dijon mustard
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Tomato paste
• Beef broth
• Dry red wine
• Bay leaves
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Celery ribs
• Tomatoes
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 275f
2. Season half the beef with salt and pepper, and then dredge in the flour
3. Shake off excess flour
4. Add olive oil to a large dutch oven over medium-high heat and brown beef cubes
5. Using a slotted spoon transfer the beef to a plate
6. Repeat with the remaining beef
7. De glaze pan with 1 cup of broth then pour all back into broth container
8. Melt the butter over medium-high heat
9. Add the onions and cook, stirring until lightly browned
10. Add the garlic and cook, stirring, until fragrant about 1 minute
11. Add the tomato paste and cook, stirring, until lightly browned, about 1 minute
12. Return the beef to the pot and add the broth and wine, and bring to a simmer
13. Stir in dijon mustard and add bay leaves
14. Season to taste with salt and pepper
15. Cover and transfer to the oven
16. Stew the meat until tender, about 1- hours
17. Remove from the oven and skim the fat from the cooking liquid
18. Add the potatoes, carrots, celery, and the tomatoes with juice, and bring to a simmer
19. Stew uncovered, on top of stove, skim and stir occasionally, until liquid has thickened and the beef and vegetables are tender, about 1 hour
20. Remove the bay leaves, season to taste and serve<|endoftext|>
