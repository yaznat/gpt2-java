Please give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Barbecued Pork Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground lean pork
• Saltine
• Green onion
• Garlic
• Creole mustard
• Barbecue sauce
• Black pepper
• Dried thyme
• Rubbed sage

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Form into 4-5 equal size patties
3. Re-chill for 30 minutes
4. Grill over charcoal
5. Baste with the same bbq used in the mix when burgers are halfway done<|endoftext|>
