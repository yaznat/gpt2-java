Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Alice S Brownies

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Margarine
• Eggs
• Flour
• Chocolate syrup
• Milk
• Chocolate chips
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Combine all brownie ingredients
2. Grease a 9x13 pan
3. Bake at 350 degrees for 25 minutes
4. For frosting
5. In a heavy sauce pan combine
6. Milk
7. Margarine
8. Sugar
9. Bring to a boil and back for 30 seconds
10. Remove from heat
11. Stir in chocolate chips and vanilla
12. Frosting will be runny but will set up on the brownies<|endoftext|>
