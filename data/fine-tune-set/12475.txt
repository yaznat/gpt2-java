Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>African Groundnut Stew

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Stewed tomatoes
• Garlic cloves
• Tabasco sauce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Water
• Peanut butter

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef and onion, drain well
2. Add stewed tomatoes, garlic, tabasco sauce, salt and pepper
3. Add water to make a medium sauce
4. Simmer 1 / 2 hour, add peanut butter and simmer 15 minutes more<|endoftext|>
