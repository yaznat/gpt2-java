What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Breaded Lamb Cutlets

Ingredients:
• Lamb cutlets
• Panko breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Fresh basil
• Fresh parsley
• Eggs
• Flour
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Trim off any excess fat from each cutlet, flatten slightly with a meat mallet
2. Combine breadcrumbs, parsley, basil and cheese
3. Coat cutlets in flour dip into egg then coat completely in breadcrumb mixture
4. Lay cutlets flat on a tray or plate in a single layer, place in refrigerator for 10 min's this makes them easier to handle
5. Heat a couple of tablespoons of oil in fry pan add lamb cook until browned on both sides turn the heat down and continue cooking until cooked as desired, turning occasionally, i do this instead of finishing in the oven as the coating tends to lose its crispiness
6. I have allowed for three cutlets per person so make 16 if you want four per person
7. Serve with mash, roasted or boiled potato and vegetable<|endoftext|>
