What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Basil Garlic Pork Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground pork
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh basil
• Cracked black pepper
• Kosher salt
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except oil and shape into four patties
2. Heat oil in a large skillet
3. Brown patties over medium heat until you get a nice crust on each side, then lower heat and continue cooking until patties are cooked through, 10 to 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
