Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Fried Rice With Shrimp Pork Shiitake Mushrooms

Ingredients:
• Oyster sauce
• Soy sauce
• Peanut oil
• Eggs
• Small shrimp
• Frozen peas
• Dried shiitake mushroom
• Ground pork
• Roast pork
• Garlic cloves
• Cooked white rice
• Scallions

Steps:
1. Combine oyster sauce and soy sauce in small bowl
2. Set aside
3. Heat 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat until hot
4. Add 1 1 / 2 teaspoons oil
5. Add eggs and cook without stirring, until they just begin to set, about 20 seconds, then scramble and break into small pieces
6. Continue to cook, stirring constantly, until eggs are cooked through
7. Transfer eggs to small bowl and set aside
8. Return skillet to medium heat and heat until hot
9. About 1 minute
10. Add 1 1 / 2 teaspoons oil
11. Add shrimp and cook, stirring constantly, until opaque and just cooked through, about 30 seconds
12. Transfer to bowl with eggs and set aside
13. Return skillet to burner, increase heat to high and heat skillet until hot, about 2 minutes
14. Add remaining 2 1 / 2 tablespoons oil
15. Add peas, mushrooms, sausage or ham, and pork
16. Cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute
17. Stir in garlic and cook for about 30 seconds
18. Add rice and oyster sauce mixture
19. Cook, stirring constantly and breaking up rice clumps, until mixture is heated through, about 3 minutes
20. Add eggs, shrimp, and scallions
21. Cook, stirring constantly, until heated through, about 1 minute
22. Serve<|endoftext|>
