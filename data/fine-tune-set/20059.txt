What's a recipe for an omelette?<|endoftext|>Sara S Best Ever Omelette

Ingredients:
• Olive oil flavored cooking spray
• Eggs
• Red onion
• Shortcut bacon
• Cherry tomatoes
• Basil leaves
• Tasty cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Spray a small saucepan with ooil
2. Cook onion and bacon until onion is just tender
3. Remove from heat, set aside
4. Spray a medium frying pan with oil
5. Heat pan to medium heat
6. Beat egg whites until thick and white
7. Spread into heated pan
8. Place cover on pan while you prepare the egg yolks
9. Beat egg yolk, and pour over egg whites
10. Place cover back on pan, and cook 1 minute
11. Sprinkle the bacon, onion, tomatoes, basil, and cheese gently over the egg mixture
12. Season with salt and pepper
13. Place lid back on pan and cook until cheese melts, about 5 minutes
14. Fold omellette in half, using an eggflip, and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
