What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>All In One Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Granulated sugar
• Light brown sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Salt
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Milk chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. In large bowl, beat butter, granulated sugar, brown sugar, vanilla and salt with electric mixer until creamy
3. Add eggs
4. Beat well
5. Stir together flour and baking soda
6. Gradually add to butter mixture, beating until well mixed
7. Stir in chocolate chips
8. Drop by rounded teaspoons onto ungreased cookie sheet
9. Bake 8-10 minutes or until set
10. Cool slightly
11. Remove from cookie sheet to wire rack<|endoftext|>
