What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Fat Free Delicious Omelette

Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• Egg substitute
• Fat-free cheddar cheese
• Diced tomato
• Onion
• Green pepper

Steps:
1. Rinse and chop vegetables to desired size
2. Spray large frying pan lightly with cooking spray, then heat pan under medium-low heat
3. Pour egg substitute or beaten egg whites into heated frying pan
4. Add all other ingredients
5. Cover and let cook
6. Omelette should be flipped when the top part appears to be getting dry
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
