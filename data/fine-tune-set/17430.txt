Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Butter
• Bisquick
• Milk
• Preserves
• Confectioners' sugar
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Cut cream cheese with butter into bisquick until crumbly
2. Stir in 1 / 4 cup milk
3. Put on floured surface and knead 10-12 strokes
4. On waxed paper, roll dough to 12x18-inch rectangle
5. Invert on greased baking sheet
6. Remove paper
7. Spread jam down center
8. Fold over
9. Bake at 355 degrees for 20 minutes
10. Let cool 5 minutes
11. Stir together confectioner's sugar, milk, and vanilla
12. Drizzle over cake<|endoftext|>
