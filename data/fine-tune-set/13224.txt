What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>20 Minute Creamy Pesto Chicken And Bow Ties

Ingredients:
• Bow tie pasta
• Butter
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Cream of chicken soup
• Pesto sauce
• Milk

Steps:
1. Boil water for pasta
2. Cook and drain
3. Meanwhile, in a skillet, melt butter or margarine and brown cubed chicken
4. Add soup, pesto sauce and milk to skillet
5. Bring to a boil and cook on low for about 5 minutes
