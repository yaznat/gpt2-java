Ingredients:
• Dried chipotle powder
• Mild chili powder
• Sugar
• Kosher salt
• Ground cumin
• Large raw shrimp
• Olive oil
• White corn tortillas
• Iceberg lettuce
• Hass avocadoes
• Salsa verde
• Lime wedge

Steps:
1. Combine first 5 ingredients in a large bowl
2. Add shrimp, tossing to coat
3. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
4. Add shrimp mixture to pan
5. Cook 1 1 / 2 minutes on each side or until done
6. Remove from heat
7. Heat tortillas in microwave according to package directions
8. Place 2 tortillas on each of 4 plates
9. Arrange 4 shrimp on each tortilla
10. Top each tortilla with 1 / 4 cup lettuce, 2 avocado slices and 1 1 / 2 tablespoons salsa verde
11. Serve with wedges of lime, if desired<|endoftext|>
