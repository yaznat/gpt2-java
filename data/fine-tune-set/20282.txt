Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Garlic cloves
• Light soy sauce
• White wine
• Sesame oil
• Onion
• Broccoli
• Baby carrots
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Steamed rice
• Egg

Steps:
1. Mix marinade ingredients in a medium bowl
2. Let stand 30 minutes
3. Heat skillet over medium-high heat
4. Stir-fry marinated chicken until lightly browned
5. Retaining juices in the pan, remove chicken with a slotted spatula and set aside
6. To the pan of juices, add onions and saute' 3-4 minutes
7. Add broccoli, carrots to the pan and saute 2-3 minutes more
8. Add chicken back to the pan with the thickener and saute 1-2 minutes until sauce is thickened
9. Fried rice: mix rice ingredients together<|endoftext|>
