Ingredients:
• Pie shells
• Rhubarb
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Lemon juice
• Salt
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 f
2. Combine the rhubarb, sugar, flour, lemon and salt
3. Turn into unbaked pie shell
4. Bake for 30 minutes
5. Combine topping ingredients
6. Mix with hands until large crumbs form sprinkle crumbs on top
7. Bake for 15 minutes more or until crumbs are brown and pie is bubbly
8. Cool on rack
