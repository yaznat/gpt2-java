What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chili Plum Stir Fry Chicken

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Chicken breast
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Spring onions
• Chilies
• Choy sum
• Oyster sauce
• Plum sauce
• Dry sherry
• Hot chili sauce
• Basil leaves
• Cashew nuts

Steps:
1. Heat a wok over high heat until hot and add 1 tablespoons oil and swirl to coat
2. Mix oyster sauce, plum sauce, dry sherry and chilli sauce to mix
3. Add a 1 / 4 of the chicken and stir fry for 2 to 3 minutes or until browned and transfer to a bowl
4. Repeat till all chicken is cooked, adding more oil as needed
5. Add 2 teaspoons oil to wok and swirl to coat and add the garlic, ginger, onions and chilli and stir fry for 30 seconds and add choy sum and stir fry for 1 minute or until bright green
6. Return the chicken to the wok and add the sauce mix and stir fry for a minute or until combined
7. Stir through cashews and basil
8. Serve with steamed rice or fried rice<|endoftext|>
