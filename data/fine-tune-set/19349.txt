What's a good donut recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Donuts Extremely Easy

Ingredients:
• Icing
• Cake mix

Steps:
1. Spray a donut pan with any cooking spray
2. Heck, butter would work!
3. Make the cake mix as directed,
4. Pour it into the donut pan
5. I would recommend to fill it to where there is just a little bit of the hole thingy showing
6. Bake on directed temperature on the box
7. Check every two minutes, these things bake fast!
8. When you think there are fully baked, take out to cool
9. Then ice!
10. Enjoy this amazing donut!<|endoftext|>
