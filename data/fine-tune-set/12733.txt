Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Red pepper flakes
• Fresh ginger
• Fresh garlic
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredient
2. Cover meat with sauce and marinate, flipping meat every hour or so
3. Marinate at least 4 hours, great if you marinate over night!<|endoftext|>
