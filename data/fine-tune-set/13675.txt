What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Ale D Pork And Sauerkraut

Ingredients:
• Sauerkraut
• Sugar
• Dark beer
• Boneless pork shoulder
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Paprika

Steps:
1. Place sauerkraut in sow cooker
2. Sprinkle sugar evenly over sauerkraut
3. Pour beer over all
4. Place pork, fat side up, on top or sauerkraut mixture
5. Sprinkle evenly with salt, garlic powder, pepper, and paprika
6. Cover
7. Cook on high 6 hours
8. Remove pork to serving platter
9. Remove sauerkraut with slotted spoon
10. Arrange around pork
11. Spoon 1 / 2 to 3 / 4 cup cooking liquid over sauerkraut, if desired<|endoftext|>
