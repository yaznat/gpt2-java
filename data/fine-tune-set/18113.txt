Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Ana Gourmet Oat Bran Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Oat bran
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Almond breeze non-dairy beverage
• Water
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• Maple extract
• Liquid stevia

Steps:
1. Put a non-stick frying pan over medium heat
2. Combine all dry ingredients in a large mixing bowl
3. Add all of the wet ingredients and whisk only until all ingredients are wet
4. Pour about 4 tablespoons of batter into the frying pan
5. Wait until bubbles form in the middle of the pancake and flip it over with a spatula
6. Cook until underside is golden brown and remove from pan
7. Repeat with remaining batter<|endoftext|>
