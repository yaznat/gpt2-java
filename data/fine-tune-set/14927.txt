Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Fried Rice With Ham

Ingredients:
• Long grain rice
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Raw shrimp
• Ham
• Dry sherry
• Light soy sauce
• Frozen peas
• Yellow onions
• Salt
• White pepper

Steps:
1. The rice should be smoothed out to get rid of any lumps
2. Day old, cold rice works best
3. If using eggs, lightly beat them with a pinch of salt
4. Heat a little oil in your wok, pour eggs in and tilt the wok back and forth to spread evenly
5. Lift the edges to let the uncooked portion come in contact with the hot pan so it cooks evenly
6. After the eggs set up a little, lightly scramble them so you have medium small pieces
7. Remove eggs to a bowl
8. Wipe out wok
9. If adding raw shrimp, heat a little more oil and stir fry them to just cook
10. Remove to a bowl
11. Wipe out wok again
12. Heat a little more oil
13. Add the onions& stir fry until they start to darken
14. Stir in the rice, breaking up any lumps you missed
15. Drizzle a little dry sherry down the sides of the wok
16. If you want the rice to look brown, drizzle in a little light soy sauce
17. Stir in all of the other ingredients and season to taste with salt and white pepper
18. Continue to stir and cook until heated through<|endoftext|>
