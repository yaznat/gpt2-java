Ingredients:
• Fresh sage leaves
• Chicken tenders
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Bacon
• Extra virgin olive oil

Steps:
1. Season tenders with salt and pepper
2. Place a sage leaf on each chicken tender, then wrap each with a bacon slice
3. In a large skillet, heat oil over medium-high
4. Add tenders, sage side down, and cook until fat is rendered and bacon is browned, about 6 minutes
5. Flip and cook until tenders are cooked through, 6 minutes
6. With tongs, transfer to a wire rack set on a rimmed baking sheet to drain<|endoftext|>
