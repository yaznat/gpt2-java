What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Herb Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Leeks
• Spring onions
• Spinach
• Parsley
• Herbs
• Walnuts
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350-degrees f
2. In a large bowl beat eggs
3. Stir in chopped vegetables, herbs and walnuts
4. Season to taste with salt and pepper
5. Whisk all ingredients together
6. Butter a large ovenproof dish and pour in egg / veggie mixture
7. Cover and bake for 30 minutes
8. Remove cover and bake for another 15 minutes or until top is golden<|endoftext|>
