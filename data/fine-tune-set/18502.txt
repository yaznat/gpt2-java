Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Good Eats Chocolate Chip Waffles Alton Brown

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Cocoa powder
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Eggs
• Unsalted butter
• Buttermilk
• Pure vanilla extract
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Whisk dry ingredients
2. In separate bowl, whisk eggs and butter
3. Then add in buttermilk
4. Plug in waffle iron
5. Pour in wet ingredients and stir with a spatula
6. Do not overstir, the batter should be lumpy and have bubbles
7. Stir chocolate chips inches
8. Let the batter sit for five minutes
9. Spray waffle iron with pam spray
10. Pour in batter and cook<|endoftext|>
