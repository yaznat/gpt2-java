Ingredients:
• Pie crust
• Bacon
• Breakfast sausage
• Swiss cheese
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. In a 9x13 pan lay the crescent roll down with out separating
2. Bake at 425f oven for 5 minutes
3. Sprinkle bacon, sausage and cheese evenly over crust
4. In a bowl, beat eggs, sour cream and parsley until smooth
5. Pour over the casserole
6. Bake for 20 minutes or until the casserole is puffy and lightly brown<|endoftext|>
