Ingredients:
• Peach
• Milk
• Peach ice cream

Steps:
1. Place peaches and the 1 / 4 cup milk in blender container
2. Cover and blend till smooth, about 10 seconds
3. Spoon in ice cream
4. Cover and blend again till softened
5. Add remaining milk
6. Mix till blended
7. Sweeten with a little sugar, if desired
8. Pour into tall glasses
9. Garnish each serving with fresh peach slices<|endoftext|>
