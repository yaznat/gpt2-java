Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Lite olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Mix ingredients and pour over 4 steaks
2. I suggest new york strip, porterhouse, filet mignon, t-bone or any tender cut
3. Turn often to coat well
4. Marinade for 1 / 2 to 1 hour
5. Remove and pat dry
6. Discard garlic
7. Place steaks on a hot charcoal or gas grill and cook until rare to medium<|endoftext|>
