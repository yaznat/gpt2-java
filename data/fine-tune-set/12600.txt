Give me a stew recipe.<|endoftext|>Beef And Porter Stew

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Beef chuck
• Onion
• Butter
• Flour
• Beer
• Low sodium beef broth
• Tomato paste
• Carrots
• Parsnips
• Celery
• Red bliss potatoes
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Put oil in large heavy bottomed pot and heat over medium high
2. Season beef
3. Brown meat well on all sides
4. Do not over crowd
5. You may have to do this in two batches
6. Cook about 8 minutes
7. Transfer to bowl and set aside
8. Add onion, celery, and butter to pot and cook until golden and carmelized, about 15 minutes
9. Sprinkle onions with flour
10. Stir well to combine
11. Cook stirring constantly about two minutes
12. Combine broth and paste and stir to dissolve paste
13. Add to pot with beer
14. Stir well scraping up brown bits from bottom of pan
15. Add veggies and beef
16. Simmer covered for about two hours
17. Season as needed<|endoftext|>
