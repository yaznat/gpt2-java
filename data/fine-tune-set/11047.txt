What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Appelkalle Swedish Apple Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Butter
• Tart apples
• Cinnamon
• Marzipan

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Place the bread on a lightly greased baking sheet
3. Using the coarse side of a grater, grate the apples, peel and all
4. Spread grated apples on top of the bread
5. Sprinkle with cinnamon
6. Grate marzipan over apples
7. Bake until marzipan turns a light gold<|endoftext|>
