Please give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>Amazing Soft And Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Caster sugar
• Self-raising flour
• Plain flour
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Baking powder
• Milk chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 160c
2. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat and set aside to cool slightly
3. Pour butter into a large bowl and stir in brown sugar and caster sugar until smooth and sugar is mostly dissolved
4. Lightly beat the egg with a whisk or fork and stir into the sugar mixture along with the vanilla extract
5. Sift flours and baking powder together into a small bowl and then gradually mix into sugar mixture until combined
6. Stir through chocolate chips
7. Drop rounded tablespoons of the cookie mixture onto lined baking trays
8. Bake for 12 minutes or until just lightly golden then remove from oven and allow to cool on trays for 3 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
