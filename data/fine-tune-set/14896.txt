Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Edamame Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Carrots
• Yellow bell pepper
• Scallions
• Tamari
• Edamame
• Cooked long-grain brown rice

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large wok or skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add carrots, bell pepper and scallions, and stir-fry until just tender, about 2 minutes
3. Add tamari, edamame and rice, and stir-fry until combined and heated through, about 5 minutes
4. Serve<|endoftext|>
