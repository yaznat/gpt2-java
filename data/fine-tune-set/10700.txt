Ingredients:
• Medium pasta shell
• Caesar salad dressing
• Salt and pepper
• Artichoke hearts
• Cherry tomatoes
• Avocados
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Gently mix together all ingredients and chill until ready to serve
2. Before serving, add additional salad dressing if needed, and more cheese if you like
3. If you're making this far in advance, add the diced avocados before serving<|endoftext|>
