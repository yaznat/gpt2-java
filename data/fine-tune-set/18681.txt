Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Sugar
• Pepper
• Cream-style corn
• Eggs
• Milk

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a 5-quart saucepan over medium heat
2. Add onion, garlic, and bell peppers
3. Cook, stirring, until onion is limp
4. Stir in flour, salt, sugar, and pepper
5. Cook, stirring constantly, until bubbly
6. Remove from heat
7. Add corn, eggs, and milk, stirring until mixture is well blended
8. Pour corn mixture into shallow, buttered 3-quart baking dish
9. Bake in a 350 degree f oven for 55 minutes or until center appears set when dish is gently shaken<|endoftext|>
