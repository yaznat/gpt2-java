What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Stir Fry Oriental

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breasts
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Cooking sherry
• Sugar
• Ground ginger
• Vegetable oil
• Frozen oriental-style vegetables

Steps:
1. Mix chicken, cornstarch, soy sauce, sherry, sugar and ginger together in a small bowl
2. In large skillet or wok, heat oil and keep at medium heat
3. Add chicken mixture and cook, stirring often, for 10-15 minutes until chicken is done
4. Add frozen vegetables and stir until vegetables are cooked through but still crisp
5. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
