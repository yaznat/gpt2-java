Generate a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Best Of The Best Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Onion
• Celery
• Carrots
• Rice
• Chicken broth
• Cooked pork
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tbs
2. Oil in large skillet
3. Add onion, celery and carrot
4. Saute until onions are transparent and carrots are crisp tender, about 5 minute
5. Push veggies to one side of skillet and scramble eggs in empty side
6. Mix eggs and veggies together
7. Set aside
8. Heat 1 tbs
9. Oil in skillet over high heat until very hot
10. Add cold rice to skillet and stir well
11. Add chicken broth to skillet
12. Stir fry rice, stirring constantly, until all liquid is gone
13. Add meat to skillet and cook for about a minute more
14. Add egg and veggies to skillet
15. Stir in soy sauce and heat until warm<|endoftext|>
