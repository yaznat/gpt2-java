What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Berta S Turkey Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Chili bean
• Pork and beans
• Tomato sauce
• Diced tomatoes with mild green chilies
• Water
• Chili powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Brown ground turkey, onion, greenpepper in a large pan
2. Add other ingredients
3. Cook over medium heat
4. This is a great tasting low fat chili
5. My family loves it
6. You can also as pasta if you like as a filler<|endoftext|>
