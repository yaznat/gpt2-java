What's a recipe for a popsicle?<|endoftext|>Pineapple And Lime Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Juice
• Natural yoghurt
• Lime
• Brown sugar
• Sticks

Steps:
1. Combine ingredients in large jug
2. Use stick blender to process till smooth
3. Pour into 8 hole popsicle mould
4. Insert sticks
5. Freeze overnight<|endoftext|>
