What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Air Fried Hash Brown Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Cooking spray
• Seasoning salt

Steps:
1. Cut potatoes in small cubes
2. Place diced potatoes in a bowl and spray with cooking spray
3. Add seasoning salt of your choice, toss to coat
4. Spread in air fryer basket
5. Set air fryer to french fry setting and cook them until crispy, stir halfway through<|endoftext|>
