Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Hickory smoked bacon
• Fresh rosemary
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Boiling potatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Season the pork with salt and pepper
3. Wrap the bacon around the pork and tie with string
4. Tuck the rosemary here and there under the string
5. Heat the oil in a large, heavy casserole dish over medium heat
6. Add the roast and brown it lightly on all sides, about 4 minutes
7. When the roast has a golden color, turn the heat off and add the potatoes and garlic
8. Mix the potatoes, garlic, and oil well
9. Season the potatoes with salt and pepper
10. Put the casserole dish in the oven and bake for 45 minutes
11. Baste the roast with pan juices while cooking
12. Check the roast for doneness by piercing with a knife
13. The roast is done when the juices that come out are clear and not pink
14. Take the roast out of the oven, remove the string, slice the meat, and serve with potatoes and some pan juice<|endoftext|>
