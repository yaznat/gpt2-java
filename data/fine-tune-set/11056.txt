Ingredients:
• Butter
• Whole wheat bread
• Raisins
• Sunflower seeds
• Apple
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Butter the bread
2. Sprinkle raisins and sunflower seeds on top
3. Arrange apple dice on top of seeds and raisins
4. Top with grated cheddar
