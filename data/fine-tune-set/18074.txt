Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Flour
• Milk
• Salt
• Butter
• Powdered sugar
• Apple pie filling
• Toasted almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. With an electric mixer or wire whisk, beat eggs until frothy
3. Whisk in flour
4. Stir in milk and salt
5. Melt butter in a 10-inch oven-proof skillet
6. Pour mixture into hot skillet and place in preheated oven
7. Bake 15 minutes or until pancake is puffed and golden
8. The center will be shaped like a bowl
9. Remove from oven
10. Fill with fruit, dust with powdered sugar and sprinkle with almonds
11. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
