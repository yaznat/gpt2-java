Give me a cookie recipe.<|endoftext|>Amazing Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients together with a handmixer in a large bowl except chocolate chips
2. Fold in chocolate chips with a spatula and spoon 2 tablespoons of dough per cookie onto an ungreased cookie sheet
3. Bake at 350 for about 10 minutes or until the edges are just slightly brown
4. Remove immediately from cookie sheet onto cooling racks<|endoftext|>
