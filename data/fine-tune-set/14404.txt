Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Fresh ginger
• Dried oregano
• Cayenne pepper
• Ground cinnamon
• Adzuki beans
• Braggs liquid aminos
• Vegetable broth
• Okra
• Cooked brown rice

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil and add the onions and ginger
2. Cook for 5 minutes or until softened
3. Add oregano, cayenne and cinnamon
4. Stir and cook for 1 minute
5. Add beans, braggs, broth and okra
6. Bring to a boil and simmer for 10 minutes
7. Serve over brown rice<|endoftext|>
