Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>21 Club Burger

Ingredients:
• Celery
• Butter
• Ground sirloin
• Breadcrumbs
• Au jus mix
• Buns

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. In a large, ovenproof skillet saute celery in 1 tablespoon butter over medium heat for about 4 minutes
3. Remove celery and let cool
4. In bowl, combine sirloin, bread crumbs, au jus, and celery
5. Form in to 4 patties
6. Melt remaining butter in skillet
7. Place patties in skillet and cook 2-3 minutes or until bron on one side
8. Flip patties and place in oven
9. Cook for 12-15 minutes for medium doneness
10. Place on buns<|endoftext|>
