What's a good custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Blueberry Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• Pie crust
• Eggs
• Brown sugar
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground cinnamon
• Plain yogurt
• Lemon juice
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 180c / 350f
2. Whisk together the eggs, sugar, nutmeg, cinnamon, yoghurt and lemon juice until smooth
3. Put the blueberries in the pie crust and carefully pour over the filling mix
4. Bake in oven for 55-60 mins or until the filling is set
5. Allow to cool on a rack and then chill for at least 1 hour before serving<|endoftext|>
