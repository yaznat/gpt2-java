Generate a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Canadian Maple Walnut Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Pure maple syrup
• Egg yolks
• Light cream
• Walnuts
• Maple liqueur

Steps:
1. In deep heavy saucepan, bring maple syrup to boil and cook over medium-high heat, without stirring, until syrup reaches soft-ball stage, about 10 minutes
2. In large bowl and using electric mixer, beat egg yolks
3. Gradually pour in hot syrup, beating constantly at medium speed
4. Increase speed to high and beat for 5 to 10 minutes longer or until mixture is cool
5. Blend in cream, walnuts and maple liqueur
6. Cover and refrigerate until thoroughly chilled
7. Freeze in ice-cream maker following manufacturer's instructions<|endoftext|>
