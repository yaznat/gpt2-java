Ingredients:
• Beef sirloin steak
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Green bell pepper
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Flour tortillas
• Salsa
• Sour cream
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a heavy skillet or wok
2. Toss the beef with the cumin and chili powder
3. Cook the beef, peppers, and onions
4. Add the mixture to the tortillas
5. Top with salsa, sour cream, and cheese
6. Roll up the burrito and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
