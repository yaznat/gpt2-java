Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Alarm Spicy Bbq Pork Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Pork butt
• Barbecue sauce
• Prepared mustard
• Red pepper flakes
• Onion
• Hot sauce
• Salt and pepper
• Liquid smoke

Steps:
1. In the crock pot place the roast and sprinkle it with salt and pepper
2. Place it in there on low for 45 minutes to 1 hour then add the bbq sauce mustard, onion and pepper flakes
3. Cook on low for 6-8 hours until the pork easily shreds apart
4. Baste the inside of the buns you intend to serve them on with a generous amount of sauce from the crock pot<|endoftext|>
