Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Salsa
• Green pepper
• Red pepper
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Ensure cream cheese is at room temperature
2. Whip in a large bowl until smooth
3. Mix in salsa
4. Add in peppers and cheddar
5. Serve with taco chips<|endoftext|>
