Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Coconut Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Unsweetened coconut milk
• Butter
• Coconut
• Ice cream topping
• Toasted almond
• Sweetened whipped cream

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and a dash of salt
2. Make a well in center of flour mixture
3. Set aside
4. In another medium bowl lightly beat eggs
5. Stir in coconut milk and melted butter
6. Stir in coconut
7. Add egg mixture all at once to flour mixture
8. Stir just until moistened
9. Pour 1 / 2 cup batter onto grids of a preheated, lightly greased waffle baker
10. Close lid quickly
11. Do not open until done
12. Bake according to manufacturer's directions
13. When done, use a fork to lift waffle off grid
14. Repeat with remaining batter
15. Drizzle baked waffles with topping, sprinkle with almonds, and top with whipped cream or dust with powdered sugar
16. Serve warm
