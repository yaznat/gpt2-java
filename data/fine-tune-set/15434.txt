Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Parmesan cheese
• Pizza sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Pre-bake the pizza crust in a 400 degrees oven until golden brown and baked
2. Place the pizza crust on a pizza pan or baking sheet
3. In a skillet cook the ground beef with onion and garlic until browned drain fat then season with seasoned salt, black pepper and 1 / 4 cup grated parmesan cheese
4. Add in the pizza sauce
5. Reduce heat to low and cook stirring for 5 minutes
6. Top the cusrt with the cooked ground beef mixture, then sprinkle with cheddar cheese, then mozzeralla cheese
7. Top with cooked crumbled bacon
8. Bake 400 degrees for 8-12 minutes<|endoftext|>
