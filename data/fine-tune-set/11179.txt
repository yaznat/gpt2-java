What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Balsamic Glazed Chicken And Bell Pepper Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Chicken breast tenders
• Balsamic vinegar
• Red bell peppers
• Onions
• Focaccia bread
• Provolone cheese
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Heat 2 teaspoons oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
2. Sprinkle 1 / 4 teaspoon salt over chicken
3. Add chicken to pan
4. Cook 1 minute on each side or until lightly browned
5. Add 1 / 4 cup vinegar
6. Cook 2 minutes or until chicken is done and vinegar is syrupy
7. Remove chicken mixture from the pan
8. Cover and keep warm
9. Wipe pan clean with a paper towel
10. Return pan to medium-high heat
11. Add remaining 2 teaspoons oil
12. Add bell pepper and onion
13. Saut 7 minutes or until tender
14. Stir in remaining 1 / 4 teaspoon salt and remaining 1 / 4 cup vinegar
15. Cook 1 minute or until vinegar is syrupy
16. Cut foccaccia loaves in half horizontally
17. Arrange chicken mixture evenly over bottom halves of bread
18. Top with bell pepper mixture
19. Arrange cheese over pepper mixture, and sprinkle with black pepper
20. Top with top halves of bread
21. Place a cast-iron or heavy skillet on top of sandwiches
22. Let stand 5 minutes
23. Cut each sandwich into 6 wedges<|endoftext|>
