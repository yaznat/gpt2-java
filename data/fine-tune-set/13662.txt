Give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>90 Minute Pork Side Ribs

Ingredients:
• Pork side ribs
• Water
• Lawry's seasoned salt
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Place the ribs in a large pot and cover with water
2. Add in seasoned salt, black pepper and garlic powder
3. Bring to a boil and simmer for about 20-25 minutes or until the meat is tender but not falling off the bone
4. Drain, then place the ribs on a large plate
5. Cover ribs loosely with foil and allow to cool for about 30 minutes or more
6. At this point you may refrigerate for up to 24 hours or until ready to cook
7. Heat grill to medium-high
8. Place the ribs on the grill and cook for about 10-15 minutes or until desired doneness, turning several times and brushing with barbecue sauce
9. Place the boiled ribs on a broiler rack on a baking sheet that has been lined with foil for easy clean-up
10. Brush both sides of the ribs with barbecue sauce
11. Bake until browned turning once you may brush with more sauce while baking if desired<|endoftext|>
