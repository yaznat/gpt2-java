What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Baked Fish With Orange

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Fresh breadcrumbs
• Garlic cloves
• White fish fillets
• Orange
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in frying pan, add the crumbs, garlic and grated orange rind
2. Stir until crumbs have absorbed all the butter
3. Place fish portions in a buttered ovenproof dish, sprinkle with the breadcrumbs, then pour over the orange juice
4. Bake uncovered for 20-30 minutes in moderate oven<|endoftext|>
