Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Zatarain's dry mix for coating meats
• Flour
• Egg
• Water
• Oil
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Butterfly the chicken breasts
2. Then cut them down the middle to separate the breast halves
3. Next cut into the shape of chicken fries
4. With meat scissors or a knife
5. Put flour and fish fri into separate resealable plastic bags
6. Lightly beat the egg with one tablespoon water
7. Shake four chicken fries at a time in the flour, shake off the excess, dip in egg wash
8. Shake off the excess, then shake in fish fry
9. Place the chicken fries on a plate to reserve for frying
10. Heat one inch of oil in a skillet to medium hot
11. Fry chicken fries in batches so they do not touch, turning, until they are golden brown
12. Drain chicken fries on paper towels and serve with dipping sauce of your choice<|endoftext|>
