Can you give me a custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Grand Marnier Custard Sauce

Ingredients:
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Heavy cream
• Half-and-half
• Grand marnier
• Orange zest

Steps:
1. Beat egg yolks and sugar with whisk in medium saucepan until light in colour, about 5 minutes
2. Scald heavy cream and half and half in separate saucepan
3. Stir hot cream gradually into yolks, whisking constantly
4. Cook, stirring constantly, over medium-low heat, until sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon
5. Do not allow to boil
6. Remove from heat
7. Stir in grand marnier and orange zest
8. Cool to room temperature
9. Place plastic wrap directly onto surface and refrigerate until cold<|endoftext|>
