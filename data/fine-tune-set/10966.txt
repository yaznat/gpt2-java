What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Ambrosia Salad Fruit Salad

Ingredients:
• Mandarin oranges
• Pineapple chunks
• Oranges
• Green apple
• Red apple
• Plain nonfat yogurt
• Sugar
• Salt
• Flaked coconut
• Mini marshmallows

Steps:
1. In large bowl, combine mandarin oranges, pineapple, orange sections, and sliced apples
2. In small bowl, whisk together 2 tbs
3. Juice reserved from mandarin oranges, yogurt, sugar, and salt
4. Pour over fruit and gently toss to coat
5. Sprinkle with coconut and marshmallows<|endoftext|>
