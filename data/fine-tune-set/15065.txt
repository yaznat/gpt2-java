Ingredients:
• Garlic
• Water
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Ricotta cheese
• Egg
• Fontina cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Basil pesto
• Boboli pizza crusts
• Sun-dried tomato
• Tomatoes
• Zucchini
• Small shrimp
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Set oven to 375 degrees
2. Leaving the heads whole, peel off as much of the papery skin as possible
3. Place the whole garlic heads in an oven-proof dish, large enough to hold them in a single layer
4. Pour the 1 / 2 cup water in the dish, sprinkle with salt, and drizzle with olive oil
5. Cover tightly with foil
6. Bake in in a preheated oven for 45-60 minutes, or until the garlic is easily pierced with a knife
7. Remove from the oven
8. Let sit until cool enough to handle
9. Squeeze out the garlic cloves from there skin into a small bowl
10. Mash into a paste
11. Raise the oven temperature to 400 degrees
12. In a medium bowl, mix together the ricotta cheese, egg, fontina, parmesan cheese and pesto until smooth
13. Place the boboli crusts on 2 large baking sheets
14. Spread a thin layer of the garlic paste, over the prepared crusts
15. Spread an even amount of the ricotta mixture over the garlic paste
16. Sprinkle both pizzas evenly with the sun-dried tomatoes, canned diced tomatoes, zucchini, shrimp and mozza cheese
17. Bake for about 15 minutes, or until the cheese is melted
18. Cut into wedges, and serve-- delicious!<|endoftext|>
