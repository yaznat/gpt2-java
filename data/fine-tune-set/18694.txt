What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Rice Pudding

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Cooked rice
• Raisins
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. In ungreased 2 quart casserole combine eggs, milk, sugar, vanilla, and 1 / 4 teaspoon salt
2. Beat until combined but not foamy
3. Stir in cooked rice and raisins
4. Place casserole in a 13x9x2-inch baking dish on an oven rack
5. Pour boiling water into baking dish around the casserole to a depth of 1 inch
6. Bake in a 325 degree oven for 30 minutes
7. Stir well
8. Sprinkle with nutmeg and cinnamon
9. Bake for 20 to 30 minutes more or until knife inserted near the center comes out clean
10. Serve warm or cold
11. To serve cold, cover and chill pudding for up to 3 days<|endoftext|>
