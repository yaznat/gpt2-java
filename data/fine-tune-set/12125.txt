Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Pie

Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Cream of tartar
• Sugar
• Egg yolks
• Whipping cream
• Fresh lemon juice
• Fresh lemon rind

Steps:
1. First step: add cream of tatar to egg whites and beat til stiff, slowly add sugar
2. Spread into a pie pan
3. Bake at 300 degrees for 1 hour& 15 minute
4. Pie filling: beat egg yolks until thick& have lemon color, add lemon juice, rinds& sugar
5. Cook over low heat stirring constately
6. Till thick, let cool
7. Beat 1 cup whipping cream til siff and spread half into pie shell then pour cooled filling on top of whip cream then the rest of whipping cream
8. Makes 1 pie<|endoftext|>
