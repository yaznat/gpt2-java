What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Crock Pot Greek Lamb Artichoke Onion Stew

Ingredients:
• Lamb shoulder
• Yellow onions
• Tomato puree
• Dry red wine
• Red wine vinegar
• Garlic
• Cinnamon stick
• Salt
• Pepper
• Sugar
• Ground allspice
• Cumin
• Dried oregano
• Artichoke hearts
• Fresh parsley
• Walnuts
• Feta cheese

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients in a large slow cooker
2. Cover and cook on low for 8 to 10 hours or until lamb and onions are tender
3. Stir in artichoke hearts
4. Cover and let stand 5 minutes
5. Remove the cinnamon stick
6. Serve sprinkled with walnuts and feta<|endoftext|>
