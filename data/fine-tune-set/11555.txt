Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Creamy peanut butter
• Powdered sugar
• Vanilla
• Milk
• Devil's food cake mix
• Vegetable oil
• Sour cream
• Eggs
• Light cream

Steps:
1. For the filling: beat the cream cheese and peanut butter until combined
2. Add the powdered sugar and vanilla and beat until combined
3. Beat in milk, 1 t at a time, until you reach desired consistency
4. Set aside
5. For the cake: preheat oven to 350 degrees
6. Spray a 12 cup bundt pan with cooking spray for baking
7. Mix cake mix, oil, sour cream and eggs in a bowl
8. Beat 1 or 2 minutes at medium speed
9. Reserve 2 cups of the batter
10. Pour remaining batter into the prepared pan
11. Put peanut butter filling in a ring on top of batter
12. Spoon reserved batter on top
13. Bake for 40-50 minutes
14. Cool 20 minutes and remove from pan onto wire rack

16. Cream until smooth
