What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Fish With Tomatoes

Ingredients:
• Cod fish fillet
• Butter
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Flour
• Brown sugar
• Dried marjoram
• Dried thyme
• Diced tomatoes with juice
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat your over to 400 degrees
2. Spray a baking dish with non-stick spray
3. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat
4. Add onion and pepper and saute until softened
5. Stir in flour, brown sugar, herbs, salt and pepper to taste, and combine well
6. Gradually stir in tomatoes with juice
7. Bring sauce to a boil
8. Keep stiring until sauce has slightly thickened
9. Place fish fillets in baking dish and top with the sauce
10. Place dish in oven and bake for 12-15 minutes or until fish is cooked through and flakes easily<|endoftext|>
