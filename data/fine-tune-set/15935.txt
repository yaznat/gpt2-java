Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Crunchy Burritos

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Ground coriander
• Salt
• Dried oregano
• Garlic powder
• Paprika
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Refried beans
• Flour tortillas
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Canola oil
• Salsa

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook beef and onion over medium heat until no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, coriander, salt, oregano, garlic powder and paprka
4. Combine cornstarch and water until smooth
5. Stir into beef mixture
6. Bring to a boil
7. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened
8. Serve with taco shells
9. Garnish with toppings if desired
10. In a small saucepan, cook refried beans over medium-low heat for 2-3 minutes or until heated through
11. Spoon about 1 / 3 cup of beans off-center on each tortilla
12. Top with about 2 rounded tablespoons of beef mixture
13. Sprinkle with cheese
14. Fold sides and ends of tortilla over filling and roll up
15. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown burritos in oil on all sides
16. Serve with sour cream and salsa<|endoftext|>
