What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Banana Buttermilk Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Flour
• Light-brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Egg
• Unsalted butter
• Canola oil
• Banana
• Maple syrup
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 250f
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine flours, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt
3. Whisk in buttermilk, egg and butter until batter is well combined and free of lumps
4. In a large non-stick skillet or griddle, swirl oil to coat bottom of pan, working in batches add batter in 1 / 4-cup portions, cook until golden brown and tiny bubbles form evenly on top, 2 to 5 minutes
5. Top with banana slices, dividing evenly
6. Flip pancakes and continue cooking until lightly puffed, 2 to 5 minutes, transfer to baking sheet
7. Keep warm in oven while you cook remaining pancakes: serve warm with maple syrup and walnuts, if desired<|endoftext|>
