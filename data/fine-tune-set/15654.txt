What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Bulk Taco Seasoning Mix

Ingredients:
• Dried onion
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Cornstarch
• Dried garlic
• Cumin
• Red pepper flakes
• Beef bouillon granules
• Dried oregano

Steps:
1. Combine the ingredients
2. Store in an airtight container in a cool dry place for up to 1 year
3. Add 2 tablespoons to 1 lb cooked and drained beef and 1 / 3-1 / 2 cup water and cook until done<|endoftext|>
