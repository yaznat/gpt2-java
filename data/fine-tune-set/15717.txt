What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Chicken Taco Rice

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken
• Oil
• Water
• Tomato sauce
• Taco seasoning mix
• Frozen sweet corn
• Red bell pepper
• Minute rice
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook and stir chicken in hot oil until lightly browned
2. Add water, tomato sauce and seasoning mix
3. Bring to a boil
4. Reduce heat, cover and simmer 5 minutes
5. Add corn and red bell pepper
6. Bring to a boil
7. Stir in rice
8. Cover
9. Remove from heat
10. Let stand 5 minutes
11. Fluff rice with a fork
12. Sprinkle top with cheese
13. Serve with tortilla chips or hot floured tortillas, if desired<|endoftext|>
