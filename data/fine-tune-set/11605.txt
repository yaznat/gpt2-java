What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>1940 S Best Carrot Cake Recipe

Ingredients:
• White sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Carrots
• Walnuts
• Confectioners' sugar
• Cream cheese
• Vanilla extract
• Butter
• Milk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. In large bowl, beat sugar and oil
3. Add eggs and beat well
4. Sift together flour, soda, cinnamon, and salt into egg mixture
5. Mix well
6. Fold in carrots and nuts
7. Place batter in greased 9x13 pan
8. Bake for 45 minutes
9. Frosting: beat together powdered sugar, cream cheese, vanilla, butter and milk
10. Spread on cooled carrot cake<|endoftext|>
