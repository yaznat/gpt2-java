Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Buttermilk
• Oil
• Eggs
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Blueberry jam
• Powdered sugar
• Fresh lemon juice

Steps:
1. Beat together eggs, oil and sugar
2. Stir in salt, baking powder and flour, but do not overmix!
3. Pour 1 / 2 the mixture into a well greased and floured loaf pan
4. Spread blueberry jam over batter
5. Top with remaining batter, and bake at 350f until a toothpick can be inserted and withdrawn cleanly
6. Allow to rest for 5 minutes
7. Meanwhile:
8. Mix powdered sugar and lemon juice and beat until smooth
9. Remove cake from pan, and pour icing over to top
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
