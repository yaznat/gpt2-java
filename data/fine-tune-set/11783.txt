Ingredients:
• Red chilies
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Lemongrass
• Ground turmeric
• Chili powder
• Aubergines
• Olive oil
• Sugar
• Shallots
• Soy sauce
• Coconut milk
• Vegetable stock

Steps:
1. Using a food processor, pulse the chilies, garlic, ginger and lemongrass to a coarse paste
2. Set aside
3. Mix the turmeric and chili powder together and rub it all over the aubergine wedges
4. Heat the olive oil in frying pan,
5. Brown the aubergines
6. Remove from the pan and set aside
7. Cook the paste, sugar and shallots for a few mins, then return the aubergines to the pan
8. Add the fish sauce, coconut milk and stock or water, mix well,
9. Bring the liquid to the boil
10. Reduce the heat and cook gently until the aubergine is tender, but not mushy, about 15 minutes
11. Season to taste and sprinkle the coriander over the top
12. Serve hot with steamed rice or bread to mop up all the juices<|endoftext|>
