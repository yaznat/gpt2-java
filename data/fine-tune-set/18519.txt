Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Ham And Cheese Waffles

Ingredients:
• Cheese
• Deli ham
• Eggs
• Milk
• Lemon juice
• Oil
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Shred cheese finely
2. I used colby jack but you can use the kind you like
3. Finely chop deli ham
4. Mix cheese, ham, eggs, milk, lemon juice, oil, flour, baking powder, and salt together
5. Bake in a waffle iron<|endoftext|>
