Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Basic Fried Rice With Variations

Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Green onions
• Oil
• Soy sauce
• Salt
• Sugar
• Sherry wine
• Eggs
• Roast pork
• Roast beef
• Lamb
• Duck
• Turkey
• Chicken
• Boiled ham
• Bacon

• Crabmeat

• Green peas
• Lettuce

• Almonds
• Peanuts
• Walnuts
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Dice or shred meat and vegetables
2. Heat oil in wok
3. Stir-fry green onions
4. Add fresh vegetables and stir-fry to soften
5. Add canned vegetables and meat
6. Stir-fry to heat and blend flavors
7. Remove from wok
8. Heat some more oil if needed, to smoking
9. Add rice and heat through completely
10. Return vegetables and meat to wok, add soy sauce, salt, sugar and sherry
11. Fold in eggs and turn off heat just as they begin to set
12. Suggested combinations:
13. Beef, onions and green pepper
14. Bacon, onions, lettuce and tomatoes
15. Chicken, onions and bean sprouts
16. Chicken, bamboo shoots, mushrooms and cucumber<|endoftext|>
