Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Walnuts
• Pure vanilla extract
• Evaporated milk
• Marshmallow creme
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Milk chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Brownie directions---------------
2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
3. Butter a 13x9 inch pan
4. Melt butter and chocolate over low heat, stir until smooth and melted
5. Cool
6. Beat sugar and eggs in mixing bowl until fluffy
7. Slowly whisk cooled chocolate mixture into egg mixture
8. Stir in flour, walnuts, and vanilla
9. Spread batter in pan, bake for 25-30 minutes until set
10. Prepare fudge topping
11. Upon removal of the brownies from the oven, immediately pour fudge into the pan
12. Cool, then place in freezer until firm
13. Cut into 1 inch squares, using a very sharp knife
14. Frequently dipping the knife in a glass of hot water and wiping off the knife after every pass of the knife, will make perfect, smooth, sharp edged pieces
15. Fudge directions--------------
16. Combine sugar, butter, and evaporated milk in very large pot
17. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly
18. Boil 5 minutes
19. Remove from heat
20. Add remaining ingredients, beat until smooth
