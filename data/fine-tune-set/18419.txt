Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Chocolate Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Cocoa powder
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Strawberry
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine flour, cocoa, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt
2. In a medium bowl, or a large measuring cup, thoroughly whisk together eggs, buttermilk, melted butter and vanilla extract
3. Add to flour mixture and whisk until just smooth
4. Preheat waffle iron and make according to directions
5. Serve immediately
6. Garnish with whipped cream and strawberries!<|endoftext|>
