What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Baked Cheese And Stuffing Casserole

Ingredients:
• Water
• Margarine
• Herb seasoned stuffing mix
• Egg
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Bring water and margarine to a boil
3. Stir in stuffing mix
4. Cover
5. Remove from heat and let stand 5min
6. Stir egg and 1 / 2c of the cheese into stuffing mixture until blended
7. Spoon into a greased 9-inch pie plate
8. Sprinkle with remaining cheese
9. Bake 20min or until cheese on top is melted<|endoftext|>
