What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Muffins With Sauce

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• Flour
• Salt
• Boiling water
• Baking soda
• Vanilla
• Tart apple
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Muffins: combine all ingredients in order given
2. Fill greased muffin tins 2 / 3 full
3. Bake at 350 degrees for 20 to 25 minutes
4. Sauce: mix brown sugar and flour together in small saucepan
5. Add water, butter, and vanilla
6. Cook over medium-low heat until thickened
7. Serve hot sauce over muffins<|endoftext|>
