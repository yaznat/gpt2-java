Ingredients:
• Pastry for double-crust pie
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Allspice
• Apples
• Raisins
• Orange juice
• Butter
• Powdered sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Pie
2. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
3. Combine all dry ingredients
4. Mix dry ingredients with apples and raisins
5. Pour into bottom crust
6. Sprinkle with orange juice
7. Dot with butter
8. Add top crust
9. Flute edges & cut steam vents
10. Bake about 40 minutes or until crust is browned
11. Cool before frosting
12. Frosting
13. Mix sugar and butter
14. Add milk a tsp at a time until spreading consistency<|endoftext|>
