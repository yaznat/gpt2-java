Ingredients:
• Light brown sugar
• Butter
• Almond extract
• Frozen yeast dinner roll dough
• Vanilla instant pudding mix
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Heat brown sugar, butter, and almond extract in microwave safe bowl on high for one minute
2. Stir til blended and microwave for an additional one minute
3. Arrange frozen rolls in a lightly greased 10" bundt pan
4. Drizzle sugar mixture evenly over rolls
5. Sprinkle with vanilla pudding mix and almonds
6. Cover and let stand for 3 hours
7. Baked uncovered at 350 degrees for 30 minutes or until brown
8. Remove from oven and let stand for 10 minutes in pan before inverting onto serving plate
9. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
