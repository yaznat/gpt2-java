Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Leeks
• Shallots
• Garlic cloves
• Carrots
• Turnips
• Celery
• Red potatoes
• Fresh rosemary
• Fresh sage
• Fresh thyme
• Beef stock
• Beer
• Bay leaves
• Cayenne pepper
• Fresh cracked pepper

Steps:
1. Chop the leeks, shallots, garlic, carrots, turnips, celery, and potato and place in the bottom of a crock pot
2. Pick the leaves from the rosemary, sage, and thyme, and finely chop
3. Place in the crockpot
4. Place the beef on top of the veggies and herbs
5. Pour the stock and the beer over everything - it should just about cover
6. Crack the black pepper and sprinkle in the cayenne
7. Place the bay leaves into the crock pot, 1 / 4 inch under the surface
8. Cook on low for 8-10 hours, or high for 5 hours if you're in a hurry<|endoftext|>
