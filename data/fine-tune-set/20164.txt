Ingredients:
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Instant beef bouillon
• Cornstarch
• Vegetable oil
• Fresh asparagus
• Boneless beef sirloin
• Yellow bell peppers
• Onion
• Carrot
• Fresh mushrooms
• Green onions
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Combine water, soy sauce, bouillon and cornstarch in a small bowl
2. Heat oil in large skillet
3. Add asparagus, beef, bell pepper, onion and carot
4. Cook, stirring frequently, until beef is no longer pink
5. Add mushrooms and green onions
6. Cook until tender
7. Stir in soy sauce mixture
8. Cook, stirring constantly, until sauce comes to a boil and thickens
9. Serve over rice
10. Season with ground black pepper<|endoftext|>
