What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Fried Rice Ii

Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Soy sauce
• Long grain rice
• Salad oil
• Chicken broth
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Celery
• Eggs
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Combine chicken, soy sauce and 1 / 2 teaspoon salt
2. Let stand 15 minutes
3. Cook rice in hot oil in skillet over medium heat until golden brown
4. Stir frequently
5. Reduce heat, add chicken with soy sauce and broth
6. Simmer, covered, 20 to 25 minutes, or until rice is tender
7. Remove cover last few minutes
8. Stir in onion, green pepper and celery
9. Cook uncovered over medium heat until liquid is absorbed
10. Push rice mixture to sides of skillet
11. Add eggs
12. Cook until almost set
13. Blend into rice mixture
14. Stir in lettuce
15. Serve at once with soy sauce<|endoftext|>
