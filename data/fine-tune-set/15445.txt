Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Ground chuck
• Onion
• Garlic
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Diced tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese soup
• Dill pickle relish
• Worcestershire sauce
• French-fried onions

Steps:
1. In a large dutch oven, cook bacon until crisp
2. Remove from pan, and drain on paper towels
3. Add ground chuck, onion, and garlic
4. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, or until beef is browned and crumbly
5. Drain well
6. Add chicken broth, tomatoes, soup, relish, bacon and worcestershire sauce
7. Cook for 10 to 15 minutes, or until soup is heated through
8. Garnish with french-fried onions if desired<|endoftext|>
