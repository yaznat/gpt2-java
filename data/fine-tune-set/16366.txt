Ingredients:
• Water chestnuts
• Lean bacon
• Brown sugar
• Ketchup
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
2. If desired, cut larger water chestnuts in half for easier eating and more pieces
3. Soak water chestnuts in soy sauce for 15 minutes, stirring a few times to make sure they are fully coated
4. Remove water chestnuts from soy sauce
5. Wrap 1 / 2 strip of bacon around each
6. Secure with toothpicks
7. Reserve soy sauce
8. Place toothpick side "up" in a 9" x 13" glass baking dish
9. Bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 20 to 30 minutes or until crispy
10. Remove from oven
11. Drain bacon fat
12. Meanwhile, mix reserved soy sauce, ketchup and brown sugar in a bowl
13. Pour the mixture over the chestnuts to coat them evenly
14. Bake an additional 20 to 30 minutes
15. Serve hot, with or without seafood sauce<|endoftext|>
