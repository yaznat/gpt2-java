What's a recipe for a wrap?<|endoftext|>A Fun And Frolic Kind Of Avocado Bacon And Tomato Wrap Yippee

Ingredients:
• Cooked bacon
• Tomatoes
• Green onion
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Lettuce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Avocado
• Tabasco sauce

Steps:
1. Cook bacon your preferred method
2. Could be in microwave, iron skillet, or baked
3. Warm tortilla's or pita in microwave for 10 seconds, or in oven wrapped in foil for 10 minutes at 350degrees
4. As tortilla's are warmed, spread sour cream, or mayonnaise thinly on inner part
5. Add cheese lightly
6. Add lettuce, tomato, bacon, green onion, and avocado nicely and roll carefully
7. Add salt and pepper, and devour at one end
8. Make sure your shoes are off, and you have a candle lit
9. It makes this extra special
10. Try it and see<|endoftext|>
