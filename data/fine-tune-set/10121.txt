Ingredients:
• Jalapeno peppers
• Red bell peppers
• Ground beef
• Kidney beans
• Tomato paste
• Ro-tel tomatoes
• Water
• Sugar
• Cocoa powder
• Chili powder
• Paprika
• Cumin
• Ground oregano
• Garlic powder
• Bay leaf
• Coarse salt
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Roast red bell peppers and jalapenos: turn on your broiler
2. Lower the rack so the peppers will be about 6 inches below your heat source
3. Spray the peppers with cooking spray or rub them with olive oil and place on broiler pan
4. Put them under the broiler and char the outside a bit, turning them every few minutes
5. When they look done to you, remove them from the oven and put them in a bowl with a lid for at least 5 minutes
6. Before handling the jalapenos, you may want to put gloves on to protect your skin from their spicy oil
7. Cut the tops off your jalapenos
8. Slice them lengthwise and remove the seeds and membranes
9. Chop jalapenos
10. Remove the skin and seeds from the bell peppers
11. Chop the peppers and set them aside to add to your chili pot later
12. Brown the ground beef
13. Remove the excess oil
14. Add the chopped jalapenos and all other ingredients
15. Stir and taste
16. Add salt, pepper, or other seasonings if you wish
17. Simmer on low with a lid on your pot until the flavors are blended well
