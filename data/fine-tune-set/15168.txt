What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Basic Pizza Dough Bread Machine

Ingredients:
• Water
• Olive oil
• Flour
• Salt
• Sugar
• Bread machine yeast

Steps:
1. Combine ingredients in bread machine pan
2. If desired, season with parsley, oregano, basil, rosemary, onion flakes, and garlic
3. Set bread machine on dough cycle
4. Roll dough to desired thickness, cover with favorite toppings, and bake at 375 degrees f for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
