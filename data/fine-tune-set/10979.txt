Give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Immoral Sandwich Filling Loose Meat

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Sugar
• Prepared yellow mustard
• Beer
• Cayenne
• Garlic
• Salt & pepper
• American cheese

Steps:
1. Brown the meat & drain fat
2. Stir in sugar, mustard, beer, cayenne, garlic and salt & pepper to taste
3. Simmer until liquid has mostly cooked away
4. Lay slices of cheese over top of meat, then cover for about 5 minutes, with heat on very low, until cheese has melted
5. Stir cheese into meat
6. Serve hot, on sandwich buns or bread, or over hot potatoes or rice<|endoftext|>
