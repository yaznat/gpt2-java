Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Salt
• Ground nutmeg
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Butter
• Onions
• Granny smith apples
• Garlic cloves
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
2. Sprinkle the chicken evenly with salt, nutmeg, and pepper
3. Add chicken to pan
4. Saut 7 minutes or until golden
5. Remove the chicken from pan
6. Keep warm
7. Melt butter in pan over medium heat
8. Add onion
9. Cook 4 minutes or until tender, stirring frequently
10. Add apple
11. Cook 6 minutes or until golden, stirring frequently
12. Add garlic
13. Cook 30 seconds, stirring constantly
14. Return chicken to pan
15. Cook 2 minutes or until thoroughly heated, stirring frequently
16. Heat tortillas according to package directions
17. Arrange 1 / 2 cup chicken mixture evenly over each tortilla<|endoftext|>
