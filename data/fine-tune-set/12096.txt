Generate a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Amazingly Easy Pie Crusts

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Lard
• Unsalted butter
• Water

Steps:
1. Cut the butter into cubes, and then toss the butter and lard into flour
2. Using your hands, crumble it up into a fine, pebbly, grainy sort of dough
3. Add water, enough to make soft dough
4. Form into a ball, and chill for about an hour
5. When chilled, knead it a couple of times on a floured surface
6. Roll it out, and proceed to fill with your choice of pie fillings<|endoftext|>
