Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• Taco seasoning mix
• Lettuce
• Black olives
• Tomatoes
• Green onion
• Green pepper
• Mexican blend cheese

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl blend the following ingredients: cream cheese, sour cream, mayonnaise
2. Stir in dry ortega taco seasoning mix
3. Spread in 9x13-inch pan and top with any / all of the optional ingredients listed above
4. Portions should be to your liking-- put on as little or as much as you would like
5. Serve with tostitos white corn chips!<|endoftext|>
