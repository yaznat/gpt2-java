Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Asian Beef Salad

Ingredients:
• Baby spinach
• Cucumber
• Carrots
• Bean sprouts
• Beef flank steak
• Cashews
• Light asian vinaigrette

Steps:
1. Marinate the flank steak overnight in your choice of teriyaki or asian marinades
2. Broil flank steak and slice thinly
3. Arrange spinach on plates, then top with carrots, bean sprouts and steak
4. Drizzle dressing and sprinkle cashews on top
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
