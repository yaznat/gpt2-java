Please give me a recipe for coffee.<|endoftext|>Amarula Coffee Surprise

Ingredients:
• Coffee
• Whipped cream
• Amarula cream liqueur
• Marshmallows
• Brown sugar
• Hot chocolate powder
• Chocolate

Steps:
1. Pour the coffee in large coffee mugs
2. Pour the amarula cream in each coffee, followed by the whipped cream, brown sugar and marshmallows
3. Sprinkle with hot chocolate powder and extra amarula cream
4. Top with grated chocolate if desired
5. Conversions: 1 tot = 30ml = 1 oz = 3cl = 1 shot<|endoftext|>
