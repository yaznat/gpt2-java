Ingredients:
• Flour
• Bread dough
• Tart apples
• Brown sugar
• White sugar
• Apple pie spice
• Nutmeg
• Butter
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f degrees
2. Spray a cookie sheet with nonstick spray
3. Sprinkle work surface with flour, roll out bread dough until slightly larger than the sheet
4. Place dough on sheet, stretching to fit, roll edges slightly to form a shallow rim
5. Spread apples evenly over dough
6. In a small bowl, combine sugars, 2 tbls flour, cinnamon and nutmeg
7. Mix well and sprinkle over apples
8. Dot with the butter
9. Sprinkle with cheese evenly over
10. Bake until the apples are tender and the cheese is bubbly, 25-35 minutes
11. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
