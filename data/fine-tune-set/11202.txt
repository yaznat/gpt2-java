What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Bologna Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Barbecue sauce
• Ketchup
• Light bologna
• White bread

Steps:
1. Mix together barbecue sauce and ketchup
2. Thin with just a little water
3. Place bologna slice in skillet over medium heat
4. Cook on both sides a few minutes until bologna is slightly browned and begins to curl up
5. Spread sauce mixture on bologna and cook about 1 minute more
6. Place on bread to make a sandwich
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
