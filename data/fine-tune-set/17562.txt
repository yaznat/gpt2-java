What's a good coffee recipe?<|endoftext|>Butterscotch Coffee

Ingredients:
• Butterscotch chips
• Brewed coffee
• Half-and-half cream
• Sugar
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. In a microwave-safe bowl, heat 1 / 2 cup butterscotch chips at 70% power for 2-3 minutes or until melted, stirring occasionally
2. Cut a small hole in the corner of a pastry or plastic bag
3. Insert a #4 round
4. Fill with melted chips
5. Pipe "mom" eight times onto a waxed paper-lined baking sheet
6. Refrigerate until set, about 10 minutes
7. In a large bowl, stir the coffee and remaining butterscotch chips until chips are melted
8. Stir in half-and-half and sugar
9. Serve in mugs with whipped cream and "mom" garnishes<|endoftext|>
