What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Bacon Tomato Sandwich With Cheddar

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onion hamburger buns
• Mayonnaise
• Country-style dijon mustard
• Tomatoes
• Red onion rings
• Cucumbers
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375f
2. Fry or microwave bacon until crispy
3. Place split onion buns on cookie sheet
4. In small bowl stir together mayonnaise and mustard
5. To assemble each sandwich, spread about 1 tablespoon of mayo mixture on each bun half
6. Top bottom half of bun with 1 tomato slice, 3 onion rings and 3 cucmber slices
7. Crisscross 2 slices cheddar cheese over bacon
8. Bake for 3 to 5 minutes or until cheese is melted
9. Top with remaining bun<|endoftext|>
