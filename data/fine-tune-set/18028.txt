Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Cheesecake Muffins

Ingredients:
• Reduced-fat cream cheese
• Sugar
• Walnut brownie mix
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Water
• Chocolate frosting
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, beat cream cheese and sugar until light and fluffy
2. Set aside
3. Spray a jumbo muffin tin with bakers joy or non-stick cooking spray and also set aside
4. Preheat oven to 350f
5. Make brownie mix according to the recipe on the box using the eggs oil and water
6. Spoon about 2 tbsp of batter into each greased muffin tray
7. Drop equal portions of cream cheese mixture into the center of each muffin, then top with remaining brownie batter
8. Bake for 20-30 minutes, or as package directs
9. Allow to cool, then spread chocolate frosting on top followed with a sprinkle of powdered sugar<|endoftext|>
