Give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>3 Day Coconut Cake

Ingredients:
• White cake mix
• Egg whites
• Oil
• Water
• Sour cream
• Frozen coconut
• Cool whip
• Granulated sugar

Steps:
1. The night before you make this cake, blend together the sour cream, coconut and sugar
2. Separate 1 cup of mixture and place in a separate bowl
3. Refrigerate both portions
4. The next day, prepare cake mix as per instructions on the back of the box, in two 8- or 9-inch round cake pans
5. Let cool
6. Using a long, serrated knife, split both layers in half horizontally, to end up with four layers
7. Place one layer of cake on the plate of a cake stand or cake saver that has a top
8. Spread all but the reserved 1 cup of coconut mixture between the layers
9. Mix cool whip together with the reserved 1 cup coconut mixture
10. Frost sides and top of cake with this mixture
11. Cover the cake and place in the refrigerator
12. Do not remove or eat for 3 days<|endoftext|>
