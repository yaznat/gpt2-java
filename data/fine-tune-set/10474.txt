Ingredients:
• Butter flavor shortening
• Crunchy peanut butter
• Granulated sugar
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Quick oats
• Raisins
• Granny smith apple
• Carrot
• Flaked coconut

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f place sheets of foil on countertop for cooling cookies
2. Combine shortening, peanut butter and sugars in large bowl
3. Beat at medium speed with electric mixer until blended
4. Beat in eggs
5. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda and cinnamon
6. Add gradually to creamed mixture at low speed
7. Beat until blended
8. Stir in oats, raisins, apple, carrot and coconut
9. Drop by measuring tablespoonfuls onto ungreased baking sheet
10. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes or until just brown around edges
11. Do not overbake
12. Cool 2 minutes on baking sheet
13. Remove cookies to foil to cool completely<|endoftext|>
