Ingredients:
• Cumin seed
• Dried apricots
• Pork fillets
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Sunflower oil
• Marsala
• Fresh rosemary

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 200c / fan 180c / gas 6
2. Lightly toast the cumin seeds in a frying pan
3. Slice the pork fillet in half horizontally, not cutting through completely, and open it like a book
4. Season the mean to taste with salt and freshly ground black pepper, spread the cumin-apicot mixture along the center of the fillet, then fold themeat back to envelop the stuffing
5. Heat the oil in a pan and brown the meat on all sides for about 5 minutes
6. Remove the pork and place on a roasting try and cover loosly with aluminum foil
7. Cook for 10-15 minutes, or until lightly pink
8. Meanwhile, add the wine and rosemary to the previously used pan and bring to the boil
9. Simmer for 5 minutes or until you have a sauce
10. Remove the meat from the oven and let rest for 5 minutes before carving into slices
11. Pour / add any remaining juices from the roasting tray into the sauce and mix well, remove the rosemary and pour over the stuffed pork slices when ready to serve
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
