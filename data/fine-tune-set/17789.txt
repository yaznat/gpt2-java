Ingredients:
• Egg
• Butter
• Sugar
• Plain yogurt
• Milk
• Quick-cooking oats
• Natural bran
• Apple
• Raisins
• Whole wheat flour
• White flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt

Steps:
1. In large bowl, combine egg, butter, sugar, yogurt, milk, oats, bran, apple, and dried fruit
2. Combine flours, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and salt
3. Add flour mixture to bran mixture
4. Stir just to mix
5. Bake at 400 degrees for 20-25 minutes<|endoftext|>
