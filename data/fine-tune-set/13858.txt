Give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>Awesome Ribs For Pork Or Beef

Ingredients:
• Pork ribs
• Yellow onions
• Lemon, juice of
• Ketchup
• Water
• Salt
• Brown sugar
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. Cut ribs into individual ribs, and place all the pieces in large roasting pan
2. Scatter the thickly sliced onions over the ribs
3. Sprinkle lemon juice over the ribs
4. Bake for one hour in an oven that has been pre-heated to 350 degrees
5. Pour off all the fat and juice that has accumulated
6. In the meantime, make the sauce
7. To make the sauce, combine the last five ingredients in a saucepan, and cook for 20-25 minutes
8. Stir frequently
9. You want the sauce to cook down and thicken up
10. Dip cooked ribs into the sauce, and place in roasting pan
11. It's best if you make a single layer of ribs at this stage so, if necessary, use more than one pan
12. Dip cooked onions into sauce, and scatter over ribs
13. At this stage, you can refrigerate the concoction for a day or two -- so the flavours can really meld
14. Cook ribs a second timefor 30-60 minutesin an oven that has been pre-heated to 325f if you have refrigerated the concoction, take out of the fridge at least two hours before the second cooking
15. Thanks to heystopthatnow for alerting me to the fact that i had omitted a cooking time for this step
16. Before the second cooking, baste the ribs
17. Then baste them again every 10 minutes
18. You want the ribs to be saucy
19. Serve with corn cobs, baked potatoes and salad
20. Garlic bread is nice, too<|endoftext|>
