Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Cold water
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Green pepper
• Tomatoes
• Sour cream
• Cheddar cheese
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Mix the bisquick and the water
3. Spread into a greased 8 x 8 baking dish
4. Brown the ground beef with the peppers, then drain
5. Add the taco seasoning
6. Set aside
7. Mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, and cheddar cheese
8. Add the ground beef to the top of the bisquick mix
9. Top with the tomatoes
10. Then spoon the cream mixture to the top of the tomatoes and spread evenly
11. Bake for 25-30 minutes<|endoftext|>
