Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Grilled 2 Potato Fries

Ingredients:
• Sweet potato
• Russet potato
• Vegetable oil
• Chipotle chile in adobo
• Salt
• Ground cumin

Steps:
1. Heat gas or charcoal grill
2. In medium bowl, combine potatoes and 1 tablespoon of the oil
3. Toss gently to coat
4. Place vegetables in grill basket
5. In small bowl, mix remaining tablespoon oil, the chiles, salt and cumin
6. Set aside
7. When grill is heated, place grill basket over medium-high heat
8. Cook 10 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until potatoes are lightly browned and tender
9. Place potatoes in serving bowl
10. Pour reserved oil mixture over top
11. Toss gently to coat<|endoftext|>
