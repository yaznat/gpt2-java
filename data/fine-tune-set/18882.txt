Ingredients:
• Cauliflower
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Butter
• Onion
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degres f
2. Cook cauliflower in boiling water and drain
3. In a small bowl, beat eggs and add milk
4. Mix well
5. Place cauliflower in a greased baking dish
6. Pour milk / egg mixture over it
7. Sprinkle with the remaining ingedients
8. Bake for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
