Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Simple Ham Or Pork Fried Rice W Sesame Oil No Wok Needed

Ingredients:
• Sesame oil
• Sweet onion
• Carrot
• Cooked ham
• Cooked white rice
• Eggs
• Frozen green pea
• Water chestnut
• Green onions
• Sesame seeds
• Kosher salt

Steps:
1. Preheat large skillet over medium high heat
2. Add sesame oil
3. Add sweet onion and carrots
4. Cook until the carrots are tender, about two minutes
5. Add the cooked ham and combine well
6. Add the rice, using a wooden spatula or spoon to separate the rice
7. Cook until the rice is well coated with the oil, adding a splash more oil if needed, but don't over do it or rice will come out oily
8. Push the rice mixture to the sides of the pan, leaving the center of the pan open to cook the egg
9. Pour in the beaten egg, cook in the center of the pan until well scrambled
10. Once the egg is cooked, stir in with the rice mixture
11. Add the water chestnuts, green onions, peas and sesame seeds
12. Stir to combine and season to taste with salt
13. Serve as is, or top with soy sauce, teriyaki sauce or my sons favorite, recipe #419060, depending on your tastes<|endoftext|>
