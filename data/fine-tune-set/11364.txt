Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Water
• Dry yeast
• Whole wheat flour
• Cornmeal
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Mahleb

Steps:
1. Combine sugar and warm water and then add dry yeast
2. Combine flours, salt in a big bowl
3. After the yeast has risen, about 10-15 minutes later, mix it with flours and also add olive oil
4. Let the dough rise twice
5. Divide the dough into 4 mini bread pieces and bake in a 400f oven<|endoftext|>
