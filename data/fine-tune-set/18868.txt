Give me a custard recipe.<|endoftext|>Bulgur Custard Bake

Ingredients:
• Reduced-calorie margarine
• Bulgar wheat
• Fat-free chicken broth
• Salt
• Onion
• Carrots
• Fresh parsley
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Skim milk
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, melt margerine
2. Add bulgar, stirring to coat
3. Add stock and salt and let simmer, covered, for 20 - 30 minutes stirring occasionally
4. Meanwhile, add carrots, onion, cheese and parsley to a 1
5. 5l casserole dish
6. Add bulgar and gently mix all ingredients
7. Beat together milk, eggs and oil
8. Pour over the ingredients in casserole dish and sprinkle with pepper
9. Cover and cook for 45 minutes at 350 degrees
10. Uncover and cook for an additional 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
