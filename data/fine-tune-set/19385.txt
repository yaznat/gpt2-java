Please give me a recipe for donuts.<|endoftext|>Filhos Portuguese Donuts

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Flour
• Eggs
• Butter
• Salt
• Sugar
• Oil

Steps:
1. Bring milk, butter and salt to boil
2. Remove from stove
3. Add flour and mix well
4. Add eggs, 1 at a time, beating well
5. Drop by teaspoonful into hot oil
6. Fry until brown
7. They will turn by themselves and pop open
8. Shake in a bag with sugar to coat<|endoftext|>
