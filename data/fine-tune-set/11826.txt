What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Curry With Apples And Raisins

Ingredients:
• Boneless beef sirloin
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Oil
• Bay leaf
• Curry powder
• Whole cloves
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Beef stock
• Apples
• Raisins
• Potatoes
• Couscous

Steps:
1. Combine flour, salt, and pepper in a ziploc bag
2. Add meat and shake until evenly coated
3. Heat oil in a skillet and brown meat in batches until caramelized
4. Remove from skillet and drain excess oil as needed
5. Saut onion with a splash of beef stock until softened
6. Add bay leaf, curry powder, cloves, splenda, and remaining stock
7. Bring to a simmer
8. Add meat and any accumulated juices
9. Cook, covered, over medium-low heat for an hour
10. Add fruit and potatoes to the pan and cook, covered, for another 45 minutes
11. Discard bay leaf, cloves, and garlic
12. Serve over the cooked couscous<|endoftext|>
