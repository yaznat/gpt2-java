What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Pineapple Appetizers Microwave

Ingredients:
• Pineapple chunks in juice
• Soy sauce
• Ground ginger
• Garlic powder
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Combine the 3 tablespoons juice, soy sauce, ginger and garlic powder in small bowl
2. Marinade the pineapple chunks in this for 1- 2 hours
3. Drain
4. Wrap a piece of bacon around each chunk
5. Secure with a toothpick
6. Line microwave carousel with several layers of paper towel
7. Arrange around the outer edge of carousel with space between each one
8. Microwave at 100% power until bacon is lightly browned
9. Serve<|endoftext|>
