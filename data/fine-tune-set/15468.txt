Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Breadcrumbs
• Evaporated milk
• Gravy
• Ketchup
• Kitchen bouquet
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Water

Steps:
1. Mix beef, onions, breadcrumbs, and milk
2. Form into patties and brown on stove top on medium
3. Place browned patties in 9x13 pan
4. Mix gravy ingredients together and pour over top of hamburgers
5. Cover and bake for 1 hour on 350 degrees
6. Serve with mashed potatoes and peas!<|endoftext|>
