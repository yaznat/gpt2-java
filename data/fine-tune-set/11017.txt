Ingredients:
• American cheese
• Tuna
• Sweet pickles
• Onions
• Hardboiled egg
• Green peppers
• Stuffed olives
• Miracle whip
• Hot dog buns

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except buns, mix lightly and spoon into hot dog buns
2. Wrap in tinfoil and bake at 250 degrees for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
