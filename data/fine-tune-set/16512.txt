What's a recipe for a smoothie?<|endoftext|>Apple Blueberry Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Apple
• Vanilla yogurt
• Milk
• Blueberries
• Ice cubes
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in blender and blend until smooth
2. Serve in a summery glass with a straw and garnished with a fresh berry or thin apple slice
3. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
