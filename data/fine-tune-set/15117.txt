Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Chicken breast
• Artichoke hearts
• Sun-dried tomato
• Ricotta cheese
• Red pepper flakes
• Basil pesto

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450
2. Prepare your pizza: top the crust with mozzarella, parmesan, chicken, artichoke hearts, and sun-dried tomatoes, in that order
3. Dollop the ricotta cheese all over the pizza
4. Sprinkle pizza with red pepper flakes, if using
5. Bake for 8-12 minutes, or until cheese is melted and pizza is warm
6. Remove pizza from oven and drizzle with little dollops of pesto sauce
7. Serve<|endoftext|>
