What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Chicken Livers

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Chicken liver
• Toothpick
• Garlic salt
• Honey

Steps:
1. Cut bacon strips in half
2. Wash and pat dry livers
3. Sprinkle livers with garlic salt if using
4. Wrap each liver in a halved slice of bacon and secure witha toothpick
5. Repeat
6. Set oven on broil
7. Place livers on a baking rack or sheet and broil 6-8 minutes on each side
8. Remove and serve hot with warm honey as a dipping option<|endoftext|>
