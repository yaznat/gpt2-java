Ingredients:
• Butter
• Peanut butter
• White sugar
• Light brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350
2. Grease an 11 x 7-inch baking pan
3. In a large microwave-safe bowl melt the butter
4. Whisk in both sugars, vanilla and peanut butter until well combined
5. Add in eggs
6. Whisk / mix until completely combined
7. In a small bowl mix together the flour, baking powder and salt
8. Add to the butter mixture and mix with a wooden spoon until combined
9. Add in chocolate chips or nuts
10. Transfer to the baking pan and spread out with a spoon
11. Bake for about 35 minutes, or until the bars test done<|endoftext|>
