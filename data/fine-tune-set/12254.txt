Ingredients:
• Walnuts
• Garlic
• Water
• Shoyu
• Rosemary
• Flax seeds
• Mushroom
• Sliced mushrooms

Steps:
1. Place walnuts, garlic water, nama shoyu, and rosemary in blender and blend until creamy
2. Add ground flax seed and blend well
3. Pour into a serving dish and add mushrooms<|endoftext|>
