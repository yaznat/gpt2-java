Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Feta cheese
• Fresh oregano
• 6-inch flour tortillas
• Ripe olives
• Diced pimentos
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Stir together the cream cheese, monterey jack cheese, feta cheese and oregano
2. Spread 1 / 4 of the filling onto one half of each tortilla
3. Top with olives, pimiento and green onion
4. Fold plain side over filling
5. Press gently to seal edges
6. Grill filled tortillas on the rack of an uncovered grill directly over medium heat for 1-2 minutes on each side or till golden brown and heated through
7. Cut each quesadilla into three wedges and serve warm<|endoftext|>
