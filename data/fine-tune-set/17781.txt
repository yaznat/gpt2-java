Ingredients:
• Fresh breadcrumb
• Garlic clove
• Butter
• Egg
• Water
• Unbleached flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Hard cheese
• Artichoke heart
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 390f
2. Grease a standard 12 cup muffin pan
3. Fry the breadcrumbs and garlic in the butter until just crisp
4. Break the egg into a measuring cup and beat it lightly with a fork
5. Add enough of the water to make a full 1 cup
6. Mix the egg and water with the flour, salt, baking powder that have been mixed in another bowl
7. Before stirring also add the cheese, artichoke and bacon
8. Stir together gently until just moistened
9. Place the batter in the prepared pan and top with the saved crumbs
10. Bake for 10 - 15 mins or until a tester comes out clean<|endoftext|>
