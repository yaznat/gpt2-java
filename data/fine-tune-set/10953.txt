Can you give me a salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Amaretto Spinach Salad

Ingredients:
• Spinach
• Bacon
• Salad oil
• Garlic cloves
• Red wine vinegar
• Lemon juice
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dry mustard
• Amaretto
• Salt and pepper
• Mushroom
• Red onion

Steps:
1. Wash spinach
2. Remove large stems and tear into bite-size pieces
3. Store in plastic bags lined with paper towels in refrigerator until ready to use
4. Place bacon in a large skillet
5. Cook over moderate heat, stirring occasionally, until bacon is crisp and all fat is rendered
6. Remove bacon, reserve 1 / 8 cup bacon fat, discard the rest
7. Return bacon fat to skillet and add oil, garlic, vinegar, lemon juice, worcestershire sauce, mustard, amaretto, salt and pepper
8. Simmer until blended
9. When ready to serve, place spinach, mushrooms, onion and reserved bacon in large salad bowl
10. If you like a wilted salad, pour the dressing over very hot
11. If not, let cool a bit before serving
12. Toss well<|endoftext|>
