Ingredients:
• Butter
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Sweetened flaked coconut
• Pure vanilla extract
• Unbaked pastry shells

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f in a large bowl using a hand held electric mixer on low speed, beat butter until just creamy
2. Add sugar, a little at a time until well mixed
3. Add eggs mixing just till well blended
4. Stir in the flour
5. Mix in a little amount of the milk at a time beating to blend
6. Add coconut and vanilla extract
7. Mix well
8. Pour into unbaked pie shell
9. Sprinkle top of pie with a small amount of coconut
10. Bake 40-60 minutes or until there is no jiggling when slightly shaken
11. Mine took 60 minutes, however
