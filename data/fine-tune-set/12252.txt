What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>Souper Easy Macaroni And Cheese

Ingredients:
• Nacho cheese soup
• Cheddar cheese
• Milk
• Prepared mustard
• Black pepper
• Elbow macaroni
• French-fried onions

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Stir together first 5 ingredients until blended
3. Stir in macaroni
4. Spoon mixture into a lightly greased 11x7 baking dish
5. Bake at 350f
6. For 25 minutes
7. Top with french fried onions and bake 5 more minutes<|endoftext|>
