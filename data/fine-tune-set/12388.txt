Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Chicken
• Onion
• Celery
• Carrot
• Garlic clove
• Bay leaf
• Dried thyme
• Celery seed
• Alphabet pasta
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except pasta, salt and pepper in slow cooker
2. Cover and cook on low 6-8 hours
3. Add pasta during the last 20 minutes
4. Discard bay leaf
5. Season to taste with salt and pepper<|endoftext|>
