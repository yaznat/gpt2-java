Give me a steak recipe.<|endoftext|>Bills Grilled Steaks

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic salt
• Pepper
• Basil
• Steaks

Steps:
1. Rub your chosen steak with olive oil
2. Add garlic salt
3. I use lowerys but any good brand will do
4. Put a good black pepper on steaks
5. Sprinkle lightly with dried basil
6. Let stand at room tempature for an hour or so
7. Cook and eat that baby
8. Spice measurements are app
9. Something i have done lately is buy a whole filet
10. Cut it in half and cook half with the seasonings
11. Then slice it thin about 1 / 4 inch
12. Serve it quickly so it does not get cold<|endoftext|>
