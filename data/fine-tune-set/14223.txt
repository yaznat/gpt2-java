Please give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Cold Teriyaki Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Soy sauce
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Sesame oil
• Sugar
• Scallions
• Sesame seeds
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Combine soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, ginger and sugar in a bowl
2. Cut tofu into 1 inch cubes
3. Marinate tofu cubes in sauce for 10 minutes
4. Top with scallions, sesame seeds and optional red pepper
5. Chill for 15 minutes
6. Delicious served over shreadded lettuce, or cabbage or bean sprouts<|endoftext|>
