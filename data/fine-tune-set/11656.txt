Ingredients:
• Flour
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Butter
• Salt
• Shortening
• Vanilla extract
• Lemon extract
• Carbonated lemon-lime beverage

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees f
2. Grease and flour 12-cup bundt pan
3. Combine flour and salt in bowl and set aside
4. Combine remaining ingredients except 7-up in large mixer bowl
5. Beat at medium speed until light and fluffy, scraping sides occasionally, 5 minutes
6. Reduce speed to low
7. Add dry ingredients and 7-up
8. Mix well
9. Pour into prepared bundt pan
10. Bake 1 1 / 2 hours @ 325 or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
11. Cool in pan 15 minutes
12. Remove from pan and cool completely<|endoftext|>
