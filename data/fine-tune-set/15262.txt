Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• Ground chicken
• Broccoli florets
• Goat cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Garlic
• Salt
• Pepper
• Dried thyme
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Saute ground chicken with a little olive oil, salt, pepper, and thyme until cooked
2. Press the two cloves of garlic and combine with some olive oil and brush over the pizza crust
3. Top the pizza with the chicken, broccoli, and cheeses
4. Bake at 450f until crust has browned and the mozzarella has melted<|endoftext|>
