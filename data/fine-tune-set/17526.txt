Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Almond paste
• Butter
• Egg
• Lemon juice
• Nonfat milk
• Blueberries
• Fresh lemon rind
• Cooking spray
• Sliced almonds
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Cake:
3. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a small bowl, stirring with a whisk
4. Place 1 / 2 cup sugar, almond paste, and 2 tablespoons butter in a large bowl
5. Beat with a mixer at medium speed until well blended
6. Add egg and lemon juice, beating well
7. Add flour mixture and fat-free milk alternately to sugar mixture, beginning and ending with flour mixture
8. Fold in blueberries and rind
9. Spoon batter into a 9-inch square baking pan coated with cooking spray
10. Topping:
11. Combine 1 / 4 cup sugar and remaining ingredients in a small bowl, tossing with a fork until moist
12. Sprinkle topping evenly over batter
13. Bake at 350 for 35 minutes or until a wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean
14. Cool in pan on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
