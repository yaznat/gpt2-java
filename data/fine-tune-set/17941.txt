Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Chocolate syrup
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Milk
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Miniature marshmallow

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, cream butter and sugar
2. Add the eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition
3. Beat in the chocolate syrup and vanilla
4. Add the flour and salt until blended
5. Pour into a greased 15x10x1-inch baking pan
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 20-25 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean
7. Cool on a wire rack for 15-20 minutes
8. Meanwhile, in a small saucepan, combine 1 cup sugar, 1 / 3 cup butter, and 1 / 3 cup milk
9. Bring to a boil
10. Boil until the sugar is dissolved
11. Remove from heat and stir in chocolate chips and marshmallows until melted
12. Pour over the brownies and quickly spread evenly
13. Refrigerate for 5 minutes before cutting<|endoftext|>
