Ingredients:
• Butter
• Eggs
• Flour
• Granulated sugar
• Brown sugar
• Walnuts
• Pecans
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Ground ancho chili pepper
• Pie crusts

Steps:
1. Preheat over to 350 degrees
2. Melt butter and allow to cool to room temperature
3. In a medium mixing bowl, beat eggs until well blended
4. Add flour and sugars, mix until smooth
5. Add cooled butter and mix well
6. Fold in nuts, chocolate chips and ground ancho chili pepper
7. Pour mixture into prepared pie crust
8. Bake 30 to 40 minutes until pie tests done
9. Serve warm, topped with fresh whipped cream
10. To grind, soften peppers in water for 30 minutes, drain and grind in a blender
11. Do not substitute chili powder
