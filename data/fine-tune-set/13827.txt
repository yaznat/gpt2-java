Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Style Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients:
• Chinese rice wine
• Soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Sesame oil
• Gingerroot
• Garlic cloves
• Pork tenderloin

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except pork
2. Add pork and marinate, covered and refrigerated, 2 hours or longer
3. Preheat over to 400 degrees
4. Drain pork, reserving marinade
5. Place on a rack over a roasting pan
6. Roast 25 - 30 minutes, until a meat thermometer registers 160 degrees, turning pork occasionally and basting with reserve marinade
7. Slice 1 / 4" to serve<|endoftext|>
