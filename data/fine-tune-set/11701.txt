Give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>Addie S Pound Cake

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Whipping cream
• Vanilla extract
• Lemon extract

Steps:
1. Cream butter and sugar
2. Add eggs one at a time
3. Sift flour three times
4. Add flour and whipping cream alternately to butter mixture
5. Add extracts
6. Grease steeple pan
7. Bake on middle rack for 1 1 / 2 hours at 350 degrees
8. Cool for 15 minutes then invert pan<|endoftext|>
