Ingredients:
• Jam
• Ketchup
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Dry mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Lamb loin chops
• Pineapple

Steps:
1. In a sml bowl, combine currant jam, ketchup, soy sauce, brown sugar, mustard & worcestershire sauce
2. Place lamb chops in lrg resealable plastic bag
3. Pour marinade over lamb chops
4. Close bag & marinate in refrigerator for 15 minutes
5. Remove lamb from marinade & discard
6. Place lamb & pineapple slices in center of cooking grate
7. Grill pineapple for 5-7 minutes or till grill marks appear, turning once halfway thru grilling time
8. Grill lamb chops 7-9 minutes for rare, 10-13 minutes for medium or 14-17 minutes for medium-well
9. Turn once halfway thru grilling time
10. Serve lamb chops w / grilled pineapple rings
11. You want to enhance the lamb flavor, not mask it!<|endoftext|>
