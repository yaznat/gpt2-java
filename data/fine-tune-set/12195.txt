Ingredients:
• Apple juice
• Apple cider
• Cinnamon sticks
• Sugar
• Everclear alcohol

Steps:
1. Combine the first four ingredients and bring to a boil
2. Set aside and let cool completely
3. Remove cinnamon sticks and add everclear or vodka
4. Stir to mix well and store in glass bottles keep cool and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
