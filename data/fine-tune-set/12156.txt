Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Light corn syrup
• Tart apples
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Macadamia nuts
• Nestl toll house butterscotch flavored morsels
• Unbaked 9-inch pie shell

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Combine eggs, corn syrup, apple, butter, vanilla extract, salt and cinnamon in large bowl
3. Mix well
4. Stir in nuts and morsels
5. Pour into pie shell
6. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes or until edge is set but center still moves slightly
7. Cool on wire rack for 2 hours
8. Serve immediately or cover and refrigerate<|endoftext|>
