Can you give me a beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Orchard Green Beans

Ingredients:
• Fresh green beans
• Carrot
• Green onions
• Water
• Sugar
• Dried thyme
• Apple juice
• Cornstarch
• Red delicious apple

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, mix together the green beans, carrots, onions, water, sugar, and thyme
2. Bring to a boil
3. Lower heat, cover and simmer for about 5 minutes or until the vegetables are crisp-tender
4. Add the apple juice to a small mixing bowl
5. Blend in the cornstarch by stirring until dissolved
6. Add to vegetables
7. Stir and add in apples
8. Continue to simmer on low heat for 2 minutes or until sauce thickens<|endoftext|>
