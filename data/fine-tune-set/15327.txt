Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Bacon
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Celery
• Green bell pepper
• Salt
• Water
• Reduced-fat milk
• Unbleached all-purpose flour
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, brown beef and bacon
2. Drain if necessary
3. Stir in potatoes, onion, celery, green pepper, salt and water
4. Cover and cook until vegetables are tender, about 15-20 minutes
5. Whisk together flour with 1 / 2 cup of the milk
6. Add to saucepan along with remaining milk
7. Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly
8. Add cheese
9. Heat and stir until cheese is melted
10. Garnish with additional cheese and sliced scallions, if desired<|endoftext|>
