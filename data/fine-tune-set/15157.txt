What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Banana Toffee Pizza

Ingredients:
• Refrigerated sugar cookie dough
• Cream cheese
• Brown sugar
• Frozen whipped topping
• Chocolate-covered english toffee bars
• Toasted sliced almonds
• Bananas
• Caramel ice cream topping

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f shape cookie dough into large circle, 12-18 inches in diameter, and bake until golden brown, approxiamately 20 minutes
2. Let cool completely
3. Mix cream cheese until smooth, then add brown sugar
4. Mix until well blended
5. Fold in whipped topping
6. Spread mixture over cooled cookie crust
7. Coarsely chop candy bars
8. Just before serving, place banana evenly over filling
9. Sprinkle candy over pizza
10. Drizzle ice cream topping over pizza, then sprinkle with almonds
11. Cut into pizza style slices
12. Refrigerate leftovers, if you have any<|endoftext|>
