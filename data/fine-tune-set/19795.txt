What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Diner Style Baked Potato Home Fries

Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Onion
• Butter
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Thoroughly wash potatoes and pierce with a fork
2. Microwave at baked potato setting until done
3. Slice potatoes as thinly as possible
4. Put onions and butter in large skillet on medium heat and cook until onions are translucent
5. Add potatoes and continue frying until the potatoes are browned around the edges
6. I like some of my potatoes to be on the crispy side
7. Season with salt and pepper
8. This is also a great way to use up left over baked potatoes
9. You could also add some chopped green pepper to the potatoes
10. If you are going to add green peppers, cook these with the onions before adding the potatoes<|endoftext|>
