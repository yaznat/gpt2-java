Ingredients:
• Boneless leg of lamb
• Vinegar
• Garlic clove
• Of fresh mint
• Sugar
• White wine

Steps:
1. Place lamb in dish with a lid pierce with 3 / 4 inch knife all over to leave slashes 1 / 2 inch wide
2. Place sliced garlic in slices that you have made in the lamb
3. Pour the white wine over the lamb cover with lid
4. Let sit over night 12-hrs at least refrigerated
5. Place in oven and cook according to directions according to size
6. Take the 2 1 / 2 cups of vinegar boil down to 1 3 / 4 to increase acidity
7. While still hot add the sugar & mint let sit till cool
8. Seperate in small dipping dishes to dip lamb in and enjoy<|endoftext|>
