Please give me a recipe for a popsicle.<|endoftext|>Just Mango Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Mangoes
• Sugar
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Peel mangoes, remove pit, and dice
2. I like to scoop the mango straight into the blender as i go
3. Taste a piece of the mango, and add sugar as needed
4. Some mangos are very sweet and won't need any sugar added, some are a little bitter and will need the full 1 / 2 cup
5. Add the lemon juice and blend until you have a homogenous puree
6. Divide puree among popsicle molds, and freeze until solid, about 6 hours
7. If you are using cups, freeze for 2 hours, then insert sticks, and freeze until solid, about 4 hours more<|endoftext|>
