Can you give me a sushi recipe?<|endoftext|>Eros Sushi Wrap

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Cream cheese
• Ham
• Pepperoni slices
• Mixed salad greens

Steps:
1. I spread half of the cream cheese onto each tortilla, cut up the ham, and put one slice on each wrap
2. Pepperoni slices over the ham, a bunch of greens, rolled up the tortilla
3. Sliced the wraps, like pinwheels, and you have you sushi wrap<|endoftext|>
