Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Orange zest
• Fresh orange juice
• Salt
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Banana
• Macadamia nuts
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Make orange butter:
2. Stir together all orange butter ingredients in a small bowl until combined well
3. Make pancakes:
4. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl
5. Whisk together buttermilk, 2 tablespoons melted butter, eggs, and vanilla in a large bowl until smooth
6. Add flour mixture and whisk until just combined
7. Cut banana into bits and fold into batter along with nuts
8. Brush a 12-inch nonstick skillet with some of remaining tablespoon melted butter and heat over moderate heat until hot but not smoking
9. Working in batches of 3, pour 1 / 4 cup batter per pancake into hot skillet and cook until bubbles appear on surface and undersides are golden brown, 1 to 2 minutes
10. Flip pancakes with a spatula and cook until golden brown and cooked through, 1 to 2 minutes more
11. Transfer to a large plate and loosely cover with foil to keep warm, then make more pancakes, brushing skillet with butter for each batch
12. Serve with orange butter<|endoftext|>
