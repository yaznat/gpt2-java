Ingredients:
• Table cream
• Cognac
• Chocolate liqueur
• Sugar
• Lime zest
• Ice
• Cinnamon
• Cinnamon stick

Steps:
1. In a blender, mix until smooth the cream, cognac, crme de cacao, sugar and lime zest
2. Place a spoonful of crushed ice into each of 6 shallow cocktail or champagne glasses
3. Pour the cream mixture over the ice and sprinkle with ground cinnamon
4. Serve immediately garnished with cinnamon sticks as stirrers<|endoftext|>
