Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Beef Bites

Ingredients:
• Hoisin sauce
• Sherry wine
• Soy sauce
• Barbecue sauce
• Green onions
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh gingerroot
• Pineapple chunks
• Olive oil
• Rib eye
• Skewer

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, mix together the hoisin sauce, sherry, soy sauce, barbeque sauce, onions, garlic and ginger
2. Set aside
3. Preheat oil in a large skillet over medium high heat
4. Sear the steak bites, turning to sear all sides
5. Turn the heat down and pour the hoisin sauce mixture over the bites and mix well, coating the bites with the sauce
6. Simmer until sauce begins to bubble
7. Add the pineapple chunks and continue to simmer until pineapple is warm, and sauce thickens
8. If sauce becomes too thick, add some of the reserved pineapple juice one tablespoon at a time to achieve desired thickness
9. Serve over hot cooked rice
10. Serve hot or cold
11. Alternate method: make hoisin sauce mixture in a saucepan and simmer
12. Thread beef bites and pineapple on skewers that have been soaked in water for 20 minutes to an hour
13. Heat bbq to medium high heat
14. Grill skewers for 510 minutes until seared on all sides
15. Remove from grill and place on a serving dish
16. Drizzle hoisin mixture over the skewers or serve sauce on the side for dipping<|endoftext|>
