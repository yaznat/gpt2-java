What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie With Oatmeal Cookie Crust

Ingredients:
• Rolled oats
• Flour
• Cinnamon
• Light brown sugar
• Regular margarine
• Vanilla
• Sliced apples
• Lemon juice
• Ground cloves
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Cookie crust
2. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and mix until thoroughly blended
3. Press firmly into the bottom and sides for a 9 inch pie pan, saving a handful for the top of pie
4. Filling
5. In a small bowl combine flour, spices, and brown sugar
6. Place apple slices in a large bowl
7. Sprinkle flour mixture over the apples
8. Toss until well mixed
9. Transfer filling to unbaked pie crust, sprinkle the reserved crust mix over the top and pat into place
10. Bake in center of oven tor about 50 minutess or until apples are soft and crust is golden<|endoftext|>
