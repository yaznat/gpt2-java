Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Banana
• Guava
• Mango
• Melon
• Papaya
• Peach
• Pear
• Pineapple
• Lemon
• Coconut
• Honey

Steps:
1. If using canned fruits: drain and save the liquid
2. Peel and remove seeds from the fresh fruit as necessary, cut fruit into bite-sized pieces
3. Combine all fruit in a glass bowl
4. Add the lemon juice, some of the liquid from the canned fruits or honey / sugar
5. Stir gently
6. There should only be enough liquid to coat the fruit
7. It does not have to be covered in liquid
8. Cover the fruit salad and allow it to stand at room temperature for a half hour before serving
9. The fruit salad may be refrigerated after it has stood up to an hour
10. It should be eaten the same day it is made
11. Garnish with freshly grated coconut or chopped roasted peanuts right before serving<|endoftext|>
