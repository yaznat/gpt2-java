Can you give me a taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Always Gone Taco Dip

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Sour cream
• Taco sauce
• Cheddar cheese
• Tomatoes
• Black olives

Steps:
1. Blend together cream cheese and sour cream until smooth
2. Spread cream cheese and sour cream mixture in bottom of a 9x13" pan
3. Layer taco sauce on top, tilting pan if need be to get an even layer throughout
4. Layer cheese, tomatoes and finish with olives keep all layers even
5. Serve cold with tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
