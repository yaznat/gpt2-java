What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Stove Top Bbq Beef Or Pork Ribs

Ingredients:
• Beef ribs
• Onion
• Ketchup
• Water
• Fresh lemon juice
• Brown sugar
• Mustard
• Salt
• Worcestershire sauce
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Cut all ribs into serving size pieces sprinkle ribs with salt in a dutch oven, brown ribs on all sides in veg oil over medium heat remove ribs from pot saute onions in pot until lightly browned return ribs to pot combine all remaining ingredients in a bowl and mix together- add to pot cover pot and cook over low heat for approx 2 hours, or until tender, stirring occasionally<|endoftext|>
