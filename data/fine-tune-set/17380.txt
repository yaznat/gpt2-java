Ingredients:
• Flour
• Yeast
• Milk
• Butter
• Granulated sugar
• Salt
• Egg
• Powdered sugar
• Ground almonds
• Almond extract

Steps:
1. In a large mixer bowl, combine 2 cups flour & yeast
2. On the stove, heat milk, butter, granulated sugar & salt until 115-120 degrees
3. Don't worry if the butter isn't completely melted
4. Add to dry mixture in bowl
5. Add egg, beating at a low speed for 1 / 2 minute
6. Be sure to scrape the bowl during mixing so as to get all yeast incorporated
7. Beat at a high speed for 3 minutes
8. Stir in the remaining flour
9. Knead on floured surface until smooth
10. Put ball in greased bowl
11. Flip to grease both sides
12. Cover with damp cloth & let rise in a warm place until double
13. Punch dough down & let rest 10 minutes
14. Roll into a rectangle
15. Make the almond filling by creaming together sugar and butter
16. Stir in slmonds & extract
17. Spread the almond filling over the pastry
18. Roll from the long edge & pinch to seal
19. Place seam side down on greased baking sheet
20. Cut every 1 / 2 inch to within 1 / 2 inch of bottom
21. Gently pull each slice alternately to left & right
22. Let rise in warm place until nearly double
23. Bake in 375 degree overn for 20-25 minutes
24. While still hot, sprinkle with powdered sugar<|endoftext|>
