What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Chili Cheeseburger Skillet

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Water
• Macaroni & cheese dinner mix
• Chili powder
• Broccoli
• Ketchup

Steps:
1. Brown meat in a large skillet
2. Drain
3. Stir in water, macaroni, and chili powder
4. Bring to boil
5. Reduce heat to low
6. Simmer five minutes, stirring twice
7. Add broccoli
8. Simmer 5 minutes or until macaroni is tender
9. Stir in cheese sauce mix and ketchup until blended<|endoftext|>
