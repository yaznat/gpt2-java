Ingredients:
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Flour
• Heavy whipping cream
• Whole milk
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Melt the cream cheese and butter over medium heat
2. Remove from heat and add the flour and the whipping cream
3. Mix well
4. Stir in 1 1 / 2 cups whole milk and whisk until smooth
5. Return to medium heat and add parmesan cheese and 1 cup whole milk
6. Stir until warm and serve over pasta<|endoftext|>
