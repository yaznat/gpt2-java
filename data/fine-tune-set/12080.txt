Give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Almost Vegan Pumpkin Pie

Ingredients:
• Soymilk
• Eggs
• Cornstarch
• Vanilla
• Pumpkin
• Rice flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Ginger powder
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Ground cloves
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Spray a 9-inch deep dish pie pan with cooking spray
3. A deep dish is recommended because this pie will rise a lot during cooking but will fall back down as it cools
4. Put the first five ingredients in the blender, and blend well
5. Add the pumpkin, and puree
6. Add the remaining ingredients and blend on high for 2 minutes, stopping to scrape the sides a couple of times to make sure everything is thoroughly blended
7. Pour into a pie pan and bake for about 60 minutes
8. The top and edges should be brown, but the edges should not be over-done
9. Remove from the oven and allow to cool on the counter
10. For best results, refrigerate until chilled before eating<|endoftext|>
