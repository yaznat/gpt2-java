Give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Breakfast Burrito Like Mc Donald S

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Bacon bits
• Tortilla
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. First, put your tortilla on a plate
2. Next, scramble the two eggs
3. Then, put the eggs in the tortilla, and spread the bacon bits around evenly
4. Now, put as much shredded cheddar cheese as you want on the egg
5. Microwave for 15-25 seconds take it out and fold the tortilla like a burrito!
6. Lastly, eat!
7. Hope you enjoy!<|endoftext|>
