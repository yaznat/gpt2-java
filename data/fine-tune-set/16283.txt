What's a recipe for a wrap?<|endoftext|>Bacon And Chicken Wraps

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Seasoning salt
• Cream cheese
• Green chilies
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. In a skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until cooked but not crisp
2. Remove to paper towels to drain
3. Keep warm
4. Flatten chicken to 1 / 4 to 1 / 8 inch thickness
5. Sprinkle chicken breasts with seasoned salt
6. In a bowl
7. Combine the cream cheese, chilies and garlic
8. Spread half the mixture on each chicken breast
9. Roll up chicken and wrap with two bacon strips
10. Secure with toothpicks
11. Slice chicken into 1 in pieces for a bit-size piece
12. Making sure that each piece has been secured with a toothpick
13. Place chicken in a greased shallow 4 1 / 2 c baking dish
14. Bake uncovered, 375 degrees for 15 minutes
15. Cover and bake 5 minutes longer or until chicken juices run clear
16. This can also be placed on the charcoal grill for about 15 minutes
17. And the green chilies can be replaced with jalapenos for a great kicking taste
18. When grilling a sweet brown sugar barbeque sauce can be used to coat the chicken when grilling<|endoftext|>
