Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Fried Rice From Every Day With Rachael Ray Mag

Ingredients:
• Cooked white rice
• Eggs
• Fish sauce
• Lemongrass
• Chili paste
• Pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Frozen peas and carrots
• Cilantro leaf

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, fluff the rice with a fork
2. Add the eggs and stir until just combined
3. In a small bowl, mix the fish sauce, lemongrass, chili paste and pepper
4. Stir into the rice mixture
5. In a large, heavy skillet, heat the oil over medium-high heat
6. Add the onion and garlic and cook until soft, about 5 minutes
7. Add the peas and carrots and cook until heated through, about 3 minutes
8. Add the rice mixture and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned, 10 to 12 minutes
9. Stir in the cilantro<|endoftext|>
