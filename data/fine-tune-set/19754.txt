Ingredients:
• Parmesan cheese
• Garlic salt
• Onion powder
• Paprika
• Cayenne
• Rosemary
• Baking potatoes
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425
2. Grease large baking pan
3. Combine parmesan cheese and rest of seasonings into a large resealable plastic bag
4. Combine potatoes and oil in bowl, stir well, covering all potatoes in oil
5. Add potatoes to bag with seasonings
6. Shake to coat all
7. Arrange potatoes in pan bake for 10 minute
8. Turn over potatoes and bake for another 10 minute<|endoftext|>
