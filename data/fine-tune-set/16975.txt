Ingredients:
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Butter
• Water
• Tomato paste
• Prepared horseradish
• Lemon juice
• Dry mustard
• Salt
• Red pepper
• Fresh horseradish
• Mayonnaise
• Sweet pickle
• Green onions
• Parsley
• Fresh lemon juice
• Bottled hot pepper sauce

Steps:
1. Cajun cocktail sauce:
2. In a large pot cook onions and garlic in butter until tender
3. Stir in the water, tomato paste, prepared horseradish, lemon juice, dry mustard, salt and ground red pepper
4. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, simmer, uncovered 5 - 10 minutes
5. Serve warm or chilled with fried fish and seafood
6. Top the cocktail sauce with grated fresh horseradish, if desired
7. Makes 3 / 4 cup of sauce
8. Cajun tartar sauce:
9. In a bowl stir tegether the mayonnaise, sweet picle, green onion, parsley, lemon juice and hot pepper sauce
10. Served with fried fish and seafood
11. Makes 1 1 / 4 cup of sauce<|endoftext|>
