Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Cream cheese
• Chives
• Pepper
• Bacon
• Butter
• Breadcrumbs
• Paprika

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Pound chicken to 1 / 2-inch thickness between two pieces of plastic wrap or wax paper
3. Spread cream cheese on chicken
4. Sprinkle chives over cream cheese
5. Roll up to enclose filling
6. Sprinkle with pepper
7. Wrap with bacon
8. Place rolls seam side down in a baking dish, and bake for 35 minutes
9. Mix melted butter and breadcrumbs in a bowl
10. Preheat broiler
11. Broil rolls for 3 minutes on each side to crisp bacon
12. Sprinkle crumb mixture over rolls
13. Top with paprika<|endoftext|>
