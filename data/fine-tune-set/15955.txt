Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Famous Burritos

Ingredients:
• Steak
• Hamburger
• Taco seasoning
• Refried beans
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Seasoning salt
• Dried onion
• Salsa
• Tortillas
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce
• Sour cream
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Cook your meat till done and drain the grease
2. Add the rest of the ingredients and cook till hot
3. Heat your tortillas
4. Add your cooked mix to your tortillas making a thick line all the way from one side to the other
5. Add your toppings to your liking
6. Roll tortillas and serve<|endoftext|>
