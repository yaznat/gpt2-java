Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Jalapeno peppers
• Cheddar cheese
• Butter

Steps:
1. Beat 2 whole eggs lightly until the yokes are broken up, but not totally homogenized
2. Add jalapeno pepper or green salsa and beat for another 30 seconds
3. Melt the butter in a frying pan and swirl until the bottom of the pan is totally covered
4. Pour in the egg mixture, and lift the edges until it has have started to set and is no longer liquid add cheese evenly over the eggs, and remove the pan from the heat
5. When half of the omelette is out of the pan, twist the pan so that the second half flops over the first half, covering the cheese inside
6. Before serving, allow the folded omelette to sit for 30 seconds so that the cheese can melt<|endoftext|>
