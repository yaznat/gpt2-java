Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Black-eyed peas
• Brown lentils
• Smooth cashew butter
• Water
• Turmeric
• Curry powder
• Cumin
• Cinnamon
• Canned pineapple chunk

Steps:
1. In advance prepare the cashew butter
2. Put it in a bowl and slowly add water to it while mixing
3. The cashew butter should be getting thinner and whiter
4. Do this until it becomes the consistency of syrup
5. Cook lentils in 1 cup and 1 / 4 water
6. Separately cook the beans in 2 cups of water
7. Then, chop the onion into small pieces
8. Place olive oil in a clean pot with the spices and turn on the heat
9. Stir mixture until fragrant and hot
10. Add onion and stir until the pieces are translucent
11. Add the cooked beans and lentils and 1 / 3 cup of water
12. Add the diluted cashew butter and stir
13. When hot add the chunks of pineapple and serve<|endoftext|>
