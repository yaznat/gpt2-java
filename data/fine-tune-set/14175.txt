Ingredients:
• White miso
• Hot water
• Vegetable oil
• Onions
• Salt
• Butternut squash
• Smoked tofu
• Fresh basil leaf
• Soba noodles
• Red pepper flakes
• Pumpkin seeds

Steps:
1. Combine the miso and hot water in a small bowl, and mash with a spoon until the miso is mostly dissolved
2. Set aside
3. Heat the oil in a large deep skillet or a saut pan over medium-high heat
4. When it is very hot, add the onion, and saut for about 5 minutes
5. Sprinkle in the salt, cover, and cook for about 10 more minutes over medium heat
6. Stir in the squash, cover and cook for 10 minutes longer, or until the squash is tender
7. Meanwhile, put up a potful of water to boil for the soba
8. Stir the miso solution, tofu, and basil, into the sauted onion and squash
9. Turn the heat way down, cover, and let it simmer very quietly while you cook the soba (5 to 8 when the noodles are done to your liking, drain them thoroughly, transfer to a serving bowl, and pour the vegetable-miso mixture over the top
10. Toss with chopsticks or a large fork, adding red pepper flakes to taste as you go
11. Sprinkle the top with pumpkin seeds, and serve hot, warm, or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
