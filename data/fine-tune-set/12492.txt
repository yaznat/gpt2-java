Ingredients:
• Onion
• Butter
• Tomatoes
• Potato
• Catsup
• Water
• Worcestershire sauce
• Pepper
• Bay leaf
• Catfish fillet

Steps:
1. Saute onion in butter in a large saucepan until transparent
2. Add tomatoes and next 6 ingredients to saucepan
3. Cover and bring to a boil
4. Reduce heat, and simmer 30 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Gently stir in fish
6. Continue to simmer 20 minutes or until fish flakes easily when tested with a fork
7. Remove bay leaf before serving<|endoftext|>
