What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Bnls Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork chops
• Onion
• Red pepper
• Brie cheese
• Bacon
• Kosher salt
• Cracked black pepper

Steps:
1. Season bnls pork chops with salt and pepper
2. Cut onion into slivers and place on top of pork chops
3. Add 1 / 4 of the red pepper skin side down
4. Cover red pepper with crumbled brie cheese and wrap with 3 pieces of bacon, if need be hold bacon in place with toothpicks
5. Bake @ 375 for 35 to 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
