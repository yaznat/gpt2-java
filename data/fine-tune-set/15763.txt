Give me a burrito recipe.<|endoftext|>Angels Easy Breakfast Burritos

Ingredients:
• Soft taco-size flour tortillas
• Eggs
• Cheese
• Jimmy dean sausage
• Frozen tater tots
• Salsa
• Salt
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Heat oven to temp as instructed on package
2. Line a baking sheet with foil then spray with cooking spray to prevent sticking
3. Lay tater tots on pan and sprinkle with salt
4. Cook until done, set aside to cool completely
5. Brown sausage in a skillet until done
6. Drain to remove fat then set aside to cool completely
7. Scramble eggs and cook until desired doneness
8. Set aside to cool completely
9. Once all cooked ingredients are cooled completely, begin making burritos
10. Lay sausage, then eggs, then cheese, then tater tots on a tortilla
11. Roll tortilla into a burrito
12. Set aside
13. Once all burritos are rolled, cover each in plastic wrap
14. Use a freezer safe plastic bag to store burritos inches
15. Use salsa when you reheat burritos
16. Burritos take 2-3 minutes to reheat, depending on your microwave<|endoftext|>
