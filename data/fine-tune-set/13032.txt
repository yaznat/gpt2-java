Ingredients:
• Fish steaks
• Salt
• Paprika
• Black pepper
• Green pepper
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Lemon juice
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. If fish is large, cut into 6 serving portions
3. Arrange fish in ungreased 8x8-inch baking dish
4. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
5. Top with green pepper rings, tomato slices, and onion slices
6. Mix together the lemon juice, oil and garlic
7. Pour seasoned lemon juice over fish
8. Cover
9. Bake in oven at 375f for 15 minutes
10. Uncover and cook until fish flakes easily with fork, about 10 to 15 minutes more
11. Garnish with lemon slices, and serve hot<|endoftext|>
