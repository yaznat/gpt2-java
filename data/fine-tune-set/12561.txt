Ingredients:
• Rump roast
• Onion
• Potatoes
• Baby carrots
• Celery ribs
• Fresh mushrooms
• Salt
• Pepper
• Tapioca
• Vegetable juice
• Red burgundy wine
• Thyme
• Bay leaf

Steps:
1. Options-try adding a can of stewed tomatoes or my favorite, quarter fresh tomatoes into the mix
2. Try adding some fresh green peas for a traditional stew
3. Preheat oven to 350f
4. Spray large roaster and layer ingredients in the order listed above
5. Reduce oven temperature to 250f
6. Cover and bake for no less than 4 hours and no more than 7 hours
7. If you plan to cook for the entire time, i'd use more liquid
8. I've found that around 5 hours is the perfect consistency for our family
9. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
