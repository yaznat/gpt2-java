What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Crusted Cherry Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Pie crust mix
• Slivered almonds
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Lemon juice
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Heavy whipping cream
• Cherry pie filling

Steps:
1. Mix pie crust as directed, but add chopped almonds with water
2. Prick only the sides of the pie crust and bake as directed
3. Cool
4. Combine condensed milk, lemon juice, vanilla, and almond extract
5. Fold in whipped cream and spoon into cooled pie shell
6. Spread cherry pie filling over cream filling
7. Chill<|endoftext|>
