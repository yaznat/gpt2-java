Ingredients:
• Bread
• Butter
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onion
• Eggs
• Italian seasoning
• Salt
• Pepper
• Milk
• Mushroom soup

Steps:
1. Butter one side of each slice of the bread
2. Place 2 cheese slices and a portion of the the green onion between 2 slices of bread, buttered side inches
3. Place sandwiches side by side in a 13 9-inch baking dish
4. Beat eggs, italian seasoning, salt and pepper with milk
5. Pour over sandwiches and let stand for 1 hour or overnight in fridge
6. Bake 1 / 2 hour at 350 degrees f or until hot through and the egg mix is set
7. Serve with cream of mushroom soup slightly diluted with milk, heated and poured over the top if desired<|endoftext|>
