Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Mixed vegetables
• Canola oil
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Chop vegetables into tiny pieces
2. Try onion, spinach, tomato, asparagus, zucchini, or mushrooms
3. Shred cheese using a cheese grater, or use a slicer to make a couple thin slices
4. Preheat stove to medium-high, and put an 8-10-inch pan on the burner to heat up
5. Crack eggs into bowl and beat until frothy with a fork or whisk
6. Add oil to the hot pan
7. Add vegetables to the pan and cook lightly, just until things brown or soften up a little
8. Add eggs, and spread around pan to evenly distribute vegetables
9. Allow the eggs to begin to set, when the top is dry and does not wiggle when you shake the pan
10. To speed the setting, you can put a lid on the pan to firm up the top faster
11. When there is no runny egg left, add cheese to the omelette
12. When cheese has begun to melt, fold omelette in half with a spatula
13. If folded omelette is not golden brown on the outsides, cook for a moment longer, flip, and cook the other side
14. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
