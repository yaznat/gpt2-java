Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>1 Brownies In The World Best Ever

Ingredients:
• Bittersweet chocolate
• Unsalted butter
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Vanilla extract
• Brewed espresso
• Kosher salt
• All-purpose flour

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 350f and arrange the rack in the middle
2. Line an 8-by-8-inch glass baking dish with aluminum foil
3. Combine chocolate and butter in a medium saucepan and cook over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until evenly melted
4. Remove from heat and let cool to room temperature
5. Combine eggs, sugar, cocoa powder, vanilla extract, espresso, and salt in a large bowl and briefly stir until just evenly incorporated
6. Add cooled chocolate and mix until uniform in color
7. Add flour and stir until just incorporated
8. Transfer batter to the prepared baking dish
9. Bake until a tester inserted in the center of the brownies comes out clean, about 25 to 30 minutes
10. Remove from the oven and cool completely before cutting<|endoftext|>
