What's a good donut recipe?<|endoftext|>Chinese Orange Donut Holes

Ingredients:
• Orange
• Granulated sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Refrigerated buttermilk biscuits
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Place zest, sugar and cinnamon in a paper bag and shake to combine
2. Place oil in a deep pan until it comes about halfway up the side of the pan
3. Heat oil to 350 degrees fahrenheit
4. Slice biscuit dough in quarters and roll into balls to form 24 donut holes
5. Working in batches, place donut holes in hot oil and fry until golden, about 6 to 8 minutes
6. Transfer hot donuts to the paper bag and shake to coat with orange sugar
7. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
