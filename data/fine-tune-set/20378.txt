What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>9X13 Casserole Base Any Flavor With Cheese

Ingredients:
• Evaporated milk
• Eggs
• Cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Spray a 9x13 pan with pam
2. Mix eggs, milk, and spices
3. Layer 1" of whatever in pan
4. Cover with most of the cheese
5. Add another inch of whatever, and then top with remaining cheese
6. Pour sauce over top
7. Bake at 350 until done
