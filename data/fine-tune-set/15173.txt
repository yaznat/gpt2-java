What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Basic Simple Pizza Crust

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Yeast
• Salt
• Hot water
• Olive oil
• Cornmeal

Steps:
1. Sift together 1 cup flour, yeast & salt
2. Add water & mix well
3. Slowly, 1 / 2 cup at a time, add in the rest of the flour
4. Pour oil over dough and knead before shaping into a ball
5. Put someplace warm to rise for 15 minutes
6. Sprinkle corn meal over warm baking stone or over pizza sheet and roll dough out
7. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes
8. Top and bake as is your preference
9. These are some other variations:
10. Add italian seasoning to the dough before setting to rise
11. Add chunks of cheese or shredded cheese while adding in the water
12. Use wheat flour in place or white flour
13. Roll pieces of string cheese into the edges to form stuft crust pizza<|endoftext|>
