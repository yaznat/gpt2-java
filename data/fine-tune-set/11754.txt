What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>African Beef Curry

Ingredients:
• Lean stewing beef
• Onion
• Bell peppers
• Diced tomatoes
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Rice
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Cut the beef into 1 / 2 inch cubes
2. Chop the onion and peppers
3. In a large saucepan, combine the beef, onions, peppers, undrained tomatoes, curry powder and salt
4. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer until meat is tender, about 1 1 / 2 to 2 hours
5. About 45 minutes to a half hour before ready to serve, prepare the rice according to package directions
6. Serve the beef over the rice and top with some raisins<|endoftext|>
