Ingredients:
• Garlic powder
• Ground cumin
• Paprika
• Dried oregano
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Ground red pepper
• Flank steaks
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Combine first 7 ingredients
2. Rub spice mixture over both sides of steak
3. Cover and refrigerate 3 hours
4. Prepare grill
5. Place steak on grill rack coated with cooking spray
6. Grill 5 minutes on each side or until desired degree of doneness
7. Let stand 10 minutes
8. Cut steak diagonally across the grain into thin slices<|endoftext|>
