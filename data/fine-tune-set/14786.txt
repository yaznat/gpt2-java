What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Aussie Version Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Instant rice
• Frozen peas
• Onion
• Bacon
• Chicken noodle soup mix
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Cook the rice with the peas according to package directions
2. I use a microwave rice cooker, works great
3. I also put in 1 chicken boullion for flavor
4. While this is cooking chop up the bacon and fry with onion in fry pan
5. I use kitchen shears to cup up bacon
6. I like to brown the bacon well, it seems to add extra flavor
7. Add the cooked rice, peas, and soup to the pan
8. Original recipe didn't call for it, but i add a bit of water at this stage and 1-2 t butter if getting dry looking
9. Cook for 15 minutes
10. Helps to put lid on skillet i find
11. It helps to soften the noodles from the soup mix
12. Need to keep an eye on the prevent sticking
13. Add 2 scrambled eggs, cook through
14. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
