Give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Baked Fries

Ingredients:
• Nonstick spray coating
• Baking potatoes
• Parmesan cheese
• Paprika
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Lightly spray a 15x10x1-inch baking sheet with nonstick coating
2. Cut potatoes lengthwise into thin wedges
3. Place potato wedges in a plastic food storage bag
4. Spray wedges with nonstick coating
5. Combine cheese, paprika, and pepper
6. Add to bag
7. Close bag
8. Shake to coat potatoes with seasonings
9. Arrange potatoes in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet
10. Bake in a preheated 425 degree fahrenheit for 30 minutes or until crisp and fork-tender, turning once
11. Season with salt<|endoftext|>
