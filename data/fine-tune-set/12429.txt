Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Celery ribs
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Carrots
• Salt
• Peppercorn
• Bay leaves
• Wide egg noodles
• Water

Steps:
1. Start by making the stock
2. In a stock pot or dutch oven place the onion or leek, garlic, carrots, celery, salt, peppercorns, bay leaf, water and chicken
3. You can add any chicken carcass pieces you have in the freezer
4. Put on medium high heat
5. When you start to see bubbles, turn down to medium low to keep simmering
6. Partially cover with a lid
7. After 30 minutes, remove chicken
8. As soon as its cool enough to handle peel the chicken off the bones and return the bones to the broth
9. Shred the chicken into small bite-sized pieces
10. Continue simmering the broth for 3-8hrs
11. Strain the broth by pouring it through a colander into a large bowl or pot
12. Add the broth, carrots and shredded chicken back to the pot and turn up to high
13. When its boiling add the noodles and cook for 8 minutes
14. Taste for salt
15. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
