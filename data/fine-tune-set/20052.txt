Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Potatoes
• Spanish onion
• Eggs
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Heat oil in 9 inch omelette pan
2. Fry garlic until golden and discard
3. Add potato slices and cook over medium heat
4. Add onions after 5 minutes
5. Cook until potatoes are tender
6. Lift and turn potatoes and onions as they cook
7. In a large bowl, beat eggs with a fork or wire whisk until slightly foamy
8. Add salt and pepper to taste
9. Add potatoes and onions to eggs, draining with a slotted spoon before you add them
10. Return egg and potato mixture to same skillet, adding more oil if necessary so that eggs will not stick
11. Spread evenly and cook over medium heat, shaking pan
12. When eggs leave sides of pan, invert a plate over the pan and flip omelette onto plate
13. Slide omelette back into pan to brown other side
14. Repeat this process one more time
15. Serve hot or at room temperature on a round china or glass plate<|endoftext|>
