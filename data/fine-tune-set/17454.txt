Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Walnuts
• Sugar
• Maple syrup
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt

Steps:
1. To make the topping mix all ingredients together using your fingers or a wooden spoon, mix in the nuts
2. Preheat oven to 350f
3. Brush an 8x8 inch square pan or a 9x2 inch round pan with melted butter and dust lightly with flour, or spray generously with cooking spray
4. Beat the butter, sugar and maple syrup with an electric mixer
5. Add the eggs all at once
6. Scrape the sides and bottom of bowl
7. Mix in the bananas
8. Sift together the flour, baking soda and salt
9. Add to the banana mixture, blending well
10. Stir in the walnuts
11. Spread into prepared pan
12. Drop the topping in small dollops onto the banana batter
13. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes or until the coffee cake is a deep golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean
14. Cool in the pan for 30 minutes
15. Invert onto a plate, then invert again so coffee cake is right side up on a serving plate<|endoftext|>
