Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Blackened Catfish Po Boy Can Sub Tilapia Or Salmon

Ingredients:
• Sweet paprika
• Dried oregano
• Dried thyme
• Cayenne
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Sugar
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Catfish fillets
• Vegetable oil
• French baguettes
• Tartar sauce
• Iceberg lettuce
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine the paprika, oregano, thyme, cayenne, garlic powder, onion powder and sugar
2. Pat catfish fillets dry with paper towels
3. Brush lightly with oil, then season well with salt and pepper
4. Sprinkle liberally with blackening spice mix, gently pressing spices onto fish to adhere
5. Coat bottom of a large, non-stick frying pan with vegetable oil
6. Heat over medium-high heat until oil is shimmering
7. Add catfish and fry until cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side
8. Do not overcrowd pan
9. Work in batches and keep warm in a low oven
10. Spread toasted rolls with tartar sauce
11. Place one fillet on each roll, then top with lettuce and tomato
12. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
