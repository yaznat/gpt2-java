Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Avocado
• Ham
• Cheese
• Limes
• Butter

Steps:
1. Butter bread if you wish
2. Place down cheese
3. Then ham on top
4. Mash avocado in a bowl with a little squeeze of lime or lemon to keep it fresh--don't do too much as you can overdo it
5. Spread on top of ham
6. Top with other piece of bread
7. Cut into 4 squares
8. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
