Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Bacon
• Onion
• Velveeta cheese
• Refrigerated buttermilk biscuits
• Ketchup
• Yellow mustard

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook the beef, bacon and onion over medium heat until meat is no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Add cheese
4. Cook and stir until melted
5. Remove from the heat
6. Flatten each biscuit into a 5-in
7. Circle
8. Spoon 1 / 3 cup beef mixture onto each biscuit
9. Fold sides and ends over filling and roll up
10. Place seam side down on a greased baking sheet
11. Bake at 400 for 18-20 minutes or until golden brown
12. In a small bowl, combine ketchup and mustard
13. Serve with roll-ups<|endoftext|>
