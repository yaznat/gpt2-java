Give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>Buckley S Cajun Salmon Pasta

Ingredients:
• Heavy whipping cream
• Basil
• Parsley
• Garlic
• Lemon juice
• Parmesan-romano cheese mix
• Parmesan cheese
• Butter
• Cajun spices
• Salt
• White pepper
• Linguine
• Salmon

Steps:
1. For the cream sauce:
2. Saut minced garlic with butter until garlic is light brown
3. Add heavy cream, basil, lemon juice, grated cheese blend, & white pepper
4. Bring to a boil on medium high heat, stirring often
5. Place on low heat after boiling & salt to taste
6. For the cajun salmon:
7. Preheat oven to 375f
8. Lightly coat salmon with cajun spice
9. Remember, the amount of spice will determine the degree of spice & heat
10. So cook salmon at 375f for 8-15 minutes depending on desired doneness
11. For the presentation:
12. Toss cooked pasta with the cream sauce
13. Portion 8 ounces of pasta per serving
14. Place the cooked salmon filets atop the portions
15. Garnish with parsley & shredded parmesan cheese
16. A slice of garlic toast & a lemon wedge will further complement this dish<|endoftext|>
