Ingredients:
• Rump roast
• Salt
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Brewed coffee
• No-salt-added beef broth
• Onion
• Dry red wine
• Garlic
• Taro root
• Sliced mushrooms
• Pitted dates
• Capers
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Chayotes

Steps:
1. Trim fat from beef and cut into 1 inch cubes
2. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
3. Heat a large saucepan over medium high heat
4. Add beef and cook 5 minutes or until browned
5. Add coffee and next 4 ingredients and bring to a boil
6. Cover and reduce heat and simmer 45 minutes
7. Add taro, mushrooms, dates, and capers
8. Bring to a boil
9. Cover, reduce heat, and simmer 20 minutes
10. Serve over rice
11. Top with chayote<|endoftext|>
