Please give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Black Forest Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa
• Salt
• 1% low-fat chocolate milk
• Egg
• Margarine
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Lemon juice
• Unsweetened cherries

Steps:
1. Combine first 6 ingredients
2. Make a well in center of mixture
3. Combine chocolate milk, egg and margarine
4. Add to dry ingredients, stirring just until dry ingredients are moistened
5. For each pancake, pour 2 tablespoons batter onto a hot griddle or skillet coated with cooking spray
6. Turn pancakes when tops are covered with bubbles and edges look cooked
7. Top with cherry sauce: combine sugar, cornstarch and water in a saucepan, stirring until smooth
8. Add lemon juice and cherries, stir well
9. Cook over medium heat, stirring, constantly, until thickened and bubbly<|endoftext|>
