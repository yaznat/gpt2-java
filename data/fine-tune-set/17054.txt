What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Cocktail Sauce The Emeril Way

Ingredients:
• Ketchup
• Prepared horseradish
• Fresh lemon juice
• Worcestershire sauce
• Tabasco sauce
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine all ingredients except salt and pepper
2. Season with salt and pepper to taste
3. Store in glass jar in refrigerator up to one week<|endoftext|>
