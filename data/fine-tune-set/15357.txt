Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cheddar Turkey Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Scallions
• Flat leaf parsley
• Poultry seasoning
• Olive oil
• Granny smith apple
• Extra-sharp cheddar cheese
• Cranberry sauce
• Grainy mustard
• Sandwich-size english muffins
• Lettuce
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine turkey, scallions, parsley, poultry seasoning, salt and pepper
2. Form into four patties, pressing your thumb in the middle of each to prevent it from bulging
3. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat
4. Add the patties and cook for 6 minutes on each side for medium
5. Top each with 3 apple slices and 2 cheese slices, tent the pan with foil and cook until the cheese melts, 1 to 2 minutes
6. In a small bowl, mix together cranberry sauce and mustard, slather on muffin tops
7. Pile some lettuce on muffin bottoms, and set the burgers in the middle!<|endoftext|>
