What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>5 Ingredient Low Fat Tuna Pot Pie

Ingredients:
• Chunk tuna
• Reduced-fat cream of chicken soup
• Mixed vegetables
• Refrigerated reduced-fat buttermilk biscuits
• Herb salad dressing

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. In a bowl, mix the tuna, soup, vegetables, and seasoning
3. Dump all into a 1 1 / 2 quart casserole dish
4. Top with piece of biscuits
5. Bake for 40 minutes<|endoftext|>
