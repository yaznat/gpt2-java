What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Cube Steaks

Ingredients:
• Cube steaks
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Fresh garlic
• Olive oil
• Parmigiano-reggiano cheese
• Flat leaf parsley

Steps:
1. In a heavy pan, heat olive oil
2. Put garlic in oil and cook until fragrant
3. Salt and pepper both sides of steaks
4. Brown cube steaks in hot pan
5. Place cube steaks in a baking dish and cover with mushroom soup
6. Foil or lid on top of dish and bake for 60 minute at 350
7. Remove from oven carefully lifting foil not to get burned
8. Place meat on serving dish
9. Top with flat leaf parsley and cheese<|endoftext|>
