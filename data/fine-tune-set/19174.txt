What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Caramel Ice Cream Delight

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Oatmeal
• Brown sugar
• Pecans
• Butter
• Caramel topping
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Combine first 4 ingredients
2. Stir in butter
3. Spread mix in thin layer on cookie sheet and bake at 350 degrees for 20 minutes or until browned
4. Cool and crumble
5. Spread 1 / 2 of crumbs in a 13 x 9 inch dish
6. Spoon caramel over the crumbs
7. Slice softened ice cream over topping and top with remaining crumbs
8. Cover and freeze until firm<|endoftext|>
