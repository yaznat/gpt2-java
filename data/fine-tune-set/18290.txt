Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Bisquick Banana Pancakes Chocolate Chips

Ingredients:
• Bisquick baking mix
• Mashed bananas
• Skim milk
• Artificial sweetener
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Mix all with a fork in order to break the banana into the rest of the batter
2. Mix until smooth
3. Grease griddle and cook em up!
4. If using chocolate chips, sprinkle onto pancake while the first side is cooking<|endoftext|>
