Ingredients:
• Mozzarella cheese
• Butter
• Salt and pepper
• Eggs
• Roll

Steps:
1. First use the butter to grease the pan
2. Crack three eggs into a bowl
3. Pour the eggs onto the pan and set the stove for about medium heat
4. Add pieces of mozzarella cheese to the egg and some salt and pepper
5. Fry the egg like a normal easy over egg
6. After the egg is done butter a hard roll and place pieces of mozzarella on the roll then put the egg in the roll and you're done! enjoy!<|endoftext|>
