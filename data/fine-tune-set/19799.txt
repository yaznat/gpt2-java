What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Easiest Low Fat French Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Cooking spray
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350'f
2. Wash and cut potatoes into wedges
3. Spray baking sheet with cooking spay
4. Arrange potatoes on sheet and spray top of potatoes with cooking spray and put alittle salt and pepper on top
5. Bake for 30 minutes
6. There is in need to turn
7. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
