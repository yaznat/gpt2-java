Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• All-purpose flour
• Wheat bran
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Dates
• Egg substitute
• Brown sugar
• Vegetable oil
• 1% low-fat milk
• Apple

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. In a lg bowl combine the whole wheat and all purpose flours, bran, baking powder, baking soda and cinnamon
3. Stir in the dates
4. In another bowl, beat the egg substitute with sugar and oil until well mixed, then stir in the milk and apple
5. Pour into the flour mixture and stir just enough to moisten, being careful not to overmix
6. Spoon into nonstick or paper-lined medium muffin tins, filling almost to the top
7. Bake in the preheated 375f oven for about 20 minute or until firm to the touch<|endoftext|>
