Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Condensed golden mushroom soup
• Cheddar cheese soup
• Frozen french fries
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef and onion together in skillet
2. Drain grease
3. Season with salt, pepper and garlic powder
4. Stir in the soups and heat through
5. Pour mixture into a 9x13 baking dish
6. Top with french fries
7. Bake 20-25 minutes at 375 until fries are golden brown
8. Top with cheese and return to oven for 5-10 minutes until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
