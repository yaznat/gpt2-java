What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Better Than Frozen French Bread Pizza

Ingredients:
• French bread
• Spaghetti sauce
• Dried oregano
• Green pepper
• Fresh mushrooms
• Fat-free parmesan cheese
• Lowfat mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Spoon spaghetti sauce over sliced bread or rolls
2. Top with mushrooms and green peppers
3. Sprinkle with oregano and cheeses
4. Bake in preheated 375 degree oven for 12 to 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
