Ingredients:
• Coconut oil
• Flour
• Arrowroot
• Sea salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Dessert tofu
• Sugar
• Soymilk
• Unsweetened dried shredded coconut

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Lightly grease a square baking pan
3. Melt the coconut oil so that it is completely liquid
4. I do this using the microwave on about half of it, and then mixing in other half which melts in and reduces the overall temperature
5. Set aside to cool
6. In a bowl combine the dry ingredients - flour, arrowroot, salt, baking powder and baking soda
7. Set aside
8. Empty the tofu into a large mixing bowl
9. Using a whisk, mix it with the sugar, and soy milk, followed by the oil which should be cooled down enough by now
10. Add the dry ingredients into the wet, combine well but don't overmix
11. Fold in the shredded coconut
12. Pour the batter into the prepared pan, sprinkle about a tsp of sugar overtop
13. Bake for about 35 minutes - do the toothpick test to be sure<|endoftext|>
