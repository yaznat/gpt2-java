Ingredients:
• Salted butter
• White sugar
• Eggs
• Gluten-free flour
• Icing sugar
• Almond extract
• Water
• Food coloring

Steps:
1. Combine butter, sugar, and 1 cup of flour, using sturdy wooden spoon or mixer with dough hooks
2. Add eggs and mix in thoroughly
3. Gradually work in the remaining flour, until dough will roll into balls without being sticky
4. Roll into one or two-bite size balls and place on ungreased cookie sheet, or silicone baking sheet
5. Bake at 350 f for 15 minutes
6. Cool on cookie sheet before moving cookies to sheets of waxed paper
7. Space the cookies at least 1 / 2" apart on the waxed paper so you don't have to move them to put icing on them
8. Combine icing ingredients and stir until smooth
9. The icing should be runny enough to drop from a knife onto the cookies, but thick enough that it doesn't all run off
10. The icing will thicken as it sits
11. Add more water if needed
12. To ice, leave the cookie on the waxed paper, and drop enough icing onto it to cover the top and drip down the sides a bit
13. While the icing is still wet, you can add little drops of icing to ensure coverage
14. Try not to have too much icing ending up on the waxed paper, or you may not have enough for the whole batch
15. Let the icing dry before placing in shallow storage containers, in a single layer<|endoftext|>
