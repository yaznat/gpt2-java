Ingredients:
• Espresso
• Lump of sugar
• Brandy

Steps:
1. Prepare espresso with premium-grade arabica beans in a long espresso cup
2. Hold a large spoon containing 1 lump of sugar and 1 tbsp of brandy over each cup and light the brandy
3. Pour into the coffee when the flames begin to die down and serve with creme brulee<|endoftext|>
