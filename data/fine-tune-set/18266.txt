Ingredients:
• Bisquick baking mix
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Milk
• Lemon juice
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Heat griddle or skillet over medium-high heat or electric griddle to 375f
2. Grease with cooking spray, vegetable oil or shortening
3. Stir all ingredients until blended
4. Pour by slightly less than 1 / 4 cupfuls onto hot griddle
5. Cook until edges are dry
6. Turn
7. Cook until golden
8. To keep warm, place in single layer on cookie sheet
9. Cover with aluminum foil, and place in 200f oven up to 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
