What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>Alfredo Bacon Chicken Pizza

Ingredients:
• Pizza crust
• Alfredo sauce
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Bacon
• Artichoke hearts
• Baby spinach
• Sun-dried tomato
• Mozzarella cheese
• Asiago cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Scallion

Steps:
1. Spread pizza crust with alfredo sauce
2. Top with remaining ingredients
3. Bake in a preheated 375f degree oven for 10 to 15 minutes or until cheese is melted
4. Top with scallions before serving<|endoftext|>
