Generate a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Thick Milkshakes

Ingredients:
• Ice cream
• Vanilla
• Whole milk
• Instant malted milk powder
• Instant pudding mix

Steps:
1. Scoop the ice cream into a blender
2. Fill the blender with whole milk until it just covers the first scoop
3. Put in the vanilla if this is a vanilla oriented shake
4. Put in the pudding mix
5. Here you can stick in whatever extra flavor you want
6. I have tried peanut butter, bananas, chocolate syrup, strawberries, or nothing
7. Last put in the malt if you want it malted
8. Blend in bursts, then blend somewhat slowly to consistency of choice
9. Enjoy thick milkshake<|endoftext|>
