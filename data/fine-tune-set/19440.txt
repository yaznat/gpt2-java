What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Potatoes Pommes Lyonnaise

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onions
• Butter
• Pork fat
• Salt and pepper
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Peel and slice the potatoes very thinly
2. Peel and slice the onions medium-fine
3. Heat the oven to 350 degrees
4. Melt the butter and pork fat, , in a large iron frying pan over a medium low heat and add the onions, cooking them for 4 to 5 minutes until transparent before adding the potatoes
5. Season well with salt and pepper
6. Turn them over occasionally with a wooden spatula and when they have coloured to the blond stage, mix in the parsley
7. Empty the contents of the pan into a large oval gratin dish and cook very slowly in the oven for at least 1 hour or until browned and crusted on the surface and melting underneath<|endoftext|>
