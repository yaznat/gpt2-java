Ingredients:
• Macaroni
• Ground beef
• Oil
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Mushrooms
• Tomato sauce with herbs
• Kalamata olives
• Canned corn
• Mozzarella cheese
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Cook macaroni in a pot of boiling water until denter to the bite
3. Drain
4. In a skillet, heat oil
5. Cook ground beef, onion, green pepper and mushrooms
6. Remove from skillet and drain
7. Transfer to an oven proof dish
8. Fold in remaining ingredients except half the cheese
9. Top with remaining mozarella
10. Cook in oven until cheese has melted<|endoftext|>
