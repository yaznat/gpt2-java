Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Red pepper flakes
• Salad potatoes
• Yellow onion
• Yellow bell peppers
• Chili powder
• Extra lean ground beef
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil, garlic, and pepper flakes over medium-high heat
2. Add potatoes, bell pepper, and onion
3. Sprinkle chili powder over vegetables
4. Cook until potatoes are almost soft and starting to turn translucent
5. Add ground beef
6. Using the leanest ground beef will mean that you can cook everything together because you won't have to drain the beef
7. It saves time and tastes richer
8. Chop up the beef as it's cooking so that it is crumbly
9. Cook until it is mostly brown
10. You may want to add more chili powder at this point
11. Add tomatoes and turn down heat slightly
12. Allow them to become soft
13. I like to gently mash them with a potato masher at this point
14. Let the whole mixture simmer for about 10 minutes
15. Serve with corn bread
16. I like to get the corn bread in the over right before i add the ground beef
