What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>Alden S Grandmother S Cornbread

Ingredients:
• Bacon grease
• Cornmeal
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Buttermilk
• Egg
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees f
2. Place the bacon grease in a 10-inch black cast iron skillet and swirl to coat the sides and bottom evenly
3. Place skillet in the oven to heat
4. In a bowl, combine the cornmeal, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and pepper and mix well
5. In a small bowl, combine the buttermilk, egg, and melted butter
6. Add to dry ingredients and stir just to combine
7. Remove the hot skillet from the oven, carefully swirl to evenly distribute hot grease, and quickly pour the cornmeal batter into the skillet
8. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until firm and golden brown on top
9. Let sit for 5 minutes, then turn out onto a cutting board
10. Cut into wedges and serve hot with butter on the side<|endoftext|>
