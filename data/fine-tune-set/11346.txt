Give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>100 Whole Wheat Irish Soda Bread

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Butter
• Raisins
• Egg
• Low-fat buttermilk
• Molasses

Steps:
1. In a big mixing bowl, combine flour, baking powder, soda, and salt
2. Cut in butter until it reaches a coarse meal consistency
3. Add raisins
4. Combine liquids separately
5. Add liquids to dry ingredients
6. Mix until a soft dough forms
7. At some point it will become hard to stir it, and it will be better to use your hands
8. Kneading the dough is the best way to combine all the ingredients completely -- knead it gently until it is smooth
9. You can knead it right there in the mixing bowl
10. Shape the dough into two balls, and place them on a greased cookie sheet
11. Flatten each ball slightly and cut an x about 1 / 4" deep in the top of each loaf
12. Bake at 375 for 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
