Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Fast rise yeast
• Salt
• Water
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. In a mixer bowl, combine 1 cup flour, yeast and salt
3. Mix well
4. Combine water and oil
5. Heat to 120 - 130 f, add to flour mixture
6. Blend at low speed until moistened
7. Beat 3 minutes at medium speed
8. By hand, gradually stir in enough remaining flour to make a firm dough
9. Knead on floured surface 3 - 5 minutes
10. Divide dough into 2 parts
11. With well-floured or greased fingers, press each half into greased 12 or 14-inch pizza pan
12. Sprinkle 1 / 4 cup of your favorite shredded cheese over dough to prevent a soggy crust
13. Spread prepared pizza sauce on each pizza
14. Add your favorite toppings
15. Cover with shredded cheese of your choice
16. Bake at 400f for 25 - 30 minutes until edge is crisp and light golden brown and cheeses are melted
17. Serve immediately
