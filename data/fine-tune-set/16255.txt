Ingredients:
• Smooth peanut butter
• Warm water
• Lemon juice
• Light soy sauce
• Garlic
• Sesame oil
• Ground ginger
• Hot pepper sauce
• Cooked chicken
• Sweet red pepper
• Seedless cucumber
• Scallions
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, wisk together peanut butter, water, lemon juice, soy sauce, garlic, sesame oil, ginger, and hot pepper sauce
2. Set aside
3. In a large bowl, mix together chicken, red pepper, cucumber, and scallions
4. Mix in 1 1 / 2 cups of the peanut sauce
5. Reserve any remaining sauce
6. Heat tortillas following package directions
7. Place about 1 cup of the chicken mixture on each tortilla and roll up
8. Serve any additional sauce on the side<|endoftext|>
