Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• White wine vinegar
• Mayonnaise
• Worcestershire sauce
• Lemon juice
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Poultry seasoning
• Garlic
• Sweet paprika
• Onion powder
• Thyme

Steps:
1. Put all ingredients in a jar with tight fitting lidf, shake well
2. Place meat in a large ziplog bag, pour over just enough marinade to cover meat, marinate in frig or 6-8 hours or longer
3. The unused marinade will last up to 1 week in frig,
4. To cook, preheat gas or charcoal grill on medium heat
