Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Olive oil flavored cooking spray
• Garlic salt
• Black pepper
• Ground allspice
• Ground cinnamon
• Honey
• Apple juice
• Worcestershire sauce
• Water
• Cornstarch
• Granny smith apples

Steps:
1. Spray skillet with olive oil cooking spray
2. Over medium-high heat, saute garlic salt for 2 minutes
3. Add pork chops
4. Brown on each side
5. Sprinkle pepper, allspice and cinnamon evenly over chops
6. Use as much, or little as your little heart desires
7. Add honey, apple juice and worcestershire sauce
8. Reduce heat and simmer 30-45 minutes until chops are tender and brown
9. Save drippings
10. Transfer chops to serving platter and keep warm
11. In small mixing bowl, combine cornstarch and water
12. Whisk into skillet over med-high heat until drippings thicken into a glaze
13. Add apple slices to skillet and cook until tender
14. Approx 5-10 mins on medium heat
15. Remove from heat and spoon over chops<|endoftext|>
