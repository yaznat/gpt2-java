Ingredients:
• Waffles
• Eggs
• Caster sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Cream
• Frozen raspberries
• Milk chocolate
• Icing sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 170 degrees celsius
2. Lightly grease 8-cup capacity ovenproof pudding dish
3. Cut waffles into 3cm cubes
4. Using electic mixer beat eggs, caster sugar and vanilla for 3 minutes until pale and thick
5. Add cream and beat until ocmbined
6. Arrange 1 / 3 waffles in dish
7. Pour 1 / 3 cream mixture over, 1 / 3 raspberries and 1 / 3 chocolate on top of that
8. Repeat
9. Allow mixture to stand 30 minutes before baking uncovered for 45 minutes or until custard is set and top is golden
10. Dust with icing sugar to serve<|endoftext|>
