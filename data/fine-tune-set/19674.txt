What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>American Fries

Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Onion powder
• Paprika
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Vegetable oil cooking spray

Steps:
1. Scrub potatoes clean
2. Cut in 1 / 4-inch thick slices
3. Pat slices dry with paper towels
4. Combine onion powder and next four ingredients in a large ziplock bag
5. Add potato slices and shake to coat
6. Arrange potato slices in a single layer on a large baking sheet coated with cooking spray
7. Bake at 425f for 12 to 15 minutes on each side or until lightly browned<|endoftext|>
