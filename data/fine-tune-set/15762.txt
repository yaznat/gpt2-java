Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Zucchini
• Onion
• Vegetable oil
• Salsa
• Water
• Sliced almonds
• Cheddar cheese
• Chilies
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Wrap tortillas in foil
2. Bake at 375f 10-15 minutes or until soft and heated through
3. Can also be wrapped in plastic wrap and microwaved for 1-2 minutes on high until soft
4. While tortillas heat, saute zucchini, onion and canned chilies in oil for three minutes on medium heat
5. Reduce heat to low, add water and salsa, cover and cook for three minutes
6. Stir in almonds
7. Remove from heat
8. Spoon 1 / 2 cup vegetable mixture down center of each tortilla
9. Divide cheese into sixths and sprinkle on top of vegetable mixture
10. Roll up tortillas
11. Place burritos on serving dish and spoon 1 tbsp sour cream and 1 tbsp salsa down length of each burrito
12. Garnish burritos with reserved almonds
13. Serve immediately
14. If desired serve with shredded lettuce, chopped tomatoes and sliced avacado on the side
15. Refried beans and spanish rice are also nice side dishes to go with the burritos and makes for a full course meal
16. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
