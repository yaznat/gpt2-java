Give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Grilled Turkey Club Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Miracle whip
• Chili powder
• Turkey breast
• Bacon
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat grill to medium heat
2. Spread tortillas with dressing and sprinkle with chili powder
3. Layer half each of the turkey and bacon and 1 / 2 slice of cheese for each tortilla
4. Fold over tortillas to cover filling
5. Grill 2-3 minutes on each side or until quesadillas are golden brown on both sides and cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
