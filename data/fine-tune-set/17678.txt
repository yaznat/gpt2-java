Give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple Caramel Muffins

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Egg
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Butter
• Apple
• Caramel candies

Steps:
1. In a large bowl mix together first 4 ingredients
2. In a small bowl blend egg, milk& vanilla
3. Add melted butter& then pour into mixture& stir-do not overmix
4. Mix in apples& caramels
5. Fill muffin cups& bake 20@ 350 or until toothpick comes out clean<|endoftext|>
