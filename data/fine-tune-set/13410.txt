Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>3 Cheese Beef Stuffed Shells

Ingredients:
• Jumbo pasta shells
• 80% lean ground beef
• Pasta sauce
• Water
• Chive & onion cream cheese
• Italian cheese blend
• Parmesan cheese
• Egg
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f cook and drain pasta shells as directed
2. (for best results, cook the pasta just until al dente
3. This will make them easier to stuff and they won't overcook
4. )
5. In 10-inch skillet, cook ground beef for 5 to 7 minutes, until thoroughly cooked
6. Drain
7. Cool slightly, about 5 minutes
8. In large bowl, mix pasta sauce and water
9. Pour 1 cup of the sauce in the buttom of an ungreased 13x9 glass baking dish
10. In a medium bowl, mix cheese spread, 1 cup of the italian cheese, the parmesan cheese, egg and cooked ground beef
11. Spoon a heaping tablespoon of the mixture into each shell
12. Arrange shells over sauce in baking dish
13. Pour remaining sauce over shells
14. Cover with foil
15. Bake 40 to 45 minutes or until bubbly and cheese filling is set
16. Sprinkle with remaining italian cheese
17. Bake 10 minutes longer, or until cheese is melted
18. Sprinkle with parlsey for presentation<|endoftext|>
