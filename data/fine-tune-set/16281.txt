Ingredients:
• Pork baby back ribs
• Light brown sugar
• Paprika
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Water
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat grill to medium
2. Lay 2 sheets heavy duty reynolds wrap aluminum foil on counter
3. Center half the ribs in a single layer on each sheet of foil
4. Combine sugar and seasonings, rub over ribs
5. Bring up foil sides
6. Double fold top and one end to seal packet
7. Pour in 1 / 4 cup water
8. Double fold remaining end, leaving room for heat circulation inside
9. Repeat to make two packets
10. Grill 45-60 minutes in covered grill
11. Remove foil, place ribs on grill
12. Brush ribs with your favorite barbeque sauce
13. Continue grilling, 10-15 minute brushing with sauce and turning every 5 minutes
14. Oven: make packets as directed
15. Preheat oven to 320f place on a cookie sheet and bake for 2 hours slice open tops of packages and brush sauce on
16. Place back in oven for 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
