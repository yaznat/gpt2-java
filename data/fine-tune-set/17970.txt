Ingredients:
• Chocolate chips
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Salt
• All-purpose flour
• Cream cheese
• Milk
• Almond extract
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Melt over hot water, 1-1 / 4 cups nestle toll house semi-sweet chocolate chips
3. Stir until smooth
4. Set aside
5. In large bowl, combine sugar and butter
6. Beat until creamy
7. Add eggs, vanilla extract and salt
8. Mix well
9. Add melted chocolate and flour
10. Mix well
11. Spread into foil-lined 9-inch square baking pan
12. Cheesecake topping: in large bowl, combine cream cheese, sugar and butter
13. Beat until creamy
14. Add eggs, milk, flour and almond extract
15. Beat well
16. Stir in remaining 3 / 4 cup nestle toll house semi-sweet chocolate chips
17. Pour over brownie base
18. Bake at 350 degrees for 40-45 minutes
19. Cool completely on wire rack
20. Cut into 2-1 / 4 inch squares<|endoftext|>
