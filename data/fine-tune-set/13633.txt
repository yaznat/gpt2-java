Ingredients:
• Pork loin chops
• Applesauce
• Brown sugar
• Clove

Steps:
1. Brown chops with a little salt and pepper
2. In baking dish, put pork chops on the bottom, mix rest of ingredients and pour on top of chops
3. Bake at 300f degrees for 1 hour or 1 1 / 2 hours
4. "hold"in warm oven until ready to serve, the longer the better!<|endoftext|>
