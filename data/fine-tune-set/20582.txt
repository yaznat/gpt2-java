Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Angel hair pasta
• Green pepper
• Marinara sauce
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Put chopped pepper in water that you will be cooking pasta inches
2. Cook pasta as directed and drain
3. Combine chicken, pasta with peppers and marinara sauce
4. Put in 9x11 pan and top with mozzarella cheese
5. Bake at 350 for 10 minutes or until the cheese has melted<|endoftext|>
