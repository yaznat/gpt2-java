Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Sour cream
• Butter
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Walnuts
• Powdered sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375f grease and flour 15x10-inch jelly roll pan
2. For the bars, in a large bowl, beat together sugar, sour cream, butter, and eggs until creamy
3. Blend in bananas and vanilla extract
4. Add flour, baking soda, salt, and blend for 1 minute
5. Stir in walnuts
6. Spread batter evenly into pan
7. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until golden brown
8. Meanwhile, for frosting, heat butter in a large saucepan over medium heat until boiling
9. Let the butter turn a delicate brown and remove from heat immediately
10. Add powdered sugar, vanilla extract and milk
11. Whisk together until smooth
12. Using a spatula, spread the brown butter frosting over the warm bars<|endoftext|>
