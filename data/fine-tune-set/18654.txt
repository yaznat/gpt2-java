What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Arroz Con Leche Rice Pudding

Ingredients:
• Long-grain white rice
• Cinnamon sticks
• Water
• Milk
• Salt
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Sugar
• Sweetened condensed milk

Steps:
1. Take a small pot and add water and cinnamon sticks, bring to a boil and cook for 10 minutes
2. Set aside
3. In a large saucepan, over medium heat place the rice and the cinnamon water and cook for 5 minutes
4. Add salt, butter, vanilla extract, 2 cups of milk, and sugar
5. Stir well to mix and cook uncovered for approximately 15 minutes
6. Reduce heat to medium low
7. Add the remaining milk and condensed milk
8. Stir with a wooden spoon
9. Cook for 1 hour and 15 minutes, or until the rice pudding thickens to desire consistency<|endoftext|>
