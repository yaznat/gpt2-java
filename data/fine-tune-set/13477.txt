Ingredients:
• Beef brisket
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Apple jelly
• Apple juice
• Black pepper
• Cloves
• Dijon mustard
• Pepper
• Curry powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Place brisket in the crockpot
2. Place onion slices over top
3. In a small bowl, whisk together all remaining ingredients
4. Pour over the brisket and onions in the crockpot
5. Cover and cook on low for about 8 hours or until desired doneness<|endoftext|>
