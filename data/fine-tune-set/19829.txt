Please give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Garlicky Baked Oven Fries Gluten Free

Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Russet potatoes
• Cornstarch
• Coarse sea salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Cut each potato into 12 wedges
2. Preheat oven to 225c / 475f
3. Combine the garlic and oil in a large bowl, warming it until the garlic is fragrant, about 1 minute
4. I used the microwave for this
5. Transfer 3 tablespoons of the oil to the baking dish, coating it well
6. Add the potatoes to the bowl with the remaining 1 tbsp garlic / oil mixture and toss to coat
7. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and microwave on high power until the potatoes are translucent around the edges, 3 to 6 minutes, shaking the bowl to redistribute the potatoes halfway through cooking
8. Combine the cornstarch, salt, pepper and cayenne in a small bowl
9. Sprinkle over the hot potatoes and toss well to coat
10. Arrange the potatoes in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet and bake, turning once, until deep golden brown and crisp, 30 to 40 minutes<|endoftext|>
