Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Onions
• Chilies
• Garlic cloves
• Boneless pork
• Prawns
• Cooked rice
• Eggs
• Light soy sauce
• Pepper
• Fish sauce
• Hot chili sauce
• Lime juice
• Sugar
• Spring onion
• Coriander leaves

Steps:
1. Mix the fish and chilli sauces, lime juice and sugar and set aside
2. Heat a wok or large pan, add oil and fry onions, chillies and garlic untill soft, chilli
3. Add pork or chicken and stir-fry untill cooked
4. Stir in prawns and cook through
5. Add the rice and str-fry untill heated through
6. Pour the eggs mixed with soy sauce and pepper into a seperate pan
7. Heat and stir until the eggs are set
8. Cut into small pieces and add to the rice
9. Pour over the sauce and mix all together over a high heat for a minute
10. Remove from the heat mix in the spring onions and coriander leaves<|endoftext|>
