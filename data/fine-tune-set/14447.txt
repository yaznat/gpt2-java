Give me a beans recipe.<|endoftext|>Another Green Bean Casserole

Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Onion
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Velveeta cheese
• French-fried onions
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place the green beans and onion in a large saucepan
2. Bring to a boil then lower temperature and simmer until about half of the liquid has evaporated
3. To the saucepan with the beans and liquid add the mushroom soup, cheese, salt and pepper
4. Continue to simmer on low until the cheese melts
5. After the cheese melts taste to see if it needs more cheese if so add now, if not pour the mixture into a greased baking dish and cover and bake on 350 for 30 minutes or until bubbly
6. Uncover and sprinkle french fried onions on top and place back in oven and bake until browned
7. Let stand about 3 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
