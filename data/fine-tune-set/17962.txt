What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Bisquick Butterscotch Brownies

Ingredients:
• Bisquick baking mix
• Light brown sugar
• Eggs
• Margarine
• Coconut
• Pecans
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease a 13" x 9" baking pan
3. In a bowl, mix all ingredients with a wooden spoon
4. Pour into prepared baking pan
5. Bake for 40-45 minutes, or until brownies test done
6. Cool on rack
7. Cut while warm<|endoftext|>
