What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>Albuquerque Salad

Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Canned black beans
• Corn
• Onions
• White vinegar
• Vegetable oil
• Fresh cilantro
• Jalapeno
• Chili powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cook rice according to package directions
2. In a medium bowl combine the rice, beans, corn and onion
3. In a bowl whisk together vinegar, oil, cilantro, jalapeo, chili powder and salt
4. Toss with the rice mixture
5. Chill
6. Stir before serving<|endoftext|>
