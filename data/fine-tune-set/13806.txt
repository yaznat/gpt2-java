What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Pork Dumplings 2

Ingredients:
• Ground lean pork
• Fresh mushrooms
• Green onion
• Celery
• Ginger powder
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Egg
• Water
• Wonton wrappers
• Oil
• Chicken broth

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine ground pork, mushrooms, green onions, celery and ginger
2. In a small bowl, whisk the cornstarch with soy sauce, add in egg white, and then add to the pork mixture
3. Mix well to combine
4. Shape into 1-inch balls
5. Set aside briefly
6. To make dumplings: in a small bowl, whisk together the egg yolk and water
7. One at a time, place the pork balls in the middle of the wonton wrapper
8. Brush one-half of the edges of the wontons with egg / water mix
9. Fold wrapper over into a triangle, and press edges to seal
10. Fold over the points, sealing once again with the egg mixture to form a neat wonton
11. Place the shaped wontons neatly on a cookie sheet
12. At this point they may be frozen if desired
13. To cook:
14. Heat oil in a skillet over high heat
15. Cook for 2-3 minutes, or until bottoms are lightly browned
16. Reduce heat to low and add in broth or water
17. Simmer covered for 12-15 minutes, or until liquid evaporates
18. Uncover, increase heat to medium-high, and cook 5 minutes longer until browned
19. Serve on a platter with dipping sauce<|endoftext|>
