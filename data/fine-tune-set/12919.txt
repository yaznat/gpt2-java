Please give me a recipe for fish.<|endoftext|>Absolutely Amazing Fish Batter

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Batter frying mix
• Hot sauce
• Cayenne
• Old bay seasoning
• Lemon zest
• Beer
• Fish fillets
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Pour oil into deep fryer and preheat to 350 degrees
2. Wash fillets and dry on paper towels
3. In a large bowl, combine batter mix, cayenne, old bay seasoning, and lemon zest
4. Add egg, hot sauce, and beer and stir until well combined and no lumps remain
5. Batter should resemble pancake batter
6. Dip fillets into batter and coat evenly
7. Carefully place into hot oil and cook 4 minutes or until golden<|endoftext|>
