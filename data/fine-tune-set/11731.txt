Please give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>All In One Pan Cake Aka Wacky Cake

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Unsweetened cocoa
• Sugar
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Water
• Canola oil
• Vanilla extract
• Cider vinegar

Steps:
1. In an 8x8 inch square baking pan combine the flour, cocoa, sugar, salt and baking soda
2. Mix until it is completely light brown
3. When it is mixed, make 4 dents in the mixture with a spoon, 2 large dents and 2 small dents
4. Pour the water into one of the large dents
5. Pour the oil into the other large dent
6. Pour vanilla into one of the small dents
7. Pour vinegar into the other small dent
8. Stir until well moistened
9. When batter is well mixed, smooth it out and spread into place with a spatula
10. Bake at 325 degrees for 30 minutes
11. Cool the cake in the pan for 30 minutes before cutting it into squares<|endoftext|>
