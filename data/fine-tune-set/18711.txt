What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Banana Bread Crumb Pudding

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Egg
• Applesauce
• Skim milk
• Honey
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground cloves
• Dry breadcrumbs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. In a bowl, combine banana, egg, applesauce, milk and honey
3. Stir in brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg and cloves
4. Fold in breadcrumbs
5. Spray a 1-quart baking dish with non-stick cooking spray
6. Pour batter into the dish
7. Bake for 30 minutes or until set<|endoftext|>
