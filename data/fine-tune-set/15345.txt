Ingredients:
• Hamburger buns with sesame seeds
• Andouille sausage
• Ground chuck
• Red bell pepper
• Red onion
• Salt
• Fennel seeds
• Cayenne pepper
• Roasted red peppers
• Mayonnaise
• Shallot

Steps:
1. Place the ingredients for the mayo in a blender or food processor and process until smooth
2. Set aside and chill for 30-45 minutes while preparing burgers
3. Mix together all ingredients for burgers and form 6 thick patties
4. Cook burgers on grill or in skillet to 160f internal temp
5. Do not press on the burgers while they are cooking
6. Serve burgers on buns with red pepper mayo, and other optional condiments, as desired<|endoftext|>
