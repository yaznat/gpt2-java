Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• Heavy cream
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter
• White bread

Steps:
1. Place cubed bread in loaf pan
2. In a mixing bowl, combine eggs and sugar
3. Mix well
4. Add cream, salt and cinnamon
5. Mix well
6. Pour custard over bread cubes
7. Press down so custard soaks in well
8. Place soft butter on top of bread
9. Bake in a water bath at 325 degrees for approximately one hour until golden brown and custard is firm
10. Serve with vanilla sauce<|endoftext|>
