Ingredients:
• Bacon
• French bread dough
• Cheddar cheese
• Deli turkey
• Deli ham

Steps:
1. Cook, drain and crumble bacon
2. Unroll dough
3. Sprinkle cheese over dough
4. Top with turkey, ham and bacon
5. Starting with long side, roll up dough, press edges to seal
6. Make 3 to 4 slits in top of loaf
7. Place on sprayed cookie sheet
8. Bake 350 degrees f for 23 to 28 minutes or until golden brown
9. Cool at least 5 minutes
10. Cut into 1 inch diagnonal slices<|endoftext|>
