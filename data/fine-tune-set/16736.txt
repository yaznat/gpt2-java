Ingredients:
• Coffee ice cream
• Milk
• Whipped cream
• Caramel sundae syrup

Steps:
1. Place all the ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth and thick
2. Top with whipped cream and a swirl of caramel syrup on top<|endoftext|>
