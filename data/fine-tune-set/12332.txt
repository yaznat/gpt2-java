Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Fresh coriander
• Boiling water
• Whole wheat bread
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Mince the garlic and mix with the salt in a soup pan
2. Add the olive oil and chopped coriander then add the water and stir well
3. Heat a separate pan of water and gently drop in your eggs to poach them
4. Serve the garlic soup into individual dishes, place a slice of bread on top of each and then gently top with one poached egg per plate<|endoftext|>
