Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Black pepper
• Thyme
• Tomato sauce
• Black beans
• Zucchini
• Corn niblets
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Season chicken breasts with chili powder, cumin, pepper and thyme
2. Grill 5 minutes on each side, or till cooked thoroughly
3. Shred chicken into small pieces and reserve
4. On stovetop, in a large pot, saute onions, zucchini, corn and garlic in a bit of oil on med-low heat for about 10 minutes
5. Add black beans and spices, and saute another two minutes
6. Add tomato sauce and let simmer, covered, for about 15-20 minutes
7. Stir in chicken and let simer, covered for another 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
