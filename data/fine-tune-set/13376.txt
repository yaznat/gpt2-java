Ingredients:
• Fresh broccoli
• Fresh carrots
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Cooked chicken
• Alfredo sauce
• Biscuit mix
• Milk
• Dill weed

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, saute the broccoli, carrots, and onion in oil until crisp tender
2. Stir in the chicken and alfredo sauce
3. Heat through
4. Transfer to a lightly greased 8-in
5. Square baking dish
6. In a small bowl, combine the baking mix, milk and dill just until moistened
7. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls onto chicken mixture
8. Bake, uncovered, at 400 degrees for 18-22 minutes or until bubbly and biscuits are golden brown<|endoftext|>
