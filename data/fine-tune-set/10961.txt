What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>Amazingly Good Chicken Salad

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Kraft mayonnaise
• Good seasonings italian salad dressing mix
• Lemon juice
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Boil chicken breasts until tender
2. Cool and remove chicken from bones and chop fine
3. Mix mayonnaise, good seasons italian seasoning, lemon juice and salt and pepper - add to chicken and mix well
4. Better if made the day before serving so flavors can meld
5. Refrigerate immediately
6. May need to add extra mayonnaise the next day and a little extra seasoning if desired<|endoftext|>
