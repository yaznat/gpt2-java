Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cream Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Half-and-half
• Cream sherry
• Dijon mustard
• Prepared horseradish
• Butter
• Onion
• Apples
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Place pork in a shallow roasting pan
3. In a mixing bowl, combine half and half, sherry, dijon mustard, and horseradish
4. Brush half of this mixture over the pork
5. Bake the pork, uncovered, basting with remaining cream mixture, until pork is done to your preference
6. Meanwhile, melt butter in skillet
7. Add onions and apples
8. Saute until soft, about 15 minutes
9. Place pork on platter and keep warm
10. Pour pan drippings and remaining cream / baste into skillet with onions, bring to a boil, and season to taste with salt and pepper
11. Serve sauce with sliced pork when serving<|endoftext|>
