What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Almond Dream Coffeecake

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Flour
• Nonfat sour cream
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Almond extract
• Sliced almonds
• Coconut
• Cinnamon
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Grease 12 cup bundt pan or 9 x 13 dish
2. In large mixer, combine 1 c sugar, butter, eggs and extract
3. Mix dry ingredients and add to creamed mixture, alternating with sour cream
4. Beat at medium speed, scraping often, until light and fluffy mixture may be thick
5. In small bowl, combine filling ingredients
6. Spoon 1 / 2 of batter into prepared pan
7. Sprinkle with filling
8. Spoon remaining batter into pan over filling
9. Bake 35-45 min or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
10. Cool completely
11. Stir together glaze ingredients
12. Drizzle over coffeecake<|endoftext|>
