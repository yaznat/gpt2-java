Ingredients:
• Water
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Butter
• Yellow cornmeal
• Blue cheese
• Garlic
• Cilantro
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Combine water, salt, pepper, and butter, and bring to boil
2. Slowly, in a thin stream, whisk in the cornmeal
3. When it starts to bubble turn off heat or it will splatter
4. Stir with wooden spoon
5. Partially cover and leave on the warm stove top, stirring every few minutes so it does not stick, for 30 minutes
6. When thick, remove from warm stove top, and add cheese, garlic, and cilantro
7. Mix well
8. Pour into a 12x8x1-inch sheet tray or baking dish
9. Smooth out the mixture until it is level
10. Chill until firm, about 2 to 3 hours
11. Preheat the canola oil in a deep pot to 350f
12. Cut polenta in strips, about 1 1 / 2 by 3 inches
13. Lightly bread the polenta "fries" with cornmeal
14. Deep fry in hot oil for 2 to 4 minutes or until crispy<|endoftext|>
