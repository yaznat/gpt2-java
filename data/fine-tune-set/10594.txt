Give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Garlic Shrimp

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Raw shrimp
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Vidalia onion
• Salt and pepper
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Pour one cup olive oil in large sauce pan over medium heat
2. Add crushed garlic and chopped onion
3. Cook until soft
4. Then add peeled shrimp and cook a few minutes until pink
5. Season generously with salt, pepper, and parsley
6. Cook pasta according to directions
7. Add pasta to the other ingredients, toss and serve<|endoftext|>
