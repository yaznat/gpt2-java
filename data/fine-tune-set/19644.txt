What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus And Potato Frittata

Ingredients:
• Baking potato
• Asparagus
• Eggs
• Egg whites
• Nonfat milk
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Parmesan cheese
• Onion

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Put potato in saucepan and cover with water by 3 inches
3. Cover pan and bring to boil over high heat
4. Boil 5 minutes
5. Add asparagus and boil an additional 2 minutes
6. Drain and set aside to cool
7. Combine whole eggs and egg whites, milk, salt, baking powder, and three tablespoons of the parmesan cheese in a bowl
8. Mix in the potato and asparagus
9. Heat an oven proof nonstick pan, spray with nonstick spray and add the onion
10. Cook, stirring occasionally for two minutes
11. Add the egg mixture and reduce heat to medium
12. Cover and cook until almost set, about ten minutes
13. Uncover the skillet and sprinkle remaining parmesan cheese
14. Transfer to the oven to cook for about five minutes, until browned
15. Invert onto a plate and cut into wedges prior to serving<|endoftext|>
