Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Cream
• Apples
• Butter
• Cinnamon
• Lemon, juice of

Steps:
1. Beat the eggs lightly and add the milk
2. Combine the flour, sugar and salt
3. Stir the flour mixture into the egg mixture, stirring in the cup of cream as you mix
4. Fry the apple slices in butter in a skillet
5. Preheat oven to 500 degree
6. Cover the bottom of an oven-proof baking dish, or heavy skillet, with apples
7. Pour the batter over slices and bake in a preheated 500 oven
8. When nearly done, remove from oven and sprinkle here and there with a mixture of sugar and cinnamon to taste
9. Place dabs of butter on the pancake and return to oven until browned
10. Just before serving, sprinkle with lemon juice, and cut into triangles<|endoftext|>
