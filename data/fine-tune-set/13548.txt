Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Serrano peppers
• Taco seasoning mix
• Cream cheese
• Enchilada sauce
• Monterey jack cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Brown beef, onions, peppers and garlic in large skillet over medium-high heat until browned and veggies are soft
2. Add taco seasoning and 1 / 2 cup of enchilada sauce, stirring until combined
3. Turn off heat and add cream cheese
4. Stir until cream cheese has melted into mixture
5. Spread 1 / 2 cup of enchilada sauce into the bottom of a greased 13x9 casserole dish
6. Spoon about 1 / 4 cup of meat mixture down the center of each warmed tortilla and roll up
7. Place enchiladas seam side down in dish
8. Top enchiladas with remaining 1 cup of enchilada sauce
9. Then top with monterey jack cheese
10. Bake for 20 minutes at 350 degrees or until cheese is melted
11. Serve with rice and beans if you so desire!<|endoftext|>
