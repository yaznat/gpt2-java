Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• Frank's red hot sauce
• Butter
• Chicken
• Onion
• Blue cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Ranch dressing

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees fahrenheit
2. Grease a 12 inch x 12 inch non-stick pizza pan or a 15 inch x 10 inch non-stick baking sheet with shortening
3. Press dough onto sheet
4. Mix hot sauce and butter together
5. Spread half of the hot sauce mixture over dough
6. Mix together the onions, chicken and remainder of hot sauce
7. Spoon mixture evenly over dough
8. Sprinkle with cheeses
9. Bake 15 to 20 minutes or until bottom of crust is golden brown
10. Drizzle ranch dressing over pizza and serve<|endoftext|>
