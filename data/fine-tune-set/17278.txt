Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Milk
• White sugar

Steps:
1. Beat butter at medium speed with an electric mixer until creamy
2. Gradually add 1 cup sugar, beating well
3. Add egg and vanilla
4. Beat well
5. Combine flour, salt, baking soda and baking powder
6. Beat into batter alternatly with milk, beginning and ending with flour mixture
7. Shape dough into 2 discs, wrap well and refridgerate at least 1 hour
8. Roll each disc to a 1 / 4 inch thickness on a floured surface
9. Cut with a 3 inch cutter, place 1 inch apart on a lightly greased cookie sheet
10. Sprinkle with sugar
11. Bake at 400 degrees for 7-8 minutes
12. Cool 1 minute and move to wire racks<|endoftext|>
