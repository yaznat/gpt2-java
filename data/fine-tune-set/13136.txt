Give me a recipe for fish.<|endoftext|>Braised Monkfish Nicoise

Ingredients:
• Monkfish fillets
• Flour
• Pure olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Tomatoes with basil
• Nicoise olive
• Reduced-sodium chicken broth
• Fresh marjoram
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Lightly dust monkfish with pan-searing flour
2. Heat oil on medium in large braising pan
3. Brown monkfish lightly on all sides, 4-6 minute remove monkfish
4. Set aside
5. Discard all but 1 tbsp oil
6. Lower heat to medium-low
7. Add garlic and onions
8. Cook 2-3 min, stirring frequently
9. Add tomatoes and olives
10. Cook 2-3 minute add broth and marjoram sprig and return monkfish to pan
11. Bring to simmer and cover
12. Cook 20-25 min, until fish reaches internal temp of 140 degrees
13. Check by inserting thermometer into thickest part of fish
14. Transfer fish to clean plate
15. Continue to simmer sauce 5 min, until desired thickness
16. Season to taste with salt and pepper
17. Pour sauce over fish<|endoftext|>
