What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Asian Crispy Tofu Salad

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Soy sauce
• Scallion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Dried lemongrass
• Sesame oil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Whisk all ingredients except for tofu in a 9x9 inch square baking dish
2. Drain and slice tofu in half lengthwise to make two thinner slabs
3. Blot with a paper towel
4. Preheat non-stick skillet over medium heat and spray with non-stick spray
5. Slide tofu slices onto skillet and grill for about 4-5 minutes per side, flipping carefully with a large spatula
6. Brown it nicely to give it color and texture
7. Slip tofu slices into marinade, and turn with spatula at least once
8. While the tofu is sitting in the marinade, cut each slab 3 times lengthwise and widthwise with a sharp knife to make 16 squares per slab
9. Toss carefully
10. Let marinate for up to 30 minutes and remove garlic and ginger slices before serving warm, at room temperature or chilled<|endoftext|>
