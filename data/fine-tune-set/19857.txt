What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Herbed Parmesan Oven Fries

Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Parmesan cheese
• Dried oregano
• Dried rosemary
• Black pepper
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees
2. Cut the potatoes lengthwise into 1 / 4 inch thick slices, then cut the slices lengthwise into 1 / 4 inch wide sticks
3. Place the potatoes in a large bowl, lightly spray with nonstick cooking spray and toss to coat
4. Add the parmesan, oregano, rosemary and pepper and toss again
5. Transfer the potatoes to 2 baking sheets, lined with parchment paper, and bake for 40 minutes, turning every 12 minutes or till the potatoes are brown and crisp
6. Place the fries on a platter, sprinkle with salt and serve<|endoftext|>
