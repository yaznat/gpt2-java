Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Ground cinnamon
• Poppy seed
• Mustard seeds
• Ground coriander
• Ground cloves
• Russet potato
• Salt
• Cauliflower head
• Sweet potato
• Collard greens
• Raisins
• Apple cider vinegar
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. In a large pot, heat the oil over medium heat and add the onions and garlic, cooking them for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally until softened
2. Add ginger, cinnamon, poppy seeds, mustard seeds, coriander, and cloves and cook, stirring frequently, for 2 minutes
3. Add potato to the mixture an stir to combine
4. Stir in 4 cups of water and turn heat up to high and bring to a boil, add the teaspoon of salt and stir well
5. Turn heat to low and simmer stew, uncovered, for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally
6. Add cauliflower, sweet potato, collard greens, and raisins, stirring to combine
7. Continue cooking for 10 minutes until potatoes are tender
8. Season stew with vinegar, and salt and pepper to taste
9. If the stew is too dry, add a little water
10. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
