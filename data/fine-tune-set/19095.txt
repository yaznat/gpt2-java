Ingredients:
• Milk
• Marshmallows
• Sugar
• Bananas
• Fresh lemon juice
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. In an electric blender, combine milk, marshmallows, sugar, sliced bananas and lemon juice
2. Blend on high speed
3. Whip heavy cream and then stir cream into blended banana mixture
