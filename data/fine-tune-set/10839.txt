What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>A Dilly Of A Pea Salad

Ingredients:
• Romaine lettuce
• Frozen peas
• Celery
• Scallions
• Dried dill weed
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Miracle whip
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Wash and drain lettuce, wrap in paper towels and chill until serving time, for a nice crisp lettuce do this at least 2 hours before serving
2. Combine peas, celery, scallions, dill, salt, pepper and miracle whip and chill 1-2 hours
3. Salt tomato, cover and leave at room temperature
4. To serve combine tomato and juice with the pea mixture, spoon over lettuce<|endoftext|>
