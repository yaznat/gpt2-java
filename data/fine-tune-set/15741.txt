Ingredients:
• Fresh cilantro
• Light sour cream
• Chipotle chile
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Fresh lime juice
• Salt
• Romaine lettuce
• Cooked boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Cherry tomatoes
• Avocado
• Red onion
• Black beans
• No-salt-added corn

Steps:
1. Dressing: combine first 7 ingredients, stirring well
2. Salad: combine lettuce and remaining ingredients in a large bowl
3. Drizzle dressing over salad
4. Toss gently to coat
5. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
