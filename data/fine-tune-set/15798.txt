Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Refried beans
• Chunky salsa
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Jalapeno peppers
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef with onions and garlic
2. Then drain
3. Add all remaining ingredients except cheese
4. Simmer for 10 to 15 minutes
5. Cool mixture to room temperature in the fridge
6. Unassembled: freeze mixture in bags
7. Thaw overnight, then heat meat mixture in a pan before assembling burritos
8. Bake at 250 degrees for 10 to 12 minutes
9. Assembled: spread chilled mixture on tortillas and top with cheese, then flash freeze and wrap individually
10. Microwave: cook on high, 1 at a time for 4 minutes
11. Oven: bake at 400 degrees for 15 to 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
