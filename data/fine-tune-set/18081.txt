Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>100 Whole Wheat Buttermilk Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Egg whites
• Low-fat buttermilk
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Canola oil
• Salt
• Flax seed meal

Steps:
1. Preheat non-stick skillet over medium heat
2. Using whisk, combine all ingredients until smooth
3. Pour approximately 1 / 4 c batter into skillet
4. When edges begin to bubble and lift from skillet, flip pancakes
5. Cook both sides until golden brown<|endoftext|>
