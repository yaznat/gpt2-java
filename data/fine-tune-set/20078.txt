What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Spinach And Cream Cheese Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Whole milk
• Fresh spinach
• Yellow onions
• Ground nutmeg
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Fresh lemon juice
• Fresh lemon zest
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Wash spinach leaves well-remove stems and slice leaves very thin
2. Set aside
3. Whip the cream cheese with lemon juice and zest if using
4. Set aside
5. Dice onion or shallot
6. Set aside
7. In a large skillet over medium heat melt 2 tbsp of the butter
8. Add the spinach and onion and saut for 3-5 minutes
9. You want the onion slightly translucent and the spinach wilted
10. Remove from heat and pour into a bowl
11. Set aside
12. Melt the remaining 1-2 tbsp butter in the same pan over medium heat
13. Whip the eggs well with the whole milk and pour into the hot pan
14. Allow eggs to cook slowly, allowing eggs to almost set completely
15. Pour 1 / 2 the cheese mixture over 1 / 2 of the omelette, add the spinach onion mixture
16. Sprinkle on the nutmeg, salt and pepper, and the remaining cheese
17. Fold the omelette over, turn off heat and let stand for 3 minutes
18. Serve<|endoftext|>
