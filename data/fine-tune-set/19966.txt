Ingredients:
• Egg white powder
• Water
• Eggs
• Salt
• White pepper
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Veggie burger

Steps:
1. To make the 6 egg whites from egg white powder, heat the water so it is warm
2. Stir the powder into the water until just moistened
3. Preheat and grease a large, oven-proof skillet on the stove over medium heat
4. Preheat the oven to 325-degrees
5. Combine the egg whites in a large mixing bowl and beat them until frothy
6. Add the water and continue beating the egg whites until stiff
7. In a separate bowl beat the 2 remaining egg yolks, the salt, and the white pepper
8. Fold the egg-yolk mixture into the egg whites until it develops a uniform color
9. Spoon the egg mixture into the preheated skillet
10. Mound the eggs towards the edges, leaving a slight depression in the center
11. Cook the eggs for 6 minutes, without stirring
12. Transfer the skillet into the oven and bake for 10-12 minutes, or until an inserted utensil comes out clean
13. On a level surface, make a shallow cut slightly off-center in the omelette
14. Load your toppings on the larger side of the cut
15. Fold the smaller side over, cut in half and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
