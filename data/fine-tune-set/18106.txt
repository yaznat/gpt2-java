Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. Mix together all the dry ingredients in a large bowl
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together all wet ingredients
3. Add the wet stuff to the dry stuff and mix just until batter comes together
4. Do not mix smooth
5. Set aside batter to rest for 5 minutes
6. Heat an electric griddle to 350 degrees or place a nonstick frying pan over medium-low heat
7. Test the griddle by flicking water on it
8. If the water dances across the surface, youre good to go
9. Rub down the griddle with a little butter or spray with nonstick spray
10. Ladle 1 scoop of batter onto the griddle
11. Cook until bubbles form in the batter and bottom is golden, approximately three minutes
12. Flip and cook until the second side is golden, another a minute or so
13. Adjust the heat as necessary as you go along
14. Serve while hot!<|endoftext|>
