Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Garlic clove
• Hot sauce
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Onion
• Butter
• Hamburger buns
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Preheat grill to medium
2. In a bowl combine beef, garlic, hot sauce and salt and pepper to taste, being careful not to over-mix
3. Warm a skillet over medium heat
4. Keep heat on skillet and fry a pinch of the beef to test seasoning
5. Adjust seasoning, if needed, then form 8 thin patties about 1 / 2-inch thick and set aside
6. Saut onions, using 1 tablespoon of twisted butter bacon, dijon mustard & green onion butter in skillet until tender, or about 5 minutes
7. Place onions in a bowl to cool
8. Form a spoonful of butter mixture into a ball, then place in the center of 4 patties then top with a spoonful of the onions
9. Top each with another patty and seal the edges
10. Grill burgers over medium-high heat, flipping once until desired doneness is reached, approximately 4 minutes each side for medium
11. Serve in buns with garnishes
12. Alternative: in a medium skillet over medium-high heat, sear the burgers 4 minutes on each side<|endoftext|>
