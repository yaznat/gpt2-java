Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Freezer Vegetarian Black Bean Burritos

Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Salsa
• Flour tortillas
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Frozen vegetables

Steps:
1. Set out a 9x11 baking pan to place folded burritos in
2. Whirl black beans in a food processor for 3-5 seconds to make a thick paste
3. Warm up tortillas in the microwave
4. Place all 12 on a plate on high for about 45-60 seconds
5. Spread bean paste on tortilla
6. Layer on veggies, salsa, and cheese,
7. Roll up, folding in ends
8. Place seam-side down into baking pan
9. Repeat process for all 12
10. Place baking pan in freezer until burritos are frozen solid
11. Wrap individually in foil / freezer paper and place in a large ziploc bag for easy grab-and-go lunch
12. To reheat: cover with paper towel and microwave for 30-60 seconds, depending on microwave
13. Top with extra salsa, sour cream or plain yogurt with cumin<|endoftext|>
