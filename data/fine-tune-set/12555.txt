Please give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Bad Hunter Stew Aka Setian Stew

Ingredients:
• Water
• Dried wild mushrooms
• Onion
• Carrots
• Turnips
• Parsnips
• Potatoes
• Shiitake mushroom
• Dried tomatoes
• Seitan
• Dried rosemary
• Dried thyme
• Dried sage
• Red wine vinegar
• Arrowroot
• Fresh parsley
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Boil one cup of the water and soak the dried mushrooms for 30 minutes
2. Save soaking water
3. If using porcini add when recommended
4. In pan over medium heat, add onion, carrot, turnips, and potatoes
5. Saut for 3 to 5 minutes until onion begins to soften
6. Add fresh mushrooms, tomato powder, and 1 / 4 cup water
7. Cook for 5 more minutes
8. Then add seitan chunks, dried herbs, and rehydrated mushrooms that have been cut in pieces
9. Cook for 5 more minutes
10. Add soaking water drained of any debris and porcini, if using them
11. Add the miso and stir
12. Cook for about 10 more minutes until vegetables are almost tender
13. Combine the remaining 1 / 4 cup water with the arrowroot and add to the pan over medium heat, stirring until thickened
14. If too thick add water 1 tablespoon at a time
15. If too thin add arrowroot 1 teaspoon at a time
16. Season with black pepper
17. Add parsley just before serving
