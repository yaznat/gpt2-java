What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Ann S Soft Sugar Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Powdered sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Cream of tartar

Steps:
1. Mix together butter, sugar, egg, vanilla, and almond extract in a large bowl
2. In another bowl stir together flour, soda, and cream of tartar
3. Slowly add the dry mixture to the wet ingredients
4. Cover and refrigerate for 2 hours
5. Preheat oven to 375
6. Divide dough in half
7. On a lightly floured surface roll each half to 3 / 16 of an inch
8. Cut out shapes as desired and sprinkle with sugar
9. Bake on cookie sheets for about 8 minutes<|endoftext|>
