What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Apple Peach Pies

Ingredients:
• Apple pie filling
• Peach slices in syrup
• Caster sugar
• Olive oil flavored cooking spray
• Shortcrust pastry
• Egg
• Double cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 200c combine the apple, peach and 2 tablespoons of sugar in a medium bowl
2. Spray four 185ml capacity ramekins or ovenproof dishes with olive oil spray to lightly grease
3. Divide the apple mixture evenly among the prepared ramekins
4. Use a 9cm-diameter round pastry cutter to cut 4 discs from the pastry
5. Place a pastry disc on top of each ramekin and gently press the edges to seal
6. Use a fork to prick the pastry of each pie
7. Brush the pies with egg and sprinkle over the remaining sugar
8. Place the pies on a baking tray and bake in oven for 20-25 minutes or until the pastry is golden brown
9. Serve the pies immediately with cream<|endoftext|>
