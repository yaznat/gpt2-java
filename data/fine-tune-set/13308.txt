Ingredients:
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Carrots
• Celery rib
• Onion
• Seasoned stuffing mix
• Chicken breasts
• Honey
• Lemon juice
• Poultry seasoning
• Parsley
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Bring the chicken broth, carrots, onion, and celery to a boil
3. Remove from heat and toss in the stuffing mix
4. Let sit covered until all the moisture is absorbed and fluff with a fork
5. Place in a prepared 3-quart casserole dish
6. Bake for 45 minute
7. Mix honey, lemon juice, poultry seasoning, and parsley
8. Brush on chicken and place 1 lemon slice on each chicken breast
9. Return to the oven and bake about 15-20 minutes longer, or until the chicken is done<|endoftext|>
