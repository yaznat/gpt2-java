Please give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Blue Cheese Lamb Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground lamb
• Parsley
• Rosemary
• Salt & pepper
• Blue cheese

Steps:
1. Mix lamb, parsley, rosemary, salt and pepper
2. Divide into 4 balls
3. Make a dent and add cheese disk, cover and flatten
4. Bbq for 4-5 minutes per side<|endoftext|>
