Ingredients:
• Chicken stock
• Potatoes
• Scallions
• Onion salt
• Ground sage
• Ground black pepper
• Paprika

Steps:
1. In a large, heavy nonstick skillet, heat 1 / 2 cup broth to boiling
2. Add potatoes, scallions, onion salt, garlic powder, sage, pepper and paprika
3. Cover and cook over high heat 5 minutes, or until liquid is absorbed, turning potatoes occasionally
4. Reduce heat to low and cook 10 minutes, adding broth as needed until potatoes are tender and browned<|endoftext|>
