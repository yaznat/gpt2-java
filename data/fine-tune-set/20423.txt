Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Amazing Onion Casserole

Ingredients:
• Vidalia onions
• Mushroom soup
• Milk
• Potato chips
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Layer onions alternately with chips and cheese in a 9x13 pan - be sure to end with cheese
2. Mix together soup and milk and pour over top of the casserole
3. Bake at 350 for an hour, covered<|endoftext|>
