Generate a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>A Different Beef Stew

Ingredients:
• Carrots
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Boneless beef cube
• Celery ribs
• Seasoning salt
• Water
• Ketchup
• Warm water
• Flour

Steps:
1. Add the water and meat into slow cooker and sprinkle on seasoning salt
2. Add in potatoes carrots, a few onions slivers and celery
3. Let cook on high for 6-8 hours until meat is cooked through and potatoes and carrots are tender
4. Mix a slurry of water and flour, mix in ketchup and any extra seasonings you may want to add
5. Mix into meat mixture and let cook for as long as you can stand without taking a bowl or until thickened to your liking<|endoftext|>
