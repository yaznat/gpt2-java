Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Prawns
• Fish
• Frozen green peas
• Eggs
• Oil
• Sesame oil
• Salt
• Instant chicken bouillon granules
• Sugar
• Light soy sauce
• Pepper
• Spring onion
• Coriander leaves
• Red chile

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a wok until hot, fry salted fish until crispy and golden
2. Dish out and set aside
3. Heat remaining oil and add in sesame oil
4. Add prawns and fry until heated through
5. Put in the rice and toss briefly and add in seasoning
6. Stir-fry well to combine
7. Make a well in the centre of the rice
8. Beat in the eggs
9. Add a dash of pepper and cover the eggs with the rice for one to two minutes
10. Toss and fry rice well until well heated through
11. Should it be a bit too dry, add a little more oil to glaze
12. Add in the peas and toss well to combine
13. Put back the fried salted fish to mix<|endoftext|>
