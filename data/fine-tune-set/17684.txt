Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Flax seed meal
• Oat bran
• Brown sugar
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Bananas
• Apples
• Golden raisins
• Nuts
• Milk
• Eggs
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Mix together flour, flax, oat bran, brown sugar, baking soda, baking powder, salt and cinnamon in a large bowl
3. Stir in apples, bananas, raisins and nuts
4. Combine honey / agave milk, beaten eggs and vanilla
5. Pour liquid ingredients into dry ingredients and stir until just moistened
6. Do not over mix
7. Fill muffin cups 3 / 4 full and bake for 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
