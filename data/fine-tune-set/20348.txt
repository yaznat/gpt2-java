Ingredients:
• Frozen broccoli
• Cream cheese
• Dry onion soup mix
• Sour cream
• Water chestnuts
• Cheddar cheese
• Ritz cracker crumbs

Steps:
1. Cook broccoli until tender in the amount of water called for on the package, drain
2. Mix together soup mix, cream cheese, sour cream and water chestnuts
3. Add drained broccoli and stir well
4. Place in lightly buttered casserole dish and top with grated cheese and cracker crums
5. Bake at 350 for 30-35 minutes or until hot throughout and bubbly<|endoftext|>
