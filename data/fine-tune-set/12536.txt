Give me a stew recipe.<|endoftext|>Audrey S Special Stew

Ingredients:
• Round steak
• Oil
• Boiling water
• Lemon juice
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic
• Onion
• Catsup
• Prepared mustard
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Pepper
• Paprika
• Sugar
• Barbecue sauce
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Celery
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Brown meat in oil
2. Drain
3. Add remaining ingredients, except potatoes, carrots, celery and cooked rice
4. Simmer for 2 hours
5. Add vegetables and contine to cook until vegetables are tender
6. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
