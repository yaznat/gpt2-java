What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Madras Curry

Ingredients:
• Sirloin steaks
• Ground coriander
• Ground cumin
• Mustard seeds
• Black peppercorns
• Chili powder
• Ground turmeric
• Salt
• Garlic
• Fresh ginger
• White vinegar
• Oil
• Onion
• Tomato paste
• Beef stock

Steps:
1. Trim the excess fat and sinew from the meat, and cut it into 1 inch cubes /
2. Place the coriander, cumin, mustard seeds, peppercorns, chilli powder, turmeric, slat, garlic and ginger in a small bowl
3. Stir to combine
4. Add the vinegar and mix to a smooth paste
5. Heat the oil in a large pan
6. Add the onion and cook over medium heat until just soft
7. Add the spice paste and stir for 1 minute
8. Add the meat and cook, stirring until it is coated with the spice paste
9. Add the tomato paste and stock
10. Simmer, covered for 1 hour 30 minutes, or until the meat is tender<|endoftext|>
