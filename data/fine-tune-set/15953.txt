• Onion powder

Steps:
1. Brown hamburger and add salt, pepper and chopped onion
2. Drain fat and add refried beans
3. Spoon mixture onto 7 inch flour tortillas, roll up and place burritos in baking dish
4. Melt together butter and flour
5. Add onion powder, garlic powder, chili powder, v-8 juice and water
6. Bring to a boil and pour over burritos and top with grated cheddar cheese
7. Bake at 350 until cheese melts and sauce bubbles
8. Top with shredded lettuce<|endoftext|>
