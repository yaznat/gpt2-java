Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• White onion
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Jalapenos
• Long-grain rice
• Beef broth
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Refried beans
• Flour tortillas
• Jalapeno
• Sour cream
• Guacamole
• Tomatoes
• Cheese
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Saute diced onions over medium-high heat until soft, add meat
2. Cook meat until nearly done, add minced garlic and turn down the heat to medium-low
3. When all the meat is cooked add half of the can of jalapenos including all of the reserved liquid
4. Cook rice according instructions on the bag except where it calls for water add broth instead
5. Heat up the beans on the stove or in the microwave
6. Place hot beans into a serving bowl, cover and keep warm
7. Wrap all tortillas tightly in between two sheets of aluminum foil, place in oven and turn oven on to 350f
8. Once tortillas are warm the rice should also be done
9. Add spices into the rice and mix gently together
10. Place a little meat, beans, rice and your choice of toppings into each tortilla
11. Fold bottom edge up and then both sides
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
