Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Fresh thyme
• Garlic
• Black peppercorns
• Salt
• Beef t-bone steaks

Steps:
1. In small saucepan cook vinegar over low heat until reduced to about 3 tablespoons and vinegar is syrupy
2. In small bowl stir together thyme, garlic, peppercorns, and salt
3. Rub on both sides of steaks
4. Coat grill rack with nonstick cooking spray
5. Preheat grill to medium direct heat
6. Grill steaks, uncovered, to desired doneness, turning once halfway through grilling
7. Serve steaks drizzled with vinegar syrup<|endoftext|>
