Give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Omu Rice Japanese Omelette

Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Carrots
• Red capsicums
• Onions
• Bacon
• Tomato paste
• Oil
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Salt and pepper
• Tomato ketchup

Steps:
1. Prepare the vegetables and bacon
2. Mix well in a bowl, and set aside
3. In another bowl, whisk the eggs well
4. Add the sugar, oil, salt and pepper
5. Set aside
6. Heat the first two teaspoons of oil in a deep saucepan, and add the vegetables
7. Cook for 5-10 minutes
8. Add the rice, and stir well
9. Add the tomato paste, and stir well
10. Cook for 2 minutes
11. Remove from heat
12. The next step has a long explanation, but it is not hard
13. It needs to be repeated for each of the four servings, and takes about 3 minutes each time
14. The series of photos will give you a good idea of part of the process
15. Put one teaspoon of additional oil in a large, heated, non-stick frying pan
16. Use a brush to spread the oil around the base of the pan
17. Pour in 1 / 4 of the egg mixture, and tilt the pan to coat the base
18. Cook for about 2 minutes
19. Scrape the egg away from the edges of the pan
20. Add 1 / 4 of the rice mixture to one side of the cooked egg
21. Flip the other side of the egg over the rice mixture
22. Invert a plate over the pan
23. Pick the pan up and turn it over, and your omelette is on the plate
24. Garnish with the tomato ketchup
25. Repeat the previous step 3 more times<|endoftext|>
