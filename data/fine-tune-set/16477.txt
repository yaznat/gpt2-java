What's a recipe for a smoothie?<|endoftext|>A Healthy Chocolate Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Ovaltine
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Chop up bananas
2. Put bananas on a plate and sprinkle ovaltine and stick in microwave for about 6-10 seconds
3. Put all ingredients in blender and mix till creamy
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
