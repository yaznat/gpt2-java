Ingredients:
• Butter
• Flour
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Walnuts
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Vanilla
• Milk

Steps:
1. Cut 1 / 3 cup butter into 3 / 4 cup flour, brown sugar and cinnamon till fine
2. Stir in walnuts
3. Sift 1 1 / 2 cups flour with baking powder, soda and salt
4. Cream 1 / 2 cup butter in bowl
5. Add sugar and cream till light
6. Add eggs one at time, beating well
7. Blend in vanilla
8. Add dry ingredients alternately wit sour cream to creamed mixture
9. Spread in 9x9-inch floured and greased pan
10. Sprinkle with walnut mixture
11. Bake at 325 for 50 minutes
12. When cool, split horizontally and fill with filling
13. Store in refrigerator
14. For the buttercream filling: combine flour, salt salt and milk in saucepan
15. Cook over low heat till thick, stirring constantly
16. Cool
17. Cream butter and sugar
18. Add flour mixture and beat till fluffy<|endoftext|>
