What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Real Spanish Rice

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Water
• Beef bouillon
• Salt
• Chili powder
• Green bell pepper
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Chopped tomato
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Using a teflon pan- place olive oil in the pan and when it is hot add rice -- fry until nicely brown
2. Reduce heat -- add bell pepper, onion, garlic and continue to saut
3. After about 5 minutes or so add water, tomato, chili powder and salt
4. Place lid on skillet and turn heat down, simmer gently
5. When the water has all evaporated-10-15 min later- slide the lid off of the skillet about half way so the steam will escape so your rice is not wet
6. Serve<|endoftext|>
