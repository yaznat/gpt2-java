Give me a donut recipe.<|endoftext|>Old Fashioned Cake Doughnuts Donuts

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Eggs
• Unsalted butter
• Milk
• Flour
• Oil
• Cinnamon sugar

Steps:
1. In a large bowl mix the sugar, baking powder, salt and nutmeg
2. Add eggs, milk and melted butter
3. Beat well
4. Add 3 cups of the flour, beating until blended
5. Add one more cup of flour and beat well
6. The dough should be soft and sticky but firm enough to handle
7. If you feel its necessary, add up to 1 / 2 cup more flour
8. Cover with plastic wrap and chill for at least one hour
9. Remove your dough from the fridge and begin heating about 1" of oil to 360f in a large metal skillet
10. Working half the dough at a time, roll it out on a floured surface to about 1 / 2" thickness
11. Cut out circles using a doughnut cutter or large biscuit or cookie cutter
12. For the center, i actually use the cap off my martini shaker
13. ) to cut the holes
14. Gently drop the doughnuts in batches into the hot oil
15. Flip them over as they puff and turn them a couple more times as they cook
16. They will take about 2-3 minutes in total and will be lovely and golden brown all over
17. Remove from the oil and set them on paper towels or brown paper bag
18. Douse them with sugar and cinnamon, icing, chocolate dipped with sprinkles or whatever you like<|endoftext|>
