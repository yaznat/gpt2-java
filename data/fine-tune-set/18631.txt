What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Apple Oatmeal Pudding In The Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Eggs
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Oats
• Non-fat powdered milk
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Spray your slow cooker with cooking spray
2. Add the cut up apples
3. Add the rest of the ingredients
4. Mix well
5. It will look like it could not possibly be enough liquid when you first start mixing
6. But stick with it, it will work!
7. Cover and cook on low for 4 hours
8. Serve hot or cold<|endoftext|>
