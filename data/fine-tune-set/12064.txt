Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Flour
• Brown sugar
• Allspice
• Salt
• Butter
• Ice water
• Eggs
• Whipping cream
• Vanilla
• Orange peel

Steps:
1. Peel and dice sweet potatoes
2. Boil until tender, about 20-25 minutes
3. Drain and mash with a potato masher
4. Put aside
5. For the crust: in a big bowl combine flour, sugar, 1 ts allspice and the salt
6. Cut in butter until mixture resembles coarse sand
7. Sprinkle 1 tbs of the ice water over the flour mixture and gently toss with a fork
8. Push moistened dough to side of bowl
9. Repeat moistening flour mixture, using 1 tbs of ice water at a time, until all mixture is moistened
10. Form dough into a ball
11. On a lightly floured surface use your hands to slightly flatten the dough
12. Roll out into a circle of 12 inches in diameter
13. Transfer dough to a 9 inch pie plate
14. If you have any extra dough, fold it under
15. Crimp edges as desired
16. Do not prick the dough
17. Preheat oven to 190c / 375f
18. For the filling: from your sweet potato mash measure 1 1 / 2 cups
19. In a medium bowl combine this, brown sugar, cream, eggs, 1 1 / 2 ts allspice, vanilla, orange peel and salt
20. Pour filling into prepared pie plate
21. Bake for 40-45 minutes or until knife inserted near the centre comes out clean
22. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
