What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Delicious French Fries And Pork Chops Or Chicken Bake

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Seasoning salt
• Butter
• Oil
• Cream soup
• Half-and-half
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• Parmesan cheese
• Old cheddar cheese
• Durkee onions
• Frozen crinkle cut french fries
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Butter a 13 x 9-inch baking dish
2. Sprinkle the pork chops with black pepper and garlic powder on both sides
3. In a skillet melt the butter with oil over medium heat
4. Brown chops on both sides, then set aside
5. In a bowl mix together the undiluted soup with half and half cream, sour cream, mayonnaise, parmesan cheese and 1 / 2 cup grated cheddar cheese
6. Mix well to combine
7. Add in the chopped crinkle fries with half a can of durkee french fried onions
8. Toss lightly to combine
9. Spread the mixture into the buttered baking dish, then place the browned pork chops on top
10. Cover with foil and bake in a 350 degree oven for about 40 minutes or until the pork are tender
11. Remove from the oven sprinkle with about 1-1 / 2 cups cheddar cheese and the remaining half of the dried onions
12. Return to the oven and bake uncovered for about 5-8 minutes or until the cheese melts<|endoftext|>
