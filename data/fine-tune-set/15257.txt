Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Onions
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Balsamic vinegar
• Curry powder
• Pizza dough
• Brie cheese
• Bosc pears
• Chives

Steps:
1. Heat pizza stone in 400f oven at least 30 minutes
2. For onions, melt butter with oil in large skillet on medium heat
3. Stir in onions, salt and pepper
4. Cook stirring often, 5 minutes or until starting to brown
5. Reduce heat to low
6. Cook, stirring occasionally, 25 minutes, until golden brown and very tender
7. Stir in vinegar
8. Turn heat to medium-low
9. Cook, stirring often, 5 minutes
10. Stir in curry powder
11. Cool to room temperature
12. Makes 1 1 / 4 cups
13. For pizza, cut dough into 3 pieces
14. On floured surface, press 1 piece of dough into 8 inch round
15. Transfer to pizza peel covered in parchment
16. Spread with about 1 / 3 caramelized onions
17. Brush exposed edges with oil
18. Put 1 / 3 of pear slices over onions, arranging like spokes on wheel
19. Arrange 1 / 3 of brie slices in between pear spokes
20. Slide pizza on parchment onto pizza stone
21. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until bottom is crisp, brie is golden and pears are tender
22. Garnish with chives
23. Repeat with remaining dough, onions, pear, brie and chives<|endoftext|>
