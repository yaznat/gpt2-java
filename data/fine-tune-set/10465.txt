Give me a cookie recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Cheddar Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Unbleached flour
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Cheddar cheese
• Apples
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy, then stir in the egg and vanilla
2. Add the combined dry ingredients, blending well
3. Stir in the cheese, apples and nuts
4. Drop rounded teaspoonfuls of the dough onto an ungreased cookie sheet and bake at 375 degrees f
5. For 15 minutes
6. Remove from the cookie sheet and cool on a wire rack or plate<|endoftext|>
