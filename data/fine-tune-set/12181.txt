Please give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Apple Hills Old Fashioned Apple Pie

Ingredients:
• Pie crust
• Apple juice
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Cornstarch
• Lemon juice
• Butter
• Apples
• Milk

Steps:
1. Prepare crust recipe and chill for one hour
2. While crust is chilling bring apple juice, sugars, cinnamon, nutmeg and cornstarch to a boil until thick, stirring constantly
3. Add lemon juice and butter
4. Save a little sauce to brush top of baked pie
5. Toss with sliced apples, reserving tablespoon of sauce to brush on crust as pie comes from oven
6. Roll out bottom crust, leaving a 1 inch overhang
7. Fill pie
8. Roll out top crust and place on top
9. Trim to 1 / 2-inch overhang
10. Crimp together and flute edge
11. Remember, the more time you spend preparing the crust, the less spillage you are apt to have
12. Use a knife to cut slits in crust for air vents
13. Brush crust with milk
14. Sprinkle with tablespoon of sugar and dash of cinnamon
15. Bake at 375 degrees fahrenheit for 1 hour, or until a knife can be inserted into pie smoothly
16. Brush with the tablespoon of reserved sauce<|endoftext|>
