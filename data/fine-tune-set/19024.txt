Ingredients:
• Milk
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Pure vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In a large heavy saucepan, bring milk to a boil
2. Meanwhile, beat eggs
3. Add sugar, cornstarch and salt
4. Mix well
5. Gradually, add a small amount of hot milk
6. Return all to the saucepan
7. Cook and stir constantly for 6-8 minutes or until mixture thickens and coasts a spoon
8. Gradually stir in condensed milk and vanilla
9. Mix well
10. Chill 3-4 hours
11. Pour into an ice cream freezer
12. Freeze according to manufacturer's directions
13. Yield: about 1-1 / 2 qts<|endoftext|>
