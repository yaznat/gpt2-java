What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Baby Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• White whole wheat flour
• Wheat bran
• Sea salt
• Baking soda
• Brown sugar
• Nonfat yogurt
• Egg
• Honey
• Butter
• Fruit

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f degrees, racks in the middle
2. In a medium bowl, combine the flour, wheat bran, salt, baking soda and sugar
3. Beat together the yogurt, egg, honey, and butter in a second larger bowl
4. Add the dry ingredients and fold in until everything comes together
5. Fold in any optional add-ins, raisins, nuts, and the like
6. Resist overmixing
7. Either grease a mini-muffin tin with butter or line the tin with small muffin papers
8. Fill each 3 / 4 full
9. Bake 10 -15 minutes, until muffins are golden on top and cooked through
10. You can also make larger muffins in a standard size muffin pan with this batter, you just need to bake them about 5 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
