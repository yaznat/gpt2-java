What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Black Bean And Corn Chicken Chili

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Garlic powder
• Dry red wine
• Black beans
• Great northern beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Whole kernel corn
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large skillet on medium-high heat
2. Add chicken, bell pepper and onion
3. Cook and stir 7 to 8 minutes or until lightly browned
4. Heat small nonstick skillet on medium heat 2 minutes
5. Add chili powder, cumin and garlic
6. Toast 30 seconds or until aromatic, stirring constantly
7. Add to chicken mixture
8. Stir in remaining ingredients
9. Bring to boil
10. Reduce heat to low
11. Simmer 20 minutes
12. Our suggested serve: plate some chili over cornbread and sprinkle with shredded cheese<|endoftext|>
