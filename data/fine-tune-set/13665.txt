What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>A Taste Of Fall Crock Pot Pork Stew

Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Potatoes
• Apples
• Onion
• Baby carrots
• Chicken broth
• Thyme
• Garlic powder
• Ground cloves
• Ground mustard
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place potatoes, apples, onions and carrots on bottom of crock pot
2. Cut pork up into large bite size pieces
3. Place pork on top of vegetables
4. Pour chicken broth on top of pork
5. Sprinkle spices on top of pork
6. No need to stir!
7. Cook on high for 4-5 hours or on low for 8 hours<|endoftext|>
