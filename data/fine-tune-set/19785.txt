Give me a fries recipe.<|endoftext|>Debbies Home Cheese Fries

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Plain flour
• Season salt
• Garlic salt
• Black pepper
• Oil
• Bacon
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Clean and scrub potatoes
2. Slice 1 / 8 inch thick
3. Place in bowl of cold water for 30 minutes or longer
4. Drain well
5. Season with season salt, garlic powder and pepper
6. Add flour and toss to coat, making sure to separate slices
7. Fry in hot oil until lightly brown
8. Drain on paper towel for a minute
9. Sprinkle with more salt
10. While hot sprinkle the cheese and bacon over the top of fries
11. Return to hot oven only until the cheese is melted
12. Sprinkle with green onions
13. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
