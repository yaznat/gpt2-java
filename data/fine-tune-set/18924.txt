Ingredients:
• Milk
• Cream
• Short-grain rice
• Eggs
• Egg yolk
• Salt
• Sugar
• Vanilla essence
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Heat milk and cream in pot
2. Add washed rice, simmer 20 minutes
3. Mix eggs, yolk, sugar, salt and vanilla in a jug
4. Remove rice from heat and pour a little into egg mixture
5. Stir constantly and add a little more
6. Pour this mix back into rice pot
7. Stir till sugar dissolved
8. Put a 5 cup dish into a roasting pan, pour in custard
9. Sprinkle nutmeg over
10. Pour hot water into roasting pan to come 1 / 2 way up sides of dish
11. Bake at 160 c for 30 - 40 minutes or till knife inserted comes out clean<|endoftext|>
