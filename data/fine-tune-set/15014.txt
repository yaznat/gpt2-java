Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Quick Chinese Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Water
• Minute rice
• Butter
• Egg
• Onion
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Boil 1 cup water and the rice, stir and let stand for 5 minutes covered
2. Melt butter in skillet
3. Add egg
4. Cook until set
5. Add onion and rice
6. Brown
7. Mix 1 / 3 cup water and soy sauce
8. Add to rice
9. Simmer 5 minutes
10. Serve<|endoftext|>
