Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Basil Beef Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Beef
• Vegetable oil
• Basil leaves
• Garlic cloves
• Thai red chili peppers
• Cooked jasmine rice
• Fish sauce
• Sugar
• Cucumber

Steps:
1. In a mortar, pound garlic and chili, set aside
2. You can use a food processor or other chopper if you don't have a mortar
3. Heat oil in pan to medium heat and stir in garlic / chili mix until aromatic
4. Add beef and stir until no pink shows
5. Add rice, fish sauce, and sugar - stir and let cook 3-5 minutes
6. Stir in basil leaves
7. Serve with sliced cucumber<|endoftext|>
