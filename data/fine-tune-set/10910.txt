Ingredients:
• Romaine lettuce hearts
• Hearts of palm
• Cherry tomatoes
• Orange juice
• Orange zest
• Fresh lemon juice
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Fresh basil leaves
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a bowl, arrange the hearts of romaine and the hearts of palm
2. Toss in the cherry tomatoes
3. Combine the zest, orange juice, lemon juice and olive oil
4. Mix well, and pour over salad when ready to serve
5. Season with salt and pepper, and garnish with reserved zest<|endoftext|>
