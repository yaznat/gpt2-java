Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Brown sugar
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Apricot nectar
• Hot water
• Orange zest
• Butter
• Black pepper
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degree
2. Butter a 13 x 9-inch baking dish
3. Place potatoes in prepared dish, and set aside
4. In a saucepan, combine brown sugar, cornstarch, salt and cinnamon
5. Stir in apricot nectar water and orange peel
6. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly, cook, and stir for 2 minutes more
7. Remove from heat
8. Stir in butter and pecans
9. Pour over sweet potatoes
10. Season with black pepper
11. Bake, uncovered for 20-25 minutes or until heated through<|endoftext|>
