Ingredients:
• Pollock fillets
• Plain low-fat yogurt
• Mustard
• Salt
• Pepper
• Fresh thyme
• Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
• Corn flakes
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Mix together yogurt, mustard, salt, pepper, and thyme
2. Place each fillet in mixture to coat, place coated fillets in a resealable plastic bag
3. Pour remaining marinade in bags
4. Place bags in refrigerator for 8 hours or overnight
5. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
6. Mix together breadcrumbs and crushed cornflakes
7. Remove fillets from bag & drain excess marinade
8. Dredge in in breadcrumb mixture until coated
9. Place on cookie sheet
10. Drizzle with oil
11. Cook 15-20 minutes until golden brown
12. Watch carefully to prevent burning of crust
13. Serve plain, with tartar sauce, or with ketchup<|endoftext|>
