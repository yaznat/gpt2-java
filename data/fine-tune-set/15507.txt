Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Barbecue sauce
• Water
• Elbow macaroni
• Velveeta cheese
• Tomatoes
• Oscar mayer real bacon bits

Steps:
1. Brown meat with onion in large skillet on medium heat
2. Drain
3. Add barbecue sauce and water
4. Mix well
5. Bring to boil
6. Add macaroni
7. Cook 8 to 10 minutes or until macaroni is tender
8. Stir in velvetta
9. Cook until completely melted, stirring occassionally
10. Top with the tomatoes and bacon pieces<|endoftext|>
