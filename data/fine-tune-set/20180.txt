Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Garlic
• Fresh gingerroot
• Oil
• Boneless sirloin steak
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Red pepper
• Celery ribs
• Water
• Cornstarch
• Curry powder

Steps:
1. Mix the soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and 2 t oil
2. Add beef
3. Toss to coat
4. Chill for 15 minutes
5. In wok heat remaining oil
6. Stir-fry beef over med heat for 2 minutes
7. Remove beef and set aside
8. Stir-fry onion for 1 minute
9. Add peppers and celery
10. Stir-fry 2 minutes
11. Return beef to wok
12. Mix water, cornstarch and curry until smooth
13. Add to wok, bring to a boil stirring constantly
14. Boil for 1 minute<|endoftext|>
