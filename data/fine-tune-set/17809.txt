What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon And Egg Breakfast Muffins

Ingredients:
• White lily self-rising flour
• Granulated sugar
• Dry mustard
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Egg
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Cooked bacon

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400 degrees
2. Lightly spray 12 muffin cups with no-stick cooking spray or use muffin cup liners
3. Combine flour, sugar, and dry mustard
4. Stir in cheese and make a well in the center of the ingredients
5. Whisk together egg, milk and oil
6. Add all at once to the flour mixture, stirring just until moistened
7. Fill prepared muffin cups 1 / 3 full
8. Combine the diced egg and cooked crumbled bacon
9. Divide evenly into muffin cups
10. Top with remaining batter
11. Muffin cups should be about 2 / 3 full
12. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes or until muffins are lightly browned
13. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
