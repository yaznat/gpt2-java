Give me a burrito recipe.<|endoftext|>Beany Burritos

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Cooked beef
• Taco seasoning mix
• Black beans
• Pinto beans
• Flour tortillas
• Green onion
• Salsa
• Sour cream
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. In a skillet, heat oil over medium heat
2. Stir / saute onion until translucent
3. Add in leftover chopped beef, taco seasoning, and beans
4. Stir until heated through
5. Warm tortillas and fill with bean / beef mixture
6. Top with garnishes as you like
7. Roll up burrito-style and eat
8. Serving suggestions--a big spinach salad and a bowl of baby carrots<|endoftext|>
