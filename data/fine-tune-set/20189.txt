Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beefy Tex Mex Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Poblano chile
• Onion
• Chili powder
• Ground beef
• Tomatoes
• Tortilla chips
• Salt
• Pepper
• Mexican blend cheese

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat
2. Add the poblano, onion and chili powder and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 4 minutes
3. Add the beef and cook, breaking up the meat, until the meat is just cooked through, about 3 minutes
4. Stir in the tomato and tortilla chips and season with salt and pepper
5. Divide the meat mixture among 4 plates and top with the cheese<|endoftext|>
