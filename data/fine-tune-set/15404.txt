What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Backyard Mediterranean Burgers

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Fresh basil
• Ground cumin
• Paprika
• Cayenne
• Salt
• Ground coriander

Steps:
1. Lightly oil grill
2. Heat barbecue to medium
3. Whisk egg in a large bowl
4. Crumble in beef
5. Sprinkle with onion, basil, cumin, paprika, cayenne, salt and ground coriander
6. Using your hands or a large wooden spoon, mix just until all ingredients are evenly distributed
7. Shape into 4 burgers about 3 / 4 inch thick
8. Grill with lid closed until firm when pressed and well done, 5 to 8 minutes per side
9. Serve in warm pitas topped with honey-mustard mayonnaise, cucumber, tomato and watercress or spinach<|endoftext|>
