Give me a quesadilla recipe.<|endoftext|>Cheese And Bean Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Refried beans
• Salsa
• Chili powder
• 10-inch flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. In med saucepan, heat beans, salsa, and chili powder
2. Cook and stir until bubbly
3. Spray griddle with cooking spray
4. Brush 2 tortillas with water, brown that side
5. Turn tortillas over
6. Spread with 1 / 2 the bean mixture, sprinkle with 1 / 2 the cheese and 1 / 2 the cilantro
7. Top with second tortilla, press down, and brush top with water
8. Carefully turn over and brown other side
9. With pizza cutter, cut each quesadilla into 6 wedges<|endoftext|>
