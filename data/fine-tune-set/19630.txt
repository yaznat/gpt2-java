Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Mayonnaise
• Garlic
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Parmesan cheese
• Onion
• Pepper
• Coarse salt
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Cook potatoes in boiling water until tender
2. Drain and cool potatoes
3. Cook bacon in oven
4. Cool and crumble bacon
5. In large bowl, combine mayonnaise, garlic, and onion
6. Mix well
7. Stir in artichoke hearts, cheese, and salad dressing
8. Add salt and pepper to taste
9. Mix well
10. In large serving bowl, combine potatoes and bacon
11. Gently stir in dressing until well mixed
12. Cover and chill thoroughly before serving
