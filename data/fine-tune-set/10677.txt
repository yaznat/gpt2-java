Ingredients:
• Fresh asparagus
• Butter
• Onions
• Salt
• Mushroom
• Tarragon
• Black pepper
• Dry white wine
• Water
• Flour
• Garlic cloves
• Pasta

Steps:
1. Steam the asparagus until just tender, transfer to a colander & rinse under cold running water then set aside to drain well
2. Melt the butter in a large skillet
3. Add onion & salt and saute for about 5 minutes
4. Add mushrooms, tarragon and pepper, cover and cook over medium heat for 15 mins, stirring occasionally
5. Add wine and water
6. Turn up heat until liquid comes to boil, then sprinkle in the flour, whisking constantly until it completely dissolves
7. Lower heat, cover, and simmer for about 10 mins, stirring frequently
8. Add garlic, cook about 5 mins more
9. Stir the asparagus into the sauce at the last minute when your pasta is done<|endoftext|>
