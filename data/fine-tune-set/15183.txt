Ingredients:
• Prepared pizza crust
• Mozzarella cheese
• Grilled chicken breast
• Barbecue sauce
• Feta cheese
• Red onion

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f
2. Place pizza crust on ungreased baking sheet and sprinkle with 1 / 2 cup of the mozzarella cheese
3. Toss chicken with barbecue sauce
4. Spoon evenly onto crust
5. Top with feta cheese, onions and the remaining 1 / 2 cup mozzarella cheese
6. Bake 10 to 12 minute or until cheese is melted and crust is golden brown
7. Let stand 5 minute before cutting to serve<|endoftext|>
