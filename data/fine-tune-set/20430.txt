Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Amish Chicken Casserole

Ingredients:
• Noodles
• Cooked chicken
• Chicken broth
• Milk
• Mushrooms
• Salt
• Pepper
• Margarine
• Flour
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Melt margarine, then add flour and stir until smooth
2. Gradually add milk and broth, then seasonings and mushrooms
3. Combine chicken, cooked noodles, and prepared sauce
4. Place in an ungreased 9 x 13 inch baking pan and top with parmesan cheese
5. Bake at 350f for 35-45 minutes, or until heated thoroughly and top is browned slightly<|endoftext|>
