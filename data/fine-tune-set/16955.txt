What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Boiled Shrimp In Beer With Cocktail Sauce

Ingredients:
• Raw shrimp
• Budweiser beer
• Chicken bouillon cube
• Sea salt
• Old bay seasoning
• Lemon pepper
• Black pepper
• All purpose greek seasoning
• Ice
• Seafood cocktail sauce
• Ketchup
• Horseradish
• Sugar

Steps:
1. For cocktail sauce, combine ketchup, horseradish and sugar in a small bowl
2. Chill until needed
3. In a large sauce pot add shrimp, beer, chicken bouillon cube, sea salt, old bay seasoning, lemon pepper, black pepper and greek seasoning
4. Stir to combine flavors
5. Bring to a boil and boil only until shrimp turns light pink about 5 minutes
6. Drain and add ice to chill shrimp<|endoftext|>
