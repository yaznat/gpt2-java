Please give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Chris Awesomely Easy Ice Cream Dessert

Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Vanilla instant pudding mix
• Milk
• Crackers
• Margarine
• Sugar
• Whipped topping

Steps:
1. In large bowl, using electric mixer, combine softened ice cream, milk, and sprinkle in the dry instant pudding mix
2. Place mixture in refridgerator while preparing crust
3. Crush crackers into a 9x13 pan, add sugar, and pour melted margarine over all
4. Mix well and spread into crust at the bottom of the pan
5. Spread ice cream mixture over the crust and top with the whipped topping
6. Refridgerate at least 2 hours before serving<|endoftext|>
