What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Back Ribs Casserole

Ingredients:
• Beef back ribs
• Sauerkraut
• Caraway seed
• Onion
• Tomatoes
• Granulated sugar

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Cut back ribs into serving size pieces
3. Sprinkle both sides of pieces with seasoned salt and pepper
4. Mix caraway seed into sauerkraut, then place into a shallow 2-quart casserole
5. Arrange onion slices over sauerkraut
6. Cover with the tomatoes and their juice, cutting any whole tomatoes into pieces
7. Sprinkle with sugar
8. Place back ribs over entire surface
9. Cover
10. Bake for 1 hour
11. Uncover and continue to cook about 1 more hour<|endoftext|>
