Ingredients:
• Stew meat
• Dry onion soup mix
• Mushroom gravy
• Potatoes
• Baby carrots
• Peas
• Mushrooms
• Dry white wine
• Black pepper
• Flour

Steps:
1. Brown meat in skillet
2. Place meat in crock pot first, then add pepper and onion soup mix
3. Add gravy, then potatoes, carrots, peas, and mushrooms, in that order
4. Pour wine over top
5. Cover and cook on low for 7-9 hours
6. Stir in flour by the teaspoon in the last hour of cooking to desired thickness, if necessary
7. Add a dash or two of worcestershire sauce, if desired<|endoftext|>
