Ingredients:
• Peanut butter
• Flour
• Granulated sugar
• Egg
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Sift together sugar, nutmeg, and cinnamon very well
3. Add egg and peanut butter, mix thoroughly
4. Mixture should be very moist
5. Add the flour and mix thoroughly
6. Roll into balls and place on cookies sheet, leaing about an inch in between each cookie
7. Bake for 15-20 minutes, checking every so often and touching the top of the cookie with a spoon
8. They are done when touching the top shows has no effect on the cookie
9. Eat with milk or alone
10. I recommend eating them when they are still warm
