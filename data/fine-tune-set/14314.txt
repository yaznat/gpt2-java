Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Jalapeno jelly
• Ketchup
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Fresh lime juice
• Sugar
• Garlic cloves
• Jalapeno
• Fish sauce
• Jicama
• Carrots
• Cilantro
• Mint
• Olive oil
• Kaiser rolls
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Place the tofu on a work surface and top with a cookie sheet and 2 or 3 heavy cans or pans
2. Let stand for about 1 hour, or until tofu is very firm and liquid has been pressed out
3. Pat the toful dry
4. In a small bowl, whisk the jalapeno jelly with the ketchup, soy sauce, sesame oil and 1 tbls
5. Lime juice
6. In a large bowl, mix sugar with garlic and minced jalapeno
7. Using the side of a spoon, mash the mixture to a paste
8. Add the fish sauce and remaining 1 tbls
9. Of lime juice
10. Stir in the jicama, carrots, cilatro and mint
11. Light a grill or turn on the broiler
12. Brush the tofu and the cut sides of the rolls with the olive oil
13. Lightly grill the rolls, about 2 minutes
14. Grill or broil the tofu until lightly charred, about 8-10 minutes
15. Brush on the jalapeno jelly glaze and grill, turning and brushing until nicely glazed, 2 to 3 minutes longer
16. Spread a thin layer of mayonnaise on both halves of each kaiser roll
17. Mound the jicama slaw on the rolls and top with the tofu steaks
18. Colse the sandwiches, cut in half and serve! enjoy!<|endoftext|>
