Please generate a milkshake recipe.<|endoftext|>Dad S Chocolate Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Cocoa powder
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine cocoa powder and ice cream
2. Add some of the milk, mashing with a potato masher or a hand held electric beater as you go
3. You can choose to leave it thicker and more lumpy by adding less milk or mashing less, or have a thinner shake by adding more milk and mashing more thoroughly
4. Add more cocoa powder if needed
5. Serve!
6. I also like topping with mini chocolate chips<|endoftext|>
