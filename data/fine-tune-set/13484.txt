Ingredients:
• Boneless beef brisket
• Onion
• Garlic
• Cloves
• Water
• Apple jelly
• Dry white wine
• Dijon mustard
• Green onions
• Salt
• Black peppercorns
• Curry powder
• Parsley
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Place brisket
2. Onion, garlic and cloves in large dutch oven
3. Add water to cover
4. Bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat, cover and simmer 2 1 / 2 to 3 hours or until tender
6. Drain brisket, cover and refrigerate up to 24 hours
7. To prepare glaze, combine apple jelly, wine, mustard, green onions, salt, peppercorns and curry powder in small saucepan and heat until jelly melts, stirring occasionally
8. Place brisket in shallow roasting pan
9. Brush with glaze and roast in preheated 325-degree oven 45 minutes, basting frequently with glaze
10. Place brisket on heated serving platter and garnish with parsley and tomato roses
11. Carve brisket into thin slices and serve with remaining glaze
12. Brisket may also be cooked on charcoal grill for 30 minutes, basting often with glaze<|endoftext|>
