Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Ricotta cheese
• Sugar
• Salt
• Vanilla
• Milk
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the egg yolks and ricotta cheese so that there are no large lumps of cheese, but make sure the mixture does not become too smooth
2. Whisk in the sugar, salt and vanilla, then the milk
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour and baking powder
4. Fold this into the egg batter just until no lumps remain
5. Separately, whip the egg whites to medium-stiff, shiny peaks, about 4 minutes
6. Gently fold these into the batter just until uniformly combined
7. Ladle a generous one-fourth cup of batter onto a medium-hot, lightly greased griddle for each pancake
8. Place 10 to 12 blueberries on each pancake and cook, about 2 minutes per side, until the cakes are goldenbrown and cooked through
9. Serve immediately with maple syrup on the side<|endoftext|>
