What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Black And White Chili

Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Onion
• Carrot
• Green pepper
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Tomato sauce
• Diced tomatoes
• Black beans
• Cannellini beans
• Water
• Chili powder
• Fresh basil
• Salt
• Ground cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Saute diced onion, diced green pepper and shredded carrot in olive oil until tender but not browned
2. Approximately when the liquid cooks out of the vegetables
3. Add garlic and ground beef and cook until ground beef is browned
4. Add all remaining ingredients and bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered
6. Cook for 30 minutes or until your desired consistency
7. Feel free to add green chili, hot sauce or other heat sources to kick it up or reduce seasonings if you feel it is too hot<|endoftext|>
