Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>A1 Turkey Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• A. 1. original sauce
• Yellow mustard
• Saltine crackers
• Garlic powder
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients together in a large bowl
2. Form into 6 patties
3. Cook patties in a frying pan on medium heat until fully cooked, flipping as needed
4. I usually do 3 patties in the frying pan at a time
5. Be careful when flipping the patty, meat is very moist
6. Serve on a bun & top with your favorite condiments<|endoftext|>
