What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Leftover Turkey Salad Hot Dish Or Sandwich Filling

Ingredients:
• Cooked turkey
• Celery
• Almonds
• Onions
• Fresh lemon rind
• Lemon juice
• Mayonnaise
• Cheddar cheese
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Red pepper flakes
• Old bay seasoning
• Garlic
• Cilantro
• Bell pepper
• Pimiento
• Pineapple
• Apple

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. In a medium mixing bowl combine turkey through lemon juice
3. Add mayonnaise to moisten, add cheese and toss lightly
4. Adjust the salt and black pepper - to taste
5. Add any options, adjusting to taste
6. Place mixture in a lightly buttered casserole dish and cover
7. Bake for 20- 25 minutes or until heated through
8. Serve hot over biscuits or rice
9. Or serve cold as a filling for sterling's seashell sandwiches recipe<|endoftext|>
