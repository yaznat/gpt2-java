Please give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Brandied Custard Crepe Filling

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Eggs
• Brandy
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In a 2-quarter saucepan, combine the milk, sugar, and cornstarch and cook until thickened
2. Add of the hot milk mixture to the beaten eggs and blend well
3. Add the egg mixture to the remaining hot milk mixture in the saucepan and add stir over medium heat
4. Remove pan from heat
5. Stir in the brandy and vanilla
6. Chill completely
7. For use as a crepe filling - recipe makes enough filling for 10 crepes
8. Place 3 tablespoons of custard in the center of each crepe
9. Add about 1 / 3 cup of sliced strawberries, if you wish
10. Then gently roll each crepe and place on a plate
11. Top each crepe with whipping cream and fresh strawberries<|endoftext|>
