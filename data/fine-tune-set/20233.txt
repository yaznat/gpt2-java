What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Catfish Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Catfish fillets
• Lemon juice
• Soy sauce
• Vegetable oil
• Carrot
• Broccoli floret
• Zucchini
• Fresh mushrooms
• Cauliflower floret
• Green onion
• Tomatoes
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Cut catfish fillets into 2 x 3 / 4 inch strips
2. In a bowl, add the lemon juice and soy sauce
3. Stir to combine
4. Add the fish strips to the bowl and let marinate for 20 minutes
5. Heat wok or large skillet to medium-high
6. Pour 2 tablespoons oil around the top, rotate pan to coat with oil
7. Add carrots to wok
8. Stirfry for 2 minutes
9. Add the next 7 ingredients and stirfry for 2 minutes
10. Transfer vegetables to a plate or bowl and set aside
11. Add 1 tablespoon oil to wok and let get hot
12. Drain fish and reserve marinade
13. Add fish to wok and stirfry for 2 minutes or until fish flakes
14. Transfer vegetables back to wok
15. Add cornstarch, water, salt, and pepper to reserved marinade
16. Stir to combine
17. Pour into wok
18. Stirfry on low heat for 2 minutes or until thickened and bubbly
19. Serve over hot cooked rice<|endoftext|>
