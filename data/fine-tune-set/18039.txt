Ingredients:
• Butter
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Pecans
• Eggs
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In a saucepan over low heat, melt the butter and chocolate chips, stirring until smooth
2. Cool
3. Add pecans
4. Stir until well coated
5. In a large bowl, combine eggs, sugar, flour and vanilla
6. Fold in the chocolate mixture
7. Fill paper lined muffin cups 2 / 3 full
8. Bake at 325 degrees for 35 to 38 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
