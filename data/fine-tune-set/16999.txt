Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Flour
• Pecan halves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Cream butter, cheese, salt, and pepper together
3. Beat in flour until smooth
4. Roll into small balls and press a pecan half on each to flatten
5. Bake 15 minutes at 350 degrees<|endoftext|>
