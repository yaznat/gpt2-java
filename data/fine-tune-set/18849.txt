Please give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Berries N Custard Parfaits

Ingredients:
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Dry marsala wine
• Heavy cream
• Berries

Steps:
1. In a large, heatproof bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, 3 tablespoons sugar and marsala wine
2. Place the bowl over a pot of simmering water and whisk continuously until hot and thick, about 8 minutes
3. Remove from the heat and immediately set the bowl into a larger bowl of ice water
4. Whisk the custard for 2 minutes, then refrigerate until cold, about 30 minutes
5. In a chilled bowl, whisk 1 cup heavy cream with 1 1 / 2 tablespoon sugar until stiff
6. Using a whisk, fold half of the cooled custard into the whipped cream, then fold in the rest
7. Layer in parfait cups with fresh berries<|endoftext|>
