What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Achingya Thoren Kerala Style Green Bean Curry

Ingredients:
• Curry leaves
• Coconut
• Ground cumin
• Cayenne
• Turmeric
• Garlic cloves
• Vegetable oil
• Brown mustard seeds
• Raw rice
• Dried hot red chiles
• Green beans
• Salt

Steps:
1. Finely chop 5 of the curry leaves, and put in a medium bowl
2. Add coconut, cumin, cayenne, turmeric, and garlic, and mix well
3. Add enough water to make coconut mixture moist enough to just hold together when pinched between thumb and forefinger, then set aside
4. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over medium heat
5. Add mustard seeds and rice, and fry until mustard seeds pop, about 2 minutes
6. Add chiles, fry for a few seconds, and carefully add remaining 10 curry leaves
7. Be careful - leaves will spatter when they hit the hot oil
8. When leaves have crackled but are still green, about 30 seconds, add green beans, salt, and 2 tbsp water, and stir to mix well
9. Cook, stirring often, until beans begin to soften yet are still slightly crunchy, about 5 minutes
10. Stir in reserved coconut mixture, and cook, stirring often, until beans are tender, about 5 minutes more
11. Add salt to taste
12. Do not eat curry or bay leaves - remove as you find them<|endoftext|>
