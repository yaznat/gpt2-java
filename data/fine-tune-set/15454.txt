What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Bacon Lovers Burger

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Egg
• Breadcrumbs
• Bacon
• Sea salt
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. For bacon: preheat the oven to 375 degrees
2. Line a cookie sheet with aluminum foil and parchment paper
3. Place all the bacon on the cookie sheet and bake for 10 minutes
4. Check the bacon
5. You might have to bake it for about 5 minutes more
6. Take bacon out of the oven and let cool on a paper towel covered plate
7. When cool enough to handle, chop 6 slices of the bacon into small bits
8. There should be 2 to 3 extra slices put aside for topping the burger
9. For the burgers: in a medium bowl, mix together the ground beef, egg, breadcrumbs, chopped bacon, and sea salt
10. Divide into 3 patties
11. Sear the patties on the stove top
12. 3 minutes for the first side on medium-high heat
13. Then turn the heat down to medium and sear the second side for about two and half minutes
14. Then, put the seared burgers on a cookie sheet lined with aluminum foil and parchment paper and pop into the oven for about 15 to 20 minutes
15. After 15 to 20 minutes take the purger out of the oven
16. Place on a plate covered with paper towels and let drain for a minute or two
17. Put on toasted bread, buns, or whatever you like to eat a burger on and top with you favorite condiments
18. Don't forget the 2 to 3 slices of bacon tha twas put aside<|endoftext|>
