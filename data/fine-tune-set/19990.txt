What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Kittencal S Greek Feta And Olive Omelette

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Eggs
• Whipping cream
• Baking powder
• Dried oregano
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Salt
• Feta cheese
• Pitted black olives

Steps:
1. In a bowl whisk eggs with whipping cream, baking powder, oregano and black pepper until well combined
2. Allow to sit for 5 minutes
3. In a medium skillet melt butter over medium heat until sizzling
4. Whisk / stir the egg mixture one more time then pour into hot skillet
5. Cook without stirring for 3 minutes or until almost set, gently lifting the edge of the eggs with a spatula to allow any uncooked egg to flow underneath
6. On one side of the ometette sprinkle the feta cheese and olives
7. Lift the uncovered half over the cheese and olives and cook for another 2-3 minutes
8. Slide onto a plate<|endoftext|>
