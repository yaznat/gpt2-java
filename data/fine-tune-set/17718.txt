What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Apple Pumpkin Muffins

Ingredients:
• Pumpkin
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Oil
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Apples
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Mix together pumpkin, sugar, eggs and oil
2. Add flour, soda, salt and pumpkin pie spice
3. Mix until blended
4. Add apples and chocolate chips
5. Spoon into muffin tins
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
