Please give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>Applesauce Drop Cookies

Ingredients:
• Raisins
• Applesauce
• Brown sugar
• Shortening
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Nutmeg
• Clove
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Mix raisins and applesauce, set aside
2. Combine sugar, shortning and egg, beat until fluffy
3. Stir in applesauce and raisins
4. Sift flour with salt, soda and spices, add and mix well
5. Stir in nuts
6. Drop soft dough by spoonfuls about 2 inches apart on greased baking sheet
7. Bake at 375 degrees for 13-15 minutes<|endoftext|>
