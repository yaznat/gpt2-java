What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>Antoine S Soupe A L Oignon Gratine

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onions
• Flour
• Beef stock
• Salt
• White pepper
• Toast
• Swiss cheese
• Romano cheese
• American cheese
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Melt butter in large soup pot and cook onions until they are golden brown
2. Stir in flour and cook for about 2 minutes
3. Add beef stock and bring to a boil
4. Turn heat down to low and simmer soup gently for 30 minutes
5. Add salt and pepper to taste
6. Mix all cheeses together
7. Ladle soup into 6 bowls that can go under a broiler, and float one piece of toast in each
8. Sprinkle toasts with the cheese mixture
9. Place bowls under broiler until cheese melts and begins to brown
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
