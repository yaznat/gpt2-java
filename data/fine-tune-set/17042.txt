Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Italian seasoning
• Kosher salt
• Extra large egg
• Butter
• Minced garlic clove
• Flour
• Chicken broth

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Mix first six ingredients and form into cocktail size meatballs
3. Place on jelly roll style pan with lip
4. Bake meatballs in oven for 15-20 minutes until done
5. Melt butter in saucepan
6. Add garlic and saut for 1-2 minutes
7. Stir in flour
8. Stir in chicken broth and cook until thickened
9. Combine meatballs and sauce
10. Keep warm in crockpot if serving for party<|endoftext|>
