What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Copycat Outback Aussie Cheese Fries

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Vegetable oil
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Onion powder
• Ground thyme
• Ranch dressing
• Chips
• Monterey jack cheese
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Bacon

Steps:
1. For the aussie chips: preheat oil to 375 degrees in a deep fryer
2. Cut potatoes into french fry slices using a vegetable slicer or mandolin
3. Its important to use a gadget for the slicing so that the fries will all be the same thickness, and will cook evenly
4. Submerge all of the sliced potatoes in a large bowl fill with cold water for 30 minutes
5. Combine all of the seasoning ingredients in a small bowl and set aside
6. Remove potato slices from the water and onto a clean towel
7. Blot dry
8. Fry potato slices for 8 to 12 minutes in hot oil until light brown
9. Drain fries on a rack or paper towels and sprinkle with some of the seasoning
10. Pile the fries on a plate and serve with ranch dressing on the side, if desired
11. For the aussie cheese fries: prepare the clone for the aussie chips and arrange the seasoned fries on an oven-safe plate
12. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
13. Mix the cheeses together and sprinkle the blend over the fries
14. Crumble the bacon and sprinkle it over the cheese
15. Bake the plate for 4 to 5 minutes in the hot oven, until cheese is melted
16. Serve with ranch dressing on the side
17. This recipe yields 4 to 6 servings of either selection<|endoftext|>
