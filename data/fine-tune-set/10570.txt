Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Oil
• Slivered almonds
• Sesame seeds
• Olive oil
• Sugar
• Apple cider vinegar
• Salt
• White pepper
• Red cabbage
• Scallions
• Water chestnuts

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions, drain and set aside
2. Heat oil in a small skillet over medium-low heat
3. Add almonds and sesame seeds
4. Saut until slightly brown, set aside
5. Combine oil, sugar, vinegar, salt and white pepper in a jar
6. Cover tightly and shake
7. Set aside
8. Combine cabbage, scallions, water chestnuts and pasta in a large bowl
9. Toss in nut mixture
10. Toss with dressing before serving<|endoftext|>
