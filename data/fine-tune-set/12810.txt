What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Salmon Steaks

Ingredients:
• Salmon steaks
• Lemon juice
• Garlic powder
• Pepper
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Soy sauce
• Liquid smoke
• Tabasco sauce

Steps:
1. Baste steaks every 3 minutes or so with a mixture of the last 5 ingredients, as they are grilling
2. When finished cooking salmon will flake easily with fork
3. Sprinkle with lemon juice, pepper and garlic powder
4. Place on serving platter and spoon more barbecue sauce over salmon<|endoftext|>
