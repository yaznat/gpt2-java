Ingredients:
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Butter
• Sugar
• Cream cheese
• Lime, zest of
• Fresh lime juice
• Egg
• Potato starch

Steps:
1. Combine crumbs with butter and 1 tbsp sugar
2. Stir until sandy in texture
3. Divide between two pint-sized mason jars or large coffee mugs
4. Pack crumbs down into bottoms
5. Stir remaining ingredients together until smooth
6. Divide between vessels
7. Place in microwave for 2 minutes on high
8. Continue to cook for 30-second intervals until the tops appear dry
9. Transfer to refrigerator for at least 1 hour before serving
10. The top will develop a dimple as it cools
11. Top with a dollop of whipped cream to cover it up<|endoftext|>
