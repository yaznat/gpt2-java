What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Baba S Three Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Red kidney beans
• Cut green beans
• Cut wax beans
• Sweet onion
• Pimento stuffed olive
• Sugar
• Vinegar
• Salad oil

Steps:
1. Drain kidney beans
2. Rinse with cold water and drain again
3. Combine all beans in a bowl with onion and olives
4. Add sugar, vinegar and oil
5. Mix lightly
6. Chill at least 24 hours
7. Use slotted spoon to serve<|endoftext|>
