What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Aunt Emma S Bread Pudding

Ingredients:
• White bread
• Milk
• Butter
• White sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Raisins
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Break the bread into small chunks in a large bowl
2. Add the white sugar and melted butter and stir
3. Add the eggs one at a time alternately with the milk stirring well so that each egg is incorporated before adding the next one until all the eggs and milk are used
4. Stir in the vanilla and raisins
5. Pour into a greased lasagna pan or a 13x9" inch pan
6. Sprinkle the brown sugar evenly across the top of bread mixture
7. Bake in a preheated 350 oven for 1 hour
8. Let cool 20 to 30 minutes as pudding will set firmer as it cools
9. Can be served warm or chilled<|endoftext|>
