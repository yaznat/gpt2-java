What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Stir Fry With Spicy Peanut Sauce

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Peanut butter
• Water
• Garlic powder
• Vinegar
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Broccoli spear
• Carrot
• Snow peas
• Cayenne pepper
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Thoroughly whisk together ingredients for marinade and marinate sliced chicken for 30 minutes at room temperature or 1-2 hours refrigerated
2. While chicken is marinating, thoroughly whisk together ingredients for sauce in a small bowl
3. Set aside
4. Heat 1 tbs
5. Oil in wok at medium-high heat
6. Drain chicken and cook for 10 minutes or until no longer pink
7. Set cooked chicken aside, covered
8. Reduce temperature to medium heat and heat 1 tbs
9. Oil in wok
10. Cook vegetables for 10 minutes or until tender but crisp
11. Return cooked chicken to pan and toss with vegetables
12. Make a well in the center of the wok and pour sauce into it, stirring constantly
13. The sauce will thicken slightly
14. If the sauce is too thick to your taste, add water a little at a time until desired consistency is reached
15. Toss sauce, chicken and vegetables in wok until sauce is thoroughly incorporated
16. Serve over rice or rice noodles<|endoftext|>
