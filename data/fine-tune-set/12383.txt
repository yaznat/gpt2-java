Please give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>Almost Vegetarian Vegetable Soup

Ingredients:
• Yellow onion
• Butter
• Chicken broth
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Frozen corn
• Diced tomatoes
• Le seuer peas
• Parsley flakes
• Ground cayenne pepper
• Salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. In a heavy stock pot, melt butter and saute onions until they are clear
2. Add broth and bring to a boil
3. Add potatoes and carrots
4. Let cook at a boil until veggies are tender
5. Add corn, and, again, bring to a boil
6. Add all remaining ingredients
7. Allow to cook together for another 10 - 15 minutes
8. Serve with corn bread or other good bread, or fix rice to add to your soup bowls<|endoftext|>
