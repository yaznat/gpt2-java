What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Potato And Cheese Omelette

Ingredients:
• Potato
• Cheese
• Eggs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in a frying pan
2. When hot, add potato and cook until golden-brown and crusty on all sides
3. Beat up eggs in a small bowl with very little salt and freshly ground pepper
4. Lower heat of stove, add eggs to pan and give them a good stir, a couple of times, bringing up cooked eggs from bottom, but redistributing uncooked eggs and potatoes
5. Sprinkle generously with shredded cheese
6. Let cook until golden-brown on bottom
7. Run spatula down center, and carefully fold one half over the other creating a half-moon
8. Turn off heat and let omelette sit in pan for 30 seconds to a minute and then carefully turn over and allow 30 more seconds
9. Allow to cool about 4-5 minutes before tucking in<|endoftext|>
