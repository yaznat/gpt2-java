What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Hamburger Fries Dinner

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Frozen french fries
• Corn
• Condensed cream of mushroom soup
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 375f
2. Cook meat, onion
3. Add corn and soup
4. Put fries in greased 9-in
5. Square baking dish
6. Cover with cheese, then meat mixture
7. Bake at 375f for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
