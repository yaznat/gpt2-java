Ingredients:
• Hamburger buns
• Olive oil
• Garlic salt
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Ketchup
• Prepared mustard
• Pepper
• Lettuce
• Chopped tomatoes
• Process american cheese
• Cooked bacon
• Dill pickle slices

Steps:
1. To make croutons, place bun cubes on a baking sheet
2. Drizzle with oil and sprinkle with garlic salt and toss to coat
3. Broil about four inches from the heat for 4-6 minutes or until golden brown, turning once
4. In a skillet, cook beef and onion over medium heat until meat is no longer pink and drain
5. Add ketchup, mustard and pepper and heat through
6. Divide lettuce among four dinner plates
7. Top each with tomatoes, meat mixture, cheese and bacon
8. Garnish with croutons and pickles<|endoftext|>
