Ingredients:
• Lime juice
• Olive oil
• Chili powder
• Coriander powder
• Salt
• Red snapper fillets
• Green cabbage
• Scallions
• Red onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Corn tortillas
• Avocado
• Lime
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Spray a 7x11-inch baking dish with nonstick spray
2. Stir together the lime juice, oil, chili powder, ground coriander, and salt in a cup
3. Place the fish in the prepared baking dish in one layer and brush with the lime juice mixture
4. Cover with plastic wrap and vent one corner
5. Microwave on high until the fish is just opaque in the center, about 3 minutes
6. Break the fish into flakes
7. Stir together all the remaining ingredients except the tortillas in a medium bowl
8. Top each tortilla with about 2 tablespoons of the cabbage mixture, 2 tablespoons of the fish, and another 1 tablespoon of the cabbage mixture
9. Fold the tortillas in half
10. Serve a plate of dressed avocado wedges alongside
11. Halve, pit, and peel an avocado
12. Cut into wedges and arrange on a plate
13. Squeeze the juice from 1 / 2 lime over the avocado and sprinkle with salt and pepper<|endoftext|>
