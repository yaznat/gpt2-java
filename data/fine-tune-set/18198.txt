Ingredients:
• Spelt flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Bananas
• Water
• Egg
• Maple syrup
• Oil

Steps:
1. Whisk together dry ingredients in a medium bowl
2. In a smaller bowl whisk together the liquids
3. Preheat griddle
4. When a drop of water 'dances' on it, pour liquids over flour mixture and stir to moisten
5. Oil griddle
6. Spoon batter onto griddle in 4 to 6-inch cakes
7. Turn when top side is thoroughly bubbled
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
