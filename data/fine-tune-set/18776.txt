What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Berry Pudding Cake

Ingredients:
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Nonfat milk
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Berries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degree f
2. Lightly coat six 6-ounce individual quiche dishes with nonstick cooking spray
3. Arrange in a 15x10x1-inch baking pan
4. Set aside
5. In a medium bowl, combine eggs, granulated sugar, vanilla, and salt
6. Whisk until light and frothy
7. Whisk in milk until combined
8. Add flour and baking powder
9. Whisk until smooth
10. Divide berries among prepared quiche dishes
11. Pour batter over berries
12. Bake about 20 minutes or until puffed and golden brown
13. Serve warm
14. If desired, sift powdered sugar over each serving<|endoftext|>
