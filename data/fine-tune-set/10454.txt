Ingredients:

Steps:

2. Mix all the ingredients together in a bowl
3. I prefer to use a mixing spoon over an electric mixer since the dough will be very stiff
4. On an ungreased cookie sheet, shape dough into round balls and then slightly press the dough down with the bottom of a cup
5. Bake for 8-9 minutes
6. Allow to cool and remove from cookie sheet
7. Makes around 24 cookies
