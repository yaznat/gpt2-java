Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Sweet onion
• Soy sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Yellow bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Butter
• White button mushrooms
• Garlic salt
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Chop up bell pepper into bite sized pieces
2. Chop or slice onion
3. In wok or skillet over medium low heat add olive oil
4. Saute onion for 15 minutes or until translucent
5. Stir frequently
6. Add 1 tablespoon soy sauce and worcestershire sauce
7. Add bell pepper and saute for an additional 15 minutes or until bell pepper is tender
8. Remove onion & peppers from the heat and set aside
9. Add butter to wok or skillet and then mushrooms
10. Saute until tender about 15 minutes
11. Stir frequently
12. Add garlic salt & pepper
13. Add peppers & onion mixture back into the wok and mix with mushrooms
14. Add 1 tablespoon soy sauce stir until fluid coats peppers, mushrooms and onions<|endoftext|>
