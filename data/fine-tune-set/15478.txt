Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Burgers

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Barbecue sauce
• Barbecue potato chips
• Onion powder
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Cheese slices

Steps:
1. Mix all the ingredients together in a bowl except the cheese
2. Form the meat mixture into 6 patties
3. Cook the patties on the grill until no longer pink in the middle
4. Top with cheese slices, if using
5. Remove from the grill once the cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
