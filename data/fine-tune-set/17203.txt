What's a recipe for tea?<|endoftext|>Anne S Teacakes Tea Cakes

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Crisco
• Buttermilk
• Vanilla
• Egg
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Flour

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, beat sugar and crisco until smooth and creamy
2. Add egg, buttermilk, and vanilla, and beat until mixed
3. In a separate bowl, sift together baking powder, baking soda, and flour
4. Add a little of the flour mixture at a time to the sugar mixture stirring by hand until it is thick, smooth, and easy to roll out
5. Sprinkle wax paper with flour, and place 1 / 2 of the dough onto the wax paper
6. Flatten out and sprinkle with a touch more flour to keep from sticking
7. Roll dough thin
8. Cut with 2 inch cookie cutter
9. Repeat with remaining dough until all dough has been used
10. Bake on cookie sheet for 10-12 minutes at 375 degrees
11. Can be eaten plain or made into sandwich cookies with chocolate icing or sweet milk and sugar icing<|endoftext|>
