Please give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>New Vanishing Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Quick oats
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. In a large bowl, beat butter and sugars on medium speed of electric mixer until creamy
3. Add eggs and vanilla
4. Beat well
5. Add combined flour, baking soda, cinnamon and salt
6. Mix well
7. Add oats and raisins
8. Mix well
9. Drop dough by rounded tablespoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheets
10. Bake 8 to 10 minutes or until light golden brown
11. Cool 1 minute on cookie sheets
12. Remove to wire rack
13. Cool completely
14. Store tightly covered
15. For bar cookies bake 30 to 35 minutes in an ungreased 9 x 13 - inch metal baking pan<|endoftext|>
