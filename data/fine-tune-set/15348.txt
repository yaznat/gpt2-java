What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Andy S Seven Season Hamburgers Especial

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Celery salt
• Chili powder
• Dried oregano
• Barbecue sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Yellow mustard
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Combine ground beef, barbeque and worcestershire sauces, mustard and seasonings, mix well
2. Form into 4 patties
3. Grill on barbeque or cook over medium heat<|endoftext|>
