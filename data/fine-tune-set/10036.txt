Steps:
1. Heat the olive oil in a large, heavy skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add the onion, garlic, salt, chili powder, and oregano and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 3 minutes
3. Stir in the tomato paste and the chipotle chile and sauce
4. Cook 1 minute more
5. Add the turkey, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, and cook until the meat loses its raw color, about 3 minutes
6. Add the beer and simmer until reduced by about half, about 8 minutes
7. Add the tomatoescrushing them through your fingers into the skilletalong with their juices and the beans
