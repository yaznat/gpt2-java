Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Arroz Verde Mexican Green Rice

Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Poblano peppers
• Jalapeno
• Scallions
• Fresh cilantro
• Fresh parsley
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic cloves
• Parboiled rice
• Lime, juice of

Steps:
1. Place the peppers, scallions, parsley, cilantro, garlic, and salt in a food processor and process until smooth
2. Add a little water if needed
3. Heat the oil in a large saucepan
4. Add the rice and stir to coat all the grains
5. Cook, stirring for about 2 minutes more
6. Add the ingredients from the blender to the rice and stir two more minutes, to incorporate well
7. Add the chicken broth or water
8. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low
9. Cover and cook until the water is absorbed, about 15 minutes
10. Turn the heat off and stir in the lime juice, making sure it's evenly dispersed<|endoftext|>
