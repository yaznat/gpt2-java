• Cake flour

• Baking powder
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Powdered sugar
• Milk

• Food coloring

Steps:
1. For the donuts, heat oven to 375f grease and flour pan
2. Sift together flour, sugar, baking powder, nutmeg, and salt in a large mixing bowl
3. Stir in buttermilk, eggs, butter and vanilla
4. Beat until just combined
5. Fill each well of a donut pan 2 / 3 full and bake 10-12 minutes, until the top of the donuts spring back when lightly touched
6. Bake 10-12 minutes, until the top of the donuts spring back when lightly touched
7. Cool 5 minutes in pan
8. Invert onto cooling rack and cool completely
9. While the donuts cools, prepare the glaze
10. Mix powdered sugar, milk and coconut oil in a bowl until it reaches a smooth consistency
11. Pour approximately cup of the mixture into a small bowl
12. Add purple and blue food coloring drops into the bowl
13. Gently mix, making marble-looking pattern using a small stick or skewer
14. Dip top of the donut into the glaze, gently lift and remove
15. Sprinkled glazed donuts with edible glitter
