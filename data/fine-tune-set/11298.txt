Ingredients:
• Light chunk tuna in water
• Mayonnaise
• Celery
• Flour tortillas
• Carrots

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, stir together tuna, mayonnaise and celery, breaking up any large chunks of tuna
2. Place half of the tuna salad on each tortilla in a line, slightly below the center of the tortilla
3. Make a line of carrot strips along the middle of the tuna salad
4. Roll into a cylinder, sushi-fashion
5. Trim ends and cut each roll across into 6 pieces
6. Place into resealable lunch containers, cut side up<|endoftext|>
