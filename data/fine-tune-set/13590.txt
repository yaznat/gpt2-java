Ingredients:
• Venison steak
• Fresh breadcrumb
• Parmesan cheese
• Garlic salt
• Italian seasoning
• Egg
• Salt and pepper
• Butter
• Spaghetti sauce
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Combine breadcrumbs, parmesan, garlic salt and italian seasoning in a small bowl
2. Beat egg with salt and pepper in a second bowl
3. Dip meat in egg, then coat well with crumbs
4. Place steaks on a plate and refrigerate 20 minutes to set crumbs
5. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
6. Melt butter in a 9x13 pan till hot in oven
7. Add steaks to pan
8. Bake 15-20 minutes
9. Flip steaks, spoon spaghetti sauce over the meat and bake another 15-20 minutes until meat is tender
10. Top with mozzarella, baking 5 more minutes or until cheese melts<|endoftext|>
