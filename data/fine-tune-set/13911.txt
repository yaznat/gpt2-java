Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Balsamic vinegar
• Fresh rosemary leaf
• Lamb cutlets

Steps:
1. Combine oil, vinegar and rosemary in a bowl, add lamb, tossing to coat in marinade, let stand for 10 minutes
2. Drain lamb, discard marinade
3. Cook lamb on heated oiled grill plate, uncovered, until cooked as desired<|endoftext|>
