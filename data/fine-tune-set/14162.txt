Give me a tofu recipe.<|endoftext|>Black Bean Marinated Tofu Cakes With Steamed Bok Choy

Ingredients:
• Honey
• Sweet chili sauce
• Black bean sauce
• Sesame oil
• Fresh garlic
• Fresh ginger
• Extra firm tofu
• Baby bok choy
• Sesame seeds
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. For the sauce, whisk the honey, chili sauce, black bean sauce, oil, garlic and ginger in a 9 inch baking dish until combined
2. Add the tofu and marinate for at least 10 minutes, or longer for more flavor
3. Remove the tofu and scape marinade off
4. Reserve the marinade!
5. Spray a non stick skillet with cooking oil and brown the tofu on both sides, approximately 3 minutes per side
6. Brush some sauce over the tofu, turn again and brush once more, cooking for 1 more minute
7. Set aside
8. Warm the remaining sauce in a small skillet over low heat
9. Steam the bok choy just until bright green, about 2 minutes
10. Do not overcook!
11. Place the bok choy on serving plates
12. Top the bok choy with the tofu and drizzle with the sauce
13. Garnish with sesame seeds and chopped cilantro<|endoftext|>
