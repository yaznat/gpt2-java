Ingredients:
• Coffee
• Water
• Granulated sugar
• Half-and-half

Steps:
1. Brew fresh ground coffee with 9 cups of water measured in coffee maker pot
2. Pour in 1 standard coffee cup add 3 teaspoons of sugar stir till mixed
3. Add 1-2 tablespoons half and half
4. Serve and enjoy<|endoftext|>
