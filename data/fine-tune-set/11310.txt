Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>500 Margarita Bread

Ingredients:
• Pitted dates
• Tequila
• Orange-flavored liqueur
• Triple sec
• Warm water
• Lemon zest
• Lime zest
• Brown sugar
• Shortening
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Pecans
• Cream cheese
• Butter
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine dates, tequila, orange liqueur, water, lemon and lime zest
2. Set aside
3. In large mixer bowl, beat sugar, shortening and egg until fluffy
4. In another bowl, combine flour, soda and salt
5. Add to creamed mixture alternately with date mixture, using low to medium speed
6. Stir in pecans
7. Turn into greased 9" x 5" by 2-1 / 2" loaf pan
8. Bake at 350 degrees for 50-60 minutes, or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
9. Or, bake 3 or 4 mini loaf pans 30-35 minutes
10. Turn out on wire rack to cool
11. Slice thin and serve with cream cheese spread
12. Cream cheese spread:
13. In small mixer bowl, beat cream cheese, butter and sugar until smooth
14. Stir in liqueur and lime zest
15. Makes 1-1 / 4 cups
16. Bread freezes well<|endoftext|>
