What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>5 Minute Wacky Vegan Microwave Chocolate Cake For One

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• White vinegar
• Vanilla
• Water

Steps:
1. Stir together all the dry ingredients in a large coffee cup
2. Add wet ingredients and mix well with a fork
3. Microwave on 50% power for 3 minutes
4. I always have my cup on the rotating plate
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
