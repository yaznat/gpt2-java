Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Ice Cream Torte

Ingredients:
• Betty crocker fudge brownie mix
• Vanilla ice cream
• Candy sprinkles
• Red maraschino cherries

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f line 2 round cake pans with foil
2. Grease bottoms only of foil with shortening or cooking spray
3. Make brownie mix as directed on box, using water, oil and eggs
4. Divide batter evenly between pans
5. Bake 22 to 26 minutes or until toothpick inserted 2 inches from side of pan comes out almost clean
6. Cool completely in pans, about 1 hour
7. Do not remove from pans
8. Spread slightly softened ice cream evenly on brownies in pans
9. Freeze at least 2 hours until ice cream is firm
10. Remove desserts from pans
11. Remove foil
12. Place on serving plates
13. Cut each dessert into 8 wedges
14. Decorate with candy sprinkles and cherries
15. Store covered in freezer<|endoftext|>
