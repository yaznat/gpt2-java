Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic
• Chili flakes
• Bacon
• Brown sugar
• Barbecue sauce
• Red kidney beans
• Butter beans
• Pork and beans
• Molasses
• Dijon mustard
• Chili powder
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350f
2. Grease a 3-quart baking dish
3. In a dutch oven brown the beef with onion, garlic and chili flakes, stirring with a wooden spoon to crumble the beef
4. Cook beef until no longer pink
5. Drain fat
6. Stir in bacon and all remaining ingredients
7. Heat until simmering, adjusting seasoning to taste
8. Transfer into prepared baking dish
9. At this point you can chill for a minimum of 8 hours and then bake later
10. Bake for about 1 hour, stirring occasionally<|endoftext|>
