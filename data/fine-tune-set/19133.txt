What's a recipe for ice cream?<|endoftext|>Boysenberry Truffle Ice Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Shortbread cookies
• Butter
• Boysenberry jam
• Cream cheese
• Chocolate chips
• Vanilla ice cream
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Combine crumbs and butter
2. Press into 9 inch pie pan
3. Blend together 1 / 4 cup jam, cream cheese and melted chocolate
4. Spread into bottom and up the sides of the crust
5. Stir together ice cream and 1 / 4 cup jam
6. Spoon into pie to fill
7. Place in freezer for 4 hours or until ice cream is firm
8. Top with remaining jam and toasted almonds<|endoftext|>
