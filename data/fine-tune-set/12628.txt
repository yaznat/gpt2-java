What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew In A Pot

Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Cooking oil
• Water
• Parsley
• Salt
• Pepper
• Dried ancho chiles
• Dried pasilla peppers
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Sesame seeds
• Garlic cloves
• Beef bouillon granules
• Ground cumin
• Zucchini
• Potatoes
• Fresh corn

Steps:
1. In a dutch oven brown meat, half at a time, in hot oil
2. Return all meat to dutch oven
3. Add the 3 cups of water, parsley, salt and pepper
4. Cover
5. Simmer 1 1 / 2 hours
6. Cut ancho and pasilla chilies open
7. Discard stems and seeds
8. Cut chilies into small pieced with scissors or a knife
9. Place chilies in bowl
10. Cover with boiling water and let stand 45 to 60 minutes, drain
11. In a blender container place undrained tomatoes, the drained chilies or crushed red pepper, onion, and the 1 / 2 cup water, sesame seed, garlic, bouillon and cumin
12. Blend till nearly smooth
13. Add to beef mixture along with zucchini, potatoes, and corn
14. Cover and simmer 25 to 30 minutes or till meat is tender and vegetables are done
15. Season to taste with salt and pepper
16. Makes 8 to 10 servings<|endoftext|>
