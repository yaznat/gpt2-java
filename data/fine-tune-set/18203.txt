Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Banana Bran Pancakes Diabetic

Ingredients:
• Skim milk
• Egg beaters egg substitute
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• Banana
• Multi-bran chex
• All-purpose flour
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Ground nutmeg

Steps:
1. In blender, process milk, eggs, oil, vanilla, and banana just to mix
2. Add bran cereal, process, add remaining ingredients
3. Process to combine, scrape sides of container
4. Let stand 5 minutes
5. Stir
6. Bake on preheated, lightly greased griddle over medium heat
7. Pour batter to form pancakes, cook<|endoftext|>
