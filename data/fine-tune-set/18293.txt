Ingredients:
• Bisquick baking mix
• Sugar
• Milk
• Fruit yogurt
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Beat all ingredients with wire whisk or hand beater until well blended
2. The batter will be thick
3. Pour batter onto hot griddle, by spoonfuls, grease if necessary, spread lightly
4. Cook until edges are dry
5. Turn, cook until golden brown<|endoftext|>
