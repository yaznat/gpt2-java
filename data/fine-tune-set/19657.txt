What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Au Gratin Hash Browns Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Hash brown potatoes
• Onion
• Half-and-half
• Margarine
• American cheese
• Cottage cheese

Steps:
1. Spread potatoes evenly into greased 9" x 13" baking pan
2. Layer diced onion over potatoes
3. In a saucepan heat half-and-half, margarine and cheese
4. Cook until melted
5. Stir in cottage cheese
6. Pour sauce over potatoes
7. Let stand at least 1 hour or overnight
8. Bake at 350 degrees for 1 to 1 1 / 2 hours<|endoftext|>
