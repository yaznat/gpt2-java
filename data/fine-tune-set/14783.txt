Ingredients:
• Water
• White rice
• Ground beef
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Grease a 9x13-inch cake pan or large covered baking dish
3. Set aside
4. Bring water to a boil
5. Stir in the rice and bring it to a boil
6. Lower heat
7. Boil rice for 15 minutes or until it is done, and set aside
8. In a large skillet, brown the meat you are using
9. If you are using ground turkey, you may need to add a little bit of water to get it to cook easily and to make it less dry no need to drain if using ground turkey
10. If there is any grease drain it
11. Add the soup, sour cream and mayonnaise to the meat
12. Stir together and heat until it is bubbly
13. Stir in the rice and mix thoroughly
14. If mixture is too thick add a little bit of water or milk to desired consistency
15. Pour mixture into the greased pan or baking dish
16. Cover with cheddar cheese
17. If using a cake pan, cover with foil
18. If using a covered baking dish cover it with the lid
19. Bake in oven for 45-60 minutes or until hot and bubbly and cheese is melted
20. Remove from oven and let stand for about 5 minutes before serving
21. Serve with salad and / or vegetables<|endoftext|>
