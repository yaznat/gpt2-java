Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Bisquick baking mix
• Paprika
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Broiler-fryer chickens

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 425 degrees
2. Place margarine in a 13x9-inch baking pan
3. Melt margarine in the oven
4. Remove pan from oven
5. Mix bisquick mix, paprika, salt and pepper
6. Coat chicken pieces
7. Place chicken in baking pan
8. Bake uncovered for 30 minutes
9. Remove pan from oven
10. Lower oven temperature to 400 degrees
11. Turn chicken over, and bake in oven for additional 30 minutes
12. Serves 4 to 6 people
