Ingredients:
• Sausages
• Streaky bacon
• Granny smith apples
• Onions
• Sunflower oil
• Tomatoes
• Green pepper
• Cake flour
• Beef stock
• Worcestershire sauce
• Bay leaf
• Dried thyme
• Dried sage

Steps:
1. Roll each sausage in 1 or two rashers of bacon and secure with a toothpick
2. Saut the apples and onions in half the oil
3. Spoon them into an ovenproof dish with a lid
4. Arrange the sausages on top
5. Braise the tomatoes and green pepper in the remaining oil, sprinkle the flour over, and cook for about three minutes
6. Add the stock and flavourings and cook for a further three minutes
7. Spoon the tomato sauce over the sausage, cover with the lid, and bake for 45 minutes in a preheated oven at 180c<|endoftext|>
