Give me a wrap recipe.<|endoftext|>Asian Lettuce Wraps Vegetarian

Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Miso
• Sesame seeds
• Lettuce leaves
• Basil leaves
• Olive oil
• Firm tofu
• Avocado
• Red bell pepper
• White radishes
• Bean sprouts
• Sesame oil
• Salt
• Paprika
• Cucumbers

Steps:
1. To make miso sauce:
2. Place brown sugar in heavy saucepan, and melt over medium-low heat, being careful not to burn
3. Remove from heat, and stir miso and sesame seeds into melted sugar
4. To make wrapping:
5. Trim edges of lettuce leaves to make them uniformly circular
6. Stack sesame or basil leaves alongside
7. To make salad:
8. Heat oil in skillet or wok over medium-high heat, and stir-fry tofu about 2 to 3 minutes
9. Remove from heat, and set aside
10. Slice avocado into 1-inch-long pieces
11. Slice red pepper into 1 -inch-long pieces
12. Set aside
13. Combine white radish with 1 teaspoon sesame oil, 1 teaspoon sesame seeds, 2 teaspoons brown sugar, teaspoon salt and 1 teaspoon red pepper powder
14. Set aside
15. Combine bean sprouts with 1 teaspoon sesame oil, 1 teaspoon sesame seeds and 1 teaspoon salt
16. To assemble rolls, place 1 lettuce leaf on flat surface, and layer remaining ingredients in following order: basil leaf, bean sprout mixture, radish mixture, tofu, cucumber, avocado and red pepper
17. Spoon about teaspoon miso sauce over top
18. Wrap by tucking ends in, and roll up
19. Repeat with remaining ingredients<|endoftext|>
