Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>A Quick And Easy White Cake

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Vegetable shortening
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Egg whites

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f degrees
2. Grease and flour 9x13 pan
3. Combine dry ingredients in mixing bowl
4. Beat in shortening
5. Add milk and vanilla
6. Beat for 2 minutes
7. Beat in egg whites, beat until very smooth
8. Bake for 35 minutes, or until done<|endoftext|>
