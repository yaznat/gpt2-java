Ingredients:
• Hot dogs
• Bacon
• Vegetable oil
• Hot dog buns
• Hass avocado
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Pat the hot dogs and bacon dry with paper towels
2. Wrap each hot dog with a slice of bacon in a spiral
3. Secure the bacon at each end with a toothpick
4. In a large skillet, heat the vegetable oil until shimmering
5. Add the hot dogs and cook over moderate heat, turning, until the bacon is crisp, about 5 minutes
6. Transfer the hot dogs to paper towels to drain
7. Discard the toothpicks
8. Tuck the hot dogs in the buns
9. Top with the sliced avocado, sour cream and salsa and serve<|endoftext|>
