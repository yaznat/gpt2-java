What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Aj De Lentejas Bolivian Spicy Lentil Stew

Ingredients:
• Dried hot pepper
• Onion
• Tomatoes
• Garlic cloves
• Italian parsley
• Dried lentils
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Water
• Sweet onion
• Flat leaf parsley
• Lime, juice of

Steps:
1. Sarsa:
2. Mix ingredients, and set aside, the longer it sits, the more the lime juice infuses everything with flavor
3. To cook the lentils:
4. Put in a saucepan, cover with water
5. Add salt, 1 / 2 garlic clove and oil
6. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium and cook for 20 minutes or until the lentils are tender
7. Drain and remove the garlic
8. Set aside
9. While the lentils are cooking, heat the olive oil in a large saucepan
10. Fry the garlic and the parsley on medium heat until the garlic is golden
11. Add the chopped onion until the onions become shiny
12. Add the ground hot pepper and a splash of water
13. Let it cook for 1 minute
14. Keep stirring
15. Add the chopped tomatoes, 1 / 4 cup of water, salt and pepper
16. Cover and cook for 25 minutes at low-medium heat
17. Add lentils and mix with sauce
18. Simmer for 5-10 minutes
19. Serve with white or brown rice and top with 1-2 spoons of sarsa<|endoftext|>
