Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin
• Cabbage
• Onion
• Peanut oil
• Black bean sauce
• Garlic and red chile paste

Steps:
1. Slice the pork loin in very thin strips
2. Slice the cabbage 1 / 2 inch thick then cut crosswise a couple of times
3. Thinly slice the onion
4. Heat oil in a large skillet
5. Add the pork to the hot skillet and stir-fry 3 to 5 minutes
6. Add the cabbage and onion and stir fry until tender-crisp
7. Stir in the black bean sauce and chili garlic paste<|endoftext|>
