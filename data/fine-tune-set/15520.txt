What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Trash Bag Taco Salad

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Lettuce
• Cheddar cheese
• Chopped tomatoes
• Onions
• Olives
• Beans
• Corn chips
• Catalina dressing
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Brown beef
2. Drain
3. Add taco seasoning and prepare according to package directions
4. Cool
5. Toss with remaining ingredients in a large plastic bag or container<|endoftext|>
