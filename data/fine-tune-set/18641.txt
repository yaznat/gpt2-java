What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Apple Sauce Banana And Raisin Bread Pudding

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Granny smith apple
• Butter
• Raisins
• Bananas
• Half and half milk
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Honey
• White sugar
• Unbleached cane sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Step 1: heat oven to 350 f
2. Step 2: melt 2 tablespoons of butter in a small pan, then add chopped apple
3. Stir and add honey, cane sugar, 1 / 2 teaspoon of cinnamon
4. Then add raisins
5. Cook until apple soft
6. Step 2: mix eggs, milk, vanilla, nutmeg, cinnamon and whip until smooth
7. Step 3: grease small bowl with remainder of butter, then layer apple sauce, sliced bananas, then bread
8. Layer to top of bowl
9. Then pour milk over top
10. Bake in oven in shallow pan of water for 45 minutes
11. Serve warm or cold with vanilla ice-cream or whipped cream
12. Enjoy your dessert<|endoftext|>
