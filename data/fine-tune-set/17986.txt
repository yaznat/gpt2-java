Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Nuts
• Margarine
• Brown sugar
• Egg whites
• Vanilla extract
• Vanilla baking chips
• Unsalted margarine
• Maple syrup
• Cream cheese
• Maple extract
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Sift together sifted flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt
2. Stir in nuts
3. Set aside
4. With an electric mixer, beat together melted butter and 1 cup brown sugar
5. Beat in egg and vanilla
6. Slowly beat dry mixture into wet mixture
7. Stir in vanilla baking chips by hand
8. Spread batter into a greased 9" x 9" x 2" baking pan
9. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 20-25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
10. Set aside to cool
11. For topping: in a saucepan over low heat, melt remaining 1 / 4 cup butter and maple syrup
12. Stir remaining 1 / 3 cup brown sugar into saucepan until it has dissolved
13. Remove saucepan from heat and beat in cream cheese and maple extract until smooth
14. Return saucepan to low heat and simmer, stirring constantly, until desired consistency
15. To serve: place 1 brownie on a plate
16. Top with 1 scoop of vanilla ice cream
17. Drizzle topping over
18. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
