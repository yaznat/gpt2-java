Ingredients:
• Ice
• Coconut milk
• Banana
• Psyllium husks

Steps:
1. Using a magic bullet or blender, pour in crushed ice, then the coconut milk [you can substitute lite coconut milk], then a peeled, sliced banana
2. Blend to desired consistency
3. Add psyllium husks [or flaxseeds or sunflower seeds] if desired for extra nutrition ~<|endoftext|>
