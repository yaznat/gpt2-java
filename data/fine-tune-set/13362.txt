What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>Ain T None Better Chicken Breast

Ingredients:
• Oregano
• Basil
• Parsley
• Garlic powder
• Parmesan cheese
• Butter
• Lemon juice
• Chicken breasts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 f
2. Combine first 4 ingredients in a shallow plate or pie pan
3. Combine butter and lemon in a shallow plate or pie pan
4. Dredge chicken in butter and completely coat in herb / cheese mixture
5. Place in 9x13 pan and bake 20-25 minutes<|endoftext|>
