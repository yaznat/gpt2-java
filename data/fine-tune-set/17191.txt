Ingredients:
• Black tea bags
• Yellow cling peaches
• Sugar
• Water

Steps:
1. In a 3qt stock pot, combine the tea bags, water and sugar
2. Brew on med-low heat
3. Open the canned peaches, and drain the syrup into the pot
4. Drop in 3-5 peach slices
5. Reserve the rest of the peaches on the side
6. Brew the tea to just under a boil
7. Remove from heat
8. Strain off the tea bags, but leave the peach slices
9. Return tea to pot, cover and let sit until tea is room temperature
10. Place tea into a a pitcher for serving
11. Refrigerate until chilled<|endoftext|>
