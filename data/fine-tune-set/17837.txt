Ingredients:
• Egg
• Vegetable oil
• Bananas
• Milk
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Jelly

Steps:
1. Grease 12 medium muffin cups
2. Beat egg, oil, banana and milk together lightly with a fork
3. Stir in sugar
4. Sift dry ingredients together into first mixture and stir only until ingredients are moistened and all dry ingredients are mixed in
5. Spoon into muffin cups, filling 2 / 3 full
6. Make a depression in top of each muffin with
7. Bake at 375 degrees for 15-20 minutes or until toothpick inserted near middle comes out clean<|endoftext|>
