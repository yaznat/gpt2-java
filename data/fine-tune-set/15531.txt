Ingredients:
• Adzuki beans
• Water
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Bouillon
• Bay leaf
• Taco seasoning
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Soak beans overnight in 4 cups water
2. Drain and rinse
3. In a large pot, sautee onions and garlic in oil
4. Add beans and 2-4 cups of water
5. Bring to a simmer
6. Add bouillon and bay leaf
7. Cook 1 hour, adding more water if necessary
8. Add taco seasoning and crushed red pepper
9. Cook an additional 30 minutes or until water is reduced
10. Remove bay leaf
11. Mash or blend about half the beans until the texture is similar to taco meat
12. Taste and stir in more seasonings if desired<|endoftext|>
