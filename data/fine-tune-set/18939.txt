What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Custard Cornbread

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Yellow cornmeal
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Milk
• Eggs
• Buttermilk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Put butter in a 9-inch square baking pan, and heat in the oven until butter melts
3. Sift cornmeal with flour, sugar, baking soda and salt
4. Beat in 1 cup milk, eggs and buttermilk
5. Pour batter over melted butter in baking pan
6. Pour 1 cup of milk carefully over the top of the batter- do not stir
7. Bake for 35 minutes, or until bread is golden brown<|endoftext|>
