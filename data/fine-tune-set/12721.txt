Can you give me a steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Applebee S Bourbon Street Steak

Ingredients:
• Beef tenderloin steaks
• Bourbon
• Brown sugar
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Salt

Steps:
1. Combine bourbon, brown sugar, salt, and pepper over steaks
2. Preheat grill
3. Place steaks on grill and cook for 13 minutes for rare, 17 for medium or 20 for well done, turning once during cooking<|endoftext|>
