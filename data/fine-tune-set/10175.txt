Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Green bell pepper
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Mexican-style stewed tomatoes
• Chili beans
• Butternut squash
• Low sodium beef broth
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Frozen corn kernels

Steps:
1. Cook beef, bell pepper, and next 2 ingredients in a dutch oven over medium-high heat until meat crumbles and is no longer pink
2. Drain well, and return to dutch oven
3. Stir in tomatoes and next 5 ingredients
4. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat
5. Cover, reduce heat to medium-low, and simmer, stirring occasionally, 15 minutes
6. Stir in corn, and cook, uncovered, 15 minutes or until squash is tender and chili is thickened<|endoftext|>
