What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Maple Muffins Low Fat

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Baking powder
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Maple syrup
• Egg whites
• Sultana raisins

Steps:
1. Combine dry ingredients and stir
2. Combine wet ingredients and stir
3. Stir wet into dry and blend just until combined and moist
4. Fold in nuts spray muffin tins, and fill each cup 3 / 4 full
5. Bake 350 degrees for 17 minutes, or until tester comes away clean
6. Remove to wire rack, let sit 6 minutes then, remove from tins, onto wire rack<|endoftext|>
