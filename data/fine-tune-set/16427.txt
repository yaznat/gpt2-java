Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 375 degrees
2. In medium frying pan fry bacon to soft transparent stage
3. Drain on paper towels
4. Line each slice around the side of each muffin cup
5. Ends may overlap
6. Break egg directly in the center of each muffin cup
7. 12 cup muffin pan
8. Slightly salt and pepper
9. Cook 15 to 20 minutes
10. Watch for whites and yolks to begin to set
11. Take out with small spatula on a serving plate
12. Serve with fork and knife
13. If you want the tops to be basted, after ten minutes of cooking, baste each egg once with 1 / 2 teaspoon of water
14. You can fry bacon the night before and drain and refrigerate for morning<|endoftext|>
