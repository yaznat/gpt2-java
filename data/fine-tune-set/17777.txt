What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Apricot Pecan Muffins

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Flour
• Milk
• Pecans
• Apricots

Steps:
1. Cream together butter and sugar
2. Add vanilla
3. Beat in eggs, one at a time
4. Combine dry ingredients and add to creamed mixture alternately with milk
5. Stir in nuts & apricots
6. Spoon into paper lined or greased muffin pans
7. Bake at 375 for 25 minutes
8. I have used this recipe for mini-muffins, regular muffins and jumbo muffins<|endoftext|>
