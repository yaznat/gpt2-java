Give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>5 Minute Individual Potato Gratins By Melissa D Arabian

Ingredients:
• Pam cooking spray
• Russet potatoes
• Swiss cheese
• Green onions
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees fahrenheit
2. Spray 8 muffin tins with vegetable spray
3. Layer potato slices, cheese, and onions into each muffin cup
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Top each gratin with 1 or 2 tablespoons of heavy cream
6. Cover with foil
7. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes, removing the foil halfway through cooking time
8. Invert gratins onto plate and serve<|endoftext|>
