Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Brownie Muffin Cups Gift Mix In A Jar

Ingredients:
• Walnut halves
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Cocoa powder
• Flour
• Eggs
• Butter
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Creating the gift in a jar:
2. Wash and thoroughly dry a 1-quart wide-mouth canning jar
3. Layer the ingredients in the jar as listed above starting with the walnut halves, brown sugar, sugar, cocoa powder and then flour - make sure to press firmly with a flat bottomed object after each addition
4. Make the layers as level as possible
5. Secure the lid and decorate as desired
6. Attach the instructions for making these cookies found below
7. Brownie cups gift tag:
8. Additional ingredients to be added by the recipient: 3 eggs, 1 cup melted butter, 1 tsp vanilla extract
9. Brownie cups instructions:
10. Preheat oven to 350
11. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, then add the butter and extract and whisk together
12. Add the contents of the jar, and stir until just mixed
13. Do not overstir
14. Spoon the batter into greased or papered muffin tins, filling each cup two-thirds full
15. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the tops begin to crack and appear dry
16. Cool for 10 minutes in the tin, then transfer to a wire rack and cool completely
17. Serve immediately or store in an airtight container for up to 1 week<|endoftext|>
