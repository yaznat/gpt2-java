Ingredients:
• Apples
• Lemon juice
• Sugar
• Flour
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Butter
• Pecans
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Filling: in large bowl toss together apples and juice
2. Stir sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg & salt - mix over apples - then add melted butter
3. Sprinkle pecan over bottom of pie shell, add apple mixture
4. Topping: in bowl combine brown sugar, flour & cinnamon - mix with melted butter & add pecans, sprinkle over apples
5. Bake pie at 425 for 15 minute
6. Reduce to 375 and bake for 25 - 30 minutes
7. Cool<|endoftext|>
