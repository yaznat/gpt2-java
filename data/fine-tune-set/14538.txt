Give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>Azore Style Feijos Beans

Ingredients:
• Dried pink beans
• Water
• Bacon
• Yellow onion
• Garlic cloves
• Cinnamon stick
• Cumin powder
• Coriander seed
• Whole cloves
• Whole allspice
• Bay leaves
• Tomatoes
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Portuguese linguica, spanish chorizo or louisiana andouille, vegetarian "soyrizo", etc
2. Are suggestions
3. My preference being bacon as i don't care for sausage or ham hock
4. For additional flavor, i usually dry roast the spices in a castiron skillet
5. Soak the beans overnight
6. Cook the bacon or ham hock and drain on paper towel
7. Next saute the onion and garlic
8. Once cooled, break up the bacon into little pieces
9. In large dutch oven or in crock pot, combine all the ingredients, including the water, excluding the salt
10. If using a dutch oven, simmer the beans on medium-low for about 1 1 / 2 to 2 hours or until beans are soft
11. If using a crock pot simmer on low 8 hours or until beans are soft
12. Salt the beans once cooked
13. Adjust seasoning before serving
14. Now season with salt
15. Remove whole cloves, cinnamon stick and whole allspice
16. Great with freshly baked, crusty bread
17. Tastes even better if prepared one day ahead then warmed<|endoftext|>
