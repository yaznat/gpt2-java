Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>A B C Vegetable Casserole

Ingredients:
• Fresh asparagus
• Fresh broccoli
• Cauliflower head
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• French-fried onions

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Combine asparagus, broccoli, and cauliflower in a pot of water and cook at medium heat for 10 minutes
3. Combine mushroom soup and cheese in a separate pot and heat until cheese melts
4. Drain the cooked vegetables and put in a casserole dish
5. Pour soup and cheese mixture over the vegetables
6. Cover with onion rings and bake at 350f for 30 minutes or until bubbly<|endoftext|>
