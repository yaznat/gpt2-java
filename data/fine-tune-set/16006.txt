Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Hass avocadoes
• Fresh lemon juice
• Salt and pepper
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Black beans

Steps:
1. Place the tortillas in the oven in two stacks
2. Heat to 200
3. In a bowl, mash the avocados with the lemon juice
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Working in batches, in a nonstick skillet, cook the bacon over medium heat until crisp
6. Drain on paper towels
7. Leave about 2 tablespoons bacon fat in the skillet, reserving the rest of the fat
8. Cut the bacon strips in half
9. Working in 2 batches, in the skillet, cook the eggs over easy
10. Transfer to a platter
11. Reserve the skillet
12. Lay the tortillas on a work surface
13. Sprinkle each with 1 / 4 cup cheese
14. Top with beans, 1 egg and bacon strips, then fold to enclose
15. In the same skillet, heat 1 tablespoon reserved bacon fat over medium heat, add 4 quesadillas and cook, turning once, until crisp and golden, about 3 minutes
16. Repeat with more bacon fat and the remaining quesadillas
17. Serve with the avocado mash<|endoftext|>
