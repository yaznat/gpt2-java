Ingredients:
• Fresh broccoli florets
• Mushrooms
• Water
• Beef broth
• Black bean garlic sauce
• Cornstarch
• Spaghetti
• Vegetable oil
• Extra lean ground beef
• Garlic
• Onion
• Dried chili pepper flakes
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Cook spaghetti in a 5-6-quart nonstick pot or pan in boiling water until barely tender drain and set aside
2. In a bowl mix the beef broth with black bean sauce and cornstarch
3. Set aside
4. Heat oil in the same pot that was used to boil the spaghetti
5. Add in ground beef with minced garlic, dried chili flakes and onion slices
6. Cook until beef is browned, season with black pepper to taste
7. Add in the uncooked broccoli florets with drained mushrooms and 1 / 2 cup water to the beef mixture
8. Cover and cook stirring occasionally until the broccoli is a bright green colour
9. Add in beef broth mixture and bring to a boil
10. Cook for 2-4 minutes
11. Add in the cooked spaghetti, stir over medium heat until heated through and well coated with the sauce
12. Add in soy sauce to taste if desired<|endoftext|>
