Ingredients:
• Ground sirloin
• Chili beans
• Stewed tomatoes with peppers and celery
• Del monte crushed tomatoes with mild green chilies
• Tomato sauce
• Mexicorn
• Tomato soup
• Carrots
• Minced garlic cloves
• Mccormick mild chili seasoning
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Oregano
• Celery ribs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Onion
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Worcestershire sauce
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Fritos corn chips

Steps:
1. Put extra virgin olive oil in large skillet
2. Add ground beef
3. Cook for about 5 minutes
4. Add onion, celery, garlic & carrots
5. Cook until beef is no longer pink and vegetables are tender
6. Pour beef mixture into crock pot
7. Add all other ingredients & stir to incorporate
8. Cook on low for 6-8 hours stirring occasionally
9. We top ours with sour cream, cheddar cheese, chopped onion and frito's<|endoftext|>
