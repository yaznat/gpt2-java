Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Alphabet Soup

Ingredients:
• No-salt-added stewed tomatoes
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Water
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Salt
• Dried basil
• Pepper
• Alphabet pasta

Steps:
1. Place tomatoes in a large saucepan & mash with a potato masher
2. Add the next 6 ingredients & stir well
3. Place over medium-high heat and bring to a boil
4. Add pasta
5. Cover, reduce heat, and simmer 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
