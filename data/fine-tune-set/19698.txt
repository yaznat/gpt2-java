Ingredients:
• Plantains
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Ground coriander
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Cut the plantains into the shape of fries
3. Spread fries on a baking sheet
4. Brush fries with olive oil
5. Combine salt, garlic powder, ground coriander, and cayenne pepper
6. Sprinkle mixture on fries
7. Bake at 400 degrees for 20 minutes
8. Turning them over about after 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
