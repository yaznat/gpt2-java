Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Campbells Beef And Broccoli Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Sirloin steak
• Vegetable oil
• Broccoli
• Garlic cloves
• Tomato soup
• Soy sauce
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. Slice beef into very thin strips
2. In a skillet or wok, heat oil
3. Add beef and stir fry until beef is cooked
4. Add broccoli and garlic and cook until broccoli is tender-crisp
5. Add soup, soy sauce and vinegar
6. Heat and stir until boiling
7. Makes 4 servings<|endoftext|>
