Ingredients:
• Rice
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Peas and carrots
• White onion
• Garlic cloves
• Eggs
• Sesame oil
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Heat sesame oil in a large skillet on medium heat
2. Add onion, garlic, peas, and carrots
3. Stir fry until tender
4. Crack eggs into pan and scramble, mixing throughout vegetables
5. Add rice, and chicken to pan
6. Stir in soy sauce and remove from heat<|endoftext|>
