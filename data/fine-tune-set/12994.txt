Ingredients:
• Butter
• Fish fillets
• White pepper
• Star fruit
• Lime juice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f
2. Select a baking dish to hold fillets in a single layer
3. Spread a little less than half the butter in the dish
4. Set fillets on butter
5. Add salt and pepper
6. Cut off star fruit
7. Slice fruit thin
8. Arrange on fish
9. Dot with remaining butter
10. Taste star fruit
11. If rather sweet, sprinkle with a healthy dose of lime juice
12. If tart, a smaller amount
13. Bake for about 8 minutes, until fish just becomes opaque in the center
14. It will continue to bake in the dish, so do not overcook
15. Serve at once<|endoftext|>
