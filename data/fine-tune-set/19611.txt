Please give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>Appled Sweet Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Apples
• Butter
• Salt
• Pepper
• Sugar
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Slice the sweet potatoes and dice the apples
2. Grease a 9x13 in baking pan
3. Arrange a layer of sweet potatoes, salt and pepper, 2 tbsp butter
4. Add layer of apples, 2 tbsp sugar, nutmeg
5. Repeat the layers
6. Bake in 350 oven for about 40 to 45 min, or until tender and to taste<|endoftext|>
