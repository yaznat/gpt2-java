What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Basque Style Fisherman S Stew

Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Onions
• Russet potatoes
• Green bell peppers
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Fish stock
• Dry white wine
• Fresh tomatoes
• Tuna
• Fresh ground pepper
• Italian parsley

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a large heavy saucepan over medium heat
2. Add the onions
3. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until translucent but not colored, about 10 minutes
4. Add the potatoes, bell peppers and garlic
5. Cook, stirring for 10 minutes
6. Season lightly with salt
7. Add the fish stock and wine
8. Simmer until the potatoes are tender and the stew has thickened slightly, about 10 minutes
9. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes
10. Add the tuna, pushing it down into the stew with a spoon
11. Cover, remove from heat and let stand until tuna is cooked to desired doneness, about 5 minutes for medium-rare
12. Season with salt and pepper
13. Ladle into bowls, sprinkle with parsley and serve<|endoftext|>
