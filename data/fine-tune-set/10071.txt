Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>American Cantina Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Vegetable oil
• Corona beer
• Diced tomatoes with juice
• Canned jalapeno slices
• Tomato sauce
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Salt
• Sugar
• Black pepper
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Kidney beans
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Brown beef in oil with onions and garlic
2. Drain
3. Stir in beer, tomatoes, jalapeno slices, tomato sauce, bouillon cubes, salt, sugar, pepper, and cumin
4. Bring mixture to a boil, then reduce heat, cover and simmer for 35-45 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Mix in chili powder and beans, and cook over medium heat, until beans are heated
6. Serve hot with tortilla chips or crackers and hot sauce on the side, if desired<|endoftext|>
