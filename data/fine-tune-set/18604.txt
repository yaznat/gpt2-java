Ingredients:
• Dry bread
• Sugar
• Cream
• Eggs
• Cinnamon
• Vanilla
• Butter
• Salt

Steps:
1. Soak torn bread in cream
2. Add sugar and butter, beaten eggs, cinnamon, salt and vanilla, stirring to blend
3. Put into a buttered baking dish
4. May add a little chopped citron, chopped raisins or dates if you wish
5. Bake approximately 40 minutes in a 375 oven<|endoftext|>
