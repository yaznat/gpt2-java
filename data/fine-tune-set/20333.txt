Ingredients:
• Boil-in-the-bag rice
• Pork tenderloin
• Canola oil
• Curry powder
• Snow peas
• Light coconut milk
• Fish sauce
• Red curry paste
• Mango
• Green onion
• Shredded coconut
• Lime wedges

Steps:
1. Prepare rice according to package directions, omitting salt and fat
2. Drain
3. Cut pork into 1-inch cubes
4. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
5. Sprinkle pork evenly with curry powder
6. Add pork and snow peas to pan
7. Stir-fry 3 minutes
8. Combine coconut milk, fish sauce, and curry paste, stirring well
9. Add milk mixture to pan
10. Bring to a simmer
11. Stir in mango and 1 / 4 cup onions
12. Cook 1 minute or until thoroughly heated
13. Remove from heat
14. Place 1 cup rice on each of 4 plates
15. Top each serving with 1 1 / 4 cups pork mixture
16. Sprinkle each serving with 1 tablespoon of remaining 1 / 4 cup onions and 1 1 / 2 teaspoons shredded coconut
17. Serve with lime wedges, if desired<|endoftext|>
