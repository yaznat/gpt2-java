Ingredients:
• Onions
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic clove
• Ground beef
• Eggs
• Fine dry breadcrumb
• Prepared mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Fresh mushrooms
• All-purpose flour
• Dry red wine
• Low sodium beef broth

Steps:
1. Saute half of chopped onion in 1 tabsp hot oil in medium skillet over medium heat until tender
2. Add garlic, and saute 30 seconds
3. Remove from heat and let cool
4. Combine cooked onion mixture, beef, and next 4 ingredients, 1 / 4 teasp salt, and 1 / 2 teasp pepper
5. Shape into 6 patties
6. Cook patties in remaining 2 tabsp
7. Hot oil over medium-high heat 3 minutes on each side or until browned
8. Remove patties and set aside
9. Add remaining onion to drippings in skillet, and saute over medium-high heat until tender
10. Add mushrooms and saute 3 minutes
11. Whisk in flour, and cook, stirring constantly, 1 minute, whisk in wine, broth, and remaining 3 / 4 teasp salt and 1 / 2 teasp of pepper
12. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low and simmer stirring occasionally, 5 minutes
13. Place patties in lightly greased baking dish, top evenly with gravy
14. Bake, covered, at 350 for 25 minutes or until done
