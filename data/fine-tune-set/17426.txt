Give me a recipe for coffee.<|endoftext|>Arabic Coffee The Saudi Way

Ingredients:
• Water
• Coffee
• Ground cardamom
• Saffron

Steps:
1. Put 3 cups of water in a pot and bring to a boil
2. Add the coffee and boil over low flame
3. Turn the heat off after 5 minutes and leave the coffee to clear
4. Pour the coffee into a kettle, leaving the coffee to settle in the pot
5. Add in the cardamom and saffron to the kettle of coffee and boil once before serving in small cups<|endoftext|>
