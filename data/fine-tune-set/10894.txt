Ingredients:
• Jicama
• Red onion
• Cucumber
• Tomatoes
• Ahi
• Salt & pepper
• Lime juice
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Cilantro
• Serrano chilies

Steps:
1. Have all ingredients chilled
2. Use an immersion blender to make dressing
3. Set aside two tablespoons, then toss veggies with remaining 1 / 4 cup
4. Arrange veggies on two plates, then top with the ahi
5. Drizzle with dressing, and, if desired, arrange avocado on top, sprinkle with chilis and cilantro<|endoftext|>
