What's a recipe for tea?<|endoftext|>Asian Honey Tea Grilled Shrimp

Ingredients:
• Medium shrimp
• Salt
• Scallions
• Brewed tea
• Honey
• Rice vinegar
• Soy sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. In a plastic bag, combine tea, honey, vinegar, soy sauce, ginger and pepper, to make marinade
2. Remove 1 / 2 cup marinade
3. Set aside for dipping sauce
4. Add shrimp to marinade remaining in plastic bag, turning to coat
5. Close bag securely and marinate in refrigerator 30 minutes
6. Remove shrimp from marinade
7. Discard marinade
8. Thread shrimp onto 8 skewers, dividing evenly
9. Grill over medium hot coals 4 to 6 minutes or until shrimp turn pink and are just firm to the touch, turning once
10. Season with salt, to taste
11. Meanwhile, prepare dipping sauce by placing reserved 1 / 2 cup marinade in small saucepan
12. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat
13. Boil 3 to 5 minutes or until slightly reduced
14. Stir in green onions<|endoftext|>
