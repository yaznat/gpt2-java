Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• Soft margarine
• Milk
• All-purpose flour
• Vanilla extract
• Coconut
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients except cinnamon in a blender in order given
2. Process until thoroughly blended
3. It will be slightly foamy
4. Pour into a buttered casserole dish
5. Sprinkle top with cinnamon
6. Bake at 375 for 45 minutes to 1 hour
7. This makes it's own crust
8. The top will be brown and puff up as it cooks but will settle down as it cools<|endoftext|>
