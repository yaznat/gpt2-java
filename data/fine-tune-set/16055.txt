Please give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Broccoli Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Burrito-size flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese
• Cooked broccoli

Steps:
1. Preheat griddle or heavy skillet over medium heat for 3 minutes
2. Add a flour tortilla or two and sprinkle each with about 1 / 2 cup cheese
3. Divide broccoli between tortillas, sprinkling it over the cheese, then divide remaining cheese over broccoli
4. Top each tortilla with a second tortilla and cook for several minutes, until bottom of lower tortilla is light gold
5. Flip quesadilla over and cook on second side until bottom is golden
6. Cheese should be completely melted
7. Remove from pan and slice into wedges
8. Serve hot or warm
9. Cut into smaller wedges for appetizers<|endoftext|>
