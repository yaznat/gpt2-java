Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Lean ground beef
• Paprika
• Ground cumin
• Dried oregano
• Chili powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Water
• Tomato paste
• Romaine lettuce
• Plum tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese
• Scallions
• Sour cream
• Salsa
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. In a large non-stick skillet heat olive oil over medium heat
2. Add onions and garlic
3. Cook until onions are translucent, about 4-5 minutes
4. Add the ground beef and turn heat to high
5. Brown meat, stirring constantly, until crumbly, about 5 minutes
6. Drain off any excess fat
7. Lower heat and add paprkia, cumin, oregano, chili powder, cayenne pepper, salt and pepper to taste
8. Add the 1 cup of water and tomato paste
9. Blend well
10. Bring mixture to a boil then reduce to low
11. Simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally
12. Assemble taco salad:
13. Place equal amounts of romaine lettuce on 4 plates
14. Spoon a healthy amount of taco mixture over lettuce
15. Top each plate equally in the following order chopped tomatoes, shredded cheddar cheese, 2 tbsp sour cream, green onions and a dollop of salsa
16. Serve & enjoy!<|endoftext|>
