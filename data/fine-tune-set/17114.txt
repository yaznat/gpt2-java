Ingredients:
• Fresh strawberries
• Fresh pineapple
• Oranges
• White rum
• Sugar
• Lime zest
• Red food coloring
• Lime slice

Steps:
1. Mix the strawberries, pineapple, oranges, rum, the 2 tablespoons sugar and lime peel in a large bowl
2. Let stand, tossing occasionally until sugar dissolves
3. About ten minutes
4. Spread about 2 tablespoons of sugar on a plate
5. Add one or two drops of red food coloring and blend until tinted
6. Or, just spread the pink tinted sugar on a plate
7. Dip rim of martini glass in water, then the sugar, to coat the rim
8. Spoon in fruit and juice
9. Slit a lime slice to garnish the side of the glass<|endoftext|>
