Ingredients:
• Peach schnapps
• Orange juice
• Grenadine
• Creme de banane
• Mineral water

Steps:
1. Pour creme de bananes, peach schnapps and orange juice into a cocktail shaker with 6 oz of crushed ice
2. Shake vigorously until slushy
3. Pour all contents into a highball glass
4. Add the grenadine so that it sinks down the side of the glass, and serve<|endoftext|>
