Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>1 Hour Smoky Ham And Lentil Soup

Ingredients:
• Dry lentils
• Smoked ham
• Water
• Ham soup base
• Celery
• Onion
• Carrot
• Cider vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Oregano flakes
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Tabasco sauce
• Liquid smoke

Steps:
1. In a stock pot, combine lentils, ham, water and base / bouillon and bring to a boil
2. Reduce heat
3. Simmer until lentils are tender, about 30 minutes
4. Add remaining ingredients
5. Simmer until veggies are tender, about 20 minutes
6. If you wish to puree: remove 3 cups of lentils, broth and veggies
7. Puree, return to soup and stir
8. For the crockpot: place all ingredients in a slow cooker and cook on low for about 10 hours
9. Follow step #4 prior to serving<|endoftext|>
