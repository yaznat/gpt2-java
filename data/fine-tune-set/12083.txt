Ingredients:
• Plain yogurt
• Pumpkin puree
• Dark brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Nutmeg
• Egg yolks
• Salt
• Pie shell

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. In a large mixing bowl combine all ingredients and whisk until smooth
3. Pour this batter into the pie shell and place on a sheet pan
4. Bake for 50 to 55 minutes
5. Remove and cool for one hour
6. Cover and refrigerate 2 hours before cutting<|endoftext|>
