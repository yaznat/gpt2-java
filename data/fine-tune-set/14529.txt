What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>Avocado Black Beans And Corn Dip Great For The Big Game

Ingredients:
• Avocados
• Black beans
• Corn
• Rotel tomatoes
• Red bell peppers
• Jalapeno peppers
• Sweet onion
• Red wine vinegar
• Balsamic vinegar
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Dijon mustard
• Sugar
• Oregano
• Olive oil
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Mix dressing ingredients well
2. In a large bowl combine the main ingredients
3. Add dressing and gently mix well
4. Best when refrigerated for at least 1 hour but usually we can't wait
5. Serve with tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
