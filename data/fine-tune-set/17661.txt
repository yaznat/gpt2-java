What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Anzac Muffins

Ingredients:
• Baking soda
• Milk
• Butter
• Golden syrup
• Brown sugar
• Desiccated coconut
• Baking powder
• Rolled oats
• Egg
• Flour

Steps:
1. Dissolved baking soda in milk, then add butter, golden syrup and brown sugar and mix well
2. Add remaining ingredients and stir until just combined
3. Three-quarters fill well-greased muffin pans and bake 190 c for 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
