Ingredients:
• Blue curacao
• Coconut rum
• Pina colada nonalcoholic drink mix
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Pour the malibu coconut rum into a pina colada glass over ice
2. Add the pina colada mix and blue curacao
3. Serve unstirred to see color difference, stir when ready to drink
4. It's very pretty
5. Serve with whipped cream on top if you wish<|endoftext|>
