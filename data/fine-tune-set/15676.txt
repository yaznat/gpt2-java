Give me a recipe for a taco.<|endoftext|>Ceviche Tacos

Ingredients:
• Limes
• Tomatoes
• Avocado
• Cilantro
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Medium shrimp
• Corn tortilla

Steps:
1. Finely grate rind from limes to measure 1 tablespoon
2. Juice limes to measure 1 / 4 cup
3. Place rind and juice in a large bowl
4. Add tomato, avocado, cilantro, salt, pepper, garlic and shrimp
5. If you want, you can add some chopped onion and fresh jalapenos
6. Toss well to combine
7. Cover and chill for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
8. Heat tortillas
9. Spoon about 1 / 2 cup of shrimp mixture down the center of tortilla
10. Fold and eat
11. You can serve it plain and / or eat with crackers or you can use hard tacos shells, tostadas, tortilla chips or whatever you like
12. My dad eats it with saltine crackers
13. He adds extra onion and jalapenos, personal preference!<|endoftext|>
