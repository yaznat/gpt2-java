What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Chicken Pizza

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Onion
• Barbecue sauce
• Prepared pizza crust
• Tomatoes
• Garlic
• Dry basil
• Olive oil
• Four-cheese mexican blend cheese
• Tomato paste
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl add tomatoes, tomato paste, salt and basil
2. In a saucepan add olive oil, garlic, and 1 / 3 of onion
3. Heat until cooked
4. Then add to the tomato and basil
5. This will be the pizza sauce
6. Dice raw chicken into medium squares
7. Using same saucepan, cook chicken until done
8. Remove from stove and stir in bbq sauce
9. With a spoon spread pizza sauce on crust evenly
10. Spread out chicken and remainder of the onion on the crust, and then cover with cheese
11. Bake in a preheated at 450 degrees for 12 to 15 minutes until cheese is completely melted<|endoftext|>
