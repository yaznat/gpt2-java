Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onions
• Parsley
• Butter
• Plain flour
• Salt
• Sour cream
• Frozen corn

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Cook bacon and crumble
3. Set aside
4. Cook onion in butter til transparent
5. Blend in flour and salt
6. Add sour cream gradually, keeping texture smooth
7. Bring mixture to a boil and add corn and parsley
8. Reduce heat and cook til heated through
9. Fold in 1 / 2 the bacon
10. Pour in greased 1 quart baking dish
11. Garnish with remaining bacon
12. Cook for 30 minutes
13. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
