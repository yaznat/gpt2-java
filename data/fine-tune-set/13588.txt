Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Milk
• Sour cream
• Dried beef
• Onion flakes
• Green peppers

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees f
2. Combine all ingredients in a small ovenproof casserole dish
3. Bake at 325 f for 30 minutes or until bubbly
4. Serve warm with crackers
5. Serving suggestions: hollow out a large round bread loaf, place dip in center, wrap in tin foil and bake, vented, until dip is heated through<|endoftext|>
