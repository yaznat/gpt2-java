What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>Kitchen Sink Soup

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Celery
• Carrot
• Spaghetti sauce
• Beef stock
• Peas
• Corn
• Red bell pepper
• Chicken leg
• Rotini pasta
• Salt
• Pepper
• Italian seasoning

Steps:
1. Saut onion, garlic and celery together until limp
2. Add carrots, and saut a few minutes
3. Add in spaghetti sauce and stock
4. Bring to a boil
5. Add seasonings
6. Add noodles, broken up, and peas and corn
7. Boil until noodles are almost done
8. Add red pepper and cook through
9. Serve hot with buns
10. This is just how i used up my left overs
