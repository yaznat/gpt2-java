Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Low fat cottage cheese
• Cream of tartar
• Splenda sugar substitute

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Separate your eggs making sure there is absolutely no yolk in the white mixture
3. In a small bowl, mix together egg yolks, cottage cheese, and sweetener until creamy
4. In a larger bowl, add egg whites and cream of tartar and beat the whites with an electric mixer on high until fluffy
5. You want this mixture to be well whipped and quite stiff - under mixing will result in a very runny mixture
6. Carefully fold egg yolk mixture into the egg whites with a large spatula, until evenly mixed
7. Try not to over-mix, you don't want to loose the 'fluffiness' of the egg whites mixture
8. Spray two cookie sheets with pam
9. With a large spoon "scoop" the mixture into 10 even rounds on the sheets
10. Place cookie sheet the middle rack and set timer for 20 minutes
11. Now you will need to watch them, because the cooking time may vary on any two batches
12. Basically, you want them to bake until they become nice and golden brown color
13. Remove from the pans and cool on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
