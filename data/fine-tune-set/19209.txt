Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Sugar-free chocolate syrup

Steps:
1. Just blend in the food processor until smooth
2. I stop occasionally to push any clumps off the side so they become part of the mix
3. Sometimes we add a tablespoon of peanut butter
4. I've also seen this recipe with other fruits added to the banana base
5. The bananas become thick and creamy so the texture is like a decadent ice cream<|endoftext|>
