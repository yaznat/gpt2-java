Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Blackened Butterflied Leg Of Lamb

Ingredients:
• Paprika
• Dry mustard
• White pepper
• Black pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt
• Fennel seed
• Thyme
• Oregano
• Butter
• Minced garlic cloves
• Leg of lamb

Steps:
1. Combine paprika, mustard, white, black and cayenne peppers, salt, fennel, thyme and oregano
2. Place on large plate or baking sheet
3. Melt butter with garlic and pour onto a separate plate or pan
4. Dip lamb into garlic butter then into seasonings, pressing firmly onto each side
5. Place lamb on greased grill on medium high setting
6. Cook for 15-20 minutes on each side, depending on thickness, or until desired doneness - 65 c for rare, 70 c for medium
7. Meat will "blacken"
8. Let rest and slice on the diagonal
9. Alternatively, in a large heavy skillet or roasting pan, brown lamb on both sides-expect a lot of smoke-and roast in a 350oven for 35-40 minutes<|endoftext|>
