What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Artichoke And Tomato Pasta Sauce

Ingredients:
• Tomatoes with juice
• Artichoke heart
• White wine
• Garlic
• Onion soup mix
• Tomato paste
• Fresh parsley
• Scallions

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in skillet and cook garlic and scallions about 2 minutes
2. Add parsley and cook one minute
3. Place garlic and parsley in saucepan, add wine, and cook until fluid reduces by about half
4. Stir in tomato paste, artichokes, diced tomatoes, and onion soup mix
5. Cook another five minutes and serve over pasta<|endoftext|>
