Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>All American Bbq Bacon Cheeseburgers

Ingredients:
• Barbecue sauce
• Lean ground beef
• American cheese
• Kaiser rolls
• Lettuce leaves
• Tomatoes
• Sweet onions
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Heat broiler
2. Mix 1 / 4 cup of bbq sauce with ground beef
3. Shape into 4 patties
4. Broil, 4-6" from heat, 7-9 minute on each side or until cooked through, brushing occasionally with remaining bbq sauce
5. If using foreman omit brushing of sauce until cooking is completed
6. Top with cheese
7. Broil 1 minute or until cheese is melted
8. Cover bottom halves of rolls with lettuce, onions, and tomatoes
9. Top with burgers, remaining sauce, bacon and tops of rolls
10. Serve with coleslaw or french fries<|endoftext|>
