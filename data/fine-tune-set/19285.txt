Give me a recipe for a popsicle.<|endoftext|>Double Orange Or Lime Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Orange jell-o
• Sugar
• Boiling water
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. Dissolve jell-o and sugar in boiling water
2. Add orange juice to orange jell-o, or limeaide to lime jello-o
3. Pour into ice cube trays, small paper cups, or frozen popsicle molds
4. Insert wooden sticks into molds or cups for handles
5. Freeze until firm<|endoftext|>
