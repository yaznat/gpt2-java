What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Bean Quesadillas With Goat Cheese

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Salsa
• Ground cumin
• Black beans
• Fresh cilantro
• Goat cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Heat 1 teaspoon olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add the onion and garlic, and saute for 3 minutes
3. Stir in 1 / 2 cup salsa, cumin, and beans, and bring to a boil
4. Reduce heat, and simmer for 5 minutes or until thick
5. Mash the bean mixture slightly with a potato masher
6. Remove from heat
7. Stir in the cilantro and goat cheese
8. Spread the bean mixture evenly over 4 tortillas
9. Top each with 1 tortilla, pressing gently
10. Heat 1 / 4 teaspoon olive oil in pan over medium-high heat
11. Add 1 quesadilla, and cook for 2 minutes on each side
12. Repeat the procedure with the remaining olive oil and quesadillas
13. Cut each quesadilla into 6 wedges
14. Serve with fat-free sour cream and 1 / 2 cup salsa<|endoftext|>
