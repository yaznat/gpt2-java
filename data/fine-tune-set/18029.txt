Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Butter
• Water
• Eggs
• Almond extract
• Maraschino cherry
• Bittersweet chocolate chips
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Combine brownie mix, butter, water, eggs and almond extract in large bowl
3. Stir just until combined
4. Stir in 1 / 2 cup cherries and chocolate chips
5. Place foil mini baking cups into mini muffin pans
6. Spoon about 1 tablespoon chocolate mixture into each cup
7. Bake for 13 to 18 minutes or until centers are just set
8. Cool completely
9. Remove from pan
10. Sprinkle with powdered sugar
11. Garnish with cherries, if desired<|endoftext|>
