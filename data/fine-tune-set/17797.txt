Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Avocado Banana And Mango Muffins

Ingredients:
• Mashed banana
• Avocado
• Mango
• Butter
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Vanilla
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Spray medium-size muffin tins with non-stick cooking spray
3. Mash fruit and add together
4. Set aside
5. Sift flour, baking soda, and baking powder in bowl
6. In a separate bowl, cream butter, sugar, and beaten eggs well, then add fruit and vanilla
7. Mix
8. Add flour mixture
9. Mix well
10. Scoop batter into muffin tin, and bake at 350 degrees for approximately 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
