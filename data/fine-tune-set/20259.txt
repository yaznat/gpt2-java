Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Sweet red pepper
• Orange
• Chicken stock
• Soy sauce
• Granulated sugar
• Cornstarch
• Sesame oil
• Red pepper flakes
• Hot pepper sauce
• Olive oil
• Gingerroot
• Garlic clove

Steps:
1. Cut chicken into thin 2 inch long strips
2. Set aside
3. Cut red pepper into strips
4. Set aside
5. Using vegetable peeler remove thin orange rind and cut into julienne strips
6. Set aside
7. Squeeze orange and reserve 1 / 4 cup juice in small bowl
8. Stir in chicken stock, soy sauce, sugar, cornstarch, sesame oil, pepper flakes and hot pepper sauce
9. Set aside
10. In wok or large skillet, heat 1 tbsp oil over high heat
11. Stir fry chicken until no longer pink inside, 3 or 4 minutes
12. Remove
13. Chicken and set aside
14. Heat remaining oil in the wok or pan
15. Stir fry gingerroot and garlic for 10 seconds
16. Add orange rind and red pepper, stir fry for 1 minute or until tender crisp
17. Stir in soy sauce mixture
18. Cook, stirring, until thickened
19. Return chicken to wok and stir until heated through<|endoftext|>
