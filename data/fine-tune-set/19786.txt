Give me a fries recipe.<|endoftext|>Deep Fried French Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Bring large pot of salted water to boil
2. Cut a potatoe into 1 / 4 inch thick slices and lengthwise into 1 / 4 inch wide sticks
3. Cook in boiling water for 3 - 4 minutes
4. Use slotted spoon and carefully remove to sheet pan with rack to drain
5. Repeat with remaining potatoes
6. Leave potatoes to drain and dry
7. This step can be done up to 4 hours ahead
8. Let stand at room temperature
9. Pour canola oil into heavy large pot or deep fryer to the depth of 4 inches
10. Working in batches fry potatoes until golden, about 6 minutes
11. Sprinkle with salt and pepper before serving<|endoftext|>
