What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Oven Burritos Ie Hamp Surprise

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Water
• Tomato sauce
• Basil
• Oregano
• Garlic powder
• Mozzarella cheese
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef in frying pan on medium heat
2. Add taco seasoning and 3 / 4 cup water
3. Add spices to tomato sauce and empty half of the contents in a 9x12 baking dish
4. Lay out tortilla wrap and fill with taco meat, about 3 / 4 of a cup per wrap
5. Place meat close to one edge, and begin rolling, bring the sides in before you reach the end
6. Add to baking dish
7. Repeat with the rest of the tortillas
8. Once your dish is full, pour the remaining tomato sauce over top, covering the wraps
9. Sprinkle your cheese on top
10. Bake in oven at 350 degrees f for 20-30 minutes
11. Can be made ahead without baking, and frozen
12. Allow more cooking time from frozen state
13. Serve with sour cream<|endoftext|>
