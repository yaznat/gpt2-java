Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ketchup
• Light brown sugar
• Sugar
• Honey
• Molasses
• Mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Liquid smoke
• Egg
• Quick oats
• Garlic salt
• Onion salt
• Ground beef

Steps:
1. Combine all sauce ingredients in a small saucepan
2. Bring to a boil, then remove from heat
3. Set aside 1 cup of sauce
4. Gently mix all burger ingredients, plus remaining sauce from the pan, until just combined
5. Form into 6 patties
6. Grill, covered, over medium heat for 6-8 minutes on each side
7. Baste with 1 / 2 cup of the reserved sauce during the last 5 minutes
8. Serve on buns with the toppings of your choce and the remaining sauce<|endoftext|>
