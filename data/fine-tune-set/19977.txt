Can you give me a omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Green Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Frozen spinach
• Pine nuts
• Gouda cheese
• Shallot
• Green pepper
• Broccoli floret
• Butter

Steps:
1. Saut the shallots and spinach for about 5 minutes
2. Set aside in a small bowl and then saut the green pepper and broccoli for 5 minutes
3. In a small break 3 eggs and mix very well with a fork
4. Melt the butter or margarine in a frying pan, on medium high heat and then add the eggs
5. Very quickly add two tbsp spinach, some pine nuts, 2 tsp shallot, two tbsp green pepper, 2 tbsp broccoli
6. Sprinkle gouda cheese over the egg and vegetables, and let set for about 3-4 minutes
7. Flip over half the omelette and continue to cook for another 2 minutes
8. Repeat two more times
9. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
