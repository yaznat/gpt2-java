Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Actual Pf Chang S Mongolian Beef Recipe

Ingredients:
• Beef tenderloin
• Oil
• Garlic
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• Rice wine
• Green onions
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. 1
2. Heat oil in a saut pan
3. Add the beef and cook for 30-60 seconds or until cooked
4. -beef should be lightly browned around the edges and gray throughout the rest of the surface
5. 2
6. Add your garlic and toss
7. 3
8. Add the rice wine, soy sauce, and sugar
9. Bring to a boil
10. 4
11. Cook and reduce the sauce around the beef until all sauce has caramelized onto the beef
12. -the saut pan should be "dry" before proceeding onto the next step
13. 5
14. Add the green onion sticks and toss to incorporate into the beef
15. A few tosses are all that are needed
16. Do not wilt the green onion sticks!
17. 6
18. Finish with sesame oil
19. 7
20. Place on a plate over cooked rice and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
