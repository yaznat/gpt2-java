Give me a cookie recipe.<|endoftext|>Amaretto Cheesecake Cookies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Granulated sugar
• Egg
• Amaretto
• Vanilla
• Almonds

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine flour and brown sugar
2. Cut in butter until mixture forms fine crumbs
3. Reserve 1 cup crumb mixture for topping
4. Press remainder over bottom of ungreased 8 inch square baking pan
5. Bake for 12-15 minutes at 350 or until lightly browned
6. In mixer bowl, thoroughly cream together cream cheese and granulated sugar
7. Add egg, amaretto, and vanilla
8. Beat well
9. Spread batter over partially baked crust
10. Combine almonds with reserved crumb mixture
11. Sprinkle over batter
12. Bake for 20-25 minutes
13. Cool and cut into squares
14. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
