Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• Olive oil
• Garlic powder
• Provolone cheese
• Pepperoni
• Italian sausage
• Spinach
• Marinara sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Spray with cooking spray a large cookie sheet or a jelly roll pan
3. Unroll the pizza crust onto the cookie sheet
4. Do not stretch it
5. Brush the crust with olive oil and then sprinkle with garlic powder
6. Lay half of the slices of cheese across one side of the crust, then layer half of the pepperoni on top of it, then half of the cooked sausage, then half of the spinach
7. Repeat the layers of cheese, pepperoni, sausage, and spinach
8. Fold the crust over and pinch all around to seal the edges
9. Bake at 400f for 15 minutes of until crust is a light golden brown
10. Serve with marinara sauce, if desired<|endoftext|>
