Ingredients:
• Ground chicken
• Taco seasoning
• Monterey jack cheese
• Sour cream
• Salsa
• Hamburger bun

Steps:
1. Combine the ground chicken and taco seasoning divide into 4 patties
2. Grill burgers, and add a slice of monterey jack cheese
3. Top with salsa and sour cream and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
