Ingredients:
• Green grapes
• Banana
• Fresh pineapple
• Apple
• Fresh spinach
• Ice

Steps:
1. Turn the machine quickly from variable speed 1 to 10 then flip the switch to high
2. Push the ingredients down into the blades until the entire mixture is blending
3. I find it easier to push the food continuously down one corner of the container and eventually it begins to blend
4. The apple may require a bit of "pounding" before it begins to mix
5. When the mixture is first blending it should be so thick and slushy that it is thick like a thick "slushy"
6. If it is too liquid at the beginning, add more ice so it is thick and "slushy"
7. This allows you to blend for 1-2 minutes without warming the drink up from the friction of the blades
8. It also allows you to break the foods down to the most bioavailable level
9. This is a "beginner" green smoothie that 98% of the population will love!<|endoftext|>
