Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Crunchy Delicious Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Eggs
• Unsalted butter
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a large bowl whisk together flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt
2. In another bowl whisk together eggs, butter, and milk
3. Warm melted butter may separate into flaky clumps when mixed with milk
4. I just stick it in the microwave for 1 minute then stir and the lumps will go away
5. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients and pour in the egg / milk mixture
6. Gently whisk together just until combined
7. Baking time and amount of batter will depend on your individual waffle maker
8. I use about a cup of batter for 2 waffles and mine take about 3 1 / 2 minutes to bake
9. I like to bake mine until they are a dark golden brown
10. Cooking spray usually isn't needed if you use 8 tbsp or more butter in the batter<|endoftext|>
