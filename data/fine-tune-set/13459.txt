Ingredients:
• Onions
• Garlic
• Butter
• Lean ground beef
• Flour
• Raisins
• Slivered almonds
• Ground ginger
• Curry powder
• Beef bouillon cube
• Water
• Peas
• Salt and pepper
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Cook the onion and garlic in butter until soft
2. Add the ground beef and brown lightly
3. Blend in the flour
4. Add the raisins through water
5. Simmer, covered, 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
6. Add the peas, salt and pepper
7. Reheat
8. Serve on hot rice<|endoftext|>
