Ingredients:
• Israeli couscous
• Asparagus
• Broth
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. While broth is coming to a boil snap the asparagus at it's weakest point to remove the tough ends
2. Then cut into bite sized pieces
3. When broth comes to a boil, add the cup of israeli couscous
4. Let it boil uncovered for 5 minutes
5. Add asparagus and continue boiling 3 minutes
6. Remove from heat
7. Add desired amount of freshly grated parmesan cheese to each bowl or the entire pot
8. To make it the consistency of pasta, use only 2 cups broth<|endoftext|>
