Give me a stir fry recipe.<|endoftext|>Asian Eggplant And Noodle Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Flat egg noodles
• Vegetable oil
• Red onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red chile
• Baby eggplants
• Brown sugar
• Light soy sauce
• Oyster sauce
• Toasted sesame oil
• Fish sauce
• Spring onions

Steps:
1. Cook noodles according to packet instructions, drain and set aside
2. Heat a little vegetable oil over a medium flame in a wok, and saute the onion, garlic and chilli, stirring frequently for 2 to 3 minutes
3. When the onion is soft, remove from wok and set aside
4. Increase heat and add 2 tablespoons oil
5. Stir fry eggplant in batches for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring constantly until golden brown
6. Remove from pan and set aside
7. Add remaining vegetable oil to wok with the brown sugar and cook 3 minutes until caramelized - be careful not to burn
8. Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil and fish sauce and stir until heated through
9. Add noodles to the wok with the onion mixture and eggplants, and toss to coat with the sauce
10. Taste and add a little extra brown sugar if desired
11. Serve garnished with spring onions and seasoned with a little salt and freshly ground pepper, to taste<|endoftext|>
