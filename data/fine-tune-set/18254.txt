Please give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Beat Your Chest Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Canola oil
• Molasses
• Unbleached flour
• Cornmeal
• Whole wheat flour
• Wheat germ
• Bran flakes
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt

Steps:
1. Mix all dry ingredients in a large bowl
2. Mix beaten eggs, oil, honey / molasses, and milk in another bowl
3. Pour wet into dry and mix well, adding more milk as needed to make pancake batter consistency
4. Preheat and oil a griddle and cook each pancake till done
5. Serve with syrup, honey or molasses<|endoftext|>
