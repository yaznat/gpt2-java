Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Coconut flour
• Almond meal
• Banana
• Baking soda
• Gelatin
• Vanilla
• Apple cider vinegar
• Sea salt

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in the blender and blend on high for 30 seconds
2. Let the batter sit for 1 minute to let the coconut flour fluff up
3. Meanwhile, heat up a large skillet on medium heat with oil or butter
4. Pour the batter to make small to medium sized pancakes
5. Once a few bubbles pop on the pancakes flip to cook on the other side for 1 minute, or until the pancake center springs back when you touch it
6. Serve with whipped cream, berries and honey<|endoftext|>
