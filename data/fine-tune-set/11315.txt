Ingredients:
• Zucchini
• Eggs
• Fat
• Sugar
• Flour
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Grate the zucchini, and measure out four cups
3. Add to large mixing bowl
4. Add eggs, fat, cinnamon, salt, baking soda, vanilla and sugar
5. Stir together well
6. Add flour to the mixture, a bit at a time
7. Stir until all ingredients are mixed
8. The mixture should resemble thick pancake batter
9. If it is too thick, add another egg
10. Add nuts, if desired
11. Lightly grease and flour your bread pans, or spray them with nonstick cooking spray
12. Carefully spoon the mixture into bread pans, stopping when the mixture is about 3 / 4 of the way to the top
13. Bake for 35 minutes, or until a golden brown
14. Take out loaves, and let them cool five minutes before removing the bread from the pans to finish cooling on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
