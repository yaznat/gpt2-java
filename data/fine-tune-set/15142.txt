Give me a pizza recipe.<|endoftext|>Bagel Pizza

Ingredients:
• Bagel
• Deli ham
• Pepperoni
• Provolone cheese

Steps:
1. Set oven to broil on high
2. Split bagel in half and place on cookie sheet
3. Top with ham first, then pepperoni, then cheese
4. Broil for about 2 or 3 minutes or until cheese is completely melted
5. Do not brown the cheese!<|endoftext|>
