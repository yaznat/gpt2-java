Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Awesome Lower Fat Brownies

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• White sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Egg
• Low-fat sour cream
• Prune baby food
• Flour
• Cocoa powder
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Mini chocolate chip

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Spray and flour the sides of an 8" x 8" square pan
3. Cream together the margarine, sugar and vanilla extract
4. Add the egg and sour cream and blend well
5. Next, beat in the prunes
6. Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, soda and salt
7. Blend in well
8. Stir in the mini chocolate chips
9. Pour into prepared pan and bake 20 to 25 minutes
10. Do a toothpick test for doneness at around 20 minutes
11. Don't overbake!
12. For skor bits brownies: substitute 1 / 2 cup cocoa for 1 / 2 cup cake and pastry flour
13. Substitute 1 / 4 cup mini chocolate chips for 1 / 3 cup skor bits
14. After removing from the oven, sprinkle with extra skor bits
15. Cut when cool<|endoftext|>
