What's a recipe for an omelette?<|endoftext|>Chimichurri Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Chimichurri sauce
• Cream
• Butter

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in a frying pan over medium heat
2. Combine the rest of the ingredients, mix well
3. Pour into heated pan, let set on one side, then flip
4. Turn the heat off and let the omelette continue cooking in the pan for another minute
5. Yum!<|endoftext|>
