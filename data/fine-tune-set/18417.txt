Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Chocolate Waffle Drops Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Nuts
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Cream butter and sugar till fluffy
2. Add eggs and vanilla
3. Beat well
4. Stir together flour, cocoa, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon
5. Stir into creamed mixture
6. Blend in nuts
7. Drop by teaspoons 2 inched apart on pre-heated waffle baker
8. Bake until done, about one minute
9. Remove to cool
10. Sift powdered sugar over cookies
11. Oven method: drop dough by teaspoons 2 inches apart on lightly greased cookie sheet
12. Bake 350 degrees till done
13. 10 to 12 minutes<|endoftext|>
