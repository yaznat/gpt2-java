What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Cinna Nilla Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Vanilla extract
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat waffle iron
2. Beat eggs in large bowl with hand beater until fluffy
3. Beat in remaining ingredients until smooth
4. Pour batter into waffle iron and cook for approximately 5 minutes
5. Serve immediately or cool to pack into freezer bags and freeze<|endoftext|>
