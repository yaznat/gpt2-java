Ingredients:
• Corn kernels
• Cheddar cheese
• Eggs
• Heavy cream
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. In a food processor or blender, combine corn, cheese, eggs, cream, sugar, baking powder, salt, pepper, and nutmeg
3. Process briefly until corn is just broken down
4. Pour into a buttered 4-cup baking dish
5. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until lightly browned<|endoftext|>
