Ingredients:
• Bread
• Pork and beans
• Bacon
• Onions
• American cheese

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 350f
2. Place toast on an ungreased baking sheet
3. Spread each slice with 3 tablespoons pork and beans
4. Top each slice with 2 strips of bacon
5. Place a few onion rings and a slice of cheese on each piece of toast
6. Bake at 350f for 15-20 minutes or until cheese is melted and lightly brown<|endoftext|>
