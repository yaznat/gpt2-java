Ingredients:
• Gingersnap cookies
• Margarine
• Dark brown sugar
• Unbleached all-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Eggs
• Low-fat buttermilk
• Whole milk
• Bananas
• Syrup

Steps:
1. Mix cookie crumbs, 1 / 2 cup parkay and brown sugar in medium bowl until well blended
2. Set aside
3. Combine flour, granulated sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt in large bowl
4. Set aside
5. Melt 4 tablespoons of the parkay
6. Place in medium bowl
7. Add eggs, buttermilk and whole milk
8. Beat with wire whisk just until blended
9. Add to dry ingredients
10. Stir just until moistened
11. Let stand 5 minutes
12. Melt the remaining 1 tablespoon margarine in large nonstick skillet
13. Ladle batter into skillet, using 1 / 4 cup batter for each pancake
14. Immediately top batter for each pancake with 4 or 5 banana slices
15. Press bananas into batter with back of spatula to allow batter to partially cover banana slices
16. Cook until bubbles form on tops of pancakes, then turn over to brown other sides
17. Serve topped with the gingersnap spread
18. Drizzle with syrup, if desired<|endoftext|>
