Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Fresh blueberries
• Powdered sugar
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Grease and lightly flour a 10-inch tube pan
2. In a bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt
3. Set aside
4. In a large mixing bowl, beat together the oil, sugar, and eggs, using an electric mixer on medium speed, for about 3 minutes or until light and thick
5. Add the dry ingredients in three parts, alternating with the milk and vanilla, beating after each addition until smooth
6. Fold in the blueberries
7. Pour batter into the prepared tube pan
8. Bake in a 325 degree oven for 60-70 minutes or until pick comes out clean
9. Let cake cool in the pan for 1 hour
10. Remove from the pan and cool completely on a wire rack
11. To make the glaze: in the top of a double boiler, over barely simmering water, combine the sugar, cream, and vanilla
12. Stir mixture for about 2 minutes or until well blended
13. Pour into a glass measuring cup and cover until ready to use
14. When the cake is completely cooled--drizzle glaze over cake
15. Let glaze set for 1 hour before slicing and serving the cake<|endoftext|>
