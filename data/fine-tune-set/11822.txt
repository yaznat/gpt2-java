Ingredients:
• Blade steaks
• Flour
• Curry powder
• Vegetable oil
• Onions
• Garlic
• Fresh ginger
• Salt
• Vinegar
• Cinnamon sticks
• Fresh lemon rind
• Beef stock

Steps:
1. Cut steak into 2
2. 5 cm cubes, dredge in the flour mixed with the curry powder
3. Lightly brown the meat in a pan with the oil and onions
4. Place in the slow cooker and add all other ingredients
5. Mix well, cover and cook on 1 for 8-9 hours, or 2 for 4 hours, or for 6-7 hours
6. Serve with fluffy steamed rice<|endoftext|>
