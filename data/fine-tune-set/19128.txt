• Water

• Lemon juice

• Ground cinnamon
• Butter
• Cheesecake flavor instant pudding and pie filling mix

1. For berry mix:
2. In a small saucepan, combine sugar and cornstarch
3. Gradually stir in water until smooth
4. Stir in blueberries and lemon juice and bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat
6. Simmer, uncovered, for 5 minutes or until slightly thickened, stirring occasionally
7. Cover and refrigerate until chilled
