Give me a beans recipe.<|endoftext|>All American Molasses Baked Beans

Ingredients:
• White beans
• Bay leaves
• Onion
• Salt pork
• Ketchup
• Dark brown sugar
• Molasses
• Dry mustard
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Place beans in a large bowl and cover with water by at least 3 inches
2. Soak overnight
3. Drain
4. In a large heavy saucepan, combine beans and bay leaves
5. Add enough water to cover by 3 inches
6. Bring to a boil and continue boiling gently for about 45 minutes or until beans are tender
7. Drain beans reserving 4 cups of the cooking liquid
8. Preheat oven to 350^f
9. In a large oven proof pot with a lid, combine the onion, salt pork, ketchup, brown sugar, molasses, dry mustard and pepper
10. Stir in the beans and 3 cups of the cooking liquid
11. Cover the pot tightly with foil and then the lid
12. Bake until the beans are very tender and the liquid has been absorbed, about 2+1 / 2 hours
13. Add more liquid as necessary
14. Season with salt and pepper<|endoftext|>
