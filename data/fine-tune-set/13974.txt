Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Brown Rice Risotto With Lamb

Ingredients:
• Boneless lamb shoulder
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Spicy vegetable juice
• Brown rice
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Carrots
• Green pepper

Steps:
1. Trim fat from meat
2. If necessary, cut meat to fit into a 3-1 / 2- or 4-quart slow cooker
3. Coat an unheated large nonstick skillet with nonstick cooking spray
4. Preheat skillet over medium heat and cook meat in hot skillet until browned, turning to brown evenly
5. Drain off fat
6. In the slow cooker, combine vegetable juice, uncooked brown rice, curry powder, and salt
7. Top with carrots and place meat on carrots
8. Cover and cook on low-heat setting for 8 to 9 hours or on high-heat setting for 4 to 4-1 / 2 hours
9. Add the sweet pepper to slow cooker
10. Cover and let stand for 5 to 10 minutes or until sweet pepper is tender<|endoftext|>
