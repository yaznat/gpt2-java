Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Cooking oil
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Garlic
• Salt
• Beef broth
• Tomatoes
• Mushroom stems and pieces
• Barbecue sauce
• Cornstarch
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In skillet brown meat in hot oil
2. Drain off fat
3. Place onion rings, green pepper and garlic in crockery cooker
4. Place meat atop
5. Sprinkle with salt and 1 / 8 teaspoon pepper
6. Add beef broth, undrained tomatoes, mushrooms and barbecue sauce
7. Stir to mix
8. Cover
9. Cook on low-heat setting for 8-10 hours
10. Turn cooker to high-heat setting
11. Blend 1 / 4 cup cold water slowly into cornstarch
12. Stir into stew
13. Cover and cook till thickened and bubbly, stirring occasionally
14. Serve over hot rice<|endoftext|>
