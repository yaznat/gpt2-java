Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Taco seasoning
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Broiler-fryer chickens

Steps:
1. In a large resealable plastic bag, combine the flour, taco seasoning and salt
2. In a shallow bowl, beat eggs and milk
3. Dip chicken pieces in egg mixture, then place in bag and shake to coat
4. Place bone side down in a greased 15-in
5. X 10-in
6. X 1-in
7. Baking pan
8. Bake, uncovered, at 350 for 55-60 minutes or until juices run clear<|endoftext|>
