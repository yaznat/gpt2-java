Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Mushrooms
• Onion
• Sun-dried tomato packed in oil
• Kalamata olive
• Goat cheese
• Dried oregano
• Pork tenderloin
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. Add olive oil and butter to a 10- or 11-inch skillet and set heat to medium-high
3. When butter melts, add mushrooms, onion, tomatoes, and olives
4. Saute for 5 to 6 minutes until mushrooms are softened
5. Remove from heat
6. Stir in the cheese and dried oregano until well combined
7. Butterfly each tenderloin by slitting lengthwise, cutting to but not through the other side
8. Place each between 2 sheets of heavy-duty plastic wrap
9. Pound with a meat mallet or a small heavy skillet
10. Thickness should be approximately 1 / 4- to 1 / 2-inch
11. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
12. Spread mushroom mixture on one side of each tenderloin, leaving about a 1 / 2-inch border
13. Roll up tenderloin, enclosing the filling inside
14. Place with the seams facing down in a roasting pan
15. Bake, uncovered, for 15 minutes
16. Reduce heat to 400 degrees and bake an additional 15 to 20 minutes or until meat reaches at least 160 degrees on an instant-read thermometer
17. Remove tenderloins from oven and allow to cool for 5 minutes before slicing<|endoftext|>
