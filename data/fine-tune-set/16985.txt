Ingredients:
• Coconut rum
• Melon liqueur
• Blue curacao
• Pineapple juice
• Prepared sweet-and-sour mix
• Carbonated lemon-lime beverage
• Maraschino cherry
• Pineapple

Steps:
1. Fill a cocktail shaker half full of ice cubes, add coconut rum, melon liqueur, blue curacao liqueur, pineapple juice, and sweet and sour mix
2. Shake well and strain in a highball glass
3. Garnish with a maraschino cherry and pineapple wedge flag<|endoftext|>
