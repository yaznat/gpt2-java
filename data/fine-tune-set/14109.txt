Ingredients:
• Green cabbage
• Carrots
• Green onions
• Salted peanuts
• Of fresh mint
• Fresh basil
• Tofu
• Lime juice
• Fish sauce
• Soy sauce
• Seasoned rice wine vinegar
• Sugar
• Garlic cloves
• Hot chili pepper

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, toss together cabbage, carrots, green onions, peanuts, mint, basil and tofu
2. To make dressing, combine ingredients in a small bowl
3. Pour over slaw mixture and toss well
4. Serve immediately or refrigerate up to 1 hour
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
