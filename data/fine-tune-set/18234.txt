Ingredients:
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Vanilla yogurt
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Bananas
• Vanilla extract
• Peanut butter
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Mix eggs
2. Put bananas in a ziplock baggy and mash until there are no lumps
3. Stir in bananas and all other ingredients until batter is not lumpy
4. Have a non-stick skillet heated on medium heat
5. Make sure the skillet is hot enough by throwing a few drops of water on it and making sure it sizzles
6. Scoop out batter with a ladle onto skillet and turn pancakes once there are many bubbles throughout
7. To make the syrup, simply put peanut butter and maple syrup in a microwavable container and heat for about 1 min 30 sec
8. Stir the syrup until thouroughly blended
9. Pour a generous portion of syrup over a giant stack of pancakes and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
