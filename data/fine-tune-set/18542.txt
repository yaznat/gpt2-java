Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Jan S Belgian Waffles

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Salt
• Milk
• Butter
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Beat egg yellow until light
2. Add a small amount of melted butter and quickly stir to temper the yolks
3. Add the remaining melted butter, vanilla and lemon juice
4. Mix well
5. Combine flour, salt, sugar and cinnamon together in separate bowl
6. Add warmed milk to flour mixture
7. Add egg yolk mixture to flour mixture and beat well
8. Beat egg white until stiff
9. Gently fold into batter
10. Pour batter onto heated waffle iron and cook until lightly crisp and golden
11. Serve with hot syrup, fresh berries, whipped cream, or bananas and chocolate chips<|endoftext|>
