Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Yellow onion
• Carrot
• Celery rib
• Red pepper
• Green pepper
• Jalapeno pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Vegetable stock
• Fire-roasted tomatoes
• Black beans
• Dark red kidney beans
• Garbanzo beans
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Smoked paprika
• Sugar
• Salt and pepper
• Corn tortillas
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Avocado
• Green onions
• Roma tomato
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. In a large skillet warm a small amount of olive oil
2. Add in onion through garlic and cook until crisp tender
3. Transfer vegetables to a crock pot
4. Add in vegetable stock through sugar
5. Season with salt and pepper, stir, cover and cook for 4 to 6 hours
6. About 30 minutes before serving time preheat oven to 425 degrees
7. Lightly brush both sides of corn tortillas with olive oil, stack and cut into 6 wedges
8. Scatter in a single layer on a couple of large cookie sheets
9. Place in preheated oven and bake, stirring one or twice, until golden and crisp
10. Remove from oven and cool slightly
11. In a medium size bowl combine the salsa ingredients
12. Season with salt and pepper to taste and drizzle with a small amount of lemon juice
13. Please
14. When ready to serve, place several baked tortilla chips around the rim of a shallow bowl, spoon in chili, sprinkle on grated cheese and top with a generous serving of avocado salsa
15. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
