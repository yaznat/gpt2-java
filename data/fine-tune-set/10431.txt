Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Flake Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Dates
• Nuts
• Rice krispies
• Vanilla
• Coconut

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan, boil butter, sugar, and chopped dates over medium heat for 3 minutes
2. Stirring constantly to bring the 3 ingredients into one thick brown mixture
3. Remove from heat
4. Fold in rice krispies, nuts, and vanilla
5. Blend well
6. Cool slightly
7. Roll mixture into 1-inch balls
8. Roll in coconut to coat evenly
9. Cool completely before storing in container with lid<|endoftext|>
