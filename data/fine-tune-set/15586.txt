Ingredients:
• Breaded fish fillets
• Coleslaw mix
• Sour cream
• Lawry's baja chipotle sauce with lime juice
• Whole wheat tortillas

Steps:
1. Bake fish according to directions on package
2. Mix sour cream & lawry marinade in a small bowl
3. Heat tortillas in microwave until warm
4. When fish is crispy, cut fish into 1 / 2 inch chunks
5. Place fish on tortilla top with sauce & cabbage
6. Roll up & enjoy<|endoftext|>
