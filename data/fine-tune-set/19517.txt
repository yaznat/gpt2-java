Give me a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>All Time Best Sweet Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Butter
• Apple cider
• Brown sugar
• Fresh cranberries
• Salt and pepper
• Orange marmalade
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Peel and slice sweet potatoes
2. Stove top instructions: melt butter in large pan over medium heat
3. Add sweet potatoes, apple cider / juice, brown sugar, cranberries and a shake or two of salt and pepper
4. Cook, stirring occasionally, until hot
5. Reduce heat and continue cooking and stirring occasionally, until potatoes are tender
6. These can remain on the stovetop on low heat until ready to serve
7. Stir in the orange marmalade towards the end of cooking
8. Add pecans just before serving
9. Oven instructions: place sweet potatoes in a 9x13 greased baking dish
10. Sprinkle with all ingredients
11. Cover and bake at 350 degrees for one hour or until potatoes are tender
12. Crock pot instructions: place all ingredients in greased crock pot
13. Cook on high setting for 30 minutes
14. Turn heat to low, stir occasionally and cook until tender<|endoftext|>
