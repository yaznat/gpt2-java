Ingredients:
• Rice
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic
• Beef broth
• Ground cumin
• Tomato paste
• Frozen green pea
• Carrot

Steps:
1. Cook rice in hot oil in 2 to 3 litre saucepan over medium heat, stirring constantly, about 2 minutes
2. Stir in onion and garlic
3. Cook, stirring about 1 minute
4. Add broth and cumin
5. Heat to boiling
6. Stir once or twice
7. Lower heat to simmer
8. Cover with tight fitting lid
9. Cook 15 minutes or until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed
10. Combine tomato paste and 1 tablespoon water
11. Stir tomato mixture, peas and carrots into rice
12. Fluff with a fork<|endoftext|>
