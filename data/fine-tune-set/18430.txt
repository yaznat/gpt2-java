Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• Egg whites
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Whisk flour, white sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl until no lumps remain
2. Pour milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract into the flour mixture
3. Stir until just combined
4. Beat egg whites until foamy in a large glass or metal mixing bowl until soft peaks form
5. Lift your beater or whisk straight up: the egg whites will form soft mounds rather than a sharp peak
6. Fold the the beaten egg whites into the batter
7. Preheat a waffle iron and coat with cooking spray
8. Pour batter onto waffle iron in batches and cook until crisp and golden brown, about 2 minutes
9. Repeat until all batter is used<|endoftext|>
