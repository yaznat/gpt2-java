What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Andalusian Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Yukon gold potatoes
• Sweet white onion
• Tomatoes
• Scallions
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Sherry wine vinegar
• Kosher salt
• Fresh flat-leaf parsley

Steps:
1. Place the potatoes in a medium-size saucepan, add enough cold water to cover them by 2 inches, and bring to a boil over medium-high heat
2. Reduce the heat to medium and cook the potatoes, partially covered, until they are completely tender when pricked with a skewer, 30 to 35 minutes
3. Drain the potatoes
4. When just cool enough to handle, peel the potatoes and cut them into a very fine dice
5. Place the diced potatoes and the onion, italian pepper, tomato, scallion, 1 / 3 cup of the olive oil, and 6 tablespoons of the vinegar in a bowl and stir to mix
6. Season with salt to taste
7. Toss everything together, crushing the potatoes slightly, so that they are half mashed
8. Let the salad stand for about 2 hours to absorb the dressing
9. Taste and add more vinegar as necessary
10. To serve, spread the potato salad on a serving plate, sprinkle with parsley on top, then drizzle the remaining oil over it<|endoftext|>
