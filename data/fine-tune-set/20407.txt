Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>All In One Egg Casserole

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Fresh mushrooms
• Green onion
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Milk
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Eggs
• English muffins
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook bacon until crisp
2. Remove bacon and set aside
3. Discard all but 2 tablespoons of drippings
4. Saute mushrooms and onions in drippings until tender
5. Set aside
6. In a large saucepan, melt butter
7. Stir in the flour, salt and pepper until smooth
8. Cook until bubbly
9. Gradually stir in milk and cheese
10. Cook and stir until thickened
11. Stir in the bacon, mushrooms and onions
12. Remove from the heat and set aside
13. For scrambled eggs, beat the eggs, milk, pepper and salt
14. Pour into a greased skillet
15. Cook and stir gently until eggs are set
16. Remove from the heat and set aside
17. Cut english muffin halves in half again
18. Place in a greased 11-in
19. X 7-in
20. Baking dish
21. Cover with half of cheese sauce
22. Spoon eggs over all
23. Top with remaining sauce
24. Sprinkle with parsley
25. Bake, uncovered, at 325 for 20-25 minutes or until bubbly<|endoftext|>
