What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Minute Whole Wheat Bread

Ingredients:
• Dry yeast
• Milk
• Whole wheat flour
• Unbleached flour
• Flax seed
• Salt
• Sugar
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl dissolve the yeast in warm milk
2. Add the rest of the ingredients and stir well
3. Pour dough into a 5 x 9 loaf pan
4. Place into a cold oven and turn the heat to 400f
5. Do not let this dough rise before putting into oven and oven is cold
6. Bake for 10 minutes then cut a slit in the top of the dough
7. After 40 minutes of baking you can brush the top of the loaf with butter or an egg wash
8. After 60 minutes reduce the heat to 350f then bake another 50 minutes
9. Remove the bread from the pan and bake another 15-30 minutes
10. Bread is done when it sounds hollow<|endoftext|>
