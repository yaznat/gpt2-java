Ingredients:
• Whole canned tomatoes
• Tomato juice
• Basil leaves
• Cream
• Sweet unsalted butter
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Combine tomatoes, tomato juice and broth in a saucepan and simmer for 30 minutes
2. Puree with basil in the blender
3. Make sure that this is smooth - i always strain it
4. Return to saucepan
5. Add cream and butter while stirring over low heat
6. Serve and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
