Ingredients:
• Navel oranges
• Garlic cloves
• Soy sauce
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Boneless sirloin steaks
• Cornstarch
• Vegetable oil
• Green onions
• Cooked brown rice

Steps:
1. Slice the meat into 1 / 2-inch thick strips
2. Mince the garlic
3. Cut the green onions into 1-inch lengths
4. Into a small bowl, finely grate the zest from one orange
5. Squeeze the juice from that orange into the bowl and add the garlic, soy sauce, and crushed red pepper flakes
6. Set aside
7. Peel the remaining 2 oranges
8. Slice them crosswise into 1 / 2-inch thick slices, then cut each slice in half
9. Remove any seeds
10. In a medium bowl, toss the meat with the cornstarch until well coated
11. In a large skilled or wok, heat the oil
12. Add the beef and cook until brown on all sides, 3-5 minutes
13. Remove the meat from the skillet,
14. Pour the juice mixture into the skillet and boil until syrupy, about 1 minutes
15. Return the beef to the skillet
16. Add the orange slices and onions
17. Toss until coated and heated through
18. Serve over hot rice<|endoftext|>
