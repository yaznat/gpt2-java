Ingredients:
• Half-and-half
• Lemon, zest of
• Cinnamon stick
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a heavy saucepan combine half-and-half, lemon zest and cinnamon stick over medium heat and bring to a simmer
2. Remove from heat and let steep for 10 minutes
3. Strain into a large bowl, discarding solids
4. Set aside and let cool
5. Whisk egg yolks and 1 / 2 cup sugar together in a large mixing bowl until thick and pale yellow
6. Whisk in half-and-half
7. Return mixture to saucepan and cook over medim low heat, stirring constantly with wooden spoon until thick
8. Remove from heat and strain into a large bowl and set aside
9. Cover and refrigerate until chilled
10. While custard is chilling, grease a cookie sheet with butter and set aside
11. In a large saucepan, combine remaining sugar and 4 tablespoons water and bring to a simmer over medium low heat, swirling pot frequently until sugar carmalizes
12. Immediately pour syrup onto prepared sheet, tilting pan to spread around so syrup is a thin layer
13. Set aside and let cool
14. Crack carmamel into small pieces
15. Stir into cooled custard
