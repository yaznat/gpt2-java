What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Absolutely Sinful Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate squares
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• White sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Sour cream
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. In the microwave or over a double-boiler, melt unsweetened chocolate and butter together, stirring occasionally until smooth
3. Sift together flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt
4. Set aside
5. In a medium bowl, beat sugar, eggs, and vanilla until light
6. Mix in the chocolate mixture until well blended
7. Stir in the sifted ingredients alternately with sour cream, then mix in chocolate chips
8. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheets
9. Bake for 8-10 minutes
10. Allow cookies to cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely
11. Store in an airtight container<|endoftext|>
