What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Grilled Salmon Quesadillas With Cucumber Salsa

Ingredients:
• Cucumber
• Salsa
• Salmon fillets
• Olive oil
• Flour tortillas
• Goat cheese
• Pickled jalapeno pepper

Steps:
1. Prepare grill for direct cooking
2. Combine cucumber and salsa in small bowl
3. Set aside
4. Brush salmon with 2 tablespoons oil
5. Grill, covered, over medium-hot coals 5 to 6 minutes per side or until fish flakes when tested with fork
6. Transfer to plate
7. Flake with fork
8. Spoon salmon evenly over half of each tortilla, leaving 1-inch border
9. Sprinkle with cheese and jalapeo pepper slices
10. Fold tortillas in half
11. Brush tortillas with remaining 1 tablespoon oil
12. Grill quesadillas over medium-hot coals until browned on both sides and cheese is melted
13. Serve with cucumber salsa<|endoftext|>
