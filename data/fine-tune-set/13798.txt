Ingredients:
• Fresh shrimp
• Egg
• Ground pork
• Water chestnuts
• Bamboo shoots
• Fresh garlic
• Green onions
• Fresh ginger
• Soy sauce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Spring roll wrappers
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Finely chop the fresh shrimp
2. In a bowl stir together the chopped shrimp 1 egg, pork and the next 8 ingredients
3. Mix well to combine
4. Cover and chill for a minimum of 4 hours to blend the flavors before using
5. Spoon about 1 tablespoon of mixture in the center of each spring roll wrapper
6. Fold the top corner of each wrapper over the filling, tucking
7. Lightly brush remaining corner with beaten egg
8. Tightly roll filled end toward remaining corner, then gently press to seal
9. Pour about 2-inches of oil in a skillet and heat to 350 degrees
10. Fry the spring rolls a couple at a time only for about 6 minutes or until golden brown
11. Drain on a brown paper bag or paper towels
12. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
