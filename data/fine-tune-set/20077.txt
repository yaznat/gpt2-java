Ingredients:
• Fresh spinach
• Onion
• Fresh mushrooms
• Feta cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Eggs
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper
• Yogurt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Heat olive oil to medium-high& add onion- saute until opaque, then add mushrooms and then spinach& saute just until spinach is wilted
3. Grease 9" square casserole dish with some olive oil beat eggs& add salt& pepper to taste
4. Add crumbled feta and spinach mixture to eggs
5. Pour into casserole dish& top with mozzarella
6. Bake at 350 degrees for approx
7. 30 minutes
8. Cool about 5 minutes, then cut into 4 servings
9. Serve with fruit and some plain yogurt on the side<|endoftext|>
