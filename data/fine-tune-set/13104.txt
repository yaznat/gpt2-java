Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Belizean Steamed Fish

Ingredients:
• White fish fillet
• Limes
• Salt
• Pepper
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Curry powder
• Coconut oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Rinse and dry fish, then drench with juice of limes
3. Drain and season with salt and pepper
4. Place 1 layer of fish in a shallow pan
5. Cover with green pepper and onion, then sprinkle with curry powder
6. Drizzle with coconut oil or melted butter
7. Squeeze remaining lime juice on fish
8. Cover tightly with lid or foil and bake for 10 minutes, then baste with liquid
9. Fish is done when it flakes easily with fork<|endoftext|>
