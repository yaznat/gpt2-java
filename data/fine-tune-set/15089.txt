Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Onion
• Garlic
• Spaghetti sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Crescent rolls

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Brown ground chuck, onion and garlic in large skillet
3. Drain and stir in spaghetti sauce
4. In 9 x 13 dish, spread a layer of sauce and top with half of cheeses
5. Then spread remaining sauce and the rest of the cheese
6. Top with crescent rolls, and pinch seams to form a crust
7. Bake 20 to 30 minutes, or until crust is golden brown<|endoftext|>
