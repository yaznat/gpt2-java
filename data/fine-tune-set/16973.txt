Give me a recipe for a cocktail.<|endoftext|>Caipirinha Brazilian Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Lime
• Superfine sugar
• Cachaca
• Ice
• Lime wedge

Steps:
1. Place lime and sugar in bottom of cocktail shaker
2. Muddle lime to release all the citrus juice
3. Add crushed ice and cachaca to the shaker, and shake well
4. Pour shaken cocktail into an old-fashioned glass, and serve with a garnish of fresh lime wedge<|endoftext|>
