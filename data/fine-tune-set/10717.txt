Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Green pepper
• Sweet red pepper
• Stewed tomatoes
• Linguine
• Jalapeno pepper

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook bacon until crisp
2. Remove bacon with slotted spoon and set aside, reserving the drippings
3. Saute onions and garlic in drippings for 3 minutes
4. Add peppers, cook and stir for 3 minutes
5. Stir in tomatoes and heat through
6. Add bacon and mix well
7. Serve over pasta
