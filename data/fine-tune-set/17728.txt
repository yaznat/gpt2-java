Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Walnut Streusel Muffins

Ingredients:
• Walnuts
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Butter
• Cinnamon
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Allspice
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Eggs
• Greek yogurt
• Apple

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Butter muffin cups or line with muffin liners
3. Put streusel topping ingredients into a medium-size bowl
4. Mix with a fork, then crumble with fingers until mixture looks like chopped walnuts
5. For muffins: thoroughly mix flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, allspice, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl
6. Break eggs into another bowl
7. Add yoghurt and melted butter, and whisk until well blended
8. Stir in diced apple
9. Pour egg mixture over flour mixture and fold in just until dry ingredients are moistened
10. Scoop batter into muffin cups
11. Top each muffin with about 2 teaspoons of the streusel topping
12. Bake for 25 minutes, or until done<|endoftext|>
