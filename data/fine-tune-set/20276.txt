Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken thighs
• Fine egg noodles
• Clear honey
• Vegetable oil
• Ginger
• Garlic
• Coriander roots
• Red chile
• Spring onions
• Carrot
• Red bell pepper
• Bok choy
• Dark soy sauce
• Ketjap manis
• Toasted sesame oil
• Kaffir lime
• Fresh cilantro leaves
• Cashew nuts

Steps:
1. Cook the noodles as per the instructions on the packet, drain and set aside
2. Heat a large wok to a high heat
3. Add a little of the vegetable oil to the wok, and when it is hot, stir-fry the chicken in batches until sealed and half-cooked
4. Return all the chicken to the wok, add the honey, and continue cooking until the chicken is fully cooked and a rich golden colour
5. Add a little water to the wok, as necessary, just to prevent the chicken and honey from sticking
6. Remove the chicken and honey mixture from the wok and set aside while you cook the remainder of the dish
7. Heat the remaining vegetable oil in the wok and saute the ginger, garlic, coriander roots and chilli for about a minute
8. Add the vegetables and stir-fry over high heat until just tender
9. Add the pre-cooked noodles and saute for a minute or so before returning the chicken to the pan
10. Add the soy sauces, sesame oil, the lime zest and juice
11. Heat through and correct the seasoning, adding a little more honey if desired
12. Finally, stir through the fresh coriander
13. Serve with a scattering of cashews over the top<|endoftext|>
