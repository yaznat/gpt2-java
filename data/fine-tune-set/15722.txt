Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Water
• Salsa
• Taco seasoning mix
• Instant rice
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Combine chicken, water, salsa and seasonings in a saucepan
2. Bring to a boil
3. Add rice, and simmer on for 5 minutes
4. Spoon mixture into center of tortillas
5. Add cheese if you want it and wrap
6. Flash freeze
7. To reheat: microwave until heated through<|endoftext|>
