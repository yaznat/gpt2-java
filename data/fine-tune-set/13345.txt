What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>African Chicken And Peanut Stew

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Boneless chicken
• Onion
• Carrot
• Celery
• Sweet potatoes
• Garlic cloves
• Curry powder
• Ground coriander
• Ground cumin
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Ground ginger
• Cinnamon
• Bay leaf
• Salt
• Chicken broth
• Tomato paste
• Peanut butter
• Lemon juice
• Roma tomatoes
• Bell pepper
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. In a large, heavy pot, heat the oil on med-high
2. When hot, add the onions and sweet potatoes, stir and cook until onions are soft and translucent
3. Add chicken, cook til opaque
4. Stir in the garlic, curry powder, coriander, cumin, and crushed red chiles
5. Cook 1 minute
6. Add the carrots, celery, ginger, cinnamon, bay leaf, salt, chicken broth, and tomato paste
7. Bring the liquid to a boil, then cover and simmer on low for 45 minutes
8. When the stew has cooked for 45 minutes, stir in the peanut butter
9. Cook another 3 minutes, uncovered for the flavors to blend
10. Stir in the chopped tomato and bell pepper
11. Simmer until the tomatoes and pepper are softened but still retain their shape, 3 to 5 minutes
12. Stir in lemon juice
13. Serve with the chopped cilantro on the top, and additional red pepper flakes on the side, if desired
14. I like to serve this stew over hearty brown rice
15. Healthy and delicious!<|endoftext|>
