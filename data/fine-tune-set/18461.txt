Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Dads Spectacular Waffles

Ingredients:
• Pancake mix
• 1% low-fat milk
• Canola oil
• Egg
• Nuts
• Banana
• Cinnamon
• Honey
• Vanilla
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Combine everything in a blender and mix until combined and nuts are crushed
2. Heat up waffle iron and grease lightly with nonstick spray
3. Cook according to manufacturers directions
4. Top with pure maple syrup, butter, fat free whipped cream, fruit or whatever else you want!<|endoftext|>
