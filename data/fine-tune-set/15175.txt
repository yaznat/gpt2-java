Give me a pizza recipe.<|endoftext|>Basil Bread Slice Pizzas

Ingredients:
• Italian bread
• Mozzarella cheese
• Fresh basil
• Cream cheese
• Tomato paste
• Italian seasoning

Steps:
1. Place tomato paste in a mixing bowl, stir in italian seasoning until well blended
2. Add cream cheese and basil
3. Slice bread into 1 / 4 inch thick slices
4. Place on an ungreased baking pan
5. Broil 6-8 inches from heat, turn after 1 minute
6. After browned on both sides remove bread slices from broiler
7. Spread 2 tablespoons of sauce on each toast slice
8. Thinly slice mozzarella and layer a slice over the sauce
9. Broil the little pizzas 6-8 inches from the heat source for 2 minutes or until the cheese is melted
10. These look beautiful served with a dash of black pepper on top with a basil leaf for garnish<|endoftext|>
