What's a good tea recipe?<|endoftext|>Australian Tropical Tea

Ingredients:
• Orange juice
• Tea bags
• Lemon juice
• Cinnamon sticks
• Whole cloves
• Ground nutmeg
• Lemon slices

Steps:
1. Bring orange juice to a boil in a saucepan
2. Remove from heat and add all other ingredients except lemon slices
3. Cover and let steep 5 minutes or until desired strength
4. Remove tea bags
5. Allow to cool, then chill
6. Strain and serve in small goblets, garnished with lemon slices<|endoftext|>
