Ingredients:
• Lemon, juice of
• Fresh parsley
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Adobo seasoning
• Skirt steaks

Steps:
1. Cut rinsed lemon in half and squeeze juice into the food processor
2. Remove large stems from the rinsed parsley and add to the lemon juice with 3 tablespoons olive oil, garlic, and adobo seasoning
3. Pulse 10-15 times until coarsely chopped and blended
4. Preheat large saute pan on medium-high 2-3 minutes
5. Add remaining oil to pan
6. Swirl to coat
7. Add steaks and cook 6-8 minutes, turning often, until internal temperature reaches 145 degrees f
8. Top steaks with the parsley mixture and serve<|endoftext|>
