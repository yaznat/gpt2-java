Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>Almost Authentic Cincinnati Chili

Ingredients:
• Water
• Ground beef
• Cinnamon
• Ground cumin
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Black pepper
• Red pepper
• Salt
• Ground allspice
• Tomato paste
• Cider vinegar
• Bay leaves

Steps:
1. Crumble raw ground beef into the quart of water
2. Add all of the other ingredients
3. Simmer uncovered for 1 / 2 hour
4. Simmer for another 2 and 1 / 2 hours covered
5. Stir occasionally<|endoftext|>
