Ingredients:
• Fat-free low-sodium chicken broth
• Cilantro
• Fresh lime juice
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Jalapeno peppers
• Skinless chicken breast half
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Onion
• Fat free tortillas
• Soft fresh goat cheese
• Fat free sour cream
• Lime wedge

Steps:
1. Combine first 5 ingredients in blender or food processor until smooth
2. Place chicken in zip-loc bag and add broth mixture
3. Seal and refrigerate for 2 hrs turning bag occasionally
4. Preheat grill
5. Remove chicken from bag, discard marinade
6. Sprinkle chicken with salt and pepper
7. Place chicken and onion on grill rack coated with cooking spray
8. Grill onion 3 minutes on each side or until browned
9. Grill chicken 6 minutes per side or until done
10. Cool slightly and cut chicken in very thin slices
11. Preheat oven to 400
12. Place 1 tortilla on baking sheet coated with cooking spray
13. Sprinkle with 3 tablespoons of cheese, half of chicken, half of onion and 1 and a half tsp cilantro
14. Top with 1 tortilla
15. Sprinkle with remaining cheese, chicken, onion and cilantro
16. Top with last tortilla
17. Coat tortilla with cooking spray
18. Bake for 15 minutes or until lightly browned
19. Cut into 4 wedges and top each with 1 tsp of sour cream
20. Serve with lime wedges if desired<|endoftext|>
