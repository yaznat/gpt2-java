Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Unsalted butter
• Espresso beans
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Pecans
• Kahlua
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees f
2. Butter and flour a 9 by 12 inch baking pan
3. Line bottom of pan with parchment paper
4. Butter and flour paper
5. Combine chocolate, butter, espresso and salt in top of double boiler over simmering water
6. Stir until chocolate melts and mixture is smooth
7. Cool
8. This can also be done in the microwave on 50% power until smooth
9. In large bowl, combine sugar, eggs and 1 teaspoon vanilla and whisk until smooth
10. Add melted chocolate mixture and whisk until smooth
11. Gently fold in flour, just until it disappears
12. Fold in pecans
13. Pour into prepared pan
14. Smooth the top
15. Bake until toothpick inserted into center comes out clean, about 35 minutes
16. Cool brownies on rack 1 hour
17. To make the glaze: whisk kahlua, vanilla and butter in small bowl until well blended
18. Add powdered sugar and whisk until smooth icing forms
19. Spread over brownies and let set at least 1 hour before cutting<|endoftext|>
