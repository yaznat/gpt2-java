Ingredients:
• Egg
• Smooth peanut butter
• Oil
• Brown sugar
• Milk
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Strawberry jam

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Beat egg, 1 cup peanut butter and the oil in large bowl with wire whisk until well blended
3. Add sugar
4. Mix well
5. Gradually add milk, mixing well after each addition
6. Set aside
7. Mix flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt
8. Add to peanut butter mixture
9. Stir just until moistened
10. Spoon enough batter into each of 12 greased muffin cups to cover bottom of cup
11. Spoon 1 / 2 teaspoons each of the jam and remaining 2 tablespoons peanut butter over batter in cups
12. Cover evenly with batter
13. Bake 15 minutes, or until lightly browned
14. Cool in pan 5 minutes
15. Remove to wire rack
16. Cool completely
17. Variation: prepare as directed, using your favorite flavor of jam and variety of peanut butter<|endoftext|>
