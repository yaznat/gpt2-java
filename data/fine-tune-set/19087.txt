Ingredients:
• Half-and-half
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Mashed banana
• Vanilla extract
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. In ice cream freezer container, combine all ingredients
2. Mix well
3. Freeze according to manufacturer's instructions
4. Store leftovers covered in refrigerator
5. Refrigerator-freezer method: omit half-and-half
6. In large bowl, combine eagle brand and vanilla
7. Stir in bananas and cinnamon
8. Fold in 2 cups whipping cream, whipped
9. Pour into 9x5 inch loaf pan or other 2 quart container
10. Cover
11. Freeze 6 hours or until firm
