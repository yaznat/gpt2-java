What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Seasoned Pork Chops With Bar B Q D Onions

Ingredients:
• Garlic powder
• Seasoning salt
• Dried basil
• Dried oregano
• Ground black pepper
• Boneless pork loin chops
• Bacon
• Maple syrup
• Olive oil
• Yellow onions
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Combine the garlic powder, seasoned salt, basil, oregano, and black pepper
2. Rub the seasoning all over the pork chops
3. Wrap a strip of bacon around the edges of each pork chop and secure with toothpicks
4. Brush maple syrup on bacon and coat each chop well with the olive oil
5. Grill the pork chops and onions over medium heat for 10 minutes, turning once
6. Transfer the onions to a large piece of heavy duty foil and top with barbecue sauce, seal edges of foil - leaving room for air to circulate
7. Cook the chops and onions for another 10 minutes
8. To serve, top the pork with cooked onions and add your favorie side dishes<|endoftext|>
