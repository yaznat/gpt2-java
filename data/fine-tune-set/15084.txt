Ingredients:
• Prepared pizza crust
• Brie cheese
• Yellow onions
• Balsamic vinaigrette
• Brown sugar
• Roasted red pepper
• Olive oil
• Fresh thyme leave

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400f place oven rack in lowest position
2. Grease cookie sheet
3. Unroll dough on cookie sheet
4. Press out to 15x12-inch rectangle
5. Bake 8 minutes or until light golden brown
6. Remove from oven
7. Meanwhile, in medium skillet over medium heat, cook and stir onions with 2 tablespoons of the balsamic vinaigrette and the brown sugar 8 to 10 minutes or until tender
8. Stir in red pepper strips and remaining 1 tablespoons balsamic vinaigrette
9. Arrange alouette baby brie over partially baked crust
10. Top with onion and pepper mixture
11. Drizzle lightly with olive oil
12. Bake 10 minutes or until alouette baby brie is melted and crust is deep golden brown
13. Sprinkle with thyme leaves
14. Cut into 20 squares and cut squares diagonally into triangles<|endoftext|>
