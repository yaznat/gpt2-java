What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Sour Cream Muffins

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Half-and-half cream
• Nutmeg
• Brown sugar
• Raisins
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Egg
• Butter
• Cinnamon
• Flour
• Milk
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Combine apples, half& half, cinnamon, nutmeg, 2 tbsp
2. Butter, 1 / 4 cup brown sugar and raisins in sauce pan
3. Simmer over low heat until apples are very soft, stirring frequently
4. Sift flour, baking powder, remaining brown sugar and salt together
5. Combine egg, milk, 3 tbsp melted butter and sour cream in mixing bowl, beat well
6. Add flour mixture
7. Mix well
8. Fold in apple mixture
9. Place cupcake liners sprayed with non-stick cooking spray in muffin cups
10. Fill 2 / 3 full
11. Bake at 400 degrees for 15 to 20 minutes or until muffins test done<|endoftext|>
