Ingredients:
• Sirloin steaks
• Italian dressing
• Worcestershire sauce
• Brown sugar
• Jalapeno pepper
• Ground allspice

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients, except steak
2. Place steak in sealable plastic bag
3. Add 1 / 2 cup marinade, seal, and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 5 hours turning occasionally
4. Refrigerate remaining marinade in sealable container
5. Remove steak from marinade-discard marinade-
6. Grill over medium-high coals, brushing often with reserved marinade, until steak 140f internal for medium-rare, or to desired doneness, approximately 16-18 minutes
7. Remove steak from grill, slice to 1 / 2-1 / 4 inches and serve with backed potato or serve over salad<|endoftext|>
