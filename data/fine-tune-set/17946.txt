Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Unsweetened cocoa
• Flour
• Salt
• Powdered sugar
• Milk
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Put butter in 9" x 13" baking pan and place in oven to melt while it is heating
3. In a medium bowl mix together sugar, cocoa, flour and salt
4. Remove pan from oven when butter is melted and add butter to bowl
5. With wire whisk blend mixture until evenly combined
6. Add eggs and vanilla and blend with whisk until all eggs are evenly mixed inches pour batter into pan
7. Bake for 25 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in center comes out with a few crumbs attached-don't overbake
8. Cool on a wire rack
9. Frost with browned butter frosting and drizzle with glaze
10. Cut into bars
11. For frosting:
12. Place butter in a small saucepan over medium heat
13. Heat until butter begins to bubble and butter turns a light golden brown, stirring occasionally
14. Remove from heat and cool until lukewarm
15. Add butter, vanilla, powdered sugar and milk
16. Beat with mixer until light and fluffy
17. Spread on cooled brownies
18. Combine cocoa and water in a small zip-lock bag
19. Knead until thoroughly mixed
20. Snip a tiny bit off one corner of bag and drizzle contents over frosting<|endoftext|>
