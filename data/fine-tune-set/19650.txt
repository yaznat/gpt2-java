What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Asparagus New Potato And Chive Frittata

Ingredients:
• New potato
• Asparagus
• Eggs
• Fat free egg substitute
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Chives
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Place potatoes in a steamer
2. Steam until almost tender, about 3 minutes
3. Add asparagus and steam for 1 minute more
4. Remove veggies from steamer and set aside
5. Preheat broiler
6. Whisk together eggs, egg substitute, salt, pepper and chives
7. Coat a 10-inch nonstick, ovenproof skillet with cooking spray and set over mediumheat
8. Add potatoes and asparagus and saute for 2 minutes
9. Reduce heat to low and stir in eggs
10. Cover and let cook until eggs are set around edges, about 5 minutes
11. Uncover and put frittata under broiler until golden, about 2 minutes, cut into wedges and serve<|endoftext|>
