What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Steak Topped With Bell Pepper Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Thai sweet chili sauce
• Soy sauce
• Balsamic vinegar
• Fresh ginger
• New york strip steaks
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Red bell peppers
• Green onions
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Whisk chili sauce, soy sauce, balsamic vinegar, and ginger in medium bowl
3. Place in large zip-loc bag
4. Add steaks and turn to coat
5. Let marinate at room temperature 15 minutes, turning occasionally
6. Heat 1 / 2 tablespoon vegetable oil in large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat
7. Scrape most of marinade off steaks and back into bowl
8. Sprinkle steaks with salt and pepper
9. Add to skillet and cook until browned, about 3 minutes
10. Turn steaks over and transfer skillet to oven
11. Roast until cooked to desired doneness, about 5 minutes longer for medium-rare
12. Transfer steaks to cutting board
13. Let rest 10 minutes
14. Meanwhile, heat remaining 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in heavy medium skillet over medium-high heat
15. Add onion and bell peppers
16. Saut until crisp-tender, about 3 minutes
17. Add green onions and all remaining marinade from steaks
18. Bring to boil
19. Stir in cilantro
20. Cut steaks crosswise in half
21. Divide steaks among 4 plates
22. Spoon vegetables over steaks and serve with jasmine rice to sop up the juice<|endoftext|>
