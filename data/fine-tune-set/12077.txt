Ingredients:
• Wide egg noodles
• Mashed potatoes
• Butter
• Onions
• Garlic
• Cheddar cheese
• White processed cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Cook noodles a couple of minutes less than directed on package, you want them a little chewy
2. Drain and set aside
3. Melt butter and saute onions until light golden
4. Add garlic if using and continue to saute 1 minute
5. Add onions to potatoes and mix well
6. Spray 13x9 inch baking dish with non-stick cooking spray
7. Add half of noodles
8. Top with potato mixture
9. Add cheddar cheese
10. Top with remaining noodles
11. Add american cheese
12. Bake at 350 for 30 to 45 minutes, until hot and bubbly
13. Serve with sour cream<|endoftext|>
