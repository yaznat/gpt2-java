Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Potatoes
• Olive oil
• Braggs liquid aminos
• Red wine vinegar
• Oregano
• Thyme
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Chili powder

Steps:
1. Slice tofu and potatoes and place in a large baking dish
2. Top with oil, braggs, vinegar and seasonings
3. Bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for about 30 minutes
