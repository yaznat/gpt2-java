Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Garlic clove
• Chopped tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Creamy peanut butter
• Chicken broth
• Balsamic vinegar
• Cayenne pepper
• Kosher salt
• White rice
• Scallions
• Salted peanuts

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan over medium, heat the oil and garlic for 1 minute
2. Add the tomatoes, tomato paste, peanut butter, broth, vinegar, cayenne, and salt, whisk to combine
3. Bring to a boil
4. Add the rice, reduce heat to low, cover and cook for 20 minutes
5. Garnish with scallions and peanuts<|endoftext|>
