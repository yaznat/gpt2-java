Ingredients:
• French bread dough
• Pizza sauce
• Meatballs
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Spray a large cookie sheet with nonstick cooking spray
3. Unroll the french loaf
4. Spread pizza sauce on top
5. Cover with meatball slices
6. Cover with mozzarella cheese
7. Add some parmesan cheese
8. Starting with the long side, roll up the dough and press the edges together to seal
9. Make several slits in the top
10. Put on cookie sheet
11. Bake for 15 minutes, then brush the top with butter or olive oil
12. Cook for another 5- 7 minutes, or until the top of the crust has browned
13. Let cool for 5 minutes, then cut and serve<|endoftext|>
