What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>A Different Seven Layer Salad

Ingredients:
• Sour cream
• Buttermilk
• Feta cheese
• Sugar
• Dill
• Basil
• Pepper
• Spinach tortellini
• Spinach
• Mushrooms
• Roma tomatoes
• Green onions
• Canadian bacon

Steps:
1. Dressing:
2. Process all ingredients together and chill
3. Salad:
4. Cook tortellini as per package directions
5. Rinse in cold water
6. In a 3 quart rectangular dish, layer the spinach, tortellini, mushrooms, tomatoes and green onions
7. Pour dressing over and sprinkle with the bacon
8. Cover and chill for 2 hours<|endoftext|>
