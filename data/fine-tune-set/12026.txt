Ingredients:
• Water
• Sugar-free lime gelatin
• Fat-free key lime yogurt
• Frozen reduced-fat non-dairy whipped topping
• Reduced fat graham cracker crust

Steps:
1. In microwave save measuring cup heat water on high for 45 seconds to 1 1 / 2 minutes or until boiling
2. Whisk in gelatin until dissolved
3. In large bowl whisk together gelatin mixture and yogurt
4. Fold in whipped topping
5. Carefully spread in crust
6. Refrigerate at least 4 hours or until set
7. Garnish as desired
8. Store in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
