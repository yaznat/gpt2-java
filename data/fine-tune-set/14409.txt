Give me a beans recipe.<|endoftext|>African Beans

Ingredients:
• Dried black-eyed peas
• Onions
• Oil
• Tomato paste
• Coconut cream
• Paprika
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Cook dry beans according to instructions on packet
2. Saut onions until they are soft and clear
3. Add tomato paste, coconutcream and seasonings, stirring over a gentle heat until they form a smooth and creamy sauce
4. Drain beans and add to sauce
5. Allow to simmer gently for 15 minutes or so, stirring from time to time
6. Serve on brown or white rice accompanied by a salad<|endoftext|>
