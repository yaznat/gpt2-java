Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Bacon
• Pears
• Apples
• Thyme
• Flour
• Milk
• Eggs
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat butter in a large frying pan
2. Add onion and bacon and cook until softened
3. Stir in pears, apples and thyme, and cook until heated through
4. Place flour in a small bowl
5. Add eggs and remaining milk and beat until smooth
6. Whisk in salt
7. Spread fruit over bottom of a 9x13-inch baking pan
8. Pour batter on top
9. Bake in 350f oven for 30 minutes until puffed and lightly browned
10. Cut in squares and serve with warmed maple syrup<|endoftext|>
