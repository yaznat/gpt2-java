What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Honey Glazed Ham Steaks

Ingredients:
• Ham steaks
• Brown sugar
• Hot mustard
• Liquid honey
• Vinegar
• Soya sauce
• Glace cherries

Steps:
1. Remove the rind from the ham and slash the edges at 2" intervals
2. Mix together the remaining ingredients for the glaze
3. Bbq about 6" from heat source, medium-high heat cook on both sides for about 3 minutes each side before basting with glaze turn frequently basting each time with the glaze total cooking time appr 10-12 minutes
4. Watch that the steak does not burn
5. Decorate with the glazed cherries and serve<|endoftext|>
