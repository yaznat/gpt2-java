What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Fried Tofu And Spinach Salad

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Tamari soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Fresh ginger
• Sugar
• Dijon-style mustard
• Salt
• Cooking oil
• Cornmeal
• Sesame seeds
• Spinach
• Watercress
• Button mushrooms
• Cherry tomatoes
• Red onion

Steps:
1. Cut tofu crosswise into eight 1 / 2-inch slices
2. In a 2-quart rectangular baking dish arrange slices in a single layer
3. Pour tamari sauce over tofu
4. Turn slices to coat
5. Let stand for 15 minutes
6. Meanwhile, for vinaigrette, in a blender container or food processor bowl combine vinegar, ginger, sugar, mustard, and salt
7. Cover and blend or process until combined
8. With blender or processor running, add the 1 / 3 cup oil in a thin steady stream
9. Blend or process for 15 seconds more
10. Drain tofu, discarding tamari sauce
11. In a shallow dish combine cornmeal and sesame seeds
12. Carefully dip tofu slices in cornmeal mixture to lightly coat both sides
13. In a large nonstick skillet cook tofu in the 2 teaspoons hot oil for 5 to 6 minutes or until crisp and hot, carefully turning once
14. In an extra-large bowl combine spinach, watercress, mushrooms, tomatoes, and red onion
15. Pour the vinaigrette over spinach mixture
16. Toss to coat
17. Divide among 4 dinner plates
18. Cut the tofu slices in half diagonally
19. Arrange the tofu over spinach mixture
20. If desired, sprinkle with additional sesame seeds
21. Makes 4 servings<|endoftext|>
