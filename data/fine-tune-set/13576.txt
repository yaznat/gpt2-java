What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Beef Empanadas Light

Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Onion
• Beef broth
• Salt
• Ground cumin
• Ground allspice
• Black pepper
• Boneless beef top sirloin steak
• Garlic clove
• Cilantro
• Cornstarch
• Cold water
• Wonton wrappers
• Cooking spray
• Fresh cilantro stem

Steps:
1. Combine first 10 ingredients in a saucepan
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring occasionally
3. Reduce heat
4. Simmer 8 minutes or until potato is done, stirring occasionally
5. Remove from heat
6. Cool
7. Preheat oven to 400f place 2 baking sheets in oven for about 10 minutes
8. Drain meat mixture in a colander over a bowl
9. Discard liquid
10. Finely chop meat mixture with a knife, or place in a food processor, and pulse 2 times or until finely chopped
11. Combine cornstarch and water, stirring with a whisk
12. Working with one won ton wrapper at a time, spoon 1 tablespoon beef mixture into center of each wrapper
13. Moisten the edges of each wrapper with the cornstarch mixture
14. Bring 2 opposite corners together
15. Pinch the points to seal, forming a triangle
16. Place filled wrapper on a wire rack
17. Repeat the procedure with the remaining beef mixture, won ton wrappers, and cornstarch mixture
18. Remove the baking sheets from the oven
19. Coat baking sheets with cooking spray
20. Arrange the empanadas in a single layer on baking sheets
21. Coat the empanadas with cooking spray
22. Bake at 400f for 8 minutes or until golden, turning once
23. Garnish with cilantro, if desired<|endoftext|>
