Ingredients:
• Fresh asparagus
• Fresh basil leaves
• Parmesan cheese
• Garlic clove
• Pecans
• Olive oil
• Spaghetti

Steps:
1. Prepare pasta according to package directions
2. When ready, drain and then toss with remaining tablespoon of olive oil
3. Meanwhile, combine asparagus, basil, parmesan cheese, garlic, pecans, and 2 tablespoons olive oil in a blender or food processor and process until smooth
4. Serve asparagus pesto over prepared pasta<|endoftext|>
