What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Eggplant Aubergine French Fries

Ingredients:
• Eggplant
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic salt
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Shake eggplant"fries" in a large ziploc bag with flour, salt, pepper, and garlic salt
2. Fry in the 1 / 2 cup vegetable oil
3. Drain on a paper towel
4. Broil in the oven to make crispy
5. Serve with marinara or alfredo for dipping<|endoftext|>
