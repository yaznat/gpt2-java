Give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Light Hash Brown Omelette

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Egg substitute
• Fat-free cheddar cheese
• Cubed ham

Steps:
1. Dice onions and add to shredded potatoes
2. Place shredded potatoes and onion in medium size fry pan prepared with vegetable spray
3. Keep potatoes separated as much as possible while browning, you do not want a patty
4. When potatoes are well browned add ham cubes and mix well
5. Lower heat to medium low
6. Pour egg substitute evenly over potato mixture and cook till almost set
7. Turn mixture over
8. When eggs are set well tear cheese slice into strips and lay across one half of the omelet
9. Fold omelet in half over the cheese slice and serve
10. Slice in half lengthwise and place in cold water for about 2-3 minutes
11. Shred each half without peeling
12. Keep the peel against your palm while shredding and most of the peel will remain in your hand
13. Discard the peel and continue with recipe<|endoftext|>
