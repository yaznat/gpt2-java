What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Fast Breakfast Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Egg
• Ham
• Milk
• Four cheese blend
• Cooking spray
• Salt and pepper
• Salsa
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Crack egg into small bowl
2. Salt and pepper and desired
3. Add tbs milk and whisk until scrambled
4. Spray a regular dinner plate with nonstick cooking spray
5. Place one tortilla on plate
6. Sprinkle liberally with shredded cheese
7. Pour egg mixture on top of cheese in a spiral shape
8. Place in microwave for one minute
9. Cut meat into small pieces
10. Sprinkle on top of egg and cheese from microwave, and top with some more cheese
11. Place 2nd tortilla on top and microwave for 30 secs - 1 minute
12. Cut with pizza cutter, add whatever toppings you desire, and serve<|endoftext|>
