What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Alpine Cabbage Soup

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Butter
• Cabbage
• Ground nutmeg
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Salt
• Vegetable broth
• Fontina cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Bring a large pot of water to boil
3. Spread the bread cubes evenly in a 9"x13" casserole dish or 2 quart ovenproof dish
4. If the cubes aren't dry, toast them quickly in the oven
5. Melt 3 tbsp of the butter and drizzle over the bread
6. Set aside
7. When the water boils, blanch the cabbage just until tender, about 2 minutes
8. Drain well
9. Spread cabbage over the bread
10. Melt the remaining 2-3 tbsp butter and stir in nutmeg, pepper, and salt
11. Pour the seasoned butter on the cabbage and bread
12. Pour the broth over everything and evenly spread the cheese on top
13. Bake until the cheese melts and starts to brown, 25-30 minutes
14. Serve immediately
15. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
