Ingredients:
• Catfish nuggets
• Egg
• Buttermilk
• White cornmeal
• Breadcrumbs
• Salt
• Paprika
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Cayenne
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Scramble the egg in a large bowl then whisk in the buttermilk follow by the rest of the ingredients, except fish
2. Heat 2-3 inches of oil in a large frying pan until hot
3. Dip the nuggets into the batter so they are coated then gently drop into the hot oil
4. Fry for 5-7 minutes, until golden brown & crispy
5. Repeat until all the fish is fried
6. Place the fried fish onto a paper towel lined plate to drain excess oil<|endoftext|>
