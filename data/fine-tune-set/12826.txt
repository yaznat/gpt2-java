What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Bbq Steak Sandwiches With A Rainbow Of Peppers

Ingredients:
• Round steaks
• Ketchup
• Barbecue sauce
• Green pepper
• Oregano
• Celery seed
• Pepsi
• Red pepper
• French baguettes
• Honey mustard
• Apple juice
• Black pepper
• Red pepper flakes
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the honey mustard, apple juice, and then add 3 tablespoons olive oil
2. Add pepper, red pepper flakes, and dried oregano
3. In a large skillet, heat the remaining olive oil over medium / high heat
4. Add the steak cubes to the skillet, and cook turning only once, leaving hardly any pink, and transfer to a plate
5. Leave in skillet, and stir in ketchup, barbecue sauce, and pepsi
6. Saute green and red peppers for 2-3 minutes or until crisp tender
7. Toast celery seeds for 1-2 minutes
8. Serve on banquettes<|endoftext|>
