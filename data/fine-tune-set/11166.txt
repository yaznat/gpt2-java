Ingredients:
• Hoagie rolls
• Italian dressing
• Deli roast beef
• Deli turkey
• Deli ham
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Tomatoes
• Onion

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Brush the cut sides of the rolls with italian dressing
3. Layer the bottom halves with meats, cheeses, onion and tomato
4. Replace hoagie tops
5. Wrap each sandwich in foil, tightly
6. Bake 12-15 minutes or until cheese is melted
7. Remove foil and serve<|endoftext|>
