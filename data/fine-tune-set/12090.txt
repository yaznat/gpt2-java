Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• Green onion
• Garlic clove
• Broccoli
• Low fat cottage cheese
• Eggs
• Milk
• Bisquick
• Salt and pepper
• Goat cheese
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Spray a 9-inch pie plate with cooking spray
2. Set aside
3. Spray a non-stick skillet with cooking spray
4. Heat over medium heat
5. Saut green onions and garlic for 5 minutes or until transparent
6. Stir in broccoli
7. Layer broccoli mixture and cottage cheese in pie plate
8. Set aside
9. Whisk eggs with milk and baking mix
10. Season with salt and pepper
11. Pour over cottage cheese in pie plate
12. Sprinkle top with goat cheese and parmesan cheese
13. Bake in a 350f oven for 25 minutes or until a knife inserted near the centre comes out clean<|endoftext|>
