Generate a recipe for a popsicle.<|endoftext|>Red White And Blue Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Vanilla yogurt
• Red food coloring
• Food coloring
• Blue food coloring
• Wax paper
• Sugar ice cream cones
• Wooden popsicle sticks

Steps:
1. To make a half dozen pops, tint 1 / 2 cup of the yogurt with red food coloring and 1 1 / 2 cups with blue food coloring
2. Leave the rest plain
3. Make waxed paper cone liners by folding six 12-inch square sheets into triangles, wrapping each around a sugar cone, and using tape to hold the shape
4. Then place the paper cones inside the sugar cones
5. Fill each cone with 1 tablespoon red yogurt, followed by 2 tablespoons white yogurt, then 3 tablespoons blue yogurt
6. Insert a popsicle stick and freeze the pops until firm, about 4 hours, using the cone box with six circles cut out to keep them upright<|endoftext|>
