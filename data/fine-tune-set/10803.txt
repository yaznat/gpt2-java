What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Cup Salad

Ingredients:
• Salad greens
• Coconut
• Mandarin orange segments
• Crushed pineapple
• Sour cream
• Miniature marshmallow

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except marshmallows
2. Chill add marshmallows right before serving
3. Also good with chopped pecans added!<|endoftext|>
