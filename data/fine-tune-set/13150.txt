What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Broiled Fish With Cheese

Ingredients:
• White fish fillets
• Butter
• Cheddar cheese
• Mustard
• Catsup
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Brush fish w / butter and broil 5-7 minutes until flaky
2. Combine remaining ingredients and spoon on fish
3. Broil 2-4 minutes until cheese is bubbly and lightly browned<|endoftext|>
