Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Chuck roast
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Red onion
• Garlic cloves
• Jalapeno
• Dried ancho chile powder
• Ground pasilla chile powder
• Ground cumin
• Dark beer
• Chicken stock
• Chopped tomatoes
• Chipotle chile in adobo
• Honey
• Black beans
• Fresh lime juice
• Creme fraiche
• Kosher salt
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Heat two tablespoon of oil in a large pot with a lid over hight heat
2. Salt and pepper the beef and layer half in the pot
3. Brown on all sides
4. Remove to a plate and repeat with the remaining oil and meat
5. Remove all but two tablespoons of fat from the pot
6. Cook onions until soft
7. Add garlic and jalapeno, cook for one minute
8. Add ancho powder, pasilla powder, and cumin
9. Cook two minutes
10. Add beer and cook until reduced by half
11. Return beef to pot, add rest of ingredients except beans and lime juice
12. Bring to a boil
13. Reduce heat to medium, cover, and simmer for 45 minutes
14. For the toasted cumin crema: whisk together creme fraiche, cumin, kosher salt and black pepper
15. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld
16. While crema is chilling, add beans to chili pot and continue cooking till the meat is fork-tender, 20 to 30 minutes
17. Remove from heat
18. Add lime juice and salt and pepper
19. Serve with a spoonful of toasted cumin crema<|endoftext|>
