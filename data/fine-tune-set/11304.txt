Ingredients:
• Imitation crabmeat
• Seedless cucumber
• Avocado
• Cooked rice
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Using a vidalia chopper, dice up cucumber and imitation crab
2. Peel avocado and dice by hand because it's too mushy to go through the chopper
3. Add avocado to the cucumber and crab mixture
4. Add rice and mix well
5. Add salt and pepper to taste
6. Chill at least one hour<|endoftext|>
