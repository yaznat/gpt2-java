What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Creamy Tofu Dressing

Ingredients:
• Silken tofu
• Balsamic vinegar
• Garlic
• Shallot
• Dijon mustard
• Water
• Olive oil
• Dill
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Place the tofu, vinegar, garlic, shallot, mustard, herbs into food processor with metal blade and blend till pureed
2. Combine the water to prcessor while machine is still running, follow with the oil salt and pepper
3. Serve immediately on your favorite salad or grilled meat<|endoftext|>
