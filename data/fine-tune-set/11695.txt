Ingredients:
• Egg
• Olive oil
• Flax seeds
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Skim milk
• Plain yogurt
• Water
• Vanilla extract
• Quick oatmeal
• Whole wheat flour

Steps:
1. Beat the egg with milk, sugar, youghurt, olive oil and water
2. Combine all dry ingredients in a bowl
3. Mix well
4. Add the dry mixture into the liquid and beat until smooth
5. Let stand for about 15 minute the batter must have a consistency of thin porridge
6. Add a table spoon of water if too thick
7. Brush a small non-stick pan with the greasing oil or spray with cooking spray
8. Spoon pancakes with a small ladle and cook on medium-high
9. Turn over when surface gets bubbly
10. No need to respray if your pan is truly non-stick
11. This makes 6 pancakes that are fluffy, tender and yummy
12. 117 cal / pancake
13. Serve with fruits, low-cal youghurt or eggs
14. I love dipping them into a soft-boiled egg
15. Optional: if you intend to serve them with fruits like apples or bananas, add a pinch of cinnamon and nutmeg into the batter
