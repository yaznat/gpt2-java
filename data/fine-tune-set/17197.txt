What's a recipe for tea?<|endoftext|>Amish Mennonite Meadow Tea For Freezing

Ingredients:
• Water
• Tea
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Boil 4 quarts of water
2. Add 1 quart tea stems and leaves
3. Bring to a boil
4. Turn off the heat, and steep for 1 / 2- 1 hour, pressing tea down in water periodically
5. Remove leaves and stems
6. Strain through cheesecloth
7. Add 4-6 cups sugar
8. Stir until dissolved
9. Place in freezer boxes, cool completely and freeze
10. To prepare: mix 1 concentrate plus 2-3 concentrate boxes full of water and serve!<|endoftext|>
