What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Curried Rice Pilaf With Lamb

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Shoulder lamb chops
• Salt and pepper
• Long grain rice
• Carrot
• Raisins
• Onions
• Salt
• Curry powder
• Slivered almonds
• Chicken broth

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease a shallow 2-quart baking dish
3. In a large skillet, brown chops in butter
4. Remove chops
5. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
6. Add rice to pan drippings
7. Cook, stirring constantly, until grains turn golden brown
8. Stir in remaining ingredients
9. Pour into prepared dish
10. Arrange chops on top
11. Bake covered at 350 degrees for 40 to 50 minutes or until rice and chops are tender and liquid is absorbed<|endoftext|>
