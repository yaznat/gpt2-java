Ingredients:
• Dried apricot
• Boiling water
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Egg
• Sugar
• Canola oil
• Orange juice
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Place apricots in a bowl and cover with boiling water
2. Soak for 1 / 2 hour
3. Preheat oven to 350f coat a 9 x 5 inch loaf pan with non-stick cooking spray
4. Drain apricots, reserving 1 / 4 cup of the soaking water
5. Finely chop apricots
6. Set aside
7. Sift together dry ingredients
8. Set aside
9. Cream together the egg, sugar and oil
10. Add flour mixture to egg mixture alternately with orange juice and reserved soaking water
11. Stir in apricots and pecans
12. Spread batter in loaf pan and bake 1-1 1 / 4 hours, or until wooden pick inserted into center of bread comes out clean
13. Turn out onto wire rack and cool<|endoftext|>
