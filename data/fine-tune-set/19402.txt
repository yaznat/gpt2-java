Give me a donut recipe.<|endoftext|>Old Fashion Sour Cream Donuts

Ingredients:
• Cake flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground nutmeg
• Sugar
• Shortening
• Egg yolk
• Sour cream
• Canola oil
• Confectioners' sugar
• Light corn syrup
• Vanilla extract
• Hot water

Steps:
1. Sift your dry ingredients in a medium size bowl, set aside
2. In another bowl, mix your wet ingredients
3. Add your dry ingredients into the wet, mix 1 / 3 of the flour mixture, stir well
4. Add another 1 / 3, mix well and so forth
5. Refrigerate for an hour
6. Flour your surface and roll your dough out to 1 / 2" thick
7. Using a biscut cutter or glass, cut out doughnuts
8. Use a smaller cutter to cut out the holes
9. If you don't have a smaller one, a pill bottle works well too to cut out the holes
10. Use your imagination, you will find something
11. Heat about 2" of oil over low-medium heat to 325, use a candy thermometer for this
12. Don't have one? get one! it can make all the difference in how a recipe turns out
13. When oil has reached 325 keep an eye on it! once you have started heating your oil, do not leave it, ever! bad things can happen, very bad!
14. Fry your donuts until browned on each side, drain on wire rack or paper towels
15. Dip in your glaze and give a few minutes for the glaze to set up
16. For the glaze: mix everything in the order it's listed, add more water for a thinner glaze<|endoftext|>
