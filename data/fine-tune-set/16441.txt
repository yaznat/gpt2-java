Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast half
• Bell peppers
• Onion
• Barbecue sauce
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Variation 1:
2. Place chicken, peppers and onion slices on greased grill over medium-hot coals
3. Grill 18 to 20 minutes or until chicken is cooked through, turning and brushing occasionally with barbecue sauce
4. Cut chicken, peppers and onions into thin slices
5. Place mixture on center of each tortilla
6. Roll up
7. Variation 2:
8. Cut chicken into strips
9. Heat 1 tablespoons oil in large skillet on medium-high heat
10. Add chicken to skillet
11. Cook and stir 8 minutes or until chicken is cooked through
12. Add green peppers and onions, cut into strips, and barbecue sauce
13. Cover
14. Cook until vegetables are tender, stirring occasionally
15. Divide mixture among tortillas
16. Roll up<|endoftext|>
