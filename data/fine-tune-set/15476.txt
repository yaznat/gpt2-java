Ingredients:
• Hamburger
• Hamburger buns
• Butter
• Kosher salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Heat griddle or cast iron pan on medium high heat
2. Without disturbing grind marks, carefully divide hamburger into 6 sections
3. Place hamburger sections on hot griddle
4. Press hamburgers down onto griddle with a heavy spatula
5. Use pressure
6. This causes the hamburgers to stick to the griddle and prevents shrinkage
7. Sprinkle tops with salt and pepper and any other spices you may want to use
8. When the edges look cooked and juices rise to the top, turn over but dont press down again
9. When bottoms are browned, and burger is to desired doneness, remove from griddle and set aside
10. Cover with foil or wax paper
11. Spread cut sides of buns lightly with butter
12. Place butter side down on griddle until lightly browned
13. Place burgers on buns and top with your favorite toppings<|endoftext|>
