What's a recipe for ice cream?<|endoftext|>Caramelized Pears With Dulce De Leche Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Bartlett pears
• Fresh lemon juice
• Vanilla extract
• Unsalted butter
• Sugar
• Dulce de leche ice cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. Place pears in large bowl
3. Toss with lemon juice and vanilla
4. Melt butter in large ovenproof skillet over med-high heat
5. Add sugar
6. Shake skillet to spread sugar evenly
7. Place pears, cut side down in skillet
8. Drizzle with any remaining lemon juice mixture from bowl
9. Cook until sugar begins to dissolve and mixture bubbles all over, shaking pan often, about 5 minute
10. Transfer skillet to oven
11. Bake until pears are soft and juices are golden, about 15 minute serve pears with juices from skillet and ice cream<|endoftext|>
