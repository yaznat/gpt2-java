What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Artichoke Chicken Pasta W Sun Dried Tomatoes

Ingredients:
• Bow tie pasta
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Pepper
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Water-packed artichoke hearts
• Sun-dried tomatoes packed in oil
• Sliced ripe olives
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Meanwhile, sprinkle chicken with the oregano, salt and pepper
3. In a large skillet, saute chicken in oil until no longer pink
4. Add garlic
5. Saute 1 minute longer
6. Stir in the artichokes, tomatoes and olives
7. Heat through
8. Drain pasta
9. Toss with chicken mixture
10. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese
11. Yield: 6 servings<|endoftext|>
