What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Broccoli With Pasta In A Savory Curry And Mustard Sauce

Ingredients:
• Rotini pasta
• Olive oil
• Curry powder
• Broccoli florets
• Red pepper
• Plain yogurt
• Chicken broth
• All-purpose flour
• Prepared mustard
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions, except omit salt
2. Meanwhile, in a large skillet add oil and curry powder heat over medium heat, stirring constantly, for 1 minute
3. Add broccoli and red pepper and stir fry 3-4 minutes or until crisp-tender
4. For sauce: in a large saucepan stir together yogurt, chicken broth, flour, mustard and salt
5. Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly, then cook and stir about 1 more minute
6. Add pasta and broccoli mixture to sauce and toss to coat well
7. Heat over low-medium heat just until heated through
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
