What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>3 Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Canned green beans
• Yellow wax beans
• Red kidney beans
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Sugar
• White vinegar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Place beans, pepper and onions in a large bowl
2. Place sugar, vinegar, salt, pepper, and oil in a seperate bowl and stir to combine
3. Pour over vegetables and stir to combine
4. Regrigerate 8 hours or longer before serving<|endoftext|>
