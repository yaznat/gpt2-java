Ingredients:
• Baked beans
• Coarse grain mustard
• Barbecue sauce
• Ham
• Cheese
• Prepared pizza crust

Steps:
1. Mix the baked beans with mustard & bbq sauce
2. Spread beans over pizza bases & top with ham
3. Sprinkle with cheese and bake at 220
4. C for 15-20 mins or until base is golden & cheese melted<|endoftext|>
