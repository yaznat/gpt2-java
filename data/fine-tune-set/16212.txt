Give me a quesadilla recipe.<|endoftext|>Jillyray S Cheese Quesadillas With Salsa

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Canola oil
• Sour cream
• Mild salsa

Steps:
1. Lay the tortilla flat on the cookie sheet
2. Sprinkle the cheese on one half of the tortilla
3. Try not to get it too close to the edge
4. Fold the tortilla in half over the cheese
5. Over medium heat, heat skillet and oil
6. With a spatula, gently transfer the tortilla to the pan, making sure none of the cheese falls out
7. Let the tortilla cook for about 2 minutes, until it is lightly brown
8. Take the spatula and gently lift the edge so you can check
9. When one side is brown, turn the tortilla over and cook the other side until it's brown and the cheese is all gooey
10. Use the spatula to put the quesadilla on the plate
11. Top it with sour cream and salsa
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
