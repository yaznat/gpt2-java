Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Balsamic vinegar
• Sugar
• Flour tortillas
• Fontina cheese
• Goat cheese

Steps:
1. Caramelized onions: heat the olive oil in a large skillet and add the onion
2. Cook for 10 minutes or until tender
3. Add the vinegar and sugar
4. Cook for 3 minutes or until sugar is dissolved and the mixture is light brown
5. Remove from the heat
6. Place 3 tortillas on a baking sheet
7. Cover each tortilla with fontina cheese, caramelized onion and goat cheese
8. Top each with a second tortilla
9. Brush the tops with olive oil
10. Bake at 350f for 8-10 minutes or until the tops are golden brown and the cheese is melted
11. Repeat until all ingredients are used
12. Cut each quesadilla into 4 wedges
13. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
