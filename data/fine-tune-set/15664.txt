What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Campfire Taco Salad

Ingredients:
• Corn chips
• Chili
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Salsa
• Iceberg lettuce

Steps:
1. Cut tops off each bag of chips
2. Set aside
3. Heat chili in saucepan until completely heated through, stirring occasionally
4. Spoon about 2 tablespoons chili into each bag of chips
5. Top with cheese, sour cream, salsa and lettuce<|endoftext|>
