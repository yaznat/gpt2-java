What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Yellow cake mix
• Eggs
• Plain yogurt
• Apple pie filling
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Flour

Steps:
1. In large mixing bowl, combine cake mix, eggs and yogurt
2. Beat for 3 minutes
3. Spread into greased and floured 13x9 pan
4. Arrange pie filling over the batter
5. Combine cinnamon, sugar and flour
6. Sprinkle on top of cake
7. Bake at 350 for 40-45 minutes
8. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
