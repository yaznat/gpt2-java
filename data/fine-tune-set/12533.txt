What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Artichoke And Garbanzo Stew

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Salt
• Garlic cloves
• Red chili pepper flakes
• Carrots
• Roma tomatoes
• Artichoke hearts
• Garbanzo beans
• Reduced-sodium chicken broth
• Fresh sage
• Fresh lemon juice
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Set a 4-to 6 quart pot over medium high heat
2. Add oil, onion and salt
3. Stir often until onion becomes translucent, about 3 minutes
4. Add garlic and chile flakes
5. Stir until garlic softens, 1 to 2 minutes
6. Add carrots, tomatoes, artichoke hearts, garbanzos, and broth
7. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat
8. Cover and simmer until carrots are tender when pierced, about 15 minutes
9. Season with sage, lemon juice, salt and pepper to taste
10. For vegan / vegetarian do not use the chicken broth option<|endoftext|>
