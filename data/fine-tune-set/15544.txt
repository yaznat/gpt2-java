Give me a taco recipe.<|endoftext|>Arizona Taco Salad

Ingredients:
• Iceberg lettuce
• Ground beef
• Pinto beans
• Fritos corn chips
• Green onions
• Black olives
• Tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese
• Pace picante sauce
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Crumble-fry the ground beef and allow it to cool
2. Mix beef with beans and lettuce
3. Stick a long spoon in the picant sauce jar
4. Put remaining ingredients in individual bowls, with appropriate tongs, spoons, etc
5. For serving
6. Put a layer of fritos in your bowl or on your plate
7. Cover with beef-bean-lettuce mixture
8. Add other ingredients as desired
9. Apply the picant sauce to taste
10. Add hot sauce as required by your palate
11. Step aside, because the first ones through the line will be coming back for more!<|endoftext|>
