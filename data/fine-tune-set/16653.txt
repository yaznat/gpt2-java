Please give me a recipe for a smoothie.<|endoftext|>Berry Berry Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Low-fat blueberry yogurt
• Grape juice
• Frozen blueberries
• Frozen blackberries

Steps:
1. In a blender container, combine yogurt and juice
2. Blend to combine
3. Add berries and process until smooth and thick
4. Pour into glasses and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
