What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Apple Muffins

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Sugar substitute
• Skim milk
• Egg whites
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Apple
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Sift together dry ingredients
2. Add wet mixture until batter is lumpy
3. Pour into muffin pans and bake at 350 for 20 to 30 min until set
4. Let cool<|endoftext|>
