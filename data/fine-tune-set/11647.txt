What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Minute Spice Cake In Microwave

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Cocoa powder
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Ginger
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Clove
• Brown sugar
• Oil
• Molasses
• Water
• Egg

Steps:
1. Put the flour, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder, ginger, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves and brown sugar into a 4 cup microwaveable bowl
2. Mix well
3. Add oil, molasses, water and egg
4. Mix well
5. Microwave for 3 minutes
6. Cool slightly, and serve with ice cream, whipping cream, or raisin sauce<|endoftext|>
