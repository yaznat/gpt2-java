Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Shank Stew A La Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Brown ale
• Tomato paste
• Wondra flour
• Kosher salt
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Garlic
• Yellow onions
• Celery
• Baby red potatoes
• Button mushroom
• Baby carrots
• Beef shanks
• Lemons, zest of
• Lemons, juice of

Steps:
1. During hot weather move the crock pot into the shade on the patio
2. No need to work the ac more than necessary
3. In a mixing bowl combine ale and tomato paste, whisk'til well mixed
4. Whisk in the wondra, salt, red chilies, pepper, and garlic
5. Mix well
6. Pour into crock pot
7. Set heat on low
8. Stir in onions, celery, potatoes, mushrooms, and carrots
9. Work beef shanks down under the surface of the broth
10. Cover, cook on low for 9 hours, 'til beef is tender
11. Stir a bit of lemon zest and juice into each bowl before serving
12. This goes well with hot, buttered, corn sticks and several cold beers
13. May be eaten while alone but not recommended
14. It's much better with company<|endoftext|>
