Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Garlic cloves
• Lemon juice
• Soy sauce
• Ground black pepper
• Halibut steaks

Steps:
1. Preheat grill for medium-high heat
2. Place butter, brown sugar, garlic, lemon juice, soy sauce, and pepper in a small saucepan
3. Warm over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until sugar is completely dissolved
4. Lightly oil grill grate
5. Brush fish with brown sugar sauce, and place on grill
6. Cook for 5 minutes per side, or until fish can be easily flaked with a fork, basting with sauce
7. Discard remaining basting sauce
8. For extra sauce on the side, i usually double recipe for the sauce, and reserve some in a separate dish prior to basting the fish<|endoftext|>
