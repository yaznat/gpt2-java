Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Big Breakfast Apple Pancake

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Fresh lemon juice
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Eggs
• Milk
• Flour
• Salt
• Butter

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in a cast iron skillet, add the peeled, cored and sliced apples and saute until tender
2. Do not overcook
3. Beat the eggs and gradually add the milk and then the flour and salt
4. Pour this batter over the apples and return to the hot oven
5. Bake until puffed up and golden - maybe 25 minutes
6. Cut a wedge and serve with syrup and bacon and / or sausage<|endoftext|>
