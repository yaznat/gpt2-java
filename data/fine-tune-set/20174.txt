Ingredients:
• Cellophane noodles
• Beef top round steak
• Cornstarch
• Beef broth
• Cold water
• Steak sauce
• Sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Gingerroot
• Zucchini
• Onion
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Soak cellophane noodles 15 minutes in enough warm water to cover
2. Drain
3. Trim fat from beef steak
4. Cut beef with grain into 2-inch strips
5. Cut strips across grain into 1 / 8-inch slices
6. Toss beef and 1 teaspoon cornstarch
7. Mix broth, water, 1 tablespoon cornstarch, the steak sauce and sugar
8. Heat wok or 12-inch skillet until very hot
9. Add 1 tablespoon oil to wok
10. Rotate wok to coat sides
11. Add beef and gingerroot
12. Stir-fry about 4 minutes or until vegetables or until vegetables are crisp-tender
13. Stir in broth mixture
14. Cook and stir about 1 minute or until thickened
15. Stir in beef mixture, cellophane noodles and tomatoes
16. Cook about 1 minute or until heated through
17. 4 servings<|endoftext|>
