Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Breakfast Burrito With Green Beans

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Potato
• Eggs
• French style green bean
• Cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Saute onion in butter until translucent
2. Dice cooked potato into small pieces
3. Add to onion
4. Take out of pan
5. Scramble eggs
6. Pour into pan
7. Cook slow
8. Add drained green beans
9. Add onion mixture
10. Heat tortillas
11. Divide egg mixture onto tortilla
12. Make burrito
13. Serve with salsa
14. Add cheese while cooking if you want<|endoftext|>
