Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sugar
• Oleo
• Flour
• Vanilla
• Nutmeg
• Coconut

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients and beat in blender for 10 seconds
2. Pour into buttered 9-inch deep pie pan
3. Bake at 350 for 1 hour or until knife comes out clean
4. Makes its own crust!<|endoftext|>
