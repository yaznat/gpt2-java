Ingredients:
• Chorizo sausage
• Red onion
• Garlic cloves
• Green chilies
• Cilantro leaf
• Egg substitute
• Corn tortillas
• Low-fat cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Spray a skillet with some cooking spray to ensure that nothing sticks
2. Break the chorizo into small pieces and cook until they're done
3. There's usually a bit of grease, so i drain that right away
4. Add in the onion, garlic, chiles, and cilantro
5. Saute until the onions are soft, which is usually about a minute or two
6. Add in the egg subsitute and scramble everything together
7. I put the tortillas in the toaster oven to warm then up
8. Once they're done put the egg mixute in them, towards the center
