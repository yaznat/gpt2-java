Ingredients:
• Egg substitute
• Fat free cheese
• Salsa
• Tortillas
• Bacon bits

Steps:
1. Spray pan with nonstick cooking spray
2. Scramble the egg beaters and bacos
3. When almost finished tear the slice of cheese into pieces and mix into the eggs
4. Place mixture into a tortilla and add salsa before folding it up<|endoftext|>
