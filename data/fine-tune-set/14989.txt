What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>My Version Of Nasi Goreng Indonesian Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Jasmine rice
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Celery
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Tomato ketchup
• Shrimp paste
• Salt and pepper
• Ketjap manis
• Sambal oelek

Steps:
1. Pour a little oil in a large skillet / wok
2. Fry chicken / bacon in skillet on medium heat until cooked thoroughly
3. Add all other ingredients except rice to the skillet
4. Add sambal if you want it hot
5. Mix and simmer until shrimp paste dissolves into mixture and everything is heated through
6. Add rice to mixture and mix it in
7. Heat until rice is heated
8. Add more ketchup to taste
9. There is usually enough ketchup when the rice is light brown from its color
10. To make omelet garnish: put olive oil in small pan
11. Whip up 2 or 3 eggs
12. Pour over olive oil on low-medium heat
13. Cook until middle sets, then turn the heat off and cover the pan to let the steam finish the edges
14. Cut into thin strips and serve over rice<|endoftext|>
