Give me a cake recipe.<|endoftext|>35 Second Chocolate Cake

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Baking powder
• Instant coffee crystals
• Salt
• Milk
• Olive oil
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Spray a coffee cup or ramekin with vegetable cooking spray
2. In the cup combine the flour, splenda, cocoa powder, baking powder, coffee crystals and salt until no lumps remain
3. Stir in the milk, olive oil, and vanilla until smooth
4. Bake in the microwave on high for 35 seconds
5. You may need to add 5 more seconds but dont be tempted to overcook or it will be rubbery! it should be moist on the bottom and will continue cooking as it sets
6. Serve with chocolate saucea sprinkle of powdered sugarwhipped cream or eat plain as a brownie<|endoftext|>
