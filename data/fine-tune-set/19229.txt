Give me a ice cream recipe.<|endoftext|>Chocolate Ice Cream Almost

Ingredients:
• Egg beaters egg substitute
• Part-skim ricotta cheese
• Milk
• Cocoa powder
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients but ice in the blender
2. Add ice and blend to a soft-serve consistency
3. You can add sugar free coffee syrups to this as well - caramel and hazelnut are great with chocolate
4. You can also use frozen raspberries, sugar free raspberry syrup and a bit less ice instead of chocolate!<|endoftext|>
