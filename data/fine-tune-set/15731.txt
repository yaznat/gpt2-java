Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• Taco seasoning mix
• Brown sugar
• Green chilies
• Frozen corn
• Mild enchilada sauce
• Green onions
• Taco shells
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Olive
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Spray a 3-4 quart slow cooker with cooking spray
2. Place chicken thighs in cooker
3. Sprinkle with taco seasoning mix and brown sugar
4. Toss to coat
5. Mix in green chiles, corn and / 2 cup of the enchilada sauce
6. Refrigerate remaining enchilada sauce
7. Cover and cook on low heat setting for 6-7 hours
8. Place chicken on cutting board
9. Use 2 forks to pull chicken into shreds
10. Return chicken to cooker
11. Stir in green onions
12. Cover and cook on low heat setting 15 minutes
13. Heat remaining enchilada sauce
14. Serve chicken mixture in taco shells with toppings and warm enchilada sauce<|endoftext|>
