Ingredients:
• Chocolate wafers
• Butter
• Pecans
• Semisweet chocolate
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Whipping cream

Steps:
1. Crust: preheat oven to 325
2. Combine chocolate crumbs and butter
3. Press into a 9" springform pan
4. Sprinkle nuts over the crust
5. Bake for 10 minutes
6. Chocolate mousse: melt chocolate chips and set aside to cool
7. Blend cream cheese, sugar and vanilla
8. Beat egg yolks, add the to cream cheese and stir well
9. Mix in cooled chocolate
10. Beat egg whites until soft peaks form
11. Add sugar slowly and beat until stiff
12. Fold into chocolate mixture
13. Whip cream and fold into the chocolate mousse
14. Pour into the springform pan
15. Cover and freeze overnight
16. To serve: remove from the freezer and chill for 5 hours before serving
17. Optional garnishes: chocolate curls / sprinkles
