Ingredients:
• Butter
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour
• Rice krispies
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Blend the first three ingredients well
2. Add the rice krispies
3. Roll into small balls, about 1 / 2 teaspoon
4. Flatten balls out on cookie sheet
5. Bake in a 350 f oven for 12 to 15 minutes
6. Cayenne pepper may be added along with the first three ingredients for added zest<|endoftext|>
