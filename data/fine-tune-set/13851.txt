What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Awesome Bbq Pork Chops And Beans

Ingredients:
• Pork and beans
• Brown sugar
• Barbecue sauce
• Pork chops
• Ketchup
• Mustard

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Spray a large glass dish with oil spray to prevent sticking
3. In the bottom of the dish, mix together pork and beans, brown sugar, and bbq sauce
4. Place pork chops on top of the pork and bean mixture
5. Sprinkle brownsugar on all chops making sure to cover entire chop
6. Pour mustard and ketchup over top of chops making sure to cover entire chops
7. Bake for 30-45 minutes<|endoftext|>
