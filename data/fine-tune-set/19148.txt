Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Butter Pecan Sauce For Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Whipping cream
• Butter
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In a heavy saucepan, combine dry ingredients, gradually stirring in cream until smooth
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly
3. Cook and stir 2-3 minutes or until slightly thickened
4. Remove from heat, stir in butter until melted
5. Add pecans
6. Serve warm over ice cream<|endoftext|>
