Ingredients:
• Applesauce
• Granulated sugar
• Pure vanilla extract
• Dried breadcrumbs
• Margarine
• Whipping cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees f, then butter a 1 1 / 2-quart ovenproof dish
2. In a bowl, combine applesauce, sugar & vanilla extract
3. In the prepared baking dish, lay down alternate layers of breadcrumbs & applesauce, ending with breadcrumbs
4. Pour melted margarine over the top & bake 25-30 minutes or until golden brown
5. Top with dollops of whipped cream & serve hot<|endoftext|>
