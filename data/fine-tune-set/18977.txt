What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Easy Thai Coconut Custard

Ingredients:
• Coconut milk
• Eggs
• Brown sugar
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Beat the eggs together, 1 at a time
3. Gradually add sugar, milk and salt
4. Beat well
5. In double broiler, stirring constantly, cook mixture until soft and texture resembles scrambled eggs
6. Pour mixture in a small casserole dish and bake 1 / 2 hour
7. When baked, brown custard under broiler<|endoftext|>
