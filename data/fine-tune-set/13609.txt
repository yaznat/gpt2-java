What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Beef Pouch Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Lean ground beef
• Green bell pepper
• Hickory flavored barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Cut 4 sheets, 12 inches square, of heavy duty aluminum foil
2. Heat oven to 450 degrees
3. In bowl, mix all ingredients
4. Divide equally among foil sheets, placing mixture on right halves of foil squares
5. To seal each pouch, fold left side of foil over mixture
6. Fold in right edge 1 / 2 inch
7. Fold again
8. Fold top and bottom edges the same way, leaving room for heat to circulate inside pouch
9. Place on baking sheet and bake in center of oven 35 minutes
10. To open pouches, with scissors, cut a cross in the top of each, then pull back points, being careful as steam is released<|endoftext|>
