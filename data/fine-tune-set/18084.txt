Ingredients:
• Maggi 2-minute noodles
• Eggs
• Plain flour
• Baking powder
• Corn kernels
• Chives
• Fresh parsley
• Milk
• Oil

Steps:
1. Lightly break the noodles into a bowl, pour over boiling water to cover and allow to stand for 5 minutes, drain noodles into a colander and return to the bowl
2. Stir in the beaten eggs, flour, corn kernels, chives, parsley, flavour sachet and milk
3. Heat a non stick pan, brush with oil, and drop spoonfuls of the mixture into the pan and cook until golden, turn over and cook until set<|endoftext|>
