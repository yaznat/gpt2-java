Ingredients:
• Cake flour
• Butter
• Apricot jam
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Milk
• Bicarbonate of soda
• Water
• White vinegar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 160f
2. Mix together the flour, sugar and butter and bi-carb
3. In separate bowl beat together milk, apricot jam and eggs
4. Lastly add the milk
5. Mix to the flour mix
6. Keep the dough aside and finish the following sauce:
7. Bring to boil in saucepan or microwave oven the water sugar and vinegar
8. Put sauce in ovenproof deep glass dish
9. Pour the dough into the sauce and bake in preheated oven for about 45 minutes or until light brown in colour
10. Optional: serve with custard<|endoftext|>
