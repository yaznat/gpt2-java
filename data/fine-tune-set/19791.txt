What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Delicious Oven Fries

Ingredients:
• Yukon gold potatoes
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Seasoning salt
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees
2. Cut potatoes lengthwise and then in half
3. Put the potatoes and olive oil in a ziplic bag and mix
4. Mix all the seasonings together
5. Pour the seasonings in the bag and mix well
6. Drizzle olive oil or cooking spray on a pan
7. Evenly spread the fries on the pan
8. Bake for 45 minutes or until desired crispness
9. Turn over several times to brown evenly
10. Add more seasoning as desired<|endoftext|>
