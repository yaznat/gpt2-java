Give me a lamb recipe.<|endoftext|>Balinese Lamb Chops

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Brown onion
• Crunchy peanut butter
• Sweet chili sauce
• Ketjap manis
• Light coconut milk
• Lamb chops
• Egg noodles
• Baby bok choy

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 200c
2. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add oil
3. Add onion and cook, stirring, for 3 minutes or until soft
4. Add peanut butter, sweet chilli, ketjap manis and coconut milk
5. Stir until well combined and bring to the boil
6. Arrange chops in a single layer in a casserole dish
7. Pour over sauce and coat the chops well
8. Bake, uncovered, for 35 minutes or until cooked through<|endoftext|>
