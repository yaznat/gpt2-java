Ingredients:
• Tomato sauce
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Brown sugar
• Vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dry mustard
• Chili powder
• Celery seed
• Garlic
• Pork
• French rolls

Steps:
1. Combine tomato sauce, onion, green pepper and sugar in a medium saucepan
2. Stir in vinegar, worcestershire, mustard, chili powder, celery seeds and garlic
3. Bring to a boil and reduce heat
4. Add meat, simmer covered until meat is heated through
5. Spoon mixture on the bottom half of rolls, cover with top halves<|endoftext|>
