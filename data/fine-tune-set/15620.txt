Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning mix
• Tomato sauce
• Cheddar cheese
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Beat cream cheese, sour cream and mayonnaise until smooth
2. Spread on a 12-14" pizza pan
3. Refrigerate 1 hour
4. Brown beef over meadium heat
5. Drain
6. Add taco seasoning and tomato sauce
7. Cook and stir 5 minutes
8. Cool completely
9. Spread over cream cheese layer
10. Refrigerate
11. Just before serving, sprinkle with lettuce, onion, tomato and cheese
12. Serve with chips<|endoftext|>
