Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>100 Whole Wheat Wholesome Muffin Mix

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Oatmeal
• Powdered milk
• Sugar
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Powdered eggs
• Fruit
• Oil
• Water
• Vanilla
• Raisins
• Egg
• Cinnamon
• Cocoa powder
• Molasses

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients together
2. For a bulk mix: double or quadruple the mix
3. Put mix in a ziploc bag
4. Label mix bag with cooking instructions
5. To make 1 batch of muffins measure out 2 cups of dry mix
6. Preheat oven to 350f
7. Mix all ingredients together
8. For banana chips or apple slices, crush and pre soak in 1 / 4 c more water let fruit stand until softened before baking
9. Spray oil on muffin tins
10. Fill muffin tins with batter
11. Bake at 350f until golden 10-15 minutes<|endoftext|>
