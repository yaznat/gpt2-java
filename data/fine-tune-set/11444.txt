Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Breakfast Bread

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Slivered almonds

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients, except almonds, in a large bowl
2. Beat 2 minutes at medium speed
3. Spoon batter into 2 greased and floured loaf pans
4. Sprinkle each loaf with 1 / 4 cup almonds
5. Bake at 350f for 1 hour or until test done with toothpick
6. Cool in pans for 10 minutes, remove from pans and cool completely<|endoftext|>
