Ingredients:
• Light rum
• Light cream
• Superfine sugar
• Club soda

Steps:
1. In shaker fill with ice, blend rum, cream and sugar
2. Shake to blend
3. Strain into highball glass with two ice cubes
4. Fill glass with club soda
5. Stir<|endoftext|>
