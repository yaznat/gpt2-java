Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Beef rump
• Onion
• Ginger
• Garlic clove
• Mild curry paste
• Coconut milk
• Yellow capsicum
• Green beans

Steps:
1. Heat half of the oil in wok
2. Stir fry beef, in batches, til browned all over
3. Heat remaining oil in wok, stir fry onion til softened
4. Add ginger, garlic and curry paste, stir fry til fragrant
5. Stir in coconut milk
6. Bring to a boil
7. Return beef to wok with capsicum and beans
8. Stry fry until vegetables are just tender<|endoftext|>
