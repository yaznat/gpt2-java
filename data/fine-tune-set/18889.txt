Ingredients:
• Refrigerated pie crusts
• Butter
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Vanilla extract
• Semisweet chocolate morsels

Steps:
1. Fit pie crust into a 9-inch deep-dish pie plate
2. Fold edges under and crimp
3. Bake at 400 degrees for 6 minutes
4. Cool crust on a wire rack
5. Reduce oven temperature to 350 degrees
6. Meanwhile, beat butter, sugar, and flour and medium speed with an electric mixer until blended
7. Add eggs, buttermilk, and vanilla
8. Beat well
9. Fold in chocolate morsels
10. Pour into prepared crust
11. Bake at 350 for 1 hour until just set and lightly browned
12. Cool completely before serving<|endoftext|>
