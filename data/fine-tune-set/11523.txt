What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Anne S Banana Bread

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Shortening
• Sugar
• Banana
• Vinegar
• Milk
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Sift together, flour, soda& salt
2. Cream shortening, blend in sugar, add eggs, one at a time and beat until fluffy
3. Add flour mixture alternately with bananas and liquid
4. Beat well
5. Add 1 / 2 c chopped nuts if desired
6. Place in greased 9x5x3 loaf pan
7. Bake 350 for 60-70 minutes
8. Remove from pan to cool<|endoftext|>
