Please give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>1898 Potato Dumplings

Ingredients:
• Mashed potatoes
• Cooked ham
• Egg
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Ham fat

Steps:
1. Set a large pot of salted water on to boil
2. Mix all the ingredients together well, except the fat
3. You should have a soft but moldable dough
4. You may need to mix in a little more flour
5. Divide the dough into 4 equal parts
6. Mold into flattish, rectangular cakes, about 2" thick
7. Gently place the cakes in the boiling water and simmer for 1 / 2 hour
8. Drain and let cool
9. It will fall apart if you try to slice it before it is cool
10. Cut in 1 / 2" thick slices and fry in bacon or ham fat, over medium heat, until nicely browned, on both sides<|endoftext|>
