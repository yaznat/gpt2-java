Ingredients:
• Pork loin chops
• Tomato paste
• Dried ancho chile powder
• Adobo seasoning
• Ground cumin
• Garlic powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Ground cloves
• Fresh lime juice
• Oil
• Water
• White tequila
• Lime juice

Steps:
1. Mix together ingredients for seasoning paste in a small bowl
2. Spread paste on both sides of pork chops and allow to rest for at least 30 minutes prior to cooking
3. Prepare grill
4. Oil grill rack before placing on fire
5. Mix together ingredients for baste, combining excess paste with ingredients for baste
6. Set aside
7. Grill chops on medium-hot coals or heat for 5-8 minutes
8. Turn chops and grill for 4-6 minutes more, basting with grill baste
9. Turn chops and cook 2-3 minutes more, basting the other side
10. Remove from heat and allow to rest 5 minutes or so before serving<|endoftext|>
