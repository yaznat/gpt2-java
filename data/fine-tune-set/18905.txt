Ingredients:
• Coconut milk
• Pumpkin
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Salt
• Coconut extract
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Mix 1 / 3 cup sugar & 1 / 2 teaspoon cinnamon - then set aside
3. Beat coconut milk, pumpkin, sugar, eggs, salt, extract and nutmeg until well mixed
4. Fill 6 custard cups with 2 / 3 cup of pumpkin mixture
5. Lightly sprinkle top with cinnamon sugar
6. Make a water bath by filling a 9" x 13" baking dish with 4 cups of hot water
7. Place each filled custard cup in baking dish
8. Make sure water comes to level of custard
9. Bake for 35 - 40 minutes, or until a knife inserted near the center of the custard comes out clean
10. Chill custard at least 2 hours or overnight
11. Before serving - sprinkle each custard with a teaspoon of sugar
12. Melt sugar with a torch or under a broiler until just golden<|endoftext|>
