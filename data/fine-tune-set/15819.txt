Give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Bountiful Baked Burritos

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Diced tomatoes
• Corn
• Black beans
• Black olives
• Sour cream
• Jalapeno
• Cilantro
• Burrito-size flour tortillas
• Salsa
• Cheese

Steps:
1. In a large sized skillet, cook chicken
2. Open cans and drain all items except the tomatoes
3. After chicken is cooked, add tomatoes, corn, beans, olives, jalapenos, cilantro, cup salsa, and cup sour cream
4. Mix so sour cream is dispersed and let simmer for 10 minutes or until ingredients are heated thoroughly
5. Then w / a slotted spoon, divide skillet mixture and cheese into 6 burrito tortillas
6. Wrap burritos, and place into a 13 x 9 baking dish sprayed with cooking spray
7. Pour remaining liquid from skillet over burritos, top with cheese
8. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 20 minutes or until cheese is melted and tortillas have crisped up a bit
9. Serve and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
