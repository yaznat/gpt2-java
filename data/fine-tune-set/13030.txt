What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Fish With Toasted Sesame Seeds

Ingredients:
• Orange juice
• Brown sugar
• Ketchup
• Soy sauce
• Lemon juice
• Vegetable oil
• Black pepper
• Fish fillets
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Place the orange juice, brown sugar, ketchup, soy sauce, lemon juice, oil, and black pepper in a small bowl
2. Stir well to combine
3. Arrange the fish in a single layer in a baking dish
4. Pour the orange juice mixture over the fillets
5. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for 2 hours, turning once
6. Remove the fillets from the marinade, reserving the marinade
7. Pour the marinade into a small saucepan
8. Bring to a boil
9. Reduce heat, and simmer for 5 minutes
10. Set half of the marinade aside
11. Place fillets on a lightly greased rack in a shallow roasting pan
12. Broil fillets 6 inches from heat for 10 to 12 minutes, or until the fish flakes easily when tested with a fork, basting occasionally with the marinade
13. Transfer fish to a serving platter
14. Sprinkle the toasted sesame seeds over the fillets, and serve with the reserved marinade<|endoftext|>
