Ingredients:
• Chicken drumsticks
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Bacon
• Squash
• Leek
• Celery
• Arborio rice
• Thyme
• Lemons
• Dry white wine
• Vegetable stock
• Bay leaves
• Lemon
• Parmesan cheese
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 180 degrees celsius
2. Heat a stovetop to oven pan on medium heat
3. Once heated, add the oil
4. Put the drumsticks in the pan & brown on all sides
5. Remove from the pan
6. Add the squash & saute for 3 minutes or until just starting to soften
7. Add the celery, leek, onion & garlic & saute for 5 minutes
8. Add the bacon & fry for a further 2 minutes
9. Add the rice & thyme & saute for 1 minute
10. Add the wine, stock & lemons and stir to combine
11. Add the chicken & stir again
12. Try to make sure that all of the rice is immersed in the liquid
13. Put into the oven & bake for 1 hour, or until the rice & chicken is cooked
14. Stir every so often during cooking
15. Garnish with the lemon & parmesan & grind over some fresh black pepper<|endoftext|>
