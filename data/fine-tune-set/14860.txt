Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken And Macadamia Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Basmati rice
• Olive oil
• Eggs
• Garlic cloves
• Cooked chicken
• Mandarin oranges
• Green onions
• Macadamia nuts
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Cook rice according to the packet directions and keep warm
2. Heat 1 tablespoon of the oil in a wok or a large saut pan over a medium-high heat
3. Add the eggs and stir-fry for 3 minutes, or until the eggs have set
4. Transfer to a small dish and keep warm
5. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil to the wok or saut pan, and add the chicken, mandarin segments, green onions, macadamia nuts, soy sauce and rice
6. Stir-fry for 4 minutes, stirring, or until mixed well and heated through
7. Add the eggs to the wok or saut pan
8. Season to taste with freshly ground black pepper
9. Stir fry for 1 further minute
10. Serve over cooked basmati rice with your favourite salad<|endoftext|>
