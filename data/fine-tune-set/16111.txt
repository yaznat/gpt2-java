What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Chipotle Chicken Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Serrano peppers
• Garlic cloves
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Chipotle chile in adobo
• Cooked chicken
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in large, heavy skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add onion and serrano chile
3. Cook, stirring occasionally, until soft and lightly browned, about 5 minutes
4. Add garlic and stir-fry 1 minute
5. Sprinkle with a pinch of salt and pepper
6. Transfer to a medium bowl and wipe skillet clean with a paper towel
7. Chope chipotle chile
8. Place in a small bowl with adobo sauce and mash with fork
9. Add to onion mixture in bowl
10. Add chicken and toss
11. Season to taste with salt and pepper
12. Place a tortilla on a cutting board and spread with 1 cup chicken mixture
13. Top with 1 / 2 cup cheese and another tortilla
14. Repeat with remaining ingredients
15. With paper towel, lightly oil skillet and place over medium-high heat until it is very hot but not smoking
16. Cook quesadillas one at a time, turning over once, until cheese is melted, 4-5 minutes total
17. Transfer with spatula to cutting board
18. Cover with aluminum to keep warm
19. Cut the quesadillas into quarters - serve with your choice of accompaniments<|endoftext|>
