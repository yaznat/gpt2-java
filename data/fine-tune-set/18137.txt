What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pancakes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Sour cream
• Apple cider
• Honey
• Butter
• Eggs
• Mcintosh apples
• Vegetable oil
• Lemon juice
• Powdered sugar
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl, stir together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and cinnamon
2. Add the sour cream, apple juice, honey, melted butter, and eggs
3. Stir until smooth
4. To prepare apple topping, peel and core apples
5. Slice thinly or chop small
6. Melt butter in a large skillet and add apples, cooking until tender and turning into sauce
7. Add brown sugar and cinnamon to taste
8. Set topping aside while finishing pancakes
9. Back to the pancakes- stir the grated apples into the pancake batter
10. Heat a small amount of oil on a griddle on medium-high heat
11. Drop batter by scant 1 / 4 cupfuls onto hot greased griddle
12. Do not crowd
13. Turn pancakes with a spatula when lightly browned on the underside and continue cooking until pancake is done
14. Remove from heat, and keep pancakes warm while finishing batch
15. Repeat with remaining batter
16. Serve pancakes topped with apple topping and powdered sugar, if desired<|endoftext|>
