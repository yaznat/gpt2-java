Ingredients:
• Baking mix
• Butter
• Granny smith apple
• Pecans
• Maple syrup
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degrees
2. Prepare pancake mix according to package directions
3. And set aside
4. Pour melted butter in 9" pie plate
5. Place apple slices in bottom of pie plate
6. Sprinkle cinnamon and pecans and drizzle syrup over apples
7. Carefully pour batter on top
8. Bake at 350 degrees for 30-35 minutes or until top springs back when touched
9. Loosen edges and invert onto serving plate
10. Cut in wedges and serve with warm maple syrup and / or fresh apple butter<|endoftext|>
