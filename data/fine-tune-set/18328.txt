Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Applesauce
• Whipped cream
• Peaches in light syrup

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl mix flour, baking powder, sugar and salt
2. In a smaller bowl, combine egg yolks and milk and beat well
3. Stir into dry ingredients
4. Add the applesauce and stir well
5. Beat the egg whites until stiff
6. Carefully fold into batter
7. Pour about 2 / 3 cup batter onto a preheated waffle iron
8. Close and bake about 3 minutes
9. These waffles are fairly soft, so if you like them crisper cook about 1 minute more
10. Serve topped with a dollop of whipped cream and a couple of peach slices<|endoftext|>
