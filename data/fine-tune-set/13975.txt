Ingredients:
• Brewed coffee
• Cognac
• Lumps of sugar

Steps:
1. You need heat resistant earthenware cups, saucers, and a heatproof coaster
2. You need 4 cls of cognac, but the zaar recipe editor would not allow me to post that - which is about a 1 jigger measure
3. Place the cup in the saucer
4. Fill the coffee cup with cold coffee, up to two centimetres below the top of the cup, then slowly pour 1 cl of the cognac onto the coffee
5. Pour the remaining 3 cls of cognac into the saucer, then place 2 3 sugar cubes in the cognac
6. Light the sugar cubes with a match
7. The cognac will slowly ignite, heating the coffee and combining with the sugar to form a cognac liqueur
8. When the flame has extinguished, wait for a little while for the handle to cool down, place the cup on the coaster
9. Empty all or part of the contents of the saucer into the cup
10. Your brulot charentais is now ready to drink
11. Salut!<|endoftext|>
