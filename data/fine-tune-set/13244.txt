Ingredients:
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• Apples
• Dried apricots
• White wine
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Place a heavy pan over high heat, then add the onion with the oil
2. Stir well and cook for 2 minutes, then add the garlic
3. Add the chicken, then cook for a few minutes, turning each piece until they all have a little colour
4. Add the chopped apples and dried apricots, stir well, pour in the wine, then bring to a boil
5. Simmer, covered, for 20 minutes, until the chicken is tender
6. Sprinkle with the parsley<|endoftext|>
