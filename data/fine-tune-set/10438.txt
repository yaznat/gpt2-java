Ingredients:
• Anise seed
• Rum
• Butter
• White sugar
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Egg
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Ground cloves

Steps:
1. Place the anise seeds in a small bowl with the rum
2. Set aside to marinate overnight
3. In a medium bowl, cream together the butter, sugar and vanilla until smooth
4. Stir in the anise seed and rum
5. Mix in the egg
6. Combine the flour, salt, baking powder and cloves
7. Stir into the butter mixture until well blended
8. Cover and refrigerate until chilled, about 1 hour
9. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees
10. On a lightly floured surface, roll the dough out to 1 / 8 inch thickness
11. Cut into desired shapes using cookie cutters
12. Place cookies on a greased cookie sheet
13. Bake for 10 minutes in the preheated oven, or until golden brown at the edges
14. Cool for a few minutes on baking sheets before removing to wire racks to cool completely<|endoftext|>
