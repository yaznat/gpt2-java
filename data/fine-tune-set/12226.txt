What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>1 French Onion Soup

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Yellow onions
• Sugar
• Flour
• Beef broth
• Low sodium beef broth
• Sherry wine
• French bread
• Swiss cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Romano cheese
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Heat the butter and 2 tb olive oil in a large pan until melted
2. Add the onions and sugar
3. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally until they are "caramalized"
4. Add the flour and stir for about 3 minutes
5. Add the beef broth, and cook the soup over low-medium heat, partly covered, for about 45 minutes
6. Add the sherry, cook for an additional 5 minutes
7. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees
8. Cut the french bread into 1" thick slices
9. Bake the bread on an un-greased cookie sheet for 15 minutes
10. Brush the bread with olive oil and rub with the cut side of the garlic
11. Turn the bread slices over and bake an additional 15 minutes
12. Ladle the soup into oven proof crocks, top each with a slice of bread and sprinkle on parmesan, romano and swiss cheese
13. Bake the soup for about 10 minutes or until the cheese begins to brown<|endoftext|>
