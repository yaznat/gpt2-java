What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Avocado Burritos

Ingredients:
• Tortillas
• Avocados
• Bacon
• Tomatoes
• Jalapeno pepper
• Sour cream
• Alfalfa sprout
• Green onions
• Salt
• Cracked black pepper

Steps:
1. Lay the tortillas out flat, spread about 1 tablespoon of sour cream in the middle of each tortilla long ways
2. Divide the avocado evenly over top of the sour cream
3. Layer of any optional ingredients: bacon, tomato or jalapeno pepper
4. Add some alfalfa sprouts, green onions, salt and pepper to taste
5. Roll it up<|endoftext|>
