Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Bacon
• Onion
• Tomatoes
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Skim milk
• Paprika
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Using a lightly greased pan, fry bacon pieces and onion to taste
