What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Jack Cookies

Ingredients:
• Light brown sugar
• Shortening
• Egg
• Flour
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Apple

Steps:
1. Cream together sugar and shortening
2. Beat in egg
3. Sift together dry ingredients
4. Add to mixture
5. Beat until well blended
6. Stir in apples
7. Drop in the shape of a ball on a greased cookie sheet
8. Bake at 375f for 12 -15 minutes<|endoftext|>
