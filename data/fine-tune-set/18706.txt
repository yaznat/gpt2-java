Give me a pudding recipe.<|endoftext|>Banana And Semolina Pudding Sheera

Ingredients:
• Fine semolina
• Milk
• Bananas
• Sugar
• Raisins
• Cardamom powder
• Clarified butter

Steps:
1. Roast the semolina in the clarified butter for 10 mins till golden brown
2. Then add 2 cups of milk / half and half
3. When cooked add the mashed bananas and the sugar
4. Then when the mixture is almost dry, add the cardamoms and raisins
5. Serve hot or cold<|endoftext|>
