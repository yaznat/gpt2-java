What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Chicken And Fruit Curry Salad

Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Pineapple tidbits
• Celery
• Cauliflower floret
• Green onion
• Salted peanuts
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Fresh lemon rind
• Curry powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Combine chicken, pineapple, celery, cauliflower, onions and peanuts
2. Blend together remaining ingredients and toss thoroughly with chicken
3. Chill well<|endoftext|>
