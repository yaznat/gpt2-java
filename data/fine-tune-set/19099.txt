Please give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Banana Split Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Evaporated milk
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Salt
• Cherries in juice
• Pineapple
• Bananas
• Pecans
• Vanilla extract
• Milk

Steps:
1. Add sugar to eggs gradually, beating until thick
2. Stir in remaining ingredients, except milk
3. Pour into ice cream freezer container
4. Fill with milk
5. Freeze using manufacturer's instructions<|endoftext|>
