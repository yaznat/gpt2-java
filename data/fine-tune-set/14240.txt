What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Crispy Tofu With Spicy Peanut Sauce

Ingredients:
• Medium firm tofu
• Safflower oil
• Cornstarch
• Satay sauce
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Drain tofu
2. Cut into 4 equal lengthwise slices
3. Place on a double thickness of tea towel, cover with another double thickness of tea towel and allow to stand for 15 minutes
4. Gently pat tofu dry
5. Using large nonstick frying pan, heat oil over medium heat
6. Coat tofu with cornstarch
7. Fry in hot oil until crispy and lightly coloured
8. Drain on paper towel
9. Place slices on dinner plate, drizzle with peanut sauce and sprinkle with green onions
10. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
