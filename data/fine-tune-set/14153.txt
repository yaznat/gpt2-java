Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Unbleached flour
• Toasted wheat germ
• Thyme
• Dill weed
• Garlic powder
• Paprika
• Black pepper
• Egg
• Milk
• Hot pepper sauce
• Safflower oil
• Rice vinegar
• Sugar
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Gingerroot

Steps:
1. Ginger sauce:
2. In small saucepan, place vinegar, sugar, 3 / 4 c water, and soy sauce
3. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes
4. Meanwhile, in a small bowl, combine cornstarch and 1 t water
5. Stir into sauce
6. Cook mixture, stirring until clear and thickened
7. Remove pan from heat
8. Stir in ginger
9. Use for batter dipped tofu and vegetable stir fry
10. Tofu:
11. While ginger sauce is simmering, cut tofu into 1" squares about 1 / 4" thick
12. Set aside
13. In a med bowl, combine flour, wheat germ, and seasonings
14. In a separate bowl, lightly beat egg
15. Add milk and hot pepper sauce
16. In a large skillet, heat oil
17. Piece by piece, dip tofu in flour, then in egg mixture, and again in flour
18. Saute until lightly browned, about 3 minutes on each side
19. Serve warm, arranged on a platter with cocktail forks and a bowl of ginger sauce
20. Surround platter with curly lettuce leaves or large sprigs of parsley
21. Variations: - use egg white only<|endoftext|>
