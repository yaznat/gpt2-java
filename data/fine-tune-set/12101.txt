Ingredients:
• Unflavored gelatin
• Egg whites
• Sugar
• Salt
• Heavy cream
• Egg yolks
• 9 in. baked pastry shell
• Lime juice
• Pistachio nut
• Water
• Lime slice
• Lime peel
• Green food coloring

Steps:
1. Thoroughly mix gelatin, 1 / 2 cup sugar, and salt in saucepan
2. Beat together egg yolks, lime juice, and water
3. Stir into gelatin mixture
4. Cook and stir over medium heat just till mixture comes to boiling
5. Remove from heat
6. Stir in grated peel
7. Add food coloring sparingly to tint pale green
8. Chill, stirring occasionally, until the mixture mounds slightly when dropped from a spoon
9. Beat egg whites till soft peaks form
10. Gradually add 1 / 2 c sugar, beating to stiff peaks
11. Fold gelatin mixture into egg whites
12. Fold in whipped cream
13. Pile into cooled baked pastry shell
14. Chill till firm
15. Spread with more whipped cream
16. Edge with grated lime peel
17. Sprinkle chopped pistachio nuts in center
18. Garnish with thinly sliced lime placed in whipped cream mounds around edge of pie<|endoftext|>
