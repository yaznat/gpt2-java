What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Pie

Ingredients:
• Graham cracker crust
• Vanilla ice cream
• Pumpkin
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Allspice
• Nutmeg
• Clove
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. Prepare graham cracker crust according to directions
2. Mix spices and orange juice with the pumpkin and fold into the softened ice cream
3. Pour into crust and freeze until firm<|endoftext|>
