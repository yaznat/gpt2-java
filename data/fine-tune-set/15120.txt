Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Red onion
• Baby arugula
• Salt and pepper
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Goat cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Heat the oil in a large skillet to med
3. Heat and add onions
4. Cook until they are soft and golden in color, about 12-15 minute
5. Add the arugula and cook until it is wilted, about 1 minute
6. Season with salt and pepper
7. Place tortillas on baking sheet and top with arugula mixture, crumble goat cheese over top and bake until tortillas are crisped and cheese is slightly melted, about 10-12 minute<|endoftext|>
