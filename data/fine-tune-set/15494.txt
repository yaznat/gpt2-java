Ingredients:
• Oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Mushroom
• Ground beef
• Crushed tomatoes
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Beef consomme
• Water
• Quick-cooking barley
• Brown sugar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Vinegar
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a kettle
2. Saute onion until they are translucent, stir in garlic and mushrooms and cook 1 minute
3. Meanwhile brown hamburger or beef cubes
4. Drain
5. Add beef to onion mixture
6. Stir in tomatoes and their juice, the consomme and 4 cups of water, barley, brown sugar, worcestershire sauce, vinegar, pepper and mixed vegetables
7. Bring to a boil, reduce heat cover and simmer 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
