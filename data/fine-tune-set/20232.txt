Ingredients:
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Chili sauce
• Toasted sesame oil
• Fresh ginger
• Snow peas
• Radish
• Scallions
• Unsalted cashews

Steps:
1. Combine soy sauce, vinegar and chile-garlic sauce in a small bowl
2. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat
3. Add ginger
4. Cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds
5. Add snow peas, radishes and scallions
6. Cook, stirring frequently, until the peas are tender-crisp, 2 to 4 minutes
7. Add the sauce mixture and stir to coat well
8. Remove from the heat
9. Stir in cashews<|endoftext|>
