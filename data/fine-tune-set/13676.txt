Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Alice S Restaurant Pork Burritos

Ingredients:
• Ground lean pork
• Fresh gingerroot
• Garlic
• Onion
• Coleslaw mix
• Sesame oil
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Lime juice
• Honey
• Ground coriander
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Flour tortillas
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Heat a large nonstick skillet over high heat
2. Add the pork
3. Cook, crumble and stir for 3 to 4 minutes, or until the pork is no longer pink
4. Add the ginger, garlic, onion, and coleslaw mix
5. Stir-fry with the pork for 2 minutes, or until vegetables are wilted
6. In a small bowl, combine all the remaining ingredients, except the tortillas and cilantro
7. Add to skillet
8. Cook, stirring constantly to blend all ingredients well, for 1 minute
9. Spoon equal portions of the mixture onto warm flour tortillas
10. Garnish with cilantro
11. Roll up to encase filling<|endoftext|>
