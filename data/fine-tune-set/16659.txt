Give me a smoothie recipe.<|endoftext|>Berry Burst Banana Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Non-fat strawberry yogurt
• Triple berry burst cheerios
• Strawberries
• Milk
• Banana

Steps:
1. In blender combine all ingredients
2. Cover and blend at high speed for 10 seconds
3. Stop and scrape down sides of container
4. Cover and blend an additional 20 seconds or until smooth
5. Pour yogurt mix into 2 glasses
6. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
