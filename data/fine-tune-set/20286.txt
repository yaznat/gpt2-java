What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Stir Fry W Frozen Mixed Vegetables

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breast
• Cooked brown rice
• Olive oil
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Egg
• Soy sauce
• Water

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in fry pan or wok
2. Stir in chicken and fry for 2 minutes, set aside heat another tbsp oil stir in egg and fry till cooked stir in rice and fry for 2 minutes, set aside heat last tbsp oil in fry pan or wok
3. Stir in vegetables, fry for 2 minutes
4. Stir in chicken stir in rice and egg mix soy sauce and water pour over fried ingredients toss together until well mixed<|endoftext|>
