Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Sour cream
• Marinara sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Italian seasoning
• Onion powder
• Garlic
• Pepperoni

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Lightly grease a 9x13 pan
3. Bring cream cheese to room temperature
4. With hand mixer or large spoon mix together the cream cheese and sour cream
5. Fold in the marinara sauce
6. Add the garlic, italian seasoning and onion powder and stir well
7. Then add half each of the mozzarella cheese, parmesan cheese, and chopped pepperoni and mix well
8. Pour into the greased baking dish and spread out evenly
9. Top with the remaining pepperoni and then the remaining cheeses
10. Cook in preheated oven for about 15 minutes or until hot and bubbly
11. Enjoy with toasted baguette slices or your favorite crackers!<|endoftext|>
