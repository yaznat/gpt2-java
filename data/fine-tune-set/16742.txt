What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Milkshake With Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Chocolate ice cream
• Cocoa powder
• Sugar
• Hot chocolate powder
• Chocolate syrup

Steps:
1. Mix milk, chocolate powder, sugar, cocoa powder in a blender
2. Process until it becomes smooth
3. Divide mixture among glasses
4. Cover bottom and drizzling syrup in a spiral pattern inside of each glass
5. Pour equal amount of ice cream mixture into each glass
6. Swirl some chocolate syrup over each serving and serve cold<|endoftext|>
