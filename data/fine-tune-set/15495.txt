What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Barley Burger Stew For 2

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Celery
• Tomato juice
• Water
• Pearl barley
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In saucepan, cook beef, onion, and celery until no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Stir in tomato juice, water, barley, chili powder, salt, and pepper
4. Bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat
6. Cover and simmer 50-60 minutes or until barley is tender<|endoftext|>
