Ingredients:
• Egg white
• Cornstarch
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Brussels sprouts
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• Rice vinegar
• Chicken broth
• Sesame oil
• Cold water
• Vegetable oil
• Shallot
• Garlic clove
• Gingerroot
• Red bell pepper
• Scallion
• Water chestnuts
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Whisk egg white and 1 1 / 2 tsp cornstarch
2. Add chicken and set aside for 10 minutes
3. Boil brussels sprouts in salted water for 2 1 / 2 minutes, or until just crisp-tender, drain and cool under cold water
4. In a small bowl combine soy sauce, sugar, vinegar, broth, remaining 1 / 2 tsp cornstarch, sesame oil and water
5. Set aside
6. Heat oil in a wok
7. Add drained chicken and stir-fry until opaque and just firm, about 1 minute
8. Remove and set aside
9. Stir-fry shallot, garlic and gingerroot for 30 seconds
10. Add red bell pepper and stir-fry for 2 minutes
11. Add the scallions and the brussels sprouts and stir-fry 1 minute
12. Make a well in the center of the stir-fry mixture
13. Whisk the soy sauce mixture and add to the well, whisking until it boils
14. Stir into the vegetable mixture and simmer for 1 minute
15. Add the chicken, water chestnuts and salt and pepper and stir-fry until chicken is heated through, about 30 seconds<|endoftext|>
