What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Beef Stew

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Boneless sirloin steak
• Lipton beefy mushroom soup mix
• Water
• Tomato paste
• New potatoes
• Peas and carrots

Steps:
1. In 12-inch nonstick skillet, heat olive oil over medium-high heat and brown steak
2. Stir in remaining ingredients
3. Bring to a boil over high heat
4. Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered 10 minutes or until steak is tender<|endoftext|>
