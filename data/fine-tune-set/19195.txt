Ingredients:
• Half-and-half
• Whipping cream
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Maraschino cherries
• Semisweet mini chocolate chips
• Vanilla extract
• Almond extract
• Red food coloring

Steps:
1. In ice cream freezer container, combine all ingredients
2. Mix well
3. Freeze according to manufacturers instructions
4. Freeze leftovers
5. Whip whipping cream
6. In large bowl, combine eagle brand, 1 / 2 cup chopped maraschino cherries, 1 / 2 cup mini chocolate chips, 2 teaspoons vanilla and 1 / 4 teaspoon almond extract
7. Mix well
8. Fold in whipped cream
9. Pour into 9x5-inch loaf pan or other 2 quart container
10. Cover
11. Freeze 6 hours or until firm
