Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Benihana Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Butter
• Onion
• Carrot
• Green onion
• Sesame seeds
• Eggs
• Soy sauce
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Cook rice according to package directions
2. In a large skillet melt butter
3. Add onions, carrots and scallions
4. Saute until carrots are translucent
5. Set aside
6. Heat oven to 350f
7. Place sesame seeds in a shallow pan
8. Bake until golden brown, shaking pan occasionally for even color
9. Lightly grease another skillet
10. Beat eggs and pour into hot skillet
11. Cook as you would scrambled eggs
12. Combine rice, vegetables, sesame seeds and eggs
13. Add soy sauce and stir
14. Salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
