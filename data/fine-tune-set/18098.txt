What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>All Bran Pancake

Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• All-bran cereal
• Cinnamon
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Pam cooking spray

Steps:
1. Place the all bran, egg, cinnamon and splenda in a blender
2. Mix all ingredients and pour into skillet with some pam until the edges get golden
3. Spray some more pam and flip
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
