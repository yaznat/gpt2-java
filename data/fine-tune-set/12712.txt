What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Amish Brown Sugar Ham Steak And Scalloped Potato Skillet Vintag

Ingredients:
• Ham
• Margarine
• Brown sugar
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Evaporated milk
• Water
• Onion
• Salt
• Pepper
• Potatoes
• Raw carrot

Steps:
1. In 10" skillet brown ham in oleo and brown sugar
2. Remove ham, pour off fat
3. Mix in same skillet, the soup, milk, water, onion, salt & pepper
4. Stir in 3 c thinly sliced potatoes and carrots
5. Cover, cook over low heat stirring now and then, until veggies are tender, about 35 minutes
6. Lift veggies from the bottom so they don't stick
7. Place whole ham slice or cut in serving size pieces and place on top of veggies
8. Cover and cook about 10 minutes more<|endoftext|>
