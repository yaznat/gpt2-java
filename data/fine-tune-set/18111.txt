What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Ameraussie S Gluten Free Oatmeal Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Old fashioned oats
• Greek yogurt
• Skim milk
• Eggs
• Flour
• Agave nectar
• Vanilla extract
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Prepare ahead, combine in one bowl the oats, yogurt and milk
2. Cover and let soak overnight, in the fridge
3. When you are ready to cook: whisk into the oat mixture the eggs, flour, agave nectar, vanilla, cinnamon, ginger, baking powder and salt
4. Mix well
5. If the mix is too runny, add more flour 1 / 4 cup at a time
6. If the mix is too dry, add more milk, about 1 / 4 cup
7. Heat griddle or skillet and lightly coat with canola oil
8. Low to medium heat
9. Using a ladle or cup, spoon about 1 / 4 cup of batter onto griddle in a 4 - 5 inch circle
10. Let cook until bubbles appear and outer edges start to look dry
11. Flip the pancake with a turner and cook the other side for about 1 - 2 minutes
12. If it's nearly done, the top of the pancake will spring back when you gently press a spot
13. Serve plain, buttered or with your favorite toppings
14. Prep and cooking time do not reflect soaking the oats
15. Cooking time is per pancake
