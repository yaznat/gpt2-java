What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cheese English Muffin Pizzas

Ingredients:
• English muffins
• Apple
• Cheddar cheese
• Cream cheese
• Dried cranberries

Steps:
1. Top toasted muffins with 1 tablespoons cream cheese, then place apple slices on top, and 2 pieces of cheese
2. Heat in toaster over 30 seconds, or until cheese melts
3. Top with dried cranberries<|endoftext|>
