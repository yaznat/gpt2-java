What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Black Forest Coffee

Ingredients:
• Brewed coffee
• Chocolate syrup
• Maraschino cherry juice
• Whipped cream
• Chocolate shavings
• Maraschino cherry

Steps:
1. In a glass pitcher combine hot coffee, chocolate syrup and maraschino cherry juice, stir to mix
2. Pour into 4 individual coffee cups and top with whipped cream, chocolate shavings and a cherry on top
3. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
