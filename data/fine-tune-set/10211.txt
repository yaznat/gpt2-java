Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Kidney beans

Steps:
1. Cook beef in skillet over medium heat until browned
2. Drain
3. Mix beef and remaining ingredients except beans in 3 1 / 2 - 6quart slow cooker
4. Cover and cook on low heat setting 6 to 8 hours or until onion is tender
5. Stir in beans
6. Cover and cook on high heat setting 15 to 20 minutes or until slightly thickened<|endoftext|>
