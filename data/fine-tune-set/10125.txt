What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Aztec Gold Chili

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Garlic cloves
• Onions
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Oregano
• Cinnamon
• Black pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt
• Ground turkey
• Diced tomatoes
• Pinto beans
• Green peppers
• Unsweetened chocolate

Steps:
1. Combine first ten ingredients in microwave safe dish
2. Microwave on high for 2 and 1 / 2 minutes
3. Stir in 1-2 t water if mixture becomes too dry
4. Microwave on high 2 and 1 / 2 to 3 minutes or till onions are tender crisp
5. Let stand 3 minutes
6. Stir in turkey
7. Microwave on high 10-13 minutes or til turkey is partially done, stirring once
8. Add tomatoes, beans, peppers and chocolate
9. Mix well
10. Microwave on medium for 40 minutes, stirring twice
11. Serve with sour cream, grated cheese, diced onions and tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
