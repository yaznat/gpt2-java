What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>11 Layer Salad

Ingredients:
• Lettuce
• Bacon
• Red onion
• Frozen peas
• Green pepper
• Cauliflower
• Fresh broccoli
• Fresh mushrooms
• Cherry tomatoes
• Italian seasoning
• Mayonnaise
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Cook, drain, and crumble bacon
2. Mix mayonnaise and sugar until blended
3. Set aside
4. Cut onions, pepper, cauliflower, broccoli, mushrooms into bite size pieces
5. In large 9x13-inch pan begin making your layers
6. Start with half of the lettuce
7. Layer with half of the mayonnaise mixture
8. Layer with half of the onions, peppers, cauliflower, broccoli, mushrooms, shredded cheese, bacon, and peas
9. Repeat this layering process one more time
10. Top with cherry tomatoes cut in half
11. Sprinkle with italian seasoning
12. Allow to set and cool in refrigerator before serving<|endoftext|>
