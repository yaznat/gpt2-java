Ingredients:
• Refrigerated crescent dinner rolls
• Eggs
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Spread rolls onto a lightly greased 12 inch pizza pan
2. Firmly pressing perforations to seal
3. Combine eggs, bacon and cheese
4. Pour over crust
5. Bake at 375'f for 12-15 minutes
6. Cut like pizza and serve
