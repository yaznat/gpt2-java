What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef And Broccoli Stir Fry Ramen

Ingredients:
• Ramen noodles
• Water
• Steak
• Oil
• Broccoli
• Green onion
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Cook noodles according to package directions and drain
2. In skillet brown beef and drain
3. Add oil, 1 / 2 seasoning packet, broccoli, onions to meat
4. Stir fry for 5 minutes
5. Add soy sauce and red pepper, simmer 5 additional minutes
6. Serve over noodles<|endoftext|>
