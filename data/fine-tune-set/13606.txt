Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Beef Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Rump roast
• Flour
• Tomato sauce
• Onion
• Brown sugar
• Lemon juice
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Chili powder
• Garlic clove
• Dry mustard

Steps:
1. Coat meat with flour and set aside
2. In crock pot, combine remaining ingredients
3. Place meat in crock pot and turn to coat with sauce
4. Cover and cook on low for 10-12 hours, or on high for 6 hours
5. Slice or shred meat into small pieces
6. Mix into sauce
7. Serve on rolls
8. Yum!<|endoftext|>
