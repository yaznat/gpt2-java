What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Authentic Thai Red Curry With Chicken

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Thai red curry paste
• Coconut milk
• Oil
• Chicken stock
• Chicken bouillon cube
• Yellow onions
• Green bell peppers
• Carrots
• Japanese eggplants
• Sugar
• Lemon juice
• Fish sauce
• Sweet basil
• Dried basil
• Cherry tomatoes
• Jasmine rice

Steps:
1. Cut onion, bell pepper, and eggplant into bite size pieces and set aside
2. Shred carrots with a vegetable peeler and set aside
3. Cut tomatoes in half and set aside
4. Cut chicken into bite size pieces and set aside
5. Pour the oil into a five-quart pot and put over medium heat
6. When the oil is hot, but not smoking, add the thai red curry paste and stir, sauting for about 2 minutes
7. Add the coconut milk and chicken stock, then throw the chicken bullion cube in the pot and stir
8. After the liquid begins to boil, add the vegetables, carrots, and tomatoes
9. Add sugar, lemon juice, sweet basil leaves, and crushed basil, then bring to a boil again
10. Add the chicken and bring to a boil
11. Turn the heat down and simmer for about five more minutes
12. Serve with hot jasmine rice<|endoftext|>
