Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Eggs
• 2% low-fat milk
• Vegetable oil
• Pecans
• Pecan halves
• Strawberries
• Honey
• Raspberry preserves

Steps:
1. Combine flour, baking powder, sugar, sale and cinnamon in a bowl
2. In a separate bowl, mix together the eggs, milk and oil until they are combined
3. Pour the bowl of liquid ingredients into the bowl of dry ingredients and mix until it is a smooth and creamy batter without lumps
4. Stir in the finely chopped pecans
5. Heat a griddle and once hot drop the batter in equal portions onto the griddle
6. Turn the pancakes when the edges are golden brown and bubbles start to appear in the middle
7. Once all the pancakes are cooked, stack them up into equal stack on five plates
8. Place two pecans in the middle of each top pancake
9. Slice one strawberry per pancake stack and arrange the slices around the two pecans
10. Place the 1 / 4 cup honey in the microwave for between 25 and 30 seconds being careful not to touch the hot honey with your fingers when you take it out
11. Stir two teaspoons of the raspberry preserves into the hot honey until they are combined
12. Let cool for 30 seconds
13. Drizzle the raspberry honey over the pancakes and serve<|endoftext|>
