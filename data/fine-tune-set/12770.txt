What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Baked Potatoes With Rib Eye Steak Hash

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Vegetable oil
• Kosher salt
• Rib eye steaks
• Red bell pepper
• Orange bell pepper
• Onion
• Red jalapeno chiles
• Heavy whipping cream
• Fresh thyme
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Rub potatoes with vegetable oil
3. Sprinkle with coarse salt
4. Pierce potato with fork several times and place directly on oven rack with a baking sheet on a lower rack to catch any drips
5. Bake until tender, about 1 hour
6. Sprinkle steak with salt and pepper
7. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
8. Add steak and sear until brown but still very rare, about 2 minutes per side
9. Transfer steak to plate
10. Reduce heat to medium
11. Add bell peppers, onion, and jalapeos to skillet
12. Saut until peppers are soft and onion caramelizes, about 30 minutes
13. Cut steak crosswise into 1 / 4-inch-thick slices
14. Halve slices lengthwise into strips
15. Add cream, chopped thyme, and steak to pepper mixture
16. Simmer until steak is heated through and cream thickens slightly, about 1 minute
17. Season to taste with salt and pepper
18. Cut cross in potatoes and press on sides to open slightly
19. Add salt, pepper, and 1 teaspoon butter to each and mash slightly with fork
20. Top each with steak mixture
21. Garnish with thyme if desired<|endoftext|>
