What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Baby Bok Choy And Mushroom Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Tamari
• Oyster sauce
• Sake
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Ginger
• Carrot
• Baby bok choy
• Mushrooms
• Salt
• Pepper
• Potato starch
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. In a bowl, mix 2 / 3 cup water, tamari, oyster sauce and sake or chinese rice wine
2. Heat oil in a pan
3. Saut garlic and ginger
4. Add carrots and bok choy and cook a few more minutes
5. Add the sauce mixture
6. Cover and cook until vegetables are almost cooked
7. Add mushrooms and cook a few more minutes
8. Season with salt and pepper
9. Dissolve potato starch in 2 tbsp of water
10. Reduce heat and add it to the pan
11. Mix well
12. Remove from heat
13. Add crushed red pepper if desired
14. Infuse love into the food and serve!<|endoftext|>
