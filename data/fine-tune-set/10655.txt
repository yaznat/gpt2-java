What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Artichoke Lemon Parmesan Pasta

Ingredients:
• Spaghetti
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Lemon
• Parmesan cheese
• Basil
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cook the spaghetti following pack instructions
2. Put the rest of the ingredients in a large bowl with 1 tbsp olive oil, season really well and mix
3. Drain the pasta, keep 2 tbsp of the cooking water, then toss everything together<|endoftext|>
