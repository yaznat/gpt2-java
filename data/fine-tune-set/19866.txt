Ingredients:
• Chicken hot dogs
• Onion
• Yukon gold potatoes
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Salt and pepper
• Cayenne
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Parboil potatoes until they are starting to tender, slightly soft with a fork poke
2. Cool and julienne or dice
3. Cut hot dogs into coins about inch thick
4. Heat oil in a large non-stick skillet
5. Add garlic and onion
6. Saut over medium heat until onion starts to soften and brown, about 5-7 minutes
7. Add hot dogs, salt and pepper to taste, and any seasonings you are using
8. Saut over medium heat for about 10 minutes, until hot dogs are browning
9. Add potatoes
10. Saut over medium heat for 10 minutes more, until potatoes are golden and heated through<|endoftext|>
