Ingredients:
• Ear of corn
• Cooked shrimp
• Diced onions
• Salt
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Garlic powder
• Goat cheese
• Cabbage
• Salsa verde
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. Remove the husk and silk from your ear of corn, wash it off, wrap it in a paper towel and cook it in the microwave for 3 minutes, then cut the kernels off the cob and set aside
2. In the meanwhile, saute the onion in about 1 tsp of olive oil for about 3 minutes on med-high until opaque
3. Add the salt, cumin, chili powder, garlic powder and shrimp, stir, cook for 30 seconds, cover and turn over the heat
4. On each tortilla, spread 1 / 8 of the goat cheese, top with 1 / 8 of the shrimp, sprinkle with some corn kernels, top with a little cabbage and spoon about 1 tsp of salsa over the cabbage
5. Serve with more salsa on the side<|endoftext|>
