Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>No Fat Low Calorie Vegetarian Omelette

Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Red onion
• Pepper
• Fat free cheese

Steps:
1. Cut the onion as desired
2. I personally prefer them cubed and place it on a microwavable small plate
3. Add egg whites to the plate
4. Add pepper and cook in microwave for 2 minutes on high with a cover
5. Add cheese and cook for another 5 seconds, or put salsa on top
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
