Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Garlic
• Teriyaki sauce
• Broccoli
• Carrots
• Red onion
• Snow peas
• Cooking oil
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Sesame oil
• Toasted sesame seeds
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. At least two hours before dinner, combine sliced chicken, garlic, and teriyaki sauce in a bowl
2. Stir, cover, and refrigerate until dinner time
3. Heat wok, or wok-style pan, to high heat
4. Add oil
5. Add chicken and stir until cooked, then add veggies
6. Continue to stir and cook until veggies are firm, then add crushed red pepper flakes to taste and garnish with sesame seeds
7. Serve immediately over rice<|endoftext|>
