Please generate a pie recipe.<|endoftext|>Absolutely Perfect Pumpkin Pie

Ingredients:
• Pumpkin puree
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Eggs
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Ground ginger
• Ground nutmeg
• Salt
• Unbaked pie shells

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees fahrenheit
2. Beat eggs in large bowl
3. Stir in cinnamon, cloves, ginger, nutmeg, and salt
4. Add pumpkin puree and mix until smooth
5. Gradually stir in condensed milk
6. Mix until smooth
7. Pour mixture into pie shell
8. Bake at 425f for 15 minutes
9. Reduce temperature to 350f and bake for an additional 40-50 minutes, until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean
10. Cool on wire rack
11. Serve or cover and refrigerate<|endoftext|>
