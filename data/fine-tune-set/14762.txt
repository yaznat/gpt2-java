What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Short Ribs With Carrots And Rice

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Boneless beef short ribs
• Diced tomatoes
• Dry sherry
• Plum sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Hot red pepper flakes
• Carrots
• Sesame seeds
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In a large dutch oven heat 1 tbls
2. Of the oil over medium heat
3. Add the onion and garlic
4. Saut until soft, about 10 minutes
5. Remove the onions from the pan and heat the remaining oil
6. Brown the beef in 2 batches
7. Remove from pan
8. Put the onions back into pan and add tomatoes, sherry, plum and hoisin sauces, the salt, pepper, and hot pepper flakes
9. Simmer the sauce for 10 minutes
10. Add the beef, cover and simmer 1 hour, stirring occasionally
11. Uncover and continue cooking 30 minutes
12. While short ribs are cooking, prepare the carrots
13. Bring a pan of water to boil, add the carrots and cook 5 minutes
14. Drain and rinse with cold water to stop the cooking
15. Add the carrots to the meat and continue to cook 15 minutes
16. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve meat and sauce over the hot rice<|endoftext|>
