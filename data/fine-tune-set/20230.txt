What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Cashew Chicken With Stir Fry Vegetables

Ingredients:
• Stir fry vegetables
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Water
• Cornstarch
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic cloves
• Stir-fry sauce
• Roasted cashews

Steps:
1. Blanch vegetables 2-3 min in large pot of boiling salted water until crisp-tender
2. Drain
3. Transfer to bowl of ice water
4. Drain and set aside
5. Place chicken in small bowl
6. Add 1 tbsp of water at a time, working in with hands until water is absorbed into chicken
7. Sprinkle cornstarch over chicken and work in with hands to coat all pieces
8. Drizzle oil around sides of stir-fry pan
9. Tilt pan to distribute evenly
10. Heat oil in pan on high until oil faintly smokes
11. Add chicken
12. Stir fry 2 minute add garlic
13. Stir fry 1 min
14. Add sauce
15. Toss to coat
16. Remove chicken and sauce from pan
17. Set aside
18. Wipe pan clean
19. Return pan to heat
20. Drizzle oil around sides of pan
21. Tilt pan to distribute evenly
22. Heat oil in pan on high until oil faintly smokes
23. Stir fry vegetables 1 minute return chicken and sauce to pan along with cashews
24. Toss to heat through, about 30 seconds<|endoftext|>
