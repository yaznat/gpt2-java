What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Corn And Noodle Casserole

Ingredients:
• Egg noodles
• Kernel corn
• Velveeta cheese
• Egg
• Butter
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cook noodles and rinse in cold water
2. Drain
3. Combine noodles, corn, velveeta cheese, egg, butter and salt in 9 x 9-inch greased baking dish
4. Bake at 350 for 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
