What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Carrot Cake Waffles Ii

Ingredients:
• Self-rising flour
• Sugar
• Old fashioned oats
• Cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Carrot
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl, mix together the flour, sugar, oats, cinnamon and baking soda
2. In separate bowl, combine the carrot, oil and egg
3. Mix well
4. Add the carrot mixture to the dry ingredients and stir until just combined and moistened
5. Fold in the walnut pieces
6. Pour approximately 1 / 2 cup mixture onto preheated waffle iron
7. Waffles will be done when steam stops -- 4 or 5 minutes
8. A nice addition to this recipe would be tablespoon of grated apple or a tiny handful raisins<|endoftext|>
