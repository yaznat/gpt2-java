Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Amaretto Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Eggs
• Milk
• Amaretto liqueur
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Grease muffin tins
3. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder and salt
4. Cut in butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs
5. In a second bowl, combine eggs, milk, amaretto and almonds
6. Add to dry ingredients, stir until mixture is just moistened
7. Batter will look rough not smooth, do not overmix
8. Spoon into muffins tins, about 3 / 4 full
9. Bake 20-25 minutes of until golden brown
10. Remove from tins and place on wire rack<|endoftext|>
