Ingredients:
• Angel food cake mix
• Crushed pineapple
• Orange marmalade
• Cornstarch
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f and lightly spray a 13x9x2" baking pan with nonstick spray
2. Put cake mix in a large bowl and stir in undrained pineapple until well combined
3. Pour mixture into baking pan and smooth down the surface
4. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until golden brown
5. Transfer to a wire rack and let cool for about 30 minutes then run a thin knife around the edges of the cooled cake before slicing and removing from pan
6. Serve alone or with the following pineapple sauce
7. For pineapple sauce: while cake is cooling, in a small saucepan, gently stir together the sauce ingredients until the cornstarch is dissolved
8. Bring to a boil over medium high heat and boil for 1 1 / 2 to 2 minutes or until thickened, stirring frequently
9. Remove pan from heat and let cool slightly
10. Serve the warm sauce over the cake slices
11. Store leftover sauce in a container with a tight fitting lid
12. You can serve additional sauce either cold or reheat in the microwave till just warm<|endoftext|>
