Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Milk
• Lemon juice
• Almond extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Banana
• Flaked coconut

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl, cream together butter and sugar
2. Beat in eggs, one at a time
3. Stir in milk, lemon juice, and almond extract
4. Sift flour again into mixing bowl along with baking powder, soda, and salt
5. Mix thoroughly
6. Stir in bananas
7. Fold in toasted coconut
8. Pour into a well-greased loaf pan
9. Bake in a moderate oven 350 degrees for 55 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
10. Cool in pan a few minutes
11. Turn out on wire rack to continue cooling
12. After the loaf is cool, wrap tightly and let it mellow for several days to enhance the flavor
13. Worth the wait!
14. Makes one loaf<|endoftext|>
