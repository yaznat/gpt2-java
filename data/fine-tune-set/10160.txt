Ingredients:
• Chicken drumsticks
• Olive oil
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cherry tomatoes
• Avocado
• Onion
• Coriander
• Lime juice

Steps:
1. Remove the skin from the chicken
2. Put the drumsticks in a medium bowl or plastic bag
3. Drizzle them with the olive oil and sprinkle in the ground cumin, chili powder, brown sugar, salt and pepper
4. Toss to coat
5. Preheat bbq
6. Grill to medium heat
7. Grill the chicken turning once, until fully cooked and browned - about 20 minutes or until a thermometer inserted into the chicken reads 165f
8. While the chicken cooks prepare the fresh salsa
9. Cut the cherry tomatoes in half and place in a medium size bowl
10. Peel and dice the avocado, dice the onion, mince the cilantro and gently combine all the fresh vegetables
11. Drizzle with fresh lime juice and toss again
12. Serve alongside the chicken<|endoftext|>
