Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Seasoned rice vinegar
• Sushi rice
• Mango
• Smoked salmon
• Pickled ginger
• Wasabi
• Soya sauce
• Orange juice
• Fresh lemon juice

Steps:
1. In small bowl, dissolve sugar in vinegar
2. Stir into the rice and set aside to cool
3. Wet hands with cold water
4. Place 2 tbsp
5. Of cooled rice into palm of one hand
6. Make a hollow and place a piece of mango in the hollow
7. Shape into a ball around the mango
8. Place a tiny ball of wasabi on the rice and drape a piece of smoked salmon over each ball
9. Serve with pickled ginger, and dipping sauce
10. For dipping sauce: whisk together all ingredients<|endoftext|>
