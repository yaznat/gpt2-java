What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Barky Burgers

Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Oatmeal
• Garlic cloves
• Mixed vegetables
• Ground beef
• Ground turkey
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Cook rice in chicken stock or water, covered over a low flame until done, about 20-25 minutes
2. Process blanched / cooked veggies in food processor until the bits are about the same size as the rice
3. This will help your pet absorb the nutrients
4. Mix with 2 eggs
5. Fold into rice mixture
6. Add ground meat and form into balls
7. If balls aren't holding together well, add the third egg and mix again
8. Form into round patties and grill, bake or broil until done
9. I use a large size scoop to make the patties equal size so they cook evenly
10. I refrigerate enough for 3 days worth of meals and then i pack away the rest in the freezer in one day servings and pull out what i'll need the night before to thaw
11. This can also be cooked in a loaf pan, sort of a meatloaf for your pup at 350 for 50-55 minutes or until an internal meat thermometer reaches 160<|endoftext|>
