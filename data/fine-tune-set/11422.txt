Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>All Bran Banana Bread

Ingredients:
• All-bran cereal
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Bicarbonate of soda
• 1% low-fat milk
• Caster sugar
• Egg
• Water
• Banana

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180 degree c
2. Combine dry ingredients and add egg, water, milk and banana
3. Mix until well combined
4. Spoon into a greased loaf tin
5. Bake in oven for 30-45 minutes or until cooked when tested<|endoftext|>
