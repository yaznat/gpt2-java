Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Pesto Fish

Ingredients:
• Fresh basil leaf
• Garlic cloves
• Roasted cashews
• Olive oil
• Parmesan cheese
• Lemon juice
• Salmon steaks

Steps:
1. Put basil, garlic and cashews into food processor
2. While processing pour the oil in a steady stream through the top, until well blended
3. Add the parmesan and process for 15 seconds
4. Spread pesto over both sides of fish and wrap securely in a buttered piece of foil
5. Place on preheated grill over medium heat for 15-20 minutes or until cooked through<|endoftext|>
