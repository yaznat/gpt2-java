Can you give me a casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Awesome Yam And Cranberry Casserole

Ingredients:
• Yams
• Fresh cranberries
• White sugar
• Orange
• Pecan halves
• Orange juice
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Mace

Steps:
1. Combine whole cranberries, white sugar, orange slices, pecan halves, orange juice, cinnamon, nutmeg and mace in 2 quart casserole
2. Bake uncovered at 375 degrees for 30 minutes
3. Stir drained yams into cranberry mixture
4. Return to oven and bake until heated through
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
