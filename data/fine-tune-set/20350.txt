What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Cheesy Tuna Casserole

Ingredients:
• Kraft macaroni and cheese
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Celery
• Butter
• Cream of celery soup
• Tuna
• Mushrooms

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Prepare mac and cheese as directed on the package, except using 3 / 4 cup milk
3. Cook onion, green or red pepper and celery in butter until tender
4. Add to the prepared mac and cheese the remaining ingredients
5. Mix well
6. Pour into a 1 1 / 2-quart casserole
7. Bake 30 minutes
8. Garnish with pepper rings if desired<|endoftext|>
