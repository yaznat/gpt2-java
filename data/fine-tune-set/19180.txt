What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Caramel Toffee Ice Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Chocolate graham cracker crumbs
• Sugar
• Egg white
• Butter
• Non-fat vanilla frozen yogurt
• English toffee-flavored candy bars
• Caramel ice cream topping

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine cracker crumbs and sugar
2. Stir in egg white and butter
3. Press onto the bottom and up the sides of a 9-in
4. Pie plate coated with cooking spray
5. Bake at 375 for 6-8 minutes or until set
6. Cool completely on a wire rack
7. Spread 2-2 / 3 cups of frozen yogurt into the crust
8. Sprinkle with half of the toffee bits
9. Drizzle with half of caramel
10. Repeat with remaining yogurt, toffee and caramel
11. Cover and freeze for 8 hours or overnight
12. Remove from the freezer 15 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
