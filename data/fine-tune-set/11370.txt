Ingredients:
• Stout beer
• Light molasses
• Baking soda
• All-purpose flour
• Ground ginger
• Baking powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground cardamom
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Dark brown sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Fresh ginger
• Cream cheese
• Unsalted butter
• Orange peel
• Powdered sugar
• Candied ginger

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Butter and flour three 8-inch pans
3. Bring beer and molasses to boil in a heavy medium-size non-reactive saucepan over high heat
4. Remove from heat and add baking soda
5. Let it stand about 1 hour until cool
6. Whisk flour and next 6 ingredients in a large bowl until blended
7. Whisk eggs and both sugars in another bowl until blended
8. Whisk in oil, then stout mixture
9. Gradually whisk liquid mixture into dry mixture
10. Stir in fresh ginger
11. Divide mixture evenly between the 3 cake pans and level tops
12. Bake until tester inserted into center of cakes comes out clean, about 25 minutes
13. Cool cakes in pans for 14 minutes, then invert onto cooling racks and allow to cool completely
14. For frosting:
15. Use electric mixer to beat cream cheese, butter, and orange peel in a large bowl until fluffy
16. Gradually beat in powdered sugar
17. Chill 30 minutes before frosting cake
18. Decorate with candied ginger arranged in a circle at top edge of cake<|endoftext|>
