What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Artichoke Casserole

Ingredients:
• Artichoke hearts
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Breadcrumbs
• Parsley
• Basil
• Lemon juice
• Parmesan cheese
• Chicken bouillon powder
• Hot water
• Butter

Steps:
1. Lightly brown garlic in olive oil
2. Add ingredients through the parmesan cheese and brown a little bit
3. Add the bouillon dissolved in hot water and the melted butter
4. Mix together and put in casserole
5. Bake in 350 oven for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
