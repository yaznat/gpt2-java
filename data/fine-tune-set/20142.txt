Ingredients:
• Scallions
• Garlic
• Fresh ginger
• Soy sauce
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Fresh lemon juice
• Dark sesame oil
• Water
• Honey
• Salt
• Vermicelli
• Tofu
• Peanut oil
• Asparagus
• Mushrooms
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine the scallions, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, red pepper, lemon juice, sesame oil, water, sugar or honey, and salt
2. Stir in the tofu
3. Cover and let stand for at least 15 minutes
4. Cook the noodles in plenty of boiling, salted water until tender
5. Drain, rinse in warm water, and drain again
6. Place a large wok over medium heat and wait a minute or two
7. Add 2 tablespoons peanut or canola oil, then turn up the heat
8. Add the asparagus and mushrooms, and stir-fry for several minutes until the asparagus is just tender
9. Add the drained noodles and stir-fry for about 3 more minutes, keeping the heat high
10. Pour in the entire bowlful of marinade-plus-tofu
11. Cook and stir another 3 minutes or so, or until the sauce is well distributed and everything is heated through
12. Serve immediately, topped with toasted sesame seeds and / or cashews<|endoftext|>
