What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Banana Caramel Self Saucing Pudding

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• White sugar
• Baking powder
• Banana
• Milk
• Unsalted butter
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Light muscovado sugar
• Golden syrup
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 180c
2. Sift the flour into a bowl with the sugar, baking powder and a pinch of salt
3. Add the banana, milk, butter, egg and vanilla extract and whisk until well combined
4. Pour the mixture into a buttered 1
5. 5 litre baking dish
6. To make the sauce, put the light muscovado sugar, golden syrup and 250 ml water in a small pan and bring to the boil
7. Pour the boiling mixture carefully over the pudding
8. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes until it springs back when lightly pressed
9. Vanilla ice cream is just right with this hot sticky pudding<|endoftext|>
