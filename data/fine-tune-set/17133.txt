Ingredients:
• Butter
• Confectioners' sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Nuts
• Vanilla caramels

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400
2. Mix thoroughly butter, confectioners' sugar and vanilla
3. Work in flour, salt and nuts until dough holds together
4. Cut each vanilla caramel into 4 small pieces
5. Divide dough in 36 portions and mold each around a small piece of caramel
6. Bake 10-12 minutes until set but not brown
7. While warm, roll in confectioner's sugar or sugar crystals and cool
8. Makes 36 cookies<|endoftext|>
