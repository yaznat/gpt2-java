Ingredients:
• Pork baby back ribs
• Seasoning salt
• Pepper
• Ketchup
• Hoisin sauce
• Lemon juice
• Sugar
• Fresh garlic
• Louisiana hot sauce
• Habanero sauce
• Cinnamon
• Allspice

Steps:
1. Set oven to 425f
2. Line a baking sheet with foil, then arrange the ribs in a single layer
3. Season with season salt and pepper
4. Bake for about 25 minutes
5. Turn the ribs over and bake for another 15 minutes or until browned
6. Meanwhile in a bowl combine ketchup, hoisin, lemon juice, garlic, louisiana hot sauce, habanero chili hot sauce, cinnamon, allspice and seasoned salt
7. Mix well to combine
8. Baste the ribs with about half the sauce
9. Cook for another 10 minutes more
10. Turn over and baste with the remaining sauce and continue to bake for another 15 minutes or until the ribs are thoroughly cooked through<|endoftext|>
