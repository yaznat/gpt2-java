Ingredients:
• Onion
• Cayenne pepper
• Coriander
• Mustard seeds
• Fenugreek seeds
• Cumin
• Cinnamon
• Peppercorns
• Cloves
• Turmeric
• Paprika
• Tamarind paste
• Fresh gingerroot
• Oil
• Frozen cauliflower
• Coconut milk

Steps:
1. Combine all but the last three ingredients in a food processor
2. Run the processor until it forms a paste
3. Add a little oil and / or a little water if ingredients have trouble processing
4. Saute in a pan with the cauliflower and oil for a few minutes until aromatic
5. Add the coconut milk and simmer until cauliflower reaches desired tenderness
6. If the curry gets too dry as it simmers, add a little water so that it finishes with a thick, gravy-like sauce<|endoftext|>
