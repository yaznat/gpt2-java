What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>3 2 1 Crock Pot Roast Beef

Ingredients:
• Roast beef
• Beef broth
• Dry onion soup mix
• Pepsi

Steps:
1. Mix the broth, soup mix and the pepsi in the crock pot and stir to mix
2. Add the roast and cook on low for @ 5-7 hours
3. Turn occasionally
4. This is the basic recipe
5. I usually add crushed garlic and chopped onions
6. If you leave the roast for 7 hours, you will get a "pulled beef", just shred it with a fork, awesome on buns
7. You can also remove the roast after @3 hours and slice the roast and then return the slices to the crock pot for @3 more hours, roast beef fit for company!<|endoftext|>
