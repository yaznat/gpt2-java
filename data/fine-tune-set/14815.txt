What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Anytime Easy Egg Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Green onion
• Mushroom
• Broccoli
• Cooked rice
• Soy sauce
• Eggs
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Cook veggies in oil or spray in non-stick frying pan
2. Add rice, and stir-fry a couple of minutes
3. Add soy sauce or braggs seasoning to taste
4. Push everything to the side of the pan, and, tilting pan, cook eggs separately, combining when mostly cooked
5. Season with pepper<|endoftext|>
