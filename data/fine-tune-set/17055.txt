Ingredients:
• Currant jelly
• Dry red wine
• Lemon
• Lemon juice
• Prepared mustard
• Smoked link sausage
• Cold water
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Combine jelly, wine, lemon peel, lemon juice and mustard in a large pan
2. Bring to boil, stirring until jelly melts
3. Add sausages
4. Cover and simmer for 5 minutes
5. Stir water into cornstarch
6. Stir cornstarch mixture into sausage mixture
7. Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly
8. Stir and cook 2 minutes longer
9. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
