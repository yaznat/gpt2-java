Ingredients:
• Fresh lemon juice
• Dry white wine
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Red snapper fillets
• Fresh shrimp
• Onion
• Parsley
• Capers
• Dried basil
• Dried tarragon
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Mayonnaise
• Parmesan cheese
• Lemon wedge

Steps:
1. Make the marinade: whisk together the first four ingredients, adding salt and pepper to taste place the fish in one layer in a baking dish, skin side up
2. Pour all but 1 / 4 cup marinade over the fish and let them marinate, covered, in the refrigerator for 3 hours
3. Stir together the shrimp, onion, garlic, parsley, capers, basil, tarragon, crumbs, mayonnaise, parmesan and the remaining marinade
4. Salt and pepper to taste, and mix until combined well
5. Bake the fillets in the marinade, skin side down, in the middle of a preheated 400 degree oven for 10 minutes
6. Spread evenly with the topping and cook for 10 minutes more, until topping is light golden brown
7. Serve with lemon wedges<|endoftext|>
