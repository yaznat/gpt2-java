Please give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Aunt Martha S Microwave Boiled Custard

Ingredients:
• Sweet milk
• Egg yolks
• Flour
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Add 1 / 2 sugar to milk and cook 5 minutes on high in microwave
2. Add flour, mix well with remaining 1 / 2 cup sugar and cook 1 minute
3. Stir
4. Cook 1 more minute
5. Beat egg yolks well and add 1 cup of hot milk mixture to eggs
6. Mix well
7. Return to milk mixture
8. Cook to desired thickness, stirring every 30 seconds
9. If you make it too thick, you can always thin it down with milk<|endoftext|>
