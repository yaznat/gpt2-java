Give me a recipe for a taco.<|endoftext|>Beth S Taco Dip

Ingredients:
• Refried beans
• Sour cream
• Avocados
• Cheddar cheese
• Mild taco sauce
• Jalapeno sauce
• Black olives
• Scallion
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Chili powder
• Garlic powder
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Spread beans evenly on the bottom of a baking dish or casserole
2. Mash avocados with chili powder, onion, pepper and garlic
3. Spread avocado mixture over the beans
4. Next, layer the sour cream over the first two layers
5. Mix together the taco sauce, cheese, jalapeo sauce, olives, scallions, and tomatoes
6. Spread over the sour cream layer
7. Serve with tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
