What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Caribbean Cruise Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Vodka
• Melon liqueur
• Peach schnapps
• Blue curacao
• Pineapple juice
• Carbonated lemon-lime beverage
• Lime wedge
• Cherries

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except soda and garnishes and shake well
2. Pour into a cocktail glass over ice and add soda to taste
3. Garnish with a lime wedge and a cherry<|endoftext|>
