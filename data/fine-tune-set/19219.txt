Ingredients:
• Chocolate syrup
• Chocolate chips
• Crispy rice cereal
• Sour cream
• Chocolate chip ice cream

Steps:
1. Butter a 8-ince pie plate
2. In a small mirowave-safe bowl mix chocolate syrup and chocolate chips microwave on high until hot around mixture should be smooth
3. Set aside 1 / 4 cup of mixture
4. Mix the remaining chocolate mixture and cereal in a medium mixing bowl you are coating the cereal with chocolate
5. Press the mixture over bottom and sides of the pie plate
6. Freeze until firm this should take around 10-15 minutes
7. Now combine the chocolate you set aside and the sour cream in a small bowl and mix well
8. Now on the pie crust spread 1 / 2 the ice cream in the pie pan
9. Now drizzle about 1 / 2 the sour cream mixture onto it
10. Top the rest of the ice cream on it and drizzle the remaining sour cream mixture onto it
11. Freeze pie until firm
12. This should take about an hour<|endoftext|>
