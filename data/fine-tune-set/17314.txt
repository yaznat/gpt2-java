Ingredients:
• Round steaks
• Salt
• Pepper
• Flour
• Milk
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Beef broth

Steps:
1. Cut steak in serving size pieces
2. In shallow bowl mix flour
3. Salt and pepper
4. In another bowl, place beaten eggs and milk
5. Heat oil in 10 inch skillet
6. Put steak in flour, then eggs, then flour again
7. Fry on med till browned
8. Place in baking pan
9. Put onions over steak
10. Pour broth on onions
11. Cover and bake at 350 for 60 minutes
12. Uncover, stir, and bake 30 minutes more<|endoftext|>
