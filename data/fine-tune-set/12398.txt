What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Alton Brown S Lentil Soup

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Carrot
• Celery
• Kosher salt
• Lentils
• Tomatoes
• Chicken broth
• Vegetable broth
• Coriander
• Cumin
• Grains of paradise

Steps:
1. With salt, sweat the onion, carrot, and celery in hot olive oil
2. Add the broth, tomatoes, lentils and seasonings
3. Bring to a boil
4. Reduce heat and cover, cooking about 35-45 minutes
5. Blend if desired<|endoftext|>
