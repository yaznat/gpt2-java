Please give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>Angel Pasta With Lobster Sauce

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Lobster tails
• Fresh mushrooms
• Green onions
• Sweet red peppers
• Sun-dried tomato
• Unsalted butter
• Olive oil
• Bacon
• Whipping cream
• Fresh basil
• Lemon juice
• Pepper
• Fresh spinach leaves
• Lemon slice

Steps:
1. Cook the pasta according to directions on the package, then set aside and keep warm
2. Place the lobster tails in boiling water and simmer for 12-15 minutes
3. Drain and remove the meat from the shells
4. Cut the meat into bite-sized pieces and set aside
5. Cook the mushrooms, onions, red and green pepper and sun-dried tomatoes in the butter and oil in a large frying pan over medium heat
6. Stir constantly until tender
7. Add lobster and bacon
8. Stir well
9. Add pasta, whipping cream, basil, lemon juice, and pepper
10. Continue stirring until all ingredients are heated through
11. Serve over fresh spinach leaves, if desired, and garnish with lemon slices<|endoftext|>
