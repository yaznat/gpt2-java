Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Tofu Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Cornstarch
• Sesame oil
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Frozen broccoli
• Baby portabella mushrooms
• Red pepper
• Extra firm tofu
• Green onion

Steps:
1. In 2-cup measuring cup, combine broth, soy sauce, brown sugar and cornstarch, until well-blended
2. In nonstick 12-inch skillet, heat sesame oil over medium-high heat
3. Add tofu and cook, stirring frequently, until heated through and lightly browned, about 4 minutes
4. Transfer to bowl
5. Add red pepper, green onions, garlic, and ginger to skillet and stir-fry until vegetables are tender-crisp, about 3 minutes longer
6. Add mushrooms and stir-fry 3 minutes
7. Add broth mixture and tofu and stir-fry until sauce has thickened slightly and boils, about 3 minutes<|endoftext|>
