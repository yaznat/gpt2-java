Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Sea scallops
• Seasoned bread crumbs
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Bacon drippings
• Onion
• Flour
• Skim milk
• Brie cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Wrap each scallop with piece of partially cooked bacon
2. Secure with toothpicks
3. For sauce, in medium skillet heat oil, butter, and bacon drippings to medium and saute onion for about 3 minutes until tender
4. Stir in flour
5. Heat while stirring for about 2 minutes to make roux
6. Pour in milk and heat until thickened, about 3-5 minutes
7. Reduce heat
8. Stir in brie and cook until melted through stirring often
9. Season to taste
10. Meanwhile for scallops, heat oil and butter in large flat skillet over medium heat
11. Dip each bacon wrapped scallop in bread crumbs to cover top and bottom
12. Press to make stick if needed
13. Place scallops in heated skillet and brown about 1-2 minutes per side
14. Place lid on skillet and reduce heat to medium low and cook about five more minutes
15. Pour some sauce on bottom of plate
16. Top with cooked scallops
17. Garnish with paprika or chopped chives<|endoftext|>
