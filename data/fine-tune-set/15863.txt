Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Burritos Panzon

Ingredients:
• Garlic clove
• Green bell peppers
• Onion
• Oil
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Pepper
• Eggs
• Cheddar cheese
• Pico de gallo
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Chop the green bell pepper and onion into a large dice
2. Fry in the oil with the minced garlic over med-high heat
3. Meanwhile, dice the tomato
4. When the peppers and onions are soft add the tomato
5. Season with salt and pepper and put the mixture aside in a bowl
6. Add a little more oil to the pan if necessary and scramble your 6 eggs, cook over med-high heat stirring constantly
7. Heat up your tortillas in the microwave individually as you assemble the burritos
8. To assemble: put 1 / 4 of the scrambled eggs on the tortilla, sprinkle with 1 / 3 cup cheddar cheese, cover with the pepper / onion mix and roll up
9. It will be huge
10. This is a knife and fork burrito for sure!
11. Sauce with about 1 / 3- 1 / 2 cup pico de gallo and serve with pinto beans on the side
12. Yummy!<|endoftext|>
