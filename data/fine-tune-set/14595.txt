Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>1949 Spanish Rice

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Spanish onion
• Green pepper
• Tomatoes
• Cooked rice
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degrees
2. Cook rice
3. Reserve in large 6 quart bowl
4. Cover so it does not dry out
5. Grease a 4 quart casserole dish, set aside
6. Fry bacon until crisp
7. Cool and crumble into pieces the size of your thumb nail
8. Reserve to add back into mixture
9. Cook onion and green pepper in reserved bacon fat
10. Cook slowly until onion is soft and yellow
11. Add cans of tomatoes and heat until simmering
12. Cover
13. Add crumbled bacon, salt and pepper to the reserved rice mixture, stirring well to combine
14. Pour hot onion, pepper and tomato mixture into rice / bacon
15. Stir well to combine
16. Place mixture in prepared casserole dish
17. Sprinkle the shredded cheddar cheese on top
18. Bake for 30 minutes until cheese is melted and slightly browned and mixture is bubbly
19. Remove from oven
20. Allow to set for 10 minutes before serving
21. This freezes exceptionally well! cut into portions
22. Freeze
23. Heat on medium in microwave for 3-5 minutes<|endoftext|>
