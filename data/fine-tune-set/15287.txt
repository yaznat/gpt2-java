What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Lighter Hamburger Stroganoff

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Butter
• Wine
• Onion
• Mushrooms
• Flour
• Fat-free chicken broth
• Nonfat sour cream
• No yolks egg noodle substitute

Steps:
1. Melt butter in large nonstick skillet
2. Add wine and onions
3. Cook until soft
4. Remove from skillet, set aside
5. Add meat to skillet
6. Cook thoroughly
7. Stir in flour
8. Slowly add broth while stirring
9. Add onion and drained mushrooms
10. Cook over med-low until thickened, stirring occasionally
11. Remove from heat
12. Add sour cream and heat through
13. Serve over cooked noodles<|endoftext|>
