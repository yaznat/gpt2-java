Ingredients:
• 2% low-fat milk
• Egg substitute
• Sugar
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Sugar-free mocha instant coffee
• Kahlua
• Chocolate

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Combine all ingredients except grated chocolate in a medium bowl
3. Whisk until foamy and instant coffee has dissolved
4. Pour into 6 individual custard cups
5. Place cups in 13x9 baking dish and fill with hot water halfway up sides of cups
6. Bake 55-60 minutes or until knife comes out clean
7. Garnish with grated chocolate, if desired
8. Serve warm or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
