What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>After The Holidays Turkey Salad

Ingredients:
• Cooked turkey
• Reduced-fat mayonnaise
• Reduced-fat sour cream
• Paprika
• Seasoning salt
• Celery rib
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Green onions
• Candied walnuts
• Red seedless grapes
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. In a large bowl whisk together the mayonnaise with sour cream, paprika and seasoned salt
2. Add in all remaining ingredients except the pepper
3. Toss until combined
4. Season with black pepper to taste
5. Cover and refrigerate for a minumum of 3 or more hours before serving<|endoftext|>
