Ingredients:
• Butter
• Mushroom
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Onion
• Dried dill weed
• Breadcrumbs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Fish fillets
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Melt 1 / 4 cup of the butter in a large frying pan over medium-high heat
3. Add mushrooms and saute until limp
4. Add onion, dill, bread crumbs, salt, and pepper
5. Stir until thoroughly mixed
6. Add thawed and drained spinach, mix but do not cook spinach
7. Spray shallow round cake pan with cooking spray
8. Spread spinach mixture over bottom
9. Arrange fish on top of spinach
10. Melt remaining butter and add lemon juice
11. Brush fish with lemon butter and bake, uncovered, basting several times with more lemon butter until fish flakes when prodded in the thickest portion with a fork
12. Cooking time is approximately 10 minutes per inch of fish thickness
13. Remove from oven and pour any remaining lemon butter over fish
14. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
