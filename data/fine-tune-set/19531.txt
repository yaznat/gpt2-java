Give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>Aloo Ka Raita Potatoes In Yogurt

Ingredients:
• Curds
• Potatoes
• Mint leaves
• Green chili
• Ground cumin
• Black pepper
• Chat masala
• Salt

Steps:
1. Add the salt, pepper powder, chaat masala to the yoghurt and mix well
2. Taste and adjust seasoning
3. Add the potatoes, green chili and mint leaves
4. Mix well
5. Transfer to a serving bowl
6. Garnish with roasted cumin powder
7. Refrigerate until required<|endoftext|>
