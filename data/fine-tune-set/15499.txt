Give me a burger recipe.<|endoftext|>Basic Hamburgers

Ingredients:
• Beef
• Onion
• Water
• Salt
• Worcestershire sauce
• Pepper
• Horseradish
• Mustard
• Chives
• Blue cheese
• Sesame seeds
• Ripe olives
• Dill pickle
• Pickle relish
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients together
2. Shape mixture into 6 patties, each about 3 / 4-inch thick
3. Broil or grill patties 4-inches from the heat, turning once, to desired doneness, 10 to 15 minutes
4. Nice served on toasted buns with favorite toppings<|endoftext|>
