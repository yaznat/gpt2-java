Ingredients:
• Coconut milk
• Vegetable broth
• Sugar
• Salt
• Red pepper flakes
• Garlic cloves
• Scallions
• Gingerroot
• Water
• Curry powder
• Vegetable oil
• Extra firm tofu
• Salt and pepper
• Red onions
• Red bell pepper
• Carrots
• Snow peas

Steps:
1. For the sauce: whisk together the first five ingredients
2. For the stir-fry: combine the garlic, scallions, ginger, water, curry and 1 tablespoon of the oil and set aside
3. Toss the tofu with salt and pepper to taste
4. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a wok or non-stick skillet over high heat
5. Add the tofu and cook, turning the cubes to lightly brown on several sides, about 5 minutes
6. Transfer the tofu to a bowl
7. Add the last tablespoon of oil to the skillet and heat until it's shimmering
8. Add the carrots, onion and pepper and cook until they begin to soften - about 2-3 minutes
9. Add the peas and immediately clear a space in the center of the wok or skillet by pushing the vegetables to the side
10. Pour the garlic mixture into the middle and cook until fragrant - about 30 seconds
11. Stir to combine the garlic mixture and the vegetables
12. Return the tofu to the skillet
13. Whisk the coconut sauce to recombine it and add it to the skillet
14. Toss the ingredients together until well coated with the sauce
15. Adjust seasoning to taste and serve<|endoftext|>
