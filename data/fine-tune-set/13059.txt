Ingredients:
• Red snapper
• Garlic salt
• Butter
• Lemon pepper
• Shallots
• Mushroom
• Parsley
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Make 3 diagonal cuts, about an inch apart on each side of the fish, in the thickest part
2. Sprinkle inside of fish with garlic salt and lemon pepper
3. In 1 tblsp of the butter, gently fry shallots and mushrooms until softened
4. Stir in parsley
5. Spoon mixture into cavity of fish and add 2 or 3 slices of lemon
6. Secure opening with small skewers
7. Lightly butter a piece of heavy foil large enough to enclose the fish
8. Put fish in centre and squeeze over juice from the rest of the lemon
9. Bring edges of foil up and fold loosely over fish, enclosing it completely
10. Bbq until fish is tender and flakes when tested with a fork 30-40 minutes<|endoftext|>
