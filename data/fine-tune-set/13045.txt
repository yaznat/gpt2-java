Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked White Fish

Ingredients:
• White fish fillet
• Tomatoes
• Sugar
• Parsley
• Butter
• Flour
• Sweet paprika
• Old bay seasoning
• Salt
• Pepper
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Grease an 8 1 / 2" x 11" pan
2. Cover the bottom of the baking dish with the sliced tomatoes
3. Sprinkle with sugar and parsley
4. Arrange fish on top
5. Mix melted butter, paprika, old bay seasoning, salt, pepper and flour
6. Spread over fish
7. Bake at 400f for 20 min or until fish flakes easily with fork
8. Squeeze lemon juice over casserole before serving and serve with additional lemon wedges
9. After a minute or so the skins will slip right off
10. For steaks, use a pan that will be nicely filled by one layer of fish
11. Makes a quick family supper dish when time is short and nothing is thawed
12. I like this with a green vegetable and white rice<|endoftext|>
