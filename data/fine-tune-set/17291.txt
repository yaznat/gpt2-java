Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Butter
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Rosemary
• Olive oil
• Milk
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Mix the flour, baking soda, salt, garlic powder, black pepper, and rosemary
2. Cut up the the butter and then mix into the dry ingredients until the butter is decimated into chunks no bigger than a centimetre
3. Mix in the olive oil
4. Mix in the cheddar cheese
5. Mix in the milk slowly, the batter should be moist but not over-saturated in milk
6. Bake for 15-17 minutes at 400f<|endoftext|>
