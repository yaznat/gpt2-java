Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Oatmeal
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Fresh lemon juice
• Heavy cream
• Eggs
• Apples
• Golden raisin
• Pecans
• Lemon zest

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients together thoroughly
2. Add lemon juice to cream and let sit for 2 minutes
3. While cream sits, beat eggs into the milk
4. Combine with cream and vanilla
5. Toss apples, raisins, pecans, and lemon zest with the dry mix
6. Preheat lightly-greased griddle medium to medium-high
7. Pour liquid mix into dry mix and stir together well to make a thick but fluid batter
8. Do not overmix or pancakes will not be as light
9. Ladle batter onto griddle to make pancakes about 4 inches in diameter
10. Turn when bubbles appear all over the pancake
11. Cook until done in the middle adjusting heat if necessary to avoid burning
12. Continue, greasing griddle as necessary, until all pancakes are cooked
13. Batter may thicken as it stands
14. It may be necessary to thin it with a little additional milk if it gets so thick that the pancakes dont cook properly in the center
15. Leftovers can be reheated in the microwave or toaster oven
16. Do not use regular toaster because of the fruit
17. Leftovers can be eaten cold for breakfast or snacks
18. Leftovers can be frozen flat on a baking sheet lined with waxed paper then loaded into freezer bags to use as needed<|endoftext|>
