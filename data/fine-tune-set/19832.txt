Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Mccormick's montreal brand steak seasoning
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic clove
• Red onion
• Goat cheese
• Yellow mustard
• Hawaiian rolls
• Sweet potato
• Olive oil
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Dried basil

Steps:
1. Pre-heat the oven to 400 degrees for the fries, and the bbq
2. For the fries: place the sweet potato matchsticks on a cookie sheet lined with aluminum foil
3. Drizzle the olive oil over the fries, and add all the spices
4. Pretty much, add the amount of spice your family will like
5. I usually shake each bottle a few times
6. Use your hands to mix everything up, and make sure the fries are evenly coated
7. End with an even layer spread out on the foil for even cooking
8. Cook in oven for 20 minutes, or until soft
9. For the burgers:
10. Combine all burger ingredients into a medium size bowl, and mix well
11. Shape into small balls that will fit on each hawaiian roll
12. Cook / bbq to your preferred doneness
13. For the construction:
14. Slice each roll in half
15. Place a small amount of yellow mustard on the bottom half, layer the meat on next, followed by small slices of the red onion
16. Spread the goat cheese on the top half, and place on the burger
17. Of course, you can use any kind of cheese, mustard / condiments you want to make these
18. This is the combo my husband and i really like
19. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
