Can you give me a custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Chili Corn Custard Solo

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Fresh corn
• Egg
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Hot sauce
• Evaporated skim milk
• Nonfat dry milk powder
• Onions
• Roasted red pepper
• Canadian bacon
• Cheddar cheese
• Green chili
• Canned shrimp

Steps:
1. Place oven rack in the center
2. Heat oven to 350 degrees
3. Grease a 2 cup casserole dish with the canola oil
4. Add the corn to the bottom of the dish
5. If you are using one of the optional ingredients, mix with corn and add at this step
6. In a small bowl beat together the egg, chili powder, salt and hot sauce
7. Make sure the eggs are well blended with no streaking of whites or yolks
8. If you are using non-fat milk mix in the dry milk powder checking that there are no lumps
9. Beat the milk into the egg mixture
10. Add in the roasted red peppers and minced onions to the custard mix
11. Pour custard over the corn and any additions you may have added
12. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes
13. Custards should be puffed but just barely set in the centers
14. Remove from oven and let stand for 5 to 10 minutes
15. Custard will set up during this time
16. For vegetarian do not use the ham or shrimp options<|endoftext|>
