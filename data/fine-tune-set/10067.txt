Ingredients:
• Ancho chilies
• Cascabel chiles
• Dried arbol chiles
• Whole cumin seeds
• Garlic powder
• Dried oregano
• Smoked paprika

Steps:
1. Toast the chiles and cumin seeds in a cast iron skillet for a few minutes
2. Don't let them cook too long and inhale the smoke, because you'll get a nasty chemical burn in lungs, nose and throat
3. Let the chiles and seeds cool
4. Place all the ingredients into a blender and blend on the highest speed you blender can dish out
5. Store in an airtight jar
6. Be sure to wash you blender carafe a few times after doing this or you fruit smoothy or margarita will taste like chiles<|endoftext|>
