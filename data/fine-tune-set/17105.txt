What's a good cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Delicious Shrimp Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Large raw shrimp
• Ketchup
• Water
• White onions
• Plum tomato
• Fresh cilantro
• Lime juice
• Hot sauce
• Salt
• Hass avocadoes
• Saltines

Steps:
1. Bring water to a boil, add salt and shrimp
2. Boil for 3-4 mins or until pink, do not overcook
3. Strain shrimp out of the water
4. Shock the shrimp in icy cold water, to stop the cooking process
5. Remove the tails
6. Run water through a strainer to strain the grits
7. Put warm water and entire bottle of ketchup in a glass container, and whisk until well blended
8. To this sauce add in the onion, tomato, cilantro, lime juice, and hot sauce
9. Stir
10. Last but not least add the shrimp
11. Seal the container and allow it to sit in the fridge for 6hrs-overnight
12. The longer the better
13. Serve with the avocados and crackers and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
