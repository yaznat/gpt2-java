Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Mix Cookies

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Peanut butter
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Water
• Brownie mix
• Chocolate chips
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Beat softened cream cheese and peanut butter
3. Add eggs and vanilla
4. Mix in brownie mixture
5. Add water as needed to make a thick dough
6. Add chips and walnuts as desired
7. Bake for 10 minutes or until firm without overcooking<|endoftext|>
