Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Flax Meal Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Flax seed meal
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Heat up your waffle iron
2. Mix dry ingredients
3. Blend in wet ingredients
4. Pour about 3 / 4 cup onto iron & cook according to manufacturer's directions
5. I also usually add chocolate chips<|endoftext|>
