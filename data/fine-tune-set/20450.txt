Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple And Pecan Breakfast Casserole

Ingredients:
• Tart cooking apples
• Pecans
• Golden raisin
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Margarine
• Eggs
• Orange juice
• Flour
• Salt
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, saute apples, pecans, raisins, brown sugar, cinnamon, and margarine until apples begin to soften, stir often, approximately 6 minutes
2. Place in a buttered 9x13 baking dish
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine eggs, orange juice, salt, and flour and beat till mixture is smooth, and stir around edges of bowl
4. Pour over apple mixture
5. Sprinkle with 4 tablespoons sugar, and cinnamon, about 1 tablespoon
6. Bake uncovered at 400 degrees for 20-25 minutes or until a knife inserted near center of casserole, comes out clean
7. Serve with maple syrup<|endoftext|>
