Ingredients:
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Golden brown sugar
• Walnuts
• Ground cinnamon
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Unsalted butter
• Egg
• Bananas
• Buttermilk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Butter and flour 8x8x2-inch metal baking pan
3. Stir chocolate chips, brown sugar, walnuts, and cinnamon in small bowl until well blended
4. Set streusel aside
5. Sift all purpose flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt into medium bowl
6. Using electric mixer, beat sugar, room temperature butter, and egg in large bowl until fluffy
7. Beat in mashed bananas and buttermilk
8. Add dry ingredients and blend well
9. Spread half of batter in prepared baking pan
10. Sprinkle with half of streusel
11. Repeat with remaining batter and streusel
12. Bake coffee cake until tester inserted into center comes out clean, about 45 minutes
13. Cool coffee cake in pan on rack<|endoftext|>
