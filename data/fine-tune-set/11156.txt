Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Denver Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Ham
• Red bell pepper
• Green onion
• Eggs
• Milk
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Arrange 4 slices of the bread, buttered-side down, in an 8" or 9" square pan
2. Sprinkle with cheese, ham, bell pepper, and green onion
3. Top with remaining slices of bread, buttered-side up
4. Beat eggs with remaining ingredients and pour mixture over the sandwiches, cover and chill in the fridge for up to 8 hours or bake at once at 375 degrees for 35-45 minutes<|endoftext|>
