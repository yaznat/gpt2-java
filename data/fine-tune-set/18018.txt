Ingredients:
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Semisweet chocolate
• Sugar
• Milk
• Butter
• Egg
• Salt
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Combine breadcrumbs, chocolate chunks, sugar& milk in the top of a double boiler until chocolate is melted
2. Stir in butter
3. In a small dish, beat egg& salt together and add into chocolate mixture, along with walnuts
4. Pour into a greased 1 quart dish
5. Set dish in a pan filled with 1" of water and bake at 350 degrees for about 55 minutes, until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
