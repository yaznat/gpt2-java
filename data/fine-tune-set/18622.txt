Ingredients:
• Butter
• Maple syrup
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Pie apples
• Cinnamon
• French bread
• Eggs
• Half-and-half
• Sugar
• Salt

Steps:
1. Boil butter, brown sugar, and maple syrup over medium heat for one minute
2. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla
3. Pour into greased 9 x 13 baking pan
4. Evenly sprinkle apple pieces over caramel layer
5. Sprinkle cinnamon to taste over apples
6. Sprinkle 1-2 tsp cinnamon over bread cubes and mix together
7. Put bread cubes over apple layer
8. Make sure you use all the bread, cramming it in there tight--otherwise the bread pudding will not turn out as well
9. Mix remaining ingredients in a blender
10. Slowly pour the mixture over the bread cubes
11. It should be enough to completely saturate all the bread
12. If not, add enough half and half to the pan to accomplish this
13. Let sit for 20-30 minutes
14. At this point, you can cover the pan and refrigerate overnight
15. Bake at 350 for 45-50 minutes, or until nicely browned and a knife inserted in the bread pudding comes out clean
16. Immediately run a knife around the edges to loosen and turn out onto serving platter and serve warm
17. Be careful, the caramel on the bottom is very hot and liquid at this point<|endoftext|>
