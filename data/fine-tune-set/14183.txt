Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Roasted cumin
• Coriander
• Garlic
• Paprika
• Salt
• Chili seasoning mix
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Marinate tofu with spices and salsa for about 30 minutes
2. Place in a lightly grilled skillet and saute triangles until well browned on both sides<|endoftext|>
