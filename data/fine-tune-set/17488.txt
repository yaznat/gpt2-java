Can you give me a coffee recipe?<|endoftext|>Black And White Coffee Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• White sugar
• Light brown sugar
• Egg
• Instant coffee powder
• Pure vanilla extract
• Ground coffee
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• White chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Lightly grease 2 baking sheets or line them with parchment paper
3. Sift together the flour, baking soda, and salt into a small bowl
4. Set aside
5. In a large bowl, with an electric mixer on medium speed, cream together the butter and sugars until light and fluffy, scraping the bowl to make sure the batter is evenly blended
6. Add the egg, instant coffee-vanilla mixture and ground coffee and blend on medium speed about 1 minute
7. Add the flour mixture and mix on medium speed until blended, about 1 minute
8. Stir in both kinds of chocolate chips with a wooden spoon
9. Drop the dough by tablespoonfuls onto the baking sheets, about 2 inches apart
10. Bake 1 sheet at a time for 10 to 12 minutes, or until the cookies are golden around the edges
11. Cool cookies on wire racks<|endoftext|>
