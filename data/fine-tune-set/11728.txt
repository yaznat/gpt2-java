Give me a cake recipe.<|endoftext|>Alien Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Bisquick baking mix
• Egg
• Milk

Steps:
1. Pour 1 teaspoon of batter on the grill in kind of an elongated oval shape
2. Right next to it pour another teaspoonful
3. These are the eyes
4. Allow "eyes" to cook until they are bubble and looking dry around the edges
5. Pour 1 / 4 c batter over the "eyes" pulling the batter down at the bottom to make a chin
6. Optionally to make a more uniform chin you can choose to arrange the eyes so the chin falls into the corner of the pan and use that for shaping the chin
7. Cook until bubbly and edges are dry
8. Turn and cook until golden brown<|endoftext|>
