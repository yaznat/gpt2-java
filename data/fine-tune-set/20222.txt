What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Carolyn S Stir Fry Rice

Ingredients:
• Roll of pork sausage
• Onion
• Carrot
• Broccoli
• Red bell peppers
• Stir fry vegetables
• Cooked rice
• Light soy sauce
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Cook sausage in skillet
2. When no longer pink dump out on a plate with two or three paper napkins to absorb grease
3. Put meat in large pot
4. Leaving fat drippings in skillet, the defrosted veggies, onions and soy sauce
5. Stir-fry for 4 minutes then add to pot with meat, mixing
6. Cook rice on stove from package directions then let rice stand five minutes
7. Make scrambled eggs, however your family likes them
8. Add cooked rice and crumbled scrambled eggs to veggies and meat mixture then sprinkle with more lite soy sauce, mix together and heat throughly
9. If there's a vegetable you don't like add something you do, very versatile dish!<|endoftext|>
