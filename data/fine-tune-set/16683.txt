Please give me a recipe for a smoothie.<|endoftext|>Berry Yogurt Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Frozen unsweetened raspberries
• Blackberry
• Vanilla yogurt
• 2% low-fat milk
• Honey
• Orange zest
• Vanilla extract
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in blender
2. Blend until thick and smooth, about 2 minutes
3. Pour into 2 large glasses
4. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
