Ingredients:
• Leeks
• Olive oil
• White fish fillets
• Coconut milk
• Mango
• Coriander
• Basil
• Lime juice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 230 degrees c
2. Cut roots and tough green leaves from leeks
3. Cut leek into julienne strips, then soak in cold water for 10 minutes to release any trapped dirt or sand
4. Drain well
5. Place 1 / 4 cup olive oil into a saucepan and add the leeks
6. Stir over a high heat unitl the leeks have wilted slightly and are coated in oil
7. Lower the heat, season and cook slowly for 30 minutes or until caramelised
8. Meawhile, oil a baking dish large enough to hold the fish, arrange the fish, then season
9. Pour the coconut milk over the fish and bake in the oven for 10 minutes
10. Slice mango finely into strips and mix with the chopped coriander and basil
11. Add lime juice and mix well
12. Remove the fish from the oven and heap mango mixture on each fillet
13. Return to the oven for 5 minutes
14. To serve, place a small mound of caramelised leeks in the centre of the plate, then place each fish fillet on the top
15. Make sure each fillet has a good quantity of mango mixture on the top<|endoftext|>
