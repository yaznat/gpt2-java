Give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>Apple And Fennel Roasted Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Fennel bulb
• Fennel leaves
• Red onion
• Canola oil
• Pork tenderloin
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Cider vinegar

Steps:
1. Position racks in upper and lower thirds of oven
2. Preheat to 475f
3. Toss apples, sliced fennel and onion with 1 tablespoon oil in a large bowl
4. Spread out on a rimmed baking sheet
5. Roast on the lower oven rack, stirring twice, until tender and golden, 30 to 35 minutes
6. About 10 minutes after the apple mixture goes into the oven, sprinkle pork with salt and pepper
7. Heat the remaining 2 teaspoons oil in a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat
8. Sear the pork on one side, about 2 minutes
9. Turn the pork over and transfer the pan to the top oven rack
10. Roast until just barely pink in the center and an instant-read thermometer registers 145f, for 12 to 14 minutes
11. Transfer the pork to a cutting board and let rest for 5 minutes
12. Immediately stir vinegar into the pan, scraping up any browned bits, then add to the apple mixture
13. Thinly slice the pork
14. Serve with the apple mixture and sprinkle with fennel fronds<|endoftext|>
