Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Garlic
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Low sodium chicken broth
• White shoepeg corn
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Jalapeno
• Ground beef
• Black beans
• Kidney beans
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Saute onions and garlic in olive oil over medium heat until softened
2. Add cumin and chili powder to pan to toast
3. Add the rest of the ingredients and stir well
4. Simmer on low for at least 30 minutes
5. Serve in bowls and top with shredded cheddar cheese<|endoftext|>
