Give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Apple Honey Pie

Ingredients:
• Pie shells
• Sliced apples
• Eggs
• Plain yogurt
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Honey
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Spread apple slices over pie shell
2. Combine eggs, yogurt, cinnamon, salt and honey to make a custard mixture
3. Pour the custard mixture over the spples
4. Sprinkle with walnuts
5. Bake 45 minutes at 375 degree
6. Cool to room tempeture before cutting<|endoftext|>
