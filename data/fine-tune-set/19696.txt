Ingredients:
• Idaho potatoes
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Coarse salt
• Ground black pepper
• Lemons, zest of
• Italian parsley
• Garlic

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees f
2. Peel potatoes and cut into 3 / 8-inch thick slices cut again into 3 / 8-inch thick fries
3. Place the potatoes into a bowl with cold water
4. Just before cooking, drain water and place potatoes on paper towel, pat dry completely
5. Put in a bowl
6. Add olive oil, salt & pepper
7. Toss well and lay out in 1 layer on nonstick baking sheets
8. Bake until light brown, approximately 30 to 40 minutes, turning frequently until golden brown
9. Remove from oven
10. Add lemon zest, parsley, garlic & more salt and pepper if desired
11. Toss well and serve<|endoftext|>
