What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Brunch Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Semisweet chocolate morsel
• Butter
• Milk
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Chocolate shavings
• Confectioners' sugar
• Fresh fruit

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine flour, sugar, baking powder and salt
2. Microwave chocolate morsels and butter in a medium microwave-safe bowl on high heat for 1 minute
3. Stir
4. Microwave at additional 10-20 second intervals, stirring until smooth
5. Cool to room temperature
6. Stir in milk, eggs, and vanilla
7. Add chocolate mixture to flour mixture
8. Stir
9. Cook in a belgian or standard waffle maker according to manufacturer's directions
10. Serve warm with your choice of toppings<|endoftext|>
