Ingredients:
• Broccoli
• Garlic
• Onion
• Butter
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cheese whiz
• Mushrooms
• Slivered almonds
• Crouton

Steps:
1. Pre-stream broccoli for about 5 minutes
2. Saute onions and garlic in butter
3. Add soup, cheese whiz and mushrooms to sauteed onion mixture
4. Put broccoli into a greased casserole dish and pour sauce over it
5. Sprinkle the almonds over this and then sprinkle the croutons on top
6. Bake at 350 degf for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
