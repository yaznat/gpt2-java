Give me a pork recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Spiced Pork

Ingredients:
• No yolk noodles
• Pork tenderloin
• Celery
• Onions
• Canola oil
• Tart apples
• Raisins
• Brown sugar
• Seasoning salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Cornstarch
• Reduced-sodium beef broth
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Cook noodles according to package directyions, and drain
2. Meanwhile in a large skillet, brown pork with celery and onion in oil, and drain
3. Add apples, raisins, brown sugar, seasoned salt, and cinnamon
4. Cook and stir over medium heat for 8-10 minutes or until pork is no longer pink, and vegetables are tender
5. In a small bowl, combine cornstarch, and broth until smooth, and gradually add to the pork mixture
6. Bring to a boil, cook and stir 2 minutes or until thickened
7. Serve with noodles
8. Sprinkle with walnuts<|endoftext|>
