What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Banh Mi Style Chicken Sandwich

Ingredients:
• French baguettes
• Light mayonnaise
• Hot sauce
• Chicken breasts
• Red chili sauce
• Jalapeno
• English cucumber
• Mint leaf
• Cilantro leaf
• Lettuce
• Carrot
• Seasoned rice vinegar

Steps:
1. To make sandwiches, spread both sides of the baguettes with mayonnasise and hot sauce
2. Arrange chicken over bottom halves and sprinkle with chili sauce
3. Add jalapenos, cucumber slices and herbs and then drizzle with more sauce
4. Toss lettuce and carrots with vinegar and then add to the sandwiches, topping with more chili sauce, if desired
