Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Better Waffle

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Ground oats
• Ground flax seeds
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Almond milk
• Sweet potato puree
• Coconut oil

Steps:
1. Combine dry ingredients, make well
2. Fold in wet ingredients until smooth
3. Bake until steam no longer comes out of waffle iron<|endoftext|>
