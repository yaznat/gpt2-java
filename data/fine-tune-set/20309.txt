What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chili Seafood Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• King prawns
• Scallops
• Black pepper
• Five-spice powder
• Red thai chile
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Peanut oil
• Red pepper
• Yellow pepper
• Bean sprouts
• Asparagus spears
• Sugar snap peas
• Light soy sauce
• Sugar
• Fresh chives

Steps:
1. Combine seafood, pepper, five-spice, chilli, garlic and ginger in a bowl
2. Heat half the oil in a wok, stir-fry seafood in batches, until cooked, remove
3. Heat remaining oil in wok, stir-fry peppers until tender, add sprouts, asparagus and peas to wok, stir-fry 1 minute
4. Return seafood to wok with soy sauce and sugar, stir-fry until hot and veg are just tender stir in 1 tablespoon of chopped chives, save the rest to sprinkle over dishes
5. Please
6. To serve: arrange spoonfuls of veg on plate top with seafood<|endoftext|>
