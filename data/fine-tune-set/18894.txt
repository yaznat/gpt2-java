Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Active dry yeast
• Custard mix
• Salt
• Milk
• Water
• Butter
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Combine 2 cups flour, yeast, custard mix and salt in a large mixing bowl
2. Mix well
3. Heat milk, water, and 1 / 4 cup butter in saucepan until warm
4. Add milk mixture and eggs to flour mixture
5. Blend at low speed until moistened, then for 3 minutes at medium speed
6. By hand, stir in enough remaining flour to make a firm dough
7. Knead on floured board until smooth and elastic
8. Place in greased bowl, turning to grease top
9. Cover and let rise in warm place until doubled in bulk
10. For filling, combine 1 / 2 cup sugar and 1 tablespoon cinnamon, and set aside
11. Punch dough down and divide into two parts
12. On lightly floured surface, roll each half to a 14 x 7 inch rectangle
13. Spread each with 1 tablespoon softened butter and sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon filling
14. Roll up tightly, pressing dough into roll with each turn
15. Pinch ends and edges to seal
16. Place in greased 9 x 5 inch loaf pans
17. Cover and let rise in warm place until doubled in bulk
18. Bake at 375 for 35-40 minutes
19. Brush tops with melted butter and sprinkle with mixture of 1 1 / 2 tablespoons of sugar and 1 teaspoon of cinnamon
20. Remove from pans and cool on rack<|endoftext|>
