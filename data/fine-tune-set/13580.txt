Ingredients:
• Beef ravioli
• Marinara sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Fresh breadcrumb
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Lightly grease 8-inch-square baking dish
3. Combine hot pasta, sauce and cheese
4. Spoon into prepared baking dish
5. Combine breadcrumbs and oil in small bowl
6. Sprinkle over pasta mixture
7. Cover and bake for 20 minutes
8. Uncover
9. Bake for an additional 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
