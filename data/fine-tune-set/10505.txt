What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>20 Minute Pasta Bake

Ingredients:
• Ziti pasta
• Lean ground beef
• Pasta sauce
• Parmesan cheese
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Cook pasta 8 minutes while cooking ground beef over medium high heat
2. Mix meat, pasta, and sauce
3. Cover with cheese
4. Bake at 325 for 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
