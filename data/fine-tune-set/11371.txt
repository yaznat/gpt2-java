What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>365 Day Buttermilk Cornbread

Ingredients:
• Bacon drippings
• Eggs
• Yellow cornmeal
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Buttermilk

Steps:
1. This cornbread is best baked in a 10-inch cast-iron skillet but if you don't have one, a pyrex dish will do just fine
2. If you halve this recipe, use an 8-inch square dish
3. If not, use a 9x13-inch dish or pan
4. Preheat oven to 450f
5. Put the bacon drippings in your baking dish or skillet and let them melt while the oven is preheating and you're mixing up the batter
6. Beat the eggs in a medium bowl until frothy
7. Add the corn meal, salt, baking soda and baking powder, and stir to thoroughly combine
8. Add the buttermilk and stir well
9. Remove the hot pan from the oven
10. Swirl the pan to make sure it is coated with melted bacon drippings, and pour the bacon drippings into the batter
11. Stir well to combine
12. Pour the batter into the pan, and bake 20 to 25 minutes
13. Cornbread will begin to pull away from the sides of the pan
14. As soon as the leavening ingredients are combined, the chemical action that makes the cornbread rise is sparked
15. No need to hurry
16. Just don't mix up the batter and then make a phone call before you pop it in the oven<|endoftext|>
