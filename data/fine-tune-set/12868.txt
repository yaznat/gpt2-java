Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Dijon mustard
• Fresh rosemary
• Worcestershire sauce
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Lager beer
• Flank steaks

Steps:
1. Whisk first 7 ingredients in medium bowl, then whisk in beer
2. Pour marinade into large resealable plastic bag
3. Add steaks, seal bag, and chill 1 day, turning bag occasionally
4. Grill over medium-high heat for 5 minutes per side for medium rare
5. Alternatively, place steaks in hot grill pan or skillet and cook until well browned and thermometer inserted into center registers 125f to 130f for medium-rare, about 5 minutes per side
6. Transfer steaks to carving board and let rest 5 minutes
7. Slice steaks thinly across grain
8. Arrange on platter
