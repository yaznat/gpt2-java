What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Aloo Poshto Potatoes Recipe

Ingredients:
• Poppy seeds
• Oil
• Red chilies
• Turmeric powder
• Green chilies
• Potatoes
• Salt

Steps:
1. 1
2. Dry roast the poppy seeds on a tava for 3 to 4 minutes
3. 2
4. Grind coarsely in a mixer and keep aside
5. 3
6. Heat the oil in a pan, add the red chillies, turmeric powder, ground poppy seeds and green chillies and saut for 1 minute
7. 4
8. Add the potatoes and salt and toss well
9. 5
10. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
