Give me a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Diabetic Custard Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Liquid stevia
• Tapioca flour
• Eggs
• Whole milk
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Stir eggs and sweetener into flour
2. Set aside
3. Heat milk but do not boil
4. Pour hot milk into egg mixture
5. Stir to mix and return to medium heat
6. Stir constantly with a wooden spoon until mixture thickens
7. Remove from heat
8. Stir in vanilla
9. This completes the custard
10. Leave plain for vanilla ice cream or stir in 3 cups mashed fruit of your choice
11. Pour custard into ice cream maker canister and follow manufacturer's instructions for making the ice cream<|endoftext|>
