Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Mayonnaise
• Buttermilk
• Vegetable oil
• Yellow cornmeal
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. In a mixing bowl, beat the eggs, mayo, buttermilk and oil until smooth
3. Combine the cornmeal, sugar, baking powder and salt
4. Add to the egg mixture and beat until just combined
5. Pour into a large greased cast iron skillet or greased round baking dish
6. Bake at 425f for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
