Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Pasta sauce
• Flour
• Hamburger buns
• Cheddar cheese
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Grease a 13 x 9-inch baking dish
2. Flavor the ground beef with your favorite ingredients
3. Shape into about 8 patties
4. Coat the hamburger patties with flour
5. In a large skillet, brown the patties on both sides in butter, then arrange in the baking dish
6. Pour half of the pasta sauce over the patties, and spread the sauce with a spoon to cover the patties
7. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese, then cheddar cheese
8. Top with remaining sauce
9. Bake in a 400f oven for about 25 minutes, or until patties are no longer pink
10. Serve the patties and the sauce on buns and sprinkle more cheese on if desired<|endoftext|>
