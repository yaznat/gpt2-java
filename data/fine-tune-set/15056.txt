Ingredients:
• Pepperoni
• Ground beef
• Mild italian sausage
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Sliced mushrooms
• Mozzarella cheese
• Provolone cheese
• Pizza sauce
• Kaiser rolls

Steps:
1. To a food processor, add the sliced pepperoni
2. Mix until most of the pepperoni is ground up, pulsing as you go
3. Add the ground beef and sausage
4. Form meat into 4 patties
5. To a large skillet over medium / med-high heat, melt the butter and olive oil until butter is melted
6. If using mushrooms, saute them until done, 1-2 minutes
7. Remove with slotted spoon and set aside
8. Add the patties to the pan and cook 4-5 minutes per side until cooked thru
9. Pat the burgers down with the spatula as needed to retain a wide burger
10. When burgers are just about done, place mushrooms on burgers, then 1 slice each of the cheeses on top of each burger
11. Reduce heat to low, cover tightly and let steam until cheese is melted
12. Place burgers on top of bottom bun then spread warmed pizza sauce over top of melted cheese
13. Close up buns and enjoy<|endoftext|>
