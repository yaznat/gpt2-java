Please generate a cake recipe.<|endoftext|>The Heavy One Cheesecake

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Granulated sugar
• Flour
• Lemon zest
• Orange zest
• Egg yolks
• Eggs
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 475 degrees f
2. In a large mixing bowl, beat the cream cheese until it is light and fluffy
3. Mix in the sugar and flour and continue beating until it is smooth
4. Add the zests, the yolks, the eggs, one at a time, thoroughly mixing after the addition of each with the mixer
5. Stir in the heavy cream, and mix only briefly until blended
6. Pour the mixture into the prepared crust in the 10-inch springform pan, and bake for 15 minutes at 475 degrees f
7. Then, reduce the oven temperature to 200 degrees f and continue to bake for 60 minutes
8. Then, turn the oven off and allow the cake to sit inside while it cools down for another 60 minutes
9. Remove the cake from the oven, allow to cool to room temperature, then chill in the refrigerator over night<|endoftext|>
