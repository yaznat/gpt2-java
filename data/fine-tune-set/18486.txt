• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter

2. Drain fat well, on paper towels
3. In a medium mixing bowl, mix the milk, eggs and butter
4. Mix well
5. Combine the dry ingredients, add to egg mixture, combining well
6. Pour half of the batter in a hot, light oiled, waffle iror
7. Sprinkle half the sausage over batter
