Steps:

2. Add marshmallows and stir until melted
3. Add 7-up
4. When cool, add drained, crushed pineapple and bananas
5. Pour into dish and let set until firm in refrigerator
6. Topping cook first four topping ingredients until thickened in a pan on top of stove
7. Cool
8. When cooled fold in whipped topping
