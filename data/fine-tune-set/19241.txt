Ingredients:
• Caramel ice cream topping
• Milk chocolate candy bars
• Shortbread cookies with pecans
• Butter
• Pecan halves
• Butter pecan ice cream
• Chocolate ice cream

Steps:
1. In a microwave-safe bowl, combine the caramel topping and candy bars
2. Microwave, uncovered, on high for 1-1 / 2 minutes or until candy bars are melted, stirring every 30 seconds
3. Cool
4. Combine the cookie crumbs and butter
5. Press onto the bottom of a greased 10-in
6. Springform pan
7. Chop 1 / 2 cup pecans
8. Set aside
9. Spoon half of the butter pecan ice cream over crust
10. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons caramel sauce
11. Sprinkle with 1 / 4 cup chopped pecans
12. Spread half of the chocolate ice cream over top
13. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons caramel sauce
14. Sprinkle with remaining chopped pecans
15. Spoon remaining butter pecan ice cream around the edge of pan
16. Spread remaining chocolate ice cream in center of pan
17. Cover and freeze overnight
18. Carefully run a knife around edge of pan to loosen
19. Remove sides of pan
20. Top with remaining pecan halves
21. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons caramel sauce
22. Serve with remaining caramel sauce<|endoftext|>
