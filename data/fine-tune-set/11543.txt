Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Eggs
• Mincemeat
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Walnuts
• Mixed candied fruit
• Maraschino cherry

Steps:
1. Butter a 9 inch springform pan
2. Line with wax paper
3. Butter again
4. Sift flour and baking soda
5. Combine eggs, mincemeat, sweetened condensed milk, chopped walnuts and candied fruit
6. Fold in dry ingredients
7. Pour into pan
8. Bake in 300f oven for 2 hours, until center springs back and top is golden
9. Cool thoroughly
10. Turn out and remove wax paper
11. Refrigerate, well wrapped, at least 24 hours before decorating, slicing and serving<|endoftext|>
