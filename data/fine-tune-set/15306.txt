Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Scallion
• Breadcrumbs
• Soy sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Fresh orange juice
• Fresh lemon juice
• Dairy-free margarine
• Lemon basil

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine ground turkey, scallions, bread crumbs, 1 tb of the soy sauce, ginger, and garlic
2. Season to taste with salt and pepper
3. Mix well
4. Divide turkey mixture into 8 equal portion
5. Shape into patties
6. Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat until hot
7. For the sauce, in a small saucepan combine orange juice, lemon juice, and the remaining 1 tb soy sauce
8. Let bubble until reduced by half
9. Add 4 of the patties and cook for 7 to 8 minutes
10. Turn patties over and cook another 8 minutes or until done
11. Repeat with remaining 4 patties
12. Keep the first batch warm in a warm oven
13. Whisk in the margarine & lemon basil to sauce
14. Pour over burgers
15. Serve with rice<|endoftext|>
