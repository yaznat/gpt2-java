What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Beefy Taco Joes

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Condensed tomato soup
• Chunky salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Sandwich buns

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, brown beef and drain
2. Add soup and salsa
3. Heat
4. Top with cheese and heat until melted
5. Serve on rolls<|endoftext|>
