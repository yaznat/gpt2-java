Ingredients:
• Pork mince
• Garlic cloves
• Green onions
• Red pepper
• Egg
• Ginger and chili paste
• Breadcrumbs
• Hamburger buns
• Cucumber
• Carrot
• Lettuce
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Combine the mince, garlic, onion, pepper, egg, sauce and breadcrumbs in a bowl
2. Chopping the vegetables finely is easier in a food processor if you have one
3. Mix together well
4. Divide mixture into 4 patties
5. Cook on a heated, oiled grill plate until browned on both sides and cooked through
6. Serve with cut and toasted buns and your choice of toppings, or just with salad!<|endoftext|>
