What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Beehoon Omelette

Ingredients:
• Rice noodles
• Eggs
• White onion
• Minced beef
• Carrot
• Tomatoes
• Red chile
• Spring onion
• Chicken bouillon cubes
• Salt
• Oil

Steps:
1. Soaked noodles in hot boiling water until soft and drain
2. Beat eggs in large bowl for 1 minute, add seasoning and beat till combine
3. Pour eggs mixture over noodle
4. Heat oil in a small pan or skillet
5. Stir fry carrots, onions, mince beef for 5 minutes
6. Drain and mix with eggs and noodles mixture
7. Mix in tomato, red chillies and spring onion
8. Heat oil, pour half of the eggs noodles mixture and cook over low heat for 3 to 5 minutes on each side or until done
9. Repeat with the other half of the mixture
10. Remove from heat and cut into wedges
11. Serve hot
12. Dip in tomato or chilli sauce<|endoftext|>
