Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Milk
• Ice cubes
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Carefully crush the cubes of ice, make it in bits and sparkly and nice
2. Set aside the crushed stuff, so that the job won't be tough
3. Put bananas in the blender, high on speed till soft and tender
4. Add milk and blend well, a good job that you can tell
5. Stir in crushed ice and vanilla, enjoy as life is utopia<|endoftext|>
