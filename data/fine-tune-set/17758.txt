Can you give me a muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Oat Muffins

Ingredients:
• Quick-cooking oats
• All-purpose flour
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Nonfat milk
• Canola oil
• Egg white
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl, combne the first seven ingrediants
2. In another bowl, combine the applesauce, milk, oil and egg white
3. Stir into dry ingrediants just until moistened
4. Fill muffin cups coated with nonstick cooking spray three-forths full
5. Combine topping ingrediatns
6. Sprinkle over batter
7. Bake at 400 for 16-18 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean
8. Cool for 10 minutes before removing to a wire rack<|endoftext|>
