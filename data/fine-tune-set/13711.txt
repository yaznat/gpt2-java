Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Butter Pork Loin

Ingredients:
• Pork roast
• Apple juice
• Apple butter
• Brown sugar
• Water
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Place pork loin in baking dish, pour apple juice over it and cover with a lid
3. Bake for 1 hour in the over
4. While baking the loin, mix together the rest of the mixture
5. After it has baked for an hour, remove from oven and spread apple butter mixture over the loin and cook for an additional 2 hours or until tender<|endoftext|>
