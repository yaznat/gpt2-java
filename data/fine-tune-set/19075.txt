Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Potato Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• French vanilla ice cream
• Cocoa
• Chocolate fudge topping
• Whipped cream
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Soften ice cream just enough to be able to form it
2. Take 2 to 3 scoops and form together into the shape of a baked potato, putting each"potato" back in the freezer until all are done
3. Once they are all formed and firming again roll them through a plate of cocoa so that they are coated completely
4. Place back into freezer until ready to serve
5. Once you are ready to serve, slice them open at the top and poor in some of your chocolate sauce so that it looks like gravy
6. Then squirt in the whipped cream so that it looks like sour cream
7. Sprinkle on almonds and serve!
8. You can use many different toppings such as coconut colored orange to look like shredded cheese, green maraschino cherries to look like green onions and so on<|endoftext|>
