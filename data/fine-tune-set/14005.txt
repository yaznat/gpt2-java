Ingredients:
• Lamb rib chops
• Garlic clove
• Fresh ginger
• Cumin seed
• Coriander seed
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Trim fat from chops and place them on a flat plate
2. Combine all the other ingredients in a small bowl, then rub the mixture over the chops
3. Cover with plastic wrap and marinate in the refrigerator about 30 minutes
4. Heat a lightly oiled grill pan over high heat until almost smoking, then add the chops and sear for about 2 minutes, flip the chops over and cook for another 3 minutes for the medium rare or 3 1 / 2 minutes for medium
5. Allow the chops to rest 5 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
