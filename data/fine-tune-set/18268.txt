Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Best Ever Buttermilk Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Egg
• Oil

Steps:
1. Using a food processor, mix together all dry ingredients and pulse 4-5 times to blend
2. Add remaining ingredients and process until smooth
3. About 10 seconds
4. Do not overprocess
5. Pour onto a hot slightly greased griddle and cook until bubbles appear on the top side
6. Flip over and brown<|endoftext|>
