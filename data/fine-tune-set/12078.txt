Ingredients:
• Lasagna noodles
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Potatoes
• Butter
• Onions

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease a 9" x 13" baking dish
3. Cook noodles until soft, remove to a bowl, toss lightly with oil to prevent sticking
4. Set aside
5. In a saute pan, saute onions in butter until brownish
6. Set aside
7. Cook potatoes
8. Add shredded cheese, potatoes must be hot to melt the cheese, and is easier to spread in the baking dish
9. In prepared baking pan, layer as follows:
10. In bottom of baking dish layer 4 noodles
11. Then cover noodles with half of the hot potatoes
12. Then half of the fried onion
13. Repeat, ending with the onions covering the top completely
14. Cover with foil
15. Bake for 40 minutes
16. Let sit for half an hour before cutting<|endoftext|>
