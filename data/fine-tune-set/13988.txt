Please give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>California Dreamin Lamb Burgers

Ingredients:
• Lean ground lamb
• Onions
• Fresh parsley
• Lemon, zest of
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Hass avocado
• Fresh lemon juice
• Plain nonfat yogurt
• Whole wheat rolls
• Tomatoes
• Alfalfa sprout

Steps:
1. Prepare coals for grilling or heat gas grill
2. Lightly combine the lamb, onion, parsley, lemon zest, salt and pepper
3. Form into four patties about 3 1 / 2 inches in diameter and 1 inch thick
4. In a separate bowl, mash the avocado with the lemon juice and yogurt
5. Season with salt and pepper and reserve
6. Grill the burgers 3 inches from the heat for about 4 to 5 minutes per side for medium-rare meat
7. Serve immediately on toasted rolls topped with the reserved avocado mixture, a tomato slice, and alfalfa sprouts<|endoftext|>
