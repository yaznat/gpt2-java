Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• White onion
• Cabbage
• Chicken broth
• Zucchini
• Mild curry paste
• Peas

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large deep skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add onion and stir for one minute
3. Reduce heat to medium and add as much cabbage as will fit
4. Add chicken broth
5. Continue to add cabbage until it's all in the pan and cover the pan: stir occasionally
6. Cook about 10 minutes
7. Add succhini and curry paste, stirring in to blend
8. Cook on medium, uncovered, until the vegetables are almost tender, about 10 minutes
9. Add frozen peas and continue to cook until the vegetables are done and liquid has evaporated, about 5 minutes
10. Salt and pepper to taste
11. Variations:
12. Add chopped garlic and / or ginger
13. Substitute any vegetable other than the cabbage
14. Add some coconut milk near the end
15. Add fresh herbs such as cilantro and basil<|endoftext|>
