What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Andrea S Fruit Pizza

Ingredients:
• Kiwi fruits
• Strawberry
• Apples
• Whipped cream cheese
• Sugar
• Milk
• Refrigerated sugar cookie dough
• Grapes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degress
2. Roll out cookie dough onto large around flat circular pan and cook for 10 minutes or until golden brown
3. Leave on pan and cool sugar cookie dough in fridge
4. Whip cream cheese, sugar and milk
5. If thats not enough milk or if you want to change the consistency, add more milk
6. Dice and mix fruit together in a medium bowl
7. Spread cream cheese onto cooled cookie dough and sprinkle with diced fruit
8. Refrigerate until serving<|endoftext|>
