Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Brownie Bon Bons

Ingredients:
• Fudge brownie mix
• Walnuts
• Egg
• Water
• Hershey chocolate kisses

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375 degrees
2. Mix brownie mix, walnuts, egg and water thoroughly
3. Wrap dough by rounded tablespoonful around each chocolate kiss
4. Seal edges
5. Put bon bon on ungreased baking sheet
6. Bake til set- 8- 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
