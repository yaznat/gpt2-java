What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Apple Pancakes With Syrup

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Oil
• Apples
• Milk
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Allspice
• Cloves
• Salt
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Brown sugar
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Apple juice

Steps:
1. For the syrup, combine in microwaveable dish, the brown sugar, splenda, apple juice, 5 ml of the cinnamon, and 500 ml of the applesauce
2. Heat to boiling
3. For the pancakes, combine in a bowl the flour, baking powder, and spices
4. Then add the milk, eggs, oil, chopped apples, and the rest of the applesauce from the jar
5. Grill pancakes on preheated grill sprayed with non-stick cooking spray
6. Respray grill between pancakes<|endoftext|>
