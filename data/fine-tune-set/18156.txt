What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Swirl Pancakes Vegan

Ingredients:
• Unbleached flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Almond milk
• Ener-g egg substitute
• Walnuts
• Batter
• Natural applesauce

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon
2. Add almond milk and egg replacer mixture and stir just until flour is moistened
3. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes while the skillet heats
4. While the batter is resting, combine all of the "swirl" ingredients, using 2 tablespoons of the prepared batter
5. Heat a non-stick griddle or skillet over medium heat until a drop of water sizzles
6. Gently stir the walnuts into the batter
7. Pour batter by scant 1 / 4 cupfuls onto hot griddle
8. Immediately drizzle the cinnamon-apple batter over the top of each pancake in a swirl pattern
9. Cook until tops are bubbly and bubbles burst
10. Edges will look dry
11. With a pancake turner, turn and cook until undersides are golden
12. Place on a warm platter
13. Keep warm
14. Repeat until all batter is used, brushing griddle lightly with canola oil, if necessary, to prevent sticking
15. Serve pancakes with syrup or other topping as desired<|endoftext|>
