Ingredients:
• Tortillas
• Pizza sauce
• Pepperoni
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Basil

Steps:
1. Heat frying pan to 350
2. Cut tortillas in half
3. One one side of tortilla spread sauce and put on pepperoni
4. Sprinkle on 1 / 2 cup mozzerella on each tortilla
5. 1 / 4 of parmeasian on each
6. And a few sprinkles of basil
7. Flip empty side of tortilla on to the full one
8. Put about a tablespoon of butter in pan and place tortilla in pan
9. After first side gets brown flip over to other side
10. When its all done sprinkle a little more chees on and some basil and let it melt
11. Serve and feel like your in heaven<|endoftext|>
