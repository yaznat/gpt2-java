Ingredients:
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Flour
• Soy flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Egg
• Canola oil
• Apricot fruit spread

Steps:
1. Lightly coat 12 muffins cups with nonstick spray
2. Set aside
3. Stir together flour, soy flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl
4. Set aside
5. Combine buttermilk, egg, and oil in another bowl or large measuring cup
6. Add buttermilk mixture all at once to flour mixture, stir just until moistened
7. Spoon about half of the batter into prepared muffins cups, filling each about one-third full
8. Place about 1 teaspoon of the spreadful fruit in the center of batter in each cup
9. Top the remaining batter, dividing equally
10. Muffin cups will be about two-thirds full
11. Bake in a 400 degree oven for 15 to 18 minutes or until golden
12. Cool in muffin cups on wire rack for 5 minutes, remove muffins
13. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
