Give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Green Chili Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Green chili sauce
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Peel and cut potatoes into french fry sticks
2. Either fry potatoes in oil or bake in a 400 oven for 15 to 20 minutes
3. Heat green chili in a saucepan
4. When potatoes are done place on a broil proof pan, i use a metal pie pan, pour green chili on top then sprinkle with cheese
5. Place back into a broiler for 5 minutes to melt cheese
6. Optional toppings: sour cream, onions, jalapenos, or guacamole<|endoftext|>
