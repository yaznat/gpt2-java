Please give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>Always Soft Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Butter
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Eggs
• Chocolate chips
• Nuts

Steps:
1. In small bowl, combine flour and baking soda
2. Set aside
3. In large bowl beat butter, sugars, vanilla and pudding until creamy
4. Beat in eggs
5. Gradually add flour mixture, and beat until combined
6. Stir in chocolate chips and nuts
7. Drop by rounded teaspoons onto ungreased baking sheet
8. Bake at 375 degrees for 8-10 minutes, or until edges are golden brown
9. Cool on baking sheet for 2 minutes before transferring to cooling rack<|endoftext|>
