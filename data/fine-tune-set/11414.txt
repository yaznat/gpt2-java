Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>Alaskan Beer Quick Bread

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Amber beer

Steps:
1. Mix together in a bowl, the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt
2. Add 12 ounces alaskan amber beer
3. Mix until evenly moistened
4. Bake in oiled loaf pan at 400f for about 45 minutes
5. Invert onto rack and remove pan
6. Serve with butter& honey
7. You could probably substitute any brand of"amber" beer in this recipe<|endoftext|>
