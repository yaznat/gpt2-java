Ingredients:
• Beef round steak
• Garlic
• Pinto beans
• Diced green chilies
• Cilantro
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Spray nonstick skillet with cooking spray
2. Heat over med
3. Heat
4. Add steak and garlic
5. Cook until desired doneness
6. Stir in beans, chilis, and cilantro
7. Cook and stir 7 minutes
8. Spoon mixture on tortillas
9. Top with cheese
10. Fold bottom up, fold sides over
