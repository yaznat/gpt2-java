What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Bacon Blue Cheese Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Blue cheese
• Cream cheese
• Fresh chives
• Cooked bacon
• Mixed baby lettuces and spring greens
• Hamburger buns
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Mix beef, seasoning salt and pepper in a medium bowl
2. Don't overmix or you'll get loose messy yucky stuff
3. Shape meat mixture into 8 to 12 patties
4. In a bowl mix together blue cheese, cream cheese, and chives
5. Divide cheese mixture equally and place on centers of half the patties
6. Top with remaining meat patties then tightly pinch edges together to seal in filling
7. Oil hot grill or grill pan to help prevent sticking
8. Grill burgers for about 4-6 minutes per side
9. Top burgers with bacon
10. Serve burgers on toasted rolls with spring mix lettuce on top<|endoftext|>
