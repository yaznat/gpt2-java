Ingredients:
• Shortening
• Eggs
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Grease a loaf or bundt pan with shortening
2. Add about a tablespoon of flour and shake around pan to coat evenly
3. Set aside
4. Preheat oven
5. Separate eggs into small bowls
6. Unwrap the sticks of butter
7. Cut the butter into small, manageable one-inch long portions and place them in a heavy, cool mixing bowl
8. Add 1 / 4cup of sugar to bowl
9. Set your mixer to its lowest speed and mix the butter and sugar until no clumps remain
10. Turn your mixer to medium speed and continue to mix for about two minutes while regularly scraping off beater buildup with a rubber spatula
11. Continue to add 1 / 4 cup of sugar at a time and keep mixing
12. Blend until fluffy and smooth
13. Beat eggs yolks and mix well
14. Add to sugar mixture
15. Add vanilla
16. Sift dry ingrdients together and gradually add into the sugar mixture
17. Take the egg whites and beat until stiff
18. Gradually add the whites to your sugar mixture
19. Pour your batter into prepared pan
20. Place in a 350 degree oven for 35 minutes
21. Turn down temp to 325 and cook for another 25 minutes
22. Cake is ready when a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean
23. Cool at least 10 minutes before removing from pan<|endoftext|>
