What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>Aimee S Quick Chicken Breasts

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Dijon mustard
• Teriyaki sauce
• Parmesan cheese
• Bacon bits

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Put chicken in a 9" x 13" baking dish
3. Blend the sauce and mustard together with whisk and slather evenly over the chicken
4. Sprinkle the bacon bits and cheese over that and bake until done, about 30 minutes, depends on thickness<|endoftext|>
