Please give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Awesome Spinach Casserole

Ingredients:
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cream cheese
• Onion
• Margarine
• French fried onion rings

Steps:
1. Cook the spinach according to directions and drain well
2. Saute onion in margarine
3. Add the soup and cream cheese
4. Heat under low heat until the cheese is melted
5. Mix together with the spinach
6. Add 1 / 2 can of onion rings and mix well
7. Turn into greased casserole
8. Top with remainder of onion rings and bake at 350 for 30-35 minutes<|endoftext|>
