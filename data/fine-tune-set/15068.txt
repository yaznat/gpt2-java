What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>A Whole Lotta Pizza Casserole

Ingredients:
• Egg noodles
• Vegetable oil
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Condensed tomato soup
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degrees
2. Cook noodles following package directions
3. Drain and reserve
4. Meanwhile, heat oil on medium-high in a nonstick skillet
5. Add ground beef, onion and garlic
6. Cook over medium-high heat for 6 minutes until beef is no longer pink
7. Drain off excess liquid
8. Stir in oregano, salt, soup, 1 / 3 cup water and noodles
9. Take off heat and add half of the cheese
10. Spoon into 11 x 7-inch baking dish and sprinkle the remaining cheese over the top
11. Bake at 350 degrees for 25-30 minutes until bubbly
12. Allow to cool for 5-10 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
