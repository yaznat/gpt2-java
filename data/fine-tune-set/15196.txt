Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onions
• Green bell pepper
• Dried italian seasoning
• All-purpose flour
• Tomato and basil pasta sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Prepared pizza crust
• Olive oil
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the first 3 ingredients and 3 teaspoons italian seasoning, stirring until the beef crumbles and is no longer pink
2. Drain well and return to skillet
3. Add flour to mixture in skillet
4. Stir until blended
5. Stir in spaghetti sauce
6. Bring mixture to a boil over med-high heat, stirring constantly
7. Spoon mixture into a lightly greased 13x9 inch baking dish
8. Sprinkle evenly with 1 1 / 2 cups mozzarella cheese
9. Unroll pizza crust and place on top of cheese
10. Brush with olive oil, and sprinkle evenly with remaining mozzarella cheese, parmesan cheese, and remaining italian seasoning
11. Bake in a 425 oven for 15-20 minutes or until golden brown<|endoftext|>
