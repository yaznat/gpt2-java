Give me a sandwich recipe.<|endoftext|>Baby Pb J Bagel Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Miniature bagels
• Peanut butter
• Strawberry jelly
• Butter

Steps:
1. Spread peanut butter evenly on cut sides of bottom halves of bagels
2. Spread jelly evenly on cut sides of top halves of bagels
3. Place top halves of bagels on bottom halves, jelly sides down
4. Brush bagels lightly with melted butter
5. Cook in a preheated panini press 2 minutes or until lightly browned and grill marks appear
6. Serve immediately
7. Cook 2 minutes on each side or until lightly browned and grill marks appear<|endoftext|>
