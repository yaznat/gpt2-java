Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Cumin powder
• Dried ancho chile powder
• Kosher salt
• Coconut oil
• Fresh cilantro
• Lime wedge
• White onion
• Radish
• Tomatillo salsa
• Cotija cheese
• Red cabbage

Steps:
1. Pulse the chicken chunks in a food processor about 6-8 times briefly to chop chicken into small pieces, being careful not to overprocess
2. In a large bowl, stir together the cumin powder, chili powder and salt
3. Place the chopped chicken into the bowl and toss with spices to combine well
4. Heat the coconut oil over medium-high heat until it shimmers
5. Add chicken in a single layer and cook, undisturbed, about one minute
6. Stir chicken and continue to cook until chicken is cooked through, about five minutes or so, stirring often and breaking up any large chunks with your spoon
7. To serve, spoon chicken mixture onto freshly made tortillas and top with the sides of your choice
