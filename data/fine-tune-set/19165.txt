Ingredients:
• Low-fat ricotta cheese
• Fat-free sweetened condensed milk
• Fat-free half-and-half
• Cinnamon
• Heavy cream
• Slivered almonds
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Place ricotta in blender or food processor container & blend till smooth
2. Add the condensed milk, half & half & cinnamon & blend till combined
3. In a 2 quart metal bowl, whip the cream till soft peaks form
4. Add ricotta / milk mixture, almonds & chocolate
5. Fold gently till combined
6. Cover bowl & place in freezer for 2 hours or until edges start to harden
7. Stir & put back in the freezer for another 2 hours or until firm
8. To serve, let stand at room temp till soft enough to scoop<|endoftext|>
