Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Canola oil
• Egg whites
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Clove
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Applesauce
• Flour
• Apple

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Grease muffin tin with cooking spray
3. Cream the first 3 ingredients together
4. Add everything down to the flour and blend well
5. Then stir in the flour until it forms a thick batter
6. Dice / slice apple
7. If using slices, place a small spoonful of batter in the bottom of each muffin tin, then arrange slices and add rest of batter
8. If using chunks just put them in before batter or after
9. Bake for 25-30 minutes<|endoftext|>
