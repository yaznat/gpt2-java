Ingredients:
• Baby asparagus
• Smoked bacon
• Japanese mayonnaise
• White onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Roll bacon on 3 steamed baby asparagus, make sure you expose the asparagus
2. Arrange on plate
3. Continue until you finish making all the asparagus bundles
4. Put mayonnaise, onion, cilantro and sugar in a food processor
5. Pulse for 30 seconds to 1 minute
6. Spoon dressing across asparagus bundles
7. Yummmmm!<|endoftext|>
