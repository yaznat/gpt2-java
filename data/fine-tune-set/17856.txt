Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Ingredient Brownies

Ingredients:
• Cocoa powder
• Flour
• Butter
• Eggs
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Melt the butter and mix in with the cocoa powder
2. Add flour to that mixture
3. In a separate bowl, beat eggs with sugar
4. Combine the contents of the chocolaty bowl with the eggy bowl, mix and pour into greased 8x8 baking dish
5. Bake at 375 for 20 minutes - they'll look raw but will cool perfectly<|endoftext|>
