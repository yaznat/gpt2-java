Please give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Beef Burritos

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Salsa
• Neufchatel cheese
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cumin
• Cilantro
• Tortillas

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef and drain off any excess fat
2. Season with 1 / 2 teaspoon salt and 1 / 2 tsp black pepper and 1 teaspoons of ground cumin
3. Add 1 1 / 2 cups chuncky salsa
4. I prefer pace medium
5. Stir together and add 4 oz neufchatel cream cheese
6. Stir until melted in and reduce over low heat until thick and creamy
7. Place about 1 / 3 cup burrito mixture onto a warmed flour tortilla
8. Top with fresh chopped cilantro
9. Fold in bottom then sides, rolling into a tight pouch
10. Leaving an opening at the top
11. Makes 6 good sized burritos
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
