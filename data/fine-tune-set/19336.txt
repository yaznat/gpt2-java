Give me a recipe for a popsicle.<|endoftext|>Yogurt Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Plain low-fat yogurt
• Banana
• Vanilla
• Fruit juice

Steps:
1. Blend ingredients together and pour into small paper cups
2. Freeze
3. Place a plastic spoon or popsicle stick in each cup when yogurt mixture is half frozen
4. To serve, turn cup upside-down and run hot water over it until the popsicle slips out
5. Keep the cups to use as a holder<|endoftext|>
