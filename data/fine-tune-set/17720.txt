Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Applesauce
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Apples
• Raisins
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Spray 12 muffin cups with nonstick cooking spray
3. In a bowl with electric mixer, beat oil, applesauce, and sugar together for 2 minutes
4. Add eggs and vanilla
5. Beat 1 minute
6. In another bowl, stir together flours, baking soda, cinnamon and salt
7. Fold in diced apple, raisins, and chopped walnuts
8. Add dry ingredients to oil mixture
9. Stir just until combined
10. Do not overmix
11. Fill muffin cups
12. Bake 20-25 minutes or until done
13. Cool in pan 5 minutes, then cool on wire rack<|endoftext|>
