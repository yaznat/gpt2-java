Please give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Grilled Chicken Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Oil
• Seasoning salt
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Green onion
• Cilantro
• Tortillas
• Pam cooking spray
• Mild taco sauce
• Guacamole
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat panini grill
2. Sprinkle chicken breasts with natures season and coat with oil
3. Brown both sides of chicken on panini press of flat grill
4. Slice chicken into small cubes and put in bowl
5. Spray bottom of one flour tortilla and put sprayed side down on large plate
6. Sprinkle generously with shredded mexican cheeses all the way to the edges
7. Top with diced chicken breasts
8. Top with sliced green onions
9. Top with cilantro
10. For added kick you can add jalapeno peppers if desired
11. Top with second flour tortilla
12. Spray top of shell with pam
13. Carefully move whole quesadilla onto heated grill
14. Flatten
15. When cheese starts oozing out of sides take off carefully and put into plate
16. Cut into 8 equal triangles with pizza cutter
17. Serve on plate with taco sauce, guacamole, and sour cream
18. Watch them disappear!<|endoftext|>
