What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Apple Carrot Nut Muffins

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Allspice
• Nutmeg
• Ground cloves
• Eggs
• Oil
• Carrot
• Apple
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees fahrenheit
2. Mix ingredients together
3. Pour batter into greased or paper-lined muffin cups
4. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
