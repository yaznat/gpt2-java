What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Gyro Burritos

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Lemon juice
• Garlic powder
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Onion powder
• Cumin
• Oregano
• Red pepper flakes
• Ground beef
• Light sour cream
• Cucumber
• Dill weed
• Burritos
• White onion

Steps:
1. Mix the cucumber sauce a couple of hours ahead of time
2. Mix the paste together and set aside
3. Brown the hamburger or turkey, add the paste and simmer for just a few moments
4. Layer center of burrito with sauce, onions and meat
5. Role up burrito -- easy as that!<|endoftext|>
