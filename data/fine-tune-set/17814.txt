Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Bacon English Muffins

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Butter
• Egg
• Salt
• Sugar
• Plain flour
• Dry yeast
• Bacon
• Parmesan cheese
• Cornmeal

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients, except bacon, cheese, and cornmeal, in the bread machine, in the order listed
2. Start the dough cycle
3. When the machine beeps, add bacon and cheese
4. When the dough is finished, sprinkle cornmeal on the work area, and turn out dough
5. Press the dough out into a 1 / 2 inch thinck rectangle
6. Turn dough over so both sides are coated in cornmeal
7. Using a large glass or large cookie cutter, cut into 10 rounds
8. Place the muffins on a tray, cover, and leave to rise for about 20 minutes
9. Heat an electric frypan, to medium-high, and cook the muffins for 5 minutes on each side
10. They should be golden brown when you turn them
11. Split with a knife, and serve warm, or toasted<|endoftext|>
