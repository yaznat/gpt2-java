What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>Al Roker S Spicy Chicken Wings

Ingredients:
• Hot pepper sauce
• Honey
• Butter
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Chicken wings

Steps:
1. Mix first 5 ingredients in a large bowl
2. Add chicken and turn to coat
3. Cover and refrigerate, turning occasionally for at least 2 hours or up to 12
4. Heat oven to 375 and line baking sheet with heavy duty foil
5. Add chicken in a single layer
6. Bake, turning chicken once for 1 1 / 4 hours or until chicken turns crispy
7. Reheat in covered baking dish in a 400 oven for 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
