Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Butter
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Diced onion
• Jalapeno
• Bacon
• Southwest seasoned hamburger
• Ground cumin
• Paprika
• Black pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Salt
• Dried oregano

Steps:
1. For hamburgers:
2. Combine all seasoning ingredrients
3. Use one heaping tblsp of seasoning mix per pound of hamburger meat
4. Mix into hamburger meat until well combined
5. Fry or grill
6. Assembling the complete burger:
7. Heat a large frying pan over medium heat
8. Spread butter on one side of each tortilla
9. Put one tortilla, butter side down in the hot pan
10. Sprinkle the cheeses evenly onto the center of the tortilla in the pan- but not all the way to the edges
11. Put the tomato, onion, bacon and jalapeno over the cheese, add the cooked seasoned burger and cover with the other tortilla butter side up
12. When the bottom is browned, about 45-90 seconds, flip the quesadilla and grill the other side
13. Remove the quesidilla from the pan and using a pizza cutter, cut throught the middle
14. Serve<|endoftext|>
