Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Brownie Caramel Pecan Bars

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Butter
• Water
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Pecan pieces
• Caramels
• Milk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Line a 9 inch square baking pan with foil, extending foil over edges of pan
3. Grease and flour foil
4. In a medium saucepan, combine sugar, butter and water
5. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until mixture boils
6. Remove from heat
7. Immediately add 1 cup chocolate chips
8. Stir until melted
9. Beat in eggs and vanilla until well blended
10. Stir into chocolate mixture
11. Spread batter into prepared pan
12. Bake 15 to 20 minutes or until brownies begin to pull away from the sides of the pan
13. When brownies are almost done, prepare caramel topping
14. Remove wrappers from the 25 caramels
15. In a medium microwavable bowl, place 1 / 4 cup butter or margarine, caramels and 2 tablespoons milk
16. Microwave on high for 1 minute
17. Stir
18. Microwave an additional 1 to 2 minutes, stirring every 30 seconds, or until caramels are melted and mixture is smooth when stirred
19. Use immediately
20. After removing brownies from oven
21. Immediately and carefully spread with prepared topping
22. Sprinkle remaining 1 cup chocolate chips and pecans over topping
23. Cool completely in pan on wire rack, being careful not to disturb chips while soft
24. Lift out of pan
25. Cut into bars<|endoftext|>
