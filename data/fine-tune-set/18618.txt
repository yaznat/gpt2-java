What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Apple Bread Pudding With Cranberries

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Cinnamon
• Lemon juice
• Butter
• Eggs
• Sucanat with honey
• Milk
• Orange zest
• Vanilla
• Bread cubes
• Dried cranberries

Steps:
1. Peel, core and cut the apple in wedges and than in thin slices, sprinkle and mix with lemon juice and cinnamon
2. Heat up butter in a shallow pan over medium heat and saut apple for 2-3 minutes to soften
3. Set aside
4. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs and honey granules until well combined
5. Whisk in the half-and-half, orange zest, and vanilla
6. Add the bread cubes and cranberries and mix well
7. Let stand for 15 minutes so the bread can soak up the custard then add the cooled down apples
8. Preheat oven to 325
9. Put your mixture into a buttered souffle pan
10. Place souffle pan into a roasting pan
11. Place both pans into the oven and fill the roasting pan with boiling water 1 / 2 way up the outside of the souffle pan
12. Bake for about 1 hour<|endoftext|>
