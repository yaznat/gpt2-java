Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Converted rice
• Chicken broth
• Artichoke hearts
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Salt
• Pepper
• Basil
• Garlic powder
• Dill weed

Steps:
1. Spray crockery insert with non-stick cooking spray
2. Place rice in crockpot and add the broth
3. Rinse chicken and pat dry
4. Cut into bite-size pieces
5. Place chicken on rice and top with artichoke hearts
6. Season cream of chicken soup to taste with salt, pepper, basil, garlic, and dill
7. Spoon soup over the layer of chicken
8. Cover and cook for 4 - 5 hours
9. Check rice and stir about an hour before cooking is complete to avoid "mushy" rice<|endoftext|>
