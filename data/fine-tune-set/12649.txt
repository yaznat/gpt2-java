Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Onion Gravy And Biscuit Dumplings

Ingredients:
• Beef tips
• All-purpose flour
• Salt & pepper
• Olive oil
• Vidalia onion
• Beef broth
• White wine
• Dried basil
• Frozen lima beans
• Half-and-half
• Cornstarch
• Refrigerated biscuits

Steps:
1. Toss the beef
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pot
3. Brown the beef in the pan
4. Once browned, reserve beef on a plate and cook the onions until soft
5. Return the beef to the pan, add the broth, white wine and basil to the pot
6. Turn down to a simmer, cover, and let cook for 2 hours
7. Toward end of cooking time preheat oven to 350 degrees
8. Stir the lima beans into the stew
9. In a measuring cup mix the half-and-half with the cornstarch until the cornstarch is dissolved
10. Stir it into the stew
11. Turn heat up to medium-high and heat to almost boiling stirring regularly
12. Transfer stew to large 9 by 13 glass pan open biscuits can and place individual biscuits on top of the stew
13. Cook in 350 degree oven for 15-20 minutes until biscuits are golden brown<|endoftext|>
