Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Feta cheese
• Fresh lime juice
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Scallion
• Vegetarian bacon bits
• Roasted sunflower seeds
• Fat-free mayonnaise
• Grape tomatoes
• Leaf lettuce
• Low-fat flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to broil
2. Put diced avocado into a mixing bowl, stir in in feta cheese, lime juice and black pepper
3. Add scallions, bacon bits, sunflower seeds and mayonnaise, stirring gently to combine
4. Stir in grape tomatoes and shredded lettuce
5. Spray tortillas lightly with nonstick cooking spray and heat in oven until softened (about 30 seconds
6. Remove from oven and place each tortilla on individual plates
7. To serve, line each tortilla with whole lettuce leaf and mound salad mixture on top<|endoftext|>
