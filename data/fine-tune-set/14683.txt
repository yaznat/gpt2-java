Give me a rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Walnut Rice Stuffing Vegan

Ingredients:
• Wild rice
• Sunflower oil
• Celery heart
• White onion
• Olive oil
• Walnuts
• Mcintosh apples
• Port wine
• Dried parsley

Steps:
1. Boil 2 cups of water in a large saucepan with a few shakes of table salt, and put the rice in it
2. Stir in the oil and cover, let cook on medium heat for 15-20 minutes or until an "al dente" texture
3. While the rice is cooking, turn on another burner and sautee the chopped onion in 2 tb oil of your choice until somewhat translucent, about 15-20 minutes
4. Turn the burners off to but leave the saucepan on with the rice and water
5. Fluff up with a wodden spoon or fork to evenly distribute the oil and you'll need the extra water too
6. In a small bowl, combine the port and parsley
7. Pour the sauteed onion, chopped celery, chopped apple, walnut bits, into the rice pan and mix
8. ,
9. Pour on the port and parsley mixture, and blend well
10. Turn the saucepan's burner back on, on medium-low heat, and cover again
11. Let cook another 8-10 minutes
12. Uncover, fluff with a wooden spoon or fork, and let stand for 5 minutes prior to serving or stuffing<|endoftext|>
