Ingredients:
• Poppy seeds
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Granola cereal
• Raisins
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Quark
• Milk
• Oil
• Applesauce
• Salt
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. In a bowl mix poppy seeds, butter, sugar, eggs, granola cereal / almonds and raisins
2. Stir until well blended
3. In a second bowl mix baking powder and flour
4. Add quark, milk, oil, applesauce, sugar, vanilla and salt
5. Using the dough hooks work into a smooth dough
6. Do not knead longer than 1 minute or dough will become sticky
7. Roll out on a floured surface into a rectangle of 35x45 cm
8. If the dough is too sticky, add a little flour
9. Spread with the poppy-seed-mixture and roll up starting on the longer side
10. Carefully place on a paper-lined baking sheet and cut a criss-cross-pattern into the top of the dough
11. Brush with a little milk
12. In the preheated oven bake at 180c / 350f for about 30-35 minutes<|endoftext|>
