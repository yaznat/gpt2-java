What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>3 Ingredient Pulled Pork

Ingredients:
• Pork loin roast
• Root beer
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Trim off excess fat, if desired
2. Place roast in slow cooker
3. Pour in can of root beer
4. Cook, covered, on low heat for 6 hours
5. Drain away liquid and shred pork with fork
6. Stir in bbq sauce
7. Serve as desired<|endoftext|>
