Give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Beef And Eggplant Stew Greek Crock Pot Version

Ingredients:
• Lean stewing beef
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Japanese eggplants
• Beef stock
• Tomato sauce
• Red wine vinegar
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Allspice
• Ground cloves
• Parsley
• Feta cheese

Steps:
1. Wash the eggplant, then top and tail
2. Don't bother to peel, just cut into 1 inch thick segments
3. In large skillet, brown the beef, onions, garlic and eggplant in olive oil until dark golden-brown
4. Drain
5. Place in crock pot
6. Combine the beef stock with the tomato sauce, vinegar, sugar, spices, and salt and pepper to taste, and stir it into the meat
7. Cover and cook on low heat for 8-10 hours
8. If serving with pasta, prepare pasta until al dente, pour onto a large serving platter and cover with meat, eggplant and sauce
9. Sprinkle minced parsley over
10. If serving on its own as a main course, garnish with parsley and feta, serve with a green salad, lots of crusty bread, and plenty of red wine<|endoftext|>
