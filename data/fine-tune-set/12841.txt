Ingredients:
• Diced tomatoes
• Garlic clove
• Fresh lime juice
• Ground cumin
• Kidney bean
• Green onion
• Jalapeno pepper
• Cilantro
• Parsley
• Beef steak
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Mix tomatoes, garlic, lime juice, cumin, beans, green onions, jalapeno and cilantro in bowl
2. Set aside
3. Season meat with salt and pepper, if desired
4. Cook over hot coals 3 to 5 minutes per side or until done as desired
5. Slice meat thin and top with tomato mixture
6. Serve over rice and garnish with lime wedges and additional cilantro, if desired
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
