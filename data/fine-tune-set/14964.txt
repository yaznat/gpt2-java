Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Carrot
• Sweet onion
• Celery
• Broccoli
• Frozen sweet peas
• Eggs
• White rice
• Soy sauce
• Olive oil
• Hot sauce
• Garlic powder
• Celery salt
• Dried onion
• Dry white wine

Steps:
1. Mix all sauce ingredients except the wine
2. Put pork in a shallow dish an pour just enough sauce to coat the pork, save the rest
3. Marinate for at least 1 hour in refrigerator
4. In a hot wok or large skillet cook pork until pink is gone, when the juices are starting to turn black & caramelize on skillet
5. Remove the pork & cut into this strips about 1 inch long & set aside
6. Add wine into the hot skillet and simmer to get all of the good caramelized bits up off of the skillet and pour all of it into the remaining sauce that was set aside
7. In hot skillet put in some olive oil to coat the bottom
8. Add carrots, wait a minute to add celery & onion
9. Keep veggies moving while cooking
10. Add broccoli & frozen sweet peas
11. Cook veggies al dente
12. Toss in eggs add pork, cooked rice & remaining sauce
13. Toss around skillet until mixed well
14. Enjoy as a meal with some eggrolls or a side<|endoftext|>
