Give me a rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Arborio Rice Coating

Ingredients:
• Arborio rice
• Semolina
• All-purpose flour
• Table salt
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Put the rice in a blender and grind until very fine
2. Shake out into a large bowl and add the semolina, flour, salt, and pepper
3. Toss until well blended
4. Store in a sealed container in the freezer to maintain maximum freshness<|endoftext|>
