Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Angela S Chocolate Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Self-rising flour
• Cocoa
• Salt
• Milk
• Egg yolks
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Pie crusts

Steps:
1. Combine 1 / 4 cup sugar, cornstarch, flour, cocoa and salt in small bowl
2. Blend well
3. Stir in 1 / 4 cup milk and egg yolks
4. In a saucepan, combine remaining 3 / 4 cup sugar and remaining 2 cups milk
5. Place over medium heat and gradually bring to a boil
6. Remove from heat
7. Stir 1 cup hot milk mixture into the cocoa mixture, then return to saucepan
8. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring constantly
9. Let mixture boil for 5 minutes while continuing to stir
10. Remove from heat and stir in butter and vanilla
11. Pour into pie shell and let chill 2-3 hours before serving
12. Top each slice with a dollop of cool whip when serving<|endoftext|>
