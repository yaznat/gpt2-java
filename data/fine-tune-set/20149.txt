Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Broccoli
• Carrots
• Water
• White sugar
• Celery
• Cauliflower
• Soy sauce
• Cider vinegar
• Garlic clove
• Ground ginger
• Skinless chicken pieces

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan, combine cornstarch, cold water, sugar, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic & ginger
2. Stir together & simmer until thickened
3. Preheat oven to 375
4. Place chicken in a greased 9x13 dish
5. Cover chicken with sauce
6. Place in oven
7. Bake for 10 minutes
8. Turn chicken & bake 10 minutes longer
9. Brush chicken with sauce every 5 minutes during baking time
10. Make sure chicken is cooked thoroughly
11. In a large skillet, add 1 t
12. Olive oil and vegetables
13. Stir & put in cut up chicken
14. Stir fry until done<|endoftext|>
