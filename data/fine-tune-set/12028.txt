Ingredients:
• Shortbread pie crust
• Milk
• Almond extract
• Cheesecake flavor instant pudding and pie filling
• Non-dairy whipped topping
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Beat milk, almond extract, pudding mixes and 1 / 2 of the whipped topping in medium bowl with wire whisk 1 minute
2. Sprinkle 1 / 4 cup of the almonds onto bottom of crust
3. Spread pudding mixture over almonds in crust
4. Spread remaining whipped topping over pudding in crust
5. Garnish with remaining almonds
6. Enjoy immediately or refrigerate until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
