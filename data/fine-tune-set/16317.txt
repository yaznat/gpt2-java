Ingredients:
• Tomatoes
• Goat cheese
• Salt and black pepper
• Olive oil
• Oregano
• Chicken breast fillets
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 375f
2. In a bowl, mix together the diced tomato and goat cheese
3. Season with salt and freshly ground pepper
4. Mix in the olive oil and around a teaspoon of the chopped oregano leaves
5. Cut a slash lengthways in each of the chicken breast fillets to form a cavity in each one
6. Stuff each cavity with the goat cheese mixture
7. Wrap each chicken breast in 3 bacon slices and secure each one with a piece of string or toothpick
8. Place the bacon-wrapped chicken breasts in a shallow, greased oven dish, sprinkle with the remaining oregano and season with freshly ground pepper
9. Roast the chicken for 20-30 minutes until cooked through
10. Serve at once with a tomato salad<|endoftext|>
