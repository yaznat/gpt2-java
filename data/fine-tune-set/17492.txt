What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Black Coffee Mascarpone

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Mascarpone
• Whipping cream
• Strong black coffee
• Brandy
• Black sambuca
• Cocoa powder

Steps:
1. Beat the egg yolks with the sugar, then blend with mascarpone and cream at high speed
2. Add coffee, brandy and liqueur
3. In separate bowl, beat the egg whites until peaks form
4. Gently stir and fold the egg white mixture through the coffee and mascarpone mixture
5. Serve in small coffee cups, dusted with cocoa powder, i use white ones, looks nicer!<|endoftext|>
