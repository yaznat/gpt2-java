What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Crock Pot Apricot Lamb

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Lamb steaks
• Chicken stock cube
• Boiling water
• Apricot nectar
• Orange, juice and zest of
• Ground star anise
• Ground black pepper
• Flour

Steps:
1. Lightly coat crock pot with olive oil, or use olive oil spray to prevent sticking
2. Dissolve chicken stock cube in hot water in a bowl
3. Add apricot nectar, orange juice, orange zest, star anise and pepper to bowl and mix well
4. Place lamb in crock pot and cover with mixture
5. Cook for 5-6 hours on low
6. Near end of cooking remove lid, sprinkle flour into sauce and mix well for a few minutes until thickened<|endoftext|>
