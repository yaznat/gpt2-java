Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Butter
• Lemon rind
• Vanilla
• Sugar
• Egg
• Apples
• Cornstarch
• Water

Steps:
1. Pudding::
2. Sift the flour, salt, baking soda, and baking powder together
3. Set aside
4. Cream butter with lemon rind, vanilla and sugar
5. Beat in egg
6. Stir in flour mixture
7. Add apples
8. Mix just until lblended together
9. Turn into a greased 8 x 8 x 2-inch pan
10. Bake in preheated 350 degree oven for 45 minutes
11. Vanilla sauce:
12. In a small saucepan, combine sugar with cornstarch and salt
13. Gradually stir in the water and mix well
14. Over medium heat, cook and stir until it reaches medium thickness
15. Remove from heat
16. Stir in butter, lemon rind and vanilla
17. Serve warm over the apple pudding<|endoftext|>
