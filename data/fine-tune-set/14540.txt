Please give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>Bab Leves Hungarian Bean Soup

Ingredients:
• Dried navy beans
• Salt
• Pepper
• Carrot
• Leeks
• Garlic cloves
• Smoked bacon
• Smoked ham stock
• Cooking oil
• Onions
• Hungarian paprika
• Flour
• Parsley
• Sour cream
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. Quick soak method - boil beans for 5 minutes in enough water to cover them completely, then cover pan and let soak for 1 hour
2. Drain and rinse the beans and put them in a large pot
3. Add the salt, pepper, carrot, leek, garlic, and meat
4. Add stock plus enough water to cover 2" above the beans and meat
5. Bring to a simmer
6. Simmer, partially covered, until the beans are tender, adding water as needed to keep the beans covered
7. Then remove meat to a separate plate
8. In a heavy saucepan, melt fat or warm oil and saut onions until they wilt
9. Mix in the paprika and then the flour
10. Gently fry, stirring constantly until flour turns golden beige
11. Add the parsley
12. Thin roux with 1 / 2 cup to a cup of the broth from the soup
13. Mix well, then slowly pour back into the soup
14. Let simmer for 10 minutes
15. Remove from heat and let cool
16. Blend some soup into the sour cream to keep it from curdling when you add it, then add it to the soup
17. Mix in the vinegar and taste soup for seasonings
18. Cut the meat into bite sized pieces and return them to the soup
19. Reheat soup before serving<|endoftext|>
