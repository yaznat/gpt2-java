Ingredients:
• Quick-cooking oats
• Boiling water
• Egg whites
• Bananas
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking cocoa
• Baking soda
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine oats and boiling water
2. Let stand for 5 minutes
3. In a mixing bowl, beat egg whites, bananas, sugars, oil and vanilla until blended
4. Combine the dry ingredients
5. Gradually add to creamed mixture
6. Stir in the oatmeal mixture
7. Spread into a 13-in
8. X 9-in
9. X 2-in
10. Pan coated with nonstick cooking spray
11. Bake at 350 for 20-25 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the centre comes out clean
12. Cool on a wire rack
13. Cut into bars<|endoftext|>
