Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Shrimp Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Small shrimp
• Carrot
• Green onions
• Garlic cloves
• Rice
• Soy sauce
• Eggs
• Salt
• Oil

Steps:
1. If your shrimp is frozen cook it in 2 tablespoons oil
2. Cook till it is thawed and all the water is gone
3. Take out of pan
4. By hand or food processor finely cut up your green onions, garlic and carrots
5. Put your shredded mix in with 3 tablespoons oil, rice, soy sauce, salt
6. Cook stirring often till all if well heated and add your shrimp and eggs
7. Continue cooking till your vegetables are done
8. Serve with soy sauce - like i said never enough soy sauce!<|endoftext|>
