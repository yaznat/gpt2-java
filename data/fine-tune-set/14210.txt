Ingredients:
• Silken tofu
• Semisweet chocolate
• Vanilla
• Graham cracker crust
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Melt chocolate in microwave, or in double boiler
2. I use the microwave, it's just easier
3. Place tofu in blender, and blend til smooth
4. Add chocolate, and extract
5. Pulse blender til mixed
6. Pour into pie crust and chill about an hour
7. You'll find silken tofu in the veggie section, next to where they have won ton wrappers - whatever size the silken package is, that's what i buy - and i've only ever seen one size
8. They have a lite version, but i've never tried that, but would probably work
9. Also, if you want a more intense chocolate flavor, just add a lil more chocolate<|endoftext|>
