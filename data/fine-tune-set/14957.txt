Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Hot Garlic Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Water
• Garlic paste
• Garlic
• Dried red chilies
• Spring onion
• Carrot
• Oil
• Salt
• Chili-garlic sauce
• Red capsicum
• Soya sauce
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Boil 4 cups of water
2. Add rice to it
3. Add salt to taste
4. Stir and allow to cook
5. Drain
6. Add cold water and drain once more
7. Now heat oil in a wok
8. Add garlic paste, chopped garlic and red chillies
9. Fry for 3-4 minutes
10. Add spring onions, carrot, capsicum and salt
11. Mix well
12. Add rice, soya sauce and garlic chili sauce
13. Mix well
14. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
