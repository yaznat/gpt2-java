What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Simple Faux Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Oatmeal
• Nonfat cottage cheese
• Egg whites
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Vanilla
• Banana

Steps:
1. Blend all ingredients in a blender or cuisinart until a smooth batter
2. Add fruit
3. Measure out ~1 / 3 cup
4. Cook on a griddle heated to 375, with pam for ~3 minute
5. Flip and cook other side for 2 minutes<|endoftext|>
