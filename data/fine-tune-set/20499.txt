Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Asparagus Casserole Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Asparagus spears
• Condensed cream of celery soup
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Saltine crackers
• Velveeta cheese
• Egg
• Slivered almonds

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients well
2. Pour into lightly greased crock pot
3. Cover and cook on high for 3 to 3 1 / 2 hours
4. After cooking dish may be left on low for up to 2 hours before serving<|endoftext|>
