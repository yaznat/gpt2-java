Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Bacon
• Monterey jack and cheddar cheese blend
• Egg roll wraps
• Egg
• Cooking oil

Steps:
1. Cook bacon pieces in frying pan
2. Spoon onto paper towel-covered plate to drain grease
3. Cook ground beef in same fry pan until browned, drain grease
4. Add bacon bits to ground beef and mix well
5. Beat egg in bowl
6. Take egg roll wrapper and put large spoonful of hamburger and bacon on one corner of wrapper
7. Add pinch of cheddar and monterey jack cheese on top of meat mixture
8. Coat edges of egg roll with egg or water
9. Roll up wrapper and repeat until you have used all the meat or all the wrappers, whichever happens first
10. In a large deep fry pan, heat about 3 / 4 inch of vegetable oil
11. Put egg rolls inches flip over when first side browned
12. When both sides browned, place on paper towel covered plate to drain grease
13. When all egg rolls are cooked, dip them in barbecue sauce and enjoy<|endoftext|>
