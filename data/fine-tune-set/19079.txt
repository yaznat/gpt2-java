Ingredients:
• Plums
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Balsamic vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Lemon juice
• Ground black pepper
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Preheat a grill pan to high heat
2. Drizzle fruit with oil to keep it from sticking and grill plums 3 minutes on each side
3. While fruit is grilling, pour balsamic vinegar into a small pot and place pot over medium high heat
4. When vinegar heats to a boil, reduce vinegar by half, 1 to 2 minutes
5. Combine sugar, vanilla and lemon juice and pepper in a small bowl
6. Whisk in warm balsamic vinegar
7. Place hot, grilled fruit on dessert plates
8. Top with a scoop of ice cream, if desired
9. Drizzle with balsamic glaze and serve<|endoftext|>
