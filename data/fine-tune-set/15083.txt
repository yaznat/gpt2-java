Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Salt & pepper
• Mozzarella cheese
• Pineapple
• Onion
• Prosciutto
• Frisee

Steps:
1. Stretch dough to fit a lightly oiled baking sheet
2. Brush with evoo and sprinkle with salt and pepper
3. Scatter mozzarella, pineapple, onion and prosciutto evenly over dough
4. Bake at 500 degrees on bottom rack until crust is crisp and cooked through, about 15 minutes
5. Top with frisee and cut into squares
6. Serves 4<|endoftext|>
