Ingredients:
• Quinoa
• Margarine
• Onion
• Fresh ginger
• Orange blossom water
• Honey
• Salt
• Ground coriander
• Ground cardamom
• Ground nutmeg
• Sweet potato
• Butternut squash
• Butter beans
• Cranberries

Steps:
1. Melt the margarine in a 2-quart saucepan over medium-high heat
2. Add the onion and ginger, and cook, stirring, until the onion is softened
3. Stir in the orange juice, water, honey, salt, coriander, cardamom, and nutmeg
4. Bring to a boil
5. Stir in the sweet potato and squash
6. Bring to a boil
7. Cook, uncovered, 7 minutes
8. Stir in the butter beans and quinoa, and return to a boil
9. Reduce the heat and simmer, covered, 15 minutes
10. Stir in the cranberries
11. Simmer, covered, 5 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
