What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Baked Custard Cups

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Vanilla
• Milk

Steps:
1. Combine first 5 ingredients, stir until blended
2. Slowly add scalded milk, stirring constantly
3. Pour into 4 custard cups
4. Set cups in 9x9 inch baking pan
5. Pour 1 inch hot water into pan
6. Bake at 350 for 35 minutes, or until knife comes out clean
7. Remove from pan, cool
8. Chill<|endoftext|>
