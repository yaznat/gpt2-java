What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Armadillo Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Paprika
• Salt and pepper
• Olive oil
• Idaho potatoes

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 450 degrees
2. In a bowl, mix together the bread crumbs, cheese, paprika, salt and pepper, and 1 tbs olive oil
3. Set the mixture aside
4. Transform each potato half into a armadillo by cutting 9 or so notches to inch deep in the rounded top
5. For each armadillo, cut a square of aluminum foil big enough to loosely wrap around it, brush the center with olive oil, then place the potato on it, flat side down
6. Sprinkle the top with some of the crumb mixture, pressing it into the grooves, then seal the potato in the aluminum foil
7. Arrange all of the potatoes on a baking sheet and bake them for 40 to 45 minutes
8. Be careful opening the pouches they're hot and probably full of steam<|endoftext|>
