Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Yellow bell pepper
• Sweet and sour sauce
• Balsamic vinaigrette
• Soy sauce
• Broccoli florets
• Instant rice

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add chicken & strips of bell pepper & cook 8 minutes or until chicken is cooked through, stirring occasionally
3. Add sweet & sour sauce, dressing, soy sauce & broccoli, mixing well
4. Cook for 2 minutes or until heated through, stirring occasionally
5. Serve over hot rice<|endoftext|>
