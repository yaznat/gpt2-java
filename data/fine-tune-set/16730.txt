What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Cafe Con Leche Milkshakes

Ingredients:
• Whole milk
• Coffee
• Coffee ice cream
• Simple syrup
• Coffee liqueur
• Chocolate-covered coffee beans

Steps:
1. Combine the milk, coffee, ice cream, simple syrup and liqueur in a blender
2. Blend until smooth
3. Pour in tall glasses and top with a few chocolate covered espresso beans<|endoftext|>
