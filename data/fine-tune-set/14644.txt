What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Rice Pudding With Dried Cherries

Ingredients:
• Nonfat milk
• Almond extract
• Egg
• Egg white
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Butter
• Lemon juice
• Dried cherries
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Lightly grease a baking dish with lid
3. Combine milk, almond extract, eggs, sugar, salt, butter and lemon juice
4. Beat well
5. Add cherries and rice
6. Stir to combine
7. Pour mixture into baking dish
8. Cover and bake until set, about 45-50 minutes<|endoftext|>
