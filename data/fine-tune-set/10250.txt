Ingredients:
• Butter
• Butter flavor shortening
• Light brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Dark chocolate chips
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Beat butter and shortening in large bowl until well blended
3. Add brown sugar and granulated sugar
4. Beat thoroughly
5. Add sugar and vanilla, beating until well blended
6. Combine flour, baking soda & salt
7. Gradually beat into butter mixture
8. Stir in chocolate chips and nuts, if desired
9. Drop by teaspoons onto greased cookie sheet
10. Bake 10-12 minutes or until lightly browned
11. Cool slightly
12. Remove from cookie sheet to wire rack
13. Cool completely
14. Serve with ice cold milk and enjoy<|endoftext|>
