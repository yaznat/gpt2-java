What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>Anti Martha Stewart Meatloaf

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Pork sausage
• Onion
• Egg
• Worcestershire sauce
• Ketchup
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Club crackers

Steps:
1. Begin preheating oven to 350 degrees
2. Combine first 9 ingredients in bowl and mix thoroughly
3. Mix in crushed crackers until well-blended
4. Pat mixture into ungreased loaf pan
5. Bake at 350 degrees for 60-90 minutes
6. At lower altitudes, it won't take as long<|endoftext|>
