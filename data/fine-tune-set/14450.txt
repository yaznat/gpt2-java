What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>Antipasto Style White Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Navy beans
• Red bell pepper
• Artichoke hearts
• Red onion
• Kalamata olive
• Hearts of palm
• Fresh parsley
• Mint leaf
• Basil
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Red wine vinegar
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Combine all vegetable and herb ingredients in a bowl, mix well
2. Toss with the vinegar, olive oil, salt and pepper
3. Refrigerate several hours, mixing occasionally to blend flavors<|endoftext|>
