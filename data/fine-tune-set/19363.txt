Ingredients:
• Flour
• Unsweetened baking cocoa
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Milk
• Butter
• Glaze
• Powdered sugar
• Hot water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Combine flour, cocoa, baking powder, and salt
3. In separate bowl mix eggs, sugar and vanilla until thick
4. Combine milk and butter
5. Alternately combine egg mixture and milk mixture with flour mixture and mix until smooth and soft
6. Spray pan lightly with cooking oil
7. Fill with batter 2 / 3 full
8. Bake 8 minutes
9. Cool
10. Carefully remove
11. Repeat with rest of batter
12. Frost or glaze
13. After the first batch is done let the pan cool, wipe clean with cloth or paper towel and repeat process for second half<|endoftext|>
