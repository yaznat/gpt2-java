Please give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Butterscotch Ice Cream Topping

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Water
• Heavy cream
• Butter

Steps:
1. Combine the sugar and water in a medium saucepan
2. Cook over high heat until the mixture is a light golden brown in color
3. Remove from heat
4. Add the cream and stir until smooth
5. Add the butter 1 tablespoon at a time
6. Blend well
7. Can be reheated<|endoftext|>
