Ingredients:
• Rice
• Sugar
• Rice wine vinegar
• Mirin
• Salt

Steps:
1. Measure out the amount of rice desired into a mesh colander
2. Rinse the rice grains three times with cold water
3. Pour the rice into a bowl and cover it with cold water
4. Cover the bowl and allow the rice to soak for two to eight hours
5. Take the lid and upper baskets off of the bamboo steamer
6. Line the bottom basket with a clean damp cloth
7. Drain the rice and pour the grains into the basket on top of the dish cloth
8. Place another damp dish cloth on top of the grains, folding the corners towards the center
9. Replace the lid on the basket
10. Fill a wok about halfway with water and bring the water to a boil
11. Place the bamboo steamer on the wok
12. The steamer should not be immersed in the water
13. Steam the rice for about 30 minutes
14. Checking every 10 minutes during the cooking process to ensure that water has not evaporated and that the cloth has not dried out
15. Check the rice for doneness after 15 minutes
16. Adding more time if needed, being careful not to overcook
17. Remove rice to a large bowl
18. Slowly heat vinegar, mirin and sugar until very hot but not boiling
19. Fold vinegar mixture into the hot rice, add enough so that the rice has a tart / sweet taste
20. Rice should be shiny, not mushy
21. Carefully clean inside rim of bowl with damp cloth, cover and let rest for 30 minutes before using<|endoftext|>
