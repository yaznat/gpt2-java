What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Artichoke Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Idaho potatoes
• Artichoke hearts
• Butter
• Milk
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Boil potatoes for 15-20 minutes or until potatoes are tender when pierced with a fork
2. Drain
3. In a large mixing bowl, pure artichoke hearts with half the butter and milk, using a hand blender or mixer
4. Add potatoes and remaining butter and milk and mix until smooth
5. Add salt and pepper to taste
6. Enjoy!
