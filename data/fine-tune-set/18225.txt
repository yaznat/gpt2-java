Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Raisin And Oatmeal Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Old fashioned oats
• Flour
• Light brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Sour cream
• Full-fat milk
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Bananas
• Raisins
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl combine the oats, flour brown sugar, baking powder, baking soda and cinnamon
2. In another bowl whisk together sour cream, milk, eggs and vanilla
3. Whisk dry ingredients into the sour cream mixture just until blended
4. Fold in mashed bananas, raisins and 1 / 4 cup melted butter
5. Mix to combine
6. Drop about 1 / 3 cup of batter onto hot skillet or griddle that has been brushed with melted butter
7. Cook pancakes until bubbles form on top and bottoms are golden brown
8. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
