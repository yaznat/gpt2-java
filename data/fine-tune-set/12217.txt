Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Egg
• Flour
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Hot water
• Vanilla
• Cooking apples
• Pecans
• Whipped cream
• Dark brown sugar
• Heavy cream
• Rum

Steps:
1. Cream butter, then gradually add sugar, beating well at medium speed with an electric mixer
2. Add egg and beat until blended
3. Combine flour, salt cinnamon and nutmeg and mix well
4. Add to creamed mixture
5. Beat on low speed until smooth
6. Stir in water and vanilla
7. Fold in apples and pecans and spoon into a greased and floured 9 inch pie pan
8. Bake at 350f for 45 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
9. For sauce
10. Combine brown sugar, sugar, butter, butter and cream in a small saucepan and mix well
11. Bring to a boil and cook for 1 minute
12. Stir in rum
13. Serve warm or at room temperature with cake and whipped cream<|endoftext|>
