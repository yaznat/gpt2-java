What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Curry Soup

Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Flour
• Onions
• Butter
• Beef broth
• Curry powder
• Bay leaves
• Potatoes
• Distilled white vinegar
• Salt

Steps:
1. Coat beef and onions in flour and brown in butter in a large pot
2. Add beef broth, curry powder, and bay leaves
3. Simmer for 30 minutes
4. Add potatoes, vinegar, and salt
5. Simmer for another 60 minutes
6. Serve over rice if desired<|endoftext|>
