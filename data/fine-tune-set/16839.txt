Give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Really Chocolate Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Chocolate syrup
• Chocolate liqueur
• Chocolate ice cream
• 2% low-fat milk
• Ground nutmeg
• Ice

Steps:
1. In a blender mix the first four ingredients together
2. Pour mixture into a glass and sprinkle with nutmeg
3. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
