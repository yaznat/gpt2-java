Ingredients:
• Egg
• Onion soup mix
• Ground beef
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Cheddar cheese
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Saltine
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. For the meatballs: in a bowl, combine egg and soup mix
2. Crumble beef over mixture and mix well
3. Divide into 36 portions
4. Set aside
5. In a bowl, combine the flour and milk until smooth
6. Add cheese and bacon
7. Mix well
8. Shape cheese mixture into 36 balls
9. Shape one beef portion around each cheese ball
10. For the coating: in a shallow bowl, beat the eggs
11. Place cracker crumbs in another bowl
12. Dip meatballs into egg, then coat with crumbs
13. In a large skillet over medium heat, cook meatballs in oil for 10-12 minutes or until the meat is no longer pink and coating is golden brown
14. Yield: 3 dozen<|endoftext|>
