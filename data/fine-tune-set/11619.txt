What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>3 Cheesecake

Ingredients:
• Ricotta cheese
• Cream cheese
• White sugar
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Sour cream
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f
2. Prepare a 10-inch springform pan by wrapping it with a layer of heavy duty alum foil
3. Cream together ricotta cheese, cream cheese and sugar until smooth
4. Mix in the eggs one at a time
5. Add flour one tablespoon at a time, mixing well
6. Stir in the sour cream and vanilla
7. Pour mixture into pan
8. Place cheesecake in a large rectangular pan filled with 2 inches of hot water
9. Carefully put into preheated oven
10. Bake for 90 minutes
11. After 90 minutes turn oven off, but leave the cake in the oven for another 60 minutes
12. Cool on counter for at-least 60 minutes and then refrigerate
13. Serve when thoroughly chilled<|endoftext|>
