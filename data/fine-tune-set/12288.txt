Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>3 C S Soup 3 Carrot Cauliflower Celery W Cheese

Ingredients:
• Head cauliflower
• Celery
• Carrots
• Water
• Margarine
• Salt
• Evaporated milk
• Milk
• Frozen peas
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In large stockpot add carrots, celery and cauliflower to 2 quarts water
2. Add margarine, salt
3. Cook 20 to 25 minutes on low heat, covered
4. Take off heat, add evaporated milk, two cups of milk and optional frozen peas
5. Put back on burner and heat on low and stir until warm and hot to serve
6. Do not boil
7. Ladle in serving bowls, pass the cheese if they want a soup topper
8. Serve with triscuits or a whole wheat cracker
9. This is a very rich soup
10. If you want to add more water, feel free<|endoftext|>
