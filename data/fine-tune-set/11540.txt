Ingredients:
• Oreo cookies
• Vanilla ice cream
• Frozen whipped topping
• Butter

Steps:
1. Prepare a 13 x 9-inch pan
2. For the crust
3. In a bowl combine the crust ingredients until well blended
4. Press gently into the bottom of the baking dish
5. Place one 20-ounce bag of sandwich cookies in a food processor, then process until coarsely crushed remove a little less than half of the crumbs to sprinkle on top of cake
6. In a bowl mix 1 / 2-gallon of the softened ice cream with the coarsely crushed cookies
7. Fold in half of the container cool whip topping until combined
8. Transfer and spread over the cookie crust
9. Sprinkle the reserved crushed sandwich cookies over the top
10. Freeze for a minimum of 5 hours or until completely frozen
11. If you are planning to freeze longer then this must be covered tightly with foil or it will pick up any freezer odors<|endoftext|>
