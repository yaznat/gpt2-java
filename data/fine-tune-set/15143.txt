Ingredients:
• Bagels
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic salt
• Cheese spread
• Pizza sauce
• Fresh parsley
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, brown the beef and onion
2. Drain the fat
3. Add salt, pepper, and garlic salt
4. Spread 2 teaspoons of cheese spread and 2 teaspoons of pizza sauce on each muffin
5. Spoon meat onto sauce
6. Sprinkle with parsley and parmesan cheese
7. Place on a cookie sheet and flash freeze
8. Then, place bagels in freezer bag
9. Store in freezer until ready to serve
10. To serve: bake frozen at 350 degrees for 20 to 30 minutes, or until bubbly<|endoftext|>
