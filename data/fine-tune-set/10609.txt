Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Shrimp

Ingredients:
• Fresh shrimp
• Angel hair pasta
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Shallot
• Asparagus spears
• Fresh mushrooms
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Chablis
• Parmesan cheese
• Fresh basil
• Fresh oregano
• Fresh thyme
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Peel and devein shrimp
2. Set aside
3. Cook pasta according to package directions
4. Drain and set aside
5. Heat a 9" skillet over high heat 1 minute
6. Add olive oil, and heat 10 seconds
7. Add shrimp, garlic, and shallot
8. Cook, stirring constantly, 2 to 3 minutes or until shrimp turn pink
9. Add asparagus and next 4 ingredients
10. Stir in wine, scaping particles that cling to bottom of skillet, if necessary, add pasta, parmesan cheese, and remaining ingredients
11. Toss gently
12. Serve immediately over angel hair pasta<|endoftext|>
