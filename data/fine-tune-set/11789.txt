Ingredients:
• Oil
• Boneless pork chops
• Onion
• Garlic
• Cooking apple
• Red bell pepper
• Chicken stock
• Cornstarch
• Curry powder
• Cumin
• Cinnamon
• Salt and pepper
• Parsley

Steps:
1. In a large saute pan, heat oil to med-high
2. Cook pork chops until browned on both sides, and almost cooked through
3. Remove from pan
4. Set aside
5. Over med heat, cook onion, garlic, apple, and red pepper strips for 2 minutes, or until softened
6. Blend chicken stock with cornstarch
7. Add to pan along with curry powder, cumin and cinnamon
8. Cook for 1-2 minutes, or until slightly thickened
9. Return pork chops to frypan
10. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper
11. Cook for 1-2 minutes, or until heated through
12. Serve pork chops with sauce, and sprinkle with fresh parsley<|endoftext|>
