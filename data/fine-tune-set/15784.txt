Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Bean Burrito Dip

Ingredients:
• Refried beans
• Pinto beans
• Sour cream
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Onion
• Rotel tomatoes & chilies
• Canned jalapeno slices
• Corn chips

Steps:
1. Mix refried beans, pinto beans, rotel tomato's, cheese and place in an 8x8 baking dish
2. Bake at 350 for 20-30 minutes ot until heated thru
3. After cooling slightly, put a layer of sour cream followed by the chopped onion and if you choose, the jalapeno's
4. Serve with dip size fritos or tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
