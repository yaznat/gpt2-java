What's a good coffee recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Strong coffee
• Milk

Steps:
1. Mix all dry ingredients
2. Add eggs, oil, coffee and milk and mix
3. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes at 350 degrees<|endoftext|>
