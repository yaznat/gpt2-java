Ingredients:
• Pinto beans
• Onion
• Salt pork
• Bacon
• Kosher salt
• Garlic
• Season-all salt
• Thyme
• Rosemary
• Black pepper
• Spice essence
• Seasoning
• Cumin
• Cayenne pepper
• Red pepper flakes
• Pickapeppa sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• A. 1. original sauce
• Soy sauce
• Chicken broth
• Beer
• White wine vinegar
• Red wine vinegar
• Jalapenos

Steps:
1. Fill a 12 quart kettle half full with hot water
2. Add 1 tbsp of kosher salt and beans
3. Soak for 1-2 hours
4. Do not throw out soaking water
5. Brings beans to a boil, turn heat to low and add chicken stock, salt pork, bacon
6. Add more water if necessary
7. Cook on low for 1 / 2 hour covered
8. Add the rest of the ingredients, cover and cook till beans are tender
9. Approximately 2-3 hours
10. If you like your beans a little spicer, add more red pepper flakes and serrano pepper
11. Stir the beans occasionally to keep them from sticking<|endoftext|>
