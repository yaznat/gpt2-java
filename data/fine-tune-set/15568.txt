Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Bacon
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Breadcrumbs
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Taco seasoning mix
• Ketchup
• Prepared mustard

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Toast bread crumbs
3. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients except 3 slices of bacon, mixing well
4. Press meat mixture into a 9 by 5 by 3-inch loaf pan, or shape into a loaf and place on a rack in a broiler pan
5. Place 3 slices of bacon on top of meatloaf
6. Bake for 40 minutes
