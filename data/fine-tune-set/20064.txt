Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sea salt
• Chives
• Pesto sauce
• Goat cheese
• Mixed salad green

Steps:
1. Use a fork to beat the eggs and salt in a small bowl
2. Beat well, until the eggs are mostly uniform in color - they seem to run around the pan more evenly when there aren't huge patches of yolk vs
3. Whites
4. In your largest non-stick skillet over medium heat pour the egg mixture and give it a good swirl so that they spread out thinly across the entire pan
5. Sprinkle the eggs with some of the chives and let them set, this happens quickly depending on the heat of your pan - 15 seconds to one minute
6. Run a spatula underneath the omelette and slide it out of the pan onto a countertop, large cutting board
7. Spread the pesto across the surface of the omelette, and then sprinkle with the cheese and salad greens
8. Starting with one end, roll the omelette away from you
9. Cut in half on a deep diagonal
10. Season with a bit more salt if needed and serve garnished with a few chopped chives<|endoftext|>
