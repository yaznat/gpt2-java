Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Black olives
• Green chili peppers
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Chipotle chiles in adobo
• Beef broth
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• White vinegar
• Flour tortillas
• Shredded cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. In a large skillet over medium heat, saute the ground beef for 5 minutes
3. Add the onion and garlic and saute until the ground beef is cooked
4. While ground beef is cooking, puree the chipotle peppers until smooth
5. Add a bit of water to the chipotles if necessary
6. Drain off any excess fat from the ground beef
7. Mix in the tomato paste with the ground beef
8. Continue cooking about 5 minutes being careful not to burn the mixture
9. Add beef broth, diced tomatoes, olives, green chilies, chipotle peppers, chili powder, cumin and vinegar
10. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 20 minutes
11. Spread a thin layer of the meat mixture in the bottom of a 4 quart casserole dish
12. Layer in the following order
13. Flour tortillas, meat mixture, cheese
14. Repeat for one layers
15. For omac, freeze at this point
16. I use 4-1 quart casserole dishes when making this for the freezer
17. Let thaw overnight before baking
18. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly<|endoftext|>
