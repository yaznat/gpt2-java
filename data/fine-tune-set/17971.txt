Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Cocoa powder
• Salt
• Vanilla extract
• Splenda sugar substitute

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Lightly grease with cooking spray an 8x8 square baking dish
3. Combine the black beans, eggs, oil, cocoa powder, salt, vanilla extract, splenda in a blender
4. Blend until smooth
5. Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish
6. Bake in the preheated oven until the top is dry and the edges start to pull away from the sides of the pan, about 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
