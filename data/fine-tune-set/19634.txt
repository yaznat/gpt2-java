Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Red onion
• Garlic cloves
• Vermouth
• Salt
• Chili pepper flakes
• Dried herbs
• Arugula
• Milk
• Blue cheese
• Mashed potatoes

Steps:
1. In a large pan heat oil over medium high heat
2. Add onions, and garlic cook about 3-4 minutes the pan
3. Deglaze the pan with vermouth
4. Add salt, pepper flakes, herbs, nutmeg and arugula
5. Remove to a dish and set aside
6. In the same pan heat milk add cheese to melt
7. Add potatoes and mix together
8. Toss in the wilted arugula
9. Season with additional salt and pepper if needed<|endoftext|>
