Generate a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>10 Min Japanese Simmered Tofu Hot Pot

Ingredients:
• Soft silken tofu
• Dried fish flakes
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• Sake
• Water
• Egg
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Cut the tofu into edible'cubes' and roughly chop the green onions on a bias for presentation
2. Evenly spread the fish flakes in a small earthenware casserole dish / pot / dutch oven and delicately arrange the cubed tofu on top
3. Add the soy sauce, sugar, sake and water
4. Cover and on moderate heat bring to a boil and then simmer for~5mins to allow the flavors to absorb into the tofu
5. Pour in a beaten egg around the tofu and top with the roughly chopped green onions as a garnish
6. Simmer for a final~30 seconds, cover and serve
7. Voila!<|endoftext|>
