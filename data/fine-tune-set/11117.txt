What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Bacon And Avocado Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Mayonnaise
• Fresh chives
• Fresh dill
• California avocado
• Fresh lemon juice
• Whole wheat bread
• Radish sprouts

Steps:
1. Cook bacon in a 12-inch heavy skillet over moderate heat, turning occasionally, until crisp, then transfer with tongs to paper towels to drain
2. Stir together mayonnaise, chives, dill, and salt and pepper to taste in a small bowl
3. Halve, pit, and peel avocado, then slice lengthwise
4. Gently toss slices with lemon juice
5. Make sandwiches with toast, herb mayonnaise, avocado, bacon, and sprouts, seasoning with salt and pepper<|endoftext|>
