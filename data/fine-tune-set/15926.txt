Ingredients:
• London broil beef
• Taco seasoning mix
• Onions
• White distilled vinegar
• Diced green chilies

Steps:
1. Preliminaries: spray inside of slow-cooker with nonstick spray
2. Rub seasoning mix over both sides of meat
3. Place meat in slow-cooker
4. Add onion, vinegar and chilies
5. Cover and cook on low setting 9 hours
6. Remove meat, reserving liquid
7. Shred meat with two forks
8. Combine meat with reserved cooking liquid
9. Stir well
10. Use meat for burritos<|endoftext|>
