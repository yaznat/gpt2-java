Ingredients:
• Cream-filled chocolate sandwich cookies
• Butter
• Cherry ice cream
• Miniature semisweet chocolate chips
• Vanilla ice cream
• Fudge sauce
• Sweetened whipped cream
• Fresh cherries

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Place 16 cookies in food processor
3. Cover and process until finely ground
4. Add butter
5. Cover and process until mixed
6. Press in springform pan, 9x3 inches
7. Bake 8 to 10 minutes or until firm
8. Cool completely, about 30 minutes
9. Wrap outside of springform pan with foil
10. Spread cherry ice cream over cooled crust
11. Freeze 30 minutes
12. Sprinkle chopped cookies and 1 / 2 cup of the chocolate chips over cherry ice cream
13. Press slightly
14. Spread vanilla ice cream over top
15. Drop fudge sauce over ice cream in small spoonfuls
16. Swirl slightly into ice cream
17. Sprinkle with remaining 1 / 2 cup chocolate chips
18. Press slightly
19. Freeze about 8 hours until firm
20. To serve, let stand at room temperature 5 to 10 minutes
21. Carefully remove side of pan
22. Cut dessert into wedges
23. Top each wedge with whipped cream and cherry<|endoftext|>
