Ingredients:
• Lamb cutlets
• Eggs
• Seasoned stuffing mix

Steps:
1. Break egg into a bowl and whisk
2. Dip cutlets into egg to coat
3. Coast cutlets in dry stuffing mix as you would to bread them
4. Place on a baking tray and cook at 350f for approx 40 minutes or until golden
5. Do not turn them over<|endoftext|>
