Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Turkey Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Red apple
• Green onions
• Lemon pepper
• Salt
• Apple pie spice
• Chili sauce
• Apple jelly
• Whole wheat hamburger buns

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix together the first 6 ingredients
2. Form mixture into 4 patties and set aside
3. In a small saucepan, mix together the chili sauce and apple jelly
4. Put saucepan over medium heat
5. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally
6. Set aside to cool slightly
7. Spray broiler pan with cooking spray
8. Broil patties 6 inches from heat for 4 to 6 minutes
9. Turn patties and broil 4 to 6 minutes longer or until patties are no longer pink
10. Place burgers on bottom halves of buns
11. Top each with 2 tablespoons of sauce
12. Cover with top halves of buns
13. Cooking time for the burgers may vary since i seem to have a very hot oven<|endoftext|>
