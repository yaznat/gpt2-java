Ingredients:
• Refrigerated crescent dinner roll
• Bacon
• Frozen hash brown potatoes
• Cheese
• Eggs
• Milk
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Unroll crescent roll dough into one long rectangle
2. Press onto the bottom and 1 / 2" up the sides of a greased 13x9" baking pan
3. Seal the seams and perforations
4. Mix together the bacon through the milk and pour on the crust
5. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese
6. Bake, uncovered, at 375f for 25-30 minutes or until eggs are completely set<|endoftext|>
