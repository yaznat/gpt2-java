Can you give me a salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Awesome Our Family Cranberry Salad

Ingredients:
• Cranberries
• Pecans
• Sugar
• Lemon jell-o gelatin
• Celery
• Hot water
• Crushed pineapple

Steps:
1. Mince up the cranberries
2. Usually takes a bag and half to get 3 cups
3. Chop the nuts med to small in size
4. Mix the lemon jello and hot water and pour in a 9x13 glass baking dish
5. Mix in the sugar and stir to dissolve
6. Chop up celery in small pieces
7. Open the pineapple can
8. Add all the remaining ingredients to the jello and cover with plastic wrap and chill in refrigerator until it sets
9. And serve when your ready<|endoftext|>
