Ingredients:
• Cream
• Tasty cheese
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Nam pla
• Tomato paste
• Fish fillet

Steps:
1. Make sauce by combining all ingredients in saucepan over medium heat until cheese is melted
2. Place fish fillets into baking dish
3. Spoon the sauce onto the fillets
4. Bake at 425 - 450 degrees for 10-15 minutes, or under the griller until cooked and top is browned
5. Fish should flake easily with a fork
6. On the other hand, it shouldn't look dried out either
7. Alternatively, you can also reduce the sauce in a frypan on a medium to hot heat on top of the stove
8. Turn heat down to medium and then cook the fillets in the sauce<|endoftext|>
