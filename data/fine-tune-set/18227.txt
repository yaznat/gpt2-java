What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Banana Rum Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Light rum
• Vanilla extract
• Bananas
• Granola cereal

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 275f
2. Sift flour, sugar, baking powder and salt into large bowl
3. Add eggs, milk, oil, rum and vanilla extract and whisk to blend
4. Heat large nonstick skillet over medium-low heat
5. Brush skillet generously with additional oil
6. Working in batches, drop batter by 1 / 4 cupfuls into skillet
7. Place 6 banana slices on each pancake, spacing evenly
8. Sprinle 1 tablespoon granola over each pancake if using granola option
9. Cook until pancakes are light and golden brown, about 3 minutes per side
10. Transfer pancakes to baking sheet and keep warm in oven
11. Repeat with remaining oil, batter, bananas and granola<|endoftext|>
