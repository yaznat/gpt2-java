What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Crispy Tofu Fingers

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Vegetarian oyster sauce
• Vegetable oil
• Salt
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Coat a cookie sheet with oil
2. Slice tofu lengthwise, about 1 / 4 inch thick, and pat dry
3. Put the sauce into a dish, and dip each piece of tofu into the sauce, coating it well
4. Place the tofu on the cookie sheet in a single layer
5. Sprinkle on desired seasoning
6. Bake at 400 degrees for 20 to 30 minutes, depending on how crispy you like it
7. Some people like it very chewy, while others prefer it when it's still soft<|endoftext|>
