Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Butter
• Water
• American cheese

Steps:
1. Melt butter in 10" non-stick pan
2. Whip eggs and water well with fork or whip
3. Add salt and pepper
4. Stir
5. Once butter melted, add egg mixture
6. In first 30 seconds make a few slits with spatula to allow eggs to cook in center
7. Over med-hi heat tilt pan back & forth so runny part of eggs cook
8. Pan should be nice & slippery for this!
9. When almost done, tear cheese slices & place over eggs
10. Allow cheese to melt slightly
11. Fold half egg over the other
12. Give about 30 seconds to set
13. Slip egg onto plate
14. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
