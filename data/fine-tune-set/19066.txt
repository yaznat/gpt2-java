Give me a ice cream recipe.<|endoftext|>Avocado And Strawberry Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Frozen strawberries
• Vanilla yogurt
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Peel the very ripe avocado -- the softer ones work better for this
2. Place everything in a food processor and run until smooth
3. If you have a sweetener in the yogurt you may skip the added sugar, or use stevia to make this sugar free
4. I use stevia, but this is the original recipe<|endoftext|>
