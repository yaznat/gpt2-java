Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Cream cheese
• Garlic powder
• Seasoning salt
• Onion powder
• Salsa
• Burrito-size flour tortillas
• Flour
• Water

Steps:
1. Mix first 6 ingredients well & set aside
2. Mix flour & water until it is pasty but not dry
3. Put about 1 / 3 cup chichen mix into each tortilla roll tightly, put paste on the bottom flap of tortilla and place flap side down for about 10 min so it will stay sealed
4. I like to cut them in half & fry at 350 until golden brown
5. These are good with salsa or sweet & sour sauce<|endoftext|>
