Ingredients:
• Pam cooking spray
• Bananas
• Whole grain bread
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Splenda brown sugar blend
• Egg white
• Nonfat milk
• Ground nutmeg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Lightly spray baking dish & arrange the banana slices in the bottom
3. Top with the bread squares, being sure to cover the banana
4. Whisk the splenda, egg whites, milk, and nutmeg together in a bowl and pour over the bread
5. Bake in the preheated oven for 30-40 minutes or until risen & golden<|endoftext|>
