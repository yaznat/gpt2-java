Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Lemon juice
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Parsley
• Bread
• Monterey jack cheese
• Tomatoes
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, mash together avocado, lemon juice, cumin and salt until smooth
2. Stir in parsley and set aside
3. Top four of the bread slices with cheese
4. Spread with avocado mixture and top with tomato slices
5. Add the four remaining bread slices on top
6. Using 1 tablespoon of the softened butter, spread some on the top of each sandwich
7. Heat a large nonstick griddle or 12-inch skillet, over medium-high heat
8. Add sandwiches, buttered sides down
9. Carefully spread the remaining softened butter over the top of each sandwich
10. Cook 4-6 minutes or until toasted, turning once<|endoftext|>
