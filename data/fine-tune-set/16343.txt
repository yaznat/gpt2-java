Ingredients:
• Whole turkey
• Kitchen twine
• Aluminum foil
• Butter
• Fresh sage
• Bacon
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 325f
2. Remove giblets and neck from turkey
3. Rinse turkey with cold running water and drain well
4. Blot dry with paper towels
5. Chop onion and garlic and mix together
6. Once chopped and mixed, stuff the onion and garlic mixture into turkey
7. Tie up the turkey's wings, legs, and drumsticks with kitchen twine as desired
8. Chop sage and mix in with softened butter
9. Rub sage / butter mixture under the skin and over the skin of the turkey
10. Salt and pepper turkey to taste
11. Place uncooked, bacon slices all over the skin of the uncooked turkey
12. They will stick onto the buttered turkey
13. Insert an oven-safe meat thermometer into the inner thigh area near the breast of the turkey but not touching the bone
14. Place turkey in a roasting pan and use foil to tent the uncooked turkey
15. Remove foil tent for last hour of cooking turkey
16. Continue to let bacon cook on turkey for an additional 15 minutes, then remove
17. Remove turkey from oven once 160 degrees is reached internally in the turkey
18. Allow turkey to rest for 20-30 minutes, then carve<|endoftext|>
