Ingredients:
• Gingerroot
• Oyster sauce
• Soy sauce
• Five-spice powder
• Brown sugar
• Cornstarch
• Dark sesame oil
• Chicken broth
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Salt
• Rice wine
• Egg white
• Cooking oil
• Peanut oil
• Garlic cloves
• Dried hot peppers
• Green onions
• Broccoli florets
• Toasted sesame seeds
• Jasmine rice

Steps:
1. Stir together sauce in a cup and set aside
2. Have all ingredients near the wok or large skillet
3. Mix together the chicken with salt and rice wine in a small bowl
4. Lightly beat the egg and stir in with the chicken, then sprinkle in the cornstarch
5. Allow this to sit for 20 minutes to absorb the wine flavor
6. Then add it to the wok
7. When it sizzles, lower heat and stir until the chicken turns white, then drain chicken on paper toweling, and remove excess oil form wok, moving it carefully out of your cooking area so it is not accidentally spilled
8. Add the combination of oils to wok, and when it is hot, toss in the garlic, hot peppers, and onions
9. When it sizzles and becomes fragrant, add the chicken again and cook for 2-3 minutes until chicken cooks through
10. Stir in sauce mixture and boil to thicken
11. Add water if you need to thin it
12. Then stir in the broccoli, trying to coat it and when it heats through, sprinkle with sesame seeds
13. Check to see if it needs any salt
14. Serve over fluffy white jasmine rice<|endoftext|>
