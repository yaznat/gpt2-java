What's a recipe for ice cream?<|endoftext|>Brown Sugar Banana Sauce For Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Light brown sugar
• Butter
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon
• Bananas
• Pecans
• Coconut
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Over low heat, combine brown sugar, butter and milk until sugar is melted and not gritty
2. Add in vanilla, cinnamon, bananas
3. Cook for 1 min
4. Remove from heat and let cool slightly
5. Top individual servings of ice cream with sauce and sprinkle toasted pecans and coconut over top
6. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
