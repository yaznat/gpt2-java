What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Amaranth Flax Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Amaranth flour
• Flax seeds
• Egg
• Water
• Butter
• Honey
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. Heat lightly greased frying pan on medium heat
2. A cast iron pan is preferred
3. In small bowl, mix amaranth flour and flax seed meal
4. Add egg to flour mixture
5. Beat with whisk until well blended
6. Add small amount of water to egg mixture then beat
7. Do this several times until the batter is thick, yet not runny
8. Pour batter into heated pan
9. Cover with glass lid
10. Flip after bubbles have formed
11. This will take approximately one minute
12. Cook for another one or two minutes
13. Place on plate
14. Allow butter to melt on pancake
15. Then drizzle honey over pancake
16. Add fruit and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
