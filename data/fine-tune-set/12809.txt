What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Round Steak

Ingredients:
• Round steak
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Paprika
• Worcestershire sauce
• Ketchup
• Salt
• Dry mustard
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Cut round steaks into crosswise slices about 1 / 2 inch wide
2. Heat oil
3. Brown steak on all sides
4. Use a slotted spoon to remove meat to a baking dish
5. Set aside
6. Pour off fat, then place remaining ingredients in skillet
7. Bring to a boil then reduce heat and simmer for 2 minutes
8. Pour over steak, cover tightly with foil, and bake at 350 for 1 hour
9. Uncover and bake for 30 minutes longer
10. Serve with rice or potatoes and a salad
11. Barbecue round steak recipe serves 4 to 6<|endoftext|>
