Generate a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Combination Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Garlic clove
• Diced onion
• Bell pepper
• Meat
• Frozen peas
• Cooked rice
• Soy sauce
• Chili-garlic sauce
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat 1 to 2 minutes
2. Add eggs and gently stir 1 minute or until softly scrambled
3. Remove eggs from skillet
4. Chop
5. Heat remaining 2 tablespoons oil in skillet
6. Add onion, garlic and bell pepper and stir-fry 3 minutes
7. Add chopped cooked meat and peas
8. Stir-fry 2 minutes
9. Add rice, soy sauce and chili-garlic sauce
10. Stir-fry 5 minutes or until thoroughly heated
11. Stir in eggs
12. Garnish with green onions<|endoftext|>
