Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Ground cinnamon
• Chocolate wafer cookies
• Coffee ice cream
• Heavy whipping cream
• Sugar
• Chocolate-covered coffee beans

Steps:
1. Mix softened vanilla ice cream and cinnamon in a large bowl until blended
2. Spread evenly over the bottom of an 8-in
3. Springform pan
4. Sprinkle with crushed cookies
5. Spoon coffee ice cream on top and press evenly with the back of the spoon
6. Cover and freeze at least 5 hours, or wrap airtight and freeze up to one month
7. To serve:
8. Run a long thin knife around edge of pan to loosen torte
9. Place on a serving plate and remove sides of pan
10. Beat whipping cream until stiff with 1 tablespoons sugar
11. Spoon whipped cream into a zipper-type gallon size plastic bag
12. Snip off a corner and pipe small dollops of cream around edge of torte
13. Sprinkle with cinnamon and decorate with coffee-bean candies if desired<|endoftext|>
