What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado Blt Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Mayonnaise
• Avocados
• Red onions
• Roasted chicken breast
• Bacon
• Lettuce leaves
• Tomatoes
• Sea salt
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Spread one side of each slice of bread evenly with mayonnaise
2. Spread half the number with mashed avocado
3. Then top with red onion
4. Place the chicken, bacon, lettuce leaves and tomato on top
5. Season with salt and pepper
6. Top with remaining slices of bread and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
