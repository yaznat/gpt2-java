• White fish fillets
• Baby bok choy
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Fish sauce
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Place a stir-fry pan over high heat until hot
2. Add oil, swirling to coat sides
3. Add fish and cook, stirring, until it turns opaque, about 1 1 / 2 minutes
4. Add rice, separating grains with back of a spoon
5. Add sliced greens, fish sauce, and soy sauce
6. Stir to combine and cook until rice is heated through, 2 to 3 minutes
