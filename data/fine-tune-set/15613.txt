Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Chunky salsa
• Taco seasoning mix
• Large refrigerated biscuits
• Mexican blend cheese
• Salsa
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Brown beef and drain off any fat
2. Add salsa, taco seasoning and 1 / 2 cup water
3. Simmer until thickened
4. With a rolling pin, roll out each biscuit into a 6-inch wide circle
5. Divide the beef mixture evenly among the biscuits
6. Place filling on half the biscuit, leaving a 1 / 2-inch margin at the edge
7. Sprinkle 1 tbs cheese over filling on each biscuit
8. Fold other half of biscuit over the filling
9. Press the edges together and crimp with a fork to seal
10. Bake at 375f 10-12 minutes until golden brown
11. Serve hot
12. If desired serve with sour cream and additional salsa<|endoftext|>
