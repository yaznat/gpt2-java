Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Minute brown rice
• Onion soup mix
• Coleslaw
• Tomato sauce
• Tomato soup
• Water
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Brown meat, onion and garlic
2. Drain well
3. Stir rice and onion soup mix into meat mixture
4. Place coleslaw in a lightly greased 9x13 glass pan
5. Spread meat mixture evenly over the cabbage
6. Mix together the tomato sauce, tomato soup and water and pour over the cabbage and meat
7. Top with cheese, if using
8. Cover and bake at 350 degrees for an hour and a half<|endoftext|>
