Ingredients:
• Chicken sausage
• Garlic clove
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Carrots
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Use cooking spray on medium heat in a large skillet or wok
2. Break up and cook sausage until browned
3. Add onion, green pepper and carrots and fry until cooked
4. Add soy sauce and cook for 2 minutes<|endoftext|>
