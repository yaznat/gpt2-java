Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Low-fat milk
• Egg
• Dark rum
• Bananas
• Gingersnaps

Steps:
1. Mix sugar and cornstarch together
2. Pour milk into medium-size microwaveproof bowl
3. Whisk in sugar mixture until cornstarch dissolves
4. Microwave on high 4 minutes, stirring once
5. Whisk a little of the hot milk mixture into beaten egg
6. Return to bowl
7. Sti r in rum
8. Microwave on high 1 to 2 minutes longer until custard thickens
9. Remove from heat
10. Cool slightly
11. Slice a layer of bananas into each of 4 individual dessert dishes
12. Add a layer of cookies and top with another layer of bananas
13. Pour custard over top bananas
14. Serve warm or chilled
