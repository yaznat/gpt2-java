What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Arroz Con Gandules Rice With Pigeon Peas

Ingredients:
• Salt pork
• Cubed ham
• Rice
• Tomato sauce
• Diced tomatoes
• Pigeon peas
• Food coloring
• Capers
• Stuffed green olives
• Ground cumin
• Italian seasoning
• Salt & pepper
• Fresh cilantro
• Red pepper
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Garlic

Steps:
1. In a large pot, fry salt pork in olive oil until halfway done
2. Add ham and continue frying
3. Add rice and saut until rice is browned
4. Add about cup to 1 cup sofrito, tomato sauce and canned tomatoes
5. Add gandules and 4 cups of water
6. Add olives and capers, cumin, oregano, salt and pepper to taste and bijol
7. Cook uncovered over medium-high heat 10 minutes, then cover, lower heat and continue cooking 1520 minutes until done
8. Sofrito: blend 1 bunch cilantro, 1 red pepper, 1 onion, 1 green pepper and 1 head garlic in blender until smooth<|endoftext|>
