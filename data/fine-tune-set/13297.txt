Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>A Different Way To Make Chicken Rice

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breast
• Chicken liver
• Tomato sauce
• Cooked rice
• Red bell pepper
• Onion
• Chicken stock
• Salt and pepper
• Cheese
• Soy sauce
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Marinate chicken meat and livers for 30 minutes
2. Saut onions, chicken meat and livers
3. Cook for 5 minutes and season with salt and pepper
4. Add tomato sauce and simmer for 5 minutes
5. Set aside
6. Heat 4 tbsp margarine over moderate flame
7. Add cooked rice and stir with wooden spoon
8. Pour stock and cook for 15 minutes stirring occasionally to prevent sticking
9. Add sauted chicken mixture, cheese and bell peppers
10. Mix thoroughly
11. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
