Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Canola oil
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Place oven rack in lowest position and preheat oven to 475
2. Coat baking sheet with cooking spray
3. Soak potatoes in bowl of hot water for 10 minutes
4. Drain, pat dry, and return to dry dowl
5. Meanwhile, heat canola oil, oilve oil, and garlic in saucepan over medium-low heat
6. Cook garlic 2 minutes, or until it begins to brown
7. Transfer garlic to small bowl with slotted spoon
8. Pour oil over potatoes, and toss to coat
9. Season with salt and pepper
10. Arrange potatoes in a single layer on prepared baking sheet
11. Bake for 20 minutes, or until, or until golden on bottom
12. Turn and bake ten minutes, or until crisp and golden
13. Meanwhile, stir parsley into reserved garlic
14. Sprinkle over fries
15. Season with salt and pepper, and serve immesiately<|endoftext|>
