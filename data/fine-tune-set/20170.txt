What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Beef And Onion Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Beef
• Onions
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic clove
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Slice beef into i inch strips
2. Cut onions into quarters
3. In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, and cornstarch
4. In a skillet, heat the oil and saute the garlic for a bit
5. Add beef and cook until almost completely cooked
6. Add onions and stir for a minute
7. Add the soy sauce mixture and cook until done
8. Add pepper and stir<|endoftext|>
