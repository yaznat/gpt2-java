Give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Burritos Made Easy

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Chili with beans
• Chunky salsa
• Green chilies
• Flour tortillas
• American cheese
• Taco sauce
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook ground beef and onion over medium heat until meat is no longer pink
2. Drain off fat
3. To meat mixture, add canned chili, salsa, and green chilies
4. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer, uncovered, for 5 minutes
5. Spoon about 1 / 2 cupful of beef mixture off-center on each tortilla
6. Top each with a slice of cheese
7. Roll up
8. Serve with taco sauce and lettuce if desired<|endoftext|>
