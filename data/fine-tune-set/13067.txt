Give me a recipe for fish.<|endoftext|>Basic Fish Fry

Ingredients:
• Haddock fillets
• Evaporated milk
• Black pepper
• All-purpose flour
• Yellow cornmeal
• Paprika
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. In one pie plate, combine the milk and pepper
2. This is your 'wet' mix
3. In another pie plate, combine the flour, cornmeal and paprika
4. This is your 'dry' mix
5. In an electric skillet, heat the oil to 350f
6. Dip fish into the 'wet' mix, then roll in the 'dry' mix to cover completely
7. Fry the fish filets, a few at a time, in the hot oil for approx 4 min or less per side, until the fish is nicely browned and serve
8. Don't fry them all at the same time or you'll cool down the oil too much and it will have to heat back up to temperature
9. If frying in a dutch oven, use a meat thermometer to check your oil temperature, before you fry
10. If using a deep fryer machine, please follow your manufacturer's instructions / booklet
11. Substitutions: if haddock isn't available, substitute hake, pollock, flounder, tilapia or cod instead
12. You can also use the skim version of evaporated milk<|endoftext|>
