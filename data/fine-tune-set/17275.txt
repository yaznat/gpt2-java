Please give me a recipe for tea.<|endoftext|>Atay Bi Na Na Mint Tea

Ingredients:
• Green tea
• Fresh mint leaves
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Warm a teapot by swirling a little hot water around the teapot then discarding it
2. Put the tea in the teapot, add the mint leaves, and 1-2 tsp sugar per person, according to taste
3. Pour in boiling water and stir once only
4. Infuse for 5 minutes then serve in your beautiful tea glasses or tea cups and serve at once<|endoftext|>
