Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Water
• Blueberries
• Cream cheese
• Fresh lemon rind

Steps:
1. Cream together 1 1 / 4 cups sugar and 1 / 2 cup butter until light and fluffy
2. Add eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each addition
3. Combine 2 cups flour, baking powder and salt
4. Add to creamed mixture alternately with milk and water
5. Toss blueberries in 1 / 4 cup flour
6. Fold into batter with cream cheese and 1 tsp lemon rind
7. Pour into greased and floured 13 x 9 inch baking pan
8. Combine remaining sugar, flour and rind
9. Cut in remaining butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs
10. Sprinkle over batter
11. Bake in preheated 375 degree oven for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
