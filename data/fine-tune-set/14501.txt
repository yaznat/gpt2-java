Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Dry sherry
• Sesame oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Black bean sauce
• Hot chili paste
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic clove
• Chicken broth
• Oyster sauce
• Yellow onion
• Vegetable oil
• Fresh asparagus
• Water

Steps:
1. For marinade: combine 2 teaspoons cornstarch, 2 teaspoons soy sauce, sherry and sesame oil in a large bowl
2. Mix well
3. Add chicken
4. Stir to coat well
5. Let stand 30 minutes
6. Combine black bean sauce, hot chili paste, ginger and garlic
7. Set aside
8. Combine remaining 3 teaspoons cornstarch, remaining 2 teaspoons soy sauce, chicken broth and oyster sauce in a small bowl
9. Mix well
10. Set aside
11. Peel onion, cut into 8 wedges
12. Separate wedges
13. Set aside
14. Heat 2 tablespoons vegetable oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat
15. Add chicken
16. Stir-fry until cooked through, about 3 minutes
17. Remove chicken from wok
18. Set aside
19. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in wok
20. Add onion and asparagus
21. Stir-fry 30 seconds
22. Add water and cover
23. Cook, stirring occasionally, until asparagus is crisp-tender, about two minutes
24. Return chicken to wok
25. Stir chicken broth mixture
26. Add to wok with black bean mixture
27. Cook until sauce boils and thickens, stirring occasionally
28. Serve over or with rice<|endoftext|>
