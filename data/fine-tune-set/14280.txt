Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Garlic
• Red pepper
• Ground cumin
• Parsley
• Firm tofu
• Tamari
• Empanada wrappers

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. While the rice is cooking, saute the garlic for 3 minutes, add the pepper, cumin, parsley, tofu, and tamari and cook for 5 more minutes
3. Add the cooked rice and mix well
4. With a spoon, scoop some of the filling in the upper half of the empanada round, making sure to leave a large enough border
5. Fold the lower half over the top part to form a semi-circle
6. Press the edges together and go around the edge with the teeth of a fork to seal it
7. Place on a non-stick cookie sheet and bake for 20 minutes or until golden<|endoftext|>
