What's a good coffee recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Coffee Biscuits

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg
• Margarine
• Banana
• Coffee
• Skim milk
• Vegetable oil cooking spray

Steps:
1. Combine first 7 ingredients in a medium bowl
2. Cut in margarine with a pastry blender until mixture resembles coarse meal
3. Combine banana, coffee and milk
4. Stir well
5. Add to flour mixture, stirring just until dry ingredients are moistened
6. Sprinkle 1 tbl flour over work surface
7. Turn dough out onto floured surface, and knead 3 to 4 times
8. Roll dough to -inch thickness
9. Cut with a 2-inch biscuit cutter
10. Transfer to a baking sheet coated with cooking spray
11. Bake at 425 degrees for 8 to 10 minutes or until lightly browned<|endoftext|>
