Give me a cookie recipe.<|endoftext|>60 Sec Chocolate Oat Peanut Butter Cookies

Ingredients:
• Quick oats
• Sugar
• Milk
• Butter
• Peanut butter
• Cocoa
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Have all ingredients measured and ready
2. Place sugar, milk, butter and the cocoa in pan
3. Bring to boil
4. Boil for 1 minute
5. Remove from burner
6. Quickly, stir in the quick oats, peanut butter, and vanilla extract
7. Drop onto wax paper by round teaspoonfuls
8. Leave to stand approximately 1 / 2 hour<|endoftext|>
