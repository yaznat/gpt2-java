Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Baileys Irish Cream Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Granulated sugar
• Salt
• Egg
• Milk
• Irish cream
• Cake flour
• Baking powder
• Butter
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Whisk butter, sugar, salt and egg in medium bowl
2. Whisk in milk and irish cream liqueur until combined
3. Add flour and baking powder to the bowl, then stir until just about smooth
4. In a large non-stick skillet over medium-low heat, heat 1 tablespoon butter
5. Drop as many 1 / 4-cup portions into the skillet as will fit without crowding, then gently spread out into a disc shape
6. When bubbles appear on the surface, flip the pancakes over and continue to cook until done, about 1-2 minutes more
7. Repeat with remaining batter
8. Serve warm with butter and syrup<|endoftext|>
