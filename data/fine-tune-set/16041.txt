Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Coriander
• Green onion
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Puree black beans and salsa together
3. Add coriander and green onions to mixture
4. Mix well
5. Divide evenly in bowl into 1 / 6's
6. Spread over tortillas
7. Sprinkle with cheese
8. Fold tortilla in half
9. Place on baking sheet so they aren't overlapping too much
10. Bake for 5 minutes and then flip over for another 4-5 minutes
11. Serve with salsa and sour cream<|endoftext|>
