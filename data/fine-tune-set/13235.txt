Ingredients:
• Butter
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh thyme
• Whole chicken
• Honey
• Vanilla extract
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Chicken broth
• 2% low-fat milk
• Flour

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f in a small bowl, mix the butter, garlic, and half of the thyme
2. Gently lift the skin from the chicken with your fingers and carefully slide some of the herbed butter underneath the skin
3. Spread remaining seasoned butter all over the outside of the chicken
4. Mix the honey, vanilla, salt, pepper, a other half of the thyme, and chicken broth in a microwave safe bowl
5. Microwave for 30 seconds or till warmed thru
6. Place the chicken into a roasting pan and pour the honey mixture over the chickens and into the pan
7. Cook for 1 1 / 2 hours or until the internal temperature reaches 165f (cover loosely with foil if the bird is browning too quickly
8. Remove from the oven
9. Drain pan to get the juices and return the chicken to the oven
10. Continue cooking to an internal temperature of 180f
11. Using a spoon, skim off the excess fat from the broth
12. Pour the broth into a saucepan on med-high heat and simmer for about 2-3 minutes
13. In a small bowl, mix milk and flour
14. Add to simmering broth along with the 3 tbsp of butter
15. Stir or whisk gently and consistently until thickened
16. Remove from heat to a gravy dish and top with the reserved sprig of thyme
17. Remove chicken from the oven and let sit about 5 minutes before serving with the gravy<|endoftext|>
