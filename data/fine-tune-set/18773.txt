What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Berry Chocolate Pudding Yogurt Parfait

Ingredients:
• Nonfat plain yogurt
• Sugar-free instant chocolate fudge pudding mix
• 1% low-fat milk
• Blackberry
• Raspberries
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. Mix 1 / 4 package of jello instant pudding powder into 1 / 4 cup milk with a spoon until thick and creamy
2. In any dessert cup, layer plain nonfat yogurt on top of chocolate pudding
3. Finally, add berries on top of nonfat yogurt
4. Dig spoon into all three layers and enjoy!
5. You can add one teaspoon of granola / muesli on top if you like<|endoftext|>
