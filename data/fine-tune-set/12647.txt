What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Fennel Orange Gremolata

Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• Eye of round roast
• Fennel bulbs
• Red bell peppers
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Carrot
• Water
• Tomato paste
• Fennel seed
• Salt
• Dried thyme
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Orange rind
• Diced tomatoes
• Beef broth
• Parsnip
• Fennel bulb
• Fennel leaves

Steps:
1. To prepare stew, heat a large dutch oven coated with cooking spray over medium-high heat
2. Add beef
3. Cook 6 minutes, browning on all sides
4. Remove from pan
5. Add 2 cups fennel bulb, bell pepper, onion, and garlic to pan
6. Reduce heat to medium, and cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Add beef, carrot, and next 9 ingredients
8. Cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer 1 hour, stirring occasionally
9. Add parsnip
10. Cover and simmer 30 minutes or until beef is tender, stirring occasionally
11. To prepare gremolata, combine 1 / 2 cup fennel bulb, fennel fronds, and grated orange rind in a small bowl
12. Spoon 1 1 / 3 cups stew into each of 6 bowls
13. Top each with 1 1 / 2 tablespoons gremolata<|endoftext|>
