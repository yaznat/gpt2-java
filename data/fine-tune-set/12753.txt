Can you give me a steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Autumn Delight Cube Steaks Slow Cooker Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Beef cube steaks
• Olive oil
• Condensed cream of mushroom soup
• Water
• Dry onion soup mix

Steps:
1. Set slowcooker to low
2. In a large skillet, brown steaks in oil
3. Place in preheated slow cooker
4. Mix cans of soup, 1 / 2 cup water for each can of soup, and soup mix
5. Pour over steaks
6. Cover and cook on low for 4 to 6 hours<|endoftext|>
