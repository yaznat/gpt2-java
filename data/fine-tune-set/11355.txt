Ingredients:
• Fresh lemon juice
• Milk
• Sweet butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Eggs
• Unbleached flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Bananas
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Butter 2 loaf pans
3. Pour the lemon juice into the milk and stir until the milk is curdled
4. Set aside
5. Beat together the butter, sugar and vanilla until creamy
6. Add the eggs, mixing thoroughly
7. Sift together the flour, baking soda and salt
8. Add this to the butter-sugar mixture alternately with the curdled milk
9. Stir in the bananas and nuts
10. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pans and bake for 1 hour and 10 minutes, or until a knife inserted into the center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
