What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Black Pepper And Lime Oven Fries

Ingredients:
• Small potatoes
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Sea salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Lime
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375, oven racks in the middle
2. In a medium bowl toss the potato wedges with the olive oil, a few big pinches of sea salt and five or six cranks of the pepper grinder
3. Arrange the potatoes cut side down on a baking sheet
4. Place in the oven for 30-35 minutes, tossing the potatoes with a spatula half way through
5. While the potatoes are baking, zest the lime and cut it into a few wedges
6. When the potatoes are cooked through, remove them from the oven, taste, and adjust the seasoning
7. Add more salt and pepper to taste
8. Serve in a big shallow bowl, or on a platter, drizzled with lime juice and dusted with the lime zest and parmesan<|endoftext|>
