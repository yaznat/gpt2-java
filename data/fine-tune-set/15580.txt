What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Taco Bowl Salad

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Kosher salt
• Taco seasoning
• Ground beef
• Lettuce leaves
• Tomatoes
• Green onion
• Sliced ripe olives
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Spray one side of each tortilla with no-stick cooking spray
2. Lightly sprinkle with salt
3. Place oiled side of tortilla over ovenproof 7x3-inch mixing bowl
4. Place 6 oz
5. Custard cup upside-down in center of tortilla
6. Gently press custard cup and tortilla into bowl
7. Bake in a preheated 375 f oven until edges are golden brown
8. Remove to wire rack
9. Remove custard cup
10. Invert shell
11. Cool completely
12. Repeat with remaining tortillas
13. In 12-inch skillet, brown ground beef over medium-high heat
14. Stir in taco spices & seasonings and continue preparing according to package directions
15. To serve:
16. Place 1 1 / 2 cups lettuce in each shell
17. Top each with 3 / 4 cup warm meat mixture
18. Sprinkle with cheese, tomatoes, green onions and olives<|endoftext|>
