Ingredients:
• Granny smith apples
• Granulated sugar
• Water
• All-purpose flour
• Light brown sugar
• Whole milk
• Egg
• Unsalted butter
• Golden syrup
• Boiling water
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. For the pudding: preheat oven to 350 degrees f, then butter the inside bottom of a 2-quart casserole dish
2. Place casserole dish on a baking tray
3. Place apples in a medium, microwave-proof bowl, then sprinkle with sugar & water
4. Cover & microwave on high for 6-8 minutes or until tender, then cool in the bowl for 10 minutes
5. Drain the apples & arrange them in the prepared casserold dish
6. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour & brown sugar
7. In another bowl, whisk together the milk, egg, melted butter & golden syrup, then add this mixture to the flour ixture & gently stir to combine
8. Spoon mixture over apples & smooth the surface
9. For the syrup: evenly sprinkle a second 1 / 3 cup of brown sugar over the pudding mixture
10. In a heatproof container, combine the second 1 / 3 cup of golden syrup with the boiling water, then drizzle this mixture over the pudding
11. Bake for 50-55 minutes or until golden & cooked through ~ if necessary, cover the pudding lightly with aluminum foil if / when the top is a deep golden
12. For a garnish: when finished baking, set aside to cool for 5 minutes, then dust with powdered sugar & serve with ice cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
