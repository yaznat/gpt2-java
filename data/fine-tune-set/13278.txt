Give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>50 S Style Creamed Chicken

Ingredients:
• Canned chicken breast meat
• Reduced-fat cream of mushroom soup
• Sliced mushrooms
• No yolks egg noodle substitute
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Nonfat sour cream

Steps:
1. Cook the onion in the oil in a large skillet over medium heat till tender
2. Dump in chicken and stir
3. Cook for one minute
4. Stir in the soup and mushrooms
5. Simmer for 10 minutes
6. Stir in the sour cream over low heat
7. Heat through and serve over the cooked noodles<|endoftext|>
