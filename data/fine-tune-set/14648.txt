Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Boneless chicken breast halves
• Salt
• Black pepper
• All-purpose flour
• Low-fat buttermilk
• Honey mustard
• Sliced almonds
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Cooking spray
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450
2. Prepare rice according to package directions
3. Keep warm
4. Place each chicken breast half between 2 sheets of heavy-duty plastic wrap
5. Pound to 1 / 2-inch thickness using a meat mallet or rolling pin
6. Sprinkle both sides of chicken with 1 / 4 teaspoon salt and pepper
7. Place flour in a zip-top plastic bag
8. Working with one piece at a time, add chicken to bag
9. Seal and shake to coat
10. Remove chicken from bag, shaking off excess flour
11. Repeat with remaining flour and chicken
12. Combine buttermilk and honey mustard in a shallow bowl
13. Combine almonds and breadcrumbs in a shallow bowl
14. Dip chicken in buttermilk mixture
15. Dredge in almond mixture
16. Place chicken in a baking dish and bake for 20 minutes or until browned on the outside and chicken juices run clear when pierced
17. Add 1 / 2 teaspoon salt and green onions to rice
18. Serve with chicken<|endoftext|>
