Can you give me a salad recipe?<|endoftext|>11Th Hour Spinach Caesar Salad

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Miracle whip light
• Low-fat mayonnaise
• Anchovy fillets
• Garlic cloves
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dijon mustard
• Lemon juice
• Parmesan cheese
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Fresh mushrooms
• Oil
• French bread
• Olive oil
• Baby spinach

Steps:
1. Bacon bits: in a non stick frypan, fry bacon until all fat is rendered
2. Cool on paper towels then coarsely chop into bits and set aside
3. Dressing: in a blender process until smooth the miracle whip, mayonnaise, anchovy filets, 4 minced cloves of garlic, worcestershire sauce, dijon mustard, lemon juice, 3 tablespoons freshly grated parmesan cheese and the 1 / 4 cup extra virgin olive oil
4. Pour into container and refrigerate until ready to use
5. Mushrooms: heat 1 tablespoon oil and saute mushrooms lightly - set aside
6. Croutons: heat oil on low and add 2 minced garlic cloves
7. Toss cubed bread with garlic oil and 1 / 4 cup parmesan cheese
8. Place in 350 degree oven until lightly toasted
9. The salad: in a large salad bowl toss the spinach with as much of the dressing as you prefer
10. Season with freshly ground black pepper
11. Add the bacon bits - sauted mushrooms - and croutons
12. Extra grated parmesan cheese can also be added<|endoftext|>
