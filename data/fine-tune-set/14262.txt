What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Delicious Scrambled Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Soy sauce
• Onion powder
• Salt
• Turmeric
• Italian seasoning
• Green bell pepper
• Tomatoes
• Green chilies
• Black salt

Steps:
1. Crumble tofu with hands
2. In a skillet mix tofu and everything except the tomatoes together well
3. Heat for 5 to 8 minutes
4. Add the tomatoes and then continue heating until thoroughly heated (about 5 to 8 minutes more<|endoftext|>
