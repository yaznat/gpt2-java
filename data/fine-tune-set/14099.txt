Ingredients:
• Teriyaki sauce
• Tofu
• Garlic cloves
• Peanut oil
• Stir fry vegetables

Steps:
1. Slice the tofu up into 1 / 4" thick slices and then cut them again into 1"x2" pieces
2. Mix teriyaki & garlic together
3. Marinate them in teriyaki and garlic for 12-hours in the refrigerator
4. In wok on medium heat add peanut oil
5. Cook up the the tofu for about 2-3 minutes, flip and cook them for another 2-3 minutes
6. Remove tofu and set aside
7. Add vegetables to wok and stir fry until tender about 3 minutes
8. Add tofu and back to the wok, cook for 2 minutes more, stir throughly coating everything
9. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
