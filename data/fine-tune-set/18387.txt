Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Bob Evans Waffles Or Pancakes Copycat

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat waffle iron
2. If preparing waffles in advance, preheat oven to 200f
3. Whisk flour, sugar and baking powder in large bowl
4. Whisk eggs and milk in medium bowl until well blended
5. Pour liquid ingredients over dry ingredients
6. Whisk until just combined
7. Stir in butter until blended
8. 1 / 2 cup of batter per waffle
9. Serve immediately or hold in oven until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
