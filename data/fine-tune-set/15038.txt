Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Simple Fried Rice With Mushrooms And Tofu

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Onion
• Thai red chili peppers
• Tamari
• Brown sugar
• Ketchup
• Salt
• Cooked brown rice
• Mushrooms
• Baked tofu
• Green peas
• Bamboo shoot
• Cucumber

Steps:
1. In a small food processor blend the onion and chilies into a coarse paste
2. Warm the oil, over medium-high heat in a wok or large saute pan
3. When hot add the onion / chili paste and cook for about 4 minutes
4. Whisk together the tamari, sugar and ketchup and add to the onion paste
5. Cook for another minute
6. Add the rice and stir until completly coated in the onion / tamari mixture
7. Add the mushrooms, tofu and salt and cook until the mushrooms are soft and the tofu heated through
8. Add the peas and cook until defrosted
9. Taste for seasoning
10. Serve with the bamboo shoots and cucumber over the top<|endoftext|>
