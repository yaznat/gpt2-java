Ingredients:
• Lasagna noodles
• Lean ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Egg whites
• Ricotta cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Chunky salsa

Steps:
1. Cook noodles according to package directions
2. Meanwhile, in a large skillet, cook beef over medium heat until no longer pink
3. Drain
4. Stir in taco seasoning
5. In a small bowl, combine egg whites and ricotta cheese
6. Drain noodles
7. In each of two 13-in
8. X 9-in
9. X 2-in
10. Baking dishes, layer four noodles, 3 / 4 cup ricotta mixture, half of the beef mixture and 1-1 / 3 cups cheddar cheese
11. Top each with four noodles, 3 / 4 cup ricotta mixture, 1-1 / 2 cups salsa and 1-1 / 3 cups cheese
12. Repeat
13. Cover and freeze one casserole for up to 3 months
14. Bake the second casserole, uncovered, at 350 for 35-40 minutes or until heated through
15. Let stand for 10 minutes before cutting
16. Yield: 2 casseroles
17. To use frozen casserole: thaw in the refrigerator for 8 hours
18. Bake as directed<|endoftext|>
