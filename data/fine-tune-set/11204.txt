Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Celery
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Butter
• Ketchup
• Water
• Brown sugar
• Vinegar
• Dry mustard
• Pepper
• Salt

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a slow cooker, cover and cook on low for 8 hours
2. Stir chicken until it shreds
