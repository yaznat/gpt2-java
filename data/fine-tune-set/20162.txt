Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Garlic Scapes Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Gingerroot
• Sesame oil
• Garlic cloves
• Pepper
• Top sirloin steak
• Garlic scapes
• Hot red pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Salt
• Beef stock

Steps:
1. Whisk first 6 ingredients together in a bowl
2. Slice beef across the grain into thin strips, add to bowl& toss to coat
3. Let stand for 15 minutes
4. Cook garlic scapes in a pot of boiling salted water until tender-crisp, about 2 minutes
5. Drain& refresh under cold water
6. Drain again, pat dry& set aside
7. Wearing rubber gloves, cut hot pepper in half lengthwise, deseed& finely slice
8. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a wok or deep skillet over high heat
9. Stir fry beef in batches, using remaining oil as necessary, until browned but still pink inside, about 2 minutes
10. Transfer to a bowl
11. Stir fry hot pepper& salt for 1 minute
12. Return beef, any accumulated juices, scapes& stock to wok and stir fry until hot, about a minute<|endoftext|>
