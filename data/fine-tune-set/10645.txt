Ingredients:
• Rotini pasta
• Olive oil
• Mushrooms
• Garlic cloves
• Dried oregano
• Frozen artichoke hearts
• Chicken stock
• Fresh lemon juice
• Lemon peel
• Dry crushed red pepper
• Butter
• Parsley
• Asiago cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cook pasta in pot of salted boiling water until al dente
2. Drain
3. Toss pasts with 1 tbs oil
4. Heat 2 tbs oil in heavy large skillet over medium-high heat
5. Add mushrooms, garlic and oregano
6. Saute until golden, about 4 minutes
7. Add artichokes
8. Saute 3 minutes
9. Add stock, lemon juice and peel and crushed red pepper and cook until slightly thickened, about 5 minutes
10. Add butter
11. Whisk until just melted
12. Add pasta and parsley
13. Toss until pasta is coated and heated through
14. Add 3 / 4 cup cheese and toss to coat
15. Season with salt and pepper
16. Transfer to a large bowl to serve and top with remaining cheese<|endoftext|>
