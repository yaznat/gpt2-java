What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>All Purpose Mild Brine For Poultry And Pork

Ingredients:
• Kosher salt
• Sugar
• Water

Steps:
1. Mix cold water, salt and sugar and stir to dissolve
2. In a non-reactive container, immerse food in brine, seal and refrigerate
3. Use 1 quart of brine per pound of meat, not to exceed 2 gallons
4. Brine for 1 hour per pound, but not less than 30 minutes or more than 8 hours
5. Will not work if meat is frozen<|endoftext|>
