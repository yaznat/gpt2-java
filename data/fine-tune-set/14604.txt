Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Toasted sesame oil
• Shrimp
• Chorizo sausage
• Onion
• Cooked white rice
• Soy sauce
• Sherry wine
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Heat a medium nonstick skillet over high heat and coat with cooking spray
2. Pour half of the eggs into the pan, swirling to coat the bottom, and let cook until set, about 1 minute
3. Remove the skillet from the heat
4. Flip the eggs and cook for 1 minute more
5. Transfer the egg tortilla to a cutting board and repeat the process with the cooking spray and the remaining egg
6. Roll up each egg tortilla and slice crosswise into 1 / 4 inch thick strips
7. In a large skillet, heat the sesame oil over high heat
8. Add the shrimp, chorizo and onion and cook, stirring, until the shrimp is just opaque, 1 - 2 minutes
9. Mix in the rice, soy sauce and sherry
10. Cook until heated through
11. Stir in the egg strips and top with cilantro<|endoftext|>
