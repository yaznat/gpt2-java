Ingredients:
• Whole boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Apple juice
• Salt
• Pepper
• Apple
• Onion
• Croutons
• Raisins
• Brown sugar
• Curry powder
• Poultry seasoning
• Garlic powder
• Apples
• Fresh thyme sprig

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Lightly grease 1 quart round baking dish
3. Arrange uncooked chicken breasts in single layer in prepared dish
4. Combine 1 / 4 cup apple juice, salt and pepper, in a small bowl
5. Brush all of juice mixture over chicken
6. Apple: peel if preferred-not mandatory
7. Cut lengthwise into quarters, remove stem, core and seeds
8. Chop into 1 / 2 pieces, place in large bowl
9. Onion: chop coarsely, toss chopped onion with apple pieces
10. Stir croutons, raisins, brown sugar, curry, poultry seasoning and garlic powder into apple-onion mixture
11. Toss with remaining 3 / 4 cup apple juice
12. Spread crouton mixture over chicken
13. Cover with foil or lid
14. Bake 45 minutes or until chicken is tender
15. Garnish if desired<|endoftext|>
