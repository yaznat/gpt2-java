Ingredients:
• Buttermilk
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Honey
• Long grain brown rice
• Vanilla
• Pie cherries
• Cherries

Steps:
1. If using soy equivalent of buttermilk, mix together soy milk and lemon juice
2. Allow to sit 10 minutes
3. Cook buttermilk, cocoa, and honey in a saucepan over medium-low heat, stirring until the cocoa and honey are dissolved
4. Add the cooked rice and bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring constantly
5. Cover, lower heat, and simmer 20-30 minutes or until the buttermilk is almost absorbed, stirring occasionally
6. You may need to leave the lid slightly ajar at first to prevent it from boiling over
7. Stir in vanilla and chopped cherries
8. Heat through and serve warm or chill and serve cold
9. Garnish with leftover cherries, if desired<|endoftext|>
