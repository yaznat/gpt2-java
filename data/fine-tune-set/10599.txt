What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Oysters And Bacon

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Red onion
• Garlic cloves
• Butter
• Oyster
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Salt
• Dry white wine
• Angel hair pasta
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. In a skillet over medium heat, cook the bacon, onion, and garlic
2. Pour off excess drippings and transfer mixture to a lightly greased 2 1 / 2 quart casserole dish
3. Using the same skillet, melt the butter over medium-high heat
4. Add in the oysters
5. Saute about 5 minutes or until lightly browned
6. Sprinkle with seasonings
7. Stir in wine and bring to a boil
8. Add oyster-wine mixture to casserole dish
9. Add pasta and gently mix
10. Bake in a 375 oven for about 20 minutes or until heated through
11. Sprinkle with parsley and serve<|endoftext|>
