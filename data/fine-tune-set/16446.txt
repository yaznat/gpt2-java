Give me a wrap recipe.<|endoftext|>Bbq Grilled Prosciutto Wrapped Asparagus Bundles

Ingredients:
• Asparagus
• Prosciutto

Steps:
1. Preheat grill and insure that the coals are not too hot
2. Wrap two bundles of 5 - 6 spears of asparagus, each with one slice of prosciutto
3. Secure prosciutto with a toothpick where needed
4. Season with pepper
5. Grill over low heat, turning once
6. Grill until prosciutto is crisp and asparagus is tender, about 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
