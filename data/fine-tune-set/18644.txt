Ingredients:
• Golden delicious apples
• Cinnamon sugar
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Butter
• Eggs
• Whole milk
• Vanilla extract
• Cinnamon-raisin bread

Steps:
1. Place oven rack in middle position
2. Heat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
3. Lightly grease 9 or 10" deep-dish pie plate
4. Have ready a roasting pan larger than the pie plate
5. In a large bowl, toss apple slices with 2 tablespoons cinnamon-sugar
6. Place a large skillet over medium heat
7. Add 1 tablespoon butter
8. Spread half of the apples to cover bottom
9. Cook 1 minute per side or until partially cooked
10. Remove to a large plate
11. Repeat with another 1 tablespoon butter and remaining apples
12. Add to plate
13. Meanwhile, put eggs, milk, vanilla and remaining cinnamon-sugar in the bowl
14. Add remaining 1 tablespoon melted butter
15. Whisk until ingredients are well blended
16. Spread bread cubes in pie plate
17. Arrange apple slices in concentric circles on top
18. Pour egg mixture evenly over apples
19. Sprinkle with reserved 1 teaspoon cinnamon-sugar
20. Set pie plate in roasting pan
21. Place pan on middle rack of oven
22. Add very hot water to roasting pan to come half way up sides of pie plate
23. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes until a knife inserted near center comes out clean
24. Remove pie plate from water
25. Serve warm, at room temperature, or cold<|endoftext|>
