Ingredients:
• Shrimp
• Vinaigrette dressing
• Bacon
• Pickled jalapeno peppers
• Toothpicks
• Mayonnaise
• Chili sauce
• Sweet pickle relish
• Red pepper
• Water

Steps:
1. Place shrimp and vinaigrette into a large resealable plastic bag
2. Refrigerate for 15 mn
3. Microwave bacon for 1 mn on high
4. Cut slices in half crosswise and let cool
5. Heat broiler
6. Coat a large broiler pan with nonstick cooking spray
7. Remove shrimp from marinade, reserving marinade
8. Put one piece of bacon on a work surface
9. Place a shrimp at one end
10. Tuck a jalapeno slice into curl of shrimp
11. Roll bacon up tightly to enclose filling, securing with toothpick
12. Repeat using all bacon, shrimp and jalapenos
13. Stir together mayo, chili sauce, pickle relish, red pepper and 1 tbl water in a small bowl
14. Place shrimp rolls on prepared pan
15. Brush with reserved marinade
16. Broil 3 mn
17. Turn, brush with marinade and broil 2 mn more
18. Serve with sauce for dipping<|endoftext|>
