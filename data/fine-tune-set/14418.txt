Ingredients:
• Broad bean
• Fresh cilantro
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Paprika
• Cayenne
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Vinegar
• Water

Steps:
1. Top & tail the bean then remove the strings
2. Leave the beans in their pod
3. Cut into bitesize pieces - approx 1" long
4. Peel & mince the garlic & place in a large, heavy bottomed pan with the beans
5. Saute for 2 minutes
6. Add the rest of the ingredients, cover & simmer until beans are tender - approx 25-30 minutes
7. Once the beans are tender, reduce the sauce to your prefered thickness & mix in the vinegar<|endoftext|>
