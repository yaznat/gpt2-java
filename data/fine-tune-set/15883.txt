Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Green chili salsa
• Mexican blend cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Alfredo sauce
• Lettuce
• Avocado
• Cilantro leaf

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Place chicken, salsa and 1 / 2 cup cheese in large mixing bowl
3. Stir to combine
4. Spoon about 1 / 2 cup chicken mixture down the middle of a tortilla
5. Fold one edge over and roll up
6. Place, seam down, in a 13-by-9-inch baking dish
7. Repeat with remaining tortillas and chicken mixture
8. Combine pasta sauce and remaining 1 1 / 2 cups cheese in mixing bowl
9. Spread over burritos
10. Bake until sauce is bubbling and browned in spots, 20 to 25 minutes
11. Remove from oven and cool 10 minutes
12. Garnish with lettuce, avocado or cilantro if desired<|endoftext|>
