What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Goulashy Beef Stew For The Slow Cooker

Ingredients:
• Boneless beef cubes
• Celery
• Carrots
• Onions
• Reduced-sodium fat-free beef broth
• Frozen baby peas
• No yolks egg noodle substitute
• Diced tomatoes
• Red wine
• Garlic salt
• Dried basil
• Worcestershire sauce
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except noodles in slow cooker
2. Cook on low for 6-7 hours, stirring occasionally if you can, until beef is cooked through and vegetables are tender
3. Add noodles and cook on low for 1 hour, or until soft<|endoftext|>
