Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Onion
• Cauliflower
• Ginger
• Garlic
• Cayenne pepper
• Cumin seed
• Curry powder
• Plum tomatoes with liquid
• Water
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Boneless skinless chicken
• Lemon, juice of
• Fresh cilantro
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Heat oil in 12 inch skillet on high
2. Lower heat and add onion, ginger, garlic, and cayenne and cook until it begins to brown
3. Add the cumin seeds and curry
4. Cook briefly
5. Add cauliflower and cook for about one minute over high heat
6. Cut up the tomatoes add them with the juice, 1 / 4 cup of water, salt and pepper
7. Cover and turn heat to medium-low
8. Cook for about 15 minutes
9. Stir occasionally until the cauliflower is tender
10. Add the chicken
11. Stir and cover for about 8 more minutes
12. If the sauce starts drying out, add some more water
13. Add the lemon juice
14. Taste and adjust seasoning
15. Sprinkle with cilantro and serve over rice<|endoftext|>
