What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Arancini Italian Rice Balls

Ingredients:
• Long-grain rice
• Chicken broth
• Butter
• Eggs
• Parmesan cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Dried breadcrumbs
• Cooking oil

Steps:
1. In large saucepan, combine rice, broth, and butter or margarine
2. Bring to a boil, stir to blend
3. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer until liquid is absorbed, about 15 minutes
4. Set aside to cool
5. Stir in egg yolks and parmesan
6. In small bowl, beat egg whites until frothy
7. Set aside
8. Cover and freeze rice mixture for 15 minutes to make them firmer to handle
9. Roll them with your hands to form balls
10. Push a small cube of mozzarella into center of each arancini, lightly pressing to seal each ball
11. Roll each arancini in beaten egg whites, then coat with bread crumbs
12. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
13. Heat oil in heavy skillet
14. Cook 8 rice balls at a time, about 4-5 minutes, turning frequently, to brown evenly
15. Place on baking sheet
16. Bake for 10 minutes
17. Serve with your favorite spaghetti sauce, to which you have added 3 / 4 cup to 1 cup of frozen green peas, uncooked<|endoftext|>
