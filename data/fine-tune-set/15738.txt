Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Bamboo shoots
• Water chestnuts
• Black mushrooms
• Lettuce
• Peanuts
• Rice noodles
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• White pepper
• Sesame oil
• Hoisin sauce

Steps:
1. Preparation: mince chicken breast meat
2. Chop bamboo shoots, water chestnuts, and black mushrooms into small pieces, then boil in hot water for one minute
3. Wash lettuce thoroughly, tear into round leaves and set on small plates
4. Mince peanuts
5. Cooking: deep fry the rice noodles and set in the center of lettuce
6. Heat one tsp
7. Vegetable oil in a pan, stir-fry minced garlic first
8. Add the chicken meat and cook over medium heat until almost dry
9. Next add bamboo shoots, water chestnuts and black mushrooms, stir-fry with soy sauce and sugar for one minute before adding a dash of white pepper and sesame oil
10. Remove from the stove and pour mixture over the fried rice noodles
11. Sprinkle the minced peanuts over chicken dish scoop two spoonfuls of the chicken mixture into one lettuce cup
12. Drizzle with hoisin sauce
13. Eat and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
