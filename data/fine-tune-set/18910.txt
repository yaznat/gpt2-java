Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Almond extract
• Milk
• Eggs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Combine flour, 2 tbsp sugar, salt, almond extract, milk and eggs in mixing bowl and whisk together
2. Batter will be lumpy
3. Cover and chill for 1-2 hours or more
4. Heat 10-12" saute pan over medium high heat
5. Add 1 / 2 tbsp butter to pan and when bubbly add the batter
6. Redistribute batter and shake pan to promote even cooking
7. After pancake is completely cooked turn onto a plate, add remaining 1 / 2 tablespoons butter to the pan and sprinkle with 1 tbsp sugar
8. Put pancake back into the saute pan and lightly brown the other side of pancake
9. Serve immediately with fruit and vanilla yogurt<|endoftext|>
