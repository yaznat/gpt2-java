Ingredients:
• Golden brown sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Unsweetened chocolate
• All-purpose flour
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat over to 325 degrees
2. Grease 8x8x2-inch baking pan
3. In large bowl beat sugar and butter until fluffy
4. Beat in eggs, one at a time
5. Beat in chocolate, then flour
6. Stir in nuts
7. Pour into pan
8. Bake 25 minutes
9. Cool in pan and cut into bars<|endoftext|>
