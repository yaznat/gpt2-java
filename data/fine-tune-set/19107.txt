Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Ben Jerry S Cappuccino Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Heavy cream
• Milk
• Instant coffee
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Whisk the eggs in a mixing bowl until light and fluffy, 1 to 2 minutes
2. Whisk in the sugar, a little at a time, then continue whisking until completely blended, about 1 minute more
3. Pour in the cream and milk, and whisk to blend
4. Add 2-1 / 2 tablespoons of the coffee and cinnamon, and whisk to blend again
5. Transfer the mixture to an ice cream maker and freeze following the manufacturer's directions
6. After the ice cream stiffens, add the remaining 1 tablespoon coffee, then continue freezing until done
7. Makes 1 quart<|endoftext|>
