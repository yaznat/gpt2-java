Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Baby Ruth Candy Brownies

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Brown sugar
• Light corn syrup
• Smooth peanut butter
• Vanilla extract
• Quick oatmeal
• Chocolate chips
• Butterscotch chips
• Spanish peanut

Steps:
1. Combine margarine, sugar and syrup in saucepan
2. Stir over low heat until margarine melts and sugar dissolves
3. Add peanut butter and vanilla extract
4. Pour over oatmeal
5. Mix well
6. Press into a greased 13 x 9-inch pan
7. Bake at 375 degrees f for 12 minutes
8. Topping:
9. Melt chocolate and butterscotch chips
10. Stir in peanut butter and peanuts
11. Spread over top of brownies after removing them from the oven
12. Cool and cut into squares<|endoftext|>
