Ingredients:
• Active dry yeast
• Warm water
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Butter
• Egg
• Oil
• Fruit jelly
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Mix the yeast, 2 tablespoons warm water and sugar in a small bowl, and leave to stand till bubbly
2. Sift the flour and salt into a bowl
3. Rub the butter or margarine into the flour
4. Make a well in the flour mixture and add the yeast mixture, the beaten egg and just enough of the warm milk / water to make a soft dough
5. Beat well and kneed for 5 minutes on a floured board
6. Divide into 12 pieces
7. Kneed each piece and place apart on a greased and floured tin
8. Brush with water and place in a greased plastic bag
9. Leave until they have doubled in size
10. Fry in oil that is not too hot 350f-360f, turning over once to brown both sides, about 3 minutes
11. Drain on absorbent paper
12. When cool enough to handle, but not to cool, insert the jelly in the middle
13. I use one teaspoon and put it in with a syringe
14. Sprinkle on confectioners sugar<|endoftext|>
