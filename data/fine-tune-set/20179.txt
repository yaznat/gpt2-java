What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Cabbage Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Cabbage
• Canola oil
• Beef sirloin steak
• Onions
• Celery
• Bean sprouts
• Bamboo shoots
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Beef broth
• Soy sauce
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In a large skillet or wok, stir-fry cabbage in oil until cabbage begins to soften
2. Add beef
3. Cook and stir for 3 minutes
4. Add the onions and celery
5. Stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes or until vegetables are crisp-tender
6. Add the bean sprouts, bamboo shoots and water chestnuts
7. In a small bowl, combine the sugar, cornstarch, broth and soy sauce until smooth
8. Stir into beef mixture
9. Bring to a boil
10. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened and vegetables are tender
11. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
