Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Light brown sugar
• Garlic clove
• Dried rosemary
• Salt
• Flank steaks
• Ground pepper

Steps:
1. Combine all marinade ingredients in a resealable bag and mix together
2. Then add the flank steak and reseal the bag and turn bag several times to coat the meat with the marinade
3. Refrigerate 20-30 minutes, turning once or twice
4. Preheat grill to high for 20 minutes
5. Remove meat from bag and sprinkle fresh ground pepper over the steak before placing on grill
6. Grill steak 5-6 minutes and then turn over and grill until done
7. Remove steak to a platter and tent with foil and let rest for 5 minutes before slicing and serving<|endoftext|>
