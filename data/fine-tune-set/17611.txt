What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>3 In 1 Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Egg whites
• Sugar
• Oil
• Vanilla
• Carrots
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Sift together flours, baking powder, cinnamon, baking soda and salt
2. In a large bowl whisk together egg whites, sugar, oil, and vanilla
3. Mix in dry ingredients until combined
4. Stir in zucchini and raisins spoon mix into prepared muffin cups
5. Bake at 375f for 20 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean
6. Makes 1 dozen<|endoftext|>
