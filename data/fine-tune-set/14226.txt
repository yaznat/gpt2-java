What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Couscous Tofu Wrap

Ingredients:
• Mushroom and garlic couscous
• Water
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Tofu
• Sandwich wraps

Steps:
1. Prepare couscous according to package directions
2. While cooking couscous in a seperate skillet spray with cooking spray and saute garlic and onion
3. Dice tofu into small cubes, add to garlic and onion, season to desired taste
4. Once couscous is prepared, add tofu mixture and mix thoroughly
5. Warm wraps, place 1 / 4 mixture in each wrap, serve and enjoy<|endoftext|>
