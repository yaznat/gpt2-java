Can you give me a smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana And Rum Smoothie Alcoholic

Ingredients:
• Low-fat milk
• Light rum
• White creme de cacao
• Creme de banane
• Bananas
• Non-fat vanilla frozen yogurt

Steps:
1. Place milk, rum, creme de cacao, banana liqueur & bananas in a blender & process until smooth
2. Add yogurt & continue to process until mixture is again smooth
3. Pour into cocktail glasses & enjoy!<|endoftext|>
