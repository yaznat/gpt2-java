Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Gobble Up Turkey Cottage Pie

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Cream cheese
• Butter
• Milk
• Salt
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Celery
• Carrot
• Ground turkey
• All-purpose flour
• Turkey base
• Boiling water
• Worcestershire sauce
• Ground black pepper
• Ground sage
• Peas

Steps:
1. In a large pot of boiling, salted water, cook potatoes until fork tender
2. Drain & pour back into hot pot
3. Add cream cheese, butter, milk & salt & mash well until potatoes are fluffy
4. Cover pot & keep potatoes warm
5. Meanwhile, while potatoes are cooking, in a large skillet heat oil over medium heat
6. Add onion, celery & carrot & cook, stirring, until vegetables begin to soften
7. Crumble ground turkey over vegetables & cook until meat loses it's pink color
8. Sprinkle flour over cooked turkey & stir to combine
9. Dissolve turkey base in the boiling water & stir into meat
10. Simmer, stirring, about five minutes, until mixture thickens slightly
11. Pour meat filling into a 2-3 quart casserole & sprinkle peas over
12. Carefully spread warm mashed potatoes over filling, sealing potatoes to edge of dish
13. Bake in preheated 350 degree oven for 30 minutes, until heated through & bubbly at edge of dish
14. Remove from oven & sprinkle cheese on top
15. Bake an additional 5 minutes, just until cheese melts<|endoftext|>
