What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>4 Ingredient Lemon Cookies

Ingredients:
• Lemon cake mix
• Instant lemon pudding
• Oil
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
2. Add all ingredients to large mixing bowl, beating until well mixed
3. Shape dough into round balls about the size of walnuts
4. Place on sprayed baking sheet, approximately 2" apart
5. Bake 15 to 18 minutes
6. Allow to cool for a few minutes on baking sheet
7. Then transfer to wire rack to cool completely
8. May be frosted with lemon frosting mix, if desired<|endoftext|>
