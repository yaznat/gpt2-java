Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Mushroom And Asparagus Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Long-grain rice
• Oil
• Boneless chicken breast
• Asparagus spear
• Onion
• Garlic
• Fresh mushrooms
• Water
• Stir-fry sauce
• Oyster sauce

Steps:
1. Cook rice according to package directions
2. Let 1 tablespoon oil get hot in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat
3. Add the chicken and stir-fry for 5-6 minutes or until chicken is not pink
4. Transfer chicken to a plate
5. Add the other tablespoon oil to skillet
6. Stir-fry asparagus, onion, and garlic for 3 minutes
7. Add mushrooms and stir fry 3 more minutes
8. Add water, cover and steam 2-3 minutes or until asparagus is tender
9. In a little bowl, mix together the stir-fry sauce and oyster sauce
10. Add sauce to skillet and also add the chicken back to the skillet
11. Stir-fry a minute or so until heated
12. Serve over hot cooked rice<|endoftext|>
