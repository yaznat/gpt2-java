Give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Wild Mushrooms

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt and pepper
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Beef stew meat
• Onions
• Tomato paste
• Dry white wine
• Beef broth
• Splenda brown sugar blend
• Potatoes
• Wild mushroom
• Carrots

Steps:
1. Place flour, salt and pepper in a shallow dish
2. Heat 1 t oil in heavy medium pot or dutch oven over medium high heat
3. Working in batches coat meat with flour mixture, place in pot and brown evenly, remove and set aside in medium bowl
4. Add additional oil as needed while browning remaining meat
5. After beef is browned, add onions and saute until transparent
6. Stir in tomato paste, then add wine
7. Bring to a boil and deglaze pot, scraping up any browned bits
8. Add broth, splenda brown sugarand beef with any accumulated juices
9. Bring to a boil
10. Cover partially, reduce heat and simmer 1 hour
11. Add potatoes and carrots, simmer uncovered 30 minutes
12. Add mushroom and continue simmering and 15-20 minutes until beef and vegetables are tender
13. Season to taste and serve<|endoftext|>
