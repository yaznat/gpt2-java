Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Al S Beef And Spinach In Oyster Sauce

Ingredients:
• Sirloin steak
• Spinach
• Garlic cloves
• Oyster sauce
• Peanut oil
• Onions
• Green peppers
• Ginger

Steps:
1. Slice the beef into thin narrow slices
2. Heat the oil in a wok or large pan
3. To test if the wok is hot enough drop in one small piece of onion
4. It should sizzle on contact
5. Add the onions and garlic
6. Fry for two minutes
7. Add the beef and fry for about 3 to 5 minutes
8. Till the beef is just underdone
9. Add the ginger and stir
10. Add the peppers and spinach and stir well for about a minute
11. Reduce the heat to a medium heat and add the oyster sauce
12. Stir till heated through
13. Ladle off some sauce into a serving jug
14. Serve on plates of noodles<|endoftext|>
