Ingredients:
• Milk
• Shortening
• Bread flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Yeast

Steps:
1. Add all ingredients to your bread machine in the order reccomended by the manufacturer
2. Set to"dough" cycle
3. When dough cycle finishes, remove dough from bread machine and punch down and knead on a floured surface
4. Spray a loaf pan with cooking spray, set dough into the pan, and set aside in a warm place and let the dough rise to just about the top of the loaf pan
5. Bake at 375 for 30 minutes
6. When dough is done, tap on the top of the loaf
7. If it sounds hollow inside, it is done
8. If not, cook in additional 5 minute increments, till bread is done
9. Let cool a bit before cutting, or the bread will get smushed<|endoftext|>
