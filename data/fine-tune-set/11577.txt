What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>1 Pan Fudge Cake

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Baking soda
• Flour
• Salt
• Vanilla
• Vegetable oil
• Water
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. In an ungreased 9 x 13 pan- yes ungreased, sift all dry ingredients
3. Add the liquids and stir just until blended
4. Bake for 25 minutes
5. Frost with your favorite frosting, cake is extremely moist so care must be taken that you don't tear up the top of the cake<|endoftext|>
