Ingredients:
• Butter
• Smoked bacon
• White onion
• Flour
• Chicken stock
• Ale
• Baking potatoes
• Old cheddar cheese
• Whipping cream
• Salt
• White pepper

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, heat butter over medium-high heat
2. Add bacon and saut for 1 minute
3. Add onion
4. Saut for 2 minutes or until tender
5. Add flour
6. Reduce heat to medium
7. Cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly
8. Add stock and ale
9. Increase heat to high and bring to a boil
10. Add potatoes
11. Return to a boil
12. Reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes or until potatoes are tender
13. Transfer soup in batched to the food processor and process until smooth
14. Add cheese and stir until melted
15. Stir in cream, salt and pepper
16. Taste and adjust seasonings<|endoftext|>
