Ingredients:
• Butternut squash
• Corn flake crumbs
• Seasoning salt
• Pure maple syrup

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 425f
2. Line two baking sheets with aluminum foil and spray with nonstick cooking spray
3. Microwave squash on high for 2 minutes
4. Cut squash in half length and scoop out the seeds
5. Peel squash with vegetable peeler
6. Cut into quarters
7. Cut quarters into 4x3 / 4-inch sticks
8. Mix together the cornflake crumbs and the seasoned salt in a shallow dish
9. Pour the maple syrup into a second shallow dish
10. Dip the squash sticks into syrup and then coat crumb mixture
11. Place coated sticks in a single layer on a prepared pan
12. Bake for 45 to 60 minutes or until squash sticks are fork tender<|endoftext|>
