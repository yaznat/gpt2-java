Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Butter
• Flour
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Whiskey

Steps:
1. Combine ingredients together in a large bowl to form a dough
2. Roll into balls and place into the lukken iron or pizzelle iron until golden brown<|endoftext|>
