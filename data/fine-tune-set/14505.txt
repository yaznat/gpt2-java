Ingredients:
• Hamburger
• Brown sugar
• Onion
• Mustard
• Catsup
• Kidney bean
• Vinegar
• Pork and beans
• Bacon
• Butter beans

Steps:
1. Brown hamburger
2. Add onion and cook until onions are transparent
3. Drain
4. Mix hamburger mixture and everything else together
5. Pour into baking dish and bake uncovered at 350 for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
