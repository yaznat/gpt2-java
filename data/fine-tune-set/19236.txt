Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Instant malted milk powder
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Heavy cream
• Malted milk balls

Steps:
1. In a heavy saucepan, combine the eggs, sugar, and malted milk powder
2. Gradually add the milk
3. Cook and stir over low heat until the mixture reaches 160 and coats the back of a metal spoon
4. Remove pan from heat
5. Cool the custard quickly by placing the pan into a bowl of ice water
6. Stir for 2 minutes
7. Stir in the vanilla
8. Press plastic wrap onto the surface of the custard
9. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight
10. Stir in the heavy cream and malted milk balls
11. Fill the cylinder of an ice-cream maker 2 / 3 full
12. Freeze according to manufacturer's instructions
13. Keep remaining custard chilled until ready to be churned
14. Allow the churned ice cream to"ripen" in the cylinder in its freezer or your refrigerator's freezer for 2-4 hours before serving<|endoftext|>
