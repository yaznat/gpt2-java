Give me a fish recipe.<|endoftext|>Amazing Fish Tikka

Ingredients:
• Butterfish
• Fresh lemon juice
• Turmeric powder
• Salt
• Fenugreek leaves
• Yogurt cheese
• Cream
• Ginger-garlic paste
• Red chili powder
• Dried fenugreek leaves
• Gram flour
• Egg yolks
• Oil

Steps:
1. Pat dry fish pieces on a clean paper towel
2. In a bowl, combine together the fish, lemon juice, salt and turmeric powder
3. Keep aside to marinate for 30 minutes
4. Soak fenugreek leaves in salted water for 10 minutes
5. Squeeze and drain out water
6. Combine fresh fenugreek leaves, cream, labneh, ginger-garlic paste, red chilli powder, kasoori methi, gramflour, egg yolks and fish
7. Mix well
8. Thread fish tikka pieces onto skewers
9. Barbecue over a hot charcoal or electric grill
10. When half done, baste fish tikkas with oil once and then cook until completely done
11. Serve hot with chutney
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
