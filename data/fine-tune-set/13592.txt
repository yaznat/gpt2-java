Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Salt
• Garlic salt
• Worcestershire sauce
• Cooked rice
• Diced tomatoes
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Brown beef and onion in a skillet and drain
3. Combine with the rest of the ingredients except the grated cheese
4. Pour into a 2 qt casserole dish
5. Top with grated cheese
6. Bake 45-50 minutes<|endoftext|>
