What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Pizza Potato Skins

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Oil
• Pizza sauce
• Green pepper
• Canned mushroom
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Set oven to broil
2. Set oven rack to second highest position
3. Grease a baking dish
4. Cut baked potatoes in half
5. Scoop out cooked potato, and reserve for another use
6. Arrange skins cut side up in baking dish
7. Rub skins all over lightly with oil
8. Brush each potato skin with 1 or 2 tbsp pizza sauce
9. Sprinkle with green pepper and finely chopped mushroom stems
10. Top each with grated mozza cheese
11. Broil in oven 5-7 mins, or until skins are crispy, and cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
