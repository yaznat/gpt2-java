What's a good tea recipe?<|endoftext|>All Purpose Green Tea Sauce

Ingredients:
• Hot water
• Matcha green tea powder
• Fresh chives
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Lemon juice
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Honey
• Minced ginger
• Red chili pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Salt

Steps:
1. Mix matcha in hot water, blending well
2. Add the remaining ingredients to the tea
3. Cover and chill for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
