Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• Almond extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Chocolate kisses with almonds
• Powdered sugar
• Milk
• Raspberry jam

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, cream butter and sugars
2. Beat in egg and extract
3. Combine the flour, baking soda and salt
4. Gradually add to creamed mixture
5. Cover and chill for 1 hour or until easy to handle
6. Roll dough into 1-inch balls, then roll in additional sugar
7. Place 2-inches apart on ungreased baking sheets
8. Bake at 325 for 10-12 minutes or until golden brown
9. Immediately press a chocolate kiss into the center or each cookie
10. Cool on wire racks
11. Combine glaze ingredients
12. Drizzle over cookies<|endoftext|>
