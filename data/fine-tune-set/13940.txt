Ingredients:
• Lamb cutlets
• Garlic cloves
• Rosemary
• Spinach
• Asparagus
• Butter
• Red wine
• Lemon, juice of
• Chocolate
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Marinate lamb cutlets in salt, pepper and extra virgin olive for 45 minutes prior to cooking
2. Heat heavy based saucepan with butter until very hot
3. Add lamb cutlets and cook 45 second each side
4. Remove and rest in warm place
5. Pour excess oil out of pan and deglaze with lemon juice
6. Add red wine and finely chopped garlic and rosemary
7. Reduce for 7 minutes
8. Sautee spinach in butter and garlic
9. Blanch asparagus
10. After the wine has reduced add finely chopped chocolate and thicken
11. Pour through a fine sieve to remove rosemary and garlic from sauce
12. Plate spinach, then asparagus on top and crown cutlets around
13. Drizzle sauce over cutlets
14. Serve and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
