Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Blueberries
• Butter
• Lemon juice
• Lemon, rind of
• Pie shell

Steps:
1. Cook the first seven ingredients until very thick
2. Fold in the 2 cups fresh or frozen blueberries and pour into a baked pie shell
3. Let pie cool and top with whipped cream<|endoftext|>
