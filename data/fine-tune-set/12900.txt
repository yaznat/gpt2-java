Ingredients:
• Top sirloin steaks
• Salt and pepper
• Flour
• Eggs
• Soft breadcrumbs
• Oil

Steps:
1. Salt and pepper the steak and cut in pieces about 1 1 / 2 in
2. Put flour in shallow container
3. Beat eggs in shallow bowl
4. Put bread crumbs in shallow container
5. Roll steak in flour, eggs, then breadcrumbs
6. Drop in to hot oil 375 and cook about 3-5 min, depending how well you want it done<|endoftext|>
