What's a good coffee recipe?<|endoftext|>Amaretto Coffee Creamer

Ingredients:
• Non-dairy coffee creamer
• Almond extract
• Ground cinnamon
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. In a screw-top lid jar, place all ingredients and shake well to blend
2. Add 1-2 tablespoons of creamer to 6 ounces of coffee
3. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
