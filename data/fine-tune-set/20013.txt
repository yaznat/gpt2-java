Ingredients:
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Mushroom
• Egg whites
• Fresh spinach
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Spray 6" or 8" good quality nonstick pan with spray
2. Heat over low-medium heat, no hotter
3. Add mushrooms to pan, spreading evenly in pan
4. Slowly pour egg whites over mushrooms and then sprinkle with spinach
5. Cover with lid
6. Let sit for about 10 minutes
7. Occasionally check to see when egg whites are no longer runny on top of the omelette
8. When set,
9. Sprinkle with salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
