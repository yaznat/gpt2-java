Ingredients:
• Oil
• Tomatoes
• Cauliflower
• Onion
• Cumin seed
• Chili powder
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in the pan and put tomatoes, cauliflower, onion and cumin seeds in the oil
2. Place the lid on the pan, until the cauliflower is cooked, about 15 minutes
3. Add salt and chilli powder to taste
4. Garnish with cilantro<|endoftext|>
