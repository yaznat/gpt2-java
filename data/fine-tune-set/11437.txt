Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Butter
• Egg
• Almond extract
• Plain yogurt
• Slivered almonds
• Strawberry
• Confectioners' sugar
• Milk
• Red food coloring

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Grease a 7-1 / 2 x 3-1 / 2 inch loaf pan
3. In a small bowl combine flour, baking soda and salt
4. In a large bowl, cream together sugar and butter
5. Add egg and almond extract and beat until fluffy
6. Add flour and yogurt alternately to creamed mixture
7. Fold in almonds, then strawberries
8. Turn batter into prepared pan and bake 45 to 50 minutes at 350 degrees f or until toothpick or knife inserted all the way to bottom comes out clean
9. While bread is baking, combine the glaze ingredients
10. Let bread cool in pan
11. While bread is still warm, drizzle with almond glaze<|endoftext|>
