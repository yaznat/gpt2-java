Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Cornstarch
• Sugar
• Soy sauce
• Rice wine
• Fresh ginger
• Mixed mushrooms
• Bamboo shoot
• Red bell peppers
• Onion
• Green onions
• Zucchini
• Chicken broth
• Oyster sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Peanut oil
• Chinese egg noodles
• Sesame oil
• Rice vinegar

Steps:
1. Cut pork into thin strips, and then into pieces
2. Mix with meat marinade for 30 minutes
3. Slice mushrooms, bamboo shoots, red pepper and zucchini into thin strips
4. Mix sauce
5. Parboil noodles for 3 minutes
6. Rinse and drain
7. Bring another pot of water to boil, and keep hot
8. Heat 2 t
9. Oil in wok
10. Stir fry all vegetables together for 2-3 minutes
11. Sprinkle on a little salt and 1 teaspoons of sugar
12. Set aside
13. Add 2 t
14. Oil, and stir fry pork until done
15. Add sauce mixture
16. Stir until thickened
17. Add the vegetables
18. Stir until well mixed
19. Put noodles into hot pot of water for 10-15 seconds, just to heat
20. Drain
21. Put back in pot, and add seasonings to noodles
22. Serve meat and vegetables over noodles<|endoftext|>
