Ingredients:
• Dried ancho chiles
• Corn oil
• Onion
• Beef stew meat
• Salt & fresh ground pepper
• Garlic clove
• Tomatoes
• Nopales

Steps:
1. Trim off the glochids and spines from two paddles of prickly pear cactus
2. Cut into 1 / 2 inch dice and cook in boiling salted water until the color changes to olive green
3. Drain and set to the side
4. Fill a small saucepan with water and bring to the boil
5. Add the chiles and cook for about ten minutes or until fork tender
6. Drain well and remove the stems
7. Whirl in a blender and filter through cheesecloth or a mesh strainer to remove seeds
8. Set to the side
9. Heat a medium skillet over medium heat
10. Add oil and sautee the onion until transparent
11. Add the meat and brown well, taking care not to scorch the onions
12. Add the salt, pepper, chile puree, garlic, tomato and about one cup of water
13. Cover and simmer lightly for about 30 minutes, or until tender
14. If meat isn't tender, you may need to cook for another 15 minutes or so
15. Add the cooked nopalitos and stir well
16. Serve while hot<|endoftext|>
