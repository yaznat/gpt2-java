Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Taco seasoning mix
• Oil
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Onion
• Tomatoes
• Ranch salad dressing

Steps:
1. Slice chicken breast into strips toss with taco seasoning
2. Heat oil in skillet
3. Add chicken and cook about 6 minutes until done
4. Dividing evenly, layer chicken and remaining ingredients on heated tortillas
5. Roll up
6. Serve<|endoftext|>
