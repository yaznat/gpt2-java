Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Paprika
• Dried oregano
• Dried thyme
• Garlic clove
• Coarse salt
• Olive oil
• Lemons

Steps:
1. Mix the paprika, herbs, garlic and salt with the oil to make a thick paste
2. Spread over the chops, cover and leave in the fridge, ideally overnight
3. Heat a griddle or heavy pan, cook for 3-4 minutes on each side
4. Serve with wedges of lemon<|endoftext|>
