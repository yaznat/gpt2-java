Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Can Chicken Enchiladas

Ingredients:
• Canned chicken
• Diced green chilies
• Enchilada sauce
• Refried beans
• Tortillas
• Cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. In a bowl, mix the chicken, beans, chiles and 1 / 2 can of enchilada sauce
3. Spoon a dollop of this mixture in the center 1 / 3rd of a tortilla, roll up and place seam side down in greased / pam'd rectangle casserole dish
4. Repeat until your pan is full of rolled up enchiladas, or you run out of filling
5. Pour the remaining enchilada sauce over the enchiladas
6. Sprinkle with cheese, if you want
7. Bake for 20 minutes and serve hot
8. Makes 6-8 enchiladas, depending how much you stuff them
9. Great when eaten with cold sour cream!<|endoftext|>
