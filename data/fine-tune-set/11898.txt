What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Carrot Apple Curry Soup

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Carrots
• Onion
• Beef stock
• Honey
• Curry powder
• Light cream

Steps:
1. Combine carrots, apples, onion and stock in a saucepan
2. Add honey and bring to a boil
3. Reduce heat and simmer until the apple and onion are soft
4. Blend in blender, return to saucepan and add spices
5. Stir in cream and slowly heat through<|endoftext|>
