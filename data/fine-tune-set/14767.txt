What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Asopao De Pollo Caribbean Chicken And Rice

Ingredients:
• Broiler-fryer chickens
• Salt
• Dried oregano
• Ground coriander
• Pepper
• Chicken broth
• Stewed tomatoes
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Uncooked rice
• Frozen green peas
• Green pepper
• Smoked ham
• Green olives
• Capers
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Sear chicken pieces and diced onion in a deep 12" skillet or dutch oven
2. Add broth, salt, oregano, coriander, blended tomatoes, onion, and garlic
3. Heat to boiling
4. Reduce heat
5. Cover and simmer for 30 minutes
6. Cook rice according to directions on bag, except add chicken broth where it calls for water
7. While the rice is cooking add peas, green pepper, ham, olives, capers and 1 tablespoon of the caper liquid to chicken dish
8. Cover and simmer the chicken dish until the rice is done
9. Serve the chicken on top of the rice, pour the sauce over and sprinkle with parmesan<|endoftext|>
