Ingredients:
• Fruit flavored gelatin
• Unsweetened flavored drink mix
• Sugar
• Boiling water
• Cold water

Steps:
1. Combine gelatin, drink mix, sugar and boiling water in a 2 quart pitcher
2. Stir until dissolved
3. Add cold water to make 2 quarts
4. Pour into popsicle molds or 3 oz plastic or paper cups and freeze until completely frozen<|endoftext|>
