Ingredients:
• Water
• Sugar
• Honey
• Corn syrup
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Pure vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Bring everything to a full boil, except the vanilla extract, stir rapidly with a wood spoon
2. Remove from heat and mix in the vanilla
3. Chill until very cold in refrigerator, store in a covered glass jar
4. To make milkshake: blend 6 scoops of ice cream with 1 / 4 cup syrup and very small amounts of whole milk or half and half<|endoftext|>
