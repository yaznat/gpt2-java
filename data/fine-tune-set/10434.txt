What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Anginetti Italian Lemon Drop Cookies

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Vegetable shortening
• Eggs
• Lemon extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Confectioners' sugar
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. For cookies, cream together sugar and shortening
3. Add eggs and lemon extract and beat well
4. Add flour, baking powder and salt
5. Mix well
6. The dough should be soft and sticky
7. With a small cookie scoop, drop dough onto a slightly greased cookie sheet or baking stone, spacing them about 2-inches apart
8. Bake for about 12-15 minutes, or until firm and lightly brown
9. Remove cookies from cookie sheet and allow to cool completely on wire racks
10. For frosting, combine confectioners' sugar, water and lemon extract and mix until smooth
11. Frost the tops of each cookie with a metal spatula
12. Allow cookies to dry before stacking
13. Store in an airtight container
