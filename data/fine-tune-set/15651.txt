Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Taco seasoning
• Water
• Salsa
• Black beans
• Refrigerated biscuits
• Garlic powder
• Dried basil leaves
• Onion powder
• Shredded cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a medium skillet, brown hamburger and chopped onion
2. While the meat is cooking, combine 2 t of the taco seasoning, onion powder, garlic powder and basil in a 1 quart plastic storage bag
3. Shake to mix well
4. Cut the biscuits into quarters
5. Drop the biscuit quarters, a handful at a time, into the bag and shake to coat evenly
6. As the biscuit quarters are coated, layer them in the bottom of a greased 2 qt baking dish, and set aside
7. Drain grease from meat mixture, then return to skillet and add 2 t of the taco seasoning, any leftover seasoning mixture from coating the biscuits, and the water
8. Cook for 3 minutes
9. Stir in salsa and black beans
10. Carefully spread the mixture over the biscuit pieces in the casserole dish
11. Bake 20 minutes
12. Remove from oven and cover with the shredded cheese
13. Return to oven and bake an additional 5-10 minutes, until cheese is melted and bubbly<|endoftext|>
