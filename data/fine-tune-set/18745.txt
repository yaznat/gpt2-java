Ingredients:
• Vanilla wafers
• Butter
• Bananas
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Eggs
• Egg yolks
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Egg whites

Steps:
1. Reserve 30 vanilla wafers
2. Coarsely crush the remaining wafers in a food processor
3. Combine with the butter until blended
4. Firmly press the crumbs from the bottom of a 9" pie plate all the way to the outer edge of the plate
5. Bake at 350f for 10-12 minutes or until lightly browned
6. Cool on a wire rack for 30 minutes
7. When completely cool, arrange banana slices in the crust
8. Vanilla cream filling: in a saucepan, combine the 3 / 4 c sugar, flour, eggs, egg yolks, and milk
9. Cook over medium low heat, stirring constantly, for 8-10 minutes or until it has thickened to the consistency of pudding
10. Remove from heat and add vanilla
11. Pour half of the vanilla cream filling over the bananas
12. Layer 20 vanilla wafers on top of the filling
13. Pour the remaining filling over the wafers
14. Meringue: beat the egg whites on high speed with a mixer until foamy
15. Add the sugar, 1 t at a time, and beat until stiff peaks form
16. Spread the meringue evenly over the filling and be sure to seal the edges
17. Bake at 350f for 10-12 minutes or until the meringue is golden brown
18. Cool on a wire rack for 1 hour or until completely cool
19. Coarsely crush the remaining 10 wafers and garnish the top of the pie
20. Chill for 4 hours before serving<|endoftext|>
