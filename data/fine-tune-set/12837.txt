What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Steak Bake

Ingredients:
• Round steaks
• Oil
• Lemons
• Onions
• Green bell pepper
• Minced garlic cloves
• Flour
• Beef stock
• Beer
• Worcestershire sauce
• Brown sugar
• Fresh parsley
• Dried thyme
• Seasoning salt
• Pepper
• Whole mushroom
• Broad egg noodles

Steps:
1. Set oven to 325 degrees
2. Cut the steak in serving pieces
3. Brown both sides in 3 tbsp oil, to which the 4 slices of lemon have been added
4. Remove steak to a large baking pan
5. Layer the onions and the garlic over the meat
6. Sprinkle the flour evenly on top of the steak
7. In a bowl, combine the beef broth, worcestershire sauce beer, brown sugar, parsley, thyme, seasoning salt, pepper and the 1 cup whole mushrooms pour evenly over the steak and the flour in the baking pan
8. Bake uncovered for 3 hours
9. Remove from oven
10. Serve on a platter with cooked rice or noodles<|endoftext|>
