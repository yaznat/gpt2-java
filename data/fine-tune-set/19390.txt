Ingredients:
• Fruit loops cereal
• Water
• Candy sprinkles
• Doughnuts

Steps:
1. Before bed, have the kids place the cereal on a cookie sheet or tray
2. Then sprinkle with milk or water
3. Place the tray in a cold oven overnight
4. Tell the kids that if they stay in bed and sleep well then the cereal will magically grow into donuts by morning
5. )
6. Very early the next morning drive to the bakery and buy a dozen freshly baked donuts
7. This is the price you pay for having kids that stayed in bed all night
8. Replace the cold cereal with the donuts and carefully destroy and hide the box or bag!
9. Wait for your little darlings to wake up and discover the magic donuts in the oven
10. By the way, chocolate milk or sprinkles makes chocolate iced donuts
11. Water makes glazed
12. Regular milk makes cream filled
13. And colored sprinkles makes - colored sprinkle covered donuts!<|endoftext|>
