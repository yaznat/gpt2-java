Give me a lamb recipe.<|endoftext|>Brandied Tomato Sauce Flambe

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Onion
• Mushrooms
• Shallots
• Leek
• Salt
• Brandy
• Madeira wine
• Tomatoes
• Chicken broth
• Thyme
• Bay leaf
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a medium skillet over medium heat
2. Cook onion, mushrooms, shallot, leek, and salt until soft, about 7 minutes
3. Add brandy and 1 / 4 cup madeira
4. Turn heat to high and carefully ignite the alcohol using a match or bbq lighter
5. Cook over high heat until flames die out
6. Reduce temperature to medium-high and cook until liquid is reduced by half
7. Add chicken stock, tomatoes, thyme, and bay leaf
8. Reduce the heat to low and simmer, uncovered, for 20 minutes
9. Puree in a food processor or blender
10. Strain if desired
11. Return to pan and place over medium heat
12. Whisk in remaining tablespoon butter, broken in small pieces, and remaining tablespoon madeira
13. Season with salt and pepper to taste
14. Spoon over cooked gnocchi or pasta<|endoftext|>
