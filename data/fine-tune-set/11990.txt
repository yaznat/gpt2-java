Give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Apple Pie

Ingredients:
• Apple juice
• Raw honey
• Whole cloves
• Everclear alcohol
• Ground cinnamon
• Cheesecloth

Steps:
1. Mix ingredence together except everclear and pour in to a ceramic jug that light will not penetrate close with cork or lid put jug in a cool dark place for 1 month
2. After 1 month shake up mixture and strain with cloth add everclear and serve cold<|endoftext|>
