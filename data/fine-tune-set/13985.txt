Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Buttery Baked Lamb Chops

Ingredients:
• Lamb loin chops
• Worcestershire sauce
• Lemon juice
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Poke holes all over the lamb chops in a baking dish
3. Now sprinkle each chop with 1 teaspoons of the worcestershire sauce and 1 teaspoons of the lemon juice
4. Top each chop with a teaspoon of butter
5. Bake for 15-20 minutes for pink meat
6. Cook longer if you prefer well done meat<|endoftext|>
