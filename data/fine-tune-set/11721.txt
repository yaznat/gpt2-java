What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>Albanian Walnut Cake With Lemon Glaze

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Plain yogurt
• Buttermilk
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Lemon rind
• Walnuts
• Water
• Cinnamon stick
• Fresh lemon juice
• Ground allspice
• Ground cloves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Cream together the butter and sugar until light and fluffy, and then mix in the eggs
3. Blend the yogurt with the buttermilk
4. Sift together the cake dry ingredients and add them alternating with the yogurt mixture into the egg mixture
5. Stir in the lemon rind and walnuts
6. Pour the batter into a buttered 9x13 baking pan and bake for 30-40 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean
7. The cake will still be moist
8. Make the glaze by simmering together all the ingredients, covered, for about 15 minutes
9. Remove the cinnamon stick
10. When the cake is done, remove it from the oven, turn off the oven, pour the glaze over the hot cake and return it to the oven for about 10 minutes
11. Cut the cake into squares and serve it warm or cool<|endoftext|>
