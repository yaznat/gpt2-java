Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Fresh mushrooms
• Butter
• Flour tortillas
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Tomatoes
• Avocado
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook the bacon over medium heat until crisp
2. Remove to paper towels
3. Drain, reserving 1 tablespoon drippings
4. Saute mushrooms in the drippings
5. Spread butter over one side of each tortilla
6. Place two tortillas, buttered side down, on a griddle or skillet
7. Top with bacon, mushrooms, cheese, tomato and avocado
8. Cover with remaining tortillas, buttered side up
9. Cook over low heat for 1-2 minutes or until golden brown
10. Turn and cook 1-2 minutes longer or until the cheese is melted
11. Cut into wedges
12. Serve with sour cream and salsa if desired<|endoftext|>
