Give me a burger recipe.<|endoftext|>Appetizer Hamburger Cheese Dip Great For Parties

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Onion
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Chili powder
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic powder
• Velveeta cheese

Steps:
1. Brown burger and onion
2. Add remaining ingredients
3. Heat until cheese is melted
4. Serve warm with tortilla chips, corn chips, doritos, etc
5. Variation: 8 oz mild taco sauce may be used instead of the green chili / tomatoes
6. Drain slightly before adding if you use this as a substitute ingredient<|endoftext|>
