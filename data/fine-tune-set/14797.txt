Ingredients:
• Cooked basmati rice
• Tomatoes
• Vidalia onion
• Cilantro
• Lemon juice
• Jalapenos
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Avocado
• Lime juice
• Romaine lettuce
• Limes

Steps:
1. In a large bowl combine rice, tomato, onion, cilantro, lemon juice, jalapenos, olive oil, salt and pepper
2. Toss avocado with lime juice
3. Stir into rice mixture
4. Cover and chill for 2 to 8 hours
5. Spoon mixture on top of shredded romaine lettuce
6. Serve with lime wedges<|endoftext|>
