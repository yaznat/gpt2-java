Ingredients:
• Black olives
• Balsamic vinegar
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Lemon juice
• Honey
• Sea salt
• Garlic cloves
• Dried parsley
• Dried oregano
• Black pepper
• Firm tofu
• Water

Steps:
1. Put all ingredients but tofu and water into food processor
2. Process until all are mixed and smooth
3. Add water and tofu and blend some more
4. Put into container and refrigerate for up to 10 days<|endoftext|>
