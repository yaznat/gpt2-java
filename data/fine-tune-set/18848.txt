Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sliced almonds
• Cream
• Egg yolks
• Cornflour

Steps:
1. Cake method
2. Preheat oven to 180 degrees celsius
3. Sift flour and baking powder
4. Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy
5. Add vanilla
6. Add eggs one at a time beating well after each addition
7. Add sifted dry ingredients alternating with milk
8. Spread batter into a greased and floured 22cm loose bottomed cake tin
9. Topping method
10. Combine all ingredients in a pot and heat until sugar has dissolved over medium heat
11. Pour over cake and spread evenly
12. Bake in oven for 35 minutes or centre of skewer comes clean
13. Custard method
14. While cake is in oven make filling
15. Scald milk and cream
16. Beat egg yolks with sugar until light and creamy
17. Add cornflour and vanilla extract
18. Then slowly add scalded milk and cream
19. Cook on top of double boiler stirring until mixture thickens
20. Stir in butter and set aside to cool
21. Once cake is cool split cake in three layers carefully and sandwich together with custard<|endoftext|>
