Give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Bayrischer Gulasch German Goulash Stew Crock Pot Or Oven

Ingredients:
• Stewing beef
• Onions
• Olive oil
• Dried herbs
• Red paprika
• Carrots
• Potatoes
• Tomatoes
• Tomato puree
• Garlic powder
• Caraway seed
• Red wine
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in a casserole, add meat and pan fry until brown
2. Add onion and heat until transparent
3. Then add carrots, potatoes and herbs and stir for a few minutes
4. Add red wine, tomatoes, tomato puree, garlic powder and caraway seeds - simmer for about 2 to 3 hours, adding some water if necessary
5. Crock pot: cook in a crock pot on high for 4 to 5 hours - reduce the liquid if cooking this way to about 1 / 4 bottle red wine
6. Serve with sour cream, dumplings or noodles<|endoftext|>
