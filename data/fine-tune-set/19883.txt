Ingredients:
• Russet potato
• Butter
• Hot sauce
• French-fried onions
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 400f place potatoes, butter, and hot sauce in large zip bag
2. Seal the bag and toss the potatoes to get a nice, even coating
3. Mix the french-fried onions and parmesan cheese in a bowl, then add to the zip bag
4. Coat the potatoes in crumb-cheese mixture, pressing firmly, making sure they're thoroughly and evenly coated
5. Arrange the potatoes in a single layer in a baking sheet coated with nonstick cooking spray
6. Bake for about 25 minutes, until potatoes are tender and golden brown<|endoftext|>
