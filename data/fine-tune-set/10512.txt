Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>4 Cheese Summer Pasta Supper

Ingredients:
• Linguine
• Zucchini
• Cheese
• Tomatoes
• Garlic cloves
• Parsley
• Dried basil

Steps:
1. Cook pasta
2. Add zucchini during last 2 minutes of cooking
3. Drain and then return to saucepan
4. Add half the cheese to pasta
5. Mix
6. Combine tomatoes, garlic, parsley and basil
7. Stir into pasta mixture
8. Garnish with remaining cheese
9. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
