What's a recipe for a popsicle?<|endoftext|>Fruit Yogurt Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Plain yogurt
• Fresh fruit
• Honey
• Wooden popsicle sticks
• Paper cups
• Aluminum foil

Steps:
1. Place the yogurt, fruit and honey in a blender
2. Blend to desired consistency
3. Pour into paper cups, filling them 3 / 4 full
4. Cover the cups with foil
5. Make slits in the center of the foil covers and insert the popsicle sticks
6. Put in the freezer for about 5 hours or until frozen solid
7. When ready, peel off the paper cup and eat<|endoftext|>
