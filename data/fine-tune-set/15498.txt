Please give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Basic Burgers

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Water
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Onion
• Dijon mustard
• Garlic clove
• Salt and pepper
• Lean ground beef

Steps:
1. In bowl, beat egg with water
2. Stir in bread crumbs, onion, mustard, garlic, salt and pepper
3. Add beef
4. Mix just until combined
5. Shape into four 3 / 4-inch thick patties
6. Place on greased grill over medium heat
7. Close lid and grill, turning once, until no longer pink inside and digital thermometer inserted sideways into centre reads 160f, about 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
