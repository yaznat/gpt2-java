Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Nif S Spanish Omelette Omelet For 2

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Chorizo sausage
• Eggs
• Milk
• Baking powder
• Fresh spinach
• Hard cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large nonstick frying pan over medium heat
2. Add chorizo and saute for about 2 minutes, just until a little browned
3. Remove sausage and place on a paper towel
4. Meanwhile, whisk together eggs, milk and baking powder
5. When sausage is removed from pan, add egg mixture
6. Sprinkle with sausage and spinach
7. Top with grated cheese
8. Cover pan with lid and turn heat down to medium-low
9. Check after about 3 minutes and see if omelette has set - the middle should not jiggle
10. Add salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
