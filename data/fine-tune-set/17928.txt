What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Barefoot Contessa S Outrageous Oreo Crunch Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Eggs
• Instant coffee granules
• Vanilla
• Sugar
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Oreo cookies

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Butter and flour an 11" x 17" pan
3. In a heat-proof medium bowl set over a saucepan of simmering water, heat butter, chocolate chips and unsweetened chocolate until melted and smooth
4. Allow to cool slightly
5. In a large bowl, whisk eggs, coffee, vanilla and sugar
6. Blend chocolate mixture into egg mixture
7. Cool to room temperature
8. In a medium bowl, sift together one cup flour, baking powder and salt
9. Add flour mixture to chocolate mixture
10. In a small bowl, stir oreos and remaining 1 / 4 cup flour
11. Add oreo mixture to chocolate mixture
12. Pour batter into baking pan and smooth top with a rubber spatula
13. Bake 35 minutes or until a toothpick inserted 3-inches from center comes out clean
14. Do not overbake
15. Allow to cool
16. Refrigerate, tightly wrapped, until cold
17. Cut into squares
18. Share them with your friends and they will be forever indebted to you!<|endoftext|>
