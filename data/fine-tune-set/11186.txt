Ingredients:
• Pancakes
• Almond butter
• Banana
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Divide almond butter between two pancakes
2. Slice banana thinly and arrange on pancakes with almond butter
3. Drizzle some maple syrup over banana slices
4. Top with remaining pancakes and more maple syrup<|endoftext|>
