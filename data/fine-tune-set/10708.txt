Ingredients:
• Shrimp
• Olive oil
• Fresh parsley
• Garlic
• Dry red wine
• Tomato sauce
• Salt
• Dry mustard
• Oregano
• Pepper
• Angel hair pasta

Steps:
1. Cook oil and garlic 1 minute
2. Add all other ingredients except shrimp
3. Place shrimp in large ziploc freezer bag and pour sauce over
4. Marinate 2 hours in refrigerator
5. Cook 10 minutes under broiler or until shrimp is done
