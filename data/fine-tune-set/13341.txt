Ingredients:
• Chicken drumsticks
• Ginger-garlic paste
• White pepper
• Fresh cream
• Cashew nuts paste
• Egg yolk
• Green chili
• Garam masala
• Ghee
• Salt
• Chat masala

Steps:
1. Clean and wash the chicken legs and make incisions
2. Apply ginger garlic paste and keep aside for 20 minutes
3. In a separate bowl, mix white pepper, fresh cream, cashewnut paste, egg yolk, chopped green chilli, garam masala and salt
4. Add this marinade to the chicken and refrigerate for 4 1 / 2 hours
5. Heat oven to medium and place chicken legs on a roasting rack
6. Bake chicken for 12 minutes
7. Take out chicken and baste it with ghee / clarified butter and further grill for 8 minutes
