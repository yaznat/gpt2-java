Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Avocados
• Lemon, juice and zest of
• Sun-dried tomatoes packed in oil
• Feta cheese
• Pine nuts
• Baby spinach
• Rocket
• Mixed fresh herbs
• Olive oil
• Capers

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to the packet
2. Toss together the avocado, tomato, cheese, nuts, spinach, rocket, herbs and pasta
3. Whisk together the oil, lemon zest and juice and the capers and drizzle over the salad<|endoftext|>
