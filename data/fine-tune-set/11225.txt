What's a recipe for sushi?<|endoftext|>Breakfast Sushi

Ingredients:
• Nori
• Egg
• Sausage
• Home fries
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Lay seaweed sheet on a flat surface with the long side closest to you
2. Gently spread a light layer of salsa over entire sheet
3. At one inch from the edge of the sheet, add ingredients in layers
4. It should make a line parallel to the long side of the seaweed
5. Starting from the edge closest to the you, gently fold seaweed sheet over filling, lightly pressing to form a roll
6. Continue rolling
7. Slice roll in one inch pieces
8. Each roll should make from 6 to 8 finished rolls
9. It prevents it from sticking to anything
10. Plus, when it comes time to roll, use the plastic to stop your hands from sticking<|endoftext|>
