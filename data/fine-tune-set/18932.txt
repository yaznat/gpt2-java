Ingredients:
• Cake flour
• Caster sugar
• Milk
• Unsalted butter
• Egg yolks
• Vanilla essence

Steps:
1. Sieve flour into a microwavable bowl
2. Stir in the sugar and mix well
3. Heat milk in a suacepan till nearly boiling but not boiling
4. Add this hot milk into the flour mixture, stirring all the time
5. Microwave for 1
6. 5 minutes in a 600w microwave oven
7. Remove from mw, stir mixture
8. Send it back to the microwave for 1
9. 5 minutes on high again
10. Add in the lightly beaten egg yolks and blend well
11. Microwave mixture for 1 minute
12. Add in the butter and vanilla essence and mix well<|endoftext|>
