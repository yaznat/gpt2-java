What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Chorizo Potato Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Chorizo sausages
• Potato
• Chipotle chiles in adobo
• Salt
• Monterey jack cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Butter
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Remove skin from sausages and crumble into small skillet
2. Cook over low heat until fat renders, taking care to avoid burning--about 5 minutes
3. Drain off all but 3 tablespoons fat
4. Add potatoes, chiles and salt
5. Cook over medium heat, stirring from time to time, until potatoes begin to brown--about 8 minutes
6. Brush one side of tortilla sparingly with butter
7. Place on medium-hot flat griddle pan
8. Spread with about 1 / 4 cup of filling and sprinkle with 2-3 tablespoons of cheese
9. Top with second tortilla
10. Brush top with butter
11. When bottom begins to brown, flip over and cook the other side until cheese is melted
12. Slip it onto a plate, slice and serve<|endoftext|>
