What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Aglio E Olio Pasta Garlic And Oil

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Garlic cloves
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Butter
• Fresh parsley
• Grape tomatoes
• Portabella mushroom
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Follow direstions for pasta
2. Meanwhile in a large sauce pan add garlic and oil and cook on medium heat until softened but not browned
3. Add butter until completely melted
4. Add tomatoes and mushrooms and parsley
5. Cook until mushrooms and tomatoes are desired consitency
6. Do not let boil or brown
7. Drain pasta and toss with the garlic and oil sauce
8. Serve immediately and top with fresh parmesan
