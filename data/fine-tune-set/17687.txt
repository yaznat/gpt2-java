What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Muffins With Crumble Topping Gluten Free

Ingredients:
• Brown rice flour
• Rice flour
• Tapioca flour
• Potato starch
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Guar gum
• Sea salt
• Canola oil
• Egg
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Apples
• Cinnamon
• Cashews

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Sieve into a medium bowl, 1 / 2 cup brown rice flour, white rice flour, tapioca starch, potato starch, 1 / 2 cup brown sugar, baking powder, baking soda, guar gum, sea salt, and 1 tsp cinnamon
3. Mix well
4. Add to it 1 / 3 cup canola oil, the egg, applesauce, and chopped apples
5. Stir lightly to combine and add additional liquid if needed
6. Line muffin tins and oil a little bit to prevent any sticking
7. Generously fill muffin cups with mixture
8. Put ground cashews or almond meal into a small bowl with the 1 tbls brown rice flour, 2 tbls brown sugar, 1 tbls canola oil, and 1 / 2 tsp cinnamon
9. Mix and sprinkle onto each muffin
10. Bake for about 26 minutes until done
11. Cool and enjoy for breakfast or a snack anytime!<|endoftext|>
