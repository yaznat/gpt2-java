Give me a popsicle recipe.<|endoftext|>Vanilla Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Paper cups
• Wooden popsicle sticks

Steps:
1. Combine milk, sugar and vanilla in a large bowl and stir until sugar dissolves
2. Pour mixture into the paper cups until they are half full
3. Freeze for 1 hour or until slushy enough to support stick
4. Put popsicle sticks in the cups for handles
5. Freeze in freezer for a total of about four hours<|endoftext|>
