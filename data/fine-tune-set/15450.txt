Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Onion
• Cooked bacon
• Spike seasoning
• Beer

Steps:
1. Combine bacon, onion and cheddar cheese
2. Set aside
3. Combine beef, spike and beer
4. Mix thoroughly and shape into 6 thin patties
5. Divide bacon / onion / cheese mixture onto three patties
6. Top with remaining patties and press edges to seal
7. Grill, broil or pan fry until well done, about 4 minutes per side<|endoftext|>
