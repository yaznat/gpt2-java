What's a recipe for a wrap?<|endoftext|>Anthony Sedlak Prosciutto Wrapped Halibut With Puttanesca Sauce

Ingredients:
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Thyme
• Prosciutto
• Olive oil
• Red onion
• Garlic clove
• Capers
• Bay leaf
• Green olives
• Jar
• White wine
• Cherry tomatoes
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Fish:
2. 1
3. Preheat oven to 400f
4. 2
5. Season fillets with pepper
6. 3
7. Place 1 thyme sprig on top of each and wrap each fillet with prosciutto
8. 4
9. Heat olive oil in a large cast iron or heavy-bottomed pan over medium-high heat
10. When oil starts to smoke, add fish, placing prosciutto seam side down
11. Sear on all sides until prosciutto is coloured and crispy
12. 5
13. Transfer to a plate and keep warm
14. Sauce:
15. 1
16. Heat olive oil in same pan over medium-high heat
17. 2
18. Add red onion, garlic, capers, and bay leaf, and saut until onion is lightly coloured
19. 3
20. Add olives and white wine and olive juice
21. Bring to a simmer, add tomatoes skin side down and squish with fork after they heat up, return fish to pan
22. 4
23. Place pan in oven and cook until fish is tender and cooked through, about 5 minutes
24. 5
25. Top with parsley<|endoftext|>
