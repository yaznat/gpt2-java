Ingredients:
• Curry powder
• Mustard powder
• Lemon juice
• Soy sauce
• Fresh ground black pepper
• White fish fillets
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Place curry and mustard powders, lemon juice, soy sauce and black pepper to taste in a bowl and mix to combine
3. Brush fish with lemon juice mixture and place in a greased baking dish
4. Dot with butter and bake for 15 minutes or until fish flakes when tested with a fork
5. Serve with tossed salad and new boiled potatoes<|endoftext|>
