What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Cheesy Shell Taco Bites

Ingredients:
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Hamburger
• Taco seasoning
• Salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Avocado
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. For shells:
2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
3. Using a tablespoon, scoop shredded cheese on to parchment and shape into a disk
4. Make sure each cheese disk does not touch the one next to it
5. Bake on a parchment-lined baking sheet for 7 minutes
6. Lift the melted cheese disks off of the parchment paper and press into mini-muffin tins
7. Let the cheese cool for 1-minute in the muffin tin so that they will hold their shape
8. Remove from muffin tin and set aside
9. For the taco filling:
10. Cook hamburger until browned
11. Drain the fat and stir in taco mix and 1 / 2 cup salsa / picante sauce
12. Simmer for 3-5 minutes
13. Assembly:
14. Spoon beef mixture in cooled cheese cups and top with the 3 shredded cheese, sour cream, salsa, cubed avocados and cilantro<|endoftext|>
