Can you give me a omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Fiesta Omelette

Ingredients:
• Salsa
• Artichoke heart
• Parmesan cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Eggs
• Light sour cream

Steps:
1. Grease 10" quiche dish
2. Spread salsa on the bottom
3. Distribute chopped artichokes evenly over salsa
4. Sprinkle parmesan cheese over the artichokes
5. Sprinkle monterey jack cheese and cheddar cheese over parmesan cheese
6. Place eggs in food processor and blend until smooth
7. Add sour cream and blend
8. Pour egg mixture over the cheeses
9. Bake uncovered in 350 oven for 30-40 minutes or until set
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
