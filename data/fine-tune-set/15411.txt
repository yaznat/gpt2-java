Ingredients:
• Smoked bacon
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Yellow onions
• Ground sirloin
• Worcestershire sauce
• Hot sauce
• Seasoning
• Kaiser rolls
• Smoked gouda cheese
• Steak sauce

Steps:
1. In large skillet over medium heat, cook bacon until crisp, about 4-5 minutes
2. Remove from pan and drain on paper towel lined plate
3. Discard all but about 1 t
4. Of the bacon drippings
5. Chop 1 / 4 of an onion finely, then slice the other 1 3 / 4 onions
6. Sweat the chopped onion in the reserved bacon drippings for 2-3 minutes
7. Remove from pan and combine with the bacon
8. Coat pan with the olive oil and drop in the sliced onions
9. Let sweat until soft and slightly browned, about 10 minutes
10. Remove and place in bowl
11. Keep warm
12. While the onions are sweating, combine the sirloin, worcestershire, hot sauce, grill seasoning, and the bacon / onion mixture
13. Form into 4 equal-sized patties
14. Cook burgers, covered, in skillet, about 4 minutes per side for medium-rare and 6 minutes per side for medium-well
15. Place a slice of cheese on each burger and cover over low heat to melt the cheese
16. Stir steak sauce into the cooked sliced onions
17. Toast the buns if desired
18. Serve burgers with the smothered onions on a toasted bun
