Give me a stir fry recipe.<|endoftext|>Chinese Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Beef flank steak
• Broccoli
• Carrots
• Onion
• French onion soup
• Soy sauce
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Rice

Steps:
1. Cut beef into strips and brown in oil with onions
2. Add bouillon cubes, a few dashes of soy sauce and onion soup
3. When it begins to boil, add vegetables and cover
4. Cook for apprx 10 mins, or until vegetables are crisp tender
5. Serve over prepared rice<|endoftext|>
