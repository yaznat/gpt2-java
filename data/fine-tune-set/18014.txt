Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Brownie Bottom Pie

Ingredients:
• Semisweet baking chocolate
• Margarine
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Nuts
• Milk
• Chocolate flavor instant pudding and pie filling mix
• Cool whip

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. Grease bottom and sides of 9 inch pie plate
3. Microwave chocolate and butter in a large microwave bowl for about 2 minutes
4. Stir until chocolate is melted
5. Stir in sugar, eggs, and vanilla
6. Mix in flour, then nuts
7. Spread in pan
8. Bake 25 to 30 minutes
9. Cool
10. Pour milk into a large bowl
11. Add pudding mixes
12. Beat well with wire whisk
13. Let stand 2 minutes
14. Spread over brownie pie
15. Top with cool whip or whipped cream
16. Garnish with grated bakers chocolate
17. Refrigerate until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
