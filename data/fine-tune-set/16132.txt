Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Frozen corn
• Black beans
• Red onion
• Monterey jack cheese
• Chili powder

Steps:
1. Place 4 of the tortillas on a work surface
2. Sprinkle one-quarter of the corn, beans, onion, cheese and chili powder evenly on each tortilla, leaving a 1 / 2-inch border
3. Top with the remaining tortillas and press down to help them stick together
4. To cook them on the stovetop: heat a large, lightly greased with cooking spray a skillet or grill pan over medium heat
5. Add 1 quesadilla and cook for 4 to 5 minutes, then flip over and cook another 4 to 5 minutes
6. It's done when it begins to get brown flecks and the cheese is melted
7. To bake: preheat the oven to 375 degrees
8. Place the quesadillas on a large, ungreased, rimmed baking sheet and bake for 5 minutes per side
9. Transfer the quesadillas to a cutting board and cut each into 4 wedges
10. Let them rest for 1 to 2 minutes before serving
11. Reheating: quesadillas are best eaten fresh
12. If they need to be reheated, avoid the microwave, which makes them soggy, and the oven, which dries them out
13. Instead, warm the quesadilla in a nonstick skillet over medium or medium-high heat, turning it once, until the cheese softens and the tortilla crisps slightly, 3 to 4 minutes total<|endoftext|>
