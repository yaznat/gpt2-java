Ingredients:
• Sugar cookies
• Cashews
• Butter
• Vanilla ice cream
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Powdered sugar
• Evaporated milk
• Vanilla
• Pretzel stick
• Caramel ice cream topping

Steps:
1. Combine all crust ingredients in medium bowl
2. Mix well
3. Press onto bottom of ungreased 13x9 inch baking pan
4. Freezer 10 minutes
5. Spread ice cream over crust
6. Cover
7. Freeze until set
8. Meanwhile, combine all fudge layer ingredients in 2 quart saucepan
9. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until mixture comes to a boil
10. Reduce heat to medium-low
11. Cook, stirring occasionally, 5 minutes or until slightly thickened
12. Remove from heat
13. Stir in vanilla
14. Cool completely
15. Pour cooled chocolate mixture over frozen ice cream
16. Freeze until set
17. Meanwhile, combine pretzel sticks and cashews in small bowl
18. Set aside
19. Before serving, let dessert stand at room temperature 10 to 15 minutes
20. Cut into servings
21. Top each serving with about 1 tablespoon pretzel mixture
22. Drizzle each with about 1 tablespoon caramel topping<|endoftext|>
