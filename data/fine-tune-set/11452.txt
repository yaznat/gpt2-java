Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Sugar
• Peach yogurt
• Almond extract
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f measure and sift the dry ingredients except for the sugar into a large bowl
2. Lightly beat eggs in another bowl
3. Add oil and sugar to the eggs and blend well
4. Mix in the yogurt and almond extract
5. Add the wet mixture to the dry ingredients
6. Add nuts and mix until just combined
7. Pour into 2 well-greased loaf pans
8. Bake for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
