Ingredients:
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Avocado
• Broccoli coleslaw mix
• Low-fat ranch dressing
• Chicken breast halves

Steps:
1. Place tortillas on serving plates
2. Lay half of the avocado slices evenly on the middle third of each tortilla, leaving about 2 inches of blank tortilla at the top and bottom
3. Sprinkle 1 / 2 cup of broccoli slaw over each avocado bed
4. Place chicken on avocado broccoli bed
5. Drizzle 2 t ranch dressing over avocado broccoli chicken bed
6. Fold left third of tortilla over mixture, then right third over left third
7. Tuck ends under
8. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
