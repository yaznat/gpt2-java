Ingredients:
• Graham cracker crust
• Cream cheese
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Cheesecake flavor instant pudding and pie filling mix
• Firm bananas
• Frozen whipped topping
• Sweetened whipped cream

Steps:
1. Beat softened cream cheese and condensed milk at medium speed until smooth
2. Add pudding mix and beat until well blended
3. Fold in the whipped topping until well combined
4. Place half of cream cheese mixture in the bottom of the prepared pie crust
5. Put a layer of sliced bananas over the filling
6. Spread the remaining cream cheese mixture over the bananas and smooth
7. Garnish with remaining sliced bananas and whipped cream, if desired
8. Cover and chill for 2 hours or more<|endoftext|>
