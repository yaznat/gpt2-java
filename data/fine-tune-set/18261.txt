Can you give me a pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Berry Pancake Waukau

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Milk
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Butter
• Berries
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Adjust oven rack to the lower-middle position and heat the oven to 375 degrees
2. Whisk the flour and salt in a medium bowl
3. Whisk the milk, egg, and vanilla in a small bowl
4. Make a well in the center of the flour mixture and add the milk mixture to the well
5. Whisk until combined
6. Melt the butter in a 12-inch ovenproof skillet over medium heat
7. Pour the batter into the center of the skillet and let it level
8. Scatter the berries over the batter, leaving a 1-inch border around the edges
9. Sprinkle the sugar over the berries, again avoiding the outer 1-inch border
10. Bake until the edges are puffed and deep golden brown, 50 to 60 minutes
11. Transfer to a serving plate and serve hot<|endoftext|>
