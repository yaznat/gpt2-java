What's a recipe for a smoothie?<|endoftext|>Berry Banana Smoothies

Ingredients:
• Vanilla yogurt
• Banana
• Frozen unsweetened strawberries
• Frozen blueberries
• Frozen raspberries
• Frozen blackberrie
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a blender or food processor, combine all ingredients
2. Cover and process until smooth
3. Pour into chilled glasses
4. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
