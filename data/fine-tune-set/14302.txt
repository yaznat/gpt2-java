Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Carrot
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Lentils
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cut tofu into 3 / 4 inch cubes
2. Cut carrot into small pieces
3. Heat oil in a saucepan
4. Using a garlic press, add garlic to heated oil
5. Cook garlic until browned
6. Add carrots and let cook for about 5 minutes, to soften a bit
7. Add tofu and let cook for another 5 minutes
8. Add lentils and cayenne pepper
9. Let cook for another 10 minutes or so
10. Serve over rice of your choice!<|endoftext|>
