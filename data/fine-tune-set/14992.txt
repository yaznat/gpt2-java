Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Nasi Goreng Istimewa Special Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Garlic clove
• Shrimp paste
• Soya sauce
• Onion
• Chili powder
• Oil
• Salt

Steps:
1. Slice your onion lengthwise
2. Heat the oil and fry the onion, garlic, chili and shrimp paste until the onion is soft
3. Add the rice and salt to taste
4. Mixing thoroughly while constantly stirring until every grain of rice is coated
5. Then add the soya sauce and mix again until the colour is even throughout
6. The rice should take on a nice orange-red tint from the chili powder
7. Cooked meat or prawns can be added with the soya sauce
8. Onion crisps is used as a garnish
9. To make it 'istimewa' or special, a lightly fried egg, some cucumber slices and kerupuk are served on top of the rice<|endoftext|>
