Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Bankruptcy Beef Stew

Ingredients:
• Round steaks
• Water
• Tomato sauce
• Potatoes
• Green pepper
• Celery
• Carrots
• Yellow onion
• Dried parsley
• Bay leaf
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a large non stick skillet brown beef cubes over medium heat
2. Add the rest of the ingredients
3. Cover and simmer for 1 hour over medium heat or until meat is tender
4. Stir occasionally
5. May add more water if mixture becomes too thick
6. Enjoy, may serve with french crusty bread<|endoftext|>
