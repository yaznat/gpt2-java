What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Almond Chicken Salad For Dips Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Celery
• Golden raisin
• Almonds
• Mayonnaise
• Frozen orange juice concentrate
• Garlic powder
• Ground black pepper
• Ground ginger
• Salt

Steps:
1. In large bowl, stir together chicken, celery raisins and almonds
2. Add remaining ingredients
3. Mix well and chill in the refrigerator 2 hours before serving
4. Serve on croissant rolls or toasted pita bread
5. Use a food processor for a creamier texture
6. Enjoy!
7. Time saver: while cooking chicken, prepare the rest of the ingredients
8. When chicken is done, cool on stove-top and serve
9. Okay if slightly warm
10. Also clean up cooking area if you are waiting for chicken to cool<|endoftext|>
