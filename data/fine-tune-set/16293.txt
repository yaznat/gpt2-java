What's a recipe for a wrap?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Buffalo Shrimp

Ingredients:
• Shrimp
• Bacon
• Louisiana hot sauce
• Blue cheese dressing
• Celery rib

Steps:
1. Parbroil bacon at 400 bacon should be browned and bendable
2. Wash shrimp
3. Wrap 1 / 2 piece of bacon around each shrimp
4. Use toothpick
5. Bake in 400 oven for 6-7 more minutes
6. While shrimp is still hot, drizzle with the hot sauce
7. Use your judgement
8. Toss lightly
9. Serve with blu cheese dressing and celery sticks<|endoftext|>
