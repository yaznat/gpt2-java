Ingredients:
• Cooked beets
• Pimento pepper
• Green onion
• Garlic clove
• Buttermilk
• Water
• Red wine vinegar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Creme fraiche
• Fresh dill

Steps:
1. To make soup: put all the soup ingredients in a blender
2. Process for 45-seconds to 1 minute, or until you have a completely smooth puree
3. Taste and adjust seasoning
4. Empty into a food-safe container and refrigerate for at least 2 hours to marry flavours
5. To make crme fraiche drizzle: combine crme fraiche with finely-minced fresh dill
6. Refrigerate until ready to use
