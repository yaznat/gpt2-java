Ingredients:
• Apple cider
• Pitted dates
• Cornstarch
• Ground ginger
• Fresh lemon rind
• Pie crusts
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Tart cooking apples

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. In a small saucepan, stir together the apple cider, dates, cornstarch, ginger, and the lemon rind
3. Bring to a boil over moderate heat, stirring constantly
4. Cook for 2 minutes or until mixture is thickened, stirring constantly
5. Remove from heat
6. Line a 9 inch pie plate with one of the pie crusts
7. Trim crust even with edge of pie plate
8. In a large bowl, stir together the sugar and flour
9. Add the apples, toss until apples are coated
10. Spoon the apple mixture into crust
11. Spoon the cider mixture over apple mixture
12. Cut slits in the remaining pie crust, place on top of cider mixture
13. Trim top crust to 1 / 2 inch beyond edge of pie plate
14. Fold top crust under bottom crust and crimp edge
15. Sprinkle with a little additional sugar
16. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes or until apples are tender and crust is golden
17. If the edge of the crust seems to be browning too quickly, cover with foil
18. Cool on wire rack for 2 hours before serving<|endoftext|>
