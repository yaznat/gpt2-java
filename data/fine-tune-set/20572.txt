Ingredients:
• Baked beans
• Hot dogs
• Jiffy cornbread mix
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Milk
• Eggs
• Creamed corn

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Put beans into large baking pan
3. Slice hot dogs into 1 / 2 inch slices and add to beans
4. Bake for 15-20 minutes
5. While bean and hot dog mixture is in the oven, mix cornbread mix, milk, eggs, creamed corn, and cheese in a large bowl
6. Once bean and hot dog mixture has baked for 15-20 minutes, spread cornbread mixture gently on top to cover
7. Bake for 20-30 additional minutes or until cornbread is cooked through
8. Cornbread will be moist because of corn, so don't overcook<|endoftext|>
