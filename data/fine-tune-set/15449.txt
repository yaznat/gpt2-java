What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Cheeseburgers With French Onion Dip

Ingredients:
• Lean ground chuck
• Hamburger buns
• Bacon
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• French onion dip

Steps:
1. Shape ground chuck into 4 patties
2. Cook burgers almost to your preferred doneness on the bbq grill, grill pan, under the broiler, in a pan, or on your george foreman grill
3. Top with cheese, continue to cook until the cheese is slightly melted
4. Assemble burgers, spreading top and bottom half of the buns with the french onion dip, and topping each burger with two slices of the bacon
5. You may want to have a little extra french onion dip for dipping your burger, as it tends to slide if you put too much on the bun, and you'll want plenty!
6. For a super sinful & greasy meal, serve with fries or onion rings
7. For a little healthier side dish, serve with a big salad with fresh tomatoes<|endoftext|>
