Ingredients:
• Frozen broccoli
• Frozen broccoli spears
• Sweet onion
• Garlic clove
• Cream of celery soup
• Milk
• Velveeta cheese slices
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Cracker

Steps:
1. Cook broccoli according to package directions
2. In a separate pan saute' the onion in 3 tbs butter for 6 minutes, add garlic and saute' for an additional 2 minutes
3. Add the soup, milk, and cheese slices to this mix and melt well
4. In a 9x13 baking dish prepared with pam spray, place your cooked and drained broccoli
5. Top with cheese and soup mixture
6. Dot the casserole with cream cheese evenly
7. Sprinkle with a cup of cheddar cheese and bake at 350 degrees for 20 minutes
8. Melt remaining 3 tbs of butter in the microwave, mix in a bowl with crackers
9. After your casserole has baked for 20 minutes, top with cracker mixture and bake for an additional 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
