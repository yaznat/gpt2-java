What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Adriana S Mexican Rice

Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Tomatoes
• Rice
• Oil
• Water

Steps:
1. Blend in the blender the garlic and tomato till pured
2. Heat oil in skillet
3. Add rice
4. Toast until light brown, being careful not to burn
5. Add water and pured mixture
6. Cover and turn down to simmer
7. Cook for 15 minutes or till all water is absorbed<|endoftext|>
