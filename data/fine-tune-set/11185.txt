What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Banana French Toast Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Milk
• White bread
• Banana
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven to 350f
2. Combine the egg and milk in a bowl
3. Mix well
4. Turn the slices of bread in the egg mixture until most, if not all, of the mixture is absorbed by the bread
5. Slice the banana on a diagonal to get long slices
6. Melt the butter in a small frying pan
7. Place one slice of bread in the pan
8. Top the slice with banana to cover in a single layer
9. Place the second slice of bread on top of the banana
10. Cook until the bottom slice is golden, 3 minutes
11. Turn over and cook on the other side until golden
12. Place the banana french toast in the oven
13. Bake 10 minutes, then serve
14. Be careful removing the banana french toast from the oven so the sandwich doesn't come apart
15. Dip it in syrup if you like<|endoftext|>
