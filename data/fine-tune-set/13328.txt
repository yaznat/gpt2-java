Ingredients:
• Chicken tenderloins
• Egg
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Water
• Flour
• Chili powder
• Pepper
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Pre-heat over to 450f
2. Marinade chicken for a minimum of 10 minutes in egg, corn starch, soy sauce and water
3. Measure flour, chile powder and pepper into bowl or plastic bag
4. Put one piece of chicken at a time into the flour, coat chicken
5. Place on cookie sheet sprayed with cooking spray first
6. After all the chicken is on cookie sheet, spray chicken generously with cooking spray
7. Cook 20 minutes at 450f<|endoftext|>
