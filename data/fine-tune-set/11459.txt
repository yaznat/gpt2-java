Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Confectioners' sugar
• Almond extract
• Salt
• All-purpose flour
• Orange, zest of
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. In a mixer or food processor bowl, beat butter, sugar, almond extract, and salt until smooth
2. With mixer on low speed, add flour and orange zest
3. Mixt until dough forms
4. With a wooden spoon, rubber spatula, or your hands, gently mix in almonds
5. Cut piece of waxed paper--12" x 6"
6. Roll dough into log shape (12" long by 2 1 / 2" wide and 1" thick
7. Place on waxed paper and wrap
8. Freeze until firm, at least 1 hour and up to 3 months
9. (if freezing longer than one day, wrap log again in double layers of plastic film
10. Preheat oven to 325 degrees f
11. Remove dough from freezer
12. With a sharp knife, slice into 1 / 4" thick slices
13. Place on ungreased baking sheet at leat 1" apart
14. Bake until edges just begin to turn golden, about 20 minutes
15. Do not overbake
16. Cool 5 minutes on sheet
17. Transfer to a rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
