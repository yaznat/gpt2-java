Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Butter
• Garlic cloves
• Saffron
• Cilantro
• Water
• Kalamata olives
• Lemon
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. In a large pot or dutch oven, heat oil over medium high heat
2. Add chicken and brown on all sides, about 10 minutes
3. Stir in butter, garlic, saffron, and cilantro
4. Cook another 10 minutes, stirring, to be sure garlic and spices evenly coat the chicken
5. Add water and bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat and simmer until chicken is tender, about 25 minutes
7. Stir in olives and lemon juice
8. Simmer 8 minutes
9. Season with salt and pepper
10. Serve over couscous or rice<|endoftext|>
