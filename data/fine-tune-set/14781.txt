What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Aunt Nancy S Cajun Rice And Beans

Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Red onion
• Chopped tomato
• Green peppers
• Frozen corn kernels
• Bay leaf
• Fresh parsley
• Salt
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Black pepper
• Water
• Kidney beans
• Brown rice
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Put the rice and 2 2 / 3 cups water or broth in a pot, and bring to a boil
2. Reduce heat to low, cover and simmer about 30 minutes
3. Meanwhile, prepare the other ingredients
4. After rice has been cooking 15-20 minutes, continue as follows
5. In a skillet, soften the onion and garlic in a little water
6. Stir the next 8 ingredients into the skillet
7. Add the water and bring to a boil
8. Reduce heat and simmer 5 minutes
9. When no water is left on the rice and it's just beginning to stick, remove from the heat and let it stand covered
10. This is important, to allow it to dry and fluff up a bit before serving
11. Slightly mash half of the beans
12. Stir them all into the skillet
13. Cook partially covered, stirring often, for 10 minutes
14. Discard bay leaf
15. Serve over rice, garnished with green onions if desired<|endoftext|>
