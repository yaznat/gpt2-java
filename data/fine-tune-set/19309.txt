What's a good popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Pineapple Coconut Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Pineapple juice
• Coconut milk
• Splenda sugar substitute

Steps:
1. Mix the ingredients together, stirring to blend in the splenda
2. Taste and add more splenda if desired
3. Pour into popsicle moulds and freeze for several hours or overnight
4. Serve cold and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
