Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>60 Minute Oatmeal Nut Bread

Ingredients:
• Bread flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Rolled oats
• Pecan pieces
• Sea salt
• Fast rising yeast
• Milk
• Water
• Orange zest
• Honey
• Butter
• Egg
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. In large bowl, mix 1 1 / 4 cups bread flour, wheat flour, oats, pecan pieces, salt, and yeast
2. In saucepan, over low heat, heat 1 cup milk, water, orange, honey and butter until hot
3. Stir into dry mixture
4. Mix in just enough reserved flour to make soft dough
5. On lightly floured surface, knead 4 minutes
6. Roll dough into 13x9-inch rectangle
7. Roll up from short side, jelly-roll style
8. Seal seam and ends
9. Place on greased baking sheet
10. Flatten slightly to form oval
11. Cover
12. Place a large shallow pan, half-filled with boiling water on counter and place baking sheet over this pan and let rise for 20 minutes or just allow to rise in warm draft-free place for 20 minutes
13. Preheat oven to 375f
14. Make 3 diagonal slashes on top of loaf
15. Brush with egg
16. Bake until golden
17. Remove from baking sheet
18. Cool on wire rack
19. In small bowl, mix powdered sugar and 1 teaspoon milk
20. Add additional milk, if necessary, to make desired consistency
21. Drizzle over loaf<|endoftext|>
