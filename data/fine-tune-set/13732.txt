Ingredients:
• Boneless pork chops
• Vegetable oil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Apple cider
• Brown mustard
• Red apples
• Green onion
• Raisins
• Cornstarch
• Cold water

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, brown pork chops in oil on both sides
2. Sprinkle with a pinch of salt and pepper
3. Combine cider and mustard
4. Pour over meat
5. Bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat
7. Cover and simmer for 13-18 minute remove chops to a serving platter
8. Keep warm
9. Add the apples, onions, and raisins to the skillet
10. Cover and cook over medium heat for 5-6 minute or until apples are tender
11. Combine cornstarch and water until smooth
12. Stir into apple mixture
13. Bring to a boil
14. Cook and stir for 2 minute or until thickened
15. Serve over pork chops<|endoftext|>
