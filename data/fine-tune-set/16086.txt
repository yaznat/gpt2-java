What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken And Jalapeno Quesadillas Nuwave Oven Flavorwave

Ingredients:
• Jalapeno-cilantro tortillas
• Vegetable oil
• Mexican cheese
• Roasted deli chicken
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Brush 2 tortillas with oil
2. Place tortillas, oil side down, on baking sheet
3. Sprinkle each with 1 / 4 of cheese, half of chicken, half of cilantro, and 1 / 4 of cheese, leaving 3 / 4-inch border
4. Top each with 1 tortilla, pressing to adhere
5. Brush top with oil
6. Bake quesadillas until filling is heated through and edges begin to crisp, about 2~3 minutes
7. Using large metal spatula, turn each over and bake until bottom is crisp, about 1~2 minutes
8. Transfer quesadillas to plates
9. Cut into wedges and serve<|endoftext|>
