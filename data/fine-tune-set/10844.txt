Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Peas
• Dill pickles
• Red bell peppers
• Mayonnaise
• Pickle juice
• Mustard
• Salt
• Spices
• Vegetarian sausages

Steps:
1. Wash the potatoes and the carrots well, then boil them unpealed until a fork can easily go in and out
2. Dice the potatoes, carrots, pickles, pickled bell peppers, and sausages
