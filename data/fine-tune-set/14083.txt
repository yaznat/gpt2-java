Ingredients:
• Lean lamb
• Garlic clove
• Olive oil
• Sweet onion
• Hot peppers
• Red bell pepper
• Salt
• Butter
• Mixed spice

Steps:
1. Cut meat into thin 2 inch long strips and mix with garlic
2. Heat oil over medium-high heat
3. Saute onion and hot peppers until onion is light golden
4. Add red pepper ans salt
5. Saute until red pepper is tender
6. Transfer onion mixture to bowl
7. In same pan, melt butter over high heat and saute meat mixture until seared all over
8. Stir in berbere spice mix to taste
9. Saute for 30 seconds
10. Serve with injera bread and tomato & cucumber salad
11. Keep extra berbere spice mix on table to sprinkle over meat as desired<|endoftext|>
