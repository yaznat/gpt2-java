Ingredients:
• Flour tortilla
• Chocolate chips
• Cinnamon
• Sugar
• Butter

Steps:
1. Lightly coat the flour tortilla with butter on both sides and start heating on a skillet
2. Place chocolate chips on one half of the tortilla and lightly dust with cinnamon and sugar to taste
3. Fold the tortilla in half and grill until lightly browned
4. Turning to brown other side as well
5. Serve hot
6. Yum!<|endoftext|>
