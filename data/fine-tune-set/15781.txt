Give me a burrito recipe.<|endoftext|>Bean Burrito Bake

Ingredients:
• Kidney beans
• Black beans
• Sweet corn
• Tomatoes
• Cayenne pepper
• Chili powder
• Ground black pepper
• Cumin seed
• Onion powder
• Monterey jack cheese
• Instant rice
• Fat free sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. To 2 quart baking dish add the black beas, kidney beans, corn, tomatoes
3. Sprinkle over the top all of the spices
4. Then add the cheese and rice
5. Mix well so the rice is dampened by the tomato juices
6. Cover and bake for 20 minutes
7. Remove from oven and add 4 oz fat free sour cream
8. Mix so it is evenly distributed through the dish
9. Return to oven and bake 10 more minutes
10. Allow to cool only briefly before serving unless you intend to use as a dip
11. In this case serve it warm but not hot<|endoftext|>
