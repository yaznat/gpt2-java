Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Bananas
• Banana liqueur
• Sour cream
• Vanilla
• Unsalted butter
• Sugar
• Extra large eggs
• Flaked coconut
• Walnuts
• Powdered sugar
• Banana
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Cake: preheat oven to 350
2. Butter a 12 cup tube pan and set aside
3. Sift flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg
4. Set aside
5. Beat butter until light
6. Slowly beat in sugar and beat until it is light and fluffy, about 5 minutes
7. At low speed, beat in eggs, 2 at a time, just until blended
8. Beat in the flour mixture and banana mixture alternately, beginning and ending with flour
9. Beat just until blended
10. Stir in coconut and nuts by hand, spread in pan smoothing top
11. Bake until cake is golden brown and begins to pull away from sides of pan, about 60 to 70 minutes
12. Test in center
13. Loosely cover with foil if cake starts to become too brown
14. Remove from oven
15. Let cool on wire rack 10 minutes in pan
16. Carefully invert cake onto cake plate
17. Brush with the 1 / 4 cup liqueur
18. Let cool
19. Glaze with either of the glazes or dust with powdered sugar
20. Glaze: combine all ingredients until well blended
21. When the cake is lukewarm, spoon over top
22. Let cake cool completely<|endoftext|>
