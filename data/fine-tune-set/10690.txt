Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Aunt B S Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Rotini noodles
• Deli turkey
• Deli ham
• Pepperoni
• Green peppers
• Cucumber
• Purple onion
• Tomatoes
• Celery
• Black olives
• Broccoli floret
• White processed cheese
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Provolone cheese
• Italian salad dressing mix

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to directions, drain and cool
2. Add all other ingredients, making sure that it is all diced small, except dressing
3. Mix italian dressing according to directions
4. Pour over entire salad, mix well
5. Serve chilled<|endoftext|>
