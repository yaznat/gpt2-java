Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Olive oil
• Sodium-free seasoning

Steps:
1. Cut taters into fry shape and rinse in a bowl of water
2. Put olive oil into bowl of washed taters and mix around
3. Kids do this with their hands so do i
4. Place on cookie sheet and sprinkle seasonings over top of fries
5. Mix them up real well so they all have some coating of seasoning on them
6. Bake for 45 min at 350 degrees
7. Times vary depending on fry size, steak fries longer, skinny fries shorter time
8. Check after 30 min to guage length
9. Options: garlic powder, oregano, basil, steak seasoning, old bay, cajun powder seasoning, chili powder
