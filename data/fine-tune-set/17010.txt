Ingredients:
• Large shrimp
• Beer
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Chipotle chiles in adobo
• Adobo sauce
• Light mayonnaise
• Chili sauce
• Red onions
• Orange zest
• Orange juice
• Fresh cilantro
• Tortilla chips
• Iceberg lettuce
• Avocado
• Cilantro leaf

Steps:
1. Place shrimp in large, deep skillet in a single layer
2. Add beer, garlic and salt
3. Turn heat to medium
4. Slowly poach shrimp 10 minute, turning shrimp after 5 minutes, until just cooked through
5. Remove shrimp with slotted spoon to bowl and cool
6. Bring beer and garlic to a boil
7. Boil 8 minutes, or until reduced to 3 tablespoons, skimming surface once or twice
8. Strain into a bowl
9. Add garlic and mash
10. Cool
11. Add chiles, adobo sauce, mayonnaise, chili sauce, red onion, orange zest and juice, and chopped cilantro to beer mixture
12. Whisk dressing until blended
13. Cut shrimp into 2 or 3 pieces each and add to dressing
14. Toss to coat
15. To serve top tortilla chips with shredded lettuce and shrimp mixture
16. Garnish with diced avocado and a cilantro leaf<|endoftext|>
