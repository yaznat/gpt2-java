Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Carrot
• Onion
• Lean stewing beef
• Butter
• Flour
• Water
• Salt
• Dried parsley flakes
• Celery seed
• Dried thyme
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place potatoes, carrots and onions in a 5 quart slow cooker
2. In a large skillet, brown beef in butter
3. Transfer beef to slow cooker with a slotted spoon
4. Stir flour into the pan drippings until blended
5. Cook and stir until browned
6. Gradually add water
7. Bring to a boil and cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened
8. Add salt, parsley, celery seed, thyme and pepper
9. Pour over beef and mix all ingredients together
10. Cover and cook on low for 8-9 hours or until vegetables and meat are tender<|endoftext|>
