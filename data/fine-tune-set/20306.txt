What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Chicken Cashew Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Reduced-sodium fat-free chicken broth
• Oyster sauce
• Cornstarch
• Honey
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Salt
• Oil
• Green onion
• Onion
• Red bell pepper
• Carrot
• Mixed mushrooms
• Snow peas
• Chicken thigh
• Pineapple chunks in juice
• Cashews
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Cooked long-grain rice

Steps:
1. Combine first 7 ingredients in a small bowl
2. Set aside
3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a stir-fry pan or wok over medium-high heat
4. Add 1 / 2 cup green onions and onion wedges
5. Stir-fry 1 minute
6. Add bell pepper and carrot
7. Stir-fry 2 minutes
8. Add mushrooms and peas
9. Stir-fry 2 minutes
10. Remove vegetable mixture from pan
11. Keep warm
12. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in pan over medium-high heat
13. Add chicken
14. Stir-fry 5 minutes
15. Add broth mixture, vegetable mixture, pineapple, cashews, and crushed red pepper
16. Bring to a boil, and cook 1 minute or until thick
17. Stir in 1 / 2 cup green onions
18. Serve with rice<|endoftext|>
