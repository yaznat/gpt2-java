What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Chili Baked Fries

Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Canola oil
• Chili powder
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Seasoning salt
• Pepper
• Dry mustard
• Cayenne

Steps:
1. Scrub potatoes
2. Leaving skins on, cut each potato lengthwise into 8 wedges
3. Rinse under cold water to remove starch
4. Pat dry
5. In very large bowl, mix all remaining ingredients
6. Add potatoes and toss to coat
7. Place on rimmed baking sheet
8. Bake in preheated 450f degree oven for about 45-50 minutes or until crisp and browned
9. Carefully toss twice during cooking<|endoftext|>
