Ingredients:
• Chicken breast
• Ground ginger
• Curry powder
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Salt
• Chicken broth
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Brown chicken in non-stick skillet
2. Add spice mixture
3. Add chicken broth
4. Bring to boil
5. Reduce to low heat, and simmer 45 minutes to an hour
6. Mix cornstarch slurry, and add to mixture
7. It should thicken into a nice gravy
8. Serve over rice or egg noodles<|endoftext|>
