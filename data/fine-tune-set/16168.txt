What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Fresh Fig Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Flour tortillas
• Cheese
• Green figs
• Red onions
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Brush a large baking sheet with olive oil
3. Place the tortillas on the baking sheet
4. Spread cup of the cheese on the bottom half of each tortilla
5. Add a layer of figs
6. Sprinkle each with about tablespoons red onion and tablespoon cilantro
7. Then top with cup of the remaining cheese
8. Fold the tortilla over to make half-circles
9. Press down lightly
10. Bake about 8 minutes or until the tortillas are golden brown on the bottom
11. Using a wide spatula, carefully turn the tortillas over
12. Bake another 3 minutes or until the bottoms are golden
13. Remove the baking sheet from the oven and let cool slightly
14. Transfer the quesadillas to a cutting board
15. Cut each one into 4 wedges
16. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
