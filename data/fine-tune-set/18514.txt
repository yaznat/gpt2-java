Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Great Grandmother Blanche S Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Using whisk or whisk attachment for mixer, beat egg whites until they form stiff, white peaks and set aside
2. Shift together all dry ingredients
3. In separate bowl beat egg yolks and add milk
4. Using paddle attachment for mix or by hand, mix together egg whites, dry ingredients and yolk / milk mixture
5. Once completely mixed, cook in waffle iron per manufacturers directions<|endoftext|>
