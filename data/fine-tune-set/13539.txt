Ingredients:
• Dried thyme
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Boneless chuck roast
• Cooking spray
• Onion
• Reduced-sodium fat-free beef broth
• Bay leaves
• Guinness stout
• Frozen pearl onions
• Button mushrooms
• Water
• All-purpose flour
• Whole berry cranberry sauce
• Cooked egg noodles
• Fresh thyme

Steps:
1. Combine first 3 ingredients in a small bowl
2. Sprinkle over beef
3. Heat a dutch oven over medium-high heat
4. Coat pan with cooking spray
5. Add beef to pan
6. Cook 6 minutes, turning to brown on all sides
7. Add chopped onion, broth, bay leaves, and stout
8. Bring to a boil
9. Cover, reduce heat, and simmer 2 hours or until beef is tender, stirring occasionally
10. Stir in pearl onions and mushrooms
11. Cook, covered, 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
12. Combine 1 / 4 cup water and flour in a small bowl
13. Add flour mixture and cranberry sauce to pan
14. Cook 5 minutes
15. Discard bay leaves
16. Serve with noodles
17. Garnish with fresh thyme, if desired<|endoftext|>
