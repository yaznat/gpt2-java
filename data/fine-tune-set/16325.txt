Ingredients:
• Jalapenos
• Cream cheese
• Bacon
• Toothpick

Steps:
1. Cut off the stem of each jalapeno and de-seed it carefully, using caution not to tear the pepper
2. It is easiest to de-seed it with a potato peeler
3. To make them cooler you can set them in ice water over night
4. Carefully fill each pepper with cream cheese
5. Wrap each pepper with a slice of bacon making sure to cover the cream cheese end
6. Close the end of bacon with a toothpick inserting it all the way through the pepper
7. Fry in a electric skillet until bacon is done<|endoftext|>
