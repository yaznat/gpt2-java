Ingredients:
• Dry yeast
• Warm water
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Sour cream
• Butter
• Salt
• All-purpose flour
• Flaked coconut
• Orange zest
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. In bowl soften yeast in water
2. Stir in eggs, just 1 / 4 cup sugar, the sour cream, and just 6 tbs
3. Butter, and salt
4. Stir in 2 3 / 4 cups flour
5. On floured surface knead enough flour in to make a moderately soft dough
6. Knead til smooth and elastic, about five minutes
7. Place in greased bowl, turn
8. Cover and let rise in a warm place about 1 / 12 hours or until double
9. Punch down turn onto floured surface
10. Divide in half
11. Shape each half into a ball
12. Cover and let rest ten minutes
13. Roll each half into a 12 inch circle
14. Brush each with 1 tbs of remaining butter
15. Combine remaining sugar, just 3 / 4 cup of coconut, and the orange peel
16. Sprinkle over dough
17. Cut each circle into 12 triangles
18. Roll up each piece starting at wide end
19. Arrange rolls, like a pinwheel in two greased 9" round pans
20. Cover
21. Let rise again til double bake in 350 oven for 25-30 minutes
22. Immediately remove from pans and put on wire racks, with waxed paper under
23. Glaze in saucepan combine sugar, the sour cream, orange juice, and butter
24. Stir over medium heat until the sugar dissolves
