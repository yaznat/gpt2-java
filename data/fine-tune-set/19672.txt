Give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Amazing Bacon Cheese Fries

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Vegetable oil
• Apple-smoked bacon
• Butter
• Onions
• Garlic
• All-purpose flour
• Chili-garlic sauce
• Dijon mustard
• Heavy cream
• Parmigiano-reggiano cheese
• Sharp white cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Cut potatoes lengthwise in 1 / 4-inch squares
2. Soak cut potatoes in water for 1 hour to remove starch
3. Heat oil to 325 degrees f and blanch potatoes for 5 minutes until very soft but not brown
4. Remove from oil
5. Drain on paper towels
6. In a medium saute pan over medium heat, cook bacon until just crisp, taking care not to overcook
7. Drain on paper towels
8. In a 2 quart saucepan, melt butter over medium heat
9. Add onion and cook about 2 minutes
10. Add garlic and flour, whisk until smooth
11. Cook 2 minutes more
12. Add chili sauce and mustard, and slowly whisk in heavy cream
13. When mixture begins to simmer, add cheeses and remove from heat
14. Taste sauce for seasoning
15. If sauce is too thick, thin with more cream
16. If too thin, thicken with a little more cheese
17. Increase oil temperature to 375 degrees f
18. Fry potatoes a second time until crisp and golden brown
19. Remove from oil and drain again, season with salt immediately
20. On a large plate, arrange fries in the center, drizzle with sauce, top with crumbled bacon
21. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
