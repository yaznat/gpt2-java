Ingredients:
• Nonfat plain yogurt
• Bananas
• Unsweetened pineapple juice
• Ice cubes
• Golden brown sugar

Steps:
1. Place yogurt in a small bowl, cover and freeze at least 6 hours or up to 2 days
2. Transfer frozen yogurt to blender
3. Add bananas, pineapple juice, ice cubes and brown sugar and blend until smooth
4. Divide among 4 glasses<|endoftext|>
