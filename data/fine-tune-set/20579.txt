What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Baked Cheese Stuffing Casserole

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Water
• Stove top stuffing mix
• Eggs
• Broccoli
• Mozzarella cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. In a pot, bring water to a boil
3. Add margarine
4. Stir in stuffing mix
5. Cover and remove from heat
6. Let stand for 5 minutes
7. Mix cheeses together
8. Mix eggs, broccoli and 1 cup of cheese mixture
9. Add to stuffing, mixing well
10. Spoon into 2 quart baking dish
11. Sprinkle with remaining cheese
12. Bake 20 minutes or until top is lightly browned and cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
