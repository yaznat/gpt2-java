Ingredients:
• Apples
• Pecans
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Grease or line 12 muffin cups
3. Mix the 2 tablespoons of sugar and 1 1 / 2 teaspoons cinnamon together for topping and set aside
4. Mix flour, sugar, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda and salt together in a large bowl
5. Whisk eggs, then add milk and oil - mix
6. Stir this into dry ingredients until just mixed
7. Fold in the apples and pecans
8. Spoon batter into muffin cups & sprinkle with the cinnamon sugar
9. Bake @ 375 for 20-25 minutes<|endoftext|>
