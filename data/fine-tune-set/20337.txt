Give me a stir fry recipe.<|endoftext|>Colourful Chicken Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Chicken breasts
• Red pepper
• Orange bell pepper
• Broccoli
• Sweet onion
• Garlic powder
• Basil
• Paprika
• Chili powder
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Salt
• Pepper
• Long grain brown rice

Steps:
1. In a frying pan, add the vegetable oil and put the heat on low
2. Slice chicken breasts into strips
3. Add to pan
4. Chop up all of your peppers, broccoli and onion and add to pan
5. Take all of your spices and add to the mix
6. Boil rice
7. Simmer the vegetables and chicken on low heat for 45 minutes or until rice is cooked, stirring occasionally, making sure it's not burning
8. Pour the vegetables and chicken over rice and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
