Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• All-purpose flour
• Ground cumin
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt
• Beer
• Tilapia fillets
• Flour tortillas
• Cucumber
• Apple cider vinegar
• Sugar
• Water
• Red onion
• Vidalia onion
• English cucumber
• Habanero pepper
• Roma tomato
• Cilantro leaves

Steps:
1. Fill a large pot with oil halfway up the pot
2. Heat oil to 350 degrees f
3. Preheat grill
4. In a medium bowl, mix together 1 cup flour, cumin, cayenne, and salt
5. Slowly whisk in beer until thick, but not runny
6. Dredge fish in remaining flour first, then dunk in the batter
7. Fry in batches until golden brown, about 5 minutes
8. Drain on paper-toweled lined plate
9. Meanwhile, grill tortillas on grill
10. Serve fish in tortillas topped with quick-pickled onion and cucumber
11. Quick-pickled onion and cucumber:
12. In a medium saucepan, bring to a boil the vinegar, sugar, water, and salt
13. Add onions and cucumber and continue to cook a few minutes more
14. Remove from heat, add chile, and transfer to a bowl
15. Let cool slightly then chill covered, for at least 1 hour
16. Toss in the tomato and cilantro, 5 minutes before serving
17. Yields: 6 cups<|endoftext|>
