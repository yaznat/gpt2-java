Give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>Anti Dracula Garlicky Beans

Ingredients:
• White beans
• Oil
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Pepper
• Onion
• Tomato paste

Steps:
1. Blend beans, oil and garlic till it becomes a paste
2. Season with salt and pepper
3. To make the garnish, fry the onion till gets brown and add the tomato paste
4. Let it fry for 2 more minutes and add on top of the mashed beans
5. Serve it with sourdough bread
6. Goes with most breads, except toast<|endoftext|>
