What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Blackened Catfish A La Mer

Ingredients:
• Catfish fillets
• Margarine
• Cajun seasoning
• Medium shrimp
• Blue crab meat
• Heavy whipping cream
• Parmesan cheese
• Green onions
• Garlic
• Unsalted butter
• Seafood stock

Steps:
1. Place the catfish filets and the melted margarine in a flat dish, saturating the catfish completely
2. Sprinkle cajun seasoning onto one side of the catfish filets, completely covering the filets with a coat of seasoning
3. Place filets seasoning side down on a white hot cast iron skillet or griddle until edges of filets are white
4. Sprinkle the same amount of seasoning on the exposed side of the filets as you did in the previous process and turn with spatula
5. Cook until filet meat is completely cooked but moist, with a blackened exterior coat of spices
6. Be cautious not to over cook as the blackening skillet is extremely hot
7. Place the butter, garlic, green onions, shrimp and crab into a two quart saucepan
8. Gently saut until lightly browned
9. Add the seafood stock
10. Cook until shrimp are just pink
11. Lower heat and add heavy whipping cream
12. Cook while stirring constantly until a gentle boil
13. Add more seafood seasoning to season to taste, mild or spicy
14. Add parmesan cheese to thicken
15. Remove from heat
16. To serve, place a catfish filet on the center of the plate, completely covering it with the sauce
17. If desired, the catfish can be served over a bed of dirty or white rice<|endoftext|>
