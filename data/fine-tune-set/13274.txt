Please generate a chicken recipe.<|endoftext|>5 Points Plus Pineapple And Honey Glazed Chicken

Ingredients:
• Crushed pineapple in juice
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Lime juice
• Ginger
• Shallot
• Jalapeno pepper
• Chicken
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Combine pineapple, soy, honey, lime, ginger, shallot, and jalapeno in large bag
2. Add chicken and marinade at least 4 hours
3. Preheat oven to 375f
4. Place rack in large roasting pan
5. Remove chicken from marinade and place on rack
6. Reserve the marinade
7. Bake chicken until browned, about 45 minute
8. Baste with marinade twice during first 25 min of cooking
9. Stir cornstarch and water in small bowl until smooth
10. Transfer chicken to platter
11. Keep warm
12. Remove rack from roasting pan
13. Use spoon to skim fat off pan juices
14. Por pan juices into small saucepan on medium heat
15. Cook until juices reduce by half
16. Add cornstarch mixture and cook, stirring constantly, until bubbles and thickens
17. Stir in parsley
18. Serve chicken with the sauce
