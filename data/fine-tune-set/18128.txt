Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Apples
• Lemon juice
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Mix flour, 1 tbsp sugar, baking powder and baking soda and salt in a bowl
2. Set aside
3. Whisk buttermilk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla
4. Mix into dry ingredients
5. Place in fridge for 125 minutes
6. Peel, core and cut the apples into small wedges
7. Sprinkle with lemon juice
8. Mix 1 / 4 cup sugar and cinnamon
9. Sprinkle over apples
10. Place 3tbsp butter in a skiller
11. Add apples and sugar / cinnamon mixture
12. Cook until sugar mixture is syrupy
13. Place apples in bottom of a round cake pan
14. Pour any remaining syrup over apples
15. Spoon batter over
16. Bake 20-30 mins at 350
17. Serve with msple syrup<|endoftext|>
