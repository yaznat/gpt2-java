Ingredients:
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cream of celery soup
• Tater tots
• Ground beef
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef in a bowl mix together all the soup and the browned ground beef
2. Spread the mixture evenly in a casserole dish grate cheese over the mixture evenly
3. Line the entire casserole dish with tator tots
4. Grate cheese over the tator tots
5. Bake at 375 until the tator tots are completely cooked<|endoftext|>
