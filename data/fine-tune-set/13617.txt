What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Pulled Beef

Ingredients:
• Boneless sirloin tip roast
• Barbecue sauce
• Beer
• Spanish onion
• Red wine vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Coarse sea salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Completely thaw roast
2. Trim excess fat if desired
3. Place in crock pot
4. In large measuring cup / medium bowl: mix together barbecue sauce
5. Beer
6. Vinegar
7. Worcestershire sauce and mixed spices
8. Cover roast with onions
9. Pour barbecue sauce mixture over roast
10. Cover and cook on high 4-6 hours until tender, falling apart
11. Remove roast from crock pot and shred
12. Return meat to crock pot and coat meat evenly with sauce
13. Serve over mashed potatoes or on hamburger / kaiser buns, or bread of your choice<|endoftext|>
