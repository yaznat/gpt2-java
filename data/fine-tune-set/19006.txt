What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Golden Pumpkin Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• Pumpkin
• Butter
• Eggs
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Milk
• Pie shell

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Beat eggs, then add sugar and flour
3. Add pumpkin, butter, salt and milk
4. Pour into a 9-inch unbaked pie crust
5. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes<|endoftext|>
