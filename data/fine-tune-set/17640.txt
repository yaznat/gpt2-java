What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Peach Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• Almond extract
• Fresh peaches
• Almonds

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine flour, sugar, salt and baking soda
2. In another bowl, beat eggs, oil and extracts
3. Stir into dry ingredients just until moistened
4. Fold in peaches and almonds
5. Fill greased or paper-lined muffin cups three-fourths full
6. Bake at 375 for 20-25 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean
7. Cool in pan for 10 minutes before removing to a wire rack<|endoftext|>
