Ingredients:
• Dark molasses
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Ground ginger
• Fresh ground pepper
• Red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Mix all of the marinade ingredients together
2. Combine with selected meat and marinade for 2 to 24 hours
3. This is enough marinade for 1 to 1 1 / 2 pounds of meat
4. Remove meat from marinade, draining excess liquid
5. Cook as desired
6. Grilling or broiling suggested<|endoftext|>
