Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Butter
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Chicken broth
• Cider vinegar
• Sugar
• Salt
• Dried thyme leaves
• Mushroom
• New potatoes
• Carrot
• Celery
• Half-and-half
• Flour
• Light sour cream

Steps:
1. Cut pork tenderloins into 1-inch cubes
2. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a 5-quart dutch oven
3. Stir in pork, onions and garlic
4. Cook until pork is browned
5. Stir in broth, vinegar, sugar, seasonings and vegetables
6. Bring to a boil
7. Cover
8. Reduce heat and simmer 30 minutes or until meat and vegetables are tender
9. In a medium bowl, slowly add light cream to flour
10. Mixing until well blended
11. Stir into pork mixture
12. Heat until thickened, stirring constantly
13. Just before serving, stir in sour cream
14. Heat until warm<|endoftext|>
