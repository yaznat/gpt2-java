Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Banana
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Shortening
• Sour milk
• Baking soda
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Milk
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Grease and flour the bottom of a 9x13 cake pan
2. Blend together all cake ingredients and pour into prepared pan
3. Bake at 350 degrees for 30-35 min
4. , until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
5. Let cool
6. Meanwhile, prepare icing
7. Place all icing ingredients into medium-sized bowl
8. Mix with electric mixer on high speed until icing becomes fluffy and thick enough to spread spread on top of cooled cake
9. Refrigerate entire cake for a few hours before serving for best taste, and serve cold<|endoftext|>
