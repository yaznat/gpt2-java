Ingredients:
• Boneless pork chops
• Barbecue sauce
• Bacon
• Fresh rosemary
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. On work surface layer slices of bacon side by side
3. Place each chop on 2 pieces of bacon
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Slather the chops with barbeque sauce
6. Flip the chops and slather the other side as well, until barbeque sauce is used up
7. Lay a sprig of rosemary on each chop
8. Wrap bacon slices tightly around each chop
9. Prepare broiler pan, by spraying surface with cooking spray
10. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until a meat thermometer inserted in thickest part of the meat should register about 150f
11. Turn oven to broil and broil on each side until bacon is crisp
12. Let chops rest for a few minutes
13. Remove rosemary sprigs before serving
14. If desired, serve with additional warmed barbeque sauce<|endoftext|>
