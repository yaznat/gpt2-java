Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Onion
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Half-and-half cream
• Cheddar cheese
• Garlic clove

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Brown hamburger in skillet with onion
3. Drain well of any excess grease
4. Remove and mix in bowl with bacon pieces, breaking up any larger clumps with a fork or pastry mixer until you have a fine mix and press into the bottom of a deep-dish pie pan
5. Set aside
6. Combine remaining ingredients in mixer bowl and whip well
7. Pour mixture over beef "crust" and bake 40-45 minutes until top is browned and "set"
8. Cool and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
