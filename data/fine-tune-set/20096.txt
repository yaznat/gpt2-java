Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Tuna Cheese And Ketchup Omelette

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Canned tuna
• Hard cheese
• Tomato ketchup
• Margarine

Steps:
1. In a small bowl mix the egg well with a dash of water or milk
2. Add salt and pepper
3. Melt the fat in small / medium non- stick frying pan
4. When it is hot-pour egg in and fry first side
5. Now work fast: after turning omlette over, sprinkle the tuna over half the area, squirt some ketchup over the whole area then place slice of cheese over the tuna
6. Fold "empty" half over the tuna+cheese and press lightly
7. You can flip it over again if you like it well done
8. Remove to plate and enjoy with a nice green salad and fresh bread<|endoftext|>
