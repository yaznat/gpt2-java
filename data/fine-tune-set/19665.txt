Please give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Skinny Fries

Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Oil
• Cajun seasoning
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Scrub the potatoes and cut lengthwise into 3 equal slices
2. Cut each slice into thirds or fourths
3. As the potatoes are cut, place them in a dish of ice water
4. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
5. When oven is hot, drain off the water, remove potatoes and pat dry with a paper towel
6. Return the dried potatoes to the bowl, sprinkle with seasoning and drizzle with oil
7. Evenly distribute the oil and seasoning with your hands
8. Place on a cookie sheet or jelly roll pan that has been sprayed with non-stick cooking spray and bake until golden brown and tender
9. Approximately 30 minutes, turning frequently
10. Sprinkle with additional seasoning if desired or salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
