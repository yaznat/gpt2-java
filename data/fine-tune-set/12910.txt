Ingredients:
• Venison steak
• Dry red wine
• Cane syrup
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Creole seasoning
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Combine the wine and cane syrup in a small sauce pan, simmer gently until reduced by two thirds -- about 18 minutes
2. Remove from heat and add pepper
3. Pat the venison with a kitchen towel sprinkle creole seasoning on both sides
4. Heat a large cast-iron skillet over medium high heat until it is very hot and just smoking, 4 to 5 minutes
5. Add the oil, swirling to coat the bottom of the skillet
6. Once its hot sear the steak until dark brown, 3-4 minutes
7. Turn the meat over, reduce heat to medium, and sear for 3-4 minutes for medium rare
8. Transfer the steaks to a platter and let rest for 5-10 minutes
9. Serve them drizzled with the sauce, and your choice of sides
10. Serves 4<|endoftext|>
