What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Glazed Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Brown sugar
• Barbecue sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 350
2. Place pork chops in a 9"x13" baking pan coated with cooking spray
3. In a smalll bowl, combine the remaining ingredients and then spoon over the chops
4. Bake, uncovered, at 350 for 20 to 25 minutes or until a meat thermometer reads 160<|endoftext|>
