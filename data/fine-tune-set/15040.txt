Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Simplest Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Long grain white rice
• Vegetable oil
• Ham
• Onion
• Eggs
• Frozen peas
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a large, deep skillet, and cook the ham and onion over low heat, stirring occasionally, for 8 minutes, or until the onion is tender
2. Add the hot rice to the pan, stirring until the rice and ham are thoroughly combined
3. Add the eggs and stir gently, combining everything, until you start to see little bits of scrambled egg forming in the pan
4. Now stir in the peas, and then stir in the soy sauce, a few drops at a time
5. Keep stirring until everything is nicely mixed
6. Youre done!<|endoftext|>
