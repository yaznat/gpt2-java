Give me a pork recipe.<|endoftext|>Asian Glazed Grilled Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients:
• Hoisin sauce
• Dijon mustard
• Honey
• Toasted sesame oil
• Soy sauce
• Gingerroot
• Garlic
• Lemon zest
• Pork tenderloin

Steps:
1. Combine all glaze ingredients in a small bowl and mix well
2. Set aside
3. Preheat grill
4. Grill pork for about 16 minutes, turning occasionally
5. Brush generously with glaze during last 5 minutes of cooking time
6. The more you baste, the better the taste
7. Place pork on cutting board, cover loosely with foil, and let rest for 5 minutes
8. Cut into thin slices<|endoftext|>
