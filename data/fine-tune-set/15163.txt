Ingredients:
• French bread
• Lean ground beef
• Tomato paste
• White sugar
• Stuffed olives
• Fresh parmesan cheese
• Fresh chives
• Salt
• Dried oregano
• Pepper
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. For the oven:
2. Cut the loaf of french bread in half lengthwise
3. Place both halves on a greased baking sheet
4. Combine uncooked beef, tomato paste, sugar, olives, parmesan cheese, chives, salt, oregano and pepper
5. Spread mixture to the edges of the cut surfaces of the bread
6. Bake uncovered in preheated 350 degree fahrenheit oven for 25 minutes or until meat is well done
7. Top with mozzarella or cheddar cheese
8. Return to oven for 5 minutes or until cheese melts
9. Cut into thick slices to serve
10. For the barbecue:
11. Precook the hamburger
