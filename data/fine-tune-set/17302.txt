Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Onions
• Salt
• Pepper
• Sugar
• Balsamic vinegar
• Filet mignon
• Bacon
• Black peppercorns

Steps:
1. Sauce
2. In a large frying pan heat oil over high heat
3. Add onions and lower the heat to medium
4. Add salt& pepper mix thoroughly
5. Sweat onions down for about 15 minutes stirring often
6. You want the onions very soft
7. Add sugar and reduce heat to low
8. Mix well& cook 5-10 minutes or until most of liquid is absorbed
9. Add balsamic vinegar
10. Mix well cook for 20-40 minutes until sauce becomes a dark rich syrup
11. Steak
12. Pre-heat ove to 450 degrees
13. Heat oil in a oven proof skillet
14. Wrap a piece of bacon around each filet, secure with toothpick if needed
15. Sprinkle steaks with crushed peppercorns
16. Sear each side of filets about 2 minutes
17. Place skillet in oven and bake 10 minutes
18. Steakes will be medium rare
19. Remove filets from skillet
20. Allow to rest 1-2 minutes pour warm sauce over top& serve<|endoftext|>
