What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Leftover Rice Pudding

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Milk
• Sugar
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Vanilla
• Raisins
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan
2. Heat over medium-high and stir often until milk and rice thicken, about 20 minutes or so
3. Put into dessert cups and chill until ready to eat<|endoftext|>
