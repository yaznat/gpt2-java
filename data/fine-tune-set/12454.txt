Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Akwa Traditional Yemeni Oxtail Stew

Ingredients:
• Oxtails
• Black peppercorns
• Caraway seeds
• Cardamom seed
• Saffron thread
• Ground fenugreek
• Turmeric
• Salt
• Tomatoes
• White pearl onions
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Rinse the oxtail, then place in a large casserole & cover with water
2. Bring to a gentle boil over a medium heat
3. Reduce the heat to low & skim the surface of any foam until there is very little left
4. Crush the peppercorns, caraway seeds & saffron together in a spice mill / coffee grinder or mortar & pestle
5. Stir in the fenugreek, turmeric & salt
6. Add the tomatoes, onions, garlic & spice mix to the oxtail
7. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to as nlow as possible - you may find a diffuser useful for this
8. Cover tightly & keep on a gentle simmer for 3 hours
9. Uncover & continue to cook until the meat is incredibly tender, for another 5-6 hours - you will see the meat falling off the bone when it hits this point and it is ready to serve!
10. To serve it in the traditional manner, serve with a rice pilaf
11. You could also eat this with pleny of bread & salad<|endoftext|>
