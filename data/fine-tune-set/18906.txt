Ingredients:
• Cooked brown rice
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Dark raisin
• Coconut
• Dark rum
• Vanilla
• Milk
• Sour cream
• Eggs
• Shredded coconut

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f degrees
2. Combine in a bowl, cooked rice, brown sugar, salt, raisins, fine coconut, dark rum and vanilla
3. Stir to mix well
4. Let stand for 30 minutes, mixing occasionally
5. Meanwhile, whisk together, milk, sour cream and eggs, when the rice is ready
6. Pour milk mix over the rice mix
7. Mix well
8. Butter a 4 to 6 cup casserole, pour rice / milk mix into casserole and stir well to mix
9. Bake custard in oven for 35 to 45 minutes, depending on how dry you want it
10. Stir once after 15 minutes
11. Spread topping and sprinkle with shredded coconut
12. Topping:
13. Whisk together sour cream, rum and brown sugar
14. Spread over the top of the hot rice custard, sprinkle shredded coconut evenly over
15. Set casserole about 6 inches under the broiler for about 2 minutes until the shredded coconut is golden
16. Watch closely that it doesn't burn
17. Serve warm or ice cold with a dollop of sour cream on top<|endoftext|>
