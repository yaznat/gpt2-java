Give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>Almond Butter Raisin Cookies Vegan Gluten Free

Ingredients:
• Raisins
• Oat flour
• Rolled oats
• Ground flax seeds
• Baking powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Almond butter
• Water
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Soak raisins in warm water for 5 minutes, then discard soaking water
2. Stir dry ingredients together
3. In another bowl, mix wet ingredients together
4. Mix wet ingredients with dry ingredients until well combined
5. Grease a cookie sheet and spoon drop about 2 tbsp worth of cookie dough for each cookie
6. Bake at 350f for 10-12 minutes
7. Let cool 5 minutes before transfering to rack to cool another 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
