What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Fried Rice Zojirushi Rice Cooker

Ingredients:
• Uncooked rice
• Ham
• Cooked chicken
• Carrot
• Eggs
• Soy sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Fresh garlic
• Spring onions
• Chicken broth
• Peanut oil
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Rinse the rice in cold water
2. Change the water as needed until the water is hardly white
3. When you are done, add the rice to the bottom of the inside of the rice cooker
4. Mix the sausage, chicken, carrot, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, chicken-broth, peanut oil, the white part of the spring onions
5. Save the green part of the spring onions, the cilantro, and the eggs for later
6. Add the rice to the rice cooker
7. Pour in the mix from step 2
8. Do not mix the two
9. The rice will cook on the bottom, the mix will cook on the top
10. Cook the rice following the rice cooker's instructions
11. I set mine to white rice using the harder cook setting
12. It takes about 40 minutes with my rice cooker
13. When the rice is done cooking, add the rest of the spring onions, the cilantro, and the cooked eggs
14. Stir the rice to mix the ingredients and close the lid
15. Leave it in the "warm" setting for 10 minutes
16. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
