Ingredients:
• Beef tenderloin steaks
• Dry mustard
• Garlic powder
• Sage
• Dried thyme
• Salt
• Cumin
• Ground red pepper
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Combine rub ingredients
2. Rub evenly over steaks
3. Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
4. Coat pan with cooking spray
5. Add steaks to pan
6. Cook 4 minutes on each or until desired degree of doneness
7. Remove from heat and let stand 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
