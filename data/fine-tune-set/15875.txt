What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Cheesy Chicken Rice Burritos

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Ground chicken
• Salsa
• Hot taco sauce
• Cheese
• Mexican rice
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Prepare the rice as directed on the package then set aside to be used later
2. Heat the oil in a frying pan then cook the chicken until no longer pink
3. Mix the salsa, taco sauce, & cheese in with the chicken
4. Add half of the rice to the pan & save the rest as a side dish
5. Put between 3-4 tablespoons of the chicken mixture onto each tortilla & roll up
6. Lay them on a baking sheet & cook on 350 for 10 minutes
7. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
