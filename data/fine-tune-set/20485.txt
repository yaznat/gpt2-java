What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Artichoke Spinach Asiago Casserole

Ingredients:
• Artichoke hearts
• Fresh spinach
• Shallots
• Garlic
• Butter
• Flour
• Asiago cheese
• Milk
• Pine nuts

Steps:
1. In a casserole dish add artichoke hearts, spinach, shallots and garlic
2. In a pan melt the butter, then add the flour, whisk for about 2 minutes
3. Then add the milk and cheese, continue stirring,
4. When cheese is melted pour over artichokes
5. Add pine nuts on top of cheese
6. Bake 20 minutes at approx 350f<|endoftext|>
