Ingredients:
• Large shrimp
• Bacon
• Sweet onion
• Fresh jalapeno peppers
• Seafood seasoning
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Wash shrimp and set aside in a bowl, starting an assembly line
3. Cut bacon in half vertically, place on a plate
4. Cut and peel onion into wedges and separate, place in a bowl
5. Wash jalapenos, slice into thirds, lengthwise, removing seeds
6. Place in a bowl
7. Start assembly with a shrimp, a slice of jalapeno, and a slice of onion
8. Holding all in your hand, wrap bacon around and secure with a wooded toothpick
9. Repeat with each shrimp
10. Place on a baking sheet
11. Sprinkle with seasoning
12. Place in the oven and roast until bacon is slightly crisp
13. Roasting time may be adjusted<|endoftext|>
