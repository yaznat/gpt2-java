What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Angie S No Bake Peanut Butter Cookies

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Milk
• Margarine
• Peanut butter
• Quick oats
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Bring milk, sugar and margarine to a boil
2. Boil for 3 to 3 1 / 2 minute
3. Add peanut butter -- stir
4. Add vanilla and oats -- stir
5. Drop teaspoon full on wax paper or foil
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
