Give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>No Cook Summer Pasta

Ingredients:
• Basil
• Garlic cloves
• Balsamic vinegar
• Flat leaf parsley
• Lemon juice
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Tomatoes
• Fettuccine
• Romano cheese
• Pine nuts
• Butter

Steps:
1. Start by toasting your pine nuts
2. Use a dry skillett and put pine nuts on over low heat
3. Do not move away from the pan because they can burn easily
4. Keep moving them around until they are golden and their aroma becomes strong
5. Remove from heat and set aside
6. In a blender or bowl of food proccesor combine basil, garlic, vinegar, parsley, and lemon juice
7. With the machine running slowly add olive oil until thick
8. Add tomatoes and blend 5 seconds
9. Place sauce in a large bowl and set aside
10. Cook pasta according to directions on package, drain
11. Optional
12. Put back in hot pot and add a tablespoon of butter
13. Toss to coat
14. Add pasta to sauce
15. Add cheese and toss
16. Sprinkle pine nuts on top
17. Serve hot or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
