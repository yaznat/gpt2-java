Ingredients:
• Peppermint schnapps
• Creme de cacao
• Vanilla ice cream
• Milk
• Miniature candy canes

Steps:
1. Put the peppermint schnapps, creme de cacao, vanilla ice cream, and one candy cane in a blender and blend until thick and smooth
2. Add milk if the mixture is too thick to blend
3. Garnish each glass with a mini candy cane, or the straight end of a full sized candy cane<|endoftext|>
