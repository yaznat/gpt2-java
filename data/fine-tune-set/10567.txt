What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta And Lemon Parsley Sauce

Ingredients:
• Lemons
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh parsley
• Angel hair pasta
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Grate yellow zest from lemons, being careful not to include any bitter white pith
2. In a small bowl, combine lemon zest, garlic and parsley
3. Set gremolata aside
4. Cook angel hair pasta in a large pot of boiling salted water until tender but still firm, between 3 and 5 minutes
5. Meanwhile, in a large frying pan, warm olive oil over medium-low heat
6. Add gremolata and salt and pepper
7. Cook 1 minute, being careful not to burn garlic
8. Drain pasta and pour into pan
9. Toss to coat
10. Sprinkle parmesan cheese to top<|endoftext|>
