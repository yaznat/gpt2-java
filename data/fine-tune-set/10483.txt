What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Applesauce
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Clove
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Cream butter and sugar together
2. Add eggs and applesauce, mix well
3. Mix baking powder, soda, salt, cinnamon, cloves and vanilla
4. Add to creamed mixture
5. Blend in flour
6. Add chocolate chips
7. Can also add 3 / 4c chopped nuts if desired
8. Drop by teaspoons onto greased cookie sheet
9. Bake at 400 degrees for 8-10 minutes, until lightly browned<|endoftext|>
