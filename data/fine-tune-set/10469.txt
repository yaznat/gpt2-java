Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Crisp Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Light brown sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Old fashioned oats
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Water
• Apple pie filling
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Line at least 3 cookie sheets with parchment paper
3. Cookies: cream together the butter and brown sugar until well blended
4. Add vanilla
5. Combine the oats, flour, baking soda, and salt
6. Add alternately to the butter mixture with water stirring with a spoon
7. Mix well after each addition
8. Add additional water, if necessary
9. Reserve 1 cup of the mixture for the topping
10. Shape remaining dough into 1" balls
11. Place 2" apart on the cookie sheets
12. Flatten each cookie to an 1 / 8" thickness with the bottom of a floured glass
13. Smooth edges
14. Bake for 5-7 minutes, or until light brown around the edges and firm to the touch
15. Do not overbake
16. Remove from oven
17. Cool on cookie sheets about 5 minutes
18. For the topping, place 1 teaspoons
19. Fie filling in the center of each cookie
20. Spread carefully to cover
21. Combine the 1 cup reserved crumbs and nuts in a small bowl
22. Toss to blend
23. Sprinkle over filling
24. Return to oven and bake 5 minutes more, or until topping is light brown
25. Do not overbake
26. Cool 2 minutes on cookie sheets, then transfer cookies to wire racks and cool completely
27. If desired, sprinkle cookies with powdered sugar or cinnamon-sugar<|endoftext|>
