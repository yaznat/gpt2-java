Can you give me a curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Curry And Noodles Microwave

Ingredients:
• Hokkien noodles
• Chicken breast
• Light coconut cream
• Thai curry paste
• Lemon juice
• Lemon rind
• Red capsicum
• Red onion
• Zucchini
• Snow peas
• Salt and pepper
• Chicken stock
• Bean sprouts
• Spring onion

Steps:
1. Prepare noodle by putting a bowl of boiling water to loosen
2. Slice chicken into strips
3. Place coconut cream, thai curry paste, lemon juice & rind into a microwave safe bowl and cook for 1 minute
4. Remove and add the
5. Chicken, capsicum, onion, zucchini, snow peas, chicken stock and seasoning to taste,
6. Cook in micowave for 8 minutes
7. When cooked add drained noodles and cook again for 1-2 minutes
8. Remove and garnish with bean shoots and spring onions to serve<|endoftext|>
