Ingredients:
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Oil
• Chicken breasts
• Apricots
• Malt vinegar
• Ginger
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Fettuccine

Steps:
1. Peel onion and chop finely
2. Crush, peel and chop garlic
3. Heat oil in a saucepan and saute onion and garlic for 5 minutes or until onion is transparent
4. Cut chicken breasts into thin strips
5. Add chicken strips to pan and stir fry 2 or 3 minutes, or until chicken is cooked
6. Drain apricots, leaving about 2 tbsp of the juice
7. Puree apricots in a food processor or blender
8. Add malt vinegar, ginger, cayenne pepper, salt and pepper
9. Mix to combine
10. Pour into the saucepan
11. Bring mixture to the boil
12. Cook fettucine in boiling salted water for 8 to 10 minutes, or until tender
13. Drain
14. Serve chicken over pasta<|endoftext|>
