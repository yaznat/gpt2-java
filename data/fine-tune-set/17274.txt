Ingredients:
• Asparagus
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• Lemon zest
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Wash asparagus and trim off bottom of stems by about 2 inches
2. In a large skillet, bring about 1 / 2-inch of water to a rapid boil
3. Season the water with salt
4. Add the asparagus and cook for 2-3 minutes or until bright green and just tender
5. Drain the asparagus and place it immediately in a bowl of ice water to shock it
6. This will help the asparagus to retain the bright green color
7. Drain
8. Add butter to the skillet to melt
9. Stir in the lemon zest and juice
10. Add the drained asparagus to the skillet
11. Toss to coat and heat
12. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
