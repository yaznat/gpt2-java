What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Carrot And Parsnip Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Sesame oil
• Vegetable oil
• Parsnip
• Carrots
• Sake
• Mirin
• Sugar
• Shoyu
• Almonds
• Green onions

Steps:
1. In a large wok, heat up both oils on high heat
2. Add the parsnips and carrots, lowering heat to medium and stir frying until light golden
3. Turn up the heat to high and add the sake, mirin, and pinch of sugar
4. Cook until liquid is absorbed
5. Add the shoyu and almonds and stir fry an additional 30 seconds to 1 minute, stirring constantly so almonds don't burn
6. Garnish with green onions or chives if desired
7. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
