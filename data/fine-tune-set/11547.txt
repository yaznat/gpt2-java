Give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>Real New York Style Cheesecake Supreme

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Lemon peel
• Butter
• Egg yolk
• Vanilla
• Cream cheese
• Salt
• Eggs
• Egg yolks
• Whipping cream

Steps:
1. Make crust:
2. Combine first three ingredients
3. Cut in butter until mixture is crumbly
4. Add egg yolk and vanilla
5. Blend thoroughly
6. Pat 1 / 3 of the dough on bottom of 9-inch springform pan
7. Bake in hot oven, 400f, about 8 minutes or until golden
8. Cool
9. Butter sides of pan, attach to bottom, and pat remaining dough on sides to height of 1 3 / 4 inches
10. Turn oven up to 450f place a 9x13 inch pan of hot water in bottom of oven, this helps to keep the custard creamy
11. Make filling:
12. Beat softened cream cheese until creamy
13. Add vanilla
14. Mix next 4 ingredients, , gradually blend in cheese
15. Add eggs and egg yolks one at a time, beating after each just to blend
16. Gently stir in whipping cream
17. Turn into crust-lined pan
18. Bake at 450f for 12 minutes
19. Reduce heat to 300f and continue baking for 1 hour
20. May need a little time longer because of water
21. Remove from oven
22. Cool
23. Loosen sides with spatula after 1 / 2 hour
24. Remove sides at end of 1 hour
25. This cheesecake is at it's best if refrigerated overnight<|endoftext|>
