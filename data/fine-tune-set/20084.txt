Give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>The Childerz Smoked Gouda And Bacon Omelette

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt and pepper
• Smoked gouda cheese

Steps:
1. Firstly, fry up your bacon until it's crispy, in a frying pan set on medium heat
2. Remove from pan
3. After it's cooled, crumple into smallish pieces and set aside
4. Dump most of the grease out of the pan, but reserve about a tbsp or so in which to cook the eggs
5. Crack the two eggs in a separate bowl and whisk with milk, salt, and pepper until a bit frothy
6. Pour into the pan
7. Top with the smoked gouda and bacon
8. Cook until the bottom of the omlette has become a light shade of brown, then attempt to flip one side of the omlette over, folding it in half
9. Continue frying until the eggs are cooked through and the cheese is nice and melted
10. Put it on a plate, and enjoy<|endoftext|>
