Ingredients:
• Active dry yeast
• Warm water
• Milk
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Butter
• Salt
• All-purpose flour
• Hazelnuts
• Brown sugar
• Raisins
• Ground cinnamon
• Confectioners' sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Lemon extract

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, dissolve yeast in water and milk
2. Add eggs, sugar, butter, salt and 3 cups flour
3. Beat until smooth
4. Add enough remaining flour to form a soft dough
5. Turn onto a floured surface
6. Knead until smooth and elastic, 6-8 minutes
7. Place in a grease bowl
8. Turn once to grease top
9. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled, about 1-1 / 2 hours
10. For filling, combine nuts, brown sugar, raisins and cinnamon
11. Set aside
12. Punch dough down and divide into fourths
13. Roll one portion into a 12-in
14. X 7-in
15. Rectangle
16. Brush with some butter
17. Sprinkle with 2 / 3 cup filling
18. Roll up, jelly roll-style, staring with a long side
19. Pinch to seal
20. Place on a greased baking sheet
21. Shape top to form a cane
22. Repeat with remaining dough, filling and butter
23. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 35 minutes
24. Bake at 350 for 25 minutes or until golden brown
25. Cool on wire racks
26. Combine the first five glaze ingredients
27. Drizzle stripes over loaves
28. Sprinkle with nuts
29. Yield: 4 loaves<|endoftext|>
