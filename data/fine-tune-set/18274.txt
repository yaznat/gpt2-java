What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Best Pancakes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Milk

Steps:
1. Pre-heat cast iron skillet on medium low or griddle to 325 degrees
2. Combine all dry ingredients
3. Add all wet ingredients to dry beating eggs before-hand
4. Us a spatula to gently combine dry and wet ingredients
5. Do not overmix! you should have lumps otherwise your pancakes will be flat and tough
6. Let batter rest for 15 minutes to 1 / 2 an hour
7. During this time your pan or griddle will be perfectly pre-heated
8. Use a 1 / 2 cup ladel to pour batter into your pre-heated cast iron pan or griddle
9. Flip and cook till golden brown and delicious!<|endoftext|>
