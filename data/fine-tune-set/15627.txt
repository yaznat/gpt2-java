Give me a taco recipe.<|endoftext|>Better Than Del Taco Chicken Soft Tacos

Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Limes
• Garlic cloves
• Taco seasoning mix
• Vegetable oil
• Brown sugar
• Buttermilk ranch dressing
• Sour cream
• Lime juice
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Iceberg lettuce
• Roma tomatoes
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Sauce:
2. For sauce combine ranch dressing, sour cream, lime juice, garlic powder and salt and whisk well
3. Refrigerate and store up to 3 weeks
4. Chicken:
5. Cut the chicken into 1 inch pieces
6. In a bowl, add lime juice, garlic, taco seasoning mix, 1 tablespoon of oil and brown sugar
7. Whisk well and add the cut up chicken
8. Cover and marinate for 4-6 hours
9. In a pan over medium heat, heat 1 tablespoon oil
10. Add chicken and cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and cooked through
11. Remove from heat and keep warm
12. Heat tortillas in oven or microwave
13. To assemble, place chicken in the middle of warmed tortilla, top with lettuce and tomato, drizzle sauce, and sprinkle cheese<|endoftext|>
