What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Heart Healthy Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Water
• Fat free chicken broth
• Long grain white rice
• Canola oil
• Onions
• Celery
• Green peppers
• Pecans
• Ground sage
• Water chestnut
• Nutmeg
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Bring water and chicken broth to boil in medium-size saucepan
2. Add rice and stir, cover and simmer for 20 minutes
3. Remove pan from heat, let stand, covered, for 5 minutes or until liquid is absorbed, reserve,
4. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet
5. Saute onion and celery over moderate heat for 3 minutes
6. Stir in green pepper, pecans, sage, chestnuts, nutmeg and pepper and cooked rice, fluff with fork before serving<|endoftext|>
