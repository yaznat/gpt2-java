Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Dijon mustard
• Scallion
• Lemon juice
• Gherkins
• Capers
• Fresh parsley
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Cooking oil
• Flour
• Egg
• Beer
• Cod fish fillets

Steps:
1. Combine all the tartar sauce ingredients in a bowl with a pinch each of salt and pepper
2. Set aside
3. Heat the oven to 200
4. Cover a cookie sheet with paper towels and top with a wire rack
5. Heat about 3" of oil to approximately 365 in a medium sized pot
6. Meanwhile, mix flour with 1 / 2 teaspoon salt
7. Whisk in egg
8. Slowly add the beer while whisking
9. Dip fish pieces in the batter and place on plate or the wire rack you will be using to drain the fried fish
10. I usually double dip in the batter if i have some left over once the batter dries on the awaiting fish
11. Place fish pieces, two at a time in the oil
12. Cook until the fish is done and the crust is lightly golden, about 4 minutes for 3 / 4-inch thick fillets
13. Remove fish with tongs and put on rack to drain
14. Sprinkle salt over the hot fish and put the baking sheet in the oven
15. Repeat in batches with the remaining fish
16. Serve with the tartar sauce<|endoftext|>
