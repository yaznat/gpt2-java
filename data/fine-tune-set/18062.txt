Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Butter
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Brownie mix
• Nuts

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine flour, baking powder, salt, sugar, and cocoa
2. Stir with a wire whisk until evenly distributed
3. Put in a large airtight container
4. Label with date and contents
5. Store in a cool, dry place
6. Use within 10 to 12 weeks
7. Makes about 15 cups brownie mix
8. One package makes an 8-inch pan of brownies or 16 brownies
9. ----------------------------------
10. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
11. Grease and flour an 8-inch-square pan
12. Combine melted butter or margerine, eggs, vanilla, and brownie mix
13. Beat until smooth
14. Stir in nuts
15. Pour into prepared pan
16. Bake 30 to 35 minutes, until edges separate from pan
17. Cut into 2-inch bars when cool
18. Makes 16 brownies<|endoftext|>
