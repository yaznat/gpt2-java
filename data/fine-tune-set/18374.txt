Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Better Homes And Gardens Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Cooking oil
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, stir together the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt
2. Make a well in the center of the flour mixture
3. In another bowl, beat the eggs, slightly
4. Stir in the milk, oil and the vanilla
5. Add the egg mixture all at once to the flour mixture
6. Stir until just moistened
7. Pour 1 to 1 1 / 4 cups batter onto the grids of a preheated, lightly greased waffle iron
8. Close the lid quickly and do not open until done
9. When done lift the waffles of the iron with a fork
10. Repeat the procedure with the remaining batter<|endoftext|>
