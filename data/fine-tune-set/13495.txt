What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Aromatic Beef Kofti

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Green chili pepper
• Prepared mustard
• Garam masala
• Salt
• Eggs
• Cilantro
• Dry methi leaves
• Oil

Steps:
1. Mix beef with onion, garlic and chili
2. Add mustard, garam masala, salt and eggs
3. Mix until thouroughly blended and bound together
4. Add the coriander and methi
5. Mix just to combine
6. Divide mixture in half and divide each half into 12 equal portions
7. Shape into small patties
8. Brush lightly with oil and grill about 2-3 minutes on each side or until browned and cooked through
9. Alternately put a small amount of oil in a frying pan and cook until browned<|endoftext|>
