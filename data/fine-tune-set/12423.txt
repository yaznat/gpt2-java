What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Andersen S Split Pea Soup Crock Pot Version Copycat

Ingredients:
• Water
• Split peas
• Celery
• Carrot
• Onion
• Thyme
• Red pepper
• Bay leaf
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Combine water, peas, celery, carrot, onion, thyme, red pepper and bay leaf in crock pot
2. Season to taste with salt and pepper
3. Cook on low until split peas and vegetables are tender
4. Press soup through fine sieve and reheat just to boiling point or use hand blender to smooth<|endoftext|>
