Give me a recipe for a cocktail.<|endoftext|>Blueberry Gin And Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Blueberries
• Gin
• Vanilla bean
• Water
• Sugar
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Throw the blueberries and gin in a mason jar, and let this sit for about a week
2. Boil the vanilla bean and sugar in a cup of water for about 1 / 2 hour or so, and stick in the fridge to cool
3. Squeeze 1 / 2 a lemon in a glass of ice, pour in vanilla syrup to taste, pour in gin
4. Shake, garnish with a couple blueberries, and drink!<|endoftext|>
