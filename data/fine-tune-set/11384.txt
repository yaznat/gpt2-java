Ingredients:
• Butter
• Flour
• Whole milk
• Salt and pepper
• Honey mustard
• Baguette
• Deli ham
• Gruyere
• Thyme
• Shallot
• White wine vinegar
• Olive oil
• English cucumber
• Radishes
• Frisee

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400
2. In a small saucepan, cook butter and flour over medium-low heat, whisking for 1 minute
3. Whisk in the milk, then season with salt and pepper
4. Remove from heat and whisk in 1 tablespoon mustard
5. Slather a quarter of the white sauce over each baguette quarter, then top with ham, gruyere, and thyme
6. Bake until the cheese is bubble, about 12 minutes
7. Meanwhile, in a small bowl, combine the shallot andvinegar
8. Stir in the remaining 3 tablespoons mustard, then whisk in the evoo
9. In a large bowl, combine the frisee, cucumber and radish
10. Toss with the dressing
11. Serve the salad with the pizza<|endoftext|>
