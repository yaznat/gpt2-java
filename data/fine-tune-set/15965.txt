Ingredients:
• Breakfast sausage
• Eggs
• Green onion
• Green pepper
• Red pepper
• Cheddar cheese
• Salt and pepper
• Flour tortillas
• Salsa
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Fry sausage
2. Crumble and drain on paper towels
3. Reserve 1- 2 tablespoons drippings
4. Beat eggs, salt and pepper and add to reserved drippings
5. Add sausage, onion, red and green peppers, and cook with the eggs
6. Put 1 / 4 to 1 / 2 cup of sausage-egg mixture down the center of each tortilla
7. Sprinkle with shredded cheddar cheese
8. Fold bottom and sides of tortilla over filling
9. Place seam side down on a cookie sheet sprayed with pam or lined with parchment paper
10. Place pan in freezer overnight or until solid
11. Wrap individual frozen burritos in waxed paper and then in aluminum foil
12. Place in freezer bags and store in the freezer for up to 1 month
13. To use frozen burritos:
14. Remove foil
15. Place a waxed paper-wrapped burrito on a microwave-safe plate
16. Microwave for 1 1 / 2 to 2 minutes or until heated through
17. Let stand for 20 seconds
18. Add salsa and sour cream if you wish<|endoftext|>
