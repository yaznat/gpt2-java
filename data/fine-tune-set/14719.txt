Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Rice
• Hot chicken broth
• Italian plum tomatoes
• Salt
• Cilantro
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a saucepan over medium heat
2. Add onions and saute until transparent
3. Add rice and cook, stirring constantly, until grains are coated with oil and rice is hot
4. Add broth, tomatoes, and salt, and bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat to very low
6. Cover and cook until done, about 20 minutes
7. Garnish with cilantro and tomatoes<|endoftext|>
