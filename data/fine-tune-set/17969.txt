Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Black And White Brownies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Oatmeal
• Coconut
• Butter
• Cream
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Method
2. For white layer
3. In a large bowl, sift together the flour, salt, baking powder and sugar
4. Add the oatmeal and coconut
5. Add the melted butter and cream
6. Mix well
7. Put mixture in a 9 x 13-inch pan
8. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 10 to 12 minutes
9. Brown layer
10. In a small saucepan, melt the chocolate and butter together
11. Allow to cool while you mix the other ingredients
12. In a small bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt
13. In a large bowl, combine the eggs, sugar, and chocolate mixture
14. Add the dry ingredients to this mixture
15. Add in the nuts and vanilla and beat until the mixture is smooth
16. Spread the chocolate layer over the white one
17. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 25 to 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
