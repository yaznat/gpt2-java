Please give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>Amazing Brown Rice Salad

Ingredients:
• Water
• Brown rice
• Red onion
• Celery
• Dried cranberries
• Light balsamic vinaigrette salad dressing
• Splenda granular

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, bring water to a boil
2. Stir in rice, cover, and reduce heat to low
3. Simmer for 45 to 60 minutes, or until done
4. Transfer rice to a serving bowl and stir in onion, celery, cranberries and salad dressing
5. Cover, refrigerate and serve cold<|endoftext|>
