Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado With Lime And Chilies

Ingredients:
• Lime peel
• Lime juice
• Brown sugar
• Olive oil
• Fresh parsley
• Jalapeno peppers
• Avocados

Steps:
1. In a small browl, combine lime peel, lime juice, brown sugar, oil, parsley and chili peppers
2. Whish until well combined
3. Spoon over avocados
4. Serve as a tangy side salad to fish, shellfish, chicken or meat dishes<|endoftext|>
