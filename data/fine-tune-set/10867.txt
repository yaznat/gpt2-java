Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Gala apples
• Celery
• Sliced almonds
• Kosher salt
• Pepper
• Lemon, juice of
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Put the chicken in a large bowl
2. Dice the apple into small pieces
3. Dice the celery just as small and add the apples celery and almonds to the chicken
4. Mix in the salt and pepper and lemon juice
5. Add the mayo with a large spoon and mix until the chicken is evenly covered
6. I know this is hard to do -- but cover and leave overnight
7. The flavors will mix well together in the fridge
8. You can eat it the day it's made, it just might have a little strong lemon taste -- that mixes overnight and blends well
9. You can always add cranberries or rasins if you want, but i like it just as is
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
