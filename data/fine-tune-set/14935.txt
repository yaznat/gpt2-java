What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Fried Rice Cuban Chinese Style

Ingredients:
• Cooked white rice
• Cooked pork
• Smoked ham
• Shrimp
• Peanut oil
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Eggs
• Soy sauce
• Scallions

Steps:
1. Peel, devein and boil shrimp
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in large skillet
3. Beat eggs, and pour into skillet and make a very thin omelette
4. Cut omelette into strips like the ham
5. Set aside
6. Heat rest of oil
7. Fry garlic until golden and discard
8. Add rice to oil and stir with a large two tined fork until rice is well coated
9. Add pork, ginger, shrimp, ham and egg
10. Stir well, add soy sauce and stir in scallions
11. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
