Ingredients:
• Red onion
• Bell pepper
• Butter
• Cooked chicken
• Barbecue sauce
• Monterey jack cheese
• Cilantro
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Saute onions and bell peppers in pan with butter
2. Remove from pan and set aside
3. In another pan heat through shredded chicken with bbq sauce
4. Lightly butter one side of a tortilla
5. Place butter side down in saute pan
6. Quickly add some of the cheese, chicken and onions to one half of tortilla
7. Then quickly fold in half forming a semi-circle
8. Continue to cook until bottom side is golden brown, then carefully flip quesadilla over
9. Brown other side and remove from pan
10. Repeat with remaining ingredients
11. Cut quesadilla into wedges and serve with additional barbecue sauce<|endoftext|>
