Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Cheese Stuffed Burgers

Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground chuck
• Onion
• Red pepper
• Green pepper
• Fresh garlic
• Barbecue sauce concentrate
• Sugar
• Mccormick's montreal brand steak seasoning
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Mix first five ingredients well and shape into 8 flat 4" burgers, make 4 of the burgers a little bit bigger
2. Top the larger patties with the cheese of your choice and top with remaining patties pinching to seal
3. Grill about 8 minutes per side
4. Try to flip only once<|endoftext|>
