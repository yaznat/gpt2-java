What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Hash Brown Casserole

Ingredients:
• Frozen hash browns
• Low-fat cream of chicken soup
• Margarine
• Light sour cream
• Cheddar cheese
• Bacon
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Thaw hash browns for 30 minutes
2. While hash browns are thawing, prepare the bacon: using clean scissors, cut the bacon into thin strips horizontally
3. Fry until crispy or desired doneness
4. In a large bowl, mix together soup & sour cream & salt and pepper
5. Add melted margarine & mix well
6. Add the cheese & bacon and then the hash browns
7. Continue mixing until well combined
8. Spread mixture into 9 x 13 pan, lightly coated with cooking spray
9. Bake for 1 hour at 375 degrees<|endoftext|>
