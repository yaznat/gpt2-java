Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Frozen sweet peas
• Swiss cheese
• Alfredo sauce
• Slivered almonds
• Bisquick baking mix
• Milk

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 425f in medium saucepan, mix chicken, peas, cheese, alfredo sauce and almonds
2. Heat to boiling over medium-high heat
3. Pour into ungreased 1 1 / 2-quart casserole
4. In small bowl, stir bisquick mix and milk until soft dough forms
5. Drop dough by about 12 spoonfuls onto chicken mixture
6. Bake uncovered 20 to 25 minutes or until golden brown<|endoftext|>
