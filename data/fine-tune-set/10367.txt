Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Tile Cookies

Ingredients:
• Egg white
• Sugar
• Flour
• Butter
• Water
• Vanilla
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f in a large bowl, with a whisk, mix egg white, sugar, flour, butter, water and vanilla until homogeneous, no more
2. Drop the dough, 1 teaspoon at a time, on 2 cookie plates with parchment paper or greased, leaving 2 inches between each
3. With a metal spatula, spread the tile in a circle of 3 inches of diameter
4. Put almonds on the tiles
5. Cook, one cookie sheet at a time, in center of preheated oven of 350 f for about 8 minutes or until the sides begin to brown
6. Put the tiles on a rack and let cool completely
7. Cook the remaining tiles the same way<|endoftext|>
