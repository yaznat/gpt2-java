Ingredients:
• Ice cubes
• Instant coffee
• Hot water
• Vanilla ice cream
• Hersheys chocolate syrup
• Milk

Steps:
1. Mix coffee and hot water until coffee is dissolved
2. Crush ice in blender
3. Add all ingredients to blender
4. Puree until smooth<|endoftext|>
