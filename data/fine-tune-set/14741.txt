What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Arroz Con Queso Rice With Cheese Bolivia

Ingredients:
• Short-grain rice
• Queso fresco
• Garlic
• Red onion
• Milk
• Water
• Salt

Steps:
1. Boil the water with the salt, then add the rice and keep boiling, uncovered, until almost all of the water has been absorbed or cooked off
2. The rice should still be pretty wet, but without pools of water and not fluffy
3. Meanwhile, saute the garlic and onions in a small amount of oil for four or five minutes
4. Take care not to let them brown
5. When the rice is nearly ready, bring the milk to a boil
6. Add the milk, cheese and onion / garlic mixture to the rice
7. Cook over low heat, stirring, until all the cheese has melted<|endoftext|>
