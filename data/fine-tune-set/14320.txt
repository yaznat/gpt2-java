What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Greek Salad With Tofu Feta Cheese

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Red wine vinegar
• Lemon juice
• Olive oil
• Oregano
• Basil
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Romaine lettuce hearts
• Cucumber
• Roma tomatoes
• Black olives
• Purple onion

Steps:
1. Mix together salad dressing ingredients in a large cup
2. Add tofu, cover and marinate overnite stirring ocasionally to make sure all tofu marinates evenly
3. Just before serving make up salad ingredients and add salad dressing and tofu
4. Toss and serve<|endoftext|>
