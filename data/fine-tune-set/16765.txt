What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Deluxe Banana Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Cinnamon
• Ice cubes
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Put the bananas in a blender
2. Add the milk, sugar, vanilla extract, and ice cubes
3. Blend on medium 1 minute
4. Add the cinnamon and serve
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
