Can you give me a donut recipe?<|endoftext|>Bunuelo Colombian Donuts

Ingredients:
• Queso fresco
• Cornstarch
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sugar
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Heat deep fryer to 325f
2. Crumble cheese thoroughly, this is a must
3. Mix in eggs, one at a time, being sure to thoroughly combine
4. Add sugar and mix
5. Add in cornstarch, a little at a time, and thoroughly combine
6. Are you sensing a pattern?
7. Mix in just enough milk to form a soft dough
8. Shape into large balls, smaller than a baseball, but about the size that fits in your palm
9. Be careful not to compact dough, it takes a light touch
10. Cook in batches in a deep fryer until dark golden brown
11. The bunuelo will "roll" over as it cooks numerous times, this is important for even cooking so do not overcrowd fryer
12. Nudge it if it doesn't roll enough
13. It will puff as it cooks and little appendages could puff out, don't worry, that is okay
14. Drain well and serve<|endoftext|>
