Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Cashew Vegetable Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Gingerroot
• Red capsicum
• Snow peas
• Baby corn
• Bean sprouts
• Bok choy
• Light soy sauce
• Chili paste
• Cashews
• Uncooked rice

Steps:
1. Prepare rice as per instructions
2. Heat oil in wok and stir fry onion and garlic for 1 minute
3. Add ginger and stir fry for a further minute
4. Capsicum, corn and snow peas next
5. Add remaining ingredients and stir-fry for another 2 minutes or until heated through
6. Spoon rice in bowl and top with mix<|endoftext|>
