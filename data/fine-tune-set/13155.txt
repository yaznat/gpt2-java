Ingredients:
• Fish fillets
• Light mayonnaise
• Lemon peel
• Cracked black pepper
• Salt
• Lemon wedge

Steps:
1. Arrange fish in single layer on baking sheet
2. Set aside
3. In bowl, stir together mayonnaise, lemon rind, pepper and salt
4. Spread over fish
5. Broil until golden brown and flaky, about 10 minutes
6. Serve with lemon wedges to squeeze over top<|endoftext|>
