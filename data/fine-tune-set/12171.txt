Ingredients:
• Pie shell
• Cream cheese
• Granulated sugar
• Egg
• Lemon juice
• Vanilla
• Tart apples
• Cinnamon
• Brown sugar
• Flour
• Walnuts
• Butter

Steps:
1. Layer one: beat together cream cheese, sugar, egg, lemon juice, and vanilla until smooth
2. Pour into prepared pie shell
3. Layer two: toss together apples, sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice
4. Spread carefully over cream cheese layer
5. Layer three: cut together brown sugar, flour, walnuts, and butter until crumbly
6. Spread evenly over apples
7. Bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes, or until apples are tender
8. Remove from oven and cool
9. Refrigerate until serving<|endoftext|>
