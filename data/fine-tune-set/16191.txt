Give me a quesadilla recipe.<|endoftext|>Grilled Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack and cheddar cheese blend

Steps:
1. Preheat grill or grill pan to medium
2. Place half of the tortillas on the grill, and drop a quarter-cup of cheese in the center of each
3. Spread cheese as close to edges as possible
4. Place a second tortilla on top of each
5. Grill until bottom tortilla is lightly browned and cheese begins to melt, about 1-2 minutes
6. Flip and continue cooking until browned on the other side
7. Cut into quarters and serve with salsa, sour cream, and guacamole
8. Optional: add grilled chicken or shrimp and / or sauteed onions and peppers at this step
9. Both will add flavor and texture, although the recipe is complete without them<|endoftext|>
