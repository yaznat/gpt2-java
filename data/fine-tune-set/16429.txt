Ingredients:
• Prosciutto
• Basil leaves
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh mozzarella balls
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180c / 160c fan forced
2. For each mozzarella, lay 2 slices of prosciutto on a flat surface side by side slightly overlapping and top with a basil leaf and a few slices of garlic and then top with mozzarella, wrap prosciutto to enclose and secure with a toothpick
3. Season with pepper and place on a baking tray and bake for 3 to 4 minutes or until mozzarella soften slightly and remove from oven and cool slightly and then remove toothpicks
4. Meanwhile, blend extra basil and reserved garlic in a food processor until finely chopped with a motor running, gradually add oil and process until smooth and season with salt
5. Spoon basil sauce onto serving plates and top with mozzarella parcels<|endoftext|>
