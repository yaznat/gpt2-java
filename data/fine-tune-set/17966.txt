What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Bittersweet Beachside Brownies

Ingredients:
• Bittersweet chocolate
• Unsalted butter
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• Dark brown sugar
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Nonstick cooking spray

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350f
2. In a medium saucepan over medium-low heat, melt the chocolate with the 1 cup butter, stirring occasionally
3. Set aside
4. In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs and both sugars
5. Stir in the melted chocolate and butter
6. Stir in the vanilla
7. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and cinnamon
8. Stir the flour mixture into the chocolate mixture until well combined
9. Grease a 13 by 9-inch pan with nonstick cooking spray or the 1-2 tablespoons of butter
10. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 30 to 40 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean
11. Let the brownies cool to room temperature before cutting into 3-inch squares<|endoftext|>
