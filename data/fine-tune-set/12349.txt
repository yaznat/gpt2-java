Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Chicken breasts
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Sweet potatoes
• Green bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Chicken broth
• V8 vegetable juice
• Ginger
• Cayenne pepper
• Crunchy peanut butter

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot
2. Add 2-3 chicken breasts, diced
3. 1 onion, chopped
4. And 3 cloves of garlic, chopped
5. Stir and cook 5 minutes or until chicken is done
6. Peel 2 medium sweet potatoes then either grate, food process, chop, or bake / microwave and mash them
7. Add the sweet potatoes to the pot along with 1 green bell pepper, chopped
8. 1 red bell pepper, chopped
9. 2 cans of chicken broth
10. 1 1 / 2 cups v-8 or tomato juice
11. 1 teaspoons ginger
12. And 1 / 2 teaspoons cayenne pepper
13. Mix well
14. Bring to a boil
15. Reduce heat
16. Cover and simmer 10-15 minutes or until veggies are tender, stirring occasionally
17. Add 1 / 2 cup crunchy peanut butter and stir constantly until well blended
18. Serve<|endoftext|>
