Ingredients:
• Basmati rice
• Salt
• Pineapple salsa
• Red curry paste
• Oil
• Eggs
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Unsweetened coconut milk
• Fresh pineapple chunk
• Fresh basil

Steps:
1. In 2-quart saucepan, bring 2 cups water to a boil
2. Add rice and salt
3. Reduce heat to low
4. Cover and simmer 18 minutes
5. Chill in 1-quart covered bowl at least 2 hours until cold
6. Whisk pineapple salsa and red curry paste together until smooth
7. In nonstick 12-inch skillet, heat 1 teaspoon oil over medium heat
8. Add eggs and scramble
9. Remove from skillet and set aside
10. In same skillet, heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil over medium heat
11. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden and tender
12. Add garlic and cook, stirring, 1 minute
13. Increase heat to medium-high
14. Add chicken and cook 4-5 minutes until golden and cooked through
15. Add chilled rice, coconut milk and salsa mixture to skillet and mix well
16. Add pineapple cubes, scrambled eggs and 1 / 3 cup basil, tossing to mix well
17. To serve, remove skillet from heat
18. Spoon contents into a serving bowl
19. Garnish with remaining basil<|endoftext|>
