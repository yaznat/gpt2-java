What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Awesome Grilled Cheese Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Onion
• Rye bread
• American cheese
• Swiss cheese

Steps:
1. Melt margarine in frying pan and grill onions until golden
2. Put margarine on one side of the rye bread
3. Put one slice of the rye bread margarine side down in frying pan
4. Top with the cheese and the onion
5. Top with remaining rye bread margarine side up
6. Grill until both sides and browned and cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
