Ingredients:
• Ground lamb
• Shallot
• Capers
• Basil leaves
• Fresh ground pepper
• Cornmeal
• Vegetable oil
• Tomatoes
• Red onion
• Sugar
• Balsamic vinegar
• Salt
• Pepper
• Olive oil
• Kaiser rolls
• Soft fresh goat cheese

Steps:
1. To make the burgers:
2. Combine the ground lamb, shallot, capers, basil and pepper in a large bowl and mix well
3. Divide mixture into four burgers
4. Spread cornmeal over a plate and press burgers into the mixture to coat
5. Preheata barbeque and brush with oil
6. Cook the burgers over a medium high heat for 6-7 minutes each side or until cooked as desired
7. To make the relish:
8. Place the tomatoes and onion into a preheated saute pan with the olive oil
9. Cook for 5 minutes
10. Add sugar, vinegar, salt and pepper
11. Remove from the heat and let cool
12. Grill the buns, cut side down, until lightly toasted
13. Spread with goat cheese and top with the burgers
14. Serve with the tomato relish
15. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
