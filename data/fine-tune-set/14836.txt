What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Beef Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Eggs
• Black pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Onions
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. In a skillet, cook beef untilno longer pink, and drain
2. In a large bowl, beat eggs, and pepper
3. In a skillet, heat 2 tablespoons, oil over medium heat
4. Add eggs to skillet
5. As eggs set, lift the edges, letting uncooked portion flow underneath
6. When eggs are completely cooked, remove from skillet, and cut into strips
7. Return to pan
8. Stir in remaining ingredients
9. Cook and stir over low heat in remaining oil for 5 minutes, or until heated through<|endoftext|>
