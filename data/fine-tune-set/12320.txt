Ingredients:
• Cabbage
• Soy sauce
• Garlic
• Sesame oil
• Chili pepper flakes
• Chicken stock
• Scallion
• Chilies
• Sweet chili sauce
• Eggs
• Noodles
• Meat

Steps:
1. Begin boiling noodles
2. Place chopped cabbage / kale into a stock pot and saute with soy sauce, garlic, sesame oil and chili pepper flakes until your cabbage is clear or kale has wilted
3. Immediately add the chicken soup stock and bring to boil
4. Reduce heat and add in sweet chili sauce to taste
5. (if you want a big kick
6. Add in chicken or beef
7. Do not add shrimp or tofu at this step
8. Bring back to a simmer
9. Beat egg, then stream into soup
10. After 15 minutes add in noodls
11. Finally, add tofu or shrimp
12. Top with scallions and salt and pepper as desire<|endoftext|>
