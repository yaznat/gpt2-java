Please give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>Almost Fat Free Chinese Salad

Ingredients:
• Cauliflower
• Cucumber
• Fresh spinach
• Fresh mushrooms
• Carrots
• Green onions
• Green pepper
• Celery
• Beef broth
• Soy sauce
• Sake
• Ground black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Place all vegetables in large salad bowl
2. Combine broth, soy sauce, sake, black pepper, garlic powder, and sugar in small mixing bowl
3. Just before serving, pour dressing over salad and toss to coat
4. Dressing
5. If you do not use it all, refrigerate leftovers for use on other salads<|endoftext|>
