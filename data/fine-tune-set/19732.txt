Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Salmon fillets
• Cajun spices
• Frozen peas
• Butter
• Salt and pepper
• Potato

Steps:
1. Peel and cut potatoes into very fat chips, heat some oil in a large frying pan and fry chips until cooked and crisp, turning constantly, to brown on all sides
2. Alternatively fry in a chip fryer if you have one
3. Coat salmon with cajun seasoning, heat a small amount of oil in a pan, place salmon skin side down and fry for 4-5 minutes, turn and fry for a further 4-5 min's
4. Cooking times will vary depending on the thickness of the fillet
5. Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil, add peas and boil for about 5 min's
6. Drain and puree peas with butter, salt and pepper in a food processor or blender
7. To serve: divide puree mix between 4 plates, place salmon on top, and stack chips on top of each other<|endoftext|>
