Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Green onion
• Fat free sour cream
• Low-fat mayonnaise
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Black beans
• Green chilies
• Tortillas
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, stir together all ingredients except tortillas and spray
2. Spread about 1 / 4 cup of filling on half of each tortilla and fold in half
3. Spray both sides of each tortilla with cooking spray
4. Place on a non-stick skillet and brown on each side until filling is heated or bake on a baking sheet for 10-15 minutes at 375 degrees<|endoftext|>
