What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Bayonne Lamb Collier De Mouton A La Bayonne

Ingredients:
• Lamb shoulder
• Onions
• Carrots
• Baby turnips
• Bay leaf
• Lemon juice
• Water
• Mushrooms
• Butter
• Flour
• Egg yolk
• Cream
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Put the lamb in a pot with the onions, carrots, bay leaf, lemon juice, salt, pepper and water
2. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover and simmer for 1 1 / 2 hours or until tender
3. 20 minutes before the end add the mushrooms
4. Arrange lamb and vegetables in a serving dish and keep hot
5. Reduce the cooking liquid to 2 1 / 2 cups by boiling rapidly
6. Make a roux with the butter and flour, add the reduced cooking liquid and simmer for another 5 minutes
7. Correct the seasonings
8. Blend together the egg and cream and add a little of the sauce to temper it
9. Add the egg / cream mixture to the sauce and return to the pan
10. Reheat but do not boil
11. Pour sauce over meat and vegetables<|endoftext|>
