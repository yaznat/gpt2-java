Ingredients:
• Onions
• Olive oil
• Tomatoes
• Fresh parsley
• Garlic cloves
• White fish fillets
• Lemon juice
• Salt and pepper
• Lemon

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, saute onions in oil until golden brown and almost caramelized
2. Peel and chop 1 tomato
3. Add to onions, along with parsley and garlic
4. Simmer for a few minutes longer
5. Place fish fillets in a lightly buttered crockpot
6. Sprinkle with lemon juice, salt and pepper
7. Spoon sauteed vegetables over fish
8. Slice remaining tomato and place on vegetables
9. Top with lemon slices
10. Cover and cook on low for about 1-1 / 2 hours, or until fish flakes when tested with a fork
11. Nice over rice!<|endoftext|>
