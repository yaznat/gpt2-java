Ingredients:
• No-sugar-added apple pie filling
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Eggs
• Honey crunch corn flakes

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan combine the apples, sugar and cinnamon
2. Heat gently until warmed through and sugar is dessolved
3. Preheat oven to 180c
4. Coat a 500ml oven-proof dish with cooking spray
5. Arrange apple mixture over the base of the dish
6. Pour over the eggs and sprinkle with the corn flakes
7. Bake in the oven for abouth 20 mins or until set<|endoftext|>
