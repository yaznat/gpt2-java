Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Egg
• Soft breadcrumbs
• Meatless sauce
• Elbow macaroni
• Salt
• Celery
• Parsley
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Green pepper ring
• Oil

Steps:
1. Lightly mix beef, seasoned salt, black pepper, egg, crumbs and 1 / 2 cup of the spaghetti sauce and press firmly into a 10-inch pizza pan
2. Cook macaroni in boiling salted water for 10 min
3. Or until tender and drain
4. Mix macaroni with celery, parsley, 1 / 2 cup of the cheddar cheese and the remaining spaghetti sauce and spread on top of meat
5. Bake at 350 f for 25-30 minutes
6. Remove from oven
7. Sprinkle with rest of cheddar cheese and parmesan cheese
8. Lightly brush green pepper rings with oil and arrange in a circle on top of pizza
9. Bake 10 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
