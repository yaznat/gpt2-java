What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Apple Cranberry Pork Roast

Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Canola oil
• Apple juice
• Granny smith apples
• Fresh cranberries
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Brown roast on all sides in skillet in canola oil, and place in slow cooker
2. Add remaining ingredients
3. Cover, and cook on low 6-8 hours<|endoftext|>
