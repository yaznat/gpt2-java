What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Bacon Topped Squash Casserole

Ingredients:
• Squash
• Onion
• Egg
• Salt
• Pepper
• Breadcrumbs
• Butter
• Cheddar cheese
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Boil or steam squash and onion until tender
2. Mash, then add salt and pepper
3. Add butter, egg, 1 cup bread crumbs and cheese
4. Mix and place into a greased 1 quart casserole dish
5. Sprinkle with remaining bread crumbs and bacon
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 20 to 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
