Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Gingerbread Waffles Healthy Low Fat

Ingredients:
• White whole wheat flour
• Ginger
• Cinnamon
• Clove
• Nutmeg
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Molasses
• Applesauce
• Skim milk

Steps:
1. Whisk together the dry ingredients in one bowl, and the wet in another
2. Combine, careful not to overmix
3. Cook in a greased, preheated waffle iron
4. Top with maple syrup, caramel sauce, or whipped cream
5. Leftovers can be frozen, then microwaved then toasted to be served again<|endoftext|>
