Ingredients:
• Roasted red peppers
• Garlic clove
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Focaccia bread
• Goat cheese
• Black olive paste
• Arugula

Steps:
1. Combine red peppers, garlic, black pepper and 2 tbs
2. Reserved artichoke marinade in mixing bowl
3. Toss to coat
4. Split focaccia or ciabatta loaves in half horizontally using a serrated knife
5. Brush insides with some remaining artichoke marinade
6. Spread goat cheese on bottom halves and black olive paste on top halves
7. Layer red pepper mixture, artichokes and arugula over goat cheese
8. Replace bread tops
9. Using serrated knife, cut each loaf into 4 portions<|endoftext|>
