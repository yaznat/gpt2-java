Ingredients:
• Graham cracker squares
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Line a large cookie sheet with foil
2. Gently cut graham crackers in half
3. Place in a single layer on foil
4. Melt butter and sugar in a saucepan on medium-high heat
5. Bring to a rolling boil
6. Add almonds and boil until syrup is bubbly
7. Pour mixture over graham crackers and bake at 350 degrees for 5-7 minutes until syrup is bubbly again
8. When slightly cooled, separate with a knife<|endoftext|>
