Give me a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>Ann S German Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Mayonnaise
• Dijon mustard
• Balsamic vinegar
• Seasoning
• Kosher dill pickles
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Cook potatoes in boiling water until just tender but not mushy
2. Drain and set aside
3. Mix mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar and nature's seasons together until well blended
4. Add to pototoes
5. Mix well
6. Dice pickles and add to potatoes
7. Crumble bacon and add to potatoes
8. Mix well
9. Refrigerate until ready to serve
10. Best served at room temperature<|endoftext|>
