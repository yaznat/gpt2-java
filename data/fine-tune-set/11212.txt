Give me a sandwich recipe.<|endoftext|>Barefoot Contessa S Roasted Pepper And Goat Cheese Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Red bell peppers
• Olive oil
• Balsamic vinegar
• Garlic cloves
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Capers
• Ciabatta
• Garlic and herb goat cheese
• Basil leaves
• Red onions

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 500 degrees f
2. Place the whole peppers on a sheet pan and place in the oven for 30 to 40 minutes, until the skins are completely wrinkled and the peppers are charred, turning them twice during roasting
3. Remove the pan from the oven and immediately cover it tightly with aluminum foil
4. Set aside for 30 minutes, or until the peppers are cool enough to handle
5. Meanwhile, combine the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, garlic, salt, and pepper in a small bowl
6. Set aside
7. Remove the stem from each pepper and cut them in quarters
8. Remove the peels and seeds and place the peppers in a bowl along with any juices that have collected
9. Discard the stems, peels, and seeds
10. Pour the oil and vinegar mixture over the peppers
11. Stir in the capers
12. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for a few hours to allow the flavors to blend
13. To assemble the sandwiches, spread the bottom half of the loaf with the goat cheese
14. Add a layer of peppers and then a layer of basil leaves
15. Separate the onions into rings and spread out on top
16. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
17. Cover with the top half of the ciabatta and cut into individual servings<|endoftext|>
