Ingredients:
• Water
• Ground coffee
• Cinnamon stick
• Dark brown sugar

Steps:
1. Bring the water to a boil
2. Add the coffee, cinnamon and sugar, and continue boiling for 30 seconds
3. Stir and strain into hot mugs<|endoftext|>
