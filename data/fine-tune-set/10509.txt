What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Pepper Pasta

Ingredients:
• Green bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Yellow bell pepper
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Red onion
• Mushroom
• Italian sausage
• Basil
• Sugar
• Red wine vinegar
• Penne pasta

Steps:
1. Brown the sausage well, crumbling with a fork
2. Remove from skillet and set aside
3. Remove excess grease
4. In a large skillet, heat olive oil
5. Add onion and cook to desired tenderness
6. Add mushrooms and garlic and cook 3-4 minutes
7. Add peppers and cook to desired tenderness
8. When the veggies are at your desired tenderness, add the red wine vinegar and sugar
9. If using sausage, add the meat back to the skillet
10. If i am using fresh basil, i add it last
11. However, if i am using dried basil, i add it with the mushrooms
12. Pour all contents over al dente penne pasta
13. I like to top with grated parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
