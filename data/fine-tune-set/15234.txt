Ingredients:
• Pizza crust
• Pesto sauce
• Boursin cheese
• Sun-dried tomatoes
• Roasted red peppers
• Salami
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Spread pesto sauce over the pizza base
2. Distribute the salami evenly over the pizza
3. Roughly chop the sun-dried tomatoes and peppers and distribute evenly over the pizza
4. Crumble the boursin cheese and distribute over the pizza
5. Sprinkle the mozzarella sparingly over the pizza
6. Transfer to the preheated pizza maker machine and cook until the crust is crisp and golden, rotating it 180 degrees half way through cooking to ensure it's cooked evenly
7. When the pizza is ready, remove, cut into pieces and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
