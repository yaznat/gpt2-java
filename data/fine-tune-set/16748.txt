Ingredients:
• Creamy peanut butter
• Vanilla ice cream
• Chocolate syrup
• Milk
• Peanuts
• Cream

Steps:
1. Combine peanut butter, ice cream, chocolate syrup and milk in a blender and blend until smooth
2. Pour into glasses
3. Serve with a dollop of whipped cream and top with chopped nuts and maraschino cherry if desired<|endoftext|>
