Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Another Minestrone Soup For Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Red kidney beans
• Chicken broth
• Diced tomatoes
• Carrots
• Fresh green beans
• Celery ribs
• Onion
• Red potatoes
• Dried basil
• Dried italian seasoning
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh basil
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Salt and pepper
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. In a 6 quart slow cooker, mix together the chicken or beef broth, tomatoes, carrots, green beans, celery, onion, potatoes, dried basil, italian seasoning, garlic, salt and pepper
2. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 hours, or until the potatoes are tender
3. Stir in the fresh basil and olive oil<|endoftext|>
