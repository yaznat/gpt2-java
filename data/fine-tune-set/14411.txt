Please give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>African Red Beans

Ingredients:
• Dried pinto bean
• Onions
• Peanut oil
• Canned whole tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Garlic clove
• Cayenne pepper
• White pepper
• Salt

Steps:
1. Soak the beans overnight in 2 quarts of water
2. Or you could quick soak them by putting the beans into a heavy pot and cover with 2 quarts of water, bring to a boil and boil for 2 to 3 minutes
3. Remove the pot from the heat and let the beans soak, uncovered for 1 hour
4. Bring the pot of soaked beans to a boil, then reduce the heat to low
5. Add 1 / 4 c of the onion and simmer, partly covered, about 1 hour, or until the beans are tender but not mushy
6. Drain the beans and set them aside
7. In a large skillet, heat the oil until it is hot but not smoking
8. Saute the remaining cup of onions until soft and translucent but not brown
9. Add the tomatoes, tomato paste, garlic, cayenne pepper, white pepper and salt
10. Stirring frequently, simmer the mixture until most of the liquid has evaporated
11. Stir in the beans and cook, uncovered, for about 5 to 10 minutes, stirring frequently<|endoftext|>
