Ingredients:
• Vanilla frozen yogurt
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Apple juice
• Apple
• Cinnamon
• Ground nutmeg

Steps:
1. Combine the frozen yogurt, applesauce, and apple juice in a blender
2. Add the apple, cinnamon, and nutmeg
3. Blend until smooth<|endoftext|>
