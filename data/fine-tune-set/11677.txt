Give me a cake recipe.<|endoftext|>A Honey Of A Honey Cake

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Brown sugar
• Canola oil
• Honey
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Mead

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f
2. In a large bowl, beat eggs, sugar and oil until pale in color
3. Add honey, and mead
4. Mix
5. In a second bowl, combine flower, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, and ginger
6. One cup at a time, add dry ingredients to wet ingredients
7. Blend until smooth
8. Transfer batter to a greased 10 x 14-inch baking dish
9. Bake for approximately 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out dry
10. Let cake cool and cut into honeycomb shapes
11. Drizzle with lavender honey and garnish with fresh lavender flowers, if desired<|endoftext|>
