Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Orange marmalade
• White bread

Steps:
1. In small mixing bowl, beat the cream cheese and marmalade until blended
2. Using a 2-inch angel-shaped or heart-shaped cookie cutter, cut out 16 angels or hearts from bread
3. Spread half with cream cheese mixture
4. Top with remaining bread<|endoftext|>
