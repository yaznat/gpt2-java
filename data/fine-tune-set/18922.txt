Ingredients:
• Water
• Margarine
• Sifted flour
• Eggs
• Cornstarch
• Sugar
• Salt
• Milk
• Egg yolks
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. For creampuffs: heat the water and margarine to boiling in a saucepan
2. Stir in all at once the sifted flour
3. Stir constantly with a wooden spoon until mixture leaves pan and forms into a ball
4. Remove from heat
5. Cool
6. Beat in the eggs, one at a time, beating until smooth after each addition
7. Beat mixture until smooth and velvety
8. Drop from teaspoonfuls onto lightly greased cookie sheet
9. Bake about 15 minutes in 425 degree oven then reduce heat to 350 degrees and bake for 10-15 minutes more
10. Remove from oven, gut gash to allow steam to escape
11. Fill with custard filling
12. For custard filling: melt margarine in a saucepan
13. Blend in cornstarch, sugar and salt
14. Gradually add milk
15. Heat to boiling over direct heat
16. Stir mixture into slightly beaten egg yolks
17. Return to heat and cook 2 minutes, stirring constantly
18. Add vanilla
19. Cool thoroughly
20. Makes 2 cups<|endoftext|>
