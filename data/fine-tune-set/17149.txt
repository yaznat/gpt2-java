What's a recipe for tea?<|endoftext|>A Secret Ingredient To Southern Style Sweet Tea

Ingredients:
• Water
• Tea bags
• Sugar

Steps:
1. On your stove top bring water to boil, with the tea bags
2. Once it reaches a boil, remove from heat & let stand to brew for a few minutes
3. Remove bags
4. Add sugar,
5. Return to heat until sugar is completely dissolved
6. Add ice, put into container, pour,
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
