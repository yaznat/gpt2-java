What's a recipe for an omelette?<|endoftext|>Family Style Deep Dish Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Cheddar cheese
• Salt
• Cracked black pepper
• Frozen chopped broccoli
• Onion
• Roasted red pepper
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. In large bowl, whisk together eggs, cheese, salt and pepper until blended
2. Stir in broccoli, onion and roasted red peppers
3. Coat 10-inch frying pan with cooking spray and heat over medium heat
4. Add egg mixture
5. Cover and cook 8 to 10 minutes until top is set
6. Slide onto platter<|endoftext|>
