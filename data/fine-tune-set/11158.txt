Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Egg Supreme Sandwich

Ingredients:
• French bread
• Butter
• Mayonnaise
• Honey mustard
• Ham
• Turkey
• Cooked bacon
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Eggs
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Cut bread lengthwise and hollow out top and bottom
2. Spread half the butter and all of mayo inside bread shell
3. Line bottom bread shell with ham, turkey and bacon
4. Top with tomato, onion and honey mustard
5. In frying pan add remaining butter and eggs cooking til almost set, but not hard scrambled
6. Spoon into bottom bread shell and top with cheeses
7. Cover with bread top
8. Wrap in greased foil and bake at 375 for 15 minutes or until heated thru
9. Cut and enjoy<|endoftext|>
