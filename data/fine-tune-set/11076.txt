What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Asparagus Grill Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Sandwich bread
• Butter
• Cooked ham
• Onions
• Tomatoes
• Asparagus spears
• Process american cheese
• Cheese sauce
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Sharp cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Spread one side of each slice of bread with butter
2. Place 4 slices of bread, buttered side down, on a hot griddle
3. On each slice place 2 slices of ham, 1 slice of onion and tomato, 4 asparagus spears, and 1 slice of cheese
4. Place remaining 4 slices of bread, buttered side up, on top of cheese
5. Cook until sandwiches are golden brown on bottom
6. Turn carefully to brown top slices of bread
7. Pour hot cheese sauce over the sandwiches before serving
8. Cheese sauce: melt butter in a heavy saucepan over low heat
9. Add flour, and cook 1 minute, stirring constantly
10. Gradually add milk
11. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thickened and bubbly
12. Add cheese, and stir until melted
13. Islice these diagonally, then drizzle with cheese sauce<|endoftext|>
