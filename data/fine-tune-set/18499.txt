Ingredients:
• Gluten-free flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Milk
• Eggs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Combine the dry ingredients
2. Mix together the 1 1 / 2 cups milk and eggs
3. Stir in the butter
4. Stir the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients
5. Add additional milk until you get a batter a little thicker than pancake batter
6. Spread a ladleful or so of batter onto the waffle iron and bake until the waffle is done, usually 3 to 5 minutes, depending on your iron
7. Serve immediately
8. Freeze leftovers in a plastic baggie with a sheet of wax paper in between each waffle
9. Use 2 cups for waffles<|endoftext|>
