Ingredients:
• Butter
• Seasoning salt
• Garlic powder
• Hot pepper sauce
• Mixed nuts
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 300
2. Melt butter with salt, garlic powder and pepper sauce
3. Mix well- add nuts
4. Toss to coat
5. In a jelly roll pan, bake for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
6. Sprinkle with worcestershire sauce and bake 15 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
