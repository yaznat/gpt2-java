Ingredients:
• Fettuccine pasta
• Frozen broccoli cuts
• Carrots
• Canola oil
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Cooked pork
• Italian dressing
• Soy sauce
• Ground ginger

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to directions on package
2. 2 to 3 minutes before pasta is done, add broccoli and carrots
3. While pasta is cooking, heat oil in a large skillet
4. When oil is hot but not smoking, add rest of ingredients
5. Stir until heated through
6. Drain pasta / vegetable mix
7. Add to meat, mixing well
