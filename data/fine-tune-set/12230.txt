What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Cream Of Pumpkin Or Squash Soup Vegan

Ingredients:
• Pumpkin puree
• Vegetable broth
• Small red potato
• Green bell pepper
• Celery
• Red onion
• Smooth cashew butter
• Banana
• Apple
• Salt
• Ground red pepper
• Fresh cilantro leaves
• Pumpkin seeds
• Pesto sauce

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients except for banana, apple and paprika, in a stock pot
2. Cover, cook on low-medium heat until vegetables are soft, about 20 minutes
3. Add banana, apple and paprika last 5 minutes of cooking
4. Heat through
5. Once heated, puree soup if desired
6. Just before serving, season soup with salt to taste
7. Garnish with cilantro leaves and pumpkin seeds
8. Or, if you wish, garnish each serving with a swirl of pesto made with pumpkin seeds
