Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Milk
• Boiling water
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. In a casserole dish add, sugar, flour, baking powder, and soda mix with a fork to blend then add milk
2. Stir until everything is incorporated
3. In a seperate bowl mix water, sugar, and nutmeg
4. Using a large spoon pour the water mixture over the spoon and onto the dough mixture gently so as not to disurb the dough
5. Place in oven and bake for 1 / 2 hour
6. Cake will bake and rise above the sauce mixture<|endoftext|>
