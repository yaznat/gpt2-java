Give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>3 Cheese Crab Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Crabmeat
• Monterey jack cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Seafood cocktail sauce
• Green onion
• Garlic
• Tortilla
• Butter

Steps:
1. Combine crabmeat, monterey jack cheese, mozzarella cheese, cheddar cheese, 1 / 4 cup cocktail sauce, green onions and garlic in medium bowl
2. Mix well
3. Spread about 1 / 2 cup mixture onto 2 tortillas
4. Sprinkle evenly with remaining monterey jack cheese
5. Top with remaining tortillas
6. Melt 1 tablespoons butter on cast iron griddle over medium-high heat
7. Add one quesadilla
8. Cook for 2 minutes on each side or until golden brown
9. Repeat
10. Cut each into eight wedges
11. Serve with cocktail sauce for dipping<|endoftext|>
