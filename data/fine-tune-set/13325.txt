Give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>Acadia S Chicken Parmesan

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
• Eggs
• Milk
• Spaghetti sauce
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Wisk eggs and milk together in bowl for 1 minute
2. Place the bread crumbs in another bowl or a
3. Plastic bag
4. Dip the the chicken in the egg / milk mixture and then
5. Coat it with the bread crumbs
6. In a skillet on medium high heat brown the coated chicken in the olive oil
7. Then place the par-cooked chicken in a 9x12x2 baking dish in a
8. Single layer
9. Cover the chicken with the mozzarella cheese, then the spaghetti sauce
10. Sprinkle parmesan cheese on top of the sauce
11. In a 350 f oven bake for 30-minutes
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
