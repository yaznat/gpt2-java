Ingredients:
• Chorizo sausage
• Potato
• Eggs
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese
• Salt and pepper
• Salsa

Steps:
1. In small pan boil potatoes until soft
2. Time depends on the size of the dice
3. Drain and set aside
4. Meanwhile, remove casings from chorizo
5. Cook over medium heat in a non stick skillet until browned
6. Drain
7. But do not wipe out skillet
8. You want a small amount of the fat to remain
9. Add cooked sausage back into pan
10. Stir in potatoes
11. Cook and stir for about one minute
12. Pour in beaten eggs
13. Stir
14. When eggs are almost done add cheese
15. Stir well
16. Cook until eggs are done and cheese is melted
17. Salt and pepper to taste
18. Meanwhile, heat tortillas in microwave about 30 seconds until warm and soft
19. Divide the egg mixture into two portions and fill tortillas
20. Add salsa to taste
21. Roll up burrito style<|endoftext|>
