Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Egg
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Butter
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Instant coffee granules
• Pecans
• Semisweet chocolate chunks
• Water
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. Beat sugar, butter and egg in large bowl -- on medium speed
3. Stir in flour, baking soda, coffee granules and salt
4. Stir in pecans and chocolate chunks --
5. Drop dough by 1 / 4" cupfuls 2" apart on ungreased cookie sheet -- bake 12-15 minutes or golden brown and edges are set
6. Cool 4 minutes, remove to rack to cool completely --
7. Drizzle with coffee drizzle
8. To prepare coffee drizzle:
9. Dissolve coffee, dry, in water in small bowl -- stir in powdered sugar with spoon till smooth and thin enough to drizzle<|endoftext|>
