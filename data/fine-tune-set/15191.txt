What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Bean Salad Pizzazz

Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Wax beans
• Kidney beans
• Red onion
• Jalapeno peppers
• Grape tomatoes
• Lemon, juice of
• White wine vinegar
• Vegetable oil
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Dijon mustard

Steps:
1. Drain and rinse all beans and place in large bowl that can be used to serve the bean salad
2. Do not yet mix
3. Add the chopped onion, minced jalapeos, and grape tomatoes
4. In a smaller bowl, combine the lemon juice, white wine vinegar, vegetable oil, salt, pepper, sugars, and dijon mustard
5. Whisk until thoroughly incorporated and smooth
6. Taste and adjust dressing to your liking
7. Pour over vegetables
8. Lightly lift and toss all the vegetables with dressing until mixed
9. Chill for at least two hours before serving
10. The flavors improve and blend over time<|endoftext|>
