Ingredients:
• Milk
• Granulated sugar
• Salt
• Mashed potatoes
• Warm water
• Instant yeast
• All-purpose flour
• Raisins
• Cinnamon
• Butter

Steps:
1. Scald the milk in saucepan
2. Remove from flame and add sugar, salt, butter and mashed potatoes
3. Cool
4. Dissolve yeast in the warm water in a seperate bowl
5. Then add this to milk mixture
6. Add raisins and flour
7. Let it rise in a nice warm place for approximately 1 1 / 2 hours
8. Divide dough into 2 loaves
9. Roll out on a lightly floured surface and spread half of the filling on each roll
10. Roll up as for jelly roll and pinch edges together
11. Place in 2 greased 9x5 loaf pan and set in a warm place to rise for about an hour
12. Bake in loaf pans at 350 for about 45-55 minutes sounds hollow when tapped
13. Remove from oven and brush top with small amount of melted butter or margarine
14. Let cool in pans 10-15 minutes and then invert onto cooling rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
