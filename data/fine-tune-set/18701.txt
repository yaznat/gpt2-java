What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Bally S Casino Bread Pudding

Ingredients:
• French bread
• Raisins
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Butter
• Light brown sugar
• Salt
• Bourbon
• Heavy whipping cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Tear bread into 2-inch pieces and place in 9-by-13-inch baking dish
3. Add raisins
4. Set aside
5. In large mixing bowl, beat eggs with wire whip until well blended
6. Stir in milk, sugar and vanilla
7. Pour over torn bread and push bread down with fingers until saturated
8. Let soak for 3 minutes
9. Place pudding pan in larger pan, then fill larger pan with about one inch of hot water
10. Bake for 65 minutes
11. Meanwhile, in medium saucepan melt butter on medium heat
12. Add both sugars and beat with wire whip until creamy
13. Remove pot from heat and beat in whipping cream and salt
14. Add bourbon and beat thoroughly
15. You may pour the sauce over the entire bread pudding or over individual servings<|endoftext|>
