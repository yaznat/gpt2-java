What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Artichoke Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Garlic clove
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Basil leaves
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese
• Poblano chile

Steps:
1. With a food processor running, add the garlic clove through the feed tube until it is finely chopped
2. Add the drained artichoke hearts and the basil
3. Pulse until mixture is finely chopped
4. Spread one half of each tortilla with a few tablespoons of the artichoke mixture
5. Sprinkle about 1 / 3 cup of the grated cheese over the artichoke spread
6. Arrange a few strips of chili over the cheese
7. Fold tortillas in half over the filling and brush both sides with some of the reserved artichoke marinade
8. Heat a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat
9. Put 2 to 3 quesadillas into the skillet and cook until lightly browned
10. Turn over and cook until golden
11. Remove from skillet and cut into 4 to 6 wedges
12. Repeat with remaining quesadillas<|endoftext|>
