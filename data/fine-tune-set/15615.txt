Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning mix
• Water
• Thick & chunky salsa
• Frozen whole kernel corn
• Instant rice
• Taco blend cheese
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Cook beef in 10 inch skillet over medium heat 8 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until brown
2. Drain
3. Stir in seasoning mix, water, salsa and corn
4. Heat to boiling
5. Stir in rice
6. Boil 1 minute
7. Remove from heat
8. Cover and let stand 8 minutes
9. Fluff rice mixture with fork
10. Sprinkle with cheese
11. Cover and let stand 1 to 2 minutes or until cheese is melted
12. Sprinkle lettuce around edge of skillet
13. Sprinkle tomato in circle next to lettuce
14. Serve with sour cream<|endoftext|>
