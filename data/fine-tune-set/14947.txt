What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Hawaiian Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Carrots
• Celery
• Yellow onion
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Brown hamburger in large skillet
2. Meanwhile, prepare celery, carrots and onion
3. Drain hamburger
4. In large soup pot combine hamburger, celery, carrots, onion, soy sauce and water
5. Bring to boil, then reduce heat to simmer until vegetables are soft
6. Add rice to pot and mix well<|endoftext|>
