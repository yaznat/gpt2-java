Ingredients:
• Beets
• Carrot
• Onion
• Egg
• Salt
• Pepper
• All-purpose flour
• Olive oil
• Low-fat sour cream

Steps:
1. Combine beets, carrots and onion in large bowl
2. Mix in egg, salt and pepper
3. Add flour
4. Stir to blend well
5. Heat 1 1 / 2 tablespoons oil in heavy large skillet over medium heat
6. Using 1 / 3 cup beet mixture for each pancake, drop 4 pancakes into skillet
7. Flatten each into 3-inch round
8. Cook until brown and cooked through, about 4 minutes per side
9. Repeat with remaining beet mixture, making 4 more pancakes
10. Serve pancakes with sour cream<|endoftext|>
