Ingredients:
• Chicken thighs
• Olive oil
• Potato
• Yucca root
• Ear of corn
• Chicken stock
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Water
• Heavy cream
• Capers
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, brown chicken in oil then add to dutch oven or pressure cooker
2. Put potatoes, yucca and corn in pot and season with salt and pepper and cayenne, if using
3. Pour chicken stock into pot
4. Meanwhile, cook onions and garlic in skillet until onion is translucent, about 5 minutes
5. Pour in 1 cup water and loosen the bits stuck to the bottom
6. Pour all into pot
7. Cover pot and cook over medium heat 40 minutes for dutch oven and 30 minutes in pressure cooker
8. Once done, taste for seasoning, stir in cream, capers, and cilantro, if desired
9. Ladle into bowls and serve with an avocado on the side<|endoftext|>
