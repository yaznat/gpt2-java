Please give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>8 Hour Beef Roast

Ingredients:
• Beef roast
• Beef broth
• Salt
• Pepper
• Basil
• Garlic powder
• Rosemary
• Oregano
• Bay leaves

Steps:
1. To prepare:
2. Brown the roast on all sides in a skillet
3. Transfer the roast to a slow cooker
4. Add the broth, salt, pepper, basil, garlic powder, rosemary, oregano and bay leaves to the slow cooker
5. Add enough water to cover the roast
6. Cook, covered, on low for 8 to 10 hours
7. Discard the bay leaves
8. Remove the roast to a platter and shred if needed
9. Serve the shredded beef on buns with pan juices for dipping
10. Rump roast is preferred for this recipe
11. Serves: 10 - 12<|endoftext|>
