Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Flour
• Vanilla
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Cream together the first 2 ingredients and
2. Add eggs, sour cream
3. Sift together the flour, salt, baking powder and baking soda and gradually add to liquid mixture
4. Add vanilla
5. Pour 1 / 2 batter into a greased and floured tube pan
6. Spread 1 / 2 of topping mixture and cover with rest of batter and topping to finish
7. Bake in 350 degree oven for 45 minutes or until a tester comes out clean
8. Let cool and dust with icing sugar<|endoftext|>
