What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Acorn Squash Custard

Ingredients:
• Acorn squash
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Dijon mustard
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground allspice
• Ground nutmeg

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f line large rimmed baking sheet with foil, spray with nonstick cooking spray
2. Generously butter 10 1 / 2 cup nonstick muffing cups
3. Cut squash in half
4. Rmove seeds
5. Place cut side down, on baking sheet
6. Bake 40 minutes until almost tender
7. Turn squash cut side up, sprinkle with brown sugar and butter
8. Bake 30-35 minutes or until tender
9. Scoop flesh of squash with butter sugar mixture into a large bowl
10. With a potato masher, mash squash until smooth
11. Stir in remaining ingredients until well combined
12. Fill muffin cups with squash mixture
13. Cover and refrigerate
14. An additional 5-10 minutes baking time may be needed
15. Bake 30-35 minutes or until brown around edges and toothpick inserted in center comes out moist but not wet
16. Cool in pan 5 minutes, invert onto baking sheet
17. With spatula, gently place custards on serving platter<|endoftext|>
