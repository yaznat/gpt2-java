Please give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Apple Brie Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Onions
• Balsamic vinegar
• Sugar
• Dried thyme
• Dried rosemary
• Vegetable oil
• Flour tortillas
• Tart apples
• Brie cheese

Steps:
1. In a skillet over medium heat, cook the onions, vinegar, sugar, thyme and rosemary in oil for 10 minutes or until onions are golden
2. Spoon the mixture over half of each tortilla and top with apples and cheese
3. Fold over
4. Cook on a griddle over medium heat for 2-3 minutes on each side or until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
