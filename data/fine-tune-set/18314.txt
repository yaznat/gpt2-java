What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Blue Cornmeal Pancakes Gluten Free And Low Sugar

Ingredients:
• Rice flour
• Blue cornmeal
• Baking powder
• Salt
• White sugar
• Milk
• Eggs
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, sift together the flour, blue cornmeal, baking powder, salt and sugar
2. Make a well in the center and pour in the milk, egg and melted butter, mix until smooth
3. Heat a lightly oiled griddle to 400 degrees f or frying pan over medium high heat
4. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1 / 4 cup for each pancake
5. Brown on both sides and serve hot<|endoftext|>
