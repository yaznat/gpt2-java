Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Chipotle pepper
• Adobo sauce
• Ground beef
• Salt and pepper
• Garlic powder
• Red onion rings
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Cooked bacon
• Pickled jalapeno peppers
• Hamburger buns
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. For the chipotle mayonnaise:
2. Mix together ingredients until combined
3. For the burgers:
4. Divide the ground beef into four 1 / 3 pound burgers
5. With a light hand, shape into patties
6. Season both sides with a salt, pepper and garlic powder
7. Set aside until ready to grill
8. May, also, be pan fried
9. Heat grill to medium - medium-high heat and grill for a 4-7 minutes on each side until desired doneness
10. During the last minute of cooking, add a slice of cheese over each burger and close cover until cheese begins to melt
11. While the burgers are cooking, add the onion slices and allow them to char a bit on each side and begin to soften
12. Remove burger and onions from grill and allow to rest for 2-3 minutes before serving
13. Place a burger on each toasted bun bottom, topping with 2 slices of bacon and 4-5 jalapeno slices
14. Slather some of the chipotle mayo on the top bun and place a grilled onion round on top
15. Add lettuce, if so desired, and top with the top half of the bun<|endoftext|>
