Ingredients:
• Lamb chops
• Butter
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Curry powder
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Tomatoes
• Water
• All-purpose flour
• Asparagus spears

Steps:
1. Trim fat from meat, brown in butter and then remove from skillet
2. Add onion and garlic
3. Cook till tender, do not brown
4. Return meat to skillet
5. Add curry powder, ground ginger, salt and tomatoes
6. Cover simmer 50 to 60 minutes, or until lamb chops are tender
7. Blend flour and water
8. Add to meat mixture, cook until bubbly and then cook 1 to 2 minutes more
9. Transfer to fancy round casserole dish, form pinwheel of hot cooked asparagus on top
10. Serve with rice
11. This can be served with condiments such as sliced onions, shredded coconut, lemon slices, raisins, and peanuts<|endoftext|>
