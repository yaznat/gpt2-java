Ingredients:
• Sandwich steak
• Beer
• Flour
• Herb-ox beef flavor cube
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Chop onion and garlic and set aside
2. Pound steaks with meat mallet
3. Season with salt and pepper
4. Dredge in flour, and set aside
5. In a non stick pan, heat 4 tablespoons of olive oil
6. Fry steaks in oil just until brown
7. Remove steaks from pan
8. While pan is still hot, add remaining 2 tables spoons of oil
9. Add chopped onion and let cook 5 minutes
10. Add garlic and let saut for 2 minutes
11. Add beer
12. Dissolve bouillon cube in beer
13. Once the cube is dissolved, add steaks back to pan
14. Let everything slowly simmer for about 30 minutes or until braising liquid has thickened up
15. If sauce needs to be seasoned, season it after it has thickened<|endoftext|>
