What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Accordion Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Italian bread
• Mayonnaise
• Italian dressing
• Parmesan cheese
• Cooked chicken
• Ham
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 375f
2. Cut bread into 16 slices, cutting to, but not through the bottom crust
3. In a small bowl stir together mayonnaise, italian dressing and parmesan
4. Tuck chicken or turkey, ham and cheese slices between every other bread slice
5. Place a well rounded teaspoon of mayonnaise mixture in the slits with the meat, spreading slightly
6. Place on a baking sheet
7. Bake loaf in the 375f oven about 15 minutes or until hot
8. For easier preparation, try putting the mayonnaise mixture on before the meat and cheese<|endoftext|>
