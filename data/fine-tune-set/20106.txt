What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Acadia S Bell Pepper Onion Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Bell pepper
• Sweet onion
• Olive oil
• Worcestershire sauce
• Soy sauce
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Chop up bell pepper into bite sized pieces
2. Chop or slice onion
3. In wok or skillet over medium low heat add olive oil
4. Saute onion for 10 minutes or until translucent
5. Stir frequently
6. Add soy sauce
7. Add bell pepper and saut for an additional 15 minutes or until bell pepper is tender
8. Add worcestershire sauce
9. Stir and cook for 1-2 minutes longer
10. Add salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
