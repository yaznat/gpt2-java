What's a good smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Berry Wonderful Berry Banana Vanilla Soy Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Frozen strawberries
• Non-fat vanilla yogurt
• Vanilla-flavored soymilk
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Starting with banana's put all ingredients except ice into blender
2. Blend until smooth and creamy, about 3-4 minutes
3. Add ice cubes, this will thicken and lighten the color of your smoothie
4. Pour into glasses, and use a straw
5. Yum!<|endoftext|>
