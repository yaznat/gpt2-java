Give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Basic Beef Stew

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Stewing beef
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Water
• Tomato sauce
• Bay leaves
• Thyme leaves
• Salt
• Pepper
• Carrot
• Potatoes
• Frozen peas
• Cornstarch
• Cold water
• Maggi seasoning

Steps:
1. In large dutch oven heat oil over medium heat
2. Add meat
3. Brown well
4. Add onion and garlic
5. Saute until transparent
6. Add 4 cups water and tomato sauce
7. Add bay leaves, thyme, salt and pepper
8. Bring to a boil
9. Cover, reduce heat and simmer 45 minutes
10. Add carrots, potatoes and peas
11. Cover and cook 30 minutes on medium low or until vegetables are tender
12. Add corn starch slurry if sauce is too watery
13. Bring stew to a boil, stirring constantly
14. Boil 1 minute
15. Add maggi seasoning and serve with crusty rolls
16. However, please check your pressure cooker instruction booklet for proper times
17. Each pressure cooker is different
18. Mine reduces time by 1 / 3
19. Some others reduce cooking time by 2 / 3<|endoftext|>
