Can you give me a steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Round Steak Roll Up

Ingredients:
• Boneless beef round steak
• Carrot
• Zucchini
• Sweet red pepper
• Green pepper
• Green onion
• Parmesan cheese
• Fresh parsley
• Garlic
• Salt & pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Meatless sauce
• Pasta

Steps:
1. Cut the meat into 6 pieces and pound each piece to about 1 / 4 inch thickness
2. Combine the vegetables, parmesan cheese and seasonings: place 1 / 3 cup in the center of each piece
3. Roll meat up around filling and secure them with toothpicks
4. Brown the roll ups in oil in a skillet
5. Transfer the roll ups to a 5 qt
6. Slow cooker and top with spaghetti sauce
7. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours or until the meat is tender
8. Cook pasta
9. Take the toothpick out and serve roll up over pasta
10. Sprinkle with additional parmesan if desired<|endoftext|>
