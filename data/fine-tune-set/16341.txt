Ingredients:
• Idaho potatoes
• Canola oil
• Smoked bacon
• Toothpicks
• Butter
• Honey
• Cracked black pepper
• Scallions
• Fresh parsley leaves

Steps:
1. Cut each potato into 8 wedges and parboil until fork tender
2. In a preheated large saute pan, heat oil on medium-high heat
3. Wrap each potato wedge with 1 strip of bacon and secure with 1 toothpick on each end
4. Pan-fry in oil until bacon and potato are golden brown, approximately 8 to 10 minutes, turning frequently with tongs to brown all sides evenly
5. Remove and drain on paper towels
6. Meanwhile, in a medium bowl, mix butter, honey, black pepper, scallions and parsley
7. Stir with a spoon until fully mixed
8. Toss potatoes in the glaze
9. Remove toothpicks and serve<|endoftext|>
