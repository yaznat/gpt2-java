Give me a beef recipe.<|endoftext|>Ann S Shopping Trip Beef Stew

Ingredients:
• Stew meat
• Tomatoes
• Mushrooms
• Soft breadcrumbs
• Tapioca
• Green peas
• Onions
• Carrots
• Celery
• Green pepper
• Salt
• Sugar
• Burgundy wine

Steps:
1. In the bottom of a large casserole, spread the stew meat
2. In the order listed above, add remaining ingredients
3. Cover and cook at 250f for 4 to 5 hours
4. Stir after 3 hours
5. This can stay in the oven longer until needed, or may be reheated
6. Freezes well<|endoftext|>
