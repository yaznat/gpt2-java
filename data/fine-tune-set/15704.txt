Ingredients:
• Taco shells
• Cooked chicken
• Taco seasoning mix
• Ranch dressing
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325 degrees
2. Heat taco shells in oven as directed on package
3. Meanwhile, in a medium microwavable bowl, place chicken
4. Sprinkle with taco seasoning mix
5. Toss gently to coat
6. Microwave uncovered on high for 2 to 3 minutes or until hot
7. Stir in 1 / 2 cup of the ranch dressing
8. Spoon warm chicken mixture into heated taco shells
9. Top with lettuce, tomato, cheese and green onion
10. Drizzle with additional ranch dressing if desired<|endoftext|>
