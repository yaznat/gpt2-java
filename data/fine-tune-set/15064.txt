Give me a pizza recipe.<|endoftext|>7 Layer Taco Pizza

Ingredients:
• Pinto beans
• Fat free tortillas
• Salsa
• Romaine lettuce
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Tomatoes
• Scallions

Steps:
1. Drain the pinto beans, reserving 2 tablespoons of the liquid
2. Rinse the beans and puree with the reserved liquid in a blender or food processor
3. Spray a 7 inch nonstick skillet with nonstick spray and set over medium high heat
4. Add 1 tortilla and cook until golden, 1-2 minutes on each side
5. Repeat with remaining 3 tortillas
6. Place a tortilla on each of 4 plates
7. Spread the bean puree evenly on each tortilla
8. Then top each evenly with the salsa, lettuce, cheese and tomato<|endoftext|>
