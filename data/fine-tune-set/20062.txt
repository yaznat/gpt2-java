What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Shrimp Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Shrimp
• Spanish onion
• Garlic cloves
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Heat oil in 8 inch skillet
2. Fry garlic until golden and discard
3. Add onion and saute until transparent
4. Add shrimp and saute until they turn pink
5. Beat eggs with salt and pepper in a large bowl until frothy
6. Add shrimp mixture and stir well
7. Return to pan, adding more oil if necessary
8. Cook over medium heat until eggs are set
9. Invert plate over the pan, flip omelette onto plate, return to the pan to brown on other side
10. Repeat process one more time
11. Serve hot
12. Garnish with diced tomatoes and shredded cheese<|endoftext|>
