Give me a custard recipe.<|endoftext|>Hill Country Peach Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• 9-inch deep dish pie crust
• Peaches
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Beat the eggs
2. Add the sugar and milk and beat well
3. Add the peaches and stir
4. Pour into the pie crust and bake at 450 for 10 minutes
5. Turn oven down to 375 and bake for another 45-55 minutes, or until tests clean<|endoftext|>
