Give me a stir fry recipe.<|endoftext|>Beef Stir Fry Asian Style

Ingredients:
• Cornflour
• Mirin
• Sesame oil
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Rump steak
• Peanut oil
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Fresh ginger
• Broccoli florets
• Green beans
• Water

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl add 'marinade ingredients' and mix well
2. Add the meat to the bowl and mix, making ensure all the slices are evenly coated
3. Set aside for 15 minutes so the beef could absorb the flavours
4. Heat wok or frypan, add oil, heat and add onion, garlic and ginger cook until soft but not browned
5. Add thinly sliced beef with the marinade and cook over a high heat for 3 minutes
6. Add the greens of your choice and beans
7. Add water to the wok and mix scrapping the sauce on the bottom of the wok
8. Cook until the vegetables are just tender but retain their vibrant green colour
9. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
