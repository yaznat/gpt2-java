Give me a wrap recipe.<|endoftext|>Barbecue Ranch Club Wraps

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Smoked turkey
• Smoked ham
• Mixed salad greens
• Monterey jack and cheddar cheese blend
• Diced tomato
• Pepper bacon
• Ranch dressing
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Place the flour tortillas on a flat surface
2. In a large mixing bowl, toss salad mix, bacon and diced tomatoes with desired amount of dressing
3. Place one slice of smoked turkey and smoked ham on each tortilla
4. Divide the tossed salad among the four tortillas, mounding the salad in the middle of each tortilla
5. Top each salad with even amounts of shredded cheese and remaining slices of turkey and ham, divided evenly among the tortillas
6. Fold the 2 sides of the tortilla over the salad to close the ends, then fold the bottom edge of the tortilla all the way over the salad and meats and cheese, completely covering it
7. Roll the wrap tightly all the way to the top edge until a burrito-like package is formed
8. Cut each wrap sandwich into fourths and secure with long frilled toothpicks
9. Display on a party tray with extra barbecue ranch dressing for spooning onto wraps<|endoftext|>
