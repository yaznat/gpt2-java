What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Busy Night Microwave Wet Burritos

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Pinto beans
• Salt
• Onion
• Flour tortillas
• Green enchilada sauce
• Cheddar cheese
• Avocado
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce
• Hot sauce
• Jalapeno

Steps:
1. Brown the beef and drain
2. Add taco seasoning and 1 / 2 cup water and cook until all liquid is gone
3. Meanwhile, drain about half of the liquid off of the pinto beans and then heat in a pan
4. When beans start to simmer, lightly mash until you get the desired consistency
5. Season to taste with salt, about 1 teaspoon
6. Lightly heat one tortilla over burner until pliable
7. Spread some beans down the center or tortilla and top with some seasoned meat
8. Sprinkle on some onions and any other options you'd like
9. Tuck in sides of tortilla and then roll up
10. Place seamside down on a microwave safe plate
11. Spoon some enchilada sauce over burrito and top with lots of grated cheese
12. Lightly cover with micro-safe paper and cook on high for about 2 minutes, until the cheese is nice and melted
13. Repeat for remaining burritos
14. Serve with sour cream or scattered tomatoes or anything you'd like!<|endoftext|>
