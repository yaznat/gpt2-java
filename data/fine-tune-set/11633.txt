Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Baking soda
• Buttermilk
• Margarine
• Crisco
• Cocoa
• Boiling water

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees
2. Mix first 6 items
3. Melt other 4 and add
4. Pour in a 9x13 pan
5. Bake in a 350 oven for 38 minutes
6. That's it
7. You don't have to grease and flour the pan
8. You don't have to cream anything
9. It is so simple there should be a law against it
10. Just be sure it has cooked through into the middle of the top
11. It may take a few minutes more or less in your oven
