Give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>Ohana Breakfast Bread

Ingredients:
• Warm water
• Egg yolk
• Butter
• Bread flour
• Salt
• Sugar
• Dry yeast
• Crushed pineapple
• Shredded coconut
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Pineapple / coconut procedure
2. Combine drained pineapple and coconut in bowl
3. Combine 1 cup sugar and cornstarch in separate bowl and mix well
4. Add sugar and cornstarch mixture with pineapple / coconut mixture and mix well
5. Refrigerate for 1 hour
6. Bread procedure
7. Mix water, 1 tbsp sugar, and yeast in small bowl
8. Let proof for 5 minutes
9. Add egg yolk to yeast mixture
10. Put flour in food processor with bread blade
11. Add salt, butter, and remainder of sugar and process until incorporated
12. While processor is running, slowly add yeast mixture through shoot in top of processor
13. Process until a soft ball forms and then run processor for about 2 min longer
14. Transfer dough to bowl coated with cooking spray, cover with plastic wrap sprayed with cooking spray and let rise in warm area until it doubles in size
15. Roll dough out onto floured surface until it is a 2" thick rectangle
16. Spread the pineapple / coconut mixture over top of dough
17. Fold dough onto itself and cut into pieces with pizza cutter
18. Place pieces into greased 9 x 13 pan to cover
19. Cover pan with plastic wrap coated with cooking spray and let rise in warm place--about 1 / 2 way up pan
20. Bake at 325f for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown
21. Let cool, cut and serve<|endoftext|>
