Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Pineapple Fried Rice With Sprimp And Ham

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Eggs
• Bacon
• Onion
• Small shrimp
• Ham
• Green onions
• Long-grain rice
• Chicken broth
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Pineapple
• Unsalted cashews
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. In a wok or a large skillet over medium-high heat, add oil, swirling to coat the sides of wok
2. Pour in eggs, tilting pan to coat
3. Cook just until the eggs are set
4. Remove from pan and allow to cool slightly then cut into pieces
5. Add more oil to the wok swirling to coat sides
6. Add in the bacon
7. Cook for 1 minute or until the bacon is limp and translucent
8. Add in shrimp, ham, green onions and rice until well blended
9. Stir in broth, soy sauce, sesame oil and salt
10. Cook and toss for 2 more minutes, or until the rice is heated
11. Remove from heat
12. Add in the chopped pineapple, egg pieces and nuts toss to mix
13. Season with black pepper and salt
14. Place in a serving bowl<|endoftext|>
