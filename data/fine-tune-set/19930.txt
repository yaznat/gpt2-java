Ingredients:
• Butter
• Egg
• Cheese
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Place butter in egg coddler and place in a pan of hot water that comes halfway up the coddler
2. Beat egg white until stiff
3. In a separate small bowl beat egg yolk, add cheese and seasonings and fold mixture into the beaten egg whites
4. Feel free to add optional ingredients you might add in a regular omelette but keep the proportion small
5. Pour into egg coddler and screw on the lid
6. Place in simmering pan of water and cook for 15-20 minutes
7. Serve in coddler<|endoftext|>
