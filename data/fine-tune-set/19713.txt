Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Beer Batter Seasoned Fries Potatoes Vegetables

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Cauliflower
• Flour
• Cornstarch
• Baking powder
• Seasoning
• Eggs
• Beer
• Oil
• Pam cooking spray
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Wrap towels around the cut potatoes, pat dry
2. Cut thin slices of each floret, across the grain
3. In large mixing bowl, mix flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and all seasoning
4. Blend well
5. In medium mixing bowl, whisk eggs until frothy, then add beer and mix
6. Add liquid to dry mixture and beat until blended
7. Batter must not be runny
8. If it is too thick add a tbsp of beer at a time until it is batter that will hold to the vegetables
9. Heat oil to 375f dip 6 to 8 potatoes slices into batter at a time, the same with the cauliflower slices
10. Fry until golden brown
11. Place each batch in a large warmed plate with cover
12. Do not use an ale or strong flavored beer
13. This batter can be used with sliced yams, long 2 inch strips and fish if desired
14. Some like to squirt lemon juice on the slices, then dip in sauce of their choice or eat with just the lemon juice and seasoned batter<|endoftext|>
