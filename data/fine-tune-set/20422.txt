Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Amazing Broccoli Cheese Casserole

Ingredients:
• Frozen chopped broccoli
• Mushroom soup
• Mayonnaise
• Eggs
• Onion
• Salt and pepper
• Butter
• Stove top stuffing mix
• Cheese whiz

Steps:
1. Cook broccoli 10 minute and drain well
2. In large bowl combine soup, mayo, eggs, onion, cheese whiz, salt and pepper
3. Stir in broccoli and mix well
4. Spoon into 9x13 inches baking dish
5. Sprinkle with cheese
6. Combine 1 / 4 cup melted butter and stuffing and sprinkle over broccoli
7. Bake, covered for 45 minutes in 350 degree oven<|endoftext|>
