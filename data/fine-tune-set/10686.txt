Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Fresh asparagus
• Olive oil
• Red bell pepper
• Garlic
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Salt
• Pepper
• Fresh basil
• Parmesan cheese
• Butter
• Pecan halves
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Prepare pasta according to package direction
2. Rinse and drain
3. Snap off tough ends of asparagus, and slice into 2-inch pieces
4. Saut asparagus in hot oil in a large skillet over medium heat
5. 4 minutes
6. Stir in red bell pepper and garlic
7. Cook, stirring occasionally, for an additional 2 minutes
8. Stir in chicken broth and bring to a boil
9. Reduce heat, and simmer 2 minutes or until asparagus is crisp-tender
10. Stir in salt and pepper
11. Toss together pasta, asparagus mixture, basil, 1 / 2 cup cheese, butter and 1 / 2 cup pecans
12. Sprinkle evenly with remaining 1 / 4 cup cheese and 1 / 2 cup pecans
13. Garnish if desired
14. Serves 6<|endoftext|>
