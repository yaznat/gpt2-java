Please give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>The Cake Of Wisdom

Ingredients:
• Carrots
• Ginger
• Lemon peel
• Eggs
• Honey
• Cinnamon
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Ground hazelnuts
• Cream cheese

Steps:
1. Clean the carrots and grate them finely
2. Add grated ginger and lemon peel
3. Preheat the oven to 180c grease a spring form
4. Divide the eggs
5. Cream egg yolks with 2 tbs warm water and the honey
6. Add cinnamon and bread crumbs as well as the carrot mixture and the hazelnuts
7. Beat the egg whites
8. Fold under dough
9. Fill the dough into the spring form and bake for approximately 40 minutes
10. Let cool for 20 minutes and then spread with icing<|endoftext|>
