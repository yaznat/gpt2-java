Give me a recipe for pudding.<|endoftext|>Barossan Rote Grutze Red Grape Sago Pudding

Ingredients:
• Grape juice
• Sago

Steps:
1. Extraction of juice from grapes: either use the finest setting on a food mill or puree the grapes in a food processor and strain the juice through a sieve, pushing down on the solids to extract as much juice as you can
2. Measure the juice into a non-reactive saucepan and bring it gently to a simmer
3. For every 550 ml of juice, gradually stir in 2 tablespoons sago
4. Simmer gently, stirring occasionally, until sago is clear and pearl-like
5. Watch as it can stick easily
6. This should take 45-50 minutes
7. Pour the rote grutze into a serving dish and allow it to cool
8. Chill, allowing it to set slightly
9. Serve with beautiful rich, runny cream<|endoftext|>
