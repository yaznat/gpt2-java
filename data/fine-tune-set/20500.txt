Ingredients:
• Rice
• Asparagus
• Butter
• Flour
• Evaporated milk
• Water
• Salt
• Pepper
• Worcestershire sauce
• Cheddar cheese
• Ham
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Place cooked rice in the bottom of a well greased 10x6 baking dish
2. Top with sliced asparagus
3. Melt butter in a saucepan
4. Blend in flour
5. Add evaporated milk and water
6. Cook until thickened
7. Turn off heat
8. Add salt, pepper, and worcestershire sauce
9. Add cheese and ham
10. Pour over rice mixture
11. Sprinkle with almonds
12. Bake at 375 degrees fahrenheit for 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
