Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Granulated sugar
• Salt
• Vanilla extract
• Lemon peel
• Orange peel
• Ground nutmeg
• Heavy cream
• Powdered sugar
• Fresh blueberries

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, combine eggs, milk, sugar and salt
2. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until custard is slightly thickened and coats the back of a spoon
3. Remove custard from the heat
4. Add vanilla, citrus peels and nutmeg
5. Mix well
6. Cool for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. In a small mixing bowl, whip the cream and powdered sugar until stiff
8. Fold two-thirds of the whipped cream into the cooled custard
9. Layer custard and blueberries in 4 parfait glasses
10. Garnish with remaining whipped cream
11. Chill for 1 hour before serving
