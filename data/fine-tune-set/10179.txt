Ingredients:
• Macaroni
• Lean ground beef
• Minced garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Dried oregano
• Salt and pepper
• Condensed tomato soup
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Stove top directions:
2. Cook macaroni according to package directions
3. Brown meat in skillet and drain off all fat
4. Add garlic, chile powder, cumin, oregano, salt and pepper
5. Mix well and cook on low heat, about 5 minutes
6. Add cooked macaroni and condensed tomato soup
7. Heat thoroughly
8. Just before serving, add grated cheese, cover skillet and heat until cheese is melted
9. Optional microwave directions:
10. Place macaroni in deep microwave dish, and cover with ample boiling water
11. Microwave, uncovered, on high for about 5 minutes
12. Test for doneness, and cook longer if required until soft
13. Drain
14. Place ground beef in a plastic, food safe colander over a pie plate and microwave on high, stirring occasionally to break up, for about 2-3 minutes, or until all pink is gone
15. Discard fat
16. Mix all ingredients, except cheese, and heat on high for 2-3 minutes
17. Just before serving, add cheese and melt on high for 3-4 minutes<|endoftext|>
