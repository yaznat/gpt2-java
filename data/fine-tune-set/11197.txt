Ingredients:
• Boneless pork roast
• Onion
• Barbecue sauce
• Honey
• Sandwich bun

Steps:
1. Place roast in greased 3 1 / 2 to 5-quart slow cooker
2. Combine onion, barbecue sauce, and honey
3. Pour mixture over meat
4. Cover and cook on low heat 6-8 hours
5. When roast is done, use two forks to shred meat
6. Serve on sandwich rolls<|endoftext|>
