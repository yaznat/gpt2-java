Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Chopped tomato
• Green chilies
• Sliced ripe olives
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Pepper
• Bisquick
• Milk
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Spray a 9-inch pie plate with non-stick cooking spray
2. Set aside
3. In a large skillet, melt butter over medium heat
4. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender
5. Remove skillet from heat
6. Stir in tomato, chiles, olives, 1 cup cheese, cumin, and salt and pepper
7. Spread mixture evenly into pie plate
8. In a bowl, add the bisquick, milk, and eggs
9. Stir until well blended
10. Pour batter over vegetable mixture
11. Bake at 400 degrees, uncovered, for 30 minutes
12. Sprinkle with remainder of cheese and bake 3-5 more minutes or until knife comes out clean
13. Let stand 10 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
