Ingredients:
• Onion
• Canola oil
• Eggs
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Green onion
• Red bell pepper
• Rice
• Peas
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Heat a large skillet over medium high heat
2. Add 1 teaspoon oil and onions, cook until onions begin to brown around the edges, about 7 minutes
3. Add eggs and cook until set, about 2 minutes
4. Set aside
5. Add 1 tablespoon oil, add chicken and cook until lightly browned, stirring occasionally, about 5 or 6 minutes
6. Set aside with eggs
7. Add remaining oil to the skillet and cook the ginger, garlic, and red pepper until softened, about 2 minutes
8. Add rice and peas, cook an additional 2 minutes
9. Stir in eggs, chicken, soy sauce and sesame oil
10. Cook until heated through, about 3 to 5 minutes
11. Sprinkle with green onions and serve<|endoftext|>
