What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Delicious Zucchini French Fries

Ingredients:
• Zucchini
• Lemon
• Flour
• Cornmeal
• Black pepper
• Seasoning salt
• Ground nutmeg
• Oil

Steps:
1. Wash, top and tail the small zucchinis
2. Slice each in half, then slice into julienne strips, thin, like thin french fries
3. At this stage they can be put into a plastic bag or bowl, and kept until needed
4. To prepare: squeeze the juice from 1 / 2 of the lemon -- you should have about 3 - 4 tablespoons
5. In a bowl, toss the zucchini strips, using a large spoon, with the lemon juice, until damp
6. Use more lemon juice if necessary
7. Mix the flour and cornflour in a bowl, and add the black pepper and seasoning salt
8. Chuck this flour mixture into the bowl of zucchini fries, and toss until more or less coated -- neat work is not necessary
9. Heat about 1 inch of oil in a medium saucepan, preferably with heavy bottom
10. When hot, using a slotted metal spoon, add a good heaped spoonful of zucchini strips to the oil
11. You can fry them until just done or you can fry them until golden brown
12. They do not colour evenly, which doesn't matter
13. Put out a few thicknesses of kitchen paper towels next to the pot
14. When the zucchini strips turn colour, take out with slotted spoon and let drain on the kitchen paper
15. Repeat process
16. The quantity given above was about 4 batches to fry
17. Flaky sea salt on unseasoned fries is delicious<|endoftext|>
