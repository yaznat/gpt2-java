Give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>5 Minute Low Fat Bean Dip

Ingredients:
• Pinto beans
• Salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Drain the beans
2. Pour everything except for the cheese into a blender
3. Blend until the mix is smooth
4. Pour into a microwave safe bowl
5. Cover and cook on high for 3 minutes, stirring once
6. Add cheese to the top while the dip is hot
7. Allow to cool for 5 minutes and serve<|endoftext|>
