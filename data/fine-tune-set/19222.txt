Ingredients:
• Whipping cream
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Instant coffee
• Pure vanilla extract
• Coffee ice cream
• Chocolate cookie pie crust
• Chocolate sandwich style cookies
• Chocolate ice cream

Steps:
1. In a saucepan bring the whipping to a mild simmer and remove from heat
2. Whisk in the chocolate chips till they melt
3. Add coffee and vanilla and whisk in till well mixed
4. Let mixture cool
5. Soften coffee ice cream to a spreadable consistency in a microwave oven
6. Spread ice cream into the crust
7. Cover with 3 / 4 of the chocolate sauce
8. Top with the crushed cookies
9. Freeze 20 to 30 minutes, till firm
10. Place scoops of the chocolate ice cream all around the edge of pie
11. Place a whole cookie between each scoop and drizzle 1 / 3 of the chocolate sauce over all
12. Freeze pie at least 2 hours
13. Cover and chill remaining sauce
14. When ready to serve pie reheat the sauce and serve it separately to pour over the pie<|endoftext|>
