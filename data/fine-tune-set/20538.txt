What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Baby S Squash Casserole

Ingredients:
• Squash
• Margarine
• Eggs
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Onion
• Cheddar cheese
• Milk
• Cracker crumbs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Combine all ingredients and mix well
3. Pour into pam sprayed dish
4. Bake for 40-45 minutes
5. Can make this into two smaller dishes and freeze one<|endoftext|>
