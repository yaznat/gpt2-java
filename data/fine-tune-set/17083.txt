Ingredients:
• Crawfish
• Heinz chili sauce
• Catsup
• Horseradish
• Lea & perrins worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Lemon juice
• Parsley
• Louisiana hot sauce

Steps:
1. Combine all sauce ingredients to make sauce
2. Pour over crawfish or shrimp, or dip the crawfish or shrimp in the sauce<|endoftext|>
