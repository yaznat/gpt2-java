Give me a recipe for fish.<|endoftext|>Basic Fish Cure Bbq Rub

Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Coarse salt
• Black pepper
• Dried dill
• Mustard powder

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl with your fingers
2. If using as a seasoning, just sprinkle on the fish before grilling
3. If using as a true rub, sprinkle on the fish and rub with your fingers
4. Let sit for 2 hours up to a day in advance prior to grilling<|endoftext|>
