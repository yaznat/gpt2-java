What's a recipe for a wrap?<|endoftext|>Avocado Salad Lettuce Wraps

Ingredients:
• Avocados
• Tomatoes
• Jalapeno pepper
• Red onions
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Fresh cilantro
• Ear of corn
• Fresh lime juice
• Romaine lettuce leaves

Steps:
1. In a medium sized bowl, mash the avocado
2. Add the remaining ingredients and stir until well mixed
3. Spread 2-3 tablespoons of the mixture onto lettuce leaves and wrap
4. I like to use butter lettuce or red leaf lettuce
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
