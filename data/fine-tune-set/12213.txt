What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Breakfast Quinoa

Ingredients:
• Quinoa
• Almond milk
• Apples
• Coconut sugar
• Cinnamon
• Allspice
• Clove
• Nutmeg
• Vanilla extract
• Salt
• Pecans
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Add everything except for pecans and maple syrup to a saucepan
2. Bring to a boil, cover and reduce to simmer for 20-25 minutes
3. When the apples are soft, and quinoa has absorbed the water, fluff the quinoa with a fork
4. Pour into bowls and top with maple syrup and pecans<|endoftext|>
