Give me a potato recipe.<|endoftext|>Armenian Bulgur And Potato Balls

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Bulgur
• Flour
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Oil
• Onions
• Olive oil
• Ground allspice
• Pine nuts
• Currants
• Tahini

Steps:
1. Scrub potatoes
2. Boil in the skins until tender, then drain, remove skins, and mash
3. Place bulgur in a small bowl and cover with cold water and set aside for 5 minutes
4. Then drain in a sieve, pressing to get all the water out as possible
5. Mix together potato, bulgur, flour, salt and pepper, blending into a thick paste
6. Place onion and oil in a skillet and saut until onion is tender, then place in a bowl with remaining filling ingredients
7. Season to taste with salt and pepper
8. Shape bulgur / potato mixture into balls and flatten in your hands
9. Place a teaspoon or so of filling in the center of the flattened potato mixture and close up the potato part around it, forming a smooth oval shaped ball
10. Repeat with remaining paste and filling
11. Heat oil and deep fry balls several at a time for about 6-8 minutes, turning to brown both sides
12. Drain on paper toweling before serving
13. Serve hot or warm<|endoftext|>
