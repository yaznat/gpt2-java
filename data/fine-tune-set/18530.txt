Generate a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Hilde S Norwegian Waffles

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Butter
• Baking soda
• Soured milk
• Flour

Steps:
1. Stir baking soda into 1 cup soured milk
2. Melt and cool the butter
3. Whisk together eggs, sugar and vanilla
4. Whisk in melted, cooled butter
5. Add in flour and baking soda / soured milk
6. Stir
7. Add the rest of the soured milk
8. You might need to add a bit extra but the batter should be pretty thick
9. You should make the waffles right away but you can keep the batter in the fridge until the next day<|endoftext|>
