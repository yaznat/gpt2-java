Ingredients:
• Cake mix
• Eggs
• Crisco

Steps:
1. Toss all ingredients into a bowl, using enough crisco to form a stiff dough
2. Form into little balls, place on a baking sheet 2 inches apart, gently flatten the balls, and bake at 350f for 8-10 minutes, till puffy, and just barely golden around the edges
3. Remove from heat, cool 5 minutes before removing to a rack to cool
4. They will shrink down a little in height and form a perfect cookie!<|endoftext|>
