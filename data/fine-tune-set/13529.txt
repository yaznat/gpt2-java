Ingredients:
• Salt
• Asparagus spears
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• Mirin
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic clove
• Green onion
• Beef tenderloin
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat grill pan until hot
2. Bring salted water to a boil over medium high heat in a saucepan
3. Blanch asparagus spears for 1 minute
4. Drain and plunge in an ice water bath to stop cooking
5. In a small saucepan combine soy sauce, sugar, mirin, ginger, and garlic
6. Heat over medium heat until reduced and slightly thickened
7. Set aside to cool
8. Cut green onions in fourths, lengthwise, so you have long strips
9. Slice beef in 1 / 4 inch slices, then pound to tenderize and thin out
10. Dip beef slice in soy sauce mixture and place on a clean work surface
11. Season with pepper
12. Place an asparagus spear and a piece of green onion on the beef slice and roll up
13. Do the same with the remaining asparagus and beef
14. Grill beef rolls about 2 minutes until slightly charred and medium rare
15. Turn several times and brush with soy sauce mixture
16. Remove to a serving platter and drizzle generously with soy sauce mixture<|endoftext|>
