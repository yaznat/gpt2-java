Ingredients:
• 2% low-fat milk
• Sugar
• Egg yolks
• Nonfat dry milk powder
• Vanilla extract
• Banana
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Combine 1 cup milk, sugar, and egg yolks in a small heavy saucepan
2. Stir well with a whisk
3. Place over medium heat, stirring constantly
4. Cook for 8 minutes or until mixture reaches 180 and is slightly thickened
5. Remove from heat
6. Pour custard into a cool bowl
7. Place over ice water to cool completely
8. Combine 1 / 2 cup milk, dry milk, and vanilla
9. Stir in custard, banana, and syrup
10. Cover and chill
11. Pour mixture into the freezer can of an ice-cream freezer
12. Freeze according to manufacturer's instructions
13. Spoon ice cream into a freezer-safe container
14. Cover and freeze 1 hour or until firm<|endoftext|>
