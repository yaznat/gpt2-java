Give me a sandwich recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Cream Dream Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Pumpernickel bread
• Cream cheese
• Apples
• Raisins
• Pecans
• Celery
• Shredded cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Toast bread lightly first
2. Spread bread slices with cream cheese
3. Place apple slices on top of the cream cheese on one of the toast slices
4. Sprinkle raisins, pecans, celery, and cheese to taste on the other piece of toast, pressing a little so these items stick to the cream cheese a bit
5. Put sandwich together, securing with a toothpick<|endoftext|>
