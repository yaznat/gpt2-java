Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Sun Dried Tomatoes Goat Cheese

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Salt
• Oil
• Yellow onion
• Garlic cloves
• Tomato paste
• White wine
• Sun-dried tomatoes
• Goat cheese
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Bring lightly salted water to a boil
2. Add in pasta, cooking 5-6 minutes
3. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water
4. Drain pasta
5. In saucepan, saute onion in sun-dried tomato oil until fragrant
6. Add in garlic, salt, and tomato paste
7. Cook over medium-high heat
8. When onions are tender, add wine to cover
9. Simmer until wine has mostly cooked away
10. Add in tomatoes, and cook until warmed through
11. Toss pasta with sauce, adding in reserved pasta water as needed
12. Top with goat cheese and parsley<|endoftext|>
