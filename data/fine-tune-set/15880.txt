Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Cooked chicken
• Long grain white rice
• Taco seasoning
• Water
• Tomato sauce
• Tomatoes
• Green onion

Steps:
1. In a large skillet place chicken, rice, seasonings, water, and tomato sauce
2. Bring mixture to a boil
3. Cover and simmer 20 minutes, or until rice is fully cooked
4. Add chopped tomato and green onion to combine evenly
5. Place 1 / 2 cup of mixture in each tortilla and serve with whatever you like
