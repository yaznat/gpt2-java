What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Blueberry Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Blueberries
• Orange zest
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Combine flour and whole wheat flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in mixing bowl and stir well
2. Whisk together eggs, milk, melted butter and vanilla
3. Mix flour mixture with egg mixture mixing until smooth
4. Fold in blueberries orange zest and pecans
5. Let batter sit for a few minutes
6. Prepare according to manufacters directions in waffle maker
7. Serve with maple syrup<|endoftext|>
