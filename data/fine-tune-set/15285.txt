What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Little Kick Jalapeno Burgers

Ingredients:
• Jalapeno peppers
• Fresh cilantro
• Light beer
• Hot pepper sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Pepper
• Salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Lean ground turkey
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Dinner rolls
• Salsa
• Fat free sour cream
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the first eight ingredients
2. Crumble turkey over mixture and mix well
3. Shape into 6 patties
4. Coat grill rack with cooking spray before starting the grill
5. Grill burgers, covered, over medium heat for 2-3 minutes on each side or until no longer pink
6. Top with cheese
7. Cover and grill 1-2 minutes longer or until cheese is melted
8. Serve on rolls with salsa sour cream and lettuce<|endoftext|>
