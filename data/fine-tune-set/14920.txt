Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Vegetable oil
• Sesame oil
• Soy sauce
• Eggs
• Green onions
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Heat vegetable oil and sesame oil in wok or large skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add cooked rice and stir until rice is coated and heated through
3. Stir in soy sauce, then eggs and green onions
4. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds, if desired<|endoftext|>
