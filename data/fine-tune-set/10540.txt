Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Onion
• Rotini pasta
• Water
• Spaghetti sauce
• Fresh mushrooms
• Red pepper
• Part-skim mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Brown meat in dutch oven or deep large skillet
2. Drain
3. Add onions
4. Cook and stir 5 minute or until crisp-tender
5. Stir in pasta, water and sauce
6. Bring to boil
7. Cover
8. Simmer on low heat 15 min

10. Add vegetables
