What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Apple Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Apple
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Mix butter, sugars, eggs and vanilla
3. Mix in flour, baking powder and salt
4. Stir in apples and nuts
5. Spread into a greased 9x9x2-inch baking pan
6. Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until toothpick inserted into center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
