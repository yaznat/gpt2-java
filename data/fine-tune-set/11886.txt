What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Cape Chicken Curry

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Vegetable oil
• Cardamom pods
• Cassia
• Tomatoes
• Roasting chicken
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh gingerroot
• Green chili pepper
• Ground cumin
• Ground coriander
• Masala
• Turmeric
• Curry leaf
• Fresh coriander leaves
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan fry the onion with cardamom pods cand cassia until soft
2. Add tomato and simmer for 5 minutes
3. Add chicken and simmer for 10 minutes
4. Pound garlic, ginger and chili together to form a paste
5. Add chicken together with cumin, coriander, masala and turmeric
6. Simmer over low heat for 15 minutes
7. Add curry and simmer a further 5 to 10 minutes
8. Garnish with coriander leaves and serve over cooked rice
9. Per chef zee - the liquid seems sparse
10. No reason why you can't add more tomato, and then also add a good pinch of sugar against possible acidity from the tomato
11. Good luck and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
