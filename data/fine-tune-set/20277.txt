Ingredients:
• Diced tomatoes
• Cornstarch
• Tomato sauce
• Chicken broth
• Red wine
• Dried basil
• Dried thyme
• Salt
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Eggplant
• Cooked spaghetti
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Drain tomatoes, reserve juice, and set aside
2. In a bowl, combine cornstarch, tomato sauce, chicken broth, wine, seasonings and reserved juice
3. Stir until smooth
4. Set aside
5. In a large skillet or wok, stir-fry chicken and garlic in hot oil until no longer pink
6. Remove and keep warm
7. In same skillet, stir-fry onion and pepper for 4 minutes
8. Add eggplant
9. Stir-fry for 4-5 minutes or until tender
10. Stir sauce
11. Add to pan
12. Bring to a boil
13. Cook and stir 2 minutes, until thick
14. Add chicken and tomatoes
15. Heat through
16. Serve over pasta
17. Top with parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
