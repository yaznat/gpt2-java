Please give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>9 Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Celery
• Cherry tomatoes
• Red pepper
• Red onion
• Red kidney beans
• Dark red kidney beans
• Pinto beans
• Green beans
• Yellow beans
• Lima beans
• Great northern bean
• Black beans
• Garbanzo beans
• Fresh oregano
• Fresh rosemary
• Fresh thyme
• Dijon mustard
• Sugar
• Fresh garlic cloves
• Fresh parsley
• Balsamic vinegar
• Cider vinegar
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Fresh basil
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Empty all the beans
2. Add all the beans into a large bowl
3. Dice up the red onion chop
4. Then cut 3 stalks of celery into small pieces
5. Next, cut the red pepper into small pieces
6. Add all the above ingredients into the large bowl with beans
7. Add the cherry tomatoes to the beans
8. When finished mixing, add 9 bean salad dressing mix to the bean mix
9. Double dressing mix if needed
10. Pour the bean salad into an air sealed container and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or over night
11. The longer it rest the better it will taste
12. Yummy! it will last up to 1 week in the refrigerator
13. Salad dressing mix:
14. Finely chop oregano, rosemary, and thyme
15. Take garlic cloves and chop until minced
16. In a large bowl combine all ingredients
17. Whisk in the olive oil
18. Mix all ingredients well
19. After all the dressing is well mixed, pour all of it over the bean salad<|endoftext|>
