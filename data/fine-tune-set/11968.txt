Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Ground chicken
• Lemongrass
• Red curry paste
• Napa cabbage
• Salt
• Water
• Sweetened flaked coconut
• Cornstarch
• Egg roll wrap
• Oil

Steps:
1. Heat skillet over medium high heat
2. Saute chicken and lemongrass in olive oil until almost cooked
3. Remove from heat
4. Stir in curry paste
5. Add cabbage, salt, coconut, water and cornstarch and stir to combine
6. Let mixture cool slightly
7. Place 3 heaping tablespoons of chicken filling in center of eggroll wrapper and roll up, moistening edges to seal
8. Heat about 1 / 2" of oil in pan to 350 degrees
9. Fry eggrolls until golden brown, turning as necessary<|endoftext|>
