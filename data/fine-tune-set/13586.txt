Ingredients:
• Sirloin tip roast
• Garlic powder
• Onions
• Beef broth
• Strong black coffee
• Salt
• Oregano
• Rosemary
• Pepper
• Flour
• Margarine

Steps:
1. Cut sirloin
2. Place in casserole with garlic powder, onions, liquids and seasonings
3. Cover and bake at 350 for 1 hour until beef is tender
4. Thicken gravy with the blended flour and margarine
5. Serve with mashed potatoes, noodles, or rice<|endoftext|>
