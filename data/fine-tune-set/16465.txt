What's a good smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>If You Like Pina Coladas Protein Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Cold water
• Vanilla protein powder
• Oat flour
• Unsweetened pineapple chunks
• Coconut extract
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Pour water into blender
2. Add protein powder and oat flour
3. Blend on medium for 15 seconds
4. Add pineapple and coconut extract
5. Blend for 30 seconds
6. Add ice and blend on high for 30 seconds
7. Pour into tall glass and serve<|endoftext|>
