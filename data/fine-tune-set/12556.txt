What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>Bademjan Delicious Persian Stew

Ingredients:
• Eggplant
• Lamb
• Tomato paste
• Lemon
• Onion
• Turmeric
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Peel the eggplant and cut it lengthwise in half
2. Lightly salt and leave it to drain in a colander for half an hour
3. Rinse and pat it dry with paper towel, or leave to dry it overnight
4. Fry the eggplant in oil until it is lightly colored
5. Remove and keep to the side
6. Put 2 tablespoons of oil in a deep pot and fry the onion, adding the meat, pepper turmeric and tomato paste
7. Saut until the onion is lightly colored
8. Add water and let it simmer until the meat is tender
9. Add more water if its required
10. When the meat is tender, add the eggplant, sugar, lemon, and salt to taste
11. Let the mixture boil for another few minutes
12. Taste and adjust the seasoning<|endoftext|>
