Ingredients:
• Cooked bacon
• Butter
• Bread
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Spread about 1 tablespoon butter into the bottom of a large skillet
2. Spread 1 tablespoon onto one side of each slice of bread
3. Place the 4 slices of bread butter-side down onto a large skillet or flat grill
4. Top with 1 slice of cheese, 2 slices of tomato and 2 slices cooked bacon, then 1 more slice of cheese
5. Cover with a slice of bread
6. Fry over medium turning once with a spatula until golden brown on both sides<|endoftext|>
