Ingredients:
• Tortillas
• Refried beans
• Monterey jack cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Oil
• Butter

Steps:
1. Divide each ingredient into four portions spread 1 / 4 beans over tortilla, add 1 / 4 cheese, top with second tortilla, in large skillet, heat together 1tbs oil and 1 tbs butter over med heat
2. Place assembled quesadilla into pan
3. Cook till bottom is browned then turn over and brown other side
4. Place on large plate and cool 1-2 min
5. Cut into eight wedges with scissors
6. Repeat three more times
7. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
