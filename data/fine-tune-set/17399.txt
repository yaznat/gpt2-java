Ingredients:
• Corn muffin mix
• Wheat germ
• Egg
• Apple butter
• Milk
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Brown sugar
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl combine muffin mix and the first 1 / 4 cup wheat germ
2. In another bowl combine the egg, 1 / 2 cup apple butter, milk, and pumpkin or apple pie spice
3. Add the egg mixture all at once to the wheat germ mixture, stirring just till combined
4. Pour the batter into a greased 8x8x2-inch baking pan
5. For topping, in a small bowl stir together brown sugar, the remaining 1 / 4 cup wheat germ, and 1 / 4 cup apple butter
6. Spoon mixture in small mounds atop batter in pan
7. Using a knife, marble the mixture through the batter
8. Bake in a 350f oven for 25 to 30 minutes or till a wooden toothpick inserted near center comes out clean
9. To serve, sift powdered sugar over each serving and serve with additional apple butter, if desired
10. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
