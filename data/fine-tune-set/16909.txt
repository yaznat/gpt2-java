Ingredients:
• Pillsbury refrigerated crescent dinner rolls
• Cream cheese
• Onions
• Milk
• Hormel real bacon bits
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375f
2. If using crescent rolls: unroll dough
3. Separate into 4 rectangles
4. Firmly press perforations to seal
5. If using dough sheets: unroll dough
6. Cut into 4 rectangles
7. In small bowl, mix cream cheese, onion and milk
8. Beat until well blended
9. Spread about 2 tablespoons cream cheese mixture evenly over each rectangle
10. Sprinkle each with bacon
11. Roll up, starting at longest side
12. Pinch edges to seal
13. Cut each roll into 8 slices
14. Place on ungreased cookie sheet
15. Sprinkle with cheese
16. Bake 12 to 15 minutes or until golden brown
17. Serve warm
18. Store in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
