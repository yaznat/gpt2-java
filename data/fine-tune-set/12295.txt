What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>30 Minute Bean Soup For 2

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Celery rib
• Carrot
• Onions
• Potatoes
• Lean ham
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Great northern beans
• Lemon juice
• Black pepper
• Instant potato flakes
• Green onion
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan over medium-high heat, melt the butter
2. Add the celery, carrots and onion
3. Saute until veggies are tender - 2-3 minutes
4. Stir in the potatoes and ham
5. Add the broth, beans and lemon juice
6. Season with pepper to taste
7. Simmer until potatoes are tender, about 10 minutes
8. Stir in the instant potatoes to thicken
9. Use more or less until you get the consistency that pleases you
10. Optional - serve topped with thinly siced green onions and / or peeled, diced tomatoes
11. Serve with hot buttered cornbread<|endoftext|>
