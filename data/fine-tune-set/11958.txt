Ingredients:
• Coconut milk
• Chicken broth
• Garlic
• Red curry paste
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Zucchini
• Yellow squash
• Red bell pepper
• Green onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Lime wedges

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425
2. Combine first four ingredients and whisk in medium bowl
3. Place chicken breasts between heavy duty plastic wrap and pound to even thickness, sprinkle salt and pepper evenly over both sides of chicken
4. Fold 4 sheets of heavy-duty aluminum foil in half crosswise
5. Open foil: layer 1 / 2 cup zucchini, 1 / 4 cup squash, 1 / 4 cup bell pepper, 2 tablespoons green onion and 1 chicken breast half on each foil sheet
6. Spoon 3 tablespoons coconut milk mixture over each seving
7. Fold foil over chicken and vegetables
8. Tightly seal edges
9. Place packets on baking sheet, bake 22 minutes
10. Remove from oven
11. Let stand 5 minutes
12. Place on plates and carefully unfold
13. Sprinkle each with 1 teaspoon cilantro, if desired
14. Garnish with lime wedges
15. Serve immediately over rice<|endoftext|>
