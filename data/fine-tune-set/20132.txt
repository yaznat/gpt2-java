Ingredients:
• White wine
• Cornstarch
• Black bean sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Oil
• Vegetable broth
• Soy sauce
• Asparagus
• Green onions
• Cashews

Steps:
1. In small bowl mix wine and cornstarch until smooth, set aside
2. In another bowl mix black bean sauce, garlic and ginger
3. Heat oil in a large pan over medium heat
4. Add black bean sauce, cook stirring constantly about 1 minute
5. Add broth and soy sauce stirring about 1 minute
6. Increase heat to medium high
7. Add asparagus and green onions, cook about 4 minutes till crisp-tender
8. If needed add additional broth to prevent sticking
9. Remove asparagus
10. Reduce heat to medium
11. Add wine, cornstarch mixture stirring until thickens, about 1 to 2 minutes
12. Pour sauce over asparagus and sprinkle with cashews<|endoftext|>
