Ingredients:
• Large shrimp
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Cornstarch
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Oil

Steps:
1. You will be using 1 bottle dinnis select bang bang sauce
2. Peel and devein shrimp
3. Sprinkle shrimp with 1 1 / 2 teaspoons salt
4. Let stand at room temperature for 12 minutes
5. Combine eggs, milk, corn starch, flour, baking powder in mixing bowl
6. Mix until smooth
7. Heat oil in deep fryer to 350f add batter to the bowl with shrimp, stir till the shrimp is well coated with batter drop shrimp in the deep fryer and fry till golden brown
8. In a separate bowl or a empty one 12 oz
9. Bottle of dinnis select bang bang sauce, drop the fried shrimp and still till all the shrimp is coated with the sauce
10. Garnish with fresh green or red hot peppers<|endoftext|>
