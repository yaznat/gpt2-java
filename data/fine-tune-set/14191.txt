Please give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Charmaine S Secret Tofu Marinade

Ingredients:
• Marinade
• Worcestershire sauce
• Soy sauce
• Tabasco sauce
• Smoked paprika
• Olive oil
• Firm tofu

Steps:
1. Mix all the ingredients together, except the tofu
2. Thinly slice the tofu
3. Pour the marinade into an oven dish, and put the tofu slices in
4. Leave the tofu to marinade for 30 to 60 minutes, then turn it over and leave the other side to marinade for another 30 to 60 minutes
5. Bake at 180 degrees celcius for 25 minutes, or until cooked<|endoftext|>
