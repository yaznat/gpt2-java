What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Sausage Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Bulk sausage
• Garlic powder
• Cooking oil
• Cooked rice
• Mixed vegetables
• Eggs
• Salt
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Cook the sausage with garlic powder in a large skillet until browned
2. Remove and drain on paper towels
3. Pour drippings from skillet
4. Add oil and heat until hot but not smoking
5. And rice and stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute
6. Add vegetables and sausage and continue to stir-fry, about 5 minutes
7. Stir in eggs and cook until set
8. Season
9. Sprinkle with onions<|endoftext|>
