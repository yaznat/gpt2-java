Ingredients:
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Ham slices
• Butter
• Frozen hash browns
• Tex-mex cheese
• Mild salsa

Steps:
1. Melt butter in large non stick skillet
2. Add hash browns and cook until golden
3. Lay tortillas out and put one slice of ham on each
4. Spoon roughly 1 / 4 cup of hash browns on each tortilla, spreading evenly
5. Sprinkle 1 / 2 cup of cheese over hash browns
6. Top with 1 tbsp of salsa
7. Tucking in the ends, roll each tortilla up then wrap tightly in wax paper or plastic wrap
8. Pop in the microwave for 15-20 seconds to melt cheese and heat through
9. Unwrap and serve<|endoftext|>
