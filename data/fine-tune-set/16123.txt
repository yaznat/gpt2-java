Ingredients:
• Cooked bacon
• Oven-roasted turkey breast
• Tortillas
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce
• Cheddar cheese
• Miracle whip

Steps:
1. Arrange 2 slices each bacon and turkey on top of each tortilla
2. Top with tomatoes, lettuce and cheese
3. Fold in half
4. Cook each tortilla in nonstick skillet on medium heat 2 min on each side or until crispy and golden
5. Cut into wedges
6. Serve each quesadilla with 1 tablespoons dressing<|endoftext|>
