What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Blue Steak Asian Style

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Dry sherry
• Mirin
• Ketjap manis
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Green onions
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Steak fillets

Steps:
1. Place soy, sherry mirin, kecap manis, garlic, ginger, pepper and spring onions in a bowl and whisk to mix
2. Put the steaks and the marinade in a ziplock bag and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, turning every half hour or so
3. When ready cook steaks on a preheated ridged grill or bbq to desired degree of doneness, basting with marinade<|endoftext|>
