Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>April S Favorite Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Rotini pasta
• Broccoli florets
• Cucumber
• Carrots
• Grape tomatoes
• Red onion
• Mozzarella cheese
• Vinegar
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Sugar
• Salt
• Paprika
• Black pepper
• Salad supreme dry seasoning

Steps:
1. Cook and drain the pasta, but do not rinse
2. Allow to cool
3. Meanwhile, place vegetables and cheese in a large bowl
4. Combine vinegar, oil, sugar, and seasonings in container with lid
5. Shake well and pour over vegetables and gently toss
6. Add cooled pasta to vegetable mixture and toss well
7. Chill until ready to serve
