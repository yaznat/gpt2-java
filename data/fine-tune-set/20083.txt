What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>The Awesome Cheese Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Cheese
• Salt
• Parsley
• Rosemary

Steps:
1. Take 2 eggs beat them together
2. Turn burner on medium low
3. When pan is heated add 2 slices of butter
4. When butter partly melted add the eggs
5. Spread 3 slices of cheese on the eggs
6. Then add the salt parsley and rosemary leaves
7. Then fold the omelette
8. Cook until done<|endoftext|>
