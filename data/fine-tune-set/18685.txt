Ingredients:
• Milk
• Blackstrap molasses
• Sugar
• Butter
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Eggs
• Yellow cornmeal
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400f
2. In a greased casserole or stone crock mix together cornmeal, molasses, sugar, butter, salt, baking soda, eggs, cinnamon, nutmeg and raisins together
3. Stir in 3 cups of hot milk
4. Bake in oven until it comes to a boil
5. Turn heat down to 275f
6. Stir in remaining milk and bake, covered for 4-6 hours, stirring every half hour or so
7. Serve warm with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream<|endoftext|>
