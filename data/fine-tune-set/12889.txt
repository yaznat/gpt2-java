Ingredients:
• Steak
• Mixed vegetables
• Baking potato
• Beef gravy
• Thyme
• Black pepper
• Refrigerated buttermilk biscuits

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f and spray 8x8 pan with nonstick cooking spray
2. Combine steak, veggies, and potato in prepared dish and then stir in gravy and seasonings
3. Bake, uncovered, 40 minutes and then remove from oven
4. Increase oven temperature to 400f
5. Top meat mixture with biscuits and bake 8-10 minutes or until biscuits are golden brown<|endoftext|>
