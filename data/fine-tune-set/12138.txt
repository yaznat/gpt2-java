Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• White sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Milk
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Thoroughly cream butter and sugar
2. Add unbeaten eggs, vanilla and milk
3. Mix well
4. Sift together flour and baking powder
5. Gradually add this dry mixture to the creamed mixture, mixing well after each addition
6. You will may need to add up to another 3 / 4 cup of flour to make a dough stiff enough to roll out
7. Chill at least 6-8 hours
8. To bake, preheat oven to 350
9. Lightly grease cookie sheets
10. Roll out a portion of chilled dough on lightly floured surface very thin
11. Cut into shapes
12. Place on lightly greased cookie sheets
13. Sprinkle with colored sugar or other sprinkles, if desired
14. Bake at 350 6-8 minutes or until edges brown slightly
15. Cool
16. Store in airtight container<|endoftext|>
