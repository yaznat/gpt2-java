Please give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Awesome Cabbage Casserole

Ingredients:
• Cabbage
• Onion
• Margarine
• Salt
• Mayonnaise
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Butter flavored crackers

Steps:
1. Coarsely chop cabbage in large casserole dish sprayed with pam
2. Sprinkle onion on top of cabbage
3. Pour melted butter over cabbage
4. Sprinkle with salt
5. Mix mayo and soup together and then spread over cabbage and onions
6. For topping, mix together melted butter, crushed crackers and grated cheese
7. Sprinkle over top of casserole
8. Bake at 350 for 45 minutes or until bubbly and brown
9. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
