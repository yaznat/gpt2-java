Ingredients:
• Cornmeal
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Xanthan gum
• Sugar
• Cumin
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Dry buttermilk
• Water
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Corn on the cob
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Jalapeno pepper

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl, blend together the cornmeal through buttermilk powder
2. In a 2 cup measuring cup, measure 1 1 / 4 cups of warm water, oil and egg yolks
3. Add the liquid ingredients to dry and combine thoroughly
4. Mix in additional water as needed to make a smooth batter
5. Stir in the corn, cheese and peppers
6. Beat the egg whites until stiff and fold into mixture
7. Preheat waffle iron as per manufacturers directions
8. Lightly coat with cooking spray
9. Spoon batter onto to hot waffle iron and bake until golden brown
10. Repeat for additional waffles
