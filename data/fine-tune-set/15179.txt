Ingredients:
• Boboli whole wheat pizza crust
• Chicken
• Barbecue sauce
• Lowfat mozzarella cheese
• Fat-free cheddar cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Red onion

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees
2. Dice the onion and saut the onion with 2 of the tablespoons of bbq sauce
3. Add chicken and cook until chicken is cooked through
4. Top your boboli pizza with the remaining bbq sauce, fat free cheddar, low fat mozzarella, onion chicken mixture, then the regular cheddar
5. Bake for approximately 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
