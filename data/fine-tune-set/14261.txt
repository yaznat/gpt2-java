What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Delicious And Healthy Brown Rice With Tofu And Veggies

Ingredients:
• Long grain brown rice
• Corn
• Mixed vegetables
• White onion
• Black olives
• Extra firm tofu
• White mushrooms
• Dried basil
• Lemon pepper
• Garlic salt
• Water

Steps:
1. Cook 2 cups of brown rice per its instructions in a large pot
2. Brown rice is usually cooked by bringing rice and water to a boil, then covering and turning heat to low for about 50 minutes
3. While rice is cooking, prepare tofu by straining 3-4 times between paper towels to remove as much moisture as possible, and then chop into small pieces
4. Dice onion and, if needed, slice olives and mushrooms
5. After rice is done, add everything into the pot, including the uncooked tofu
6. Stir and cook on medium heat for about 5-7 minutes until tofu and vegetables are hot
7. Add more water if needed, to keep the mixture from getting too dry<|endoftext|>
