Give me a lamb recipe.<|endoftext|>Braised Garlic Lamb Shanks

Ingredients:
• Lamb shanks
• Turnips
• Carrots
• Fennel seed
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Onions
• Black pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Beef stock
• Chicken stock
• Red wine
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven at 325 degrees
2. Combine salt and pepper and season the lamb
3. In a large stainless steel pan pre-heat olive oil med / high heat
4. Sear the lamb in the oil 3-4 minutes each side or until browned
5. Remove from oil and add carrots, onion, garlic and turnip and fry lightly 2-3 minutes
6. Add wine and scrape the bottom of pan of all the glazed on bits
7. Add the the stock and tomatoes and bring to a good boil
8. Add the lamb back in and cover the pan with foil or pan cover and place into oven 2 to 2 1 / 2 hours<|endoftext|>
