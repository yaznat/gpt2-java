Ingredients:
• White bread
• Mayonnaise
• Butter
• Tuna
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Open the tuna and drain it
2. Mix the tuna with as much mayo as you want
3. Spread butter on one slice of bread and mayo on the other
4. Spread the tuna-mayo mixture on
5. Put on lettuce
6. Put both slices together and serve<|endoftext|>
