Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Joy Chocolate Pie

Ingredients:
• Miniature almond joy bars
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Granulated sugar
• Cornstarch
• Cocoa powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees f
2. Crust:
3. Place crust ingredients in a food processor and pulse until mix is blended
4. Press onto bottom and up sides of a lightly buttered 9" pie plate
5. Bake 10 minutes
6. Remove and allow to cool completely
7. Filling:
8. Stir together sugar, cornstarch, cocoa and salt
9. Blend in milk and cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until mixture thickens
10. Remove from heat, add vanilla and candy pieces, stirring till candy is melted
11. Pour filling into pie crust and press plastic wrap onto surface
12. Refrigerate until set
13. Top with whipped cream when ready to serve<|endoftext|>
