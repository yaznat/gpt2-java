Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Cranberry Puffed Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Flour
• Milk
• Butter
• Granny smith apples
• Cranberries
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Lemon juice
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. In a medium bowl, thoroughly whisk eggs
3. Slowly add the flour, whisking all the while to prevent lumps
4. Gradually add the milk and whisk to form a smooth batter
5. Set aside
6. In a medium skillet, melt 2 tablespoons of butter, then add the sliced apples, cranberries, cinnamon, and sugar
7. Cook over medium heat until fork tender, about 15 minutes
8. In a 9-inch oven-safe pie plate, melt 2 tablespoons of butter, taking care to not burn it
9. Fill the plate with the cooked apple mixture, and top with the prepared batter
10. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes or until puffed and golden
11. Top the pancake with a sprinkling of lemon juice, if desired, and finish off with a liberal dusting of powdered sugar
12. Slice into portions and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
