Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>Bandito Chili Dogs

Ingredients:
• Hot dog
• Chili without beans
• Cheddar cheese soup
• Green chilies
• Hot dog buns
• Onion
• Corn chips
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Place hot dogs in a slow cooker
2. In a bowl, combine the chili, soup, and green chilies
3. Pour over hot dogs
4. Cover and cook on low for 4-5 hours
5. Serve hot dogs in buns
6. Top with chili mixture, onion, corn chips, and cheese<|endoftext|>
