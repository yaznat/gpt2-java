What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Baked Beef Tenderloin With Diane Sauce

Ingredients:
• Beef tenderloin
• Beef broth
• Cornstarch
• Oil
• Green onion
• Fresh parsley
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Dijon mustard
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400
2. Place sheet of foil on 15 x 10 x 1-inch baking pan or cookie sheet
3. Place beef tenderloin on foil
4. Tuck narrow end under
5. Turn edges of foil up around tenderloin to contain juices
6. Bake at 400 for 30-40 minutes for medium doneness
7. Meanwhile, in small bowl, combine broth and cornstarch
8. Blend well
9. Set aside
10. Heat oil in small nonstick skillet until hot
11. Add onions
12. Cook about 1 minute
13. Stir in parsley, pepper, mustard, and lemon juice
14. Add cornstarch mixture
15. Cook and stir over medium heat for 4 - 6 minutes or until sauce is bubbly and slightly thickened
16. To serve, cut tenderloin into 1 / 2-inch slices
17. Serve sauce over slices<|endoftext|>
