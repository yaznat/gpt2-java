Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>Absolutely Perfect Roast Chicken

Ingredients:
• Whole chickens
• Garlic cloves
• Kosher salt
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400
2. Remove the giblets and any excess fat from the chicken cavity
3. Peel the garlic and put it through a garlic press
4. In a small bowl or on your cutting board, mash the garlic together with the salt
5. When you have a nice paste, smear half of it inside the chicken's cavity
6. Rub the rest of the salt and garlic paste all over the outside of the chicken
7. Then put the chicken on a rack and put the rack in a roasting pan just big enough to hold the chicken
8. Pour the water into the bottom of the pan
9. Bake the chicken for 1 hour and 15 minutes, or until the drumstick just begins to move in the socket
10. Do not baste
11. Check a few times to be sure the water has not evaporated
12. Add more during cooking, if needed
13. Cut the chicken into serving size pieces and serve with pan juices
14. I like to serve the juice in individual small bowls to dip the chicken
15. It is more difficult to get the same results with a larger roaster chicken
16. Also
17. Chicken must be on a rack in order for the chicken to brown well<|endoftext|>
