What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Cumin Honey And Mint Marinated Lamb Chops

Ingredients:
• Of fresh mint
• Balsamic vinegar
• Ground cumin
• Dry mustard
• Honey
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Lamb loin chops

Steps:
1. Combine first 7 ingredients in a small bowl, stirring well
2. Place lamb in a single layer in a shallow dish
3. Rub spice mixture evenly over both sides of lamb
4. Cover and refrigerate for 4 hours
5. Prepare grill
6. Place lamb on a grill rack coated with cooking spray
7. Grill 4 minutes on each side, or until desired degree of doneness
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
