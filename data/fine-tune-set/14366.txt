What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>3 Kinds Of Beans Baked Bean Dish

Ingredients:
• Baked beans
• Baby lima beans
• Red kidney beans
• Bacon
• Onion
• Catsup
• Brown sugar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Velveeta cheese

Steps:
1. Brown bacon in skillet, then remove, and set aside
2. In a 3 quart casserole baking dish, combine the beans, onion, catsup, brown sugar, worcestershire sauce, and bacon
3. Cover, and bake 1 hour in a 350 degree oven
4. Remove from oven, top with the velveeta slices, return to oven and continue to bake until the cheese melts<|endoftext|>
