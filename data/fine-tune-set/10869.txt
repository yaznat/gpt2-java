What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>Acini Di Pepe Salad

Ingredients:
• Acini di pepe pasta
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Red wine vinegar
• Kosher salt
• Grape tomatoes
• Pine nuts
• Radicchio
• Red onion
• Basil leaves
• Feta cheese

Steps:
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil
2. Cook the pasta for about 6 minutes or until tender
3. Drain and rinse under cold water to stop the cooking process
4. Toss the pasta with the olive oil, red wine vinegar, and salt
5. Fold in the tomatoes, pine nuts, radicchio, onion, and basil
6. Sprinkle with the feta cheese
7. Serve at room temperature<|endoftext|>
