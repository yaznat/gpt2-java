Ingredients:
• Thai chiles
• Serrano peppers
• Green onion
• Rice vinegar
• Apple cider vinegar
• Sesame oil
• Salt
• Sugar
• Honey
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Red food coloring

Steps:
1. To a small saucepan, add all ingredients and stir to combine
2. Heat and gently simmer over med-low heat, stirring occasionally, until reduced by half or when it is the consistency of a thick maple syrup, about 15-20 minutes
3. Be sure to keep an eye on it so it does not overcook or burn
4. Remove from heat, let cool slightly, then use an immulsion blender or a mini food processor to chop everything up 'fine'
5. If using, add food coloring to show level of heat
6. Serve warm over fish, pork, chicken, vegetables, etc
7. Cover and refrigerate any extra
8. It will last 6 months or more in the fridge
9. This will get hotter the longer it is stored!
10. Canning: please follow your canning booklet for sterilization instructions
11. Using 4 oz jars, hot pack in boiling water bath for 10 minutes
12. Stored, it should last up to 2 years<|endoftext|>
