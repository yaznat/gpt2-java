Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Apple Banana Casserole With Almonds

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Almonds
• Bananas
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Cinnamon
• Whipping cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Grease a 8x8 or other casserole dish
3. Wash, peel, core and thinly slice apples
4. Layer in dish and sprinkle with almonds
5. Peel banana and break into pieces into a medium bowl
6. Add eggs, vanilla, cinnamon, and cream and beat well
7. Pour over the apple slices
8. Bake 30 minutes
9. Serve hot, this is great served with a couple of scoops of vanilla ice cream
10. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
