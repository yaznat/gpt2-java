Ingredients:
• Corn
• Bacon
• Water
• String

Steps:
1. Carefully expose the corn kernels by pulling back the husk, but do not remove the husk
2. Remove the corn silk
3. Put water in a large container, add the corn, and soak the corn in the husks for 30 minutes
4. This will help to prevent the husks from charring on the grill
5. Preheat grill to medium temperature
6. Remove the soaked corn from the water and pat dry
7. Wrap strips of bacon around the corn kernels on each ear
8. Fold the husks back over the bacon and kernels
9. Tie the husks down with butcher string
10. Repeat with all ears of corn
11. Grill over medium coals / heat, turning occasionally, for about 15 to 20 minutes and until the bacon is cooked and the corn is tender
12. Cut the butcher string away from the husks and serve
13. Take them out for cooking when thawed
14. Put them in the coals for reheating
