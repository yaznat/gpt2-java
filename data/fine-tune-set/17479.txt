Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cream cheese
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Blueberries
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Lightly grease a 13 x 9 x 2 inch baking pan
3. Set aside
4. In small bowl stir together flour, baking powder and salt
5. Set aside
6. In a large bowl beat cream cheese and butter with an electric mixer on medium speed for 3 minutes or until creamy
7. Add 2 cups sugar
8. Beat until combined
9. Add eggs, 1 at a time, beating well
10. Add flour mixture
11. Beat until combined
12. Stir in vanilla
13. Gradually fold in blueberries
14. Batter will be very thick
15. Pour into prepared pan and smooth the top
16. In small bowl stir together 3 tablespoons sugar and the cinnamon
17. Sprinkle over batter
18. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
19. Cool on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
