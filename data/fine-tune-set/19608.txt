What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Potato Soup

Ingredients:
• Red onion
• Olive oil
• Celery ribs
• Leeks
• Garlic cloves
• Tart apples
• Russet potatoes
• Celery root
• Vegetable broth
• Apple juice
• Fresh ginger
• Salt
• Pepper
• Milk
• Fresh chives
• Fresh thyme
• Apple
• Sugar
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Saute onion in hot oil over medium-low heat in a soup pot or dutch oven 10 minutes or until soft and translucent
2. Add celery and leek and saute 5 minutes
3. Add garlic and saute 2 minutes
4. Stir in apples and next 8 ingredients
5. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer, uncovered, 45 minutes
6. Meanwhile, preheat oven to 350 and thinly slice apple for apple rings using a mandoline if possible
7. Lightly coat a baking sheet with cooking spray, and place apple slices in a single layer on sheet
8. Sprinkle with sugar and bake about 15 minutes
9. Sprinkle evenly with salt, if desired
10. Remove soup from heat and let cool slightly
11. Process with an immersion blender, or place soup in a food processor or blender and process in batches until smooth
12. Return to pot
13. Stir in milk or half-and-half and next 2 ingredients
14. Cook over low heat, stirring occasionally, until hot
15. Top with oven-baked apple slices, bacon and chives<|endoftext|>
