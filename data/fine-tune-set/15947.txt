Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Tex Mex Burritos

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Black beans
• Corn
• Ranch dressing mix
• Taco seasoning
• White rice
• Tortilla
• Cheddar cheese
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Cut chicken into 1 / 2 inch cubes
2. Begin cooking chicken in large skillet
3. Boil 2 cups of water and add rice
4. Once chicken is done add 1 / 2 cup of water to skillet and begin adding your seasoning packets
5. Reduce heat and let simmer
6. Add in can of black beans, drained and corn drained into skillet, heat through
7. Once rice is finished add it to the skillet with the rest of the ingredients
8. Stuff tortillas with the filling, add cheddar cheese and salsa if desired
9. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
