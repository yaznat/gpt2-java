Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Bacon
• Garlic powder
• Seasoning salt
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Mix cheese and bacon in a bowl
2. Flatten approximately 1 / 8 - 1 / 4 of the ground chuck in the palm of your hand
3. Squeeze together about 1-2 tablespoons of the bacon and cheese mixture
4. Put it in the center of the ground chuck
5. Bring up the sides of the meat over the filling, making sure the cheese is completely covered by the meat
6. Flatten slightly to form into a patty
7. Season patties to taste with garlic powder, seasoned salt, and ground black pepper
8. Grill burgers approximately 6-8 minutes on each side for medium<|endoftext|>
