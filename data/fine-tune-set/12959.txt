Ingredients:
• Cod
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Black pepper
• Flour
• Eggs
• Fresh parsley
• Water
• Oil
• Salt

Steps:
1. If using salted cod, soak fish overnight and rinse, drain next day
2. Cook fish
3. Shred it
4. Combine the fish, onion, garlic and pepper
5. Mix well
6. Add the flour and stir a few times, then beat in the eggs one at a time
7. Add about 1 / 2 cup water and stir until a batter forms-it should be a bit thicker than pancake batter, but not much, then stir in the parsley
8. Let the mixture rest while you put about 3 inches of oil in a deep saucepan and heat it over medium high
9. Using a tablespoon, gently drop small
10. Spoonfuls of mixture into oil and fry, turning once until golden brown 3 to 5 minutes
11. Don't crowd fritters
12. Drain on paper towels and serve with lime wedges or keep in a warm oven until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
