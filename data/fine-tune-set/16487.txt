Can you give me a smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Acadia S Raspberry Citrus Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Orange juice
• Pineapple juice
• Milk
• Raspberry sherbet
• Orange sherbet
• Non-dairy coffee creamer

Steps:
1. In a blender add orange juice, pineapple juice, milk, sherbet and non-dairy creamer
2. Blend them together for 2-3 minutes on high, or until all lumps are gone
3. Serve immediately!<|endoftext|>
