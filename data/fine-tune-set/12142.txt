Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Cooking apples
• 9 inch pie shell
• Walnuts
• Whipping cream
• Sugar
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Cinnamon
• Vanilla
• Nutmeg
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Slice apples, either in your cuisinart or by hand
3. Transfer slices to pie crust
4. Mix cream, sugar, egg, flour and seasonings thoroughly for about 10 seconds
5. Pour over apple slices
6. Sprinkle top with walnuts
7. Bake for 10 minutes at 450 f
8. Reduce heat to 350 f
9. Bake until apples are tender, 35 to 40 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
