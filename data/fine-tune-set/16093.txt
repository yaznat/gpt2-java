Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Rotel tomatoes
• Black beans
• Tomato sauce
• Whole kernel corn
• Chili powder
• Garlic powder
• Cumin
• Oregano
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Cut chicken into small diced pieces, and saute in large skillet with olive oil and onions over medium-high heat until cooked throughout
3. Add rotel, beans, tomato sauce, corn, and spices to chicken
4. Cook for 5 minutes until fully heated
5. Salt and pepper to taste
6. In a 9"x13" greased casserole dish, put just enough of the mixture to cover the bottom of the dish
7. Layer tortillas, half of the remaining mixture, half of the cheese, tortillas, and the remainder of the mixture
8. Cover with the remainder of the cheese
9. Bake for 15 minutes, and then allow casserole to cool 5 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
