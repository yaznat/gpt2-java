Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Margarine
• Milk
• Eggs
• Brown sugar
• Pecans
• Cinnamon
• Firm butter
• Powdered sugar
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Spray two 9-inch square cake pans with cooking spray
3. Mix all dough ingred
4. Together until moist, and beat 1 minute
5. Spread 1 / 4 of the dough in each pan
6. Mix all filling ingred
7. Together
8. Divide filling in half and sprinkle over dough in each pan
9. Then divide remaining batter between each pan
10. Prepare streusel topping by mixing all ingred
11. Together until crumbly and divide between two pans
12. Bake 30-35 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean
13. Meanwhile beat powdered sugar and butter together until creamy
14. Add vanilla and mix well
15. Add water as needed, a tablespoon at a time, until it reaches drizzle consistency
16. Drizzle the baked cakes while warm<|endoftext|>
