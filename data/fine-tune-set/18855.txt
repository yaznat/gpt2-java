What's a good custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Blackberry Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• Deep dish pie shells
• Sour cream
• Eggs
• Flour
• Butter
• Blackberries
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Place berries into the pie shell
3. Beat the 2 eggs together, then add the sour cream, 1 cup of sugar, 1 / 3 cup of flour
4. Pour over the berries
5. Combine the butter, 1 / 2 cup of sugar, and the 1 / 2 cup of flour to make a crumble topping
6. Sprinkle crumble onto the top of the pie
7. Bake for 50-55 minutes<|endoftext|>
