Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Healthy Pumpkin Pie

Ingredients:
• Pumpkin
• Skim milk
• Egg whites
• Reduced fat graham cracker crust
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Pumpkin pie spice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Mix pumpkin, milk, and egg whites until smooth
3. Gradually stir in splenda
4. Add the pumpkin pie spice
5. Taste and add more if need be
6. Pour into crust and spread evenly
7. Bake in the oven for 15 minutes then reduce the temperature to 350f and bake for another 45 minutes
8. Let cool and serve your favorite way
9. To make to crust less likely to turn soggy, beat some egg whites and brush over the crust and bake at 350f for about 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
