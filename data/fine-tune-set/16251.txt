Can you give me a wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Chicken Lettuce Wraps

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Ground chicken
• Soy sauce
• Thai sweet chili sauce
• Toasted sesame seeds
• Pine nuts
• Rice vinegar
• Garlic
• Fresh ginger

Steps:
1. Heat oil and cook chicken until brown
2. Add soy, mae ploy, sesame seeds, pine nuts, rice vinegar, garlic, and ginger and cook one minute
3. Serve in ramikin garnished with ground peanuts and fresh chopped cilantro
4. Scoop into iceberg lettuce leaves<|endoftext|>
