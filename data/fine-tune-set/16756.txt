Please give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Cookies Cream Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Chocolate ice cream
• Chocolate cookies
• Milk
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In a blender, place the low-fat ice cream, and low-calorie chocolate cookies and 1 percent milk
2. Add the pure vanilla extract and blend untill most of the cookies are broken, leaving a few larger chunks
3. Pour into 4 glasses and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
