Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Onions
• Button mushroom
• Dry white wine
• Cod
• Thyme
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Country bread
• Paprika
• Garlic powder
• Gruyere cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Heat 1 t of oil in a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat
3. Add onions and cook stirring often, until just starting to soften, 5-7 minutes
4. Add mushrooms until starting to get dry
5. Add wine, increase heat to high and cook, stirring often, until the wine is slightly reduced, 2-4 minutes
6. Place cod on the onions and sprinkle with thyme, salt, pepper, & 2 pinches crushed red pepper flakes
7. Cover the pan tightly with foil
8. Transfer to the oven and bake for 12 minutes
9. Toss the bread with the remaining 1 tablespoon oil
10. Paprika and garlic powder in a small bowl
11. Spread the bread mixture over the fish and top with cheese
12. Bake, uncovered, until the fish is opaque in the center, about 10 minutes more<|endoftext|>
