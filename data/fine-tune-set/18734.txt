Ingredients:
• Fat-free sugar-free instant vanilla pudding mix
• Low-fat milk
• Fat-free condensed milk
• Cool whip
• Bananas
• Lemon juice
• Reduced-fat vanilla wafers

Steps:
1. Mix the first four ingredients in a large bowl and let set in fridge
2. Slice bananas and coat with lemon juice to prevent browning
3. Layer bowl starting with nilla wafers, bananas and pudding mixture
4. Layer 3 times
5. Garnish with crumbs remaining from the nilla wafers
6. Refrigerate for several hours before serving<|endoftext|>
