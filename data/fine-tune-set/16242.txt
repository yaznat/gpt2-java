Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Biscuit dough
• Butter
• Apples
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease 8x8x2 in baking pan
3. Combine sugar and cinnamon
4. Mix well
5. Dip biscuits into butter
6. Coat with cinnamon-sugar mixture
7. Arrange 3 apple slices in center of each biscuit
8. Fold to enclose filling
9. Seal edges
10. Arrange in prepared pan
11. Sprinkle remaining cinnamon-sugar mixture over biscuits
12. Drizzle orange juice over top
13. Bake for 30 minutes, or until done<|endoftext|>
