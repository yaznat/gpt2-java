What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Chocolate Chip Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Low-fat milk
• Banana
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Mini chocolate chip

Steps:
1. Place dry ingredients in large mixing bowl and stir until mixed well
2. Add remaining ingredients and combine until well blended and smooth
3. Let batter sit 5 minutes before using
4. Preheat waffle iron
5. Use 1 / 2 cup batter per waffle and cook until done
6. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
