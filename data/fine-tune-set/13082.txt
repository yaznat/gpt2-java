Ingredients:
• Lemon rind
• Fresh lemon juice
• Pepper
• Fresh bay leaves
• Lemons
• Garlic clove
• Swordfish steak
• Cherry tomatoes
• Green bell peppers
• Vegetable oil cooking spray

Steps:
1. Combine first 6 ingredients in a large zip-top heavy-duty plastic bag
2. Add swordfish
3. Seal bag, and marinate in refrigerator 1 hour, turning bag occasionally
4. Remove swordfish from bag, reserving marinade
5. Thread 5 swordfish cubes, 1 bay leaf, 1 lemon slice, 1 cherry tomato, and 1 bell pepper square alternately onto each of 4 skewers
6. Reserve 1 / 4 cup marinade
7. Discard remaining bay leaves and lemon slices
8. Place kabobs on a broiler rack coated with cooking spray
9. Place rack on a broiler pan
10. Broil 5-1 / 2 inches from heat 3 minutes
11. Turn kabobs over, and broil 3 minutes or until fish flakes easily when tested with a fork, basting occasionally with reserved 1 / 4 cup marinade
12. Yield: 4 servings
