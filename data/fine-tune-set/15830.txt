Give me a burrito recipe.<|endoftext|>Breakfast Burritos Oamc

Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Bacon
• Onion
• Egg
• Milk
• Salt and pepper
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Cook bacon until almost done, don't over cook the bacon will burn while you cook potatoes
2. Add onions and cook for a minute or two, until softened
3. Add potatoes, stir to coat in bacon drippings and cover
4. Cook covered for approximately 15 minutes, or until potatoes are done, stirring occasionally
5. Put the tortillas on top of the potatoes while they cook, this steams them and makes them moist
6. While you are waiting for potatoes to finish cooking, wisk the eggs and milk
7. Mix well
8. Salt and pepper your potatoes, cook uncovered a minute longer
9. Add eggs to potato mixture, cook stirring almost constantly until egg is finished
10. Be very careful to not overcook the egg
11. Egg must still be very moist but not overcooked
12. It alters the flavor otherwise
13. Remove from heat
14. Place 1 / 2 cup measurements into containers
15. Wrap tortilla's in wax paper, seal well and place on top of potato egg mixture then cover and freeze
16. Alternatively you can fill and wrap them, the problem is the yucky ends are harder to control
17. Make sure when you steam your tortillas you don't let them get dry afterwards
18. You want them drenched in steam, fill them and tightly tightly wrap them in 2 big pieces of plastic wrap
19. Either serving should be reheated on high for 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
