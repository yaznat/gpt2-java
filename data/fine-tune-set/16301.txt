Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Cream cheese
• Fresh chives
• Bacon
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Milk
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Ground black pepper
• Salt
• Toothpick

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Chicken: pound the chicken breasts until flat
3. Put a slice of cream cheese and a few chopped chives in the middle of each and roll up
4. Wrap each breast with a slice of bacon and secure with toothpicks
5. Sauce: combine the soup, mayo, milk, lemon juice, pepper, and salt
6. Mix all together until smooth
7. Place wrapped chicken breast pieces in a 9 x 13 baking dish and cover with the sauce mixture
8. Bake at 325 for one hour or until chicken is done<|endoftext|>
