What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Green Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Rice vinegar
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Sesame oil
• Cherry tomatoes
• Peanuts
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In a bowl toss all ingredients together
2. Season to taste with salt and pepper
3. Serve at room temperature<|endoftext|>
