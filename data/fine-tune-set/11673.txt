Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>A Different Chocolate Eclair Cake Dessert

Ingredients:
• Club crackers
• Vanilla instant pudding mix
• Milk
• Cool whip
• Chocolate squares
• Butter
• Light corn syrup
• Vanilla
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Arrange club crackers on the bottom of a 9 x 13 pan, to completely cover bottom in a single layer
2. Mix pudding and milk, then fold in cool whip
3. Pour pudding mixture on layer of club crackers, then add another layer of crackers, to completely cover top in a single layer
4. Refrigerate 30 minutes
5. Melt chocolate and butter together
6. Once combined, add remaining ingredients, stirring until all combined
7. Pour frosting over chilled cake and refrigerate at least 3 hours before serving
8. You will have club crackers left<|endoftext|>
