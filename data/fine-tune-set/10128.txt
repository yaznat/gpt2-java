Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red bell pepper
• Corn
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Chili beans
• Chicken stock
• Barley
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Oregano
• Salt and pepper
• Cooked chicken breasts

Steps:
1. Cook onion, garlic and bell pepper in olive oil until soft
2. Add remaining ingredients, except chicken, bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer for 40 minutes
3. Add chicken and continue cooking for 20 more minutes<|endoftext|>
