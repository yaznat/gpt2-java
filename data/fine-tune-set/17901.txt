Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Aunt Sharon S Best Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Water
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Semisweet chocolate morsel
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Flour

Steps:
1. Combine butter, sugar and water in a small saucepan
2. Heat on medium and stir until sugar is melted
3. Remove from heat
4. Add vanilla, eggs and 1 cup of chocolate morsels and stir until chocolate is melted
5. In a medium sized bowl, combine baking soda, salt and flour, mix well
6. Add warm chocolate mixture and stir gently until combined
7. Spray bottom of a 9"x13" baking pan with spray release of choice, then pour in the brownie batter
8. Wait a minute or two for the batter to even out over the pan, then sprinkle with the additional cup of chocolate morsels
9. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 30-35 minutes<|endoftext|>
