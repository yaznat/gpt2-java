Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Ranch dressing
• Sour cream
• Cilantro
• Pickled jalapeno pepper
• Garlic
• Salt
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Jalapeno juice
• Iceberg lettuce
• Cheddar cheese
• Tomatoes
• Cool ranch doritos

Steps:
1. Grill chicken until no longer pink
2. Remove from heat and slice
3. Place dressing ingredients in the bowl of a food processor and pulse until blended
4. Chill until ready to serve
5. In a large bowl, combine salad ingredients
6. Add chicken
7. Add dressing and toss well to mix
8. Add doritos to salad
9. Toss and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
