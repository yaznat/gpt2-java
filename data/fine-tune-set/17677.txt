Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple Butter Streusel Muffins

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil cooking spray
• All-purpose flour
• Wheat germ
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Apple butter
• Skim milk
• Margarine
• Egg whites
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Line 30 mini-muffin pan cups or 12 medium muffin cups with paper liners, or spray bottoms only with cooking spray
3. In medium bowl, combine flour, wheat germ, brown sugar, baking powder and salt
4. Mix well
5. In small bowl, combine apple butter, milk, margarine and egg whites until well blended
6. Add to flour mixture, stirring just until dry ingredients are moistened
7. Fill muffin cups almost full with batter
8. Prepare streusel topping
9. Sprinkle evenly over batter, patting gently
10. Bake mini-muffins 12 to 15 minutes, and larger muffins 20 to 22 minutes, or until wooden toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
11. Serve warm
12. Streusel topping: in small bowl, combine all ingredients
13. Mix until well blended
14. Seal, label and freeze
15. To reheat muffins, unwrap
16. Microwave on high about 30 seconds per muffin<|endoftext|>
