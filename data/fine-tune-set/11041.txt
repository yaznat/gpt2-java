What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Antipasto Chicken Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Focaccia bread
• Tapenade
• Cooked boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Sun-dried tomato packed in oil
• Roasted red pepper
• Prosciutto
• Fontina cheese
• Pepperoncini peppers

Steps:
1. Spread bottom half of focaccia with olive paste
2. Arrange chicken on top of paste
3. Arrange artichokes, tomatoes, peppers, and prosciutto over chicken
4. Sprinkle with cheese
5. Top with top half of focaccia
6. Press gently
7. Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium heat
8. Add sandwich to pan
9. Place a cast-iron or heavy skillet on top of sandwich
10. Press gently to flatten
11. Cook 2 minutes on each side or until bread is lightly toasted
12. Cut into 4 wedges
13. Serve with pepperoncini<|endoftext|>
