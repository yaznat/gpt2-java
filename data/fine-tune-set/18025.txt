Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Caramel Walnut Bars

Ingredients:
• White sugar
• Butter
• Water
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Walnuts
• Caramel topping

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. Line 9-inch baking pan with foil extending over edges
3. Grease and flour
4. Combine sugar, butter, and water in medium saucepan, and cook over low heat stirring constantly
5. Remove, immediately add 1 cup chocolate chips and stir until melted
6. Beat in eggs and vanilla until well-blended
7. Stir together flour, baking soda, and salt
8. Stir into chocolate mixture
9. Spread batter in pan, bake 15-20 minutes until brownies begin to pull away from pan
10. Remove brownies from oven
11. Immediately spread caramel topping
12. Sprinkle remaining chips and nuts over topping
13. Cool completely in pan on wire rack
14. Cut into bars<|endoftext|>
