Please give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Beef Stew Daisyfields

Ingredients:
• Beef chuck
• Oil
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Chili powder
• Thyme
• Bay leaf
• Cloves
• Tomatoes
• Green pepper
• Beef stock
• Boiling water
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Onions
• Celery
• Peas

Steps:
1. Brown meat slowly in oil in heavy kettle or dutch oven
2. Sprinkle with sugar and continue browning until all meat is well seared
3. Dust lightly with flour and continue to brown
4. Add seasonings, bay leaf, cloves, tomatoes, green pepper, stock, and boiling water
5. Cover kettle
6. Simmer over low heat for 2 1 / 2 hours until meat is tender
7. Add vegetables, except peas, and continue to cook 1 hour longer
8. Add peas and cook 15 minutes longer
9. Small buttered biscuits complement this stew<|endoftext|>
