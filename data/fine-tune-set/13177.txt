Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Olive oil
• Lemon juice
• Dried oregano
• Dried dill
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Red onion
• Rosemary
• Kalamata olive
• Feta cheese
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a gallon sized ziploc bag
2. Marinate in fridge for 6 hours, turning 2-3 times
3. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
4. Pour bag contents into a 13 x 9 x 2 inch baking dish
5. Cook uncovered, spooning juices over chicken occasionally, for about 30 minutes
6. Remove pan from oven, garnish with lemon slices and crumbled feta
7. I serve this over white rice with pan juices and a final dash of black pepper<|endoftext|>
