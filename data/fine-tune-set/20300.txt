Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Garlic powder
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Vegetable oil
• Broccoli florets
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Ground ginger

Steps:
1. Combine 2 tbsp cornstarch, 2 tbsp water and garlic powder in a bowl and mix until smooth
2. Add chicken and toss until well coated
3. Stir-fry chicken in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat in 1 tbsp oil until meat is no longer pink, about 10 minutes
4. Remove meat from skillet and keep warm
5. Stir-fry broccoli in the remaining oil for about 5 minutes, then return chicken to pan
6. Mix together soy sauce, brown sugar, ginger and remaining 1 tbsp of cornstarch and 1 / 2 cup water until smooth
7. Add soy mixture to skillet
8. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring frequently
9. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
