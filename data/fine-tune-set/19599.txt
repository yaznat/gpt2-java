What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cheddar Pie With Sweet Potato Crust

Ingredients:
• Cooked sweet potato
• Egg
• Butter
• Fresh orange juice
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Apple
• Sweet wine
• Cheddar cheese
• Eggs
• Cream
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Crust:
2. In a bowl combine all ingredients
3. Mix well
4. Press dough into a large greased pie plate
5. Filling:
6. Marinate apple in wine for about 2 hours approx
7. Drain well
8. Sprinkle apple and cheese over base of pie crust
9. Beat eggs and cream together
10. Add nutmeg
11. Pour into pie crust and bake for 180c for 45 minutes in a preheated oven
12. Leave to stand in the plate for 15 minutes before cutting<|endoftext|>
