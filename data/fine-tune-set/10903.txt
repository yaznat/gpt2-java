Give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>Alexander S Orzo And Wild Rice Salad

Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Lemon juice
• Fresh garlic
• French dijon mustard
• Sugar
• Canola oil
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Fresh basil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Orzo pasta
• Cooked wild rice
• Red onion
• Currants
• Canned corn niblet
• Toasted almond
• Parsley
• Red peppers
• Yellow peppers
• Green onion
• Garlic granules

Steps:
1. For dressing: dissolve vinegar, lemon juice, garlic and sugar with a hand whip
2. Fold in the mustard, basil, salt and pepper
3. Slowly add oils, while whisking vigorously
4. Refrigerate
5. Use only 1 / 2 cup of dressing for salad
6. Place all other ingredients in a mixing bowl and mix well
7. Serve ice cold, 38-40f shelf life mixed is two hours<|endoftext|>
