Ingredients:
• Sausage
• Cheddar cheese
• Hash brown potatoes
• Pizza crust
• Parmesan cheese
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Brown and break up sausage, drain well
2. Roll out crust on a nonstick cookie sheet
3. Layer sausage, hash browns, and cheddar
4. Whisk in the milk with the eggs and season
5. Pour on the pizza
