Generate a recipe for fish.<|endoftext|>Broiled Halibut With Herbs Can Be Used On Any Fish

Ingredients:
• Halibut
• Butter
• Onions
• Salt
• Garlic salt
• Pepper
• Dried thyme
• Dried tarragon
• Dried basil
• Dried parsley
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Place fish in broiler pan lined with foil
2. Cream butter with seasonings
3. Add lemon juice slowly
4. Spread 1 / 2 of butter over fish
5. Broil in preheated broiler 2 inches from heat for 3 minutes
6. Turn fish apply remaining butter and broil 3 to 5 minutes
7. Serve on platter with sauce from pan poured over the fish<|endoftext|>
