Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Baked In A Brown Paper Bag

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• Ice water
• Granny smith apples
• Cornstarch
• Fresh lemon juice
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. In a food processor combine flour, sugar and salt
2. Add butter and pulse into pea-sized pieces
3. Add water and pulse briefly, it will still look crumbly
4. Transfer the crumbs to 2 pieces of wax paper and form into 2 disks
5. Wrap with plastic wrap and chill overnight
6. Roll out disks on a lightly floured workstation to 2" bigger than the 9" pie plate
7. Line the plate with one disk, and chill the other
8. Preheat oven to 375 degree f
9. Filling:
10. Toss apple slices, sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, vanilla, cinnamon, salt and nutmeg together
11. Transfer to pie shell and dot with butter
12. Brush edges of crust with water and place top crust on and pinch the edges together
13. Slide pie into a brown paper bag and fold top down
14. Staple and bake for 1 hour
15. Remove pie from oven and cut a large circle in the top of the bag and return to the oven to brown the crust
16. It should take approx 15-25 minutes
17. Let pie cool at least 30 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
