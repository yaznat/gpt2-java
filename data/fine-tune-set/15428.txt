Ingredients:
• Hamburger
• Ground pork
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese
• Eggs
• Breadcrumbs
• Mayonnaise
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Ketchup
• Prepared mustard
• French-fried onions

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. In a large bowl, combine hamburger and pork, and next 8 ingredients, mixing well
3. In a small bowl, combine the ketchup and mustard
4. Stir 1 / 4 cup ketchup mixture into meat mixture, reserving remaining ketchup mixture
5. Press meat mixture into a 9 x 5 x 3 inch loaf pan, or shape into a loaf and place on a rack in a broiler pan
6. Spread remaining ketchup mixture over loaf
7. Bake 45 minutes
8. Top with french fired onions
9. Bake another 15 minutes, or until meat is no longer pink<|endoftext|>
