Ingredients:
• Plain flour
• Yellow cheese
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Egg
• Milk
• Sunflower oil
• Bacon
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Grease muffin cups or place paper liners in tin
2. Stir flour, cheese, sugar, baking powder, salt and pepper in a large bowl
3. Combine milk, oil and egg in a small bowl
4. Add wet ingredients all at once to dry ones and stir until flour is just moistened
5. Fold in bacon and fill muffin cups
6. Top with a little extra grated cheese
7. Bake at 180c on the lower oven rack for 20-25 minutes
8. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
