What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>Asian Style Three Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Edamame
• Canola oil
• Rice wine vinegar
• Apricot preserves
• Sugar
• Gingerroot
• Black beans
• Scallions
• Salt

Steps:
1. Put the green beans and frozen edamame into a steamer basket and steam for 4 minutes
2. Drain well, then transfer to a large bowl and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes
3. In a small bowl, whisk together oil, vinegar, apricot preserves, sugar, and ginger
4. Add the black beans and scallions to the green bean mixture and drizzle with dressing
5. Toss to coat and season with salt
6. Serve at room temperature or chilled<|endoftext|>
