Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Apple
• Egg
• Skim milk
• Butter

Steps:
1. In large bowl, combine flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, salt and nutmeg
2. Combine apple, egg milk and butter
3. Add to dry ingredients, stirring just until combined but not over mixed
4. Spoon into lightly greased or paper-lined muffin cups, filling almost to top
5. Bake in 400 f oven for 15-18 minutes or until golden and tops are firm to touch<|endoftext|>
