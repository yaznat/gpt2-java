What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Baked Carrot Pudding

Ingredients:
• Carrots
• Butter
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Coriander
• Brown sugar
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Grate the carrots and preheat the oven to 200c
2. Combine flour, sugar and spices in a bowl
3. In a second bowl cream the butter
4. Add to the flour-mixture
5. Add the carrots and the raisins
6. Blend well
7. Fill into two oven-proof dishes, sprinkle with some sugar and bake for 40-45 minutes
8. Serve with vanilla ice-cream, whipped cream or custard<|endoftext|>
