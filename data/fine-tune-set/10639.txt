What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Tuna Pasta Casserole

Ingredients:
• Macaroni
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Process american cheese
• Tuna in water
• Tart apples
• Breadcrumbs

Steps:
1. Cook macaroni according to package directions
2. Drain
3. In saucepan, melt 6 tb butter or margarine over low heat
4. Blend in flour and 3 / 4 ts salt
5. Add milk all at once
6. Cook quickly, stirring constantly, until mixture thickens and bubbles
7. Add cheese
8. Stir until cheese is melted
9. Stir in tuna, diced apples and drained macaroni
10. Turn into 12x7x2 inch baking dish
11. Combine melted butter and crumbs
12. Sprinkle on top of casserole
13. Bake in 350 oven for 30 minutes, or until apples are tender<|endoftext|>
