What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Death By Chocolate Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Chocolate ice cream
• Chocolate syrup
• Chocolate milk

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients
2. Start blending on low speed gradually increasing to high and blend for about 3 minutes
3. This can be dairy-free if soy milk and soy ice cream are used!<|endoftext|>
