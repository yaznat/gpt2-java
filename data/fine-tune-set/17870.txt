Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Semisweet chocolate
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Cream cheese
• Almond extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f grease a 9x13in pan
2. In a small bowl, combine flour, baking powder and salt
3. In a large saucepan, melt butter and chocolate over low heat, stirring until smooth
4. With a wooden spoon, beat in 1 1 / 2 cups of sugar
5. Add 4 eggs and 2 teaspoons of vanilla, beat until well blended
6. Stir in flour mixture just until blended
7. In a small bowl, with mixer at medium speed, beat cream cheese until smooth, gradually beat in remaining sugar
8. Beat in remaining egg, almond extract and vanilla until just blended
9. Spread 1 / 2 of the chocolate batter in prepared pan
10. Spoon creamcheese mixture in large dollops on top of chocolate
11. Spread remaining chocolate batter over cream cheese mixture
12. "marble" with a butter knife by swirling the knife in broad strokes
13. Bake until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out almost clean, about 35-40 minutes
14. Cool completely before cutting<|endoftext|>
