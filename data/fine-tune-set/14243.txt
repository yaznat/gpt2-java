Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Cornstarch
• Vegetable oil
• White onion
• Yellow bell pepper
• Broccoli floret
• Carrot
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Sweet and sour sauce
• Water

Steps:
1. Press moisture out of the tofu and cut into cubes
2. Place tofu in a gallon-sized plastic bag with the corn starch
3. Shake bag to coat tofu
4. Fry tofu in vegetable oil over medium heat until it has a crunchy coating, about 15 minutes on each side
5. Put tofu and vegetables into the crock-pot
6. Pour bottle of sweet & sour sauce over the top
7. Add a tablespoon of water to the "empty" jar
8. Shake and pour over the top of the tofu & vegetables
9. Stir to combine
10. Cover and cook on low for 3 hours<|endoftext|>
