Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>For Diabetics Veg Chinese Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Rice
• French beans
• Carrot
• Red capsicum
• Celery
• Spring onions
• Soya sauce
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Green chili

Steps:
1. Boil rice in 2 cups of water
2. Ensure that each grain of cooked rice is separate
3. Heat oil in a non-stick pan
4. Add all the veggies except the spring onion greens
5. Sprinkle 2 tbsps
6. Water and cook for 5 minutes
7. Add the rice, soya sauce, spring onion greens and salt
8. Mix well and saute for 2 minutes
9. Serve hot with chillies in vinegar
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
