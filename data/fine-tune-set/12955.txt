Ingredients:
• Fish steaks
• Lime juice
• Oil
• Dijon mustard
• Fresh ginger
• Cayenne pepper
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. 1
2. In a bowl, combine the lime juice, 1 tablespoon oil, ginger, cayenne pepper and enough freshly ground black pepper to suit your taste
3. 2
4. Marinate the fish in the marinade for 45-60 minutes
5. Turn steaks 2-3 times
6. 3
7. Have the grill prepared with white coals and brush the cooking grill with the remaining one tablespoon oil
8. 4
9. Grill the fish, brushing several times with the marinade, until cooked through and opaque in the center
10. 5
11. Turn fish after about 4-5 minutes
12. Total grilling time will depend on your grill and the heat of the coals
13. 6
14. To broil instead, use a broiler pan brushed with oil and broil until center is opaque
15. Will take about 10 minutes total in broiler
16. Turn steaks after 5 minutes, and baste often with marinade<|endoftext|>
