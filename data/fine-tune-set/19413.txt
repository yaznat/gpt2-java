Can you give me a donut recipe?<|endoftext|>Where Did They Go Donut Holes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Sea salt
• Ground nutmeg
• Egg
• Half and half milk
• Butter
• Oil

Steps:
1. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, and nutmeg in a bowl
2. In another bowl, mix together the egg, milk, and butter
3. Add to dry ingredients and mix well
4. Heat oil to 375 degrees
5. Drop dough in by heaping teaspoonfuls, about six at a time, into hot oil
6. Fry until golden brown, turning once
7. Drain on paper towels
8. Roll in sugar<|endoftext|>
