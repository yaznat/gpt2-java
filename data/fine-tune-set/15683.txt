Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Potato
• Vegetable oil
• Water
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Cajun seasoning
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oil to medium high heat and fry potato pieces until crispy on the outside, and soft on the inside, like homefries
2. Drain on a paper towel
3. Meanwhile, in a seperate skillet, brown ground beef until done through
4. Drain well
5. Return beef to skillet and add water, chili powder, cumin and cajun seasoning
6. Stir to make a taco sauce
7. Add potatoes and stir until sauce coats potatoes
8. Add cheese and stir until melted
9. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
