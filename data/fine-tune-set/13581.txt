Ingredients:
• Beef
• Bacon
• Breadcrumbs
• Eggs
• Salt and pepper
• Hard-boiled eggs

Steps:
1. Mince the beef, add breadcrumbs, salt and pepper, minced bacon, the hard boiled eggs, chopped and 2 beaten eggs
2. Mould into a long sausage and bake in a slow oven for 3 hours<|endoftext|>
