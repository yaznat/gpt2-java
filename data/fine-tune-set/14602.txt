Give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>5 Spice Beef Rice

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Pepper
• Bay leaf
• Garlic powder
• Thyme
• Oregano
• Mushroom soup
• Tomatoes
• Brown rice
• Olive

Steps:
1. Brown meat and onion
2. Stir in seasonings, soup, tomatoes and rice
3. You may need a little bit of extra water if using brown instant rice
4. Bring to a boil, and then reduce the heat
5. Simmer for 5 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until rice is cooked
6. Serve with sliced olives
7. Salt and pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
