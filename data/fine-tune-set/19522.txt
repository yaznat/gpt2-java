Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Water
• Shredded coconut

Steps:
1. Boil the sweet potatoes in their jackets until tender, about 25 minutes
2. Let cool and then cut into slices about 1 1 / 2 inches thick
3. In a large skillet melt butter
4. Stir in brown sugar and water and cook on medium heat for about 5 minutes
5. Reduce heat and add sweet potatoes
6. Cook gently, tossing lightly until sweet potatoes are glazed
7. Top with coconut before serving<|endoftext|>
