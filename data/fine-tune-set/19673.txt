Give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Amazing Vegan Sweet Potato Fries Baked

Ingredients:
• Garlic salt
• Pepper
• Ground cayenne pepper
• Sweet potatoes
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine the garlic salt, and peppers
2. Set aside
3. Preheat the oven to 400f
4. Peel the sweet potatoes and cut in half lengthwise
5. Cut each half into 6 wedges
6. In a large bowl, combine with the olive oil and the spice mixture
7. Toss until evenly coated
8. Place wedges in a single layer on a baking sheet and place on the middle shelf of the oven
9. Bake for approximately 30 minutes or until cooked through and edges are crisp
10. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
