Ingredients:
• Chocolate sandwich style cookies
• Chocolate syrup
• Vanilla ice cream
• Bananas
• Strawberry ice cream
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Crumble cookies into a 9-inch pie plate, or cake pan
2. Pat down to form an even layer
3. Drizzle 1 cup of chocolate topping evenly over the cookies with a small spoon
4. Scoop the vanilla ice cream on top of the chocolate and smooth down
5. Cover with half of the sliced bananas
6. Top with strawberry ice cream
7. Cover and freeze until firm
8. Before serving, top with 1 / 4 cup chocolate topping, whipped cream, and sliced bananas<|endoftext|>
