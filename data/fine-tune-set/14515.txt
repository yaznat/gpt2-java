Give me a beans recipe.<|endoftext|>Aunt Vera S Baked Beans

Ingredients:
• Pork and beans
• Bacon
• Ketchup
• Yellow mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Onion
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Fry bacon until crisp
2. Cool and crumble
3. Saut onion in the bacon drippings
4. In a large bowl, combine the pork and beans, bacon, ketchup, mustard, worcestershire sauce, sauted onion and brown sugar and mix well
5. Pour into a large casserole dish and bake for 1 hour at 300f<|endoftext|>
