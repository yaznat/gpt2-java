What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>African Spiced Vegetable Salad

Ingredients:
• Fresh broccoli florets
• Cauliflower floret
• Carrot
• Salt
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Ground ginger
• Ground cumin
• Ground coriander
• Ground nutmeg
• Nonfat sour cream
• Cider vinegar
• Honey
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Steam the vegetables, covered 2 minutes
2. Rinse under cold water
3. Drain well
4. Combine spices in a skillet over medium heat and cook 2 minutes stirring constantly
5. In a medium bowl, combine spice mixture, sour cream, vinegar, and honey
6. Add vegetables and mix well<|endoftext|>
