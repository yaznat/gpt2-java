Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Crepes Or Omelette With Tasty Ground Meat

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Flour
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Wine
• Tomato concentrate
• Ground beef
• Tomatoes
• Dried oregano leaves
• Ground cinnamon
• Cumin powder
• Chili
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients together until smooth
2. Grease your pan
3. Pour a ladle full of batter into the pan, swirling the pan to spread it all around
4. Cook till the top starts to bubble and then flip
5. Prepare all the omelettes in the same way
6. During this time you can begin to prepare the meat sauce
7. Keep the omelettes warm until the sauce is ready
8. Fry onion in large pan till golden
9. Add beef and fry on higher heat for about 5 minutes
10. Add tomato concentrate and fry 2 more minutes
11. Add wine and continue to cook until evaporated
12. Add tomatoes and spices until sauce gets thick
13. Season with salt and adjust spices to your taste
14. Serve with the sauce<|endoftext|>
