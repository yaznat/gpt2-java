What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Chili Ever

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Lean ground beef
• Onions
• Yellow bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Chili powder
• Tomato paste
• Ground cumin
• Unsweetened baking cocoa
• Ground cinnamon
• Fennel seeds
• Red pepper flakes
• Beans
• Green chilies
• Water

Steps:
1. In a very large, deep pre-heated skillet, add olive oil, then add chopped onions and peppers
2. I also add a little bit of water to the pan
3. Cover and cook on medium-low heat, just until onions and peppers begin to sweat
4. Uncover and add ground beef, using spatula to break up chunks of meat
5. Cover and cook on low heat until meat is cooked through, about 20 minutes or so
6. While the meat is cooking, i add the chili powder
7. I start with 1 tbsp
8. , and add as i go
9. Once the meat is browned, add the tomato paste, stirring to incorporate completely
10. Cover and cook on lower heat for 30 minutes
11. Add the 5 ingredients, stir to incorporate, cover and cook on low for another 60 minutes
12. Add beans and green chiles, stirring to incorporate
13. At this point, add more water if you need to do so
14. Cover and cook on low for another 40 minutes
15. Total cooking time is about 2-1 / 2 to 3 hours on stovetop
16. I know this sounds like a long time, but it really needs that much time to simmer and let the flavors blend
17. Serve with chopped red onion, sour cream, hot sauce, and shredded cheddar, colby and / or jack cheese<|endoftext|>
