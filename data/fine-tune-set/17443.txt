What's a good coffee recipe?<|endoftext|>Baileys Coffee Frappe

Ingredients:
• Baileys irish cream
• Scotch whisky
• Milk
• Honey
• Instant coffee
• Boiling water

Steps:
1. Blend two heaped teaspoons of instant coffee with one small cup of boiling water
2. Blend all ingredients with 2-3 ice cubes
3. Garnish with coffee beans<|endoftext|>
