Can you give me a beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado White Bean Hummus

Ingredients:
• White beans
• Avocado
• Lemon juice
• Tahini
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Drain white beans and set liquid aside to balance consistency at the end
2. Combine remaining ingredients in blender or food processor
3. Blend until thoroughly mixed and smooth
4. Serve immediately with fresh, warm or toasted pita bread, corn chips, or cover and refrigerate
