What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>African Split Pea Soup

Ingredients:
• Dried split peas
• Water
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Bay leaves
• Ground cumin
• Cinnamon
• Cayenne
• Ground cardamom
• Vegetable stock
• Fresh lemon juice
• Cooked rice
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Rinse peas, put in soup pot with 3 cups water and bring to a boil
2. Reduce heat, cover and simmer for 45 minutes to an hour
3. Meanwhile, saute the onions and garlic in oil until translucent
4. Mix in spices and saute for 5 - 10 minutes more, stirring frequently to prevent burning
5. Set aside
6. When the split peas are cooked, stir in the onion mixture along with the vegetable stock
7. Add the lemon juice and cooked rice, season with salt and pepper
8. Stir in the parsley and gently reheat if necessary<|endoftext|>
