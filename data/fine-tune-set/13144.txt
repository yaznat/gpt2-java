Ingredients:
• Halibut
• Leeks
• New potato
• Tomatoes
• Celery heart
• Salt
• White pepper
• Thyme
• Bay leaf
• Parsley
• Water
• Egg yolk
• Cream

Steps:
1. Wash the leeks thoroughly, and cut the whites only into 1" rounds
2. Peel the potatoes, and cut them into chunks to fit a soup spoon
3. Skin the tomatoes, and deseed them
4. Dice the celery heart into small pieces
5. Put the above ingredients into a soup pan with 1 quart of water
6. Add the salt, pepper, thyme, and bay leaf
7. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 25 minutes
8. Discard the thyme and the bay leaf
9. Clean the fish, skin it and fillet it carefully
10. Cut into chunks
11. Add the fish to the vegetables and broth, and simmer this over very low heat for 15 minutes
12. Beat the egg yolk with the cream in a bowl
13. Add some of the broth to this, mix it around, and put the whole lot into the soup pan
14. Keep over very low heat, and stir until the broth thickens somewhat
15. Don't boil it!<|endoftext|>
