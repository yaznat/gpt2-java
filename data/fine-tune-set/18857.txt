Ingredients:
• Butter
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sugar
• Lemon juice
• French bread cubes
• Fresh blueberries
• Fresh lemon rind
• Salt
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Pudding: melt butter in a 13x9x2-inch baking dish
2. Set aside
3. Combine next 4 ingredients in a large bowl
4. Beat well
5. Add bread cubes, and let stand 5 minutes
6. Fold in blueberries and lemon rind
7. Spoon into prepared dish
8. Bake at 350 for 35 minutes or until lightly browned and puffed
9. Serve warm with custard sauce
10. Yield: 10 servings custard sauce: combine eggs, sugar, and salt in top of a double boiler, beating well
11. Gradually stir about 1 / 2 cup milk into egg mixture
12. Add remaining milk, stirring constantly
13. Bring water in bottom of double boiler to a boil
14. Reduce heat to low
15. Cook custard over hot water, stirring occasionally, about 15 minutes or until mixture thickens
16. Cool slightly
17. Add vanilla and lemon rind
18. Yield: 1 1 / 3 cups<|endoftext|>
