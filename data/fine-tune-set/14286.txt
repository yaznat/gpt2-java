Give me a tofu recipe.<|endoftext|>Fillet O Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Sherry wine vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Hot pepper sauce
• Eggs
• Flour
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Slice the tofu into four even slices
2. Wrap each slice in a paper towel and place on a cookie sheet
3. Top with another cookie sheet and a 28 oz can of tomatoes
4. Let sit for 1 hour
5. Combine vinegar, worcestershire sauce and hot sauce
6. Marinate tofu sauce in this, 15 minutes per side
7. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium high heat
8. Drain tofu slices on paper towels
9. Dredge in flour, then coat the slices with a thin coat of beaten egg
10. Gently slide the slices into the hot oil, cook for 2 minutes, carefully turn the slices over and cook another 2 minutes
11. Gently move to serving plate<|endoftext|>
