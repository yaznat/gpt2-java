What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Don T Eat The Yellow Snowman Cookies

Ingredients:
• Lemon cake mix
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Flaked coconut
• Decorative candies
• Miniature chocolate chip
• Candy sprinkles

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. In large bowl, combine cake mix, oil and eggs
3. Beat until well mixed
4. Place coconut in shallow bowl
5. For each cookie, use a cookie dough scoop to shape a 1-1 / 2-inch ball for the body, and a melon baller to shape a 1 / 2-inch ball for the head
6. Roll each ball in coconut
7. Place on ungreased cookie sheet with smaller ball touching larger ball
8. Place cookies 1-1 / 2-inches apart
9. Bake at 350 for 10-12 minutes or until coconut just begins to brown
10. Immediately press eyes into head and buttons down the front of the snowman's body for each cookie
11. Remove from cookie sheet to cool, taking care not to let the head separate from the body!<|endoftext|>
