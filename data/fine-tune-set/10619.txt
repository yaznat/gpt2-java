Ingredients:
• Seafood
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Bell pepper
• Garlic clove
• Diced tomatoes
• Oregano
• Salt & pepper
• Angel hair pasta

Steps:
1. In skillet, combine olive oil, onion, bell pepper & garlic
2. Saut over medium heat until veggies are tender
3. Add tomatoes, oregano, salt & pepper
4. Bring to a boil for 1 minute
5. Reduce heat & simmer for 10 minutes
6. Add seafood & simmer another 3 minutes
7. Serve warm sauce over pasta
