Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Steak fillets
• Celery rib
• Garlic cloves
• Brown onion
• Dry white wine
• Beef stock granules
• Double cream
• Mixed peppercorns
• Fresh thyme leave

Steps:
1. Heat half the oil in a large frying pan, cook beef on both sides, until cooked as desired
2. Transfer to a warm oven to keep warm
3. If like me you have very thick steaks i find i can not cook them to a medium rare on the stove, without burning the outside so i finish them off in the oven for about 15 min's, on a higher heat setting
4. Heat remaining oil in same pan, add celery, onion and garlic, cook, stirring until vegetables soften
5. Add wine
6. Stir until liquid is reduced by half, add cream, peppercorns and stock, bring to the boil
7. Reduce heat
8. Simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, about 5 min's or until sauce thickens slightly
9. Stir in thyme leaves, serve immediately over steak
10. I served mine over celeriac mash and a carrot casserole as a side but serve with your favourite veg and potato<|endoftext|>
