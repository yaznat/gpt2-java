What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>30 Minute Cream Of Mushroom Soup

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Celery
• Leeks
• Sliced mushrooms
• Flour
• Milk
• Chicken stock
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in your soup pot over medium heat
2. Add all the vegetables and saute until the onions are opaque and the celery and leek have softened - approximately 5 - 10 minutes
3. Add flour and mix well
4. Add milk and chicken stock and mix well
5. Season with slat and pepper
6. Reduce heat to medium low - cover and cook for 25 minutes stirring occasionally
7. Use a hand blender or a regular blender to puree the soup
8. Serve<|endoftext|>
