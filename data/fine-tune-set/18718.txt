Ingredients:
• Dark brown sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Pecans
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Ground nutmeg
• Banana
• Nonfat milk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• Boiling water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine brown sugar and 2 teaspoons flour, set aside
3. Combine 1 1 / 3 cups flour and next 7 ingredients in a medium bowl, stir with a wire whisk
4. Make a well in the center of mixture
5. Combine banana and next 3 ingredients, add flour mixture, stirring just until moist
6. Pour batter into a 8 inch square baking pan, sprinkle with brown sugar mixture
7. Pour 1 1 / 2 cups boiling water over batter
8. Bake at 350 for 40 minutes or until pudding is bubbly and cake springs back when lightly touched in center
9. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
