What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Pizzaiola

Ingredients:
• Beef sirloin
• Stewed tomatoes
• Garlic
• Fresh basil
• Oregano
• Cracked pepper
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in a pan
2. Add beef strips and stir well
3. Top with tomatoes, garlic, oregano, basil, and pepper
4. Place lid on pan and cook on medium / high for 10 minutes
5. Reduce heat to low and cook for 30-45 minutes, or until meat is tender
6. Serve over rice, or egg noodles<|endoftext|>
