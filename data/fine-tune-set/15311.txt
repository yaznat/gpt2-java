Ingredients:
• Warm water
• Maple syrup
• Vinegar
• Oil
• Gluten
• Whole wheat flour
• Instant yeast
• Salt

Steps:
1. Mix the first 7 ingredients on speed 1 until moistened
2. Cover and let rise until it reaches the top of the bowl
3. Add 1 t salt
4. Add enough flour until the dough pulls away from the side of the bowl
5. I usually add about 2 more cups
6. Knead on speed 1 for 5 minutes
7. Form large balls - 6-9 per cookie sheet
8. Use parchment paper
9. Let rise 45 minutes
10. Bake for 25 minutes at 400 until golden brown
11. Brush with butter while still hot
12. Use for hamburgers, sloppy joes, anything you would use a bun for
13. Or eat it with butter!<|endoftext|>
