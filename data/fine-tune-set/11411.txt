Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Al S Garlic Bread

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Garlic powder
• Italian seasoning
• Dried oregano
• Pepper
• Italian cheese blend
• French bread

Steps:
1. Combine first 5 ingredients and 1 / 3 cup of cheese
2. Spread mixture evenly on cut sides of bread
3. Sprinkle evenly with remaining 2 / 3 cup of cheese
4. Place on a baking sheet
5. Bake at 375 degrees fahrenheit for 15 to 20 minutes, or until cheese is melted and golden
6. Let stand 2 to 3 minutes before slicing and serving<|endoftext|>
