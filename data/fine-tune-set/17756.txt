What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Multigrain Muffins

Ingredients:
• Multi-grain hot cereal
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter
• Unbleached flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Nonfat milk
• Sunflower oil
• Egg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f line twelve muffin cups with paper baking cups or lightly oil bottoms only
2. Prepare topping
3. Mix oats, brown sugar, and cinnamon together
4. Add melted butter and mix well
5. Prepare muffins
6. Mix oats, flour, baking powder and soda, and cinnamon in a bowl
7. In separate bowl, mix applesauce, milk, brown sugar, oil, and egg
8. Add to dry ingredients and stir just until dry ingredients are moistened
9. Fill cups with mix, and top each muffin with topping
10. Then bake 18-20 minutes
11. Serve warm if possible
12. Yum! simple and delicious<|endoftext|>
