Please give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>Alexandra Soup

Ingredients:
• Barley
• Lentils
• Butter
• Onion
• Water
• Tomato puree
• Milk
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Scald the barley
2. Wash the lentils
3. Put barley and lentils into a pan
4. Add water, butter and onion
5. Bring to the boil and simmer for 1 1 / 2 hours
6. Add tomato puree and cook for 20 minutes longer
7. Sieve soup
8. Reheat, season with salt and pepper and add milk if desired<|endoftext|>
