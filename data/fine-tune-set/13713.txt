Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cider Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin chops
• Butter
• Yellow onions
• Salt
• Apple cider
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Brown chops on both sides in a large skillet, remove and set aside
2. Melt butter in same large skillet
3. Thinly slice onion and cook in butter for 15 minutes or until caramelized
4. Add apple cider to onion and then nestle chops down in the onion and cider
5. Cook over medium heat for 10 minutes until chops are cooked through and cider reduces by a little more then half<|endoftext|>
