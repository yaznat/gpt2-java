Ingredients:
• Seedless cucumber
• Red ripe tomatoes
• Red onion
• Red bell pepper
• Water-packed artichoke hearts
• Italian parsley
• Kosher salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Lemon, juice of
• Shallot
• Red wine vinegar
• Oregano
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Tuna in vegetable oil
• Pepperoncini peppers
• Kalamata olive

Steps:
1. Arrange the cucumbers, tomatoes, onion, red pepper, artichoke hearts and parsley on a platter
2. Squeeze the lemon juice over the platter
3. Season with salt and pepper
4. Place shallots, vinegar and oregano in a bowl and let stand for 5 minutes
5. Whisk in olive oil in a slow stream
6. Pour dressing over salad
7. Garnish salad with chunks of tuna, pepperoncini peppers and olives<|endoftext|>
