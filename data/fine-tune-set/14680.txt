Give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>Apple Rice Pilaf

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Granny smith apple
• Apple juice
• Uncooked rice
• Chicken broth
• Ground thyme
• Salt

Steps:
1. Saute onions and apple in butter until tender
2. Add apple juice and bring to a boil
3. Add rice, salt, and thyme and simmer on low for 15 minutes or until almost done
4. Add broth and simmer until absorbed<|endoftext|>
