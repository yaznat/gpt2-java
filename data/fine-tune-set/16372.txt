What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapper Water Chestnuts

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Water chestnuts
• Lemon juice
• Worcestershire sauce
• Seasoning salt

Steps:
1. Combine lemon juice, worcestershire sauce, and seasoning salt in a bowl
2. Add water chestnuts to bowl or plastic baggy and let marinate for 15 minutes
3. Drain water chestnuts and wrap one half slice of bacon around each one, secure with toothpicks
4. Arrange on broiler pan and broil for 2-5 minutes on each side
5. Bacon should be crispy
6. Place on serving dish and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
