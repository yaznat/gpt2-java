What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Cinnamon Cheesecake Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Part-skim ricotta cheese
• Low fat sweetened condensed milk
• Skim milk
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Mix all of the ingredients in the food processor
2. Place in fridge to harden for 1-2 hours
3. Place in ice cream maker and make it according to your ice cream maker's directions<|endoftext|>
