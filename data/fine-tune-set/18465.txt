Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Double Chocolate Waffles

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Egg
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Miniature chocolate chip
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Preheat your waffle iron
2. Melt butter
3. Combine flour, cocoa, sugar, baking powder and salt
4. Separate egg and beat yolk with milk and vanilla
5. Stir milk mixture and melted butter in to flour mixture until dry ingredients are well-moistened- batter may be slightly lumpy
6. Stir in chocolate chips
7. Beat egg white until it holds stiff peaks
8. Gently fold into batter
9. Pour batter onto waffle irion using about 1 / 2 cup for two 4" waffles
10. Bake until crisp and browned
11. Serve with whipped cream and fruit or ice cream and chocolate sauce<|endoftext|>
