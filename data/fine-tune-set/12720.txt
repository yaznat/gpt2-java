Ingredients:
• Vidalia onion
• Fresh ground pepper
• Butter
• Ham steak
• Cornstarch
• Unsweetened apple juice
• Apple butter

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, saute onion and pepper in butter until crisp tender
2. Remove from pan and set aside
3. Cut ham steak into 4 pieces and in the same skillet, brown ham on both sides
4. While ham is browning, in a small bowl, combine the cornstarch and apple juice until smooth
5. Then pour over ham
6. Bring juices to a boil and cook while stirring for 1-2 minutes or until slightly thickened
7. Reduce heat and spread apple butter over ham
8. Top with sauteed onion
9. Cover pan and simmer for 5-7 minutes or until thoroughly heated through<|endoftext|>
