What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Ingredient Triple Fudge Cake

Ingredients:
• Chocolate pudding mix
• Chocolate cake mix
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Prepare pudding following directions on the package
2. Stir in cake mix
3. Spread in a greased 13"x9" baking pan
4. Sprinkle with chocolate chips
5. Bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 35 minutes
6. Cool<|endoftext|>
