Give me a recipe for stir fry.<|endoftext|>Bea S Shrimp And Green Veggie Stir Fry With Mushrooms

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Onion salt
• Shrimp
• Celery
• Broccoli
• Asparagus spears
• Green pepper
• Mushrooms
• Green onions
• Baby bok choy

Steps:
1. Using an electric wok at high setting, heat 2 tbsp oil, tbsp ginger and 2 tbsp garlic, until sizzling
2. Cook shrimp until heated through, drizzle with tsp onion salt
3. Set aside
4. Clean wok and add 2 tbsp oil, tbsp ginger and 2 tbsp garlic and bring to a sizzle at high heat
5. Add celery, broccoli, asparagus and green pepper, cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally
6. Toss in the mushrooms and white part of green onions and cook for 3 minutes
7. Toss in the green part of green onion and the baby bok choy and tsp onion salt and cook for another 2 minutes
8. Add cooked shrimp, toss and serve over rice or noodles or by itself<|endoftext|>
