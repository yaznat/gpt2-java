Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Red bell peppers
• Dark raisins
• Lemon juice
• Fresh parsley
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Fresh rosemary leaves
• Salt
• Cracked black pepper
• Sliced almonds
• Greens

Steps:
1. In an oven
2. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
3. Roast the sweet potatoes for 45 minutes or until fork tender
4. Set aside to cool then dice
5. If desired, line the salad bowl with greens of your choice
6. Place the sweet potatoes in salad bowl
7. Add the other ingredients except for the almonds
8. Season with salt and pepper to taste
9. Serve immediately or cover and chill until ready to eat
10. Just before serving, garnish the salad with the almonds<|endoftext|>
