Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Amish Mennonite Dried Beef Gravy Mmmmmmmm

Ingredients:
• Dried beef
• Butter
• Flour
• Milk
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Melt butter in skillet
2. Tear the dried beef into small pieces & stir into the butter
3. Brown meat lightly
4. Stir in flour
5. When flour is dissolved into butter, add milk, stirring constantly
6. Cook over low heat until the mixture thickens<|endoftext|>
