Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Flour
• Sugar
• Whole almonds

Steps:
1. Beat egg whites until very stiff
2. Gradually add sugar and beat until of meringue consistency
3. Fold in flour and whole almonds
4. Put in lightly greased, lined loaf tin
5. Bake in a 325 degrees oven for 40 to 50 minutes
6. Leave in tin until completely cold
7. Take out of tin and wrap in foil and store in refrigerator for one or two days
8. With a very sharp knife cut loaf into wafer thin slices
9. Place on cookie sheets and put in slow oven for about 45 minutes, until dried out
10. Cool and store in airtight container
11. Will keep for 3 or 4 months<|endoftext|>
