Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh basil leaf
• Avocado
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Cook and drain pasta according to directions on package
2. While pasta is cooking, heat oil in 3 quart saucepan over medium heat
3. Cook garlic in oil about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until garlic is tender but not brown
4. Remove from heat
5. Stir basil, avocado and tomatoes into garlic in saucepan
6. Toss vegetable mixture and pastas
7. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
