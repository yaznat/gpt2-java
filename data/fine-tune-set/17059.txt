What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Cocktail Team Vanilla Shooter

Ingredients:
• Vanilla vodka
• Coffee
• Cream
• Simple syrup
• Tequila

Steps:
1. Chill a shot glass in the freezer
2. Pour vodka, coffee, cream, simple syrup and tequila into a shaker filled with ice and shake 30 seconds til cold
3. Pour into chilled shot glasses and serve!<|endoftext|>
