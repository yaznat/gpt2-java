Ingredients:
• Almond paste
• Salt
• Sugar
• Butter
• Egg
• Flour
• Almond extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Butter a 9x9 pan, line with foil, then butter the foil
3. In food processor, pulse almond paste until broken in small bits, then add 1 / 4 c sugar and salt, processing 1 minute more
4. In a large bowl, beat together butter and remaining sugar, 3 minutes
5. Add almond mixture, egg yolk, and almond extract, beat 2 minutes more
6. Reduce speed, then add flour
7. Mix until combined
8. Spread batter evenly in pan and brush with egg white
9. Bake 35-40 minutes
10. Cool in pan 1 hour
11. Cut into 25 squares<|endoftext|>
