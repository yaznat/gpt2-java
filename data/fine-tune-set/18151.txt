Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Apple cider
• Cornstarch
• Lemon juice
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Butter
• Egg
• Cinnamon
• Pancake mix
• Apple
• Milk
• Bulk pork sausage
• Oil

Steps:
1. Cider syrup: in medium saucepan, combine sugar, cornstarch and pumpkin pie spice
2. Stir in apple cider and lemon juice
3. Cook, stirring constantly, until mixture thickens and boils for 1 minute
4. Stir in butter
5. Reduce heat just to keep syrup warm while making pancakes
6. Heat griddle
7. Pancakes: in small bowl, beat egg on high speed until thick and lemon colored, about 5 minutes
8. Stir in pancake mix, milk, oil and cinnamon
9. Fold in apples and sausage
10. Grease griddle lightly before making each batch
11. Pour batter from 1 / 4 cup measuring cup onto hot griddle
12. Bake until bubbles appear, about 2 minutes
13. Flip and cook another 2 minutes
14. Serve with hot cider syrup<|endoftext|>
