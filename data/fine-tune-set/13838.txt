Ingredients:
• Pork
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Red pepper flakes
• Eggs
• Oil
• Water
• Cornstarch
• Vinegar
• Fish sauce
• Chili pepper flakes
• Carrot
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. For the pork:
2. Put pork in a bowl, cover with fresh cool water, add about 1 1 / 2 teaspoons salt
3. Rub the pork, and then let set for about 15 minutes
4. After 15 minutes, rinse in clean water
5. This is a step that most chinese do to clean the pork
6. Slice pork 1 / 4 inch thick and in 1 x 1 inch pieces
7. Combine the rest of the ingredients in a non-metalic container
8. Add pork and mix thoroughly with the marinade
9. Put in the refrigerator at least 6 hours, or even better, overnight
10. The longer the pork sits, the better the flavor
11. Use a deep fry pan - wok is better
12. Heat 2 inches of oil over medium / high heat
13. Fry the pork in batches until golden brown
14. Remove with slotted spoon to a tray
15. For the sauce:
16. Mix / whisk all and bring to boil either in a small saucepan or in a microwave safe bowl
17. Continue whisking so that the cornstarch does not lump up
18. Set aside
19. Serve warm with dipping sauce<|endoftext|>
