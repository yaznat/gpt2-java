Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Easy Peasy Breakfast Waffles

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Milk

Steps:
1. Cream butter with sugar and vanilla sugar
2. Add the eggs one by one
3. Mix baking powder and flour and add to the egg-mixture alternating with the milk
4. Pour portions of the dough into a well-heated and lightly greased waffle iron and bake until golden brown
5. Keep warm in the oven until you have made all the waffles and are ready to eat them<|endoftext|>
