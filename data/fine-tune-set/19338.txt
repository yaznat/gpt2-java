Give me a recipe for donuts.<|endoftext|>Apple Cider Cake Donuts

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Egg
• Brown sugar
• Apple butter
• Maple syrup
• Apple cider
• Plain yogurt
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven 400 deg
2. Coat molds with cooking spray
3. In a bowl, blend together first 5 ingredients
4. In another bowl, combine remaining ingredients and add dry ingredients
5. Stir until just blended
6. Put 1 / 4 cup dough into each of the four molds
7. Bake 12 minutes
8. Cool slightly and remove
9. Clean and spray molds again and use the other half of the dough
10. Bake 12 minutes
11. Cool slightly and enjoy with coffee<|endoftext|>
