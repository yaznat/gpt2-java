What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Crystallized Ginger Tofu

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Firm tofu
• Garlic
• Canola oil
• Vegetable broth
• Crystallized ginger
• Soy sauce
• Sweet potato
• Green pepper
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. In a shallow dish, combine flour, salt and black pepper
2. Dredge tofu in the seasoned flour
3. Shake off excess
4. In a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat, cook garlic in hot oil about 10 seconds
5. Add tofu and stir-fry 2 to 3 minutes, or until light golden
6. Remove tofu to a plate
7. Add broth, ginger, soy sauce and sweet potato to the wok and bring to a boil
8. Cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer 10 minutes, or until potato is just tender
9. Return broth to a boil
10. Add green pepper and tofu and cook, stirring, 2 minutes or until tofu is just cooked through and sauce has thickened slightly
11. Sprinkle with cilantro<|endoftext|>
