Ingredients:
• Vanilla wafer cookies
• Shredded coconut
• Peppermint patties
• Sesame seeds
• Vanilla frosting
• Honey
• Green food coloring
• Red food coloring
• Yellow food coloring
• Potato sticks

Steps:
1. Place coconut into a ziploc sandwich bag and drop in several drops of green food coloring
2. Roll around in your hands until all the coconut is tinted
3. Set aside
4. In a separate bowl, tint some frosting red like ketchup and in another bowl, tint frosting yellow like mustard
5. Set frosting aside
6. Decide how many cookies you need, unwrap that many peppermint patties and set out twice as many wafter cookies
7. Set half the wafer cookies, flat side up, onto a cookie sheet
8. Put a small dab of vanilla frosting onto cookie and gently press down a peppermint patty
9. Spread some "ketchup" and some "mustard" on top of the patty, sprinkle some "lettuce" on it and then place another cookie on top
10. Brush a little honey on the top "bun" and sprinkle some sesame seeds onto them
11. Set aside
12. Take triangularly cut sheets of paper and fold them to resemble a french fry 'bag'
13. If you want, use markers first to draw some golden arches and write "mc ___ on them
14. Then, tape them together and fill with some potato sticks
15. Place a cheeseburger and bag of fries on a small paper plate and serve!<|endoftext|>
