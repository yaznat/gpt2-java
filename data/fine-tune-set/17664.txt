What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Apple Walnut Muffins

Ingredients:
• Self raising flour
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Oil
• Vanilla
• Walnuts
• Apples

Steps:
1. Mix together the flour, baking soda, and cinnamon
2. Stir in the sugar
3. Beat oil, eggs and vanilla together and add to dry ingredients
4. Add walnuts and grated apple
5. Stir to just moisten
6. Do not overmix
7. Add mix evenly to a 12 hole muffin pan
8. Bake at 190'c for 15-20 minutes
9. Remove from oven and leave in muffin pan for 10 minutes before turning out onto a wire rack<|endoftext|>
