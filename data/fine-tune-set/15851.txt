Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco seasoning
• Refried beans
• Salsa
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Olive

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix together ground beef and taco seasoning
2. Form mixture into a loaf shape and place in a loaf pan for baking
3. Cook for 30-45 minutes at 375 degrees
4. Remove loaf from pan
5. Drain any drippings or grease from the bottom of the pan
6. In the bottom of the pan spread refried beans
7. Put meatloaf back on top of beans
8. Pour salsa on top of meatloaf
9. Sprinkle top with cheese
10. Return to oven for 10 minutes, until cheese is melted
11. Slice to serve
12. Top each serving with a dollop of sour cream and a sprinkling of sliced olives<|endoftext|>
