Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Sweet green pepper
• Chili powder
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Pepper
• Diced tomatoes
• Black beans
• Corn kernel

Steps:
1. Trim any fat from chicken breasts
2. Cut into 1-inch cubes
3. In large heavy saucepan, heat oil over medium-high heat
4. Cook chicken for about 5 minutes or until no longer pink inside
5. Transfer to plate
6. Add onion, green pepper, chili powder, oregano, salt and pepper to pan
7. Cook over medium heat, stirring often, for about 5 minutes or until vegetables are softened
8. Add tomatoes and beans
9. Increase heat and boil, stirring often, for 10 minutes
10. Add corn
11. Return chicken to pan
