Ingredients:
• Pork
• Eggs
• Skim milk
• Dijon-style mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper
• Breadcrumbs
• Italian parsley
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 400f
2. Line a baking pan with a few layers of aluminum foil and set aside
3. In a large bowl, add the eggs, milk, mustard, worcestershire, salt and pepper
4. Whisk until everything is evenly combined
5. Add the ground pork, bread crumbs and parsley
6. Mix until combined
7. Transfer the meat to the prepared baking sheet and form it into a 9 by 5-inch loaf
8. Arrange the bacon across the top of the meatloaf and bake for about 55 to 65 minutes until the internal temperature of the meatloaf reaches 160f, on an instant-read meat thermometer
9. Remove from the oven to a cutting board and let cool for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing and serving
