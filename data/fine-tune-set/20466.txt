Ingredients:
• Pine nuts
• Butter
• Pearl barley
• Scallion
• Chicken broth
• Dried apricot
• Golden raisin

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Grease a 13x9 baking dish
3. In a large skillet add 2 tbsp butter, heat pine nuts until lightly toasted
4. Remove and set aside
5. In same skillet heat remaining butter, saute barley and scallions until tender, 2-3 minutes
6. Add broth, bring to a boil
7. Stir in apricots, raisins, and nuts
8. Pour into the grease baking dish and bake uncovered for 1 1 / 4 hrs or until barley is tender<|endoftext|>
