Ingredients:
• Cod
• Mango
• Jalapeno pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Onion
• Lemon juice
• Salsa verde
• Low-fat sour cream
• Flour tortillas
• Vegetable broth

Steps:
1. Microwave the cod until the ice is melted, but the fish isn't fully cooked
2. On a frying pan on medium heat, pour in the lemon juice and then the fish
3. Saut until the fish is almost cooked through, then turn off the heat
4. On a cutting board, dice the mango into 1 inch cubes
5. Also cut up the jalapeo pepper
6. Put both of these, along with the onion wedges, in a roasting pan, and set in the oven at medium-high heat
7. Cook for 15 minutes, or until mango and onions caramelize slightly
8. Take the vegetables out of the oven and place them in a frying pan along with the fish
9. Add the vegetable broth
10. Saut for about 3 minutes
11. Add the cayenne pepper, then spoon the mixture into the tortillas
12. Add a tablespoon of sour cream and a tablespoon of salsa on each one before serving
13. Bon appetit!<|endoftext|>
