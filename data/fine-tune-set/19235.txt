What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Malt W O Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Ice cubes
• Part-skim ricotta cheese
• Instant malted milk powder
• Chocolate syrup
• Skim milk
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Put all in a blender and puree until smooth
2. If not smooth, ice chips will distract from flavor
3. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
