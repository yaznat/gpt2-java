Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>All In One Warm Artichoke Bread Dip

Ingredients:
• Monterey jack cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Kraft 100% parmesan cheese
• Onion
• Garlic
• Artichoke hearts
• Mayonnaise
• Sourdough bread

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Cut the top off the bread to make a "lid"
3. Hollow out the bread to make a nice big bowl to put the dip into
4. I buy an extra sourdough bread, cuz this goes fast! cut the extra sour dough bread into pieces as you might run out of the first helping!
5. Cut the hollowed out stuff into bite sized pieces for dipping
6. Mix the remaining ingredients all together in a large bowl
7. Put it in the hollowed out bread bowl
8. Replace the bread lid and cover the whole top with some foil wrap
9. Place it on a cookie sheet and pop in the oven
10. Bake x 60 minutes
11. Remove from oven, take off the foil and the bread lid and let cool about 10-15 mins before serving<|endoftext|>
