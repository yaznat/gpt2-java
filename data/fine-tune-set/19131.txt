What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Blueberry Sour Cream Ice Cream Low Fat No Added Sugar

Ingredients:
• Fresh blueberries
• Stevia powder
• Lemon juice
• Reduced-fat sour cream
• Almond milk
• Almond extract
• Berries

Steps:
1. Puree blueberries in a food processor
2. Blend in all other ingredients and chill for 2-4 hours
3. Freeze in your ice cream machine according to manufacturers instructions<|endoftext|>
