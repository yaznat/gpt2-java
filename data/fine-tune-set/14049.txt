What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Curried Lamb With Apricots

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Lamb
• Onions
• Garlic
• Jalapenos
• Ginger
• Cardamom pods
• Cinnamon stick
• Turmeric
• Cumin
• Ground coriander
• Salt
• Fresh pepper
• Beef stock
• Apricots
• Cooked white rice
• Mango chutney

Steps:
1. In a non stick skillet heat oil and brown lamb in batches, place in crockpot add onions, garlic, jalapeno, ginger and the rest of the spices, stir for 1 minute until fragrant
2. Add beef stock, mix well, pour over lamb in crockpot
3. Cook on low for 8-10 hours or high for 4-5 hours
4. Add apricots, cover and cook for 30 minutes more
5. Discard cinnamon stick, serve over rice with mango chutney<|endoftext|>
