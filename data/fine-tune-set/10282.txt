Please generate a cookie recipe.<|endoftext|>5 Spice Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

Ingredients:
• Sweet unsalted butter
• Light brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Unbleached all-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Chinese five spice powder
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Quick oats
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Beat butter and sugars together on medium speed until fluffy
3. Add eggs and vanilla, mix well
4. In a seperate bowl, whisk flour, soda, spices and salt until well combined
5. Mix flour mixture into the butter mixture
6. Stir in oats and raisins
7. Drop by heaping tbsp onto ungreased cookie sheet
8. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes
9. Cool slightly then remove from cookie sheet<|endoftext|>
