Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Onion
• Mushroom
• Garlic cloves
• Heavy cream
• Fresh oregano
• Fresh thyme
• Pepper
• Salt
• Ground pork
• Ground beef
• Ground veal
• Egg
• Smoked bacon

Steps:
1. In large skillet over medium heat heat olive oil and saute onions until translucent
2. Add mushrooms and garlic and cook 3-5 minutes
3. Stir in cream, oregano, thyme, salt and pepper
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer about 5 minutes
5. Transfer to large bowl and let cool
6. Mix beef, pork and veal
7. Stir in egg and mix well
8. Add cream mixture and combine well
9. It will look a bit too liquid, but it is just fine
10. Position 9" x 5" x 2 1 / 2" loaf pan with short side facing you
11. Line bottom and sides of pan with bacon, placing them parallel to the short end, slightly overlaping each other
12. Put meat mixture in pan and smooth top
13. Fold bacon striips over the meat, enclosing it completely
14. Place loaf pan in the center of a roasting pan
15. Fill roasting pan half way up with boiling water
16. Cover both pans with aluminum paper
17. Carefully place pans in middle shelf of hot oven and bake 1 hour
18. Remove foil and bake until instant thermometer inserted in center of meatloaf registers 165 f
19. Remove pan of meatloaf from oven and let rest 10 minutes
20. Pour juices from pan into sauceboat
21. Cut loaf into 8 slices, taking care to cut bacon completely
22. Drizzle with pan juices<|endoftext|>
