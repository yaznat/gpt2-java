Ingredients:
• Morningstar farms spicy black bean burgers
• Salsa
• Tex-mex cheese
• Taco seasoning
• Flour tortillas
• Butter
• Lettuce
• Bell peppers

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, toss together veggie burgers, salsa, cheese, and seasonings
2. Use pizza cutter to cut tortillas into 7 inch triangles
3. Place about 2 tablespoons burger mixture in the center of each triangle
4. Fold corners of large triangles over filling, completely enclosing filling and making small triangles
5. Secure with toothpicks, and brush with butter
6. Place on grill with toothpick sides up
7. Grill at 375 degrees for 9-12 minutes, or until cheese completely melts
8. Arrange on top of shredded lettuce, and sprinkle with bell pepper<|endoftext|>
