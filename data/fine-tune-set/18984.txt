Ingredients:
• Kadaifi
• Butter
• Sugar
• Water
• Lemons, juice of
• Fresh lemon rind
• Orange slices
• Flour
• Cornstarch
• Milk
• Egg yolks
• Vanilla extract
• Whipped cream
• Toasted almonds

Steps:
1. Crust: preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Pull apart the 'threads' of the kadaifi into a 13x9-inch glass baking dish, packing down lightly with finger
3. In small pot, combine remaining crust ingredients
4. Bring to a boil and simmer 5 minutes
5. Remove citrus rinds
6. Spoon syrup over kadaifi and cool
7. Custard: combine flour, cornstarch, and sugar in small bowl
8. Add cold milk and whisk to remove any lumps
9. Whisk in egg yolks
10. Strain mixture through sieve into hot milk
11. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly until it thickens
12. Add vanilla and place plastic wrap directly on surface to prevent skin from forming
13. Cool for 1 1 / 2 hours
14. Spread custard evenly over kadaifi crust
15. Refrigerate 1 1 / 2 to 2 hours minimum
16. Then top with layer of whipped cream and decorate with slivered, toasted almonds or pistachios
