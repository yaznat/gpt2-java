Ingredients:
• Rye cocktail bread
• Hot pork sausage
• Velveeta cheese
• Oregano
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic powder
• Mushrooms
• Green pepper
• Cayenne

Steps:
1. Brown sausage and drain
2. Melt cheese, add remaining ingredients
3. Spread on rye cocktail rounds
4. Place on cookie sheet and freeze
5. After freezing, they can be stored in plastic bags until ready for use
6. Heat on cookie sheet in preheated oven at 350f for 5 minutes till cheese bubbles<|endoftext|>
