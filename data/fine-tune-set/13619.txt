Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Lean ground beef
• Onions
• Fresh garlic
• Jalapeno peppers
• Chili powder
• Molasses
• Brown sugar
• Barbecue sauce
• Liquid smoke
• Prepared yellow mustard
• Pork and beans
• Kidney beans
• Butter beans
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Grease a 4-quart casserole dish
2. In a large skillet cook the bacon until crisp
3. Remove to plate cool slightly then crumble
4. To the same skillet brown the ground beef with onions, garlic and jalapeno peppers
5. Cook over medium high heat until meat is no longer pink
6. Drain fat and return the meat to skillet then add in chili powder
7. Cook until meat is well browned
8. Add in all remaining ingredients along with the cooked crumbled bacon
9. Bring to a simmer, stirring constantly
10. Season with salt and pepper
11. Transfer to prepared baking dish
12. Set oven to 350 degrees
13. Bake uncovered for 1-1 / 2 hours or until thickened, stirring occasionally<|endoftext|>
