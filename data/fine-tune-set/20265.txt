What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Chicken And Vege Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Garlic clove
• Ginger
• Chili pepper
• Chicken breast fillets
• Red capsicum
• Spring onions
• Broccoli florets
• Snow peas
• Chinese cabbage
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Water
• Cashew nuts

Steps:
1. Heat a wok or large frypan
2. Add 1 tablespoon of oil and the chicken
3. Stir-fry quickly until evenly browned
4. Remove the chicken from the wok and set aside
5. Heat wok
6. Add the remaining tablespoon of oil, the crushed garlic, ginger and chilli
7. Cook one minute
8. Add all of the vegetables to the wok
9. Stir-fry for a few minutes
10. Return the chicken to the wok
11. Add the soy sauce, honey and water / stock
12. Bring to the boil
13. Cover and cook approx 5 minutes or until the chicken is cooked
14. Add the cabbage and stir-fry another couple of minutes, until the cabbage is wilted down and cooked
15. Serve boiled rice or cooked rice noodles
16. Sprinkle over the cashews to serve<|endoftext|>
