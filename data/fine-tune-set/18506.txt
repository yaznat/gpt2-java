What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Grandma B S Super Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sour milk
• Eggs
• Margarine

Steps:
1. Sift together: 2 cups all-purpose flour 3 teaspoons baking powder 1 teaspoon baking soda 1 teaspoon salt
2. Combine: 2 cups sour milk or butter milk 4 eggs well beaten add the milk and eggs to dry ingredients, beat until smooth with a hand mixer or electric mixer on high speed
3. Stir in 3 / 4 cup melted margarine or salad oil
4. Bake in preheated waffle iron until crisp and golden
5. For large maker 2 cups for small maker 1 cup batter
6. Serve with your favorite toppings<|endoftext|>
