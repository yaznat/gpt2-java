Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking soda
• Eggs
• Fruit cocktail
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Mix all ingredients together and pour into a greased and floured 13 x 9 x 2 inch cake pan
3. Put brown sugar on top and bake in oven for 30-35 minutes<|endoftext|>
