Ingredients:
• Water
• Minute rice
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Green onion
• Soy sauce
• Pork rib chops

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, bring the 1 3 / 4 cups water to boiling
2. Remove from heat
3. Stir in rice
4. Cover
5. Set aside
6. In skillet cook bacon till crisp
7. Remove bacon and crumble, reserving 2 t
8. Drippings
9. Set aside
10. In bowl combine eggs and the 2 t
11. Water
12. In skillet, cook eggs in 1 t
13. Of the reserved bacon drippings till set, stirring occasionally
14. Cut eggs in narrow strips
15. Add rice, bacon, onion and the 3 t
16. Soy sauce to eggs
17. Mix well
18. Turn into a greased 1 1 / 2 quart casserole
19. In skillet brown chops on both sides in remaining 1 t
20. Drippings
21. Arrange chops atop rice mixture
22. Brush with additional soy sauce
23. Bake, covered, at 350 till chops are tender, about 40 minutes<|endoftext|>
