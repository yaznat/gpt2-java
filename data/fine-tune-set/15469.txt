Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Sweet potatoes
• Green beans
• Corn

Steps:
1. Lay out a piece of foil, sprayed with pam for each serving
2. Place a hamburger patty on each piece of foil
3. Add sliced potatoes, then green beans, then corn
4. Top with salt and pepper
5. Wrap up each packet
6. Cook at 400^ for one hour<|endoftext|>
