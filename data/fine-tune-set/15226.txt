Give me a pizza recipe.<|endoftext|>Blueberry Cheesecake Pizza

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Powdered sugar
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Lemon juice
• Vanilla extract
• Granulated sugar
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. Combine flour and powdered sugar
2. Add butter, mixing well
3. Pat dough out in a 14 inch pizza pan
4. Bake at 350f for 10 minutes or until lightly browned
5. Combine cream cheese, condensed milk, lemon juice and vanilla
6. Mix well and spread on cooled crust
7. Chill
8. Combine sugar and cornstarch in a large saucepan
9. Add water, mixing until smooth
10. Cook over medium heat until thickened stirring constantly
11. Add blueberries
12. Cool completely
13. Spread blueberry mixture over cream cheese layer
14. Chill, cut into wedges to serve<|endoftext|>
