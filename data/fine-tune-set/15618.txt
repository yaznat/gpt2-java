Give me a recipe for a taco.<|endoftext|>Beef Tacos

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Ground sirloin
• Cumin
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt and pepper
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Avocado
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce
• Taco sauce
• Cooking oil
• Taco shells

Steps:
1. Coat a frying pan with 1 tbsp olive oil
2. Use a food processor or blender to make the onions into a paste and add to hot frying pan
3. Saut onion for a minute or so and then add the ground sirloin, cumin & cayenne pepper
4. Once browned, add salt and pepper to taste
5. Meanwhile prepare the condiments
6. Grate the cheeses
7. Cut the avacado, tomatoes & lettuce
8. Once you have everything else ready, cook the shell
9. Heat the 2 cups of cooking oil in a frying pan
10. To test whether it's hot enough, tear a small piece off the shell and toss in the pan
11. If it sizzles it's ready
12. Corn shells cook fast, so once you get the shell in the oil, use tongs to shape it into a taco shape
13. Never leave your tacos in oil unattended!
14. Once you get it to your desired taste, let set upside down to drain off the grease
15. Fill tacos with meat and then head over to your taco bar!
16. Add black olives or even green olives and a dollop of sour cream if you like
17. You can also serve beans and rice as side dishes
