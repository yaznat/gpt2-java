What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Spicy V 8

Ingredients:
• Stew meat
• Spicy vegetable juice
• Diced tomatoes
• Green beans
• Red potatoes
• Baby carrots
• Onion
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Brown meat in small amount of oil
2. Add canned tomatoes and let cook for about 10 minutes
3. Add vegetables and enough v-8 juice to cover
4. Let simmer until cooked through
5. You can season as desired with salt / pepper<|endoftext|>
