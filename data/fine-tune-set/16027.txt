What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Bbq Sausage Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Hot sausage
• Barbecue sauce
• Red onion
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. In skillet over medium heat crumble and cook sausage and onions until brown
2. Stir in barbeque sauce
3. Lay 2 of the tortillas on a flat surface
4. Top each with 1 / 4 c cheese
5. Divide sausage mixture between the two
6. Sprinkle each with 1 / 4 c more cheese
7. Top with remaining tortillas
8. Heat a large skillet over medium heat
9. Add a small amount of oil to coat the bottom of the pan
10. Cook one quesadilla at a time until golden brown on each side, about 2-3 minutes per side
11. Cut into wedges and serve with sour cream and salsa<|endoftext|>
