Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Oatmeal Muffins

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Quick oats
• Egg
• Skim milk
• Vegetable oil
• Apple

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
2. Mix together the dry ingredients in a big bowl
3. In a separate bowl, mix the wet ingredients
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry, stir until just moist
5. Add the apples, stir only until they're well distributed
6. Pour into greased muffin tins, fill almost to the top
7. Bake at 425 degrees for 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
