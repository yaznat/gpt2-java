Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>Aunt Liz S Chicken Spaghetti Casserole

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Spaghetti noodles
• Celery
• Red bell pepper
• Onion
• Chicken broth
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine first 5 ingredients in a large bowl
3. Combine broth, salt, pepper, and soup in a medium bowl, stirring with a whisk
4. Add soup mixture to chicken mixture
5. Toss
6. Divide mixture evenly between 2 square or 2 baking dishes coated with cooking spray
7. Sprinkle 1 / 2 cup cheese over each casserole
8. Cover with foil coated with cooking spray
9. Bake at 350 for 35 minutes
10. Uncover and bake an additional 10 minutes
11. Bake a frozen casserole, covered, for 55 minutes at 350
12. Uncover and bake an additional 10 minutes or until hot and bubbly<|endoftext|>
