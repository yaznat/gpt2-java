Give me a pie recipe.<|endoftext|>100 Year Old Old Fashioned Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Evaporated milk
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Unbaked pie shells

Steps:
1. Beat egg whites stiff
2. Add white sugar, flour and salt
3. Add egg yolks and beat again
4. Add evaporated milk that has been poured into a one pint cup and finished filling with water
5. And add brown sugar --
6. Add vanilla and mix well
7. Pour into 10" unbaked pie shell
8. Bake at 400 until light brown
9. Reduce oven heat to 325 and bake for an additional 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
