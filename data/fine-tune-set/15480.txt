Ingredients:

• Barbecue sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Monterey jack cheese
• Red onion
• Sesame seed rolls
• Lettuce leaf
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. In a bowl, mix the first 7 ingredients together until well combined
2. Shape into 6 patties, each 1-inch thick
3. Lightly oil the grill or grill pan over med-high heat
4. Cook the burgers to the desired degree of donenessat least 5 minutes on each side for medium-rare
5. Top with the cheese and onion for the last minutes of cooking
6. Serve it up on toasted sesame see buns
