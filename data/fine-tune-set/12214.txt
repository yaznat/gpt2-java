What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie By Grandma Ople

Ingredients:
• Pastry for a double-crust 9-inch pie
• Unsalted butter
• Cornstarch
• Apple cider
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Allspice
• Tart apples

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Process the apple slices
3. Melt the butter in a large dutch oven
4. Stir in cornstarch to form a paste
5. Add the apple juice, white sugar and brown sugar, and bring to a boil
6. Reduce temperature and let simmer
7. When very bubbly and slightly thickened, remove from heat and add the vanilla and spices
8. Dump the apple slices into the syrup and stir thoroughly to coat
9. Place the bottom crust in your pan
10. Fill with apples, mounded slightly and packed well
11. Gently spoon the caramel-coated apples, minus the syrup, into the crust
12. Then pour most of the syrup evenly over the apples, reserving enough to brush over the top crust
13. Cover with a lattice work of crust
14. Pour or brush the sugar and butter liquid over the crust
15. Bake 15 minutes in the preheated oven
16. Reduce the temperature to 350f
17. Continue baking for 35 to 45 minutes, until apples are soft and crust is a light golden brown<|endoftext|>
