What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>3 Corn Casserole Bread

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sour cream
• Egg
• Whole kernel corn
• Cream-style corn
• Corn muffin mix

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Mix butter, sour cream and egg
3. Stir in both corns
4. Blend in dry muffin mix and spoon into a well-greased casserole dish
5. Bake for one hour until puffed up, golden brown and crispy on top<|endoftext|>
