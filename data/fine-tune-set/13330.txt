Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Vegetable oil
• Nigella seeds
• Mustard seeds
• Fennel seed
• Dried red chili pepper
• Onion
• Tomato puree
• Kosher salt
• Chili powder
• Turmeric
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, heat oil over medium high heat
2. Add mustard seeds, nigella seeds, fennel seeds, and dried peppers and saute a few minutes until fragrant
3. Add onions and cook until they begin to soften, about 5 minutes
4. Remove onions and set aside
5. Add chicken and brown on all sides
6. Lower heat, add tomato puree and cook until cooked through, about 3 minutes
7. Return onions, season with salt, chili powder, and turmeric
8. Simmer 5 minutes
9. Garnish with cilantro<|endoftext|>
