Ingredients:
• Milk
• Butterscotch pudding mix
• Vanilla ice cream
• Pumpkin pie spice

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in large pitcher with tight-fitting lid
2. Cover
3. Shake vigorously 1 minute or until well blended
4. Pour evenly into 4 glasses
5. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
