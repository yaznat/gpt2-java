Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Parmesan Pasta

Ingredients:
• Pasta
• Garlic clove
• Butter
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook pasta as directed on package
2. Meanwhile, cook and stir garlic in butter in large saucepan on medium heat until tender but not browned
3. Add drained pasta to saucepan, mix lightly
4. Sprinkle with cheese
5. Toss to coat
6. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
