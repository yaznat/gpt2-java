Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Fresh blueberries
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Pecans
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, add butter, sugar and use a hand mixer to cream
2. Add eggs and mix inches fold in sour cream and vanilla
3. In a separate bowl, mix dry ingredients
4. Add dry ingredients to wet ingredients and fold it inches then use the hand mixer to mix until smooth
5. Fold in blueberries
6. Use bakers joy and grease the bundt pan well
7. Pour 1 / 3 of the batter in the pan and smooth out
8. Combine brown sugar, cinnamon and nuts for filling
9. Sprinkle 1 / 2 the filling over the batter, then 1 / 3 more batter, remaining filling and remaining batter
10. Bake at 350 degrees for 55 to 60 minutes
11. Cool in pan
12. Remove from pan by flipping it onto a flat surface
13. Sift powdered sugar over top
14. Serves 12 to 16<|endoftext|>
