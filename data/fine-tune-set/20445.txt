What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Any Kind Casserole

Ingredients:
• Tuna
• Celery
• Potatoes
• Cream
• Pimiento
• Potato chips

Steps:
1. Mix all the ingredients together in a bowl
2. If the casserole seems dry add 1 / 2 cup milk or stock
3. Place into a buttered casserole dish
4. Bake at 350 for 30 to 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
