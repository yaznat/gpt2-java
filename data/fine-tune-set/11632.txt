Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Step Cheesecake

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Graham cracker crust

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees
2. Beat cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla with an electric mixer on medium speed until well blended
3. Add eggs
4. Mix until just blended
5. Pour into the crust
6. Bake 40 minutes or until center is almost set
7. Cool
8. Refrigerate 3 hours or overnight
9. Store leftovers in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
