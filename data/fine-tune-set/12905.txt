Give me a steak recipe.<|endoftext|>Black Pepper Honey Steak

Ingredients:
• Red wine
• Honey
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Steak
• Olive oil
• Salt

Steps:
1. Combine the wine, honey, garlic and pepper in a shallow dish, add the steak and coat with mixture
2. Let stand and room temperature while you set up the rest of the meal
3. Heat the olive oil in a 10-inch saut pan over medium high heat
4. Pat steak dry
5. Add to pan and brown quickly on both sides
6. Turn heat to medium low and cook, turning often, about 10 minutes or until steak reaches an internal temperature of 125 to 130
7. Remove to a platter and let rest 5 to 10 minutes - the steak finishes cooking, the juices redistribute into the meat, and the steak will be much juicier for the wait<|endoftext|>
