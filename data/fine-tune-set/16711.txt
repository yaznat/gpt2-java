What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Milkshake Indian

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Milk
• Sugar
• Green cardamoms
• Ice

Steps:
1. Peel apple and cut in small pieces
2. Now blend milk, sugar and apple in a mixer
3. Grind elaichi to a powder and mix in the shake
4. Now pour shake in the glasses
5. Put some crushed ice and serve it chilled<|endoftext|>
