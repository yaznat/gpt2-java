Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>3 2 1 Cake

Ingredients:
• Angel food cake mix
• Cake mix
• Water

Steps:
1. Mix angel food cake mix and your choice flavor cake mix
2. Put both in zip lock bag and mix well
3. When ready for your quick cake snack attack, take 3 tablespoons cake mix
4. Put in microwavable small ramekin or bowl
5. Mix in 2 tablespoons water
6. Mix well
7. Microwave on high, 1 minute
8. Cool and top with whipped cream or icing of your choice or just eat it plain
9. Just enough for that quick fix, snack attack!
10. Prep time includes opening and mixing the cake mixes in zip lock bag and measuring out mix and water and mixing together
11. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
