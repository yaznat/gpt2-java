Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Layer Russian Honey Cake

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Baking soda
• Liquid honey
• Margarine
• Flour
• Sour cream
• Honey
• Plain sweet biscuit crumbs
• Ground walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180c
2. Beat eggs well with sugar
3. Add baking soda and liquid honey
4. Melt the margarine / butter in a casserole or pot
5. Add the mixture
6. Put the casserole on a low heat, add the flour while mixing and mix until the mass is without lumps
7. Remove from heat and cool
8. Dip the dough in flour, divide, roll each layer very thin, cut circles as round as possible, and bake at 180c for 2-4 minutes or until golden brown in colour
9. Repeat with remaining dough and allow to cool before assembling
10. Beat sour cream, sugar and honey until the sugar dissolves-the mixture will still be fairly liquid
11. Coat each layer with a generous layer of cream
12. Assemble the cake, coat the sides with cream and sprinkle top and sides with crumbs and ground walnuts
13. Allow to set at least 6 hours before eating<|endoftext|>
