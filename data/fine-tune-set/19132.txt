What's a recipe for ice cream?<|endoftext|>Bob Carol Ted Alice S Killer Ice Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Oreo cookies
• Unsalted butter
• Coffee ice cream
• Chocolate bars

Steps:
1. Crush the oreo cookies, add the butter, and blend together
2. Press the mixture into a 9-inch pie pan to form a pie crust
3. Add the ice cream
4. In a double boiler, melt the chocolate and then drizzle it over the ice cream
5. Cover the pie with plastic wrap and place in the freezer for at least 2 hours, or overnight
6. Serve when the ice cream has reset<|endoftext|>
