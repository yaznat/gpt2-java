Give me a fish recipe.<|endoftext|>Australian Fish

Ingredients:
• Fresh mushrooms
• Butter
• Oil
• Parmesan cheese
• Fillets of sole
• Salt
• Pepper
• Green peppers
• White wine
• Lemon juice
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Melt butter in skillet over low heat
2. Add mushrooms and saute over medium heat until tender
3. Rub oil over shallow 4 quart baking dish and sprinkle with grated cheese
4. Place fish on cheese
5. Sprinkle fish with salt and pepper
6. Add green pepper and top with mushrooms
7. Pour wine over fillets
8. Bake at 450f for 20 minutes or until fish flakes
9. Sprinkle with lemon juice and parsley<|endoftext|>
