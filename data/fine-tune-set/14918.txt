Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Fried Rice For The Rice Cooker

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Chicken broth
• Water chestnut
• Mixed mushrooms
• Bean sprouts
• Egg
• Soy sauce
• Red chili paste
• Carrots
• Onion
• Butter

Steps:
1. Melt the butter over medium heat in a medium-sized skillet
2. Add onion and carrot and stir until onion is soft, about 5 minutes
3. Add rice and cook another 5 minutes until browned
4. Add rice mixture and remaining ingredients to rice cooker
5. Set to cook
6. Beat egg in small bowl
7. Wipe out sauce pan and cook egg on medium low heat
8. Chop egg and set aside
9. When rice is done, add egg and stir, then let sit for 5-10 minutes
10. Serve and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
