Ingredients:
• Cocoa
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Nuts
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Combine cocoa, butter and sugar
3. Stir well to dissolve sugar
4. Beat in eggs and vanilla
5. Quickly stir in remaining ingredients
6. Spread batter in a well greased 8" x 8" square baking pan
7. Bake in a preheated oven for 40-45 min
8. After removing from oven, scatter chocolate chips over top
9. After a few minutes, use a spatula to spread melted chips over brownies
10. Let cool<|endoftext|>
