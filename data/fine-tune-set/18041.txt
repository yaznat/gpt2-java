Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Drop Cookies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Baking powder
• Butter
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Light brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• White chocolate chips
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. Line a cookie sheet with foil, coat with nonstick spray
3. Whisk flour, cocoa, and baking powder in a medium bowl until thoroughly blended
4. Melt butter and one cup of the semi sweet chocolate chips in a medium saucepan over low heat, stirring as needed
5. Remove from heat
6. Whisk in sugar, then eggs and vanilla until blended
7. Scrape into flour mixture, stir until smooth
8. Stir in 1 / 2 cup semi sweet chocolate chips, 1 cup of white chocolate chips and nuts
9. Let stand 5 to 10 minutes, then drop 1 / 4 cupfuls 2 inches apart on prepared baking sheet
10. Bake immediately for 12 minutes or until cookies are puffed and shiny
11. Cool on foil for 1 to 2 minutes, then remove to a wire rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
