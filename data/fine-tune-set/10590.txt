Ingredients:
• Pine nuts
• Fresh basil
• Fresh spinach
• Garlic
• Parmesan cheese
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Water
• Angel hair pasta

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Arrange the pine nuts on a cookie sheet lined with foil and roast in oven for 7 to 8 minutes
3. In a food processor, mix roasted pine nuts with the next 6 ingredients to form a paste
4. Blend in water to achieve desired consistency
5. Meanwhile, cook pasta according to package directions
6. Drain and keep warm
7. The sauce can be refrigerated in a tightly covered container or frozen before serving, if desired
8. Serve pasta with sauce<|endoftext|>
