Ingredients:
• Fresh asparagus
• Saltine crackers
• Onion
• Condensed cream of asparagus soup
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Hot sauce
• American cheese
• Slivered almonds
• Egg

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients and stir well
