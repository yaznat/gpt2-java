Give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Avocado And Tomato Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Tomatoes
• Mayonnaise
• Salt
• Pepper
• White bread

Steps:
1. Evenly spread mayo on 2 slices of bread
2. Arrange slices of tomato and avocado on top of mayo
3. Salt and pepper to taste
4. Top off with last two pieces of bread
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
