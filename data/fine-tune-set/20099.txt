Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Salt and pepper
• Butter
• Carrot
• Button mushrooms
• Red bell pepper
• Garlic
• Dried tarragon
• Paprika
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Chop the vegetables as directed
2. Melt the butter in a large frying pan over medium-high heat
3. Add the onion, carrot and pepper
4. Saute 1-2 minutes
5. Add the garlic, and saute another 1-2 minutes
6. Add the tarragon and paprika
7. Lower the heat and let the vegetables cook slowly and soften
8. Add more butter if necessary
9. Meanwhile, mix the eggs, water, soy sauce and salt and pepper in a bowl
10. Add the mushrooms into the vegetables in the frying pan
11. Cook another 5-10 minutes on low heat
12. Cover pan to retain moisture
13. Spread vegetables in pan with spatula so they are pressed down and as flat as possible
14. Pour the egg mixture over them
15. Sprinkle the cheese over the top
16. Turn the heat up slightly
17. Using a metal spatula, gently lift the edges of the omlete that is forming
18. Tilt the pan to let the unset egg flow down against the edges
19. Cover the pan and let the egg set further
20. This should take another 5 minutes or so
21. Lift around the edges with the spatula to loosen the omelet
22. Slide omlet on to a serving plate
23. Serve with crusty bread, and a nice white wine<|endoftext|>
