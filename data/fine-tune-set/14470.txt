Ingredients:
• Artichoke hearts
• Cannellini beans
• Garlic clove
• Olive oil
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Of fresh mint
• Fat free croutons

Steps:
1. Puree artichoke hearts and beans in the food processor
2. Heat the oil over medium heat in a small saucepan
3. Add the garlic and saute
4. Add the artichoke / bean puree and the broth and heat through
5. Season with salt and pepper
6. Garnish with mint and croutons<|endoftext|>
