Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• French fries
• Frozen peas
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Water
• Ketchup
• Dried parsley flakes
• Worcestershire sauce
• Prepared mustard
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a skillet, cook the beef and onion until beef loses its pinkness, then drain
2. Grease a 13" x 9" x 2" baking pan, and layer half of the french fries, peas or corn, and meat
3. Repeat the layers
4. In a medium bowl, combine the soup, water, ketchup, parsley, worcestershire sauce, mustard, salt and pepper
5. Pour over casserole
6. Bake, uncovered, at 350f for 30-40 minutes, or until hot<|endoftext|>
