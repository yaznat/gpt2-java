Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Flour
• Liquid

Steps:
1. Cream margarine and vanilla with sugar
2. Mix salt and baking powder with flour
3. Incorporate into margarine mixture, alternating with the liquid
4. If they are too dry, add a little bit of liquid
5. Preheat oven to 350f
6. Roll into 1-inch balls and place on a cookie sheet
7. Flatten slightly with a fork
8. Bake 25-30 minutes, until bottoms are nice and golden<|endoftext|>
