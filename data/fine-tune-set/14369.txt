Generate a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>3 Bean Baked Beans

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Tomato sauce
• Diced tomatoes
• Ketchup
• Brown sugar
• Dry mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Pork and beans
• Beans

Steps:
1. Fry bacon until crisp, reserving 2 teaspoons drippings
2. Crumble and set aside
3. In a large saucepan saute chopped onion and remove
4. Brown ground beef and then add onions
5. Add tomato sauce, diced tomatoes, ketchup, brown sugar, dry mustard, worcestershire, salt and bacon drippings
6. Simmer for 1 minute and remove
7. Lightly stir in beans and crumbled bacon
8. Pour into a 3 quart baking dish
9. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 25 minutes
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
