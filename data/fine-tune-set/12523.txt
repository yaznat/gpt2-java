Ingredients:
• Chicken
• Frozen corn
• Frozen lima beans
• Frozen green beans
• Celery
• Carrots
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Diced tomatoes
• Salt
• Chicken stock powder
• Potatoes
• Pepper
• Bay leaves
• Italian seasoning
• Ground sage
• Cold water

Steps:
1. Wash chicken, add to large stock pot
2. Add 6 cups cold water
3. Open canned tomatoes, pour in with juice
4. Add frozen vegetables and seasonings
5. Turn on heat to med / low
6. Cover 1 hour, then vent it a little so it won't boil over
7. Cook about 2 1 / 2 hrs longer, stirring every 1 / 2 hour or so
8. Take chicken out and skin, and remove bones
9. Add back into pot and heat through
10. This can be frozen up to 3 months
11. Also this tends to be a thinner stew, add less water if you desire a thicker stew
12. Serve with yeast rolls, or corn bread<|endoftext|>
