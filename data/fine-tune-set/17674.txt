What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• Buttermilk
• Butter
• Molasses
• Wheat bran
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Apple
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Combine first 5 ingredients
2. Combine bran, flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt
3. Stir into liquid ingredients
4. Mix well
5. Stir in apple and raisins
6. Spoon into 12 greased muffin cups
7. Bake 400 degrees 17 - 23 minutes, or until set<|endoftext|>
