Ingredients:
• Candy cane
• Strawberry vodka
• White creme de menthe
• Cranberry juice
• Ice cube

Steps:
1. Place crushed candy canes on a small plate or saucer
2. Wet the outside rim of a chilled martini glass with water
3. Holding the glass by the stem, rotate the rim to coat with candy
4. In a cocktail shaker, combine vodka, creme de menthe, cranberry juice, and ice
5. Shake until well combined
6. Strain into prepared glass
7. Serve immediately
8. Garnish with a small candy cane hooked over the edge of the glass<|endoftext|>
