Give me a tofu recipe.<|endoftext|>Chinese Soup With Tofu

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Carrots
• Onion
• Ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Sesame oil
• Black pepper
• Firm tofu
• Bok choy
• Chinese cabbage
• Snow peas

Steps:
1. In a large pan, saute the carrots, onion, ginger and garlic in oil until onion is tender
2. Add the water, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil and pepper
3. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
4. While soup is simmering, prepare tofu
5. Remove excess water and slice tofu into three large slabs
6. Fry in oil until both sides are browned
7. Drain on a paper towel
8. Cut tofu slabs into 1 / 2 inch cubes
9. After soup has simmered for 15 minutes, add tofu cubes, bok choy, cabbage and snow peas
10. Simmer for 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
