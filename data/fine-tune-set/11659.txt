Give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>7 Up Chocolate Chip Pound Cake

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Cocoa
• 7-up soda
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Cream butter with an electric mixer
2. Add sugar and vanilla
3. Beat until light and fluffy
4. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition
5. Stir in flour and cocoa, blending thoroughly
6. Stir in 7up
7. Fold in chocolate chips
8. Spoon into a well greased 10-inch tube pan
9. Bake at 350 for 1 hour
10. Cool in pan on a wire rack for 10 minutes
11. Remove from pan, cool completely<|endoftext|>
