Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Wax bean
• Olive oil
• Kosher salt
• Thyme leaves
• Oregano

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400f trim beans
2. In a jelly-roll pan, toss beans with oil, salt and oregano until evenly coated
3. Roast 20 minutes, turning beans once or twice for even browning<|endoftext|>
