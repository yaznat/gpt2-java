What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Baked Sweet Potato Shoestring Fries

Ingredients:
• Orange juice
• Vegetable oil
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Ground red pepper
• Sweet potatoes
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Combine first 5 ingredients in a small saucepan
3. Bring to a boil
4. Reduce heat
5. Simmer 2 minutes or until slightly thick
6. Remove from heat
7. Let cool
8. Combine juice mixture and potatoes in a large bowl
9. Toss well
10. Remove potatoes from bowl
11. Discard juice mixture
12. Arrange potato strips in a single layer on a baking sheet coated with cooking spray
13. Bake at 400 degrees for 30 minutes or until edges are crisp<|endoftext|>
