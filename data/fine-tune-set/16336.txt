Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Garlic powder
• Seasoning salt
• Dried basil
• Dried oregano
• Pork tenderloin
• Butter
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Preheat an oven to 400 degrees f
2. Place the bacon in a large, oven-safe skillet, and cook over medium-high heat, turning occasionally, until lightly browned and still flexible, 6 to 7 minutes
3. Drain the bacon slices on a paper towel-lined plate
4. Remove any excess bacon grease from the skillet
5. Combine garlic powder, seasoning salt, basil, and oregano in a small bowl
6. Set aside
7. Wrap the pork tenderloin with the bacon strips securing with 1 or 2 toothpicks per strip of bacon
8. Slice the tenderloin between each bacon strip to create the medallions
9. Dip both sides of the medallions in seasoning mix
10. Melt butter and oil together in the same skillet over medium-high heat
11. Cook each medallion for 4 minutes on each side
12. Place skillet into the preheated oven and bake until the pork is no longer pink in the center, 17 to 20 minutes
13. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center should read 145 degrees f<|endoftext|>
