Can you give me a popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Popsicle

Ingredients:
• Vanilla vodka
• Banana liqueur
• Orange juice
• Pineapple juice

Steps:
1. Pour vanilla vodka and banana liqueur over ice in a shaker adding equal parts of orange juice and pineapple juice to the spirits
2. Shake and strain over ice cubes into a collins glass<|endoftext|>
