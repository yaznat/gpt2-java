Give me a taco recipe.<|endoftext|>Authentic Indian Taco Fry Bread

Ingredients:
• Warm water
• Compressed yeast cakes
• Flour
• Margarine
• Milk
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Salt
• Oil

Steps:
1. Yeast mixture: mix the first 3 ingredients and let rest for 10 minutes
2. Batter mixture: in a separate mixing bowl, combine the remaining ingredients except for the 12 cups of flour
3. Mix well
4. Add yeast mixture to batter
5. Add 12 cups of flour
6. Mix well
7. Dough does not have to be kneaded, but should be well-mixed and should rest for awhile
8. I've found that if you let it double in size, it's even softer and easier to work with
9. Form into small "pats, " depending on the size you're looking for
10. Roll or stretch until thin
11. Fry until golden brown and cooked through
12. Top with your favorite toppings
13. We top with hamburger, refried beans, shredded lettuce, tomato, salsa, and sour cream
14. They're also wonderful with honey butter
15. If you don't eat them all the first day, you can keep the dough in the fridge and fry it up in the next day or two
16. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
