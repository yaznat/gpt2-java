Ingredients:
• Banana
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Ice cube

Steps:
1. Take out ye olde blender and pour in a cup of milk -- feel free to guestimate
2. Peel the banana, break it in half, and add it to the blender
3. Add 1 / 2 teaspoon vanilla and the ice cubes
4. Blend for about 20 seconds
5. Poor into a glass and enjoy<|endoftext|>
