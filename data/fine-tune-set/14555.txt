Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Carrot
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Cannellini beans
• Plain yogurt
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Cook bacon in med saucepan over med heat, stirring, until crisp
2. Using slotted spoon, transfer bacon to small bowl and set aside
3. Discard bacon fat
4. Add 2 t olive oil, the carrot, onion, and garlic to saucepan and cook over med heat, stirring til tender, 6-8 minute add beans, cover and cook over med-low for 5 minute let cool
5. Using food processor, puree bean mixture, yogurt and 2-3 t water til nearly smooth
6. Add reserved bacon and pulse to incorporate
7. Season with salt and pepper<|endoftext|>
