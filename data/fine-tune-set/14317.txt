Please give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Golden Fried Tofu Bites

Ingredients:
• Medium firm tofu
• All-purpose flour
• Cornstarch
• Seasoning salt
• Peanut oil

Steps:
1. Rinse the tofu
2. Place tofu on a plate, cover with two large heavy plates and let drain for 30 minutes
3. Dry the tofu and cut into small bite-sized cubes
4. In a large bowl, mix the flour, cornstarch and seasoning salt
5. Carefully dredge the tofu bites in the seasoned flour mixture, making sure they are well-coated
6. Fry, half at a time, in peanut oil at medium-high heat for about five minutes or until golden and crispy
7. Repeat with other half of the tofu
8. Best eaten piping hot as soon as they are cooked
9. May be eaten plain, dipped into liquid honey, or they go very well with red thai curry<|endoftext|>
