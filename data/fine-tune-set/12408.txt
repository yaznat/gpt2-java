Ingredients:
• Butter
• Carrots
• Green onions
• Chicken broth
• Bay leaf

• Egg noodles
• Cooked chicken
• Frozen peas

Steps:
1. Cook carrots in melted butter 4 min
2. Stir in onion, broth and bay leaf
3. Season with salt and pepper to taste
4. Bring to a boil, then simmer 5 min until carrots are almost tender
5. Return to a boil
6. Add noodles, chicken, peas and simmer until noodles are tender
