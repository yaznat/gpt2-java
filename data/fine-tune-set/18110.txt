Please give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Amazing Light Whole Wheat Blueberry Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Egg
• Skim milk
• Vegetable oil
• Nonfat yogurt
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. Sift dry ingredients into a large mixing bowl
2. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg, milk, oil and yogurt together well, then add to dry ingredients
3. Stir until just combined then fold in blueberries
4. Fry pancakes on a hot oiled pan or griddle on medium high heat, wait until top is filled with bubbles to turn, turning once
5. Makes 6 pancakes<|endoftext|>
