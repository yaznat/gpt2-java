Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Cod fish fillets
• Salt and pepper
• All-purpose flour
• Garlic powder
• Paprika
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Egg
• Beer

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a deep fryer to 365 degrees f
2. Rinse fish, pat dry, and season with salt and pepper
3. Combine flour, garlic powder, paprika, 2 teaspoons salt, and 2 teaspoons pepper
4. Stir egg into dry ingredients
5. Gradually mix in one beer until a thin batter is formed
6. You should be able to see the fish through the batter after it has been dipped
7. Dip fish fillets into the batter, then drop one at a time into hot oil
8. Fry fish, turning once, until both sides are golden brown
9. Drain on paper towels, and serve warm<|endoftext|>
