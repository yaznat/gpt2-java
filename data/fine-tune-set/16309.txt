Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Sea scallops
• Maple syrup
• Butter
• Orange juice
• Orange peel

Steps:
1. Cut each bacon slice in half
2. Cook over medium heat 5-6 minutes
3. Wrap each scallop in a bacon piece
4. In a saucepan over medium-high heat, bring syrup to a boil
5. Cook, uncovered, about 5 minutes, until reduced to about 2 / 3 cup
6. Remove from heat
7. Add butter, orange juice, and orange peel and stir until butter is melted and mixture is combined
8. Coat grill rack with cooking spray
9. Heat grill to a medium-high heat
10. Using 6 skewers, thread 4 wrapped scallops onto each skewer
11. Brush syrup mixture over scallops
12. Grill scallops for 4-6 minutes, turning once or twice, until scallops are opaque and bacon is crisp<|endoftext|>
