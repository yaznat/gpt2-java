Can you give me a cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Chimayo Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Tequila
• Apple cider
• Fresh lemon juice
• Creme de cassis

Steps:
1. Fill a cocktail shaker half full with ice
2. Add tequila, cider, lemon juice and cassis and shake well
3. Fill two glasses with ice and strain the cocktail evenly between them
4. Garnish each with thin slice of apple if desired and serve<|endoftext|>
