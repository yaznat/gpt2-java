Give me a recipe for tea.<|endoftext|>A Most Excellent Raspberry Iced Tea

Ingredients:
• Cold water
• Granulated sugar
• Green tea leaves
• Fresh raspberries
• Limes
• Vanilla bean
• Peppercorn
• Allspice berry
• Star anise
• Cinnamon stick

Steps:
1. In a 4qt pot, combine water & sugar
2. This is the time to add a vanilla bean or your whole spice of choice
3. Bring to a boil
4. Turn off heat & add tea leaves
5. Cover & let stand to steep & cool an hour or so
6. When somewhat cooled, add fresh raspberries
7. Continue to let steep / cool a couple more hours
8. Mash raspberries to be sure all juice is extracted
9. Pour through a fine mesh strainer, pushing on solids to get all the liquid, into a 2qt
10. Pitcher
11. Add lemon or lime slices, if desired
12. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled<|endoftext|>
