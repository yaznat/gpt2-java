Ingredients:
• Pumpkin puree
• Brown sugar
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Ground cloves
• Allspice
• Soft tofu

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Cream the tofu, pumpkin and sugar in a food processor
3. Add salt and spices, and mix thoroughly
4. Pour mixture into pie shell and bake for 15 minutes
5. Lower heat to 350 f and bake for another 40 minutes
6. Chill and serve<|endoftext|>
