Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Adobo seasoning
• Garlic powder
• Parmesan cheese
• Salt and pepper
• Vegetable oil cooking spray

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Use a baking stone or a cookie sheet
3. Drain water from the potatoes and place on paper towel cover with another paper towel and dab dry
4. If using oil lightly spray the fries while they are on the paper towel, turn& spray other side
5. Sprinkle the adobo spice, garlic, salt& pepper over the fries
6. Potaoes on the baking stone arranged in a single layer
7. Place in preheated oven for 30 minutes
8. Remove from oven, turn the fries and sprinkle with the parmesan cheese
9. Return to oven for a further 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
