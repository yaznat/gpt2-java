What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Apricot Glazed Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Apricot jam
• Cider vinegar
• Soy sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Cayenne pepper
• Pork chops
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat grill to med-hot fire
2. In a small pan, combine the jam and vinegar
3. Over a low heat, cook until jam melts
4. Stir the mixture constantly
5. Add the soy sauce, ginger, garlic, cayenne pepper
6. Remove from heat
7. Separate the glaze into 2 small containers, and set aside
8. Trim pork chops
9. Lightly brush chops with oil
10. Add pepper
11. Place chops on a well-oiled grill
12. Brush glaze on one side of chops, flip over chops, and cook 2-3 minutes
13. Brush glaze on the other side, flip the chops over, and cook 2-3 more minutes on that side
14. Cut center to check for doneness
15. You want a light pink blush near the bone
16. Add salt at the table
17. Use remaining container of glaze on chops at the table<|endoftext|>
