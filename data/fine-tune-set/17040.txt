Ingredients:
• Sourdough cocktail bread
• Butter
• Dijon mustard
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Lightly spread 1 side of each piece of bread with about 1 / 4 teaspoons butter
3. Place half the bread slices onto foil-lined cookie sheets, buttered side down
4. Top each with a dab of mustard and 1 tablespoons cheese
5. Top with another bread slice, buttered side up
6. Bake the sandwiches, turning once, for 10-13 minutes, or until golden and the cheese is melted
7. Cut each sandwich in half diagonally and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
