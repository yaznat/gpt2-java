What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Beefy Bean Burritos

Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Oatmeal
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Salsa
• Black beans
• Corn kernel
• Monterey jack cheese
• Tortillas

Steps:
1. In a qt size freezer bag combine the meat and oatmeal
2. Seal the bag and knead the two together a bit
3. Squeeze out air (very important! ice is the enemy in freezer meals
4. Flatten the bag, like a pancake
5. In a gallon freezer bag add, chili powder, cumin, dranied spinach, salsa, black beans, corn and salt and pepper to taste
6. Smoosh around to cimbine then squeeze out as mush air as you can
7. In a qt size freezer bag add the cheese
8. Seal the bag
9. In a large freezer bag place the meat, the salsa mixture, cheese and even the tortillas if you would like
10. I do so that my dinner "kit" as ready when i am
11. Thaw in fridge
12. In a large skillet, brown the meat mixture 8-10 minutes
13. Remove from heat, drain if need be
14. Add in bean and spinach mixture and heat thru
15. Stir in cheese just to melt
16. Serve in heated tortillas
17. If you are plan on cooking this and not freezing, brown meat and oatmeal, drain, then add in other ingredients like above<|endoftext|>
