Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Shrimp
• Pineapple chunks
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Heat oil in nonstick skillet over med-high heat
2. Place a chunk of pineapple in the curve of each shrimp
3. Wrap each shrimp in a slice of bacon and secure with a toothpick
4. Place in the hot oil and cook, about 3 minutes a side, until shrimp is opaque<|endoftext|>
