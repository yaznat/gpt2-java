Ingredients:
• Fat free egg substitute
• Nonfat milk
• Reduced-fat cheese
• Bell pepper
• Sliced mushrooms
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Reduced-fat parmesan cheese
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Ketchup
• Salsa
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Into a deep, round casserole dish sprayed with nonstick spray, pour egg substitute and milk
2. Add shredded cheese, tomatoes, garlic powder, and black pepper
3. Mix well, and then set aside
4. Bring a large pan sprayed with nonstick spray to high heat
5. Stirring occasionally, cook peppers, onions, and mushrooms for 2 minutes
6. Add veggies to the casserole dish, and mix well
7. Place dish in the oven and cook for 30 minutes
8. Next, carefully remove the dish from the oven and evenly cover with parm-style topping
9. Return it to the oven, and cook for 20 - 25 minutes longer
10. Allow to cool slightly before cutting
11. Then, if you like, finish off with the optional ingredients
12. Makes 4 servings<|endoftext|>
