What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Anna S Granola Cookies

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Light brown sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Whole wheat flour
• Unbleached white flour
• Baking soda
• Granola cereal
• Dried apricot
• Sunflower seeds

Steps:
1. Combine the margarine, brown sugar, egg and vanilla extract in the bowl of a food processor or electric mixer, and process to mix well
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the flours and baking soda
3. Add the flour mixture to the margarine mixture, and process to mix well
4. Stir in the remaining ingredients
5. Coat a baking sheet with nonstick cooking spray
6. Drop slightly rounded teaspoonfuls of dough onto the baking sheet, placing them 1 inches apart
7. Flatten each cookie slightly with the
8. Bake at 300f for about 15 min, or until golden brown
9. Cool the cookies on the baking sheet for 1 minute
10. Then transfer the cookies to wire racks, and cool completely
11. Serve immediately, or transfer to an airtight container<|endoftext|>
