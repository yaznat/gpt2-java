Give me a fries recipe.<|endoftext|>Loaded Buffalo Oven Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Vegetable oil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Hot sauce
• Butter
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Blue cheese
• Green onions
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f
2. Place cut potatoes in a large stock pot and cover with cold water
3. Bring to a boil and once bubbling, only boil for 5 minutes
4. Drain potatoes very well
5. Place potatoes in a large bowl and drizzle with oil, salt, pepper and garlic powder
6. Gently toss to evenly coat
7. Spread on a baking sheet in one layer
8. Roast for 25-30 minutes, then flip and roast for 25-30 minutes more
9. The potatoes are done when they're deeply golden and crunchy on the outside
10. While potatoes are cooking, heat buffalo wing sauce and butter in a small saucepan over low heat until combined
11. Remove potatoes from the oven and switch on your broiler to the highest setting
12. Drizzle wing sauce over the potatoes and sprinkle both cheeses evenly over top
13. Place under the broiler for 2-3 minutes, until cheese is bubbly and golden
14. Remove immediately and sprinkle with green onions and chopped cilantro
15. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
