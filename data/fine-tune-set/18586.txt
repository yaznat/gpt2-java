Ingredients:
• Seedless raisin
• Dry sherry
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Light cream
• Blanched almonds
• Ground cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Soak the raisins in the sherry or orange juice for 20 minutes
3. Drain
4. Beat the eggs in a bowl
5. Gradually beat in the sugar until light and fluffy
6. Mix in cream, then almonds, cinnamon, nutmeg, and raisins
7. Spoon into 6 buttered custard cups or ramekins
8. Set in a pan of warm water
9. Bake for 25 minutes, or until set and lightly browned on top
10. Serve warm or ice cold, with whipped cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
