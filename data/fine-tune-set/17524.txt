Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Butter
• Sour cream
• Blueberries
• Fresh thyme
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Butter or grease & flour a 9" x 13" pan
3. In a large bowl, mix whole wheat flour, all-purpose flour, baking powder, baking soda, and a pinch of salt together
4. Mix in sugar
5. In another bowl, mix together the butter, 3 eggs and sour cream
6. Mix in the blueberries and thyme
7. Gently fold the two batters together
8. Spread coffee cake batter into pan
9. Top with chopped walnuts and sprinkle with a little brown sugar
10. Bake at 350 degrees for 40-45 minutes
11. Serve at room temperature and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
