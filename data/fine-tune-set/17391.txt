Ingredients:
• Cherry pie filling
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Refrigerated buttermilk biscuits
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Spoon pie filling into a greased 10" tube pan and set aside
3. Mix cinnamon with sugar and place in a shallow bowl
4. Place melted butter in another shallow bowl
5. Squash flat each biscuit, then dip into first the butter, then dredge in the sugar mixture
6. Arrange in flat layers over the pie filling
7. Bake at 375 f for 30-35 minutes
8. Let stand 10 minutes in pan upright, then invert onto a serving plate
9. Cut into wedges to serve<|endoftext|>
