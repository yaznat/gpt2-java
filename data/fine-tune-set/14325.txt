Ingredients:
• Garlic clove
• Fresh gingerroot
• Tamarind paste
• Rice vinegar
• Green tea leaves
• Firm tofu
• Peanut oil
• Tamari
• Fresh mushrooms
• Red onion
• Red pepper
• Broccoli florets
• Toasted sesame oil

Steps:
1. Place garlic, ginger, tamarind sauce, rice vinegar and tea leaves in a container with a tight fitting lid
2. Add tofu cubes and toss well
3. Marinate all day or overnight, shaking the container occasionally to distribute the marinade
4. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
5. Drain the tofu and reserve the marinade
6. Add tofu and tamari to taste to hot oil and cook, stirring almost constantly for 5 to 7 minutes, or until tofu is very hot
7. Transfer to a bowl and set aside
8. Add additional oil to the hot skillet if needed, followed by the vegetables and remaining marinade
9. Again, season to taste with tamari
10. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring frequently, or until broccoli is crisp-tender and pan juices have evaporated
11. Return tofu to skillet and toss to combine
12. Drizzle with sesame oil and serve<|endoftext|>
