Can you give me a custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Custard Tarts

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• White sugar
• Milk
• Salt
• Miniature tart shells

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Combine eggs, sugar, milk and salt, beat well till fluffy
3. Place tart shells on a cookie sheet
4. Spoon filling into tart shells, filling nearly to the top
5. Bake 20-30 minutes or till tart filling cracks
6. Can be topped with a fresh strawberry slice or a raspberry<|endoftext|>
