Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Evaporated milk
• Salt and pepper
• Butter
• Cooked turkey
• Onions
• Tomato sauce
• Green peppers
• Processed cheese

Steps:
1. Combine eggs, evaporated milk, salt and pepper
2. Beat well
3. Combine turkey, onion, tomato sauce, and green pepper
4. Melt butter in an 8 inch skillet, preferably with rounded sides, over medium heat
5. Pour in the egg mixture
6. Let cook, without stirring, until it is about three-quarters cooked, with just the top not cooked
7. Carefully loosen with a metal spatula and turn
8. Try not to break it
9. When the underside is cooked, spread the turkey mixture on one half of the omelette
10. Spread the torn cheese pieces over the turkey mixture
11. Fold omelette over to cover the filling
12. Serve as soon as the cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
