Give me a recipe for a popsicle.<|endoftext|>Fruity Strawberry Popsicle

Ingredients:
• Skim milk
• Frozen sliced strawberries in syrup
• Fat-free strawberry frozen yogurt
• Paper cups
• Wooden popsicle sticks

Steps:
1. Partially thaw the berries
2. Put in blender with milk and frozen yogurt, blend until smooth
3. Pour into paper cups or popsicle tray and freeze until firm
4. Try it with different fruit, too!<|endoftext|>
