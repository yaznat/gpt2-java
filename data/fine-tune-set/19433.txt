What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Reasonable Lemon Chicken And Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Lemon-pepper seasoning
• Potatoes
• Butter
• Lemon juice
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cut chicken breasts in half and place in large baking dish
2. Using about 4 tablespoons of the lemon-pepper seasoning, coat breast pieces in seasoning on both sides
3. Place potatoes over chicken
4. In medium bowl, mix butter and lemon juice
5. Pour butter mixture over potatoes
6. Sprinkle remaining lemon-pepper seasoning and salt over potatoes
7. Cover dish with tinfoil and place it in 350 degree oven for 25 minutes
8. Turn oven to 400 degrees and bake for 15 minutes more or until potatoes are soft
9. Remove foil and bake for 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
