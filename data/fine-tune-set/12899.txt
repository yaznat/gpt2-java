Ingredients:
• Beef round tip steak
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Light soy sauce
• Pepper
• Red onion
• Sliced mushrooms
• Roasted red pepper
• Dry red wine
• Muenster cheese
• Rolls

Steps:
1. Stack beef steaks
2. Cut lengthwise in half and then crosswise into 1" strips
3. Heat 1 tbl
4. Olive oil in non-stick skillet over med-high heat til hot
5. Stir-fry beef strips and garlic, 1-2 minutes or til outside surface is no longer pink
6. Remove from skillet and season with 2 tbl
7. Soy sauce and pepper
8. Reduce heat to medium
9. Add 2 teaspoons oil to pan, and stir-fry onion 5 minutes
10. Add mushrooms and continue cooking 2-3 minutes or til veggies are tender
11. Add red pepper, wine and remaining 1 tbl
12. Soy sauce, bring to a boil
13. Reduce heat and return beef to skillet
14. Heat through
15. Pile beef mixture onto bottoms of rolls
16. Top each with 2 slices cheese
17. Place on cookie sheet with tops next to bottoms cut side up, and broil just til cheese is melted and inside tops are lightly toasted<|endoftext|>
