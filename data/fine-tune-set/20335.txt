Give me a recipe for stir fry.<|endoftext|>Colorful Dragon Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Whole chicken breast
• Fresh broccoli
• Stir-fry sauce
• Canola oil
• Onion
• Carrot
• Water

Steps:
1. Cut chicken into 1 / 2 inch strips
2. Remove flowerets from broccoli
3. Set aside
4. Peel stalks
5. Cut into thin slices
6. Coat chicken with 1 tbsp stir-fry sauce
7. Heat 1 tbsp oil in hot wok or large skillet over high heat
8. Add chicken and stir-fry for 3 minutes
9. Remove from pan
10. Heat remaining 2 tbsp oil in the same pan
11. Add onion
12. Stir-fry 1 minute
13. Add broccoli and carrot
14. Stir-fry 2 minutes longer
15. Pour water into pan
16. Reduce heat and simmer covered 3 minutes
17. Stir once
18. Add remaining 4 tbsp stir-fry sauce and chicken
19. Cook and stir just until chicken and vegetables are coated
20. Serve immediately
21. Hint is good served with white rice<|endoftext|>
