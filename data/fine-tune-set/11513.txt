Give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>Angel S Rich And Buttery Pan Rolls Bread Machine

Ingredients:
• Water
• Egg
• Lemon juice
• Butter
• Salt
• Sugar
• Powdered coffee creamer
• Instant potato flakes
• Bread flour
• Bread machine yeast

Steps:
1. Add the ingredients in the order listed in your bread machine manual
2. Select dough cycle and push start
3. When cycle is done, remove the dough to a lightly floured board, cut into 12 - 24 pieces
4. Shape each piece into a roll and place in a lightly greased muffin pan
5. Cover and let rise until double in size
6. Bake in a preheated 375 degree oven for 10 - 20 minutes or till golden
7. You can also add sesame seeds or poppy seeds on the rolls after you egg wash them prior to baking! : ) the possibilities are endless! : )
8. For those watching their sodium content you can use as little as 1 / 4 tsp of salt and they will still turn out wonderful<|endoftext|>
