Ingredients:
• 95% lean ground beef
• Cayenne pepper sauce
• Lettuce
• Blue cheese dressing
• Carrot
• Celery
• Fresh cilantro
• Taco shells
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef in large nonstick skillet over medium heat 8 to 10 minutes or until beef is not pink, breaking up into small crumbles
2. Pour off drippings, if necessary
3. Stir in pepper sauce
4. Cook and stir 1 minute or until heated through
5. Meanwhile heat taco shells according to package directions
6. Evenly spoon beef mixture into taco shells or tortillas
7. Add lettuce
8. Drizzle with dressing
9. Top evenly with carrot, celery and cilantro
10. Garnish with carrot and celery sticks or cilantro sprigs, if desired<|endoftext|>
