Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Chicken And Garlic

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat angel hair pasta
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Chicken strips

Steps:
1. Cook the past according to package directions
2. Drain
3. In a large saucepan, under low heat, mix olive oil and chopped garlic, stirring constantly until garlic start browning
4. Turn off the heat, add angle hair, toss
5. Add salt, if necessary, and black pepper to taste
6. Serve with grilled chicken strips and lots of grated mozzarella and parmesan cheese on top
7. Use extra virgin olive oil
8. When heating the olive oil with garlic be very careful so you don't burn the garlic<|endoftext|>
