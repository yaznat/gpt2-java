Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Ginger Pear Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Ground ginger
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground allspice
• Nutmeg
• Apple juice
• Soymilk
• Applesauce
• Corn oil
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Bosc pear

Steps:
1. Preheat the waffle iron according to the manufacturer's directions
2. Sift together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and spices in a large mixing bowl
3. In a med-sized bowl, mix together the juice, soy milk, applesauce, oil, sugar, and vanilla until well combined
4. Create a well in the center of the flour mixture and slowly blend the wet ingredients into the dry until combined
5. Fold in the grated pear
6. Make your waffles according to the directions on the iron<|endoftext|>
