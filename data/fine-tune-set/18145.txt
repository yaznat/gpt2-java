Ingredients:
• Maple syrup
• Cooking apple
• Pancake mix
• Water
• Cheddar cheese
• Bacon bits

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan, combine maple syrup and apples
2. Simmer over medium low heat while preparing pancakes, stirring occasionally
3. In a medium bowl, combine pankake mix and water
4. Stir in cheddar cheese and bacon bits
5. Using a 1 / 4 cup measuring cup as a guide, pour batter onto a hot griddle or skillet sprayed with cooking spray lightly brown on both sides
6. For each serving, place 2 pancakes on a plate and spoon about 1 / 3 cup syrup mix over top<|endoftext|>
