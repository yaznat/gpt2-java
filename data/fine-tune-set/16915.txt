Ingredients:
• Baileys irish cream
• Amaretto
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Combine the baileys and amaretto in a small saucepan
2. Heat over medium-low heat until warm but not hot
3. Stir in the 1 / 2 teaspoon cinnamon
4. Pour into martini glasses, sprinkle with cinnamon, and serve<|endoftext|>
