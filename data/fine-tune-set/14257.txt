Please give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Deep Fried Tofu

Ingredients:
• Sea salt
• Chinese five spice powder
• Sugar
• White pepper
• Garlic
• Tofu
• Peanut oil
• Green onions

Steps:
1. In a bowl mix salt, 5 spice powder, sugar, pepper and garlic
2. Add tofu 1 at a time and turn over to coat evenly
3. Let stand 1 hour
4. In a wok, heat oil until smoking
5. Add tofu and deep fry for 5 minutes until puffy and golden
6. Drain on paper towels and serve immediately with green onions<|endoftext|>
