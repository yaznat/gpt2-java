What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Gluten Free Rice And Soy Waffles

Ingredients:
• Rice flour
• Soy flour
• Potato starch
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Milk

Steps:
1. Place dry ingredients in a bowl and mix together thoroughly
2. In another mixing bowl, beat the eggs, oil and milk
3. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and fold in gently with a spoon
4. Fold in until just combined-do not overbeat
5. Bake in a heated waffle iron and serve<|endoftext|>
