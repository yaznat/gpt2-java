Ingredients:
• Salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Raw shrimp
• Canola oil
• Garlic
• Green onion
• Snow peas
• Vegetables
• Chicken broth
• Lemon, zest of

Steps:
1. Toss shrimp with salt& cayenne
2. Heat oil, on high, in non-stick pan
3. Saute shrimp, stirring often, about 2 minutes
4. Remove shrimp
5. In the same pan, saute garlic and green onions for about 1 minute
6. Add peas, vegetables and chicken broth
7. Reduce heat to medium and cook for about 3 minutes, until vegetables are crisp-tender
8. Return shrimp to pan, with lemon zest and cook for 1 more minute
9. Serve with rice<|endoftext|>
