Please give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>All In One Pot Saucy Pasta

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Water
• Pasta sauce
• Pasta
• Sliced mushrooms
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef and onion in large frying pan
2. Add water, pasta sauce, and pasta
3. Bring to a boil and cover
4. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Add mushrooms and continue cooking for 5 minutes
6. Stir in cheese<|endoftext|>
