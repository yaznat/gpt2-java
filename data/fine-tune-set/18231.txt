What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Walnut Flax Pancake

Ingredients:
• Pancake mix
• Banana
• Flax seed
• Walnut pieces
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon
• Raisins
• Muesli
• Margarine
• Sweet 'n low
• Water

Steps:
1. Heat and spray pan with oil to medium heat
2. Smash ripe banana
3. Add remaining ingredients
4. Add water slowly until reaching desired consistency, i had it somewhere between a pancake and a muffin batter consistency
5. Dump batter on pan, smooth around into a circle about 1 / 2 to 3 / 4 inch thick, roughly 5-6 inches wide
6. I think it took 4 minutes on first side
7. It is a bit tough to flip, i kept checking with spatula until i felt i could get under it and flip it over
8. Cook other side til browned
9. 1-2 minutes
10. Just eat plain, its yummy enough alone
11. I used a 5-grain pancake mix
12. Choose whatever pancake mix you have, i go for whole grains and variety<|endoftext|>
