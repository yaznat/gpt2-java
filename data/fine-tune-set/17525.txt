Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Fresh blueberries
• Low-fat buttermilk
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Almond extract
• Egg
• Cooking spray
• Almonds
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Lightly spoon flour into dry measure, level with knife
3. Combine first 5 ingredients in large bowl, add 2 / 3 cup of the blueberries, toss well
4. Combine buttermilk, butter, extracts and egg - stir well with whisk
5. Add to flour mixture and mix just till moistened
6. Spoon batter into 8 inch square baking pan coated with cooking spray, spreading evenly
7. Top with remaining 1 / 3 blueberries
8. Combine almonds, brown sugar and cinnamon, sprinkle over blueberries
9. Bake at 350 degrees for 35 min or till wooden pick comes out clean from center
10. 8 servings - per serving: 188 calories / 3
11. 9g protein / 30
12. 6g carbo / 282mg sodium / 5
13. 7g fat / 1
14. 3g fiber<|endoftext|>
