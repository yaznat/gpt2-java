Ingredients:
• Long grain rice
• Evaporated milk
• Butter
• Sugar
• Water

Steps:
1. Boil rice until done
2. Place cooked rice in a baking dish
3. Add milk, water, butter, sugar
4. Stir all of it together and bake until done about 1 hr at 350 degrees
