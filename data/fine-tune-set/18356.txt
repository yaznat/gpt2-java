Ingredients:
• Active dry yeast
• Milk
• Flour
• Granulated sugar
• Salt
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Sprinkle yeast over the 1 / 2 cup warm milk in a small bowl
2. Set aside about 5 minutes to soften
3. In a large bowl, mix together flour, sugar and salt
4. Lightly beat the egg yolks with a fork
5. In a small bowl mix melted butter, 1 1 / 4 cup warm milk, vanilla, egg yolks
6. Add the butter, milk, vanilla, egg yolks to flour mixture
7. Mix until smooth
8. Beat egg whites until soft peaks form
9. Stir in the yeasted milk
10. Fold egg whites gently into batter
11. Leave it out on the counter until doubled in volume, then bake
12. The flavor is better if refrigerated overnight<|endoftext|>
