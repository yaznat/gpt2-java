Ingredients:
• Egg noodles
• Cream of chicken soup
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Milk
• White onion
• Curry powder
• Sliced ripe olives
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Mix the chicken, soup, milk, 3 cups shredded cheese, onion, curry powder, salt and pepper
2. Stir well
3. Let sit to absorb curry flavor if desired
4. Add the cooked egg noodles
5. Mix together and pour into greased 9x13 dish
6. Top with sliced black olives and the rest of the shredded cheese
7. Bake 20 minutes at 350f continue cooking until cheese is melted and browned
8. Let rest for 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
