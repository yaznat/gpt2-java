Can you give me a wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Chicken And Lettuce Wraps

Ingredients:
• Ground chicken
• Red bell pepper
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Fresh lemon juice
• Hoisin sauce
• Brown sugar
• Sesame oil
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Pepper sauce
• Cilantro
• Iceberg lettuce

Steps:
1. In large skillet saut ground chicken / turkey over medium heat, crumbling and cooking until no longer pink
2. Pour into dish, set aside
3. Rinse out pan
4. Saut bell pepper, onion, garlic and ginger for 2 minutes
5. Add meat to pan
6. In small bowl, blend lemon juice, hoisin sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, soy sauce and hot pepper sauce
7. Pour into pan and stir to coat chicken
8. Reduce heat and simmer, stirring until sauce is absorbed, about 4 minutes
9. Stir in chopped cilantro
10. Serve with lettuce cups<|endoftext|>
