Ingredients:
• Fat-free sugar-free instant chocolate pudding mix
• Fat-free cool whip
• Mini chocolate chip
• Red cherries
• Peanuts

Steps:
1. Follow directions for making pudding
2. Refrigerate until almost set, fold in cool whip, peanuts, cherries and chocolate chips and chill until ready to serve
3. For presentation, serve with graham crackers crumbled over the top<|endoftext|>
