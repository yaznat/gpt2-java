Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Leftover Roast Mexican Casserole

Ingredients:
• Cooked beef
• Oil
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Black beans
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Ground cumin
• Garlic salt
• Chicken broth
• Corn tortillas
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Shred cooked meat and remove any bones or fat
2. Heat oil in a large skillet
3. Add onion and pepper and saute until tender
4. Add all ingredients except corn tortillas and cheese
5. Stir together
6. Bring to a boil
7. Reduce heat to medium or medium-low and boil for one hour, allowing most of the liquid to cook off
8. Watch and reduce heat if necessary
9. Preheat oven to 350
10. Spray a 9x13 pan with non-stick spray
11. Place 3 corn tortillas in the bottom of the pan, tearing to fit
12. Spoon 1 / 3 of the meat mixture over the tortillas
13. Spread to cover
14. Sprinkle with 1 / 3 of the cheese
15. Repeat steps 7-9 two times
16. Bake at 350 for 30 minutes
17. Can also be refrigerated in the morning and baked later that day
