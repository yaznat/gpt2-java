Can you give me a muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Amaranth Whole Wheat Muffins

Ingredients:
• Amaranth flour
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Baking powder
• Vanilla
• Milk
• Oil
• Maple syrup
• Raisins
• Almonds
• Egg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f grease muffin cups or line with parchment paper
2. In a medium bowl, blend the amaranth flour, pastry flour and the baking powder
3. In a large bowl, beat together the egg, milk, oil and honey
4. Mix until dry ingredients are moistened
5. Fold in the raisins and the almonds
6. Fill each muffin cups 3 / 4 full
7. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean<|endoftext|>
