What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Acapulco Golden Burgers

Ingredients:
• Lean ground turkey
• Fine dry breadcrumbs
• Chunky salsa
• Chili seasoning mix
• Dried parsley flakes
• Light cheddar cheese
• Hamburger buns
• Nonfat sour cream

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine meat, bread crumbs, 1 / 4 cup salsa, chili seasoning, parsley flakes and cheddar cheese
2. Using a 1 / 3 cup measuring cup as a guide, form into 6 patties
3. Place patties in a large skillet sprayed with olive oil flavored cooking spray and brown about 4 to 5 minutes on each side or to desired degree of doneness or cook on gril for same amount of time
4. For each serving, place a patty between a bun and top with 1 tablespoon salsa and 1 / 2 tablespoon sour cream<|endoftext|>
