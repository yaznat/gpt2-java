What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Easy Cocktail Weiners

Ingredients:
• Cocktail franks
• Prepared yellow mustard
• Grape jelly

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in crock pot
2. Heat on low 3-4 hours stirring occasionally
3. Serve warm
4. You can adjust how spicy to prepare according to your tastes, add more mustard, less jelly, etc
5. This is a great recipe base that takes all kinds of changes according to individual tastes easily<|endoftext|>
