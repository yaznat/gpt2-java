Please give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Black Bean And Plantain Burritos

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Red onion
• Garlic clove
• Plantains
• Black beans
• Cilantro
• Green onion
• Ground cumin
• Hot sauce
• Adobo seasoning
• Ground coriander
• Ground black pepper
• Corn tortillas
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large saucepan over medium-high heat
2. Add red onion and garlic, and saute until browned
3. Stir in plantains, and cook 5 minutes
4. Add black beans, cilantro, green onion, cumin, hot sauce, adobo seasoning, coriander and pepper
5. Cook 5 more minutes
6. Spoon filling into warmed corn tortillas
7. Top with cheese and mango salsa, if desired
8. Roll up
9. And serve<|endoftext|>
