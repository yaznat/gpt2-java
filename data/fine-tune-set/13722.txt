Ingredients:
• Pork sirloin roast
• Salt
• Pepper
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Apple cider
• Apple jelly
• Apple cider vinegar
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f trim excess fat from roast
2. Sprinkle with salt and pepper on all sides
3. In heavy skillet, brown roast on all sides in oil over med
4. Heat
5. Remove pork and add remaining ingredients to skillet
6. Cook and stir over med heat until sugar dissolves and mixture comes to a boil
7. Place pork in roasting pan and roast at 400f for 30 minutes
8. Pour apple cider mixture over all and roast 35-45 minuteslonger, basting occasionally with sauce, until thermometer reads 160f cool pork and sauce in refrigerator
9. Thinly slice pork and place in freezer bag along with sauce
10. Seal bag, label, and freeze
11. To thaw and reheat: thaw overnight in fridge
12. Place pork and sauce in heavy skillet and bring to simmer over med heat
13. Cook 4-5 minutes until thoroughly heated<|endoftext|>
