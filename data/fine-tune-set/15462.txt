Ingredients:
• Worcestershire sauce
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Ketchup
• Salt and pepper
• Yellow mustard
• Butter
• Sugar
• Pork and beans

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef and onion
2. Drain
3. Remove from heat
4. Add all other ingredients and stir well
5. Place in a casserole dish and bake, covered, at 325 for 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
