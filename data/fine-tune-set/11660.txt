Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>7 Up Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Oil
• Pancake mix
• 7-up

Steps:
1. Beat egg and oil
2. Add pancake mix
3. Add 1 cup 7-up
4. Stir until most lumps have disappeared
5. Gently stir in additional 7-up
6. Bake on medium heat griddle, turning once<|endoftext|>
