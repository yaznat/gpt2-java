Ingredients:
• Milk
• Egg yolks
• Butter
• Bread flour
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Mace
• Bread machine yeast
• Strawberry preserves
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Add first 8 ingredients to bread machine
2. Select dough cycle
3. When cycle is complete, remove and punch dough down
4. Let rest 10 minutes
5. Grease an 11x7x11 / 2 inch baking pan
6. Pat 2 / 3 of dough into pan
7. Combine remaining ingredients and spread over dough
8. Roll remaining dough into an 13 x 7 inch rectangle
9. Cut into 1 / 2 inch wide strips
10. Weave strips in a lattice pattern over filling
11. Trim excess dough
12. Cover and let rise in a warm place, about 1 hour
13. Bake at 375 degrees for 20-30 minutes<|endoftext|>
