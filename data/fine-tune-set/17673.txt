Give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple And Pecan Muffins Gluten Free Or Not

Ingredients:
• Sorghum flour
• Gluten-free flour
• Baking powder
• Caster sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Pecans
• Salt
• Apple
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Milk
• Brewed coffee
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Grease or line a 12 hole muffin tin and pre-heat oven to 170c / 340f
2. Combine all of the dry ingredients, except apple, in a large mixing bowl and whisk to combine
3. Add apple and lightly blend only until the apple is coated
4. Set aside
5. Add all of the wet ingredients to a jug and whisk well
6. Add the wet ingredients to the dry and lightly stir until just combined
7. Do not beat or over-mix as this will result in a tough dry muffin
8. Pour into muffin cases and top each muffin with a pecan and sprinkle with sugar and ground cinnamon
9. Bake approximately 20 minutes or until done
10. Cool on trays
11. Gluten-free muffins are best made the day of baking and can be frozen
12. Wam for a few seconds in the microwave to enjoy the next day<|endoftext|>
