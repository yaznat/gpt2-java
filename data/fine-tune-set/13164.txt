Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>Evil Chicken

Ingredients:
• Broiler
• Dry sherry
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Minced ginger
• Garlic clove
• Peppercorn
• Onion
• Black tea leaves
• Orange zest
• Orange, juice of

Steps:
1. Combine sherry, soy sauce, brown sugar, ginger, garlic, crushed peppercorns, onion, tea leaves, orange peel and juice from orange in a bowl and mix well
2. Pour over chicken and marinade in refrigerator for at least 2 hours
3. If using a whole chicken bake at 350 for 1 1 / 2 to 2 hours or until chicken is done
4. If using boneless breasts i prefer to cook it in a slow cooker on low for at least 2 hours but you can also bake at 350 for at least 45 minutes or until chicken is done
5. Use remaining marinade from roasting pan or slow cooker as a gravy<|endoftext|>
