Ingredients:
• Asparagus
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Plum tomatoes
• White beans
• Dried rosemary
• Vegetable broth
• Salt and pepper
• Linguine
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Saute asparagus and garlic in oil in a large skillet until crisp-tender, about 3-4 minutes
2. Stir in remaining ingredients, except salt, pepper and cheese
3. Heat to boiling
4. Reduce heat and simmer until mixture has thickened, 3-5 minutes
5. Season to taste with salt and pepper
6. Serve over linguini, topped with cheese<|endoftext|>
