Please give me a recipe for a wrap.<|endoftext|>Asian Beef Lettuce Wraps

Ingredients:
• Lemons
• Oranges
• Honey
• Soy sauce
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Beef
• Water
• Brown rice
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Red wine vinegar
• Ginger powder
• Sesame seeds
• Olive oil
• Cornstarch
• Bibb lettuce
• Green onions
• Cucumber
• Carrots
• Black beans
• Avocado
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Combine first six ingredients then divide in half
2. Add beef to half of marinade and let sit at least 1 hour at room temperature
3. Reserve remaining marinade for cooking later
4. Bring water to a boil
5. Add rice, reduce heat to just below medium, and cook 38 min then let sit 15 min without opening lid
6. Drain meat, discarding marinade
7. Heat oil over med-high heat 1 min then add sesame seeds and continue to heat 1 min more, stirring
8. Add beef to pan and cook, stirring gently, 4 min or so until beef is mostly cooked
9. Combine cornstarch and fresh, unused marinade and pour over cooked beef
10. Cook 1 min, stirring, then remove from heat
11. To partly cooled rice, add soy sauce, vinegar, honey, and ginger
12. Stir
13. To assemble wraps, layer rice, beef, beans, onions, cucumber, carrots, avocado, and cilantro on leaf of lettuce and roll up<|endoftext|>
