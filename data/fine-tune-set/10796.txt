What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>4 Ingredient Tomato Cucumber Onion Salad

Ingredients:
• Heirloom tomatoes
• Sweet onion
• English seedless cucumbers
• Seasoned rice vinegar
• Crouton
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Salad -- the tomatoes should be lightly seeded and then rough chopped then added to a medium size bowl
2. Add the cucumbers, onion and toss lightly
3. Add the nakano rice vinegar, salt and pepper and toss lightly
4. If you only have access to plain rice vinegar
5. 1 / 8 teaspoon dried oregano and dried basil can be added to 1 / 2 cup to plain rice wine vinegar to obtain the same flavor
6. But the nakano is well worth purchasing even online
7. It can be used for so many dishes
8. Croutons -- in a small baggie or ziplock bag, add the croutons
9. With a meat mallet, rolling pin, can, or just your hand, "crunch" up the croutons
10. Not to fine, just a rough texture
11. Finishing -- top the bowl with the croutons and lightly toss
12. Serving -- i love to serve this on a lettuce leaf and a sprig of fresh basil for a elegant presentation
13. I usually serve a toasted baguette on the side
14. Or if it is just everyday, just serve it as is
15. It is a great simple salad!<|endoftext|>
