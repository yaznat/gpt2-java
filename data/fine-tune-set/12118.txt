Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Evaporated milk
• 9" unbaked pie shell

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 f
2. In a large bowl, beat together the butter, brown sugar, eggs, flour, and milk until well blended
3. Pour the filling into the pie shell
4. Bake for 15 minutes at 350f or until crust is light golden brown
5. Turn off oven and leave the pie in the oven for 45 minutes to 1 hour<|endoftext|>
