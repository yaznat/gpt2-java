What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Onion
• Pinto beans
• Kidney beans
• Stewed tomatoes
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Salt
• Salsa

Steps:
1. In a large pot, brown meat with chopped onion
2. Drain grease, if necessary
3. Add beans, tomatoes, garlic, chili powder, cumin, salt and salsa to meat mixture
4. Cook until heated through
5. Serve with cornbread, cooked pasta, cooked rice, or on top of a baked potato
6. Sprinkle with cheese if desired<|endoftext|>
