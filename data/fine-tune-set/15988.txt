Give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Absolute Best Chicken Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Butter
• Cooked chicken
• Cooked bacon
• Fresh tomato
• Red onions
• Cheddar cheese
• Barbecue sauce
• Picante sauce
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Butter one side of each tortilla
2. Place one tortilla into a nonstick skillet
3. Evenly sprinkle chicken, bacon, tomatoes, onion
4. Drizzle bar-b-que sauce over all
5. Sprinkle cheese over and top with remaining tortilla
6. On medium heat, cook approximately 3 minutes, watching underside of tortilla for browning
7. Press with spatula while cooking
8. Carefully flip entire tortilla over using spatula
9. Cook approximately 3 minutes until light brown and cheese is melted
10. Remove to a plate and cut into 6 triangle pieces
11. Serve with picante sauce and sour cream<|endoftext|>
