Ingredients:
• Water
• Apricot nectar
• Superfine sugar
• Tea bags
• Frozen sliced peaches

Steps:
1. In a large pitcher, stir together cool water, apricot nectar and superfine sugar
2. Add tea bags
3. Let steep 5 to 7 minutes, dunking bags occasionally
4. Once tea is desired strength, add frozen or fresh peaches
5. Pour over ice and serve
6. For raspberry iced tea: switch to berries: replace apricot nectar with 1 1 / 2 cups cranberry-raspberry drink
7. In blender, puree 1 / 2 pint fresh raspberries
8. Strain out seeds
9. Add to cold water with superfine sugar
10. Replace peaches with 1 pint fresh raspberries<|endoftext|>
