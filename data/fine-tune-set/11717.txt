Ingredients:
• Crushed pineapple in juice
• Cherry pie filling
• Devil's food cake mix
• Pecans
• Mint chocolate chips
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Spray 13 x 9-inch pan with cooking spray
3. Spread pineapple and juice in bottom of pan
4. Top with cherry pie filling
5. Sprinkle cake mix evenly over the fruits
6. Scatter chopped pecans and chocolate evenly over the top
7. Spread butter slices evenly over the top
8. Bake in preheated oven approximately 40 minutes or until an inserted toothpick comes out clean<|endoftext|>
