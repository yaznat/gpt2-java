Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Banana Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Bananas
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Cream butter
3. Add bananas, sugars, vanilla, and eggs and mix well
4. Add flour, soda, and salt and mix well
5. Mix in 1 / 2 cup of chocolate chips
6. Spread into greased 13x9 inch baking pan
7. Sprinkle the remaining 1 / 2 cup of chocolate chips on top
8. Bake at 350 for 5 minutes
9. Use a knife to swirl the melted chips on top
10. Return to oven and bake another 25-30 minutes
11. Cool and cut into squares<|endoftext|>
