Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Home Fries

Ingredients:
• Russet potatoes
• Onion
• Salt
• Pepper
• Paprika
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Place unpeeled whole potatoes into a pot of water
2. Bring to a boil
3. Turn off heat when water begins to boil
4. Leave potatoes in pot for 10 minutes
5. Drain water and remove from pot
6. When cooled, peel the potatoes and cut into cubes
7. Heat 2 tbs of oil in skillet
8. Place cubed potatoes and sliced onions in large bowl
9. Pour on 2 tbs of oil
10. Add salt, pepper, and paprika
11. Mix with hands
12. Pour into skillet and cover
13. Cook on low for 15-20 minutes
14. Stir and cover
15. Cook another 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
