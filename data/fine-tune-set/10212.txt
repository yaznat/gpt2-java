What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Bev S Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Chili beans
• Black beans
• Diced green chilies
• Rotel chili fixins tomatoes
• Diced tomatoes
• Onion
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cook hamburger until done
2. Drain fat
3. Put in crockpot
4. Add remaining ingredients except onion
5. Cook on high until it starts to boil
6. Put diced onion on top
7. Turn crock pot to low and cook for 6-8 hours
8. Stir in onion before serving<|endoftext|>
