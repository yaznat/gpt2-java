Ingredients:
• Navy beans
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Lemon juice
• Garlic clove
• Green onions
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in food processor and blend until smooth
2. This dip gets thicker after it has been refrigerated
3. Variations: add green olives, chives, chopped fennel, sun dried tomatoes or rosemary<|endoftext|>
