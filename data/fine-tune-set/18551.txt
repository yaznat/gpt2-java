Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Lemon Cranberry Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Fresh lemon juice
• Lemon rind
• Dried cranberries

Steps:
1. Separate the eggs
2. Beat the egg whites until frothy
3. Add 1 tbsp of sugar and beat until peaks starts forming
4. Set aside
5. Spray pam on waffle iron
6. Preheat waffle iron
7. Sift the dry ingredients into a medium sized bowl
8. Add the egg yolks and the rest of the ingredients to the bowl
9. Beat until no lumps
10. Fold the stiff egg whites into the batter using a spatula
11. When the waffle iron is hot, add cup of batter to the center of the waffle iron, quickly spread batter horizontally
12. Close the top and cook for 2-2'15 minutes
13. Leftover waffles can be frozen
14. When you want to eat them later on, just pop them quickly into the toaster oven for a few minutes and they'll taste just like fresh waffles!<|endoftext|>
