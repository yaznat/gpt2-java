What's a good custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Crustless Baked Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Eggs
• Butter
• Sugar
• Flour
• Vanilla
• Salt

Steps:
1. In blender, combine all ingredients, cover and process until smooth
2. Pour into greased 9" deep dish pie plate
3. Bake at 350 for 50 minutes or until knife inserted near center comes out clean
4. Cool on wire rack for 1 hour
5. Refrigerate until serving<|endoftext|>
