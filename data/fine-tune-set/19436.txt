Can you give me a potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Ohana Cheesy Scalloped Potatoes Walt Disney World

Ingredients:
• Potato
• Heavy cream
• Cheddar cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Sweet onion
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Place a layer of potato slices and onion in a buttered shallow dish
2. Sprinkle with some of the cheeses and salt and pepper
3. Repeat layers and then pour cream over the potatoes
4. Bake at 350 for 1 hour and then let stand 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
