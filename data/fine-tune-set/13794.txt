Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Molasses
• Fresh spearmint
• Soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Water
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Hot pepper
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Combine everything except the tenderloin and the vegetable oil
2. Mix well
3. Place tenderloin in a large resealable bag
4. Pour marinade over top
5. Turn to coat
6. Refrigerator for 8 hours
7. Preheat grill and coat grates in vegetable oil
8. Remove tenderloins from marinade and place on grill over medium high heat
9. Grill for 20 minutes, turning occasionally
10. Baste with mariande after 10 minutes then discard remaining mariande
11. Remove from grill when done, let sit for 5 minutes, then carve into thin slices<|endoftext|>
