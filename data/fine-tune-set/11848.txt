Ingredients:
• Bombay duck
• Garlic cloves
• Gingerroot
• Green chilies
• Red onions
• Cumin seed
• Green cardamoms
• Cinnamon stick
• Bay leaf
• Dried red chilies
• Mustard oil
• Salt
• Turmeric powder
• Coriander powder
• Red chili pepper
• Coriander

Steps:
1. Wash, pat dry and apply salt and turmeric to the fish pieces and keep aside for 15 minutes
2. Meanwhile, heat mustard oil in a flat non-stick girdle or deep pan
3. When smoking, add dried red chillies, whole spices, cumin seeds
4. Let splutter
5. Add sliced onion
6. Cook for 2 minutes add ginger-garlic- green chilly paste / mix
7. Fry well for 2 minutes in high flame
8. Add the spice powders and 4 tbspn of water to cook the spices
9. Move the spice mix to the side of the pan
10. Add the fish pieces to the same pan, side by side
11. Reduce flame to medium, and allow the fish to cook in its juices for 5 minutes
12. With a spoon, spread the cooked spice mix over the fish, add a cup of water
13. Let the fish cook in the gravy for 5 minutes since this fish is delicate, take care not to stir too much so that the pieces remain intact
14. You may add 1 tsp of lemon juice
15. Garnish with chopped coriander leaves
16. Remove from flame
17. Serve hot
18. Tastes best with rice<|endoftext|>
