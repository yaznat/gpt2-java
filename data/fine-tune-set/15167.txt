Ingredients:
• Strong white flour
• Salt
• Dried yeast
• Olive oil
• Water
• Oregano

Steps:
1. Sift together the flour and salt into a large bowl and stir in the dried yeast and oregano
2. Make a well in the centre of the flour mixture
3. Add the olive oil and gradually add enough of the warm water to make a soft dough
4. The amount of water will vary according to the flour but the dough must be malleable
5. Knead the dough on a floured surface until smooth and elastic - about 10 minutes
6. Place dough back in clean lightly oiled bowl and turn it around to coat it with some of the oil
7. Cover with cling wrap and leave in a warm place to "prove"
8. After the dough has doubled in size, knock it back and knead again for about 3 minutes
9. Shape and top as desired<|endoftext|>
