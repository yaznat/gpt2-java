Please give me a recipe for steak.<|endoftext|>Blackened Steaks With Horseradish Cream

Ingredients:
• Olive oil flavored cooking spray
• Steaks
• Blackening seasoning
• Cream cheese spread
• Half-and-half
• Prepared horseradish

Steps:
1. Coat grill rack with cooking spray
2. Preheat grill
3. Cut steak into four portions, if desired
4. Season both sides of steaks with blackening seasoning
5. Place steaks on grill
6. Close lid
7. Grill 4-6 minutes on each side or until internal temperature reaches 145f but i like well done! use a meat thermometer to accurately ensure desired doneness
8. Meanwhile, whisk remaining ingredients, in small bowl, until blended and smooth
9. Serve horseradish cream with steaks<|endoftext|>
