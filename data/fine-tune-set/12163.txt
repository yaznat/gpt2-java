Ingredients:
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Tart apples
• Sugar
• Flaked coconut
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Using a mixer, combine the butter and the cream cheese well
2. Using your hands, mix in the flour and the salt till completely combined
3. Shape into a ball and chill for 15 minutes before using
4. Pre-heat oven to 400f
5. Divide the dough ball in two and roll each half out into a circle about 1 / 8 inch thick
6. Line a 10 inch deep-dish pie pan with one circle
7. Arrange the apples in this unbaked pie shell
8. You will want to do this as neatly as possible in order to get them all inches
9. In a bowl, combine the sugar, flour and salt
10. Sprinkle evenly over the apples
11. Next, cover the apples evenly with the coconut
12. Sprinkle the vanilla over the top, and dot with the butter
13. Place the remaining circle of pastry over the filling
14. Seal and flute edges
15. Cut slits in top to allow steam to escape
16. Bake at 400f for 10 minutes, then reduce heat to 300 and bake an additional 35 to 40 minutes until the top is golden and the juices are bubbling<|endoftext|>
