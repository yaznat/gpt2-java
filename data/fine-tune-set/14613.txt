What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Adas Polo Persian Rice With Lentils

Ingredients:
• Lentils
• Basmati rice
• Water
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Pepper
• Turmeric
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Cardamom
• Cumin
• Saffron
• Raisins
• Dates
• Potato
• Canola oil
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, bring salted water to a boil and simmer lentils on low for 20 - 30 minutes, or until tender
2. Drain water from lentils and set aside
3. In a pan, saute the onions and garlic in oil
4. Add raisins, dates, and spices, except saffron
5. Mix well and set aside
6. Cook rice as you would like
7. For a sure-fire way, i use my rice cooker
8. Rinse the rice under cold water until the water runs clear
9. Add to rice cooker and cover with 2 cups water
10. Add 1 / 2 teaspoon salt and a drizzle of oil
11. Cook
12. Once rice is done, transfer cooked rice to a large bowl
13. In the same pot, add oil to just cover the bottom surface
14. Add potato slices
15. Add a layer of rice
16. Add a layer of lentil-raisin mixture
17. Continue layering, ending with a final layer of rice
18. Cover and cook for 10 minutes
19. Drizzle melted butter and saffron water over your rice
20. Cover the top of your pot or rice cooker with a tea towel to prevent steam from escaping, and top with lid
21. Cook on low for 30 minutes
22. You want to make the potatoes crispy, so they must cook slowly
23. In the rice cooker, i continuously hit the cook button so this process can happen
24. It's totally worth it!
25. Serve<|endoftext|>
