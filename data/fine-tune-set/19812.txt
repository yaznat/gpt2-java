Give me a fries recipe.<|endoftext|>Fast Spicy Fries

Ingredients:
• French fries
• Mccormick's montreal brand steak seasoning
• Kosher salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450
2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil
3. Spray with nonstick cooking spray
4. Spread fries in a single layer on prepared baking sheet
5. Bake for 7 minutes
6. Stir
7. Spray fries with nonstick cooking spray
8. Sprinkle evenly with steak seasoning and salt
9. Bake 7 minutes longer
10. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
