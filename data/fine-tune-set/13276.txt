Ingredients:
• Low-fat italian salad dressing
• Soy sauce
• Onion
• Dry mustard

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f and lightly spray a baking pan with cooking spray
2. Mix the dressing with the soy sauce, onion and mustard
3. Pour over the chicken, cover with foil and bake 40-50 minutes till browned<|endoftext|>
