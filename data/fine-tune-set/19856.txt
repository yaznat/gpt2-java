What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Herbed Home Fries

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Garlic
• Oregano flakes
• Parsley flakes
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in large non-stick pan over medium-high heat
2. Add all ingredients
3. Fry until tender, stirring frequently<|endoftext|>
