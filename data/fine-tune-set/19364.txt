Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Shortening
• White sugar
• Milk
• Butter
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine flour, baking powder, salt and nutmeg
3. In another bowl, beat egg, shortening, vanilla and sugar until well blended
4. Add the flour mixture to the creamed mixture and beat in milk until smooth
5. Fill each cup of lightly greased muffin tin 2 / 3 full
6. Bake 20 minutes, or until a light golden color, or until an inserted toothpick comes out clean
7. Melt butter
8. In a small bowl, combine 1 / 2 cup sugar, cinnamon and nutmeg
9. While puffins are still warm, dip each one in butter, then in sugar mixture, coating thoroughly
10. Serve warm, or allow to cool and store in an airtight container<|endoftext|>
