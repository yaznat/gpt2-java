What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Ever Chili Recipe

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• White onion
• Flour
• Tomato sauce
• Whole tomatoes
• Chili beans
• Kidney beans
• Oregano
• Chili powder
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Brown sugar
• Vinegar
• Corn chips
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef and onions together in skillet
2. Sprinkle flour over cooked meat
3. Puree whole tomatoes in blender
4. Combine meat, pureed tomato, and remaining ingredients in crock pot
5. Cook for 8hrs on low or 4hrs on high
6. Top with fritos-style corn chips and sour cream<|endoftext|>
