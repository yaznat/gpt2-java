What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Allison S Taco Wings

Ingredients:
• Chicken wings
• Taco seasoning
• Vinegar
• Oil
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Clean chicken wings, cut at the joints into three pieces and discard wing
2. In a large resealable plastic bag, combine the taco seasoning, vinegar, oil and cayenne by "squishing" the bag to make a paste
3. Add the chicken wing pieces, seal the bag and "squish" until the paste is distributed evenly over the chicken pieces
4. Marinate chicken 1 hour at room temperature or overnight in the refrigerator
5. Preheat oven to 400f
6. Spread wings pieces on a baking sheet lined with foil
7. Try to maintain some space between each piece - use a second baking sheet if necessary
8. Brush pieces with any extra paste
9. Bake at 400 for 1 to 1 1 / 4 hours, turning half way through the baking time
10. Can be held in a crock pot to serve at a party or buffet<|endoftext|>
