Give me a quesadilla recipe.<|endoftext|>Blender Chicken Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breasts
• American cheese
• Jalapeno peppers
• Onion
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees
2. Cook the chicken breasts
3. Add all desired ingredients into a blender and blend on low until finely chopped and blended
4. On a slightly greased cookie sheet or pie dish, lay one tortilla down, and cover with chicken mixture from blender
5. Then place another tortilla on top and put in the oven for 5-10 minutes or until cheese is completely melted<|endoftext|>
