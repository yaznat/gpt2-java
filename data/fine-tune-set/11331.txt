Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>1 Lb Abm Hawaiian Sweet Bread

Ingredients:
• Pineapple juice
• Egg
• Milk
• Butter
• Coconut extract
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Sugar
• Bread flour
• Instant potato flakes
• Yeast

Steps:
1. Add ingredients to your bread machine pan in the order listed above
2. Use light setting and regular bread
3. This can be a high-rising bread
4. Also notable is that i always look at my bread once the machine has warmed up and it begins its first blending -- if it's too dry, i add a little milk or juice, if it's a little "wet" i add flour in teaspoons at a time -- just "eyeballing" the consistency
5. Your entire house will fill with the aroma of this lovely sweet bread while it bakes!<|endoftext|>
