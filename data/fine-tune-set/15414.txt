Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Mushroom
• Bacon
• Dried parsley
• Salt
• Pepper
• Worcestershire sauce
• Breadcrumbs
• Oatmeal

Steps:
1. Chop mushrooms and bacon, add to ground beef
2. Add the rest of the ingredients and mix until blended
3. Form into a meatloaf or meatballs
4. For meatloaf, bake at 350 for an hour or so, or until cooked
5. For meatballs, cook in a frying pan or in the oven
6. Can be frozen<|endoftext|>
