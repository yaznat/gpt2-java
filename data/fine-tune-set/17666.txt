What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Almond Muffins

Ingredients:
• Almond flour
• All-purpose white flour
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cardamom
• Milk
• Egg
• Applesauce
• Apple
• Almonds
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Grease or line a 12 muffin tin
3. Whisk the flours, sugar, baking powder, salt, and spices in a medium bowl
4. In a small bowl, whisk the milk and egg until beaten
5. Add the applesauce and mix to combine
6. Pour wet ingredients into dry
7. Add the chopped apples and optional nuts and stir to combine
8. Pour batter into muffin tins
9. Mix crunch topping ingredients in a small bowl and sprinkle topping over unbaked muffins
10. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until cooked through<|endoftext|>
