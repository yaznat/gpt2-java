Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Pie 63

Ingredients:
• Caramels
• Half-and-half
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Margarine
• Oil
• Water
• Egg
• Apples
• Lemon peel
• Lemon juice
• Cream cheese
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375f in small saucepan, combine caramels and half-and-half
2. Cook over low heat, stirring occasionally, until caramels are melted
3. Keep warm
4. Lightly spoon flour into measuring cup
5. Level off
6. In large bowl, combine 2 1 / 2 cups flour, 1 / 4 cup sugar and salt
7. With pastry blender or fork, cut in margarine until mixture resembles coarse crumbs
8. Add oil, water and egg
9. Mix well
10. Press crust mixture evenly in bottom and up sides of ungreased 15x10x1-inch baking pan
11. In large bowl, combine all apple filling ingredients
12. Toss lightly
13. Spoon into crust-lined pan
14. Drizzle warm caramel sauce over apples
15. In small bowl, combine all topping ingredients except nuts
16. Beat until smooth
17. Spoon over apples, spreading slightly
18. Sprinkle with nuts
19. Bake at 375f for 35 to 45 minutes or until light golden brown
20. Cool
21. Cut into squares
22. Store in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
