Ingredients:
• Plain yogurt
• Curry paste
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Garam masala
• Fresh coriander leaves
• Lemon juice
• Lamb
• Peanut oil
• Tomato paste
• Tomatoes
• Green capsicum
• Red chilies

Steps:
1. Combine yogurt, curry paste, garlic, salt, garam masala, coriander and lemon juice in a non-reactive bowl
2. Mix well
3. Add the lamb
4. Stir until well coated
5. Cover and marinate in fridge for 1 hour
6. Heat the oil in a wok over a medium-high heat
7. Add the lamb and marinade gradually, stirring constantly until colour changes
8. Stir fry until lamb is tender-about 8-10 minutes
9. Add tomato paste, tomato, capsicum and chilies
10. Cool for 3 minutes, stirring frequently<|endoftext|>
