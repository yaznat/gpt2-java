What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>Amen For Ramen Asian Style Salad

Ingredients:
• Ramen noodles
• Cabbage
• Scallions
• Carrots
• Snow peas
• Mayonnaise
• Sambal oelek
• Lemon juice
• Peanuts
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Bring water to boil
2. Add half of seasoning packet
3. Boil noodles for only two minutes
4. Drain and set aside to cool
5. Meanwhile for dressing, combine mayo, sambal olek, and lemon juice
6. In a medium to large sized bowl toss cabbage, carrots, scallions, snow peas
7. Add cooled noodles
8. Toss with dressing
9. To serve, top with peanuts and cilantro
10. Season to taste<|endoftext|>
