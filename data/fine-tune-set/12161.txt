What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Apple Cheesecake Pie

Ingredients:
• Graham cracker pie crusts
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Lemon juice
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Apple pie filling
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Combine cream cheese, sugar, lemon juice, and vanilla in a bowl
3. Mix until well blended
4. Add eggs, one at a time, mixing after each addition
5. Place crust on baking sheet
6. Pour in filling
7. Bake for 25 minutes or until inserted knife comes out clean
8. Topping: spread apple filling evenly on top of cooled cheesecake
9. Garnish with toasted pecans
10. Helpful hints-----------
11. Soften cream cheese in microwave on high 15 to 20 seconds
12. Toasted pecans-- spread out pecans evenly on a baking sheet
13. Place into a 375 degree oven for 3-5 minutes or until pecans are well toasted<|endoftext|>
