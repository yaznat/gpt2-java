Give me a ice cream recipe.<|endoftext|>Classic Vanilla Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Heavy cream
• Half-and-half
• Vanilla extract
• Sugar
• Salt

Steps:
1. Combine ingredients in a blender or food processor until sugar dissolves
2. Refrigerate until needed for ice-cream maker
3. Pour into the ice cream maker and follow your ice-cream maker's instructions for freezing<|endoftext|>
