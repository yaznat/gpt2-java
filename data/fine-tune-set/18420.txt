Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Egg
• Buttermilk
• Butter
• Whipping cream
• Powdered sugar
• Strawberries

Steps:
1. To make strawberry cream: in a mixing bowl, beat the whipping cream until foamy
2. Gradually add in powdered sugar and beat until soft peaks form
3. Fold sliced strawberries into whipped cream
4. To make the waffles: in a mixing bowl, mix together the flour, soda, salt, sugar, and cocoa
5. In another bowl, combine the egg yolk, buttermilk, and butter
6. Add egg mixture to the flour mixture, stirring until dry ingredients are moistened
7. Beat egg white until stiff peaks form
8. Carefully fold into batter
9. Pour appropriate amount of batter onto preheated, oiled waffle iron
10. Bake until done
11. Cut waffles to make eight 4-inch squares
12. Serve waffle squares with strawberry cream
13. Garnish with a strawberry fan<|endoftext|>
