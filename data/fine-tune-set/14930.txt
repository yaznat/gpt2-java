Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Fried Rice With Prawn Egg

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Eggs
• Prawns
• Rice
• Yellow capsicum
• Mushrooms
• Spring onions
• Soy sauce
• Fish sauce
• Sugar
• Lime juice

Steps:
1. Heat wok or large frying pan until very hot
2. Add peanut oil then beaten eggs and stir fry until set
3. Cut the egg up as you go with a spatula
4. Add the prawns and stir fry for one minute
5. Place the wok back on the heat
6. Add more peanut oil
7. As you stir keep breaking up the rice
8. Stir fry for about 3 minutes
9. Now add the cooked egg and prawn mixture and stir well
10. Add the spring onions, soy sauce, fish sauce and sugar and stir well
11. Taste for seasoning
12. Serve hot with a squeeze of lime juice<|endoftext|>
