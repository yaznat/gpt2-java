Give me a cocktail recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Sparkler Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Brandy
• Apple juice
• Soda water
• Apple

Steps:
1. Fill a martini shaker halfway with ice cubes
2. Add brandy and apple juice
3. Cover an shake vigorously until very cold
4. Strain into 2 champagne glasses
5. Add 2 tablespoons soda water into each glass and garnish with apple slices<|endoftext|>
