Ingredients:
• Pancake batter
• Butter
• Apples
• Pecans
• Maple syrup
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cardamom

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f degrees degrees
2. Pour melted butter into a 9-inch pie plate
3. Place apples in plate, then sprinkle with the cinnamon and cardamom
4. Add pecans on top, and drizzle with the syrup
5. Pour batter on top
6. Bake for 30 minutes
7. Serve with maple syrup<|endoftext|>
