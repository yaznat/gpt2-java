Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Oil
• Eggs
• Garlic cloves
• Carrot
• Red bell pepper
• Scallions
• Frozen peas
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. If you're making fresh rice for this, take a
2. Finely chop the garlic, bell pepper, and scallions
3. Grate the carrot
4. Heat a large non-stick skillet over high heat
5. Add about half the oil to the center
6. Add the eggs and quickly scramble, then push them off to the edges
7. Add the rest of the oil to the center of the skillet
8. Add the garlic, carrot, bell pepper and scallions, and quick-fry them for 2 minutes
9. Add the rice, combine with the veggies, then incorporate the scrambled eggs and fry for another 3 minutes
10. Add the peas and soy sauce and fry for 1 minutes
11. This is good with different kinds of meat too - i like to add already cooked shrimp or cubed chicken with the soy sauce and peas<|endoftext|>
