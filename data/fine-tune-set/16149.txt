Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Butter

Steps:
1. Using uncooked tortillas, cut them out with an egg shaped cookie cutter
2. You get 3 "eggs" per tortilla
3. I cut smaller dots from the scraps and stuck them on with water
4. I used a rick rack design roller i have for fondant on the other
5. In a non stick frying pan melt the butter over medium heat
6. Lay one egg shape in the pan and sprinkle with grated cheese
7. Top with a second agg shape
8. When cheese has melted flip to cook the other side
9. It will cook when they are on the griddle<|endoftext|>
