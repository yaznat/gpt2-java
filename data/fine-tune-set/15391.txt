Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Eggs
• Canadian bacon
• Pineapple rings
• Cheddar cheese
• Beets
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce leaves
• Ketchup
• Dill pickle relish
• Mayonnaise
• Mustard
• Kaiser rolls

Steps:
1. Preheat an outdoor grill for high heat
2. When the grill is ready, lightly oil the grilling surface
3. Form the ground beef into four patties, and grill for 5 minutes per side, or until cooked through
4. Meanwhile, melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat
5. Add onions, and fry until soft
6. Remove the onions from the skillet, and crack the eggs in the same skillet over medium heat
7. Cook until the yolks are solid, turning over once
8. Remove the eggs, and set aside
9. Place the canadian bacon in the same skillet, and fry until toasted
10. Remove the bacon, and turn the heat to high
11. Quickly fry the pineapple rings in the bacon drippings just until browned on each side
12. To assemble sandwiches: set bottom of kaiser rolls on a plate, and top with burger, a slice of cheese, a slice of canadian bacon, one fried egg, fried onions, a few slices of beet, a slice of pineapple, a slice of tomato, and a leaf of lettuce
13. Decorate the top bun with ketchup, mustard, relish and mayonnaise as desired
14. Place over the burger
15. Repeat with remaining burgers<|endoftext|>
