Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Sweet apples
• Whole milk
• All-purpose flour
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. You will need a high temp oven proof shillet for this
2. Put oven rack in middle position and preheat oven to 450f
3. Melt butter in skillet over moderate heat, then transfer 2 tablespoons to a blender
4. Add apple wedges to skillet and cook, turning over once, until beginning to soften, 3 to 5 minutes
5. While apple is cooking, add milk, flour, eggs, granulated sugar, vanilla, and salt to butter in blender and blend until smooth
6. Pour batter over apple and transfer skillet to oven
7. Bake until pancake is puffed and golden, about 15 minutes
8. Dust with confectioners sugar and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
