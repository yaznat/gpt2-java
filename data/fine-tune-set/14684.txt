What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Apple Wild Rice Breakfast

Ingredients:
• Apple
• Butter
• Wild rice
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Core apple and cut into bit size pieces
2. Melt butter in saut pan
3. Add apple and saut for 3-4 minutes
4. Add remaining ingredients and heat through
5. Serve in a bowl with cream or milk<|endoftext|>
