Ingredients:
• Broccoli
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter
• Swiss cheese
• Salt and pepper
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Steam or boil broccoli until tender, yet crispy
2. Drain and then dry broccoli with paper towels
3. Coarsely chop and place in a buttered 1 1 / 2 quart baking dish
4. Scatter cheese on top of broccoli
5. Mix the eggs, milk, butter, and seasonings together in a medium bowl
6. Pour custard over broccoli and cheese, and bake in a preheated 350 degree oven for 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
