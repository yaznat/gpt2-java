Ingredients:
• Sweet unsalted butter
• Bananas
• Light brown sugar
• Light rum
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat
2. Add bananas and saute until they turn brown, about 3 minutes
3. Sprinkle brown sugar over the bananas and cook until the sugar melts, about another 3 minutes
4. Remove from heat and pour rum into skillet
5. Ignite with a match and let the flames die out
6. Divide ice cream among 4 serving dishes and spoon bananas over
7. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
