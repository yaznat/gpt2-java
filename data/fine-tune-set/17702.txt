Ingredients:
• Cortland apples
• All-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Whole milk
• Eggs
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Chop apples roughly
2. Preheat oven 400 degrees f
3. Grease muffins tins lightly
4. Mix all dry ingredients together
5. Beat eggs, mix in the oil and milk
6. Add wet mix to the dry mixture and stir just until combined
7. Add apples and stir again, just til mixed
8. Pour into muffin pans
9. Bake 25 minutes
10. This is the original oven temp, and time
