Ingredients:
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Water
• Granulated sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Egg
• Egg whites
• Low-fat sour cream
• Golden syrup
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Icing sugar
• Light ricotta cheese
• Light cream cheese

Steps:
1. To make icing: in a small food processor or in a bowl and using a whisk or an electric mixer, beat all icing ingredients until smooth
2. Chill while preparing cakes
3. Preheat oven to 350f spray two 9-inch round cake pans with cooking spray
4. To make cake: in a small microwave-safe bowl, heat chocolate chips and water on high in the microwave for 30 seconds or just until chips begin to melt
5. Stir until smooth
6. In a large bowl and using a whisk or an electric mixer, beat together chocolate mixture, sugar, cocoa, egg, egg whites, sour cream, corn syrup, oil, and vanilla
7. In another bowl, stir together flour and baking powder
8. With a wooden spoon, stir flour mixture into chocolate mixture just until combined
9. Divide between prepared pans
10. Bake in centre of oven for 15 to 20 minutes or until a tester inserted in centre of cake comes out clean
11. Cool in pans for 10 minutes on a wire rack
12. Invert cakes onto a wire rack to cool completely
13. Place one cake on a serving platter
14. Spread some icing over top
15. Top with remaining cake
16. Spread icing over top and side of cake
17. Sprinkle with icing sugar or flaked chocolate<|endoftext|>
