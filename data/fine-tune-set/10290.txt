Give me a recipe for cookies.<|endoftext|>A Honey Of An Oatmeal Cookies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Sugar
• Light brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Honey
• Quick-cooking oats
• Dried cranberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt then set aside
3. In a mixing bowl cream butter, sugars, eggs, vanilla, and honey until well blended
4. At low speed, slowly add flour mixture
5. Add oats and dried fruit, mix until well incorporated
6. Drop dough by rounded tablespoons onto greased or parchment papered cookie sheet 2" apart
7. Bake 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
