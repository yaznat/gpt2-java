Ingredients:
• Haddock
• Mayonnaise
• Dry mustard
• Onion
• Cayenne
• Dill weed

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. Place fish in lightly greased shallow baking dish
3. Stir mayonnaise, mustard, onion, cayenne and dill weed in a small bowl until blended
4. Spread mixture on the fish
5. Bake at 400f for approx 20 minutes or until fish flakes easy with a fork<|endoftext|>
