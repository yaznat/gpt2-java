Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Salt
• Pepper
• Margarine
• Onions
• Jumbo pasta shells

Steps:
1. Combine potatoes and cheese together
2. Mash until smooth
3. Stir in salt and pepper
4. Set aside
5. Melt the margarine in a skillet
6. Add in onions
7. Stir / saut 10-15 minutes
8. Spread half the onions in a 13x9-inch pan
9. Stuff each pasta shell with potato mixture
10. Arrange shells over onions
11. Sprinkle remaining onions over shells
12. Bake in a 350f oven for 20-25 minutes<|endoftext|>
