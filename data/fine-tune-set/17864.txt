Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Cocoa powder
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Icing sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. For brownies: in mixing bowl or food processor blend well, butter, sugar, cocoa, eggs and vanilla
2. Combine flour, baking powder and salt
3. Add to cocoa mix
4. Bake in greased 8x8 square pan for 20-25 min at 350
5. Make them gooey-moist by taking them out just after the middle has puffed up or risen leaving them in longer will give you a cake-like brownie
6. Meanwhile make icing
7. Allow to cool before icing or you can dust with icing sugar
8. They are even good naked
9. For chocolate butter icing: cream butter with cocoa and 2 cups of icing sugar
10. Beat in 2 tsp vanilla, 2 tbsp milk and 1 cup of icing sugar
11. If needed, gradually add 1-2 tbsp milk until desired spreading consistency
12. Makes enough icing for 2 batches of brownies
13. Will keep in the fridge for a week<|endoftext|>
