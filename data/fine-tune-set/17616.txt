Ingredients:
• All-bran cereal
• Flour
• Shortening
• Sugar
• Buttermilk
• Baking soda
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients together in a large bowl
2. Bake in muffin tins on 400f for 10 to 12 minutes
3. Store in air tight containers and refrigerate for up to 6 weeks<|endoftext|>
