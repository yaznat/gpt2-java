Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>20 Minute Chicken Rice With Broccoli K

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Minute white rice
• Broccoli florets

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large non-stick skillet on medium-high heat
2. Add chicken, cover and cook 4 minutes on each side or until cooked through
3. Remove chicken from skillet
4. Add soup and 1 soup can water to skillet
5. Stir
6. Bring to boil and stir in rice and broccoli
7. Top with chicken
8. Cover and cook on low heat 5 minutes
9. Season to taste<|endoftext|>
