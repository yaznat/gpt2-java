What's a recipe for a wrap?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Corn

Ingredients:
• Ears of corn
• Bacon
• Chili powder

Steps:
1. Wrap each ear with a bacon strip
2. Place each ear on a piece of heavy duty aluminum foil
3. Sprinkle with chili powder
4. Wrap securely, twisting ends to make handles for turning
5. Grill uncovered, over medium-hot heat for 20 minutes or until corn is tender and bacon is cooked, turning once<|endoftext|>
