Give me a stir fry recipe.<|endoftext|>Asparagus Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Vegetable oil
• Asparagus
• Gingerroot
• Garlic
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, add the broth, cornstarch, and soy sauce
2. Stir to combine
3. In a large skillet, add the oil
4. Heat over high heat
5. Add the asparagus, gingerroot, garlic, and crushed red pepper flakes
6. Stir-fry for a few minutes or until almost crisp-tender
7. Add in the broth mixture
8. Bring to a boil, stirring frequently
9. Stir and continue cooking until sauce is thickened
10. Serve hot over hot cooked rice<|endoftext|>
