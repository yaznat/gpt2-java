Ingredients:
• Cinnamon graham crackers
• Fat free cream cheese
• Cool whip free
• Sugar-free instant banana pudding mix
• Vanilla
• Skim milk
• Banana
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Crush graham crackers in a baggie with rolling pin until course crumbs
2. Set aside
3. Using a whisk, whisk cream cheese in a large bowl until smooth
4. Add pudding mix, milk and vanilla
5. Whisking until smooth
6. Fold in 2 ounces of whipped topping
7. Reserve 2 ounces cool whip to top parfaits
8. To make two parfaits
9. The layering really doesn't matter - do whatever you think looks the best! here's how i do the layers for my dh and i: layer each parfait with graham cracker crumbs, 1 / 4 banana, 1 / 4 pudding mixture, 1 / 2 ounce whipped topping, 1 / 4 banana, 1 / 4 pudding mixture, 1 / 2 ounce whipped topping and lastly remaining graham cracker crumbs
10. Sprinkle with cinnamon
11. Can be eaten immediately or set in the fridge for 15-20 minutes to allow crumbs to soften
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
