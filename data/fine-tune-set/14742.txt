Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Rice
• Fat free chicken broth
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Frozen peas
• Mexican-style tomatoes
• Carrot
• Garlic salt
• Cumin

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large skillet and saut rice until golden
2. Add 1 / 2 cup broth, onion and bell pepper and saut for 2 more minutes
3. Stir in remaining broth, peas, tomatoes, carrot and seasonings
4. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover and simmer for 20 minutes or until broth is absorbed<|endoftext|>
