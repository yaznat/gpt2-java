Ingredients:
• Hungry jack cheesy scalloped potatoes
• Margarine
• Boiling water
• Milk
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Ketchup
• Pepper
• Tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400f in ungreased 8-inch square glass baking dish, combine potato slices, contents of sauce packet from potatoes, margarine and boiling water
2. Mix well
3. Stir in milk
4. Bake at 400f for 20 minutes
5. Meanwhile, cook ground beef and onion in large skillet over medium-high heat until beef is thoroughly cooked and onion is tender, stirring frequently
6. Drain if necessary
7. Stir in ketchup and pepper
8. Keep warm
9. Remove baking dish from oven
10. Stir potatoes gently
11. Spoon meat mixture over potatoes
12. Top with tomato slices
13. Sprinkle with cheese
14. Return to oven
15. Bake an additional 15 to 20 minutes or until potatoes are tender and cheese is melted
16. Let stand 5 minutes before serving to thicken sauce<|endoftext|>
