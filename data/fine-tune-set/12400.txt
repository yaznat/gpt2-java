Ingredients:
• Salsa
• Black beans
• Vegetable broth
• Fresh cilantro
• Lime juice

Steps:
1. Pour salsa into a large sauce pan and heat on medium heat
2. Rinse beans in a collander, then add beans and broth to salsa
3. Bring to a boil and let simmer for 15 minutes
4. In the meantime, mince cilantro
5. Use a knife or a food processor
6. Set cilantro aside
7. After about 15 minutes of simmering, remove about 1 / 2 of the soup from the pan and put it in a food processor
8. Puree the soup and then put the pureed soup back into the pan
9. Continue to simmer
10. Add minced cilantro and lime juice to soup
11. Cook soup for a few more minutes
12. The longer you cook it, the more it will reduce and become thicker
13. Eat as is or add cheese / sour cream on top as a garnish if you desire<|endoftext|>
