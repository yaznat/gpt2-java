Give me a recipe for steak.<|endoftext|>Big Daddy S Cheesesteak Soup

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Onions
• Green bell pepper
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• All-purpose flour
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Beef tenderloin
• Water
• Beef bouillon cube
• American cheese
• Bread bowl
• Chives
• Fresh parsley leaves

Steps:
1. In a large stockpot or dutch oven, heat oil
2. Add onions and saute for 2 to 3 minutes on high until fragrant and tender
3. In a large bowl, mix salt, pepper, flour, onion powder, and garlic powder together
4. Add meat to seasoned flour
5. Dredge to coat and add flour and meat to pot with onions and green bell pepper
6. Saute on medium high heat until meat is lightly browned, stirring frequently for about 8 to 10 minutes
7. Add water and beef bouillon to pot and continue to cook and stir on high until soup begins to thicken
8. Bring to a boil
9. Turn heat off and add cheese
10. Cover the pot with a lid and allow the cheese to sit and melt
11. Pour into bread bowls and garnish with chives and parsley
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
