Ingredients:
• Yam
• Olive oil
• Aluminum foil
• Butter
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Select yams that are about the same size
2. Wash thoroughly
3. Using a sharp knife, cut out a nickel-size wedge from the side of each yam
4. This keeps the yams from exploding during cooking
5. Coat yams with olive oil
6. Wrap each one securely in foil
7. Repeat foil process 3 times, rotating yam each time
8. Continue foiling each yam until all are finished
9. Place yams in slow cooker
10. Add 1 / 4 cup water to bottom
11. Turn cooker to low
12. The yams will be ready to go in about 4-5 hours, but you can keep them on low all day if you need too
13. When ready to serve, upwrap yams, add butter and brown sugar and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
