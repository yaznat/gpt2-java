Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Best Overnight Waffles

Ingredients:
• Yeast
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Milk
• Butter
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Combine first 8 ingredients
2. Cover and let rest overnight in the fridge
3. In the morning, add eggs
4. Bake according to your waffle iron directions
5. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
