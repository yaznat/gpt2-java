Please give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Fried Tofu

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Tamari
• Red star nutritional yeast
• Seasoning
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Lightly grease a non-stick pan with oil
2. Put tamari in a bowl
3. In another bowl mix yeast and spices
4. Slice tofu into 1 / 4-inch slices
5. Dip tofu in the tamari and then in the yeast mixture
6. Fry until golden
7. Flip and brown the other side
8. Add a bit of oil if necessary<|endoftext|>
