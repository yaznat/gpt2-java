Ingredients:
• Real maple syrup
• Spicy brown mustard
• Soy sauce
• Apple cider vinegar
• Salt and pepper
• Pork tenderloin
• Bacon
• Mustard

Steps:
1. Sauce:
2. Combine ingredients
3. Simmer till thickened approximately 15-20 minutes
4. Pork tenderloin
5. Coat pork with mustard
6. Wrap with bacon
7. Roast at 350 degrees till internal temp of 170 degrees
8. Approximately 1-1 1 / 2 hours<|endoftext|>
