Ingredients:
• Cooking apples
• Butter
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Ground cloves
• Brown sugar
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Walnut pieces
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Eggs
• Buttermilk

Steps:
1. Syrup:
2. Peel, core, and thinly slice the apples, set aside
3. In a large saucepan, melt the butter over low heat
4. Stir in the spices and brown sugar all at once
5. Mix the cornstarch into the 1 cup of water until dissolved and immediately add the water mix, stir until the sugar is dissolved
6. Add the apples and cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until the apples are nearly tender and the syrup is bubbly
7. While the apples are cooking, make the pancakes
8. Serve
9. Pancakes:
10. Mix the dry ingredients together in a large bowl
11. Mix the egg, butter, and buttermilk together in a small bowl
12. Make a depression in the dry ingredients and add the liquid mixture
13. Stir until moistened, some lumps will remain
14. Add more water to get the right consistency, if required
15. Do not over mix
16. Pour about 1 / 3 cup of batter onto a hot, lightly greased griddle
17. Cook for about two minutes on medium heat or until the edges are set and the bubbles remain open
18. Turn over and cook on the other side<|endoftext|>
