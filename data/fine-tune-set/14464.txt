What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Arizona Beans

Ingredients:
• Red kidney beans
• Lima beans
• Pork and beans
• French style green beans
• Green pepper
• Chili sauce
• Turkey bacon
• Splenda brown sugar blend
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Cut up the bacon and cook it
2. Stir-fry green pepper and onions in bacon drippings
3. Combine all ingredients and cook for 10 minutes at 350, then lower to 250
4. Cook for one hour
5. Serve hot or cold<|endoftext|>
