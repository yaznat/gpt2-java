Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Brownie Macaroons

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Coconut
• Water
• Oil
• Egg

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. Lightly grease cookie sheets or line with parchment paper
3. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients
4. Beat 50 strokes with spoon
5. Shape dough into 1 1 / 2 inch balls
6. Place 3 inches apart on cookie sheet
7. Flatten slightly
8. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until edges are set
9. Remove from cookie sheets<|endoftext|>
