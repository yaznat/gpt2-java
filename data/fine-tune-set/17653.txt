Can you give me a muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Amy S Extra Special Banana Muffins

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Milk
• Flour
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees
2. Cream the butter, eggs, and sugar together
3. Add the milk and bananas, mixing throughly
4. Add the flour, one cup at a time, until a batter forms
5. Fill muffin tins liked with paper about 2 / 3-3 / 4 way full
6. Bake until golden brown
7. Bake time is approximate and will vary depending on your oven<|endoftext|>
