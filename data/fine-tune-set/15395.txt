What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Avocado Bacon Burger

Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Seasoning salt
• Hamburger bun
• Cheese
• Fat free sour cream
• Avocado
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Make burger patty and generously season with seasoned salt
2. Grill
3. I grill 8 minutes per side on low to medium-low heat
4. Place sour cream on insides of bun
5. During last 6 minutes of grilling add cheese to meat, and place bun off heat on grill
6. To serve, top with avocado and bacon<|endoftext|>
