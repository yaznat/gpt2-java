Ingredients:
• Chicken bouillon powder
• Soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Oyster sauce
• Chili sauce
• Sesame oil
• Hot sauce
• Chicken broth
• Eggs
• Onion
• Green onions
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Cooked rice
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Combine all your sauces, broth, and seasoning in a small bowl
2. Heat oil in your wok / pan at medium heat
3. Cook your vegetables and poultry to your liking
4. I prefer it 7 mins for cooking them
5. Add salt and pepper to taste
6. Chop your onions and garlic
7. Place them in the wok / pan
8. Cook for 3 more minutes place your mixture into a large bowl
9. Crack your eggs into the wok / pan
10. Stir to scramble and mix the whites with the yolks
11. This might take 3-5 minutes remove from heat and place into that large bowl with the rest of your mixture
12. Put a little bit more oil into your wok / pan and add your rice
13. Stir occasionally until the rice can seperate from each other
14. May take a while --
15. Add in your sauce over the rice and cook for about 5 minutes
16. Stir in your mixture and cook for another 10 minutes the rice will soak in your broth
17. Place onto plates or bowls and garnish with some scallions, cilantro, and parsley
18. Serve hot and enjoy! (:<|endoftext|>
