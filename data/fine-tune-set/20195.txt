What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Bok Choy Broccoli Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Gingerroot
• Garlic
• Salt
• Red pepper flakes
• Fresh broccoli florets
• Bok choy
• Lemon juice
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat
2. Add onion, ginger, garlic, salt, and red pepper flakes
3. Stir-fry for 2 minutes
4. Add in broccoli and bok choy
5. Stir-fry for 1-2 minutes
6. Add lemon juice and sugar
7. Stir-fry 3 minutes or until crisp-tender<|endoftext|>
