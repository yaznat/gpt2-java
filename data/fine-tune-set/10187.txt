What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Beef Heart Chili

Ingredients:
• Beef heart
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Chopped tomato
• Coconut oil
• Tabasco sauce
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Paprika
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Cut heart into cubes heat oil, cook heart until lightly brown
2. Remove set aside
3. Add oil cook peppers and onions 2minutes add garlic cook few minutes
4. Add all beef cook through
5. Add tomatoes stir everything
6. Add beef heart
7. Add tabasco / spices
8. Stir and simmer a few hours
9. Stir once in awhile and keep tast testing<|endoftext|>
