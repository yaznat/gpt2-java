Ingredients:
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Fresh oregano
• Garlic clove
• Olive oil
• Top round steaks
• Onions
• Apple cider vinegar

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine salt, pepper, oregano, garlic and one tablespoon of the olive oil
2. Mix together to form paste and rub the paste on both sides of the steak
3. Heat a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat
4. Add one tablespoon of olive oil
5. Add the steaks and cook until browned, about 2 minutes per side
6. Remove to a plate and set aside
7. Add the last tablespoon of olive oil to same skillet and reduce heat to medium
8. Add the sliced onion, stirring frequently to scrape up any browned bits
9. Cook until onions are soft, about 10 minutes
10. Stir in apple cider vinegar
11. Slice steak into thin strips and return meat and any juices back to skillet with onions
12. Stir to heat through
13. Serve!<|endoftext|>
