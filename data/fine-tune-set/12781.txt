What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Swiss Steak

Ingredients:
• Top round steaks
• All-purpose flour
• Vegetable oil
• Celery
• Carrots
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Whole tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Ketchup
• Worcestershire sauce
• Prepared horseradish
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Dredge steak in flour
2. Brown steak in vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium heat
3. Drain well
4. Place steak in a deep 4-quart baking dish
5. Top with chopped celery, carrot, onion, and green pepper
6. Combine tomatoes, tomato sauce, ketchup, worcestershire sauce, horseradish, salt, and pepper
7. Stir well, and pour over vegetables
8. Cover and bake at 325 degrees for 2 to 2 1 / 2 hours or until the meat is tender<|endoftext|>
