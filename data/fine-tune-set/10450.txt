Ingredients:
• Oats
• Wheat flour
• Natural-style peanut butter
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Honey

Steps:
1. Blend dry ingredients up in a food processor
2. Mix together peanut butter, applesauce, and honey or agave nectar in a bowl
3. Once the wet ingredients are mixed well, add the dry ingredients and mix together
4. I usually finish by mixing with my hands
5. Form small balls and set them on a cookie sheet after spraying it with a nonstick spray
6. Use a fork to press down on the ball to flatten the cookies
7. Bake in an oven for 10 minutes only at 350 degrees
8. Remember that peanut butter cookie's burn easily so you need to take them out right away even if they don't look done, because by the time they are brown on top they are usually burnt on the bottom<|endoftext|>
