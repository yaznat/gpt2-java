Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Vegetarian refried beans
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Minced garlic clove
• Flour tortillas
• Scallions
• Tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese
• Salsa
• Low-fat sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. Combine black and refried beans in a saucepan, and add the chili powder, cumin and garlic
3. Simmer for 5 minutes
4. Mash the bean mixture into a thick puree
5. On a plate, lay a tortilla flat and place 2 tablespoons of the bean mixture in the center of the tortilla
6. Top with some scallions, tomatoes and cheese
7. Roll up into a tube shape and place into a 9x13-inch baking pan, seam side down
8. Repeat until you've used all the tortillas and / or bean mixture
9. Bake the burritos until they are browned, about 15 minutes
10. Top with salsa and / or sour cream as desired<|endoftext|>
