What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Best All American Chili

Ingredients:
• Chili beans
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Dried tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Water
• Black pepper
• Salt
• Chili powder
• Oregano
• Sugar
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, cook the beef, onion, and green pepper until the meat is browned
2. Drain the excess grease
3. Stir in all remaining ingredients, except the cheese
4. Bring to a boil
5. Cover, reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes
6. Serve hot, topped with cheese, if desired<|endoftext|>
