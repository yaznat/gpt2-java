What's a good stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Winter Vegetables

Ingredients:
• Beef
• Dry red wine
• Garlic
• Dried thyme
• Ground cinnamon
• Black pepper
• Beef broth
• Onions
• Diced tomatoes
• Butternut squash
• Baby spinach

Steps:
1. In a resealable bag, combine the beef, wine, garlic, thyme, cinnamon and pepper
2. Seal and marinate in the refrigerator over night
3. The next day, preheat the oven to 325 degrees
4. Pleace the meat mixture, broth, onions and tomatoes, with juice, in a dutch oven
5. Place over high and bring to boil
6. Cover, and place in oven
7. Bake 1 hour
8. Stir in squash, cover and bake for 1 1 / 2 hours
9. Remove from oven, and stir in the spinach until it is wilted
10. Serve<|endoftext|>
