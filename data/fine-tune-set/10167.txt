Ingredients:
• Chorizo sausage
• Stewing beef
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Dried oregano leaves
• Cumin seed
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Jalapeno peppers
• Habanero pepper
• Tomatoes
• Condensed beef broth
• Worcestershire sauce
• Red kidney beans
• Monterey jack cheese
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Preheat crockpot
2. In a large skillet cook sausage over med-hi until crumbled and no longer pink
3. Transfer to slow cooker
4. Drain all but 1 t
5. Fat from pan
6. Add beef to pan and brown in batches
7. Transfer to slow cooker
8. Reduce heat to medium and add onions
9. Cook until soft
10. Add garlic through habanero pepper to pan
11. Cook and stir for 1 minute
12. Stir in tomatoes, reserved juice, beef broth and worcestershire sauce
13. Bring to a boil
14. Pour mixture over meat in crockpot and stir well
15. Cover and cook on low for 8-10 hours or hi for 4-5 hours
16. Add cheese and stir until it melts or garnish with shredded cheese
17. Garnish with chopped green onions
