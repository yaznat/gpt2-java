Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Amy P S Black Bottom Brownies

Ingredients:
• Chocolate chips
• Cream cheese
• Egg
• Salt
• Sugar
• Flour
• Cocoa
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla
• Baking soda
• Water
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. Combine cheese / chip mix except for the chips
2. Beat well
3. Then stir in chocolate chips
4. Set mix aside
5. Mix dough mix
6. Pour batter into a greased 9x13 inch pan
7. Drop teaspoonfuls of cream cheese mix randomly over the batter
8. Bake 15 to 20 minutes at 350f
9. Cool and refrigerate<|endoftext|>
