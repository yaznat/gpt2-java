What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Alaska Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Worcestershire sauce
• Cumin
• Cracked black pepper
• Smoked cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Mix beef, onion, worcestershire, cumin and pepper in a bowl
2. Divide into 4 portions
3. Place a slice of cheese in the middle of each portion and form into patties
4. Heat a frying pan to medium
5. Cook the burgers 5-6 minutes on each side
6. Served topped with tomato slices and lettuce<|endoftext|>
