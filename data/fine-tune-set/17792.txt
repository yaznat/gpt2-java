Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground cloves
• Ground cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Canned pumpkin
• Canola oil
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Walnuts
• Raisins
• Cooking spray
• Confectioners' sugar
• Orange peel
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f spray 18 muffin tin openings with non stick cooking spray
2. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, cloves, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt in medium bowl
3. Set aside
4. In bowl of an electric mixer, combine pumpkin, oil, sugar, and eggs, then mix until well blended
5. Add set-aside dry ingredients and mix well
6. By hand, stir in walnuts and raisins
7. Pour batter evenly into prepared muffin tin openings
8. Bake 20-23 minutes
9. Cool in pan 15 minutes
10. Remove from pan and cool completely
11. For orange glaze:
12. Combine confectioner's sugar, orange peel, and orange juice in small bowl
13. Stir with spoon to blend
14. Spoon glaze over top of cooled muffins, letting excess glaze run down sides
15. Sprinkle with additional nuts before glaze hardens<|endoftext|>
