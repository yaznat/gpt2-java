Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Garlic cloves
• Yellow onion
• Green pepper
• Italian seasoning
• Lean ground beef
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Chicken broth
• Soy sauce
• Whole wheat elbow macaroni
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Or just use vegetable oil to coat pan
2. Add garlic, onion, , and italian seasoning and cook until onion just begins to soften, about 5 minutes
3. Increase heat to high
4. Add beef and saute, breaking up with a wooden spoon, until lightly browned, about 5 minutes
5. Mix in diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, chicken broth, soy sauce and macaroni
6. Bring to a simmer, reduce heat to low, cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until pasta is tender, about 14 minutes
7. Season to taste with salt and pepper and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
