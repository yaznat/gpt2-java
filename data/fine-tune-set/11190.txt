Ingredients:
• Carrot
• Daikon radish
• Cider vinegar
• Sugar
• Kosher salt
• Chili-garlic sauce
• Pork tenderloin
• Cooking spray
• Salt
• Fat-free mayonnaise
• French baguettes
• Cucumber
• Fresh cilantro stems
• Green onions
• Jalapeno pepper

Steps:
1. Combine the first five ingredients
2. Cover and let stand for 15 mins to and hour
3. Then drain
4. Preheat oven to 400f
5. Combine the chili garlic sauce and 1 1 / 2 teaspoons of sugar
6. Stir well
7. Place pork on the rack of a small roasting pan or broiling pan coated with cooking spray
8. Spread 2 tablespoons of the chili garlic mixture evenly over pork
9. Sprinkle the pork with 1 / 2 teaspoon salt
10. Bake at 400f for 20 minutes or until a thermometer registers at 155f cool
11. Cover, and refrigerate
12. Combine mayo and the remaining chili garlic mixture
13. Cover and refrigerate
14. Cut each baguette horizontally
15. Cutting into but not completely through
16. Spread mayo garlic mixture evenly on cut sides
17. Thinly slice the pork
18. Divide evenly between baguettes
19. Top evenly with carrot mixture, arrange 8 cucumber slices and 8 cilantro sprigs on each baguette
20. Top evenly with the green onions and jalapeno
21. Press gently to close
22. Cut each baguette in to four servings<|endoftext|>
