Give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple And Cheddar Cheese Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Egg
• Buttermilk
• Oil
• Tart apple
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Grease muffin pans
3. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon and cheeses in large mixing bowl
4. Beat egg, buttermilk, oil and apple together thoroughly
5. Add liquid ingredients all at once to dry ingredients
6. Stir just until moistened
7. Fill greased muffin cups 3 / 4 full
8. Sprinkle tops with 1 / 4 cup cheddar cheese if desired
9. Bake at 400f for 18-23 minutes or until top springs back when lightly touched<|endoftext|>
