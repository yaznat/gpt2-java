What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Avocado And Ham Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• French baguettes
• Mayonnaise
• Dijon mustard
• Coarse black pepper
• Sea salt
• Ham
• Hass avocadoes

Steps:
1. Split each baguette horizontally along one side, leaving the other side intact
2. Open the pieces so they lie flat
3. Stir the mayonnaise or butter, mustard and pepper together until smooth, and spread on both sides of the bread
4. Layer with the ham and avocado
5. Sprinkle with the sea salt
6. Keep chilled and serve within 3 hours<|endoftext|>
