Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Flour
• Nutmeg
• Half-and-half
• Eggs
• Lemon juice
• Lemon, rind of
• Margarine
• 9" unbaked pie shell
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, thoroughly blend sugar, flour and nutmeg
2. Stir in half and half, add eggs, mix well
3. Add lemon juice, peel and margarine
4. Beat well to blend
5. Pour into pie shell, bake in preheated 375 oven 45 minutes or until center is almost firm
6. Cool
7. Serve with whipped cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
