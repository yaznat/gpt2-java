Ingredients:
• Beef
• Onion
• Carrots
• Celery
• Dried bay leaves
• Peppercorns
• Water

Steps:
1. In a stockpot, cover the bones with cold water, bring to a simmer
2. Drain off the water and rinse the bones until the water runs clear
3. After rinsing, add 3 quarts of water to the bones, then the onion, carrots, celery, bay leaves, and peppercorns
4. Simmer for at least 7 hours, never allowing the liquid to boil this may be done in a crockpot, if you like
5. Strain through a medium mesh
6. Discard the solids, cool to room temperature, then refrigerate until cold<|endoftext|>
