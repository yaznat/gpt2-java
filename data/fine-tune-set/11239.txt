Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Simply potatoes diced potatoes with onion
• Green onions
• Bacon
• Salt and pepper
• Sour cream
• Sun-dried tomato
• Taco seasoning
• Chicken

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large skillet over medium heat
2. Mix together the potatoes, white part of the scallions, and bacon
3. Season with salt and pepper and add to the hot oil
4. Cook until potatoes are golden brown
5. Remove from skillet with a slotted spoon and place in a large bowl
6. Mix the sour cream, half of the chopped tomatoes and the taco seasoning together
7. Add most of it to the cooked potato mixture reserving only 2 tablespoons for a dipping sauce- place that in a small bowl
8. Garnish dipping sauce with a few reserved scallion greens
9. Lay the slices of chicken vertically out on a cutting board
10. Take a spoonful of potato mixture and place near the bottom third of a slice of chicken
11. Roll up like a sushi / jellyroll and squeeze together
12. Continue until all are done
13. Go back to the rolls and slice each in half
14. Secure with a toothpick and top each with a sundried tomato garnish
15. To serve: place on a sushi dish and add reserved dipping taco sour cream
16. Serves 4<|endoftext|>
