What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>2 Minute Microwave Brownie In A Mug

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Water
• Flour
• Salt
• Sugar
• Cocoa powder
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Add 2 tablespoons of butter or oil
2. Melt butter
3. Add 2 tablespoons of water
4. Add a pinch of salt
5. Add cocoa or chocolate powder
6. Add 1 tablespoon of sugar for chocolate powder or 2 tablespoons of sugar for cocoa powder
7. Add 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
8. Stir it up
9. Microwave it for 2 minutes
10. Enjoy! : d<|endoftext|>
