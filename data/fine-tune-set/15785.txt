Please give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Bean Burritos Enchilada Style

Ingredients:
• Refried beans
• Old el paso mild enchilada sauce
• Flour tortilla
• Four-cheese mexican blend cheese
• Onion

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350
2. Take a flour tortilla and put beans down the center with some cheese on it roll it up put on a plate and pour enchilada sauce on top then top with some more cheese and place in the oven until bubbly
3. I usually do one burrito per plate take it out of the oven and top with onions<|endoftext|>
