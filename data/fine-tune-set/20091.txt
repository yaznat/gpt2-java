Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Eggs
• Baking powder
• Salt and pepper
• Oil

Steps:
1. Cut the potatoes into chips and fry
2. Add the baking powder and seasoning to the beaten eggs and soak the chips in it for a few minutes
3. Add a little oil in a pan, and on a low heat, add the egg and chips
4. Cook for a few minutes and then using a plate, turn and cook other side<|endoftext|>
