Ingredients:
• Boneless lamb shoulder
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• All-purpose flour
• Olive oil
• Carrots
• Barley
• Chicken broth
• Oregano

Steps:
1. In a medium mixing bowl, toss the lamb with the salt, pepper and flour
2. Heat 1 / 2 tablespoon olive oil in a 4 to 5-quart dutch oven set over medium-high heat until it shimmers
3. Add half of the lamb, and brown on all sides, approximately 7 to 8 minutes total
4. Remove the lamb to a bowl and repeat with the remaining oil and lamb
5. Remove the second batch of lamb to the bowl and add the carrots to the pot
6. Saute the carrots for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Return all of the lamb to the pot along with the grits and stir to combine
8. Add the chicken broth and bring to a boil
9. Once boiling, decrease the heat to low, cover and simmer for 30 to 45 minutes or until the liquid is absorbed and the lamb and grits are tender
10. Five minutes before the stew is finished, add the oregano
11. Taste for seasoning and add salt and pepper as desired
12. Serve in bowls<|endoftext|>
