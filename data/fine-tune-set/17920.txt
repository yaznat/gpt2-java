What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Banana Cream Brownie Squares

Ingredients:
• Peanuts
• Brownie mix
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Water
• Bananas
• Milk
• Vanilla instant pudding mix
• Cool whip
• Strawberries
• Semisweet chocolate

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Prepare brownie mix according to package directions
3. Stir in 1 / 2 cup of peanuts
4. Pour into 9" square baking pan
5. Bake according to package directions
6. Cool completely
7. Slice 2 of the bananas
8. Place in single layer over brownie
9. Whisk pudding mix into milk just until it begins to thicken
10. Gently fold in 2 1 / 2 cups of the cool whip
11. Quickly spread mixture over the bananas
12. Refrigerate 30 minutes
13. Sprinkle remaining peanuts over pudding mixture
14. Top with remaining cool whip
15. Grate chocolate evenly over dessert
16. Slice remaining banana and strawberries
17. Slice into 9 squares
18. Garnish each square with banana and strawberry slices<|endoftext|>
