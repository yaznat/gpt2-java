Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Sauced Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Garlic clove
• Meat tenderizer
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Water
• Applesauce
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Brown chops
2. Drain off fat and place in casserole
3. Mix minced garic clove, meat tenderizer, flour and salt and pepper
4. Pour boiling water in pan and add flour mixture
5. Mix well and add applesauce and lemon juice
6. Pour over chops
7. Cover and bake one hour at 375 degrees<|endoftext|>
