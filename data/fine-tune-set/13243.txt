What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>30 Minute Almond Chicken

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• Italian dressing
• Chicken broth
• Green beans
• Minute white rice
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Toss chicken with dressing in plastic bag or container with lid
2. Add chicken with dressing to large nonstick skillet on medium-high heat and cook 4 minutes on each side
3. Stir in broth and bring to a boil
4. Cover and simmer for 10 minutes
5. Add beans and cook 5 minutes or until chicken is cooked through
6. Remove chicken from skillet
7. Add rice and almonds and remove from heat, cover and let stand for 5 minutes
8. Seat chicken on green beans over the almond-rice<|endoftext|>
