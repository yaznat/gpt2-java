Generate a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Quick Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Frozen peas and carrot
• Cooked white rice
• Soy sauce
• Oyster sauce
• Sesame oil
• Egg
• Cooked chicken

Steps:
1. Place sesame oil in a wok and heat
2. Add rice, veggies, soy sauce and oyster sauce
3. Saut ingredients in oil making sure to thoroughly mix rice and sauce together
4. After 5 minutes of sauting on medium heat move rice so that the middle of the wok is empty
5. Add egg into the wok and scramble until cooked
6. Finally mix egg into rice and serve<|endoftext|>
