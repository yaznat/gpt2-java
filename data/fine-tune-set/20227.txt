Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Garlic clove
• Chicken breast halves
• Hot pepper sauce
• Sugar
• White wine vinegar
• Dry sherry
• Chicken broth
• Cooking oil
• Roasted cashews
• Green bell peppers
• Onion
• Asparagus spears
• Fresh ginger
• Ground ginger
• Cooked long-grain rice

Steps:
1. In a bowl, stir together the first three ingredients and add chicken
2. Coat well and set aside
3. In another small bowl, mix the cooking sauce ingredients and set aside
4. Place a wok over medium-high heat
5. When wok is hot, add 2 tablespoons of the oil
6. When oil is hot, add the cashews and stir until browned
7. Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside
8. Add the chicken mixture and stir-fry until meat is no longer pink in the center
9. Remove from wok and set aside
10. Pour the remaining tablespoon oil into the wok
11. When the oil is hot, add the bell peppers, onion, asparagus and ginger
12. Stir-fry until the pepper is crisp-tender to the bite
13. Return the chicken to the wok
14. Stir cooking sauce to blend and add to wok
15. Stir until bubbly and sauce has thickened
16. Stir in the cashews and immediately serve over the rice<|endoftext|>
