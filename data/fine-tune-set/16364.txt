Ingredients:
• Venison tenderloin
• Bacon
• Yellow onion
• Portabella mushrooms
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Ground pepper
• Salt
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
2. Combine t olive oil, garlic, pepper, and salt
3. Rub seasonings onto the tenderloin
4. Wrap the tenderloin in bacon and place into the roaster
5. Add the remaining oil to a frying pan and cook the onions and mushrooms until theyre tender
6. Put onions and mushrooms into the roaster with the tenderloin and add the water
7. Bake for 30-40 minutes<|endoftext|>
