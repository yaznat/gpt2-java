Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Dried chili pepper flakes
• Onions
• Garlic
• Green bell pepper
• Dried italian seasoning
• Seasoning salt
• Pepper
• Parmesan cheese
• Flour
• Pasta sauce
• Sliced mushrooms
• Mozzarella cheese
• Prepared pizza crust
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Set oven to 425 degrees
2. Grease a 13 x 9-inch casserole dish
3. In a large skillet cook the ground beef with onions, garlic, green bell pepper and dried chili flakes until the beef is no longer pink drain and return to skillet
4. Add in the 1 / 4 cup grated parmesan cheese
5. Add in 1 / 4 cup flour
6. Stir until well blended
7. Add in pasta sauce and mushrooms
8. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly
9. Simmer for about 10 minutes, season with salt and pepper to taste
10. Spoon the ground beef mixture into the baking dish, then sprinkle with about 2 cups mozza cheese
11. Unroll the pizza crust and place on top of the cheese
12. Brush the top of the crust with olive oil, then sprinkle with about 1-2 cups mozza cheese, then sprinkle with about 1 / 4 cup parmesan cheese
13. Sprinkle with about 1 teaspoon italian seasoning on top of the cheeses
14. Bake in a 425 degree oven for about 20 minutes or until browned
15. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
