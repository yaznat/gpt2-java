Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Hot water
• Milk
• Instant mashed potatoes
• Cheese
• Lean ground beef
• Egg
• Meatloaf seasoning mix

Steps:
1. Move rack to middle of oven and preheat to 350
2. Stir melted margarine, 2 cups hot water, 2 / 3 cups milk, dry potatoes and cheese in 1 1 / 2 quart casserole or ovenproof bowl until well-blended
3. Mix beef, egg and seasoning packet in medium bowl until blended
4. Press beef mixture into 12 ungreased regular-sized muffin cups, each 3 / 4 full
5. Bake potatoes and mini-meat loaves uncovered 30-35 minutes or until meat loaves are no longer pink the centers
6. Carefully remove from the oven
7. Squeeze desired amount of ketchup on top of mini meatloaves<|endoftext|>
