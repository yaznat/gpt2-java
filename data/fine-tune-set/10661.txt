Ingredients:
• Heavy cream
• Chicken bouillon cube
• Asiago cheese
• Cornstarch
• Sun-dried tomato
• Bow tie pasta
• Bacon
• Butter
• Red onion
• Garlic cloves
• Green onion
• Grilled chicken
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan over medium heat, cook 2 cups of cream until just bubbling
2. Watch carefully to ensure that it doesn't boil over
3. Stir in bouillon and asiago cheese with whisk until dissolved
4. Add cornstarch mixture and simmer until sauce is thickened, stirring constantly
5. Mix in the sun-dried tomatoes and set aside
6. Cook pasta as directed
7. Cook bacon in until evenly brown, drain and crumble
8. Melt butter in large saucepan over medium heat
9. Saute red onion until softened and translucent
10. Stir in garlic and cooked bacon and cook for 2 minutes
11. Stir un green onions, chicken and remaining 1 cup of cream
12. Cook, stirring constantly until cream is heated through
13. Add asiago cream sauce and heat through
14. Toss with pasta until evenly coated and sprinkle with chopped parsley<|endoftext|>
