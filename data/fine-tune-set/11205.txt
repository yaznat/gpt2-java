Ingredients:
• Butter
• Dijon mustard
• Fresh thyme
• Fresh parsley
• Garlic
• Pepper
• Cooked ham
• Barbecue sauce
• Coleslaw
• White bread

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine the butter, mustard, thyme, parsley, garlic and pepper
2. Set aside
3. In a large saucepan, cook ham and barbecue sauce over medium heat for 3-4 minutes or until heated through
4. Place ham and coleslaw on four bread slices
5. Top with remaining bread
6. Spread outsides of sandwiches with butter mixture
7. In a large skillet, toast sandwiches for 1-2 minutes on each side or until golden brown<|endoftext|>
