What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Apricot Curry Dressing

Ingredients:
• Light mayonnaise
• Light cream cheese
• Low-calorie apricot jam
• Curry powder
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Finely chop or crush the garlic
2. Mix all ingredients together in a shaker or mix with a whisk
3. Refrigerate until ready to use<|endoftext|>
