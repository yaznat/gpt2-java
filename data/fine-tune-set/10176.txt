Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Chorizo sausage
• Onion
• Garlic
• Black beans
• Tomatoes
• Cumin
• Tomato juice
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cook beans a day in advance
2. Brown the beef and chorizo in a heavy pot
3. Drain off any excess fat
4. Add the onion and garlic and cook until just soft
5. Add the remaining ingredients
6. Bring to a boil
7. Reduce the heat and simmer for 45 minutes
8. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
