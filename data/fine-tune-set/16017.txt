Give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Baked Shrimp Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Jalapeno peppers
• Garlic
• Shrimp
• Mayonnaise
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Combine butter and oil
2. Set aside
3. Saut onion, peppers and garlic in 4 tb butter mixture in medium saut pan on medium heat until tender
4. Add shrimp, if using raw shrimp, saut till pink
5. Remove pan from heat, add next 3 ingredients
6. Place tortillas on foil lined baking sheets
7. Brush 1 side of each tortilla with remaining butter mix
8. Turn tortilla over so buttered side is down
9. Spread with shrimp mixture evenly over half of each tortilla, and sprinkle with cheese
10. Distribute mixture evenly between all tortillas
11. Fold over in half pressing a little
12. Bake at 475 for 8-10 minutes or until golden
13. Cut in half with a pizza cutter for appetizers or serve whole for brunch
14. Serve warm with salsa and sour cream if desired<|endoftext|>
