Ingredients:
• Lean ground sirloin
• Salt and pepper
• Worcestershire sauce
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red bell pepper
• Jalapeno
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Beef stock
• Diced tomato
• Tomato sauce
• Black beans
• Cilantro
• Corn muffin mix
• Milk
• Eggs
• Oil
• Scallions
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a large pot, brown beef over med-high, seasoning with salt and pepper
2. Drain grease and add worcestershire, onion, garlic, red peppers, and jalapeno
3. Cook 5 min then add chili powder and cumin, and cook 2 more minute stir in broth and scrape up drippings
4. Stir in tomatoes, tomato sauce, and beans
5. When it comes to a bubble, reduce heat to a simmer and cook 15 minutes
6. Stir in cilantro just before serving
7. Mix together muffin mix, milk, eggs, oil and scallions
8. Batter should be thick
9. Heat a nonstick skillet or griddle over moderate heat
10. Nest a pat of butter into a folded paper towel and wipe hot pan surface with it to lightly grease
11. Pour corn cake batter onto hot pan in 3-4 inch rounds
12. Cook 2-3 min on each side, until golden
13. Transfer to a plate and repeat
14. To serve, top bowls of chili with corn cakes
15. Eat the chili and corn cake as you would a pot pie, grabbing some of the corn cake with each spoonful of chili<|endoftext|>
