Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Ground beef
• Bacon
• Condensed tomato soup
• Cheddar cheese
• Barbecue sauce
• Prepared mustard

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Meanwhile, in a large skillet, cook beef over medium heat until no longer pink
3. Drain and set aside
4. In the same skillet, cook bacon until crisp
5. Remove with a slotted spoon to paper towels
6. Discard drippings
7. Drain pasta
8. Add to the skillet
9. Stir in the soup, beef and bacon
10. Heat through
11. Sprinkle with cheese
12. Cover and cook until the cheese is melted
13. Serve with barbecue sauce and mustard if desired<|endoftext|>
