Generate a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Easy Tofu For Everyone

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Red curry paste

Steps:
1. To drain tofu: cut tofu into thirds to yield three 1 / 2 inch thick flat rectangles
2. Place rectangles side-by-side between several layers of paper-towels or dish-towels and cover with a cookie-sheet or anything else flat
3. Place weight on the top
4. Leave it for at least half an hour
5. The longer you leave it the drier it will be and the less time it will take to cook
6. Two hours is probably a little excessive
7. In a bowl, mix together soy sauce, sesame oil, and curry paste
8. Once tofu is drained, crumble it into bowl with your hands, and mix with a fork till all pieces are covered
9. Don't worry of the pieces are different sizes
10. Leave it while you preheat oven to 350 degrees
11. Spread tofu in the bottom of an ungreased 9 x 13 pyrex pan or foil-lined cookie sheet
12. Bake for 15 - 30 minutes, stirring at least once in the middle
13. When it's tosty and golden-brown all over it's done<|endoftext|>
