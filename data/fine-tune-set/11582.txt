Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• All-purpose flour
• Fast rising yeast
• Sugar
• Salt
• Water
• Butter
• Cinnamon
• Karo light corn syrup
• Brown sugar
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Mix batter ingredients in a pre-sprayed 9-1 / 2 inch deep dish pie plate
2. Combine cinnamon sugar topping ingredients in a small bowl and set aside
3. Stir together corn syrup, brown sugar and butter in a small bowl
4. Add pecans and mix well
5. Top batter evenly with cinnamon sugar topping
6. Spoon the caramel pecan topping evenly over the batter
7. Bake by placing in a cold oven
8. Set temperature to 350f bake 25 minutes, until lightly browned and firm in center
9. Cool slightly
10. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
