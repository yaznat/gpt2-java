What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>4 Layer Lemon Pie

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Flour
• Pecans
• Cream cheese
• Confectioners' sugar
• Whipped topping
• Condensed milk
• Egg yolks
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. 1st layer: combine butter, flour and pecans and press into a 13" x 9" casserole dish
2. Bake at 350 degrees for 25 minutes
3. Let cool
4. 2nd layer: combine cream cheese, sugar and 1 / 2 of large whipped topping
5. Spread over cooled crust
6. 3rd layer: combine condensed milk, egg yolks and lemon juice, spread over 2nd layer
7. 4th layer: top with remainder of whipped topping
8. Garnish with lemon slices and mint, if you like<|endoftext|>
