What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Au Gratin Hash Browns Casserole

Ingredients:
• Cream of chicken soup
• Sour cream
• Margarine
• Dried onion flakes
• Salt
• Frozen hash brown potatoes
• Cheddar cheese
• Durkee onions

Steps:
1. Oven@ 350
2. In a large bowl combine first 5 ingredients
3. Stir in potatoes and cheese
4. Transfer to a greased casserole dish
5. Sprinkle durkee french fried onions over top of casserole
6. Bake for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
