Ingredients:
• Dark corn syrup
• Evaporated milk
• Eggs
• Egg whites
• Pumpkin
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Ground nutmeg
• Pie crusts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Mix first nine ingredients together until smooth
3. Pour into 1 unbaked 9-inch pastry shell with high fluted edge
4. Bake in oven 1 hour or until knife inserted in center comes out clean
5. Cool<|endoftext|>
