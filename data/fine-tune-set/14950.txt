Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Healthy Vegetarian Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Green onion
• Shiitake mushroom
• Celery
• Frozen peas and carrot
• Ginger
• Minced garlic cloves
• Cooked white rice
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Vinegar
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Heat large skillet or wok, add oils, onions, mushrooms and celery
2. Stir fry for about 3 minutes
3. Add peas and carrots, garlic and ginger stir fry 4 more minutes
4. Add rice, soy sauce and vinegar stir fry about 8 minutes until heated through<|endoftext|>
