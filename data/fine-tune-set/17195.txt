Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Egg
• Onion
• Crackers
• Milk
• Parsley
• Oregano
• Basil
• Water
• Flour

Steps:
1. Soak crackers in milk
2. Mix together all ingredients for beef patties
3. Shape mixture into 6 oblong patties and brown slightyl on each side
4. Save the drippings
5. Place them in a baking dish --
6. Prepare a gravy by adding 3 cups water to the beef drippings
7. Bring to a boil, make a smooth paste with flour and 1 cup of water
8. Add paste to boiling drippings in a thin stream
9. Stir quickly and constantly to prevent lumps
10. Add seasonings and heat till thickened
11. Pour gravy over beef patties
12. Bake at 400 for 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
