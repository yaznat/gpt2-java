Ingredients:
• Banana
• Blueberries
• Strawberry
• Green tea
• Cranberry juice
• Ice cubes
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. In a blender, add banana, blueberries, strawberries, green tea, cranberry juice, ice cubes and canola oil
2. Blend until combined
3. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
