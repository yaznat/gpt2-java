Ingredients:
• Coconut milk
• Sweetened flaked coconut
• Eggs
• Frozen limeade concentrate
• Sugar
• Flour
• Coconut extract
• Salt
• Deep dish pie shells

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Adjust rack to lowest level for baking
3. Put all ingredients except the pie shell in a blender
4. Blend until mixed, and pour into the pie shell
5. Bake for 15 minutes
6. Reduce oven to 350 degrees and bake for 40 to 45 more minutes or until set
7. Cool on rack for 30 minutes and refrigerate until cold before cutting
8. Garnish with sweetened whipped cream, lime peel curls or fruit of your choice<|endoftext|>
