Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Onion
• Celery
• Mushroom
• Cooked shrimp
• Cooked rice
• Soy sauce
• Salt
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a skillet over low heat
2. Add onion and cook until tender but not brown, about 3 minutes
3. Add celery, mushrooms and shrimp, and cook about 5 minutes
4. Stir in rice, soy sauce and salt, and cook about 2 minutes
5. Add eggs and stir well
6. Cook, stirring, until eggs are cooked, 2-3 minutes<|endoftext|>
