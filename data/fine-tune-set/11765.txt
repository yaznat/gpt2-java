Can you give me a curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Anytime Two Step Green Pea Curry

Ingredients:
• Green peas
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Tomatoes
• Yogurt
• Salt
• Red chili powder
• Coriander powder
• Oil

Steps:
1. Prepare a puree of ingredients
2. Heat oil, add the puree and spices
3. Saute for 7 minutes on medium heat till the raw smell is gone
4. Add green peas and saute for 3 minutes
5. Add 1 / 2 cup of water and cook covered for 8 minutes
6. Serve hot with steamed rice, pasta, indian rotis or arabic bread<|endoftext|>
