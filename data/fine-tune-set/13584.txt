Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Medium egg noodles
• Mushrooms
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cream cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Milk
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. In large skillet, brown ground beef, drain
2. Add diced onions, stir on med low for 5 minutes
3. In same skillet, layer uncooked noodles over the beef and onion
4. Next layer is the frozen mushrooms
5. Mix soup, cream cheese, cheddar cheese, milk & garlic powder
6. Pour over the top of noodles and mushrooms
7. Cover and bake at 350f for 1 hour
8. Noodles are al dente<|endoftext|>
