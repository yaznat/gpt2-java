Ingredients:
• Celery root
• Olive oil
• Cajun seasoning

Steps:
1. Pre heat oven to 425
2. Clean, peel and cut celery root into fry size
3. In a large bowl mix olive oil and cajun seasoning
4. Add celery root and toss till well coated
5. Bake 30 to 40 minutes flipping ever 10 to 15 minutes to prevent burning on the bottom and promote even cooking
6. Enjoy while hot<|endoftext|>
