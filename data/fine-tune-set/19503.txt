Ingredients:
• Tiny new potatoes
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Mayonnaise
• Fresh garlic
• Fresh parsley
• Fresh basil
• Salt and pepper
• Parmesan cheese
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Halve potatoes and set aside
2. In a large bowl, combine the next seven ingredients
3. Whisk to combine well
4. Add potatoes and toss
5. Place potatoes in a roasting pan, uncovered, and roast at 350 for 35-40 minutes, stirring often, or until potatoes are tender
6. Garnish potatoes with parmesan and parsley<|endoftext|>
