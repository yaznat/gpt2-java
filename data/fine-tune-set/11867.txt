Can you give me a curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Burmese Chicken Curry

Ingredients:
• Onions
• Dried hot chili peppers
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Turmeric
• Whole chickens
• Vegetable oil
• Canned tomato
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Lemon zest

Steps:
1. Slice three of the onions and set aside
2. Cut the remaining two onions into chunks, and process with the chile peppers, ginger and garlic, until pureed
3. Mix together the salt and turmeric and rub the chicken with the mixture
4. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat, add the chicken and cook until it is browned, turning over once, about 10 minutes
5. Remove the chicken
6. Add the sliced onions and the pureed onion mixture to the pan and cook, stirring occasionally, until the sliced onion is very limp
7. Process the tomatoes with their liquid until smooth
8. Add to the skillet along with the water, soy sauce and zest
9. Return the chicken except for the breasts to the pan, cover, and simmer for 25 minutes
10. Add the breast pieces and continue to simmer, covered, for about 20 more minutes, or until the meat is no longer pink
11. Remove the chicken from the skillet to a serving dish and cover to keep warm
12. Boil the sauce, uncovered, stirring constantly, until it is reduced to about 3 cups
13. Skim off and discard the fat
14. Spoon the sauce over the chicken<|endoftext|>
