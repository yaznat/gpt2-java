Ingredients:
• Water
• Cinnamon stick
• Green cardamom pods
• Whole cloves
• Star anise
• Fresh ginger
• Black tea leaves
• Honey
• Whole milk

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan, bring the water, all of the spices and the ginger to a boil over medium heat
2. Turn off the heat and allow it to steep for 15 minutes
3. Bring the mixture back to a boil, then add the tea
4. Turn the heat off, and allow it to steep for 3 to 5 minutes depending on how strong you like your tea
5. Stir in the honey and add milk to your liking
6. Strain it into a bowl or pitcher and serve!
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
