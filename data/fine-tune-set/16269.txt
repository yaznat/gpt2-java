What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus And Avocado Wraps

Ingredients:
• Asparagus
• Avocado
• Lime juice
• Garlic clove
• Cooked long-grain brown rice
• Plain nonfat yogurt
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Cilantro leaf
• Red onions

Steps:
1. In a medium saucepan over high heat, bring 2 inches watger to a boil
2. Place the asparagus in a steamer basket, cover, and steam until just tender, 6-8 minutes
3. Remove the asparagus and immediately rinse in cold water to stop the cooking
4. Drain thoroughly
5. In a small bowl, mash the avocado, lime juice, and garlic into a coarse puree
6. In another small bowl, stir together the rice and yogurt to mix well
7. Heat a dry large frying pan over medium heat
8. One at a time, heat the tortillas in the hot pan until softened, about 20 seconds per side
9. Lay the tortillas flat on a clean work surface
10. Spread the avocado mixture equally among the tortillas
11. Top each with an equal amount of the rice mixture, asparagus, cilantro, and onion
12. Fold in both sides and the bottom of each tortilla up over the filling, then roll to close
13. If made in advance, cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for up to 1 hour
14. Return to room temperature before serving
15. To serve, cut each wrap in half crosswise<|endoftext|>
