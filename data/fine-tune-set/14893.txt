Ingredients:
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Eggs
• Sesame oil
• Nut oil
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Bean sprouts
• Water chestnuts
• Spring onions
• Frozen peas
• Ham
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Prepare your ingredients by slicing the water chestnuts, chopping the spring onions and ham
2. Then arrange your ingredients on a plate or in bowls ready
3. This makes it easier when coming to the actual stir frying section as you can just grab your ingredients
4. Beat the eggs together with the sesame oil and set aside
5. Place your wok over a high heat and add the groundnut oil
6. Heat until very hot
7. Add the cooked rice and stir fry for 3 minutes
8. Stir in the ham, peas, salt, pepper and soy sauce
9. Then stir fry for another minute or two
10. Add the beaten egg and sesame oil mix and stir through
11. Keep moving and stir fry for 1 minute
12. This should at first go kind of shiny and gloopy looking at this stage, then the eggs start to cook and distribute throughout the rice
13. Add the bean sprouts, water chestnuts and spring onions
14. Stir fry for a further 2 minutes then serve with your favorite chinese dish -- or on it's own! delicious!<|endoftext|>
