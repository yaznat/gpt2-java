Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Sour cream
• Pecans
• Cinnamon
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Cream butter and sugar together
3. Sift in dry ingredients, alternating with the eggs
4. Mix batter thouroughly with hand mixer on low speed, then add vanilla and sour cream
5. Pour half of the batter into a well-greased bundt pan, then add half of the filling and then the rest of the batter
6. Top with remaining filling
7. Bake for 65-70 minutes<|endoftext|>
