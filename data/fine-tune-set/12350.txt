Ingredients:
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Dried chili pepper flakes
• Tomatoes
• Unsalted dry roasted peanuts
• Onion
• Chicken stock
• Water
• Chicken breast
• Butter
• Flour
• Lemon wedges

Steps:
1. In food processor, blend ginger, garlic, and chili peppers until finely chopped
2. Add to processor 2 cans tomatoes, peanuts, and onion
3. Leave chunky place mixture in soup pot and add 1 can tomatoes, chicken stock, water, and chicken
4. Make a paste of butter and flour and add to pot
5. Bring to a boil
6. Let cool to 145 and serve each bowl topped with a few peanuts and juice from one lemon wedge
7. Serve with extra crushed chili peppers, and a loaf of french bread with butter<|endoftext|>
