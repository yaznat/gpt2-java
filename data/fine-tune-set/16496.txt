Ingredients:
• Almonds
• Water
• Soymilk
• Carob powder
• Bananas
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Place almonds in blender with water and leave overnight
2. In morning, blend mixture on high for 3 minutes
3. Add soy milk, carob powder and bananas
4. Blend 1 minute
5. Add ice, blend half a munute
6. Pour and enjoy!
