Ingredients:
• Coconut oil
• Mustard seeds
• Ground turmeric
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Cauliflower
• Water
• Green peas
• Sea salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Red pepper flakes
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, melt the coconut oil over medium heat
2. Add the mustard seeds and turmeric and saute until they begin to pop, about 30 seconds
3. Add the onion and saute until translucent, about 7 minutes
4. Then add the garlic and ginger, and saute until fragrant
5. Add the cauliflower and saute until lightly browned, about 5 minutes
6. Add the water, peas, salt, pepper and red pepper
7. Cover, reduce the heat to medium low and cook, stirring occasionally, until the cauliflower is fork tender, but not mushy
8. Serve in a bowl or platter, garnished with parsley<|endoftext|>
