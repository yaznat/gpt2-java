Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Butter
• Maple syrup
• Bread
• Eggs
• Salt and black pepper
• Heavy cream
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Place the bacon in a large, deep skillet, and cook over medium-high heat, turning occasionally, until evenly browned, about 10 minutes
2. Drain the bacon slices on a paper towel-lined plate
3. Crumble and set aside
4. Preheat an oven to 375 degrees f
5. Grease muffin cups
6. Combine the melted butter and maple syrup in a small bowl
7. Flatten a piece of bread with a rolling pin
8. Brush the bread with the butter mixture
9. Line the muffin cup with the flattened bread
10. Repeat with the remaining bread slices
11. Bake in a preheated oven until the bread crisps, about 3 to 5 minutes
12. Sprinkle crumbled bacon into the bottom of the toast cups
13. Crack an egg into each cup
14. Season with salt and pepper to taste
15. Top each egg with 1 tablespoon of heavy cream and 1 tablespoon cheese
16. Bake until the whites are just set, about 10 to 14 minutes or until the eggs are cooked to your taste<|endoftext|>
