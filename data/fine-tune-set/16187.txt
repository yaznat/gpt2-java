What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Grilled Feta Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Fat free cream cheese
• Reduced-fat mexican cheese blend
• Feta cheese
• Dried oregano
• Flour tortillas
• Pitted ripe olives
• Diced pimentos
• Green onion
• Nonstick cooking spray

Steps:
1. In a small mixing bowl, beat cheeses with oregano until blended
2. Spread 3 tablespoons of cheese mixture over half of each tortilla
3. Top with olives, pimentos and green onion
4. Fold tortilla over
5. Coat grill rack with nonstick cooking spray before starting grill
6. Grill quesadillas, uncovered, over medium heat for 1-2 minutes on each side or until golden brown
7. Cut each quesadilla into three wedges
8. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
