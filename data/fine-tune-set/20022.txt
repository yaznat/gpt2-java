Ingredients:
• Pine nuts
• Olive oil
• Bacon
• Onion
• New potatoes
• Spinach leaves
• Feta cheese
• Eggs
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Toast the pine nuts in a dry skillet over medium heat until fragrant, and then set aside
2. Add the olive oil and the chopped bacon to the skillet, and cook until the bacon is crispy
3. Add the onion with a pinch of salt, and cook until the onion is soft and translucent, about 5 minutes
4. Add the potato slices with another pinch of salt, and cook for 2-3 minutes
5. Add 2 tablespoon of water to the pan, cover, and cook until the potatoes are tender
6. You may need to add a few more tablespoons of water
7. When the potatoes are tender, add 2 more tablespoons of water to the pan, then add the spinach a handful at a time, adding more as it wilts down
8. When the spinach is nicely wilted, stir in the pine nuts, and remove the skillet from the heat
9. Butter a large non-stick skillet over medium heat
10. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt until frothy, and add to the skillet
11. Cook the eggs until almost set
12. Spread the filling ingredients over 1 / 2 of the eggs, and sprinkle with black pepper
13. Cook a few minutes more, and then fold the other half over
14. Slide onto a plate<|endoftext|>
