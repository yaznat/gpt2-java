Ingredients:
• Lean beef
• Olive oil
• All-purpose flour
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh thyme
• Bay leaf
• Salt & pepper
• Diced tomatoes
• Beer
• Ready-made pie crust
• Egg
• Puff pastry sheets
• Tomato sauce

Steps:
1. Season meat with salt and pepper
2. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat
3. Add the beef and saute stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes or until golden brown
4. Add the onion, garlic and thyme and cook, stirring, for about 10 minutes or until onion is caramelize and soft
5. Sprinkle flour on onions, stirring well to combine
6. When flour is absorbed, stir in the tomatoes and beer
7. Cover and bring to a simmer
8. Reduce heat to low and cook until tender, approximately 1 hour
9. Set aside for 30 minutes to cool
10. Preheat oven to 425 degrees f
11. Place a baking sheet on the middle shelf
12. Cut the pastry sheet into quarters and line 4 round 5-inch tins with the pastry, allowing the sides to overhang
13. Brush the edges with egg
14. Divide the beef mixture evenly among the pastry shells
15. Cut each puff pastry sheet in half diagonally
16. Top the pies with puff pastry and trim excess and press the edges to seal
17. Brush the pies with egg
18. Cut a small cross in the center of each pie
19. Place the pies on the baking tray and bake for 25 minutes or until golden and puffed
20. Serve with tomato sauce<|endoftext|>
