Give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Cheesy Tofu Strips Italian Style

Ingredients:
• Spaghetti sauce
• Dried breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Firm tofu
• Reduced-fat mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f
2. Coat a 9-by-13-inch baking dish with cooking spray
3. Spread 1 / 2 cup of spaghetti sauce evenly around bottom of dish
4. On a plate, combine bread crumbs and parmesan cheese
5. Set aside
6. Slice tofu in half lengthwise, then cut each half lengthwise into thirds
7. Cut each piece lengthwise again into halves to create 12 sticks
8. Coat each stick in crumb / cheese mixture, and place in baking dish
9. Cover the sticks with remaining spaghetti sauce and top with mozzarella
10. Bake 20 to 25 minutes<|endoftext|>
