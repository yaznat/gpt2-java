Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Awesome Sour Cream Muffins

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Sour cream
• Butter
• Sugar
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt

Steps:
1. Beat egg with a whisk in a large mixing bowl until light
2. Add sour cream, butter, and sugar
3. Whisk together until smooth
4. Measure dry ingredients into a bowl or on waxed paper
5. Add to wet ingredients and stir / fold until no more dry ingredients show
6. Spray pam lightly into 12 muffin cups, or use paper liners
7. Pour dough into each cup
8. Preheat oven to 400f mix crumb topping, until small crumbs form
9. Sprinkle evenly over each muffin
10. Bake at 400 f for 20-25 minutes, until light golden brown and top is puffed
11. Remove from pan, serve immediately or let cool, place in closed container to be used later<|endoftext|>
