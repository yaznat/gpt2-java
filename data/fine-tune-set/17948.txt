Ingredients:
• Butter
• Granulated sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Semisweet chocolate

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit or 175 degrees celsius
2. Melt the butter and lightly grease a pan of your liking
3. In a large bowl
4. Combine the flour, cocoa powder, salt, and baking soda
5. In another large bowl
6. Combine the sugar, melted butter, and vanilla extract
7. Once combined beat in the eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each until thoroughly blended
8. Gradually add in the flour mixture to the egg / sugar mixture
9. Toss the chocolate chips in a little flour
10. Mix the chocolate chips into the batter
11. Bake for 20-25 minutes
12. (the brownies are done if you stick a toothpick in the middle and it comes out clean<|endoftext|>
