Please give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>100 Year Old Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Egg yolks
• Butter
• Buttermilk
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, beat egg whites until stiff
2. In a large bowl, beat egg yolks until blended, add melted butter and beat together
3. Stir the buttermilk into the beaten egg yolks, than sift in the flour, sugar, salt, baking powder, and baking soda
4. Fold in egg whites
5. Cook in a pan greased with butter<|endoftext|>
