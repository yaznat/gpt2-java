What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Vegetarian Chili

Ingredients:
• Kidney beans
• Black beans
• Fire-roasted tomatoes
• Salsa
• Water

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a pot
2. Cover and simmer over medium heat for 10 minute
3. Serve with cheese, hot sauce, and / or tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
