Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Mexican blend cheese
• Chicken
• Green chilies
• Butter
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Top one half of each tortilla with cheese, chicken and green chilies
2. Fold other half over filling
3. Press down with back of pancake turner
4. Lightly brush melted butter on both sides of each filled tortilla
5. Heat 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat
6. Cook 2 filled tortillas at a time 3 to 4 minutes, turning once, until golden brown and hot
7. Cut into wedges
8. Serve with salsa<|endoftext|>
