Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Chicken drumsticks
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Ras el hanout spice mix
• Ginger
• Turmeric
• Cinnamon
• Sweet paprika
• Chicken stock
• Water
• Canned chick-peas
• Canned tomatoes
• Preserved lemon
• Fresh cilantro
• Lemon, juice of

Steps:
1. Place oil in a large pan, add the chicken & fry to seal all over
2. Add the spices & fry for a minute more
3. Add all the other ingredients except the coriander / cilantro, chickpeas & the lemon juice
4. Bring to the boil then reduce heat, cover & simmer 1 hour
5. Add the chickpeas to the pan
6. Take the chicken from the pan & remove all the meat from the bones, shred & put back into the pan
7. Simmer for a further 20 minutes
8. If the soup is the consistency you like, you can add the coriander / cilantro & lemon juice now
9. Simmer for 1 minute, re-season if desired & then serve
10. If you feel the soup needs to be a little thicker, you can either reduce it down by cooking further or thicken with plain flour<|endoftext|>
