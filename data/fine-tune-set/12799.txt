Ingredients:
• Salt
• Flank steaks
• Salt and pepper
• Butter
• Red bell peppers
• Sliced mushrooms
• Water
• Frozen corn kernels
• Scallion
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Sprinkle salt on bottom of heavy skillet
2. Heat about 1 minute
3. Add steak and cook over fairly high heat about 3 minutes per side for medium rare, 4 to 5 minutes per side for a larger steak
4. Remove steak from skillet
5. Season lightly with salt and pepper
6. Set aside
7. Off the heat, add the butter to the skillet
8. Add the peppers and mushrooms
9. Saute over medium heat for 3 or 4 minutes
10. Add the water, cover and cook for about 3 minutes, stirring a couple of times
11. Add the corn and scallions
12. Cover and cook another 3 or 4 minutes
13. Uncover and reduce until there is just enough liquid to moisten the vegetables
14. Thinly slice meat, across the grain, at a 45-degree angle
15. To serve, put cooked rice on plate or in a wide bowl
16. Add vegetables
17. Add slices of steak<|endoftext|>
