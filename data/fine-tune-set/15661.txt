Give me a recipe for a taco.<|endoftext|>California Soft Tacos

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Kidney beans
• Cheddar cheese
• Romaine lettuce
• Chopped tomato
• Avocado
• Ranch dressing

Steps:
1. Heat up the tortillas so that they are firm enough to hold all of the above ingredients
2. Throwing them in the skillet for about 6 minutes should do the trick
3. Take about a quarter cup of cheddar and place it down the center of the tortilla
4. Add about 3 oz of kidney beans
5. Add a quarter of the chopped tomato
6. Add lettuce
7. Add a quarter sliced avocado
8. Pour as much ranch dressing as desired
9. Enjoy your salad in a tortilla!<|endoftext|>
