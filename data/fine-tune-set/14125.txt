What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Ziti W Tofu Shirataki

Ingredients:
• Tofu shirataki noodles
• Ground buffalo meat
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Pasta sauce
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Italian seasoning
• 2% mozzarella cheese
• Fat-free cheddar cheese
• Fresh parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Lightly grease a 8x8 baking dish
3. Set aside
4. Tofu noodles: bring pot of water to boil, add well drained tofu noodles and return to boil
5. Boil for two minutes and leave to drain in colander until needed
6. Or just rinse and drain noodles, then microwave for one minute and place in colander to drain while preparing remaining ingredients
7. In a large saucepan over medium high heat, cook ground meat, onions, green pepper, garlic and black pepper over medium heat until meat is browned
8. Drain fat
9. Add pasta sauce, crushed red pepper & italian seasoning and cook until mixture is hot, stirring occasionally
10. Meanwhile, cut tofu noodles into manageable pieces
11. Then add noodles to the sauce mixture and mix well
12. Stir in 1 / 4 cup of the mozzarella cheese and 1 / 4 cup cheddar cheese, mixing until well combined
13. Pour mixture into the prepared baking dish
14. Top with remaining cheeses - 1 / 4 cup of the mozz, 1 / 4 cup cheddar and 1 / 4 cup parmesan
15. Cover with foil
16. Bake at 350f for 15 minutes
17. Remove foil / lid and bake for an additional 15 minutes until edges are bubbly
18. Allow to stop bubbling before serving<|endoftext|>
