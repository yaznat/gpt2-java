Give me a recipe for curry.<|endoftext|>Balti Sauce Basic Sauce For Anything Goes Curry

Ingredients:
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground coriander
• Ground turmeric
• Paprika
• Fenugreek leaves
• Mustard seeds
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Light oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Crushed tomatoes
• Water
• Sea salt
• Bay leaves
• Cardamom powder
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Measure out the 8 spices into a bowl
2. If using fresh chilis then leave those out for now
3. Warm up a large skillet or pot and bring to med-high heat
4. Gently roast the spices in the dry skillet -- watching the mixture doesn't burn, keep stirring
5. After about a minute it will become fragrant and begin to get dark - take it off the heat and pour back into the bowl - set aside
6. Put the skillet back on the heat, add in the oil and bring back to medium high heat
7. Add in the chopped onion and cook thru until translucent
8. Once the onion is cooked, add in the ginger and garlic and mix well - watching the garlic doesn't burn
9. Next add the spice mixture back in, followed by the tinned tomatoes
10. Mix in about 1 cup of water - you want a thick sauce, but not too paste-like
11. Add in the salt, bay leaves and cardamom seeds
12. Grate the zest off of the lemon, add to the pot, along with the juice from 1 / 2 of the lemon
13. Save the other half for later, you may want to add more
14. Let it all simmer on medium-low
15. For a nice smooth sauce, remove the bay leaves and blend until smooth<|endoftext|>
