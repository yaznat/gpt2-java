Can you give me a salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Almost Empty Dijon Mustard Jar Vinaigrette Salad Dressing

Ingredients:
• Dijon mustard
• Red wine vinegar
• Sugar
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. In the almost-empty mustard jar, combine dijon, vinegar, sugar, salt, and pepper
2. Shake well
3. Transfer to a bowl and, whisking constantly, slowly add olive oil<|endoftext|>
