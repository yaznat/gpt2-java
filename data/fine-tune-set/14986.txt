Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Fresh mushrooms
• Onion
• Cooked pork
• Cooked rice
• Light soy sauce
• Eggs
• Green onions
• Fresh parsley
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat
2. Add mushrooms and onions, and cook until beginning to soften, stirring or tossing several times
3. Reduce heat to medium-high, add pork or shrimp, and continue cooking for 2 minutes
4. Add rice and soy sauce
5. Stir to combine, and continue cooking for about 5 minutes, stirring or tossing several times
6. Make a well in center of rice, add eggs, and cook until partially set, stirring just the egg portion of the mixture
7. When mostly set, stir into the rice, breaking up the eggs
8. Add green onions, parsley, and pepper, and continue cooking, tossing or stirring regularly, until heated
9. Season to taste with more soy sauce or salt as desired<|endoftext|>
