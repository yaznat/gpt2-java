Ingredients:
• Apples
• Eggs
• Salt
• Sugar
• Vanilla sugar
• Quark
• Semolina
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Using your mixer beat egg whites and salt until nearly stiff
2. Add 2 tbs of the sugar and beat until stiff peaks have formed
3. In another bowl beat egg yolks, 3 tbs of the sugar and the vanilla sugar until creamy
4. Mix in quark and semolina
5. Then fold in egg whites and raisins carefully
6. Pour into 4 well greased ramekins of 10 cm in diameter
7. Stick the apple slices into the dough until nearly covered
8. Sprinkle with remaining sugar
9. Bake in the pre-heated oven at 180c / 350f 30-35 minutes until toothpick inserted in centre comes out clean
10. Enjoy with topping of your choice
