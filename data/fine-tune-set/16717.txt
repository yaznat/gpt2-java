Please give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Bailey S Milkshakes For The Big Kids

Ingredients:
• French vanilla ice cream
• Hersheys chocolate syrup
• Milk
• Eggnog
• Banana
• Baileys irish cream

Steps:
1. Add in blender ice cream, chocolate syrup, bailey's, and either the milk or the egg nog
2. Blend lightly until just mixed to desired consistency
3. Pour or spoon into tall glasses and garnish with whipping cream, chocolate swirl, piroulines, or vanilla wafers and a mint sprig
4. Enjoy!
