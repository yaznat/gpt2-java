What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Lemon Pudding

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Lemon juice
• Lemon, zest of
• Eggs
• Butter
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine sugar, flour and salt
2. Stir in lemon juice, zest, beaten egg yolks, butter and milk
3. In a separate bowl, beat egg whites until stiff but not dry
4. Fold into lemon mixture
5. Pour into buttered 6 cup casserole dish
6. Place this casserole inside a large pan
7. Pour hot water into the 9x13 pan to a depth of about 1 inch
8. Bake at 350 degrees for about 40 minutes or until the topping is set and golden brown
9. During baking this separates into a cake-like topping with a lemon sauce beneath<|endoftext|>
