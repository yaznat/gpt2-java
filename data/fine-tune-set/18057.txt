What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Brownie Krispies

Ingredients:
• Milk chocolate chips
• Butter
• Light corn syrup
• Vanilla
• Peanut butter
• Powdered sugar
• Crispy rice cereal
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In large saucepan, combine chocolate chips, butter and corn syrup
2. Stir over low heat until chocolate melts
3. Remove from heat and add vanilla, peanut butter and powdered sugar
4. Mix well
5. Stir in cereal and pecans and mix ell
6. Spread into greased 9x13 pan
7. Chill until firm<|endoftext|>
