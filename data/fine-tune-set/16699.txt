Ingredients:
• Raw black sesame tahini
• Raw cashews
• Pitted dates
• Vanilla
• Water
• Ice

Steps:
1. Blend all ingredients but the ice in a blender until smooth
2. Add the ice and blend until smooth
3. Serve immediately
4. This keeps in the fridge for 4 days<|endoftext|>
