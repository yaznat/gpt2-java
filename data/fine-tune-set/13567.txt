Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Butter
• Lean ground beef
• Distilled white vinegar
• Diced green chilies
• Garlic cloves
• Dried oregano
• Ground cumin
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Salt
• Lettuce
• Chopped tomato
• Cheese
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Wrap tortillas in foil and warm in a 300 oven while making the filling
2. Brown ground beef and drain
3. Mix in vinegar and seasonings to the drained meat, but do not cook the mixture further
4. Remove tortillas from oven and increase oven temperature to 500
5. Brush each side of tortilla with melted butter
6. Spoon about 1 / 4 cup ground beef filling onto center of each buttered tortilla
7. Fold in sides, then roll up tortilla from the bottom
8. Place seam-side down on a foil-lined and rimmed baking sheet
9. Bake at 500 for 8-10 minutes, until golden brown
10. Serve on a large bed of shredded lettuce and chopped tomatoes
11. Top with sour cream, shredded cheese, and salsa<|endoftext|>
