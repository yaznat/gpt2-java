Ingredients:
• Top round steaks
• Frozen baby carrots
• Peas and pearl onions
• Baking potato
• Beef gravy
• Thyme
• Black pepper
• Refrigerated buttermilk biscuits

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f and spray or grease a 2 quart casserole dish
2. Combine beef, frozen vegetables and potato in prepared dish
3. Stir in gravy, thyme and pepper
4. Bake uncovered for 40 minutes and remove from oven
5. Increase temperature to 400f and top the casserole with biscuits
6. Bake for 8-10 minutes more or until golden brown<|endoftext|>
