Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Red wine
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Kosher salt
• Rib eye steaks
• Blue cheese

Steps:
1. Combine 1st four ingredients in a ziploc bag or shallow pan
2. Add steaks to marinade
3. Marinate for 1-2 hours, turning steaks in marinade frequently
4. Grill steaks to desire doneness
5. I grill for 3-5 minutes on each side for medium-rare / medium doneness
6. While steaks are grilling, combine vinegar and bleu cheese to a creamy consistency
7. Serve sauce as a condiment to steaks<|endoftext|>
