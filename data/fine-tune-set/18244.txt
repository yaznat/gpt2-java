Ingredients:
• Barley flour
• Oat flour
• Sodium-free baking powder
• Salt
• Nonfat milk
• Eggs
• Light olive oil
• Clear honey
• Vanilla extract
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together barley flour, oat flour, baking powder, and salt
2. In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together nonfat milk, eggs, light olive oil, clear honey, and vanilla extract
3. Add wet ingredients to dry, and whisk until just combined
4. Heat a large non-stick skillet over low-medium heat
5. Pour about 3 tablespoons of batter into hot skillet and cook until bubbles begin to appear on top, and bottom is golden brown
6. Then flip pancake / s and cook until other side is also golden brown
7. Serve warm with maple syrup or honey<|endoftext|>
