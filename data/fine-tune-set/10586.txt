What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Clams

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Minced clams
• Fresh parmesan cheese
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Cook pasta by following the package directions
2. Drain
3. While pasta is cooking, heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat
4. Add in garlic
5. Stir / saute 2 minutes
6. Drain clams, reserving liquid
7. Add clam liquid to skillet
8. Simmer 5 minutes
9. Add in clams
10. Simmer 5 minutes
11. Combine drained pasta and clam mixture in a serving bowl
12. Toss gently
13. Sprinkle with cheese and pepper
14. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
