Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Agnes Sugar Cookies Thin Crispy

Ingredients:
• Granulated sugar
• Sweet unsalted butter
• Eggs
• Heavy cream
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cream together the butter and the sugar
2. Beat in the eggs, cream, and vanilla
3. Combine flour, baking powder, and salt, then stir into the creamy mixture
4. Cover and chill until firm
5. Roll out on a very lightly floured surface very thin
6. Cut with lightly floured cookie cutters and set onto lightly greased cookie sheets
7. Bake in a preheated 350f oven for 12-15 minutes<|endoftext|>
