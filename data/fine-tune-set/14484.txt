Ingredients:
• Fresh green beans
• Soy sauce
• Vegetable oil
• Vinegar
• Sesame oil
• Garlic clove

Steps:
1. Wash the beans and snap the ends off
2. Blanche them in boiling water for 3-4 minutes
3. Drain immediately and put into an ice cold water bath to retain crispness
4. Mix remaining ingredients in a small bowl
5. Pour over cooled green beans and refrigerate for at least half an hour, then serve
6. The longer they marinate the more intensely the flavors will develop<|endoftext|>
