What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Barb S Hearty Beef And Vegetable Soup

Ingredients:
• Stew meat
• Onion
• Bay leaves
• Beef base
• Water
• Tomatoes
• Celery
• Potatoes
• Carrot
• Green beans
• Peas
• Corn
• Zucchini
• Cabbage
• Dried basil
• Orzo pasta
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Brown stew meat in a large stew pot sprayed with cooking spray
2. Add the chopped onion, bay leaves and the water
3. Cook until meat is tender
4. Add celery, carrots, potatoes, and cook until just tender
5. Add canned tomatoes, green beans, peas, corn, squash, cabbage, basil, salt and pepper to taste
6. Let simmer 45 to 55 minutes so that flavors can blend
7. Add pasta and cook until pasta is tender, taste & adjust spices
8. Serve with your farorite cornbread or crusty bread, enjoy<|endoftext|>
