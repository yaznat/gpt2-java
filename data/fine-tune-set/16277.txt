Ingredients:
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Fat-free mayonnaise
• Deli turkey
• Tomatoes
• Jalapeno peppers
• Reduced-fat cheddar cheese
• Fresh cilantro
• Avocado

Steps:
1. Spread tortillas with mayonnaise
2. Top each with turkey, tomato, jalapeno, cheese, cilantro and avocado
3. Roll up and cut in half<|endoftext|>
