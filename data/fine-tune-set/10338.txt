Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Chocolate Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Baking cocoa
• Almond extract
• Vanilla extract
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, beat the butter, sugars, cocoa and extracts until creamy
2. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition
3. Combine the flour and baking soda
4. Gradually add to sugar mixture
5. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls 2 inches apart onto ungreased baking sheets
6. Bake at 375 for 7-9 minutes or until edges are firm
7. Remove to wire racks to cool<|endoftext|>
