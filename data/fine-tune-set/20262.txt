Ingredients:
• Chicken breast fillets
• Chinese five spice powder
• Fresh ginger
• Sesame seeds
• Shallots
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Hoisin sauce
• Snow peas

Steps:
1. Roll chicken in five-spice powder, coat a pan with cooking spray and cook chicken until browned, remove from pan
2. Recoat the pan and add ginger, sesame seeds and shallots
3. Stir-fry for 2-3 minutes, add the combined soy, honey and hoisin sauces, then add the snow peas cook until peas are bright green and tender-crisp
4. Return the chicken to the pan and cook until heated through
5. Serve with noodles or rice<|endoftext|>
