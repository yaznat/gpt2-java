What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Asian Pork Tenderloin With Spicy Asian Cole Slaw

Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Chinese five spice powder
• Kosher salt
• Ground black pepper
• Peanut oil
• Plum sauce
• Honey
• Tomato paste
• Soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Fresh ginger
• Pineapple juice
• Chili-garlic sauce
• Fresh chives
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Green cabbage
• Red bell pepper
• Carrot

Steps:
1. For the tenderloin:
2. Preheat oven to 400f
3. Combine five-spice, salt and pepper, and rub over tenderloins
4. Heat oil in oven-proof saute pan over medium-high
5. Sear pork on all sides, 5-8 minutes
6. Transfer pan to oven and roast 20 minutes, until internal temperature reaches 140f
7. Remove pork from pan and let rest for 10 minutes before slicing
8. For the slaw:
9. Whisk first 6 ingredients together in a bowl and set aside
10. In oil, saute cabbage, bell pepper, carrot and ginger in skillet on high heat
11. After 1 minute, add salt
12. Continue to saute for 2 minutes or until cabbage begins to wilt
13. Stir in reserved sauce and cook 1 minute until slightly thickened
14. Place slaw on platter, slice pork and lay over top of slaw, and serve with a dollpop of pan sauce<|endoftext|>
