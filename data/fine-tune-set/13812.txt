Ingredients:
• Pork steaks
• Teriyaki marinade
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Garlic
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Combine teriyaki, soy sauce, and garlic in a large zipped bag or casserole dish
2. Add pork steaks and marinate 6 hours or overnight for more flavor
3. Fry the pork steaks over medium heat, with enough oil covering the bottom of the pan for 5-7 minutes per side
4. Or you can broil the steaks in a 350f oven for about 45 minutes, turning once after 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
