Ingredients:
• Boil-in-the-bag rice
• Vegetable oil
• Ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Pork tenderloin
• Edamame
• Carrot
• Green onion
• Red bell pepper
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Cilantro
• Rice vinegar
• Chili sauce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Dry roasted peanuts

Steps:
1. Cook rice according to pkg
2. Set aside to cool
3. Heat 1 / 2 t oil in a wok or large skillet over med-high
4. Add ginger and garlic
5. Saute 1 minute add pork
6. Saute 3 min or til pork loses its pink
7. Place pork mixture in a large bowl
8. Cover to keep warm
9. Add 1 t oil to pan
10. Add edamame and carrot
11. Saute 2 minute add onion and bell pepper
12. Saute 2 minute add carrot mixture to pork
13. Cover
14. Heat remaining 2 1 / 2 t oil in pan
15. Add rice and soy sauce
16. Saute 3 minute return pork and veggies to pan
17. Add broth and next 5 ingredients
18. Cook 2 min or til heated through
19. Top with peanuts<|endoftext|>
