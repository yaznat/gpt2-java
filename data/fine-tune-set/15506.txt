Ingredients:
• 96% lean ground beef
• Salt
• Center-cut bacon
• Light hamburger bun
• Reduced-fat swiss cheese
• Red onion
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. On wax paper, shape beef into a tight 4-inch patty
2. Lightly salt both sides
3. Place in freezer 5 minutes
4. Preheat grill to high
5. Meanwhile, cook bacon until crispy
6. Drain on paper towels
7. Grill chilled patty to desired doneness
8. Toast bun on grill rack 30-60 seconds, watching carefully
9. Top burger with cheese a few seconds before removing from grill
10. Assemble all ingredients
11. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
