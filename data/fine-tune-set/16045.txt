What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Black Bean And Goat Cheese Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Goat cheese
• Tomatoes
• Corn kernel
• Scallions
• Fresh oregano
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. Mash beans in a medium bowl until chunky
2. Add goat cheese, tomato, corn, scallions, and oregano
3. Season with salt and pepper, and stir until well combined
4. Heat a grill pan over medium heat
5. Spread 1 / 2 cup bean mixture evenly over 1 tortilla, and top with another tortilla
6. Repeat with remaining bean mixture and tortillas
7. Raise heat to medium-high
8. Grill quesadillas, 1 or 2 at a time, until grill marks appear and filling is hot, about 2 minutes per side
9. Cut into wedges, and serve warm<|endoftext|>
