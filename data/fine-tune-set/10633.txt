Please give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>Antipasta Sauce Canning

Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Pickling onions
• Purple onion
• Green peppers
• Red peppers
• Cauliflower
• Cucumbers
• Black olives
• Mushrooms
• Green olives
• Shrimp
• Ketchup
• Oil
• Vinegar

Steps:
1. Take first 9 ingredients, cut into small pieces or use a food processor put into a large pot, add oil ketchup vinegar stir boil 10 minutes
2. Add shrimp and gr olives
3. Bring to a boil then put into hot sterilized jars
4. Process 15 minutes
5. Takes about 3 hours total depending on if you use a processor or not<|endoftext|>
