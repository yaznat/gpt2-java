Ingredients:
• Chicken breast halves
• Bacon
• Lime juice
• Lemon juice
• Garlic salt
• Avocado
• Swiss cheese
• Texas toast thick bread

Steps:
1. Marinate breast halves in lemon, lime juice, pepper and garlic salt unrefrigerated for about 1 / 2 hour
2. Wrap chicken with two pieces of bacon, using toothpicks to hold bacon
3. Place breast halves on a hot grill and grill until chicken is done, turning frequently
4. When chicken is near done, place avocado slices on top of chicken then top with swiss cheese
5. Grill just until cheese is melted
6. Remove chicken from grill and place on texas toast or buns with toppings of choice<|endoftext|>
