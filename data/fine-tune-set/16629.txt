Give me a recipe for a smoothie.<|endoftext|>Bananas Foster Smoothie Alcoholic

Ingredients:
• Low-fat milk
• Dark rum
• Creme de banane
• Honey
• Bananas
• Dark brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Frozen yogurt

Steps:
1. Place milk, rum, banana liqueur, honey, bananas, brown sugar & cinnamon in a blender & process until smooth
2. Add yogurt & continue to process until again smooth
3. Pour into brandy balloon glasses & enjoy!<|endoftext|>
