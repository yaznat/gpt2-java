Ingredients:
• Apples
• Brown sugar
• Flour
• Rolled oats
• Butter
• Spices

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f / 190c
2. Grease bottom and sides 10 inch square pan with butter or margarine
3. Distribute apples in the mold
4. In bowl combine remaining ingredients and mix well
5. With your hands form crumbles
6. Sprinkle the crumbles over apples
7. Bake about 30 minutes or until topping is golden brown and apples are tender
