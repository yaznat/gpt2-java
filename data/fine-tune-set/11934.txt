Give me a recipe for curry.<|endoftext|>Chettinad Curry Eggs

Ingredients:
• Hard-boiled eggs
• Coriander powder
• Chili powder
• Fennel seed
• Cumin seed
• Turmeric
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Coconut milk
• Oil
• Fenugreek seeds
• Cinnamon sticks
• Onions
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Lime, juice of

Steps:
1. Put the coriander, chilli, cumin seeds, turmeric, ginger and garlic into a blender with 2 tbsp water and blend to a paste
2. Heat the oil and fry the fennel seeds, fenugreek seeds and cinnamon for 10 seconds
3. Add the onion, cook till just coloured and add the spice paste
4. Cook for 8 minutes
5. If the mixture sticks to the pan add a little water
6. Add the tomatoes and cook for 2 minutes
7. Add 3 cups of warm water and salt to taste, simmer for 20 minutes till you have a smooth gravy
8. Add the coconut milk, bring to boil, reduce heat, add the lime juice stir well
9. Halve the eggs and put into the gravy, yolks uppermost
10. Serve with plain boiled rice and chettinad chicken curry<|endoftext|>
