Ingredients:
• Boneless country-style ribs
• All-purpose flour
• Seasoning salt
• Ground black pepper
• Bacon grease
• Campbell's golden mushroom soup
• Soup mix
• Oregano

Steps:
1. In a medium sized bowl combine all gravy ingredients and set aside
2. In a large gallon size plastic zipper type bag, combine flour, seasoned salt, pepper and shake well to combine
3. Place meat into bag and shake well to coat pieces
4. Heat grease until hot but not smoking, and cook meat until lightly browned on all sides
5. Put half of meat into crockpot, and top with half of gravy mixture
6. Top with rest of meat and gravy mixture
7. Cook for 5 hours on low<|endoftext|>
