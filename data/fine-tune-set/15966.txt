Give me a burrito recipe.<|endoftext|>Fried Burritos

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Refried beans
• American cheese
• Oil

Steps:
1. Warm the tortillas for 15 seconds in the microwave
2. This will prevent them from cracking when you work with them
3. If they are not sufficiently heated, heat for an additional 10 seconds until fairly warm
4. Once heated, keep them warm by placing them in a tortilla warmer, or two inverted plates
5. Put the oil in a deep skillet
6. The oil should be about an inch deep
7. Turn the burner to medium-low
8. The oil is ready when a drop of water "dances" on it
9. Remove one tortilla at a time from the warmer and place on clean surface
10. Spread 1-2 tablespoon refried beans in a line down center of tortilla
11. Place half a piece of cheese, followed by an additional fourth a piece cheese on the beans
12. Fold the top and bottom of the tortilla in, then the sides
13. Place, seam side down, in the oil
14. Fry until browned on one side
15. This will not take long
16. Turn and brown on other side
17. Prepare the remaining tortillas as you did before and place them to the oil
18. You should have no more than 3 frying at one time
19. When you remove them from the oil, place on a thick layer of paper towels to dry<|endoftext|>
