Ingredients:
• Whole milk
• Grape-nuts cereal
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Pure vanilla extract
• Salt
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine the cereal, salt and milk while preparing the remainder
3. Grease with butter a 2
4. 5 qt casserole dish
5. In a small bowl or 2 cup measuring cup combine the eggs, vanilla and sugar
6. Mix until thoroughly combined and emulsified
7. Pour the cereal and milk mixture into a mixing bowl and add the egg and sugar mixture
8. Stir well though do not allow to get overly foamy
9. Pour into the greased casserole and bake 60 minutes
10. This is done when a knife inserted halfway from the edge to the middle comes out clean
11. Do not attempt to overbake, it will appear underdone in the middle
12. Carry over cooking will handle that
13. Overcooked and it makes a nice albeit sweet hockey puck!<|endoftext|>
