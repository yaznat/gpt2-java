Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Jacked Up Frozen Fries

Ingredients:
• Frozen french fries
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Dried parsley flakes

Steps:
1. Coat fries with olive oil, then add spices and mix well
2. Bake at 400 degrees for 20-25 minutes turning once or twice till crisp<|endoftext|>
