Ingredients:
• Cooked sticky rice
• Imitation crabmeat
• Sriracha sauce
• Mayonnaise
• Garlic
• Nori goma furikake
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Flatten the rice 1 1 / 2 inches thick in an 11x7 dish
2. Shake furikake evenly over the rice
3. In a separate bowl, shred imitation crab flakes into 1 / 4 inch slivers
4. Add mayo, sriracha sauce, garlic, salt, and pepper to crab flakes, mix well
5. Spread crab mixture evenly over the rice and nori
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
