What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Bangkok Chicken Curry

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Brown rice
• Garlic cloves
• Red bell peppers
• Green bell pepper
• Onions
• Fresh basil
• Curry paste
• Fish sauce
• Bamboo shoots
• Light coconut milk
• Canola oil
• Chilies
• Sriracha sauce
• Salt
• Cracked black pepper

Steps:
1. 1
2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil
3. Add rice and boil for 40 minutes, or until rice is tender, then drain
4. 2
5. While rice is boiling, begin cleaning and prepping the vegetables and other ingredients
6. When cooking with a wok, its important to have everything right next to the wok and ready to go because of the high cooking temps
7. Youll need to be prepared to be stirring constantly
8. 3
9. When rice has been drained and plated, heat the wok on high until it starts to smoke slightly
10. Then add oil and dried chilis
11. Next add chicken and cook stirring constantly until its cooked on the outside
12. Add curry paste and stir, allowing the curry paste melt in the hot wok
13. After its been mixed thoroughly with the chicken- add garlic, sriracha, and fish sauce
14. Continue stirring constantly and add bell peppers
15. Cook for 1 minute then add onion and cook until fork tender, but still slightly crisp
16. Stir in bamboo shoots, salt and pepper
17. Stir in coconut milk and bring to a boil
18. Turn off heat, add fresh basil and serve over cooked brown rice<|endoftext|>
