Ingredients:
• Cocoa
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Kahlua
• Butter
• Unsifted flour
• Baking powder
• Dried ancho chile powder
• Salt
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350f
2. Lightly grease a 8 x 8 pan
3. Using a spoon, stir eggs with 3 / 4-cup sugar and 1 teaspoon vanilla
4. Add melted butter
5. Sift ground chocolate with flour, baking powder, and salt
6. Stir into egg mixture
7. Add nuts
8. Stir in chips
9. Spread into greased pan
10. Bake 20 to 30 minutes
11. For chewy brownies, use 8" pan and shorter baking time
12. For cake-like brownies, use 9" pan and longer baking time
13. Cut into squares<|endoftext|>
