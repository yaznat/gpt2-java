What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>California Taco Salad

Ingredients:
• Romaine lettuce
• Black beans
• Cheddar cheese
• Avocado
• Mangoes
• Black olives
• Cooked chicken
• Salt
• Chili powder
• Lime juice
• Vinaigrette dressing
• Tortilla chips
• Light sour cream
• Mild salsa

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix together lettuce, beans, cheese, avocado, mango, olives, and chicken
2. Sprinkle with lime juice and dressing and toss thoroughly
3. Top salad with broken chips and, if desired, dollops of sour cream and salsa<|endoftext|>
