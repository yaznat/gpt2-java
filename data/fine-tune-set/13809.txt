What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Pork Roast

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin
• Dry sherry
• Soya sauce
• Garlic
• Dry mustard
• Gingerroot
• Fresh thyme
• Dried thyme
• Red currant jelly

Steps:
1. You'll need 2 heavy-duty large plastic bags
2. Place one inside the other, then place roast inside inner bag
3. In bowl, combine all other roast ingredients and mix well
4. Pour over the roast in the bag
5. Press out any air, tie it up with a twist-tie or elastic band, and place in a large bowl and stash in your fridge
6. Marinate for about 6 hours, occasionally squishing bag to make sure all the meat gets contact with the marinade
7. You can get away with 3 to 4 hours though
8. Preheat oven to 325f degrees
9. Remove roast from bag, discarding marinade
10. Place roast on a rack in a roasting pan and roast until pork reaches internal temp of 170f and juice is no longer pink-- about 3 hours
11. Let stand for about 15 minutes before carving to allow juices to settle back into meat
12. To make the sauce, combine all sauce ingredients in a small saucepan and heat over medium heat until jelly melts
13. Stir well and serve with pork<|endoftext|>
