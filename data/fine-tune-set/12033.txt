Please give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>A Pie To Try

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Cream of tartar
• Sugar
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Lemons
• Vanilla wafer

Steps:
1. A pie you say?
2. Tis easy this way
3. First take 3 eggs and separated and to the whites add 1 teaspoon of cream of tartar
4. Then beat the poor things until you're ready to scream
5. To atone for the beating add 3 tablespoons of sugar
6. To quietly take first prize, beat the same length of time as before
7. Then let the whites rest and return to the yolks which have patiently waited
8. Combine a can of sweetened condensed milk
9. Then add the juice of the three lemons
10. Quite sour you know, but that makes the magic
11. For low and behold, a custard results from mixing it all together
12. The pie crust, you crush one box of vanilla wafers and place on a greased pie pan to make a lucious mouthful
13. Pour the custard and add a crown of stiff egg whites
14. Pop it into the oven and brown it<|endoftext|>
