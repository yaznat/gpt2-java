What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Basic Stir Fry Marinade By Penzeys

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Sherry wine
• Chinese five spice powder
• Honey
• Garlic cloves
• White pepper

Steps:
1. Whisk ingredients together to coat 1lb of thinly cut sliced meat, chicken or seafood
2. Cover and refrigerate until flavor penetrates the meat
3. At least an hour
4. Stir-fry with four cups assorted raw vegetables, chopped<|endoftext|>
