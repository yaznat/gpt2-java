Can you give me a quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Gf Chicken Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Green bell peppers
• Purple onion
• Cumin
• Chicken breasts
• No-salt-added diced tomatoes
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt
• Ground pepper
• Fat free tortillas
• Reduced-fat monterey jack cheese
• Butter-flavored cooking spray
• Fresh cilantro stem

Steps:
1. Place oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until hot
2. Add chopped pepper and onion
3. Cook 3 minutes, stirring often
4. Add cumin, and cook 1 minute, stirring often
5. Add chicken and tomato
6. Cook 3 minutes, stirring often
7. Stir in minced cilantro, salt, and ground pepper
8. Place about 1 / 2 cup chicken mixture on half of each tortilla
9. Sprinkle cheese evenly over chicken mixture on tortillas
10. Fold tortillas in half
11. Grill folded tortillas until cheese is melted and outsides are lightly browned
12. Set aside, and keep warm
13. Repeat procedure with remaining tortillas
14. Garnish with cilantro sprigs, if desired
15. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
