Ingredients:
• Hot dogs
• Bacon
• Hot dog buns
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Mustard
• Onion
• Pickle relish
• Canned jalapeno slices
• Sauerkraut

Steps:
1. Wrap wieners tightly with bacon and place in the freezer for at least 20 minutes to help the bacon adhere to the wieners better
2. Heat a non-stick stove top grill or outdoor grill, oiling lightly, and cook wieners until the bacon is slightly crispy
3. Open buns and toast lightly over the grill or in a broiler
4. Insert wieners into buns and top with cheese
5. Wrap with foil and return to grill for a few minutes to allow cheese to melt
6. Serve with desired condiments along with potato salad or potato chips and baked beans<|endoftext|>
