Ingredients:
• Milk
• Cocoa powder
• Sugar
• Non-dairy powdered coffee creamer
• Vanilla extract
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in blender and blend on high until there are no chunks of ice left
2. But make sure you're using one or the other of either cocoa powder or chocolate milk mix, not both
3. You can change this recipe so many ways to create your own-- add an 1 / 8 tsp of peppermint extract for a chocolate mint milkshake
4. Add some coffee or 1 / 2 teaspoon instant espresso powder to make a "mocha" flavor
5. Omit the chocolate and powdered coffee creamer altogether to make vanilla
6. With a vanilla base, add any kind of berries-- or replace the milk with 1-1 / 2 cups orange juice & 1 / 2 cup water to make the best orange julius just like you get in the mall!
7. Play with the amount of sugar, cocoa, etc to create the best taste for you and your family<|endoftext|>
