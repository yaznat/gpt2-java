Can you give me a muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Spice Muffins

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Raisins
• Almonds
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Granulated sugar

Steps:
1. Grease or line 12 large muffin cups and set aside
2. In a large bowl, cream together butter and brown sugar
3. Beat in egg
4. Stir together flour, baking powder, soda, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg
5. Combine with raisins and almonds
6. Stir dry ingredients into the batter in 3 parts, alternating with the applesauce in 2 parts
7. Mix just until blended
8. Transfer to muffin cups, almost filling each cup
9. Topping: combine all topping ingredients and spoon equally over muffins
10. Bake at 375 f
11. For 20 minutes or until well risen and firm to the touch
12. Let cool on a rack and serve for breakfast or snacks
13. Makes 12 big muffins
