Ingredients:
• Beans
• Onion
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Celery
• Basil
• Carrots
• Tomatoes
• Garlic
• Black pepper
• Smoked turkey
• Ham hock
• Chicken stock

Steps:
1. Finely chop onion, red and green bell pepper, celery, carrot, tomatoes, and garlic
2. Rinse beans and drain
3. Add chicken stock
4. Stir in onion, bell peppers, celery, basil, carrot, tomatoes, garlic, and black pepper
5. Stir in ham hock and smoked turkey
6. Cover and bring to a boil
7. Reduce heat to medium
8. Uncover and simmer for 2 1 / 2 hours, until beans are tender
9. Remove ham hock and remove any meat from the bone and return to beans<|endoftext|>
