What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Bean Chili Over Sweet Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Red bell peppers
• Onion
• Garlic
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Diced tomatoes with juice
• Black beans
• Yellow squash
• Jalapeno
• Lime

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees
2. Pierce sweet potatoes with a fork and place on a baking sheet
3. Bake about 1 hour and 15 minutes, or until tender
4. Saute bell pepper and onions in a small amount of broth or water over medium heat until soft and golden, about 10 minutes
5. Add garlic and cook for 2 minutes more
6. Stir in spices, tomatoes, and beans and bring to boil
7. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes
8. Add squash and jalapeno
9. Cook, covered, about 6 minutes or until squash is crisp-tender
10. Place one peeled, slightly mashed sweet potato on each plate
11. Top with chili and drizzle with freshly squeezed lime juice, if desired<|endoftext|>
