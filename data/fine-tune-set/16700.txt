Ingredients:
• Blackberry
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Plain fat-free yogurt
• Skim milk
• Mint leaf

Steps:
1. Reserve three blackberries and put remainder into a blender or food processor
2. Add applesauce, yogurt and milk
3. Blend or process for 15 to 20 seconds until smooth
4. Pour into a glass and decorate with reserved blackberries and mint<|endoftext|>
