Ingredients:
• Whole wheat bread
• Apricots
• Mild cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Butter one side of each slice of bread, then cut in half
3. Arrange 2 halves of an apricot on each half slice of bread, cut side down, then top with a slice of cheddar
4. Bake 5 to 10 minutes or until cheese melts & bubbles<|endoftext|>
