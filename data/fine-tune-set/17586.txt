What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Cappuccino Coffee Creamer

Ingredients:
• Hot chocolate powder
• Non-dairy coffee creamer
• Allspice
• Ground cinnamon
• Nonfat dry milk powder

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in a jar with a screw-top lid
2. Shake until well combined
3. Add 1-2 tablespoons of creamer to 6 ounces of coffee
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
