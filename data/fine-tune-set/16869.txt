What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Vanilla Pear Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Pear
• Vanilla ice cream
• 2% low-fat milk
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Add more ice cream or reduce the amount of milk to thicken the shake to desired thickness
2. Also, if you do not like pears, don't be afraid to switch it for something such as a banana or another fruit
3. Chop the pear into quarter-sized pieces
4. Add pear, ice cream, milk, and vanilla into blender
5. Blend ingredients on low for 30-60 seconds
6. Serve you can also try serving this shake in a frosted glass for extended clill
7. Enjoy: )<|endoftext|>
