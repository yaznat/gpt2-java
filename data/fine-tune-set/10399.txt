What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazing Oatmeal Cookies

Ingredients:
• Oats
• Rice krispies
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Egg
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Butter
• Unsweetened coconut
• Walnuts
• Dark chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine first five ingredients in a bowl
3. In a separate bowl cream butter together with two sugars
4. Beat the egg into the sugar and butter mixture
5. Slowly combine the wet and dry ingredients
6. Fold the walnuts, coconut, and chocolate chips into the batter with a rubber spatula
7. Drop by the spoonful onto a cookie sheet
8. Bake for 10-12 minutes until edges of the cookies are golden brown<|endoftext|>
