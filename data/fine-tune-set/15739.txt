Can you give me a taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Chinese Tacos Low Carb

Ingredients:
• Skinless chicken breasts
• Lime juice
• Chili peppers
• Light soy sauce
• Sugar
• Fresh cilantro
• Ginger
• Garlic
• Hot pepper sauce
• Sesame oil
• Five-spice seasoning
• Lettuce
• Green onions
• Peanuts
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients, except sesame oil& fixings
2. Let marinate one hour or overnight, saute in the 1 tablespoon sesame oil for 8 to 10 minutes add chinese 5 spice if desired
3. Fixings: to assemble tacos, wash and pat dry lettuce leaves
4. Place chicken mixture into individual lettuce leaves to form a lettuce taco shell
5. Fill with garnish of peanuts onions and cilantro<|endoftext|>
