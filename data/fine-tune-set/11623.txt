What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Ingredient Ice Cream Sandwich Cake

Ingredients:
• Ice cream sandwiches
• Chocolate whipped cream
• Oreo cookies

Steps:
1. Arrange 4 ice cream sandwiches long sides touching on a serving plate
2. Squirt an even layer of whipped cream over top
3. Scatter with 1 / 3 cup chopped oreos or malted milk balls
4. Repeat layers 2 times, garnishing top with a few larger chunks of oreos or malted milk balls
5. Freeze 6 hours or until firm
6. Slice to serve
7. May be frozen for up to a week<|endoftext|>
