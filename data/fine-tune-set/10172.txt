What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Beef And Beer Chili Easy Crockpot Recipe

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Beef top round steak
• Onion
• Garlic
• Paprika
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Oregano flakes
• Mexican-style stewed tomatoes
• Light beer
• Chili pepper
• Low-sodium kidney beans
• Sharp cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat the oil over medium-high heat in a large, nonstick frying pan or skillet
2. Add beef, onion, and garlic, stirring occasionally, until browned
3. Spoon beef mixture into crock pot
4. Add paprika, chili powder, cumin, oregano, and stewed tomatoes, beer, jalapeno if desired, and beans
5. Stir to combine
6. Cover and turn crock pot on low
7. Cook 8-10 hours
8. Sprinkle each serving with minced onion and grated cheese if desired<|endoftext|>
