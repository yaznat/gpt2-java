Ingredients:
• Lean ham
• Apricot fruit spread
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. Coat a large nonstick skillet with cooking spray
2. Place over medium-high heat until hot
3. Add ham
4. Cook for 2-3 minutes on each side or until lightly browned
5. Add apricot spread and orange juice to skillet, stirring until spread melts
6. Reduce heat and simmer 5-6 minutes or until ham is glazed
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
