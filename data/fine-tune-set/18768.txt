Ingredients:
• Day-old white bread
• Milk
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Salt
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Heat some of the milk to help dissolve the sugar
2. Use another small amount of milk to dissolve the cornstarch
3. Beat the eggs
4. Mix all liquids together and pour into baking dish
5. Slice bread into cubes and drop into liquid
6. Dot the top generously with butter and sprinkle nutmeg on it
7. Place baking dish in a larger broiler pan with at least 1 inch of water and bake for 1 1 / 2 hours at 350 degrees

9. For an extra flavor soak raisins for at least an hour in brandy, rum, etc
