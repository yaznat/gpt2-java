Can you give me a pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Beer Dough Pizza

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Active dry yeast
• Sugar
• Beer
• Italian style breadcrumbs

Steps:
1. Mix all dry ingredients
2. Add beer and knead to smooth
3. Let rise until doubled in size in a well-oiled bowl
4. Turn out and knead again
5. Let rise
6. Roll dough out onto pizza pan that has been greased and sprinkled with italian style bread crumbs
7. Add toppings of your choice, sprinkle crust with more bread crumbs
8. Bake at 400 until golden brown<|endoftext|>
