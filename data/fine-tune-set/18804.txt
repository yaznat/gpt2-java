Ingredients:
• Acorn squash
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Brown sugar
• Elephant garlic
• Milk
• Eggs
• Ground nutmeg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f using a sharp knife, cut the squash in half lengthwise
2. Using a sturdy spoon, scoop out the seeds and fibrous membranes
3. Rub squash halves lightly with half the oil and season with salt, pepper and a sprinkling of brown sugar
4. Place on a baking sheet
5. Use the remaining oil to coat the whole garlic bulb
6. Place it on the tray with the squash
7. Bake the squash for 30 minutes
8. Remove and cool to room temperature
9. Bake the garlic for an hour or until very soft
10. Remove and cool
11. Cut the garlic in half and squeeze out the soft cooked garlic into a bowl
12. Set aside
13. Meanwhile, in a sauce pan, bring the milk to a simmer
14. Whisk the eggs and garlic together
15. Gradually whisk the hot milk into the eggs
16. Season with the salt, pepper and nutmeg
17. Prepare a baking dish that will hold all four halves of squash
18. Pour one cup of water into the bottom
19. Cut a small sliver off the bottom of each squash to stabilize it in the baking dish
20. Place the squash in the baking dish, in the water
21. Pour enough egg mixture into the halved squash to fill the cavity to a half inch from the top
22. Bake for 50-60 minutes or until custard is firm
23. Remove from the oven and serve warm
24. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
