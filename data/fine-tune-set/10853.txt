What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>A Refreshing Salad To Usher In Summer

Ingredients:
• Watermelon
• Mint leaf
• Feta
• Limes, juice and zest of
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Remove skin, seed and the white part from the watermelon
2. Cut into bite size pieces
3. Place watermelon in a serving bowl
4. Drizzle lime juice over and season with a little salt and freshly ground black pepper
5. Add feta, mint and lime zest
6. Garnish with whole sprigs of mint
