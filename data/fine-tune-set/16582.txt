What's a good smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Kale Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Kale
• Vanilla yogurt
• Fat-free hazelnut-flavored nondairy liquid coffee creamer
• Coconut extract
• Ice

Steps:
1. Make sure you remove the kale leaf from the central stem
2. Place all ingredients in blender
3. Blend till smooth
4. I love coconut and hazelnut so i used those two flavors
5. Using the christmas pumpkin spice creamer and vanilla extract is an awesome variation as well
6. Another is fat-free half & half with blueberry syrup and vanilla extract
7. The flavors don't match the color, this smoothie is bright green
8. Makes one huge meal-sized smoothie or two smaller ones<|endoftext|>
