What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Healthy Pumpkin Belgian Waffles

Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Brown sugar
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Pumpkin puree
• Skim milk
• Applesauce
• Vegetable oil
• Egg beaters egg substitute
• Wheat germ
• Ground flax seeds
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat belgium waffle machine
2. Combine dry ingredients in large mixing bowl or stand mixer
3. Make a well in the center, and add in remaining wet ingredients
4. Mix in walnuts, if using
5. Pour batter into waffle iron per your units' instructions
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
