Ingredients:
• Red onions
• Red bell pepper
• Extra lean ground beef
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Sugar
• Salt
• Dried oregano
• Red kidney beans
• No-salt-added diced tomatoes
• Low sodium beef broth
• Beer
• Yellow cornmeal
• Fresh lime juice

Steps:
1. Combine first 4 ingredients in a large dutch oven over medium-high heat
2. Cook 5 minutes or until beef is browned, stirring to crumble
3. Stir in chili powder, cumin, sugar, and salt
4. Cook 1 minute
5. Add oregano and next 4 ingredients to pan
6. Bring to a boil
7. Reduce heat, and simmer 15 minutes
8. Stir in cornmeal
9. Cook 5 minutes
10. Stir in juice<|endoftext|>
