What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Balsamic Roasted Apple Jack Cheese Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Green apples
• Unsalted butter
• Brown sugar
• Balsamic vinegar
• Salt
• Flour tortillas
• Jalapeno jack cheese

Steps:
1. In a saut pan melt the butter over medium heat
2. Add the apples and saut 3-5 minutes, tossing often
3. Add the brown sugar, balsamic vinegar and salt, continue cooking, about 4 minutes more, tossing often
4. Remove from the heat and set aside
5. In a non-stick saut pan, over medium heat, warm two tortillas by heating each of them for 1 minute on each side
6. Leave one tortilla in the pan and spread half of the cheese on the tortilla
7. Top the cheese with half of the apple mixture
8. Place the other warmed tortilla on top
9. Cook for 30 seconds, then flip the quesadilla and cook for 30 seconds more
10. Remove form the pan, cut into wedges and serve hot<|endoftext|>
