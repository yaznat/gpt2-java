What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Clouds And Earth Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Kahlua hazelnut
• Dark creme de cacao
• Butterscotch schnapps
• Half-and-half

Steps:
1. Pour kahlua, creme de cacao and schnapps in exact order over ice
2. Slowly add half and half
3. The effect is like clouds hanging over a dark sky
4. Stir briskly before drinking<|endoftext|>
