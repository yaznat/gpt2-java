Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Oil
• Zucchini
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Vanilla
• Miniature chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Beat together eggs, sugar and oil
3. Add zucchini and remaining ingredients, mixing well
4. Bake in 4 well-greased loaf pans for about one hour
5. Freezes well, if there's any left after your family goes through the kitchen<|endoftext|>
