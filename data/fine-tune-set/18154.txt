Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Granny smith apples
• Cheddar cheese
• Bacon

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix flour, baking powder, and salt
2. In a small bowl, whisk melted butter, eggs and milk
3. Whisk milk mixture into flour mix to make a pancake batter
4. If it appears too thick, thin with a little more milk
5. Do not overbeat- the mixture should be slightly lumpy
6. Gently fold in apples, cheese, and bacon into pancake batter
7. Heat a large nonstick skillet lightly coated with some oil over medium high heat
8. Using a 1 / 4 cup measuring cup, pour into skillet
9. Cook pancake until done
10. Set aside, keep warm
11. Repeat with remaining batter
12. Serve pancakes with maple syrup<|endoftext|>
