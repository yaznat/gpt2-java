Give me a pudding recipe.<|endoftext|>Addictive Chocolate Tapioca Pudding

Ingredients:
• Dry tapioca
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Sugar
• Rice milk
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Whisk the tapioca, cornstarch, salt, sugar and milk together in a saucepan
2. Let sit for 10 minutes
3. Toss in the chocolate
4. Over medium heat, whisking gently bring to a boil
5. Remove from heat and pour into a bowl
6. Cool slightly before stirring in the vanilla
7. Great eaten warm or chilled<|endoftext|>
