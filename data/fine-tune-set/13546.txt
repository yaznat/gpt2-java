Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Awaze Sigga Tibs Ethiopian Style Sauteed Beef

Ingredients:
• Round steak
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Smart balance light butter spread
• Curry powder
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Sweet red pepper
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Prep beef by washing and cutting up into bite size cubes
2. Set aside
3. Heat oil in a large skillet
4. Add garlic and onion and cook on medium heat until onion appears clear
5. Add green and red peppers
6. Stir in first tbsp berbere spice
7. Cook until vegetables are all soft
8. Add meat and tbsp of butter
9. Stir in second tbsp of spice
10. Continue to cook on medium heat, stirring often
11. Salt and pepper to taste
12. Serve over prepared couscous or with injera<|endoftext|>
