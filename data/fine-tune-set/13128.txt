Ingredients:
• Fresh garlic
• Shallot
• White wine
• Fresh lemon juice
• Unsalted butter
• Salt

Steps:
1. Place the first 4 ingredients, into a sauce pan and simmer over medium high heat until the volume is reduced by half
2. Reduce heat to low
3. Now slowly begin adding the pieces of cold butter, whisking continuously after each addition to maintain a smooth consistency
4. Add salt and strain sauce into a small pan to remove any solids
5. Sauce should be creamy and smooth
6. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
