Ingredients:
• Scallops
• Bacon
• Dark brown sugar
• Fresh ginger
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Mix sugar, ginger, soy sauce
2. Put scallops in soy mixture and marinate 20 minutes or longer if you like
3. Wrap each scallop with bacon slice and secure with toothpick
4. Sear scallop / bacon on med-hot bar-b-que until bacon is well done, or broil in your oven 15 minutes brushing scallops with leftover marinade during cooking
5. Prep time includes marinating time<|endoftext|>
