Ingredients:
• Butternut squash
• Sweet potato
• Carrots
• Plain yogurt
• Chicken broth
• Mandarin orange
• Thyme
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Honey
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Peel and cube the squash and sweet potato
2. Chop the carrots
3. Put in a pot and add water to just cover the vegetables
4. Boil until vegetables are soft, then mash with a fork
5. Add the yogurt and the broth
6. Squeeze the juice out of the mandarin and add
7. Stir well so that the solids mix with the liquids
8. Add spices, honey, salt and pepper
9. You're done!<|endoftext|>
