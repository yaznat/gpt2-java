Ingredients:
• Honey
• Ketchup
• Apple cider vinegar
• Canola oil
• Sea salt
• Paprika
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Garlic
• Chunky salsa
• Olive oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Ground cumin
• Barbecue seasoning
• Cayenne pepper
• Broccoli slaw mix
• Black beans
• Chickpeas
• Canned corn
• Fresh cilantro leaves
• Green onion
• Grape tomatoes
• Cucumber
• Lime, juice of
• Tortilla chips
• Sour cream
• Cheese

Steps:
1. For dressing, combine the first 8 ingredients, making sure to mix very well
2. Add the salsa and combine well
3. Cover and keep refrigerated until ready to use
4. In a large nonstick skillet, heat 1 t-2 t olive oil over medium-high heat
5. In a medium-sized bowl or plastic ziploc bag, add chicken, cumin, barbecue seasoning, cayenne pepper, if using, sea salt and black pepper
6. Shake to coat chicken
7. Toss the chicken mixture in the skillet and cook until nicely browned and it is no longer pink in the middle, about 10-15 minutes
8. In large salad bowl, add broccoli slaw and then layer the beans, chickpeas, corn, cilantro, onions, tomatoes, cumcumber and chicken
9. Squeeze the juice of the lime over the entire salad
10. Top with the amount of dressing you prefer
11. Serve with tortilla chips, sour cream and cheese, if desired<|endoftext|>
