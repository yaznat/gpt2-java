What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Brownie Oat Cookies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• White sugar
• Quick-cooking oats
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Egg whites
• Light corn syrup
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. 1
2. Spray cookie sheet with non-stick spray
3. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
4. 2
5. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, oats, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt
6. Mix in the egg whites, syrup, and vanilla
7. Drop by spoonfuls on the prepared cookie sheet
8. 3
9. Bake 10 minutes in the preheated oven<|endoftext|>
