Ingredients:
• Stewing beef
• Flour
• Shortening
• Seasoning salt
• Onion
• Tomato juice
• Water
• Parsley flakes
• Worcestershire sauce
• Pepper
• Garlic salt
• Potato
• Carrot
• Celery
• Kernel corn
• Frozen peas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. While heating shortening in large frying pan, stir together flour and seasoned salt
3. Dredge meat in this mixture and brown well in hot shortening
4. Add onions and continue to cook until soft
5. Place in mediun roaster
6. Add the rest of the ingredients in the order given and stir well
7. Cover and place in hot oven for two hours
