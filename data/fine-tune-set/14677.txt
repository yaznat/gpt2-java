What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Apple Pecan Crusted Salmon Over Wild Rice

Ingredients:
• Salmon fillets
• Apple jelly
• White wine
• Thyme
• Rosemary
• Pecans
• Salt and pepper
• Wild rice
• Dried cranberries
• Apple
• Chicken stock

Steps:
1. Make the apple glaze
2. In a microwavable bowl add the apple jelly and white wine to combine
3. Takes about 1 minute on medium
4. Remove and stir in the rosemary and thyme
5. Let cool a few minutes
6. Let the salmon come to room temperature brush on the apple glaze, add salt and pepper, just a little to each fillet and then sprinkle the top with pecans
7. Save the extra glaze to drizzle over the salmon
8. Place the salmon on a baking / cookie sheet lined with parchment or foil and cook at 400f for about 15-20
9. Cooking time will vary on the thickness and size of the fillets
10. Half way through, drizzle more glaze on the fillets
11. Cook the rice according to directions
12. But rather than using water -- substitute chicken broth for a rich flavor
13. Half way through cooking the rice which depending on what brand you get will be between 10 and 25 minutes, add the diced apples and cranberries
14. Serve with roasted green beans, broccoli, brussels sprouts or asparagus
15. Cook right along with the fish
16. Just toss with olive oil, salt and pepper and roast
17. Nothing more needed<|endoftext|>
