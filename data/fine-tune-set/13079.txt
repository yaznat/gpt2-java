Ingredients:
• Hake fillets
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Water
• Paprika
• Adobo seasoning
• Salt
• Flour
• Cornstarch
• Baking powder
• Milk

Steps:
1. I use 3 long thick pieces of fish
2. I cut it in 3 inch chunks
3. Dry the chunks of fish with paper towels
4. Set aside
5. In a small mixing bowl, beat eggs, water, salt and paprika
6. In a medium mixing bowl, add flour, cornstarch and baking powder
7. Mix well
8. Add egg mixture and beat in, if too thick, add milk 1 tablspoon at a time
9. The batter should be thick to hold on to the fish, not runny
10. Preheat frying pan to medium high heat with 1 / 4 cup vegetable oil
11. Use tongs to dip the fish chunks into batter
12. Use a spoon to place batter on the top of the chunk after it is in the pan
13. After bottom is golden brown, turn once, press down on top slightly with spatula to insure heat distribution
14. Fry fish in two separate batches using the balance of vegetable oil for the second batch
15. Keep warm in the oven until all fish is cooked
16. The batter is very light tasting, not soaked with oil
17. Use a dipping sauce of your choice
18. If you use this batter on thin fillets, use a slotted spoon to dip the fish into batter<|endoftext|>
