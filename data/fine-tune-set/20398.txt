Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>After The Holiday Casserole W Pineapple Fresh Cranberries

Ingredients:
• Mashed sweet potatoes
• Cooked turkey
• Pineapple tidbits
• Granulated sugar
• Ground nutmeg
• Salt
• Fresh cranberries
• Bulk pork sausage
• Dry breadcrumbs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Mix all ingredients together except pork sausage and bread crumbs
3. Pour into a well greased 2 1 / 2 quart casserole
4. Brown the sausage meat, crumbling it, until it just starts to brown
5. Drain sausage fat and sprinkle the meat over the casserole
6. Top with bread crumbs and bake for about 25-30 minutes<|endoftext|>
