Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Apfelpfannkuchen Apple Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Apples
• Butter
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Sift together the flour, sugar, and the salt
2. Beat eggs and milk together
3. Gradually add flour mixture
4. Beat until smooth
5. Saute apples in 1 / 4 c of butter until tender
6. Mix sugar and the cinnamon together
7. Toss with apples
8. Melt 2 t butter in a deep frypan
9. Pour in the batter to a depth of about 1 / 4-inch
10. When set, place 1 / 4 of the apples on top
11. Cover with more batter
12. Fry pancake until lightly browned on both sides
13. Keep warm
14. Repeat the procedure 3 times, until all batter and apples are used
15. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
