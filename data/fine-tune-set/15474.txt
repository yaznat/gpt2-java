Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Bangkok Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Brewed coffee
• Salt
• Soy sauce
• Butter
• Poppy seed rolls

Steps:
1. Mix ground beef lightly with coffee and salt - shape into 4 patties
2. Patties should be about 1 inch thick
3. Brush patties with soy sauce
4. Pay fry over medium heat in butter for 8 minutes turn and brush again with soy sauce
5. Cook 8 additional minutes for medium
6. Serve on rolls<|endoftext|>
