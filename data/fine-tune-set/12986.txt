What's a good fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Cod Fish With Anise

Ingredients:
• Anise
• Onion
• Lemon
• Salt and pepper
• Cod
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Garlic powder
• Basmati rice
• Water

Steps:
1. Cod fish with anise cooking instruction:
2. Slice the onion and the anize and mix in a bowl
3. Arrange half of the sliced vegetables at the bottom of glass dish and dash with salt and pepper
4. Then layer the cod fish on top, and then spread the rest of the sliced vegetables on top of the fish
5. Cut the lemon and squeezed all over and dash with salt and pepper
6. Take two tablespoons of the butter and drop small pieces at different area of the dish
7. Put 3 tbsp of olive oil and cover with foil bake for 45 minutes to 1 hr at 350f there should be enough juice when this is done to put over the rice
8. Rice - cooking instruction:
9. Put 1 cup of rice in a microwavable dish with a flat bottom
10. Make sure the dish is deep enough as rice will double in size
11. First, rinse the rice with cold water to remove the starch
12. Add two cups of water, make sure the rice is evenly spread out
13. Spread it evenly with your fingers
14. Put in the microwave for 25 minutes
15. Remove from microwave once cooked and mixed with spoon to loosen the rice, then cover with a lid
16. Let it cool for 5 minutes then serve
17. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
