Ingredients:
• Seville oranges
• Confectioners' sugar
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. If using the seville oranges, grate the zest of two of the oranges
2. Squeeze the juice of all three oranges into a bowl with the zest and sugar
3. If using the one orange and two limes, grate the zest of the orange and one lime, juice all and add the sugar as before
4. Stir to dissolve the sugar and add the heavy cream
5. Whip everything until it holds soft peaks, and then turn into a shallow airtight container with a lid
6. Cover and freeze until firm, about 3 to 5 hours
7. Remove to ripen for 15-20 minutes at room temperature or 30-40 minutes in the refrigerator before eating<|endoftext|>
