Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh parsley leaves
• Pepper
• Dried red pepper flakes
• Olive oil
• White vinegar
• Water
• Salt
• Pork tenderloin
• Bacon

Steps:
1. If you'd like to do this, double the recipe for the chimichurri sauce as you'll discard the original cup after the pork marinates
2. Press garlic and chop fresh parsley, and add remaining ingredients for the chimichurri sauce to a food processor
3. Blend for a few seconds until parsley is finely chopped
4. Slice the pork tenderloin into 1
5. 5 inch medallions
6. Using a large zip lock bag, add sliced pork medallions and chimichurri sauce
7. Allow to marinate for at least 1 hour
8. Remove pork medallions and discard marinade
9. Wrap each pork medallion with a slice of bacon
10. Begin to thread pork medallions onto skewers
11. Important - do not over-thread skewers allowing bacon to cook
12. Use as many skewers as necessary
13. Preheat grill to medium heat
14. Once heated, add skewers and cook for 4 minutes on all 4 sides for a total grilling time 16 minutes
15. Internal temperature should be 145 degrees fahrenheit
16. Serve with the additional cup of chimichurri sauce in a small molcajete or ramekin<|endoftext|>
