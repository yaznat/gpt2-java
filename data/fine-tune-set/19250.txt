Ingredients:
• Chocolate wafers
• Chocolate hazelnut spread
• Chocolate ice cream
• Toasted hazelnuts

Steps:
1. Spread flat side of each wafer with hazelnut spread
2. Set aside
3. Scoop 8 ice cream balls, about 3 tablespoons each
4. Keep in freezer until ready to assemble
5. To assemble sandwiches, remove ice cream balls from freezer
6. Let stand 1 minute to soften
7. Place 1 ice cream ball on 8 hazelnut-spread wafers
8. Top with remaining wafers
9. Press gently together
10. Sprinkle edges with nuts
11. Place sandwiches on baking sheet lined with waxed paper
12. Freeze 3 hours or until firm
13. To store, freeze up to two weeks in self-sealing plastic bags
14. Makes 8 sandwiches<|endoftext|>
