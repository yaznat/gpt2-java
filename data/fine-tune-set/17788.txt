What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Aunt Kelly S Banana Muffins

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Grease and flour and loaf pan
3. Cream butter with sugar
4. Add eggs and bananas and combine
5. Separately, sift together flour, baking soda and salt, then add to creamed mixture
6. Add vanilla
7. Spoon into muffin tins and bake until the tops spring back when you lightly press them
8. Aprox 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
