Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Butter Pie

Ingredients:
• Apple butter
• Egg
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Evaporated milk
• Ground cinnamon
• Pie shells
• Cool whip

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. In a large bowl, combine egg, sugar, apple butter, flour and milk, mix well
3. Pour into pie shell
4. Top with sprinkled cinnamon
5. Bake 45-50 minutes or until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean
6. Let cool
7. Top with cool whip
8. Store in the refrigerator<|endoftext|>
