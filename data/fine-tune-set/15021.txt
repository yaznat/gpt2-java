Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Romi S Delicious Korean Kimchi Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Pork loin
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• Corn oil
• Napa cabbage
• Cooked rice
• Green onions
• Sesame seeds
• Fish cake

Steps:
1. Heat wok / stir-fry pan
2. Whisk the 2 eggs with a little bit of oil and add to wok
3. Working with a wooden spatula, separate into small pieces
4. Remove from wok and set aside
5. Cut pork into small thin strips
6. Season with soy sauce and sugar
7. Add a little oil to the wok and stir-fry pork until cooked through
8. Remove from wok and set aside
9. Heat 3-4 tbsp corn oil in the wok and add kimchi
10. Fry until translucent
11. Stir in rice and mix well
12. Lower heat and fold in eggs, pork, green onions, sesame seeds and fish cake strips
13. Season with salt and red pepper to taste<|endoftext|>
