What's a good smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Pina Colada Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Coconut cream
• Pineapple chunks
• Bananas

Steps:
1. Dump everything in a blender and process until smooth
2. Add ice and process until ice is crushed
3. Serve in glasses and garnish with a piece of pineapple and a cherry, if desired<|endoftext|>
