Give me a pie recipe.<|endoftext|>Amigo Pie

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Chopped tomato
• Sliced ripe olives
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cornmeal
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Hot water
• Butter
• Sharp cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Brown meat
2. Drain
3. Add onion, tomato, olives and seasonings
4. Cook until onion is tender
5. Combine cornmeal, flour, salt and baking powder
6. Add water and butter or margarine
7. Mix until smooth
8. Stir in 1 cup cheese and olives
9. Pour cornmeal mixture into greased 9-inch pie plate
10. Spoon meat mixture into center of cornmeal mixture and press down gently
11. Bake in a 400 degree preheated oven for 25 minutes
12. Top with remaining cheese
13. Garnish with additional olive slices, if desired<|endoftext|>
