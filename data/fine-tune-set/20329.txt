Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Orange juice
• Balsamic vinegar
• Garlic cloves
• Orange peel
• Ground ginger
• Hot pepper sauce
• Carrot
• Sweet red pepper
• Green pepper
• Canola oil
• Fresh mushrooms
• Snow peas
• Green onion
• Salted cashews
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine the first seven ingredients until blended
2. Set aside
3. In a large nonstick skillet or wok, stir-fry carrots and peppers in oil for 5 minutes
4. Add mushrooms and snow peas
5. Stir-fry for 6 minutes
6. Add green onions
7. Stir-fry for 3 minutes or until the vegetables are crisp-tender
8. Stir orange juice mixture and add to pan
9. Bring to a boil
10. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened
11. Stir in cashews
12. Serve with rice<|endoftext|>
