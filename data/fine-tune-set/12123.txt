What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Andreas Viestad Onion Pie With Jarlsberg And Thyme

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Red onions
• Garlic cloves
• Bay leaf
• Black peppercorns
• Whole cloves
• Sea salt
• Puff pastry
• Jarlsberg cheese
• Fresh thyme

Steps:
1. In large skillet, heat butter over medium-high heat
2. Add onions, garlic, bay leaf, peppercorns and cloves
3. Reduce heat to medium-low and cook until onions are soft, but not brown
4. About 20 minutes
5. Season with salt to taste
6. Line medium-size overproof dish with puff pastry set aside
7. Remove cloves, peppercorns and bay leaf from onions and discard
8. Place onions in dish, add cheese and 1 teaspoon of the thyme
9. Bake in 400f oven on lowest rack until golden brown, about 15 minutes
10. Just before serving, sprinkle with remaining fresh thyme leaves<|endoftext|>
