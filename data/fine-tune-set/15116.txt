What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Artichoke Olive And Goat S Cheese Pizza

Ingredients:
• Prepared pizza crust
• Pasta sauce
• Marinated artichokes
• Kalamata olives
• Garlic clove
• Goat's cheese
• Olive oil
• Fresh oregano

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to a hot 220c
2. Place the pizza base on a baking tray, then spread with the tomato pasta sauce
3. Evenly scatter the artichoke pieces, olives and the garlic over the pasta sauce, then top with the crumbled goat's cheese
4. Lightly drizzle the surface of the pizza with olive oil and bake for 20 minutes, or until golden
5. Sprinkle with fresh oregano and season with salt and freshly ground black pepper
6. Cut into wedges and serve<|endoftext|>
