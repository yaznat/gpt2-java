Ingredients:
• Milk
• Chocolate malt powder
• Cocoa powder
• French vanilla ice cream
• Chocolate syrup

Steps:
1. Scoop ice cream into tall glass
2. Add chocolate powder, you can tweak the amounts, i just guessed--it depends on how chocolatey you want it!
3. Fill the glass halfway with milk and start mushing it all around with a good strong spoon, keep on stirring and adding milk till the glass is full
4. This usually is a bit hard to do since the ice cream is thick, but it makes the shake nice and thick
5. If you like chocolate syrup you can add it as well
6. Enjoy the shake!<|endoftext|>
