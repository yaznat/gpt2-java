Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Sliced mushrooms
• Onion
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Salt
• Pepper
• Milk
• Chives

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Drain
3. Meanwhile, for sauce, in a medium saucepan cook the mushrooms and onion in the butter until vegetables are tender
4. Stir in cream cheese, salt and pepper
5. Cook and stir over low heat until cheese is melted
6. Gradually stir in milk and chives
7. Heat through
8. Pour sauce over pasta
9. Toss to coat
10. Serve immediately
11. Makes 4 side servings<|endoftext|>
