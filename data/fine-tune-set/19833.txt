Ingredients:
• Frozen french fries
• Heavy whipping cream
• Romano cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Gorgonzola
• Salt
• Cracked pepper
• Nutmeg
• Parsley
• Mozzarella cheese
• Fresh cracked pepper

Steps:
1. Bake french fries according to package directions
2. While fries are in the oven, combine cream, romano and parmesan cheeses, salt, pepper and nutmeg in a sauce pan
3. Heat the sauce on medium then add gorgonzola cheese and parsley
4. Heat to a bubble
5. Remove from heat and stir in mozzarella cheese to give sauce a gooey texture
6. Drizzle the sauce over a large bowl of fries
7. Garnish with crumbled gorgonzola, cracked pepper and parsley<|endoftext|>
