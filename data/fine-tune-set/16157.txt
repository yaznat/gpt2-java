Can you give me a quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Eggplant Aubergine Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Eggplant
• Garlic-infused olive oil
• Salsa
• Asiago cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Brush eggplant slices with oil and season with salt and pepper
2. Grill eggplant until just browned
3. Remove and let cool
4. Mix salsa
5. Add asiago cheese and chopped eggplant
6. Spoon half of the mixture onto 1 tortilla
7. Top with another tortilla and repeat process
8. Grill quesadillas on both side until brown
9. Let cool and slice into wedges<|endoftext|>
