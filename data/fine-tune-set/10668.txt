Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Fresh gingerroot
• Soy sauce
• Peanut butter
• Honey
• Chili paste
• Garlic cloves
• Penne
• Green onion
• Peanuts

Steps:
1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil
2. Add noodles and cook until tender according to package directions
3. Drain
4. Meanwhile, combine chicken broth, ginger, soy sauce, peanut butter, honey, chili paste, and garlic in a small saucepan
5. Cook over medium heat until peanut butter melts and is heated through
6. Add noodles, and toss to coat
7. Garnish with green onions and peanuts<|endoftext|>
