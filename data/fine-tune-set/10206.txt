Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>Best Midwest Chili You Ll Ever Eat No Noodles Or Kidney Beans

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Chili beans
• Mushroom pieces
• Tomato soup
• Tomato sauce
• Garlic powder
• Parsley
• Basil
• Oregano
• Chili powder
• Tortilla chips
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Fry ground meat
2. As it finishes, add the onion and saut together
3. While that is working, open all the cans and dump into slow-cooker
4. Add the seasonings
5. Stir to mix
6. When meat is browned and onions are transparent or golden brown, add the green pepper, cook 1-2 more minutes
7. Then add to the bean mixture
8. Mix well
9. Cook on high for 3-4 hours
10. Medium for 4-6
11. Low for 6-8 hours
12. Serve with corn / tortilla chips in bottom of bowl, chili next, and cheese sprinkled over the top
13. Onions & sour cream optional<|endoftext|>
