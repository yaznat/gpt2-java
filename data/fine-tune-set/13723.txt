What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Apple Glazed Stuffed Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork ribs
• Cinnamon raisin bread
• Apple
• Pecans
• Salt
• Orange zest
• Ground cinnamon
• Pepper
• Orange juice
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Apple juice
• Butter

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. With sharp knife, make pocket in each pork chop by cutting into side of chop toward bone
3. In medium bowl, mix stuffing ingredients
4. Stuff each pocket with 1 / 4 of stuffing mixture, place chops in ungreased 13x9-inch pan
5. Bake uncovered 30 minutes
6. Meanwhile, in 1-quart saucepan, mix sugar and cornstarch
7. Stir in apple juice
8. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until mixture boils and thickens
9. Remove from heat
10. Stir in butter
11. Pour glaze evenly over chops
12. Bake uncovered 30 to 35 minutes longer or until pork is no longer pink in center<|endoftext|>
