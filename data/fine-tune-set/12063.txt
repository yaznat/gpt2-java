Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Alligator Hush Puppies

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Alligator meat
• Creamed corn
• Eggs
• Onion
• Jalapeno pepper
• Self rising flour
• Seasoning salt

Steps:
1. Heat oil to 365 f in a deep fryer, or per manufacturers instructions
2. In a skillet, saut alligator meat until done
3. Set aside
4. In a medium mixing bowl, combine all other ingredients, mixing well
5. Add sauted alligator and stir to blend
6. Let stand for 10 minutes
7. Use a serving spoon to scoop up tablespoon size pieces of batter and gently drop them into the oil
8. Rinse and dry the spoon as needed to keep the batter from sticking
9. Cook the hush puppies, 8 to 10 at a time, until golden brown
10. Remove from the oil with a slotted spoon and roll on paper towels to briefly drain
11. Serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce<|endoftext|>
