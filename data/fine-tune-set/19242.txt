What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Sherbet Ice Cream Maker Not Necessary

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Sugar
• Low-fat milk

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan over medium heat, whisk cocoa, sugar, and 2 tablespoons water to form a smooth paste
2. Add another 2 tablespoons water
3. Stir until mixture is warm and sugar dissolves
4. Remove from heat
5. Stir in milk
6. Pour mixture into a metal bowl, cover, and chill until cold, about 1 hour
7. Process in an ice cream maker according to manufacturer's directions
8. Or, pour mixture into a shallow metal pan, or ice cube tray and freeze 3 hours or up to overnight
9. Then whirl in a food processor, adding up to 2 tablespoons water to smooth mixture if necessary
10. Transfer mixture to a freezer-safe container, cover, and freeze until hard, about 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
