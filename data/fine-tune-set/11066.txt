What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Apricot Ham Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Multigrain bread
• Dried apricots
• Ham
• Light cream cheese

Steps:
1. Spread some of the cream cheese on first slice of bread
2. Top with 1 / 2 of the apricots and 2 slices of ham
3. Place 2nd slice of bread on top and spread remainder of cream cheese on top, then add remaining apricots and ham
4. Top with final piece of bread and slice it into 4 pieces
5. This recipe can be adjusted for an entire loaf, just follow the basic idea and adjust cheese / apricots to your liking
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
