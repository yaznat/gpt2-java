What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazing Broccoli Cheese Soup With Ham

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Flour
• Milk
• Chicken broth
• Broccoli
• Ham
• Velveeta cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a large pot
2. Add flour, salt, and pepper when butter is melted and bubbly
3. Over med
4. Heat, mix until it becomes a smooth, bubbly paste
5. Add milk and broth all at once, and stir until butter / flour mixture dissolves and it comes to a boil
6. Add chopped broccoli and ham, and cheeses
7. Stir until cheeses are melted and serve!
8. Yum!<|endoftext|>
