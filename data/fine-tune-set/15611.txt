What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Beef Taco Frito Skillet

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Tomato soup
• Salsa
• Water
• Fritos corn chips
• Cheese
• Green chilies
• Taco seasoning mix
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin

Steps:
1. Cook beef in a large skillet with onion till done
2. Drain well
3. Return meat to skillet and add everything except the cheese and fritos and heat till hot
4. When meat mixture is hot again add the fritos
5. When serving top each portion with cheese and your favorite taco toppings
6. Variation #1 creamy mexican style
7. Stir in 1 / 2 cup sour cream with the soup, omit the fritos and add torn flour tortillas
8. Variation #2 ranchero style
9. Omit the fritos and add torn corn tortillas or broken taco shells
10. If you bake this
11. Heat oven to 350
12. Bake for 20 minutes or till hot, shut oven off
13. Top with cheese and and place back in oven long enough for cheese to melt<|endoftext|>
