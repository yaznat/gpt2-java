Give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>Arabian Rice Aroz Mofalfal

Ingredients:
• Long grain rice
• Boiling water
• Vermicelli
• Butter
• Salt

Steps:
1. Break vermicelli noodles in small pieces
2. Fry with butter till slightly brown
3. Add boiling water and salt
4. Stir till boiling
5. Add rice slowly
6. Let it boil before covering
7. Stir
8. Put a thin layer of tin foil under the pan to prevent burning
9. Reduce heat to low and leave to cook for 30 minutes
10. Open the pan
11. Stir the top and see if rice is done
12. If rice is still a little hard, cover and cook longer
13. Serve with any vegetables<|endoftext|>
