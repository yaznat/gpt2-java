What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Mustard Glazed Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork chops
• Vegetable oil
• Apple jelly
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Apple juice
• Dijon-style mustard

Steps:
1. Season chops with pepper
2. Heat oil in large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
3. Brown chops on one side
4. Turn chops
5. Add apple juice, jelly, and mustard to skillet
6. Reduce heat to low, cover, and cook 8 to 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
