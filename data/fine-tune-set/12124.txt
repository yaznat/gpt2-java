Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Andrew S Chocolate Pie

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Semisweet chocolate
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Vanilla extract
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease pie pan, i use pam butter flavored spray
3. Mix all ingredients together
4. Pour into prepared pie pan
5. Bake for 25-30 minutes
6. Top with whipped cream<|endoftext|>
