What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>20 Minute White Bean Chili

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Vegetable oil
• Ground turkey
• Stewed tomatoes
• Chicken broth
• Dijon mustard
• Chili powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Cannellini beans
• Corn
• Tortilla chips
• Cheddar cheese
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. In 3 quart saucepan, over medium heat, saute onions and garlic in oil until tender
2. Add turkey
3. Cook until done, stirring occasionally to break up meat
4. Drain
5. Stir in tomatoes, chicken broth, mustard, chili powder and pepper
6. Heat to a boil
7. Reduce heat
8. Simmer for 10 minutes
9. Stir in beans and corn
10. Cook 5 minutes
11. Top with tortilla chips, shredded cheese and cilantro, if desired<|endoftext|>
