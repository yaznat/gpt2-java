Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Barbecue sauce
• Pizza dough
• Cheddar cheese
• Canned black beans
• Red onion
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f- rack in center spray a non-stick skillet and stir-fry chicken over medium-high heat, about 10 mins
2. Transfer chicken to medium-size bowl and toss with half barbecue sauce
3. Place pizza crust on baking sheet
4. Sprinkle with 3 / 4c cheese
5. Arrange chicken and black beans over crust
6. Sprinkle with onion and cilantro
7. Drizzle with remaining barbecue sauce
8. Add remaining 3 / 4 cup cheese
9. Bake 10 minutes or until cheese bubbles- serve immediately<|endoftext|>
