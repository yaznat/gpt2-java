What's a good donut recipe?<|endoftext|>Awwami Crisp Donut Balls Middle East Palestine

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Cornstarch
• Dry yeast
• Sugar
• Water
• Salt
• Syrup
• Oil

Steps:
1. Mix flour, salt, and cornstarch
2. Dissolve yeast in 1 / 4 cup of water and sprinkle with 1 / 4 teaspoons sugar
3. Add yeast mixture and 1 cup of water to flour and cornstarch
4. Beat well until smooth
5. Set aside one hour to rise
6. Heat oil in a deep-frying pan on medium to high fire
7. Dip a tablespoon in water, and pick up a small portion of the batter and drop it into the hot oil
8. Repeat until all the batter is used
9. Fry until light brown
10. Remove and dip in prepared cold attar
11. Serve while hot<|endoftext|>
