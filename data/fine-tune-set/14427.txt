Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Cherry tomatoes
• Red onion
• Balsamic vinegar
• Vegetable oil
• Salt and pepper
• Lettuce leaf
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Bring 2 in of water to a boil in a large skillet
2. Add green beans and cook 5 minutes or until crisp tender
3. Immediately drain and rinse under cold water
4. In a large salad bowl, toss beans with tomatoes, onions, vinegar, oil, salt and pepper
5. Line serving plates with lettuce leaves and top with green bean mixture, top with almonds<|endoftext|>
