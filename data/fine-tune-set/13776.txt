What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Apricot Glazed Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper
• Apricot jam
• Spicy brown mustard

Steps:
1. Preheat broiler and set rack 4 inches from heat
2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil
3. Run pork with oil and season with salt and pepper
4. Broil 10 minutes
5. Meanwhile, whisk together jam and mustard in a small saucepan until melted, 3-4 minutes
6. Remove from heat
7. Transfer half the sauce to a small bowl for brushing
8. Cover pan to keep sauce warm
9. Remove pork from broiler and brush with reserved sauce
10. Continue to broil until pork is blackened in spots, 5-10 minutes more
11. Serve drizzled with warm sauce<|endoftext|>
