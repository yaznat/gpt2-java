Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Butter
• Chocolate chips
• Cocoa
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Walnuts
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. In a bowl combine flour, baking powder, and cinnamon
3. Set aside
4. In a small pot melt butter over low heat
5. Add chocolate chips and cocoa while stirring constantly
6. Remove from heat when chocolate chips are just about melted
7. In a large bowl combine eggs, sugar, and vanilla
8. Add in flour mixture and chocolate mixture alternately stirring just until blended
9. Do not overmix
10. Spoon into well greased muffin tin or paper cups that have been lightly sprayed with oil
11. Sprinkle muffins with chocolate chips and chopped nuts
12. Bake for 20 minutes or until a toothpick inserted comes out clean<|endoftext|>
