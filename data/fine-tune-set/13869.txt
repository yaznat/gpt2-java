What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Aegean Lamb With Orzo

Ingredients:
• Lamb shoulder
• Dried oregano
• Lemon rind
• Salt
• Fresh lemon juice
• Fresh spinach
• Cooked orzo pasta
• Feta cheese
• Oregano sprig
• Lemon slice

Steps:
1. Trim fat from lamb
2. Place lamb in an electric slow cooker, and sprinkle with oregano, rind, and 1 / 4 teaspoon salt
3. Pour in juice
4. Top lamb with spinach
5. Cover with lid
6. Cook on high heat for 1 hour
7. Reduce heat setting to low
8. Cook 7 hours
9. Remove spinach and lamb from slow cooker, reserving cooking liquid
10. Cool
11. Remove meat from bones
12. Discard bones, fat, and gristle
13. Chop meat
14. Place a zip-top plastic bag inside a 2-cup glass measure
15. Pour drippings into bag, and let stand 10 minutes
16. Seal bag
17. Carefully snip off 1 bottom corner of bag
18. Drain drippings into a large bowl, stopping before fat layer reaches opening
19. Discard fat
20. Add 1 / 4 teaspoon salt, lamb, spinach, orzo, and feta to strained drippings
21. Garnish with oregano sprigs and lemon slices, if desired<|endoftext|>
