Ingredients:
• Plain yogurt
• Lemon, juice and zest of
• Garlic clove
• Basil leaves
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Olive oil
• Yellow onion
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Roasted red pepper
• Balsamic vinegar
• Arugula leaf
• Focaccia bread

Steps:
1. Mix the yogurt, lemon juice and zest, garlic and basil together in a large mixing bowl
2. Add the chicken and coat it evenly with the yogurt mixture
3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour or overnight
4. Meanwhile, prepare the red pepper relish
5. Heat the olive oil in a small skillet over medium heat
6. Add the raw red pepper, onion and garlic and season with salt and pepper
7. Cook until the vegetables soften, about 5 minutes
8. Add the roasted red peppers and balsamic vinegar and cook for 5 more minutes
9. Remove from heat, cool and stir in the basil
10. This can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 1 week
11. For the chicken preheat the grill to high heat
12. Remove the chicken from the marinade and discard the marinade
13. Season the chicken with salt and pepper
14. Grill the chicken on both sides until it is cooked through, about 6 to 8 minutes per side
15. Let the chicken cool slightly and slice it on the bias
16. For the sandwiches spread 4 halves of the focaccia with some of the red pepper relish, then top with sliced chicken and arugula
17. Finish the sandwiches with a dollop of relish and the other half of the focaccia<|endoftext|>
