Ingredients:
• Rice flour
• Egg
• Gluten free bread
• Italian seasoning
• Dill weed
• Fresh parsley
• Chicken breasts
• Honey mustard sauce
• Mayonnaise
• Honey
• Mustard
• Tartar sauce
• Sweet relish
• Lemon juice
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
2. Line a cookie sheet with parchment or spray with non-stick coating
3. If your chicken is fresh, freeze for 15 minutes to ease the slicing or if frozen only partially thaw
4. Slice the small
5. If you are using fish cut to same size
6. Blend the italian seasoning and parsley with the bread in either a food processor or a blender until very fine crumbs
7. If it's sticky and doesn't want to crumb add rice flour a teaspoon at a time until it crumbs nicely
8. Put the rice flour in one shallow bowl the egg in another and the crumbs in a third
9. Dredge each piece through the rice flour to coat thoroughly then the egg than the crumbs
10. Bake chicken for 10 minutes flip and bake for 5 to 10 more minutes until done
11. For fish 5 minutes on the first and 3 on the second is usually sufficient,
12. They reheat nicely in a toaster oven
13. We serve the chicken with the honey mustard sauce and fish with the tartar sauce<|endoftext|>
