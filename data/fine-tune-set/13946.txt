Ingredients:
• Ground cumin
• Ground aniseed
• Ground red chili pepper
• Ground turmeric
• Ground ginger
• Ground cinnamon
• Vegetable oil
• Lamb loin chops
• Red lentil
• Chicken stock
• Garlic cloves
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Bananas
• Yogurt

Steps:
1. Combine the spices and oil in a large bowl and add the lamb, mix well
2. Cover and refrigerate for about 3 hours or overnight
3. Combine the lentils, stock and garlic in a medium pan, bring to the boil and simmer uncovered for about 10 minutes until the lentils are tender
4. Cook the lamb in batches in a large heated oiled pan until browned both sides and cooked to your liking
5. Banana raita: melt butter and sugar in a medium pan and cook the banana slices until browned lightly and soft
6. Combine the bananas with the yogurt in a medium bowl
7. Serve lamb and lentils with the banana raita<|endoftext|>
