Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Barley
• Vegetable broth
• Frozen corn
• Onion
• Lime juice
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Ground red pepper
• Garlic clove

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in crock pot
2. Stir well
3. Cover, and cook on low setting 4-5 hours
