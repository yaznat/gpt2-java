What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Another Cornbread Casserole

Ingredients:
• Canned corn
• Creamed corn
• Cornbread mix
• Egg
• Butter
• Paprika
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Mix everything together and pour into a greased 117x2 inch casserole
3. Bake for 25-30 minutes, until the top is golden and the sides are darkish<|endoftext|>
