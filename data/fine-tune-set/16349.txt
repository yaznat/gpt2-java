Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Shrimp
• Barbecue sauce
• Canola oil
• Lemon juice
• Dijon mustard
• Chipotle chiles in adobo
• Red pepper flakes
• Cayenne pepper
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Soak 5 to 8 bamboo skewers in water for about 20 minutes to keep them from burning on the grill or under the broiler
2. Meanwhile, cook the bacon in a large skillet until halfway done, about 4 minutes
3. Drain and cool on paper towels
4. Wrap a piece od bacon around the middle of each shrimp
5. Skewer with bamboo through the point where the bacon ends meet to keep it from unraveling
6. Thread 3 to 5 shrimp on each skewer
7. Puree the barbecue sauce, oil, lemon juice, mustard, chipotles, red pepper flakes, cayenne pepper and 1 / 4 teaspoon freshly ground pepper in a blender
8. Set aside half of the sauce for dipping
9. Preheat a grill, grill pan or broiler
10. Grill or broil shrimp for 5 minutes, basting with the sauce once they begin to turn pink
11. Flip, baste again and grill or broil until just cooked through, about 4 more minutes
12. Serve with extra sauce<|endoftext|>
