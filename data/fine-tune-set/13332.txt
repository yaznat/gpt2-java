Generate a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>Addicting Chicken Tetrazzini

Ingredients:
• Noodles
• Chicken
• Butter
• Green onions
• Mushrooms
• Milk
• Flour
• Nutmeg
• Dry sherry
• White pepper
• Parmesan cheese
• Sour cream
• Reduced-sodium fat-free chicken broth

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 350 degrees
2. Cook chicken, add salt and pepper taste
3. Place in 8x11 casserole dish to reserve
4. In same pan add butter, onion, and mushrooms
5. Saute until onions are soft
6. Add chicken broth, sherry, and 1 / 2 cup milk
7. In seperate bowl combine the other 1 / 2 cup milk, flour, 2 tbls
8. Cheese, nutmeg and pepper
9. Mix to combine
10. Slowly add mixture to bubbling pot, stir constantly until thickened
11. Add sour cream
12. Add in chicken and noodles
13. Place back in 8x11 baking dish, sprinkle with remaining cheese
14. Bake for 30 minutes or until bubbly hot<|endoftext|>
