Ingredients:
• Beef
• Olive oil
• Balsamic vinegar
• Garlic
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place meat in a shallow glass dish
2. Whisk together olive oil, vinegar, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper
3. Pour over the meat, using about 1 / 2 cup per lb of meat
4. Cover and marinate several hours or overnight, turning occasionally
5. Remove the meat and place the marinade in a small saucepan
6. Boil for about 1 minute and then grill the meat, basting frequently with the marinade<|endoftext|>
