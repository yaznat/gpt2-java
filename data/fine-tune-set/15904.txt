Ingredients:
• 10-inch flour tortillas
• Chicken chilli
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 f degrees
2. Grease 15 x 10 x 1 in
3. Jelly roll pan
4. Wrap tortillas in damp paper towels: microwave until warm and flexible about 1 minute, or wrap tortillas in foil, heat in 350 oven for 10 minutes spread 1 tortilla on work surface
5. Keep remaining tortillas warm
6. Top tortilla with 3 / 4 cup chicken chili
7. Sprinkle with 1 / 2 cup cheddar cheese, wrap up tortilla envelope fashion, to enclose filling
8. Transfer burrito, seam side down to prepared jelly roll pan
9. Repeat with the remaining tortillas, chili and cheddar cheese bake in 350f degree oven until tortillas are crispy, filling is heated through and cheese is melted, about 30 minutes
10. Let burritos cool slightly
11. Serve with sour cream and salsa on the side<|endoftext|>
