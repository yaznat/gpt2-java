Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Stew With Beer Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Stewing beef
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Bacon
• Onions
• Garlic clove
• Mushroom
• Beer
• Soy sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Thyme
• Bay leaf

Steps:
1. Mix flour, salt and pepper together and coat beef cubes
2. Fry bacon until crisp
3. Discard fat
4. Mix together beer and seasonings
5. Place beef, bacon, garlic and onions in cooker
6. Pour liquid mixture over them
7. Cook on low for 10 hours or on high for 6 hours<|endoftext|>
