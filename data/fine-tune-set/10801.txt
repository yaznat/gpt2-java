Ingredients:
• Coconut
• Mandarin orange segments
• Crushed pineapple
• Sour cream
• Miniature marshmallow

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except marshmallows
2. Chill for several hours
3. Add marshmallows right before serving
4. Also good with chopped pecans added!<|endoftext|>
