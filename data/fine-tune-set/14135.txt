Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Oyster sauce
• Garlic
• Spicy brown mustard
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Platetofu: place block of tofu in between 2 plates with paper towels on top and bottom
2. Place some thing heavy on top plate
3. Alow to sit for at least 20 min, the longer it sits, the "meatier" the tofu
4. Preheat oven to 400
5. Slice block in half from left to right so you have two pieces the same as the original block's width and length
6. Mix remaining ingredients together
7. Baste top, bottom and sides of each tofu block and place onto pan sprayed with cooking spray
8. Bake for 40 minutes, flip halfway through and rebaste if desired<|endoftext|>
