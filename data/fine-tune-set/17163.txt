Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Vegetable shortening
• Milk
• Jam

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees f
2. Measure the sifted flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt and mix with fork in large mixing bowl
3. Cut in vegetable shortening until it looks like coarse bread crumbs
4. Add the milk and mix it into the flour with fork-but only until the mixture will form a soft ball
5. Place the ball of dough on a lightly floured surface and knead it 12 times
6. Rub some flour on rolling pin and roll the dough until it's about 1 / 4 inch thick
7. With a large biscuit cutter cut circles, very close together, in the dough
8. Use a straight downward motion and do not twist the cutter
9. With a spatula lift half of the circles, one at a time onto a cookie sheet and arrange them about 1 inch apart
10. With a small cutter cut a hole in the center of the rest of the circles to make rings and lift out the centers
11. Set the centers aside
12. Place the rings on top of the circles on the cookie sheet
13. Place a teaspoon of red jam or jelly in each ring
14. Bake biscuits for 12-15 minutes until golden
15. Immediately take bicsuits off cookie sheet with spatula
16. With centers bake into little biscuits<|endoftext|>
