Give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>Alan Jackson S Chocolate Cake And Frosting

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Salt
• Water
• Margarine
• Vegetable oil
• Cocoa
• Buttermilk
• Baking soda
• Vanilla
• Cider vinegar
• Milk
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Cake:
2. Sift flour, sugar and salt together in mixing bowl
3. Heat water, 1 stick parkay, oil, 1 / 4 cup of the cocoa
4. Boil, stirring constantly
5. Pour into flour mixture and stir until smooth
6. Blend in buttermilk, eggs, baking soda, 1 teaspoon of the vanilla and vinegar until smooth
7. Pour into a greased and floured 13x2 inch baking pan
8. Bake at 350 degrees f for 35 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
9. Cool completely
10. Frosting:
11. In a sauce pan, over medium-high heat, melt remaining 1 stick of parkay, add the milk and the remaining 1 / 4 cup cocoa and bring to a boil
12. Remove from heat
13. Stir in powdered sugar and the remaining 1 teaspoon vanilla
14. Stir until smooth
15. Pour over the cake
16. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
