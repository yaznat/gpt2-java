Can you give me a beans recipe?<|endoftext|>10 Bean Soup

Ingredients:
• 10 bean soup mix
• Fat-free low-sodium chicken broth
• Vegetable broth
• Water
• Diced tomatoes
• Canola oil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Onion
• Carrots
• Celery ribs
• Garlic cloves
• Frozen green beans
• Frozen corn

Steps:
1. Soak beans in water overnight
2. Drain the beans the next morning or afternoon
3. In large soup pot saute onion, carrot, and celery in oil for about five minutes
4. Add garlic, salt, and pepper and saute another five minutes
5. Add chicken broth, vegetable broth, diced tomatoes, water, and beans to vegetable mixture
6. Bring to a boil
7. Reduce heat to a low boil and cover
8. Cook for about 2 hours
9. Stir occasionally
10. Add green beans and corn and contine to cook covered for about 1 more hour or until beans are tender
11. Prep time is soaking beans overnight and chopping vegetables<|endoftext|>
