Ingredients:
• Onion
• Sauerkraut
• Butter
• Cream
• Water
• Instant potato flakes

Steps:
1. Saute onion in butter
2. Add sauerkraut you can drain it, if thats what you do
3. Saute until onion light gold
4. Add water
5. Bring to a boil
6. Add potatoes
7. Mix in cream to desired thickness<|endoftext|>
