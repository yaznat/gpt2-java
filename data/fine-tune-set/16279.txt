Give me a wrap recipe.<|endoftext|>Avocado Bacon And Shrimp Wraps

Ingredients:
• Flat bread
• Mayonnaise
• Lettuce
• Avocado
• Tomatoes
• Shrimp
• Cooked bacon

Steps:
1. Spread the flatbreads or tortillas with mayonnaise
2. Lay a lettuce leaf on each
3. Top each with avocado and tomato slices
4. Divide the shrimp and bacon evenly among the sandwiches
5. Top each with the remaining lettuce
6. Roll the flatbread to make a tight cylinder
7. If made ahead, wrap well in plastic wrap and refrigerate
8. I've only kept them as long as the next day, for lunch so don't know how well they'd keep any longer than that<|endoftext|>
