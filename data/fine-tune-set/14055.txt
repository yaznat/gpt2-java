Ingredients:
• Ground lamb
• Curry powder
• Mixed spice
• Olive oil
• Salt and pepper
• Cilantro
• Mint
• Garlic clove
• Lemon juice
• Plain yogurt
• Pita bread

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix the lamb, curry powder, masala spice and salt and pepper well
2. Form lamb into 4-5 patties, depending on how thick you like your burgers
3. Place patties on a plate, and set aside
4. In a small bowl, mix the cilantro, mint, garlic and squeeze of lemon juice
5. Add the yogurt and stir until mixed
6. Set aside
7. Coat lamb patties with a little olive oil and either cook in a cast iron skillet on the stove or on the grill
8. Either way, the patties will need about 3 minutes on either side
9. Serve the patties in the pita bread, topped with the sauce<|endoftext|>
