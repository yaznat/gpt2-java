Ingredients:
• Rolled oats
• Nonfat milk
• Whole wheat flour
• Ground flax seeds
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Allspice
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Egg whites
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Raisins
• Walnuts
• Light brown sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. Topping:
2. Struesel topping:
3. In a small bowl, combine all the struesel ingredients and stir until well combined and crumbly
4. Muffin-tops:
5. Soak the oats in milk for about 30 minutes
6. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees
7. Spray muffin pan with cooking spray
8. Combine the applesauce with the egg
9. Mix the oat mixture with the applesauce mixture, and stir until combined
10. In a separate bowl measure and whisk the dry ingredients together
11. Add wet ingredients to dry and mix until just combined
12. Stir in raisins or craisins
13. Do not over mix the batter or the muffins will be tough
14. Spoon approximately 1 / 2 cup of muffin mixture into muffin-top pan
15. Sprinkle streusel on top
16. Bake until golden brown and the top springs back, approximately 15-20 minutes
17. Remove pan from the oven and allow to cool for 5 minutes before removing muffin-tops to wire rack to cool
