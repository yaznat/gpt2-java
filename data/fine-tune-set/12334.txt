Ingredients:
• Acorn squash
• Canola oil
• Onion
• Granny smith apple
• Curry powder
• Fat-free low-sodium chicken broth

Steps:
1. Pierce acorn squash with a fork, and place on a paper towel in the microwave for 8-10 minutes
2. Pierce with
3. Carefully remove from microwave
4. Allow to cool, then halve the acorn squash lengthwise
5. Scoop out seeds and fibers
6. Meanwhile, heat oil in a medium pot or dutch oven
7. Saut onions and apples for 5 minutes, until softened
8. Sprinkle curry powder and stir well
9. Scoop the flesh of the acorn squash from the skin and add to pot
10. Discard skin
11. Pour in chicken or vegetable broth
12. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer for 15 minutes
13. Using a hand blender or a regular blender, puree the soup until smooth
14. Serves 4
