What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Braised Lamb Shoulder Chops

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Shoulder lamb chops
• Sweet onion
• Carrots
• Celery ribs
• Lemon
• Red wine
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Tomatoes
• Curry powder
• Cumin
• Coriander
• Garlic
• Light soy sauce
• Water
• Cornstarch
• Of fresh mint

Steps:
1. Trim fat from chops
2. Heat oil in large deep non stick frypan or dutch oven
3. Brown chops on both sides, about 5 minutes each side
4. Remove from pan, set aside
5. In the same pan, add onion chunks, saute until soft, about 5 minutes
6. Add carrots, celery, salt and pepper and lemon slices, saute until caramelized, 15 to 20 minutes
7. Stir in wine or broth, bring to simmer and deglaze pan
8. Meanwhile, empty tomatoes, undrained, into a bowl, add curry powder, cumin, coriander, garlic and soy sauce
9. Stir to mix, pour tomato mixture into pan
10. Stir to mix with caramelized veggies
11. Return chops to pan and spoon mixture over them
12. Bring to boil, reduce heat and simmer covered for 1 hour or until chops are fork tender
13. Make a slurry of cold water and cornstarch, stir into pan and bring to boil, simmer until juices are thickened
14. Sprinkle with chopped fresh mint
15. Serve over mashed potatoes or rice or noodles<|endoftext|>
