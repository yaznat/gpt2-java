Ingredients:
• French-fried potatoes
• Fat-free vegetarian chili
• Hot dogs
• Onion
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Prepare fries according to package directions
2. Meanwhile, in a microwave-safe dish, combine the chili, hot dogs and onion
3. Cover and microwave on high for 5 or 6 minutes or until heated through, stirring once
4. Serve over fries
5. Sprinkle with cheese<|endoftext|>
