Ingredients:
• Pineapple juice
• Fresh orange juice
• Soy sauce
• Dark brown sugar
• Fresh pineapple
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Soak 36 toothpicks in water for 30 minutes
2. Drain and set aside
3. In large bowl, combine juices, soy sauce and brown sugar
4. Add pineapple, cover and chill 30 minutes
5. Drain pineapple, discarding marinade
6. Preheat oven to 400f line a rimmed baking sheet with foil
7. Wrap each pineapple chunk with 1 / 3 slice bacon and secure with a toothpick
8. Place on prepared baking sheet and bake 30-35 minutes or until bacon is crisp
9. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
