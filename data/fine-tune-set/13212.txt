Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Garlic Lemon Chicken

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Flour
• Salt and pepper
• Oil
• Chicken broth
• Garlic
• Lemon juice
• Butter

Steps:
1. Flatten chicken slightly
2. Dust with flour
3. Salt and pepper chicken
4. Fry in hot oil 5 minutes each side
5. Remove chicken from pan
6. To pan add broth, garlic, lemon juice and butter
7. Cook 2 minutes
8. Pour over chicken<|endoftext|>
