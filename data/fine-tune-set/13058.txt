What's a recipe for fish?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Fish

Ingredients:
• Fish fillet
• Ketchup
• Lemon juice
• Worcestershire sauce
• Sugar
• Onions
• Hot sauce
• Lemon wedge

Steps:
1. Place fish in an baking pan, 13 x 9
2. Mix ketchup, lemon juice, worcestershire, sugar, onion
3. Pour sauce over fish
4. Turn so both sides are coated
5. Cover a refridgerate for 30 minutes
6. Heat oven to 400 degrees
7. Bake uncovered for 15 - 20 minutes
8. Garnish with lemon<|endoftext|>
