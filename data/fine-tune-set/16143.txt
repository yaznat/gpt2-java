Give me a quesadilla recipe.<|endoftext|>Creamy Ranch Pineapple Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Pineapple
• Tortillas
• Chicken breasts
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Hidden valley original ranch dressing
• Onion powder
• Pepper
• Salt
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Heat frying pan with 1 / 4 cup olive oil, mixed with the pepper, salt, and onion powder
2. Thoroughly cook cubed chicken 7-8 minutes add extra pinches of spices where you see fit
3. Meanwhile, prepare tortillas by adding a bed of sharp cheddar, followed by dollops of ranch in the center and around the edges
4. Add chopped pineapples to taste
5. When chicken is finished, add chicken to both tortilla set-ups
6. Then add more cheddar and and ranch around the edges as a sealant
7. Place 3rd and 4th tortillas ontop to finish
8. Add quesadillas to same frying pan
9. Do not clean off, taste from chicken cooking must remain
10. "fry" on either side until cheese has melted
11. Cut into 4 squares and serve!<|endoftext|>
