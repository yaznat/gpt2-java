Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Jalapeno pepper
• Green onions
• Whipping cream
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Old cheddar cheese
• Pork sausage
• Sour cream
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Cook the sausage meat with green onions in a skillet until browned
2. Drain fat and transfer to a bowl
3. Set aside
4. In a bowl mix / whisk together the eggs with jalapeno pepper, whipping cream, salt and pepper
5. Melt the butter in the same skillet over medium heat
6. Add in the egg mixture
7. Cook without stirring until the eggs begin to set on the bottom
8. When the eggs start to set sprinkle the cream cheese cubes over the egg mixture
9. Place a spatula across the bottom of the skillet to lift the eggs so that the uncooked will flow underneath
10. Cook until eggs are set but not overcooked
11. Divide and sprinkle about half of the shredded cheddar cheese onto each tortilla
12. Divide and top with the egg mixture, then cooked sausage meat
13. Then sprinkle with the remaining half of the grated cheddar cheese
14. Fold tortillas over the filling
15. If desired you can place the tortillas on a baking sheet, cover loosley with foil, then warm slightly in oven
16. Serve with sour cream and salsa
17. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
