What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Butter And Ras El Hanout Pasta

Ingredients:
• Pasta
• Almond butter
• Mixed spice
• Salt

Steps:
1. Boils pasta according to package directions
2. On your plate, mix almond butter with ras-el-hanout
3. Drain cooked noodles and pour over almond butter, sprinkle with salt to taste and mix well
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
