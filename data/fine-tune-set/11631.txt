What's a good cake recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Layer Dark Chocolate Cake

Ingredients:
• Boiling water
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Instant coffee granules
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees f
2. Set oven to second-lowest rack
3. Grease three 9-inch round cake pans, then line the bottoms with parchment paper
4. In a heat proof medium mixing bowl mix together the boiling water with cocoa powder and coffee granules until completely combined
5. Allow to cool for about 20 minutes or until room temperature
6. In another bowl sift the flour with baking soda, baking powder and salt
7. Set aside
8. In a large bowl cream butter with both sugars and vanilla until fluffy
9. Add in eggs and beat for about 3-4 minutes
10. Add in the flour mixture alternately with the cocoa mixture until combined
11. Divide the mixture evenly beweeen the cake pans
12. Bake for about 25-35 minutes or until cakes test done<|endoftext|>
