Give me a recipe for stir fry.<|endoftext|>Citrusy Beef And Broccoli Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Low sodium beef broth
• Soy sauce
• Lemon juice
• Cornstarch
• Canola oil
• London broil beef
• Broccoli florets
• Orange zest
• Fresh orange juice
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Whisk broth, soy sauce, lemon juice and cornstarch together and set aside
2. Warm oil in a large skillet over medium high heat until hot and then add beef, turn heat to high and cook, stirring until no longer pink, about 5 minutes
3. Add broccoli and cook, stirring until warmed through, about 3-5 minutes
4. Reduce heat to medium high and then stir in reserved sauce, orange zest and orange juice
5. Cook, stirring constantly, until sauce thickens, about 2-3 minutes
6. Season with salt and / or pepper to your taste
7. Serve over steamed brown rice for extra flavor<|endoftext|>
