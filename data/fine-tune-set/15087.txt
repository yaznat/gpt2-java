Please give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Amy S Fruit Pizza

Ingredients:
• Refrigerated sugar cookie dough
• Cream cheese
• Cool whip
• Marshmallow creme
• Fresh fruit
• Sugar
• Salt
• Cornstarch
• Orange juice
• Lemon juice
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Press cookie dough into greased pizza pan
3. Bake 10-12 minutes
4. Cool
5. Beat cream cheese until fluffy
6. Fold in cool whip and marshamallow creme
7. Spread over cooled crust
8. Arrange fruit on top
9. In saucepan, combine sugar, salt, cornstarch, orange juice, lemon juice and water
10. Cook over medium heat, stirring often
11. Bring to boil and cook until thickened
12. Allow to cool, but not set up
13. Spoon over pizza and chill at least 2 hours<|endoftext|>
