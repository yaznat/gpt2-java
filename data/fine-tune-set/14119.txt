What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus With Tofu And Balsamic Butter

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Garlic cloves
• Butter
• Balsamic vinegar
• Water
• Asparagus
• Black pepper
• Firm tofu
• Thyme

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat
2. Add garlic and cook 1 minute
3. Add butter, vinegar and water
4. Cook another minute then add asparagus and pepper
5. Cook 4 more minutes, until tender
6. Place the asparagus in bowls
7. Put tofu slices in the pan with the sauce, add thyme, and cook until heated through
8. Place on the asparagus, and serve with the remaining sauce<|endoftext|>
