Give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Fakhitha Bel Laban Leg Of Lamb With Yogurt

Ingredients:
• Leg of lamb
• Yogurt
• Tomato paste
• Garlic cloves
• Black pepper
• Cardamom
• Cinnamon
• Cumin
• Saffron
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Clean the meat, slash in several places and put in a baking dish
2. Mix the spices, salt, garlic, yogurt, tomato paste, mayonnaise and oil
3. Spread half of the mixture over the meat
4. Cover with foil, and put in the oven at 350 degrees until the meat is cooked
5. Just before serving, pour the remaining yogurt on top of the meat
6. I marinated the meat overnight
7. Weigh the leg of lamb to determine the amount of time it will need in the oven for rare, medium, or well-done
8. I like to cook roasts at lower temperatures for a long time, so i veered off from the given recipe, and cooked it for 3 hours at 250 fahrenheit
9. If i had had more time, i would probably have cooked it 4 hours at 200 degrees fahrenheit
10. The meat was tender due to both the yogurt and the long cooking
11. My leg of lamb was 1
12. 3 kilos, or 2
13. 8 pounds
14. Serve with flavored basmati rice<|endoftext|>
