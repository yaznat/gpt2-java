Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Bacon
• Chipotle chiles in adobo
• Sour cream
• Milk
• Chicken bouillon cubes

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 350f
2. Wrap chicken breast in a slice or two of bacon depending on size of breast vs
3. Size of bacon
4. Place wrapped chicken breast onto a non-stick oven safe baking sheet / dish
5. Cook until completely done, usually takes about an hour, hour and a half
6. When chicken breast lack about 20 mins from being done, you can start preparing the chipotle sauce
7. Combine chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, , with sour cream and milk, blend in the blender until there are little to no chunks left of the peppers
8. Once you have this blended together add to a small sauce pan and simmer on low to medium heat and add the 2 cube of chicken bullion for flavor
9. Add salt and pepper as desired
10. To serve: place chicken on plate and pour chipotle sauce on top
11. Also works greats as an appetizer at parties, cut chicken into small bite size pieces and serve sauce in a bowl<|endoftext|>
