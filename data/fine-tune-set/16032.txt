Ingredients:
• Cooked extra lean ground beef
• Taco sauce
• Burrito-size whole wheat tortillas
• Blue cheese
• Frozen chopped spinach
• Red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Stir together the beef and taco sauce
2. Lay 2 tortillas flat and divide beef mixture between them
3. Sprinkle each tortilla with half the cheese, half the spinach and half the red pepper flakes
4. Top each with a remaining tortilla
5. Put a large, heavy nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
6. Place one quesadilla in skillet and cook until brown spots form on bottom of tortilla, a few minutes each side
7. Flip and repeat
8. Remove to a plate and cover to keep warm
9. Repeat with the remaining ingredients
10. Cut each quesadilla into wedges and serve<|endoftext|>
