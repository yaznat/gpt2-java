• Frozen bread dough
• Butter
• Garlic salt
• Dried basil
• Pizza seasoning
• Deli ham
• Mozzarella cheese
• Smoked turkey breast
• Cheddar cheese
• Pizza sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. On a baking sheet coated with non-stick cooking spray, roll dough into a small rectangle
3. Let sit for 5 to 10 minutes

5. Roll out dough into a 14 inch by 10 inch rectangle
6. Brush with half the butter mixture
