Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Allergy Friendly Double Chocolate Brownies

Ingredients:
• Mashed banana
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Pitted dates
• Agave nectar
• Pure vanilla extract
• Chocolate chips
• Buckwheat flour
• Brown rice flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Baking powder
• Xanthan gum
• Sea salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180 c / 350f
2. Lightly grease a square 8-by-8-inch baking pan
3. Sift together the cocoa powder, flours, baking powder, xanthan and salt into the bowl of your stand mixer
4. Gently heat on a low flame one cup of the chocolate chips and agave until just melted and then stir in vanilla
5. Pour this mixture into the dry ingredients and mix gently until well combined
6. Mix together mashed banana and apple sauce
7. Fold in this mixture into the chocolate batter
8. Fold in chopped dates
9. Spoon mixture into pan and spread evenly
10. Bake for 20 minutes and then sprinkle the extra 1 / 2 cup chocolate chips over the mix
11. Place back in the oven for another 15-20 minutes
12. The brownies are done when an inserted skewer comes out clean
13. Let cool at room temperature before serving<|endoftext|>
