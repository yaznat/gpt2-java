Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazing Italian Tomato Soup

Ingredients:
• Fire-roasted tomatoes
• Roma tomatoes
• Celery
• Onion
• Minced garlic clove
• Chicken broth
• Italian seasoning
• Ground beef

Steps:
1. Brown and drain your ground beef
2. Set it aside in a covered bowl until ready to use
3. Chop your onions, celery, and garlic finely
4. Slice your tomatoes discarding the end pieces
5. Lightly spray the bottom of a 5 quart stewpot with cooking spray
6. Saute your onions, celery, and garlic until the celery is fork-tender
7. Add your chicken broth
8. If you prefer your soup with a thicker consistency, reduce the amount of chicken broth, or increase the amount of fire roasted tomatoes
9. Bring to a gentle boil, then add all other ingredients except the ground beef
10. Continue to boil about 5 minutes
11. Reduce heat to simmer and add your browned ground beef
12. Omit the groud beef if you want a vegetarian soup
13. Cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes
14. Add salt, pepper, and more italian seasoning to suit your taste preferences
15. Serve with a variety of toppings on the side which your guests can add on their own
16. Shredded italian cheeses, grated parmesan cheese, chopped spring onions, and seasoned bread crumbs work well
17. Your guests will think you were born in sicily as they warm up their taste buds with a bowl of your soup<|endoftext|>
