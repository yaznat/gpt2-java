Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Shortening
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Eggs
• Skim milk

Steps:
1. Preheat donut bites machine for 5 minutes
2. While it is preheating, mix all dry ingredients together
3. Add and mix wet ingredients with dry ingredients
4. Mix until smooth
5. Lightly oil the surface of the donut bites machine
6. Fill each donut hole with a heaping tablespoon of batter
7. Close and lock unit into place
8. Bake for 3 - 3 1 / 2 minutes or until donuts are browned
9. Remove donuts from machine and repeat with remaining batter
10. For chocolate flavored donuts: add 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder and adjust sugar to taste<|endoftext|>
