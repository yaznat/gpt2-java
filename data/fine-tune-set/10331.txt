Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Egg
• Almond extract
• All-purpose flour
• Confectioners' sugar
• Milk
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f beat butter and sugar, in large bowl, with electric mixer on high speed until creamy
2. Add egg and almond extract, mixing well
3. Gradually add flour, mixing well
4. Rolldough into 1-inch balls
5. Place 2 inches apart on greased baking sheet
6. Dip a flat-bottomed glass into sugar and flatten balls
7. Bake 10-12 minutes until bottom of cookies are golden brown
8. Cool 1 minute on baking sheet, then remove to wire racks to cool completely
9. To prepare frosting, combine sugar and almond extract until smooth
10. Stir in milk, as needed, until frosting is spreading consistency
11. Spread on each cookie
12. Top with an almond slice<|endoftext|>
