Ingredients:
• Alligator sausage
• Crayfish tail
• Smoked sausage
• Converted rice
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Onion soup
• Beef consomme
• Green onion
• Mushroom
• Garlic
• Bay leaves
• Creole seasoning
• Black pepper
• Butter

Steps:
1. Mix alligator sausage with remaining ingredients
2. Place in a covered casserole or pan and cook in preheated 350 degree oven for 1 hour
3. After 30 minutes, stir well and continue cooking for the remaining time
