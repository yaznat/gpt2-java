What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Marinated Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Lemon juice
• Ground ginger
• Garlic
• Pork loin chops
• Butter

Steps:
1. N a small bowl, combine soy sauce, lemon juice, ginger, and garlic
2. Mix well
3. Place chops in a 9- x 13-inch baking dish and pour soy sauce mixture over top, turning chops to coat thoroughly
4. Cover and chill 2 hours, turning after 1 hour
5. Remove chops from marinade and discard marinade
6. In a large skillet over medium heat, melt butter
7. Cook chops 5 to 7 minutes per side, or until cooked through
8. Serve immediately
9. To make a sauce for the pork chops, reseve the marinade and bring it to a boil
10. Simmer 2 to 3 minutes, the pour over the chops<|endoftext|>
