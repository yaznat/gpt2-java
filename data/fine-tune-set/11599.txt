Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Butter
• Egg
• Flour
• Salt
• Cocoa
• Cinnamon
• Clove
• Nutmeg
• Buttermilk
• Baking soda
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Dissolve baking soda in the buttermilk and set aside
3. Cream sugar, butter, and buttermilk together
4. Sift dry ingredients together
5. Add alternately with buttermilk, beating well after each addition
6. Pour into greased and floured 9x13" pan
7. Bake 25-30 minutes
8. Spread icing over hot cake
9. For icing
10. Mix 5 t
11. Butter, 7 t
12. Brown sugar, 5 t
13. Cream, and 1 / 2 cup cocoa together
14. Spread over hot cake and return to oven and broil until icing bubbles and is light brown on cake<|endoftext|>
