Ingredients:
• Mixed salad greens
• Chili
• Black olives
• Tortilla chips
• Chopped tomato
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Chives
• Ranch dressing

Steps:
1. Place mixed greens in a large salad bowl
2. Add warmed chili meat, sliced olives, tortilla chips, tomatoes and shredded cheddar cheese
3. Add a dollop of sour cream and chives on top
4. To serve, add ranch dressing and mix thoroughly
5. Divide among 4 salad plates
6. Place additional dollop of sour cream and chives on each plate and serve
7. Yummy!<|endoftext|>
