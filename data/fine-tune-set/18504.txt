What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Good For You Strawberry Waffles

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• All-purpose flour
• Ground flax seeds
• Sugar substitute
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Strawberry
• Skim milk
• Natural applesauce
• Vanilla extract
• Egg

Steps:
1. Combine flours, flax seed, splenda, baking powder and salt into a medium-sized bowl with a whisk
2. Form a well in the center in preparation of wet ingredients
3. Into a blender, place strawberries, milk, applesauce, vanilla and egg and puree until smooth
4. Add the wet strawberry mixture to the dry flour mixture and whisk just until all is moistened
5. Do not overmix
6. Spoon about 1 / 2 cup batter onto preheated waffle iron that's been coated with cooking spray
7. Cook until done and crispy
8. Serve hot with toppings of your choice: we like sugar-free syrup, cool whip free and extra sliced strawberries<|endoftext|>
