Ingredients:
• Frankfurter
• Hot dog bun
• White onion
• Cheddar cheese
• Ground chuck
• Tomato paste
• Sugar
• Yellow mustard
• Dried onion
• Chili powder
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Celery seed
• Ground cumin
• Black pepper
• Water

Steps:
1. Cook the ground chuck in a pan and break into small pieces
2. Season with salt and pepper
3. Add in the remaining ingredients
4. Simmer for about 30 minutes until thickened
5. Boil some water in a pot, and add in however many frankfurters you want
6. Boil for about 8 to 10 minutes
7. Place the frankfurter in a hot dog bun and spread on 3 tablespoons of the
8. Sauce you just made
9. Spread the cheddar cheese on top, and you're done!<|endoftext|>
