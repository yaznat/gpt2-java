Give me a cake recipe.<|endoftext|>A Healthier Apple Nut Snack Cake

Ingredients:
• Egg beaters egg substitute
• Vanilla
• Splenda brown sugar blend
• Cinnamon
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Apples
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f and grease 8-inch square pan
2. In large mixing bow, beat egg beaters and vanilla until frothy
3. Gradually add splenda brown sugar, continuing to beat after each addition
4. Add cinnamon, baking powder and salt
5. Gradually add flour continuing to beat after each addition
6. Stir in apples and nuts
7. Pour into greased pan
8. Bake 30 to 45 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
9. Serve warm or cool
10. Can be topped with whipped cream<|endoftext|>
