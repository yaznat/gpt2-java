Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Curried Lamb Loaf

Ingredients:
• Ground lamb
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Eggs
• Cayenne pepper
• Curry powder
• Plain breadcrumbs
• Soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Diced tomatoes
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. Combine lamb with onion, garlic, eggs, curry, 1 / 2 tsp cayenne, salt and breadcrumbs
2. Shape into a loaf, and place in a 9x12-inch pyrex baking dish
3. Combine tomatoes with worcestershire, reserved cayenne, soy sauce, and brown sugar
4. Pour this over the loaf, and bake in a preheated 400f oven for 45 minutes to one hour
5. Serve with chutney and cooked basmati rice<|endoftext|>
