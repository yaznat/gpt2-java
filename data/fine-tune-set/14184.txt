What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Cambodian Manor Kho To Hu Caramelized Pineapple And Tofu

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Cornstarch
• Olive oil
• Pineapple chunk
• Vegetarian oyster sauce
• Water
• Garlic clove
• Sugar
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Green onions
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Place tofu between paper towels and press with cast iron frying pan or something heavy for about 10 minutes to get rid of excess moisture
2. Cut into bite size pieces
3. Dredge in cornstarch to coat, shaking off excess
4. Fry in oil till lightly browned and crispy, about 10 minutes
5. If using canned chunk pineapple, drain juice
6. Put pineapple, garlic, vegetarian oyster sauce and water in a small pot
7. Add sugar, salt, black pepper and green onion
8. Stir well
9. Cook on medium heat till the water is reduced and vegetables coated
10. Add tofu and stir to coat
11. Top with cilantro
12. Serve hot with rice<|endoftext|>
