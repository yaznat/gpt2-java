Ingredients:
• Lean lamb
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Butter
• Beef broth
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Ground ginger
• Tomatoes
• All-purpose flour
• Carrot
• Frozen peas
• Cooked brown rice

Steps:
1. Over medium heat, melt the butter in a large non-stick skillet
2. Add the lamb, onion, and garlic and cook about 5 minutes
3. Stir in the broth, curry powder, salt, ginger, tomato, and flour
4. Stir to mix well
5. Cover and simmer 30 to 40 minutes, or until the lamb is tender
6. Stirring occasionally and adding water if the liquid evaporates
7. Toss the grated carrot and peas with the rice in a small non-stick saucepan, heat through
8. Divide the lamb mixture onto serving plates and spoon the rice pilaf in the middle<|endoftext|>
