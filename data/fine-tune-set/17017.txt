Ingredients:
• Plum tomatoes
• Red onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Jalapeno pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh lime juice
• Sugar
• Salt
• Chili powder
• Pepper
• Water
• Fresh shrimp
• Avocado
• Lime slice
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Process first 10 ingredients in a blender or food processor until smooth, stopping to scrape down the sides
2. Cover and chill sauce for up to a week
3. Bring 6 cups water to a boil
4. Add shrimp and cook 2-3 minutes or just until shrimp turn pink
5. Drain and rinse with cold water
6. Chill up to 24 hours
7. Peel shrimp, leaving tails on
8. Devein, if desired
9. Stir avocado into sauce
10. Spoon sauce evenly into 6 chilled martini glasses or small bowls
11. Arrange 5 shrimp around the edge of each glass, with the fat part in the glass and the tails hanging down the sides of the glass
12. Garnish, if desired, with lime slices
13. Serve with lime-flavored tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
