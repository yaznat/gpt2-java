Ingredients:
• Pepperoncini pepper
• Marinated artichoke hearts
• Green olives
• Deli ham
• American cheese
• Burrito-size flour tortilla

Steps:
1. -preheat oven to 375f
2. -place a piece of foil on a sheet pan, and treat with nonstick cooking spray
3. -microwave tortilla for 20 seconds on high power
4. -place vegetables in center of warm tortilla and roll up egg roll-style, and place seam-side down on sheet pan
5. -cut two slits in top of sandwich
6. -bake for 20 minute
7. -let stand for a couple of minutes
8. -cut in half and enjoy<|endoftext|>
