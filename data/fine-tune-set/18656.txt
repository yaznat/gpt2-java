Can you give me a pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Aunt Carrie S Indian Pudding

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Water
• Yellow cornmeal
• Butter
• Molasses
• Flour
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Egg
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f grease an 8" 8" baking dish
2. Set aside
3. Put 2 cups of the milk and 2 cups hot water into a medium pot and bring just to a boil
4. Slowly pour in cornmeal while whisking constantly
5. Reduce heat to medium and cook, whisking constantly, until very thick, 12-14 minutes
6. Remove from heat and whisk in remaining butter
7. Set cornmeal mixture aside
8. Whisk together molasses, flour, cinnamon, ginger, salt, and egg in a large bowl
9. Pour molasses mixture into cornmeal mixture while whisking constantly
10. Transfer batter to prepared baking dish
11. Pour remaining milk evenly over batter
12. Bake, rotating once, until pudding is set but still soft and a skin has formed over the top, about 1 hour
13. Set pudding aside to let cool to room temperature
14. Spoon pudding into bowls and top with scoops of vanilla ice cream<|endoftext|>
