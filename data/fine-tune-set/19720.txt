What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Blue Potato Oven Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Ground pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. Cut potatoes into 1 / 2" wedges
3. Drop in cold water and soak 45 - 60 minutes
4. Thouroughly drain potatoes and pat dry with paper towels
5. Toss potatoes with oil and salt and pepper
6. Place in shallow baking pan and bake 20 minutes
7. Stir and cook an additional 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
