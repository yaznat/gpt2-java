Ingredients:
• Vital wheat gluten
• Nutritional yeast
• Ground cumin
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Mushroom stock
• Soy sauce
• Vegetarian worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 325f
2. Mix together the dry ingredients in a large bowl
3. Mix together the liquid ingredients in a smaller bowl, then stir them into the dry ingredients until completely blended
4. Knead the dough in the bowl for a couple minutes, then place on a sheet of foil and shape into a log about 6 to 8 inches long
5. Wrap the foil tightly around the log and twist the ends
6. Bake for 80 minutes
7. Slice or chop as desired, and serve immediately or store in the refrigerator or freezer<|endoftext|>
