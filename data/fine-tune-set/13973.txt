What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Broiled Seasoned Lamb Chops

Ingredients:
• Parsley
• Vegetable oil
• Lemon juice
• Soy sauce
• Garlic clove
• Ground ginger
• Lamb loin chops

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients, except the lamb, in a small bowl
2. On rack in broiler pan, arrange the chops
3. Brush chops with the mixture and broil 4" from the heat for 10 minutes, or until desired doneness
4. Turning once and brushing with the parsley mixture occasionally<|endoftext|>
