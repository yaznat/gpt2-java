Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>Amish Pumpkin Bread

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Pumpkin
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Clove
• Cinnamon
• Allspice
• Nutmeg
• Water
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Mix sugar, oil and eggs together
2. Add pumpkin
3. Then add dry ingredients and finally water
4. Stir just until mixed
5. Add nuts if desired
6. Pour into 2 greased and floured pans
7. Bake at 350 for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
