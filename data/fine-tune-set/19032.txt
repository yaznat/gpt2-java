What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Individual Frosty Pumpkin Custards

Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Pumpkin pie mix
• Whipped topping
• Walnut halves

Steps:
1. Line 6 muffin cups with paper baking cups
2. Soften 1 / 2 pin vanilla ice cream slightly
3. Fold 1 / 2 can pumpkin pie mix into ice cream
4. Divide among aper-lined cups
5. Cover and freeze until firm, at least 3 hours
6. Garnish with whipped topping and walnut halves<|endoftext|>
