Ingredients:
• French style green beans
• Vegetable stock
• Dill
• Chives
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Mince dill and chives
2. Bring vegetable stock to a boil, add beans and cook 1-2 minutes
3. Reduce heat to low and cook for 10 minutes or until tender
4. Remove beans from stock and toss with chives and dill
5. Cover tightly and let sit for 1-2 minutes
6. Add pepper if desired<|endoftext|>
