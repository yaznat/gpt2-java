What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Almond Joy Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Egg
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Sour cream
• Vanilla extract
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Sweetened flaked coconut
• Toasted almond

Steps:
1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl
2. In a separate bowl beat the egg
3. Add the milk, oil, and vanilla
4. Add the wet to dry ingredients and mix
5. Add the sour cream to the mixture until smooth
6. Add the chocolate chips, the coconut and the toasted almonds, and just mix until blended
7. Grease 6 jumbo muffin tins, and fill them 3 / 4 full
8. Top the muffins with few chocolate chips, almonds and coconut, and bake for 25 minutes at 375<|endoftext|>
