Give me a sandwich recipe.<|endoftext|>Bagel Chicken Salad Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Bacon
• Celery
• Green onions
• Dried basil leaves
• Garlic powder
• Mayonnaise
• Bagels
• Cucumber

Steps:
1. In medium bowl, mix chicken, bacon, celery, onions, basil, garlic powder and mayonnaise
2. Spread chicken mixture on bottom halves of bagels
3. Top with cucumber slices
4. Cover with top halves of bagels
5. Serve immediately, or wrap each sandwich in plastic wrap and refrigerate up to 24 hours<|endoftext|>
