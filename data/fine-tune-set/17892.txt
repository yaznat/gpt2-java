Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Cocoa powder
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Water
• Shortening
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Apple pie filling
• Lemon juice
• Ground cinnamon
• Whipped topping

Steps:
1. Prepare apple topping: stir together apple pie filling, lemon juice and ground cinnamon in small bowl
2. Set aside
3. Heat oven to 375f
4. Grease and flour 9-inch square baking pan
5. Stir together flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder and salt in medium bowl
6. Add water, shortening, egg and vanilla
7. Beat until smooth and well blended
8. Spread into prepared pan
9. Carefully spoon apple topping evenly over chocolate batter to within 1 / 2-inch from edges
10. Do not stir
11. Bake 35 to 40 minutes or until chocolate is set and cake-like
12. Cool completely in pan on wire rack
13. Cut into squares
14. Serve topped with whipped topping or ice cream, if desired<|endoftext|>
