What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>A Beautiful Salad

Ingredients:
• Baby spinach leaves
• Red onion
• Mandarin oranges
• Dried cranberries
• Sliced almonds
• Feta cheese
• Balsamic vinaigrette

Steps:
1. Simply put all ingredients except the nuts, into a large salad bowl
2. Toss with vinaigrette
3. Serve and enjoy!
4. I do not add the nuts to the salad, but serve them separately at the table to avoid their becoming less then crisp<|endoftext|>
