Ingredients:
• Pepperoni
• Bacon
• Mushrooms
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Mozzarella cheese
• Tomato sauce
• Garlic
• Oregano
• Thyme
• Salt
• Beer
• Olive oil
• 15 inch pizza crusts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450
2. In large skillet brown pepperoni and bacon in oil
3. Drain and set aside
4. Saute mushrooms, green peppers and onions until soft
5. Add all other ingredients except cheese and stir well, simmering for 15 minutes
6. Divide sauce between 2 pizzas and cover with cheese
7. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until crust is golden brown<|endoftext|>
