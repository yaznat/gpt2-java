What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Anise Coffee And Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Ground coffee
• Anise seeds
• Cold water
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Measure coffee and anise seeds into a filter-lined coffeemaker basket
2. Pour water into water compartment
3. Place pot on heating element
4. Let water drip through basket into pot
5. Meanwhile, carefully cut ice cream from package and cut into 8 long pieces
6. Place ice cream into cups
7. Pour about 1 / 4 cup of coffee around ice cream
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
