Can you give me a muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Wheat Germ Muffins

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Honey
• Eggs
• Powdered milk
• Wheat germ
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Almond extract
• Vanilla
• Slivered almonds
• Raisins
• Coconut

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Throw everything in a bowl and mix until well blended
3. Pour batter into greased or lined muffin tins and bake for 15-20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
