Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>My Super Easy Microwave Omelette

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Skim milk
• Mushroom
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Ground black pepper
• Fresh parsley
• Marinara sauce

Steps:
1. Cut mushroom into chunks and fry until tender
2. Alternately use canned mushrooms
3. Rinse microwave safe bowl with cold water
4. Combine egg, milk, chili powder, salt, and pepper
5. Beat with fork until frothy
6. Sprinkle with mushrooms
7. Cover with microwave safe plate or plastic wrap
8. Cook 30-60 seconds on high in microwave until omelet starts to rise and look fluffy
9. Put some tomato puree or your favorite pasta sauce on top and sprinkle with fresh parsley to serve<|endoftext|>
