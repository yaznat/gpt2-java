What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>Almond Jelly With Fruit Salad

Ingredients:
• Caster sugar
• Gelatin powder
• Almond essence
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Lychees

Steps:
1. Place sugar and 2 cups water in a large saucepan and bring to a boil
2. Stir throughout to dissolve the sugar
3. Remove from heat
4. Sprinkle the gelatine over and stir until dissolved
5. Stir in the almond essence, condensed milk and 3 cups of water until well combined
6. Pour mixture into a 19 x 29 cm slice tin and refrigerate for 6 hours or overnight until set
7. Using a sharp knife, cut jelly into 2 cm cubes
8. Divide among bowls with fruits<|endoftext|>
