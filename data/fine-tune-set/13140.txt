Ingredients:
• Clam
• Mussels
• Yellow cornmeal
• Water
• Vegetable oil
• Onions
• Red bell pepper
• Yellow bell pepper
• Jalapeno pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Tomatoes
• Fresh gingerroot
• Orange rind
• Clam juice
• Tomato paste
• Medium shrimp
• Green onions
• Fresh parsley
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt
• Pepper
• Evaporated skim milk
• Coconuts
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Instructions
2. Place clams and mussels in a large bowl
3. Cover with cold water
4. Sprinkle with cornmeal
5. Let stand 30 minutes
6. Drain and rinse
7. Bring 2 cups water to a boil in a large dutch oven
8. Add clams and mussels
9. Cover and cook 6 minutes or until shells open
10. Discard any unopened shells
11. Remove clams and mussels from pan
12. Set aside
13. Discard cooking liquid
14. Heat oil over medium heat
15. Add onion, bell peppers, jalapeno pepper, and garlic
16. Saute 8 minutes
17. Add tomato, ginger, orange
18. Rind, clam juice and tomato paste
19. Coook 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
20. Add shrimp
21. Cook 5 minutes or until done
22. Remove from heat
23. Stir in clams, mussels, green onions, and next 5 ingredients
24. Ladle soup into large
25. Bowls
26. Speinkle with coconut
27. Garnish with cilantro sprigs<|endoftext|>
