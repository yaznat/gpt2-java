Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Half-and-half cream
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Bananas
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease a 13" x 9" baking pan
3. Cream 3 / 4 cup butter and sugar until light and fluffy
4. Add eggs and vanilla, beating well
5. Stir in cream
6. Mix well
7. Sift together dry ingredients
8. Add to creamed batter
9. Mixing well
10. Then fold in sliced bananas
11. Spread mixture into prepared baking pan
12. For streusel: mix together brown sugar, flour and cinnamon
13. Cut in butter, until mixture is crumbly
14. Sprinkle on top of coffeecake
15. Bake for 45-50 minutes, or until cake tests done<|endoftext|>
