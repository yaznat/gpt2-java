Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Annika S Pizza Pasta Sauce

Ingredients:
• Tomatoes
• Diced tomatoes
• Juice
• Onion
• Garlic
• Basil
• Thyme
• Oregano
• Brown sugar
• Tomato paste

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in slow cooker and cook on low, overnight or 8 hours
2. Puree in batches, in a blender until smooth
3. Reheat and serve however you like it
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
