Give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>Absolutely Addictive Roast Pork Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Pork roast
• Sea salt
• Cracked pepper
• Butter
• Au jus mix
• Water
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• French bread

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees farenheit
2. Sprinkle pork roast with salt and pepper and place on a rack in a roasting pan
3. Roast for two hours or until temperature of center of pork registers 150-160 degrees
4. Melt butter over roast while it is still in the roasting pan
5. Remove roast from pan, saving the drippings and juice
6. Add au jus mix and water to drippings and cook according to directions on package
7. Place bread in the oven and let it crisp a bit
8. The heat will help to melt the cheese better than just the heat of the meat will
9. Let roast cool for about 10 minutes, then slice thinly
10. Pile pork and cheese on bread while meat is still hot
11. Serve with au jus dipping gravy on the side and lots o' napkins<|endoftext|>
