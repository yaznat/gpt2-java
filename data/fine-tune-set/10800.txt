Please give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>5 Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Cut green beans
• Wax beans
• Garbanzo beans
• Dark red kidney beans
• Black beans
• Red bell pepper
• Red onion
• Balsamic vinegar
• Dijon mustard
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Mrs. dash seasoning mix

Steps:
1. Mix the oil, vinegar, mustard and mrs
2. Dash in a shaker jar
3. Pour the dressing over all ingredients in a large bowl and toss thoroughly but gently to avoid breaking the green beans and wax beans into pieces<|endoftext|>
