Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Alfredo sauce
• Cheese tortellini
• Pepper
• Fresh parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat a 325 degree oven
2. If using dry tortellini, boil water and add tortellini, cook as directed and drain
3. In a large oven safe skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat
4. Press garlic through garlic press and saute in olive oil until slightly browned
5. Add diced chicken breasts and saute until juices run clear
6. If using package of alfredo sauce, mix ingredients for sauce and cook as directed
7. Add tortellini, alfredo sauce and fresh grated pepper to chicken
8. Mix well
9. Grate parmesan cheese on top of mixture and put in oven until cheese starts to melt<|endoftext|>
