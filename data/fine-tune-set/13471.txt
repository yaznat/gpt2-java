Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Another Beef Slow Cooker Pot Roast Hubby Kids Favorite

Ingredients:
• Chuck roast
• Light olive oil
• Carrots
• Celery ribs
• Onion
• Potatoes
• Beef broth
• Ketchup
• Brown sugar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Lawry's seasoned salt
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. 1
2. Salt and pepper meat and brown in olive oil on both sides-then set aside
3. 2
4. Put half of the carrots, onion, celery and potatoes into the crock pot
5. 3
6. Place meat over
7. 4
8. Put the other half of the veggies / potatoes on top of the meat
9. 5
10. Mix together the beef broth, brown sugar, ketchup, worcestershire sauce, and seasoned salt
11. Pour mix over the meat / veggies
12. 6
13. Cook on high setting for first hour then turn down to low and cook for another 7 hours
14. Test seasonings before serving and add additional salt and pepper if neccessary<|endoftext|>
