What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Caramelized Mushrooms Onions Cheese Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Mushroom
• Onions
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Brown sugar
• Fresh garlic
• Sherry wine
• Flour tortillas
• Cooked chicken breast
• Arugula
• Gruyere cheese
• Jalapeno cheddar cheese
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Heat a large nonstick skillet that has been sprayed with cooking spray over medium-high heat
2. Add olive oil to pan
3. Swirl to coat
4. Add mushrooms, sliced onions, salt, pepper, crushed red pepper & sugar to pan
5. Saut 20-25 minutes
6. Stir in garlic, and saut 30 seconds
7. Add vinegar or sherry
8. Cook 30 seconds or until liquid almost evaporates
9. Use rondele spreadable cheese on half of each tortilla
10. Arrange half of mushroom mixture over half of each tortilla
11. Top each tortilla with 1 / 2 cup chicken, 1 / 2 cup arugula or spinach, and 1 / 4 cup cheese
12. Fold tortillas in half
13. Wipe pan clean with a paper towel
14. Heat pan over medium heat
15. Coat pan with cooking spray
16. Add tortillas to pan
17. Place a heavy skillet on top of tortillas
18. Cook 2 minutes on each side or until crisp and cheese is a bit runny
19. Serve with a corona y que les aproveche!<|endoftext|>
