Ingredients:
• Cold water
• Cinnamon sticks
• Whole cloves
• Tea bags
• Honey

Steps:
1. Place the water in a large pot
2. Tie the cinnamon sticks and cloves in cheesecloth
3. Place in the water
4. Bring to a boil
5. Simmer about 10 minutes
6. Remove pot from heat
7. Remove the cheesecloth with the cinnamon sticks and cloves, and discard
8. Add tea bags to pot, if using, and allow to steep about 5 minutes
9. Discard tea bags
10. Add desired amount of sweetener, if using
11. Stir until sweetener is dissolved
12. Refrigerate until ready to serve
13. To serve, pour in tall glasses filled with ice
14. Variation: mint iced tea
15. For minty iced tea, follow the same instructions as above, except: add about 1 cup of fresh mint leaves, slightly crushed to release their flavor, or two tablespoons of dried mint to the boiling water, and eliminate the cinnamon and cloves
16. Remove tea bags, and strain mint leaves before adding sweetener
17. Sweeten as desired
18. Serve with ice<|endoftext|>
