Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Sour cream
• Vanilla extract
• Apples
• Crumb topping
• Glaze
• Light brown sugar
• Water

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees f
2. Lightly grease a 13 by 9-inch glass baking dish with 2 teaspoons of the butter
3. In a large bowl, cream together the remaining stick of butter and sugar until light and fluffy
4. Add the eggs 1 at a time, beating after the addition of each
5. In a separate bowl or on a piece of parchment, sift together the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt
6. Add to the wet ingredients, alternating with the sour cream and vanilla
7. Fold in the apples
8. Pour into the prepared baking dish, spreading out to the edges
9. Topping
10. In a bowl, combine the sugar, flour, cinnamon, and butter and mix until it resembles coarse crumbs
11. Sprinkle the topping over the cake
12. Bake until golden brown
13. Remove from the oven and let cool for at least 10 minutes
14. Glaze
15. In a bowl, combine the sugar, vanilla, and water and mix until smooth
16. Drizzle the cake with the glaze and let harden slightly
17. Cut into squares and serve<|endoftext|>
