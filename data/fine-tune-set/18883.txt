Give me a custard recipe.<|endoftext|>Cherry And Almond Clafouti Baked Custard

Ingredients:
• Cherries
• Almond meal
• Caster sugar
• Milk
• Eggs
• Icing sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180c
2. Grease a 20cm round pie dish with butter or margarine
3. Spread the fruit in the dish
4. In a mixing bowl combine the almond meal and the sugar
5. Separately, whisk together the milk and the eggs
6. Slowly add the eggs and milk to the almond meal and sugar
7. Mixing until fully combined
8. Pour the almond custard mixture over the cherries
9. Bake for approx 30 minutes or until the clafouti is just set
10. Dust with icing sugar
11. Serve with cream or ice-cream if desired
12. The clafouti can also be made in ramekin dishes for individual serves
13. Just watch the cooking time as you will need less time<|endoftext|>
