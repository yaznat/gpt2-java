Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Whole chestnuts
• Ketchup
• Peach baby food
• Sugar
• Salt

Steps:
1. Cut bacon strips lengthwise and then halfwise
2. Wrap each chestnut, secure with a toothpick
3. Place in an ungreased 1392 baking pan
4. Bake uncovered for 25 minutes at 350
5. Can turn if needed
6. In a small bowl combine the remaining ingredients
7. Drizzle over water chestnuts
8. Bake 25 to 35 minutes longer or until bacon is crisp<|endoftext|>
