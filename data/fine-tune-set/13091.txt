Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Beer Batter For Fish

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Water
• Beer
• Egg
• Vegetable oil
• Fish fillets

Steps:
1. Stir together flour, baking powder and salt
2. Make a well in center
3. Pour in water, beer and egg, whisking to make a smooth batter
4. Let stand 20 minutes
5. Heat oil in large saucepan to 350
6. Dip fish fillets in batter, adding to hot oil one at a time
7. Cook about 5 minutes, turning once or twice, until golden and crisp
8. Remove to paper towel-lined plate
9. Repeat with remaining fish<|endoftext|>
