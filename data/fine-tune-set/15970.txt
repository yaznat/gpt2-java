Ingredients:
• Beef roast
• Beef bouillon
• Onion
• Stewed tomatoes
• Diced green chilies
• Green chili salsa
• Oregano
• Garlic powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cornstarch
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Place roast in slow cooker with bouillon
2. Cook on low at least 10 hours, until meat shreds easily with fork
3. Reserve broth
4. Shred meat into 3 quart saucepan
5. Add broth, onion, tomatoes, chiles, and salsa
6. Add oregano, garlic, salt and pepper
7. Thicken with cornstarch mixed with a bit of broth
8. Bring to boil and heat through, until onion is translucent
9. Put mixture in center of warmed tortilla and fold<|endoftext|>
