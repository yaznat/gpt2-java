What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Beef Loaf

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Fresh breadcrumbs
• Milk
• Eggs
• Onion soup mix
• Ketchup
• Brown sugar
• Ground mustard
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. For the meat loaf mix together the ground beef, breadcrumbs, milk, eggs, and onion soup mix
3. I mix it together in the food processor
4. Grease a loaf pan
5. Add meatloaf to pan and press lightly
6. Bake for 1 hour and drain any drippings from pan
7. Be careful the loaf does not fall out of the pan as you drain the fat
8. Mix together ketchup, brown sugar, mustard and worcestershire sauce
9. For my family, i double the sauce ingredients
10. Spoon over meat loaf
11. Place meat loaf back into the to oven for 30 minutes or until desired temperature is reached
12. Cut into slices to serve<|endoftext|>
