Can you give me a pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Alfredo Prosciutto Pizza

Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• Prosciutto
• Green onions
• Alfredo sauce
• Fresh basil
• Monterey jack cheese
• Cornmeal

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 450 degrees
2. Spread pizza dough out onto a 10x15 ornmeal lined baking sheet,
3. Spread alfredo sauce evenly over crust
4. Divide basil, green onions and prosciutto evenly over sauce
5. Distribute jack cheese over all
6. Bake for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
