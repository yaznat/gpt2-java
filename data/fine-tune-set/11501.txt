Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Sugar
• Butter
• Ground cinnamon
• Bread mix
• Water
• Yeast
• Pecans
• Sultana

Steps:
1. Mash the bananas, and mix mashed bananas with butter, sugar and cinnamon
2. Add all ingredients to bread machine pan in the order the manufacturer tells you to do so
3. Turn bread machine on - use the 'sweet' cycle if yours has one
4. If not, you will need to watch out that the bread doesn't burn at the end
5. Keep an eye on the dough as it's mixing - if it looks too sticky, try adding a tablespoon of flour at a time til it looks right
6. Don't add more than this at a time, otherwise it may be too much!
7. If using, add the pecans and / or sultanas when the beeper sounds
8. This recipe will never make a full size loaf for many bread machines, so don't worry if it doesn't look 'full'
9. If i want mine to rise a bit more i just turn the machine off just before it should bake for an extra 20 mins or so, then turn it back on and set it to 'bake only' for 40 minutes
10. Check the bread after about 40 mins of baking - my bread maker has a tendency to burn this loaf if left in for longer than this
11. Enjoy fresh and warm, cold, or toasted, with plenty of butter!<|endoftext|>
