Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Acadia S Salmon Mushroom Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Salmon
• Cooking oil
• Butter
• Celery
• Vidalia onion
• Salt
• Garlic
• Cremini mushroom
• Sesame oil
• Soy sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Honey

Steps:
1. Chop onion into 1 / 8" pieces
2. In wok, add cooking oil and butter on medium heat
3. When the oil starts to bubble around the edges, add celery and onions
4. Sprinkle with salt
5. Add garlic
6. Saute until onions are translucent
7. Add mushrooms and saute until tender about 20 minutes
8. Remove mushroom mixture from wok and set aside
9. Cut raw salmon into 3 / 4" chunks
10. Set aside
11. Add sesame oil, soy sauce, worcestershire and honey to wok mixing well for about 5 minutes on medium low heat
12. Add salmon chunks and saute in sesame oil mixture until salmon is medium rare for about 5 minutes stirring frequently
13. Reduce heat to low
14. Add mushroom mixture and stir to coat fish and mushrooms with sesame oil mixture and cook until mushrooms are hot - 5 more minutes
15. Serve and enjoy<|endoftext|>
