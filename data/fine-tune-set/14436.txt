What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Schnitzel Beans

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onions
• Fresh green beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Salt
• Pepper
• Boiling water

Steps:
1. Dice bacon and fry until crisp
2. Add sliced onions and fry until a light brown
3. Add green beans and brown slightly
4. Add tomatoes, salt, pepper and water
5. Lower heat and cook until beans are tender<|endoftext|>
