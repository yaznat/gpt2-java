Ingredients:
• Pasta
• Green pepper
• Garbanzo beans
• Monterey jack cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Provolone cheese
• Green onions
• Hard salami
• Sliced ripe olives
• Pepperoni
• Fresh chives
• Plum tomatoes
• Basil vinaigrette
• Olive oil
• Red wine vinegar
• Fresh basil
• Garlic clove
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a large salad bowl, combine first 12 ingredients
2. Combine dressing ingredients in a small bowl, pour over salad
3. Toss to coat
4. Cover and refrigerate
