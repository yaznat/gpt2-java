Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Red onion
• Coriander roots
• Coriander leaves
• Red chilies
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Ground turmeric
• Chicken stock
• Chicken breast fillets
• Light coconut milk
• Butternut pumpkin
• Baby corn
• Bean sprouts
• Fresh coriander leaves

Steps:
1. Cook rice in large saucepan of boiling water unril tender, drain
2. Meanwhile, blend onion, coriander root and stem mixture, coriander leaves, chilli, garlic, ginger, and turmeric until smooth
3. Cook paste in medium frying pan, stirring, until fragrant
4. Add stock, chicken and coconut milk
5. Bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat
7. Simmer, covered, 10 minutes
8. Add pumpkin and corn to pan
9. Simmer, uncovered, 10 minutes
10. Serve rice topped with curry, sprouts and extra coriander<|endoftext|>
