Ingredients:
• Swordfish steaks
• Salt
• Pepper
• Olive oil
• Green onion
• Parsley
• Chopped tomato
• Lime wedge

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Sprinkle swordfish with salt and pepper
3. Place fish in a single layer in a baking dish and brush with olive oil to coat heavily
4. Sprinkle green onions over the fish
5. Bake uncovered for about 20 minutes, or until fish flakes easily with a fork
6. Transfer to platter and sprinkle with parsley and garnish with tomato and lime wedges<|endoftext|>
