Ingredients:
• Baking chocolate squares
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Flour
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Melt chocolate and butter in saucepan
2. Over low to medium heat
3. Remove from heat and add all other ingredients and stir well -- until blended
4. Pour into a sprayed 9x13" pan -- bake 20 minutes at 350 --<|endoftext|>
