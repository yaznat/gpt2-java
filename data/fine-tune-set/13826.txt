Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Boneless pork
• Cold water
• Hoisin sauce
• Sesame oil
• Peanut oil
• Sliced mushrooms
• Fresh broccoli florets
• Green onions
• Fresh garlic
• Fresh ginger
• Red pepper flakes
• Fresh orange juice
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl whisk together 2 teaspoons cornstarch with 2 tablespoons soy sauce until smooth
2. Add in the chicken or pork strips, toss to coat and let sit for about 30 minutes
3. In another bowl whisk 2 teaspoons cornstarch with soy sauce, water and hoisin sauce
4. Set aside for a couple of minutes
5. Heat sesame oil and peanut oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat for about 2 minutes
6. Add in chicken or pork mixture and stir-fry for about 2 minutes
7. Add in sliced mushrooms, broccoli florets, green onions, garlic, ginger and red pepper flakes
8. Stir-fry for 2 minutes
9. Stir in the hoisin sauce mixture
10. Stir-fry for 1 minute or until thickened
11. Add in the orange juice
12. Stir until heated through
13. Serve over cooked rice<|endoftext|>
