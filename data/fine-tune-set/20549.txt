Ingredients:
• Turkey gravy
• Extra-sharp cheddar cheese
• Green onion
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Frozen hash brown potatoes
• Nonfat sour cream
• Cooking spray
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine turkey gravy, 1 / 2 cup cheese, onions, pepper, potatoes, and sour cream
3. Spoon into a shallow 1-quart casserole coated with cooking spray
4. Bake at 350 for 35 minutes
5. Sprinkle with 1 / 4 cup cheese and bacon
6. Bake an additional 5 minutes or until the cheese melts<|endoftext|>
