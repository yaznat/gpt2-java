Ingredients:
• Crawfish tail meat
• Olive oil
• Sour cream
• Green onions
• Garlic powder
• Cumin
• Monterey jack cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Guacamole

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. In a skillet, saute green onions in olive oil with the garlic powder
3. Lower the heat and slowly add sour cream, a little bit at a time, blending well
4. Blend in the cumin
5. Add the crawfish and combine well over low heat, about 5 minutes
6. Tear off an extra long piece of foil, so that it lines the bottom of a 13x9 pan, and has plenty hanging off of each end
7. Place a tortilla in one end of the pan
8. Cover it with a big handful of cheese
9. Then spoon on 1 / 6 of the crawfish mixture
10. Cover with another tortilla
11. Place another tortilla right next to this, so that you will be forming two stacks, side by side
12. Repeat process, making another quesadilla, so that you now have two right next to each other
13. Continue making quesadillas until you have two stacks of three
14. Fold the foil over the stacks to completely cover
15. Heat in the oven for 15 minutes, or until cheese has melted
16. Cut each quesadilla in 4-6 pieces
17. Serve with spicy guacamole<|endoftext|>
