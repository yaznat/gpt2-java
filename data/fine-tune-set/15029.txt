Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Short Cut Chicken Fried Rice Oamc

Ingredients:
• Long grain white rice
• Water
• Chicken bouillon cube
• Egg beaters egg substitute
• Chicken breast tenders
• Onion
• Frozen peas and carrots
• Teriyaki marinade
• Light soy sauce
• Ground ginger
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Dissolve bullion in water and cook rice according to directions
2. Plunge container into cold water to cool after cooking or make rice the day before
3. Cook egg beaters in skillet till fluffy
4. Set aside keep warm
5. Saute chicken and onion in same skillet till done about 3 to 4 minutes
6. Add marinade, peas and carrots, and rice
7. Mix well
8. Add soy sauce, ginger, garlic and egg beaters
9. Continue to cook till heated though
10. Cool your favorite way and pack in quart sized freezer bags
11. 1 serving = 1 1 / 2 cups approximately<|endoftext|>
