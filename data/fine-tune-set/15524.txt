Give me a recipe for a taco.<|endoftext|>15 Minute Taco In A Pan

Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Water
• Taco seasoning mix
• Minute white rice
• Mexican blend cheese
• Lettuce
• Chopped tomatoes

Steps:
1. Brown meat in large nonstick skillet
2. Drain
3. Add water and seasoning mix
4. Stir
5. Bring to boil
6. Stir in rice
7. Sprinkle with cheese
8. Cover
9. Reduce heat to low
10. Simmer 5 minute top with lettuce and tomatoes<|endoftext|>
