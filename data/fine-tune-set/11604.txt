Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Shortening
• Milk
• Egg
• Butter
• Fresh strawberries
• Sugar
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Thinly slice strawberries, toss with sugar, & slightly crush
3. Set aside
4. Sift together flour, salt, and baking powder
5. Cut in shortening until mixture resembles coarse crumbs
6. Stir in milk and egg
7. Mix lightly
8. Grease 11x8-inch baking pan
9. Divide dough in half
10. Roll each piece out to fit baking pan
11. Place 1st portion of rolled out dough in greased pan
12. Brush with melted butter
13. Place the remaining portion of rolled out dough onto the first one and brush with more melted butter
14. Bake until browned, about 25 minute let cool then cut into 8 serving pieces
15. For each shortcake, split cake portion and spread sweetened, slightly crushed strawberries between and on top of split cake
16. Serve with whipped cream<|endoftext|>
