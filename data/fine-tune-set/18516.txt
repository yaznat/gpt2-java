Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Greek Style Cheese And Spinach Waffles

Ingredients:
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Garlic powder
• Dried dill
• Milk
• Feta cheese
• Spinach
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Heat a waffle maker to hot
2. In a bowl, beat the egg white until stiff
3. Set aside
4. In another bowl, mix togather the dry ingredients
5. Set aside
6. Combine the egg yolk, milk, feta cheese and oil
7. Add to the dry ingredients, mixing just until blended
8. Fold in the beaten egg white just until mixed
9. Bake as directed in a waffle maker<|endoftext|>
