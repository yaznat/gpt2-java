What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Asian Turkey Burgers With Spicy Yogurt Sauce

Ingredients:
• Lean ground turkey
• Egg
• Spinach
• Dried onion
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Ginger
• Salt and pepper
• Greek yogurt
• Lemon juice
• Chili powder
• Cayenne

Steps:
1. Mix together turkey, onions, spices, soy sauce, honey and egg
2. Pure spinach using food processor and combine with turkey mixture
3. Heat skillet and put 3 tablespoons olive oil inches brown patties on both sides, remove from skillet and place on a greased baking sheet
4. Finish cooking at 350 degrees until cooked through
5. For yogurt sauce, just combine all ingredients and chill until burgers are cooked
6. Serve asian turkey burgers with spicy yogurt sauce on top!<|endoftext|>
