Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Tomokra Stewed Tomatoes And Okra

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Fresh okra
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Garlic
• Basil

Steps:
1. Wah and clean the okra
2. Saute onion until tender
3. Add okra, garlic, sauce, and tomatoes
4. Stir to blend
5. Cover, reduce to a simmer
6. Simmer for about an hour, or until okra is nice and tender, stirring as needed
7. Stir slowly, because the okra will fall apart
8. Season to taste
9. Optional, sprinkle diced bacon on top before serving<|endoftext|>
