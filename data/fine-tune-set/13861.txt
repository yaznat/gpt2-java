What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>B B Q Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Brown sugar
• Vinegar
• Ketchup
• Salt
• Dry mustard
• Water
• Onions

Steps:
1. Brown pork chops and place in baking dish
2. Mix remaining ingredients and pour over pork chops
3. Cover and bake at 350 degrees for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
