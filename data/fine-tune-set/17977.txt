Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate squares
• Butter
• Black pepper
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Kahlua
• Vodka
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Walnuts
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Line bottom of 13x9 inch baking pan with waxed paper
3. Melt chocolate and butter with pepper in small saucepan over low heat
4. Remove from heat and cool
5. Combine eggs, sugar and vanilla in large bowl, beat well
6. Stir in cooled chocolate mixture, kaluha and vodka
7. Combine flour, salt and baking powder and add to chocolate mixture
8. Stir until blended
9. Add walnuts
10. Spread in prepared pan
11. Bake in preheated oven just until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean, about 25 minutes
12. Do not over bake
13. Cool in pan on wire rack
14. Cut into bars
15. Sprinkle with powdered sugar, if desired<|endoftext|>
