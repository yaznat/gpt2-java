Ingredients:
• Whole pecans
• Dates
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Stuff each date with a nut
3. Wrap each with bacon, securing with a toothpick
4. Place on a baking sheet
5. Bake until bacon is crisp, 20-30 minutes
6. Transfer to paper towels to drain
7. Let cool briefly
8. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
