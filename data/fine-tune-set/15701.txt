What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Chicken Mango Tacos

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Mangoes
• Taco seasoning
• Mexican blend cheese
• Flour tortilla

Steps:
1. Heat a few tbsp of olive oil in pan
2. Cut chicken into strips and add to hot oil
3. Cover with taco seasoning and cook chicken throughly
4. Peel and cut mango into strips
5. Add mango to chicken and heat for about 1 min
6. Put mango and chicken into tortillas and cover with cheese
7. Serve warm!<|endoftext|>
