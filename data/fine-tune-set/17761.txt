Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>Applesauce Raisin Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Raisin bran cereal
• Nonfat milk
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Egg
• Brown sugar
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat over to 400f
2. Prepare a muffin tin with paper liners or by spraying with cooking spray
3. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, cereal and milk in large bowl
4. Let stand 3 minutes
5. Stir in egg, applesauce, sugar and butter
6. Stir just until moistened
7. Fill each muffin cup 2 / 3 full
8. Bake at 400f for 20 minutes or until golden brown, and they test done with a toothpick<|endoftext|>
