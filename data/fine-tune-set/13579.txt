Ingredients:
• Cheese ravioli
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Diced tomatoes
• Condensed tomato soup
• Dried basil
• Dried oregano
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Cook ravioli according to package directions
3. Drain well
4. Return to pan
5. Cover and keep warm
6. Meanwhile, in a large skillet, cook ground meat, onion, and garlic over medium heat until meat is brown and onion is tender
7. Drain off fat
8. Stir in undrained tomatoes, soup, basil, and oregano
9. Gently stir in cooked ravioli
10. Gently stir in cooked ravioli
11. Transfer mixture to an ungreased 3-quart rectangular baking dish
12. Sprinkle with mozzarella cheese and parmesan cheese
13. Bake, uncovered, for about 20 minutes or until heated through<|endoftext|>
