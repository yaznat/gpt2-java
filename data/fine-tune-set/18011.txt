Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>Brownie Bottom Bourbon Pie

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Egg yolks
• Sugar
• Unflavored gelatin
• Water
• Bourbon
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Bake 1 brownie mix according to directions, but bake a few minutes less, in the bottoms of 2 pie tins
2. Beat egg yokes until thick and lemon colored
3. Slowly beat in sugar
4. Soften gelatin in cold water and add 1 / 3 of the bourbon
5. Heat this mixture of bourbon over boiling water until gelatin dissolves
6. Pour into yolks and stir briskly
7. Add remaining bourbon
8. Whip 1-cup of cream and fold into mixture
9. Pour filling into brownie crust and chill 4 hours
10. Top with remaining cream whipped with a pinch of salt & sugar to taste
11. Sprinkle shaved chocolate on top<|endoftext|>
