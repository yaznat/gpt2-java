Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Sugar
• Water
• White wine vinegar
• Garlic
• Grey poupon dijon mustard
• Lime juice
• Lemon juice
• Fresh parsley
• Dried basil
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Whisk everything in a bowl
2. Microwave on high for one minute or until the mixture bubbles rapidly around the edge of the bowl
3. Whisk for one minute until mixture forms an emulsion
4. Cover and chill for one hour before serving
