Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>Bj S Restaurant Brewhouse Chili By Todd Wilbur

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Ground pork
• Diced onions
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Dried thyme
• Ground black pepper
• Cayenne
• Water
• All-purpose flour
• Pinto beans
• Crushed tomatoes
• Light beer
• Cheddar cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Sour cream
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Brown ground meats in a large saucepan over medium heat
2. Drain off excess fat
3. Add onion, chili powder, salt, garlic powder, thyme, black pepper, cayenne, and spices, and saut for an additional 5 minutes
4. Combine flour with water and add mixture to pan
5. Add remaining ingredients, bring chili to a simmer, and then let it simmer uncovered for 1 1 / 2 hours, stirring occasionally
6. Serve 1 1 / 4 cups of chili in a bowl or in a carved-out round of sourdough bread
7. Mix shredded cheeses and then top chili with 1 / 4 cup of the cheese blend, a tablespoon of sour cream and a tablespoon of chopped green onions on top<|endoftext|>
