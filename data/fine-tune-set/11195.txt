What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Beef Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Beef brisket
• Water
• Vinegar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Chili powder
• Barbecue sauce
• Kaiser rolls

Steps:
1. Trim fat from brisket
2. Cut brisket, if necessary, to fit in a 3-1 / 2 to 4-quart crock pot
3. Add water, vinegar, worcestershire sauce and chili powder
4. Cover and cook on low setting 10 to 12 hours, or on high setting 4 to 5 hours
5. About 1 hour before serving, remove the meat from the crock pot
6. Discard cooking liquid
7. Using two forks to pull, shred meat return to crock pot
8. Stir in barbecue sauce
9. Cover and cook on high setting 30 minutes or until heated through
10. Serve on rolls or hamburger buns<|endoftext|>
