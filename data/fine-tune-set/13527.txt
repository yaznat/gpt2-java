Ingredients:
• Water
• Asparagus spears
• Chive & onion cream cheese
• Prepared horseradish
• Roast beef

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, bring water to a boil
2. Add asparagus, cover and boil for 2-3 minutes or until crisp-tender
3. Drain and immediately place aspargus in ice water
4. Drain and pat dry
5. In a small mixing bowl, combine cream cheese and horseradish
6. Pat beef slices dry with paper towels
7. Spread each slice with a thin layer of cream cheese mixture
8. Top with an asapargus spear and roll up tightly
9. Refrigerate until serving<|endoftext|>
