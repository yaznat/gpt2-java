What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Best Pizza Margherita

Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• San marzano tomatoes
• Dried oregano
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Buffalo mozzarella
• Basil leaves

Steps:
1. Make the dough: make your favorite or use store bought
2. Prepare the toppings: meanwhile, set a pizza stone in the oven and preheat the oven to 500, allowing at least 45 minutes for the stone to heat
3. Pass the tomatoes through a food mill set over a medium bowl or pulse them in a food processor until coarsely chopped
4. Stir in the oregano and 1 tablespoon of the olive oil and season generously with salt and pepper
5. Spread 1 / 4 cup of the tomato sauce over the dough to within 1 inch of the edge
6. Spread one-fourth of the cheese over the pizza and drizzle with 1 tablespoon of oil
7. Season with sea salt and pepper and slide the pizza onto the stone
8. Bake until the bottom is charred and the cheese is melted, about 8 minutes
9. Scatter one-fourth of the basil on top and let stand for 3 minutes before serving
10. Repeat with the remaining dough and toppings<|endoftext|>
