Give me a fish recipe.<|endoftext|>Basic Fish Stock

Ingredients:
• Fish bones
• Water
• White wine
• Black peppercorns
• Onion
• Fresh ginger
• Celery
• Carrot

Steps:
1. Put all ingredients in a large stockpot over medium heat
2. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, partially cover, and simmer for 2 hours
3. Strain
4. Refrigerate or freeze until ready to use
5. I like to freeze in ice cube trays, then place in a ziplock freezer bag<|endoftext|>
