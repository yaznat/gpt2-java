Can you give me a tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecued Tofu With Onions And Peppers

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Reduced sodium ketchup
• Dijon mustard
• Water
• Safflower oil
• Garlic
• Ground black pepper
• Liquid smoke
• Hot pepper sauce
• Onions
• Green peppers
• Cooked brown rice

Steps:
1. First the tofu should be waterpacked or cotton
2. Do not use silken it won't work
3. Freeze the tofu
4. Thaw completely
5. Then press to drain
6. To press: wrap tofu in cheesecloth or paper towels to help keep shape if desired
7. Next place on a cutting board set at a slant
8. Place a heavy dinner plate on top
9. Let the plate do it's work
10. Let for at least 10 minutes
11. Seed the green peppers and cut into strips
12. Now lets do some cooking
13. Preheat the oven to 375
14. In baking dish [2qt], combine ketchup, mustard, water, oil, garlic, black pepper, liquid smoke and hot sauce
15. With long side of tofu facing you cut into 1 / 2 inch slices starting at short end
16. Dip both sides of the slices in the kechup mix
17. Put on plate and set aside
18. Dip onions in ketchup mix
19. Set aside
20. Put the green peppers in the mix
21. Let them in the dish they form the bottom
22. Arrange the tofu on top of the peppers then arrange the onions on top of the tofu
23. Use foil to cover and bake 15 minutes
24. Place on the rice and serve
25. You may also marinate the tofu in the ketchup mix if you like for a while
