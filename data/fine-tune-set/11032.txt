Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>An Easy Chicken Avocado Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Bagel
• Avocado
• Mixed salad green
• Chicken strips
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Toast bagel until golden
2. Cut the avocado into thin strips and place on bagel
3. Place chicken on top of avocado
4. Place lettuce on top of chicken and finish with the sour cream
5. Add top of bagel and enjoy!
6. If you want you can also add some cillantro for an extra kick!<|endoftext|>
