Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Parmesan Fish With Pasta

Ingredients:
• Nonfat parmesan cheese
• All-purpose flour
• Fresh thyme sprigs
• White fish fillets
• Angel hair pasta
• Onion
• Mushroom cap
• Green onion
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Combine cheese, flour and thyme in a shallow bowl
2. Gently coat each piece of fish on all sides in the mixture
3. Place fillets in a baking pan with rack
4. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 20 minutes, or until fish flakes easily when tested with a fork
5. Cook pasta as directed on package
6. Drain
7. Keep warm
8. Heat large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
9. Add onion, mushrooms, green onions and garlic
10. Cook, stirring frequently, until onions are tender
11. Season with salt and pepper to taste
12. Place cooked pasta on oven-warmed plates
13. Place fish over pasta
14. Top with mushroom mixture
15. Garnish with thyme if desired<|endoftext|>
