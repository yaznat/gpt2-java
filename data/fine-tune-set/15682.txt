Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Fiesta nacho cheese soup
• Water
• Taco seasoning mix
• Taco shells
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Cook beef in skillet until browned
2. Pour off fat
3. Add 1 / 2 can soup, water and taco seasoning mix
4. Heat through
5. Heat remaining soup in saucepan, stirring often
6. Spoon about 1 / 3 cup beef mixture into taco shells
7. Top each with remaining soup and lettuce<|endoftext|>
