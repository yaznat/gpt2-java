Can you give me a muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Even I Like These Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Brown sugar
• Oil
• Milk
• Molasses
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Natural bran
• Raisins
• Sesame seeds
• Sunflower seeds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375c
2. Beat eggs, add sugar, oil, milk and molasses
3. Mix thoroughly
4. In another bowl, add all flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and bran together
5. Mix thoroughly
6. Add raisins, sesame seeds, and sunflower seeds to the dry mix
7. Mix again
8. Add dry ingredients to wet ingredients
9. Mix well
10. Mixture will appear quite wet, but will thicken up quickly
11. Line muffin pan with muffin cups
12. Fill 3 / 4 full
13. Bake for 14-15 minutes<|endoftext|>
