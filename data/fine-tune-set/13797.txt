Ingredients:
• Hoisin sauce
• Soy sauce
• Lemon, juice of
• Fresh ginger
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Rice wine vinegar
• Cardamom pods
• Red pepper flakes
• Pork

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Add pork and toss to coat
3. Refrigerate 1-3 hours
4. Skewer pork and bbq or broil until done--doneness will depend on the size of the pork chunks and how far from your heat source you are
5. I usually do 1-1 1 / 2" chunks and broil 3-4" from the heat for 5-6 minutes per side<|endoftext|>
