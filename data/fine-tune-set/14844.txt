Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Best Ever Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Eggs
• Yellow onion
• Roast pork
• Cooked long-grain rice
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Iceberg lettuce
• Frozen green pea
• Salt
• Pepper
• Green onion
• Dark sesame oil

Steps:
1. Heat canola oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add eggs
3. Stir-fry for 1 minute or until done
4. Remove from pan
5. Add yellow onion to pan
6. Saute 2 minutes or until translucent
7. Stir in chinese-style roast pork, or whatever meat you have chosen
8. Stir-fry 5 minutes over medium-high heat
9. Add rice and soy sauce
10. Stir-fry 2 minutes
11. Return eggs to pan
12. Cook 1 minute
13. Stir in iceberg lettuce, green peas, salt and pepper
14. Stir-fry 1 minute
15. Sprinkle with green onions, and drizzle with sesame oil<|endoftext|>
