Give me a recipe for a sandwich.<|endoftext|>Avocado Butter With Baby Shrimp Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Egg bread
• Butter
• Avocado
• Lemon juice
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Baby shrimp
• Olive oil
• Hot pepper sauce
• English cucumber
• Watercress leaf

Steps:
1. Beat together butter, avocado, lemon juice, chili powder and salt in a bowl
2. In a second bowl, marinate the baby shrimp with lemon juice, olive oil, hot pepper sauce and salt to taste
3. Lightly sprinkle salt on cucumber slices and let sit for 20 minutes
4. Pat cucumber dry with paper towels
5. Cut bread in half and spread slices with avocado butter
6. Top with overlapping cucumber slices and pile shrimp on top
7. Garnish with a watercress sprig
8. Repeat for remaining sandwiches<|endoftext|>
