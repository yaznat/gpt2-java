Generate a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>15 Minute Marinated Chicken

Ingredients:
• Dijon mustard
• Fresh lemon juice
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dried tarragon
• Pepper
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves

Steps:
1. In a small bowl combine the first five ingredients, whisk to blend well
2. Place chicken on a plate and spread the mixture on both sides of chicken
3. Let the chicken marinate at room temperature for 10-15 minutes or in the refrigerator for several hours
4. Over medium heat on the grill, cook chicken for approximately 10-15 minutes or until the juices run clear
5. Turn only once, serve immediately<|endoftext|>
