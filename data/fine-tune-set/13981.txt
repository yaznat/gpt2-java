Ingredients:
• Butterflied leg of lamb
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Pepper
• Rosemary
• White wine
• Curry powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Lay out butterflied leg of lamb - fat side down - on a pan with a rack
3. Mix remaining ingredients in a small bowl, and lightly coat both sides of the meat
4. Bake on center rack of oven for 50 minutes for rare, 1 hour for medium, or to your preference
