What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Braided Stuffed Pizza Bread

Ingredients:
• Pizza dough
• Spreadable cheese with garlic and herbs
• Canadian bacon
• Pepperoni
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Egg yolk
• Water
• Poppy seed
• Pizza sauce

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400f spray cookie sheet with cooking spray
2. Unroll dough on cookie sheet, forming 14x11-inch rectangle
3. Spread garlic and herb cheese lengthwise down center 1 / 3 of rectangle to within 1 / 2 inch of short ends
4. Top with canadian bacon, pepperoni, mozzarella and parmesan cheese
5. With scissors or sharp knife, make cuts 1 inch apart on long sides of dough to within 1 / 2 inch of filling
6. Alternately cross strips over filling
7. Fold ends under to seal
8. In small bowl, mix egg yolk and water until well blended
9. Brush over dough
10. Sprinkle with poppy seed
11. Bake 11 to 16 minutes or until golden brown
12. Cut into crosswise slices
13. Serve with pizza sauce<|endoftext|>
