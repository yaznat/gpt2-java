Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Ketchup
• Ground ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Vegetable oil
• Green onions
• Green bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Chinese noodles

Steps:
1. Mix soy sauce, ketchup, ginger, and garlic in a resealable heavy-duty plastic bag
2. Add chicken
3. Seal bag and turn to coat with marinade
4. Let stand 15 minutes
5. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a 10-inch skillet over medium-high heat
6. Add onion and peppers
7. Stir-fry until crisp-tender
8. Remove from skillet
9. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon oil in skillet
10. Add chicken
11. Stir-fry 4 to 5 minutes or until no longer pink in center
12. Stir in bell pepper mixture
13. Serve with noodles or rice<|endoftext|>
