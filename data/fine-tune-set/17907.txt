Give me a brownie recipe.<|endoftext|>B B S Brownies

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Butter
• Flour
• Cocoa
• Vanilla
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Grease 8x8 inch pan, flour, and line with wax paper
2. Separate eggs, set whites aside
3. In a large bowl, by hand, beat egg yolks with sugar, until thick and creamy
4. Add butter, cocoa, and vanilla and mix
5. Add sifted flour, and stir in gently and thoroughly
6. Beat egg whites until stiff
7. Gently fold egg whites into batter
8. Bake 30 minutes at 375 degrees
9. When done, dust with confectioners sugar
10. When cooled 5 minutes, cut into 16 squares and remove from pan<|endoftext|>
