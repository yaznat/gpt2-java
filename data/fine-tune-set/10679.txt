What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus Pasta Salad Vegetarian

Ingredients:
• Fresh asparagus
• Tri-colored pasta
• Sweet potato
• Tomatoes
• Ripe olives
• Kraft light zesty italian dressing
• Dill weed

Steps:
1. Put the pieces of asparagus stem in a steamer basket & steam for 8 to 10 minutes before adding the top pieces, then steam another 5 to 7 minutes or until all is crisp-tender
2. Drain & cool
3. In a large bowl, combine asparagus & the remaining ingredients, tossing to coat
4. Cover & refrigerate 3 to 4 hours or overnight<|endoftext|>
