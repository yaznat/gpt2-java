Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>5 Star Oven Fried Chicken

Ingredients:
• Buttermilk
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Corn flakes
• Bisquick baking mix
• Ranch dressing mix
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. 1
2. Heat oven to 400f
3. Spray cookie sheet with cooking spray
4. 2
5. Pour buttermilk into shallow glass or plastic bowl
6. Add chicken
7. Turn to coat
8. Let stand 5 minutes
9. 3
10. Meanwhile, in 2-quart resealable food-storage plastic bag, crush cereal with rolling pin
11. Add bisquick mix and dressing mix to cereal in bag
12. Remove chicken from buttermilk
13. Discard buttermilk
14. Add chicken to cereal mixture
15. Seal bag
16. Shake to coat
17. 4
18. Place chicken on cookie sheet
19. Spray with cooking spray
20. Bake 45 to 50 minutes or until juice of chicken is clear when center of thickest part is cut<|endoftext|>
