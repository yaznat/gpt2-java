Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Chocolate Chip Cheesecake

Ingredients:
• Fudge brownie mix
• Eggs
• Oil
• Cream cheese
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Vanilla extract
• Mini chocolate chip

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease bottom only of 9-inch springform pan
3. Prepare brownie mix as package directs for chewy brownies
4. Spread evenly in prepared pan
5. Bake 35 minutes or until set
6. In large bowl, beat cream cheese until fluffy
7. Gradually beat in eagle brand
8. Add eggs, vanilla and mix well
9. Stir in chocolate chips
10. Pour into prepared pan
11. Reduce oven temperature to 300 degrees and bake 50 minutes or until set, cool
12. Chill thoroughly
13. Remove side of springform pan
14. Garnish as desired
15. Store leftovers covered in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
