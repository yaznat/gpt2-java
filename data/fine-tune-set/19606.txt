Steps:
1. Spray a 9x13-inch baking dish with non-stick cooking spray
2. Place alternate layers of sweet potatoes and apples in baking dish, ending with apples
3. Pour orange juice and zest over mixture and sprinkle with cinnamon
4. Bake, covered with foil, in preheated 375 degree oven for 40 minutes
5. Remove foil, top with crushed cereal or bread crumbs, and spray with non-stick cooking spray
