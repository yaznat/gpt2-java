Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Beggar S Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Soy sauce
• Beef bouillon granules
• Bacon
• Long grain white rice
• Yellow onion
• Green peas

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, beat together the eggs, soy sauce and beef bouillon
2. In the same skillet you cooked the bacon, cook the chilled rice in the reserved bacon grease / drippings over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring often
3. Add the egg mixture to the rice in the skillet, cooking and stirring until the eggs are 'set'
4. Add the onions and peas and stir until heated through
5. Serve garnished with the bacon
6. Suggestions: try the lower sodium bacon or turkey bacon
