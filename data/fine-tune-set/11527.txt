Ingredients:
• Shortening
• Icing sugar
• Vanilla
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Salt
• Milk
• Butter

Steps:
1. To prepare base, cut shortening into dry ingredients, mix well
2. Pat firmly and evenly into an 8 inch square pan and bake at 350 deg
3. F
4. For 10-12 minutes
5. Cool
6. For cake: cream shortening, and sugar
7. Add eggs and vanilla, beating well until fluffy
8. Sift flour, baking powder and salt together
9. Add to creamed mixture alternately with milk
10. Pour batter into a greased and floured 8 inch square pan
11. Bake at 350 deg
12. F for 25 - 40 minutes
13. Frosting: in small saucepan, stir tog
14. Milk and flour
15. Cook, stirring constantly, until mixute is thickened and smooth
16. Cool
17. On highest speed of mixer, beat cooled flour mixture with butter, shortening and vanilla until smooth and fluffy
18. Blend in icing sugar and salt
19. Continue beating until frosting is very fluffy
20. This will take at least 15 minutes
21. To assemble cake: place shortbread base on serving plate
22. Spread with small amount of frosting
23. Place cake on base
24. Cover top and sides of cake with remaining frosting
25. If desired, garnish sides of cake with shaved semi-sweet chocolate<|endoftext|>
