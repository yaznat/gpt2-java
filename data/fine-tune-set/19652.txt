Ingredients:
• Cauliflower
• Mayonnaise
• Lemon juice
• Sugar substitute
• Dried mustard
• Green onions
• Jalapeno peppers
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cook cauliflower in a large pot of boiling water 10 minsutes or until tender
2. Drain and rinse with cold water
3. In a large mixing bowl, mix mayonnaise, lemon juice, sugar substitute and mustard
4. Add cauliflower, green onions and pepper
5. Mix well until vegetables are evenly coated with dressing
6. Add salt and pepper to taste
7. Chill 30 mins<|endoftext|>
