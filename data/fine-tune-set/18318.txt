Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Aimee S Cinnamon Spice Waffles

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• All-purpose flour
• Ground flax seeds
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Milk
• Butter
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl combine first seven ingredients
2. In a separate bowl whisk together the last 5 ingredients
3. Pour liquid ingredients in to dry and mix well
4. Pour onto hot waffle iron and sprinkle with chopped nuts, cook until lightly browned
5. Serve with powdered sugar or warm maple syrup<|endoftext|>
