Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Asian Beef Kabobs

Ingredients:
• Top sirloin steak
• Green onions
• Brown sugar
• Dry sherry
• Soy sauce
• Dark sesame oil
• Garlic cloves
• Ground ginger

Steps:
1. Trim all fat from steak
2. Cut steak crosswise into 1 / 4-inch thick strips
3. Make marinade by combining brown sugar, sherry, soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic and ginger
4. Place beef and half of the marinade mixture in sealable plastic bag, turning to coat
5. Close securely and marinate in refrigerator 20 minutes
6. Reserve remaining marinade
7. Meanwhile, soak 16 bamboo skewers in water 10 minutes
8. Remove beef from marinade and discard marinade
9. Alternately thread beef and green onion onto skewers
10. Place kabobs on rack in broiler pan so surface of kabobs is 3-4 inches from heat
11. Broil 5-6 minutes, turning once
12. Brush kabobs with reserved marinade during last 2 minutes<|endoftext|>
