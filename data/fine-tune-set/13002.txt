• Parmesan cheese
• Chicken flavor instant bouillon
• Parsley flakes
• Garlic powder
• Dry onion flakes
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Salt
• Pepper
• Fish fillet
• Butter

Steps:
1. Combine first nine ingredients
2. Cut fish in serving size pieces
3. Dip fish in melted butter turning to coat both sides
4. Dip fish in crumbs, coating well
5. Place fish on lightly butter shallow pan
