Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Hormel chili without beans
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Spread cream cheese on bottom of casserole dish
2. Top with chili
3. Sprinkle cheddar over chili
4. Bake or broil until cheddar cheese melts
5. Serve with taco chips<|endoftext|>
