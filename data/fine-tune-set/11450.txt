Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Canola oil
• Unsalted butter
• Vanilla
• Coconut extract
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cocoa powder
• Buttermilk
• Sweetened coconut
• Almonds
• Chocolate chips
• Water
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Combine the eggs, sugar, canola oil, butter, vanilla, and coconut extract into a large mixing bowl
3. Beat ingredients on medium speed for 2 minutes
4. In a separate bowl, combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and cocoa powder
5. Starting with the flour mixture alternate flour, and then buttermilk till all is combined
6. Add egg mixture
7. Mix until just combined
8. Do not over mix
9. Fold in coconut, chocolate chips and almonds
10. Pour into two loaf pans that have been greased and floured or sprayed with cooking spray
11. Bake at 350f for 1 hour
12. Remove from oven and place on rack to dry
13. While cakes are still warm, poke several holes in the top of the cake with a fork
14. Drizzle coconut glaze into the holes
15. Coconut glaze:
16. Place ingredients into a small saucepan
17. Bring to a boil
18. Allow to boil for about a minute
19. Turn heat off but leave saucepan on burner
20. Drizzle over bread
21. Cool<|endoftext|>
