Ingredients:
• Shortening
• Brown sugar
• Applesauce
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground cinnamon
• Vegetable oil
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Beat the shortening and brown sugar at medium speed of an electric mixer until blended
2. Add the applesauce and egg
3. Beat well
4. Combine the flour, baking soda, salt, nutmeg and cinnamon
5. Add to the shortening mixture
6. Beat well
7. Roll the dough to 1 / 2 inch thickness on a lightly floured surface
8. Cut the dough with a floured 2-1 / 2 inch doughnut cutter
9. Pour the oil to a depth of 2 inches in a large dutch oven
10. Heat oil to 375 degrees fahrenheit
11. Drop in 3 to 4 doughnuts at a time
12. Cook 1-1 / 2 minutes on each side
13. Drain on paper towels
14. Combine 1 / 2 cup sugar and 2 teaspoons of cinnamon
15. Roll the doughnuts in cinnamon-sugar mixture
16. Cool on wire racks<|endoftext|>
