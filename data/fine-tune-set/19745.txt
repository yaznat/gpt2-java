Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Condensed golden mushroom soup
• Condensed cheddar cheese soup
• Frozen french fries
• Ketchup
• Pickle
• Mustard
• Chopped tomato

Steps:
1. In a skillet, brown the beef, drain
2. Stir in mushroom soup and cheddar cheese soup
3. Pour into a greased 13x9 baking dish
4. Arrange french fries on top
5. Bake, uncovered, at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 50 to 55 minutes or until the fries are golden brown<|endoftext|>
