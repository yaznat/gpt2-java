What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Easy And Fast Fruit N Cream Waffles

Ingredients:
• Waffles
• Cream cheese
• Canned peaches
• Brown sugar
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Toast waffles
2. Cover with a layer of cream cheese
3. Add a layer of fruit
4. Sprinkle with brown sugar
5. Cover completely with whipped cream<|endoftext|>
