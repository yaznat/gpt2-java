What's a recipe for beans?<|endoftext|>Annis Black Bean Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Dried black beans
• Olive oil
• Yellow onion
• Minced garlic cloves
• Red chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Greek oregano
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Chicken stock
• Cocoa
• Sour cream
• Chevre cheese

Steps:
1. Rinse the dry beans, cover with water and simmer till tender
2. Drain the water and set aside
3. You can also use 2 cans of black beans
4. Brown the onions and garlic in the olive oil
5. Add the meat and continue browning, about 5 to 7 minutes
6. In a small non-stick skillet toast the chile and cumin till fragrant takes about 5 minutes
7. Do not skip this step as the toasting process brings out all the flavor of the spices
8. Add the toasted spices to the meat and onions
9. Add the oregano and 2c of the chicken stock and all the beans
10. Season with salt and pepper
11. Simmer for about 20 minutes or until you reach the consistency you like for the chili
12. Start with only 2c of the stock and gradually add to reach the consistency you like
13. Right before serving add the cocoa and blend well
14. Do not heat over a slight simmer as the chocolate will become bitter
15. Serve with the sour cream and chevre that you have blended together
16. Make some jalapeno cornbread and you have a dynamite meal! this recipe can easily be doubled<|endoftext|>
