Give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>B O B Aka Pork Roll Egg Cheese

Ingredients:
• Kaiser roll
• Egg
• Pork roll
• American cheese

Steps:
1. If you want to use any other meat go ahead
2. Canadian bacon, regular bacon, ham, use what you have available in your neck of the woods
3. In a small microwave bowl, nuke the meat, then the egg for about 30 seconds each, pile 0n a roll and top with cheese
4. Salt and pepper to taste
5. For some reason these taste better after they have been wrapped in handy wax sheets
6. I think it steams the sandwich a little and makes it all taste better
