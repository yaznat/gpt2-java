What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Grandma S Buttermilk Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, add flour, baking powder, salt, and soda
2. Stir to mix
3. Add in eggs, buttermilk, and butter
4. Mix well
5. Pour batter into a preheated prepared waffle iron
6. Cook until steaming stops and waffles are crisp and golden<|endoftext|>
