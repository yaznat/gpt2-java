What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Barnwell School Chiliburgers

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onions
• Potatoes
• Sage
• Salt
• Curry powder
• Chili powder
• Ketchup
• Water
• Hamburger bun

Steps:
1. Fry onions and ground beef
2. Drain off fat
3. Add remaining ingredients
4. Cook in oven for approx 2 hours at 325 degrees
5. Stir occasionally
6. This will fill 30 hamburger buns
7. Can be cut in half or doubled very easily
8. Yumpy<|endoftext|>
