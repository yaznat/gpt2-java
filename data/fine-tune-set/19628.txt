Give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>Armenian Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Onions
• Vegetable oil
• Tomato paste
• Water
• Paprika
• Salt
• Pepper
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Wash and peel potatoes, cut potatoes and onions in chunks
2. In a large bowl mix together the oil, tomato paste, water, salt, paprika, combine with potatoes
3. Add onions, pepper to taste, parsley and toss to mix
4. Bake in a covered dish in a preheated 325 degree oven for about 45 minutes or until potatoes are soft<|endoftext|>
