Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Addictive Oatmeal Molasses Cookies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Rolled oats
• Nuts
• Raisins
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Molasses

Steps:
1. Combine first eight ingredients in a bowl and mix well
2. In a separate bowl cream together the remaining four ingredients and mix with dry mix
3. Drop by teasoonfuls on cookie sheet and bake at 325 degrees for 12-15 minutes<|endoftext|>
