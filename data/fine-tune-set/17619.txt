What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>A Pail In The Fridge 6 Week Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Oil
• White sugar
• Milk
• Flour
• Natural bran
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Preheat over to 400
2. Spray muffin tins with cooking spray
3. Beat eggs, add oil and milk
4. Mix together
5. Mix dry ingredients together separately and mix well
6. Add to wet ingredients and mix again
7. Mix in raisins
8. Pour enough mix into muffin tins to fill about 2 / 3 of the way full
9. Bake at 400 for 15 minutes or until tops of muffins are nice and brown and starting to crack a bit
10. Keep mix in fridge for up to 6 weeks in an ice cream pail with lid
11. Recipes doubles easily and will fill and ice cream pail nicely
12. The longer the mix is in the fridge the darker the batter and more powerful the bran will be
13. Great warm with butter and honey<|endoftext|>
