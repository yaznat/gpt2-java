Can you give me a beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Baby Bella And Green Bean Salad

Ingredients:
• Fresh green beans
• Garlic cloves
• Red wine vinegar
• Dijon mustard
• Coarse salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Olive oil
• Green onions
• Baby portabella mushrooms
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Heat salted water in a large saucepan until boiling
2. Cook the beans until tender, about 7 minutes
3. Drain beans
4. Set aside to cool
5. Whisk together the garlic, vinegar, mustard, salt and pepper to taste in a small bowl
6. Slowly whisk in the olive oil, adding in a steady stream, until smooth
7. Combine the reserved beans, green onions, mushrooms and parsley in a large bowl
8. Pour dressing over vegetables
9. Toss lightly
10. Chill 30 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
