Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin chops
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Yellow mustard
• Flour
• Unsalted butter
• Chicken stock
• Yellow onion

Steps:
1. Season flour with salt and pepper
2. Spread mustard on chops and dredge in flour
3. Heat a skillet on med high, and melt butter
4. Brown chops in butter on both sides for 2 minutes
5. Add onion and broth and cover, simmer 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
