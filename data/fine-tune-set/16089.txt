Give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Chicken For Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Taco seasoning
• Dried cilantro

Steps:
1. Cut the chicken into small pieces, this goes quickly if the meat is half frozen
2. Place the taco seasoning and cilantro into a plastic bag
3. Place the chicken into the bag about 1 cup at a time, coating the meat with the seasoning
4. Spray a 9x13 pan with cooking spray, then place the chicken pieces into the pan
5. Bake uncovered for 10 min's, stir, then bake another 10 min's
6. Serve in your favourite quesadilla recipe<|endoftext|>
