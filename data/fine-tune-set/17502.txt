Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• 2% low-fat milk
• Water
• Vanilla
• Fresh blueberries
• Cream cheese
• Lemon zest

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Rinse blueberries in cool water to slightly thaw
3. Gently coat in 1 / 4 cup flour
4. In a large bowl cream together sugar, butter and eggs
5. In a smaller mixing bowl, combine 2 cups flour, baking powder and salt
6. Combine milk and water
7. Add ingredients to the first bowl alternating the milk and flour mixtures
8. When well blended, add vanilla and lemon zest
9. Dice cream cheese into small cubes
10. The smaller the better
11. Add to batter
12. Fold in blueberries
13. Pour into a greased and floured 13 by 9 pan
14. Mix together all topping ingredients using a fork
15. Consistency should resemble crumbs
16. Sprinkle over the top of the cake mixture
17. Bake 1 hour or until golden brown on top
18. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
