What's a good cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Chi Chi Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Vodka
• Pineapple juice
• Cream of coconut
• Confectioners' sugar
• Ice
• Pineapple
• Maraschino cherries

Steps:
1. Into a blender, combine the vodka, pineapple juice, cream of coconut, sugar, and ice
2. Blend on high speed until frothy and well combined, about 30 seconds
3. Pour into 2 chilled collins glasses, and garnish with the pineapple slices and umbrellas
4. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
