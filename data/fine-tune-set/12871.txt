What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Benihana Teriyaki Steak Chicken

Ingredients:
• Sake
• Soy sauce
• Mirin
• Sugar
• Boneless beef top loin steaks
• Spring onions

Steps:
1. Pour all teriyaki sauce ingredients in a pan
2. Stir the mixture well
3. Put the pan on low heat and simmer for a couple of minutes
4. Remove from the heat and cool the mixture
5. Heat the oil in a frying pan or flat grill until very hot and stir-fry the meat for 2 minutes
6. Add spring onions and stir-fry for 2 minutes
7. Add teriyaki sauce and stir-fry for 1 minute<|endoftext|>
