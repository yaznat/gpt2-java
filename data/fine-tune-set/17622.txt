Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Applesauce
• Honey
• Yellow cake mix
• Wheat bran
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, beat eggs, oil, applesauce and honey
2. Combine the dry cake mix, bran and cinnamon
3. Add to egg mixture
4. Mix just until blended
5. Fill greased or paper-lined muffin cups two-thirds full
6. Bake at 350 for 20-25 minutes or until muffins test done
7. Yield: 1-1 / 2 dozen<|endoftext|>
