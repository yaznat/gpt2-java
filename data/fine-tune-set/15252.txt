Ingredients:
• Breakfast sausage
• Refrigerated crescent dinner rolls
• Eggs
• Milk
• Pepper
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Crumble and cook sausage until no longer pink
2. Drain well
3. Unroll crescent dough onto a lightly greased cooking sheet, pressing dough 1 / 2 inch up the sides and sealing seams
4. Sprinkle with sausage
5. Beat eggs, milk, and pepper together
6. Pour over sausage
7. Sprinkle with cheese
8. Bake uncovered at 400 degrees for 15 minutes or until the crust is brown and the cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
