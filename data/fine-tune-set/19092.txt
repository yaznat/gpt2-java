Give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Banana Ice Cream Crepes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Salt
• Milk
• Butter
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Whipped cream
• Chocolate syrup
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients for the crepes
2. Melt the butter, and let it cool some
3. Whisk the milk and eggs together
4. Add the butter into the milk / egg mixture
5. Slowly mix in the dry ingredients
6. Heat pan to medium heat and spray pan with cooking spray
7. Pour batter onto heated pan, making sure they are spread out and thin
8. Once bottom side is browned, flip to next side
9. Allow your finished crepes to cool slightly
10. Once crepes are cool, scoop ice cream in small scoops and place in the center of the crepe
11. Roll it like a burrito
12. Shower the crepe with bananas
13. Add whip cream on top
14. Add chocolate sauce to finish the dish
15. And enjoy!<|endoftext|>
