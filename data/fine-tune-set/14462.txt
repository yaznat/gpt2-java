Please give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>April S White Bean Dip

Ingredients:
• Cannellini beans
• Lemon juice
• Plain yogurt
• Fresh parsley
• Creole seasoning
• Salt
• Pepper
• Hot sauce
• Garlic
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. In a blender, add all ingredients except for tortilla chips
2. Cover and process until smooth
3. Chill at least 3 hours
4. Serve with tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
