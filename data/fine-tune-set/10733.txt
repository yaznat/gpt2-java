Ingredients:
• Philadelphia cream cheese
• Orange juice
• Orange rind
• Sugar
• Apples
• Celery
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Combine softened cream cheese, orange juice, orange rind and sugar, mixing until well blended
2. Add remaining ingredients
3. Mix lightly
4. Chill<|endoftext|>
