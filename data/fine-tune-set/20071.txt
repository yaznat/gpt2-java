Give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Spanish Omelette Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Chorizo sausage
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Green bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Oregano
• Boiling potato
• Italian bread
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Eggs
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Cook sausage over medium heat, breaking up into pieces with fork, about 5 minutes
2. Transfer to bowl with slotted spoon
3. Add onions and garlic to skillet
4. Cook until golden-brown, stirring frequently, about 4 minutes
5. Add peppers and oregano
6. Cook until peppers are tender, stirring occasionally, about 3 minutes
7. Add to sausage and gently stir in potato
8. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
9. Cut bread in half horizontally with long, serrated knife
10. Remove doughy center, leaving a 1 inch shell
11. Flatten shells lightly with rolling pin
12. Brush shells with 2 tblsps olive oil
13. Reassemble loaf and wrap with foil
14. Place in oven to warm
15. Beat eggs to blend in large bowl
16. Mix with sausage mixture and season to taste
17. Remove bread from oven
18. Preheat broiler
19. Heat remaining 1 tblsp oil in 8 inch non-stick skillet over medium heat
20. Add egg mixture and swirl with rubber spatula
21. Cook until bottom is light brown and eggs are slightly soft on top, about 5 minutes
22. Transfer omelette to broiler and cook until top is just set, about 2 minutes longer
23. Unwrap bread and slide omelette onto bottom half
24. Cover with top, cut into 6 wedges and serve<|endoftext|>
