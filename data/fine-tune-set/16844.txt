Give me a milkshake recipe.<|endoftext|>Scrummy Chocolate Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Instant chocolate drink mix
• Vanilla ice cream
• Boiling water
• Fresh cream
• Milk

Steps:
1. Add the chocolate drinking powder and the boiling water in to a bowl
2. Mix into a smooth paste
3. Add the milk gradually
4. Add the fresh cream
5. Add the ice cream
6. Serve into 2 glasses and most importantly
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
