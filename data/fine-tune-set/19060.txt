Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Ann S Almond Chocolate Ice Cream Dairy Free

Ingredients:
• Almond milk
• Vanilla extract
• Amaretto
• Egg yolks
• Semisweet chocolate
• Slivered almonds
• Chocolate-covered almonds

Steps:
1. In a food processor or mixer beat the egg yolks until light and fluffy
2. Gradually pour the hot almond milk into whipped egg yolk mixture making sure you keep whisking constantly so the eggs don't curdle
3. Place the bowl over a saucepan of simmering water and, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon, cook until the custard thickens enough that it coats the back of a spoon
4. Immediately remove the custard from the heat and continue to stir the custard for a few minutes so it does not overcook
5. At this point stir in the chocolate till melted, vanilla extract and the amaretto
6. Cover and let cool to room temperature and then refrigerate the custard until it is completely cold
7. Transfer the cold custard to the container of your ice cream machine and process according to the manufacturer's instructions
8. Just before removing ice cream add toasted nut to mix inches then transfer the ice cream to a chilled container and store in the freezer
9. If the ice cream becomes too hard place in the refrigerator for about 30 minutes before serving so it can soften
10. Garnish with chocolate covered almonds<|endoftext|>
