Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Banana Oatmeal Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Oatmeal
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Butter

Steps:
1. Combine flour, oats, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and nutmeg
2. Whisk buttermilk and eggs together in a separate bowl
3. Stir buttermilk into flour mixture
4. Stir in bananas and melted butter
5. Bake waffles in a waffle iron<|endoftext|>
