What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Ever Eat Em Up Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Egg
• Milk
• Sour cream
• Butter

Steps:
1. Sift flour with dry ingredients into mixing bowl
2. Beat egg together with milk and sour cream
3. Stir into flour mixture
4. Add melted butter and whisk until smooth
5. Cook on hot griddle as usual<|endoftext|>
