Please give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Amazing Real Pumpkin Pie

Ingredients:
• Pie crusts
• Cooked pumpkin
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Salt
• Almond extract
• Vanilla
• Evaporated milk

Steps:
1. Cut and cook pumpkin
2. Preheat oven to 425f
3. Use a blender, if you don't it will be stringy
4. Put pumpkin in blender with, eggs, sugar, cinnamon, pumpkin pie spice, salt, almond extract, and vanilla
5. Put evaporated milk in a large bowl
6. In the large bowl mix evaporated milk and every thing from blender
7. You will need two 9-inch pie pans with crust
8. Pour in and cook 425f for 10 minutes than lower temperature to 350f and cook for 60 minutes<|endoftext|>
