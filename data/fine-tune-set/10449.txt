What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Annie Glenn S Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Butter
• Shortening
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f degrees
2. In a mixing bowl, combine flour, baking soda and salt
3. Set aside
4. In a large mixing bowl, with an electric mixer, cream together butter, shortening, sugars and vanilla
5. Add eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each addition
6. Mix in the flour mixture
7. Fold in the chocolate chips
8. Drop spoonfuls onto greased cookie sheets and bake in preheated oven for 10 to 12 minutes, or until golden brown<|endoftext|>
