Ingredients:
• Orange zest
• Fresh orange juice
• Soy sauce
• Rice vinegar
• Cornstarch
• Vegetable oil
• Pork tenderloin
• Garlic cloves
• Scallions
• Broccoli

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl combine orange zest, juice, soy sauce, vinager and cornstarch, set stir fry sauce aside
2. In a large non stick skillet heat 1tsp oil over medium heat
3. Working in 2 batches cook pork until browned on one side 1-2 minutes
4. Transfer to a plate and set aside
5. In same skillet add remaining oil, garlic and scallion whites, cook stirring occasionally until scallions wilt 1-2 minutes
6. Add broccoli and 1 / 2 cup water, cover and cook until broccoli is crisp tender and water has evaporated, 2-4 minutes
7. Add pork and stir fry sauce to skillet
8. Cook stirring, until pork is cooked through and sauce has thickened 1-2 minutes
9. Top with scallions greens<|endoftext|>
