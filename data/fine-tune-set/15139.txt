Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Whole wheat pita bread
• Olive oil
• Dried basil
• Red onions
• Tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f
2. In a large skillet, cook bacon over medium-high heat, stirring frequently, until crisp, 5 to 6 minutes
3. Removed with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels
4. Place unsplit pita breads, concave sides up on a baking sheeet
5. Drizzle with oil and sprinkle with basil
6. Divide onion, tomatoes, and bacon over pitas and sprinkle with cheese
7. Bake about 8 minutes, until cheese is melted and bubbly
8. Grind pepper over pizza and cut into wedges to serve<|endoftext|>
