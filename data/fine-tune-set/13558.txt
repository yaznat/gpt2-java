Ingredients:
• Lean ground chuck
• Evaporated milk
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Onion
• Green chilies
• Red enchilada sauce
• Velveeta cheese
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Lightly grease a 13 x 9 baking dish
3. In skillet, brown meat until done, drain
4. Stir in milk, soups, onion, green chiles and enchilada sauce
5. In the baking dish, spread a little of the sauce on the bottom
6. Layer 1 / 2 of the tortillas, meat mix then cheese, repeat
7. Finish with cheese on top
8. Bake for 30 minutes or until cheese is bubbly!<|endoftext|>
