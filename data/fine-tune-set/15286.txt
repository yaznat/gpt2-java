What's a good burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Loco Moco Style Hamburger Steak With Gravy

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Bread
• Milk
• Egg
• Onion
• Catsup
• Salt
• Pepper
• Beef
• Water
• Salt and pepper
• Cooked rice
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Mix bread cubes with milk or water until bread is softened
2. Mix together ground beef, softened bread cubes and rest of ingredients
3. Mix well
4. Form into patties
5. Pan fry or broil to desired doneness
6. Place cooked hamburgers on a warm platter and keep warm while preparing the gravy
7. Remove oil, leaving brown particles in pan
8. Pour broth into pan and heat to boiling, stirring and loosening brown particles,
9. Mix flour with water to make a smooth paste
10. Slowly add to heated broth, stirring constantly, until smooth
11. Add salt and pepper
12. Serve the hamburger steak on hot steamed rice, with fried egg on the hamburger and gravy over all<|endoftext|>
