Ingredients:
• Chipotle chile in adobo
• Reduced-fat sour cream
• Black beans
• Frozen whole kernel corn
• Flour tortillas
• Salsa
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine sour cream and chile in a medium bowl
3. Let stand 10 minutes
4. Place half of beans in a food processor
5. Process until finely chopped
6. Add chopped beans, remaining beans, and corn to sour cream mixture
7. Spoon 1 / 2 cup bean mixture down the center of each tortilla
8. Roll up tortillas
9. Place, seam side down, in an 11 x 7-inch baking dish coated with cooking spray
10. Spread salsa over tortillas
11. Sprinkle with cheese
12. Cover and bake at 350 for 20 minutes or until thoroughly heated<|endoftext|>
