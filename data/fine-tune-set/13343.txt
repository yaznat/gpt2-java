What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>African Apricot Chicken Skewers

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breasts
• Garlic cloves
• Salt and pepper
• Onions
• Oil
• Coriander
• Cumin
• Curry powder
• Brown sugar
• Fresh lemon juice
• Apricot jam
• Cornstarch
• Dried apricot halves
• Onion
• Bay leaves

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine chicken pieces, garlic, salt and pepper
2. Set aside
3. In a medium frying pan, fry onions in oil until golden
4. Stir in coriander, cumin and curry powder
5. Stir to coat onions, then add brown sugar, lemon juice and jam
6. Add 1 / 2 cup water
7. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly
8. Remove from heat
9. When cool, pour over chicken
10. Add bay leaves and refrigerate overnight
11. The next day, thread meat with onions and apricots on skewers
12. Grill over coals or broil in oven
13. While meat grills, remove bay leaves from marinade and transfer it to a heavy pot
14. Bring to a boil and add the cornstarch / water mixture to thicken a bit
15. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
