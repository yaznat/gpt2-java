Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Dry mustard
• Onion soup mix
• Water
• Cheddar cheese
• Swiss cheese
• Frozen french fries

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef in skillet
2. Drain grease
3. Stir in pepper, garlic powder, mustard, soup mix and water
4. Blend well
5. Mix in 1 c of the cheddar and the swiss cheese
6. Pour mixture into a 9x13 baking dish
7. Top with french fries
8. Bake 20-25 minutes at 400 until fries are golden brown
9. Top with remaining cheddar cheese and return to oven for 5-10 minutes until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
