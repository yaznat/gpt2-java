Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Orange juice
• Oranges, zest of

Steps:
1. Mix together cream and sugar in a bowl
2. Add eggs one at a time and mix completely each time
3. Add orange juice and zest
4. Pour in 4 coffee cups evenly
5. Bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 20-30 minutes
6. Cool and enjoy
