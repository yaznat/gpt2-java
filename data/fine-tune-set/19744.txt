What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Cheeseburger Fries Casserole With Veggies

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Condensed golden mushroom soup
• Condensed cheddar cheese soup
• Frozen shoestring french fries
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Carrots
• Zucchini
• Mushroom
• Broccoli

Steps:
1. Brown turkey in a large skillet
2. Drain
3. Add garlic powder, onion powder, carrots, broccoli
4. Saute about 5 minutes or until carrots are tender
5. Add mushrooms and zucchini
6. Saute about 3 minutes
7. Stir in soups
8. Pour into a greased 9 x 13 pan
9. Arrange fries on top
10. Bake uncovered at 350 for 50-55 minutes or until fries are golden brown
11. Let stand 5 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
