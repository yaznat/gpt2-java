What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Ambrosia 1 2 3 4 Salad

Ingredients:
• Crushed pineapple
• Mandarin oranges
• Sour cream
• Shredded coconut
• Maraschino cherries

Steps:
1. Drain juice from fruit
2. Let stand for about 10 minutes before mixing ingredients
3. Stir the sour cream and coconut together
4. Add the two fruits
5. Pour into a pretty clear dish if you have it
6. Place the cherries on top
7. Chill for at least 30 minutes before serving
8. Keep in the refridgerator till ready to serve
9. It looks very inviting and delicious! and it is!<|endoftext|>
