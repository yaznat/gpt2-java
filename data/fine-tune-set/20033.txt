Give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Omelette Wrap

Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• Eggs
• Mayonnaise
• Dill
• Lemon juice
• Watercress
• Smoked salmon
• Lebanese cucumber

Steps:
1. Spray medium frying pan with cooking oil spray, cook half the eggs over medium heat, swirling the pan to a make a thin omelette
2. Remove from the pan
3. Cool on baking paper covered wire rack
4. Repeat with remaining eggs
5. Combine mayonnaise, dill and lemon juice in a small bowl
6. Spread each omelette with half of the mayonnaise mixture
7. Top each with half the watercress, salmon and cucumber
8. Roll omelette to enclose filling and serve<|endoftext|>
