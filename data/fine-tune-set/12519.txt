Ingredients:
• Oysters
• Oyster liquor
• Unsalted butter
• Flour
• Celery ribs
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Fresh curly-leaf parsley
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Milk
• Heavy cream

Steps:
1. Combine oyster liquor with 1 cup water in a 2 quart saucepan
2. Bring to a simmer over medium heat
3. Add the oyster and simmer until their edges just begin to curl, about 2 minutes
4. Strain oysters through a fine sieve set over a medium bowl
5. Reserve oysters and cooking liquid separately
6. Heat butter in a 4 quart saucepan over medium-high heat
7. Add flour and cook, whisking constantly, until goldn brown, 3-4 minutes
8. Reduce heat to medium, add celery, garlic, onions, parsley, salt, pepper, and cayenne
9. Cook, stirring frequently with a wooden spoon, until onions and celery are very soft, about 25 minutes
10. Stir in milk, cream, and reserved oysters with their cookingliquid and cook, stirring occasionally, until just hot, about 5 minutes
11. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
