Ingredients:
• Large shrimp
• Cajun seasoning
• Canola oil
• Green peppers
• Red peppers
• Garlic cloves
• White wine
• Lemon juice
• Hot sauce
• Cooked brown rice

Steps:
1. Toss shrimp with cajun spice seasoning and set aside
2. Heat 1 tsp oil in large skillet and saute' peppers for 6 minutes over medium-high heat
3. Turn heat down and add minced garlic, stirring constantly so that garlic doesn't burn
4. Remove peppers and garlic from pan, wipe pan out with paper towel
5. Return pan to medium-high heat, and heat remaining tsp of oil
6. Add shrimp and cook approximately 3 minutes, stirring frequently
7. Add wine and lemon juice, peppers and garlic, and cook for 2 to 3 minutes
8. Season with hot sauce
9. Serve each portion over 1 / 2 cup cooked brown rice<|endoftext|>
