Ingredients:
• Spinach
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt and pepper
• Paprika
• Chili powder
• Butter
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Clean spinach then steam about 3 minutes
2. Leaves will look limp but still green
3. In a bowl combine eggs, milk, spices, blend well with a fork
4. In a pan melt 1 tbs butter, heat, add part of the egg mixture
5. Add spinachleaves, as many as you like, flip omelette carefully over to brown other side
6. Put on a plate, spread sourcream to your taste, close omelette, and serve
7. Extremeley delicious if you love spinach and sour cream<|endoftext|>
