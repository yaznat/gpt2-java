Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cookie Crisp

Ingredients:
• Cooking apples
• Vanilla
• Sugar
• Flour
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Butter
• Oatmeal raisin cookie dough

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325 degrees
2. Place butter in 8x8 baking dish, tilt dish to coat sides
3. Toss apple slices with vanilla in a large bowl
4. In a separate bowl, stir dry ingredients together until well mixed
5. Sprinkle dry ingredients over apples, stirring until well coated
6. Place apples in baking dish, bake at 325 degrees for 15-20 minutes, until apples are softening
7. While apples are baking, chop cookie dough into small pieces, a bit bigger than chocolate chips
8. Remove apples from oven, evenly sprinkle cookie dough over apples and return to oven for 10-15 minutes, until cookie topping is lightly browned
9. The cookie dough will spread a bit, so there is no need to cover the apples completely<|endoftext|>
