Ingredients:
• Oreo cookies
• Vanilla ice cream
• Cool whip topping
• Chocolate
• Milk
• Chocolate pudding mix

Steps:
1. Allow the ice cream and whipped cream to thaw on the counter
2. Meanwhile, make chocolate pudding, according to the directions on the box
3. Set aside
4. Mix the partially thawed creams together
5. Mix them in well to resemble a
6. Soft ice cream texture
7. Pull out 12 oreos
8. Eat one
9. Put 11 in a bag and press down hard on the stack of them to crush them a few times
10. Mix half of the pudding with the cookies and line the bottom of a 9x9 glass dish
11. Layer half of the cream mixure on top
12. Pull out 12 oreos
13. Eat one
14. Put 11 in a bag and press down hard, again
15. Mix in the rest of the pudding with the oreos and layer them atop the cream mixture
16. 9
17. Layer the rest of the cream mixture on top of the pudding mixture
18. Pull out 12 oreos
19. You guessed it - eat one
20. Repeat crushing technique
21. Sprinkle these last oreos on top
22. Drizzle your beautiful cake creation with chocolate syrup
23. I used the hot fudge kind
24. 13
25. Cover with foil and freeze for at least a couple of hours
26. 14
