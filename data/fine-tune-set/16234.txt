Ingredients:
• Parmesan cheese
• Bacon
• Sea scallops
• Shrimp
• Scallions
• Toothpicks

Steps:
1. Heat a saut pan over medium-high heat for 5 minutes
2. Fry bacon strips on each side for 1 minute until almost crisp, but still pliable
3. Set aside onto a paper towel
4. Drain grease from pan
5. In the same saut pan over medium-high heat, sear scallops on both sides for 2 -3 minutes until caramelized on top, or opaque
6. Set aside
7. Saut the shrimp in a saut pan over medium high heat for 2-3 minutes
8. Set aside
9. Spread each bacon strip with 1 / 2 tablespoons of alouette
10. Wrap the alouette covered bacon around the side of the scallop
11. Skewer with a toothpick and arrange on a platter
12. Wrap each shrimp also with the alouette covered bacon and skewer with a toothpick
13. Sprinkle with chopped scallions
14. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
