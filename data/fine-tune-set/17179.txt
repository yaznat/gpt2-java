Ingredients:
• Water
• Tea bags
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Almond extract
• Lemons, juice of

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, bring 2 cups of water to a gentle boil
2. Add the tea bags
3. Cover and remove pan from the heat source
4. Steep for 10 minutes
5. Take out tea bags and don't squeeze them
6. Pour the tea into a 2-quart pitcher
7. Add in the sugar
8. Stir until dissolved
9. Add in the vanilla, almond extract, and lemon juice
10. Stir to mix
11. Add the remaining 6 cups of water and stir
12. Let tea cool to room temperature, then chill in the refrigerator
13. Serve in glasses over ice<|endoftext|>
