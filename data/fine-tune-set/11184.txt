Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Whole wheat bread
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• Honey
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Arrange banana slices in single layer on two slices of bread
2. Top with remaining bread
3. In bowl, combine eggs, milk, vanilla, cinnamon and salt
4. Whisk to blend
5. Soak each sandwich in egg mixture for a minute or two or until bread is saturated
6. Melt butter in skillet over medium heat
7. Place sandwiches in skillet
8. Cook until bottoms are golden, 2-3 minutes
9. Flip sandwiches
10. Place pan on sandwiches to press
11. Cook until crisp and golden, 2-3 minutes
12. Cut sandwiches into quarters
13. Serve warm with drizzle of honey and sprinkling of confectioners' sugar<|endoftext|>
