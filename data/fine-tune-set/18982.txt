Ingredients:
• Pie crust
• Sugar
• Eggnog
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Partially bake the pie crust
2. In a large bowl, combine eggnog, sugar, and eggs
3. Blend well
4. Pour into crust
5. Sprinkle with nutmeg or cinnamon
6. Bake at 350 for 35 minute
7. Or until almost set<|endoftext|>
