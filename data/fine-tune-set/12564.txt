Ingredients:
• Brussels sprout
• Green lentil
• Dried thyme
• Dried savory
• Black pepper
• Water
• Onion
• Celery
• Carrot
• Rutabagas
• Bay leaves
• Fresh gingerroot
• Low sodium soy sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f degrees
2. In the bottom of each sprout, cut an"x"
3. Place in a large casserole dish
4. Add all other ingredients except soy sauce and combine well
5. Bake uncovered, stirring occasionally, until lentils and vegetables are tender, about 1 hour
6. Check halfway through, and add more water if necessary
7. After removing from oven, stir in soy sauce and serve, preferably over rice<|endoftext|>
