Ingredients:
• Lowfat chocolate milk
• Banana
• Creamy peanut butter
• Low-fat vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Place milk, banana and peanut butter in blender
2. Cover and process on high power until smooth
3. Add ice cream
4. Process until smooth
5. Variations:
6. Can replace vanilla with 1 cup chocolate ice cream or ice milk, if desired<|endoftext|>
