What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Cran Sake Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Sake
• Cranberry juice
• Lime wedges
• Sprite

Steps:
1. Fill a martini shaker with ice
2. Add sake, cranberry juice and juice of 1 lime wedge
3. Shake-shake-shake the cocktail and strain into a martini glass or some other attractive glass with a stem
4. This cocktail could also be served in a highball glass, but stemware looks so much more elegant
5. If you want it sweet, top the glass off with sprite to give the drink a little sparkle
6. Garnish with other lime wedge<|endoftext|>
