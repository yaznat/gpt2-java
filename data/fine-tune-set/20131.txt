Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Style Vegetable Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Honey
• Stir-fry sauce
• Red pepper flakes
• Peanuts
• Broccoli florets
• Mushrooms
• Onion
• Carrot

Steps:
1. Combine honey, stir-fry sauce and pepper flakes in small bowl
2. Set aside
3. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
4. Add vegetables
5. Cook and stir vegetables for 2-3 minutes or until tender
6. Add honey mixture
7. Cook and stir for about 1 minute or until vegetables are glazed and sauce is bubbly
8. Serve vegetables over steamed rice or noodles<|endoftext|>
