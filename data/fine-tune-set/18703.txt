Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Lemon juice
• Sugar
• Salt
• Orange rind
• Eggs
• Milk
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Mash bananas, then add lemon juice
2. Stir in 2 / 3 cups sugar, salt, orange rind, egg yolks, milk and rice
3. Pour into baking dish and set in pan of water
4. Bake at 350 degf for about 35 minutes
5. Beat egg whites until stiff and add remaining sugar
6. Spread over pudding and bake about 15 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
