Can you give me a pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Blt Bacon Lettuce Tomato Pizza

Ingredients:
• Pizza crust
• Mozzarella cheese
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Bacon
• Roma tomatoes
• Lettuce
• Tzatziki

Steps:
1. Brush olive oil over the pizza base
2. Spread 3 / 4 of the mozzarella over the oiled surface
3. Distribute bacon pieces over the cheese
4. Place enough tomato slices over the bacon to cover the pizza in one single layer
5. Spread another layer of mozzarella over the tomatoes
6. Transfer to the preheated pizza maker machine and cook until the crust is crisp and golden and the cheese at the centre is bubbling
7. While pizza is cooking mix the tzatziki or mayonnaise with the lettuce
8. When the pizza is ready, remove, cut into pieces and add the dressed lettuce to each piece
9. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
