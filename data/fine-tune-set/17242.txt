Ingredients:
• Flank steak
• Black pepper
• Olive oil
• Fresh lime juice
• Soy sauce
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Score the steak 1 / 4 inch deep on both sides
2. Rub the black pepper on both sides of the steak, amount depending on your tolerance for black pepper! place in a large shallow dish or a thick ziplock plastic bag
3. Combine olive oil and the remaining ingredients in a small bowl
4. Stir well
5. Pour the marinade mixture over the steak
6. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for 8 hours
7. Remove the steak from marinade, discarding the marinade
8. Grill, covered, over medium-hot coals for 4 minutes on each side or to desired degree of doneness
9. Let rest for 5 minutes, covered
10. To serve, slice the steak diagonally across the grain into thin slices
11. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
