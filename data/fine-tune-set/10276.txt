What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>35 Calorie Pumpkin Cookies

Ingredients:
• Canned pumpkin
• Egg
• Plain yogurt
• Honey
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Splenda granular
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Nutmeg
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Combine the first 5 ingredients in a large mixing bowl
3. Stir until blended
4. In another bowl combine all of the remaining ingredients
5. Slowly add the dry ingredients to the pumpkin mixture stirring until well blended
6. Cover cookie sheets with aluminum foil, and lightly grease with a non-stick cooking spray
7. Drop small spoonfuls of dough onto the sheet and bake for 10-15 minutes
8. Allow to cool
9. I love to refrigerate these cookies and serve them cold, but i'm sure they would taste great at room temperature as well<|endoftext|>
