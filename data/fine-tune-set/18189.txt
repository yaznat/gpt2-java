Ingredients:
• Egg
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Spray an 8-inch pie plate with vegetable cooking spray
3. In a large bowl combine egg with melted butter
4. Whisk until well combined
5. Sift together flour, sugar, baking soda, baking powder and salt in a small bowl
6. Combine with egg mixture
7. Pour in buttermilk and stir until just combined<|endoftext|>
