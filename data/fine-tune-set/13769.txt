Give me a pork recipe.<|endoftext|>Apricot Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Apricot preserves
• Italian dressing
• Dijon mustard
• Garlic salt
• Pork chops

Steps:
1. Blend all ingredients except pork chops in plastic bag
2. Pour 3 / 4 cup of the marinade over the chops, turn to coat
3. Marinade, at least 3 hours turning occasionally
4. Put the 1 / 2 cup of marinade in refrigerator
5. After, put chops on grill turning once and brush frequently with leftover 1 / 2 cup marinade, until chops are done<|endoftext|>
