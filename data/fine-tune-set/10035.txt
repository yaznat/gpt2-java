Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>30 Minute Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Black beans
• Reduced-sodium beef broth
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Chili powder
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Fresh cilantro
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium heat
2. Add the ground beef and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, breaking it into 3 / 4 inch or so crumbles and stirring occasionally
3. Drain off the fat
4. Stir in the beans, broth, tomatoes and chili powder, and bring to a boil
5. Reduce heat, cover and simmer 20 minutes to develop flavors, stirring occasionally
6. Serve with toppings, as desired<|endoftext|>
