Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>7 Layer Casserole

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Carrots
• Celery
• Onion
• Sliced mushrooms
• Stewed tomatoes
• Cracker crumbs
• Water
• Ground beef
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 350f
2. Layer potatoes, carrots, onions, mushrooms, celery, tomatoes and ground beef in a baking dish
3. Pour juice and water over mixture
4. Salt & pepper to taste
5. Sprinkle crackers or panko bread crumbs over top
6. Bake covered in preheated oven at 350 degrees for 2 hours
7. Uncover the last half hour to brown<|endoftext|>
