Ingredients:
• Silken tofu
• Chicken broth
• Vegetables
• Parmesan cheese
• Onion
• Celery & leaves
• Butter
• Garlic cloves
• Dried bay leaves
• Curry powder
• Ground coriander
• Salt
• Dried thyme
• Dried tarragon

Steps:
1. Saute celery & onion in butter or oil over medium-low heat, until tender
2. Meanwhile, discard liquid from tofu package and place tofu in blender along with cooked vegetables, 2 cups of broth, and cheese or yeast flakes
3. Blend tofu mixture until smooth
4. Add garlic to pan with celery and saute another 2 minutes
5. Remove from heat and let cool a minute or two
6. Stir in tofu mixture and return pan to heat
7. Add all spices
8. Stir well, and add more broth if too thick for your liking
9. Cover and simmer on low heat, stirring occasionally, 20-30 minutes
10. Taste and correct seasonings, adding more salt or broth if needed
11. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
