Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Beer Batter Waffles

Ingredients:
• Self-rising flour
• Beer
• Unsalted butter
• Milk
• Eggs
• Honey
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Preheat a waffle iron according to manufacturer's instructions
2. Combine flour, beer, butter, milk, egg yolks, honey, and vanilla in a large bowl
3. Stir until the mixture is smooth
4. In a separate bowl, beat egg whites until stiff peaks form
5. Gently fold egg whites into batter
6. Spray preheated waffle iron with non-stick cooking spray
7. Ladle the batter into waffle iron
8. Cook the waffles until golden and crisp
9. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
