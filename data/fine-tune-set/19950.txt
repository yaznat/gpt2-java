Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Canola oil
• Sesame oil
• Yellow onion
• Crabmeat
• Light soy sauce
• Scallion
• Red chile

Steps:
1. Lightly beat eggs with salt and pepper, set aside
2. Heat wok with 2 tsp oil and sesame oil
3. Add onion and fry until soft
4. Push to one side of the wok, add crabmeat or shrimps and stir-fry well
5. Stir in soy sauce, more salt and pepper if desired, then remove crabmeat mixture and set aside
6. Heat the remaining 2 tsp oil in the wok
7. Add beaten eggs
8. Swirl pan around to spread the omelette evenly
9. When eggs have set, add the cooked crabmeat / shrimp mixture and sprinkle with spring onion and red chillies
10. 'fold'in the four corners of the omelette to cover the filling
11. Cover pan with a lid for 1 minute, then turn out omelette onto a plate and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
