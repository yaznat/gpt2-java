Ingredients:
• Apple pie filling
• Raisins
• Cinnamon
• Flour tortillas
• Mexican blend cheese

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine apple pie filling, raisins and cinnamon
2. Coat a 10 inch skillet with cooking spray
3. Heat over medium heat until hot
4. Place one tortilla in skillet
5. Top with 2 t cheese and 1 / 4 cup pie filling mixture
6. Cook 1-2 minutes or until cheese is melted and bottom of tortilla is golden brown
7. Using a large spatual, fold tortilla in half
8. Transfer to a foil lined baking sheet
9. Repeat until all eight quesadillas are made
10. Sprinkle remaining cheese evenly over top of quesadillas
11. Bake at 350 degrees for 2-3 minutes or until cheese is melted and quesadillas are heated through
12. Cut each quesadilla into 3 wedges<|endoftext|>
