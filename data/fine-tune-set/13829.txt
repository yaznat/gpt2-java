Ingredients:
• Pork
• Brown sugar
• Hoisin sauce
• Soy sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Sesame oil
• Green onion
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. In a large bowl combine all ingredients except the pork strips
2. Mix until blended
3. Add in the pork strips and toss to coat with the mixture
4. Cover and refrigerate for a minimum of 8 hours
5. Set oven to 350f
6. Remove the pork from the marinade and dry the strips with paper towels
7. Place on a single layer in a greased baking sheet
8. Bake for about 25-30 minutes or until the pork is just tender and lightly browned<|endoftext|>
