Please give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>African Yam Stew

Ingredients:
• Vegetable broth
• Onion
• Yams
• Canned chick-peas
• Brown rice
• Salt
• Peanut butter
• Collard greens
• Fresh lemon juice
• Black pepper
• Braggs liquid aminos
• Chili sauce

Steps:
1. Heat 2 tblsp of stock in a large pot
2. Add the onion and saute over medium heat for 5 minutes, adding more stock if necessary
3. Add the remaining stock, yams, chickpeas, rice and salt
4. Simmer for 45 minutes
5. In small bowl, blend the peanut butter with 1 / 2 cpu of liquid from the stew to make a smooth paste
6. Stir into the stew along with the greens and cook for 5 minutes
7. Stir in the lemon juice, pepper and tamari or braggs
8. Add chili sauce to taste
9. Adjust seasonings and serve<|endoftext|>
