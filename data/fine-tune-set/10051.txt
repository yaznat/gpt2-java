What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Adopted Chili

Ingredients:
• Beef brisket
• Beef bouillon cube
• Lean pork
• Budweiser beer
• Onion
• Water
• Wesson oil
• Chili powder
• Cayenne
• Garlic cloves
• Cumin
• Green chilies
• Dry mustard
• Tomato sauce
• Brown sugar
• Salt and pepper
• Oregano
• Masa harina

Steps:
1. In a large kettle or dutch oven, brown the beef, pork and onions in hot wesson oil
2. Add salt and pepper to taste
3. Add remaining ingredients
4. Stir well
5. Cover and simmer 3 to 4 hours, until meat is tender and chili is thick and bubbly
6. Stir occasionally<|endoftext|>
