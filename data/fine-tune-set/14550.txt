What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Baby Potatoes And Green Beans Meatless

Ingredients:
• Baby red potatoes
• White potatoes
• Fresh green beans
• Butter
• Sea salt

Steps:
1. Cook the unpeeled potatoes and green beans separately in salted water 'til slightly fork-tender
2. I use the microwave
3. Or you can steam them
4. The green beans cook faster than the potatoes will
5. Drain well and let set in the cooking pan with a lid on for about 4 minutes
6. Pour the potatoes and beans into a large bowl
7. To the potatoes, add the butter and sea salt and gently toss together
8. Pile into a serving bowl and serve
9. Option: sprinkle with grated parmasan cheese or crispy, crumbled bacon<|endoftext|>
