Ingredients:
• Round steak
• Tomato soup
• Dry mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Onion
• French dressing
• Raw potatoes
• Water

Steps:
1. Trim fat from steak and cut into seving-size pieces
2. Place in 13" x 9" pan
3. Cover with sliced raw potatoes
4. Combine soup, dry mustard, worchestershire sauce, onion, and dressing, and pour over meat and potatoes
5. Cover tightly and bake at 325f for 2 hours<|endoftext|>
