What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Banana Bread Pudding W Wheat Bread

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat bread
• Milk
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Almond flavoring
• Cinnamon
• Bananas
• Sea salt

Steps:
1. Cut bread into 1" cubes
2. Put bread and milk in bowl and set in fridge for 1 hour, stirring occasionally to make sure all bread gets soaked
3. Heat oven to 325 degrees
4. Spray / oil 9x13 glass baking dish
5. In separate bowl, whisk together sugar, eggs, vanilla, almond flavoring, and cinnamon until frothy
6. Remove bread from fridge
7. Sprinkle with salt and stir
8. Add egg mixture and stir until well blended
9. Fold in bananas, stirring gently until banana is evenly distributed
10. Pout into baking dish and bake for 1 hour 15 minutes, or until golden brown and mostly firm
11. Cool and serve<|endoftext|>
