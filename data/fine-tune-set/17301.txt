Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Vegetable oil
• Cajun seasoning
• Salt
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f
2. Wash then slice the potatoes, lengthwise, into 8 long strips
3. Place them in salted water, to cover, for at least 15 minutes
4. This will remove the surface starch
5. Dry, then coat well with oil, butt kickin' blacken, and salt
6. Place on a sheet pan
7. Bake at 450 for 20 - 25 minutes, or until they get a little soft, and begin to brown
8. Cut each piece of bacon into 2 long strips, and wrap around the potatoes
9. You won't need toothpicks if you tuck the ends under the potatoes when you place them back in the pan
10. Place back in the oven and cook for another 10 - 15 minutes, or, until the bacon looks crispy
11. Serve immediately with "bacon sour cream"<|endoftext|>
