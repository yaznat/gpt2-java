Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>711 Kirkwood S Barley Casserole

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Shortening
• Onion
• Celery
• Bell pepper
• Barley
• Tomato paste
• Water
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In a large skillet brown the first 5 ingredients together until meat is done-do not drain
2. Add the remaining ingredients to the skillet, mix well and simmer on low until done, around 30 minutes
3. You may need to add quite a bit of water if the casserole is drying out, depending on the barley used, and the altitude you live at<|endoftext|>
