Can you give me a custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Honey Coconut Custard

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Coconut
• Flour
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Honey

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients except honey in a blender or food processor
2. Blend until combined
3. With blender or food processor running, slowly pour in honey mixing to combine
4. Spray eight custard cups with nonstick cooking spray
5. Divide mixture evenly into cups
6. Place cups on cookie sheet
7. Bake at 325f for 30 to 35 minutes or until coconut is lightly browned<|endoftext|>
