What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Four Grain Waffles

Ingredients:
• Low-fat buttermilk
• Egg
• Egg whites
• Vegetable oil
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Cornmeal
• Rolled oats
• Baking powder
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Mix buttermilk, egg, egg whites, and vegetable oil together
2. In a seperate bowl mix all purpose flour, whole wheat flour, cornmeal, oats, baking powder and sugar together
3. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients
4. Stir until mixed
5. Spray preheated waffle iron with oil spray
6. Pour a portion of batter onto iron and cook acourding to the directions on iron
7. Top waffles with what you like
8. I enjoy maple syrup or yogurt<|endoftext|>
