What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Easy Fat Free Seasoned French Fries

Ingredients:
• Baking potato
• Cooking spray
• Mixed italian herbs
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. Cover an 8"x8" pan with tin foil and spray with cooking oil and sprinkle on salt
3. Cut potato to desired wedge sizes
4. Place potato pieces into quart size bag and spray in some cooking oil and add the herbs
5. Shake until the pieces are coated evenly
6. Pour into the 8"x8" pan and add some more salt on top
7. Place in oven and bake for 15-20 minutes for softer fries and 25-30 minutes for crispier fries
8. Enjoy with sauce of your choice!<|endoftext|>
