What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Chilled Tofu With Applesauce And Granola

Ingredients:
• Tofu
• Salt
• Applesauce
• Raisins
• Cinnamon
• Granola cereal

Steps:
1. Divide tofu in chilled serving bowls
2. Mash tofu with salt
3. Mix in applesauce and raisins
4. Top with cinnamon and granola<|endoftext|>
