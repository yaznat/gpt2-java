Ingredients:
• Stewing beef
• Red onion
• Garlic cloves
• Dark beer
• Beef broth
• Mushrooms
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Dried thyme
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Peel the garlic and the onion and cut it in half rings
2. Heat up some olive oil in a large pot and brown the meat
3. Stir in the garlic and the onion and let it simmer for about 2 minutes
4. Warm up the beer and the broth and add it to the meat
5. Add the thyme, pepper & salt to taste
6. Let it all simmer with the lid on, for about 2 hours until well done
7. Clean and cut the mushrooms in half and add them to the stew
8. Mix the cornstarch with a bit of cold water and thicken the stew to taste<|endoftext|>
