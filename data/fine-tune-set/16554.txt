Can you give me a smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana And Kiwi Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Kiwi fruits
• Skim milk

Steps:
1. Peel and slice a banana
2. Put it into a freezer-proof container and freeze for minimum 2 hours or overnight
3. Peel and chop the kiwi fruits
4. Place the banana, kiwi and milk in a blender
5. Process until smooth
6. Serve chilled!<|endoftext|>
