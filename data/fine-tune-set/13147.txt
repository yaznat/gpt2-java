Ingredients:
• Spaghetti
• Butter
• Cilantro
• Lime, juice and zest of
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Catfish fillets
• Ground pecans

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. While it is cooking, combine butter, minced cilantro, lime zest and juice, salt and pepper to taste in a small bowl
3. Heat broiler to high
4. Place catfish in roasting pan
5. Brush half of the butter-cilantro mixture over catfish
6. Broil until fish is browned, flaky and cooked through, about 6 minutes
7. Set aside and keep warm
8. Drain pasta, reserving 1 / 2 cup cooking liquid
9. Toss pasta with remaining butter-cilantro mixture, reserved liquid and 1 tablespoon of the pecans
10. Arrange pasta on a platter
11. Top with fish, garnish with remaining nuts and cilantro sprigs
12. Totoast pecans: place in a dry skillet over medium-low heat
13. Cook, stirring occasionally, until golden brown, 1-2 minutes- watch carefully to avoid burning<|endoftext|>
