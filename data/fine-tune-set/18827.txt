Generate a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Baked Custard

Ingredients:
• Skim milk
• Egg white
• Honey
• Salt
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. In a large saucepan, scald until nearly boiling the 4 and a half cups milk
3. Combine in a large bowl: the egg whites, honey and salt
4. Pour into scalded milk and add the vanilla
5. Blend well
6. Pour custard into a large ovenproof casserole dish and place the dish in a baking pan filled with one inch of water
7. Set in oven
8. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes, or until knife inserted in center comes out clean
9. Variations: sprinkle with a little cinnamon just before baking
10. 2% milk makes a firmer custard
11. Don't use reconstituted nonfat dry milk in this recipe
12. Serve hot or chilled, whatever your preference<|endoftext|>
