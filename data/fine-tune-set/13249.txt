Ingredients:
• Onion
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Kosher salt
• Pepper
• Fresh mushrooms
• Zucchini
• Garlic cloves
• Diced tomatoes
• Dried basil
• Dried oregano
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f
2. Grease a 13 x 8 inch baking dish and place onions on bottom
3. Place chicken breast on onions and sprinkle with salt and pepper
4. Layer mushrooms and zucchini over the chicken
5. Combine garlic, tomatoes, basil and oregano and pour over vegetables
6. Cover and bake for 30 miuntes or until juices run clear in chicken
7. Remove from oven and sprinkle with parmesan cheese
8. Allow to sit for about 10 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
