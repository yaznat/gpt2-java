Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Yellow onion
• Fresh thyme
• Ground black pepper
• Fresh rosemary
• Ground coriander
• Ground fennel
• Cayenne
• Egg
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Kosher salt
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, gently mix together the gound beef, onions, thyme, pepper, rosemary, coriander, fennel, cayenne, egg, and oil, if usiing
2. Season with salt
3. Divide the meat into 4 portions
4. Shape them into 1"-thick patties
5. Wrap patties in plastic wrap
6. Refrigerate until cold
7. This will help to firm them up, as they're moister than most burgers owing to the addition of the oil and the egg
8. Prepare a medium-hot charcoal fire or heat a gas grill to medium-high
9. Cook burgers, flipping once, until cooked to desired doneness, about 12 minutes total for medium rare
10. Serve on buns
11. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
