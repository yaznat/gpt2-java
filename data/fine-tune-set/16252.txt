Ingredients:
• Ground chicken
• Oil
• Dried chili pepper flakes
• Fresh garlic
• Fresh ginger
• White button mushrooms
• Water chestnuts
• Teriyaki sauce
• Reduced-fat peanut butter
• Sesame oil
• Rice vinegar
• Hoisin sauce
• Green onions
• Salt and black pepper
• Iceberg lettuce

Steps:
1. In a skillet cook the ground chicken or turkey in 1 teaspoon oil with the chili flakes garlic, ginger and mushrooms until the meat is no longer pink
2. Add in the water chestnuts, teryaki sauce, peanut butter, sesame oil, rice vinegar and hoisin sauce
3. Cook for about 3-4 minutes, stirring constantly
4. Add in the chopped green onions
5. Cook for about 1 minute
6. Season the mixture with salt and pepper
7. Spoon the mixture between large lettuce leaves
8. Roll up tighly
9. Serve with hoisin sauce on the side
10. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
