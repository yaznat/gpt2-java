Ingredients:
• Mashed sweet potatoes
• Sugar
• Salt
• Eggs
• Margarine
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Oleo
• Light brown sugar
• Pecans
• Flour

Steps:
1. Mix first seven ingredients and spread in a 13" x 9" baking dish
2. Mix topping ingredients and spread over sweet potato mixture
3. Bake 25 minutes in 350 degree oven<|endoftext|>
