Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• Quick-cooking oats
• Apple butter
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Nuts
• Raisins

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, cream butter and sugar
2. Beat in egg, oats, and apple butter
3. Combine dry ingredients
4. Gradually add to creamed mixture along with the milk
5. Beat until blended
6. Stir in nuts and raisins
7. Chill well
8. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto a lightly greased or parchment-lined cookie sheet
9. Bake at 350 for 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
