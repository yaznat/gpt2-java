What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>5 Minute Easy Vegan Chicken Guacamole Tacos

Ingredients:
• Taco shells
• Avocado
• Garlic clove
• Onion
• Vegetarian chicken pieces
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Combine avacado, salt, lemon juice, garlic, and onion
2. Mush into a paste
3. Microwave nuggets for 1 minute and then chop
4. Fill shells with guacamole, 'chicken', and top with tomatoes!
5. Serve with whatever you like
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
