Can you give me a tea recipe?<|endoftext|>Apricot Tea Delight

Ingredients:
• Tea
• Apricot nectar
• Orange juice
• Sugar
• Lemon juice
• Ginger ale
• Ice cube

Steps:
1. Advance preparation: combine hot tea, apricot nectar, orange juice, sugar, and lemon juice
2. Chill tea-apricot mixture thoroughly in large pitcher
3. When chilled carefully pour in ginger ale
4. Serve over ice cubes<|endoftext|>
