Ingredients:
• Low-fat milk
• Ice cream
• Non-dairy coffee creamer
• Strawberry
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In blender, add cold milk, non dairy creamer, ice cream, strawberries and vanilla extract and blend on high until smooth
2. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
