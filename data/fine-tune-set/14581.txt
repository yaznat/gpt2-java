What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>1 2 3 Rice And Chili Burritos

Ingredients:
• Chili with beans
• Instant rice
• Water
• Cheese
• Salsa
• Tortillas

Steps:
1. Heat chili in saucepan
2. Once heated, add uncooked rice and water
3. Stir together and let simmer over medium heat about 10-15 minutes until rice is cooked
4. Don't forget to stir!
5. Once cooked throughout, turn heat down
6. Place about 1 / 3 cup mixture on tortilla and top with 1-2 tablespoons shredded cheese and a few tablespoons salsa and roll up
7. Once all burritos have been made, zap in microwave for 30-60 seconds to melt cheese and reheat<|endoftext|>
