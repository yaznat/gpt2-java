Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Allspice
• Ginger
• Egg
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Apple butter
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Mix the first 8 ingredients in a medium sized bowl
3. Combine the milk, egg and oil
4. Stir into dry ingredients until just blended, do not overmix
5. Grease or paper line 12 standard sized muffin cups
6. Fill cups with a rounded tablespoon full of batter
7. Top the batter in the cups with a rounded teaspoon of apple butter
8. Cover carefully with the remaining batter
9. Mix together topping ingredients and sprinkle over muffins
10. Bake for 15- 18 minutes
11. Cool in pan for 10 minutes
12. Remove to wire rack to continue to cool before serving<|endoftext|>
