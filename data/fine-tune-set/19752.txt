Ingredients:
• Simply potatoes diced potatoes with onion
• Spinach
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Bell peppers
• Butter

Steps:
1. Lightly cover the bottom of a 10-inch non-stick skillet with butter
2. Heat butter over medium-low heat until hot
3. Add one package simply potatoes diced potatoes with onion to skillet
4. Cover with lid
5. Cook for 10 minutes, turning every 3 to 4 minutes
6. Add 3 cups of chopped bell peppers
7. Cover with lid
8. Cook for 3-4 minutes, turning every 2 minutes
9. Add 2 cups of grated sharp cheddar cheese and 4 cups of spinach
10. Cook for 2 minutes, turning constantly until cheese is melted and spinach is wilted<|endoftext|>
