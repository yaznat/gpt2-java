What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Campbell S Creamy Chicken Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Stir fry vegetables
• Condensed cream of mushroom soup
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Cooked rice
• Sambal oelek

Steps:
1. Spray skillet with cooking spray and heat over medium-high heat 1 minute
2. Stir-fry chicken in 2 batches until browned
3. Remove from pan and set chicken aside
4. Remove pan from heat
5. Spray with cooking spray
6. Add vegetables and stir-fry over medium heat until tender crisp
7. Add soup, water and soy sauce
8. Heat to a boil
9. Return chicken to pan and heat through
10. Serve over hot rice<|endoftext|>
