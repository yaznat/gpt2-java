Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>A Golden Chicken

Ingredients:
• Mexican oregano
• Kosher salt
• Garlic powder
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Roasting chickens
• Bay leaves
• Annatto oil

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine mexican oregano, kosher salt garlic powder and black pepper
2. Rinse chicken and pat dry
3. Sprinkle the spice mixture into the breast cavity and add 2 small bay leaves
4. Place the bird on a rack in a roasting pan
5. Brush skin well with annatto oil
6. Roast the chicken in a preheated 375 degree oven for 45-50 minutes or until juices run clear when pricked<|endoftext|>
