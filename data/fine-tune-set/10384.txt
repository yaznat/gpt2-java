Give me a cookie recipe.<|endoftext|>Amaretti Cookies No Flour And Low Fat

Ingredients:
• Slivered almonds
• Powdered sugar
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Egg whites

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees f
2. Line a cookie sheet with parchment or waxed paper, then generously grease the paper
3. Process the almonds in 2 batches in a food processor until finely ground
4. In a bowl combine the almonds with powdered sugar, vanilla and almond extract until blended, then stir in the egg whites
5. Mix to combine
6. Drop from a teaspoon onto greased cookie sheet
7. Bake for abouit 15 minutes, or until lightly browned<|endoftext|>
