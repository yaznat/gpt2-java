Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>3 Cheese Chicken Spinach Pasta Bake

Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Olive oil
• Fresh mushrooms
• Onion
• Bell pepper
• Frozen spinach
• Dried oregano
• Pepper
• Cottage cheese
• Cooked chicken
• Cheddar cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Milk
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Drain
3. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
4. Heat olive oil in large skillet over medium high heat
5. Cook mushrooms, onion and bell pepper until tender
6. Add spinach, oregano, and pepper
7. Mix well
8. Remove from heat
9. In a large bowl combine pasta, chicken, spinach mushroom mixture, soup, milk, 3 / 4 cup cheddar cheese, 1 / 4 cup parmesan cheese and mix well
10. Salt and pepper to taste
11. Coat a 9 x 13 inch baking dish with nonstick cooking spray
12. Pour chicken pasta mixture into baking dish
13. Sprinkle with remaining cheese
14. Bake in preheated oven for about 25 minutes or until bubbly and lightly browned<|endoftext|>
