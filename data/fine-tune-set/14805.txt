Ingredients:
• Orzo pasta
• Wild rice
• Red bell pepper
• Pine nuts
• Craisins
• Red onion
• Fresh basil
• Dijon mustard
• Rice wine vinegar
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Kosher salt
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Make the vinaigrette first by whisking the mustard and vinegar together
2. Slowly add oil in a steady stream, whisking constantly until all the oil is added and dressing is creamy and emulisified
3. Add the salt and pepper
4. Season with additional salt& pepper if desired
5. Toss the orzo, rice, red bell pepper, red onion, toasted pine nuts, basil, craisins or currants with the dijon vinaigrette and chill for a couple hours before serving<|endoftext|>
