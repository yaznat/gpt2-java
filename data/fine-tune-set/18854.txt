Give me a custard recipe.<|endoftext|>Best Ever Rice Custard Pudding

Ingredients:
• Cooked rice
• Eggs
• Whole milk
• Salt
• Sugar
• Nutmeg
• Vanilla essence
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Beat eggs and salt a little, add sugar then milk with splash of vanilla essence
2. Put rice or sago in a buttered dish, pour over the egg mixture and sprinkle with a pinch of nutmeg
3. Set in a dish of water and bake slowly in the oven at 160 c until custard sets
4. Extra info: the milk in new zealand is not watered down, still has cream in it, making it a creamy rice pudding
5. If not in nz or australia, then you can use half and half
6. I also add sultanas to this recipe on occasion, which i cook to plump, with the rice beforehand
7. Sometimes i dot the top of the rice pudding with knobs of butter in order to get a brown skin<|endoftext|>
