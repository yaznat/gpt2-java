What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Apple Sweet Potato Bake

Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Baking apples
• Marshmallows
• Brown sugar
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Butter a 3-quart casserole dish or 12"x8" baking pan
3. Arrange sweet potatoes in prepared dish and pour apples over them
4. Sprinkle brown sugar, salt, and marshmallows over apples
5. Top with cinnamon and dot with butter
6. Cover with foil and bake at 350 degrees f for 35-40 minutes<|endoftext|>
