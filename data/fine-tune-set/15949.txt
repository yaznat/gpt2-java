Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Easy Wet Burrito

Ingredients:
• Burritos
• Mild enchilada sauce
• Green onion
• Black olives
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Microwave burritos on a plate 5 at a time for 4-5 minutes
2. Place burritos in a dark baking pan
3. Pour 2 cans enchilada sauce over burritos
4. Cover with shredded cheese, green onion and black olives
5. Bake in oven at 350f for 15-20 minutes or until cheese is completely melted or sauce is bubbling<|endoftext|>
