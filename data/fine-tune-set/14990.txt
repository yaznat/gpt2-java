Give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>N Y C Chicken Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Onion
• Chicken breasts
• Soy sauce
• Carrots
• Bell pepper
• Celery
• Cooked rice
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large skillet
2. Add onion, saute until soft, add chicken & 2 table spoons soy sauce & stir fry for 5-6 minutes
3. Stir carrots, celery & peppers to chicken and soy sauce mixture and stir fry 4-5 minutes then add rice
4. Stir in eggs and 1 / 3 cup soy cause,
5. Heat and serve hot!<|endoftext|>
