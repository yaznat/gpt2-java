Give me a recipe for beans.<|endoftext|>Apple Bean Bake

Ingredients:
• Great northern beans
• Butter
• Cooking apples
• Brown sugar
• White sugar
• Catsup
• Cinnamon
• Molasses
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, melt butter, add cubed apples, and cook until tender
2. Add brown and white sugar, stir and cook til sugar disolves
3. Stir in catsup, cinnamon, molasses and salt
4. Add beans and mix well
5. Pour mixture into a 2 quart casserole
6. Bake in 400 degree oven for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
