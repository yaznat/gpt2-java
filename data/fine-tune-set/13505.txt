Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Beef And Cabbage Salad

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Plum sauce
• Napa cabbage
• Scallions
• Carrots
• Cilantro
• Rice vinegar
• Canola oil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef
2. Stir in plum sauce
3. Toss together cabbage, scallions, carrots, cilantro, vinegar, and oil
4. Season with salt and pepper to taste
5. Serve with the beef<|endoftext|>
