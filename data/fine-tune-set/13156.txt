What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>Chicken Lickin Good Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Lean pork chops
• Flour
• Salt
• Dry mustard
• Garlic powder
• Oil
• Chicken rice soup

Steps:
1. Dredge pork chops in mixture of flour, salt, dry mustard and garlic powder
2. Brown in oil in a large skillet
3. Place browned pork chops in a crock pot
4. Add the can of soup, undiluted
5. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours<|endoftext|>
