Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Mix Double Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• All-purpose flour
• Canola oil
• Eggs
• Water
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. In a medium bowl, combine all ingredients
3. Stir until well blended
4. I do not use the nuts
5. Drop mix from a teaspoon onto a cookie sheet lightly sprayed with cooking spray
6. Bake for 9 to 10 minutes
7. Once cookies have lost their gloss, yet still feel soft to the touch, remove from oven
8. Do not over bake these cookies
9. Let cool 1 minute, then remove to racks to finish cooling
10. Store in airtight container for up to 1 week<|endoftext|>
