Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Oil
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Crushed pineapple
• Fresh blueberries
• Pecans
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Grease 13x9x2-inch baking pan
3. Set aside
4. In medium bowl stir together flour, baking powder, baking soda, 1 teaspoons cinnamon, and 1 / 2 teaspoons salt
5. Set aside
6. In large bowl, stir together sugar, 1 / 2 cup brown sugar, oil and eggs
7. Add flour mixture and buttermilk alternately to sugar mixture, stirring after addition
8. Stir in pineapple
9. Spoon batter into prepared pan
10. For blueberry topping stir together all ingredients for topping
11. Sprinkle over batter
12. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes or until wooden toothpick inserted near center comes out clean
13. Cool in pan on wire rack for 45 minutes
14. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
