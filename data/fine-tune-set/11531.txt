What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>No Bake Cookie Crumble Cheesecake

Ingredients:
• Gelatin
• Milk
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Miniature semisweet chocolate chips
• Prepared graham cracker crusts
• Cookie

Steps:
1. In blender, sprinkle gelatin over cold milk
2. Let stand 2 minutes
3. Add hot milk and process at low until dissolved, about 2 minutes
4. Add cream cheese, sugar and vanilla and process until blended
5. Arrange mini chocolate chips in the bottom of the crust
6. Pour in gelatin mixture
7. Sprinkle with your favourite crushed cookies
8. Chill until firm, about 2 hours<|endoftext|>
