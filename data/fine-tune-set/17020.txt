Ingredients:
• Ice cube
• Vodka
• Apple schnapps
• Apple cider
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. 1
2. Fill a cocktail shaker halfway with ice cubes
3. Add vodka, schnapps and cider
4. Shake well
5. 2
6. In a shallow bowl, mix the sugar & cinnamon
7. Moisten the rims of two champagne flutes and coat with the cinnamon sugar
8. Strain the cocktail into the flutes<|endoftext|>
