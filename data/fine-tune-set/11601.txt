Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>1917 War Cake

Ingredients:
• Corn syrup
• Cold water
• Salt
• Clove
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Crisco
• Baking soda
• Flour
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f
2. Put first 6 ingredients in saucepan
3. Bring to a boil and cook 3 minutes after reaching boiling point
4. Add crisco
5. When cool add soda dissolved in a little hot water
6. Add flour and baking powder
7. Stir
8. Pour into greased tube pan
9. Bake for 1 hour at 325f<|endoftext|>
