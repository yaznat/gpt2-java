What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Cake Mix Butterscotch Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Yellow cake mix
• Instant butterscotch pudding mix
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Oil
• Water
• Eggs
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Mix together oil, water and egg
2. Add cake mix and puddings
3. Mix well
4. Pour half of batter into greased 9x13" pan
5. Top with half of crumb topping, then rest of batter, finally with last half of the topping
6. Bake at 350 for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
