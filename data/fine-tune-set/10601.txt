What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Pesto And Tomatoes

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Olive oil
• Diced tomatoes
• Pesto sauce
• Sweet onion
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Pepper
• Garlic salt
• Lemon pepper
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Prepare angel hair pasta to pkg
2. Directions and drain
3. In a large skillet heat olive oil
4. Saute onion and garlic
5. About 2 minutes
6. Add tomatoes and pesto
7. Add salt and pepper, continue to cook until hot
8. Add cooked pasta, parmesean cheese, garlic salt and lemon pepper
9. Toss and serve<|endoftext|>
