What's a good tea recipe?<|endoftext|>Autumn Holiday Tea

Ingredients:
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Mix ingredients well and store in air tight container
2. Boil milk for mug of tea
3. Add 1 plain tea bag per mug, pour hot milk over teabag in mug, sprinkle a pinch or two of dry mixture into hot milk and teabag
4. Stir
5. Leave teabag in mug
6. If you really like this, you can always make more of the dry ingredients, just make sure its in equal amounts
7. You can always add a little extra sugar in tea if you like it sweeter<|endoftext|>
