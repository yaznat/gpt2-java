Ingredients:
• White fish fillets
• Oil
• Onion
• Tomato sauce
• Celery
• Garlic cloves
• Mixed italian herbs
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180c
2. Arrange fish in a single layer on an oiled, shallow baking dish
3. Heat oil in a frying pan and saute onion until translucent
4. Add tomato sauce, celery, garlic, herbs, salt and pepper
5. Mix until well combined
6. Spoon sauce over fillets
7. Cover and bake 25 minutes
8. Lay foil on your baking tray
9. Spray with oil
10. Spread on the fish and top with the sauce
11. Lay another layer of foil over the top and firmly fold all of the foil's edges together to enclose the fish
12. Alternatively you can cook the fish in individual foils and serve in the foil parcel<|endoftext|>
