What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Nif S Mushroom And Cheddar Omelette Omelet 1 1 2 Ww Pt

Ingredients:
• Egg substitute
• Egg whites
• Mushroom
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Spray medium nonstick pan with cooking spray and heat to low-medium
2. Mix egg substitute, egg whites and mushrooms
3. Pour into pan
4. Sprinkle cheese on top
5. Do not stir!
6. Cover pan with a lid
7. It should set in about 5-10 minutes
8. Check it to see that the middle is set
9. Season, fold and serve!<|endoftext|>
