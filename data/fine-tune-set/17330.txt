Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Shallots
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ground pepper
• Fresh basil leaves
• Chopped tomato
• Gorgonzola
• Basil
• Chives
• Salt & fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Marinade:
2. Combine all ingredients in a blender and pulse till garlic is chopped up
3. This will be thick
4. Lay your steaks in a non-corrosive baking dish and poke with a fork
5. Spread with marinade, flipping steaks to cover all sides
6. Cover and refrigerate at least 12 hours
7. Topping:
8. Gently stir all ingredients together till well blended
9. Grill steaks to your preference, sprinkling with salt on each side after searing
10. Just before serving spoon topping on steaks
11. Cooking time is not included<|endoftext|>
