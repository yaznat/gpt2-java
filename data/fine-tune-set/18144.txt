Ingredients:
• Apple
• Water
• Brown sugar
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Sugar
• Salt
• Milk
• Eggs
• Oil
• Vanilla extract
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Cook apple with water until just barely softened
2. Sprinkle with brown sugar and set aside
3. Combine flour, baking powder, sugar and salt
4. Set aside
5. Beat milk, eggs, oil and vanilla together and stir into flour mixture
6. Carefully, fold in pecans and apples
7. Pour 1 / 3 cup of batter onto a hot griddle
8. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface
9. Flip
10. Cook until golden<|endoftext|>
