Ingredients:
• Shortening
• Granulated sugar
• Orange
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Ground almonds
• Confectioners' sugar
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees fahrenhet
2. Whisk the shortening in a large bowl until light and aerated
3. Use an electric mixer if possible
4. Gradually beat in the granulated sugar
5. Grate the rind of the orange onto a plate and squeeze juice into a cup
6. Be careful to remove any seeds from the juice
7. Whisk the eggs, orange rind and orange juice into the shortening mixture and continue for 3 to minutes after combined
8. Sift flour together with baking powder in a separate bowl
9. Stir the flour mixture and almonds into the shortening mixture until it forms a dough
10. Lightly flour a flat surface and roll out the dough until it's about 1 / 2-inch thick
11. Use a round cookie cutter to cut out 36 round cookies and place them on baking sheets
12. Bake for 10 minutes at 400 degrees f until golden
13. Remove from oven and allow to cool and firm on baking sheets for about 10 minutes
14. Mix confectioner's sugar with cinnamon and sift this mixture evenly over the baked cookies as they cool<|endoftext|>
