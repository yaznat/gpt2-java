Ingredients:
• Avocados
• Lemon, juice of
• Sliced almonds
• Ginger
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Tagliatelle pasta noodles
• Coriander leaves
• Flaked sea salt
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Bring a large saucepan of water to the boil
2. Pasta can be cooked before hand and then warmed up in the microwave
3. Preheat the oven to 180c to roast the almonds
4. This wont take long so keep an eye on them
5. Remove from oven when ready
6. About 5 minutes or until golden brown
7. In a bowl place the avocado chopped with a squeeze of the lemon juice over the cut avocado
8. In a large bowl place the warmed pasta with the oil and minced ginger, toss
9. Add some salt and 3 / 4 of the roasted almonds, toss
10. Then add the coriander and then the rest of the roasted almonds and toss
11. Add the avocado last, serve with some freshly ground black pepper and salt to taste, and some lemon juice then add the shaved parmigiano
12. Serve<|endoftext|>
