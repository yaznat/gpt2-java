Give me a pie recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Pie 1

Ingredients:
• Unbaked pie crusts
• Water
• Sugar
• Cream of tartar
• Ritz crackers
• Butter
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Lemon, juice and rind of

Steps:
1. Make your favorite pie crust
2. Boil water, sugar and cream of tartar
3. Add whole crackers, 1 at a time
4. Boil 2 minutes
5. Add remaining ingredients
6. Cool
7. Pour into prepared pie shell
8. Cover with top crust
9. Slit top for steam vents
10. Bake at 425 degrees for 10 minutes
11. Reduce heat to 350 degrees
12. Continue to bake 20 to 25 minutes or until golden brown
13. Remove from oven
14. Cool<|endoftext|>
