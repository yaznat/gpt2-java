Generate a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Beer Battered Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Beer
• Flour
• Cornmeal
• Paprika
• Garlic pepper seasoning
• Parsley
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Canola oil
• Tartar sauce

Steps:
1. Mix beer and flour in a bowl until you have a thin batter
2. Sometimes this uses more or less flour
3. Just adjust until it looks like a thin pancake batter
4. In a second bowl, mix remaining 6 ingredients
5. Cut tofu into 1 / 4 inch thick slices
6. I got about 10 or 12
7. Place slices on 1 piece of paper towel then place a couple of paper towels on top of the slices and pat to dry a little
8. Heat oil in heavy skillet or cast iron pan until one drop of water splashed in spits around the pan
9. Carefully dip slices of tofu in beer batter shaking off the excess
10. Place in cornmeal mixture and tap each side to get coating to stick
11. Place in hot oil and fry until golden about 3 minutes each side
12. Remove from pan and place on paper towels to drain
13. Serve with tartar sauce or sweet chili sauce<|endoftext|>
