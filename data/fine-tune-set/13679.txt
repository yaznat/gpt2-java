What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Almond Crusted Orange Mustard Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork chop
• Ground almonds
• Salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Black pepper
• Egg
• Water
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Mandarin oranges
• Dijon mustard
• Soy sauce
• Fresh parsley
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Mix ground almonds, salt, and peppers
2. In a small bowl mix egg and water
3. Spread almond coating in a shallow dish or plate
4. Dip chops in egg wash and then dredge in almond mixture
5. Set aside
6. Heat oil in a pan and saut onions and garlic until lightly browned
7. Remove from pan and set aside
8. Brown chops in oil over medium heat
9. 2-3 minutes on a side or until golden brown
10. Meanwhile mix liquid from oranges, soy sauce, and mustard
11. Add sauce to chops and turn heat down to low let simmer for 5 minutes or until chops are cooked through
12. Top with mandarin oranges and fresh parsley<|endoftext|>
