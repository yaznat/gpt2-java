Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Water
• Salt
• Pepper
• Parsley
• Chives
• Tarragon
• Thyme
• Butter
• Swiss cheese

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl whisk eggs and water together and then season with salt and pepper
2. Stir in herbs
3. In a skillet, melt the butter over medium high heat and add the egg mixture to pan
4. If using cheese, add cheese on top of eggs
5. Let eggs set around edges and then lift edges and tilt pan, so uncooked eggs will go under the cooked eggs
6. Cook until eggs are almost set and then using a spatula, roll 1 / 3 of omlet to center
7. Tilt pan and slide omlet onto plate
8. Garnish with chives, if desired<|endoftext|>
