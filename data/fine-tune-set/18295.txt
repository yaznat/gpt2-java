What's a good pancake recipe?<|endoftext|>Bisquick Pancakes Aka Silver Dollars

Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Eggs
• Milk

Steps:
1. Stir milk and eggs until blended
2. Stir into bisquick mix
3. Do not overstir
4. Pour by slightly less than 1 / 4 cupfuls onto hot greased griddle
5. For silver dollars drop by tablespoon onto griddle
6. Cook until pancake starts to bubble & edges are slightly dry
7. Turn
8. Cook until golden
9. Serve with butter and maple syrup<|endoftext|>
