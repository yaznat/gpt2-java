Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Arni Psito Roast Leg Of Lamb

Ingredients:
• Leg of lamb
• Olive oil
• Lemon, juice of
• Dried rosemary
• Dried thyme
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Potatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 500f
2. Place lamb fat-side up in shallow roasting pan
3. Rub lamb with olive oil and lemon juice
4. Season with rosemary, thyme, oregano, salt, pepper and garlic
5. Place in oven, reduce heat to 350f and rost lamb for 2 1 / 2 to 3 hours, or until internal temperature reaches 175-180f
6. To add the optional potates, about 40 minutes before lamb is done, peel and quarter 6 large potatoes and place them around the lamb
7. Sprinkle with additional lemon juice, salt, pepper and oregano to taste
8. After 20 minutes of baking, turn potatoes over to brown other side
9. Continue roasting until potatoes pierce easily with a fork
10. Let lamb stand 20 minutes before carving<|endoftext|>
