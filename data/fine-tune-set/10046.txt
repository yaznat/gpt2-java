Ingredients:
• Hamburger
• Taco seasoning
• Condensed tomato soup
• Diced tomatoes
• Diced tomatoes with jalapenos
• Black beans
• Baked beans

Steps:
1. Spray crockpot liner with pam or other cooking spray
2. Brown hamburger, drain, rinse to remove excess grease and place in crockpot
3. Sprinkle taco seasoning over the top of the hamburger
4. Add soup, tomatoes and beans, without draining liquid from tomatoes and beans
5. Mix well
6. Cover and let simmer on low for 8-10 hours
7. Serve with cheese, sour cream and tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
