Ingredients:
• Flank steaks
• Oil
• Salt and pepper
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• 7-up soda
• Tomato soup
• Cooked noodles

Steps:
1. Brown steak slowly in oil
2. Season to taste with salt and pepper
3. Add onion and green pepper the last 5 minutes of browning
4. Combine 7up and soup
5. Pour over steak
6. Cover, cook slowly 1 1 / 2 hours or until fork tender
7. Slice meat thinly
8. Place on platter
9. Surround with noodles
10. Pour sauce over meat or serve separately<|endoftext|>
