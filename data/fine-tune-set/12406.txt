What's a recipe for soup?<|endoftext|>Amazing Vegetable Soup South Beach Diet

Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Green cabbage
• Celery
• Leek
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh green beans
• Zucchini
• Summer squash
• Diced tomatoes
• Navy beans
• Parmesan cheese
• Basil pesto

Steps:
1. Combine broth, cabbage, celery, leek, garlic, and green beans in an 8-quart soup pot
2. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer until green beans are just tender
3. Add tomatoes, zucchini, and squash and cook an additional 45 minutes
4. Serve hot, sprinkled with parmesan cheese or with a spoonful of pesto stirred in, as desired<|endoftext|>
