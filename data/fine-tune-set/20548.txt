Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Milk
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until crisp
2. Or use the ready made bacon that takes about 40 seconds in the microwave to cook
3. In a large bowl, beat eggs
4. Add milk, cheese, sour cream, salt and pepper
5. Pour into a greased 13-in
6. X 9-in
7. X 2-in
8. Baking dish
9. Crumble bacon and sprinkle on top
10. Bake, uncovered, at 325 for 40-45 minutes or until knife inserted near the center comes out clean
11. Let stand for 5 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
