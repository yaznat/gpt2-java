What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amaretto Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Smart balance butter spread
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Amaretto liqueur
• Vanilla
• Egg
• Whole wheat flour
• Flour
• Oatmeal
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Chocolate chips
• Cherry-flavored dried cranberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. In a medium bowl cream butter and sugars until smooth
3. Add eggs, vanilla, and amaretto then cream again
4. In a separate bowl mix flours, salt, and baking soda
5. Add to wet mixture little by little
6. Fold in chocolate chips and craisins
7. Drop by level tablespoons onto greased cookie sheets and bake 9 minutes or until golden brown<|endoftext|>
