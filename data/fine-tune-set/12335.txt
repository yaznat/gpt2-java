Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Acorn squash
• Shallots
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Vegetable broth
• Light cream
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f / 190c
2. Cut the sweet potato, squash and shallot in half lengthwise
3. Brush the cut sides with oil
4. Put the vegetables, cut sides down in a shallow roasting pan with the garlic cloves
5. Roast in the preheated oven for about 40 minutes until tender and light brown
6. When cool, scoop the flesh from the potatoe and squash halves and put in a pan with the shallots
7. Remove the garlic peel and add the soft insides to the other vegetables
8. Add the broth and a pinch of salt
9. Bring to just a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer, partially covered, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are very tender
10. Let the soup cool slightly, then transfer to a blender and puree until smooth
11. Return the soup to the pan and stir in the cream
