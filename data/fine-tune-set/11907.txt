Ingredients:
• Cauliflower
• Unsalted butter
• Curry powder
• Milk
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Chop the cauliflower fairly small
2. Discard core
3. Cook the cauliflower in 1 tablespoon of the butter over medium heat until golden brown
4. Add the curry powder
5. Saute for five minutes, stirring regularly
6. Add the milk and bring to the boil, stir regularly
7. Add pinch of salt and generous pepper
8. Drop the heat, and simmer for six minutes
9. Remove from the heat
10. Taste
11. Add a pinch of salt, and retaste
12. When cool enough, process in a blender or food processor until smooth
13. Strain
14. Stir in the remaining butter
15. Taste
16. Continuing tasting and salting until all traces of bitterness are gone, and the puree tastes flavorful and sweet
17. Thin with milk, if necessary<|endoftext|>
