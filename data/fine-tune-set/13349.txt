Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil cooking spray
• Sweet potato
• Onion
• Red bell pepper
• Garlic clove
• Jalapeno pepper
• Cooked chicken breast
• Salsa
• Ground cumin
• Chicken broth
• Low-fat low-sodium chicken and rice soup
• Black beans
• Peanut butter

Steps:
1. Place a large dutch oven coated with cooking spray over medium high heat until hot
2. Add sweet potato, onion, bell pepper, garlic, and jalapeno
3. Saute 5 minutes
4. Stir in chicken and next 5 ingredients
5. Bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat
7. Simmer 10 minutes
8. Add peanut butter, stirring with a whisk
9. Cook 2 minutes<|endoftext|>
