Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Blueberry Cookies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• Sugar
• Egg yolk
• Whole milk
• Almond extract
• Lemon zest
• Almonds
• Frozen blueberries

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, combine flour, baking powder, and salt
2. In another medium bowl, cream together the butter and sugar using a hand mixer
3. Add egg and beat to incorporate
4. Add milk, almond extract, and lemon zest
5. Stir the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients
6. Fold in the almonds, then the blueberries
7. Chill the dough in the refrigerator for 30 minutes
8. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees f
9. Using two small spoons, dollop mix onto cookie sheets
10. Bake until golden brown around the edges, about 15 minutes
11. Cool the cookies on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
