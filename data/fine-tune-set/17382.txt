Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Water
• Almond extract

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, combine the sugar and water and bring to a rolling boil
2. Stir to prevent scorching
3. Remove from heat
4. Cool slightly and stir in extract to desired strength, starting with 1 / 2 teaspoons
5. Store in a closed container in the refrigerator for up to a week<|endoftext|>
