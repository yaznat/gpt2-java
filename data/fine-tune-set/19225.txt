Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Butter
• Icing sugar
• Eggs
• Dark chocolate
• Thickened cream
• Ladyfingers
• Hundreds and thousands

Steps:
1. Remove ice cream from freezer and stand for 10-15 minutes
2. Ice cream should be just softened but not melted
3. Meanwhile, using an electric mixer, cream butter and sugar until pale
4. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition
5. Mixture may curdle but that's ok: -)
6. Combine chocolate and cream in a bowl and microwave, stirring every minute, until melted and smooth
7. Add to butter mixture and beat until smooth
8. Sprinkle 1 packet of hundreds and thousands over base of a line springform cake pan
9. Carefully spoon over half the chocolate mixture so as not to disturb the sprinkles
10. Cut biscuits in half lengthways to make 2 thin, flat fingers
11. Arrange in a single layer to cover chocolate mixture, trimming if necessary
12. The easiest way to do this is to lay them pointing from the edge towards the centre, and then fill the centre with smaller bits
13. Spoon vanilla ice cream over biscuits
14. Press another layer of biscuits into surface of ice cream and pour over remaining chocolate mixture
15. Sprinkle with remaining hundreds and thousands
16. Cover tightly with plastic wrap and foil and freeze overnight or up to 4 weeks
17. Remove from pan and serve<|endoftext|>
