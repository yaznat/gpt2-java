Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Ground cumin
• Garlic clove
• Dried red pepper flakes
• Cooked beef
• Tortillas
• Lettuce leaves
• Avocado
• Tomatoes
• Colby-monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f
2. Mix the cream cheese, cumin, garlic & chili flakes in a bowl until well blended
3. In a skillet, brown beef pieces until warm
4. Divide the following between each tortilla, layering in this order
5. Spread a thin layer of the cream cheese mixture
6. Then place a lettuce leaf on each tortilla & top with beef
7. Sprinkle with avocado, tomatoes & cheese
8. Starting at one end, gently roll up each tortilla into a tight tube
9. Grate remaining cheese on top of wraps
10. Cook about 15 min till cheese is melted & wrap is heated<|endoftext|>
