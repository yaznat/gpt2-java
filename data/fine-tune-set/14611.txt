Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Acapulco Mexican Restaurant S Rice

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Long grain rice
• Lard
• Chicken broth
• Tomato juice
• Diced tomato
• Parsley
• Garlic
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• White pepper

Steps:
1. Saut onion and rice in lard until browned, 6 to 10 minutes, stirring constantly
2. Turn into baking dish
3. In large saucepan, combine broth, tomato juice, tomatoes, parsley, garlic, cumin and salt and pepper and bring to boil
4. Add broth to rice
5. Cover and bake at 350 degrees f for 20 to 30 minutes or until rice is fluffy
6. Use fork to fluff rice<|endoftext|>
