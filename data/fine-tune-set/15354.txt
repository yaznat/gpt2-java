Give me a recipe for a burger.<|endoftext|>Apple Butter Barbecue Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground chuck
• Sweet onion
• Light brown sugar
• Garlic powder
• Cajun seasoning
• A. 1. original sauce
• Ground sage
• Italian breadcrumbs
• Tomato sauce
• Apple butter
• Worcestershire sauce
• Kaiser rolls

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, combine ground beef, onion, brown sugar, garlic powder, cajun seasoning, steak sauce, ground sage, and italian breadcrumbs
2. Form mixture into four patties
3. For sauce combine tomato sauce, apple butter, and worcestershire sauce in a small saucepan
4. Bring just to boiling
5. Grill or broil burgers for 4 to 6 minutes per side or to desired doneness, brushing with sauce during grilling and after flipping
6. Heat any remaining sauce just until bubbly, serve with burgers on toasted kaiser rolls<|endoftext|>
