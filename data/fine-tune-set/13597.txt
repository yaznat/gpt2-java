Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Cabbage
• Onion
• White mushrooms
• Red wine
• Water
• Beef base
• Salt & pepper
• Season salt
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Tomato paste
• Mixed vegetables
• Pasta

Steps:
1. Put ground beef, cabbage, onion, and mushroom in a large stock pot
2. Brown on medium high heat until ground beef is fully cooked and cabbage is wilted and almost transparent
3. Add red wine
4. Let cook 1 minute
5. Add water, beef base, and seasonings
6. Bring to a rolling boil
7. Add tomato paste, if using
8. Reduce heat and simmer until 10 minutes prior to serving
9. 15 minutes prior to serving add in bagged frozen vegetables
10. 10 minutes prior to serving add pasta
11. Simmer until veggies and pasta are done
12. Serve immediately
