What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Easy And Simple Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic
• Onions
• Salt
• Cooked rice
• Eggs
• Green onion
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat
2. Add ginger and garlic
3. Cook 10 minutes or until browned and crisp, stirring constantly
4. Remove from pan
5. Set aside
6. Add sliced onion to pan
7. Reduce heat to medium-low
8. Cover and cook 10 minutes or until soft
9. Stir occasionally
10. Sprinkle salt over onion
11. Increase heat to medium-high
12. Add rice
13. Cook 4 minutes or until heated, stirring frequently
14. Make a well in center of rice
15. Add eggs, stirring to scramble
16. Gradually incorporate eggs into rice
17. Stir in ginger mixture, green onions, soy sauce, and sesame oil
18. Garnish with cilantro, if desired<|endoftext|>
