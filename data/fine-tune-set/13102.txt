Ingredients:
• Cod fish fillet
• Dark beer
• Baking potato
• Canola oil
• All-purpose flour

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees f
2. Combine fish and 1 / 4 cup beer in a medium bowl
3. Cover and chill for 1 hour
4. Place potatoes on a jelly-roll pan coated with cooking spray
5. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon oil, and sprinkle with 1 / 4 teaspoon salt
6. Toss well
7. Bake at 450 degrees for 20 minutes or until browned and crisp, stirring after 10 minutes
8. Drain fish, discard liquid
9. Sprinkle fish with 1 / 4 teaspoon salt
10. Lightly spoon flour into dry measuring cup, level with a knife
11. Combine remaining 3 / 4 cup beer, flour and 1 / 2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper in a medium bowl
12. Add fish to beer mixture, tossing gently to coat
13. Heat remaining 3 tablespoons oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
14. Remove fish from bowl, shaking off excess batter
15. Add fish to pan
16. Cook 3 minutes or until browned
17. Turn fish over, cook 3 minutes or until done
18. Sprinkle fish with 1 / 4 teaspoon salt
19. Serve immediately with chips<|endoftext|>
