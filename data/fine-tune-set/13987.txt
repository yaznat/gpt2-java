Ingredients:
• Large shrimp
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Red onion
• Tequila
• Cilantro
• Cajun seasoning
• Heavy cream
• Monterey jack cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet
2. Add shrimp
3. Cook 1 minute on each side, then add garlic and onion, stirring continuously for 1 minute
4. Add tequila and cilatro and flambe until flame subsides
5. Add cream and turn heat down to medium-low
6. Add cajun seasoning
7. Stir, then take shrimp out and set aside while sauce thickens
8. Check sauce for flavor, and add salt and pepper to taste
9. Place shrimp in a baking dish
10. Cover with sauce then top with cheese
11. Place in 350 degree oven until cheese is bubbly
12. Approx 5 minutes
13. Garnish with whole cilantro leave if desired and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
