Ingredients:
• Chili
• Potatoes
• Jalapeno jack cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Tabasco sauce

Steps:
1. Cut up potatoes into french fries
2. Fry in oil in a deep fryer at 350, until nice and crispy
3. Take out, drain, and put on paper towels to soak up grease
4. Place on cooking sheet, and put in oven on warm, until the fries you are making in the fryer are done
5. When all fries are done, put each serving in a ovenproof dish
6. Top with hot chili
7. Add cheeses to top, and put in oven, under broiler, for 2-3 minutes, or until cheese is melted
8. Sprinkle with hot sauce of your choice, , and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
