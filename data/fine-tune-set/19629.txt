Can you give me a potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Artichoke And Potato Hash

Ingredients:
• Artichokes
• Red potatoes
• Onions
• Scallions
• Capers
• Garlic
• Rosemary
• Chicken broth
• Salt and pepper
• Butter
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. This to me is best in a cast iron skillet or a stainless pan vs non stick
2. But use what you have
3. A one pan dish -- in a large sauce pan, melt the butter and add the oil
4. Add the onion and garlic and cook 2 minutes
5. Add the potatoes and a little broth and cook until the potatoes start to get slightly brown and soft
6. This will take around 8-10 minutes
7. During that time add a little broth at a time for a little moisture
8. Then the last 5 minutes, add the artichokes, capers, s / p, scallions, and rosemary
9. A little broth if necessary and cook another 5 minutes until everything is soft and it resembles a nice hash
10. Serve with fresh slices of flank or skirt steak or a nice fillet
11. Chicken is also great with this too<|endoftext|>
