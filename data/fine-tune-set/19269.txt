Ingredients:
• Chocolate graham cracker crumbs
• Dry roasted peanuts
• Light margarine
• Sugar-free vanilla ice cream
• Crunchy peanut butter
• Sugar-free whipped topping
• Sugar-free chocolate syrup
• Strong coffee

Steps:
1. In bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs, peanuts and melted margarine, stirring until combined
2. Pat into bottom of 13x9x2-inch pan and freeze for 30 minutes
3. While crust is freezing, allow ice cream to sit out on counter to soften
4. Once softened, with a large wooden spoon mix together the ice cream and peanut butter
5. You can either mix it completely or you can leave peanut butter ribbons throughout the pie
6. Totally up to you
7. Remove crust from freezer and pour ice cream mixture into the crust
8. Smooth top and return it to the freezer for at least 6 hours
9. About 15 minutes prior to serving, remove pie from freezer to soften slightly
10. In a 1 quart saucepan over low heat, stir chocolate sryup until melted and hot
11. Remove from heat and add in the coffee
12. Spoon hot fudge into plastic sandwich bag, close the top and place in freezer for 3-5 minutes
13. Remove from freezer, cut a small hole in the corner of the baggie and pipe fudge onto top of pie in zig-zags
14. Serve pie with a dollop of whipped topping!<|endoftext|>
