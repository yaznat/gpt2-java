Give me a fish recipe.<|endoftext|>Boiled Crawfish For A Crowd Justin Wilson

Ingredients:
• Salt
• Crab boil
• Lemons
• Cayenne pepper
• White onions
• Garlic
• Potatoes
• Smoked sausage
• Corn
• Crawfish

Steps:
1. Bring seasonings to boil for 10 minutes
2. Add potatoes, corn, and smoked sausage
3. Boil for another 10 minutes
4. Add crawfish
5. Bring back to boil
6. Cut fire off immediately
7. Let soak for 20 to 30 minutes
8. Drain
9. Peel and eat
10. While water is coming to a boil, cull and clean crawfish
11. Rinse well with garden hose and remove any dead ones
12. Always wear gloves when you fool with crawfish, but remember, they can still pinch you through gloves<|endoftext|>
