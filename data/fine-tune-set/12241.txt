Ingredients:
• Butter
• Flour
• Garlic clove
• Milk
• Onion powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Dried thyme
• Dried parsley

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan over medium heat, melt butter
2. Add minced garlic and saute 1 minute
3. Add flour and stir quickly, forming a paste and mixing well
4. Slowly add milk a little at a time, while stirring constantly to avoid scalding
5. Once milk is added and mixed well, reduce heat to medium-low and add herbs
6. Add salt / pepper to your liking
7. Turn off the heat and let the soup simmer for a couple of minutes, stirring occasionally
8. Use as a soup base or pour into your casserole<|endoftext|>
