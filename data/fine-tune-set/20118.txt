Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Chicken broth
• Apricot preserves
• Soy sauce
• Sesame seeds
• Green pepper
• Red pepper
• Green onions
• Garlic
• Ground black pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Medium shrimp
• Water chestnuts
• Baby corn
• Cooked brown rice

Steps:
1. In bowl, combine cornstarch, broth, preserves, soy sauce, and sesame seed until blended
2. Set aside
3. In large non-stick skillet or wok, stir-fry peppers, onions, garlic, and ground black pepper in oil for 5 min
4. , until crisp-tender add shrimp, water chestnuts, and corn
5. Stir-fry until shrimp turn pink
6. Stir broth mixture, and add to pan
7. Bring to boil
8. Cook and stir for 2-3 minutes or until thickened
9. Serve over hot cooked rice<|endoftext|>
