Can you give me a casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Acadia S Eggplant Casserole

Ingredients:
• Eggplant
• Yellow bell pepper
• Vidalia onion
• Margarine
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Low-fat monterey jack cheese
• Pam cooking spray
• Kosher salt

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven at 350 degrees f
2. Chop onion into 1 / 4" pieces
3. In skillet add 1 tablespoon of margarine
4. On medium-low heat saute onion until translucent
5. Stir frequently
6. Add bell pepper and eggplant pieces with onion
7. Sprinkle salt over mixture
8. Saute until tender
9. Spray pam in 2-quart baking dish
10. Add eggplant, pepper and onion mixture into baking dish
11. Mix cheeses together
12. Sprinkle cheese mixture on top of eggplant mixture
13. Bake in oven for 15-minutes or until cheese has melted
14. Serve and enjoy<|endoftext|>
