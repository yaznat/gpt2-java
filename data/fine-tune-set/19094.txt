What's a recipe for ice cream?<|endoftext|>Banana Ice Cream Without An Ice Cream Maker

Ingredients:
• Mashed bananas
• 2% low-fat milk
• Sugar
• Yogurt
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Put bananas in bowl and add lemon juice
2. Add sugar and mix
3. Add milk and yogurt and mix
4. Freeze until solid
5. Defrost mixture just enough to where you can get it out of the bowl and break it into pieces
6. Put the pieces in a blender or food processor until smooth
7. Mixture should come out like soft serve
8. You can not store this ice cream<|endoftext|>
