Give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>108 Mile Spinach Bacon Salad

Ingredients:
• Baby spinach
• Mushrooms
• Bacon
• Hardboiled egg
• Unsalted sunflower seeds
• Plain yogurt
• Buttermilk
• Fresh lemon juice
• Garlic
• Sugar
• White pepper

Steps:
1. Toss all the salad ingredients
2. Place in the refrigerator until you are ready to serve
3. Whisk the dressing ingredients together
4. When you are ready to serve, pour the dressing over the salad and toss well so the dressing is throughout the whole salad
5. Serve at once on cold plates<|endoftext|>
