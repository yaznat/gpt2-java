Ingredients:
• Beef
• Oil
• Onion
• Garlic
• Coriander seeds
• Cumin seeds
• Black peppercorns
• Dried red chilies
• Fresh ginger
• Ground turmeric
• Beef stock
• Salt
• Desiccated coconut
• Water
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Cube meat to 1 1 / 2 inches
2. Heat oil in medium saucepan
3. Add the onion and garlic and cook slowly until soft
4. Crush the black pepper, coriander and cumin seeds
5. Add to saucepan with the chilies, and cook for a minute
6. Add the grated ginger and turmeric
7. Stir for 1-2 minutes
8. Add the beef, turning quickly to coat with spices
9. Pour in the beef stock simmer for 15 minutes, and then add the salt
10. Combine coconut with hot water in liquidiser, blend for 1-2 minutes
11. Pour mixture into saucepan
12. Squeeze in the juice of the lemon
13. Bring back to boil and simmer uncovered for 40 minutes
14. Thicken with corn flour if necessary
15. Serve with basmati rice and accompaniments<|endoftext|>
