Ingredients:
• Almonds
• Butter
• Sugar
• Egg
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Clove
• Salt
• Raisins
• Dried cranberries

Steps:
1. Roughly chop the almonds
2. Set aside
3. Cream butter, sugar and egg until frothy
4. In a second bowl mix flour, baking soda, spices and salt
5. Add this to the butter mixture gradually
6. Fold in almonds, raisins, cranberries and mix with a spoon
7. Using your hands form dough balls the size of a walnut
8. Place on a paper-lined baking tray and flatten slightly
9. Bake in the preheated oven at 170c / 350f for 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
