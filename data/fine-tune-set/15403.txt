Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Awesome Turkey Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Egg
• Onion
• Oil
• Breadcrumbs
• Soy sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic powder
• Poultry seasoning
• Ground mustard
• Ground pepper
• Salt

Steps:
1. Combine egg, onion, oil, bread crumbs, soy sauce, worcestershire sauce, and seasonings in a large bowl
2. Add ground turkey and mix well
3. Make into patties and refrigerate at least 20 minutes before frying or grilling
4. Cook until done and serve with your favorite burger toppings and condiments
5. We did the works -- lettuce, tomato, onion, cheese, relish, catsup, and mayo
6. Serve with french fries or potato chips<|endoftext|>
