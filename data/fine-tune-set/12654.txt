2. Mix tomatoes and remaining ingredients except the 1 / 4 cup water and flour
3. Cover and cook on high setting for 5 hours or low setting for 9 hours or until beef and vegetables are tender
4. Mix 1 / 4 cup water and the flour
5. Stir into stew
