Give me a burger recipe.<|endoftext|>Asian Spiced Turkey Burgers

Ingredients:
• Fresh white breadcrumbs
• Egg
• Green onion
• Soy sauce
• Garlic
• Minced ginger
• Ground turkey
• Vegetable oil
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine bread crumbs, egg, green onions, soy sauce, garlic, and ginger
2. Add turkey and gently mix with a fork just until ingredients are evenly blended
3. Divide turkey mixture into 4 equal portions
4. With your hands, shape each portion into a 3 / 4-inch-thick patty
5. If making ahead, lay patties side by side on a plate, cover airtight, and chill up to 24 hours
6. Prepare a charcoal or gas grill for direct, medium-high heat (the charcoal or gas flame is directly under the cooking area, and you can hold your hand just above grill level only 3 to 4 seconds
7. For charcoal grill: before you put the grill over the hot coals, brush grill with a generous coat of oil
8. For gas grill: when hot, brush grill with a generous coat of oil
9. Lay patties on well-oiled grill and cook 5 minutes, then turn burgers with a wide spatula
10. Continue cooking until meat is opaque in center, 5 to 6 minutes longer
11. Transfer burgers to buns and garnish to taste<|endoftext|>
