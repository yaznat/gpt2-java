Ingredients:
• Butter
• Milk
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Smooth peanut butter
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Quick-cooking oats

Steps:
1. Mix butter, milk, sugar, cocoa and salt in a saucepan and melt
2. Boil for 1 minute
3. Remove from heat and add peanut butter, vanilla and oats
4. Stir until combined
5. Drop by teaspoon on to wax paper and let cool
6. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
