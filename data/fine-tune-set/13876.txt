Ingredients:
• Frozen puff pastry
• Quark
• Heavy cream
• Nutmeg
• Bacon
• Onion

Steps:
1. On a lightly floured surface, roll the pastry to 1 / 8-inch thickness
2. Cut into disks about 3-1 / 4 to 4 inches in diameter and place on a wax paper-lined baking sheet
3. Leave in freezer until dough is frozen
4. Mix together quark, cream, nutmeg, salt and pepper
5. Preheat oven to 400f
6. Remove crusts from freezer
7. Poke pastry with a fork
8. Spread cheese mixture equally on each crust
9. Sprinkle with onion and bacon
10. Bake on bottom rack of oven for ten minutes, or until bottoms of pizzas are a dark, golden brown
11. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
