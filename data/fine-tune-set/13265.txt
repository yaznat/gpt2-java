What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>40 Clove Garlic Chicken

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Paprika
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper
• Olive oil
• Chicken thighs
• Garlic
• Celery
• Yellow onion
• Dry white wine
• Chicken broth
• Fresh rosemary
• Fresh thyme
• Bay leaves

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees with rack in the center
2. Combine flour, paprika, salt, and pepper in a large plastic bag
3. Coat chicken in seasoned flour
4. Shake off excess flour
5. Heat oil in a large ovenproof saute pan over medium-high heat
6. Brown chicken on both sides
7. Remove from pan
8. Add garlic, celery and onion
9. Saute until lightly browned
10. Add wine, broth, and herbs, then arrange browned chicken on top, skin side up
11. Bring to a simmer
12. Cover the pan
13. Transfer to the oven and braise 45 minutes
14. Makes 6 chicken thighs with sauce<|endoftext|>
