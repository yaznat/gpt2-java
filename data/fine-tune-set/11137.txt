Ingredients:
• Refrigerated crescent dinner rolls
• Cheddar cheese
• Bacon
• Milk
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Seperate crescent rolls into triangles
3. Lay half of the triangles on an ungreased cookie sheet or in a shallow baking pan, and pinch up the edges to make rims around the triangles
4. Put cheese slices over dough and trim to fit inside
5. Sprinkle bacon over cheese
6. Mix eggs and milk together and spoon mixture into triangle shells
7. Top with remaining triangles, but don't seal
8. Bake for 20-35 minutes<|endoftext|>
