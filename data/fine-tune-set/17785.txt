What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Aubby Gobby Nana Muffins

Ingredients:
• Mashed bananas
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Flour

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl
2. Drop by heaping spoonfuls into muffin tins
3. Bake at 350 f for 10-15 minutes, until muffins begin to brown on top
4. Serve warm with butter<|endoftext|>
