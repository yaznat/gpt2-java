What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Stir Fry With Cashews

Ingredients:
• Oyster sauce
• Fish sauce
• Brown sugar
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Cooking oil
• Onion
• Red chili peppers
• Garlic
• Unsalted cashews
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In a small bowl combine oyster-flavoured sauce, fish sauce or soy sauce, brown sugar, and cornstarch
2. Stir in water
3. Set aside
4. Rinse chicken and pat dry
5. Cut into bite-size strips
6. Set aside
7. In a large skillet or wok heat oil over medium-high heat
8. Add onion and stir-fry 1 minute
9. Add peppers and garlic and stir-fry 1- 2 minutes more or till onion is crisp tender
10. Remove with slotted spoon and set aside
11. Add chicken to wok
12. Stir sauce
13. Add to skillet
14. Cook and stir 1 minute more
15. Stir in cashews
16. Serve over hot cooked rice<|endoftext|>
