Can you give me a pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Jello Chocolate Pudding Pops

Ingredients:
• Chocolate milk
• Instant chocolate pudding mix
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Beat milk, vanilla and mix for 2 minutes
2. Spoon into paper or plastic cups
3. Insert wooden pop stick or plastic spoon into each for handle
4. Freeze 5 - 7 hours
5. To remove pop from cup run under warm water for 15 seconds
6. Don't twist or pull on the stick when removing!
7. You can also experiment with add in's - sprinkles, peanuts, marshmallows, chocolate chips, etc<|endoftext|>
