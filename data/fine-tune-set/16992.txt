Ingredients:
• Fresh lime juice
• Shrimp
• White onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Ketchup
• Hot sauce
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Cucumber
• Diced tomato
• Avocado
• Salt and pepper
• Garlic clove
• Popped popcorn

Steps:
1. Boil 1 quart salted water, adding 2 tbls lime juice
2. Add shrimp and cover, bring water back to a boil
3. Remove immediately from heat and set lid slightly askew
4. Pour out all liquid, replace cover, and let shrimp steam off heat for 10 minute
5. Spread shrimp out in a pan to cool
6. De-vein shrimp when cooled
7. Toss shrimp in remaining lime juice and refrigerate for an hour
8. Dice onion, avocado, cucumber, cilantro, garlic, and tomato
9. Add to shrimp bowl and toss with ketchup, hot sauce, and olive oil
10. Cover and refrigerate if not served immediately
11. Serve in martini glasses for a stylish presentation
12. Sprinkle unsalted popcorn on top for crunch, and serve remaining popcorn in a bowl on the side
13. You may want to add more popcorn when initial popcorn is eaten
14. Do not add too much popcorn at once because it will get soggy before eaten
15. Add a little bit at a time as you enjoy
16. Garnish with lime slices and cilantro sprigs<|endoftext|>
