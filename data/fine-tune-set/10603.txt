What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Portabellas Leeks And Balsamic Vinegar

Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Leeks
• Garlic cloves
• Portabella mushroom caps
• Balsamic vinegar
• Salt

Steps:
1. Pour the oil into a saute pan and heat over medium heat for about 30 seconds
2. Saute the leek for about 2 minutes until softened
3. Add the garlic and saute for another 30 seconds
4. Add the mushrooms and saute, stirring frequently, until the mushrooms are wilted and the liquid they have given off has evaporated
5. Add the balsamic vinegar, stir well, and season with salt to taste
6. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil and add the pasta, stirring to make sure the pasta is well separated
7. Boil 2 to 3 minutes, until done
8. Drain and transfer to a large bowl<|endoftext|>
