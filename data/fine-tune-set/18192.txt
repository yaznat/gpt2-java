Ingredients:
• Milk
• Butter
• Egg substitute
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Lightly grease an 8x8-inch baking dish
3. In a large mixing bowl, beat together milk, butter and egg substitute
4. Add the sugar, then gradually beat in the flour
5. Carefully stir in the baking powder and salt
6. Pour batter into prepared pan
7. Bake in preheated oven for 30 minutes
8. Cut into 16 square servings<|endoftext|>
