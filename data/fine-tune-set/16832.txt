What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Pineapple Passion Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Ice cube
• Crushed pineapple
• Buttermilk
• 2% low-fat milk
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Vanilla ice cream
• Orange sherbet

Steps:
1. Mix ice cubes, crushed pineapple, milks, sugar and vanilla in blender until smooth
2. Add ice cream and sherbet and blend till just smooth
3. Serve with a fresh pineapple wedge if desired<|endoftext|>
