What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Corn Dog Fries

Ingredients:
• Yellow cornmeal
• Flour
• Kosher salt
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cayenne pepper
• Jalapeno
• Cream-style corn
• Onion
• Buttermilk
• Cornstarch
• Beef hot dogs

Steps:
1. Preheat oil in deep fryer to 375
2. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the cornmeal, flour, salt, baking powder, baking soda, and cayenne pepper
3. In a separate bowl, combine the jalapeno, corn, onion, and buttermilk
4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients all at once, and stir only enough times to bring the batter together
5. There should be lumps
6. Set batter aside and allow to rest for 10 minutes
7. Slice the hot dogs lengthwise into 8ths
8. Making long strips
9. Scatter the cornstarch and flour into a medium sized bowl
10. Toss the strips of hot dogs in the mixture and shake well to remove any excess
11. Place the hot dog strips into the bowl of batter
12. Immediately and carefully one by one place each strip into the oil, and cook until coating is golden brown, about 4 or 5 minutes
13. With tongs or a wire strainer, remove the golden curls to cooling rack or paper towels, and allow to drain for 3 to 5 minutes
14. Serve with hot dog dipping sauces<|endoftext|>
