Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Blue Cheese And Caramelised Onion Pizza

Ingredients:
• Onions
• Butter
• Balsamic vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Thin pizza crust
• Roasted garlic
• Blue cheese
• Sun-dried tomatoes

Steps:
1. Thinly slice onions and place in a heat proof dish
2. Dot with butter, add vinegar and brown sugar
3. Bake, covered in foil, for 45 minutes at 350
4. Stir and continue cooking until brown and caramelised
5. This part can be made days ahead, just cover and chill
6. When ready to use, bring to room temperature
7. Spread garlic puree on unbaked crust
8. Cover with onions
9. Bake at 450 for about 13 minutes
10. Add crumbled blue cheese and tomatoes
11. Bake for about 3 minutes
12. Cut into slices and serve<|endoftext|>
