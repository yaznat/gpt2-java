Ingredients:
• Flour
• Butter
• Salt
• Sugar
• Ground almonds
• Lemon zest
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Combine butter, salt and sugar in mixer with paddle attachment
3. Add ground almonds and mix together
4. Mix into firm dough and form into 1 inch ball
5. Flatten with fingers
6. Bake for 10-14 minutes
7. Best if baked light brown, not dark and crispy
8. Cool on rack and dust with powdered sugar while still warm<|endoftext|>
