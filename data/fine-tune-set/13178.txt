What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>Magic Chicken Noodle Soup

Ingredients:
• Water
• Chicken bouillon
• Carrots
• Celery ribs
• Chicken
• Diced onion
• Wide egg noodles
• Oregano
• Pepper
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Put water in a tall pot and heat to a boil
2. Add the chicken bullion, diced onions, oregano, garlic powder, and pepper
3. -slice carrots and add to the boiling water
4. - slice celery
5. -cut chicken into bite size pieces
6. Add carrots to the pot and allow them to boil for about 5 minutes before adding the celery, chicken, and about half the package of wide egg noodles and allow to boil until the noodles are tender
7. Now it's ready to serve
8. I love serving it with some buttered dinner rolls on the side
9. Remember, you can adjust the seasoning and amount of veggies to your taste<|endoftext|>
