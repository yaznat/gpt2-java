What's a recipe for an omelette?<|endoftext|>Hawaiian Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Pineapple chunk
• Ham
• Monterey jack cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Water
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Beat together eggs, water, salt and pepper
2. Thinly coat your skillet with vegetable oil spray
3. Heat over medium high
4. Pour egg mixture into pan
5. When eggs are almost set, sprinkle half of the omelette with remaining ingredients
6. Fold other half of omelette over filling
7. Continue cooking until cheese melts, about 2 minutes
8. Cut omelette into 3 wedges and serve<|endoftext|>
