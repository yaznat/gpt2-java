What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Corn And Crab Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Whole kernel corn
• Fresh cilantro
• Green onion
• Diced pimentos
• Pepper
• Ground red pepper
• Fresh lump crabmeat
• Sun-dried tomato tortillas
• Butter
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. In medium bowl, mix cream cheese, corn, cilantro, onions, pimientos, pepper and red pepper
2. Fold in crabmeat
3. Spread 2 / 3 cup of the crabmeat mixture over each tortilla
4. Fold tortilla in half, pressing lightly
5. Brush both sides of each tortilla with butter
6. In 12-inch skillet, cook 3 tortillas at a time over medium-high heat about 5 minutes, turning once, until light brown
7. Garnish with sour cream and cilantro<|endoftext|>
