Ingredients:
• Glazed doughnuts
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Fruit cocktail
• Eggs
• Raisins
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter
• Confectioners' sugar
• Rum

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Cube donuts into a large bowl
3. Pour other ingredients on top of donuts and let soak for a few minutes
4. Mix all ingredients together until donuts have soaked up the liquid as much as possible
5. Bake for about 1 hour until center has jelled
6. Top with butter rum sauce
7. Butter rum sauce:
8. 1 stick butter
9. 1 pound box confectioners' sugar
10. Rum, to taste
11. Melt butter and slowly stir in confectioners' sugar
12. Add rum and heat until bubbly
13. Pour over each serving of donut bread pudding<|endoftext|>
