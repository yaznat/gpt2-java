Ingredients:
• Cauliflower
• Salt
• Butter
• Flour
• Fresh parsley
• Milk
• Eggs
• White pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350f
2. Place the cauliflower in a saucepan, cover with water, add 1 / 2 teaspoon salt and bring to a boil
3. Reduce heat and simmer until cauliflower is just tender-crisp
4. Drain
5. Butter a 1-1 / 2 quart casserole and arrange the flowerets in it
6. In a small saucepan melt the butter
7. Stir in the flour, 1 / 2 teaspoon salt and the parsley, stirring until smooth
8. Slowly add 1 cup of the milk, stirring to incorporate, and cook until thickened
9. Pour mixture over the cauliflower
10. In a mixing bowl, beat the eggs
11. Into the egg mixture, stir in the remaining 1 / 2 teaspoon salt, white pepper and remaining milk
12. Pour over the creamed cauliflower in the casserole
13. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until the custard is set
14. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
