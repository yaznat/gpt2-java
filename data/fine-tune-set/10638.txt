Give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>Anytime Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Rotini noodles
• Broccoli
• Red onion
• Green pepper
• Red pepper
• Zucchini
• Pea pods
• Pitted black olives
• Cherry tomatoes
• Sweet basil
• Salt
• Lemon juice
• Garlic-flavored red wine vinegar
• Olive oil
• Fresh parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to instructions, rinse and let cool
2. Blanche broccoli and pea pods for 2 minutes, drain and rinse with cool water
3. Add all vegetable to noodles
4. Mix well with dressing and chill overnight
5. You can add or take out vegetable that you want in your pasta salad<|endoftext|>
