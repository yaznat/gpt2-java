Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onions
• Fresh thyme
• Fine sea salt
• Water
• Dry white wine
• Sugar
• Yukon gold potatoes
• Tart apples

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Butter a 13x9 pyrex
3. Melt 6 t butter in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat
4. Add onion, thyme and 2 t salt
5. Saute until onion are translucent, about 10 minutes
6. Increase heat to medium-high
7. Saute until onion are tender and begin to color, about 8 minutes longer
8. Remove from heat
9. Add remaining 6 t butter, 2 / 3 c water, wine, and sugar to skillet
10. Stir and swirl skillet to combine
11. Bring to a boil, then cool onion mixture to lukewarm
12. Combine potatoes, apples, remaining 2 t salt and onion mixture in large bowl
13. Toss gently to blend
14. Transfer to prepared baking dish, spreading evenly
15. Cover dish with parchment paper, then cover with foil, shiny side down
16. Bake gratin until potatoes are tender, about 55 minutes
17. Uncover and bake until top browns and juices bubble thickly, about 20 minutes longer
18. Let gratin stand 15 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
