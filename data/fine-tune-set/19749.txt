Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Fresh garlic
• Green bell pepper
• Jalapeno pepper
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Frozen corn
• Barbecue sauce
• Old cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Set oven to 425 degrees
2. Grease a 15 x 10-inch baking sheet
3. Spread the potatoes onto the baking sheet and then sprinkle with salt
4. Bake for about 25-30 minutes or until the potatoes are fork-tender and lightly browned
5. Meanwhile in a skillet cook the ground beef with onion, garlic, bell pepper and jalapeno pepper until the meat is browned drain fat and season with seasoned salt and black pepper
6. Add in barbecue sauce and corn, stir with a wooden spoon for about 5 minutes
7. Remove the fries from the oven and top with gound beef mixture
8. Sprinkle with grated cheddar cheese and return to oven for about 5 minutes or until the cheese has melted and is bubbly<|endoftext|>
