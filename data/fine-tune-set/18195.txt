What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Baked Peach Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Bisquick baking mix
• Milk
• Eggs
• Fresh peaches
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 400f
2. Place 2 tablespoons butter in each of two 9-inch pie plates
3. Heat in oven until melted
4. Stir together bisquick, milk and eggs
5. Arrange half of the peach slices in each pie plate
6. Divide batter evenly between pie plates
7. Stir together sugar and cinnamon
8. Sprinkle over batter
9. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until puffed and golden brown
