Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Goody Bars

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Vanilla frosting
• Salted peanuts
• Crisp rice cereal
• Creamy peanut butter
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degrees
2. Make brownies as directed on package for 13" x 9" pan, and cool completely
3. Frost brownies with frosting
4. Sprinkle with peanuts, refrigerate
5. Measure cereal into large bowl
6. Set aside
7. Melt peanut butter and chocolate chips in 1-quart saucepan over low heat, stirring constantly
8. Pour over cereal in bowl, stirring until evenly coated
9. Spread over frosted brownies
10. Cool completely in refrigerator before cutting<|endoftext|>
