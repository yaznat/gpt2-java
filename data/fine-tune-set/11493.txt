What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Friendship Gingerbread

Ingredients:
• Amish starter
• Egg
• Oil
• Applesauce
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Molasses
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Clove
• Nutmeg
• Instant butterscotch pudding mix
• Candied orange peel
• Cream cheese frosting

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Combine wet ingredients: starter, egg, oil, applesauce, milk or cream, vanilla, and molasses
3. Stir well
4. Combine dry ingredients: flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, spices, and pudding mix in a separate bowl
5. Whisk until well-combined
6. Add dry mix gradually to wet mix, and stir until barely combined
7. Stir in candied peel if using
8. Spread in greased loaf pan
9. Bake approximately one hour
10. Check it at fifty minutes to determine baking time for your oven!
11. Remove from oven and cool on a rack
12. When barely warm to the touch, spread cream cheese frosting over top of loaf
13. I like runny, glazey, drippy frosting--if you like it thicker, you'll need more frosting and a light hand to keep from tearing pieces off the top of this tender loaf!<|endoftext|>
