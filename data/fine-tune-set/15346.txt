Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Andrew S Spicy Bbq Burgers

Ingredients:
• Minced beef
• Red onions
• Garlic
• Curry powder
• Worcestershire sauce
• Cayenne pepper
• Italian seasoning
• Mustard powder
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Mix all the ingredients together, using breadcrumbs if required, until the meat holds together well
2. Divide into four lumps then gently pack each burger into patties
3. Barbeque for 10-15 minutes or back in a 450f oven for 25 minutes until crispy
4. Serve in a bun with salad<|endoftext|>
