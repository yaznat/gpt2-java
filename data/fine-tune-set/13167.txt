What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>Just Trust Me Bistro Chicken Salad

Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Canola oil
• Orange juice
• White vinegar
• Salt
• Cooked rice
• Green grapes
• Celery
• Pineapple tidbits
• Mandarin oranges
• Slivered almonds
• Mayonnaise

Steps:
1. Marinade
2. Create a marinade, by mixing oil, orange juice, vinegar, & salt
3. Whisk to blend
4. Toss into cooked chicken and allow to marinate for several hours in a plastic bag in the refrigerator before adding other ingredients
5. Assembly
6. In a very large bowl, gently toss all ingredients except mayo and reserved juice
7. Dressing
8. Whisk together mayonnaise and pineapple juice
9. Pour over mixed ingredients and toss
10. Let rest
11. Cover and refrigerate for 1 hour before serving<|endoftext|>
