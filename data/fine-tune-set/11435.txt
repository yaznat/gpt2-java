Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Orange rind
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Allspice
• Nuts
• Orange juice

Steps:
1. Cream together butter and sugar
2. Stir in the eggs and orange rind
3. Combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, allspice, and nuts
4. Add to creamed mixture alternately with the orange juice
5. Stir until combined
6. Pour into a greased and floured 9" x 5" x3" pan
7. Bake in a preheated 350f oven for approximately 1 hour, or until done<|endoftext|>
