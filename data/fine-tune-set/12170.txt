What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>Apple Cream Pie

Ingredients:
• Baking apples
• 9" unbaked pie shell
• Sugar
• Flour
• Whipping cream
• Lemon, rind of
• Ground cinnamon
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. Place the apples in the pie shell
3. In a small bowl mix the sugar and flour, stir in the cream until smooth and pour over apples
4. Sprinkle with the cinnamon and nuts
5. Bake at 400 for 40 minutes or until a knife comes out clean
6. Cool on a wire rack-chill in the fridge<|endoftext|>
