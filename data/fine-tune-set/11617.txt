Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>24 Karrot Cake

Ingredients:
• Raisins
• Orange juice
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• White flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Clove
• Canola oil
• Maple syrup
• Soymilk
• Apple cider vinegar
• Vanilla extract
• Orange extract
• Carrots

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f and prepare two 8-inch round cake pans or line cup cake tins with paper liner
2. If using cake pans, line the bottoms with parchment paper and grease and flour the sides
3. For cupcakes, a quick shot of pan spray inside the liners will help avoid sticking
4. Soak the raisins in the orange juice for about 10 minutes
5. Drain the raisins and reserve the orange juice
6. If you are in a hurry, you can put them in the microwave for 3 minutes or so to encourage rehydration
7. Sift the dry ingredients in a bowl and set aside
8. In another larger mixing bowl measure out the oil, maple syrup, soymilk, vinegar, vanilla and orange extracts, plus the orange juice from soaking the raisins
9. Pour dry ingredients into wet and whisk until blended
10. Fold in the carrots and raisins
11. Pour into cake or cupcake tins and bake for 30-35 minutes or 20 minutes
12. Cool for 10 minutes and remove from tins<|endoftext|>
