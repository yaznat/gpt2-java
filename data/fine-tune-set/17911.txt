Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Garfava flour
• Potato starch
• Arrowroot
• Unrefined sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Xanthan gum
• Salt
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Pure vanilla extract
• Brewed coffee
• Gluten-free chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Grease three 12-muffin mini-muffin pans with oil
3. In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, potato starch, arrowroot, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, xanthan gum, salt, and unsweetened cocoa powder
4. Set aside
5. In a large bowl, mix together applesauce, 1 / 2 cup canola oil, and vanilla
6. Slowly add flour mixture, stirring to combine
7. Stir in coffee until it forms a batter
8. Fold in chocolate chips
9. Place 1 tablespoon batter into each greased muffin cup
10. Bake until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, approximately 12 to 15 minutes
11. Let cool completely on a wire rack before removing from tins<|endoftext|>
