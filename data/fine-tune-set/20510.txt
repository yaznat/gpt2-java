Ingredients:
• Mushrooms
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Hot sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• White cheddar cheese
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cooked white rice
• Shrimp
• Lump crabmeat
• Onion
• Celery
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Melt butter in fry pan, add oil
2. Saute mushrooms, celery and onion in butter / oil mixture until mushroom juices are released
3. Add mushroom soup
4. Remove from heat and add hot sauce and worcestershire sauce
5. In a large bowl, gently fold rice, cheese, crab and shrimp together
6. Add cooked sauce
7. Place all ingredients in a large, lightly oiled casserole dish, and bake for 1 hour at 325 uncovered
8. Sprinkle with fresh ground pepper to taste
9. Serve with salad and crusty bread<|endoftext|>
