Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Best Zucchini Brownies Ever

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Margarine
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Cinnamon
• Baking soda
• Cocoa
• Zucchini
• Chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Cream sugar & margarine
2. Add eggs and vanilla
3. Add remaining ingredients
4. Stir in shredded zucchini last
5. Spread in a 13 x 9 greased pan
6. Sprinkle 1 cup chocolate chips on top along with 1 tbls sugar
7. Bake @ 350 for 25 to 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
