What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Alice S Chicken Pot Pie

Ingredients:
• Stewing chicken
• Salt
• Pepper
• Celery
• Onion
• Thyme
• Savory
• Bay leaf
• Carrot
• White onions
• Unsalted butter
• Mushroom
• Flour
• Milk
• Chicken broth
• Fresh lemon juice
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Place chicken in a large pot
2. Add salt, pepper, celery, onion, thyme, savory and bay leaf
3. Cover with hot water
4. Cover the pot and let simmer until chicken is tender
5. Remove chicken from the broth, skin and cut meat into 1" pieces
6. Place the chicken in a bowl and cover with the hot broth
7. Cover and refrigerate for 12 hours
8. Cook the carrots and the celery in water until just tender
9. Do not over cook cook the pearl onions salted water for 15 minutes
10. Brown the mushrooms in butter over high heat for 2 minutes
11. Remove the chicken from the jellied broth
12. Cook the broth down to 3 cups
13. Melt 3 / 4 cups of butter, stir in 3 / 4 cups of flour
14. Stir to blend and then add the milk and 3 cups of broth
15. Cook and stir until smooth and creamy
16. Add the cooked vegetables, mushrooms chicken, lemon juice and parsley
17. Heat for a few minutes and taste for seasoning
18. Place in a casserole and cover with the pie crust of your choice
19. Bake in a 400 degree oven for 40 to 50 minutes<|endoftext|>
