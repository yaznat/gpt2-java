Ingredients:
• Top sirloin steaks
• Bell peppers
• Onion
• Red wine vinegar
• Light soy sauce
• Brown sugar
• Garlic cloves
• Garlic powder
• Dried onion flakes
• Ground ginger
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Put meat cubes into a large resealable bag
2. Add ingredients for marinade and mix well
3. Squeeze out any excess air and marinade for 2-3 hours
4. If using bamboo skewers, soak in water for a few minutes so they do not burn on the grill
5. Arrange food on skewers in whatever order pleases you
6. I did pepper, onion, meat, repeat
7. It worked out well
8. Grill skewers over medium heat, flipping occasionally, for 10 minutes
9. You may wish to pour a little bit of marinade over them in the beginning of cooking
10. Serve over rice or alone, enjoy!<|endoftext|>
