Give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>10 Minute French Bread

Ingredients:
• Bread flour
• Fast rise yeast
• Sugar
• Kosher salt
• Olive oil
• Hot water

Steps:
1. Mix water, yeast, and sugar
2. Then add salt, 4 cups flour, and oil
3. Add a little more flour if dough is not thick enough to turn out of bowl
4. Turn out on to 1 cup flour
5. Knead for 5 to 10 minutes until dough is elastic and smooth
6. Add more flour if needed
7. (it usually takes about 5 minutes until the dough is not so sticky when you press
8. I press my dough to flatten it some
9. Turn it over
10. Press a little
11. Fold
12. Press
13. Repeat
14. So it's 1
15. Press 2
16. Turn over 3
17. Press 4
18. Fold 5
19. Press 6
20. Turn
21. Divide into 2 to 4 loaves with a knife
22. Lay the sticky side down on a little flour to form a little round loaf
23. You can bake it just like that
24. If you want a sub roll, roll with a rolling pin into an oblong sheet and roll the sheet up into a loaf
25. Spray with water if you want crunchy bread
26. You can also put a mug of water into the oven to make your bread crunchy
27. Place on lightly floured cookie sheet
28. Bake at 375 for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
