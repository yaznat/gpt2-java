Ingredients:
• Tilapia fillets
• Lemon pepper
• All purpose greek seasoning
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic
• Diced onion
• Artichoke hearts
• Diced tomatoes
• White wine
• Black olives

Steps:
1. Preheat large saute pan on medium-high for 2-3 minutes
2. In the meanwhile, season both sides of the fish fillets with pepper and 1 / 2 t of the greek seasoning
3. Wash your hands
4. Place oil in pan
5. Swirl to coat
6. Add garlic and onion
7. Cook 1-2 minutes until onion is soft
8. Add fish fillets
9. Top fish with remaining ingredients
10. Cover and cook 8-10 minutes, stirring veggies once
11. Cooking time will depend on the thickness of the fish
12. Fish is done when it has an internal temperature of 145 degrees and flesh is opaque and separates easily with a fork<|endoftext|>
