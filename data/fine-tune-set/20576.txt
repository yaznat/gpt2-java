Ingredients:
• Basmati rice
• Celery
• Butter
• Tuna
• Hardboiled egg
• Lemon juice
• Mayonnaise
• French fried onion rings

Steps:
1. Saute celery in butter until tender crisp
2. Add brown rice, tuna, eggs and lemon juice
3. Fold in mayonnaise
4. Season to taste
5. Turn into shallow buttered 1 1 / 2 quart casserole
6. Top with onion rings
7. Bake at 350 for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
