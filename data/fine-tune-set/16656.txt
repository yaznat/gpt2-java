Give me a recipe for a smoothie.<|endoftext|>Berry Blast Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Apple juice
• Lemonade
• Frozen raspberries
• Frozen strawberries
• Raspberry sherbet

Steps:
1. Pour all liquid ingredients into smoothie maker or blender
2. Add all the frozen ingredients
3. Blend at low setting for 30 seconds then high setting until smooth
4. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
