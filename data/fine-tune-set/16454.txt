Ingredients:
• Reduced-fat mayonnaise
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Deli roast beef
• 2% cheddar cheese
• Baby spinach leaves
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Spread mayonnaise onto tortilla
2. Top with remaining ingredients
3. Roll up tortilla
4. Place on microwaveable plate
5. Microwave on high for 1 minute or until velveeta is melted<|endoftext|>
