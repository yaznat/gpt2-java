Ingredients:
• Green beans
• Bacon
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Soy sauce
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Place the drained green beans in a 913 baking pan
3. Cook bacon in a frying pan on your stove top or in a microwave on a microwave-safe plate until bacon strips are cooked enough to chop into large pieces
4. Sprinkle the bacon pieces on top of the green beans
5. Combine the brown sugar, melted butter, soy sauce, and garlic powder in a small bowl
6. Pour this mixture over the green beans and bacon
7. Bake uncovered at 350 degrees for 40 minutes
8. Remove from oven and serve<|endoftext|>
