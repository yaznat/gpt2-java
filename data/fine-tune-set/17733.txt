What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Apple Raisin And Nut Muffins

Ingredients:
• Cooking apples
• Brown sugar
• Sunflower oil
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Raisins
• Walnuts
• Orange zest
• Whole wheat flour
• Self raising flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Mixed spice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 200c and line muffin pan with paper cases or grease them
2. Combine apples, sugar, oil, eggs, vanilla and orange peel, mix well
3. Add raisins and nuts
4. Combine flours, baking powder, baking soda and spice
5. Add dry ingredients to the apple batter and mix just until combined, do not overmix
6. Pour into muffin pans and sprinkle with a mixture of brown sugar and spice, pressing lightly
7. Bake 20-25 minutes or until skewer inserted in the center comes out clean, let cool on a rack<|endoftext|>
