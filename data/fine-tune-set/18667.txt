Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Milk
• Flour
• Raisins
• Baking powder
• Butter
• Ground nutmeg
• Boiling water

Steps:
1. Mix the first 5 ingredients together well and pour into a greased casserole dish
2. In a bowl put the next 4 ingredients, mix well
3. Pour into casserole dish on top of first ingredients, bake at 350 for half an hour<|endoftext|>
