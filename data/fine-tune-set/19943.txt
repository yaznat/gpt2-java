Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Cocoa powder
• Chocolate
• Strawberries

Steps:
1. Whisk together the eggs and drinking chocolate powder
2. Warm a non-stick pan and pour egg mixture straight in and cook for 3 to 4 minutes
3. Sprinkle over the chocolate buttons and place under a hot grill until the buttons and melted
4. Slice and serve<|endoftext|>
