Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar

• Walnuts

• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Vanilla
• Nutmeg
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Cream cheese
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Sift and mix all dry ingredients
2. Add all wet ingredients
3. Pour batter into well-greased tube pan
4. Bake in 350 degree oven for approximately 1-1 / 4 hours or until cake tester comes out clean
