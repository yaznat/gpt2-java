Ingredients:
• Almonds
• Sea salt
• Pitted dates
• Orange
• Water
• Apples
• Raisins
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. 1
2. To make crust, pulse almonds and salt in food processor until nuts are in small pieces
3. You want your crust to have chunks of almonds in it, so don't overprocess
4. Use some of the finer powder to "flour" the bottom of your pie dish
5. Slowly add dates into processor in small batches to mix with almond bits
6. The dates will bind the almonds to form a dough
7. Press dough into the bottom of "floured" pie pan
8. Set aside: )
9. 2
10. To make syrup, place orange into your blender first
11. Then add dates and blend
12. If needed, add small amounts of water to help everything mix well
13. Set aside
14. 3
15. To make filling, place sliced apples in a large bowl with raisins
16. Toss with cinnamon and syrup
17. Spoon filling into pie crust
18. Will keep for two days in the fridge
19. For 16 servings, per serving: calories 230, protein 5g, carbs 39g, fat 8g, sugar 21g<|endoftext|>
