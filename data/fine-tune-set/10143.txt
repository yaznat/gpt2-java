What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Baked Potato Pockets With Chili

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Chili
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Warm the chili
2. Grate the cheese
3. Bake potatoes and slice in half and squeeze to break the potato up a little or use a fork and scrape it
4. Top with chili and sprinkle with cheese<|endoftext|>
