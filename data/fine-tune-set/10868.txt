Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Flour
• Salt
• Eggs
• Lemon juice
• Oil
• Acini di pepe pasta
• Mandarin oranges
• Pineapple chunks
• Pineapple tidbits
• Mini marshmallows
• Shredded coconut

Steps:
1. Combine sugar flour and salt in a saucepan
2. Gradually stir in 1 3 / 4 cup of pineapple juice and eggs and cook until thick
3. Add lemon juice and stir well
4. In a separate pan boil 3 quarts of water, 1 teaspoon of oil and the dry pasta
5. Cook until soft
6. Drain and rinse with cold water
7. Let cool to room temperature
8. Combine with egg mixture and refrigerate overnight in an air tight container
9. Mix lightly and add the mandarin oranges, pineapple, marshmallows and coconut
10. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
