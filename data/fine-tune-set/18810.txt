Ingredients:
• Yellow cake mix
• Flaked coconut
• Butter
• Apples
• Water
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Grease and flour a 13"x9"x2" baking dish
3. Combine cake mix and coconut in large bowl
4. Cut in butter with a pastry knife
5. Put sliced apples in prepared baking dish and spread evenly
6. Sprinkle with 'crumb' mixture
7. Combine water and lemon juice in a 2-cup measure
8. Pour evenly over top top
9. Do not stir
10. Bake at 350 degrees for 45-50 minutes or until is lightly browned and set
11. Cool slightly before serving<|endoftext|>
