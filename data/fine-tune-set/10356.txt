What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Almond Macaroons The Passover Cookies

Ingredients:
• Almonds
• Sugar
• Almond extract
• Salt
• Fresh lemon rind
• Egg whites
• Slivered almonds

Steps:
1. Combine all the ingredients, except for the egg whites
2. Stir in the egg whites
3. The dough will be like thick, wet sand
4. Roll into one inch balls
5. Place on a greased cookie sheet and flatten slightly
6. Press an almond sliver into each cookie
7. Bake at 350f
8. For 15-20 minutes, until dry and lightly brown<|endoftext|>
