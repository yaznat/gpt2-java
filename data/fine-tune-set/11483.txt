Ingredients:
• Warm water
• Sugar
• Yeast
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Flour

Steps:
1. Dissolve sugar in warm water in a bowl
2. Add yeast and allow to sit 10 minutes
3. Add oil, salt and flour
4. Mix well
5. Knead well
6. Place in a well oiled bowl, and cover for one hour to one and one half hours
7. Knead down
8. Let rest 10 minutes
9. Form into 2 loaves
10. Place in greased loaf pans
11. Allow to rise, covered, for one hour
12. Bake 350f 30 minutes til golden<|endoftext|>
