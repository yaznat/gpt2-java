Ingredients:
• Spice cake mix
• Egg whites
• Water
• Canned solid-pack pumpkin
• Pecans
• Gala apples
• Apple juice
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degree f
2. Coat a springform or bundt pan with cooking spray
3. In a medium mixing bowl, combine cake mix, egg whites, water and pumpkin
4. Using an electric mixer on low speed, beat until moistened, about 30 seconds
5. Scrape sides and beat on medium speed 2 or until smooth
6. Sprinkle bottom of pan with pecans and lay out apple slices accordion fashion
7. Carefully pour batter over apples and bake 1 hour or until toothpick comes out clean
8. Mean while, in a small saucepan, combine apple juice and corn starch and stir until completely dissolved
9. Bring to a boil and cook 1 minute or until thickened
10. Remove from heat and cool completely
11. When cake is done, remove from oven and cool 5 minutes on a wire rack
12. Inverting onto a serving platter, remove sides and bottom of springform pan, and pour apple glaze all over<|endoftext|>
