Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Powdered sugar
• Salt
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Anise seed

Steps:
1. Grease and flour baking sheets
2. Beat egg yolks, powdered sugar and salt in a large bowl until pale and creamy
3. Beat egg whites until very stiff
4. Fold into egg yolk mixture
5. Siftflour, baking powder and aniseed onto egg mixture
6. Fold in quickly but thoroughly
7. Fill a pastry bag fitted with a plain nozzle with cookie mixture
8. Pipe in small rounds onto prepared baking sheets
9. Let stand overnight to dry out
10. Preheat oven to 325f
11. Bake cookies 20 minutes
12. Cool on rack<|endoftext|>
