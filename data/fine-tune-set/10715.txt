What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Bacon N Veggie Pasta

Ingredients:
• Stewed tomatoes
• Fresh broccoli florets
• Carrot
• Salt
• Italian seasoning
• Dried oregano
• Dried basil
• Bacon
• Fresh mushrooms
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Medium pasta shells
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, combine the first seven ingredients and bring to a boil
2. Reduce heat, cover and simmer for 25-30 minutes or until broccoli and carrots are tender
3. Cook the pasta according to package directions
4. Drain and place in a serving bowl
5. In a large skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until crisp
6. Remove to paper towels and drain, reserving 1 tablespoon drippings
7. In the drippings, saute the mushrooms, green pepper, onion and garlic until tender and add to tomato mixture and heat through
8. Top pasta with vegetable mixture
9. Sprinkle with bacon and parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
