Give me a beans recipe.<|endoftext|>3 Cheese Bean Dip

Ingredients:
• Fresh tomatoes
• Sun-dried tomatoes packed in oil
• No-salt-added black beans
• Cayenne pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Cumin
• Shallot
• Smoked gouda cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Cream cheese
• Chipotle chile in adobo

Steps:
1. Process all ingredients in a food processor
2. Plave in an microwaveable dish
3. Heat in microwave oven till cheese is melted
4. Stir and enjoy<|endoftext|>
