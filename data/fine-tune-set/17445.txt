Ingredients:
• Smoked ham
• Brown sugar
• Cider vinegar
• Dijon mustard
• Brewed coffee
• Breadcrumbs

Steps:
1. Place the ham in a very large stockpot and place water in the pot until it just covers the ham
2. Put the pot on the stove and turn the heat on high
3. When the water begins to boil, reduce heat to low and let the ham poach for 1 1 / 2 hours
4. While the ham is cooking prepare the glaze
5. Combine the brown sugar, vinegar, mustard, and black coffee in a bowl, and stir with a fork until the sugar is dissolved
6. Add the breadcrumbs and blend in
7. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
8. Remove the ham from the pot of water and place in a large baking pan
9. Carve away the excess fat, if desired, and some of the rind
10. Spread the glaze evenly over the surface of the ham
11. Reduce the oven heat to 375 degrees, and bake the ham in the oven for 20-30 minute, or until the crust is hot and dark brown
12. Remove the ham from the pan and place on a cutting board
13. Allow to rest for at least 15 minutes before carving<|endoftext|>
