Ingredients:
• Dried porcini mushrooms
• Potatoes
• Olive oil
• Dried oregano
• Pepper
• Salt
• Garlic

Steps:
1. Soak mushrooms in a cup of lukewarm water for 20 minutes
2. Drain and squeeze mushrooms dry
3. Chop
4. Cut each potato lenghtwise in 8 wedges
5. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl
6. Place potatoes on a baking sheet coated with a little olive oil or cooking spray
7. Bake at 400 degrees for 60 minutes, tossing once or twice<|endoftext|>
