Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Healthy Low Fat Whole Wheat Apple Spice Waffles

Ingredients:
• Cornmeal
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Allspice
• Nonfat plain yogurt
• Brown sugar
• Water
• Applesauce
• Egg white
• Apple
• Ground flax seeds

Steps:
1. Pour ground flaxseed and water together into a sauce pan, stirring to kill the lumps, then leaving to simmer until it is thickened and gelatinous
2. Refrigerate while mixing the other ingredients so it cools slightly before adding it to the wet ingredients
3. Whip the egg white until soft peaks form
4. In a large bowl, combine cornmeal, whole wheat flour, baking powder, baking soda, spices, and salt
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together yogurt, brown sugar, flax+water, water, and applesauce
6. Add to dry ingredients, stirring in the apples and whipped egg white
7. Bake in waffle iron, then enjoy! or completely cool these on a cooling rack, wrap in foil, and freeze
8. The mornings you want these just microwave them until not so frozen, then pop them in the toaster! they are lovely and crispy<|endoftext|>
