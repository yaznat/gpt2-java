Ingredients:
• Tofu
• White button mushrooms
• Red onion
• Buffalo wing sauce
• Hard rolls
• Cooking spray
• Blue cheese dressing

Steps:
1. Press tofu well and cut into reasonably sized slices
2. Sprinkle with a little salt and pepper
3. Spray a skillet or add a small bit of butter / oil and brown the tofu on both sides
4. While tofu cooks saute your onion and mushrooms with a little salt and pepper until onion is tender and mushrooms are just browned
5. Set aside
6. Turn tofu down to low
7. Spoon buffalo sauce over your tofu slices and, shuffle them around to coat
8. Let cook a few minutes on med-low heat
9. Pile your tofu onto rolls and top with mushrooms and onion<|endoftext|>
