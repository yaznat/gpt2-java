What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Steak In Beer

Ingredients:
• Steak
• Onion
• Mushrooms
• Beer
• Nonfat sour cream

Steps:
1. Sautee onions and mushrooms until softened
2. Dust meat with flour and brown in a little oil added to the onions and mushrooms
3. Pour beer over all when meat has browned
4. Reduce heat
5. Season with salt and pepper and simmer until meat is tender
6. Stir in sour cream just before serving and allow to heat through
7. Great served with mashed potatoes or rice<|endoftext|>
