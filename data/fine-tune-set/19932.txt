Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Garlic clove
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Spinach
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Nutmeg
• Parmesan cheese
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Lightly beat eggs and set aside
2. Saute garlic and onion in the oil until just witted
3. Add spinach
4. Heat through, blend in with the onion and garlic
5. Add salt, pepper and nutmeg
6. Mix
7. Pour beaten egg over vegetables
8. Cook over medium heat, shaking pan to keep omelette loose from the bottom
9. Loosen around edges with spatula
10. Just before eggs are all set on top, sprinkle with cheese
11. Slip under broiler until browned
12. Slide onto platter
13. Garnish with tomatoes
14. Cut in wedges to serve
15. Good served with crisp hot french bread and butter<|endoftext|>
