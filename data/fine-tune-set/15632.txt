Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Taco sauce
• Chili powder
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Pizza dough
• American cheese
• Egg
• Water

Steps:
1. For filling, cook ground beef in a small skillet until brown
2. Drain off fat
3. Stir in taco sauce, chili powder, onion powder, and garlic powder
4. Set aside
5. Unroll pizza dough
6. Roll dough into a 14x10-1 / 2-inch rectangle
7. Cut into twelve 3-1 / 2-inch squares
8. Divide filling among dough squares
9. Sprinkle with cheese
10. Brush edges with water
11. Lift one corner of each square and stretch dough to the opposite corner, making a triangle
12. Press edges well with a fork to seal
13. Arrange on a greased baking sheet
14. Prick with fork
15. Combine egg and the 1 teaspoon water
16. Brush onto turnovers
17. Bake in a 425 degree f
18. Oven for 8 to 10 minutes or until golden
19. Let stand 5 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
