What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Syle Spaghetti With Sweet Chili Lime Sauce

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat spaghetti
• Bok choy
• Frozen asian-style vegetables
• Sweet chili sauce
• Lime juice
• Ground ginger
• Garlic

Steps:
1. Cook spaghetti
2. While spaghetti is cooking, microwave frozen vegetables in order to defrost
3. Drain spaghetti
4. Combine all ingredients in the pot with the cooked spaghetti
5. Serve warm and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
