Can you give me a salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazing Shrimp Louie Salad

Ingredients:
• Baby shrimp
• Hard-boiled egg
• Cucumbers
• Grape tomatoes
• Black olives
• Canned asparagus
• Thousand island dressing
• Iceberg lettuce

Steps:
1. For the perfect hard-boiled egg, courtesy of ab: place a steamer in a large sauce pan with a well-fitted lid
2. Bring about 1 / 4 inch of water up to boiling
3. Place no less than 3 and no more than 6 eggs in the steamer
4. Cover and steam for 12 minutes
5. Remove eggs from steamer and drop directly into iced water
6. The eggs will be firm but tender, easy to peel, and will have a solid and creamy but not dry yolk
7. Thanks, ab!
8. Place your salad bowl in the freezer for 30 minutes before assembling the salad
9. All the ingredients should be well-chilled before beginning
10. Begin assembling the salad with the lettuce
11. Add the cucumber slices, tomato halves, asparagus spears, black olives, and sliced hard-boiled egg
12. Top with shrimp
13. Serve dressing in a separate container so the diner can use as much dressing as they prefer<|endoftext|>
