Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Arroz Con Pollo Chapina Guatemalan Chicken And Rice

Ingredients:
• Skinless chicken pieces
• Corn oil
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Tomatoes
• Raw rice
• Carrot
• Stuffed green olive
• Capers
• Chicken broth
• Green peas
• Pimento pepper
• Hard-boiled egg
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. In a large skillet brown the chicken in the oil over medium heat for 20 minutes
2. Sprinkle with 1 / 2 tsp salt and the black pepper
3. Remove the chicken and set aside
4. In the same skillet with the chicken fat, fry the onion, garlic and tomato for 2 minutes
5. Add the rice and fry for 2 minutes more
6. Add the carrots, olives and capers and mix everything together
7. Pour in the broth and chicken pieces
8. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, cover skillet and simmer until broth has been absorbed, about 10 minutes
9. Add the green peas
10. Cover skillet with aluminum foil and punch 8 holes in the top to allow steam to escape
11. Bake in a 300f oven for 30 minutes
12. Fluff up the mixture once or twice during the baking time
13. Serve warm
14. Decorate the surface with the pimiento strips and egg slices and sprinkle with the cheese
15. The rice should be dry, loose and not sticky
16. Serve with fried ripe plantain slices, a salsa picante and pickled vegetables<|endoftext|>
