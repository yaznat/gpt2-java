Ingredients:
• Phyllo pastry
• Butter
• Asparagus spears
• Salad leaves
• Shallots
• Bay leaf
• Dry white wine
• Fresh herb
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper
• Fresh chives

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Keep the phyllo you are not working with covered with a damp towel
3. Brush each sheet with the melted butter and fold one corner down to create a wedge shape
4. Lay 4 spears on top of the longest edge and roll toward the shortest edge
5. Make 3 more rolls with the remaining phyllo
6. Lay the rolls on a greased baking sheet
7. Brush with remaining butter
8. Bake for 8 minutes or until the roll is golden
9. Sauce:
10. Mix shallots, bay leaf and wine in pan and reduce over high heat until wine is about 3 to 4 tablespoons
11. Strain wine mix into bowl and whisk in the butter a little at a time until smooth and glossy
12. Stir in herbs
13. Add salt and pepper to taste
14. You may serve the sauce separately garnished with chives<|endoftext|>
