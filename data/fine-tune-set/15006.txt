Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic clove
• Fresh ginger
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Cooked rice
• Light soy sauce
• Crushed pineapple in syrup
• Green onion

Steps:
1. In large nonstick skillet, heat oil over medium-low heat until hot
2. Add garlic, ginger and red pepper, cook and stir 2 minutes
3. Increase heat to medium-high
4. Add rice and soy sauce
5. Cook 2 minutes, stirring frequently
6. Add pineapple, tossing to combine
7. Heat rice through
8. Sprinkle with green onions before serving<|endoftext|>
