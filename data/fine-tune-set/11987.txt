Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Olive oil
• Lemon juice
• White vinegar
• Garlic cloves
• Dried parsley
• Dried basil
• Dried oregano
• Garlic salt
• Salt
• Cracked black pepper
• Sandwich buns

Steps:
1. Dice chicken breasts into 1" cubes
2. Whisk all other ingredients together to form marinade
3. Set some aside for basting or sauce if desired
4. Add marinade to chicken and refrigerate overnight, stirring occasionally
5. Thread 5 or so cubes onto metal or soaked bamboo skewers and grill for a few minutes either side, just until meat is firm
6. Place two skewers on a fresh bread roll or italian bread, grip the meat firmly with the bread and pull out skewers
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
