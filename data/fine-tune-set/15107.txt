Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Apple Anise Pizza

Ingredients:
• Boboli pizza crusts
• Shallots
• Olive oil
• Granny smith apples
• Walnuts
• Honey
• Anise seed
• Swiss cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees f
2. Place bread shell on pizza pan
3. Bake 8 minutes or until hot
4. In large skillet cook shallots in hot oil for 3 minutes or until tender
5. Stir in apples
6. Cook and stir for 5 minutes or until apples are crisp-tender
7. Stir in walnuts, 2 tablespoons honey, anise seeds, and dash ground black pepper
8. Spoon apple mixture evenly over bread shell
9. Sprinkle with cheese
10. Bake 5 to 8 minutes more or until cheese is melted
11. Drizzle with additional honey
12. To serve, cut in wedges<|endoftext|>
