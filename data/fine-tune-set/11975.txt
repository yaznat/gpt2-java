Ingredients:
• Plain low-fat yogurt
• Garlic cloves
• Curry powder
• Ground coriander
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Ground red pepper
• Chicken parts
• Vegetable oil
• Chopped tomato
• Bay leaf

Steps:
1. In bowl, combine yogurt and seasonings
2. Add chicken parts, turning to coat
3. Let stand at room temperature for 30 minutes
4. In 12 inch skillet heat oil
5. Add onion and saute until lightly browned
6. Add tomatoes and bay leaf and let simmer for 5 minutes
7. Add chicken and marinade mixture and stir to combine
8. Bring mixture to a boil
9. Reduce heat, cover and let simmer, turning once to twice, until chicken is tender, about 30 minutes
