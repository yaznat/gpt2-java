Please give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Shake Summer Heat With Cantaloupe Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Muskmelon
• Sugar
• Ice
• Milk
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Halve melon and remove the seeds
2. Peel and finely chop one cup melon
3. Chop remaining into big pieces, place in a blender and add sugar, ice cream, milk / cream / water, ice, depending on whether you want juice or shake
4. Blend well till smooth and frothy
5. Serve immediately, putting a teaspoon of chopped melon in each
6. Top with more ice cream if desired<|endoftext|>
