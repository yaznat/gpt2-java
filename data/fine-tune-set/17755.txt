Ingredients:
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Agave nectar
• Egg
• Butter
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Salt
• White flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Mix the applesauce, nectar, egg and butter together
2. Set aside
3. In a separate bowl, mix together baking soda, baking powder, salt and flours
4. Mix wet and dry ingredients all together
5. Pour into greased muffin tins, and bake in 350 degrees oven for approximately 30 minutes
6. Muffins are done when a toothpick stuck in the middle comes out clean
7. Do not over fill muffin tins as these muffins tend to spread out instead of rising up<|endoftext|>
