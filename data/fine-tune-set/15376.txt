Give me a burger recipe.<|endoftext|>Asian Turkey Burgers With Sesame Mayo

Ingredients:
• Sandwich bread
• Ground turkey breast
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Hoisin sauce
• Scallions
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Toasted sesame oil
• Whole grain buns
• Reduced-fat mayonnaise
• Nonfat plain yogurt
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Scallion
• Sesame oil
• Toasted sesame seeds
• Apricot preserves
• Cider vinegar
• Coarse grain mustard
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. Preheat grill to medium-high
2. Place bread in a food processor and pulse into fine crumbs
3. Transfer to a large bowl
4. Add ground turkey, water chestnuts, hoisin, scallions, ginger, garlic and salt
5. Mix well
6. With dampened hands, form the mixture into four 1 / 2-inch-thick patties, make them thinner at the center, to prevent them from steaming as they cook
7. Oil the grill rack
8. Brush the patties with sesame oil
9. Grill until browned and no longer pink in the center, about 5 minutes per side
10. Sesame mayonnaise:
11. Combine all ingredients and whisk until well blended
12. Then fold in the scallions
13. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds, optional
14. Apricot mustard - optional - dipping sauce:
15. Combine all ingredients with the exception of the crushed red pepper flakes, in a saucepan
16. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring often
17. Reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes
18. Add crushed red pepper flakes, if using
19. Let cool to room emperature before serving<|endoftext|>
