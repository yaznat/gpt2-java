What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>Albania Gjell Me Arra Chicken With Walnuts

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Walnuts
• Egg yolks
• Garlic cloves
• Butter
• Chicken breasts
• Chicken stock

Steps:
1. Cook the chicken breasts in olive oil until they are done
2. When the chicken is done, take it out of the pan and add the flour
3. Keep stirring until brown, then add half of the butter, garlic, walnuts and egg yolks
4. Continue to stir--you don't want the yolks to solidify
5. If your recipe turns out like mine did, you will end up with a kind of lump of walnuts
6. Although the original recipe did not call for chicken stock, i found i had to add some just to get it to a sauce-like consistency
7. Just use as much as it takes to make the sauce smooth -- more if you like a watery sauce
8. When it's ready, remove from the heat and add the chicken, stirring to coat<|endoftext|>
