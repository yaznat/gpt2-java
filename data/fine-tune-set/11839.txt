Please give me a recipe for curry.<|endoftext|>Black Eyed Peas Keema Curry Pita Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Black-eyed peas
• Ground beef
• Garlic cloves
• Ginger
• Onion
• Carrot
• Green bell pepper
• Canola oil
• Curry powder
• Tomato ketchup
• Soy sauce
• Cocoa powder
• Maple syrup
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Pita bread

Steps:
1. Mince onion, garlic cloves, carrot and green bell pepper
2. In a large skillet, heat canola oil and cook garlic and ginger over medium heat until fragrant
3. Stir in onion, carrot, ground beef, green bell pepper and curry powder
4. Cook until ground beef are browned
5. Then stir in black eyed peas
6. Stir in tomato ketchup, soy sauce, maple syrup, salt and pepper
7. Saut for about 1 minute
8. Then add cocoa powder
9. Stir to coat
10. Turn off the heat
11. Halve each pita bread and spoon the keema curry into each pita bread<|endoftext|>
