Give me a recipe for tea.<|endoftext|>Apple Carrot Finger Tea Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Lemon juice
• Granny smith apple
• Light cream cheese
• Brown sugar
• Low-fat milk
• Carrot
• Golden raisin
• Pecans
• Cinnamon
• Bread

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, toss the lemon juice with the finely chopped apple
2. Add the softened cream cheese, brown sugar and milk and beat well
3. Stir in all remaining ingredients
4. Mix well
5. Spread onto crustless bread, sandwich and cut into squares or triangles<|endoftext|>
