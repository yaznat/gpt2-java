Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Kidney beans
• Whole kernel corn
• Tomato sauce
• Diced tomatoes
• Green chilies
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Ground cumin
• Sugar
• Garlic powder
• All-purpose flour
• Cornmeal
• Baking powder
• Egg
• Milk
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. In a dutch oven or soup kettle, over medium heat, cook beef, onion and green pepper until meat is no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Add remaining ingredients
4. Bring to a boil, stirring occasionally
5. Reduce heat
6. Cover and simmer for 10 minutes
7. Meanwhile, combine flour, cornmeal, baking powder and salt in a bowl
8. Beat egg, milk and sour cream until smooth
9. Stir into dry ingredients just until moistened
10. Transfer chili to an ungreased 13-in
11. X 9-in
12. X 2-in
13. Baking dish
14. Drop batter by heaping teaspoonfuls onto hot chili
15. Bake, uncovered, at 400 for 15-17 minutes or until biscuits are lightly browned<|endoftext|>
