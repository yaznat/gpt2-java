Give me a stir fry recipe.<|endoftext|>Beef Asparagus Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Sugar
• Fresh ginger
• Sirloin steak
• Cornstarch
• Beef broth
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Rice wine vinegar
• Fresh asparagus
• Red pepper
• Mushroom
• Water chestnuts
• Green onion
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. In a shallow glass container, combine 1 tablepoon of oil, sugar, and half of the ginger
2. Add beef and turn to coat
3. Let stand for 15 minutes
4. In a bowl, combine the cornstarch, broth, soy sauce, sesame oil, vinegar and remaining ginger until blended
5. Set aside
6. In a large skillet or wok, brown beef in 1 tablespoon of oil
7. Remove and keep warm
8. Add 1 more tablespoon of oil to the pan
9. Stir fry asparagus, red pepper and mushrooms for a minute or two
10. Add water chestnuts and onions
11. Stir-fry for 1 minute
12. Add cornstarch mixture to the skillet
13. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly
14. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened
15. Return beef to the pan
16. Heat through
17. Serve over hot rice
18. Garnish with sesame seeds<|endoftext|>
