Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Soymilk
• Bananas
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Stir eggs and sugar together in a saucepan, blend well
2. Add cornstarch, salt, and milk
3. Cook on stovetop using medium-high heat, stirring constantly until pudding is thick
4. Remove from heat and add your vanilla extract and bananas
5. Stir well
6. Pour pudding into a glass bowl and cover with plastic wrap
7. Cool in refridgerator or serve warm
8. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
