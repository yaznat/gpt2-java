Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>A Different Chicken Dijonaisse

Ingredients:
• Onions
• Garlic clove
• Butter
• Whole chicken breasts
• Dijon mustard
• Sour cream
• Salt and pepper
• Dry white wine
• Parsley sprig

Steps:
1. Saute or sweat onions and garlic in butter until onion is soft
2. Remove onions
3. Add chicken to skillet along with a little more butter if needed, and cook and stir until chicken is opaque and cooked through, about 5 minutes
4. Do not overcook
5. Add the cooked onions and the mustard and cook and stir for 1 minute
6. Add sour cream, salt and pepper to taste
7. Add a little dry white wine or sherry or milk or cream to thin the sauce a little
8. Stir together for 1 or 2 minutes or until heated through but do not boil
9. Serve over favorite noodles and sprinkle with chopped parsley<|endoftext|>
