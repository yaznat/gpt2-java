Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Onions
• Chicken broth
• Sour cream
• Curry powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Cumin
• Ginger
• Ground turmeric
• White pepper
• Parsley
• Ground coriander
• Cooked turkey
• Steamed rice

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a medium skillet
2. Add the onions and cook over medium heat until softened, but not browned
3. Drain fat from skillet and add remaining ingredients, except turkey and rice
4. Mix well, bring to just a boil, add the turkey and simmer for 4-5 minutes to heat through
5. Place turkey over rice and pour sauce over turkey
6. Serves 4<|endoftext|>
