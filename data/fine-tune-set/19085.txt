Give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Banana Avocado Pudding Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Banana
• Honey
• Soymilk

Steps:
1. Peel the banana, cut into half inch slices
2. Cut the avocado in half, de-stone, slice it into chunks and remove the chunks with a spoon
3. Pour both fruits into a medium bowl
4. Add the honey / syrup, and soy or almond milk a tablespoon at a time
5. Blend until smooth
6. Garnish as your sunshiney heart desires
7. Have a beautiful day<|endoftext|>
