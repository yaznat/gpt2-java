Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sliced almonds
• Dark brown sugar
• Light corn syrup
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Ice cream

Steps:
1. In a small bowl combine the flour and almonds
2. Set aside
3. In a medium medium saucepan bring the brown sugar, corn syrup and butter to a full boil over medium heat
4. Remove from heat
5. Stir in flour mixture and vanilla
6. Line a large cookie sheet with parchment paper
7. For each cookie cup, drop about 3 tablespoons of batter about 5 inches apart onto the cookie sheet
8. Bake in a 350 oven for 10-12 minutes or until bubbly and deep golden brown
9. Let stand on cookie sheet about 2 minutes
10. When cookies are firm, but still pliable, place them on inverted custard cups to form small bowls
11. Cool to room temperature
12. To serve fill the cups with ice cream, sorbet, mousse, or whatever dessert you would serve in a bowl where you can eat the 'bowl' too!<|endoftext|>
