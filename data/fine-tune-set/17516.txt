Ingredients:
• Granola cereal
• Cinnamon
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• White sugar
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Vanilla yogurt
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Spray 10 cup bundt pan with baking spray
3. Place on baking sheet
4. Stir together the granola and 1 / 2 teaspoons cinnamon in a small bowl, set aside
5. In a large bowl, whisk together the flours, baking powder, baking soda, 1 / 2 teaspoons cinnamon, and salt
6. In a mixing bowl, cream the butter and sugars until light and fluffy
7. Beat in eggs, one at a time
8. Beat in the vanilla
9. Beat in the flour mixture alternately with the yogurt, ending with the flour, just until blended
10. Sprinkle half the granola mixture and 1 / 4 cup blueberries into the bottom of the bundt pan
11. Spread half the batter over the granola
12. Sprinkle remaining granola over the batter in bundt pan
13. Mix the remaining 1 cup blueberries into the remaining batter
14. Top the granola layer with the blueberry / batter mixture
15. Bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes, then cover top loosely with foil to prevent overbrowning
16. Continue to bake another 15 minutes, or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
17. Cool in pan 5 minutes before turning out onto a rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
