What's a recipe for pie?<|endoftext|>4 Layer Pie

Ingredients:
• Powdered sugar
• Flour
• Margarine
• Cream cheese
• Cool whip
• Vanilla
• Chocolate instant pudding
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Milk

Steps:
1. 1st layer:
2. Cut together the powdered sugar, flour and margarine
3. Press into a 9 x 13 pan and bake for 35 minutes
4. Allow to cool for at least 15 minutes
5. 2nd layer:
6. Beat cream cheese, powdered sugar, and cool whip to mix well and spread on cooled 1st layer crust
7. 3rd layer:
8. Mix the puddings and milk as directed on the box and spread on top of the 2nd layer
9. Cover and refrigerate for 6 hours
10. Passive cooking time includes time to refrigerate
11. 4th layer:
12. Before serving, cover with remaining cool whip<|endoftext|>
