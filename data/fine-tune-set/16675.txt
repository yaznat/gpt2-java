What's a good smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Berry Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Light vanilla yogurt
• Banana
• Frozen mixed berries
• Cinnamon
• Walnuts
• Artificial sweetener

Steps:
1. Put frozen banana and berries on microwave-safe dish and defrost in microwave approximately 1 minute
2. This makes it easier to blend, but still keeps it chilled
3. Put one container of yogurt, the banana, cinnamon and sweetener in blender or "magic bullet" and blend until smooth
4. Add remaining fruit and the second container of yogurt and blend until smooth and pink or purple!
5. Add walnuts and blend until smooth<|endoftext|>
