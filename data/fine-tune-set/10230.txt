Give me a chili recipe.<|endoftext|>Black Bean And Kielbasa Chili

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Kielbasa
• Onions
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Black beans
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Diced tomatoes with juice
• Chili powder
• Sugar
• Red wine vinegar
• Bay leaves
• Dried oregano
• Ground cumin

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in heavy large pot over medium-high heat
2. Add kielbasa
3. Saut until beginning to brown, about 12 minutes
4. Using slotted spoon, transfer kielbasa to bowl
5. If necessary, add enough oil to pot to measure 2 tablespoons
6. Add onions, both bell peppers, and garlic
7. Saut until beginning to brown, about 10 minutes
8. Add beans, broth, tomatoes with juice, chili powder, sugar, vinegar, bay leaves, oregano, and cumin
9. Bring chili to a boil, stirring occasionally
10. Reduce heat to medium, cover, and simmer 30 minutes
11. Return kielbasa to pot
12. Reduce heat to low
13. Simmer, uncovered, until chili is thick, stirring occasionally, about 30 minutes
14. Season with salt and pepper
15. Ladle chili into bowls
16. Top with sour cream and green onions if desired<|endoftext|>
