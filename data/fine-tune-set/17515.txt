Ingredients:
• Amish starter
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Almond extract
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Frozen blueberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f lightly grease two loaf pans or a bundt pan
2. Combine all ingredients, except blueberries, mixing thoroughly
3. Gently fold in blueberries
4. Pour into prepared pan
5. For loaf pans, bake 35-40 minutes or until test done with a toothpick
6. For bundt pan, bake 60 minutes
