Give me a pudding recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Rice Pudding

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Apples
• Sugar
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Short-grain rice
• Flour
• Milk
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. In a deep pan, melt the butter over medium-high heat
2. Add the apples, half of the sugar, the cinnamon and salt
3. Cook, stirring, until apples are softened
4. Remove the apples to a bowl
5. Put the pan back on medium-high heat
6. Stir in the rice and sugar
7. Whisk the flour into the milk and slowly stir into the rice
8. Heat until steaming, stirring often
9. Reduce heat to low
10. Cover and simmer for about 25 minutes
11. Make sure you stir occasionally
12. Stir in the apples and vanilla
13. Cool slightly and serve, or chill, and serve cold
14. Sprinkle with added cinnamon, if desired<|endoftext|>
