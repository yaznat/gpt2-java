Ingredients:
• Brussels sprouts
• Bacon
• Pepper
• Sour cream
• Whole grain mustard
• Brown sugar
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Microwave 10 bacon slices in batches, between paper towels at high for 1 1 / 2 minutes
3. Cut slices in half crosswise
4. Wrap bacon piece around each brussels sprout half
5. Secure with a wooden pick
6. Place sprouts, cut sides down, on a lightly greased wire rack on a baking sheet
7. Sprinkle with pepper to taste
8. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes or until bacon is crisp and brussels sprouts are tender
9. Serve with creamy mustard
10. Creamy mustard sauce: stir together all ingredients
