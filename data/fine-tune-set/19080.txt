Ingredients:
• Strawberries
• Caster sugar
• Balsamic vinegar
• Poundcake
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Place the strawberries into a frying pan, on a medium high heat, with the caster sugar
2. Heat for 1-2 minutes
3. Add the balsamic vinegar and cook for a further two minutes
4. Serve the balsamic strawberries piled on top of the toaste pound cake, and serve with ice cream, to your taste<|endoftext|>
