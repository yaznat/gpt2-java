Give me a custard recipe.<|endoftext|>Australian Bread And Butter Custard

Ingredients:
• Butter
• White bread
• Golden raisin
• Eggs
• Milk
• Heavy cream
• Sugar
• Salt
• Vanilla
• Ground nutmeg

Steps:
1. Thinly butter bread
2. Cut each slice into four squares
3. Arrange half of the bread in the buttom of a buttered, 1-quart casserole dish
4. Sprinkle raisins over bread, then cover with remaining bread
5. Beat eggs with milk, cream, sugar, salt and vanilla
6. Pour it over the casserole things
7. Let stand 30 minutes to let mixture soak into bread
8. Preheat oven to 350
9. Sprinkle nutmeg over dessert
10. When oven is ready, bake 40 minutes, or until golden brown
11. Serve custard warm, either by itself or with canned fruit<|endoftext|>
