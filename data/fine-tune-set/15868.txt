Please give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>California Breakfast Burritos

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Olive oil
• Green onions
• Green chilies
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Cheddar cheese
• Eggs
• Cayenne pepper
• Cilantro
• Green chili salsa

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325f
2. Wrap tortillas in foil and heat in oven for 5 to 10 minutes
3. In a medium nonstick skillet, heat oil over medium-high heat
4. Add green onions, chiles, tomato, salt and pepper
5. Saute 4 minutes
6. Push mixture to side of pan
7. Sprinkle cheese over top of vegetable mixture
8. Add eggs and cayenne to skillet
9. Cook 1 to 2 minutes, stirring occasionally with spatula, until soft, creamy curds form
10. Stir vegetable mixture into eggs
11. Divide mixture among warm tortillas
12. Sprinkle with cilantro, a tablespoon of salsa, and 2 tbls
13. Cheese
14. Roll up tortillas
15. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
