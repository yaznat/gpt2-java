What's a recipe for salad?<|endoftext|>Amalfi Salads

Ingredients:
• Pine nuts
• Salad leaves
• Tomatoes
• Black olives
• Sun-dried tomato paste
• Balsamic vinegar
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. The day before
2. Make the dressing
3. Mix the tomato paste and vinegar in a small bowl
4. Slowly whisk in the olive oil to form a smooth emulsion and season to taste
5. Store it in a screw-top jar if you want to get ahead and re-whisk before using on the night
6. The pine nuts can be dealt with the day before to save time
7. Heat a small frying pan
8. To serve
9. Put the leaves in a large bowl with the tomatoes and olives
10. Pour in the dressing and mix well
11. Divide between eight glasses
12. Sprinkle with the toasted pine nuts<|endoftext|>
