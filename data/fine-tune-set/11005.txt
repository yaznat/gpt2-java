What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>A Lighter Side Of Cc S Oven Grilled Reuben Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Cider vinegar
• Instant apple cider drink mix
• Cabbage
• Reduced-fat mayonnaise
• Dijon mustard
• Rye bread
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Reduced-fat swiss cheese
• Corned beef

Steps:
1. Adjust oven rack to middle position, place baking sheet on rack, and heat oven to 450 degrees
2. In a small saucepan over medium heat, stir together vinegar and apple cider mix till dissolved
3. Add coleslaw, cover, and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until coleslaw is tender
4. Remove lid and simmer until liquid has evaporated, 1-2 minutes
5. Line a baking sheet lined with parchment paper
6. Spray one side of each bread slice with non-stick cooking spray and turn over onto the parchment
7. Combine mayonnaise and mustard in small bowl
8. To each slice, spread mayo mixture, followed with 1 slice of cheese, the slaw, the corned beef, and then top with another slice of cheese
9. Bake until toasted, about 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
