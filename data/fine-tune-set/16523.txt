Ingredients:
• Boiling water
• Dried apricot
• Orange juice
• Plain yogurt
• Dark brown sugar

Steps:
1. Put quartered apricots in a small bowl & pour boiling water over them, before covering them & leaving to soak overnight
2. In the morning, put the apricots & the water into a food processor or blender & process until pureed
3. Add orange juice to the mixture & process until combined
4. Pour into two tall glasses & top each with 1 tablespoon of yogurt & a sprinkling of brown sugar<|endoftext|>
