Can you give me a omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Breakfast Omelette Ww

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Egg whites
• Salt
• Pepper
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Tomatoes
• Fat-free cottage cheese
• Reduced-fat italian cheese blend
• Basil
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. In a bowl whisk together eggs, egg whites, 1 / 2 teaspoon of the salt and 1 / 8 teaspoon of the pepper
2. Set aside
3. Spray a non-stick skillet with pam and set over medium-high heat
4. Add onion, garlic and remaining salt and 1 / 8 teaspoon pepper
5. Cook, stirring occasionally for 2-5 minutes or until onion is softened
6. Add tomato and cook an additional 2 minutes
7. Transfer mixture to a bowl and stir in cheese and basil
8. Set aside
9. Wipe out skillet with a paper towel
10. Add oil to skillet and heat over medium-high heat
11. Pour in egg mixture and cook until eggs are set, lifting edges to allow uncooked eggs to flow under
12. Spoon cheese mixture over half of the eggs, folding the other half of the eggs over the cheese mixture to enclose the filling
13. Cook turning once for about 2 more minutes or until filling is heated through
14. Cut into 4 serving pieces and serve<|endoftext|>
