Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Garlic salt
• Chili powder
• Lettuce
• Cucumber
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Chunky salsa
• Tomatoes
• Green onions
• Fat free ranch dressing
• Baked corn tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Sprinkle chicken with garlic salt and chili powder
2. Broil 2 minutes or until done
3. Cool and cut into strips
4. In a bowl, toss lettuce with chicken, cucumber and cheese
5. To serve, top with salsa, tomato, green onions, dressing and crushed tortilla chips<|endoftext|>
