Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>All About The Oatmeal Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Oats
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 375 degrees
2. Cream butter and sugars until fluffy
3. Add egg and vanilla
4. Beat well
5. Mix together flour, baking soda, cinnamon and salt and add to wet ingrediants in about three parts
6. Mix together oats and raisins and add in intervals to dough
7. Roll dough into small balls and flatten with spoon on cookie sheet
8. Bake 8-9 minutes for slightly chewy
9. 10-11 minutes for crisp
10. Variations- bar cookies: press dough onto bottom of ungreased 13 x 9-inch baking pan
11. Bake 25 to 30 minutes or until light golden brown
12. Cool completely in pan on wire rack
13. Cut into bars
14. Store tightly covered<|endoftext|>
