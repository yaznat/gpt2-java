What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Adobo Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin chops
• Brown sugar
• Olive oil
• Orange juice
• Cilantro
• Red wine vinegar
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Ground red pepper
• Ground cinnamon
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Trim fat from pork chops
2. Place chops in a plastic bag set in a shallow dish
3. Combine remaining ingredients and pour over chops in bag
4. Marinate in the refrigerator for 2 to 24 hours, turning occasionally
5. Drain chops, discarding marinade
6. Grill over medium heart about 12 to 15 minutes until chops are done, turning once halfway through grilling<|endoftext|>
