Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Heart Stopping Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Onion
• Fresh ginger
• Soy sauce
• Sesame oil
• Medium-grain white rice
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Cut bacon into half inch pieces and fry in a wok until crisp
2. Remove bacon and set aside leaving all the bacon grease in the wok
3. Sautee the peppers and onions in the bacon grease until almost tender
4. The onions should be slightly translusent
5. Add in the ginger
6. Push the vegetables to the side of the wok and add the rice, soy sauce, and sesame oil
7. Fry the rice until it shows signs of browning or crisping
8. It is okay if the vegetables begin to mix in at this point
9. Push aside the rice to make room to scramble the eggs
10. Once the eggs are cooked blend them into the rice and vegetables
11. It's okay if the vegetables and rice get into the eggs during the scrambling
12. Add reserved cooked bacon and mix well
13. Serve with extra soy sauce to taste<|endoftext|>
