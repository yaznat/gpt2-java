Ingredients:
• Prepared mustard
• Poppy seed
• Worcestershire sauce
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Buns
• Deli turkey
• Deli ham
• Cheddar cheese
• Swiss cheese

Steps:
1. Melt butter, add first four ingredients and set aside
2. Put together 12 sandwiches in a 9x13 pan, using deli meat and cheese
3. You can also use whatever deli meats or cheese you prefer
4. Pour marinade over tops of finished sandwiches
5. Cover with foil and
6. Refrigerate overnight
7. Half an hour before serving, bake covered at 350 for 25 minutes, then uncovered for 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
