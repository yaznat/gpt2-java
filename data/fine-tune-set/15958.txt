Ingredients:
• Mashed potatoes
• Mixed vegetables
• Cheddar cheese
• Garlic sauce
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Lemon wedge

Steps:
1. Because this is a copycat recipe, all measurements are estimated
2. Prepare the garlic sauce
3. Thicken the sauce by removing 1 / 4 cup of sauce into a small bowl, adding 1 / 2 tablespoon of white flour, whisk, and return to pot and whisk well to break up any lumps
4. With immersion blender, puree sauce until smooth
5. Heat up the mashed potatoes and vegetables separately until warm
6. Divide the mashed potatoes and mixed vegetables evenly among the tortillas
7. Sprinkle with salt, freshly ground black pepper, and a few drizzles of lemon juice
8. Spoon about 2-3 tablespoons of the garlic sauce on each burrito
9. Lastly, garnish with cheese and cilantro
10. Roll up burrito style
11. To do this, begin by rolling up the tortilla 1 / 4 of the way, stop, fold in the sides and roll up to the end
12. Serve seam down
13. Eat
