Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef And Green Bean Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Sweet red pepper
• Lean ground beef
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic cloves
• Gingerroot
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Green beans
• Beef stock
• Hoisin sauce
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Seed, core and cut red pepper into thin strips, set aside
2. In a wok stir fry beef over high heat until no longer pink, about 3 minutes
3. Using a slotted spoon, transfer beef to plate
4. Drain fat from wok, add oil, stir fry garlic, ginger, salt and pepper over medium heat until fragrant, about 1 minute
5. Add green beans, red pepper and 2 tablespoons water, cover and steam until beans and pepper are tender-crisp, about 3 minutes
6. Return beef and any juices to wok
7. Whisk together beef stock, hoisin sauce, soy sauce, and cornstarch
8. Stir into wok
9. Bring to a boil, boil stirring until sauce is thickened and glossy, about 1 minute
10. Sprinkle with green onions and serve<|endoftext|>
