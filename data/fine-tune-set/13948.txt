What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Boneless Leg Of Lamb

Ingredients:
• Lamb
• Olive oil
• Fresh parsley
• Fresh rosemary
• Fresh thyme
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Generously rub leg of lamb with olive oil
2. Season lamb with all the ingredients listed, making sure that they are evenly distributed
3. Place leg of lamb on a rack in a roasting pan
4. Preheat oven to 325 degrees and bake leg of lamb uncovered for 30 minutes per pound or until internal temperature reaches 120 to 130 degrees at the thickest point<|endoftext|>
