What's a good custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Custard Pudding Microwave

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Flour
• Salt
• Milk
• Egg yolks
• Butter
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Combine sugar, cornstarch, flour and salt in 2-qt
2. Glass bowl
3. Slowly stir in milk
4. Microwave on high, uncovered, for 8-9 minutes, or until mixture comes to a full boil
5. Stirring 2 or 3 times
6. Blend a small amount of the hot mixture into egg yolks
7. Return to hot mixture, blending well
8. Microwave on high, uncovered, for 45 seconds to 1 minute, or until bubbly around the edge, stirring once or twice
9. Stir in butter and vanilla
10. Serve warm or cold<|endoftext|>
