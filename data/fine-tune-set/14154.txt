Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Tomato juice
• Lite olive oil
• Paprika
• Chili powder
• Oregano
• Thyme
• Dry mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Hot sauce
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cut tofu into 1 x 1 1 / 2" cubes and place in shallow dish
2. In a saucepan combine the tomato juice, oil, paprika, chili powder, oregano, thyme, mustard, worcestershire sauce, hot pepper, and salt & pepper
3. Heat until boiling
4. Brush all over the tofu so all sides are coated
5. Marinate at room temperature for 1 hour or you can marinate in the fridge for up to 8 hours
6. Thread tofu on four skewers
7. Place on greased grill and bbq for about 20 minutes, turning occasionally<|endoftext|>
