What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Steak Noodles

Ingredients:
• Round steaks
• Green bell pepper
• Onion
• Celery
• Green cabbage
• Minced garlic clove
• Oregano
• Basil
• Thyme
• Salt and pepper
• Oil
• Tomato sauce
• Tomato soup
• Ketchup
• Noodles

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tblsp
2. Oil in large skillet
3. Season meat with salt and pepper to taste
4. Brown on both sides
5. Remove to a 9 x 12" casserole dish
6. In same pan, heat remaining oil
7. Saute green pepper, onion, celery, cabbage, and garlic 10 minutes
8. Stir in tomato sauce, tomato soup, and ketchup
9. Add oregano, basil, thyme, and salt and pepper to taste
10. Simmer 10 minutes
11. Pour sauce mixture over meat in casserole
12. Cover and bake at 350 for 1 1 / 2 hours or until meat is tender
13. Cook noodles in plenty of boiling, salted water, 10-12 minutes
14. Drain
15. Serve meat and sauce over noodles
16. Add a tossed salad, garlic bread and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
