Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>American Traditional Pumpkin Pie

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Pumpkin mush
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Clove
• Salt
• Evaporated milk
• Pie shell
• Pumpkin

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees
2. In a large non-metal bowl combine sugars and eggs
3. Add in the pumpkin mush, the spices, salt, and evaporated milk
4. Pour the filling into the pie shell
5. Bake for 10 minutes, and then reduce heat to 350 and bake for another 50 minutes, or until pie sets
6. --------pumpkinmush-----------
7. Cut pumpkin in half
8. Prick the skin several times with a fork, and place on a cookie sheet, cut-side up
9. Bake for 50 minutes or until very soft when poked with a fork
10. Let the pumpkin cool, then scoop out the seeds with a spoon
11. Scoop out the pumpkin meat, and throw away the skin
12. Mash the pumpkin meat with a potato masher or puree in a blender / food processor
13. Makes about 4 cups<|endoftext|>
