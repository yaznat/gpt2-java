Can you give me a popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Sugar Free Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Sugar-free jello
• Sugar-free kool-aid mix
• Boiling water
• Cold water

Steps:
1. Combine complimentary flavors of sugar-free jello and kool-aid in a 2-quart container and mix in boiling water
2. Stir until the jello is disolved
3. Add cold water to make 2 quarts
4. Pour into popsicle molds or dixie cups and place in freezer until frozen<|endoftext|>
