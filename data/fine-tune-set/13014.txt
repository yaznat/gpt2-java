Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Mushroom
• Lemon juice
• Garlic
• Salt
• Pepper
• Fish fillets
• Scallion
• Dry vermouth
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. In small skillet, heat margarine until bubbly and hot
3. Add mushrooms, lemon juice, garlic and seasonings and saute over high heat, stirring occasionally, until most of liquid has evaporated
4. Place fish in 1-qt
5. Casserole and top with mushroom mixture, scallions and vermouth
6. Bake until fish is opaque and flakes easily when tested with a fork, about 10 minutes
7. If desired, sprinkle with parsley just before serving<|endoftext|>
