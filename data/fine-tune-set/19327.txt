What's a good popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Sunset Watermelon Popsicles

Ingredients:
• Watermelon
• Orange juice
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Cut watermelon into chunks, and puree in a blender until you have 5 cups
2. Add orange juice and sugar
3. Blend
4. Pour into popsicle molds and freeze
5. Cooking time is freezer time
6. This recipe will fill two popsicle molds of 10 each<|endoftext|>
