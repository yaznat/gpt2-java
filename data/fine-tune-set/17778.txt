Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Dried apricot
• Pecans
• Orange juice
• Vegetable oil
• Brown sugar
• Egg whites
• Canned pumpkin
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Lightly oil 8 muffin cups or spray with a nonstick cooking spray
3. In a large bowl, combine both flours, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and cloves
4. Mix well
5. Mix in apricots and pecans
6. In another bowl, combine remaining ingredients
7. Beat with a fork or wire whisk until blended
8. Add to dry mixture, mixing until all ingredients are moistened
9. Divide mixture evenly into prepared muffin cups
10. Bake 15 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center of a muffin comes out clean
11. Remove muffins to a rack to cool<|endoftext|>
