Ingredients:
• Pasta
• Miracle whip
• Taco seasoning mix
• Chunky salsa
• Pitted black olives
• Colby-monterey jack cheese
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves

Steps:
1. Cook pasta as directed on package
2. Mix dressing, seasoning and salsa in large bowl
3. Add drained pasta and all remaining ingredients
4. Mix lightly
5. Serve immediately
6. Or, cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour or until chilled<|endoftext|>
