Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chin Jao Ro Su Bamboo And Bell Pepper Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Pork
• Egg
• Sake
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Oil
• Bell pepper
• Bamboo shoot
• Oyster sauce
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Combine first 5 ingredients in a bowl
2. Stir fry in oil with the garlic and ginger until brown
3. Add bell peppers and bamboo shoots
4. Stir fry until done, about 5 minutes
5. Add sauce during the last minute or so of cooking time<|endoftext|>
