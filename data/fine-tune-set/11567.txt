Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• All-purpose flour
• Baking cocoa
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Plain yogurt
• Zucchini
• Carrot
• Chocolate frosting

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy
2. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition
3. Stir in vanilla
4. Combine the flour, baking cocoa, baking powder, baking soda and salt
5. Add tot he creamed mixture alternately with the yogurt, beating well after each addition
6. Fold in zucchini and carrots
7. Fill paper-lined muffin cups 2 / 3 full
8. Bake at 350 degrees for 18 - 22 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean
9. Cool for 10 minutes before removing from pans to wire racks to cool completely
10. Once cool frost cupcales and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
