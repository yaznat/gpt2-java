Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Egg
• Milk
• Jellied cranberry sauce
• Butter
• Light corn syrup

Steps:
1. Combine bisquick, egg and milk in a mixing bowl and beat with mixer until smooth
2. Fold in the 1 / 2 cup of cranberry sauce
3. Heat butter, as needed in griddle and pour 1 / 4 cup of batter into pan to make pancakes
4. When bubbles appear around the edges, flip over until done
5. Meanwhile make the cranberry syrup
6. Heat the 1 1 / 2 cups of cranberry sauce left and the corn syrup in a saucepan on low heat for 10 minutes
7. Serve with the pancakes
8. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
