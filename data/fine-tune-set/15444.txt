Ingredients:
• Ground sirloin
• Butter
• Onion
• Celery
• Carrot
• Beef broth
• Water
• Low-fat cheddar cheese
• Skim milk
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Low-fat sour cream

Steps:
1. Brown beef in a skillet, and remove meat
2. Wipe out skillet with a paper towel, and add butter and vegetables and salt and pepper
3. Saute until the vegetables are soft
4. Add the broth and water and bring to a low boil and cook for about 10 minutes
5. Add cream and cheese and cook until the cheese is melted
6. Add beef back to soup and stir in the sour cream
7. Heat through
8. Ladle into bowls and sprinkle with bacon<|endoftext|>
