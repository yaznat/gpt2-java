• Lemon juice
• Hot pepper sauce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Rolls
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except rolls and lettuce in a medium bowl
2. Slice rolls in half, and toast if desired
3. Lay lettuce leaves on bottom halves of rolls, and divide chicken salad between them
