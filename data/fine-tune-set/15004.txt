Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic cloves
• Spanish onions
• Firm tofu
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Vegetable broth
• Fish sauce
• Carrots
• Celery
• Green cabbage
• Vegetarian sausages
• Rice noodles
• Salt & fresh ground pepper
• Scallions
• Lemons

Steps:
1. Press tofu by cutting in half and placing paper towels on top, then placing a heavy cast iron skillet on top and pressing for 15-20 minutes, to squeeze out moisture
2. Cut tofu into 1 1 / 2" strips and roll in cornstarch to coat
3. In a wok, heat oil over medium-high heat
4. Add garlic and onions and stir-fry until translucent
5. Add tofu and sear until tofu is browned
6. To make a chiffonade, stack the cabbage leaves one on top of the other and roll tightly
7. Cut in thin slices
8. Add soy sauce, vegetable broth, fish sauce, carrots, celery, cabbage, and vegetarian sausage to wok
9. Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes or until vegetables are al dente and wok is almost dry
10. Drain rice noodles, stir into wok and cook for 5 to 7 minutes on medium-low heat with tofu and vegetables
11. Season, to taste, and serve immediately on a warmed plate
12. Garnish with scallions and a lemon wedge<|endoftext|>
