What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Balsamic Marinated Tomato Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Balsamic vinegar
• Red wine vinegar
• Fresh tarragon
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Garlic clove
• Beefsteak tomatoes
• Red onion
• French bread
• Boston lettuce
• Avocados

Steps:
1. In small bowl, whisk together oil, balsamic vinegar, red wine vinegar, tarragon, 1 / 4 teaspoon salt, 1 / 4 teaspoon pepper, and garlic
2. Place half the tomato slices and half of the onion slices in a shallow baking dish
3. Drizzle with half of the olive oil mixture
4. Add remaining tomato and onion slices
5. Drizzle with remaining olive oil
6. Let stand at least 2 hours, spooning juices over tomato and onion 4 or 5 times
7. To assemble sandwiches: split bread loaves and place, cut side up, on a platter
8. Brush cut sides with marinade
9. Divide tomato and onion mixture among loaves
10. Top with lettuce and avocado
11. Sprinkle with remaining 1 / 4 teaspoon salt and 1 / 4 teaspoon pepper<|endoftext|>
