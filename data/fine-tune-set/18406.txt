What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Cheese Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Milk
• Eggs
• Oil
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Combine flour, milk, egg yolks, oil, and salt
2. Just before baking fold in baking powder and stiffly beaten egg whites
3. Pour batter into waffle iron and sprinkle as much shredded cheese as you desire on top of the batter<|endoftext|>
