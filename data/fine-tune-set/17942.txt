Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Butter
• Water
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Baking cocoa
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat to 350
2. Spray 9x13' baking pan with cooking spray
3. Stir butter, sugar and water together until combined
4. Add eggs and vanilla
5. In separate bowl, combine flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt
6. Stir in nuts, if using
7. Stir dry ingredients into butter mixture until combined, place in prepared pan
8. Bake 18 to 25 minutes until toothpick comes out with little crumbs attached, but not wet<|endoftext|>
