Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Anti Depressant Brownies

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Baking soda
• Canola oil
• Boiling water
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Sugar
• Egg whites
• All-purpose flour
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350f
2. Spray a 9x13" baking pan with cooking oil and set aside
3. In a large bowl, whisk together the cocoa and baking soda
4. Blend in the oil, and mix until smooth
5. Pour in the boiling water, stirring until the mixture thickens
6. Stir in the applesauce, the sugar, and the egg whites
7. Mix until smooth
8. Add the flour and the vanilla
9. Blend completely- there should be no lumps
10. Pour into the prepared baking pan
11. Spread the batter evenly to avoid crispy edges
12. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until just set in the middle
13. Let cool and cut into squares<|endoftext|>
