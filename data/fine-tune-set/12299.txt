Give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>30 Minute White Bean And Tomato Soup

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onion
• Chicken broth
• Great northern beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Dried thyme
• Ground cumin

Steps:
1. Cut bacon into 1 inch slices and cook over medium high heat in a soup pot
2. Cook, stirring frequently, for 5 minutes or until beginning to brown
3. Add onion and cook for 5 minutes stirring occasionally
4. Remove any excess pan drippings
5. Stir in broth, beans, tomatoes, thyme and cumin
6. Over high heat bring to a boil
7. Reduce heat to medium low and simmer 5-10 minutes
8. With a potato masher, mash beans about 15 times to slightly thicken the soup
9. Serve with garlic bread!<|endoftext|>
