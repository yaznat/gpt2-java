Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Cheddar cheese
• Corn tortillas
• Water
• Tomato soup
• Chunky salsa

Steps:
1. Brown beef in skillet, drain fat
2. Add in soup, salsa, water, tortillas, and half of cheese
3. Heat to a boil
4. Cover and let cook for 5 minutes or until hot
5. Add remaining cheese before serving!<|endoftext|>
