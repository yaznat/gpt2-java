Ingredients:
• Pitted dates
• Whole almonds
• Smoked bacon

Steps:
1. Stuff each date with one almond
2. Cut bacon into half or thirds
3. Wrap bacon around date and secure with a round toothpick
4. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
5. Place dates on a foil lined baking pan and cook until bacon is crispy
6. Drain on paper towel and serve warm or room temperature
7. Watch them disappear!<|endoftext|>
