Ingredients:
• Enchilada sauce
• Instant rice
• Green bell pepper
• Vegetarian refried beans
• Flour tortillas
• Tomatoes
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. In medium saucepan, bring the sauce to a boil
2. Add rice and pepper
3. Cover, remove from burner and let stand 5-10 minutes then fluff with a fork
4. Next, spread 1 / 4 cup of the beans evenly over tortillas to within 1 inch of edges
5. Top each with 1 / 4 of the rice mixture, tomato and cheese
6. Spoon in the sour cream down centers of tortillas
7. Roll tortillas up
8. Serve right away while warm<|endoftext|>
