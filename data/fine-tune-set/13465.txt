Ingredients:
• Beef sirloin
• Rice vinegar
• Salt
• Ground turmeric
• Russet potatoes
• Canola oil
• Yellow onion
• Fresh ginger
• Cayenne

Steps:
1. If using raw beef, freeze 20 minutes and slice paper-thin
2. Rub the beef with the vinegar, 1 tsp salt and 1 / 2 tsp turmeric
3. Bring the whole potatoes to a boil in slightly salted water to cover
4. Boil for 5 minutes, then remove from heat and let sit in the hot water for 15 minutes
5. The potatoes will be perfectly done
6. Peel the potatoes and cut into 6 to 8 wedges each
7. Coat with the remaining 1 / 4 tsp turmeric and 1 / 4 tsp salt
8. Heat 3 tbl oil in a large wok and fry the onion and ginger until slightly softened
9. Add the cayenne and the marinated beef
10. Cook over high heat for 5 to 8 minutes or until tender
11. If not sufficiently tender, add 1 / 2 cup water and cook over medium-low heat until it's done to your likeness
12. Meanwhile, heat the remaining 3 tbl oil in a large skillet and gently fry the potato wedges over low heat until golden brown and crisp
13. Serve meat with the potato wedges
14. To be authentically anglo-indian, serve some ketchup on the side<|endoftext|>
