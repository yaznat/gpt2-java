Can you give me a chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>African Grilled Chicken Breast

Ingredients:
• Paprika
• Chili powder
• Lemon juice
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Salt
• Chicken breasts

Steps:
1. Stir together all ingredients but chicken and then rub mixture over chicken breats and let marinate in fridge for at least 4 hours
2. Grill chicken on pre heated grill and for about 15 minutes, or until chicken is done<|endoftext|>
