What's a good wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Pretzels

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Pretzel sticks
• Brown sugar
• Chili powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil, place a cooling rack inside of foil-lined pan
3. Lightly grease rack
4. Wrap 1 piece of bacon around each pretzel stick
5. In a shallow dish, combine brown sugar and chili powder
6. Roll each wrapped pretzel in sugar mixture to coat
7. Place on prepared rack
8. Bake 20 minutes, turning once, or until bacon is crispy
9. Remove from racks, and let cool
10. To reheat after freezing, place frozen pretzels on a rimmed baking sheet
11. Cover lightly with aluminum foil
12. Bake at 350 degrees 10-15 minutes, or until reheated
13. Watch carefully, as bacon will burn<|endoftext|>
