Give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>African Okra Soup

Ingredients:
• Okra pods
• Tomatoes
• Fresh corn
• Water
• Bacon
• Lemon
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Trim off both ends of okra pods
2. Slice 1 / 4 inch thick
3. Carefully place the tomatoes into a small pot of boiling water for about 30-45 seconds
4. Remove from water and peel off skin
5. Slice tomatoes in half and scoop out the runny insides, discard this part
6. Coarsely chop the fleshy part of the tomatoes
7. Dice the bacon and fry until crisp
8. Remove to papertowels
9. Save grease
10. Place all ingredients into a saucepan and bring to a boil
11. Reduce the heat and simmer for about 15 minutes, or until the okra is just tender
12. 1-2 tbl
13. Bacon grease can be added to enhance the soup's flavor
14. The lemon can be thickly sliced and cooked right in the pot of soup, or it can be zested and halved and the fresh juice can be squeezed over each portion when served to taste, and garnished with the zest<|endoftext|>
