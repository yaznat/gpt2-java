Can you give me a tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Chinese Tofu Marinade

Ingredients:
• Silken tofu
• Light soy sauce
• Cilantro roots
• Fresh ginger
• Scallions
• Ketjap manis
• Malt vinegar
• Chili oil
• Sesame oil
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Invert tofu into a shallow bowl
2. Slice widthways into 8 slices
3. Mix all other ingredients well and pour over tofu
4. Sprinkle with sichuan pepper and salt and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
