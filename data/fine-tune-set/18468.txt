Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Easy Bisquick Peanut Butter And Jelly Waffle French Toast

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Bisquick
• Peanut butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Egg
• Bread
• Mini chocolate chips
• Jelly
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Heat waffle iron and grease
2. Stir milk, bisquick, peanut butter, sugar, vanilla, and egg until blended
3. Dip bread into batter on both sides
4. Place in waffle iron and close
5. Bake until steaming stops
6. Remove waffle
7. Sprinkle each waffle with choc
8. Chips, jelly, and powdered sugar if you like<|endoftext|>
