Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Taco seasoning mix
• Cheddar cheese
• Water
• Crescent roll dough
• Green bell pepper
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Pitted black olives
• Salsa
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Set oven to 375f
2. In a bowl, combine the cooked ground beef, taco seasoning mix, cheese and water
3. On a lightly greased pizza pan, or round baking stone, arrange the crescent triangles in a circle, with the larger sides of the dough overlapping each other a bit in center, and the points to the outside
4. Spoon a small amount of meat mixture over rolls
5. Fold points of triangles over the filling, and tuck under base at center
6. Bake 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown
7. Cut off the top of the bell pepper
8. Fill the pepper with salsa, and place in the center of the baked ring,
9. Mound lettuce, onion, tomato and olives around the pepper
10. Garnish with sour cream -- serve!<|endoftext|>
