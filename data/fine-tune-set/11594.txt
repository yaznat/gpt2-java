Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>14 Karat Cake

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Allspice
• Oil
• Eggs
• Carrots
• Ground cloves
• Margarine
• Cream cheese
• Vanilla
• Powdered sugar
• Nuts
• Coconut
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Measure all ingredients and mix in a large bowl- beat for 4 minutes
2. Pour into a 9"x13" greased pan
3. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes at 350 degrees
4. To make frosting, cream margarine, cream cheese and vanilla
5. Fold in remaining ingredients
6. Frost cake when cake is cool<|endoftext|>
