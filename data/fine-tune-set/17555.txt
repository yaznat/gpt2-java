Please give me a recipe for coffee.<|endoftext|>Buttered Rum Coffee Mix

Ingredients:
• Coffee
• Nutmeg
• Rum extract
• Butter flavor extract

Steps:
1. Place coffee and nutmeg in a blender or food processor fitted with a steel blade
2. With processor running, add flavorings
3. Stop processor and scrape sides of container with a spatula
4. Process 10 seconds longer
5. Store in refrigerator
6. Yields: mix for eight 6-ounce servings
7. To use:
8. Place minimum of 1 tsp in a 1 cup measuring cup and add boiling water
9. Stir and strain into a 6 oz cup<|endoftext|>
