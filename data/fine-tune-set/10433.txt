Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Crisco
• Eggs
• Lemon extract
• Orange juice
• Butter
• Confectioners' sugar
• Milk
• Candy sprinkles

Steps:
1. Cream crisco and sugar until well blended, add eggs, mix well, then the extract & orange juice, blend in, then add the baking powder and baking soda, mixing well, finally adding the flour mixing well
2. Bake at 450 degrees
3. Preheat oven prior to baking
4. Bake for 5-6 minutes
5. Can leave in a minute longer for a very slightly browned cookie
6. Do not overbake, or they will be no good, as this is not a moist type of cookie
7. Drop by teaspoonful fairly closely set, as they do not really expand out
8. Depending upon "your teaspoonful size" you will get several dozen of these
9. Or you can roll pscs
10. Into small logs and then spiral
11. Glaze:
12. Melt the butter in medium pan, adding all the other ingredients, whisking until sugar melts and it is heated through
13. Consistency should not be too loose nor too thick, should coat cookie evenly without a great deal of dripping
14. You can add additional small amounts of confectioners sugar if necessary to thicken a bit more
15. Apply glaze with spoon or dipping into the pan, and immediately sprinkle with the colored sprinkles
16. Let dry
17. Hope you enjoy this little bit of italian flavor!<|endoftext|>
