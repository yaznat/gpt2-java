Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Chocolate syrup
• All-purpose flour
• Vegetable oil
• Baking powder
• Butter
• Baking soda
• Strawberries
• Granulated sugar
• Whipping cream
• Sour cream
• Milk

Steps:
1. Beat egg whites until stiff
2. Set aside
3. Mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda and sugar together
4. Beat the egg yolks with the sour cream, milk and chocolate syrup
5. Mix the oil with the melted butter, using a small bowl
6. Add the sour cream mixture - alternate
7. With the oil and butter mixture - to
8. The dry ingredients
9. Mix until smooth
10. Fold in the egg whites
11. Pour 1 / 2 cup of batter into a lightly greased waffle maker
12. Bake for 4 or 5 minutes or until the steaming stops
13. Repeat for the remainder of the batch of waffles
14. Whip the whipped cream until stiff and fluffy but not dry
15. Top hot waffles with sliced strawberries and whipped cream
16. Serve at once<|endoftext|>
