Please give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Big Beefy Bacon Bean Burritos

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Lean ground beef
• Sweet onions
• Chili sauce
• Barbecue sauce
• Liquid smoke
• Baked beans
• Cocoa powder
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Red pepper flakes
• Flour tortillas
• Tortilla chips
• Monterey jack and cheddar cheese blend

Steps:
1. Fry the bacon in a skillet until crisp
2. Drain on paper towel
3. Saut the onions in the bacon fat in the same skillet until soft
4. Add the beef and continue sauting until the beef is browned
5. Add the chili sauce, barbecue sauce, liquid smoke, cocoa powder, chili powder, cumin, beans and red pepper flakes
6. Stir gently until all ingredients are mixed thoroughly
7. Spoon of the mixture onto each tortilla and fold into burritos
8. Generously sprinkle shredded cheese and crumbled chips on top of each burrito
9. Bake in 350 degree oven for 10 minutes, or broil, until the cheese is bubbling
10. Garnish with sour cream or guacamole, if desired
11. Serve with mexican rice and taco salad<|endoftext|>
