Ingredients:
• Beef sirloin steaks
• Dark beer
• Teriyaki sauce
• Brown sugar
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. Use a fork to poke holes all over the surface of the steaks, and place steaks in a large baking dish
2. In a bowl, mix together beer, teriyaki sauce, and brown sugar
3. Pour sauce over steaks, and let sit about 15-30 minutes
4. Sprinkle with 1 / 2 the seasoned salt, pepper, and garlic powder
5. Set aside for 10 minutes
6. Preheat grill for high heat
7. Turn steaks over, sprinkle with remaining seasoned salt, pepper, and garlic powder, and continue marinating for 10 more minutes
8. Remove steaks from marinade
9. Pour marinade into a small saucepan, bring to a boil, and cook for several minutes
10. Lightly oil the grill grate
11. Grill steaks for 7 minutes per side, or to desired doneness
12. During the last few minutes of grilling, baste steaks with boiled marinade to enhance the flavor and ensure juiciness<|endoftext|>
