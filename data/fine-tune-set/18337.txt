Ingredients:
• Bacon
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Unsalted butter
• Eggs
• Maple syrup

Steps:
1. Cook bacon until crisp, drain and crumble
2. Plug in electric waffle iron to preheat
3. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl, whisk together to blend
4. Pour milk in a glass measuring cup and microwave for 1 minute at full power to heat slightly
5. Pour into medium bowl, whisk in melted butter and eggs
6. Add liquid ingredients to flour mixture, whisking just until blended
7. Grease waffle iron with cooking spray
8. Spread about 1 / 3 cup batter over each iron and sprinkle 1 / 8 of bacon on top
9. Close wafle iron and cook until done
10. Repeat with remaining batter and bacon
11. Serve with warm syrup<|endoftext|>
