Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Whole milk
• Warm water
• Unsalted butter
• Honey
• Instant yeast
• Butter

Steps:
1. Mix milk, water, butter, honey and yeast together
2. Combine 3 1 / 2 cups flour and salt in bowl of stand mixer
3. Gradually add milk mixture to flour
4. Increase mixer speed to medium, mix for 10 minutes, until dough is smooth
5. Turn dough out onto floured surface, knead briefly to form a small, round ball
6. Place dough in a greased bowl, turn to coat
7. To proof: original recipe suggests while you are making the dough, heat an oven to 200 for 10 minutes, then turning off
8. Place bowl covered with plastic wrap in oven to proof
9. However, i cover with a towel and use my warming burner
10. Let rise 40 - 50 minutes, or until doubled in size
11. Turn onto floured work surface, gently roll dough into a 1 x 9 inch rectangle
12. Roll the short side into a tight cylinder
13. Pinch the seams closed
14. Place the seam-side down in a greased 95-inch loaf pan
15. Cover with plastic wrap or towel
16. Set in a warm spot until the dough almost doubles in size, 20-30 minutes
17. Preheat oven to 350
18. Placing a glass dish with boiling water inside
19. Bake 40 -50 minutes or until an instant-read thermometer reads 195
20. Brush with melted butter, cool on wire rack
21. To freeze: cool completely, cover with plastic wrap or foil
22. Place in freezer bag
23. Thaw on counter overnight<|endoftext|>
