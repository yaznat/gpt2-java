Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amy Vanderbilt S Honey Cream Peach Pie

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Flour
• Honey
• Heavy cream
• Powdered ginger
• Ground nutmeg
• Butter
• Peaches

Steps:
1. Prepare your pastry, line a 9 inch pie shell with it
2. Sprinkle bottom evenly with 1 tbsp of sugar and 1 tbsp flour
3. Preheat oven 425f
4. Combine remaining sugar and flour, stir in honey and cream, mixing until smooth
5. Pour over preaches
6. Mix gently to coat
7. Do not break them
8. Gently
9. Spoon gently into the pastry shell
10. Sprinkle with ginger and nutmeg
11. Dot with butter
12. Bake 45 minutes or until the pastry is done and the preaches are tender and top is brown
13. Serves 6<|endoftext|>
