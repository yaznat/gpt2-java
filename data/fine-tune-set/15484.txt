Give me a burger recipe.<|endoftext|>Barbecue In The Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Scallion
• Lemon, zest of
• Tomato paste
• Garlic cloves
• Brown sugar
• Soy sauce
• Chili powder
• Dijon mustard
• Black pepper
• Kosher salt

Steps:
1. Add all ingredients, except salt, to a large bowl
2. Combine ingredients by lightly folding with fingers
3. Shape into 6-8 patties, sprinkle both sides with salt and grill or pan fry until juices run clear when pierced with a fork<|endoftext|>
