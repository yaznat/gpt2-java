Can you give me a taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Bean And Chicken Tacos

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Black beans
• Frozen corn
• Salsa
• Taco shells
• Sour cream
• Cheddar cheese
• Lettuce
• Olive
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Cook chicken until done
2. Add salsa
3. Drain beans in a colander and add corn
4. Run hot water over them to thaw corn and rinse beans
5. Drain well
6. Add beans and corn to pan
7. Mix well and cook until heated through, about 5 minutes
8. Spoon into taco shells and garnish with sour cream, cheese, lettuce, olives, or tomato
9. We like to crunch up corn chips into a bowl and make it into a taco salad<|endoftext|>
