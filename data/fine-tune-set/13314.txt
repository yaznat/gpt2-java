What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>Aarsis Ultimate Hariyali Chicken Tikka

Ingredients:
• Garlic cloves
• Green chili pepper
• Cilantro leaves
• Mint leaves
• Cumin powder
• Green bell pepper
• Scallion
• Vegetables
• Chicken breasts
• Kosher salt
• Fresh lemon juice

Steps:
1. In a food processor / blender, mix cilantro leaves, mint leaves, scallion, bell pepper, cumin powder, green chili and garlic cloves
2. Add oil to the above mixture and blend to form a smooth paste
3. Remove the prepared green mixture in to a zip lock bag along with the chicken tenders
4. Seal the bag and massage the marinade in to the chicken tenders
5. Place this bag in the refrigerator overnight or for at least ten hours
6. Use skewers to place the marinated chicken tenders
7. Sprinkle salt on each side of them
8. Heat the grill and rub it with some oil
9. Place the skewers on it to get grill marks on each side and cook until the chicken is done
10. Drizzle some fresh lemon juice and enjoy with your favorite dipping sauce and loved ones!
11. This will result in a watery marinade, which is not desired<|endoftext|>
