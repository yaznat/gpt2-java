Ingredients:
• Soya sauce
• Lemon juice
• White vinegar
• Red wine vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Smooth peanut butter
• Ginger
• Garlic cloves
• Black pepper
• Flour
• Scallops
• Chicken breasts
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt and pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Onion
• Green beans
• Celery
• Carrots
• Zucchini
• Sesame oil
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Clean and chop all your vegetables, place in bowl and set aside
2. Rinse and drain scallops
3. Mix all of sauce ingredients until a nice constancy
4. Divide and place half in medium sized bowl, add scallops stirring until coated
5. Set aside to marinate
6. Chop meat if not already done and place in separate bowl, sprinkle with salt and pepper and pour over worcestershire sauce
7. Let stand about 2 minutes
8. Heat oil in wok or similar pan
9. Cook chicken till half way done about 5 minutes
10. Add scallops continue cooking for about 3-4 minutes until throughly cooked but not overdone!
11. Remove meat and set aside
12. Add vegetables to wok and add remaining sauce
13. Stir fry about 5 minutes until tender-crisp
14. Return meat and heat through
15. Serve over your rice or rice noodles!<|endoftext|>
