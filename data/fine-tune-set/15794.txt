What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef And Veggie Burrito Filling

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Bell pepper
• Tomatoes
• Salt
• Sugar
• Chili powder
• Garlic powder
• Paprika
• Dried cilantro
• Dried chipotle powder
• Water

Steps:
1. Brown the ground beef on medium-high heat
2. Drain beef if needed
3. Add onions, bell peppers, and tomato
4. Add salt, sugar, and spices
5. Stir well
6. Add water and allow to simmer for flavors to combine
7. Cook until water evaporates
8. Serve in tortilla wraps with your favorite toppings<|endoftext|>
