Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Condensed tomato soup
• Salsa
• Milk
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Brown the ground beef in a medium skillet over medium-high heat
2. Stir to separate meat
3. Pour off fat
4. Add and mix soup, salsa, milk, tortillas and half the cheese
5. Spoon into 2-quart shallow baking dish
6. Cover
7. Bake at 400f for 30 minutes or until hot
8. Sprinkle with remaining cheese<|endoftext|>
