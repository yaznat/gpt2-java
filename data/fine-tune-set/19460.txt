Please give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>4 Ingredient Sausage Potato Bake

Ingredients:
• Whole kernel corn
• Smoked sausage
• Campbell's cream of chicken soup
• Raw potatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Place all items into casserole dish and stir
3. Cook about one hour
4. Season to taste with salt and pepper and serve!
5. This can also be made in the crockpot
6. Just add all items and cook on high for 4 hours or low 6-8 hours<|endoftext|>
