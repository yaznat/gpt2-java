Please give me a recipe for pudding.<|endoftext|>Aunt Edna S Persimmon Pudding

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Persimmon pulp
• Buttermilk
• Baking soda
• Butter
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground cloves
• Nutmeg
• Ground ginger
• Cinnamon
• Flour
• Brown sugar
• Water
• Clove

Steps:
1. Beat eggs, sugar and persimmon pulp in a large bowl
2. Wisk buttermilk and baking soda together in a 2 cup container
3. Add to mixture and blend
4. Fold in flower, spices, baking powder, and salt
5. Add melted butter last and blend well
6. Pour mixture in an 9" x 13" buttered baking dish and bake 40 minutes at 350 degrees f
7. Add all ingredients for sauce to a small sauce pan and boil for several minutes until thick
8. Remove pudding from oven, pour sauce over it and allow to cool<|endoftext|>
