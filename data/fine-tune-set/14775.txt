Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Red onion
• Red bell pepper
• Garlic clove
• Cooked rice
• Sun-dried tomato
• Black olives
• Fresh parsley
• Dried oregano leaves
• Feta cheese
• Lemon juice
• Ground black pepper
• Fresh oregano

Steps:
1. Heat oil in large skillet over medium-high heat until hot
2. Add onion, red pepper and garlic
3. Cook and stir until onion is tender
4. Stir in rice, tomatoes, olives, parsley and oregano
5. Heat thoroughly
6. Remove from heat
7. Add cheese, lemon juice and black pepper
8. Stir until well blended
9. Garnish with oregano
10. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
