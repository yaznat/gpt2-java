Ingredients:

• Eggs

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Coat a 13" x 9" baking pan with cooking spray & dust lightly with flour
3. Streusel:
4. Combine all ingredients & mix until crumbly
5. Cake:
6. Mix all ingredients on medium speed for 2 minutes
7. Batter will be thick
8. Pour into pan & spread if necessary to even out
9. Top with blueberries
10. Sprinkle streusel over berries
11. Bake for 45-55 minutes or until cake tests done in the center<|endoftext|>
