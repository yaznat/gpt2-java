Ingredients:
• English cucumber
• Mint leaf
• Kumquat
• Lime juice
• Vodka
• Orange-flavored liqueur
• Club soda
• Ice

Steps:
1. Fill 4 cocktail glasses evenly with the cucumber, mint, and kumquats
2. Add 1 teaspoon of superfine sugar to each glass
3. Using a muddler, smash the ingredients in each glass slightly to release flavors and juices
4. Add 1 tbs lime juice into each glass
5. Add crushed ice into each glass filling 3 / 4 full
6. Add the vodka, orange liquer, and club soda in order to each glass
7. Serve with a wedge of cucumber as a garnish<|endoftext|>
