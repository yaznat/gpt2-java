Give me a recipe for pudding.<|endoftext|>Banana Pudding

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Whole milk
• Egg yolks
• Margarine
• Vanilla
• Vanilla wafers

Steps:
1. Separate egg yokes and whites
2. Add the vanilla and magarine to the egg yokes
3. Beat and set aside
4. Mix sugar, flour, and salt together thoroughly in medium saucepan
5. Slowly add milk, a little at a time, stirring to prevent lumps
6. Turn burner to medium heat and stir constantly until smooth and somewhat thick
7. Remove from heat and pour small amount into bowl with egg mixture, stirring while you add the hot pudding
8. Then add that mixture back into the hot pudding, stirring well
9. Let pudding cool in pan for 15 minutes, stirring one time about half way through cooling
10. While it's cooling layer large bowl with vanilla wafers and place a layer of sliced bananas on top of wafers
11. After the 15 minutes, pour 1 / 2 of pudding over banana and wafer layer
12. Repeat layer of wafer and banana then pour remainder of pudding over that
13. Top with a layer of sliced bananas with wafers on top this time
14. Chill and serve
15. There are always a few extra wafers
16. Place a slice of banana on a wafer and top with a dollop of pudding -- presto 'banana bites' to tempt the tastebuds for pudding later<|endoftext|>
