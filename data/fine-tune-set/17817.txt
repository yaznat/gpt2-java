What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Walnut Muffins

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Fresh thyme
• Salt
• Eggs
• Milk
• Cooking oil
• Bacon
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Grease twelve 2 1 / 2-inch muffin cups
2. Set muffin cups aside
3. In a medium mixing bowl stir together flour, baking powder, thyme, and salt
4. Make a well in the center of dry mixture
5. In another medium mixing bowl combine eggs, milk, and oil
6. Add all at once to the dry mixture
7. Stir just until moistened
8. Fold in crumbled bacon and walnuts
9. Spoon batter into the prepared muffin cups, filling each almost full
10. Bake in a 400f oven about 20 minutes or until golden
11. Cool in muffin cups on a wire rack for 5 minutes
12. Remove muffins from muffin cups
13. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
