Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Butterscotch Waffles

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Brown sugar
• Flaked coconut
• Evaporated milk
• Butter
• Butterscotch chips

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff
2. Set aside
3. In another bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, salt, brown sugar and coconut, and set aside
4. Combine egg yolks, evaporated milk and butter
5. Add to dry ingredients, mixing just until blended
6. Add in the butterscotch chips, and fold in the beaten egg whites just until mixed bake in a waffle maker until done<|endoftext|>
