Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Lime juice
• Salt
• Ground red pepper
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Flank steak
• Italian bread
• Salad greens
• Tomatoes
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Mash the avocado with a fork
2. Stir in lime juice, 1 / 4 teaspoon of the salt and red pepper
3. Set aside
4. Combine cumin, chili powder and remaining salt
5. Rub seasoning mix over both sides of steak
6. Grill or broil until done as desired, about 5 minutes on each side for medium
7. Remove meat to a cutting board and let rest for 5 minutes
8. Cut diagonally in thin slices
9. Cut bread horizontally almost through
10. Spread avocado on top half
11. Layer greens, beef and tomato on bottom then sprinkle with chopped cilantro
12. Close the sandwich and cut crosswise into 4 pieces<|endoftext|>
