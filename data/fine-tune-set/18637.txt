Give me a pudding recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Pudding With Bacardi Rum Cuban

Ingredients:
• Apples
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Butter
• Breadcrumbs
• Cinnamon
• Rum

Steps:
1. Peel apples and chop fine
2. Add everything else
3. Mix well until it forms a paste
4. Beat the egg whites until stiff
5. Add them to the apple mixture
6. Mix again
7. Pour mixture into a greased mold
8. Place mold in a pan of hot water
9. Bake at 350 for 30 - 40 minutes or until pudding is set<|endoftext|>
