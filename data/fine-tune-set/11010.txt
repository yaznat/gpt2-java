Ingredients:
• Cheddar cheese
• Mayonnaise
• Green olives
• Summer sausage
• Chives
• Roasted red pepper
• Onion powder
• Garlic salt
• Submarine bun

Steps:
1. Gently mix the first eight ingredients in a small bowl
2. Cut the submarine bun in half and place on broiler pan
3. Using toaster oven or broiler, lightly toast the bun, then remove from oven
4. Spoon the cheese mixture over both sides of the bun and return the pan to the broiler to melt and lightly brown the cheese
5. Serve open faced or as a sandwich<|endoftext|>
