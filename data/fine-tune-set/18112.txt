What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Americas Best Pancakes Or Waffles From That Chicken Ranch

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Lemon juice
• Egg
• Butter
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt

Steps:
1. Whisk milk and lemon juice in a medium bowl and set aside to let thicken
2. Whisk the egg and cooled melted butter into the milk mixture
3. In a separate bowl, whisk dry ingredients together - flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt
4. Create a little "well" in the center of dry ingredient bowl and pour the milky mixture in the center
5. Whisk gently until just combined, there should still be lumps
6. Stirring too much makes the cakes tough, you want them light and airy!
7. Heat a pan, griddle or waffle maker to medium heat and coat with butter or vegetable oil
8. For pancakes: pour pancake mixture by 1 / 4 cups and heat until large bubbles come to the top
9. Flip the pancake and wait another 1-2 minutes
10. For waffles: experiment with your waffle maker settings- you want a soft brown exterior with a lightly done interior- don't overcook!<|endoftext|>
