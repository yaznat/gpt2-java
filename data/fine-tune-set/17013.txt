Ingredients:
• Cocoa powder
• Superfine sugar
• Chocolate liqueur
• Irish cream
• Grand marnier
• Orange twist
• Ice

Steps:
1. Mix two parts bittersweet chocolate powder and one part superfine sugar on a small plate
2. Rub the outside rim of a martini glass with water and then dip the outside rim into the chocolate-sugar mixture
3. Set aside
4. Add godiva liqueur and bailey's to a shaker filled two-thirds with ice
5. Shake for 30 seconds and strain into martini glass
6. Float gran marnier centenaire over the top and garnish with orange twist<|endoftext|>
