Ingredients:
• Banana
• Blackberry
• Fresh orange juice
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Put all ingredients in blender and blend until smooth
2. Add more orange juice if you like your consistency thinner
3. Taste your smoothie, you may want to add more or less sugar to your own taste
4. Makes 1 generous sized portion<|endoftext|>
