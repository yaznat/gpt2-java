Give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Amigos Crisp Chicken Burritos

Ingredients:
• Canned chicken
• Green chilies
• Cream cheese
• Green onions
• Flour tortillas
• Cooking oil

Steps:
1. Let cream cheese come to room temperature
2. Drain the canned chicken
3. Mix cream cheese, green chiles, green onion and chicken until well blended
4. Cut two sides of the tortillas so you have two straight sides on each side
5. Spread chicken mixture thinly on 3 / 4 of the tortilla
6. Roll tortilla up starting with the end you spread the chicken mixture on
7. Seal the plain end with water
8. Heat oil to about 375-400 degrees
9. Drop burritos into hot oil and fry for about 2 minutes
10. Keep turning so they fry evenly
11. When they start to turn golden, they are done
12. Drain on a paper plate covered with a paper towel
13. These are so easy and goes great with salsa, guacamole, or ranch dressing<|endoftext|>
