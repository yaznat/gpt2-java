Ingredients:
• Pearl barley
• Canola oil
• Red onion
• Red bell pepper
• Fresh shiitake mushrooms
• Snow peas
• Scallions
• Ginger
• Orange juice
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Bring 2 cups water to boil in deep saucepan
2. Add barley
3. When liquid returns to boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer 30 to 40 minutes, or until barley is almost tender
4. Makes 2 cups cooked barley
5. Heat the oil in a medium, non-stick skillet over high heat
6. Stir-fry onion and red pepper 1 minute
7. Add mushrooms and stir-fry until they look moist, about 1 minute
8. Add snow peas, scallions, ginger, and stir-fry 15 seconds
9. Add cooked barley, orange juice, teriyaki sauce, and salt and pepper to taste, if desired
10. Cook until barley is heated through
11. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
