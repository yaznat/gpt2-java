What's a recipe for a burger?<|endoftext|>Adirondack Red Wing Burger

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Carrot
• Celery rib
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Ground turkey
• Fresh dill
• Poultry seasoning
• Salt and pepper
• Butter
• Hot pepper sauce
• Blue cheese
• Sandwich-size english muffins
• Cheddar cheese
• Iceberg lettuce
• Pickle relish

Steps:
1. In a small skillet, heat 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1 turn of the pan, over medium heat
2. Add the carrot, celery, onion and garlic and cook until tender, about 5 minutes
3. Transfer to a bowl and let cool
4. Reserve the skillet
5. Mix the turkey, dill and poultry seasoning into the vegetable mixture
6. Season with salt and pepper
7. Form into 4 patties
8. In the reserved skillet, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil over medium heat
9. Add the patties and cook, turning once, until firm, about 12 minutes
10. Transfer to a plate
11. Add the butter to the skillet to melt, then stir in the hot sauce
12. Add the patties to the sauce, turning to coat
13. Top each with some of the blue cheese and a cheddar slice
14. Tent with foil, turn off the heat and let stand until the cheese is melted
15. Top each muffin bottom with some lettuce, a patty, some relish and a muffin top<|endoftext|>
