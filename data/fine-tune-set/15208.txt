What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Best Pizza Dough

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Yeast
• Warm water
• Salt
• Oil

Steps:
1. Put flour in bowl and make a well in the center
2. Put yeast in the center, stir in water and sprinkle salt over dough
3. Knead till smooth
4. Coat dough with oil and place in a covered bowl
5. Let rise 1 hour<|endoftext|>
