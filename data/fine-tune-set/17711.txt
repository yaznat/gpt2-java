Ingredients:
• Water
• Apple juice concentrate
• Cooking apples
• Oat bran
• Whole wheat flour
• Unbleached white flour
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325f lightly oil muffin pan
2. Peel and core apples
3. Chop them coarsely
4. Set aside
5. In a mixing bowl, stir together pastry flour, white flour, oat bran, baking soda, cinnamon, and nutmeg
6. Add thawed apple juice concentrate, chopped apples, and enough water to make a light batter
7. Mix just enough to moisten all ingredients
8. Divide batter among the muffin cups and bake till lightly browned, 25-30 minutes
9. Remove muffins from cups while hot<|endoftext|>
