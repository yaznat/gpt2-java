What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Brown Rice

Ingredients:
• Slivered almonds
• Light margarine
• Onion
• Celery
• Poultry seasoning
• Dried thyme
• Pepper
• Brown rice

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, over med-high heat, cook almonds until browned
2. Add onion, celery, poultry seasoning, thyme & pepper
3. Cook till veg
4. Is tender crisp
5. Stir in cooked rice and cook 2 more minutes to combine flavours
6. Serve
7. Optional: preheat oven to 375f and put rice mix into a baking dish, cover and bake for about 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
