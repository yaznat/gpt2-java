What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Arroz Central Cafe Central Cafe Rice

Ingredients:
• Basmati rice
• White onion
• Jalapeno pepper
• Garlic
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Chicken stock

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Melt the butter in a medium saute pan, add olive oil
3. When oil is warm, add the rice, onion, pepper, and garlic
4. Saute for 2 to 3 minutes or until rice browns slightly
5. Pour in chicken stock, stir and pour into a 1 1 / 2 quart baking dish that has been sprayed with non-stick cooking spray
6. Cover and bake for 25 to 30 minutes or until water is absorbed and rice is tender<|endoftext|>
