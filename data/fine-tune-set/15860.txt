Please give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Burritos El Grande

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Green pepper
• Refried beans
• Cheese
• Sour cream
• Salsa
• Minute rice
• Flour tortillas
• Lettuce

Steps:
1. Brown meat
2. Drain
3. Add salsa and green pepper
4. Mix
5. Bring to boil
6. Stir in rice
7. Cover
8. Reduce to low and simmer 5 minutes
9. Meanwhile, heat beans up to warm
10. Spread beans down center of tortillas
11. Top evenly with meat mixture, cheese and lettuce
12. Serve with sour cream<|endoftext|>
