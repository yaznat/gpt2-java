Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Almost Fat Free Vegetarian Butternut Squash Soup

Ingredients:
• Vegetable broth
• Butternut squash
• Zucchini
• Red lentil
• Red bell pepper
• Curry powder
• Nutmeg
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Remove seeds, peel, and cut the squash into small 1 inches cubes
2. Cut the zucchini and bell pepper into small chunks
3. Put all ingredients except spices into a 2 quart saucepan, and bring to a boil
4. Simmer, covered, on low heat for 30 minutes
5. Do not drain the liquid
6. Pour the soup into a blender, add spices, and blend until smooth<|endoftext|>
