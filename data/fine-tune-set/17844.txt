What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Better Than Crack Brownies

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Water
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Salted peanuts
• Reese's peanut butter cups
• Milk chocolate chips
• Peanut butter
• Butter
• Puffed rice cereal

Steps:
1. Combine brownie mix, water, oil, and eggs until moistened
2. Bake for 20-25 minutes in a 9x13 baking dish
3. Remove brownies from oven, top with peanuts and peanut butter cups
4. Bake for 4-6 minutes more
5. Melt chocolate chips, peanut butter, and butter
6. Stir in cereal
7. Remove brownies from oven and pour chocolate-peanut butter mixture over hot brownies
8. Refrigerate for two hours, then serve<|endoftext|>
