Give me a beef recipe.<|endoftext|>Badazz Barbecue Beef

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Celery
• Malt vinegar
• Mustard powder
• Brown sugar
• Paprika
• Chili powder
• Seasoning salt
• Pepper
• Chili sauce
• Olive oil
• Reduced-sodium worcestershire sauce
• Tabasco sauce
• Barbecue sauce
• Liquid smoke
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic
• No-salt-added tomato sauce

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, saut the beef, onion, celery, green pepper, and garlic in olive oil until the beef is completely done
2. In the meantime, make a seasoning mixture by combining the worcestershire sauce, tabasco sauce, brown sugar, seasoning salt, pepper, vinegar, mustard, paprika, and chili powder in a measuring cup and mixing well
3. When the meat is done, add the seasoning mixture
4. Stir in well and cook for 20 minutes
5. Add the tomato sauce, badazz bbq sauce, liquid smoke, and chili sauce or ketchup
6. Bring to a boil, remove from heat, and transfer to a crockpot
7. Cover and simmer on low setting for several hours<|endoftext|>
