Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Cake flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Bananas
• Peanuts
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. In a medium mixing bowl sift together the first five ingredients, set aside
2. Cream together the 10 ounces of butter with 2 cups of sugar in another medium mixing bowl
3. Lightly beat eggs and blend into creamed mixture, set aside
4. In a large mixing bowl mash 8 bananas and add coarsely chopped peanuts
5. Alternately add sifted dry ingredients and creamed mixture to bananas and peanuts
6. Combine well and pour into greased 9 x 12 inch baking pan
7. Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the middle comes out clean
8. In a small mixing bowl, combine 1 / 2 cup sugar with 1 teaspoons cinnamon
9. When cake comes out of oven and still warm, sprinkle sugar and cinnamon mixture on top<|endoftext|>
