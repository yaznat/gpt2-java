Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Ba7 7Ari Meat With Rice Bahrain

Ingredients:
• Stewing lamb
• Mixed spice
• Cooking oil
• Onion
• Garlic
• Tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Water
• Skewers
• Salt
• Basmati rice

Steps:
1. Rub meat with spices
2. Fry in cooking oil until browned on all sides
3. Remove to plate
4. Fry onions and garlic in same pan until very soft, adding tomatoes part way through so they get cooked as well
5. Add meat to a large pot along with tomato mixture, add in tomato paste, mix thoroughly
6. Add water to cover, pierced dried limes and salt to taste
7. Bring to the boil
8. Simmer covered on low until meat is tender
9. Remove liquid so that the amount of liquid left in the pot is what you would usually cook rice inches
10. Add soaked white basmati rice
11. Simmer covered until the rice is done<|endoftext|>
