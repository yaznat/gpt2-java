What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>All Bran Original Jumbo Raisin Spice Cookies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Ground cloves
• Salt
• All-bran cereal
• Raisins
• Shortening
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Molasses
• Granulated sugar

Steps:
1. ()
2. Stir together flour, soda, cinnamon, ginger, cloves and salt
3. Mix in cereal and raisins
4. Set aside
5. Cream together shortening and brown sugar in large mixer bowl
6. Beat in eggs and molasses
7. Stir in dry ingredients mixing until well combined
8. Shape dough into 4 cm balls
9. Roll in granulated sugar if desired
10. Place on lightly greased cookie sheets and flatten with a fork, allowing 6 cookies per sheet
11. Bake at 190c 7 - 8 minutes or until lightly browned
12. Cool on baking sheets 2 - 3 minutes, then remove to racks to cool completely
13. Store in airtight container<|endoftext|>
