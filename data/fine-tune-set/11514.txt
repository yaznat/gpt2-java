Give me a bread recipe.<|endoftext|>Angela S Garlic Bread

Ingredients:
• Crusty italian bread
• Butter
• Parmesan cheese
• Oregano
• Garlic powder
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Split loaf in half length wise
2. Generously cover both sides of the bread with softened butter
3. Add garlic powder or fresh garlic to both sides of the bread
4. Slice tomato into paper thin slices, add to one side of the loaf, the bottom side
5. Add oregano
6. Add parmesan cheese
7. Close bread and wrap in foil
8. Place in a 350 degree oven for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
