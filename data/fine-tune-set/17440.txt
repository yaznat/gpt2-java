Ingredients:
• Butter
• White sugar
• Evaporated milk
• Irish cream
• Instant coffee crystals
• Marshmallow cream
• Vanilla
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. In a small bowl combine bailey's and coffee crystals
2. Microwave on high for 10 seconds
3. Or until melted and dissolved
4. In a medium saucepan, over medium heat, melt butter, evaporated milk, sugar and marshmallow cream
5. Slowly add the bailey's coffee mixture
6. Stir well
7. Boil for 5 minutes
8. Remove from heat and stir in chips and vanilla extract
9. Stir for approximately 3-4 minutes until smooth
10. Pour into a foil lined 8x8" pan
11. Chill to set<|endoftext|>
