Please give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Aunt Dianna S Blender Whole Grain Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Wheat berries
• Eggs
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Oil
• Honey
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Put milk and wheat kernels in blender
2. Blend on highest speed for 4 or 5 minutes or until batter is smooth
3. Add eggs, oil, baking powder, salt and honey or sugar to above batter
4. Blend on low
5. Pour out batter into pancakes from the actual blender jar onto a hot greased or pam prepared griddle or large frying pan
6. Cook -- flipping pancakes when bubbles pop and create holes
7. Serve with homemade jam or honey syrup
8. Food storage substitutions - choose one or more of these to try
9. 1 cup milk = 3 t
10. Powdered milk and 1c water
11. 2 eggs = 2 t
12. Powdered eggs 1 / 4c water
13. 1 cup wheat berries = use 1 cup of any whole grains<|endoftext|>
