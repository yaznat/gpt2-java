Give me a burger recipe.<|endoftext|>Bayou Burgers

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Green onion
• Garlic salt
• Dried basil leaves
• Dried thyme leaves
• Hot pepper sauce
• Onion rolls

Steps:
1. In medium bowl, combine all ingredients except rolls
2. Shape into 4 patties
3. Grill or broil to desired doneness
4. Serve on rolls and top, if desired, with grilled onions<|endoftext|>
