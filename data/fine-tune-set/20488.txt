Please give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Asian Green Bean Casserole

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Fresh ginger
• Cream cheese
• Roasted garlic
• Lemon
• Sour cream
• Soy sauce
• Toasted sesame oil
• Green beans
• Panko breadcrumbs
• Toasted sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f
2. Melt butter in small saucepan
3. Add chopped onion & ginger
4. Saute until softened
5. Remove from heat & add cream cheese through sesame oil
6. Combine thoroughly
7. In a large bowl, combine sauce with cooked green beans
8. Pour into a small casserole dish
9. Top with panko bread crumbs & sesame seeds
10. Bake until bubbly, about 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
