What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Blackened Tofu

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Butter
• Smoked paprika
• Onion powder
• Garlic powder
• Cayenne
• White pepper
• Whole black peppercorn
• Dried thyme
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Unbleached white flour
• Lemon wedge

Steps:
1. Drain the tofu and cut into 1 / 2" slices
2. Drain on wire cookie rack with two heavy pans on top for weight
3. The weight of the pans will help in removing the excess water from the tofu
4. Drain at least 30 minutes but up to 45 minutes
5. Melt the butter and set aside in shallow dish
6. In coffee mill or food processor, blend all the spices together to a fine powder
7. Place the spice mixture on a piece of wax paper
8. Stir in the flour with the spices
9. Heat some butter in a cast iron skillet on medium to medium-high heat, but do not burn
10. Dredge the tofu slices one at a time in the melted butter then in the spice mixture
11. Fry tofu slices on one side for five minutes, until blackened but not burnt
12. Turn over and fry another 5 minutes until blackened
13. Season with salt to taste
14. Serve lemon wedges on the side for drizzling
15. Makes an ideal appetizer if cut up into strips
16. Really tasty with tabasco sauce!
17. Red beans and rice would complement this perfectly!<|endoftext|>
