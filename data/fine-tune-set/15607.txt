Give me a taco recipe.<|endoftext|>Beef Chapatis African Version Of Tacos

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Water
• Salt
• Olive oil
• White onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red chilies
• Ground beef
• Salt and black pepper
• Lettuce
• Chopped tomato
• Cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Chapatis:
2. In a mixing bowl, use your hands to combine the flour, water, salt and 2 t of the olive oil
3. Mix until well combined and a sticky dough forms
4. Remove the dough from the bowl and place it on a floured surface
5. Knead the dough for about 3 to 5 minutes
6. Refrigerate for 20 minutes
7. Return the dough to the floured work surface and roll it into 4 six-inch circles / discs
8. Put the remaining 2 t of olive oil in a saute pan over medium heat and make sure to get really hot before adding the chapatis, or the chapatis will soak up the oil instead of browning properly
9. Put one or two chapatis into the hot pan and brown both sides of the chapatis
10. Remove from pan and keep warm and do the other two
11. I also like to lightly salt the cooked chapatis
12. Filling:
13. In a hot saute pan over medium heat, saute the onion and garlic in the olive oil until the onion gets translucent, about 2 minutes
14. Add the chiles and saute for 1 minute
15. Add the ground beef and continue to saute until the meat is completely cooked - approximately 10 minutes
16. Season the filling with salt and pepper and serve on a piece of chapati
