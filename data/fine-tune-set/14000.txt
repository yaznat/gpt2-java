What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Chicken Or Lamb Curry

Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Hard-boiled eggs
• Butter
• Onion
• Flour
• Chicken broth
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cayenne
• Curry powder

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat
2. Add the chopped onion
3. Add the flour
4. Stir until smooth and bubbly
5. Remove the saucepan from the heat
6. Add the chicken broth
7. Return saucepan to the burner and stir constantly until boiling and thickened
8. Add salt, pepper, cayenne, and 2 tsp curry powder to start, then adjust to taste
9. Add in your chicken and hard boiled eggs
10. Lower to a simmer and let the flavors meld for a few minutes
11. You can simmer on extra low for as long as you like, just stir is occasionally
12. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
