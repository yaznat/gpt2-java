What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Chinese Pork Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Instant rice
• Onion
• Peas and carrots
• Soy sauce
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. In a small pot bring the water to a boil
2. Stir in the rice
3. Cover with a lid and set aside
4. Cut the pork into tiny diced pieces
5. In a large skillet, place the vegetable oil over medium-high heat and get it hot
6. Place onions into skillet and fry until almost transparent
7. Add the peas and carrots, stirring until thawed and heated thoroughly
8. The rice should now be ready
9. Pour rice into skillet
10. Add the diced pork, soy sauce and sesame oil
11. In a small bowl, with a fork, beat the eggs well
12. Slowly pour the beaten eggs into the rice mixture, constantly stirring with a fork in a back and forth action, rather than a circular action
13. You do not want the eggs to solidify like scrambled eggs or fried eggs
14. You could omit the pork and have just chinese fried rice<|endoftext|>
