Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Salsa
• Lime juice
• Fresh cilantro
• Frozen corn
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Avocado

Steps:
1. Pour beans into a shallow 2- to 3 - quart casserole
2. Stir in 1 / 2 cup salsa, 1 tablespoon of the lime juice, and the cilantro
3. Top bean mixture with corn, then sprinkle with cheese
4. Bake in a 400 d f oven until mixture is hot throughout and cheese is bubbling, about 15 minutes
5. Meanwhile, stack tortillas and wrap in foil
6. Place in the oven until tortillas are warm, about 5 minutes
7. While bean mixture and tortillas heat, pit and peel avocado
8. Cut into thin wedges and arrange on a small serving plate
9. Sprinkle avocado with remaining lime juice
10. To assemble tacos, spoon bean and corn mixture into warm tortillas
11. Add avocado wedges and additional salsa to taste<|endoftext|>
