What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>1 Potato 2 Potato

Ingredients:
• Mashed potatoes
• Sour cream
• Cheddar cheese
• Garlic cloves
• Salt and black pepper
• Frozen potato wedges
• Bacon
• Green onions
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees fahrenheit
2. Spray a quiche dish with nonstick cooking spray
3. Mix mashed potatoes, sour cream, cheddar cheese, and garlic in a bowl
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Stir to blend
6. Spread mashed potato mixture evenly into the quiche dish
7. Arrange the frozen potato wedges in a spoke wheel fashion on top of the mashed potatoes
8. For curly fries, make a random pattern, breaking in half any large fries
9. Sprinkle with bacon bits, green onion and parmesan cheese
10. Bake for 25-30 minutes
11. Cut into wedges and serve<|endoftext|>
