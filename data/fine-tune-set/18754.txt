What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Banana Rice Pudding Served With A Raspberry Coulis

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Vanilla pod
• Pudding rice
• Caster sugar
• Cinnamon
• Creme fraiche
• Honey
• Banana
• Brown sugar
• Raspberries
• Icing sugar

Steps:
1. Place milk into saucepan with vanilla pod, bring to the boil
2. Pour in rice and sugar and stir
3. Add a pinch of cinnamon
4. Grease an ovenproof dish and pour in rice pudding then grease a small circle of greaseproof paper
5. Put in a preheated oven 160c for 2 hours
6. Raspberry sauce: place raspberries in a food processor with the icing sugar and blend to a smooth pouring consistency
7. Strain the sauce to remove the seeds and put aside
8. Take the pudding out of the oven and mix in the creme fraiche
9. Mash the banana and mix with the pudding
10. Add honey and crushed nuts and mix
11. Then pack pudding into a ramekin dish, sprinkle the top with brown sugar and place under the grill
12. Place ramekin on to a plate and decorate with the sauce
13. Garnish with fresh sprigs of mint<|endoftext|>
