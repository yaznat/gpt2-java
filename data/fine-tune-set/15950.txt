What's a recipe for a burrito?<|endoftext|>Easy Simple Black Bean Burritos

Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Cheese
• Red bell pepper
• Onion
• Seasoning salt
• Red chili pepper flakes
• Garlic salt
• Tortillas

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients into a medium frying pan
2. Cook on high heat until bean liquid is almost completely reduced
3. Remove from heat, add cheese, wrap in tortillas and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
