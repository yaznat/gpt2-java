What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Basic Waffle

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Sift dry ingredients
2. Beat egg whites until stiff
3. Add egg yolks, milk and oil to the dry ingredients
4. Mix thoroughly
5. Gently fold egg whites into batter
6. Pour into preheated waffle iron and bake<|endoftext|>
