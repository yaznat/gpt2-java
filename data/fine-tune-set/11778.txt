Ingredients:
• Coconut milk
• Red curry paste
• Medium shrimp
• Water
• Bamboo shoots
• Sugar
• Paprika
• Thai fish sauce
• Salt
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Sweet basil
• Steamed rice

Steps:
1. In a large pot, bring half the coconut milk to a slow boil, stirring constantly
2. Add the curry paste and shrimp, stir well, and cook until done, about 5 minutes
3. Add the remaining coconut milk, water, bamboo shoots, sugar, paprika and fish sauce, and bring to a boil slowly
4. Add salt
5. Add bell peppers and basil leaves, and remove from heat
6. Serve immediately over steamed rice<|endoftext|>
