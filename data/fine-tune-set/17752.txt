Ingredients:
• All-bran cereal
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Egg
• Dates
• Light brown sugar
• Margarine
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f degrees
2. Spray muffin tins with nonstick cooking spray
3. In a medium bowl, combine bran cereal and applesauce, let stand 5 minutes
4. Stir in egg, dates, brown sugar and margarine
5. In a large bowl, blend flour, baking soda, cinnamon and salt
6. Stir cereal mixture into dry ingredients until just moistened
7. Spoon batter into muffin cups
8. Bake for 25 minutes or until set and golden
9. Cool on rack
10. This can also be made into a loaf, bake for 45 min to 1 hour<|endoftext|>
