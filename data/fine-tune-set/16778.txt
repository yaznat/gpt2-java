What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Frozen Cookies N Cream Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Ice cube
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Oreo cookies
• Candy sprinkles
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. In blender, process ice cream, ice cubes, milk and vanilla extract until thick and smooth
2. Stir in oreos and sprinkles
3. Pour into 4 milkshake glasses
4. If desired, garnish with whipped cream and additional sprinkles<|endoftext|>
