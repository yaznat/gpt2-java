Ingredients:
• Butter
• Granulated sugar
• Light brown sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla extract
• Almond extract
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Flour
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Sweetened coconut

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Cream the butter and sugar together
3. Add the egg, vanilla, and almond
4. Blend well
5. In another bowl, mix the flour, salt, and baking soda
6. Add dry ingredients to the wet ingredients
7. Mix well
8. Stir in the chocolate chips
9. Spoon the dough onto a greased cookie sheet
10. Bake for 8-10 minutes or until golden<|endoftext|>
