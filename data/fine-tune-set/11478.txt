Give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>Amazing Pineapple Bread

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Sugar
• Egg substitute
• Crushed pineapple
• Flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Mix together butter and sugar
2. Add egg substitute and pineapple, mix well
3. Add in flours, soda, and salt, blend together
4. Add vanilla
5. Pour into greased & floured loaf pan
6. Bake at 350 degrees for 50-60 minutes<|endoftext|>
