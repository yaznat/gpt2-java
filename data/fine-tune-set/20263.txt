Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Bean sprouts
• Vegetable oil
• Gingerroot
• Garlic cloves
• Scallion
• Carrot
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Honey

Steps:
1. In a wok or large heat 1 1 / 2 tablespoons oil over high heat
2. Add sprouts and cook, stirring quickly and frequently, until lightly browned and tender, 1 to 2 minutes
3. Transfer sprouts to plate and keep warm
4. To same wok add remaining oil and heat for 30 seconds to 1 minute
5. Add chicken, garlic, carrots and ginger and stir-fry for 2 minutes
6. Add scallions and continue stir-frying until chicken is tender and browned on all sides, 2 to 3 minutes longer
7. Add soy sauce and honey and mix well
8. Cook, stirring constantly for 1 minute longer
9. Top warm sprouts with chicken mixture<|endoftext|>
