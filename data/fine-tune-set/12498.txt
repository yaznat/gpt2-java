Ingredients:
• Leg of lamb
• Carrots
• Yellow zucchini
• Chickpeas
• Onion
• Salt
• Ras el hanout spice mix
• Curry powder
• Cumin
• Ground coriander
• Water
• Oil

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a large pot, e
2. G
3. One for soup
4. Add the onion and the meat and let it take some colour
5. Add enough water or broth to cover the meat, usually about 1 liter
6. Stir in the spices
7. Add the chickpeas right now, or they will never become soft!
8. Let it simmer on low for about 1 hour
9. After 1 hour, add the vegetables, make sure everything is slightly covered with water / broth
10. Let simmer for another hour
11. After these two hours, vegetables should be soft, and you should be able to tear apart the meat with a spoon, as this is how it is eaten!
12. Check for seasoning and serve with steamed couscous!<|endoftext|>
