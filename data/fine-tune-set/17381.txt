Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Milk
• Rhubarb
• Sliced almonds
• Sugar
• Butter

Steps:
1. Beat brown sugar, oil, egg and vanilla until smooth
2. Combine flour salt and baking soda
3. Add to sugar mixture alternately with milk
4. Beat until smooth
5. Stir in rhubarb and almonds
6. Pour into 2 greased 9-inch round cake pans
7. For topping, combine sugar and butter
8. Stir in almonds
9. Sprinkle over batter
10. Bake at 350 degrees f for 30-35 minutes or test done<|endoftext|>
