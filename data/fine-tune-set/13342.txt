Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic
• Turmeric
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Tomatoes
• Chicken stock
• Spinach
• Yogurt
• Lemon juice
• Pine nuts

Steps:
1. Flour the chicken breasts
2. Sear in olive oil
3. Add onion
4. Saute 2 minutes
5. Add garlic
6. Add turmeric, nutmeg, and cinnamon
7. Cook 1 minute
8. Do not burn
9. Add tomatoes and chicken stock
10. Bake in oven at 350 degrees fahrenheit covered for 1 hour until meat is tender
11. Remove dish from oven
12. Add spinach
13. Stir until wilted
14. While spinach is wilting, place pine nuts on cookie sheet
15. Roast for 3 minutes in oven
16. Do not burn
17. Cool slightly
18. Add yogurt and lemon juice
19. Add toasted pine nuts to top<|endoftext|>
