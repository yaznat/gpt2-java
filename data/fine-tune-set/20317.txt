Give me a recipe for stir fry.<|endoftext|>Chinese Chicken Oven Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breast
• Boneless chicken thighs
• Carrot
• Celery
• Red bell pepper
• Water chestnuts
• Baby sweet corn cobs
• Brown sugar
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Water
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Pepper
• Scallions
• Cornstarch
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Spray a 9 x 13 pan and add chicken and veggies of your choice
2. Except for scallions and corn starch mix together sauce ingredients adjusting the amount of sugar and spices to suit your preferences and pour over chicken and veggies
3. Toss to coat
4. Preheat oven to 400 and bake uncovered for about 45 minutes or until chicken and veggies are done
5. Stir once at about 30 minutes and check for doneness
6. About 10 miutes before it is done mix water and corn starch and sesame oil and mix into chicken and continue baking to thicken sauce<|endoftext|>
