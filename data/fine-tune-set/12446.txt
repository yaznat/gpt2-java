Please give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>Apple Fennel Soup

Ingredients:
• Low sodium chicken broth
• Water
• White wine
• Golden delicious apples
• Carrot
• Onion
• Fennel
• Bay leaf
• Dried thyme leaves
• Peppercorns
• Plain nonfat yogurt

Steps:
1. In a large pot, combine broth, water, wine, apples, carrots, onion, fennel, bay leaf, thyme, and peppercorns
2. Bring to boil
3. Reduce heat and simmer, covered, 20 minutes
4. Strain soup, reserving liquid
5. Remove bay leaf from apple-vegetable mixture in strainer
6. In blender or food processor, puree mixture
7. Add reserved liquid and blend well
8. Reheat soup, if necessary
9. Ladle into soup bowls and serve with a dollop of yogurt if desired<|endoftext|>
