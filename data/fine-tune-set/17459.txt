Ingredients:
• Flour
• Banana
• White sugar
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Clove
• Cooking oil
• Egg
• Milk
• Bananas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Grease and flour 9x13 pan
3. Mix all of the dry ingredients together
4. Stir in oil with a fork until mixture is crumbly
5. Remove 1 cup of the crumbs and set aside to use for a topping
6. Stir egg and milk-soda mixture into dry ingredients
7. Beat until smooth
8. Pour into prepared pan
9. Slice banana's into halves then slice halves lengthwise into about 4 slices per half
10. Place on top of batter in pan, sprinkle bananas with brown sugar and then top with reserved crumbs
11. Bake for 40-45 minute<|endoftext|>
