Ingredients:
• Boneless beef round steak
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Lime juice
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Cayenne pepper
• Taco shells
• Fat-free refried beans
• Lettuce
• Reduced-fat mexican cheese blend
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Sprinkle steak with salt and pepper
2. Place 3 tablespoons of the lime juice, garlic, jalapeno, onion, chili powder, cumin and cayenne pepper in a slow cooker
3. Place beef on top and cook on high for 6 hours or low for 8 hours
4. Remove beef to a cutting board and when cool enough to handle, shred and place in a large bowl
5. Strain remaining liquid and add the strained liquid and remaining 1 tablespoon of lime juice to beef and stir to combine
6. Warm shells in oven according to directions
7. While tostadas are baking, heat refried beans in microwave for 1 1 / 2 minutes on high or until warm
8. Top shells with beans, meat, lettuce, cheese, and salsa and enjoy<|endoftext|>
