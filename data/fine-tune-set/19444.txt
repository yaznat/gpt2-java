Please give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>10 Minute Low Fat Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Plain fat-free yogurt
• Salad dressing
• Dijon mustard
• Spicy mustard
• Pickle
• Green olives

Steps:
1. Wash, dry, and stab potatoes
2. Place on a microwave safe plate and nuke for 6 min 40 sec
3. Meanwhile, stir the yogurt and mayo together in your serving bowl, add the mustards and set aside
4. Dice the pickle, add it and the olives to the dressing mix
5. When the potatoes are done, slit each into quarters, and rinse in a strainer under cold water until cool enough to handle
6. Chop them into bite sized pieces, and rinse again until they are cooled completely
7. Toss the potatoes in the dressing and -tada- you have fresh potato salad in under 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
