Can you give me a stew recipe?<|endoftext|>Barley Stew With Saffron And Chickpeas

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Yellow bell pepper
• Garlic clove
• Olive oil
• Smoked paprika
• Saffron
• Pearl barley
• Chickpeas
• Chicken broth
• Cherry tomatoes
• Frozen peas
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, soften the onion, bell pepper, and garlic in the oil with the paprika and saffron
2. Season with salt and pepper
3. Add the barley, chickpeas, and broth and bring to a boil
4. Cover and simmer gently, stirring frequently, for about 50 minutes or until the broth has been absorbed and the barley is tender
5. Add the tomatoes and peas
6. Continue cooking, stirring frequently, for 3 to 4 minutes
7. Adjust the seasoning<|endoftext|>
