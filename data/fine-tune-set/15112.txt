Please give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Armenian Pizza Lahmajoun

Ingredients:
• Ground lamb
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Parsley
• Minced garlic clove
• Crushed tomatoes
• Cumin
• Salt
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Black pepper
• Mint
• Tortillas

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients except tortillas together in a large bowl
2. Adjust seasonings to taste
3. Spread meat mixture thinly on each tortilla
4. Bake on large cookie sheets at 375 degrees for 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
