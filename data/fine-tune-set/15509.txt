Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Ground beef
• Hamburger buns
• American cheese
• Vidalia onion
• Salt & pepper
• Barbecue sauce
• Seasoning

Steps:
1. In a frying pan, start cooking the bacon
2. While the bacon is frying, cut up the onion and set aside
3. Finish cooking the bacon and remove and put on paper towels
4. Butter the buns and set aside
5. Drain the skillet that has the bacon grease into a bowl and set aside
6. Make 5 patties out of the ground beef and place in the skillet
7. Fry on medium heat until cooked thoroughly
8. In the meantime in another skillet, lightly toast the buns
9. Set aside
10. By this time the burgers should be of the way done, add cheese to the top of the burgers to melt
11. Put the burgers on a paper towel whenever finished
12. In the meantime, pour a little bacon grease in the same skillet used for the buns and add the onions and burger seasoning and saute for up to 3 minutes
13. Add the bbq sauce directly to the onions and heat for about 1 minute
14. Dress your burgers, add the patty and cross the bacon on top then add the bbq onion mixture
15. Serve right away<|endoftext|>
