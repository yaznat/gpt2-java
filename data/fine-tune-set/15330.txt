Give me a burger recipe.<|endoftext|>Almond Burgers

Ingredients:
• Ground almonds
• Tahini
• Onion
• Carrot
• Tamari
• Lemon, juice of

Steps:
1. Mix together the almonds, tahini, onion and carrot in a bowl
2. Stir in half the tamari and half of the lemon juice
3. If mixture seems dry, add up to the remaining half of each ingredient until the mixture holds together in patty form, but is not sloppy
4. Form 6 patties and fry in a little oil or broil until golden brown and a bit crispy
5. Serve on a roll or in a pita pocket with your favorite condiments<|endoftext|>
