Ingredients:

• Water
• Margarine
• Honey
• Salt
• Millet
• Amaranth
• Quinoa
• Ground flax seeds
• Sunflower seeds
• Gluten
• Whole wheat flour
• Yeast

Steps:
1. Load ingredients in order listed into bread machine
2. Select and run "whole wheat dough" setting
3. Remove dough, work out air bubbles, and shape into two loaf pans
4. Let rise approximately 45 minutes
5. Bake in preheated 350f oven for 35 minutes, or until loaves sound hollow when tapped on the underside
6. Remove from pans and cool on wire rack for at least 10 minutes before slicing
7. Enjoy one of life's simplest and healthiest delights--homemade bread!<|endoftext|>
