What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Breakfast Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Monterey jack cheese
• Canadian bacon
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Preheat the broiler
2. Spray a baking sheet with non-stick cooking spray
3. Place the tortillas on the baking sheet and broil until blistered and softened, about 15 seconds on each side
4. Sprinkle the cheese over 1 tortilla, top with bacon and salsa
5. Top with the remaining tortilla, spray with non-stick cooking spray
6. Place on the baking sheet and broil until the cheese melts, about 1 minute on each side
7. Cut into wedges to serve<|endoftext|>
