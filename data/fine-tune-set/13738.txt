Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil cooking spray
• Pork loin chops
• Fine dry breadcrumbs
• Granny smith apple
• Dried cranberries
• Extra-light vegetable oil spread
• Orange juice
• Water
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Sage
• Dried rosemary

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 deg
2. Spray a 9x13 inch baking pan with vegetable oil cooking spray
3. Set aside
4. Trim all visible fat from pork chops
5. Cut chops in half
6. Cut a pocket in each pork chop half for stuffing
7. Combine next 9 ingredients in a medium mixing bowl
8. Stuff approximately 1 / 3 cup of mixture into each pork chop half
9. Place stuffed chops in prepared pan
10. Sprinkle with rosemary
11. Bake uncovered for 40-50 minutes, or until cooked throughly<|endoftext|>
