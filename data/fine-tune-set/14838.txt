What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Boneless beef top sirloin steak
• Stir fry vegetables
• Reduced-sodium fat-free chicken broth
• Catalina dressing
• Light soy sauce
• Instant brown rice
• Mandarin orange segments
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Brown steak in nonstick wok or large skillet sprayed with cooking spray on high heat 3 minute
2. Add stir-fry vegetables, broth, dressing, soy sauce and rice
3. Stir
4. Bring to boil
5. Cover
6. Simmer 5 minute remove from heat
7. Let stand 5 minute add oranges and onions
8. Fluff with fork<|endoftext|>
