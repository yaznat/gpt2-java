Ingredients:
• Medium shrimp
• Milk
• Panko breadcrumbs
• Cornstarch
• Cabbage
• Scallion
• Shrimp sauce

Steps:
1. Soak shrimp in bowl with milk
2. In a bowl put panko bread crumbs and another bowl put corn starch
3. Dredge the shrimp in the corn starch, then again in the milk and finally in the panko bread crumbs & set aside
4. Heat a large skillet to 300 degrees f
5. Drop shrimp in and fry until golden brown
6. Remove from oil
7. Drain fried shrimp on paper towels
8. In a large bowl combine fried shrimp with sauce until evenly coated
9. Pile on a bed of shredded cabbage and garnish with scallions<|endoftext|>
