What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Bob Evans Waffles Or Pancakes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat waffle iron if making them
2. Whisk flour, sugar and baking powder in large bowl
3. In medium bowl, whisk eggs and milk in until well blended
4. Pour liquid ingredients over dry ingredients
5. Whisk until just combined
6. Stir in butter until blended<|endoftext|>
