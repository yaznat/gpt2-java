What's a good omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Plantain Omelette Vegan

Ingredients:
• Plantains
• Spelt flour
• Baking powder
• Coconut oil
• Cashews
• Lemon juice
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Shoyu
• Cilantro
• Green onion
• Carrot

Steps:
1. Cut the ends off the plantains and score the sides
2. Place the scored plantains in a 3 quart sauce pan, cover with water, and boil for 20 minutes until you can easily poke a fork through them
3. While waiting on bananas, combine cashews, lemon, shoyu, garlic and oil in a food processor and process until smooth, set aside
4. Drain the water from the bananas and let cool
5. Remove peels which should come off easily and place plantains in a large mixing bowl
6. Mash with a fork or masher
7. Add flour and baking powder to plantains and knead into a dough
8. Separate dough into 4 pieces
9. Roll into balls
10. Flour the counter and roll the balls into 5 inch circles
11. Heat a skillet over medium heat until hot
12. Pour a small amount of coconut oil into skillet and fry cake on one side until golden, approximately 3 minutes
13. Flip and cover of cooked side with nut cheese, green onions, carrots and cilantro
14. Once underside is done fold over like an omelet and serve hot<|endoftext|>
