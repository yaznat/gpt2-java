Give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>Anytime Hamburger Soup

Ingredients:
• Hamburger
• Whole tomatoes
• Mixed vegetables
• Tomato soup
• Onion soup
• Beef-flavor ramen noodles

Steps:
1. To the cooked and drained hamburger, add the above ingredients
2. Then fill each can you emptied with water and add to the mix
3. Add the ramen beef packets
4. Stir well and cook for 30-40 minutes
5. We sometimes would add parmesan cheese to our individual servings along with the crackers or no crackers -- makes alot!<|endoftext|>
