What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Cheddar Apple Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Granny smith apple
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Sharp cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat a large nonstick skillet over med-high heat
2. Fan out the apple slices over the bottom half of each tortilla
3. Top with the cheddar cheese
4. Fold the tortillas in half
5. Working in batches, place the quesadillas in the skillet and weigh them down with a smaller heavy skillet or an ovenproof plate topped with a heavy can
6. Cook until cheese is melted and tortillas are golden brown, about 1 1 / 2 minutes each side
7. Cut in half and eat warm<|endoftext|>
