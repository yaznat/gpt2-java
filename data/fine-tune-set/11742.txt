Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Almond extract
• Flour
• Sour cream
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Dried apricot
• Sliced almonds
• Toffee pieces
• Icing sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease and flour 9-inch round cake pan
3. Combine sugar, butter, eggs and almond extract in large bowl
4. Beat at medium speed until well mixed
5. Add flour, sour cream, baking powder & soda and salt
6. Continue beating until well mixed
7. Spread half the batter into prepared pan and sprinkle all the chopped apricots over batter
8. Sprinkle 2 tablespoons toasted almonds and 2 tablespoons toffee bits over apricots
9. Spread remaining batter over filling then sprinkle with remaining almonds and toffee bits
10. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes
11. Cool 10 minutes then remove from pan and cool completely
12. Stir together glaze ingredients in small bowl
13. Drizzle over cooled coffee cake<|endoftext|>
