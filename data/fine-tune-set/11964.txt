Ingredients:
• Broccoli
• Chicken breasts
• Onion
• Water chestnuts
• Mayonnaise
• Cream of chicken soup
• Curry powder
• Lemon juice
• Croutons
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Mix curry sauce - mayonnaise, cream of chicken soup, curry powder and lemon juice
2. Brown diced chicken breasts with chopped onions
3. Layer all ingredients in a casserole dish
4. Layer 1 - chicken and onions
5. Layer 2 - broccoli
6. Layer 3 - water chestnuts
7. Layer 4 - curry sauce
8. Bake in oven for 30 minutes at 350 degrees f with lid on
9. Add layer of grated cheese
10. Cover with croutons
11. Bake for additional 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
