Give me a burger recipe.<|endoftext|>Asian Flavoured Chicken Burgers Oamc

Ingredients:
• Ground chicken
• Egg
• Breadcrumbs
• Gingerroot
• Garlic clove
• Fresh coriander
• Scallions
• Red chile

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in a bowl and mix until well combined - your hands will do the best job of this
2. Form into patties
3. At this step either flash freeze by placing on a baking tray in the freezer until partially frozen and then packing into a plastic freezer bag to use at a later date or cook over medium high heat in an oiled frying pan until cooked through
4. When defrosting, defrost in the fridge overnight and then continue to cook as above<|endoftext|>
