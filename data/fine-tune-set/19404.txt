2. Microwave on medium-high for 30 to 40 seconds or until heated through
3. Stir in 1 tablespoon butter
4. Sift flour into a bowl
5. Stir in yeast, 1 / 4 cup sugar and 1 / 2 teaspoon cinnamon
6. Make a well in the centre
7. Add milk mixture
8. Mix to form a soft dough
9. Turn out onto a lightly floured surface
10. Knead for 10 minutes or until smooth and elastic

12. Cover with plastic wrap
