Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Faye S Awesome Wholesome Pancake Waffle Mix

Ingredients:
• Old fashioned oats
• Oat bran
• Soy flour
• Spelt flour
• Non-fat powdered milk
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda

Steps:
1. Mix together and store in tight container
2. To make pancakes
3. 1 1 / 4 c mix
4. 1 c water
5. 2 t
6. Canola oil
7. 1 egg
8. Mix together-- if you want it thicker add mix, if you want it thinner, add water
9. I like to add some cinnamon to taste
10. Spoon onto hot griddle and cook
11. To make waffles
12. 1 1 / 2 c mix
13. 1 c water
14. 2 t
15. Canola oil
16. 1 t
17. Butter
18. 1 egg
19. Separate egg and beat whites until thick
20. Mix remaining ingredients and fold in whites
21. Spoon into hot waffle maker
22. Batter will thicken as it sits<|endoftext|>
