Can you give me a soup recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazing French Onion Soup

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Vidalia onion
• Sugar
• Flour
• Provolone cheese
• Beef broth
• Crouton
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Saute onions in butter
2. Add sugar and flour to make a paste, then add broth stirring to mix well and simmer for 30 minutes
3. Place 4-6 croutons in bottom of broiler safe bowls / crocks
4. Pour broth mixture over and croutons will rise to the top
5. Add 2 slices of provolone cheese on top of each bowl / crock
6. Put on cookie sheet and broil for a minute or 2 till cheese starts to brown
7. Let sit for a few minutes<|endoftext|>
