What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>California Burrito

Ingredients:
• Meat
• Sour cream
• French fries
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Pico de gallo
• Flour tortilla

Steps:
1. Make sure meat, french fries and tortilla are fresh and warm
2. Fill tortilla with carne or pollo asada add fries, sour cream, pico and cheese
3. Serve immediately
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
