Ingredients:
• Ground cumin
• Dried oregano
• Fresh cilantro
• Cayenne pepper
• Olive oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Onion
• Minced garlic cloves
• Chicken broth
• Green chili
• White beans
• Jalapeno jack cheese

Steps:
1. Cube the chicken breast halves
2. Heat the olive oil and saute chicken until golden
3. Add the onions and garlic, saute until tender
4. Add the spices and cook for 30 seconds
5. Add beans, broth and chilies
6. Simmer for 15 minutes
7. Reduce heat and stir in cheese
8. Stir until melted and well blended
9. Season to taste
10. Serve with sour cream, crackers and / or bread<|endoftext|>
