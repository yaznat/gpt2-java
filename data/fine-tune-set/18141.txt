What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Apple Pancakes With Lemon Spiced Butter

Ingredients:
• Self raising flour
• Buttermilk
• Egg
• Butter
• Apple
• Mixed spice
• Fresh lemon rind
• Fresh strawberries

Steps:
1. For the pancakes
2. Sift the flour into a large bowl and make a well in the centre
3. Whisk the buttermilk and egg together in a jug
4. Gradually pour the buttermilk mixture into the flour whisking well to make a smooth batter
5. Grease a non-stick pan with some of the butter and heat until the butter is foaming
6. Gently pour 1 / 3 cup of batter into the pan and top batter with apple slices
7. Depending on the size of your pan you can cook two or three at a time
8. Cook pancakes for about 1 minute, bubbles will appear on the top and the bottom should be golden
9. Turn the pancake and cook for a further minute or two until the second side is golden
10. Repeat with remaining batter and apple slices, greasing pan before each batch
11. For the spiced lemon butter
12. Combine all the ingrediants in a small bowl and mix together well
13. To serve
14. Arrange 2 or 3 warm pancakes in a stack, top with the flavoured butter and fresh berries<|endoftext|>
