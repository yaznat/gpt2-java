What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almost Healthy And Surprisingly Good Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
• Coconut flour
• Oat flour
• Powdered milk
• Food-grade diatomaceous earth
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Stevia powder
• Butter
• Sea salt
• Eggs
• Yogurt
• Honey
• Vanilla
• Chia seeds
• Chocolate chips
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. If you don't have pre-made chia gel, soak 2 tablespoons chia seeds in 6 tablespoons water, to make 1 / 2 cup chia gel
2. This should be soaked several hours
3. Preheat oven to 350f
4. Combine the coconut flour, oatmeal flour, powdered milk and diatomaceous earth, cocoa powder and stevia in a bowl
5. Mix and set aside
6. Mix butter and salt in blender
7. Add eggs to butter, but don't worry if it doesn't mix perfectly
8. Add yogurt, honey, vanilla and chia seed and beat well
9. Mix in chocolate chips and walnuts
10. Place on cookie sheet and bake for 15-20 minutes<|endoftext|>
