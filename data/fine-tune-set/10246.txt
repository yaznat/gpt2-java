What's a recipe for cookies?<|endoftext|>Joe S Peppermint Cookies N Cream Brownies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Sugar
• Salt
• Vanilla extract
• Peppermint extract
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Peppermint oreos
• Candy cane

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Line a 913-inch baking pan with aluminum foil, and grease lightly
3. In a medium-large saucepan over low heat, melt together butter and chocolate, stirring occasionally
4. Stir in sugar, salt and vanilla & peppermint extracts
5. Remove from heat
6. Whisk in eggs one at a time, waiting until each is fully incorporated before adding the next
7. Stir in flour and cocoa powder until mixture is uniform
8. Stir in crushed cookies
9. Spread batter into prepared pan
10. If desired, crush 3-4 additional cookies finely and scatter on top of batter
11. You can also crush 1 candy-cane and sprinkle it on top
12. It will melt slightly but still be pretty and festive
13. Bake for about 35 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist crumbs, but not coated with batter
14. Cool brownies in pan on wire rack for 20 minutes, then lift brownies in the foil out of the pan and place on a wire rack to cool completely before slicing<|endoftext|>
