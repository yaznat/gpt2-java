Give me a cake recipe.<|endoftext|>Ace S Ruby Red Grapefruit And Orange Cake By Melissa Winner

Ingredients:
• Baker's joy
• Flour
• White sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Kosher salt
• Eggs
• Canola oil
• Banana
• Ruby red grapefruit juice
• Orange juice
• Sour cream
• Orange zest
• Grapefruit zest
• Grapefruit juice
• Confectioners' sugar
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Prepare bundt cake:
2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
3. Spray a large bundt pan or 10-inch tube pan with nonstick baking spray with flour like baker's joy, or use recipe #78579
4. Sift together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt
5. Then put mixture into a stand mixer add eggs, and mix until combined
6. Then add oil
7. Mix until blended
8. Next add grapefruit juice, orange juice, and banana, and mix until smooth and creamy
9. Add sour cream and zests
10. Just mix until combined and smooth
11. Put cake batter into the prepared pan and bake for about 35 to 45 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
12. When done turn pan onto serving platter let stand about 15 minutes then poke small holes in top
13. I use a straw
14. Be neat about it
15. Prepare glaze:
16. Mix juice and sugar until combined
17. If it is too thick add more juice a tablespoon at a time
18. Stir in zest and pecans
19. Pour glaze on partially cooled cake
20. Let cool about 1 hour and serve<|endoftext|>
