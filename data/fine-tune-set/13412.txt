What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>3 Day Barbecued Marinated Beef Brisket

Ingredients:
• Liquid smoke
• Worcestershire sauce
• Condensed chicken broth
• Garlic
• Barbecue sauce
• Beef brisket

Steps:
1. First day: combine liquid smoke, worcestershire sauce, beef concentrate, garlic and 1 cup barbecue sauce
2. Marinate brisket in this mixture in the refrigerator overnight, tightly covered
3. Second day: bake, uncovered, in a 300 degree oven for 4 hours
4. Cool, wrap tightly in foil, and refrigerate overnight
5. Third day: remove meat, discard foil, slice brisket across grain and pour remaining barbecue sauce over meat
6. Reheat at 350 degrees for 30 minutes
7. Serve with sauce<|endoftext|>
