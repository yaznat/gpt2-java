Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>All In One Tuna Casserole

Ingredients:
• Onion soup mix
• Milk
• Frozen peas and carrot
• Egg noodles
• Tuna
• Cheddar cheese
• Cream of mushroom soup

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. In large bowl, blend onion soup mix with cream of mushroom soup and milk
3. Stir in peas & carrots, cooked noodles, cheese and tuna
4. Turn into greased 2-quart baking dish
5. Bake 30 minutes or until bubbling<|endoftext|>
