Ingredients:
• Bow tie pasta
• Albacore tuna
• Red bell pepper
• Red onion
• Capers
• Fresh parsley
• Garlic clove
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Rice vinegar
• Olive oil
• Dijon mustard
• Lemon zest
• Lemon
• Sugar
• Salt
• Dried dill weed

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Rinse under cold water to halt cooking
3. Drain
4. Broil or pan fry fresh tuna
5. Set aside
6. Combine dressing ingredients in non-reactive bowl and blend well
7. Break up the fresh tuna into bite size pieces and place in serving bowl with the pasta
8. Toss dressing gently with the pasta, tuna and remaining salad ingredients
9. Keep covered and chilled until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
