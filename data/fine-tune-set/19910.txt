Ingredients:
• Butter
• Cooked bacon
• Eggs
• Light cream
• Blue cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Melt butter in frying pan over medium heat
2. Add bacon and heat until sizzling
3. Meanwhile, whisk eggs and cream together in a small bowl
4. Pour over bacon
5. Allow to cook until firm enough to flip
6. Cook until the underside is firm
7. Sprinkle 1 1 / 2 tbls
8. Of blue cheese over half the omelette and fold the other half of the omelette over the top
9. Sprinkle remaining blue cheese over the top and sprinkle with salt and pepper<|endoftext|>
