Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Cream of tartar
• Baking soda
• Vanilla
• Orange juice
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Pour boiling water over raisins and let sit while preparing rest of cake
2. Cream butter and sugar, beat in eggs
3. Sift flour, cream of tartar and soda
4. Add to creamed mixture alternately with orange juice and vanilla
5. Drain raisins
6. Add to batter
7. Pour into 9"x13" pan or two 9-inch layer pans which have been buttered and floured
8. Bake at 350 f for 45 minutes for large pan or 30 minutes for layer pans
9. Can be iced or served with sliced berries and whipped cream
10. Really good plain too!<|endoftext|>
