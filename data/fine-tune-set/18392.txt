What's a good waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Buttermilk Cornmeal Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Cornmeal
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Canola oil
• Egg whites
• Vanilla
• Wheat germ

Steps:
1. Combine the first 4 ingredients in a large mixing bowl
2. Set aside
3. Stir buttermilk and oil together add vanilla as well
4. Set aside
5. Blend egg whites in a dry mixing bowl until stiff peaks form
6. Set aside
7. Mix buttermilk mixture into flour mixture
8. Blend until smooth
9. Gradually fold in egg whites
10. Pour by cupfuls onto a hot, greased waffle iron
11. Heat according to waffle iron manufacturer's instructions<|endoftext|>
