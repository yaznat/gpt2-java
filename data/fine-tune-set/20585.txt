What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Baked Corn Like A Corn Casserole But No Cheese Or Sour Cream

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Creamed corn
• Sugar
• Club crackers
• Salt
• Milk
• Butter

Steps:
1. Beat the eggs, add corn, sugar, salt, milk and 1 / 2 of your crushed club crackers
2. Dump into a greased or sprayed small baking dish
3. I use the 9x9 glass ones
4. Cover with the remaining half of cracker crumbs and dot with butter
5. Cover with foil and bake at 350f for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
