What's a good casserole recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Cheese Grits Casserole

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Water
• Quick-cooking grits
• Salt
• Cheddar cheese
• Velveeta cheese
• Butter
• Eggs
• Garlic powder
• Black pepper
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Bring water to a boil in large saucepan
3. Add grits and salt and stir until mixture comes to a boil
4. Simmer uncovered for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Remove from heat and add cheeses, bacon, butter, garlic, black pepper, and hot sauce
6. Stir well
7. Stir in eggs and pour into an ungreased 1 1 / 2 quart casserole
8. Bake about 50 minutes
9. May refrigerate up to 24 hours before baking<|endoftext|>
