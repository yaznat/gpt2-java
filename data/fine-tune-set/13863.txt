What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Lamb Chops Sensory Explosion

Ingredients:
• Lamb chops
• Olive oil
• Fresh rosemary
• Fresh oregano
• Dill
• Salt
• Pepper
• Rye flour
• Fresh mozzarella cheese
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. In medium size bowl whisk together 1 tbsp olive oil, rosemary, oregano, pepper, salt
2. Rinse and pat dry lamb chops, place in bowl, turning to coat with seasoning mix
3. Let marinate at least 15 min and up until 6 hrs in fridge
4. Place chops in a casserole dish and bake in oven for 30 min at 350 degrees
5. You may need to check and add a small bit of water to pan so chops do not stick or burn on bottom
6. Check to see if all pink is cleared, place sliced tomatoe and sliced mozzerella on top of chop in order stated, use additional pepper to taste and bake for 5 more minutes
7. Remove from oven and let sit for 5 minutes so juices can set and meld into meat
8. Serve with a crusty loaf of bread and your favorite sides
9. You may also choose to add red pepper flakes for heat i used 1 / 2 tsp on 2 chops, sprinkled after in oven
10. Thickness of chop will effect cooking time as well so if thin check after 20 minutes
11. Ovens vary so please check to be sure at the 30 min time frame<|endoftext|>
