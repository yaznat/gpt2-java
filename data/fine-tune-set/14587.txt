Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Chicken Broccoli And Rice Dinner

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Vegetable oil
• Condensed cream of chicken soup
• Milk
• Minute rice
• Broccoli florets

Steps:
1. Brown chicken in oil in large frying pan on medium heat for 5 minutes on each side
2. Remove from pan
3. Add soup and milk to pan
4. Bring to boil
5. Stir in rice and broccoli
6. Top with chicken and cover
7. Cook on low heat for 5 minutes<|endoftext|>
