Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Auntie Zilla S Almond Meal Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Ricotta cheese
• Almond meal
• Cream
• Water
• Almond extract

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients
2. I personally never add sweetener, but aunt zilla adds a tablespoon of sweetener
3. Heat butter in a skillet and cook like regular pancakes
4. These do tend to crumble a bit more so be sure they are cooked all the way to the edge before flipping them
5. I personally like rather small pancakes and this makes about three dozen
6. I eat 3 or so in a breakfast serving
7. I really like them under asian food instead of rice, usually using 2
