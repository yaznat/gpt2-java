Ingredients:
• Butter
• Beef top round steak
• Carrot
• Butternut squash
• Dried peaches
• Beef broth
• Stewed tomatoes
• Whole kernel corn
• Dried oregano
• Salt
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Melt butter in 2 quart saucepan and add beef
2. Cook on medium-high heat 3-4 minutes until browned, stirring every now and then to prevent sticking
3. Add all remaining ingredients and bring to a boil (about 3-4 minutes
4. Reduce heat to medium cover and cook 14-16 minutes until veggies are tender
5. Stir in parsley and serve<|endoftext|>
