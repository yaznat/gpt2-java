What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Catalina Taco Salad

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Lettuce
• Catalina dressing
• Onion
• Cheddar cheese
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Brown the ground beef and drain off the grease and allow to room temperature
2. Make sure you drain the grease off well or it will sift to the bottom of your salad bowl
3. Chop the lettuce to bite sized pieces
4. In a large salad bowl place in the lettuce, cheese, ground beef and onion
5. Crush the tortilla chips lightly onto them and mix it up
6. Add enough catalina to cover your salad
7. I used about half of a bottle for this
8. You want a nice coating though
9. Serve<|endoftext|>
