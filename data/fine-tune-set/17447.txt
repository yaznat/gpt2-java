Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Light brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Unsalted butter
• Walnuts
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Ground nutmeg
• Salt
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Banana
• 2% low-fat milk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f coat a 9-inch-square baking pan with cooking spray
2. For the topping, combine flour, brown sugar, cinnamon and butter in a food processor and process until mixture resembles fine crumbs
3. Stir in walnuts
4. For the cake, combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, nutmeg and salt in a medium bowl
5. Beat butter with a mixer at medium speed about 30 seconds or until smooth
6. Gradually add sugar and beat 3 to 4 minutes or until fluffy
7. Add eggs 1 at a time, beating well after each
8. Add vanilla and bananas and beat until well blended
9. Add flour mixture alternately with milk, mixing after each addition only until smooth
10. Pour batter into prepared pan
11. Sprinkle with topping
12. Bake about 35 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
13. Cool in pan on a wire rack
14. Serve warm or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
