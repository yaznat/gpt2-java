What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Caesar Seafood Tacos

Ingredients:
• Crabmeat
• Caesar salad dressing
• Romaine lettuce
• Black olives
• Miniature taco shells
• Fresh parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, combine crabmeat and 1 / 4 cup of the salad dressing
2. Toss to coat
3. In another medium bowl, combine lettuce, sliced black olives, and remaining 1 / 4 cup salad dressing
4. Toss to coat
5. Layer crabmeat mixture and lettuce mixture in each taco shell
6. Top each with cheese<|endoftext|>
