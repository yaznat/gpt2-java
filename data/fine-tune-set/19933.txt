Can you give me a omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Chef Flower S Simple Sunday Brunch Omelette

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Dried parsley
• Dried mint
• Spanish onions
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Place the eggs in a pouring bowl beat using a whisk or electric blender, then add parsley & mint
2. Spray olive oil on the non stick pan
3. Place pan on the stove top, heat on a medium to low heat
4. Pour 1 / 4 mixture of eggs on pan
5. Add 1 / 4 of onion, bacon and cheese on half a side of the eggs
6. Cook until the egg is slightly brown
7. Flip half of the cooked eggs and press down lightly
8. Cook for about 2 minutes
9. Serve<|endoftext|>
