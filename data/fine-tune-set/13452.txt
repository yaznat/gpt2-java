What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Alpine Beef Goulash With Noodles

Ingredients:
• Boneless round steak
• Oil
• Onion
• Garlic
• Tomato sauce
• Brown sugar
• Paprika
• Salt
• Caraway seed
• Dill seed
• Worcestershire sauce
• Pepper
• Sour cream
• Noodles

Steps:
1. Brown beef in oil
2. Add onion and garlic, and saute until tender
3. Add tomato sauce and all seasonings except sour cream and noodles
4. Cover and cook slowly about 2 1 / 2 hours
5. Add sour cream just before serving
6. Serve over hot buttered noodles<|endoftext|>
