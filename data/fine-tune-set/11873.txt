What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Butternut Curry Soup

Ingredients:
• Water
• Butternut squash
• Onion
• Chickpeas
• Corn
• Carrots
• Parsnip
• Scallion
• Cabbage
• Red wine
• Garlic paste
• Cumin powder
• Coriander powder
• Ground sage
• Salt
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. In a large soup pot, bring the water to a boil
2. Add the butternut squash, onion, parsnip, 3 / 4 of the chopped carrots, 1 / 2 of the chopped scallions, garlic / paste, cooking wine, cumin, corianger, sage, and black pepper
3. Stir, return to a boil, and then simmer covered on medium heat for 30 minutes
4. While saving the liquid, remove most of the vegetables from the pot, and puree in a blender
5. Then return the vegetable puree and liquid to the pot
6. Add the chickpeas, cabbage, corn, and the remaining carrots and scallions
7. Adjust seasonings to taste and serve
8. This goes well with crusty bread and a spinach salad
9. Enjoy!
10. Suggested accompaniments: crusty bread and a spinach salad<|endoftext|>
