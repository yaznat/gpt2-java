Ingredients:
• Flour
• Egg
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Granulated sugar
• Oil
• Baking powder
• Beer

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients, except beer, in medium size bowl
2. Gradually add enough beer, starting with about a 1 / 2 a cup, to make batter consistency of thickened pancake batter
3. Mix til smooth
4. Dip fish, veggies in batter, gently place into moderately hot oil approximately, 375 degrees, fry until puffy and golden brown, about 3 minutes, each side
5. Remove and drain on paper towel<|endoftext|>
