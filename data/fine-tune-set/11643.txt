Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>5 Layer Peach Cobbler Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Peach pie filling
• Peaches in light syrup
• Brown sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Quick oats
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Vanilla extract
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Powdered sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Grease a 13x9-inch baking pan
3. Stir together all filling ingredients in medium bowl and set aside for now
4. Combine all topping ingredients in medium bowl until it resembles coarse crumbs
5. Set aside
6. Combine the sugar and 1 cup butter in a large mixer bowl
7. Beat at medium speed, scraping bowl often, until creamy
8. Add eggs, the sour cream and vanilla extract
9. Continue beating, scraping bowl often, until well mixed
10. Add all the remaining coffee cake ingredients
11. Continue beating and scraping the bowl often, until smooth
12. Spread half the batter into pan
13. Spread peach filling over batter
14. Drop spoonfuls of remaining batter over filling
15. Sprinkle with topping
16. Bake 60-70 minutes or until toothpick in center comes out clean
17. Cool 15 minutes
18. Stir together powdered sugar and enough milk in small bowl for glazing consistency
19. Drizzle over the cooled coffee cake<|endoftext|>
