Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Amazing Overnight Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Yeast
• Sugar
• Salt
• Milk
• Butter
• Egg

Steps:
1. Combine the flour, yeast, sugar and salt in a medium sized bowl
2. Add the milk and whisk until blended
3. Cover the bowl, tightly with plastic wrap
4. Place the bowl in the refrigerator over night
5. In the morning, preheat your waffle iron
6. Melt the butter and beat the egg in a seperate bowl then beat the mixture into the batter
7. The batter will be quite thin
8. Lightly spray the waffle iron with cooking spray
9. Add just enough batter to cover the cooking surface
10. Cook 2 to 3 minutes depending on your waffle iron
11. Don't over bake
12. It's okay to peek
13. Serve hot with your favorite toppings<|endoftext|>
