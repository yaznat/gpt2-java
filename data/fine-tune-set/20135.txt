Ingredients:
• Brown rice
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Gingerroot
• Sesame oil
• Dry crushed red pepper
• White pepper
• Safflower oil
• Fresh asparagus
• Scallions
• Sweet red pepper
• Garlic
• Unsalted dry-roasted cashews
• Mandarin orange section
• Toasted sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Cook or reheat brown rice according to package directions
2. In small bowl, combine soy sauce and cornstarch
3. Stir in remaining sauce ingredients
4. Set aside
5. Heat a wok or large heavy skillet over high temperature
6. When very hot, add the oil
7. Add the asparagus, scallions, pepper, and garlic and stirfy until vegetables are crisp-tender
8. Stir the sauce mixture again, then pour it over the vegetables and stir until it is thickened and bubbly
9. Reduce heat
10. Add cashews and stir
11. Cover and cook 1 minute, until cashews are heated through
12. Garnish with mandarin orange sections and / or toasted sesame seeds, and serve over cooked rice<|endoftext|>
