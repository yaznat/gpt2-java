What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Brownie Cupcakes With Peanut Butter Frosting

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Golden brown sugar
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Walnuts
• Salt
• Powdered sugar
• Creamy peanut butter
• Whipping cream
• Chocolate shavings

Steps:
1. For cupcakes
2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
3. Line standard muffin cups with paper liners combine butter, 1 / 2 cup chocolate chips, and unsweetened chocolate in the top of double broiler set over simmering water
4. Stir until mixture is melted and smooth, remove from water
5. Whisk both sugars into chocolate mixture, then whisk in eggs 1 at a time
6. Whisk in vanilla, then flour, walnuts, salt and remaining 3 / 4 cup chocolate chips
7. Divide batter among muffin cups
8. Bake cupcakes until tester inserted into center comes out with moist crumbs attached, about 20 minutes
9. Transfer cupcakes to rack and cool completely
10. Frosting
11. Put powdered sugar and the next 3 ingredients in a medium bowl
12. Using electric mixer, beat until mixture is smooth, adding whipping cream by teaspons if frosting is to thick to spread
13. Spread frosting in swirls over tops of cupcakes, sprinkle with chocolate shavings or sprinkles<|endoftext|>
