Ingredients:
• Beer
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Salt
• Dried oregano
• Dry mustard
• Cayenne pepper
• Fresh ground pepper
• Tilapia fillets
• Canola oil
• Whole wheat tortillas
• Green cabbage
• Pico de gallo

Steps:
1. Place all the beer batter ingredients in a blender
2. Blend until smooth, scraping down the sides as necessary
3. Transfer the batter to a shallow baking dish, add fish, turning to coat all sides
4. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
5. Transfer one-third of the battered fish to the pan, placing each piece into a little oil
6. Cook until golden brown, 3 to 4 minutes per side
7. Transfer cooked fish to a plate
8. Keep warm
9. Add 1 tablespoon oil and half the remaining fish to the pan
10. Cook as directed above, reducing the heat if necessary
11. Cook the remaining fish with the remaining 1 tablespoon oil
12. Serve immediately
13. To make tacos: fill tortillas with fish and cabbage then top with salsa
14. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
