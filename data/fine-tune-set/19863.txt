Ingredients:
• Canned sliced potatoes
• Garlic powder
• Margarine
• Sweet onion

Steps:
1. Open cans and remove water
2. Dump potatoes out on a paper towel and blot excessive moisture off
3. Slice the onion and then chop it the other direction
4. In a skillet on medium-high heat melt 1-tablespoon of margarine
5. Saute the onion until tender
6. Remove onion from skillet and set aside
7. Add another tablespoon of margarine and melt it
8. Spread melted margarine so it coats bottom of the skillet
9. Place potato slices on to the skillet so each piece is touching the surface
10. As potatoes brown, turn them over
11. (since i double the recipe, i remove the browned potatoes and add more
12. Uncooked ones to the skillet as they all don't fit in the pan at once
13. )
14. Add the rest of the margarine to the skillet
15. After all of the potatoes have reached a golden brown on both sides, add
16. Sauteed onions and fold into potatoes
17. Sprinkle garlic powder over cooking potatoes
18. Heat the onions & potatoes for another 20 minutes, flipping them every 5-minutes
19. Serve
20. Yum!<|endoftext|>
