Can you give me a stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Stir Fry With Vegetables And Peanuts

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Garlic
• Dried red pepper flakes
• Cornstarch
• Canola oil
• Green beans
• Carrots
• Snow peas
• Red bell pepper
• Fresh ginger
• Green onions
• Dry roasted salted peanut
• Oyster sauce

Steps:
1. In a bowl combine the chicken with 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 2 tablespoons honey, 1 tablespoon garlic and the red pepper flakes
2. In another small bowl mix / whisk together remaining 6 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoon honey and 2 teaspoons cornstarch
3. Set aside
4. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a wok or a large non stick skillet over high heat, add in the chicken
5. Stir-fry for 1 minute, then transfer to a dish
6. Add in another couple tablespoons oil if needed, and add in the green beans, red pepper strips, snow peas and minced ginger
7. Stir-fry until crisp-tender about 3 minutes
8. Return the chicken back to the wok or skillet
9. Add in the reserved soy sauce / cornstarch mixture
10. Stir until heated through and thickened
11. Season with salt and pepper then add in the oyster sauce if using
12. Mix to combine
13. Sprinkle with chopped green onions and salted peanuts<|endoftext|>
