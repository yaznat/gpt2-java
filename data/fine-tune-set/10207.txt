Ingredients:
• Nonstick cooking spray
• Garlic cloves
• Yellow onions
• Ground turkey
• Chopped tomatoes
• Yellow bell peppers
• Jalapeno peppers
• Dry red wine
• Fresh parsley
• Chili powder
• Sugar
• Dried oregano
• Celery seed
• Cumin
• Cayenne
• Salt
• Pepper
• Ground cloves
• Canned red kidney beans
• Low-fat sour cream
• Scallion

Steps:
1. In a heavy pot or dutch oven coated with nonstick spray, saut garlic 1 minute over medium-high heat
2. Add onion and saut 4 more minutes, stirring
3. Add turkey and cook until browned, about 5 minutes, stirring to break up as necessary
4. Stir in next 14 ingredients, bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer uncovered 30 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Stir in beans and cook 5 more minutes
6. Place in individual bowls, top each with 1 tbsp sour cream and sprinkle with 1 tbsp minced scallion and serve hot<|endoftext|>
