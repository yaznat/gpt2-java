Ingredients:
• Yukon gold potatoes
• Salt
• Shallots
• All-purpose flour
• 1% low-fat milk
• Asiago cheese
• Fresh chives
• Fresh ground pepper
• Bacon
• Fresh parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Put potatoes in a saucepan
2. Cover with water
3. Bring to a boil
4. Reduce heat and simmer 5 minutes or until almost tender
5. Drain
6. Sprinkle potatoes evenly with 1 / 4 teaspoon salt
7. Set aside and keep warm
8. Heat a saucepan coated with non-stick cooking spray over medium heat
9. Add in the shallots
10. Stir / saute for 2 minutes or until tender
11. Sprinkle flour over shallots
12. Gradually add 1 / 2 cup milk and stir with a whisk until well blended
13. Gradually add 1 1 / 2 cups milk and stir with a whisk
14. Cook over medium heat for about 9 minutes, stirring frequently until thick
15. Take pan from heat source and add in 3 / 4 teaspoon salt, asiago cheese, chives, pepper, and bacon
16. Stir to combine
17. Place half the potatoes in an 8-inch square baking dish that has been coated with cooking spray
18. Pour half the cheese sauce over the potatoes
19. Top with the remaining potatoes and cheese sauce
20. Sprinkle parmesan cheese over the top
21. Bake in a 350 oven for about 35 minutes or until cheese is bubbly and lightly browned<|endoftext|>
