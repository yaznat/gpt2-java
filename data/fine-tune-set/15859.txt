Give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>Burritos Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Refried beans
• Taco seasoning
• Tomato sauce
• Water
• Flour tortilla

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except tortillas in a greased slow cooker
2. Cover and cook on low heat 6-10 hours
3. Spread hot bean mixture on flour tortillas
4. Fill with favorite burrito toppings
5. Fold and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
