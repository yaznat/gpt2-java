Ingredients:
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Whole grain cornmeal
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Pumpkin pie spice
• Egg whites
• Pumpkin
• Skim milk

Steps:
1. Coat a waffle iron with nonstick cooking spray, and preheat according to manufacturer? s directions
2. Combine the whole wheat flour, cornmeal or brown rice flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and pumpkin pie spice in a large bowl, and stir to mix well
3. Set aside
4. Place the egg whites in the bowl of an electric mixer, and beat on high until soft peaks form
5. Set aside
6. Add pumpkin and milk to the flour mixture, and stir to mix well
7. Gently fold in the egg whites
8. Spoon 1 1 / 4 cups of batter onto prepared waffle iron
9. Bake for six to eight minutes, or until the iron has stopped steaming and the waffles crisp and brown
10. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
