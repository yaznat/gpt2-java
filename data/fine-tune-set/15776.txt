Ingredients:
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Fresh mushrooms
• Vinaigrette dressing
• Salsa
• Cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. In large saute pan over medium heat
3. Saute vegetables in the salad dressing until just cooked
4. Add salsa and heat through
5. Add 1 / 2 cup of the cheese
6. Stir until melted
7. Lay tortillas on flat surface
8. Divide veg mixture among them
9. Fold each into burritos
10. Place on cookie sheet lined with parchment paper
11. Sprinkle remaining cheese over
12. Bake for 15-20 min or until just crisp
13. Add toppings of your choice<|endoftext|>
