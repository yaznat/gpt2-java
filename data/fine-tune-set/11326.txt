Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Bread Machine 2 Lb Traditional White Bread

Ingredients:
• Water
• Canola oil
• Salt
• Bread flour
• Sugar
• Active dry yeast

Steps:
1. Measure and add liquid ingredients to the baking pan
2. Measure and add oil to baking pan
3. Measure and add dry ingredients to the baking pan
4. Sprinkle the yeast on top of the flour
5. Snap the baking pan into the breadmaker and close the lid
6. Choose the "basic" setting
7. Choose the "light" crust color setting
8. Press start
9. Remove the baking pan after about 55 minutes of the baking period
10. Gently shake the bread loaf out of the baking pan onto a cooling rack
11. If necessary, remove the kneading blade from the loaf
12. Be careful, it is hot!
13. Set the loaf upright, cover with a dishtowel and let cool for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
