What's a good pudding recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Rice Pudding Betty Crocker

Ingredients:
• Whole milk
• Long grain rice
• Raisins
• Butter
• Eggs
• Granulated sugar
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Ground nutmeg
• Ground cinnamon
• Light cream

Steps:
1. In a heavy medium sized saucepan, bring 2 cups milk, uncooked rice, and raisins to a boil
2. Reduce heat
3. Cover and cook over very low heat, about 15 minutes or until rice is tender
4. Remove from heat
5. Stir in butter or margarine till melted
6. In a mixing bowl stir together the eggs, 2 cups milk, sugar, vanilla and salt
7. Gradually, stir in the rice mixture into egg mixture
8. Pour into a 10x6x2' pan or baking dish
9. Bake in a 325- for 30 minutes
10. Stir well
11. Sprinkle with nutmeg and cinnamon
12. Bake for 15-20 minutes more or until knife in center comes out clean
13. Serve warm or chilled with cream<|endoftext|>
