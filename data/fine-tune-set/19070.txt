Ingredients:
• Maple bacon
• Brown sugar
• Maple syrup
• Half-and-half cream
• Heavy cream
• Vanilla sugar
• Kosher salt
• Maple flavoring

Steps:
1. Bacon:
2. Pre heat oven to 400f
3. Mix the 1 tablespoon of syrup and the 1 / 4 cup brown sugar to form a paste
4. Lay the bacon out on a cooling rack on a lined sheet pan (lining the sheet pan will save you some nasty clean up
5. Spread the paste on one side of the bacon and then bake for 10 minutes
6. Pull out bacon and turn each slice over
7. Spread the paste the other side of the bacon and out back in the oven for another 10 minutes
8. Pull bacon out and turn it over again, then bake for another 5-10 minutes, or until bacon is evenly glazed
9. Cool completely and then chop into small pieces
10. Maple ice cream:
11. Combine all the ice cream ingredients except the bacon in a medium sauce pan and heat to 170f
12. Cool completely before adding to your ice ream maker
13. Follow you ice cream makers directions, but the basics are
14. Make sure you machine has been in the freezer for 24 hours and that the mixture is chilled completely
15. Add the mixture to the machine while it's running
16. Wait until the ice cream is at a soft serve consistancy before adding the bacon pieces
17. Put the ice cream in an airtight container and put in the freezer for a few hours before enjoying<|endoftext|>
