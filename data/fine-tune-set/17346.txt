What's a good tea recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbara Pratt S Tea Biscuits

Ingredients:
• Salt
• Flour
• White sugar
• Baking powder
• Butter
• Milk
• Egg

Steps:
1. Mix in a bowl salt, flour, white sugar and baking powder
2. Then cut in 1 / 4 cup cold butter
3. Then take milk and egg and beat together and add to dry ingredients
4. Don't mix to much or biscuits will be tough
5. Roll dough into a rectangular shape
6. Spread with softened butter, spread another layer of brown sugar and then sprinkle with cinnamon
7. Roll and cut into 3 / 4 inch segments
8. Bake at 425f for 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
