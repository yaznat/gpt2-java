Give me a muffin recipe.<|endoftext|>All Bran Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-bran cereal
• Skim milk
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Cinnamon
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Egg white
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Combine cereal and milk in medium size bowl
2. Set aside for 15 minutes
3. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and cinnamon in another bowl
4. Mix well
5. Add egg white and applesauce to cereal / milk mixture
6. Mix well
7. Stir in flour mixture
8. Stir until moistened
9. Add a splash of extra milk if batter seems too dry
10. Fold in raisins
11. Spray muffin tins well with no-stick spray
12. Do not use paper baking cups
13. Divide the batter into 12 muffin cups
14. Bake at 350 deg for 15-17 minutes, or until toothpick inserted into the middle comes out clean
15. Let muffins cool in the muffin tin for 5 minutes before removing<|endoftext|>
