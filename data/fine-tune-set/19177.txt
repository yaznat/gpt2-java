Give me a ice cream recipe.<|endoftext|>Caramel Marshmallow Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Marshmallows
• Thickened cream
• Vanilla ice cream
• Caramel

Steps:
1. Combine marshmallows and cream in pot
2. Stir over low heat till marshmallows have melted
3. Cool
4. Slightly soften ice cream in large bowl
5. Swirl through marshmallow mix and topping to give a marbled effect
6. Return to ice cream container, cover and freeze till firm again<|endoftext|>
