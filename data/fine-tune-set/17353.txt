Ingredients:
• Beef sirloin steaks
• Beer
• Onion
• Soy sauce
• Black pepper
• Shredded cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Place steak in plastic bag
2. Combine beer, onion, soy sauce and pepper
3. Pour over steak
4. Press air out of bag
5. Tie top securely
6. Refrigerate 8 hours or overnight, turning over occasionally
7. Remove steak from marinade and place on grill 4" to 5" from hot coals
8. Cook 7 to 10 minutes on each side, or to desired degree of doneness
9. Sprinkle cheese evenly over top side of steak during last few seconds of cooking time
10. To serve, cut across grain into thin slices
11. Makes 3 to 4 servings<|endoftext|>
