Ingredients:
• Ranch dressing
• Taco seasoning
• Frozen french fries
• Bacon
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven and bake the fries according to package directions
2. Meanwhile, combine ranch dressing and taco seasoning
3. Set aside
4. When fries are done, turn off oven
5. Sprinkle the cheese and bacon over the top of fries
6. Return to hot oven only until the cheese is melted
7. Serve with the sauce mix for dipping<|endoftext|>
