Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Country Waffles With Maple Pecan Butter

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Maple syrup
• Pecans
• Eggs
• Milk
• Oil
• Self-rising flour

Steps:
1. In medium bowl, combine butter and syrup
2. Heat waffle iron
3. In medium bowl, beat eggs
4. Add milk and oil
5. Blend well
6. Add flour
7. Mix well
8. For each waffle, pour batter into hot waffle iron
9. Bake until steaming stops and waffle is golden brown
10. Serve waffles with maple pecan butter<|endoftext|>
