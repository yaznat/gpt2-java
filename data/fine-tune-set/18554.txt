Ingredients:
• Frozen blueberries
• Apple juice
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Fresh lemon juice
• All-purpose flour
• Poppy seeds
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Unsalted butter
• Lemon, rind of

Steps:
1. For blueberry sauce: bring blueberries, 1 / 2 cup apple juice and sugar to boil in a heavy medium saucepan
2. Simmer over medium heat until reduced to 2 cups, about 15 minutes
3. Dissolve 1 tablespoon cornstarch into remaining 2 tablespoons apple juice and add to blueberry mixture
4. Add lemon juice
5. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly
6. Simmer until thick, about 1 minute
7. Cool slightly
8. For waffles: whisk first 6 ingredients in large bowl to blend
9. Whisk eggs, buttermik, melted butter and lemon peel in small bowl to blend
10. Add buttermilk mixture to flour mixture all at once and whisk until just blended
11. Let stand for about 15 minutes
12. Preheat waffle iron according to manufacture's instructions
13. Spoon batter onto waffle iron
14. Cover and cook until golden and cooked through, about 7 minutes
15. Repeat with remaining batter
16. Serve immediately with warm blueberry sauce<|endoftext|>
