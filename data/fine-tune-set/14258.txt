Can you give me a tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Deep Fried Tofu With Asian Plum Sauce Or Thai Peanut Sauce

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Sesame seeds
• Cornstarch
• Flour
• Sugar
• Vegetable oil
• Salt and pepper
• Plum sauce
• Peanut sauce
• Your favorite sauce

Steps:
1. Mix together sesame seeds, sugar, cornstarch & flour
2. Roll tofu in sesame mixture till well coated
3. Heat oil in skillet till hot
4. Fry squares till well browned
5. Drain on paper towels & set aside
6. Arrange on a platter & serve warm with the sauce<|endoftext|>
