Ingredients:
• Lean bacon
• Apple
• Onion
• Salt and pepper
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Cut bacon in inch squares and fry out
2. Remove bacon
3. Reserve grease
4. Clean the fry pan
5. Add 1 tablespoon of bacon fat to hot fry pan and add onions
6. Brown the onions until golden and translucent
7. Add more fat if needed
8. Add apples and cook until tender
9. Taste, if mixture is a bit sharp, add brown sugar
10. Pour apples / onions into a hot baking dish
11. Top w / crumbled bacon
12. Cover& keep warm in oven until serving time<|endoftext|>
