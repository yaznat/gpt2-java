Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Amazing Vegan Brownies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Water
• Brown sugar
• Sugar
• Salt
• Vanilla extract
• Baking cocoa
• Oil
• Baking powder
• Nuts
• Semisweet vegan chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients
2. Mix wet ingredients
3. Mix together, 50 strokes, or until well blended
4. Fold in nuts and chocolate chips
5. Spread mixture into a greased 13x9" pan
6. Bake at 350 for 25 minutes, or until a knife can be inserted and come out clean<|endoftext|>
