What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Chicken And Broccoli Stir Fry With Peanut Sauce

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Onion
• Gingerroot
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Water
• Broccoli florets
• Red bell pepper
• Soy sauce
• Cooked rice
• Plain fat-free yogurt
• Peanut butter
• Lemon juice
• Sugar
• Chili powder
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Heat oil in wok or 10-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat
2. Add garlic, onion and gingerroot
3. Stir-fry about 2 minutes or until onion is tender
4. Add chicken
5. Stir-fry 4 to 5 minutes, adding water if necessary to prevent sticking, until chicken is white
6. Add broccoli, bell pepper and soy sauce
7. Stir-fry about 3 minutes or until broccoli is crisp-tender
8. Add 1 tablespoon water
9. Reduce heat
10. Cover and cook about 2 minutes or until chicken is no longer pink in center
11. Serve over rice with peanut sauce
12. To prepare peanut sauce, mix all ingredients
13. Serve at room temperature
14. Cover and refrigerate any remaining sauce<|endoftext|>
