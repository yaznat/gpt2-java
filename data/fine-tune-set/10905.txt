Can you give me a salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Algerian Cucumber Salad

Ingredients:
• Cucumber
• Red bell pepper
• Sweet onions
• Green olives
• Parsley
• Fresh mint leaves
• Cilantro
• Paprika
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Rice wine vinegar
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Toss the cucumber in a salad bowl with the red pepper, onions, olives, mint, and parsley
2. Add the cilantro, paprika, olive oil, and vinegar, season with salt and pepper, toss again, and serve
3. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
