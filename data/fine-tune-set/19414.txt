Give me a donut recipe.<|endoftext|>Yeast Donuts

Ingredients:
• Water
• Dry active yeast
• Butter
• Sugar
• Salt
• Nonfat dry milk powder
• Eggs
• Flour

Steps:
1. Normal dough procedure:
2. Mix yeast with a little water for about 15 minutes
3. Mix sugar, salt, milk powder and eggs together
4. Add rest of water, yeast mixture and the egg mixture slowly to the flour
5. Mix for a few minutes then add softened butter
6. Knead for about 10 minutes
7. Let dough rise until doubled in size
8. Punch down and deflate dough, if its too sticky knead lightly
9. Allow dough to relax for 5 minutes
10. Roll out to 1
11. 2 cm or
12. 5 inch
13. Cut out shapes
14. Reroll excess scrap
15. Place on a floured tray and allow to rise until approximately doubled in bulk
16. Depending on temperature this may take a few hours
17. Fry at 190deg c, using two spoons to flip donuts over
18. Well risen donuts should have a white band around the middle
19. When cool roll in cinnamon sugar or ice with glaze:
20. Icing sugar
21. Cocoa
22. 1 / 2 tsp butter
23. Boiling water
24. For vanilla glaze add 1 / 2 tsp vanilla essence
25. Add icing sugar until consistency is correct
26. Store in freezer<|endoftext|>
