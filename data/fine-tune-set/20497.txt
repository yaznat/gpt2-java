Ingredients:
• Fresh asparagus
• Hard-boiled eggs
• Butter flavored cracker
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Milk
• Salt and pepper
• Italian seasoning

Steps:
1. Drop fresh asparagus into boiling water
2. As soon as they turn"bright" green, remove and plunge them into ice water to stop the cooking process
3. Drain
4. Mix soup and milk together
5. Place asparagus in 9x13x2-inch casserole
6. Top with sliced eggs
7. Sprinkle with crackers
8. Sprinkle with cheese
9. Pour soup mixture over casserole
10. Bake at 350 degrees until hot, just bubbling and cheese is melted
11. I usually add a few more crackers than the original recipe calls for<|endoftext|>
