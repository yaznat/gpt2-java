What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>A Great Monte Cristo Sandwich Longmeadow

Ingredients:
• Turkey breast
• Vegetable oil
• Oregano
• Sea salt
• Pepper
• Egg white
• Skim milk
• Cayenne pepper
• Dijon mustard
• Bread
• Spinach leaves
• Pear
• Low-fat swiss cheese

Steps:
1. In a bowl, put turkey, oil, and seasonings, rubbing over turkey
2. Heat a saute' pan on medium heat, spraying with non-stick spray
3. Add turkey and cook 3 minutes a side
4. Set on plate
5. In a shallow dish, mix together egg white, milk, and salt & pepper if desired, whisk thoroughly
6. Spread approximately 2 teaspoons mustard on 2 slices of bread
7. Put bread with mustard side up on work area
8. Layer spinach, pear and turkey, and cheese onto each mustard topped bread
9. Spread other 2 bread slices with mustard, and top the layered sandwich, mustard side down
10. In same skillet heat remaining oil over a medium heat
11. Using a spatula, dip sandwich in egg / milk mixture
12. Flip sandwich, and do the same to the other side
13. Do the same with second sandwich
14. Transfer to pan, and heat and saute' sandwich turn sandwich and cook on second side
15. Cut in half and serve on porch with gloves and a scarf, or summer shorts, your choice
