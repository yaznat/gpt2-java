Ingredients:
• Cooked shrimp
• Tomatoes
• White onions
• Cilantro
• Jalapeno
• Avocado
• Tomato juice
• Lime juice
• Salt and pepper
• Garlic salt

Steps:
1. After you chop the tomatoes, onions and cilantro mix together in a bowl, add the diced jalapeno pepper
2. Add about 1-1 / 2 cups of tomato juice
3. Then add about 2 tablespoons or more to taste of lime juice add garlic salt to taste do the same to the salt and pepper
4. Add the shrimp and avocado
5. Do a taste test to see if you may need to add one or more of the above ingredients
6. Cover and refrigerate about an hour or until ready to serve
7. Serve with crackers<|endoftext|>
