Please give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Another Broccoli Casserole

Ingredients:
• Broccoli
• Cheddar cheese
• Eggs
• Mayonnaise
• Onion
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Stove top stuffing mix
• Margarine
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a 13x9 inch casserole dish, layer broccoli, then cheese
2. Mix remaining ingredients together and spoon mixture over the top of the cheese
3. Top with stove top stuffing mix
4. Drizzle with melted margarine
5. Bake@ 350 f for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
