Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Brown sugar
• Grand marnier
• Butter
• Sugar
• Banana rum
• Lemon, juice of
• Lemon, zest of
• Oranges, juice of
• Vanilla ice cream
• Whipped cream
• Nuts
• Cinnamon
• Oranges

Steps:
1. Place bananas, brown sugar, grand marnier and lemon juice
2. Marinate 15 minutes
3. Heat butter and sugar, cook until caramelized
4. When golden brown pour in orange juice
5. Mix well
6. Cook 2 or 3 minutes
7. Stir constantly
8. Add banana mix to orange mix
9. Add lemon zest and cook 1 minute
10. Pour in banana rum and flambe
11. Cook 1 minute on high
12. Place on plate ice cream, top it with banana, topped with whipped cream sprinkle nuts and sift cinnamon garnish with orange slice<|endoftext|>
