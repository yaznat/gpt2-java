Ingredients:
• Green onions
• Parsley
• Butter
• Lump crabmeat
• Salt and pepper
• Tabasco sauce
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Saute green onions and 1 tsp parsley in butter until soft, being careful not to let butter brown
2. Add crabmeat, tossing lightly so as not to break up lumps
3. Season to taste and add lemon juice
4. Prepare basic omelette
5. When eggs begin to set, add crabmeat mixture and turn down heat
6. When omelette is set, fold two sides toward center, over filling
7. Garnish with the remaining parsley<|endoftext|>
