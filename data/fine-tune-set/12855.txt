Ingredients:
• Boneless cross-rib roast
• Vegetable oil
• Bacon
• Butter
• Onions
• Mushrooms
• Tomato paste
• Flour
• Beer
• Grainy mustard
• Salt and pepper
• Puff pastry
• Egg

Steps:
1. Cut beef into 1 inch cubes and brown in batches in the vegetable oil over medium high heat
2. Transfer to bowl with a slotted spoon
3. Add bacon to pan and cook until crisp
4. Add to beef
5. Drain the fat from the pan and melt the butter over medium heat
6. Add onions and mushrooms
7. Cook for about 15 minutes, until the liquid has evaporated
8. Stir in tomato paste and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes
9. Sprinkle with flour and cook, stirring, for 1 minute
10. Whisk in beer, mustard, salt and pepper until smooth
11. Return beef mixture with any juices to pan
12. Cover and bake in 350 oven for 1 hour or until beef is tender
13. Let cool for 30 minutes and proceed or refrigerate up to one day and reheat
14. Put mixture into an 8 cup oval casserole
15. On floured surface, roll out pastry to fit top of dish plus 1 inch extra
16. Trim edges
17. Brush edge of dish with water and centre pastry over filling, gently pressing edges to adhere to the casserole dish
18. Brush with egg and make 4 slashes for steam vents
19. Bake in 400 oven for 30 minutes until puffed and golden, about 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
