Ingredients:
• Hoisin sauce
• Sesame oil
• Eggs
• Water
• Peanut oil
• Swiss brown mushrooms
• Gai lan
• Bean sprouts

Steps:
1. Combine the hoisin sauce and sesame oil in a small bowl
2. Whisk together eggs and water in a jug
3. Heat 2 teaspoons of peanut oil in a wok over high heat
4. Add one-quarter of egg mixture, tilting wok to cover base and side
5. As omelette sets, lift edge so that any uncooked egg runs underneath
6. Cook for 2-3 minutes or until just set
7. Slide onto a plate and cover
8. Repeat, in 3 more batches, with 6 teaspoons of oil and the remaining egg mixture, reheating the wok between batches
9. Heat remaining peanut oil in wok over medium heat
10. Add mushroom and cook, stirring, for 2-3 minutes or until tender
11. Add the gai lan and cook, stirring, for 1-2 minutes or until gai lan just wilts
12. Divide omelettes among serving plates
13. Place one-quarter of bean sprouts on half of each omelette
14. Top with the mushroom mixture
15. Fold in half to enclose the filling
16. Drizzle over hoisin sauce mixture to serve<|endoftext|>
