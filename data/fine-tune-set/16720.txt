What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Banana Milkshake With Vanilla

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Milk
• Vanilla ice cream
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Blend banana and milk in a blender for 20 seconds
2. Add ice cream and vanilla extract and blend again for another 20 seconds on high until smooth and still thick
3. Drink!<|endoftext|>
