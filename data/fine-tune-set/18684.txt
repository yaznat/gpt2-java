Ingredients:
• Onions
• Sage
• Fresh white breadcrumbs
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Salt
• Plain flour
• Egg
• Water
• Milk
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Make the yorkshire pudding batter: sift the flour into a large bowl
2. Break the egg into the centre of the heap of flour
3. Mix the water and the milk together in a jug
4. Pour the mixture slowly onto the flour and egg
5. As you start to pour the water / milk slowly beat the mixture together with a whisk
6. Add the salt and continue to beat
7. The puddings will be lighter if the batter includes a little air
8. Once all the ingredients have been beaten together leave to stand, covered by a cloth, for 40 minutes or so
9. Peel the onions, then boil them in water until they are soft
10. Allow them to cool and chop them into small pieces
11. Put the chopped onions into a large bowl
12. Add the sage, parsley or mint, bread crumbs, black pepper and salt
13. Blend together the mixture
14. Now slowly blend in the yorkshire pudding batter making sure that all is well mixed
15. Heat the dripping in an oven proof dish or roasting tray and pour over the herby batter mixture
16. Bake in the centre of the pre-heated oven for 40 minutes until golden brown and well risen<|endoftext|>
