Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Chicken pieces
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Red chili peppers
• Curry powder
• Dried oregano leaves
• Salt
• Black peppercorns
• Condensed chicken broth
• Tomato sauce
• Bay leaf
• Peanut butter
• Sherry wine
• Red bell pepper

Steps:
1. In a nonstick skillet, heat oil over medium-high heat
2. Add chicken, in batches, and brown on all sides
3. Transfer to slow cooker stoneware
4. Reduce heat to medium, add onions and cook, stirring, until softened
5. Add garlic, chili pepper, curry powder, oregano, salt and peppercorns and cook, stirring, for 1 minute
6. Stir in chicken broth, tomato sauce and bay leaf and bring to a boil
7. Pour mixture over chicken
8. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours or on high for 3 hours, until juices run clear when chicken is pierced with a fork
9. In a bowl, combine peanut butter and sherry
10. Add a little cooking liquid and stir to blend
11. Add to slow cooker along with red pepper
12. Cover and cook on high for 20 minutes, until pepper is tender
13. Discard bay leaf
14. Serve over hot white rice<|endoftext|>
