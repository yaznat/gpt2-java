What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>4 Hour Beef Stew

Ingredients:
• Stew meat
• Onion
• Carrots
• Celery
• Potatoes
• Sugar
• Salt
• Dry tapioca
• V8 vegetable juice
• Delmonte ketchup

Steps:
1. Place meat on bottom of 9 x 13 pan
2. Spread veggies over top
3. Mix sugar, salt and tapioca in small bowl and then sprinkle over all the meat and veggies
4. Mix v8 and ketchup in small bowl and pour over all
5. Cover tightly with foil and bake in oven at 250 for about 4-5 hours, no peeking<|endoftext|>
