Ingredients:
• Hawaiian bread
• Mexican blend cheese
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Milk
• Salt
• Pepper
• Mustard powder
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Cube the bread and arrange in a grease 13x9-inch pan
2. Sprinkle with cheese and bacon
3. Whisk together eggs, milk, salt, pepper, mustard and worcestershire
4. Pour over bread
5. Cover and chill overnight
6. Let stand 30 minutes before baking at 350 for 35-40 minutes
7. Serve with salsa or tomatoes<|endoftext|>
