Ingredients:
• Leg of lamb
• Vinaigrette dressing
• Dry red wine
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Bone and clean all membrane and fat from lamb
2. This will yield 2 or 3 large chunks and 2-3 small ones depending on how clean you get the lamb, how large your leg is and if you bought the whole leg or just a section
3. Marinate in dressing for at-least 4 hours, but preferably overnight
4. The straight dressing tends to be a bit sweet for some folks, so cut it with dry red wine and olive oil if desired
5. Grill or broil lamb on skewers until desired degree of doneness is reached
6. You can add some blanched onion, mushroom and tomato to the skewers if desired
7. Serve with rice and sumac<|endoftext|>
