Ingredients:
• Apricot preserves
• Cinnamon
• Dry sherry
• Brown sugar
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Put all ingredients in a small saucepan
2. Bring to a boil over a medium heat, stirring the whole time
3. Turn off heat and allow topping to cool slightly before serving over ice cream<|endoftext|>
