Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Shrimp
• Seafood cocktail sauce
• Parmesan cheese
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Spread cream cheese onto bottom of 9-inch pie plate or shallow bowl
2. Toss shrimp with cocktail sauce
3. Spoon over cream cheese
4. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese and onions<|endoftext|>
