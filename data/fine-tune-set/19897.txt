Ingredients:
• Simply potatoes garlic mashed potatoes
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Bacon
• Scallion
• Refrigerated pie crusts

Steps:
1. - preheat the oven to 350 degrees
2. - line a stainless steel baking sheet with parchment paper
3. - warm the potatoes in the microwave for 5 minutes
4. - place the mashed potatoes into a bowl and whip with a hand mixer for 1-2 minutes
5. Add in the shredded cheese, crumbled bacon, and scallions
6. Fold together with a rubber spatula
7. - unroll one of the pie crusts directly onto the parchment paper
8. - gently spread the mashed potato mixture onto the pie crust, leaving 1 / 4" border all around
9. - top with the second pie crust, gently pressing the crust into the mashed potatoes
10. Press the edges together
11. - using a pizza wheel or a wheel with a decorative edge, cut one inch strips horizontally
12. Create fries that are approximately 2" long by cutting vertical strips
13. - separate the fries
14. - bake for 12-15 minutes, until the crust is golden and crispy<|endoftext|>
