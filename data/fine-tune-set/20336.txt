What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Colorful Turkey Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened pineapple chunks
• Rice wine vinegar
• Cornstarch
• Sugar-free apricot preserves
• Soy sauce
• Ground ginger
• Vegetable oil
• Turkey fillets
• Green pepper
• Tomatoes
• Sliced water chestnuts

Steps:
1. Drain pineapple and reserve juice
2. Add enough water to make 1 cup
3. Place cornstarch in a small bowl and slowly stir in juice mix
4. Add preserves, soy sauce and ginger
5. Finely slice the turkey fillet into strips or buy prepackaged fresh white meat turkey strips
6. Use 2 teaspoons of oil in skillet and saute turkey strips until no longer pink
7. Remove from pan and set aside
8. Use remaining oil to saute bell pepper, cook 2-3 minutes then add pineapple juice mixture
9. Stir until thickened
10. Stir turkey back in, then add pineapple, tomato and water chestnuts and simmer until hot
11. Serve with rice if desired<|endoftext|>
