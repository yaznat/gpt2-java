Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Shortening
• Egg
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Flour
• Instant coffee powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees f
2. Mix sugars, shortening, egg, milk and vanilla
3. In a separate bowl, mix flour, coffee, salt, baking soda and baking powder
4. Stir flour mixture into sugar mixture
5. Shape dough into 1 inch balls
6. Place about 2 inches apart on ungreased cookie sheet and flatten with a fork dipped in sugar
7. Bake until light brown about 7 to 8 minutes<|endoftext|>
