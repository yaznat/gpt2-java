Ingredients:
• Lite silken soft tofu
• Frozen concentrated orange juice
• Light vanilla soymilk
• Vanilla extract
• Splenda granular

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in a blender or food processor and process till smooth
2. Pour into molds, cups or pie plate and freeze at least 4 hours
3. Cut or unmold and serve<|endoftext|>
