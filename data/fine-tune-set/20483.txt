Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Artichoke Pasta Casserole

Ingredients:
• Pasta shells
• Artichoke hearts
• Butter
• Mushroom
• Red onion
• Parmesan cheese
• Sour cream
• Mayonnaise
• White wine
• Oregano leaves
• Seasoning salt
• Paprika
• Pepper
• Garlic powder
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Rinse under cold water and drain well
3. Mix pasta with artichoke hearts
4. Saute mushrooms and onion in butter until soft
5. Mix together pasta and artichoke mixture with mushrooms, parmesan cheese, sour cream, mayonnaise, white wine and seasonings
6. Pour mixture into a medium casserole dish
7. Top with tomato slices
8. Cook casserole at 350f for 25 to 30 minutes or until hot
9. Garnish with fresh oregano if desired
10. You can also microwave this dish
11. The only part you would change is step 3
12. You would place mushrooms and onion with butter in a microwave dish
13. Cover and microwave at high for 3 minutes continue to follow directions above and then microwave at high for 8 to 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
