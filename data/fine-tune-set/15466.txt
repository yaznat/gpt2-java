Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Salt
• Pepper
• Croutons
• Corn
• Tomato sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Eggs
• Milk
• Italian seasoning

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees f
2. In a large frying pan brown the ground beef with the chopped onion
3. Drain grease
4. Season with salt and pepper to taste
5. Place half of the croutons in the bottom of a large baking dish
6. Mix the tomato sauce and the corn with the meat mixture
7. Spoon meat mixture evenly over the croutons in baking dish
8. Top with the mozzarella cheese
9. Spread remaining croutons on top
10. In a medium bowl, beat eggs slightly
11. Mix milk and italian seasoning into eggs
12. Pour the milk / egg mixture over the croutons / meat mixture
13. Cover dish with foil and bake for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
