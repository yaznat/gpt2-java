Ingredients:
• Ground lamb
• Onion
• Carrots
• Garlic cloves
• Dried rosemary
• Red wine
• Lamb gravy
• Lamb stock cube
• Cold water
• Bisto

Steps:
1. Pre-heat your oven to 200c
2. Chop the onions, garlic and carrots, and soften in a frying pan with a little oil
3. Add the ground lamb and cook for another couple minutes until browned
4. Add the rosemary and red wine and cook for approximately 5 mins until most of the alcohol has burned off
5. Meanwhile, make the gravy
6. If using my recipe, first mix the bisto powder with a little cold water to form a paste and then add the remaining water
7. Break the knorr stock cube up and add to the saucepan
8. Cook for a few minutes until it starts to thicken but is not properly cooked
9. Transfer the contents of the frying pan into a casserole dish and cover with the gravy
10. Bake in the oven for approximately 30-40 minutes<|endoftext|>
