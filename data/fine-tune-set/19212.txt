Ingredients:
• Egg yolks
• Light cream
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Almond extract
• Vanilla extract
• Maraschino cherry
• Almonds

Steps:
1. To toast the almonds, heat the oven to 350f
2. Spread the almonds in a thin layer in a shallow baking pan
3. Bake 5 to 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until light golden brown
4. Cool before using
5. Beat egg yolks and 2 cups cream in a large bowl on low to medium speed of mixer, the set aside
6. Stir together sugar and cocoa in a large saucepan
7. Gradually stir in the egg yolk mixture
8. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture is very hot
9. Do not boil!
10. Remove from heat and cool to room temperature
11. Stir the remaining 4 cups cream, almond extract and vanilla into the chocolate mixture, blending well
12. Cover and refrigerate mixture at least 6 hours
13. Pour the chocolate mixture in your ice cream maker or a suitable piece of tupperware
14. Immediately fold in cherries and almonds
15. Freeze for several hours or overnight<|endoftext|>
