Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Brandy
• Pears
• Lemon juice
• Butter
• Honey
• Vanilla bean
• Orange peel
• Lemon peel
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground allspice
• Ground nutmeg

Steps:
1. Stir together ice cream and brandy until well blended
2. Cover and freeze until serving time
3. Peel, halve and core pears
4. Immediately sprinkle with 2 tablespoons lemon juice to prevent browning
5. In a 9 by 13 inch baking dish, mix together remaining lemon juice, butter, and honey
6. Slit vanilla bean lengthwise and scrape seeds into the butter mixture
7. Stir in grated lemon and orange peel, cinnamon, nutmeg and allspice
8. Score round portion of each pear half diagonally 1 / 8 inch deep in one direction and then in the opposite direction, creating a diamond pattern
9. Place pears cut side down in baking dish and spoon sauce over them
10. Bake in a 350 degree oven, basting often, until very tender when peirced with a fork, about 40 minutes
11. Arrange pears on serving plates, rounded side up and serve with brandy / vanilla ice cream on the same plate<|endoftext|>
