What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>100 Calorie No Bake Whey Protein Bar Cookies

Ingredients:
• Raw sugar
• Skim milk
• Butter
• Molasses
• Vanilla protein powder
• Creamy peanut butter
• Quick oats

Steps:
1. In a saucepan over medium heat, add the butter, milk, sugar, and molasses
2. Stir quickly until it bubbles up and then lower the heat
3. Whisk in the peanut butter and protein powder, then remove from heat
4. Stir in oats last while the mixture is still hot
5. Gently roll out onto a pan or plate, push down until about 1 / 2" thick
6. Let rest for 5 minutes and cut into squares<|endoftext|>
