Can you give me a rice recipe?<|endoftext|>15 Minute Red Beans Rice

Ingredients:
• Green pepper
• Kidney beans
• Onion
• Chicken bouillon cubes
• Oil
• Hot pepper sauce
• Water
• Minute white rice

Steps:
1. Cook and stir green pepper and onion in oil in large skillet on medium heat until tender
2. Stir in water, beans, bouillon and pepper sauce
3. Bring to boil
4. Stir in rice
5. Cover
6. Remove from heat
7. Let stand 5 mins<|endoftext|>
