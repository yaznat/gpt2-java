What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Aim S Best Batch Of Brownies

Ingredients:
• Chocolate chips
• Sugar
• Self-rising flour
• Butter
• Eggs
• Salt
• Vanilla
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f line an 8x8-inch pan with foil
2. Spray the foil
3. Combine 1 cup chips and the butter
4. Melt in the microwave for 1 minute, stirring until smooth
5. Whisk in the sugar and eggs
6. Stir in the flour and salt
7. Fold in the 1 / 2 cup nuts and vanilla
8. Bake for 23-25 minutes or until done
9. Finely chop the remaining nuts
10. Top the baked brownies with the remaining nuts and chips
11. Return to the oven for 2 minutes to melt the chips to form a glaze
12. Cool completely and cut into squares<|endoftext|>
