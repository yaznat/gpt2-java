Give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Blue Moon Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Sugar
• Salt
• Half-and-half
• Vanilla extract
• Whipping cream
• Blue food coloring
• Vanilla pudding mix
• Flavored oil

Steps:
1. 1 / 2 cup of milk scalded in pan
2. Add 1 / 2 cup of sugar and 1 / 4 tsp of salt
3. Stir till dissolved
4. Add 1 / 2 cup of half and half
5. Add 11 / 2 cups of heavy whipping cream
6. Add vanilla, raspberry, lemon flavoring
7. Add blue food coloring
8. Chill in fridge till very cold
9. Put in ice cream machine!<|endoftext|>
