Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Burgers Mix

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Celery
• Hamburger
• Tomato sauce
• Catsup
• Vinegar
• Sugar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Tabasco sauce

Steps:
1. Brown chopped vegetables in skillet
2. Add hamburger
3. When hamburger is browned add rest of ingredients
4. Simmer 30 minutes
5. Serve on hamburger buns<|endoftext|>
