Please give me a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>Anita S Rice Hot Dish

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Rice
• Celery
• Chicken broth
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Soy sauce
• Water chestnuts
• Cream of mushroom soup

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350 degrees
2. Brown ground beef and onoin
3. Drain off fat
4. Mix together beef, onion and all other ingredients
5. Place into a casserole dish and bake uncovered for one hour or until rice is tender
6. May top with cashews to serve!<|endoftext|>
