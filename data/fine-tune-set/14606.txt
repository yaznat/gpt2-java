What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>A Quick Chicken And Rice Meal For Busy Nights

Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• Boneless chicken breasts
• Low-fat cream of chicken soup
• Water
• Paprika
• Black pepper
• Garlic powder
• Cooked brown rice
• Broccoli florets

Steps:
1. Spray skillet with cooking spray
2. Add chicken and cook until browned
3. Set chicken aside
4. Add the cream of chicken soup, water, paprika, pepper and garlic powder to the skillet
5. Heat to a boil
6. Stir in the cooked rice and broccoli
7. Top with the chicken
8. Season the chicken with some more paprika and pepper
9. Cover and cook over low heat about 5-7 minutes, until heated through
10. Good served with a nice salad and some rolls
11. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
