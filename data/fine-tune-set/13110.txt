Please give me a recipe for fish.<|endoftext|>Bill S Swordfish Salsa Supreme

Ingredients:
• Swordfish steaks
• Olive oil
• Salt & pepper
• Chunky salsa

Steps:
1. Prepare barbecue-medium high heat
2. Brush fish with oil
3. Sprinkle generously with salt and pepper
4. Grill 4 minutes
5. Turn over
6. Spoon salsa over fish
7. Grill 4 minutes more, or until done
8. Serve passing extra salsa<|endoftext|>
