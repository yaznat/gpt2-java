Ingredients:
• Hamburger
• Onion
• Heinz 57 steak sauce
• Ketchup
• Hot sauce
• Rolls

Steps:
1. Cook onions over low to med heat until tender
2. Add hamburger, cook until brown
3. Add 57 sauce, ketchup and hot sauce
4. Simmer 15 minutes
5. Spoon over the buns of you choice<|endoftext|>
