Ingredients:
• Pure wesson canola oil
• Red onion
• Garlic
• Brown rice
• Hunt's tomato sauce-no salt added
• Water
• Chili peppers
• Ground cumin
• Ranch style black beans
• Garbanzo beans
• Ro*tel original diced tomatoes & green chilies
• Frozen whole kernel corn
• Fresh cilantro
• Lime juice
• Hot pepper sauce
• Kosher salt
• Reduced-fat mexican cheese blend
• Romaine lettuce
• Reduced-fat sour cream

Steps:
1. Heat oil in medium saucepan over medium heat until hot
2. Add 2 / 3 cup of the onion and the garlic
3. Cook 5 minutes or until tender, stirring occasionally
4. Add rice
5. Cook and stir 2 minutes
6. Add tomato sauce, water, ground chile and 1 teaspoon of the cumin
7. Bring to a boil
8. Reduce heat to low, cover and simmer 45 minutes or until all liquid is absorbed
9. Meanwhile, place both beans, drained tomatoes, corn, the remaining onion, cilantro, lime juice, hot sauce and the remaining cumin in large bowl
10. Mix well
11. Set aside
12. When rice is done, stir in salt
13. Divide hot rice mixture among 8 individual bowls or spread in 13x9-inch dish
14. Top with cheese, lettuce and bean mixture
15. Serve immediately and top each serving with sour cream<|endoftext|>
