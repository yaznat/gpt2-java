What's a good smoothie recipe?<|endoftext|>Berry Bananarama Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Plain yogurt
• Banana
• Pineapple-orange juice
• Orange juice
• Strawberry
• Frozen blueberries
• Ice cube

Steps:
1. Place yogurt, sliced banana, juices, and both kinds of berries in a smoothie maker or blender
2. Pulse or mix for 15 seconds
3. Add ice cubes and blend into mixture is smooth<|endoftext|>
