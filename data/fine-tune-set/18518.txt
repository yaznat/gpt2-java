Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Acorn squash
• Mcintosh apple
• Pecans
• All-purpose flour
• Yellow cornmeal
• Double-acting baking powder
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Apple cider
• Pure maple syrup
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Preheat waffle iron
2. If you want to hold the finished waffles until serving time, preheat your oven to 200
3. Melt butter
4. Reserve
5. Use the large-holed side of a box grater to grate the acorn squash
6. You should have 1 cups packed shredded squash
7. Place the squash in a bowl
8. Peel and core apple
9. Cut it into small dice and add it to the bowl with the squash
10. Add in pecans, stir to mix
11. Set aside
12. In a big bowl, whisk the flour, cornmeal, baking powder, salt, cinnamon, and cloves
13. In another bowl, whisk the cider, syrup, and eggs together until very well mixed
14. Pour the liquid ingredients over the dry ingredients and stir with a whisk until just combined
15. Stir in the squash, apple, and pecan and fold in the melted butter
16. Lightly butter or spray the grids of your waffle iron
17. Brush or spray again only if subsequent waffles stick
18. Spoon out a scant 1 cup of batter onto the grids
19. Use a metal spatula or wooden spoon to spread the chunky batter evenly across the grids
20. Close the lid and bake until golden
21. If you are not serving the waffles immediately, put them in a single layer on a baking sheet, loosely covered with foil, in the preheated oven while you make the rest of the batter<|endoftext|>
