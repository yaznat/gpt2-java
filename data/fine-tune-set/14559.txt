Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Onions
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic
• Great northern beans
• Molasses
• Ketchup
• Barbecue sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Dijon mustard
• Apple cider vinegar
• Salt and black pepper
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Cook the bacon in a heavy pot over medium heat until crisp transfer to paper towels
2. Drain off some of the fat but not all of it
3. Add in onions, bell pepper and garlic
4. Saute for about 7-8 minutes until tender
5. Add the bacon back into the pot with all the remaining ingredients
6. Stir to combine
7. Season with salt and pepper if desired
8. Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for about 30-35 minutes, or place in the oven to bake<|endoftext|>
