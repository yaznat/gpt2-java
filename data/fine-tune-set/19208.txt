Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Whipping cream
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Salt
• Chocolate-covered english toffee bar

Steps:
1. In large bowl, combine sugar and cocoa powder
2. Add whipping cream, milk, vanilla, and salt
3. Stir until sugar is dissolved
4. Pour cream mixture into freezer canister of 1 or 1 1 / 2 quart ice cream freezer
5. Freeze according to manufacturer's directions
6. Stir in toffee pieces
7. If desired, ripen ice cream about 4 hours
8. Chocolate-dipped cones
9. For a fun way to dress up ice cream cones with chocolate, place 1 / 2 cup semisweet chocolate chips in a microwave-safe bowl
10. Microwave, uncovered, on 70 percent power for 1 minute
11. Stir
12. Microwave for 1 1 / 2 to 3 minutes more or until the chocolate is melted and smooth, stirring every 15 seconds
13. Spoon chocolate over the tops of ice cream cones to cover about 1 / 3 of each cone
14. Place cones, top sides down, on baking sheet lined with waxed paper
15. Chill the cones about 15 minutes or until set<|endoftext|>
