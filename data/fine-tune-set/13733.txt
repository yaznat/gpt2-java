Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Sage Stuffed Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork chops
• Onion
• Celery
• Butter
• Whole wheat bread
• Apple
• Dried sage
• Coriander
• Flour
• Salt
• Pepper
• Oil
• Chicken broth

Steps:
1. Cut a pocket into each pork chop and trim fat
2. Saute onion & celery until soft in 2 t
3. Butter
4. Add bread cubes, apple, sage & coriander
5. Mix well & moisten with 2 t
6. Broth
7. Stuff the chops with the dressing
8. Hold together with toothpicks if you wish
9. Mix together flour, salt& pepper
10. Dredge the chops in mixture
11. Heat oil & 1 t
12. Butter
13. Sear the chops & put into a sprayed baking dish
14. Pour remaining broth over chops, cover & bake at 350 degrees for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
