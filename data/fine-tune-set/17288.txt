Ingredients:
• Water
• Long-grain white rice
• Refried beans
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Beef steaks
• Avocados

Steps:
1. Preheat an outdoor grill for high heat, and lightly oil grate
2. In a medium saucepan, bring water to a boil
3. Stir in rice, reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes
4. Place refried beans in a medium saucepan over medium heat, and season with salt and garlic powder
5. Cook, stirring occasionally, until heated through
6. Heat oil in a medium skillet over medium heat
7. Saut onions until browned and tender
8. On the preheated grill, cook steaks 7 to 10 minutes, to an internal temperature of 145f
9. Spread steaks with refried beans, and top with onions and avocado slices
10. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
