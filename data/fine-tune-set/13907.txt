Ingredients:
• Lamb
• Butter
• Rice
• Salt and pepper
• Flour
• Yogurt
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Cooking instructions:
2. Cut meat in 4 serving pieces, sprinkle each piece with salt and pepper, and bake in a moderately-heated oven with half the butter, sprinkling the meat with its gravy now and then
3. When meat is half-baked, add rice
4. Remove the baking pan from the oven and leave it aside while you prepare the yogurt sauce:
5. Saut flour in butter until mixed thoroughly
6. Mix yogurt with salt, pepper and eggs until a uniform mixture is obtained, and finally stir in the flour
7. Put the sauce mixture in the baking pan
8. Saut it with the meat pieces and bake at 375 degrees f for about 45 minutes
9. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
