Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Black Forest Pizza

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Cream cheese
• Confectioners' sugar
• Frozen whipped topping
• Cherry pie filling
• Chocolate sprinkles

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Coat a 12-inch pizza pan with non-stick cooking spray
3. Prepare brownie mix according to package directions
4. Spread the prepared batter evenly over the bottom of the pan
5. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until a wooden toothpick inserted in center comes out clean, let cool
6. In a 2-3 quart mixing bowl, beat the cream cheese, confectioners sugar and whipped topping until smooth
7. Spread the mixture evenly over the brownie crust
8. Spoon on the cherry pie filling, spread to about 1 inch of the outer edge
9. Sprinkle chocolate candy sprinkles on the outer white rim
10. Chill and serve
11. Makes 12 slices<|endoftext|>
