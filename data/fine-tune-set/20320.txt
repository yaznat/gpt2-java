What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Chinese Pork Vegetable Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Pork tenderloin
• Assorted fresh vegetables
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Lipton recipe secrets onion soup mix
• Water
• Orange juice
• Soy sauce
• Garlic powder

Steps:
1. 1
2. In a 12 inch skillet, heat 1 tablespoon olive oil over medium-high heat
3. Brown pork
4. Remove and set aside
5. 2
6. In same skillet
7. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil and cook assorted vegetables
8. Stirring occasionally, 5 minutes
9. Stir in water chestnuts, soup mix blended with water, orange juice, soy sauce and garlic powder
10. Bring to boil over high heat
11. Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 3 minutes
12. Return pork to skillet and cook 1 minute or until heated through<|endoftext|>
