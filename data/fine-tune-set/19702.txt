Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Black pepper
• Table salt
• Potatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees f
2. Spray a nonstick baking sheet with nonstick cooking spray
3. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients
4. Toss to coat
5. Transfer to the baking sheet
6. Bake until potatoes are barely tender, about 15 minutes
7. Increase the oven temperature to broil
8. Broil until crispy, about 10 minutes
9. Serve at once<|endoftext|>
