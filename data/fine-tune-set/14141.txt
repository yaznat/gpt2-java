Ingredients:
• Soft tofu
• Corn oil
• Water
• Bananas
• Pitted dates
• Vanilla extract
• Salt

Steps:
1. Open up the tofu, and drain off the water
2. Peel the bananas and cut them into chunks
3. Cut the tofu into small cubes and place it in a blender
4. Add the corn oil, water or soy milk, banana chunks, dates, vanilla and salt
5. Process for 2 to 3 minutes, or until the mixture is smooth with small date flecks blended throughout
6. You may have to stop the blender, and scrape down the sides a few times during blending
7. Pour the pudding into 4 small 1 / 2-cup-sized, resealable plastic containers, or a big bowl and chill<|endoftext|>
