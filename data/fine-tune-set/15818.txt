What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Bodacious Burritos

Ingredients:
• Lean stewing beef
• Beef broth
• Oil
• Diced green chilies
• V8 vegetable juice
• Garlic
• Sharp cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Brown meat in oil and drain
2. Add remaining ingredients except cheese and simmer over low heat, or cook in a crockpot on low setting, for 3 hours
3. Shred meat with fork
4. Drain excess liquid
5. Serve on tortillas with cheese<|endoftext|>
