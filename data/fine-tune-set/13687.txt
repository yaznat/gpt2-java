Ingredients:
• Garlic powder
• Brown sugar
• Ground ginger
• Ground pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Water
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients together
2. Marinade 4 pork chops or 3-4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts for 1-2 hours, then grill
3. Boil any leftover marinade for at least 5 minutes and pour over grilled meat<|endoftext|>
