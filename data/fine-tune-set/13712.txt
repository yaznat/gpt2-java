What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cider Marinated Rack Of Pork Roast

Ingredients:
• Pork rib roast
• Grainy mustard
• Fresh ground pepper
• Paprika
• Dried thyme
• Caraway seed
• Garlic clove
• Honey
• Olive oil
• Cider vinegar
• Apple cider

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients, except pork, in a bowl and whisk until combined
2. Place roast in a zipper-type gallon baggie
3. Pour marinade over pork and close baggie
4. Place in refrigerator for 4-6 hours, turning occasionally
5. Preheat oven to 325f
6. Spray a medium sized roasting pan and a wire rack to fit pan, with nonstick cooking spray
7. Remove pork from marinade and reserve marinade
8. Place pork on rack in pan and put in oven on center rack
9. Roast 15 minutes per pound, basting occasionally with reserved marinade, until instant-read thermometer inserted in center reaches 160f remove roast from oven and let sit on carving board about 10 minutes
10. Place roasting pan on stove burner, over medium heat, and pour in reserved marinade
11. Stir to loosen browned bits on bottom of pan
12. Heat until marinade and pan juices are hot
13. Slice roast between rib bones and serve, with pan juices drizzled on chops<|endoftext|>
