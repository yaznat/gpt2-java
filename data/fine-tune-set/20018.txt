Ingredients:
• Maggi 2-minute noodles
• Eggs
• Cheese
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Lightly beat eggs
2. Add salt & pepper to taste
3. Drain cooked noodles & add to eggs
4. Mix with a fork
5. Add cheese & mix with fork
6. Heat frying pan & add a little butter
7. Pour in egg & noodle mixture ensuring the noodles are spread evenly within the mixture
8. Cook until both sides are slightly browned<|endoftext|>
