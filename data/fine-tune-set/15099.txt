What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Anthony S French Bread Pizza

Ingredients:
• French bread
• Pizza sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Pepperoni slices

Steps:
1. Slice the french bread lengthwise in half
2. Spread the pizza sauce on each half with cut side up
3. Sprinkle the cheese equally between the two halves
4. Layer the pepperoni slices
5. Bake in a preheated oven at 375 degrees for 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
