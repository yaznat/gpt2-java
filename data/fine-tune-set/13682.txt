Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Breadcrumbs
• Sliced almonds
• Dried rosemary
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Salt
• Egg whites
• Cooking spray
• Dried sweetened cranberries
• Boiling water
• Dried apple
• Raisins
• Crystallized ginger
• White wine vinegar
• Sugar
• Ground red pepper
• Ground allspice
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Strawberry jam

Steps:
1. Conserve:
2. Combine the first 5 ingredients, cover and let stand 30 minutes
3. Combine vinegar and next 5 ingredients in a small saucepan, and bring to a boil, stirring mixture frequently
4. Add the fruit mixture
5. Bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat, and simmer for 5 minutes
7. Stir in jam
8. Remove from heat, and cool to room temperature
9. Meat:
10. Preheat oven to 425f
11. Trim fat from pork
12. Combine breadcrumbs and next 4 ingredients in a shallow dish
13. Dip pork in egg whites
14. Dredge in breadcrumb mixture
15. Place pork on a broiler pan coated with cooking spray
16. Insert a meat thermometer into thickest part of pork
17. Bake at 425f for 30 minutes or until thermometer registers 160
18. Cover with foil, let stand 10 minutes
19. Cut into 1 / 4 inch slices
20. Serve pork with conserve<|endoftext|>
