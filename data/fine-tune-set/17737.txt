What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Soy flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Ground cinnamon
• Eggs
• Applesauce
• Vegetable oil
• Red cinnamon candies

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375 f
2. Generously grease muffin pan or line with paper baking cups
3. In large bowl combine all-purpose flour, soy flour, sugar, baking powder, salt and cinnamon
4. Set aside
5. In medium bowl, combine eggs, applesauce and oil
6. Stir liquids into flour mixture just until dry ingredients are moistened
7. Gently, but quickly, stir in cinnamon candies
8. Fill muffin cups 2 / 3 to 3 / 4-full
9. Bake 20 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
10. Cool on wire rack 5 minutes
11. Remove muffins from pan to cool completely<|endoftext|>
