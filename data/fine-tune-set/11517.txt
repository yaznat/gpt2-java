Ingredients:
• Whole milk
• Eggs
• Egg yolk
• Vanilla extract
• Anise flavoring
• Salt
• Granulated sugar
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Gluten
• Active dry yeast

Steps:
1. Place in order of your manufacturers instructions for your bread machine
2. Place on sweet bread setting, light crust
3. Checking as mixing that it forms a smooth slight tacky ball of dough
4. If dough is sloppy, add a tsp, flour at a time
5. If dough is too hard like, add a tsp milk, at a time
6. Although at this time i did not have to adjust my dough upon mixing
7. Remove when completed baking, to a wire rack
8. Cool thoroughly before cutting with a serrated bread knife, or before storing<|endoftext|>
