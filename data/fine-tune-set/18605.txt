Give me a pudding recipe.<|endoftext|>Amish Corn Pudding

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Black pepper
• Low-fat milk
• Cream-style corn
• Corn kernel
• Unsalted butter
• Paprika
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Butter a 1 1 / 2 quart souffle or round baking dish and place in the oven
3. Meanwhile, in a large bowl, with an electric mixer on high, beat the eggs, flour, sugar, and pepper until smooth
4. Stir in the milk, creamed corn, and corn kernels and pour into the hot dish
5. Dot with the butter and sprinkle with the paprika
6. Bake, uncovered, for 40 minutes or until set
7. Let stand for 10 minutes, then sprinkle with the chopped parsley<|endoftext|>
