Ingredients:
• Apricot preserves
• Ginger
• Ground thyme
• Garlic cloves
• Green onion
• Pork loin

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Put preserves, ginger and thyme int the blender
3. Rough chop the garlic and put into the blender
4. Slice the green onion and add to the blender
5. Puree until well mixed
6. Pour mixture into a gallon baggie
7. Add the pork loins to the baggie
8. Seal and coat them well with the preserve mixture
9. Place the loins on a roasting rack
10. Cook until internal temperature is 170
11. Slice and serve<|endoftext|>
