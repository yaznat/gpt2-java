What's a recipe for potatoes?<|endoftext|>Amy S Roasted Red Skin Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Vidalia onion
• Butter
• Parmesan cheese
• Olive oil
• Flat leaf parsley
• Garlic powder
• Seasoning
• Dried basil
• Salt & pepper
• Pam cooking spray

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
2. Spray 9x13 pan with pam
3. Place potatoes and onions in pan, toss with butter, and olive oil
4. Add remaining ingredients, and toss again to coat
5. Cover with foil, and bake for 20 min, take out, stir, and return covered to oven
6. Bake an additional 40 minutes or until tender<|endoftext|>
