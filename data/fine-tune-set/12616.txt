Ingredients:

• Carrots
• Onion

• Sweet potato
• Worcestershire sauce
• V8 vegetable juice
• Water
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Oil

Steps:
1. 1
2. Brown meat in oil add onions
3. When browned transfer to crock pot
4. 2
5. Add the remaining ingredients
6. Cook on high for 4 to 6 hours, or low 8 to 10 hours
