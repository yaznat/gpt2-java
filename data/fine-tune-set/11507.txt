What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Ancient Roman Bread

Ingredients:
• Dry yeast
• Water
• Whole wheat flour
• Rye flour
• Unbleached white flour
• Salt
• Cornmeal

Steps:
1. Put water in mixer bowl and dissolve the yeast
2. Put 4 cups of the flour into the bowl and whip it for 10 minutes
3. Add the salted water
4. If your mixer is heavy duty, put the dough hook on and add the remaining flour let the dough hook work until it is smooth and elastic
5. Put the dough on the counter and cover with an inverted steel bowl
6. Let it rise once
7. Punch it down and let it rise again
8. Punch it down and form into 2 or 3 loaves
9. Place loaves on baking sheet dusted with cornmeal and rise until doubled
10. Bake in preheated 450f oven for about 25 minutes or until the crust is golden
11. The loaves will sound hollow when thumped
12. Use common sense in raising the dough
13. I frequently use my warming drawer
14. Prep time does not include raising time as it will vary based on your conditions<|endoftext|>
