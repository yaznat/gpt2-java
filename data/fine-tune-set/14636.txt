What's a good rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Rice

Ingredients:
• Chicken stock
• Dry parsley flakes
• Instant minced onion
• Oregano leaves
• Long grain rice
• Butter
• Slivered almonds

Steps:
1. Combine first four ingredients in a saucepan over high heat
2. Bring to a boil and stir rice and butter
3. Cover pan and reduce heat to medium low
4. Cook 20 minutes
5. Remove from heat and let stand 5 minutes
6. Fluff rice with a fork
7. Gently toss in toasted almonds and serve<|endoftext|>
