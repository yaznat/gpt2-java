Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Place oatmeal in a blender or processor for 2 min or till makes course flour
3. In a bowl combine first 7 ingredients
4. Make a well in the center and add egg, milk and margarine, stir just till moist, don't over stir
5. Add apples
6. Fill greased muffin tins 2 / 3 full
7. Streusel:
8. Place all ingredients in a small bowl and blend with a fork
9. Sprinkle over tops of muffins
10. Bake 15-20 minutes or till tops test done
11. While they bake, place all caramel drizzle ingredients except powdered sugar in sauce pan and bring to a boil
12. Cook and stir 2 minutes
13. Remove from heat and blend in powdered sugar
14. Remove muffins from oven and then from pan
15. Place on wire rack and drizzle all with caramel drizzle
