Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Christine S Pecan Oatmeal Waffles

Ingredients:
• Rolled oats
• Soymilk
• Pecans
• Maple syrup
• Maple sugar
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Combine soy milk and oats in a blender
2. Blend on high speed for 1 minute until smooth and creamy
3. Transfer to medium bowl and refrigerate for 2 hours until thick and batter like
4. Stir in nuts, maple syrup, maple sugar and cinnamon until well combined and set aside
5. Lightly grease and preheat waffle iron on a moderate to low setting until hot
6. Spoon the batter about 2 / 3 cup per waffle into prepared iron cook on low setting until steaming stops about 4-5 minutes<|endoftext|>
