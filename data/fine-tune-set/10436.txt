Give me a cookie recipe.<|endoftext|>Anise Cookie Sticks

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Anise seed

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Cream butter and cream cheese
3. Add sugar gradually
4. Beat in eggs one at a time
5. Add rest of ingredients and mix well
6. Divide dough into 2 rolls the length of the cookie sheet and 1 1 / 2 inch wide
7. Bake 25 minutes until light brown
8. Remove from oven and cut into slices about 3 / 4 inch thick
9. Place on cookie sheet cut side down
10. Return to oven and bake 10 minutes until toasted and crisp<|endoftext|>
