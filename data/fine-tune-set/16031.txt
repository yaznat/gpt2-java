Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Refried beans
• Green chilies
• Dried oregano
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Flour tortillas
• Butter
• Taco cheese
• Paprika

Steps:
1. In a skillet, cook the beef over medium heat until no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Stir in the beans, chilies, oregano, cumin, chili powder and salt
4. Cook over medium-low heat for 3-4 minutes or until heated through
5. Brush one side of each tortilla with butter
6. Spoon 1 / 2 cup of the meat mixture over half of the unbuttered side
7. Sprinkle with 1 / 3 cup cheese
8. Fold in half
9. Place on a lightly greased baking sheet
10. Sprinkle with paprika
11. Bake at 475 degrees for 10 minutes or until crisp and golden browned
12. Cut into wedges<|endoftext|>
