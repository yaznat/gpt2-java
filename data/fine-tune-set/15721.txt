Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Diced tomatoes with green chilies
• Diced tomatoes
• Diced green chilies
• Kidney beans
• Black beans
• Whole kernel corn
• Taco seasoning mix
• Ranch dressing mix
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Shred the chicken, if desired
2. Do not drain the remaining cans
3. Combine all the ingredients into a crock-pot and stir
4. Cook on high for 2 hours or on low for 4 hours
5. Garnish as desired<|endoftext|>
