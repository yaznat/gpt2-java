What's a good ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>7 Sins Ice Cream Dessert

Ingredients:
• Oreo cookies
• Butter
• Ice cream
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Chocolate syrup
• Vanilla
• Whipped topping
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Mix cookie crumbs with melted butter
2. Press into 9x13" pan
3. Freeze 30 minutes
4. Top with softened ice cream
5. Freeze another 30 minutes
6. Make a syrup with the condensed milk, chocolate syrup and butter by combining in saucepan and bringing to a boil
7. Simmer 5 minutes
8. Stir in the vanilla
9. Pour on top of ice cream
10. Freeze another 30 minutes
11. Spread cool-whip over the top
12. Sprinkle with chopped nuts
13. About 30 minutes before serving, place in refrigerator to help ease in serving<|endoftext|>
