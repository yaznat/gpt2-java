Ingredients:
• Hard-boiled eggs
• Spam
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Mayonnaise
• Parmesan cheese
• Hamburger bun

Steps:
1. Place first four ingredients in food processor
2. Process till finely chopped
3. Add enough mayonnaise to moisten mixture to your preference
4. Spread mixture on halves of split hamburger buns
5. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese
6. Broil until bubbly and golden brown
