Ingredients:
• Penne pasta
• Oil
• Lamb shanks
• Onions
• Button mushrooms
• Fresh garlic
• Dried basil
• Dried rosemary
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Dry white wine
• Beef broth
• Worcestershire sauce
• Tomato puree
• Salt and pepper
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Set oven to 325 degrees
2. Cook the penne in boiling water until just firm-tender, drain and transfer to a bowl toss with 2-3 tablespoons oil and set aside, this can be done before, during or after you cook the lamb shanks)
3. Heat oil in a dutch oven over high heat
4. Rub oil lightly on the lamb shanks and then season them with salt and pepper
5. Add to the pot and brown evenly on all sides
6. Transfer the lamb to a platter
7. Add in chopped onions, garlic, mushrooms, basil, rosemary and crushed red pepper flakes
8. Saute until the veggies are tender
9. Return the lamb to the pot
10. Add in wine and bring to a boil
11. Simmer for about 6-8 minutes
12. Add in beef broth, worcestershire sauce and crushed tomatoes and return to a simmer
13. Season with salt and pepper to taste
14. Cover the pot and transfer to the oven
15. Cook for about 1-1 / 2 hours, or until meat is desired tenderness
16. At this point you can remove the meat from the bones and return the meat to the sauce, or just serve the bone with the meat on them with the sauce
17. Serve the sauce over cooked pasta, and sprinkle with grated parmesan cheese
18. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
