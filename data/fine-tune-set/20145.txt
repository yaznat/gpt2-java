Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Fresh ginger
• Baby bok choy
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Heat oil over medium-high heat and cook ginger for about 30 seconds
2. Add bok choy, soy sauce, and sugar
3. Stir-fry 3-5 minutes until cooked to desired tenderness
4. Remove ginger slices, add sesame oil and serve<|endoftext|>
