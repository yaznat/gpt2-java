Ingredients:
• Garlic
• Olive oil
• Fresh sage
• Frozen baby lima beans
• Tomatoes
• Water
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Separate the garlic into individual cloves
2. Peel and slice them
3. In a large heavy-botomed frying pan, combine the garlic, oil and sage
4. Cook over low heat, stirring frequently, for 5 minutes, or until the garlic begins to turn golden
5. Don't let the garlic burn or turn brown
6. Add the beans, tomatoes and water
7. Bring to a boil over medium heat
8. Simmer for 20 minutes, or until thick
9. Season with the pepper<|endoftext|>
