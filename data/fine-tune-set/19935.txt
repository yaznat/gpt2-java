Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Eggs
• Heavy cream
• Red onions
• Jalapeno
• Capers
• Fresh basil
• Fresh parsley
• Cherry tomatoes
• Parmesan cheese
• Salt and pepper
• Sour cream
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Combine all the ingredients, except the sour cream
2. Heat a small nonstick skillet and pour in olive oil
3. Swirl pan to coat with the oil
4. Heat the broiler
5. Pour half the egg mixture into the skillet and cook until set underneath, about 1-2 minutes
6. Lift sides of omelette with a spatula to check doneness
7. Place the pan under the broiler and cook until omelette is golden on top, about 2 minutes
8. Fold omelette in half and remove to serving plate
9. Repeat with remaining mixture
10. Top each with sour cream
11. Garnish with parsley
12. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
