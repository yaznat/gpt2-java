Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Green onions
• Cheddar cheese
• Sausages

Steps:

2. Fry the onions and sausages for a while, or until they go redish
3. Beat in the eggs with the cheese
4. Keep scarmblling the eggs with the onions and sausages until eggs are well done
