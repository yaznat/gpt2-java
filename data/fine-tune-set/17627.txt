What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Albers Sweet Corn Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Yellow cornmeal
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Milk
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f grease or paper-line 18 to 20 muffin cups, or a pan for cornbread
2. Combine flour, sugar, corn meal, baking powder, and salt in medium bowl
3. Combine milk, eggs, vegetable oil, and butter in small bowl
4. Mix well
5. Add liquid mixture to flour mixture
6. Stir until just blended
7. Pour into prepared muffin cups filling 2 / 3 full
8. Bake for 18-20 minutes or until wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean
9. Cool in pans on wire racks for 5 minutes, remove to wire racks to cool slightly
10. Serve warm and enjoy!
11. If you are making cornbread, pour into greased 8-inch square baking pan, and bake for 35 minutes
12. For high altitude bakers, change corn muffin time to 20-24 minutes
13. Cornbread time remains unchanged<|endoftext|>
