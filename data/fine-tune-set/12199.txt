Ingredients:
• Cooking apples
• Sugar
• Fresh lemon juice
• Apple pie spice
• Vanilla wafer crumbs
• Low-fat vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Combine the first 4 ingredients in a saucepan and bring to a boil
2. Cover and reduce heat to simmer for 5 minutes
3. Uncover and simmer 5 minutes more or until tender, stir occasionally
4. Spoon into a bowl and cover and chill
5. Spoon 1 tablespoons cookie crumbs into each of 8 glasses or sundae dishes
6. Top each with 1 / 4c of apple mixture and 3 tablespoons ice cream
7. Repeat the layers 1 time, ending with ice cream
8. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
