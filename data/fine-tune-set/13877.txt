What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Amazing Simple Rack Of Lamb With Cabernet Sauce

Ingredients:
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper
• Paprika
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Dried thyme
• Dried rosemary
• Dried basil
• Dried marjoram
• Dried oregano
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Cabernet sauvignon wine
• Beef broth
• Water
• Dijon mustard
• Parsley
• Lamb racks
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 425
2. Mix together seasoning ingredients in a bowl
3. Heat olive oil in large skillet to medium
4. If you like you can trim some fat from the racks
5. You also can slice them in half for smaller racks
6. Sprinkle seasoning on each rack
7. Sear each rack on all sides until browned put racks into roasting pan and bake for 15 minutes to 130 degrees
8. Or 20 mins for a light pink med well
9. When lamb is done take it out of the oven and cover pan with foil and let lamb sit for 10 minutes
10. While lamb is cooking, make cabernet sauce by melting butter in the searing skillet
11. When melted add flour and cook for a minute or so
12. Stir in other ingriedents starting with the wine and beef broth, then simmer for a bit to reduce the liquid
13. You can add a bit of cornstarch if you want it thicker
14. When everything is ready you can cut the racks into 2 bone chops or leave it whole depending on your presention
15. Serve with cab sauce on the side
16. The cab sauce is also great on mashed pototoes
17. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
