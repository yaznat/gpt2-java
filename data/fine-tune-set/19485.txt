Give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>Absolutely Delightful Scalloped Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Cream of chicken soup
• Evaporated milk
• Salt and pepper
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Grease well a 3 qt casserole dish
2. Heat soups and evaporated milk in saucepan and stir until blended well
3. Cover bottom of dish with one layer of potatoes
4. Sprinkle with salt and pepper
5. Pour enough soup mixture over potatoes just to cover
6. Sprinkle with cheese
7. Repeat layers ending with cheese
8. Cover and bake in 350 oven for 1 hr - 1 and 1 / 2 hours<|endoftext|>
