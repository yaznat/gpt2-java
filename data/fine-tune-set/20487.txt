Ingredients:
• Plain flour
• Beef steaks
• Olive oil
• Yellow onions
• Carrots
• Star anise
• Fresh ginger
• Lemongrass
• Beef stock
• Brandy
• Tomato paste
• Green beans
• Fresh coriander leaves
• Steamed rice

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 180c / 350-375f / 4-5 gas mark
2. Place the flour on a plate and season with salt and pepper, add the beef and toss to coat
3. Shake off any excess
4. Heat half the oil in a 4l capacity flameproof, ovenproof casserole dish over a medium heat
5. Add half the beef and cook for 2-3 minutes each side or until brown
6. Transfer the cooked beef pieces to a plate
7. Repeat with remaining oil and beef, reheating the dish between batches
8. Add the onion and carrot to the pan and cook, stirring, for 5 minutes or until the onion is soft
9. Add the star anise, ginger and lemon grass, and stir to combine
10. Add the beef, stock, brandy and tomato paste to the dish
11. Cover and bake for 1 1 / 2 hours or until the beef is tender
12. Meanwhile, cook the beans in a medium saucepan of boiling water for 3-4 minutes or until they are bright green and tender crisp
13. Drain
14. Add the beans to the beef mixture and stir to combine
15. Top the beef mixture with coriander and serve with steamed rice
16. Freezing<|endoftext|>
