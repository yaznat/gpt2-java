Please give me a recipe for pie.<|endoftext|>Apple Pie Ala Mode Oatmeal

Ingredients:
• Water
• Oatmeal
• Apple pie filling
• Sugar
• Heavy cream
• Butter
• Vanilla ice cream

Steps:
1. Prepare oatmeal according to directions given on box
2. When done, add the apple pie filling, sugar, and stir
3. Stir in heavy cream, and butter
4. Divide oatmeal into 2 bowls, and top with on scoop of vanilla ice cream each
5. Serve immediately while oatmeal is hot and ice cream is still firm<|endoftext|>
