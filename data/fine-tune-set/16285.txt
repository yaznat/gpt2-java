Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Water chestnuts
• Sugar
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Combine soy sauce and sugar
2. Marinate chestnuts in mixture for 30 minutes
3. Wrap bacon slice around each chesnut
4. Fasten with toothpick
5. Place on rack in shallow pan or broiler
6. Bake at 350 for 30-35 minutes until bacon is crisp
7. Serve hot<|endoftext|>
