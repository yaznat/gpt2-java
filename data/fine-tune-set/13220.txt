What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>2 In 1 Herbed Chicken Leftover Chicken Salad

Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breasts
• Margarine
• Onions
• Garlic
• Thyme
• Salt
• Pepper
• Rosemary
• Sage
• Marjoram
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Mayonnaise
• Dill
• Onion
• Black olives

Steps:
1. Herbed chicken:
2. Mix margarine with spices
3. Coat chicken with herb mixture
4. Place in baking dish skin side up bake in preheated 375 degree oven for 30 to 40 minutes or until done
5. For leftover chicken salad:
6. Mix all leftover ingredients together
7. Delicious served on a plate of lettuce- suppose you could put in a sandwich
8. Probably would be best served the next day to let flavors blend mine didn't last that long<|endoftext|>
