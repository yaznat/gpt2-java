Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Bottom Cheesecake Bars

Ingredients:
• Brownie mix
• Oil
• Water
• Eggs
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Sour cream
• Semi-sweet chocolate baking squares

Steps:
1. Please read all directions before beginning
2. The procedure is a bit out of the ordinary
3. Heat oven to 350
4. Line 13x9 inch pan with foil
5. I use a disposable foil casserole pan
6. Grease or spray w / cooking spray
7. Make sure pan is deep, as cheesecake portion of this recipe tends to puff up
8. When they are cool, put them in the fridge and they "calm down"
9. I also line any pan i use with foil, extending the foil over the edges
10. This makes it easy to get the whole thing out for slicing
11. Prepare brownie mix according to directions on box and pour into pan
12. Bake 15 minutes or until top is shiny and center is almost set
13. At this point it is only partially done
14. Beat cream cheese, sugar and vanilla until well blended
15. Add sour cream
16. Mix well
17. Add eggs, one at a time, mixing on low speed after each egg to mix well
18. Pour over partially cooked brownie batter in pan
19. Bake 40 minutes or until center of cheesecake part is almost set
20. Run knife / spatula around edge to loosen cake
21. After cooling, refrigerate 4 hours or overnight
22. Drizzle the melted chocolate squares over top
23. Let stand until chocolate is firm
24. Remove from pan using the foil to lift it out
25. Dip a knife in hot water to cut these
26. Cut into 16 pieces, or more, to serve<|endoftext|>
