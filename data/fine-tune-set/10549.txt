Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Alton Brown S Eggplant Pasta

Ingredients:
• Eggplants
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Tomatoes
• Heavy cream
• Basil chiffonade
• Parmesan cheese
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Salt

Steps:
1. Peel each eggplant, leaving 1" of skin unpeeled at the top & bottom
2. Slice the eggplant thinly lengthwise, about 1 / 4" thick
3. Evenly sprinkle each slice with salt & lay out for 30 minutes on a sheet pan fitted with a wire rack
4. Rinse with cold water & roll in paper towels
5. Slice the pieces into thin fettucine-like strips
6. Heat the oil in a large saute pan over a medium heat
7. Add the garlic & crushed red pepper, & cook until garlic is golden & aromatic
8. Add th eggplant 'pasta' & toss to coat
9. Add the tomatoes & cook for 3 minutes
10. Add the cream & increase the heat to thicken the sauce
11. Finally, add the basil & parmesan
12. Toss to combine
13. Season with black pepper but remember the eggplant will already be salty enough!<|endoftext|>
