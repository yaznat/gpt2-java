Ingredients:
• Egg white
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Onion powder
• Sweet potatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450f
2. In large bowl whisk together egg and spices until frothy
3. Add potatoes
4. Toss to coat well
5. Place on nonstick baking sheet in single layer
6. Bake until golden brown and crisp
7. About 20 minutes stirring once during baking<|endoftext|>
