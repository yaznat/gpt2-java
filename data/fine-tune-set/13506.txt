Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Lean round steak
• Beef broth
• Head cauliflower
• Green pepper
• Light soy sauce
• Garlic clove
• Fresh gingerroot
• Cornstarch
• Sugar
• Water
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In a skillet, brown meat on all sides
2. Add the broth and cover and simmer until beef is tender, about an hour
3. Add cauliflower, green pepper, soy sauce, garlic, and ginger root if using
4. Cover and simmer until the veggies are tender
5. Combine cornstarch, sugar and water
6. Stir into meat mixture
7. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly
8. Cook until thickened
9. Serve on rice<|endoftext|>
