What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Cutman S Favorite Raised Yeast Waffles

Ingredients:
• Yeast
• 2% low-fat milk
• Water
• Butter
• Salt
• Sugar
• Flour
• Eggs
• Baking soda
• Vanilla flavoring
• Butter flavoring

Steps:
1. Combine yeast, sugar, and warm water in a large mixing bowl
2. Let sit for about 10 minutes, so yeast can proof
3. Add milk, butter, salt, butter flavoring, vanilla flavoring, and flour to the yeast mixture and beat until smooth
4. Cover the bowl with saran plastic wrap and let stand overnight, or at least 6 hours at room temperature
5. The next morning, add the eggs and baking soda
6. Place in lightly oiled waffle iron, bake until desired doneness
7. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
