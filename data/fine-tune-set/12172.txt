Ingredients:
• Pie crust
• Baking apples
• Lemon, juice of
• Sour cream
• Flour
• White sugar
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Butter
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Line the bottom of a deep dish pie plate with pastry, flute edges
2. Peel and slice apples thinly and place in a bowl
3. Squeeze the lemon juice over the sliced apples
4. Toss to coat with the juice
5. Then mix in the 3 tablespoons sour cream, mix well
6. Set aside
7. Prepare the crumb topping
8. To make the crumb topping: in a small bowl mix the flour and sugar together
9. Cut in the cold butter until crumbly
10. In another small bowl, mix the 1-1 / 2 tablespoon flour, 1 / 3 cup sugar, cinnamon and nutmeg
11. Pour the flour mixture over the apples
12. Toss well to coat
13. Spoon the apple mixture into prepared pie shell
14. Drizzle with the 3 tablespoons melted butter
15. Sprinkle with the crumb topping
16. Place the pie on a foil-lined cookie sheet
17. Bake on the lowest oven rack position at 375 degrees for approximately 1 hour, or until the filling is bubbly and apples feel soft<|endoftext|>
