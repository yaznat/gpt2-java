Ingredients:
• Fresh cilantro
• Balsamic vinegar
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Adobo seasoning
• Dried oregano
• Pepper
• Red chili pepper flakes
• Rib eye steaks
• Herb seasoning mix
• Butter

Steps:
1. To make chimichurri, combine cilantro, vinegar, oil, garlic, adobo, oregano, pepper, and chile flakes
2. Rinse beef, pat dry, and rub all over with steak seasoning mix
3. In a 10- to 12-inch nonstick frying pan over medium-high heat, melt 1 teaspoon butter
4. Lay steaks in pan and cook, turning as needed, to brown all sides, including edges, about 10 minutes total for very rare
5. For medium rare, cook 2 or 3 minutes more
6. To reduce spattering, wipe fat from pan with paper towels
7. Transfer steaks to a platter
8. Keep warm
9. If dark charred bits are present in pan, wipe out with a paper towel
10. Add remaining butter to pan and stir
11. When melted, add chimichurri sauce
12. Cut meat into 4 portions and set on plates
13. Pour meat juices from platter into frying pan, then ladle sauce over steaks<|endoftext|>
