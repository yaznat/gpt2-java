Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Whole wheat bread
• Lime
• Salt

Steps:
1. Smash avocado on a plate with a fork
2. Spread between two pieces of bread
3. Serve
4. Omit the bread and the paste alone makes a good first baby food
5. If i'm packing this for lunch sometimes i'll rub the avocado with a cut lime before making the paste
6. For the adults we add a pinch of salt
7. Baby / toddler - no salt<|endoftext|>
