Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown rice flour
• Cornstarch
• Tapioca flour
• Xanthan gum
• Kosher salt
• Baking soda
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• Egg yolk
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over low heat
2. In separate bowl, combine brown rice flour, cornstarch, tapioca flour, xnthan gum, salt & baking soda
3. In mixing bowl, pour melted butter over brown sugar & sugar
4. Mix together until creamed
5. Add egg & egg yolk, milk & vanilla & mix until incorporated
6. Slowly add flour and mix until combined
7. Add chocolate chips and stir until combined
8. Chill in the refrigerator until firm, about 1 hour
9. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper
10. Scoop dough into 1 inch balls
11. Bake in 375 oven for about 10-12 minutes, or until golden brown
12. Remove from the oven, let cool on the pans for a couple minutes
13. Remove from the pan to finish cooling<|endoftext|>
