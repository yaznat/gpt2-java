What's a good custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Banana Coconut Custard

Ingredients:
• Half-and-half
• Coconut milk
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Coconut
• Banana
• Vanilla
• Salt

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients together
2. Butter a 1 1 / 2 quart souffle dish and pour in custard mixture
3. Bake at 350 for 50 minutes<|endoftext|>
