Please give me a recipe for coffee.<|endoftext|>Apple Coffee Can Bread

Ingredients:
• Cooking apples
• Sugar
• Pecans
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Ground allspice
• Ground nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Butter
• Vanilla extract
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees
2. Grease and flour three 1-pound coffee cans
3. Tie a double band of aluminum foil around the cans to extend 2 inches above the edges of the cans
4. Grease the foil
5. Peel, core, and finely dice the apples
6. Place in a large bowl and mix with the sugar and pecans
7. Sift in the flour, baking soda, and spices
8. Mix well
9. Melt the butter in a small saucepan and stir in the vanilla
10. Lightly beat the eggs
11. Stir the eggs and butter into the apple mixture
12. Spoon the batter into the cans and bake for 1 1 / 4 hours or until a skewer inserted in the center comes out clean
13. Cool in the cans for 10 minutes
14. Remove to a wire rack to finish cooling<|endoftext|>
