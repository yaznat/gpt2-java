Give me a pizza recipe.<|endoftext|>100 Whole Grain Pizza Dough

Ingredients:
• Honey
• Active dry yeast
• Warm water
• Salt
• Whole wheat flour
• Buckwheat flour
• Soy flour
• Gluten

Steps:
1. Dissolve honey and yeast in warm water, let sit for about 7 minutes
2. Stir in salt, flours, and gluten
3. Knead for 5 minute place dough in bowl sprayed with cooking spray
4. Cover and let rise 45 minutes
5. Punch down and divide in half
6. Roll out and place on 2 12 inch pizza pan sprayed with cooking spray
7. Preheat oven to 450 degrees and bake for 5 minutes
8. Remove from oven and put on selected toppings
9. I use roasted veggies
10. Bake for 15 minutes
