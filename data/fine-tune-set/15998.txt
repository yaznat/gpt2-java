Give me a quesadilla recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Or Cherry Or Blueberry Pie Quesadilla

Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• Butter
• Flour tortillas
• Apple pie filling
• Cheddar cheese
• Sugar
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Spray iron skillet or cookie sheet with cooking spray
3. Microwave butter, cover with a paper towl and microwave about 15 seconds until melted
4. Place 1 tortilla in skillet or cookie sheet
5. Spoon fruit onto middle of tortilla leaving about 1 / 2 inch all around
6. Sprinkle with cheese
7. Place the second tortilla on top
8. Drizzle melted butter evenly over the crust
9. Mix the sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl
10. Sprinkle over tortilla
11. Bake about 8-10 minutes until just beginning to brown
12. Remove from oven and cut into 4 wedges
13. Serve with vanilla ice cream!<|endoftext|>
