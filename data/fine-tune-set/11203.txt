Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Barbecue sauce
• Mayonnaise
• Sweet onion
• Celery
• Salt
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Kaiser rolls
• Tomatoes
• Lettuce leaves

Steps:
1. Place the chicken in a large resealable plastic bag
2. Add barbecue sauce
3. Seal the bag and turn over to coat chicken
4. Refrigerate at least 2 hours, preferably overnight
5. Grill chicken, covered, over medium hot heat for 6-8 minutes on each side or until juices run clear
6. Or broil for about same amount of time
7. Cool
8. Cover and refrigerate chicken until chilled
9. Chop chicken and place in a bowl
10. Stir in the mayonnaise, onion, celery, salt and pepper flakes
11. Serve on roll or bun with tomato and lettuce
12. Enjoy!
13. Makes 8 servings<|endoftext|>
