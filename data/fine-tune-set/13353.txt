Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Lemon juice
• Salt
• Onions
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic
• Fresh ginger
• Chili powder
• Diced tomatoes
• Dried thyme
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Natural-style peanut butter
• Chicken stock

Steps:
1. Stir lemon juice with chicken pieces and season with salt
2. Set aside for 10 minutes
3. Heat vegetable or peanut oil in skillet and saute chicken until cooked through
4. Refrigerate chicken
5. Saute onions and peppers until tender in vegetable or peanut oil
6. Add garlic, ginger and chili powder and cook, stirring constantly, 5 minutes
7. Stir in tomatoes, thyme and crushed red pepper
8. Bring to simmer for 5 minutes
9. Add peanut butter and stir until blended
10. Stir in chicken stock or broth and return to simmer
11. Add cooked chicken
12. Reduce heat and simmer, stirring frequently, until thickened and reduced
13. Remove from heat and cool
14. Freeze in bags or pans
15. Reheating: defrost
16. Transfer to saucepan and heat thoroughly
17. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
