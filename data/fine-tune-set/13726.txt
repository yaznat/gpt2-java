What's a good pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Onion Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Applesauce
• Brown sugar
• Dry onion soup mix
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Salt and pepper chops and quickly brown
2. Mix the remaining ingredients
3. Place chops in baking dish and top with applesauce mixture
4. Bake 375 for 40 minutes<|endoftext|>
