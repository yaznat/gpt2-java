Generate a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>The Ramchandani S Homemade Famous Mango Milkshake Amras

Ingredients:
• Alphonso mangoes
• Sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Put the diced mangoes in a large mixing bowl
2. Add sugar
3. Using an electric beater, beat well until the mango pieces break and become mushy
4. Beat really well!
5. Then add cold milk, a little at a time
6. Beat well again until not even a tiny piece of mango remains -- you will get an aromatic mango smell emanating from the mixing bowl and a beautiful thick milkshake
7. Add some freshly grated coconut for a nice variation
8. Cover the bowl
9. Refrigerate until it's nice and cold
10. Serve chilled
11. This is to be served with puris
