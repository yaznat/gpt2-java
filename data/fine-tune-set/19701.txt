Give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Baked Sesame Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Olive oil
• Gomashio

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400f
2. Dice the potatoes to desired size and place in a bowl with some cold water
3. Let sit 5 minutes
4. Drain potatoes and toss with the oil and gomashio
5. Lay potatoes on a non stick cookie sheet
6. Bake 30 minutes, flipping after 15 minutes
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
