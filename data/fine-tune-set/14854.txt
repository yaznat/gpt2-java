What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Cajun Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Toasted sesame oil
• Garlic cloves
• Gingerroot
• Tabasco sauce
• Crayfish tails
• Eggs
• Salt
• Peanut oil
• Onion
• Cooked rice
• Bean sprouts
• Frozen green pea
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In small bowl combine soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and 2 tsp tabasco
2. Mix well
3. Stir in crawfish and set aside
4. In another bowl beat eggs with 1 / 4 tsp salt, remaining sesame oil, and tabasco
5. Heat wok or large skillet until hot
6. Add 1 tbsp oil and crawfish mixture
7. Stir-fry 3-4 minutes or until crawfish are done
8. Remove from wok and set aside
9. Add another tbsp oil to wok
10. Add onion
11. Stir-fry 1-2 minutes and remove
12. Heat remaining 1 tbsp oil in wok
13. Add egg mixture
14. Stir-fry 1 minute or until done
15. Add cold rice and stir-fry 3-4 minutes
16. Add everything to wok and cook 2 minutes longer<|endoftext|>
