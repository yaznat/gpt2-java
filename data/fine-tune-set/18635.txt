Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon
• Vanilla
• Apples
• Pecans
• Boiling water
• Cornstarch
• Cold water
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. For apple pudding:
2. Cream butter and sugar
3. Add egg and beat well
4. Add vanilla
5. Combine flour, soda, salt, nutmeg and cinnamon
6. Add to creamed mixture
7. Add grated apples and pecans
8. Spoon mixture into a greased 8 x 8 inch baking pan
9. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 30 minutes, serve warm with butterscotch sauce
10. May garnish with toasted pecans
11. For sauce: boil water in a saucepan and add brown sugar
12. Stir until smooth
13. In a small bowl, mix cornstarch, salt and cold water until smooth paste
14. Add slowly to sugar mixture while stirring constantly
15. Cook for approximately 10 minutes
16. Add vanilla, butter and lemon juice
17. Serve warm over apple pudding<|endoftext|>
