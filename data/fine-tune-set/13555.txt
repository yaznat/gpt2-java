Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Awesome Shredded Beef Burritos Or Tacos Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Beef roast
• Ortega taco seasoning
• Diced tomatoes with green chilies
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Place roast in crock pot
2. Sprinkle with taco seasoning, salt and pepper
3. Top with diced tomatoes
4. Cook on low 8-10 hours
5. Remove beef from crock pot and shred with two forks
6. Return beef to crock pot for 30 minutes or so
7. Serve rolled in flour tortillas with your favorite toppings or in taco shells with your favorite toppings<|endoftext|>
