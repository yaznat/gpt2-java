What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Air Fried Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork loin chops
• Dijon mustard
• Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cayenne pepper
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to broil
2. Thickly coat pork chops with dijon mustard
3. In a shallow bowl, combine bread crumbs and seasonings
4. Dredge pork chops in crumbs
5. Arrange pork chops on a wire rack set on top of a sheet pan
6. Spritz bread crumb coating with a little bit of oil spray
7. Place under broiler and cook on both sides until coating is brown, about 5 minutes on each side<|endoftext|>
