Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Basic Buttermilk Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Sift together the dry ingredients
2. Add the buttermilk, eggs and melted butter, mixing until there are no more lumps
3. This will make a very thick batter
4. Bake in a waffle iron according to the manufacturer's directions<|endoftext|>
