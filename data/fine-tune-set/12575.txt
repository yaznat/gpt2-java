Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Onion
• Garlic
• Beef broth
• Water
• Pearl barley
• Ketchup
• Paprika
• Salt
• Pepper
• Fresh mushrooms
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Brown meat, onion and garlic in 2t
2. Olive oil in a 3-4qt
3. Saucepan
4. Stir in remaining ingredients except mushrooms and sour cream
5. Bring mixture to a boil
6. Cover
7. Cook over low heat for about 1 hour and 15 minutes
8. Add mushrooms
9. Continue cooking 15 more minutes
10. Remove from heat and stir in sour cream
11. Serve over hot cooked noodles or rice
12. Crock pot variation: reduce water to 1 / 2cup
13. Combine all ingredients except mushrooms and sour cream and place in crock pot
14. Cover
15. Cook on low heat 7-8 hours
16. Stir in mushrooms
17. Cover, cook on high heat for 15 minutes
18. Turn off heat and stir in sour cream<|endoftext|>
