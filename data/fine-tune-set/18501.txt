Give me a waffle recipe.<|endoftext|>Golden Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Egg yolks
• Milk
• Butter
• Egg whites

Steps:
1. Sift together the dry ingredients
2. Beat the egg yolks
3. Add the milk
4. Add to the dry ingredients
5. Mix until smooth
6. Add the melted butter
7. Beat egg whites until stiff
8. Fold into batter
9. Bake in a waffle iron until golden<|endoftext|>
