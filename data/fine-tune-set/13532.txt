Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Lavender honey
• Lavender
• Garlic clove
• Lemon
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Mix all the marinade ingredients together and brush over beef, lamb or chicken
2. Allow the meat to marinate in the marinade for 2 hours minimum and then cook as required
3. This marinade is great for the barbeque
4. If making ahead, store in a covered bottle or jar and keep in the fridge until required<|endoftext|>
