What's a good curry recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus With Curry Butter

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Curry powder
• Lemon juice
• Salt
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Shallot
• Asparagus

Steps:
1. Combine butter, curry powder, lemon juice and salt in a small bowl, creating a curry butter
2. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat
3. Add shallot and cook, stirring, until softened, about 2 minutes
4. Add asparagus and cook, stirring, until just tender, 3 to 5 minutes
5. Stir the curry butter into the asparagus
6. Toss to coat and serve<|endoftext|>
