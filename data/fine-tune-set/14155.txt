Ingredients:
• Beef
• Green onions
• Firm tofu
• Green asparagus
• Fresh ginger
• Cornflour
• Water
• Oil
• Low sodium beef broth
• Brown sugar
• Oyster sauce
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Bring 2 to 3 cups water to a boil in a saucepan over medium-high heat
2. When it is boiling rapidly, add all the asparagus except the
3. When the water begins boiling again, add the
4. The aim is for the asparagus to still be crunchy, so judge cooking time by whether it is thin or thick
5. Drain and reserve
6. Mix cornflour with water and keep handy
7. Heat oil in a frying pan or wok
8. Cook the beef, green onion and grated ginger until the beef shows just a little red
9. Add the beef broth, sugar, oyster sauce, soy sauce and tofu, being careful not to break up the tofu very much
10. Simmer over medium heat 2 to 3 minutes, until the pieces of tofu are warm inside
11. Stir in the asparagus
