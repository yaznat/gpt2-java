Ingredients:
• Bearnaise sauce
• Fresh asparagus
• Fresh crabmeat
• Garlic
• Butter
• Lemon juice
• Beef tenderloin steaks
• Paprika

Steps:
1. Prepare bearnaise sauce according to package directions
2. Steam asparagus to desired doneness
3. In a skillet, saute crab and garlic in butter for 3-4 minutes or until heated through
4. Stir in lemon juice and keep warm
5. Grill steaks to desired doneness
6. Top with crab mixture, asparagus and bearnaise sauce
7. Sprinkle with paprika<|endoftext|>
