Ingredients:
• Canola oil
• Scallions
• Fresh ginger
• Small shrimp
• Green cabbage
• Toasted sesame oil
• Brown rice
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a very large nonstick skillet or wok over a high heat
2. Add the scallions, ginger and shrimp and cook stirring frequently until the shrimp turn pink, about 1 1 / 2 minutes
3. Add the cabbage and continue cooking until it begins to soften, but is still somewhat crisp, about 2 minutes more
4. Transfer the shrimp-cabbage mixture to a bowl
5. Heat the sesame oil in the same skillet or wok over a medium-high heat
6. Add the rice and cook stirring frequently, until heated through, about 3 minutes
7. Add the shrimp-cabbage mixture back to the skillet, stir in the soy sauce and sesame seeds and serve<|endoftext|>
