Ingredients:
• Oil
• Soy sauce
• Dried onion
• Garlic cloves
• Lime juice
• Lemon juice
• Cayenne
• Tandoori paste
• Dark brown sugar

Steps:
1. In a food processer or blender, put in oil, soy sauce, onion, garlic, lime and lemon juices, cayenne, tandoori paste, and sugar and puree
2. In a gallon size zip top bag, put pieces of selected meat and pour in marinade
3. Seal bag, removing as much air as possible
4. Allow steak to marinate for at least 1 hour in refrigerator
5. Then grill or broil selected meat to doneness<|endoftext|>
