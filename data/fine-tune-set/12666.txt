Ingredients:
• Beef stew meat
• Onion
• Garlic
• Carrots
• Celery
• Potatoes
• Beer
• Salt and pepper
• Oregano
• Tomato paste
• Butter
• Flour

Steps:
1. Cut the beef into 1" squares
2. Put all but the flour and butter into a slow cooker
3. Put the cooker on low and cook 8-10 hours
4. After 8-10 hours, mix the butter with the flour into a paste
5. Stir the paste into the stew
6. Adjust seasonings with salt and pepper or anything else you desire
