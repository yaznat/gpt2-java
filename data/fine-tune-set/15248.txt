Ingredients:
• Refrigerated crescent dinner rolls
• Bacon
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Milk
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Green onion
• Red bell pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Spray 13 x 9-inch baking dish with nonstick cooking spray
3. Separate biscuit dough and arrange in rectangle on lightly floured surface
4. Roll into 14x 10- inch rectangle
5. Place in prepared dish
6. Pat edges up sides of dish
7. Bake 15 minutes
8. Remove from oven and set aside
9. Meanwhile, place bacon in single layer in large skillet
10. Cook over medium heat until crisp
11. Remove from skillet
12. Drain on paper towels
13. Crumble and set aside
14. Melt butter in medium saucepan over medium heat
15. Stir in flour, salt and black pepper until smooth
16. Gradually stir in milk
17. Cook and stir until thickened
18. Stir in cheese until melted
19. Spread sauce evenly over baked crust
20. Arrange bacon, green onions and bell pepper over sauce
21. Bake, uncovered, 20 minutes or until crust is golden brown<|endoftext|>
