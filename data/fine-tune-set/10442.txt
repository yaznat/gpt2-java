Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Icing sugar
• Kirsch
• Anise
• Salt
• Plain flour

Steps:
1. Mix sugar and eggs using a blender until they are very soft
2. Add the remaining ingredients and knead into dough
3. For aniskrabeli form on a floured surface rolls 1
4. 5 cm thick
5. Cut pieces of 4-5 centimeters of length
6. Make 3 oblique cuts and fold your cookies slightly
7. For anisbroetli you need a stamp
8. In this case roll out your dough on a floured surface or make small balls with your hands
9. Print the shape with your favorite stamp
10. Put the small pieces onto a baking tray and dry for about 12- 24 hours at room temperature
11. Bake them for 15-25 minutes with 150 degrees celsius
12. These cookies shouldnt turn brown!
13. Let them cool down and enjoy<|endoftext|>
