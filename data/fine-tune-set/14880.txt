Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Creole Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Long-grain rice
• Chicken broth
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• Creole seasoning
• Vegetable oil
• Andouille sausages
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Okra
• Plum tomatoes
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Cook rice according to package directions, substituting chicken broth for water
2. Spread cooked rice in a thin layer on a baking sheet
3. Let cool 30 minutes or until completely cool
4. Cut chicken thighs into 1-inch pieces, and toss with 1 teaspoons creole seasoning
5. Cook chicken in hot oil in a large skillet over medium heat 3 minutes
6. Add sausage, and cook 3 to 4 minutes or until lightly browned
7. Add onion, bell pepper, and garlic, and cook 5 minutes or until onion is tender
8. Stir in okra and remaining 1 / 2 teaspoons creole seasoning
9. Increase heat to high
10. Add rice, and cook, stirring constantly, 4 minutes or until thoroughly heated
11. Stir in tomatoes
12. Sprinkle with sliced green onions, and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
