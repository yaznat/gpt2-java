Ingredients:
• Cooked brown rice
• Black beans
• Garlic cloves
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Red chile
• Ricotta cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Salsa
• Sharp cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Heat oil in a medium skillet
3. Add onion and chilies
4. Cook until softened, about 5 to 7 minutes
5. Add garlic, cumin and chili powder and cook 2 minutes more
6. In a large bowl, combine rice, beans and onion mixture
7. Spray a 2-quart casserole with non-stick spray
8. Spread 1 / 3 of the rice / bean mixture
9. Top that with 1 / 2 of the ricotta cheese
10. Top that with 1 / 2 of the monterey jack cheese
11. Repeat, ending with the rice / bean mixture
12. Cover and bake for 30 minutes
13. Remove from the oven
14. Cover casserole with the salsa
15. Sprinkle evenly with cheddar cheese
16. Bake 5 to 10 minutes more, until cheese is melted and lightly browned<|endoftext|>
