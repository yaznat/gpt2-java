Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Vidalia onions
• Garlic cloves
• Water
• Fresh spinach
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Plum tomatoes
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Cooked bacon
• Balsamic vinegar
• Angel hair pasta
• Asiago cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add onion
3. Saut 2 minutes
4. Add garlic
5. Saut 1 minute
6. Add water, crushed red pepper & spinach
7. Cover and cook 2 minutes or until spinach wilts
8. Stir in tomato, chicken, bacon, and vinegar
9. Cook 1 minute or until thoroughly heated
10. Serve over pasta
11. Sprinkle with cheese<|endoftext|>
