What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>All American Apple Pie Spread

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Apple juice
• Brown sugar
• Apple pie spice
• Apple
• Pecans

Steps:
1. In small mixing bowl, beat cream cheese and juice on medium speed until fluffy
2. Beat in brown sugar and apple pie spice or cinnamon
3. Stir in apple and pecans
4. Garnish as desired
5. Serve with crackers<|endoftext|>
