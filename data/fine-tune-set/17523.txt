Ingredients:
• Butter
• White whole wheat flour
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Walnuts
• Grapeseed oil
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Flax seed meal
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Low-fat buttermilk
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Lightly butter and flour a 9-inch spring form pan
3. To make the streusel topping: combine the butter, flour, brown sugar and cinnamon
4. Cut the butter in with your fingers until the mixture forms coarse crumbs
5. Stir in the nuts and set aside
6. To make the cake: in a large bowl, combine the oil, sugars, eggs and vanilla and beat with a wooden spoon or electric mixer until smooth
7. In a medium bowl, combine the flours, flaxseed, baking powder, baking soda and salt
8. Stir to blend
9. Add the creamed mixture alternately with the buttermilk or yogurt in 2 increments
10. Beat until smooth
11. Stir in the berries
12. Turn into the prepared pan and sprinkle evenly with the topping
13. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, or until cake is golden brown and a cake tester inserted in the center comes out clean
14. Let cool in the pan on a wire rack for 10 minutes, then, remove the pan sides and let cool completely
15. Cut into wedges to serve<|endoftext|>
