Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Egg
• Sugar
• Water
• Vegetable oil
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Melt chocolate in microwave
2. Cool
3. Mix flour, baking soda, and salt
4. In lg bowl, beat egg and sugar
5. Beat in water and oil
6. Stir in chocolate and dry ingredients
7. Pour into greased 8x8 baking pan
8. Sprinkle with chocolate chips
9. Bake at 350 for 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
