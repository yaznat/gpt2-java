Ingredients:
• Round steaks
• Egg
• Milk
• Flour
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Paprika
• White pepper
• Cumin
• Ground habanero chile pepper
• Oil
• Water
• Chicken bouillon granule

Steps:
1. Beat together the egg and milk then set aside
2. Combine the flour and spices, mix well, and spread onto a flat tray or plate
3. Dip each steak into the milk / egg mixture and dredge each in the flour mixture, then dip back into the milk / egg mixture
4. Heat 1 / 2 inch deep cooking oil in a large heavy skillet over medium-high heat for a few minutes
5. Check the temperature with a drop of the egg / milk mixture
6. If it pops and sizzles, it's ready
7. Carefully place each steak into the hot oil, allowing to fry for 3 to 5 minutes, then turn over and cook another 3 to 5 minutes, depending upon the size of the cutlets
8. Remove steaks from oil and allow to drain on paper towel
9. Leave about 3-4 tablespoons of the oil from frying the steak in the skillet and reduce heat to medium
10. Combine all ingredients for gravy on a bowl and mix until flour is desolved
11. Pour into skillet and cook until desired thickness is reached<|endoftext|>
