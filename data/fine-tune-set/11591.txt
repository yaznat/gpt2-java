Ingredients:
• Oil
• Yellow cake mix
• Instant vanilla pudding
• Water
• Apple pie filling
• Egg

Steps:
1. In 9x13 pan, pour oil
2. Covering bottom and sides
3. Add cake mix, pudding, water and egg
4. Mix with fork
5. Moistening all dry ingredients
6. Pour in pie filling
7. Swirl into mixture
8. Bake at 350 for 35 minutes, or until toothpick comes out clean<|endoftext|>
