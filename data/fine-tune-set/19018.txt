Ingredients:
• Ground pecans
• Sugar
• Unsalted butter
• Semisweet chocolate
• Heavy cream
• Grapefruit juice
• Fresh orange juice
• Eggs
• Grapefruit, zest of
• Unflavored gelatin
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Crust:
2. In a small bowl, combine the pecans, sugar and melted butter
3. Pat into a 9" glass pie dish
4. Chill for 30 minutes
5. Preheat oven to 375f
6. Bake for 10 minutes
7. Set aside to cool to room temperature
8. Spread the melted chocolate over the inside of the crust and set aside
9. Custard filling:
10. In a 2-quart saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat
11. Add the sugar and stir until dissolved
12. Add the cream, 1 / 2 cup grapefruit juice, the orange juice, eggs and grapefruit zest
13. Sprinkle the gelatin over the remaining grapefruit juice and set aside
14. Cook the cream-citrus mixture over low heat, stirring constantly, until it thickens and coats the back of a spoon
15. Do not allow it to boil
16. Add the gelatin mixture and stir until dissolved
17. Cool to room temperature
18. Pour custard into the chocolate-coated crust and refrigerate until set, about 2 hours
19. Serve with whipped cream<|endoftext|>
