Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Cookie Bites

Ingredients:
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Butter
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Egg
• Granulated sugar
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Melt 1 / 2 cup morsels and butter over hot water, stirring until smooth
2. Let cool slightly
3. Combine flour baking powder
4. Set aside
5. Beat egg and sugar in large mixer bowl at high speed, about 3 minutes or until mixture is thick
6. Stir in vanilla and melted chocolate mixture
7. Gradually blend in flour mixture
8. Stir in remaining 1 cup chocolate chips
9. Drop by level tablespoons onto greased baking sheets
10. Bake in preheated 350 degree fahrenheit oven for 8 to 15 minutes or until cookies are puffed and tops are cracked and moist
11. Baking time will depend on how small or big you make the cookies
12. Let stand for 5 minutes
13. Remove from baking sheets
14. Cool on wire racks<|endoftext|>
