What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Awesome Lasagna Casserole

Ingredients:
• Bulk italian sausage
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Carrot
• Garlic cloves
• Chicken stock
• Tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Bay leaf
• Basil
• Campanelle pasta
• Fresh parsley
• Part-skim ricotta cheese
• Part-skim mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Brown sausage and beef until cooked through, breaking it up as it cooks
2. Drain off any fat
3. Add onion, carrot and garlic
4. Cook until vegetables are soft
5. Add chicken stock and deglaze pan
6. Then add crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, bay leaf and basil
7. Add salt and pepper to taste
8. Bring to a boil then turn down and simmer for 20 minutes
9. Cook pasta as directed in salted water until just al dente
10. Drain
11. Spread pasta into a 9x13" pan
12. Remove bay leaf from sauce and stir in parsley
13. Combine sauce with pasta
14. Drop spoonfuls of ricotta into pasta and stir until combined
15. Sprinkle with cheeses
16. Bake for 20-30 minutes at 350 degrees until cheese is browned and sauce in bubbley<|endoftext|>
