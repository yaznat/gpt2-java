Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f
2. Lightly grease two 9x5 loaf pans
3. Pour your starter into a large plastic bowl and use a wooden spoon to mix in the next 12 ingredients
4. Put the apples and bananas in a food processor for a minute than add to bowl
5. Then mix in the walnuts, dates, and raisins
6. Divide the batter evenly into both pans then cover it loosely with foil
7. Bake for about an hour and twenty minutes<|endoftext|>
