What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Brunch Pizza Squares

Ingredients:
• Pork sausage
• Refrigerated crescent dinner rolls
• Eggs
• Milk
• Pepper
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a skillet, cook sausage over medium heat until no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Unroll crescent roll dough into a lightly greased 9x13 baking pan
4. Press dough 1 / 2 inch up the sides and seal seams
5. Sprinkle with sausage
6. In a bowl, beat the egg, milk and pepper
7. Pour over sausage
8. Sprinkle with cheese
9. Bake, uncovered at 400 for 15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
