Ingredients:
• Milk
• Pumpkin
• Minute white rice
• Sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Ground cloves
• Eggs
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Combine milk, pumpkin, rice, sugar, cinnamon, ginger, salt and cloves to a boil in a large saucepan
2. Stir mixture constantly
3. Reduce heat to low
4. Simmer 5 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Remove pan from heat
6. Beat eggs and vanilla in a large bowl
7. Slowly stir in hot pumpkin mixture, blending well after each addition
8. Pour into greased 2 quart casserole
9. Bake at 375 f for 45 to 50 minutes or until pudding is set
10. Cool slightly
11. Serve warm or refrigerate until ready to serve
12. Garnish as desired
13. Dollop of whipped cream, dash of cinnamon, maybe
14. Put leftovers in the refrigerator<|endoftext|>
