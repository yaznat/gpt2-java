Can you give me a beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Aunt Sharon S Pork And Bean Bake

Ingredients:
• Pork & beans
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Bacon
• Molasses
• Ketchup
• Prepared mustard
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl combine pork & beans, green pepper, onion, bacon, molasses, ketchup, mustard, and worcestershire sauce
2. Stir to incorporate all ingredients
3. Pour into a baking dish and bake at 350 for 30 minutes or until heated through<|endoftext|>
