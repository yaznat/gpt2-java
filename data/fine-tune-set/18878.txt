Ingredients:
• Raisins
• Water
• Orange juice
• Bread
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Eggs
• Baking powder
• Vanilla extract
• Milk
• Half-and-half
• Butter

Steps:
1. Cover raisins with water and orange juice
2. Soak while preparing other ingredients, or cover and refrigerate up to 24 hours
3. In a saucepan, heat milk, half and half, and butter but do not allow to boil
4. Cool slightly
5. Combine brown sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg
6. Reserve 2 tablespoons of brown sugar mixture for sprinkling on top later
7. In a separate saucepan, add the remaining brown sugar mixture, vanilla, and eggs
8. Whisk until smooth
9. Pour heated milk mixture into sugar mixture
10. Add baking powder and whisk until smooth
11. Place 1 / 2 of bread cubes in a 2-quart casserole dish
12. Thoroughly drain raisins and discard soaking liquid
13. Spread 1 / 2 cup raisins over the bread
14. Top with rest of bread cubes
15. Sprinkle remaining raisins on top
16. Pour the milk mixture over the bread until the bread is saturated
17. Let stand for 5 minutes
18. Sprinkle reserved 2 tablespoons of sugar mixture over top
19. Place bread pudding dish in a larger pan
20. Carefully, add very hot water to larger pan to within 1 / 2 inch of tops of casserole dish
21. Bake at 350f degrees for 1 hour 10 minutes to 1 hour 20 minutes or until knife comes out clean when inserted 1 inch from rim
22. Serve warm or cold<|endoftext|>
