Generate a recipe for donuts.<|endoftext|>Youtiao Chinese Deep Fried Donuts

Ingredients:
• Self-rising flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Water
• Peanut oil

Steps:
1. Mix flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt
2. Stir in water
3. Lightly knead dough
4. Loosely cover for about 20 minutes, or enough time for the dough to rise
5. On floured surface, roll the dough into 2 inch wide and 14 inch long strips
6. Twist together in pairs, pinching the ends
7. Holding each end of twists, pull until 9 inches long
8. Deep fry each twist in peanut oil until golden brown
9. Let cool and eat<|endoftext|>
