Please give me a recipe for a quesadilla.<|endoftext|>Corn Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Corn kernels
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Scallion
• Water
• Oil
• Flour tortillas
• White cheddar cheese
• Cilantro
• Poblano chiles
• Guacamole
• Mexican crema
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in a medium pan and add corn, salt and pepper
2. Cook over medium heat 1 minute
3. Add scallion and water, cover and cook until corn is tender, 3 to 4 minutes
4. Transfer to a bowl
5. Heat a large cast-iron skillet or griddle and add just enough oil to coat
6. Place 1 tortilla in pan and heat 30 seconds until soft, flip and heat other side
7. Repeat to heat all tortillas, adding oil to pan as needed
8. Keep them warm by covering with clean kitchen towels
9. Spread 1 / 3 cup corn mixture on half of each tortilla
10. Follow with cheese and cilantro, distributing evenly over corn
11. Top each with diced chiles
12. Fold tortilla over filling
13. Heat skillet, adding oil as needed, and place 1 or 2 quesadillas in pan
14. Cook over medium-low heat, turning when crisp and golden to cook other side
15. Serve whole or cut in wedges
16. Garnish and serve with guacamole, crema, cilantro sprigs and salsa<|endoftext|>
