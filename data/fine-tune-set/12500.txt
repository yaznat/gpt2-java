Give me a recipe for stew.<|endoftext|>Alicha Ethiopian Stew

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Red onions
• Garlic
• New potatoes
• Carrots
• Cabbage
• Chili peppers
• Salt and pepper
• Nutmeg
• Cardamom
• Parsley
• Basil

Steps:
1. Cook onions and garlic in oil until transparent
2. Wash and chop vegetables and add them to the onions, lightly browning
3. Add nutmeg, garlic and cardamom then cook slowly for 30 mins
4. Season to taste then add chilli, parsley and basil just before serving<|endoftext|>
