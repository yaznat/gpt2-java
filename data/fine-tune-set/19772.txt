Ingredients:
• Apple cider
• Butter
• Coarse salt
• Apples
• Thyme
• Corn flakes
• Salt
• Parsley
• Chicken breast
• Sweet chili sauce

Steps:
1. In large saucepan over mediumhigh heat, boil cider until reduced to 1 / 3 cup, about 20 minutes
2. Remove from heat
3. Whisk in butter and salt
4. Preheat oven to 400 f
5. In bowl, toss apples with 2 tbsp of the glaze and 1 / 2 tbsp of thyme
6. Arrange in a single layer on a rimmed baking sheet
7. Set aside
8. In bowl combine corn flake crumbs, salt and parsley
9. Set aside
10. Dip chicken strips in sweet chili sauce and then coat with corn flakes
11. Arrange in a single layer on a greased baking sheet
12. Place chicken on the top third and the apples on the bottom third of the oven
13. Roast for 10 minutes
14. Turn chicken, drizzle apples with remaining glaze and switch baking sheets so the apples are now on the top third of oven
15. Continue to roast for 10 minutes and remove chicken from oven
16. Let chicken fingers cool while apples finish caramelizing, about 5 to 10 minutes more
17. Sprinkle apples with remaining thyme and additional salt if desired<|endoftext|>
