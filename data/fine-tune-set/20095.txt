Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Double cream
• Truffles
• Butter
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Make sure your truffle is thoroughly clean, slice it thinly or grate it and poach it in a little water with the salt and pepper, for just four or five minutes
2. Reduce the water to a dessertspoonful, allow it cool and add it to the cream
3. Whisk your eggs, add the poaching water and cream to them and whisk again
4. Heat your pan gently & add the butter to the pan, allow it become very hot, but not discoloured, then
5. Then add the sliced truffles
6. Allow the top to almost set baveuse - fold and serve immediately
7. Serve with a fresh green salad, some warm crusty bread and a good bottle of french red wine<|endoftext|>
