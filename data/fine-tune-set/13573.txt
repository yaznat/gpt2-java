Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Garlic clove
• Salt and pepper
• Onion
• Bell pepper
• Mushrooms
• Chopped tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Worcestershire sauce
• Red chili pepper
• Brown sugar
• Red wine
• Cheddar cheese
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. In a large oven-proof fry pan, brown the beef until no longer pink, about 8-10 minutes
2. If the beef is really lean, add a little oil to the pan so it doesn't stick
3. Stir in the grated garlic, salt and pepper
4. Remove beef with a slotted spoon to a bowl and set aside
5. Do not drain the pan
6. Add the onion and pepper to the pan and saute until onion becomes translucent, about 5 minutes
7. Add mushrooms and continue to cook another 5-7 minutes
8. In the meantime, mix together the chopped tomatoes, tomato paste, worcestershire sauce, chili pepper, brown sugar and wine
9. Return the meat to the pan and mix thoroughly
10. Add the tomato mixture and stir to combine
11. Lower heat and let simmer for 5-10 minutes
12. If mixture seems dry, add some more wine
13. While mixture is simmering, preheat your oven to 190c / 375f
14. Put the pan in the oven and bake for 30-40 minutes
15. Sprinkle the cheese over top and return to oven for a further 5-10 minutes until cheese melts and starts to turn golden
16. Remove from oven and let sit 5 minutes
17. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
