Give me a recipe for ice cream.<|endoftext|>Allspice Cream Cheese Frosting

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Butter
• Ground allspice
• Confectioners' sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a medium bowl, beat cream cheese, butter and allspice until blended
2. Gradually add confectioner's sugar and mix until well combined
3. Stir in vanilla
4. Gradually add milk, beating until frosting is of spreading consistency<|endoftext|>
