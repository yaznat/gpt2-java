What's a recipe for pudding?<|endoftext|>Apple Prune And Peach Christmas Pudding

Ingredients:
• Pitted prunes
• Dried peaches
• Currants
• Brown sugar
• Butter
• Orange
• Apples
• Golden syrup
• Eggs
• Breadcrumbs
• Self-raising flour

Steps:
1. Place dried fruit, sugar, butter, juice and rind in a medium saucepan
2. Bring to boil stirring occasionally until butter has melted and sugar dissolved
3. Add apple
4. Transfer to a large bowl to cool
5. Grease a 6 cup pudding basin and line base with baking paper
6. Pour over the golden syrup
7. Stir the eggs into the fruit mixture and mix well
8. Fold through the breadcrumbs and flour
9. Spoon the pudding mixture into prepared basin packing down firmly as you go
10. Cover the basin with a circle of buttered non-stick baking paper and two circles of foil
11. Tie securely with kitchen string
12. Stand pudding on a trivet inside a large saucepan or stockpot
13. Fill with enough hot water to come 2 / 3 up the side of basin
14. Cover pot with a lid and simmer for 5 hours, topping up the pot with boiling water as needed
15. Don't let it boil dry
16. Cool and store in basin in fridge until christmas
17. To reheat, boil pudding using the method above, for 2 hours
18. Serve with custard, brandy cream or icecream and decorate with berries<|endoftext|>
