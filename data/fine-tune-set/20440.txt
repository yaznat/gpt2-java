Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>Another Good Casserole

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Cream cheese
• Milk
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Spaghetti sauce
• Biscuits

Steps:
1. Cook the ground beef and the onion together
2. Drain
3. Mix everything except the biscuits together
4. Pour into a greased 2 quart casserole dish and top with the biscuits
5. Bake at 375 for 30-40 minutes or until biscuits are done and mixture is bubbly<|endoftext|>
