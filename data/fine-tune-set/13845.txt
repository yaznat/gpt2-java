Ingredients:
• Pork loin
• Garlic salt
• Vegetable oil
• Apple juice
• All-purpose flour
• Tart apples
• Raisins
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Whipping cream
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Cut pork into -inch slices
2. Sprinkle each with garlic salt
3. In large skillet, brown pork slices in oil, a few slices at a time
4. Return pork and any juices to skillet
5. Combine apple juice and flour
6. Pour into skillet
7. Add apple slices, raisins, cinnamon and ginger
8. Cook, stirring frequently, until mixture comes to a boil and thickens
9. Cover
10. Reduce heat and simmer 10 minutes or until pork is tender
11. Remove pork, apples and raisins to serving dish
12. Keep warm
13. Bring pan juices to a boil
14. Blend in whipping cream
15. Cook until slightly thickened
16. Pour cream mixture over pork, apples and raisins
17. Sprinkle with green onions<|endoftext|>
