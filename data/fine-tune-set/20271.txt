What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Chicken Broccoli Stir Fry For 2

Ingredients:
• Chicken broth
• Soy sauce
• Ginger
• Pepper
• Cornstarch
• Water
• Peanut oil
• Onions
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Broccoli
• Fresh mushrooms
• Water chestnuts

Steps:
1. Combine broth, soy sauce, ginger and pepper
2. Set aside
3. Mix cornstarch and water
4. Set aside
5. Heat oil in wok or large skillet
6. Add onion and stir fry 2 minute
7. Push away from heat
8. Add chicken and stir fry 2 to 3 minutes
9. Push away from heat
10. Add broccoli matchsticks, mushrooms
11. Stir fry 2 minutes
12. Add water chestnuts, combining with all cooked ingredients, and stir fry 1 minute
13. Add chicken broth mixture
14. Heat until bubbly
15. Slowly stir in cornstarch mixture
16. Heat and stir until thickened and smooth
17. Gently stir until all foods are well coated with sauce
18. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
