Ingredients:
• Cauliflower
• Carrot
• Salt
• Ground ginger
• Ground cumin
• Ground coriander
• Ground nutmeg
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Low-fat sour cream
• Cider vinegar
• Honey
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Steam first 2 ingredients, covered, 2 minutes
2. Rinse mixture under cold water
3. Drain well
4. Combine salt and next 5 ingredients in a small skillet
5. Cook over medium heat 2 minutes or until lightly aromatic, stirring constantly
6. Add vinegar while of the heat
7. Combine spice mixture, sour cream, and honey in a bowl
8. Stir well
9. Add cauliflower mixture
10. Toss well to coat
11. Chill for 1 hour
12. Top with green onions just before serving<|endoftext|>
