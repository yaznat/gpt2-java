What's a good fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Cheese Fries

Ingredients:
• Frozen french fries
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onion
• Bacon
• Ranch salad dressing

Steps:
1. Cook french fries as package directs
2. Place fries on a broiler-proof dish or platter
3. Sprinkle with cheese, onions, and bacon
4. Broil for 1-2 minutes or until cheese is melted
5. Serve with ranch dressing<|endoftext|>
