What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Beyond Easy Chili Cheese Dip

Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Fiesta nacho cheese soup
• Chili

Steps:
1. Beat cream cheese until smooth
2. Combine with soup
3. Fold in chili until well blended
4. I warm mine in a little dipper crockpot
5. For microwave heating, use 30 second intervals & stir well between each<|endoftext|>
