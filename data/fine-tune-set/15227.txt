Ingredients:
• Yellow cake mix
• Quick-cooking rolled oats
• Butter
• Egg
• Nuts
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Blueberry pie filling

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. Grease a 12-inch pizza pan or a 9 x 13 pan
3. In a large bowl, beat cake mix, 1 cup oats and 6 tablespoons butter at low speed until crumbly
4. Reserve 1 cup of crumbs for the topping
5. To the remaining crumbs blend in the egg
6. Press in prepared pan
7. Bake at 350 for 12 minutes
8. Meanwhile, to reserved crumbs in same large bowl, add remaining 1 / 4 cup oats, 2 tablespoons butter, nuts, sugar and cinnamon
9. Beat until well mixed
10. Remove the base from the oven and spread with the pie filling
11. Sprinkle with reserved crumb mixture
12. Return to oven and bake 15-20 minutes or until crumbs are light golden brown
13. Cool completely
14. Cut into wedges or squares<|endoftext|>
