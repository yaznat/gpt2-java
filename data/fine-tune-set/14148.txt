Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• Barbecue sauce
• Hoagie rolls
• Canola oil
• Onion
• Fine sea salt
• Cajun seasoning
• Garlic mayonnaise
• Lettuce leaves
• Tomatoes
• Pickle

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 375f spray a 9x13-inch baking dish with nonstick spray
2. Press tofu gently with paper towels to remove excess water
3. Slice into 8 pieces and transfer to the baking dish
4. Spread with barbeque sauce and bake until firm and lightly browned, about 30 minutes
5. Wrap rolls in foil and heat in the hot oven for last 10 minutes of cooking tofu
6. Meanwhile, heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat
7. Add onion and salt
8. Cook until just beginning to brown, 6 to 7 minutes
9. Cover the skillet, lower the heat and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until onion is browned and meltingly tender, about 10 minutes more
10. Stir in cajun seasoning
11. Open hot rolls and spread with mayonnaise
12. Stuff with tofu, onions, lettuce, tomato and pickle, if using<|endoftext|>
