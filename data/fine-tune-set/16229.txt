Can you give me a wrap recipe?<|endoftext|>A Humdinger Of A Wrap

Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Thai sweet chili sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Chili powder
• Sriracha sauce
• Swiss cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Spinach
• Portabella mushroom
• Red bell peppers
• Summer squash
• Sweet onions
• Fresh basil
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Tortillas
• Chopped tomato
• Avocado
• Chips
• Mixed sprouts

Steps:
1. Mix the mayonnaise, thai sweet chili sauce, worcestershire sauce, chili powder and sriracha sauce in a bowl
2. Add to the sauce cheeses, spinach, mushrooms, bell pepper, squash, onion and basil
3. Stir well to mix thoroughly
4. Season to taste with salt and pepper
5. Divide the mixture between the tortillas
6. Top with the optional topping / toppings if using
7. Wrap and eat immediately
8. Keep napkins handy! enjoy!
9. If tortillas are stiff, you can warm them about 10-15 seconds to make more flexible
10. To wrap:
11. Fold right and left edges of tortilla over the fillinig, toward the center
12. Fold the bottom edge of tortilla toward the center and gently roll until completely wrapped aroung the filling<|endoftext|>
