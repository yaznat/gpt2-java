Ingredients:
• Wild rice
• Apple cider
• Water
• Butter
• Pecans
• Golden raisin
• Currants
• Dried cranberries
• Candied ginger
• Shallot
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Apple

Steps:
1. Wash rice three times, drain well
2. Combine rice, cider and water in pan
3. Bring rice mixture to boil, reduce heat to low and cover and simmer 45 - 60 minutes
4. In a large frying pan, melt butter on low, add pecans, raisins, currants, and cranberries, ginger and shallots, stirring for 5 minutes
5. Next add the salt, pepper, cinnamon, nutmeg, fresh apple and wild rice
6. Cook on low for 2 minutes until heated, serve warm -- enjoy<|endoftext|>
