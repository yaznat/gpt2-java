Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Dry sherry
• Soy sauce
• Garlic clove
• Light brown sugar
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Olive oil
• Chicken breast
• Red onion
• Red cabbage
• Red bell peppers
• Navel orange

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, blend cornstarch with sherry, soy sauce, garlic, brown sugar & red pepper flakes, then set aside
2. In a large nonstick skillet, warm 2 teaspoons of the oil over medium-high heat until hot, but not smoking, then add the chicken, stir-frying until just barely cooked, about 2 minutes
3. With a slotted spoon, transfer the chicken to a plate & cover loosely to keep warm
4. Add 2 teaspoons of the oil to the skillet & heat, then add the onion, stir-frying until softened, about 3 minutes
5. Add the remaining 2 teaspoons of the oil as well as the cabbage & bell peppers & stir-fry until crisp-tender, about 2 minutes
6. Add reserved sherry mixture to the skillet along with the chicken & cook over medium-high heat until the sauce thickens & the chicken is cooked through, about 3 minutes
7. Serve the stir-fry topped with the orange slices<|endoftext|>
