What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Arroz A La Mexicana Mexican Rice

Ingredients:
• Rice
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Chicken stock
• Tomatoes
• Olive oil
• Green peas
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Chili pepper
• Fresh coriander
• Avocado

Steps:
1. Puree the tomatoes, onion, garlic, and cup of the stock in an electric blender or food processor
2. Heat the oil in a saucepan and saut the rice until it turns golden
3. Add the tomato puree, remaining stock, and salt and pepper to taste
4. Bring to a boil, lower the heat to a simmer, and cover until almost all of the liquid has been absorbed
5. Mix in the peas and continue cooking until all the liquid has been absorbed
6. Garnish with the optional garnishes<|endoftext|>
