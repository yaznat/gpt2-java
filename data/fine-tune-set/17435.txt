Ingredients:
• Whole cloves
• Cinnamon sticks
• Green cardamoms
• Brewed coffee
• Brown sugar
• Whipped cream
• Chocolate shavings
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Brew a pot of your favorite coffee, a little too strong
2. In a small pan, add 1 1 / 2 cup water, 4-7 cloves, the cinnamon stick broken up, 4-7 whole cardamom - split the
3. Heat up to boiling then reduce to simmer for 3-4 minutes
4. Mix the coffee and the spice-mix, pour into cups top with whipped cream and sprinkle with chocolate and cinnamon
5. Add more sugar if desired
6. This also makes a great drink with some kahlua or brandy, for a more adult evening drink<|endoftext|>
