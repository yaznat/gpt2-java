Please give me a recipe for chili.<|endoftext|>Best Ever Chunky Chili

Ingredients:
• Dry pinto beans
• Dried navy beans
• Water
• Bay leaf
• Chili powder
• Cumin powder
• Salt
• Dry mustard
• Onion powder
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Celery rib
• Tomato puree
• Tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Ketchup
• Lemon juice
• Brown sugar
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. Cover beans with water and bring to a boil
2. Set aside to soak at least 1 hour
3. Rinse, discarding bean water and recover beans with more water by 2-inches
4. Season and pressure cook,
5. Cook 15 minutes @ sea level or consult the ball blue book
6. Softening beans: never add acidic foods before the beans are fully softened
7. In a 2nd large stock pot, brown the meat and veggies, add remaining ingredients and softened beans, simmer uncovered 1 hour<|endoftext|>
