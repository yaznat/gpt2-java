Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Red pepper flakes
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Poppy seed

Steps:
1. Cream butter
2. Add cheese, salt, pepper and poppy seed
3. Add flour a little at a time
4. Roll in small balls
5. Place on ungreased cookie sheet
6. Flatten with fork
7. Bake 15 minutes at 350f<|endoftext|>
