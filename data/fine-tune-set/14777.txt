Ingredients:
• Pie pastry
• Raspberry jam
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Almond extract
• Salt
• Rice flour
• Icing sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350f
2. Line a 9" square pan with the pastry
3. You may wish to line the bottom of the tin with parchment paper first
4. Spread the jam over the pastry
5. Cream the butter and sugar
6. Beat in the eggs and almond extract
7. Mix in the salt and rice flour
8. Scrape the batter over the jam, and spread it out carefully and evenly
9. Bake at 350f for 30 minutes
10. Let cool
11. Cream the remaining butter, and beat in the icing sugar and almond extract
12. Thin with a little milk until a good spreading consistency is reached
13. About a tablespoon or so should do it
14. Spread the icing over the squares, then cut them and remove them from the pan<|endoftext|>
