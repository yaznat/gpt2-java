Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Salt
• Brown sugar
• Egg
• Egg whites
• Canola oil
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Low-fat sour cream
• Mini chocolate chip

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. In a small bowl, mix together flour, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt
3. In a medium bowl, blend together brown sugar, whole egg, egg whites, and oil with an electric mixer over medium speed
4. Add vanilla, almond extract, and sour cream
5. Beat on low speed until well blended
6. Gradually add flour mixture to sour cream mixture, beating on medium speed
7. Fold in chocolate chips with a spoon
8. Spray and 8x8 pan with non-stick spray
9. Spread batter evenly in pan
10. Bake for 40-45 minutes, until wooden toothpick inserted in center comes out clean
11. Be sure to chekc cake after 40 minutes, as you don't want to overbake it
12. Remove from oven and let cool in pan for 15 minutes
13. Cut into 12 squares
14. For maximum moistness, store at room temperature in an air tight container<|endoftext|>
