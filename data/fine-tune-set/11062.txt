Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Tuna Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Fat-free mayonnaise
• Celery
• Walnuts
• Onions
• Sweet pickle relish
• Sugar
• Salt
• Lemon pepper
• Light chunk tuna in water
• Apple
• Red seedless grapes
• Reduced-calorie whole wheat bread
• Lettuce leaves

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine the eight ingredients
2. Stir in tuna, apple and grapes
3. Spread 1 / 2 cup tuna mixture on three slices of bread
4. Top with lettuce and remaining 3 slices of bread<|endoftext|>
