What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>African Sweet Potato Stew Vegetarian

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Cabbage
• Garlic cloves
• Canned sweet potatoes
• Tomatoes
• Tomato juice
• Apple juice
• Fresh gingerroot
• Red pepper flakes
• Frozen cut green beans
• Natural-style peanut butter

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add onion
3. Cook, stirring, until tender, about 5 minutes
4. Mix in cabbage and garlic
5. Cook, stirring, until cabbage is tender-crisp, about 5 minutes
6. Stir in sweet potatoes, tomatoes, tomato juice, apple juice, ginger, and red pepper flakes
7. Reduce heat to medium-low
8. Cover
9. Simmer until hot and bubbling, about 6 minutes
10. Stir in green beans and simmer, uncovered, for 5 minutes
11. Stir in peanut butter until well blended and hot, about 1 minute
12. Serve stew with crusty bread and a salad, or spoon it over rice or mashed potatoes
13. Helpful hint: to save time, use packaged cabbage slaw mix instead of chopping up a head of cabbage
