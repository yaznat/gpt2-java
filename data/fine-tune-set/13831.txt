Give me a pork recipe.<|endoftext|>Aunt Jeanie S Pork And Beans

Ingredients:
• Dry white bean
• Water
• Oil
• Onion
• Salt
• Bay leaf
• Molasses
• Catsup
• Prepared mustard
• Worcestershire sauce
• Ground ginger
• Salt pork
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Combine the soaked beans, water, oil, salt and bay leaf
2. Bring to a boil, then simmer until beans are tender
3. Add more hot water if necessary
4. Drain, reserving 1 cup liquid
5. Combine the 1 cup liquid with the remaining ingredients in a small bowl
6. Pour over the beans in a casserole dish
7. Cover and bake at 300 degrees f for 1 to 1 1 / 2 hours, adding more liquid if necessary so beans don't dry out
8. This gets better with time-leftovers are great!<|endoftext|>
