Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>Bachelor Pasta

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Roma tomatoes
• Red bell pepper
• Green bell pepper
• Mushroom
• Basil
• Garlic
• Cooked spaghetti
• Olive oil
• Sausage
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Heat the olive oil in a sausepan until it is just starting to smoke
2. In a heavy metal strainer over a heavy metal bowl, put spaghetti noodles
3. Top with the onion, tomatoes, peppers, mushrooms, basil, and garlic
4. Pour the hot oil over the veggies and pasta slowly, being sure to pour oil directly on all veggies
5. Toss veggies and pasta, lift strainer to get rid of excess oil
6. Divide pasta and veggies between two plates, top with sausage and shredded cheese<|endoftext|>
