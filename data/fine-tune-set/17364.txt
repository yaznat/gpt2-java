Please give me a recipe for coffee.<|endoftext|>A Nudge A Coffee Nudge

Ingredients:
• Creme de cacao
• Coffee liqueur
• Hazelnut-flavored liqueur
• Brandy
• Coffee
• Vanilla ice cream
• Whipped cream
• Chocolate-covered coffee beans

Steps:
1. Put the scoope of ice cream in the bottom of a tall mug
2. Pour the creme de cacao, kahlua, brandy and frangelica over the ice cream
3. Slowly pour in the coffee, add the whipped cream and sprinkle the ground coffee beans on top
4. Sit back, relax and enjoy<|endoftext|>
