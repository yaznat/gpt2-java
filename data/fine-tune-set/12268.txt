Ingredients:
• Mushrooms
• Water
• Butter
• Onion
• All-purpose flour
• Seasoning salt
• Evaporated milk

Steps:
1. Drain mushroom liquid into a measuring cup, and add water to make 2 cups
2. Melt butter in large saucepan over medium heat, add onion and cook until softened
3. Remove from heat
4. Stir in flour and seasoning salt
5. Slowly add the mushroom liquid mixture and milk, stirring to prevent lumps
6. Return to heat
7. Add mushrooms and cook until mixture comes to a boil<|endoftext|>
