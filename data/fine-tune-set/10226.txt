Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Bean Turkey Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Red onion
• Garlic
• Water
• Diced tomatoes
• Black beans
• Chili powder
• Sea salt
• Frozen whole kernel corn
• Worcestershire sauce
• Cayenne pepper sauce
• Celery
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Using a pot that accommodates at least three quarts, fry the ground turkey, onion and garlic until the turkey is no longer pink
2. Drain off and remove any excess grease from the fried turkey
3. Add the remaining ingredients and simmer for at least 30 minutes
4. Enjoy with a dollop of sour cream on top!<|endoftext|>
