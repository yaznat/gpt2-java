Can you give me a brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Brownies

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Flour
• Cocoa powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Ground beef

Steps:
1. Mix sugar, vanilla, cocoa, and butter
2. Add eggs one at a time
3. Mix well then add flour and salt
4. Fold in ground beef
5. Bake at 375 in a 10 x 15 pan for 25 minutes
6. Don't over bake<|endoftext|>
