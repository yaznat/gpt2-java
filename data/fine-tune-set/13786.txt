Can you give me a pork recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Barbecued Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients:
• Orange juice
• Splenda granular
• Light soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Sesame oil
• Ginger
• Pork tenderloin

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except pork in small bowl
2. Add pork to shallow dish and coat with sauce
3. Cover and marinate pork several hours or overnight in refrigerator
4. Preheat oven to 425f prepare baking sheet by lining with foil
5. Remove pork from sauce and place on baking sheet
6. Baste with marinade and cook for 20 minutes or until internal temperature reaches 145-150f
7. Slice thin across grain and serve<|endoftext|>
