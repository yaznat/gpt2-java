Can you give me a omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Cheese And Mushroom Pizza Omelette

Ingredients:
• Mushrooms
• Olive oil
• Eggs
• Dried oregano
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Tomato and basil pasta sauce
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Heat half the oil in a frying pan, add the mushrooms and saut for 3-4 mins or until golden
2. Transfer to a plate and set aside
3. Beat the eggs together with the oregano, salt and freshly ground black pepper
4. Heat the remaining oil in the frying pan, add the eggs and cook over a medium heat for 2-3 minutes during this time, use a spatula to push the cooked egg into the centre of the omelette, then
5. Place the omelette under a hot broiler and cook for 1-2 mins or until the top has set
6. Spoon over the tomato sauce and top with the cheese and mushrooms
7. Season with more black pepper and a little salt, to taste
8. Pop back under the boiler and cook for 2 mins or until the cheese is pale golden
9. Serve in wedges with salad<|endoftext|>
