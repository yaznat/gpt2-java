What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Crispy Baked Tofu With Pineapple Avocado Corn Salad A1

Ingredients:
• Extra firm tofu
• A. 1. original sauce
• Cornstarch
• Pineapple juice
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Avocado
• Pineapple
• Red bell pepper
• Frozen corn
• Baby spinach
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cut the tofu into bite-size cubes and place in a medium bowl
2. Toss with 1 / 4 cup a1 sauce
3. Marinate in the refrigerator for 30 minutes or more
4. Toss with the 1 tablespoon cornstarch
5. Preheat oven to 350
6. Using a slotted spoon, transfer tofu cubes to a large nonstick baking sheet and bake for 25-45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and crisp
7. Let cool on baking sheet
8. Make the salad while the tofu is baking
9. Stir together the a1 sauce, pineapple juice, and olive oil in a small bowl
10. Using a large bowl toss the dressing with the remaining ingredients, seasoning with salt and pepper to taste
11. Transfer to a serving bowl and top with the a1 baked tofu<|endoftext|>
