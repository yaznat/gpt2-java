What's a recipe for stew?<|endoftext|>Award Winning Green Chili Stew

Ingredients:
• Hamburger meat
• Flour
• Diced tomatoes
• Green chilies
• Garlic salt
• Cajun seasoning
• Onion
• Potatoes
• Water

Steps:
1. Brown meat
2. Drain off grease
3. Stir in flour
4. Cook together 5 minutes
5. Add tomatoes, chili, onion
6. Stew 5 minutes
7. Add water a few tablespoons at a time, for desired thickness
8. Bring to a boil
9. Add potatoes
10. Slow cook till potatoes are done
11. Salt and pepper to taste
12. May be frozen
13. May also be cooked in crock pot cook meat first, then add all remaining ingredients
14. Slow cook for 5 to 6 hours<|endoftext|>
