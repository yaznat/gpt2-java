Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Black White Vegetarian Chipotle Chili Crock Pot

Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Navy beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Chili powder
• Sweet hungarian paprika
• Dried cilantro
• Fresh coarse ground black pepper
• Chipotle chile

Steps:
1. Stir everything together in a 4 1 / 2 quart or larger slow cooker
2. Set power to low and cook for 6 hours or longer
3. Serve with cheese or sour cream if you wish<|endoftext|>
