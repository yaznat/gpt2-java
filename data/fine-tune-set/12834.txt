Give me a steak recipe.<|endoftext|>Beef Steak

Ingredients:
• Steaks
• Lemon juice
• Soy sauce
• Water
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Flour
• Ground black pepper
• Onion
• Corn oil

Steps:
1. Marinate the steak in lemon juice, soy sauce, garlic, ginger and pepper for 30 minutes
2. Pan fry in 2 tbsp of oil add the marinated steak cook slow until done
3. When steak are cooked remove them from the pan onto a serving dish
4. Dissolve the flour with water
5. Add the remaining oil in the same pan and pan fry the onion until tender and add the dissolve flour for 1 minute
6. Pile on the of steaks serve hot with plain rice<|endoftext|>
