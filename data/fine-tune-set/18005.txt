Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Brownie Batter

Ingredients:
• Dates
• Soymilk
• Cocoa powder
• Water
• Spinach
• Protein powder

Steps:
1. Begin by blending the dates, cocoa powder, protein powder, soymilk, and water
2. The mixture should be completely smooth
3. Next, add 2-3 oz spinach to the blender
4. Blend again, stopping to push the spinach down into the blender container if necessary
5. Add the next 2-3 oz spinach, and blend again until the mixture is completely smooth
6. Pour into a glass and enjoy your antioxidant-filled treat with 2 servings of fruit and a whopping 4-6 servings of vegetables!<|endoftext|>
