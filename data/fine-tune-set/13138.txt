Give me a recipe for fish.<|endoftext|>Brazilian Fish Stew

Ingredients:
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Fresh ginger
• Pickled jalapeno pepper
• Chicken broth
• Tomato sauce
• Diced tomatoes
• Shredded coconut
• Peanut butter
• Shrimp
• White fish fillet
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Saute onion, garlic, ginger and jalepenos till onions translucent
2. Add tomato stuff, broth, coconut and peanut butter
3. Simmer for 20-30 minute
4. Add shrimp and cook 4 minute
5. Cut white fish into bitesize pieces, add and cook 4 minutes
6. Dish up and garnish with cilantro<|endoftext|>
