Ingredients:
• Bagels
• Olive oil
• Cannellini beans
• Marinated artichokes
• Spring onions
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 180c
2. Place bagel slices, in a single layer, on 2 baking trays
3. Drizzle evenly with 1 tbs of the oil
4. Bake in oven, swapping trays halfway through cooking, for 8 minutes or until golden brown and crisp
5. Meanwhile, place the cannellini beans, artichokes, shallot and 1 tbs of remaining oil in the bowl of a food processor and process until almost smooth
6. Taste and season with salt and pepper
7. Transfer to a bowl and drizzle with the remaining oil
8. Serve with bagel crisps<|endoftext|>
