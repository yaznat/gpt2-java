Ingredients:
• Blue cornmeal
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Half-and-half
• Butter
• Blueberries

Steps:
1. In a mixing bowl, add the cornmeal, flour, sugar, and baking powder
2. Stir to combine
3. In another bowl, add the eggs, vanilla, and half-and-half
4. Stir to combine
5. Add the egg mixture to the cornmeal mixture
6. Stir just to moisten
7. Stir in melted butter
8. Place a nonstick pan or griddle, sprayed with cooking spray, over medium heat
9. Pour 1 / 4 cup batter onto hot skillet
10. Sprinkle with a few blueberries
11. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges start to dry
12. Turn pancake over and cook until done
13. Serve warm with warm maple syrup<|endoftext|>
