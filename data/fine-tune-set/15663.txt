Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Salt
• Pepper
• Tomato sauce
• Water
• Tomato paste
• Chili powder
• Sugar
• Oregano
• Cumin
• Rice

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef with the onion, garlic, salt and pepper until red is gone
2. Add the rest of the ingredients except the rice and simmer uncovered 30 minutes
3. Add 2 / 3 cup minute rice
4. Cover and simmer 3-5 minutes
5. Serve over corn or tortilla chips and top with cheese, letuce, tomato, onions or whatever you would like<|endoftext|>
