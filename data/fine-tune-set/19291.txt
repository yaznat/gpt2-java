Give me a popsicle recipe.<|endoftext|>Fudge Popsicles Fudgesicles

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Hersheys cocoa powder
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a small saucepan, combine sugar, cornstarch, cocoa, and milk
2. Cook on medium until thick
3. Remove from heat and add vanilla and butter and stir until combined
4. Place in molds and freeze until firm<|endoftext|>
