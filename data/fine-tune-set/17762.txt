What's a good muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>Applesauce Raisin Muffins

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Oil
• Applesauce
• Salt
• Flour
• Cinnamon
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Beat eggs slightly, add sugar, oil& applesauce& beat thoroughly
2. Add dry ingredients& blend until just combined
3. Stir in raisins
4. Spoon into prepared pans& sprinkle with brown sugar
5. Bake at 375 degrees for 15 to 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
