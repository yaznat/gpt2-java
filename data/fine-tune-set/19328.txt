What's a recipe for a popsicle?<|endoftext|>Super Easy Banana Popsicle

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Wooden popsicle sticks

Steps:
1. Cut bananas in half
2. Push a popsicle stick in one end of the banana, making sure it doesn't poke out the side when you do
3. Wrap each half in saran wrap or non-stick foil and put in plastic ziplock bag
4. Freeze until solid
5. Unwrap and give to a happy kid
6. These can be dipped in peanut butter and coated with wheat germ or chopped nuts -- can also be used for fondue -- just use your imagination!
7. 1 banana= 2 popsicles<|endoftext|>
