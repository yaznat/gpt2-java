Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Soft tofu
• Lemon juice
• Dijon mustard
• Fresh dill
• Salt
• Pepper
• Corn oil
• Fusilli
• Asparagus

Steps:
1. Heat oil in small skillet, add garlic and cook over medium low heat about 3 minutes or until fragrant
2. Blend garlic, tofu, lemon juice, mustard, dill, salt, and pepper in a food processor until smooth add corn oil, and blend until emulsified
3. Meanwhile, cook pasta until tender, adding asparagus in the last 3-4 minutes drain and toss with sauce<|endoftext|>
