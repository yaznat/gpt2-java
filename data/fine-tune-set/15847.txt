Ingredients:
• Refried beans
• Mild green chilies
• Onion
• Ground beef
• Sharp american cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Salsa

Steps:
1. Combine beans, 2 tablespoons of the chili peppers, onion and 3 / 4 salt
2. Add beef and mix well
3. Form into 8- 5 inch patties
4. Cut cheese slices in half
5. Place 1 / 2 cheese slice on each beef patty
6. Fold to seal in the cheese, forming a semicircle
7. Grill or fry 5 to 6 minutes
8. Turn and grill or fry 4 to 5 minutes more or until done
9. Serve burgers in warmed tortillas
10. Add lettuce, chopped tomatoe, salsa and remaining chilies as desired<|endoftext|>
