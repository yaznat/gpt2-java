What's a recipe for a quesadilla?<|endoftext|>Chicken Quesadillas With Poblano Pesto

Ingredients:
• Poblano chiles
• Pumpkin seeds
• Cilantro
• Serrano peppers
• Olive oil
• Lime juice
• Salt
• Flour tortillas
• Cooked chicken
• Monterey jack cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. Put poblanos, pumpkin seeds, cilantro and serranos in a food processor and pulse until pured
2. Add the oil in a steady stream with the machine running
3. Add the lime juice and salt, mixing well
4. Taste and adjust seasoning if necessary
5. Spread 1 / 4 cup of the pesto on each tortilla
6. Distribute 1 / 2 cup chicken on each tortilla and sprinkle with monterrey jack and parmesan cheese
7. Fold tortillas in half
8. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat
9. Saut quesadillas for 3 to 4 minutes on each side or until the tortillas are golden brown and all the cheese is melted
10. Let cool for 1 minute and cut each quesadilla into thirds
11. Serve with any remaining pesto or salsa of your choice<|endoftext|>
