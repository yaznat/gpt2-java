Ingredients:
• Strawberry
• Apricot halves
• Nonfat vanilla yogurt
• Apricot nectar
• Flax seed meal
• Cold water

Steps:
1. Place first 5 ingredients in blender and mix well
2. Add water til you get the desired consistency
3. Taste and add a bit more apricot nectar if it's not sweet enough for you!<|endoftext|>
