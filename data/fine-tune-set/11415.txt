Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Bananas
• Fat-free buttermilk
• Lemon juice
• Canola oil
• Egg whites
• Egg yolk
• Lemon zest
• Blueberries
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f in a bowl combine mashed ripe bananas, fat-free buttermilk, lemon juice, canola oil, egg whites, egg yolk and 1 tsp lemon zest
2. Stir blueberries into flour mixture then stir everything into original mixture
3. Pour batter into 9x5x3 inch loaf pan coated with baking spray
4. Bake 1 hour or until toothpick inserted into middle of loaf comes out clean
5. Cool loaf in pan on wire rack 10 minutes
6. Remove from pan
7. In a bowl combine confectioner's sugar, lemon juice, and 1 / 2 tsp grated lemon zest
8. Spread over top loaf
9. Cool completely<|endoftext|>
