Give me a recipe for chili.<|endoftext|>Aunt Glo S Turkey Chili

Ingredients:
• Ground turkey
• Green peppers
• Onion
• Stewed tomatoes
• Chili beans
• Tomato sauce
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Garlic

Steps:
1. Brown turkey in large sauce pan with chopped onions and peppers
2. Put turkey, into crockpot and add tomatoes, chili beans, tomato sauce, cumin, garlic, salt and pepper to taste, and chili powder
3. Simmer on low all day long!<|endoftext|>
