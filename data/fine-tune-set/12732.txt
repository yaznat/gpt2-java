What's a good steak recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Flank Steak

Ingredients:
• Flank steaks
• Soy sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Fresh lemon juice
• Cilantro
• Fresh ginger

Steps:
1. Whisk the ingredients for the marinate
2. Add the steak and marinade in a zip lock bag and marinate, turning occasionally
3. Prepare barecue
4. Drain marinade into a small saucepan and bring to a boil
5. Grill steak for about 5 minutes per side for rare
6. Transfer steak to platter
7. Let stand 10 minutes
8. Thinly slice steak across grain
9. Pass marinade as sauce<|endoftext|>
