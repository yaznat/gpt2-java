Ingredients:
• Cilantro
• Tomatoes
• Serrano peppers
• Fresh ginger
• Fresh lime juice
• Extra firm tofu
• Sesame oil
• Light coconut milk
• Eggplant
• Broccoli
• Zucchini
• Sea salt
• Turmeric
• Frozen peas

Steps:
1. Blend cilantro, tomato, peppers, ginger and lime juice in a food processor
2. In a large saucepan, lightly brown the tofu in the sesame oil over medium-high heat
3. Add milk, broccoli, eggplant, zucchini, salt and turmeric
4. Simmer 10-15min, stirring frequently at first until veggies settle down in the pan, until the veggies are tender
5. Stir in cilantro mixture and peas
6. Turn off heat, cover and let sit for at least 5 minutes
7. Serve with brown rice or noodles<|endoftext|>
