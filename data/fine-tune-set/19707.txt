Ingredients:
• Eggplant
• All-purpose flour
• Panko breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• Za'atar spice mix
• Kosher salt
• Cayenne pepper
• Eggs
• Salt
• Tahini
• Sour cream
• Lemon juice
• Apple cider vinegar
• Tamari
• Garlic powder
• Water

Steps:
1. To make the eggplant fries, preheat the oven to 425f wedge a wire rack atop a baking sheet
2. Cut the eggplant into 1 / 2-inch thick rounds
3. Then cut each round into 1 / 2-inch thick spears
4. Set aside
5. Pour the flour into a small bowl
6. Beat the eggs together in another small bowl
7. In a third small bowl, combine the breadcrumbs, parmesan, za'atar, salt and cayenne
8. Roll the eggplant spears in the flour, dunk them in the egg, and then roll them into the bread crumb mixture
9. Lay each spear close to one another on the prepared baking sheet
10. Bake until golden brown and crispy, about 15 minutes
11. Sprinkle the tops with flaky salt
12. While the fries are baking, combine all the ingredients for the dip except the water in a small bowl
13. To thin, add a drizzle of water and stir vigorously until you reach the desired consistency
14. Serve the fries with the dipping sauce while the fries are still hot<|endoftext|>
