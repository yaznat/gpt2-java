Please give me a recipe for fries.<|endoftext|>Herb And Cheese Oven Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Olive oil
• Dried italian seasoning
• Salt and pepper
• Parmigiano-reggiano cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 500 deg
2. Place sliced potato wedges on a cookie sheet
3. Drizzle with oil to coat and season with herb seasoning
4. Roast potatoes and turn after after 15-20 minutes
5. Sprinkle with cheese liberally on potatoes and roast another 10 minutes<|endoftext|>
