Ingredients:
• Agave nectar
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Raw cacao powder
• Half-and-half
• Egg yolks
• Salt

Steps:
1. Over low heat melt chopped chocolate and agave nectar just until chocolate is melted
2. Remove from heat
3. In another saucepan, combine half half and half and cacao powder whisking constantly just until boils, whisk additional minute and remove from heat
4. Add this mixture to agave mixture
5. Whisk egg yolks in bowl, set aside
6. Warm remaining half and half and salt in separate saucepan, whisk into egg yolks and then place back in saucepan, cook and stir constantly until it reaches 170 degrees
7. Remove from heat
8. Strain egg mixture through fine metal sieve into chocolate mixture
9. Cool slightly and blend in blender until smooth
10. Cool completely in refrigerator
11. Follow your ice cream maker instructions<|endoftext|>
