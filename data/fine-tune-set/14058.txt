Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Delicious Lamb Stew

Ingredients:
• Boneless lamb
• Onions
• Carrots
• Oil
• Red wine
• Salt & pepper
• Parsley
• Thyme
• Marjoram
• Garlic cloves
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Cut the meat into 2 inch pieces, discarding any skin and fat
2. Peel and chop the onions
3. Peel and slice the carrots
4. Heat the oil in a large saucepan and fry the onions 5 minutes, add the carrots and fry 5 minutes longer
5. Remove the vegetables to a plate, draining as much oil as possible back into the pan
6. Add the meat to the pan and cook over a high heat, turning it all the time to brown it on all sides
7. Pour the wine into the pan, return the vegetables and season the mixture with salt and pepper, add the parsley, thyme, marjoram and garlic
8. Cut the bacon into small pieces and add to the pan
9. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 2 hours
10. Allow to cool, then place in the refrigerator overnight, next day remove the layer of solidified fat from the top
11. Reheat, check the seasoning, and serve
12. Serve with mashed potato
13. You may cook this the oven
14. Cook it at 300-325f about 2 - 2 1 / 2 hours or until tender
15. It freezes well too
