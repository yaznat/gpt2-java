Ingredients:
• Chicken breast halves
• Vegetable oil
• Green cabbage
• Cornstarch
• Ground ginger
• Garlic powder
• Water
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Cut chicken breasts into strips
2. Heat oil in a frying pan
3. Add chicken strips and stir fry over medium-high heat, turning constantly until done
4. Add cabbage and saut 2 minutes until cabbage is crisp-tender
5. Mix cornstarch and seasonings
6. Add water and soy sauce, and mix until smooth
7. Stir sauce into chicken / cabbage mixture
8. Cook until sauce has thickened and chicken is coated, about 1 minute
9. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours<|endoftext|>
