What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Strawberry Milkshake S

Ingredients:
• Strawberries
• Milk
• Powdered sugar
• Pure vanilla extract
• Strawberry ice cream

Steps:
1. Place the first four ingredients into a blender and puree to smooth
2. Add the ice cream and process until well blended-garnish with a fresh berry or whipped cream<|endoftext|>
