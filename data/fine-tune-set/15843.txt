Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Black beans
• Tequila
• Epazote
• Ancho chilies
• Taco seasoning
• Cocoa powder
• Black pepper
• Hot sauce
• Water
• Sour cream
• Salsa verde
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Minute rice
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Dump the beans, tequila and epazote into a saucepan and begin simmering over low heat
2. Stir occasionally
3. Mix the taco seasoning, cocoa, and black pepper in a bowl
4. In a large four-quart pot, brown the meat and then add the water and taco seasoning mixture
5. Bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat to simmer, and add the chopped chilies and hot sauce
7. Stir and simmer for 15 minutes
8. In the meantime, put the beans, sour cream, and salsa into a blender and puree the mixture
9. It should have a smooth consistency when you are done
10. Pour the blender contents into the pot and heat on medium-low
11. Add the cheese and let it melt
12. Simmer and stir for about 10 minutes
13. Remove from heat and mix the minute rice into the pot
14. Let this stand for about 5 minutes
15. Pre-heat oven to 350
16. Scoop about 2 ounces of the filling right down the middle of a tortilla
17. Gently fold the tortilla over, fold the right edge in, and then roll again
18. Place onto foil covered cookie sheets
19. Continue until the cookie sheets are full, but do not layer
20. Bake for 15 minutes and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
