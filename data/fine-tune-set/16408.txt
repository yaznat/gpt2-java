What's a recipe for a wrap?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Sausages

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Smoked sausage
• Chili pepper flakes
• Brown sugar
• Toothpick

Steps:
1. Cut the bacon strips in half length-wise
2. Cut the sausage into pieces that are about the same width of the bacon strips
3. Just toss the ends of the sausages
4. Wrap the 1 / 2-strips of bacon around the sausages and hold it in place with a toothpick
5. Place on top of a cookie sheet
6. Now i like to do these two ways: for a spicy kick shake a few pepper flakes on top of each sausage or for more of a sweeter flavor try a small sliver of candied ginger
7. Top each sausage with a loose 1 / 2 - 3 / 4 tsp scoop of brown sugar
8. The brown sugar will be used to gauge when the sausages are done
9. When it melts down and turns into a little volcano of brown sugar they are ready!
10. If your grill is already full then put these in your oven at 300f for approximately 45-60 minutes
11. Its nearly impossible to overcook these, but you do want to keep some of the molten brown sugar on top for flavor
12. So don't forget to give your guests this treat<|endoftext|>
