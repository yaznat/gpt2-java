Ingredients:
• Rolled oats
• Dark brown sugar
• Raisins
• Salt

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients in a small bowl, while the kettle boils
2. Pour on 1 / 4-1 / 3 cup boiling water, give it a little stir, and cover with a plate for a few minutes to let the water be soaked up
3. Enjoy!
