Ingredients:
• Apples
• Cinnamon
• Sugar
• Orange juice
• Orange zest
• Brandy

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in saucepan
2. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer until apples are tender, approx 15-20 minutes
3. Add more water as needed while simmering
4. Softened apples can be left chunky or processed in a blender for a smoother sauce
5. Return to pan and add brandy as desired
6. When ready to serve on pancakes, re-heat and top with applesauce and whipped cream<|endoftext|>
