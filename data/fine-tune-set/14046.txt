Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Yellow onions
• Garlic cloves
• Curry paste
• Ground lamb
• Fresh parsley
• Egg
• Breadcrumbs
• Pumpkin
• Potatoes
• Milk
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a small pan and cook the onion, garlic and paste stirring until the onion is soft
2. Using your hands combine the onion mixture, lamb, half the parsley, egg and breadcrumbs in a large bowl
3. Mix well
4. Shape 1 / 4 cups of mixture into sausages
5. Cover and refrigerate until firm
6. Cook the sausages in a large oiled pan in batches, until browned all over and cooked through
7. Meanwhile boil, steam, or microwave the potato and pumpkin separately until soft and tender
8. Drain
9. Mash the potato and pumpkin with the milk and cheese
10. Serve with the sausages, sprinkled with remaining parsley<|endoftext|>
