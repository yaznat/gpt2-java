What's a good pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Pasta Salad

Ingredients:
• Dry linguine
• Broccoli florets
• Cherry tomatoes
• Green onions
• Carrots
• Soy sauce
• Sesame seeds
• Brown sugar
• Dark sesame oil
• Lemon juice
• Hot sauce
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. In large pot with boiling water, cook pasta
2. Drain, rinse pasta with cold water and let drain again
3. Place pasta in a large salad bowl, add broccoli, next 3 ingredients and toss well
4. In a small bowl or jar with lid, combine soy sauce and remaining 6 ingredients
5. Whisk together or cover tightly and shake vigorously
6. Pour mixture over pasta and toss gently<|endoftext|>
