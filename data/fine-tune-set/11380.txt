• Seven-grain cereal

Steps:

2. Fill the cup to the 3 / 4 mark with the seven grain cereal
3. Add ingredients to bread machine in the order listed
4. After dough has mixed and risen, remove and form into 5 mini loaves
5. Let rise for about 1 hour
6. Bake for 22 minutes at 350 degrees on convection setting
