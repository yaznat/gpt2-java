Ingredients:
• Applesauce
• Honey
• Ground cinnamon
• Water

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients in saucepan
2. Warm over medium heat for about 5 minutes
3. Serve warm over pancakes, oatmeal, granola, etc<|endoftext|>
