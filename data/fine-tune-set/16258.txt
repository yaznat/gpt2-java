Ingredients:
• Scallions
• Garlic clove
• Peanut oil
• Ground turkey breast
• Tomato puree
• Hoisin sauce
• Ground ginger
• Cabbage leaves
• Whole wheat tortillas

Steps:
1. Saute the scallions and minced garlic in the peanut oil in a large nonstick skillet until tender
2. Crumble in the turkey and cook, stirring, 2 minutes
3. Add the tomato puree, hoisin sauce, and ground ginger
4. Bring just to a boil and simmer 5 minutes
5. Set aside
6. Place a large cabbage leaf on each of the 2 warmed tortillas and place half the turkey filling on top of each one
7. Roll up to enclose<|endoftext|>
