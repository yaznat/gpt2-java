Ingredients:
• Peanut butter
• Brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Egg
• Vanilla

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Mix sugars, egg, and vanilla together until blended
3. Add peanut butter and flour
4. Mix together just until blended and the mixture has thickened
5. Scoop out dough using a tablespoon and place on a cookie sheet
6. I line with parchment paper, but it isn't 100% necessary!
7. Bake for about 14 minutes or until cookies are set
8. They won't brown very much
9. Allow cookies to cool on the cookie sheet for 5 minutes before removing to a rack to cool completely<|endoftext|>
