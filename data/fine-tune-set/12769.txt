Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Beef cube steaks
• Olive oil
• Mushrooms
• Onion
• Pizza sauce
• Part-skim mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f combine flour and salt in a shallow dish
2. Dip cube steaks into flour mixture, coating well
3. Heat oil in a 12in
4. Skillet over medium-high heat until hot
5. Add cube steaks
6. Reduce heat to medium
7. Cook until browned on both sides
8. 4-6 minutes
9. Place browned cube steaks in a 3qt
10. Baking dish
11. Top with mushrooms and onions
12. Spoon pizza sauce over top
13. Cover tightly with aluminum foil
14. Bake steaks 30 minutes uncover
15. Sprinkle with mozzarella
16. Continue baking uncovered until steaks are fork-tender and sauce thickens about 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
