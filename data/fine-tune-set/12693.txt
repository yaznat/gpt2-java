Can you give me a steak recipe?<|endoftext|>A1 Garlic Steak Marinade

Ingredients:
• Beef steak
• Steak sauce
• Vegetable oil
• Cracked black pepper
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Combine steak sauce, oil, pepper, and garlic
2. Place steak in plastic bag
3. Add marinade, turning to coat
4. Close bag securely and marinate in refrigerator for at least 1 hour, turning once
5. Remove steak from marinade
6. Place on rack in broiler pan so surface of meat is 3-4 inches from heat source
7. Broil 15-18 minutes to desired doneness, turning once<|endoftext|>
