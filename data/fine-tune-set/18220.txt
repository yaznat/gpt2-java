Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Banana Pancakes Grain Free Milk Free

Ingredients:
• Banana
• Eggs
• Nut butter

Steps:
1. Beat 3 eggs, add mashed banana and nut butter and mix together until fairly smooth
2. Batter will be pretty thin
3. Pour about 1 / 4 cup for each pancake onto a hot skillet or pan, and flip when the outer edge begins to set
4. Cook on each side until golden
5. These cook fast so keep an eye on them!<|endoftext|>
