Give me a soup recipe.<|endoftext|>Alphabet Beef Soup

Ingredients:
• Sirloin steaks
• Italian-style diced tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Water
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Alphabet pasta

Steps:
1. Cut meat into bite size cubes
2. Combine meat, tomatoes, tomato sauce, water, bouillon, and vegetables in slow cooker
3. Cook on low for 6 to 8 hours or high for 3 to 4 hours
4. Before serving, turn crockpot to high and add alphabet noodles
5. Cook on high for 15 to 20 minutes or until the noodles are fully cooked<|endoftext|>
