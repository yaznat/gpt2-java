Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Milk
• Cooked chicken breasts
• Corn
• Onion
• Enchilada sauce
• Taco seasoning
• Cheddar cheese
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Mix together bisquick and milk and spread into a lightly greased square baking dish
2. Combine the rest of the ingredients except for topping in a bowl, then spread on top of bisquick crust
3. Top with more cheese if you like and chips
4. Bake covered for about 30 minutes at 375f
5. Broil for a few minutes for if you want the top cheese layer to be crunchy<|endoftext|>
