Ingredients:
• Butter beans
• Kidney beans
• Garbanzo beans
• Green beans
• Wax beans
• Red onion
• Green bell pepper
• Water
• Sugar
• Balsamic vinegar
• Oil
• Oregano
• Garlic

Steps:
1. Rinse and drain beans in a colander
2. Transfer to a large bowl with lid
3. Stir onion and bell pepper into beans
4. Dissolve sugar in water for 1 minute in microwave safe measuring cup
5. Whisk in remaining ingredients until combined
6. Pour over beans and toss to coat evenly
7. Allow to marinate 4 or more hours<|endoftext|>
