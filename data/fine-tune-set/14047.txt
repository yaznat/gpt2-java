Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Curried Lamb Steaks

Ingredients:
• Lamb
• Garlic cloves
• Salt
• Fresh ginger
• Pepper
• Ground turmeric
• Ground coriander
• Ground cumin
• Oregano
• Soy sauce
• Lemon juice
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Trim excess fat from lamb, but leave in the bone
2. Crush garlic with the salt
3. Add ginger, pepper, turmeric, coriander, cumin and oregano
4. Stir in soy, lemon juice and vegetable oil
5. Coat evenly over the lamb
6. Cover and refrigerate for 3 hours to 3 days
7. Prepare bbq grill for coals to be white-ash covered
8. Grill meat 5 minutes each side or broil<|endoftext|>
