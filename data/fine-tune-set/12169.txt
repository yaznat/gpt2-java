Ingredients:
• Butter
• White sugar
• Egg
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Baking soda
• Flour
• Walnuts
• Apples
• Vanilla
• Hot water
• Cream cheese
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Set oven to 350 degrees
2. Grease 9-inch pie plate
3. In a bowl, combine 1 / 4 cup softened butter, white sugar, egg, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, baking soda, flour, 1-1 / 2 teaspoons vanilla and hot water
4. Mix well to combine
5. Add in apples and nuts
6. Mix well to combine
7. Pour batter into greased pie pan
8. Bake for about 40-45 minutes
9. To make the cream cheese topping: mix cream cheese, butter, vanilla and confectioners sugar until smooth enough to spread over the baked apples
10. Spread over the warmed baked pie
11. Serve hot or warm, or refrigerate and let the cream cheese topping set<|endoftext|>
