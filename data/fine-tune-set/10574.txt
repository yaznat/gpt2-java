Give me a recipe for pasta.<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Anchovy Sauce

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Anchovy fillet
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Angel hair pasta
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a small saucepan over medium low heat, add garlic and cook about 30 seconds
2. Add anchovies along with the oil in the can, stir, cook 2 minutes
3. Season with pepper to taste and pour over pasta and toss
4. Top with freshly grated parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
