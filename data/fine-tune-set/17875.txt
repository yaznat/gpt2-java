What's a recipe for brownies?<|endoftext|>Almond Joy Fudge Brownies

Ingredients:
• Duncan hines chewy fudge brownie mix
• Vegetable oil
• Eagle brand condensed milk
• Miniature almond joy bars
• Toasted almond

Steps:
1. Prepare mix according to brownie package directions, except only use 3 tablespoons
2. Of vegetable oil
3. Pour into a greased 9 x 13" pan
4. Pour and spread 1 cup of condensed milk
5. Sprinkle with chopped candy bars and toasted almonds
6. Bake at 350 for 35-38 minutes
7. Let cool completely before cutting into 2 dozen bars<|endoftext|>
