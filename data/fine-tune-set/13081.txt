Ingredients:
• Fish fillets
• Self raising flour
• Skim milk
• Egg
• Butter
• Salt
• Cajun seasoning
• Oil
• Plain flour

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 180c
2. In a 10" x 3" plastic tub, place the flour, egg, salt, seasoning, milk & melted butter
3. Blend really well using an immersion blender - or liquidize & pour into the tub
4. Rinse your fillets & cut into 5" pieces
5. Dust each piece with a little flour
6. Heat your fryer to approximately 180 - 190c
7. Dip the fillerts one by one in the batter & coat both sides evenly
8. When the oil has reached the correct temp, gently lay one fillet at a time onto the top of the oil
9. Cook until golden brown on both sides - takes me about 4-5 mins, then remove & place on a tray with a wire rack in the oven to stay crisp & hot
10. Repeat with the remaining fish
11. Serve with chunky chips, peas & lots of salt & vinegar --<|endoftext|>
