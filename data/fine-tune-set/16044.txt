Ingredients:
• Corn kernels
• Goat cheese
• Cilantro
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Salsa
• Ground cumin
• Canned black beans
• Corn tortillas
• Salsa verde
• Nonfat sour cream

Steps:
1. Heat skillet over med-high heat
2. Add corn, saute 2 minutes or until browned
3. Place corn in small bowl, add goat cheese and cilantro
4. Mix until well-blended and set aside
5. In 1 tsp olive oil, saute onion and garlic for 3 minutes
6. Stir in 1 c salsa, cumin, and beans and bring to a boil
7. Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes or until thick
8. Mash the bean mixture slightly
9. Remove from heat, stir in the corn / cilantro / goat cheese mixture
10. Spread the mixture evenly over 8 tortillas, top each with 1 tortilla, pressing gently
11. Cook each quesadilla in frying pan with cooking spray until golden - about 2 minutes a side
12. Quarter each quesadilla, serve with salsa verde, sour cream and more salsa or green onions if desired<|endoftext|>
