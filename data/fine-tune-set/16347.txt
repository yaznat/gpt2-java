Ingredients:
• Sesame breadsticks
• Bacon
• Parmesan cheese
• Garlic salt
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Ranch dip mix
• Chives
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Combine cheese and garlic salt / powder
3. Wrap bacon slices in a spiral around each breadstick
4. Place breadsticks on greased or parchment lined pan
5. Cook for 15 minutes or until bacon is browned just a bit
6. Remove and carefully roll the hot breadsticks in cheese mixture
7. Let cool and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
