What's a recipe for cake?<|endoftext|>All To Myself Cheesecake

Ingredients:
• Oreo cookies
• Cream cheese
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• Chocolate chips
• Shortening

Steps:
1. Beat cream cheese, sugar, vanilla and eggs together until smooth place an oreo with frosting side up in the bottom of a lined muffin cup
2. Top with cream cheese mixture
3. Top that with crushed oreo tops
4. Bake at 325 for 25 minutes
5. Remove from oven and cool
6. Melt shortening and chocolate chips until smooth
7. Drizzle over cooled cheesecakes<|endoftext|>
