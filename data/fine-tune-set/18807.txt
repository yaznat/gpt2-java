Ingredients:
• Almond milk
• Eggs
• Egg whites
• Sugar
• Salt
• Tea bags
• Orange peel
• Whole almond

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees f
2. Bring almond milk to a boil
3. Steep tea 4-5 minutes or until a desired tea flavor is reached
4. Meanwhile, whisk together eggs, whites, sugar and salt in a medium bowl until blended
5. Very gradually whisk hot milk mixture into egg mixture and stir until sugar is dissolved
6. If lumps appear, strain mixture through a fine sieve into a bowl or pitcher
7. Stir in orange zest
8. Divide mixture between 6 ramekins or tea cups and bake in a water bath until set but still a bit jiggly when shaken, 50-60 minutes
9. Let cool, then wrap and chill for at least 4 hours
10. Garnish with almonds and serve<|endoftext|>
