Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Cinnamon Roll Waffles

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Granulated sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Vegetable oil
• Vanilla extract
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Ground cinnamon
• Cream cheese
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Too prepare the waffles: in a medium bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt
2. Make a well in the center of the mixture
3. Set aside
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together eggs, buttermilk, oil and vanilla
5. Pour into the bowl with the dry ingredients
6. Stir just until moistened
7. Batter will be slightly lumpy
8. Preheat a waffle iron and spray with nonstick spray
9. Pour batter into waffle iron grid and close the lid
10. Bake until waffles are completely done- follow your waffle iron's instructions
11. Use a fork to remove finished waffles
12. Repeat with remaining batter
13. Keep waffles warm until ready to serve
14. To prepare the cinnamon topping: in a medium bowl, mix butter, brown sugar and cinnamon
15. Scoop the filling into a small zip baggie and set aside
16. To prepare the cream cheese topping: in a medium, microwave-safe bowl- heat butter and cream cheese for 30 to 60 seconds
17. Just until melted
18. Stir together until smooth
19. Whisk in powdered sugar and vanilla extract
20. When ready to serve, place waffle on plate, drizzle cinnamon topping, then drizzle cream cheese topping to taste
21. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
