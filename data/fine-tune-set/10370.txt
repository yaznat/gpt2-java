Ingredients:
• Sugar cookie mix
• Butter
• Almond extract
• Egg
• Almond paste
• Sugar
• Dried apricot
• Cream cheese
• Eggs
• Lemon juice
• Vanilla baking chips
• Whipping cream
• Sliced almonds

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375f
2. In large bowl, stir cookie base ingredients until soft dough forms
3. Spread dough in bottom of ungreased 13x9-inch pan
4. Bake 10 to 15 minutes or until set
5. Cool 10 minutes
6. Meanwhile, in large bowl, beat almond paste and sugar with electric mixer on low speed until crumbly but blended
7. Add apricots
8. Beat on low speed just until combined
9. Add cream cheese, 2 eggs and the lemon juice
10. Beat on medium speed until well blended
11. Pour over warm cookie base
12. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until set
13. Cool 30 minutes
14. Place baking chips in small bowl
15. In 1-quart saucepan, heat whipping cream just to boiling over low heat, stirring occasionally
16. Pour over baking chips
17. Let stand 1 minute
18. Stir until chips are melted and mixture is smooth
19. Pour and spread over filling
20. Sprinkle with almonds
21. Refrigerate about 2 hours or until set
22. For bars, cut into 9 rows by 4 rows
23. Store covered in refrigerator<|endoftext|>
