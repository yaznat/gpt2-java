Ingredients:
• Spaghetti
• Lemon
• Avocado
• Garlic cloves
• Olive oil
• Pepper
• Parsley
• Salt
• Black olives

Steps:
1. Put garlic lemon juice zest and oil in blender
2. Add parsley
3. Add peeled avocado add salt and pepper and blend
4. Top pasta and toss garnish
5. Top with olives<|endoftext|>
