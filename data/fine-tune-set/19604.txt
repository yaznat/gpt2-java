Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Apple pie filling
• Golden raisin
• Butter
• Apple pie spice
• Pecans
• Sugar

Steps:
1. Spray a 4 quart slow cooker lightly with non-stick cooking spray
2. Add the sweet potatoes, apple pie filling, raisins, 3 tablespoons butter, and apple pie spice to the slow cooker
3. Gently stir to mix
4. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours
5. In a large skillet, add the pecans, sugar, and 2 tablespoons butter
6. Over medium heat, cook, stirring constantly, for 8-10 minutes or until sugar mixture that adheres to the nuts turns golden and starts to melt
7. Pour nut mixture out onto a piece of foil
8. Let cool completely
9. Coarsely crush the nut mixture
10. Serve potatoes topped with nuts<|endoftext|>
