Ingredients:
• Cream cheese
• Granulated sugar
• Sour cream
• Vanilla extract
• Non-dairy whipped topping
• Graham cracker pie crust
• Strawberry

Steps:
1. In a large mixing bowl beat cream cheese until softened
2. Gradually add sugar
3. Blend in sour cream and vanilla until well mixed
4. Gently fold in non-dairy whipped topping, blending well
5. Spoon into prepared graham cracker crust and chill
6. Garnish with sliced strawberries<|endoftext|>
