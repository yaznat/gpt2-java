Give me a cake recipe.<|endoftext|>A Dream Of Peaches N Cream Cake

Ingredients:
• Butter recipe cake mix
• Eggs
• Oil
• Water
• Peaches
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Sour cream
• Whipping cream

Steps:
1. Make the cake per the boxes directions and bake in 2 round pans
2. Cool cake and split each cake layer in half making 4 layers
3. Cook the peaches with the next 3 ingredients until thick
4. Cool the peaches until they are completely cool
5. Place a 1 / 3 of the peach mixture on bottom layer
6. Cover with a third of the sour cream
7. Repeat the layering twice use the whipped cream to ice the sides and top of the cake
8. Chill the cake until set<|endoftext|>
