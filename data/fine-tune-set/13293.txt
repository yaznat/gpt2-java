Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>A Chicken In Every Pot Pie

Ingredients:
• Cooked chicken
• Chicken broth
• Frozen peas
• Carrots
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Salt
• Pepper
• Bisquick baking mix
• Milk
• Garlic powder
• Celery seed
• Paprika

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, combine chicken and next 6 ingredients
2. Bring to a boil, stirring occasionally
3. Meanwhile, combine bisquick and next 3 ingredients
4. Pour the hot chicken mixture into eight greased 10 oz
5. Ramekins
6. Immediately spoon 1 / 4 cup of biscuit mixture evenly on top of each
7. Sprinkle with paprika
8. Bake, uncovered, at 350 degrees for 30-35 minutes or until topping is golden brown<|endoftext|>
