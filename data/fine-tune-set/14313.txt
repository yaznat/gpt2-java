Ingredients:
• Water
• Dry sherry
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch
• Sugar
• Canola oil
• Fresh ginger
• Fresh asparagus
• Summer squash
• Green onions
• Extra firm tofu
• Pine nuts
• Cooked brown rice

Steps:
1. For sauce, in a small bowl stir together water, dry sherry or wine, soy sauce, cornstarch, and sugar
2. Set aside
3. Pour oil into wok or large skillet
4. Preheat the wok or skillet over medium-high heat
5. Stir fry the ginger in hot oil for 15 seconds
6. Add fresh asparagus and squash
7. Stir-fry for 3 minutes
8. Add green onions
9. Stirring about 1 1 / 2 minutes more or until asparagus is crisp-tender
10. Remove vegetables from wok
11. Add tofu to wok or skillet
12. Carefully stir for 2 to 3 minutes or until lightly browned
13. Remove from wok
14. Stir the sauce
15. Add sauce to hot wok
16. Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly
17. Return cooked vegetables and tofu to the wok
18. Stir all ingredients together to coat with sauce
19. Cover and cook about 1 minute more or until heated through
20. Stir in toasted pine nuts or almonds, serve over rice<|endoftext|>
