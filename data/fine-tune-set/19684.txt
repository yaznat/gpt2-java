Ingredients:
• Oil
• Avocados
• Flour
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Salt
• White pepper
• Eggs
• Milk
• Breadcrumbs
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Habanero pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Roma tomatoes
• Bay leaf
• Allspice
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Cider vinegar
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Prepare ketchup
2. Set aside
3. Heat oil in deep fryer to 350 degrees
4. Cut avocados in half lengthwise and remove seeds
5. Carefully slice each one into 4 wedges and remove skin
6. Combine flour, cumin, chili powder, salt and pepper in small bowl
7. Whisk eggs and milk together in second bowl
8. Dredge avocados in flour, dip into egg mix to coat
9. Coat each wedge in breadcrumbs
10. Deep-fry until golden brown
11. Drain on paper towels
12. Serve with habanero ketchup
13. Heat oil in heavy saucepan on medium heat
14. Saut onion and garlic until onion is soft
15. Add peppers and tomatoes
16. Saut until peppers are tender
17. Add bay leaf, allspice, cinnamon, cloves, sugar and vinegar
18. Simmer until mix is very soft
19. Remove bay leaf and puree
20. Return to saucepan and simmer 5 minutes
21. Add salt to taste, cover and refrigerate
22. Keeps 1 week in fridge<|endoftext|>
