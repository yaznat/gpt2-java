What's a recipe for pasta?<|endoftext|>Alfredo With Angel Hair Pasta And Chicken

Ingredients:
• Chicken breast tenders
• Heavy whipping cream
• Butter
• Parmesan cheese
• Romano cheese
• Cracked black pepper
• Angel hair pasta

Steps:
1. Boil angel hair pasta until al dente and set aside, keeping warm
2. Boil chicken tenders until done and dice after cooled
3. In large saucepan melt 1 1 / 2 sticks butter, reserving other 1 / 2 stick for thickening
4. Add heavy cream and all cheeses to saucepan stirring constanty over medium heat
5. In a microwavable dish, melt remaining butter
6. Add 3-4 tbs
7. Flour being sure to smooth out any lumps
8. Add butter and flour mixture to saucepan and stir until sauce thickens just a bit
9. Salt the sauce to taste
10. Stir diced chicken into sauce and serve over the angel hair pasta<|endoftext|>
