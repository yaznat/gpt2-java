Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Multigrain bread
• Apricot jam
• Turkey breast
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Red onions
• Swiss cheese
• Dijon mustard

Steps:
1. In a skillet, cook the bacon over medium heat until crisp
2. Remove to paper towels to drain
3. Spread 2 slices of toasted bread with 1 tbsp of apricot jam each
4. Top each slice with 3 oz sliced turkey, 1 piece lettuce, 2 slices bacon, 1 slice tomato, 1 slice onion, and 1 slice of cheese
5. Spread 2 more slices of toasted bread with the dijon mustard
6. Place on top
7. Slice in half and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
