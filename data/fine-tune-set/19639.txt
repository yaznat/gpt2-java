Can you give me a potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Potato Salad

Ingredients:
• Small red potatoes
• Salt
• Mayonnaise
• Soy sauce
• Light brown sugar
• Ground ginger
• Sliced water chestnuts
• Red bell pepper
• Scallions

Steps:
1. Place the potatoes in a large pot and add enough water to cover
2. Add salt and bring to a boil over medium-high heat
3. Cook for 10 to 12 minutes, or until fork-tender
4. Drain
5. Let cool for 10 minutes, then cut into quarters and place in a large bowl
6. Meanwhile, in a medium bowl, combine the mayonnaise, soy sauce, brown sugar, and ginger
7. Mix well
8. Add the remaining ingredients to the potatoes, stir in the mayonnaise mixture, and mix well
9. Cover and chill for at least 2 hours before serving<|endoftext|>
