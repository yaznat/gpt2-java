Give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>30 Minute Vegetable Beef Soup

Ingredients:
• Beef broth
• Potatoes
• Cooked beef
• V8 vegetable juice
• Whole canned tomatoes
• Frozen mixed vegetables
• Dried thyme leaves
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Place broth and potatoes in saucepan
2. Heat to a boil
3. Cover and cook over low heat 5 minute or until potatoes are tender
4. Add beef, vegetable juice, tomatoes, vegetables, thyme and black pepper
5. Cover and cook 15 minute or until vegetables are tender
6. Use leftover or ready-cooked pot roast to jump-start this flavorful soup
7. Serve with crusty rolls and a wedge of swiss or cheddar cheese<|endoftext|>
