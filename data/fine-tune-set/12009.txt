Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>2Bleu S Everything Mud Pie

Ingredients:
• French vanilla instant pudding
• Sugar-free instant chocolate fudge pudding mix
• 2% low-fat milk
• Reduced-fat cream cheese
• Chocolate graham wafer pie crust
• Bananas
• Cool whip lite
• Chocolate syrup
• Caramel syrup
• Peanuts

Steps:
1. Using an electric mixer on low speed, beat puddings, milk and cream cheese until smooth
2. Change to medium-high speed and continue beating for another 2 minutes
3. Pour into pie crust
4. Top filling with banana slices
5. Spread cool whip on top of pudding mixture
6. Drizzle light streams of chocolate syrup haphazardly over top
7. Do the same with the caramel syrup
8. Chill pie at least 4 hours before serving<|endoftext|>
