Ingredients:
• Tropical fruit salad
• Mandarin oranges
• Miniature marshmallow
• Flaked coconut
• Bananas
• Fat-free evaporated milk
• Fresh strawberries

Steps:
1. Combine all ingredients except the strawberries in a large bowl
2. Refrigerate a minimum of 30 minutes
3. Stir in strawberries before serving<|endoftext|>
