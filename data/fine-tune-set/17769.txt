Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Shallot
• Dried apricot
• Honey
• Semi-soft goat cheese
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Fat-free half-and-half
• Fresh thyme

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Line fifteen 2 1 / 2-inch muffin cups with paper bake cups
3. Set aside
4. In a medium bowl combine flour, baking powder, and salt
5. Set aside
6. In a large skillet melt 2 tablespoons of the butter over medium heat
7. Add shallots
8. Cook and stir until tender
9. Stir in dried apricots and the 2 tablespoons honey
10. Set aside
11. In a large bowl combine the remaining 2 tablespoons butter and the goat cheese
12. Beat with an electric mixer on medium to high speed for 30 seconds
13. Add brown sugar
14. Beat until combined
15. Beat in eggs until combined
16. Alternately add flour mixture and half-and-half to brown sugar mixture, beating after each addition just until combined
17. Stir in apricot mixture and thyme
18. Spoon batter into the prepared muffin cups, filling each about two-thirds full
19. Bake in the preheated oven for 15 to 17 minutes or until tops spring back when lightly touched
20. Cool in muffin cups on a wire rack for 5 minutes
21. Remove from muffin cups
22. Serve warm
23. If desired, serve with additional honey<|endoftext|>
