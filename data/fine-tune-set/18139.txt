Ingredients:
• Brown sugar
• Unsweetened apple cider
• Butter
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Biscuit mix
• Eggs
• Milk
• Canola oil
• Apples
• Lemon peel

Steps:
1. To make syrup: in a small saucepan, combine the syrup ingredients over medium heat, bring to a boil, reduce heat
2. Simmer, uncovered, for 5 minutes or until slightly reduced
3. For pancakes: preheat grill
4. Place biscuit mix in large bowl
5. In medium bowl, combine the eggs, milk, and oil
6. Stir into biscuit mix just until moistened
7. Stir in apple and lemon peel
8. Pour batter by 1 / 4 cupfuls onto a greased hot griddle
9. Turn when bubbles form on top
10. Cook until the second side is golden brown
11. Serve apple syrup with pancakes<|endoftext|>
