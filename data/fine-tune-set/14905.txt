Please give me a recipe for fried rice.<|endoftext|>Filipino Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Cabbage
• Carrots
• Garlic cloves
• Roast pork
• Cooked rice
• Frozen peas
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. In a wok fry bacon until crisp
2. Drain well
3. Re-heat wok
4. Add the pepper, onion, cabbage, carrots and garlic
5. Stir-fry 3 minutes
6. Add pork
7. Cook 1 minute
8. Add rice
9. Cook 1 minute
10. Stir in peas and soy sauce
11. Cook 30 seconds<|endoftext|>
