Ingredients:
• Beef sirloin
• Salt
• Black pepper
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Carrots
• Tomato sauce
• Beef broth
• Chicken broth
• Green beans
• Frozen peas
• Frozen corn
• Alphabet pasta

Steps:
1. Sprinkle beef with salt and pepper
2. Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over high heat
3. Add beef, a few pieces at a time, and cook 2 minutes on each side
4. Transfer to slow cooker
5. Add onion and carrots to skillet and saute 3 minutes
6. Transfer contents of skillet to slow cooker and add tomato sauce, beef and chicken broths, and green beans
7. Cover and cook on low for 5 to 6 hours
8. Remove lid and add the peas, corn, and noodles and stir inches
9. Cover and cook for an additional 45 minutes to an hour before serving<|endoftext|>
