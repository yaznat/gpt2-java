Ingredients:
• Biscuits
• 1% low-fat milk
• Margarine
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Nutmeg
• Vanilla flavoring
• Cinnamon
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Crumble biscuits into large bowl and add milk and melted margarine with other ingredients
2. Mix well to a soupy consistency
3. Let set for 30-45 minutes
4. Pour into baking dish and bake in a moderate oven for 350 degrees for approximately 45 minutes
5. After the first 25 minutes, combine 3 tablespoons of sugar with 1 tablespoon of cinnamon and sprinkle over top of pudding
6. Return to oven
7. Bake 20 minutes more<|endoftext|>
