Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Paprika
• Ground allspice
• Salt and pepper
• Lean ground beef
• Tomatoes
• Tomato paste
• Sweet green pepper
• Fresh parsley
• Flour tortillas
• Cucumber
• Feta cheese
• Pickled hot peppers

Steps:
1. In nonstick skillet, heat oil over medium-high heat
2. Cook onion and garlic, stirring often, for 3 minutes
3. Add paprika, allspice, salt and pepper
4. Cook, stirring, for 1 minute
5. Add beef
6. Cook, breaking up with spoon, until no longer pink, about 5 minutes
7. Drain off any fat
8. Add tomatoes and tomato paste, breaking up tomatoes with fork
9. Bring to boil
10. Reduce heat to medium
11. Add green pepper and parsley
12. Cook, stirring often, until most of the liquid is evaporated, 10 to 15 minutes
13. Divide beef mixture among tortillas
14. Fold tortillas in half to form pockets
15. Sprinkle filling with cucumber, feta cheese, and pickled pepper
16. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
