Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Hot Dog Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Cooking oil
• Eggs
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cooked jasmine rice
• Frozen peas and carrot
• Hot dogs
• Soy sauce
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Place wok over high heat until hot
2. Add 2 tablespoons oil, swirling to coat sides
3. Add eggs, salt and pepper and lightly scramble
4. Remove scrambled eggs and set aside
5. Add sliced hot dogs and stir fry for 2 minutes
6. Add remaining 1 tablespoon oil
7. Add rice
8. Stir fry with hot dogs for 2 minutes
9. Add peas and carrots
10. Stir to mix well
11. Add soy sauce, green onions, and scrambled eggs
12. Stir-fry until heated throughout, about 2-3 minutes<|endoftext|>
