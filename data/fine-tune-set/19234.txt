Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Unsweetened baking cocoa
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Water
• Vegetable oil
• White vinegar
• Vanilla
• Chocolate fudge topping
• Vanilla ice cream
• Malted milk balls
• Whipping cream

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Grease bottom and side of springform pan, 9x3 or 10x2 3 / 4 inches, with shortening
3. Lightly flour
4. In large bowl, mix flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda and salt
5. Add water, oil, vinegar and vanilla
6. Stir vigorously about 1 minute or until well blended
7. Immediately pour into pan
8. Bake 30 to 35 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center of cake comes out clean
9. Cool completely, about 1 hour
10. Spread 1 cup fudge topping over cake
11. Freeze about 1 hour or until topping is firm
12. In large bowl, mix ice cream and coarsely chopped candies
13. Spread over cake
14. Freeze about 4 hours or until ice cream is firm
15. In chilled medium bowl, beat whipping cream with electric mixer on high speed until stiff peaks form
16. Remove side of pan
17. Place cake on serving plate
18. Top with whipped cream
19. Melt 1 / 4 cup fudge topping
20. Drizzle over whipped cream
21. Garnish with additional candies<|endoftext|>
