Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Taco seasoning
• Refried beans
• Flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Roma tomatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Place chicken and taco seasoning in a large zip lock bag and shake to coat chicken
3. Place a tortilla on flata surface and spread beans down center of tortilla
4. Top with chicken, cheese, onions, and tomatoes
5. Roll up and wrap in foil
6. Repeat for each tortilla
7. Bake packets for 15 minutes
8. Serve with sour cream, guacamole, and salsa<|endoftext|>
