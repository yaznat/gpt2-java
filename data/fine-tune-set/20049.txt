Ingredients:
• Eggs
• White potatoes
• Bacon
• Milk
• Cooking oil
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Boil potatoes until tender
2. Drain
3. Beat eggs with about 1 / 2 as much milk
4. Fry up bacon in a medium pan with a little oil
5. Add potatoes and fry a little
6. Pour egg mixture over bacon & potato
7. Let cook over medium heat until just a little runny on top
8. Put under grill to set top
9. Sprinkle a little grated cheese over and brown, if desired
10. Variation: add pretty much anything you want, i've used frozen veggies and mushrooms<|endoftext|>
