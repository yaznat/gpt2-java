Ingredients:
• Spaghetti
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Bacon
• Ketchup
• Dill pickle
• Barbecue sauce
• Prepared mustard
• Shredded cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Cook spaghetti according to package directions
2. Meanwhile, in a large skillet, cook beef and onion over medium heat until meat is no longer pink
3. Drain
4. Stir in the bacon, ketchup, pickles, barbecue sauce and mustard
5. Bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat
7. Simmer, uncovered, for 5 minutes
8. Drain spaghetti
9. Stir into meat mixture
10. Sprinkle with cheese
11. Remove from the heat
12. Cover and let stand until cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
