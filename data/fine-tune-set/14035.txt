Ingredients:
• Boneless lamb shoulder
• Flour
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Oil
• Onion
• Fresh garlic
• Carrots
• Celery ribs
• Brown sugar
• Dried thyme
• Cumin
• Paprika
• Cinnamon
• Clove
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Salt and black pepper
• Dry red wine
• Beef broth
• Button mushroom

Steps:
1. Place the flour, seasoning salt and black pepper in a medium bowl
2. Toss slightly to combine
3. Prepare a large dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot
4. Add in oil to cover bottom of the pot over medium-high heat
5. Coat the lamb cubes with flour then brown in hot oil in two batches
6. Transfer the browned lamb to a plate
7. Add in the onion and cook for about 4-5 minutes, then add in garlic, cook for 1 minute
8. Add in carrots, celery, mushrooms brown sugar, thyme, crushed red pepper flakes and spices
9. Cook stirring for 2 minutes
10. Add in wine, broth and browned lamb
11. Mix to combine
12. Simmer, covered for about 3 hours, stirring occasionally until thickened and the lamb is very tender
13. Season with salt to taste and more black pepper if desired<|endoftext|>
