Ingredients:
• Baking potatoes
• Bacon
• Red onion
• Savoy cabbage
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In large saucepan, add the potatoes and enough salted water to cover
2. Bring to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes
3. Drain and cool
4. Quarter each piece lengthwise, then slice crosswise
5. Meanwhile, in a large nonstick skillet, cook the bacon over medium heat until crisp, about 8 minutes
6. Transfer to paper towels to drain
7. Reserve the rendered bacon fat, leaving 1 t in the skillet
8. Add the onion to the skillet and cook, stirring, until softened, about 3 minutes
9. Add the cabbage and 1 / 2 cup water and cook, stirring occasionally, until crisp-tender, about 4 minutes
10. Transfer to a bowl and keep warm
11. Wipe out the skillet
12. Return the reserved bacon fat to the skillet
13. Add the olive oil and heat over medium heat
14. Add the potatoes, season with salt and pepper and cook, turning occasionally, until crisp and golden-brown, about 8 minutes
15. Stir in the cabbage mixture and cook until heated through<|endoftext|>
