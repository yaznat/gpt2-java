Ingredients:
• Sushi rice
• Firm tofu
• Hot chili oil
• Mayonnaise
• Scallion
• Black sesame seed
• Nori

Steps:
1. Cook tofu how desired, steaming, boiling and frying are all acceptable
2. I like to cut mine into very, very small squares and swirl around a hot dry pan
3. When golden, remove from heat and mash with the mayo and chili oil
4. If you have a sushi rolling mat lay it on the table, otherwise lay a clean tea towel down and cover it with cling film
5. Lay a sheet of nori on top shiny side down, if it is untoasted you may wish to briefly pass it through a flame
6. And place a layer of sushi rice on top
7. For thin sushi layer about 1 / 8 inch thick and for fat rolls layer about 1 / 4 - 1 / 2 think
8. Leaving a gap of around 1 inch at each side
9. Place a line of the tofu mix 1 inch from the edge of the rice
10. Lay the spring onion on top
11. Starting at the edge roll up like a swiss roll keep the rolling a tight as possible using the sushi mat / towel to aid the process
12. When you reach the end moisten the remaining edge with water and stick down
13. Roll up in the towel and allow to sit while you make the rest
14. Using a wet, sharp knife slice into 1 / 2 inch thick slices
15. Remove the ends and eat
16. Place the rest on a platter and serve<|endoftext|>
