Can you give me a custard recipe?<|endoftext|>Canadian Blueberry Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• Blueberries
• Granulated sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Lemon rind
• Lemon juice
• Egg yolks
• Light cream
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. You will need sufficient pastry for a deep 1-crust 9 inch pie
2. Line a pie shell with the pastry
3. Preheat oven to 425f
4. To make the filling: measure the blueberries into a medium bowl
5. Combine the sugar, flour, salt and lemon rind, toss with the berries and pour into the pie shell
6. Sprinkle on the lemon juice
7. Arrange the berries evenly
8. Line the edges of the pastry with strips of aluminum foil to keep them from browning too fast
9. Bake for 15 minutes, then reduce heat to 350f and bake for another 15 minutes
10. While the pie is baking, prepare the custard by beating the yolks and sugar together, then mixing in the cream
11. Spoon the custard gently over the berries in the baked pie
12. Grate a light film of nutmeg over the top
13. Continue baking at 350f for 20 to 35 minutes
14. The custard is ready when the
15. Cool and serve freshly made
16. Yields 6 servings
