What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Aunt Annie S Chicken Chili

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red bell pepper
• Cumin seeds
• Chili powder
• Oregano
• Ro-tel tomatoes
• Tomato sauce
• Beer
• Black beans
• Corn
• Ripe olives
• Cilantro
• Cheddar cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Heat 3 t
2. Oil in dutch oven and saute chicken until no longer pink
3. Remove chicken and wipe pot dry
4. Heat remaining oil and saute onion, garlic, pepper and cumin 5 minutes
5. Stir in chili powder, oregano, tomatoes, tomato sauce and beer
6. Add chicken, cover and simmer for 20 minutes
7. Add beans, corn and olives
8. Simmer 15 minutes longer
9. Stir in cilantro
10. Top with cheddar and sour cream
11. Serve<|endoftext|>
