Can you give me a cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Spritz Press Cookies

Ingredients:
• Shortening
• Sugar
• Egg
• Sifted flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Almond extract
• Food coloring

Steps:
1. Cream shortening, adding sugar in gradually
2. Add unbeaten egg, sifted dry ingredients, flavoring and a few drops of food coloring
3. Mix well
4. Fill cookie press
5. Form cookies on ungreased cookie sheets using the tree plate
6. Decorate with tiny multi-colored sprinkles
7. Bake at 400f for 6-8 minutes
8. Cool just slightly and remove from cookie sheet to cooling rack<|endoftext|>
