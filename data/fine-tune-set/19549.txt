Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Cauliflower
• Red onion
• Oil
• Mustard seeds
• Cumin seed
• Garlic
• Ginger
• Coriander powder
• Red chili powder
• Salt
• Garam masala
• Mango powder
• Green coriander

Steps:
1. Wash and peel the potatoes
2. Cut into long half inch thick pieces
3. Microwave for 6 to 8 minutes, covered, for faster cooking
4. Heat the oil in a wide skillet on high heat
5. Add mustard seeds and cover
6. As soon as the seeds start popping, reduce the heat and add cumin seeds
7. As they change color, add garlic
8. Add the half cooked potatoes, cauliflower pieces, and remaining seasonings
9. Cook covered until ready
10. Stir and sprinkle water if needed
11. Lower the heat to prevent burning
12. Mix in garam masala, mango powder, and chopped red onion
13. Serve hot garnished with green coriander<|endoftext|>
