Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Beef
• Onions
• Green bell peppers
• Salt
• Pepper
• Tortillas

Steps:
1. On a hot skillet, fry bacon & remove excess fat
2. Add beef fajitas and cook until medium rare
3. Add onions, peppers, salt & pepper
4. Finish cooking and serve on tortillas, spice up with hot salsa<|endoftext|>
