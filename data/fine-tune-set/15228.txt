What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Boboli Pizza

Ingredients:
• Boboli pizza crust
• Oil
• Pepper
• Garlic clove
• Tomatoes
• Blue cheese
• Dried oregano

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Place crust on a slightly greased baking sheet or pizza pan
3. In a small bowl, combine the oil, pepper, and garlic
4. Brush generously over the crust
5. Scatter the tomato, oregano, and cheese onto the crust
6. Bake at 400 degrees for 10-15 minutes, or until the cheese melts<|endoftext|>
