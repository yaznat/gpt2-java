What's a recipe for pancakes?<|endoftext|>Baked Pancakes

Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Milk
• Egg
• Oil
• Mini chocolate chip

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees f
2. Mix bisquick, milk, egg and oil
3. Batter will be lumpy
4. Stir in chips
5. Pour into 15 1 / 2 x 10 1 / 2 x 1" pan
6. Bake for approximately 10 minutes
7. Cut into squares and serve with butter and syrup<|endoftext|>
