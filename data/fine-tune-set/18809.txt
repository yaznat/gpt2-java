Give me a custard recipe.<|endoftext|>Ann S Squash Custard

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter
• Salt and pepper
• Green chili
• Squash
• Tortilla chips

Steps:
1. Beat together eggs, milk, butter, salt and pepper
2. Add squash and then chiles
3. Top with crushed tortilla chips
4. Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes, or until set<|endoftext|>
