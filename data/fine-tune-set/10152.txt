Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• All-purpose flour
• Water
• Pearl barley
• Tomato sauce
• Beef bouillon granules
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Dried oregano

Steps:
1. In a large saucepan, cook beef and onion over medium heat until meat is no longer pink
2. Drain
3. Stir in flour
4. Add the remaining ingredients
5. Bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat, simmer, uncovered, for 1 hour or until barley is tender<|endoftext|>
