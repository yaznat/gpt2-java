What's a recipe for chili?<|endoftext|>Black Bean Chili

Ingredients:
• Oil
• Chorizo sausage
• Beef
• Ground pork
• Onions
• Green peppers
• Garlic cloves
• Jalapenos
• Tomatoes
• Beer
• Chili powder
• Cumin
• Salt
• Pepper
• Black beans
• Frozen corn

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tablespoon oil
2. Cook chorizo
3. Drain
4. Cook ground beef and pork together
5. Drain 1 tablespoon oil
6. Cook vegetables about 5 minutes
7. Add to vegetables: meats, tomato, beer, chili powder, cumin, salt and pepper
8. Cover and cook 1 hour
9. Stir in beans and corn
10. Cook 15 minutes longer
11. Serve with warm corn tortillas and butter or cornbread<|endoftext|>
