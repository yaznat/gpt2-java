What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Bean And Chicken Taco Salad

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Head iceberg lettuce
• Sweet onion
• Black beans
• Avocado
• Cheddar cheese
• Nacho cheese flavored tortilla chips
• Taco seasoning
• Fresh cilantro
• Thousand island dressing

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine chicken, lettuce, onion, beans, avocado and cheese
2. Toss to combine well
3. Add crushed chips-mix well
4. In a small bowl, combine taco seasoning, cilantro and thousand island dressing and stir well
5. Pour over salad and toss to coat<|endoftext|>
