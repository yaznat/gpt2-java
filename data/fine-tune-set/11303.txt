Give me a sushi recipe.<|endoftext|>Ya Taki Sushi

Ingredients:
• Sushi rice
• Nori
• Sweet potato
• Shiitake mushrooms
• Soy sauce
• Mirin
• Sesame oil

Steps:
1. Boil the sweet potato until tender
2. Steam the mushrooms until tender
3. Place the potato, mushrooms and liquids in a bowl and either mash or blend together
4. If you have a sushi rolling mat lay it on the table, otherwise lay a clean tea towel down and cover it with cling film
5. Lay a sheet of nori on top shiny side down, if it is untoasted you may wish to briefly pass it through a flame
6. And place a layer of sushi rice on top
7. For thin sushi layer about 1 / 8 inch thick and for fat rolls layer about 1 / 4 - 1 / 2 think, leaving a gap of around 1 inch at each side
8. Spread the mix in a line about 1 inch from the edge of the rice
9. I like to puree my mix and pipe a line using an icing bag
10. Starting at the edge roll up like a swiss roll keep the rolling a tight as possible using the sushi mat / towel to aid the process
11. When you reach the end moisten the remaining edge with water and stick down
12. Roll up in the towel and allow to sit while you make the rest
13. Using a wet, sharp knife slice into 1 / 2 inch thick slices
14. Remove the ends and eat
15. Place the rest on a platter and serve<|endoftext|>
