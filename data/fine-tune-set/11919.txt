Ingredients:
• Oil
• Potatoes
• Cayenne powder
• Green onions
• Salt

Steps:
1. In a large nonstick frying pan put the oil and heat over high heat
2. Once the pan is hot, add the potatoes
3. Stir-fry frequently until the potatoes are golden brown, around 20-25 minutes
4. You may need to reduce the heat to medium-high after the potatoes have cooked a bit
5. The last 2 minutes of cooking stir in the cayenne, green onions and salt
6. Serve at once
7. This type of recipe doesn't usually have onions, but i like the mild flavor of the green ones<|endoftext|>
