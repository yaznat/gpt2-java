What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>The Creamed Dried Beef Chipped Beef Recipe

Ingredients:
• Dried beef
• Butter
• Flour
• Milk
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Melt butter in 2 quart sauce pan or larger
2. Shred dried beef by hand into small pieces
3. Add dried beef to melted butter and cook over medium-high heat until edges curl
4. Stir in flour and mix thoroughly
5. Add milk, reduce heat to medium low and continue stirring frequently until mixture is smooth & bubbly
6. When it's as thick as you'd like, bring to a boil, stirring constantly and boil 1 minute
7. Remove from heat and serve over toast and / or home fried potatoes<|endoftext|>
