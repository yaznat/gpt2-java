Can you give me a burger recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Wrapped Cheeseburgers

Ingredients:
• Cheddar cheese
• Egg
• Ketchup
• Parmesan cheese
• Worcestershire sauce
• Salt
• Pepper
• Ground beef
• American cheese
• Bacon
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Preheat an outdoor grill and lightly oil grate
2. In a large bowl, combine first 8 ingredients
3. Crumble beef over mixture and mix thoroughly
4. Shape into 8 patties
5. Wrap bacon around each patty and secure with a toothpick
6. Place burger patties on prepared grill, top with cheese slices at the end of grill time
7. Serve with lettuce, tomatoes, pickles and onions<|endoftext|>
