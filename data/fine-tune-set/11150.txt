Can you give me a sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Beans Cheddar And Pickle Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Rye bread
• Extra-sharp cheddar cheese
• Vegetarian baked beans
• Kosher dill pickle

Steps:
1. Toast the bread, as desired
2. Smash the beans down onto one slice, top with the pickle and cheese, cover and enjoy!
3. Must use a seriously sharp cheese!
4. I use heinz vegetarian baked beans, but the flavor of any sort of sweet baked beans should work with the sharp cheddar and pickles
5. I eat this with cold or warm beans, but always solid cheese<|endoftext|>
