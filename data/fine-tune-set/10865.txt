What's a good salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Abidjan Cabbage Salad

Ingredients:
• Cabbage
• Carrot
• Pineapple chunk
• Lemon, juice of
• Orange, juice of
• Salt
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Pile the cabbage, carrots, pineapple into a large bowl
2. Mix the dressing by whisking all the ingredients until creamy
3. Thoroughly mix the salad and dressing
4. Serve immediately or refrigerate until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
