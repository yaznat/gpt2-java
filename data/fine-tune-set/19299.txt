What's a good popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Mango Popsicles Durofrios De Mango

Ingredients:
• Mangoes
• Sugar
• Water
• Cracked ice

Steps:
1. If using fresh mangoes, place a mango on its side on a cutting board
2. With a sharp knife, cut straight down on either side of the large, flat-sided pit in the middle of the mango
3. Scoop out the mango pulp from both sides
4. Repeat with the second mango
5. Place the mango pulp, sugar, 1 / 2 cup water, and the ice in a blender or a food processor with steel knife blade
6. Blend until smooth and frosty
7. Pour the mixture into popsicle mold or ice trays and freeze until solid, about 2 hours<|endoftext|>
