Give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>A Little Different Cranberry Salad

Ingredients:
• Cherry jell-o
• Hot water
• Sour cream
• Whole berry cranberry sauce
• Walnuts
• Celery

Steps:
1. After mixing the jello and hot water until jello is dissolved, put in refrigerator until slightly set
2. Remove from refrigerator and add the sour cream and cranberry sauce and the walnuts and celery if using
3. Put back in the refrigerator until set<|endoftext|>
