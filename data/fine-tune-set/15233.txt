What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Bountiful Brunch Pizza

Ingredients:
• Hash browns
• Egg
• Salt and pepper
• Eggs
• Milk
• Ham
• Green bell pepper
• Fresh mushrooms
• Green onion
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Cover 15" round baking stone with sheet of parchment paper
3. For crust, combine potatoes, egg, salt, and pepper in a 2 quart bowl
4. Mix well
5. Spread potato mixture into a 14" circle on prepared stone
6. Pat down with spatula
7. Bake 10 minutes
8. Remove from oven
9. For egg topping, whisk eggs and milk in a 1 quart bowl
10. Season with salt and pepper
11. Microwave on high 3 minutes
12. Stir
13. Microwave an additional 3 minutes
14. Stir
15. Spread the cooked egg mixture evenly over the potato crust
16. Chop ham and bell pepper
17. Slice mushrooms and green onion
18. Sprinkle ham, bell pepper, mushrooms, onions, and cheese over the eggs
19. Bake 10 minutes
20. Cut as you would a pizza and serve<|endoftext|>
