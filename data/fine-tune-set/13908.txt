Give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Baked Lamb Chops And Lentils

Ingredients:
• Lamb chops
• Dried rosemary
• Garlic
• Brown onion
• Water
• Dry white wine
• Dried oregano
• Dried thyme
• Chili powder
• Brown lentils
• Crushed tomatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 220 deg celsius
2. Trim chops of excess fat, leaving a small amount for browning
3. Heat a non-stick pan to high and brown chops well on both sides
4. Remove to a lidded casserole big enough so the chops are crowded but in 1 layer
5. Sprinkle chops with rosemary and then top with the garlic
6. Drain fat from pan and wipe clean
7. Return pan to heat and dry fry onions, adding a small amount of water if needed
8. Once soft and translucent transfer from pan to casserole, layering over garlic
9. Place casserole in oven
10. Return pan to heat and add water, wine, oregano, thyme and chilli and bring to the boil
11. Add lentils and return to the boil
12. Cover and lower heat to simmer
13. Cook for five minutes
14. Add canned tomatoes to lentils and return to boil
15. Taste and season with salt and fresh ground black pepper, as preferred
16. Remove casserole from oven
17. Transfer lentils and tomatos to casserole being careful of spatter
18. Push chops around so liquid goes to bottom but do not mix
19. Cover and return to oven for approximately 1 hour
20. Meat should almost fall off the bone
21. Remove from oven, stir and let sit, covered, for 10 minutes before adjusting the seasoning and serving<|endoftext|>
