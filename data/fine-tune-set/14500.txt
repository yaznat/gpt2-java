What's a good beans recipe?<|endoftext|>Asparagus Bundles Or Green Bean Bundles

Ingredients:
• Asparagus spears
• Bacon
• Garlic
• Light brown sugar
• Butter

Steps:
1. Gently wrap approximately 5 beans or asparagus stalks with 1 / 2 slice bacon and secure with toothpick
2. Continue until all beans / stalks are wrapped
3. Place in casserole dish
4. Simmer garlic, brown sugar and butter for 5 minutes and pour over beans / stalks
5. Bake at 375f for 25 minutes or 350f for 35 minutes<|endoftext|>
