What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Alaska Molasses Cookies

Ingredients:
• Applesauce
• Sugar
• Butter
• Dark molasses
• Egg
• Flour
• Whole wheat pastry flour
• Baking soda
• Ground cinnamon
• Salt
• Ground ginger
• Ground cloves
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Place applesauce onto several layers of heavy-duty paper towels
2. Spread to 1 / 2-inch thickness
3. Cover with additional paper towels
4. Let stand 5 minutes
5. Scrape into a bowl using a rubber spatula
6. Combine applesauce, 1 cup sugar, and butter
7. Beat at medium speed for 3 minutes
8. Add molasses and egg
9. Beat well
10. Combine flours and next 5 ingredients, stirring well with a whisk
11. Gradually add flour mixture to sugar mixture, beating until blended
12. Cover and freeze dough 30 minutes or until firm
13. While dough chills, preheat oven to 375f
14. With moist hands, shape dough into 32 balls
15. Roll balls in 1 / 4 cup sugar
16. Place 3 inches apart on baking sheets coated with cooking spray
17. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes
18. Cool on pans 5 minutes before removing from pans to cool completely on wire racks<|endoftext|>
