Please give me a recipe for bread.<|endoftext|>Almost Breadmaker Bread

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Sugar
• Salt
• Margarine
• Whole wheat flour
• White flour
• Yeast

Steps:
1. Combine ingredients in order given in breadmaker pail
2. Set for dough setting
3. When done, remove from pail to a floured surface
4. Punch down
5. Grease 2 regular loaf pans
6. Divide dough into 2 equal parts
7. Roll into loaf size and shape
8. Place in greased pans
9. Cover with a cloth, and let rise 35 minutes, or until doubled in size, in a warm place
10. Preheat oven to 350f
11. Make 2 diagonal slashes on the top of each loaf
12. Place bread in centre of oven
13. Bake 30 minutes
14. Remove to cooling rack, and turn out of pans to cool<|endoftext|>
