Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Soymilk
• Ham
• Spring onion
• Butter
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Wash and chop spring onion
2. Wash and slice the ham
3. In a mixing bowl, combine eggs, ham, milk and onion
4. Beat well
5. Heat a frying pan on medium and melt margarine
6. Pour egg mixture into pan
7. As the omelette begins to set, lift up edges with egg flipper so uncooked portion of egg can flow under the cooked portion
8. When eggs are set but the top is still moist, sprinkle of the cheese over it
9. Fold one edge of the omelette over the cheese, then sprinkle the remaining cheese over the top
10. Slide omelette onto the plate and cut into portions<|endoftext|>
