Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Abc Bread

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Olive oil
• Applesauce
• Banana
• Carrot
• Cinnamon
• Allspice
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Vanilla
• Whole wheat flour

Steps:
1. Beat eggs
2. Add sugar, oil and vanilla
3. Add applesauce, bananas, and carrot
4. Mix well
5. Mix spices into bowl then add flour gradually
6. Pour equally into 2 greased loaf pans
7. Bake at 350 for 1 hours or until knife inserted comes out clean<|endoftext|>
