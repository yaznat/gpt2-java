Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Barbecue Beefy Buns

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Butter
• Water
• Ketchup
• Sugar
• Prepared mustard
• Vinegar
• Salt and pepper
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef with onion and green pepper in butter in skillet, stir frequently and then drain
2. Add remaining ingredients except buns of course
3. Mix well
4. Simmer, covered for 15 minutes
5. Serve on buns<|endoftext|>
