What's a recipe for tofu?<|endoftext|>Creamy Alfredo With Tofu Shirataki

Ingredients:
• Neufchatel cheese
• Milk
• Dried chives
• Garlic salt
• Lemon juice
• Fresh ground pepper
• Tofu shirataki noodles

Steps:
1. Drain and rinse shirataki noodles
2. Cook in microwave for 1 minute then rinse, drain, pat dry and chop noodles into manageable lengths
3. Melt reduced fat cream cheese and milk in the microwave on medium heat for 1-3 minutes
4. Stirring every 30 seconds until smooth and warm
5. Add chives, garlic salt, lemon juice, ground pepper and tofu noodles
6. Return to microwave, heating for another 1-2 minutes stirring every 30 seconds until desired temperature is reached<|endoftext|>
