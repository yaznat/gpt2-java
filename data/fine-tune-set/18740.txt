Ingredients:
• Milk
• Vanilla instant pudding mix
• Sour cream
• Firm bananas
• Sugar
• Pecans
• Egg

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine milk, pudding mix and sour cream
2. Whisk until mixture begins to thicken, about 1 minute
3. Fold in bananas
4. Pour into a 1-1 / 2 quart serving bowl
5. Cover and refrigerate
6. For topping, combine sugar, pecans and egg
7. Spoon onto a greased 15-in
8. X10-in
9. X1-in
10. Baking pan
11. Bake at 350 for 20 minutes or until browned and crunchy
12. Cool
13. Using a spatula, loosen pecan mixture from pan and break into small pieces
14. Sprinkle over pudding just before serving<|endoftext|>
