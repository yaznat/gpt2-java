What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Cinnamon Cookies

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Baking soda
• Cream of tartar
• Ground cinnamon
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Blanched almond
• All-purpose flour
• Powdered sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Cookies:
2. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
3. In a large bowl, beat butter with an electric mixer on medium to high speed for 30 seconds
4. Add the sugar, baking soda, cream of tarter, and cinnamon
5. Beat until combined, scraping sides of bowl occasionally
6. Beat in egg and vanilla until combined
7. Beat in ground almonds
8. Beat in as much of the flour as you can with the mixer
9. Using a wooden spoon, stir in any remaining flour
10. Shape dough into 1-inch balls, or scoop dough with a 1-inch cookie scoop
11. Place balls 2 inches apart on an ungreased cookie sheet
12. Bake about 13 minutes or until cookies are light brown
13. Transfer cookies to a wire rack
14. Cool
15. Drizzle cookies with browned butter drizzle
16. Drizzle:
17. In a small saucepan, heat butter over medium heat until butter turns the color of light brown sugar, stirring frequently
18. Remove from heat
19. Slowly stir in powdered sugar, vanilla, and enough milk to make of drizzling consistency<|endoftext|>
