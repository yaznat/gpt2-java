What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>100 Whole Wheat Bread Low Fat And Good

Ingredients:
• Water
• Yeast
• Egg
• Salt
• Honey
• Applesauce
• Whole wheat flour

Steps:
1. Combine yeast and warm water and let sit for about 5-10 minutes
2. Add a pinch of sugar to hurry it along
3. Mix egg, salt, honey, water and applesauce in large bowl
4. Stir in 1-2 cups of flour until smooth
5. Add in foamy yeast mixture
6. Gradually add the rest of the flour, stirring with a wooden spoon until it becomes too difficult, and then begin to knead with both hands
7. Turn dough out onto floured counter-top and knead until dough become elastic and smooth, form into a ball
8. Grease large bowl and put dough in it, turning to coat it
9. Cover with a damp towel, leaving it on counter to rise, or cover and refrigerate to bake later
10. When well-risen turn out on counter, punch dough down and divide into three pieces
11. Roll each out into a long rectangle and roll up
12. Place each roll up piece of dough into a greased bread pan and let rise for 45-60 minutes
13. Bake in oven at 350f for exactly 30 minutes
14. Enjoy!
15. Freeze warm bread i bread bags to lock in moistness
16. May freeze extra loaves for up to 2 months
17. A family of four can easily eat two loaves in a week and half, leaving you to give one away, without freezing any
18. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
