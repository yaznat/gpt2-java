Give me a steak recipe.<|endoftext|>Best Ever Skirt Steak And Bacon Wrapped Chicken Kabobs

Ingredients:
• Skirt steak
• Boneless skinless chicken cutlets
• Teriyaki sauce
• Sesame seeds
• White vermouth
• Garlic
• Onion
• Bacon
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Trim all excess fat from skirt steak and slice into kabob sized slices
2. Cut chicken into pieces that corresponds to the cooking time of the steak
3. Combine teriyaki sauce, vermouth, garlic and onion in a large bowl
4. Add meats and marinate 4-6 hours, turning occasionally
5. Meanwhile, soak wooden skewers in water until ready to make kabobs
6. When ready, wrap 1 / 2 slice bacon around each piece of chicken
7. Make kabobs by alternating steak and chicken on skewers, making sure to stab through the bacon to keep it wrapped around the chicken
8. Cook over med-high heat on bbq, turning once, cooking about 15 minutes total
9. Baste with leftover marinade, and squirt with lemon juice during cooking
10. If you cant find teriyaki sauce with sesame seeds, add lots of sesame seeds to the marinade -- i dont know if it makes a difference, but why take the chance for such a small thing
11. I found soy vay to be the absolute best brand of teriyaki sauce
12. Time does not include marinating time, but the prep is so quick that i did all of it while getting my kids out the door for school in the morning
13. Then, after setting up the kabobs i refrigerated them until ready to cook that evening<|endoftext|>
