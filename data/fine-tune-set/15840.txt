Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Buffalo wings seasoning blend
• Water
• Sour cream substitute
• Diced green chilies
• Black beans
• Tortillas

Steps:
1. Put everything into crock, except for chicken and tortillas, and mix around
2. Then add chicken in and cook for 6-8 hours
3. Take chicken out and shred
4. Put back in and get all pieces moist
5. Put on table and let people serve themselves<|endoftext|>
