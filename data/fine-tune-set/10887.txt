Give me a salad recipe.<|endoftext|>African Spiced Broccoli And Cauliflower Salad

Ingredients:
• Broccoli floret
• Cauliflower floret
• Carrot
• Salt
• Ground ginger
• Ground cumin
• Ground coriander
• Ground nutmeg
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Soy sauce
• Nonfat sour cream
• Cider vinegar
• Honey
• Green onions

Steps:
1. Steam first 3 ingredients, covered, 2 minutes
2. Rinse broccoli mixture under cold water
3. Drain well
4. Combine salt and next 5 ingredients in a small skillet
5. Cook over medium heat 2 minutes or until lightly browned
6. Stirring constantly
7. Combine spice mixture, soy sauce, sour cream, vinegar and honey in a bowl
8. Stir well
9. Add broccoli mixture
10. Toss well to coat
11. Stir in green onions just before serving<|endoftext|>
