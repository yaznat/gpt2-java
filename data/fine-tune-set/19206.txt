Ingredients:
• Chocolate ice cream
• Angel food cake
• Amaretto liqueur
• Cocoa powder

Steps:
1. You may have to fiddle around with the cuts if you can't find it that way
2. I also used no sugar added triple chocolate ice cream
3. Line a loaf pan with plastic wrap leaving a good size overhang on both sides
4. This will allow you to pull it out later with ease, as well as to wrap it up
5. Cut your rectangular cake lengthwise into 4 even slices
6. Cut 1 of the slices again lengthwise in half
7. Cut a second slice in half the other direction
8. These are your building blocks for the cake
9. Lay one of the full slices on the bottom on the loaf pan
10. Place the 2 smaller pieces on the ends, and the 2 longer half slices on the sides
11. You should have a sort of cake shell now
12. Drizzle amaretto liqueur all over the cake pieces, holding your fingers over the bottle so it doesn't come out to fast
13. Soak it as little or as much as you'd like!
14. Scoop 4 heaping scoops of ice cream into the center of the cake shell
15. Drizzle more amaretto liqueur onto the last slice of cake and place it on the top shutting the ice cream inches then fold the plastic wrap over it and press to really close it all together
16. Place in freezer for at least an hour to let the ice cream freeze back up and serve in slices with cocoa powder sprinkled over each piece!<|endoftext|>
