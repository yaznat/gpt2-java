Generate a recipe for custard.<|endoftext|>Custard Drink

Ingredients:
• Custard powder
• Sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. In a mug, combine custard powder and sugar
2. Add a few tablespoons of the milk until it becomes a paste
3. Boil water, and add, topping off with milk to taste
4. Let it stand for a few minutes, for thickness- you can also serve with a dollop of ice-cream on the top!<|endoftext|>
