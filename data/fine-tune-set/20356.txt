Give me a recipe for a casserole.<|endoftext|>Missy S Sweet Potato Casserole

Ingredients:
• Cooked sweet potatoes
• Sugar
• Butter
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon
• Flour
• Light brown sugar
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Mash the sweet potatoes
3. Mix the sugar, melted butter, eggs, milk, vanilla and cinnamon into the sweet potatoes
4. Spray a baking dish with non-stick cooking spray and pour potato mixture into it
5. Mix the topping ingredients together in a small bowl
6. Sprinkle topping over the sweet potato mixture
7. Bake for approximately 35 minutes, or until the casserole gets brown and bubbly<|endoftext|>
