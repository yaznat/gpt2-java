Please give me a recipe for soup.<|endoftext|>Acorn Squash Soup

Ingredients:
• Pumpernickel bread
• Butter
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Garlic clove
• Onion
• Celery
• Flour
• Chicken bouillon granule
• Dill weed
• Curry powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Kosher salt
• Chicken broth
• Evaporated milk
• Acorn squash
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Remove crust from bread
2. With a cookie cutter, make desired shape
3. Discard scraps
4. In a small bowl, combine butter, olive oil and salt
5. Brush over both sides of bread
6. Place on a greased baking pan
7. Bake in a preheated 300 degree oven for 10 to 15 minutes or until toasted
8. Cool on a wire rack
9. In a large saucepan, saute onion, garlic, and celery in 2t butter until tender, but not browned
10. Stir in flour, bouillon, dill, curry and cayenne until well blended
11. Cook 1 minute
12. Gradually add broth and evaporated milk
13. Bring to a boil
14. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened
15. Stir in mashed squash and pepper to taste and heat through
16. In a blender, process the soup in batches until smooth
17. Remember to hold unto the cover of the blender or you'll have a mess
18. Pour soup into bowls
19. Garnish with pumpernickle cutouts<|endoftext|>
