Give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Mocha Caramel Kahlua Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Whole milk
• Instant espresso powder
• Chocolate syrup
• Caramel sauce
• Vanilla ice cream
• Kahlua
• Whipped cream
• Chocolate-covered coffee beans

Steps:
1. Combine the milk, espresso powder, chocolate syrup, caramel sauce, and kahlua in a blender and blend until combined, about 5 seconds
2. Add the ice cream and blend until smooth, about 10 seconds
3. Serve immediately, garnished with the whipped cream and chocolate-covered espresso beans and a drizzle of caramel sauce, if desired<|endoftext|>
