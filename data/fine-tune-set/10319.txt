Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Unsalted butter
• Granulated sugar
• Light brown sugar
• Egg yolk
• Vanilla extract
• Water
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Steps:
1. With the rack positioned at the highest level, preheat the oven to 400f
2. Combine the flour, baking soda, and salt and set aside
3. With the mixer on low speed, combine the butter with both sugars, the egg yolk, vanilla, and water
4. Increase the speed to high and beat until thoroughly mixed and light
5. With a spoon or dinner fork, combine the creamed mixture with the dry ingredients
6. Stir or hand kneed in the chocolate chips
7. Using a heaping tablespoon of batter as a guide, make 13 evenly sized balls and place 2 inches apart onto a cookie sheet
8. Alternatively, using the
9. Decrease the oven temperature to 350f
10. Bake the cookies for 5 minutes, then reverse the pan so the back is in the front
11. Increase the oven temperature to 375f
12. And bake for 4 to 5 minutes longer
13. The cookies appear golden brown with small pale centers
14. Cookies will complete cooking as they cool on the pan
15. The shorter baking time produces a chewier cookie
16. Cool cookies in a pan on a wire rack for 2 minutes
17. Remove the cookies to a wire rack for complete cooling<|endoftext|>
