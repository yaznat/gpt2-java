Ingredients:
• Fresh strawberries
• Sugar
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Eggs
• Sour cream
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Whipped cream

Steps:
1. Place strawberries and 3 tablespoons sugar in food processor, cover and pulse just until coarsely chopped
2. In a bowl, combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and remaining 2 tablespoons sugar
3. Combine eggs, sour cream, milk and oil and stir into dry ingredients just until combined
4. Preheat waffle iron, fill and bake according to manufacturer's directions
5. Serve with strawberry topping and whipped cream<|endoftext|>
