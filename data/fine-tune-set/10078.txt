Ingredients:
• Boneless chicken breast
• Salt
• Pepper
• Ground ancho chili pepper
• Flour
• Sugar
• Egg whites
• Vegetable oil
• Barbecue sauce
• Chili sauce
• Honey
• Brown sugar
• Butter

Steps:
1. Salt and lightly pepper both sides the chicken breast
2. Liberally add the ancho chili pepper onto both sides of the breasts
3. Cut the breasts into 1 inch cubes
4. Whisk the egg whites
5. In a ziploc bag or mixing bowl, combine the flour, sugar, and 2 tablespoons of acho chili pepper
6. Take a handful of the cut chicken and dip into the egg whites
7. Place the chicken in the flour mixture and shake well
8. Place the chicken in the deep fryer at 355 degrees for 6 to 7 minutes
9. Drain on rack and set aside on paper towel
10. For sauce:
11. Melt butter in non stick pan
12. Stir in honey and brown sugar until well blended
13. Stir in chili and barbeque sauces
14. Add remaining tablespoon of ancho chili pepper
15. Stir
16. Bring to boil then let simmer for 5 minutes
17. Place the chicken in a tupperware bowl
18. Pour sauce over chicken
19. Put the top over tupperware and shake chicken and sauce until chicken is evenly coated with sauce
20. Serve over rice, with cajun fries, or food of your choice<|endoftext|>
