Ingredients:
• Yukon gold potatoes
• Peanut oil
• Kosher salt

Steps:
1. Combine potatoes and oil in large dutch oven
2. Cook over high heat until oil has reached a rolling boil, about 5 minutes
3. Continue to cook, without stirring, until potatoes are limp but exteriors are beginning to firm, about 12-15 minutes
4. Using tongs, stir potatoes, gently scraping up any that stick, and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until golden and crisp, 5-10 minutes longer
5. Using skimmer or slotted spoon, transfer fries to thick paper bag or paper towels
6. Season with salt and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
