Please give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Good Eats Waffles Alton Brown

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Sugar
• Kosher salt
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Unsalted butter
• Buttermilk

Steps:
1. Whisk dry ingredients
2. In separate bowl, whisk eggs and butter
3. Then add in buttermilk
4. Plug in waffle iron
5. Pour in wet ingredients and stir with a spatula
6. Do not overstir, the batter should be lumpy and have bubbles
7. Let the batter sit for five minutes
8. Spray waffle iron with pam spray
9. Pour in batter and cook<|endoftext|>
