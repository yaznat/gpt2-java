Give me a fried rice recipe.<|endoftext|>Guy Fieri S Bbq Pork Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Oyster sauce
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic
• Sesame oil
• Pork tenderloin
• Sweet and sour sauce
• Canola oil
• Red bell pepper
• Yellow bell pepper
• Red onion
• Green cabbage
• Snap peas
• Carrot
• Celery
• Short-grain white rice
• Eggs
• Green onion
• Toasted sesame seeds

Steps:
1. Combine soy sauce, oyster sauce, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil in a medium bowl
2. Add pork
3. Cover and marinate for 1 hour in the refrigerator
4. Heat grill to medium-high, and grill pork on both sides until done
5. Heat remaining marinade to 165 degrees f and use to baste meat as it cooks
6. When meat is almost cooked through, glaze both sides with sweet and sour sauce and let finish cooking while sauce caramelizes on meat
7. Remove from grill and allow to stand for 10 minutes
8. While pork is standing, heat oil in wok or large skillet on high heat until oil almost begins to smoke
9. Add ginger and all vegetables except garlic
10. Cook for 3 minutes, or until vegetables are cooked, then add garlic
11. Before garlic browns, add rice and mix rapidly so rice does not stick to sides of wok or pan
12. Pour beaten eggs over rice and vegetables
13. Toss again rapidly until all egg is cooked
14. Add the soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil and mix thoroughly
15. Cut pork in 1 / 2" pieces and stir inches
16. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds<|endoftext|>
