Ingredients:
• Instant coffee
• Unsweetened cocoa powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Granulated sugar
• Cool whip

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients, except the cool whip
2. To make a cup of coffee: put 1 tbsp of mixture in a large coffee mug
3. Pour 1 1 / 2 cup boiling water over mix, and stir
4. Top with a dollop of cool whip, or reddi whip
5. To make a smaller cup of coffee: use 1 / 2 tbsp of coffee mixture to 3 / 4 cup boiling water<|endoftext|>
