Ingredients:
• Granny smith apples
• Brown sugar
• Lemon juice
• Cinnamon
• Gluten-free flour
• Xanthan gum
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sugar
• Butter
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Almond extract
• Buttermilk
• Pecan pieces
• Powdered sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350f
2. Lightly grease an 8-inch round cake pan
3. In a small bowl, combine apples, brown sugar, lemon juice and cinnamon
4. Set aside
5. In a large bowl, whisk together the gf flour, xanthan gum, baking soda and salt
6. In a separate bowl, beat the butter and sugar together with a hand held mixer on medium speed until well-blended
7. Add the egg and extracts
8. Beating well
9. Add the flour mixture to the egg and sugar mixture alternating with buttermilk, beginning and ending with the flour mixture
10. Spoon the batter into the prepared cake pan, spreading the top with a spatula to even it out
11. Arrange the apple mixture over the cake
12. Sprinkle with the pecan pieces
13. Bake for 25 minutes or until the cake begins to pull away from the sides of the pan
14. Cool in the pan on a wire rack for 10 minutes
15. Prepare the glaze: in a small bowl, whisk together the powdered sugar, milk and vanilla extract
16. Drizzle glaze over cake
17. Serve cake warm or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
