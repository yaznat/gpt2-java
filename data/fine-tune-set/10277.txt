What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>3X Chocolate Cookies

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Semisweet chocolate
• Butter
• Light brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Water
• Egg
• Chocolate chips
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Line two cookie sheets with foil, set aside
2. Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt
3. Place the unsweetened and semisweet chocolate and butter in top of double boiler
4. Cover for a few minutes until chocolate begins to melt
5. Uncover and stir until completely melted
6. Remove from heat and allow to cool for 2-3 minutes
7. Add sugar, vanilla, water, egg
8. Stir until smooth
9. Add dry ingredients
10. Stir until incorporated
11. Mix in chocolate bits
12. Drop rounded teaspoonfuls onto foil, about 1 1 / 2 inches apart
13. Top each cookie with a nut half
14. Bake at 350 for 12-13 minutes
15. Slide foil off baking sheets, letting cookies stand on foil for a few minutes before removing them
16. Finish cooling on racks<|endoftext|>
