Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Eggs
• Flour
• Chives
• Salt
• Pepper
• Milk
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Cut 4 slices of bacon in half
3. Cut remaining bacon into 2" pieces
4. Place all bacon in a 10" oven-proof skillet and cook until crisp
5. Drain on paper towels, shaping the 8 large pieces into curls
6. Tilt frying pan to coat with fat
7. Drain excess fat
8. Line pan with small pieces of bacon
9. Beat eggs with flour, 3 teaspoons chives, salt and pepper
10. Beat in milk
11. Pour over bacon in pan
12. Place in oven and bake 35-40 minutes, or until golden and set
13. Top with bacon curls and remaining chives
14. Serve in wedges, garnished with tomato wedges<|endoftext|>
