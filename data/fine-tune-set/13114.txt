Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Bistro Catfish

Ingredients:
• Sun-dried tomato packed in oil
• Garlic
• Catfish fillets
• Salt and pepper
• Fresh spinach

Steps:
1. Heat 2 tablespoons reserved oil in a large skillet, over medium heat
2. Saute garlic for 1 to 2 minutes or until golden
3. Sprinkle both sides of the fish with salt and pepper
4. Place fish in the skillet
5. Cover, and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, or until fish flakes easily with a fork
6. Add the spinach and sun-dried tomatoes to the skillet
7. Cover and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, or until spinach is wilted but still bright green
8. Place the spinach on a serving plate
9. Top with fish fillets
10. Drizzle pan drippings on top and serve<|endoftext|>
