What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Toblerone Adult Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Kahlua
• Baileys irish cream
• Liquid honey
• Milk
• Chocolate ice cream

Steps:
1. Put the ice cream and milk in a blender and blend till smooth add the honey, kahluha, and the bailey's-blend
2. Pour into tall glass and enjoy!
3. Recipe can be made with milk, chocolate syrup and blended with ice instead of ice cream<|endoftext|>
