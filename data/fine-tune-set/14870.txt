Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Onion
• Fresh garlic
• Dried chili pepper flakes
• Red bell pepper
• Cooked white rice
• Cooked pork
• Light soy sauce
• Sliced mushrooms
• Green onion

Steps:
1. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a skillet or wok, add in the eggs and cook breaking up into small pieces
2. Remove to a bowl or plate and set aside
3. Add in 1 more tablespoon oil
4. Heat until hot
5. Add in the chili flakes chopped onions, and red bell pepper
6. Saute for about 4 minutes or until soft, adding in the garlic the last 2 minutes of cooking
7. Add the eggs back into the wok or skillet along with the cooked cold rice and cooked pork and mushrooms
8. Add in the soy sauce mix until heated thoroughly
9. Place in a large bowl and sprinkle with chopped green onions
10. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
