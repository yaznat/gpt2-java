What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>14 Bean Soup

Ingredients:
• Water
• Beans
• Salt
• Chicken broth
• Celery
• Carrots
• Onions
• Tomatoes
• Basil
• Lemon juice
• Parsley
• Chicken bouillon cubes
• V8 vegetable juice
• Raw rice
• Deli ham

Steps:
1. Wash and sort beans
2. Place first 7 ingredients in a large pot
3. Bring to a boil, cover and remove from heat
4. Allow to stand for 1 hour
5. Return to heat and bring to a boil
6. Reduce heat and simmer till veggies are tender adding water if necessary
7. Add the rest of the ingredients and simmer about 30 minutes
8. Serve with bread and a green salad<|endoftext|>
