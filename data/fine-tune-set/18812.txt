Ingredients:
• Apples
• Sugar
• Milk
• Eggs
• Flour
• Salt
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350-degrees f
2. Butter an 8 x 8 baking dish
3. Roll apples in sugar until they are coated evenly
4. Place apples close together in prepared dish
5. Using a blender mix together the remaining ingredients and pour over apples
6. Sprinkle with nutmeg, if desired
7. Bake for 30- 40 minutes or until apples are tender
8. Custard will finish setting while apples are cooling<|endoftext|>
