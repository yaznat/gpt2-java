Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Milk
• Amaretto
• Self-rising flour
• Flaked coconut

Steps:
1. Beat the butter and sugar at medium speed of an electric mixer until light and fluffy
2. Add eggs
3. Beat well
4. Add the milk, amaretto, and flour, beating well
5. Stir in the coconut
6. Pour the mixture into a lightly greased 9-inch pieplate
7. Bake at 350 degrees for 35 minutes or until set
8. Cool completely on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
