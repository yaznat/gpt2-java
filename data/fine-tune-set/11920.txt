What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Ceylon Beef Curry

Ingredients:
• Ground coriander
• Ground cumin
• Stewing beef
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh ginger
• Lemon, juice of
• Cinnamon sticks
• White vinegar
• Salt
• Ground turmeric
• Coconut milk
• Ghee
• Coconut cream

Steps:
1. Put the coriander and cumin into a dry frying pan and roast over low heat till dark brown in colour
2. Add the meat and all of the other spices, coconut cream and meat, simmer till meat is tender
3. Add the coconut milk and simmer for 15 minutes strain and keep the liquid, and set the meat on one side
4. Melt the ghee in a pan and fry the cooked meat for three mins, add the reserved liquid to the pan, bring to the boil and add the salt and lemon juice
5. Served with boiled rice<|endoftext|>
