Ingredients:
• Taco shells
• Oil
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Taco seasoning mix
• Hidden valley ranch dressing mix
• Head lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Mexican blend cheese
• Green onions

Steps:
1. In a small bowl mix together the dry ranch dressing mix with 1 cup milk and 1 cup of mayonnaise mix until well combined
2. Refrigerate until ready to use
3. Heat 2 tablespoon oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
4. Add in the chicken cubes and toss with a spoon until cooked through, seasoning with the taco mix just before the chicken is finished cooking transfer to a bowl
5. Divide the cooked chicken cubes between each taco shell, then drizzle the top of the chicken with a small amount of prepared ranch dressing
6. Top with a small amount of shredded lettuce, chopped tomatoes, shredded cheese, then green onion
7. Drizzle the top of the tacos with more ranch dressing
8. Delicious!<|endoftext|>
