Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Frozen hash brown potatoes
• Bacon
• Onion
• Green pepper
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• Milk
• Salt

Steps:
1. Grease the inside of the crockpot with butter
2. Now layer your ingredients bottom to top using half of each ingredient each time listed
3. 1
4. Potatoes
5. 2
6. Bacon
7. 3
8. Onions and peppers
9. 4
10. Cheese
11. 5
12. Potatoes
13. 6
14. Bacon
15. 7
16. Onions and peppers
17. 8
18. Cheese
19. Now, beat the eggs, milk, and salt together
20. Pour this over the whole mixture
21. Cook on low for 7-8 hours<|endoftext|>
