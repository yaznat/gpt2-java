Ingredients:
• Rice vinegar
• Fresh lemon juice
• Sugar
• Wasabi paste
• Cooked white rice
• Carrot
• Cucumber
• Edamame
• Scallion
• Salt
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, blend together the first 4 ingredients
2. Set aside
3. In a large bowl, toss the rice, carrot, cucumber, edamame, scallions and vinegar mixture together
4. Season with salt to suit taste and garnish with sesame seeds<|endoftext|>
