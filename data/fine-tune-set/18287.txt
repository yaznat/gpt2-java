Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Big B S Pancakes Dairy Free Diabetic Friendly

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Water
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Egg

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients with an electric beater until smooth batter
2. Heat a non-stick frying pan on high
3. Pour enough batter into pan to cover the bottom
4. Shake the pan gently after 2-3 minutes
5. If the pancake lifts, it is cooked
6. If not, cook for another few minutes and try shaking it again until cooked
7. Flip or turn and fry on other side for 2-3 minutes until brown
8. Fill with mince or cheese for a savoury meal, or with cinnamon & sugar for a snack or ice cream & chocolate sauce for dessert<|endoftext|>
