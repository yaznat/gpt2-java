Can you give me a salad recipe?<|endoftext|>Albanian Tossed Salad

Ingredients:
• Romaine lettuce
• Red onion
• Pitted black olives
• Red bell pepper
• Tomatoes
• Cremini mushroom
• Cucumber
• Feta cheese
• Sliced almonds
• Olive oil
• Dried oregano
• Paprika
• Lemon
• Ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Toast almonds or sesame seeds in a dry skillet for 3 minutes or so, stirring and watching carefully so as not to burn
2. Set aside
3. Add lettuce, red onion, black olives, green bell pepper, tomatoes, cucumber, mushrooms and feta cheese to large serving bowl
4. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, dried oregano, paprika, fresh lemon juice, and ground black pepper
5. Pour vinaigrette over vegetables and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
