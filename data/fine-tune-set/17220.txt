Give me a recipe for tea.<|endoftext|>Apple Iced Tea

Ingredients:
• Boiling water
• Tea bags
• Sugar
• Frozen apple juice concentrate
• Cold water
• Cinnamon stick

Steps:
1. Pour a heat-proof bowl or use a saucepan pour 3 cups boiling water over the tea bags
2. Cover and steep for about 5 minutes
3. Remove tea bags and discard
4. Stir in sugar, juice concentrate and 2 cups cold water
5. Stir well
6. Serve over ice
7. Garnish with a cinnamon in each glass<|endoftext|>
