Ingredients:
• Velveeta cheese
• Tomatoes and green chilies
• Cooked chicken
• Green onions
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Combine velveeta and rotel in 1 1 / 2 quart microwaveable bowl
2. Microwave on high 3 minutes or until velveeta is completely melted and mixture is well blended, stirring after 2 minutes
3. Stir in chicken and onions
4. Spray large nonstick skillet with cooking spray and heat on medium-high heat
5. Spoon about 2 / 3 cup chicken mixture onto half of 1 tortilla
6. Fold in half
7. Add to skillet, cook 1 minute on each side or until golden brown on both sides
8. Remove from skillet
9. Repeat with remaining tortillas and filling
