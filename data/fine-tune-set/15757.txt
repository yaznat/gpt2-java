Give me a recipe for a burrito.<|endoftext|>5 Minute Wet Beef Green Chile Burritos

Ingredients:
• Roast beef in gravy
• Diced green chilies
• Garlic powder
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Beef broth
• Wondra flour
• Refried beans
• Monterey jack pepper cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Place roast beef in saucepan and mash with fork
2. Mix in green chiles, garlic powder, chili powder, ground cumin, , beef broth and wondra flour
3. Bring to boil and simmer 2 minutes
4. Place refried beans in a microwave safe bowl, cover with plastic wrap and heat in microwave about 2 - 3 minutes or until heated through
5. Heat tortillas separately over open flame on stove, turning frequently until heated through or heat on griddle or frying pan, turning once
6. Place warmed tortilla on plate and spread with refried beans
7. Remove a serving of beef from saucepan with slotted spoon and place over refried beans
8. Top with shredded cheese
9. Fold sides of tortillas in about 2 inches
10. Roll the burrito, starting with the side closest to you and roll away from you
11. Ladle some beef & green chile sauce over burrito and top with cheese
12. Garnish with a dollup of sour cream<|endoftext|>
