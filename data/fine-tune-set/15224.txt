Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Blt Ranch Salad Pizza Pampered Chef

Ingredients:
• Pillsbury refrigerated classic pizza crust
• Iceberg lettuce
• Plum tomatoes
• Cream cheese
• Ranch dressing
• Garlic clove
• Cheddar cheese
• Olive
• Onion
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Bake crust according to instructions
2. Cool completely
3. Combine cream cheese, 1 / 4 cup ranch dressing & garlic
4. Spread evenly over crust
5. Top with lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, olives, onion & bacon
6. Drizzle with remaining dressing
7. Serve!<|endoftext|>
