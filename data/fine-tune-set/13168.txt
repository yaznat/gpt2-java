Ingredients:
• Buttermilk
• Oil
• Hot pepper sauce
• Dijon mustard
• Fresh garlic
• Seasoning salt
• Black pepper
• Onions
• Chicken pieces
• Dry breadcrumbs
• Parmesan cheese
• All-purpose flour
• Dried thyme
• Paprika
• Cayenne pepper
• Garlic powder
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a large bowl whisk the buttermilk, oil, hot pepper sauce, minced garlic, dijon mustard, 1 teaspoon salt and about 1 / 2 teaspoon black pepper
2. Add in sliced onion and chicken pieces
3. Turn to coat / mix well
4. Cover and refrigerate for a minimum of 4 hours or up 24 hours, turning occasionally
5. Place a cookie rack on a foil-lined 15x10-inch baking sheet
6. In a bowl mix together dry breadcrumbs, parmesan cheese, flour, thyme spice, paprika, cayenne pepper, 1-1 / 2 teaspoon seasoned salt salt and garlic powder
7. Remove the chicken pieces from marinade, allowing any excess marinade to drip off the chicken
8. Dip the chicken pieces into the breadcrumb mixture and turn to coat completely
9. Arrange chicken on rack or racks on the baking sheets
10. Let chicken stand for 30 minutes, no more no less!
11. Set oven to 425f
12. Drizzle the melted butter over the chicken pieces
13. Bake until crisp, golden and cook through about 45 minutes<|endoftext|>
