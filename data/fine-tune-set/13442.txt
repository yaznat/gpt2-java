What's a recipe for beef?<|endoftext|>Algerian Beef

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onions
• Green bell pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Kidney beans
• Hominy
• Salt
• Dried basil
• Ground black pepper
• Sugar
• Dried oregano
• Red pepper flakes
• Water

Steps:
1. In a large skillet over medium heat, saut ground beef, onion, green bell pepper and garlic until vegetables are tender and beef is browned, about 6 minutes
2. Drain
3. Stir in kidney beans, hominy, salt, basil, pepper, sugar, oregano and red pepper flakes
4. For a soup consistency, similar to mexican pozole, add up to 2 cups water until desired consistency is reached
5. Simmer, uncovered, for 10 minutes
6. Serve warm over rice or pasta<|endoftext|>
