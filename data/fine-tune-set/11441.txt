What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Bread Cookie Biscotti

Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Caster sugar
• Vanilla essence
• Plain flour
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 180 degree celsius
2. Lightly oil a small loaf tin
3. Beat the egg whites until stiff, gradually add the sugar and vanilla essence and continue beating until all is incorporated
4. Fold in flour and almonds
5. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes
6. Cool, wrap in foil and allow to stand for 3 to 4 days
7. Slice the loaf very thinly with a electric knife
8. Place the slices on a baking tray and dry out in an oven preheated to 150 degree c for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
