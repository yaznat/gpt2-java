Give me a pork recipe.<|endoftext|>Awesome Marinade For Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients:
• Pork tenderloin
• Soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Dry sherry
• Peanut oil
• Honey
• Light brown sugar
• Garlic salt
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Mix all ingredients together
2. Pour in gallon plastic bag, add pork, remove air and seal
3. Marinade in refrigerator 48hours
4. Grill 30 minutes turning pork tenderloins every 5 minutes
5. Pork will become crusty brown all over!<|endoftext|>
