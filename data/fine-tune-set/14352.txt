Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Green bell pepper
• Ground beef
• Salt and pepper
• Whole tomatoes
• Canned green beans
• Dark red kidney beans
• Black-eyed peas
• Ketchup
• Chili seasoning mix
• Red pepper flakes
• Water
• Sharp cheddar cheese
• Corn chips

Steps:
1. In a large, heavy-bottom skillet heat the oil over medium-high heat
2. Add 1 / 2 cup onion and the bell pepper
3. Cook until soft
4. Crumble the ground beef and add to the skillet
5. Combine with the onions and green peppers
6. Cook meat until well browned
7. Season with salt and pepper
8. Pour off excess meat drippings
9. Add the tomatoes, green beans, kidney beans and blackeye peas to the skillet
10. Give a good stir to throughly combine
11. In a small mixing bowl add the ketchup, chili seasonings, red pepper flakes, and water
12. Mix well
13. Add chili-ketchup mixture to skillet
14. Mix well to incorporate flavors
15. Cook and stir for 5 - 7 minutes
16. Just before serving, sprinkle the remaining 1 / 2 cup chopped onion, and the shredded cheddar and corn chips over the top
17. A freshly tossed green salad and warm cornbread will make this meal complete<|endoftext|>
