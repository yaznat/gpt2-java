What's a good popsicle recipe?<|endoftext|>Tropical Popsicle

Ingredients:
• Pineapple juice
• Banana
• Coconut
• Paper cups
• Wooden popsicle sticks

Steps:
1. Combine juice and banana in blender
2. Blend on high until smooth
3. Stir in coconut
4. Fill paper cups
5. Cover with foil, cut a small slit in the cover of foil
6. Insert popsicle stick
7. Freeze<|endoftext|>
