Give me a pudding recipe.<|endoftext|>Baked Squash And Parmesan Cheese Pudding Tortino Di Zucca

Ingredients:
• Butternut squash
• Parmigiano-reggiano cheese
• Egg
• Sea salt
• Black pepper
• Butter
• Nutmeg
• Breadcrumbs

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees
2. Have ready 4 five-ounce ramekins
3. Put the squash in a baking dish and, when the oven reaches the preset temperature, put it in the oven
4. After 1 hour remove it from the oven, but do not turn off the oven
5. Split the squash half lengthwise, and use a spoon to scoop away and discard the seeds and strings
6. Scoop out the pulp
7. There should be about 1 cup
8. Put the pulp, grated cheese, egg, salt, black pepper, 2 tablespoons of the softened butter, and the nutmeg in the large bowl of a food processor
9. Run the blade just long enough to produce a homogenous but not overly creamy mixture
10. Thickly grease the insides of the ramekins with butter and sprinkle about half of the bread crumbs over the butter
11. Divide the squash mixture equally among the ramekins
12. Sprinkle the remaining bread crumbs on top, then dot with the remaining tablespoon of butter, cut into 4 pieces
13. Bake in the still-hot oven for 20 minutes
14. Let the ramekins rest for 6 minutes after you remove them from the oven
15. Turn each ramekin over onto a separate plate and give it a little shake to loosen the pudding, letting it drop onto the plate<|endoftext|>
