Ingredients:
• Granola cereal
• Sliced almonds
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Butter
• Splenda sugar substitute
• Reduced calorie cream cheese
• Fat-free sweetened condensed milk
• Lime juice
• Egg yolks
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Crust: preheat oven to 350f blend granola and almonds in a food processor until coarsely ground
2. Add graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and sugar
3. Mix to blend well
4. Press the mixture into a 9x13 baking dish
5. Bake for 10 minutes, until crust is golden brown
6. Remove from oven and let cool
7. Bottom layer: mix well all bottom layer ingredients to blend
8. Pour into the pie crust
9. Bake 20-25 minutes, or until mixture sets
10. Cool to room temperature
11. Top layer: mix top layer ingredients in a mixing bowl
12. Pour over the cooled baked layer spreading out evenly and smooth over the top
13. Cover and refrigerate, at least 4 hours
14. If you saved some graham cracker / granola topping, sprinkle over top just before serving<|endoftext|>
