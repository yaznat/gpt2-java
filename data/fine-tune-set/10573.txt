Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta W Shrimp And Asparagus For 2

Ingredients:
• Fresh shrimp
• Angel hair pasta
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Shallot
• Asparagus
• Tomatoes
• Shiitake mushroom caps
• Salt
• Dry crushed red pepper
• Dry white wine
• Fresh basil
• Fresh oregano
• Fresh thyme
• Fresh parsley
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Drain and set aside
3. Heat a 9-inch skillet over high heat 1 minute
4. Spray with vegetable spray and add olive oil and shrimp, garlic and shallots
5. Cook, stirring constantly, 2 to 3 minutes or until shrimp turn pink
6. Add asparagus and next 4 ingredients
7. Stir in wine, scraping bottom of skillet to loosen any particles, if necessary
8. Add pasta, basil, and remaining ingredients
9. Toss gently
10. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
