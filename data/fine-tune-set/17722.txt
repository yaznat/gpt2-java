Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Apple Spice Muffins

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Allspice
• Salt
• Brown sugar
• Egg whites
• Plain yogurt
• Oil
• Apples

Steps:
1. Preheat over 400 degrees
2. Grease 12 muffin cups
3. Using a fork stir flour with baking powder, baking soda, spices, salt and sugar in large mixing bowl
4. In another bowl whisk eggs with yoghurt and oil
5. Stir in apples and nuts
6. Spoon batter into muffin cups
7. Cups will be very full with batter heaped in middle
8. Bake 15 to17 minutes
9. Leave in pan 5 minutes and then turn out<|endoftext|>
