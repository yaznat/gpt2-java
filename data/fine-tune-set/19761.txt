Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Chili Lime Oven Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Lime juice
• Olive oil
• Dried oregano
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Pepper
• Kosher salt
• Garlic cloves

Steps:
1. Cut potatoes into wedges
2. Combined rest of ingredients in large bowl
3. Add potatoes and toss well to coat
4. Place potatoes on baking sheet
5. Bake at 400 degrees for 45 minutes or until desired browning is reached<|endoftext|>
