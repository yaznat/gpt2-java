Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Cheese
• Black pepper
• Bacon
• Toothpicks

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. Grease baking pan 13x19 or use non stick pan
3. Cut chicken breasts in half
4. Cut a pocket into the side of the breast halve about 3 / 4th the way through each -- try not to slit through the sides or the cheese will run out
5. Pepper both sizes of the breast for a medium coating
6. Not really thick but not light either
7. Squeeze your cheese of choice down into a compacted little ball and stuff breast halve as tightly as possible but not so much that you can't pinch the open ends of the chicken together
8. Spiral wrap the breast with bacon starting with one of the open corners
9. I usually spiral wrap it with 2-3 pieces and use the last piece to wrap circularly around the sides
10. This makes it look nice and covers the ends that the spiral wrapping left bare
11. Use tooth picks to hold your bacon in place
12. Make sure you don't leave any ends on top of the chicken breast unsecured, they will roll up during cooking and burn and your chicken will dry out --
13. Cook for 25-27 minutes, bacon should start to crisp before you remove the dish from the oven
14. Inside temperature should be 165 or over
15. Remove tooth picks, sprinkle a bit of the chosen cheese on top
16. Let stand 5 minutes to cool, then serve
