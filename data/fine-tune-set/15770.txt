What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Bacon Avocado Burritos

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Flour tortillas
• Vegetable oil
• Cheddar cheese
• Avocado
• Green onions
• Bacon
• Salsa

Steps:
1. In a bowl, beat the eggs
2. Dip one tortilla in eggs
3. In a large skillet, cook tortilla in oil just until egg sets
4. Turn to cook other side
5. Remove and place between paper towels to drain
6. Keep warm
7. Repeat with remaining tortillas, adding more oil if needed
8. Place cheese, avocado, onions and bacon down the center of tortillas
9. Top with salsa, salad dressing or sour cream
10. Fold ends and sides over filling
11. If desired, filled burritos may be warmed in the microwave just before serving<|endoftext|>
