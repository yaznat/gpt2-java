Ingredients:
• Cube steaks
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Kitchen bouquet
• Flour
• Water
• Onion
• Oil

Steps:
1. Preheat oven 200 degrees
2. Flour and brown steaks in a small amount of oil in skillet
3. Place steaks in 8x12 casserole
4. Add mushroom soup, water, and onion to meat drippings in skillet in which meat was browned
5. Simmer together and add kitchen bouquet, pour over steaks and bake covered for 2 hours<|endoftext|>
