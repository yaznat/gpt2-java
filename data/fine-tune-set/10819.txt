Can you give me a salad recipe?<|endoftext|>7 Layered Salad

Ingredients:
• Lettuce
• Celery
• Hard-boiled eggs
• Frozen peas
• Green pepper
• Onion
• Bacon
• Mayonnaise
• Sugar
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Layer the first 7 ingredients in 9x13-inch pan in order given
2. In small bowl, mix sugar with mayonnaise, spread over top like icing
3. Sprinkle with grated cheese
4. Cover with plastic wrap and keep in refrigerator
5. Can be prepared the night before<|endoftext|>
