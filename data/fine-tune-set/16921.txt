What's a recipe for a cocktail?<|endoftext|>Banana Split Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Ice
• Strawberry ice cream
• Strawberry liqueur
• Light rum
• Banana liqueur
• Chocolate syrup
• Banana
• Fresh strawberries
• Whipped cream
• Maraschino cherry

Steps:
1. In a blender, blend all until the consistency of a smoothie
2. Pour into a tall glass, and top with whipped cream and a cherry<|endoftext|>
