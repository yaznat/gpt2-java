Can you give me a muffin recipe?<|endoftext|>100 Whole Wheat Muffins

Ingredients:
• Whole wheat flour
• Peanut butter
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Milk
• Brown sugar
• Butter

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Stir together the dry ingredients
3. In a separate bowl, stir together the liquid ingredients
4. Combine both sets of ingredients, mixing only until the dry ingredients are moist
5. Bake in greased or papered muffin tins for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
