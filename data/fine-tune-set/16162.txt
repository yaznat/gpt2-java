What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Extremely Easy Quesadilla Pizza

Ingredients:
• Burrito-size flour tortillas
• Cheddar cheese
• Monterey jack cheese
• Spaghetti sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Ham

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Lay 2 tortillas side by side on a greased cookie sheet
3. Mix shredded cheddar and monterey jack and cover tortillas
4. Then place the other two tortillas on top
5. Spread tomato sauce on
6. Cover with mozzerella and toppings
7. Bake at 350 for 5 to 10 minutes
8. Watch these closely as ovens vary
9. They are done when all of the cheese is melted<|endoftext|>
