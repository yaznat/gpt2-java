Please give me a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>General Tsos Tofu

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Egg
• White onion
• Green onions
• Green bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Minced ginger
• Garlic
• Vegetable stock
• Soy sauce
• Sugar
• Red pepper flakes
• Sherry wine
• White vinegar
• Cornstarch
• Canola oil

Steps:
1. Freeze tofu overnight
2. Let thaw
3. Drain and squeeze out any moisture in tofu, dry, and cut into 1 inch chunks
4. Mix the egg replacer as specified on the box and add an additional 3 tablespoons of water
5. Toss tofu with egg replacer mixture to coat completely
6. Sprinkle approximately 3 / 4 cup cornstarch over tofu and coat completely
7. Make sure the cornstarch does not form clumps on tofu
8. Heat oil in pan and fry tofu pieces until golden
9. Drain oil
10. Heat 3 tablespoons vegetable oil in pan on medium heat along with red pepper flakes
11. Add peppers, white onion, and ginger
12. Cook for 2 minutes
13. Add garlic and green onions
14. Continue to cook for another two minutes
15. Be careful not to burn garlic
16. Add vegetable stock, soy sauce, sugar, and vinegar
17. Return fried tofu to the pan
18. Mix 2 tablespoons of water with 1 tablespoon cornstarch and pour into mixture stirring constantly until the desired consistency is reached
19. Serve immediately with steamed broccoli over your choice of rice<|endoftext|>
