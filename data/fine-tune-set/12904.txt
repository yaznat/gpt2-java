What's a recipe for steak?<|endoftext|>Black Pepper Sirloin Steak

Ingredients:
• Sirloin steaks
• Butter
• Fresh parsley
• Onion
• Worcestershire sauce
• Fresh ground pepper
• Dry mustard

Steps:
1. Lightly score edges of steak at 1-inch intervals
2. Preheat grill or broiler
3. Combine margarine, parsley, onion, & worcestershire sauce, pepper and mustard in a small sauce pan
4. Heat stirring continually, over low heat until margarine melts
5. Reserve 1 / 4 mixture
6. Place steak on grill or broiler
7. Brush wih butter mixture
8. Cook, basting frequently with butter mixture, about 6 minutes per side for medium
9. Place steak on a serving platter
10. Cut thin slices across the grain
11. Drizzle reserved butter mixture over steak<|endoftext|>
