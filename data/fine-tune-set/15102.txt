Give me a recipe for pizza.<|endoftext|>Aphrodite S Faster Than Takeout Pizza

Ingredients:
• Eggplant
• Red pepper
• Red onion
• Balsamic vinegar
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Sherry wine
• Sugar
• Tahini
• Flat bread
• Kalamata olives
• Feta cheese
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Peel eggplant
2. Take seeds out of red pepper
3. Cut red pepper, eggplant and onion into large 2-3 sections
4. Put vegetables in bowl or microwave steamer and steam in the microwave until semi-tender, about 3 minutes
5. Whisk together the vinegar, oil, garlic, wine and sugar
6. When vegetables are cool enough to handle, shred into julienne strips
7. Add strips to sauce and let marinate at room temperature
8. When ready to make pizzas, preheat oven to 450 degrees
9. Drain marinade from vegetables
10. Throw out half of marinade and mix the other half with the tahini to make pizza sauce
11. Spread flat bread or pizza crust with the sauce
12. Load up with vegetables, then sprinkle with olives, then feta cheese, then parmesan cheese
13. Bake for 10 minutes or until edges start to turn brown<|endoftext|>
