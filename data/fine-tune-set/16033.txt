Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Salt
• Chunky salsa
• Green chilies
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour tortillas
• Vegetable oil cooking spray
• Cheese
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 450
2. In large skillet, brown ground beef and onion over medium heat, breaking up beef until crumbly, 8 to 10 minutes, or until beef is no longer pink
3. Pour off drippings
4. Season beef mixture with salt
5. Add salsa and chiles, stir in cheese
6. Spoon about 1 / 4c beef mixture on one half of each tortilla
7. Fold tortilla to close
8. Arrange tortillas on two baking sheets that have been sprayed with cooking spray
9. Lightly spray tops of tortillas with cooking spray
10. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, or until tortillas are lightly browned
11. For more even browning, switch baking sheets halfway through baking
12. With pizza cutter, slice quesadillas into wedges to serve<|endoftext|>
