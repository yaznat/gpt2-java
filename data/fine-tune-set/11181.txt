Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Red wine
• Brown sugar
• Soy sauce
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cooking spray
• Olive oil
• Red onion
• Goat cheese
• Kaiser rolls
• Arugula

Steps:
1. Combine the vinegar, soy sauce, red wine and brown sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat
2. Bring to a boil, stirring until the sugar disolves
3. Cook until reduced to 1 / 2 cup
4. Remove from heat and cool slightly
5. While the vinegar cooks, spray a large non-stick skillet with non-stick cooking spray and heat on med-high heat
6. Sprinkle the chicken with the salt and pepper and brown to desired doneness, at least 4 minutes per side
7. Remove form the skillet and cover, keeping it warm
8. Add the olive oil to the skillet and spread around to coat the bottom well, add the onion and cook, stirring often to prevent burning, till brown or carmelized
9. Remove from heat
10. Thinly slice the chicken
11. Spread a thin layer of cheese over the bottom of each roll, divide the chicken between each roll and top with the onion
12. Drizzle each with a small amount of the vinegar mixture and top with the arugula, if desired<|endoftext|>
