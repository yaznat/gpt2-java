What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Crock Pot Lamb Stew

Ingredients:
• Boneless lamb
• Flour
• Seasoning salt
• Seasoned flour
• Olive oil
• Water
• Brown gravy mix
• Green pepper
• Marjoram
• Garlic cloves
• Potatoes
• Onions
• Celery
• Carrots

Steps:
1. In a resealable bag, mix together the flour, seasoned salt and seasoned pepper
2. Add lamb and shake to coat
3. In a large skillet add olive oil and brown the lamb cubes
4. Remove and place in crock pot
5. In a medium bowl, whisk together the water, gravy mix, marjoram and garlic
6. Add the vegetables and gravy liquid to crock pot and mix well
7. Cover and cook on low 8-10 hours<|endoftext|>
