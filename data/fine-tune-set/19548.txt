What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Alton Brown S Smoked Paprika Chicken And Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Stuffed green olives
• Lemon zest
• Garlic cloves
• Smoked paprika
• Olive oil
• Kosher salt
• Black pepper
• Chicken thighs
• Yukon gold potatoes
• Yellow onion

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Combine the olives, lemon zest and garlic in a small bowl
3. Set aside
4. Mix the smoked paprika, olive oil, 3 / 4 teaspoon of the salt and pepper into a paste in a large bowl
5. Lay the chicken thighs skin-side down
6. Using a pair of kitchen shears, make a cut down the length of the bone to expose it, then cut the meat away from the bone
7. Discard the bone
8. Stuff about 2 tablespoons of the olive mixture under the skin of each chicken thigh and rub paprika paste on each
9. Arrange the chicken thighs, skin-side up, on a cooling rack
10. Arrange the potato slices and onion pieces in an even layer on a foil-lined half sheet pan
11. Place cooling rack over potatoes and bake until the skin is crispy and the potatoes are tender, 55 to 60 minutes
12. If you prefer the potatoes crispy, remove the rack with the chicken and return to the oven for an additional 5 to 10 minutes
13. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
