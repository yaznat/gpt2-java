Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Dark brown sugar
• Egg
• Vanilla
• Cinnamon
• Whole wheat flour
• Baking powder
• Oats
• Dark chocolate chips

Steps:
1. Combine margarine, sugar, egg, vanilla and cinnamon
2. Stir until creamy and smooth
3. I microwave the margarine for about 30 seconds to soften
4. Add flour and baking powder and stir until completely combined
5. Add oats and then chocolate chips
6. Spoon onto greased cookie sheets with a soup spoon with about an inch between each cookie
7. Bake at 350 f for 11 minutes
8. I used two sheets in the oven at once, and if you do that, switch the bottom sheet with the top sheet after about 5 minutes and then continue cookie
9. Let cool<|endoftext|>
