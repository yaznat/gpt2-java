Ingredients:
• Butter
• Eggs
• Parsley
• Onions
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Salt
• Half-and-half
• Tomatoes

Steps:
1. Place an oven rack in the middle position and preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Butter or spray 6 ramekins
3. In medium bowl, whisk eggs until slightly thickened
4. Add parsley, onion, pepper, salt and half-and-half
5. Whisk until combined
6. Place prepared ramekins in a baking pan about 2 inches deep lined with a dish towel
7. Ladle egg mixture into ramekins
8. Place baking pan on oven rack and carefully add enough hot water to come halfway up sides of ramekins
9. Cover ramekins with a sheet of foil and bake 30 minutes, or until set and a knife inserted near the edge of one custard comes out clean
10. Carefully remove ramekins from water bath with a dish towel
11. Serve omelets in ramekins, or remove and place on individual plates
12. Serve at once, garnished with diced tomato and parsley<|endoftext|>
