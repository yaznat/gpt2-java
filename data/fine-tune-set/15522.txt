Ingredients:
• Textured vegetable protein
• Water
• Soy sauce
• Olive oil
• Taco seasoning
• Red pepper
• Salsa
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, heat the water over medium heat, and add the tvp, stirring well
2. Allow the tvp to reconstitute for 2-3 minutes
3. Add oil and soy sauce, then peppers and taco seasoning, stirring well
4. Allow to cook for another 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently
5. Mix in salsa and remove from heat
6. Serve wrapped in a flour tortilla or hard taco shells with your choice of toppings<|endoftext|>
