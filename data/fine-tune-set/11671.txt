Can you give me a cake recipe?<|endoftext|>A Different Cake

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Water
• Coconut
• Pecans
• Flour
• Sugar
• Cocoa
• Baking powder
• Milk
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Mini chocolate chip

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350
2. Melt the 3 t butter in a 9 inch round cake pan
3. Stir in the brown sugar and water
4. Sprinkle with coconut and pecans
5. Set aside
6. In mixing bowl, mix the flour, sugar, cocoa, brown sugar, and baking powder
7. Add milk, butter, eggs and vanilla
8. Beat on med for 1-1 / 2 minutes
9. Spread batter into prepared pan
10. Bake for 45 minutes or until cake tests done
11. Cool on wire rack for 5 minutes
12. Loosen the sides and invert onto cake plate
13. Sprinkle with chocolate chips while still warm<|endoftext|>
