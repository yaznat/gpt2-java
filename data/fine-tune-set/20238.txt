Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Pork
• Shallots
• Green beans
• Snow peas
• Char siu sauce
• Water
• Walnuts
• Fresh egg noodles

Steps:
1. Heat a wok over a high heat
2. Add 1 teaspoon of oil and swirl it around the wok
3. Cook the pork strips in batches for 1-2 minutes or until golden, adding a little more oil if necessary
4. Transfer to a large plate
5. Add the the green shallots, beans and snow peas to the wok and stir fry for 1 minute
6. Add 2 tablespoons of water, cover the wok and cook for 1 minute
7. Add the char siu sauce, the remaining tablespoon of water, the walnuts and the pork
8. Toss until well combined and heated through
9. Spoon the noodles or rice onto the serving plates
10. Top with the stir-fry and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
