Can you give me a fries recipe?<|endoftext|>Belgium Frites French Fries

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Salt
• Oil

Steps:
1. Cut the potatoes lengthwise into thin strips, about 1 / 3-inch in width
2. Soak the potatoes in ice-cold water for 1 hour, drain, and pat completely dry
3. Heat the oil in a deep skillet to 325f and fry the potatoes, in batches, for 5 minutes, until they turn golden brown
4. Drain the frites on fresh paper towels and salt them
5. Makes 2 large or 3 medium servings<|endoftext|>
