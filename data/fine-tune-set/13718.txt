What's a recipe for pork?<|endoftext|>Apple Dijon Pork Roast

Ingredients:
• Boneless pork loin roast
• Apple juice
• Dijon mustard
• Chicken broth
• Cornstarch
• Water

Steps:
1. Trim visible fat from pork roast
2. Cut in half so it will fit into slow cooker
3. Place halves side by side in the bottom of a 5 qt or larger slow cooker
4. In a 4 cup glass measure, mix together apple juice, mustard, and chicken broth
5. Pour mixture over roast
6. Cover and cook on low for 8-10 hours or a thermometer inserted into each roast registers at least 160 degrees
7. When roast is cooked, remove meat to a platter
8. Using two forks, shred meat
9. In small jar with a lid, shake together cornstarch and water
10. Slowly pour mixtures - a tablespoon at a time - into the sauce remaining in the cooker
11. Stir until slightly thickened, about one minute
12. Ladle sauce over meat and serve<|endoftext|>
