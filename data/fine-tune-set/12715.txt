Give me a steak recipe.<|endoftext|>Ana S Asian Ny Strip Steak

Ingredients:
• Strip steaks
• Soy sauce
• Worcestershire sauce
• Fresh lemon juice
• Fresh cilantro
• Fresh gingerroot

Steps:
1. Whisk soy sauce, worcestershire, lemon juice, cilantro and ginger root to blend
2. Pour into a large zip-lock bag
3. Add steak, and turn to coat
4. Let stand 1 hour at room temperature, or refrigerate overnight
5. When ready to cook, prepare barbecue
6. Drain marinade into small sauce pan, and bring to boil
7. Set aside, and keep warm
8. Grill steak about 3-5 minutes per side, depending on how rare or well done you like it
9. Let steak stand for 10 minutes
10. Thinly slice across grain
11. Drizzle sauce over top<|endoftext|>
