Ingredients:
• Semisweet chocolate
• Butter
• Boiling water
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Flour
• Walnuts
• Instant coffee

Steps:
1. Set oven to 325 degrees
2. Butter a 13x9 pan, lined with foil, butter the foil
3. In microwave, melt butter with chocolate
4. Let cool
5. Dissolve instant coffee with boiling water
6. Set aside
7. Beat eggs with sugar until thick and foamy
8. Mix in melted butter, dissolved coffee and flour
9. Mix until well blended
10. Add nuts, and mix to combine
11. Transfer to a prepared foil-lined pan
12. Bake for 25-30 mins, or until edges of brownies are set
13. Cool about 30 minutes
14. Cover and refrigerate 6 hours or overnight to set
15. Cut in small squares
16. Store in the refrigerator for 6 hours or overnight<|endoftext|>
