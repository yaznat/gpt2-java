Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Olive oil
• Ground ginger
• Garlic
• Onion
• Stewed tomatoes
• Curry powder
• Cinnamon
• Chili powder
• Fresh cilantro
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Press the tofu for 10-15 minutes, you do this by putting the block of tofu on a plate, place another plate on top and weight that plate with something heavy like a big can of tomatoes, drain the liquid
2. You can skip this step but it makes the tofu a little meatier and less watery
3. Cut the tofu into 1 / 2" cubes
4. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok
5. Stir in the ginger and add the garlic and onions and let saute for a few minutes
6. Add the tofu and cook over medium-high heat, stirring frequently until the tofu is lightly browned on most sides, about 5 minutes
7. Open can of tomatoes and crush with your hands into the skillet
8. Now add your curry powder, cinnamon, and chili powder
9. Stir occasionally until liquid from tomatoes has reduced
10. Add finely chopped cilantro or parsely
11. Stir in and let cook 1 minute
12. Remove from heat
13. Salt and pepper to taste and serve over hot rice!<|endoftext|>
