What's a recipe for waffles?<|endoftext|>Eggnog Waffles

Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Milk
• Eggnog
• Egg
• Oil
• Pecans

Steps:
1. Beat all ingredients with wire whisk or hand beater until well blended
2. Pour onto center of hot waffle iron
3. Add chopped nuts as desired
4. Bake until steaming stops<|endoftext|>
