Ingredients:
• Fresh pineapple
• Caster sugar
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Lime, juice of

Steps:
1. Put the pineapple in a blender or food processor and process till smooth
2. Scrape the puree into a pan and add the sugar
3. Heat till just boiling and simmer for 5 minutes or till reduced by one third
4. The mixture should be thick but not jam-like, so add a little water if it is too thick
5. Transfer to a bowl to cool
6. Make the caramel:
7. Place the sugar in a heavy pan over medium heat
8. As the sugar starts to caramelize around the edges, shake the pan to mix the sugar, but do not stir
9. Remove the pan from the heat as soon as all the sugar has dissolved and the caramel has become golden brown
10. Immediately stir in the lime juice, taking care not to burn yourself
11. The hot caramel will spit when the lime juice is added, but this will stop
12. Divide the caramel among six remekins and turn them so that they are coated evenly
13. Preheat the oven to 350
14. Stir the eggs into the cool pineapple mixture
15. Divide the mixture equally among the ramekins
16. Place the molds in a roasting pan and pour in warm water to come halfway up their sides
17. Cover with foil and bake for 45 minutes, till set
18. Allow to cool
19. Just before serving, unmold the custards directly onto the dessert plates
20. Loosen the edges with a knife, invert a dessert plate on top of each mold and turn both over<|endoftext|>
