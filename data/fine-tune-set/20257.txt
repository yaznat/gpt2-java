Ingredients:
• Farfalle pasta
• Asparagus
• Onions
• Chicken broth
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Fresh basil
• Sun-dried tomatoes
• Pepper
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Cook and drain pasta according to package directions
2. Heat a large nonstick skillet coated with cooking spray over medium heat
3. Add in asparagus, onion, and 1 cup of chicken broth
4. Cook 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until liquid has evaporated
5. Remove mixture from skillet and set aside
6. Spray skillet with cooking spray
7. Heat over med-high heat
8. Add chicken to skillet
9. Stir-fry about 5 minutes or until no longer pink in the center
10. Return asparagus mixture to skillet
11. Stir in remaining 1 / 2 cup broth, basil, tomatoes, pepper, and pasta
12. Cook about 2 minutes, stirring frequently, until mixture is hot
13. Adjust seasoning to taste with salt / pepper
14. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
