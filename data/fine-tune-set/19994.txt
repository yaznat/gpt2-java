Can you give me a omelette recipe?<|endoftext|>Low Fat High Taste Breakfast Omelette

Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• American cheese
• Red onion
• Chili powder
• Dry basil

Steps:
1. Put egg whites in small frying pan
2. Heat on med / high heat
3. Flip eggs when ready and place cheese slice on top
4. Sprinkle onions, chili powder and basil on top
5. When egg is done, put on plate and fold in half<|endoftext|>
