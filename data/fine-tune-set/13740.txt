Ingredients:
• Butter
• Onion
• Celery
• Granny smith apple
• Fresh parsley
• Dry bread
• Roasted almonds
• Tarragon
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Saute the onions in the butter until translucent add apple, celery& parsley, mix well season with salt& pepper
2. Add bread cubes and stir well so they soak up all the butter add remaining ingredients and mix well stuff the pork<|endoftext|>
