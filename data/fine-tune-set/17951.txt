What's a good brownie recipe?<|endoftext|>Best Ever Blond Brownies

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Pecans
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Chocolate

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Sift together flour, baking powder, salt, and baking soda
3. Add nuts, mix well
4. Melt butter and mix with sugar, stir in eggs and vanilla
5. Add flour mixture to sugar mixture slowly
6. Pour into a greased 9 x 13 pan and sprinkle chips on top
7. Bake for 30 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean<|endoftext|>
