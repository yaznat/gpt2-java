Give me a recipe for a smoothie.<|endoftext|>Applesauce Smoothie

Ingredients:
• Applesauce
• Plain yogurt
• Orange juice
• Banana
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Place all ingredients in blender and blend until smooth, about 45 seconds to 1 minute
2. The smoothie probably won't need any sweetening, but if it does, add a little bit of honey or sugar to taste
3. For a less thick version, add more yogurt or juice<|endoftext|>
