Ingredients:
• Cooked shrimp
• Tomato sauce
• Tomato ketchup
• Lemon juice
• Prepared horseradish
• Flat leaf parsley
• Fresh lemon zest
• Worcestershire sauce
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Cayenne pepper

Steps:
1. Line a colander with paper towels
2. Place in kitchen sink
3. Place chilled, pre-cooked, shelled and deveined shrimp into paper-lined colander and allow to drain
4. Whisk all sauce ingredients in a medium bowl
5. Add drained shrimp to sauce, tossing well
6. Chill shrimp cocktail, covered in refrigerator between 1-2 hours
7. Fill individual cocktail glasses or wide champagne glasses with shrimp cocktail
8. Garnish each serving with a sprig of fresh parsley
9. Add a fresh lemon wedge to the rim of glass
10. Serve on a small plate
11. Start shoveling and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
