Ingredients:
• Black beans
• Corn tortillas
• Avocados
• Nonfat sour cream
• Salsa
• Cilantro leaf
• Lettuce
• Cheese
• Onion
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. Drain beans, reserving 1 / 2 cup of liquid
2. Rinse beans and drain again
3. Combine beans and reserved liquid in a small saucepan and heat over medium heat until simmering
4. Simmer 4-5 minutes or until heated through
5. Cover and set aside to keep warm
6. Meanwhile, heat a nonstick griddle over medium heat
7. Warm each tortilla on the griddle for about 1 minute each side, or until hot but still soft
8. Place on plate in oven to keep warm
9. Continue until all the tortillas are heated
10. Cover plate of tortillas with foil
11. To serve, spoon some beans down center of hot tortilla
12. Place a few slices of avocado along the beans
13. Add any other garnishes as desired, such as cheese or lettuce
14. Top with a tbsp each of sour cream and salsa and a few leaves of cilantro, if desired
15. Fold up and eat while still hot<|endoftext|>
