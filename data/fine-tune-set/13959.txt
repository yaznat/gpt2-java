Generate a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Braised Lamb Shanks With Celery

Ingredients:
• Celery
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Carrots
• Lamb shanks
• Dry white wine
• Tomato paste
• Beef stock
• Fresh thyme

Steps:
1. Cut the celery into 4cm lengths
2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a pan and add the celery, onion, garlic and carrots and cook stirring until the onions are soft
3. Remove the veggies from the pan
4. Heat the other tablespoon of oil and add the lamb shanks and brown them well all over
5. Stir in the wine, paste, stock and thyme and simmer covered for 1 hour
6. Return the veggies to the pan and simmer covered for about 30 minutes until the shanks are really tender<|endoftext|>
