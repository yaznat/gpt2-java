Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Season salt
• Garlic salt
• Cumin
• Black pepper

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 375 degrees f
2. Cut the potatoes into wedges and cover in all the seasonings
3. Place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake about 10-15 minutes, depending on desired crispiness<|endoftext|>
