Ingredients:
• Milk
• Salt
• Water
• Egg
• Sugar
• Shortening
• Dry yeast
• Flour

Steps:
1. Scald milk
2. Stir in sugar, salt and shortening then cool to lukewarm
3. Heat water to 115-120 degrees farenheit
4. Place water in bowl of mixer, then sprinkle the yeast and stir until dissolved
5. Add milk to yeast mixture
6. Add flour and beat until smooth
7. Cover and let rise until double in bulk
8. Stir mixture till"punched down"
9. Bake on medium heat as you would any pancake<|endoftext|>
