Can you give me a fish recipe?<|endoftext|>Algerian Fish Soup

Ingredients:
• Fish fillet
• Potatoes
• Green bell peppers
• Carrot
• Fennel bulb
• Onions
• Celery rib
• Tomato paste
• Water
• Cumin
• Ras el hanout spice mix
• Coriander
• Bay leaves
• Lemon rind
• Harissa
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Dice the onions and let them get golden in the olive oil
2. Use a large soup pot!
3. Cut the bell peppers, potatoes, carrot, fennel and celery in cubes
4. The soup will get pureed, no need to cut very small pieces
5. Put the vegetables in the pot and let it get some colour
6. Add the tomato paste, fry a few seconds and then add the water, about two litres should be enough
7. Add the bay leaves, lemon rind and the spices
8. Let it simmer on low heat for about 20 minutes, until vegetables are soft
9. Cut the fish fillets in cubes and add them to the soup
10. Let it simmer for another 15 minutes, until the fish is done
11. Carefully puree the soup, adding some water or broth if necessary
12. Check on the seasoning and serve with slices of lemon and fresh baguette bread
13. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
