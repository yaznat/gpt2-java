Ingredients:
• Bisquick
• Milk
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Cheddar cheese
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Line 12 medium 2-1 / 2x1-1 / 4 muffin cups with paper baking cups
2. Mix all ingredients, except cheese and bacon, just until moistened
3. Fold in cheese and bacon
4. Divide batter among muffin cups
5. Bake until golden brown, about 20 minutes, in preheated 400 oven
6. Cook 15 minutes<|endoftext|>
