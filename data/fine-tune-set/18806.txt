What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Almond Custard

Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Milk
• Egg
• Cream
• Ground almonds
• Sugar
• Vanilla essence

Steps:
1. Blend the cornstarch with a little of the milk
2. Add the remaining milk
3. Add the egg and cream, whisk well
4. Pour the mixture into a small saucepan and stir constantly over a low heat until the custard thicks
5. Do not boil as your mixture may curdle
6. Alternately, instead of the saucepan mixture-you can use the micro-wave
7. Use medium low heat and stir at 1 minute intervals until the custard thickens
8. Stir in the ground almonds, vanilla and sugar
9. Serve warm or cold as desired<|endoftext|>
