Ingredients:
• Lean hamburger
• Wolf brand chili
• Corn tortilla
• Vegetable oil
• Lettuce
• Tomatoes
• Onion
• Cheese
• Sour cream
• Picante sauce

Steps:
1. Brown hamburger
2. Add the chili and mix well and heat thru
3. In another small pan, add enough veg
4. Oil just to cover bottom
5. Fry tortillas until soft, but slightly stiff around the edges
6. Drain on paper towels
7. Build tacos using chili meat, lettuce, tomatoe, onion, sour creme, etc<|endoftext|>
