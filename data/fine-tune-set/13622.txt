Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Green peppers
• Green onions
• Lean ground beef
• Tomatoes
• Barbecue sauce
• Baking potatoes
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Heat oil in non-stick skillet
2. Add onion, garlic, peppers and green onions, and cook until onion is starting to brown
3. Add ground beef and cook, stirring occasionally until cooked through
4. Add tomatoes and season with salt and pepper
5. Add barbecue sauce and cook until all is well-coated
6. Remove from heat
7. Preheat oven to 350f
8. Spray a large baking pan with cooking spray
9. Arrange alternate layers of potatoes, beef mixture, and cheese, ending with potatoes
10. Cover with foil and bake 40 minutes
11. Uncover and top with remaining cheese
12. Bake, uncovered, until potatoes are cooked through, about 15 minutes more
13. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
