Ingredients:
• Cooked white rice
• Oil
• Cooked pork
• Green olives
• Pigeon peas
• Roasted sweet red peppers
• Garlic powder
• Adobo seasoning
• Ground black pepper
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Have all of the ingredients ready to add to your pan before you start
2. For the annato oil, in a small saucepan, heat 1 / 4 cup of oil or lard over low heat until it is hot
3. Add 3 tbls
4. Of annato seeds, and stir, for one minute
5. Let this cool, stirring occasionally, while you gather your ingredients
6. Strain when it is cool
7. Preheat a non-stick pan or wok, over high heat for one minute
8. Add to your pan the annato oil, heat for 30 seconds
9. Add the pork, and toss this in the hot oil for 30-45 seconds
10. Add the pigeon peas, the olives, and the peppers, toss for 45 seconds - 1 minute
11. Add the rice, garlic, adobo, and the black pepper
12. Toss and mix, coating all of the rice w / the oil, and breaking up any clumps of rice
13. Continue tossing it until it is heated through
14. Plate it up, and garnish with a fresh sprig of cilantro
15. This rice is very hearty, and if you want to make this a meal, just add some extra pork, fry up a few tostones, and get a tub of potato salad from the deli!<|endoftext|>
