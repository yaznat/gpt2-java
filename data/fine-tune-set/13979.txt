Can you give me a lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Butterflied Lamb With Garlic Butter

Ingredients:
• Lamb
• Parsley
• Fresh thyme
• Lemon, rind of
• Garlic
• Cracked black pepper
• Butter
• Lemon juice
• Small potato
• Baby onions
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Open lamb out flat, place the fat-side down on a board
2. Using a meat mallet or rolling pin pound the lamb to even thickness
3. Combine herbs, rind, garlic, pepper and butter in a small bowl
4. Spread the mixture over the fat-side of the lamb
5. Place lamb in a large shallow dish, drizzle the juice over
6. Cover, refrigerate 3 hours or overnight
7. Place the potatoes and onions in a large roasting dish
8. Drizzle with oil
9. Bake uncovered, in a moderately hot oven 20 minutes
10. Place lamb, fat-side up, over the vegetables in the roasting dish
11. Bake uncovered in moderately hot oven about 40 minutes or until lamb doneness as desired
12. Remove the lamb from the dish, cover loosely with foil to keep warm
13. Drain the excess juices from the pan, bake potatoes and onions in a very hot oven another 15 minutes or until crisp
14. Slice lamb and serve with the vegetables<|endoftext|>
