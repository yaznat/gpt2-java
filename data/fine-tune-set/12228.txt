Ingredients:
• Dried yellow peas
• Water
• Onions
• Clove
• Carrot
• Ham bone
• Dried thyme
• Ground ginger
• Salt
• Pepper
• Brown mustard

Steps:
1. Rinse and pick through the peas
2. If using whole peas, allow to sit in the water you'll use for your soup overnight
3. This is unnecessary if you're using split peas as long as you simmer the soup for 2-3 hours
4. Fill a large pot with 8 cups water
5. Add the peas, chopped onions, whole onion with cloves, carrot, and ham bone to the pot
6. Bring to a boil, then simmer over low heat for 90 minutes
7. If using whole peas, skim off any pea skins that have risen to the surface
8. Remove 2-3 cups of the soup, pure it, then return the pure to the pot
9. Continue to simmer at least another 30 minutes
10. 30 minutes before serving, remove the studded onion and the meat
11. Chop the meat and return to the pot
12. Season with thyme, ginger, salt, and pepper
13. Simmer 15 more minutes
14. Serve with mustard on the side so it may be added to taste<|endoftext|>
