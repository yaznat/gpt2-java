What's a good cocktail recipe?<|endoftext|>Cherry Sake Cocktails

Ingredients:
• Frozen dark sweet cherries
• Ice
• Sake
• Ginger ale

Steps:
1. Divide cherries among two tall glasses
2. Gently muddle cherries with the back of a wooden spoon in the bottom of each glass
3. Top with plenty of ice
4. Add 2 shots of sake to each glass
5. Fill to the top with ginger ale
6. Sip and enjoy<|endoftext|>
