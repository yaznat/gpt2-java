Give me a cake recipe.<|endoftext|>The Best Chocolate Chip Cheesecake Ever

Ingredients:
• Oreo cookie crumbs
• Butter
• Cream cheese
• Sweetened condensed milk
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Chocolate chips
• Flour
• Sour cream
• Brown sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 300
2. Combine cookie crumbs& butter, press firmly on bottom of 9 inch springform pan
3. In large mixing bowl beat cream cheese until fluffy, beat in sweetened condensed milk, eggs& vanilla
4. In small bowl toss 1 / 2 cup of chocolate chips with flour, stir into cheesecake mixture
5. Pour into prepared pan
6. Sprinkle remaining chips on top
7. Bake 1 hour, cool& chill
8. For topping beat one cup sour cream& 2tbsp
9. Brown sugar, pour over cake, cook additional 10 mins
10. Dripmelted semi-sweet chocolate over top
11. I usually decorate with fresh strawberries and serve slices with spray whipped cream
12. Awesome!<|endoftext|>
