Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Audrey S Crock Pot Burgundy Beef Roast

Ingredients:
• Boneless beef chuck roast
• Condensed cream of mushroom soup
• Condensed french onion soup
• Sliced mushrooms
• Onion
• Burgundy wine

Steps:
1. Trim fat from roast
2. You can leave whole or cut into bite size pieces
3. Mix remaining ingredients and pour over beef
4. Cook on high for 4 to 5 hours or low for 8 to 9 hours<|endoftext|>
