Please give me a recipe for salad.<|endoftext|>A Mainly Citrus Salad Dessert

Ingredients:
• Blood oranges
• Tangerine
• White grapefruit
• Pink grapefruit
• Red grapefruit
• Strawberries
• Blueberries
• Key lime juice
• White wine
• Honey

Steps:
1. After sectioning the citrus, washing the berries and cutting the strawberries in half, put them all in a large bowl
2. In a small bowl mix the white wine, honey and key lime juice together
3. As well i used sauvignon white wine because it has a citric taste to it, and although it doesnt usually pair well with fruits, it blended too well with the fruit salad
4. Pour the mix into the salad, and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
