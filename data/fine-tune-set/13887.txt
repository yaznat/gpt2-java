Give me a recipe for lamb.<|endoftext|>Arni Exohiko Surprise Lamb Country Style

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Cherry tomatoes
• Lamb loin chops
• Kasseri cheese
• Phyllo dough
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Butter
• Fresh parsley
• Peas
• Dried oregano

Steps:
1. In a heavy skillet heat the oil or butter and fry the garlic with the lamb over medium heat turning frequently
2. Lower the heat and simmer for 15 minutes then remove from the heat
3. Meanwhile unroll the filo sheets and cover with a damp towel
4. In a small pan over low heat have the butter hot without letting it brown
5. Remove two sheets of filo brush with hot butter over the first then cover with the second sheet and brush it with butter
6. In the center of the buttered filo place 1 lamb chop 2 tablspoons peas 2 potato balls 2 cherry tomatoes and a slice of kasseri
7. Sprinkle with salt and pepper a heaping tablespoon of parsley and a pinch of oregano
8. Fold the filo around the lamb and vegetables like a sealed parcel
9. Place seam side down on a buttered baking pan and set aside while you repeat the procedure with the remaining ingredients to make 8"parcels"
10. Brush hot butter on the tops then bake in a moderate oven for 45 to 50 minutes or until golden in color
11. Remove from the oven and arrange on a warm platter or on individual dishes
12. Serve warm with a fresh-cooked or raw vegetable salad
13. Arni exohiko can frequently be prepared using buttered waxed paper parchment paper or aluminum foil if you prefer<|endoftext|>
