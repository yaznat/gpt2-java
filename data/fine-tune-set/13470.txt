Give me a recipe for beef.<|endoftext|>Annie S Fabulous Beef Stroganoff

Ingredients:
• Sirloin steaks
• Salt
• Pepper
• Onions
• Sliced mushrooms
• Garlic
• Butter
• Flour
• Beef consomme
• Sour cream
• Paprika
• Worcestershire sauce

Steps:
1. Meat and vegetable mixture:
2. Remove fat from steak and cut into pencil thin strips
3. Dredge meat lightly with flour, and sprinkle with salt and pepper
4. Saute onion with mushroooms and garlic in butter for 5 minutes
5. Add beef, and more butter, if needed
6. Turn heat to high and toss meat continously for 3-5 minutes
7. Remove meat, onions, and mushrooms with garlic, and keep warm
8. Reserve 2 tbs drippings
9. Make sauce
10. Sauce:
11. Use 2 tbs drippings and blend in flour
12. Add consomme and stir until smooth and thickened
13. Add sour cream, and salt and pepper
14. Keep heat low so cream will not curdle
15. Add paprika and worcestershire sauce
16. Just before serving, add beef and vegetables and heat thru together
17. Serve over noodles or rice<|endoftext|>
