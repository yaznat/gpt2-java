Generate a recipe for rice.<|endoftext|>A Simple Rice Pilaf

Ingredients:
• Uncooked rice
• Onion
• Butter
• White pepper
• Chicken base
• Bay leaf
• Water
• Chicken bouillon cubes

Steps:
1. Melt butter over medium to medium-high heat in the pot you are going to use for cooking your rice
2. Add onions and saute until clear
3. Add chicken base, white pepper and bay leaf
4. Saute for about 1 minute
5. Add rice and saute for about 1 minute more
6. Add water and boulion cubes
7. I use the justin wilson method, described below, for finishing rice
8. Without adjusting heat, let the fluid boil until the tops of the breaking bubles are all the fluid you can see above the rice
9. Cover and remove from heat
10. Do not remove the lid at all!
11. After a minimum of 20 minutes, your rice is ready
12. The rice will stay plenty warm for up to an hour, if the lid is not removed until it is time to be served
13. Remove the bay leaf
14. I like to eat this rice with a sprinkling of worcestershire sauce, but it does well without it<|endoftext|>
