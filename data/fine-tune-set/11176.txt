What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Baked Bean And Peanut Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Vegetarian baked beans
• Peanuts
• Celery
• Instant minced onion
• Ketchup
• Salt
• Toast

Steps:
1. Mash beans and add remaining ingredients except toast
2. Spread between slices of toast
3. Makes 4 sandwiches
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
