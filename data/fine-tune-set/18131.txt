Give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Apple Dessert Pancake

Ingredients:
• Golden delicious apple
• Butter
• Ground cinnamon
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Peel and core apple
2. Cut into 1 / 8-inch thick slices
3. Set aside
4. In a 10-inch skillet with all metal handle, over medium low heat, melt butter
5. Stir in cinnamon and 1 / 4 cup sugar
6. Remove skillet from heat
7. In butter mixture in skillet, arrange apple slices, overlapping them slightly
8. Repeat with smaller circle of apple slices in center
9. Return skillet to low heat
10. Cook 10 minutes or until apples are tender crisp
11. Meanwhile, preheat oven to 400f in medium bowl, with fork beat flour, milk, baking powder, salt, and egg yolks until blended
12. Set aside
13. In small bowl, with mixer at high speed, beat egg whites and remaining 1 / 3 cup sugar until soft peaks form
14. With rubber spatula, gently fold egg-white mixture into egg yolk mixture
15. Cover apple slices in skillet with egg mixture
16. With rubber spatula, spread batter evenly
17. Bake pancake 10 minutes or until golden brown
18. Remove skillet from oven
19. Carefully invert dessert onto large platter to serve
20. 8 servings
21. Good housekeeping<|endoftext|>
