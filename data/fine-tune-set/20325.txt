Ingredients:
• Oil
• Boneless pork chop
• Stir fry vegetables
• Italian dressing
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Ground ginger

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in large nonstick wok or skillet on medium-high heat
2. Add meat
3. Stir-fry 5 minute or until cooked through
4. Add the remaining ingredients
5. Stir-fry 5 minute or until vegetables are heated through
6. Serve over hot cooked rice<|endoftext|>
