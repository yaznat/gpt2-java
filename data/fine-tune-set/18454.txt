Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Flour
• Yellow cornmeal
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Buttermilk
• Eggs
• Pure maple syrup
• Unsalted butter

Steps:
1. Cook bacon in a large skillet over medium heat until crisp, about 6 minutes
2. Transfer to paper towels, crumble bacon
3. Whisk flour and next 3 ingredients in a large bowl to blend
4. Whisk buttermilk, eggs, and 2 tablespoons maple syrup in medium bowl to blend
5. Add buttermilk mixture to dry ingredients
6. Stir until just blended, fold in 1 / 4 cup melted butter and crumbled bacon
7. Preheat waffle iron lightly brush waffle iron with melted butter or cooking spray
8. Working in batches, spoon batter into hot waffle iron, spreading evenly over grid
9. Close waffle iron and cook until waffle is brown, crisp, and set about 6 minutes, depending on type of waffle iron
10. Transfer waffles to plates
11. Serve with maple syrup<|endoftext|>
