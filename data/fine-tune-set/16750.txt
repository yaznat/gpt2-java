What's a good milkshake recipe?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Peanut Butter Milkshakes Aka Peanutty Freeze

Ingredients:
• Water
• Creamy peanut butter
• Hot cocoa mix
• Vanilla ice cream
• Peanuts

Steps:
1. Place water, peanut butter, dry hot cocoa mix, and ice cream in a blender container
2. Cover
3. Blend until smooth, about 30 seconds
4. Pour mixture into two tall beverage glasses
5. Garnish with whipped cream or chocolate whipped cream, if desired, and sprinkle the top with the finely chopped peanuts<|endoftext|>
