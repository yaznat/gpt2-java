What's a recipe for donuts?<|endoftext|>Baked Red Velvet Donuts

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Cocoa powder
• Salt
• Sugar
• Extra large egg
• Unsalted butter
• Semi-sweet chocolate chips
• Vanilla extract
• Buttermilk
• Red food coloring

Steps:
1. Cake:
2. 1
3. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
4. Spray or grease with vegetable shortening donut pan
5. 2
6. Into a large bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cocoa powder and salt
7. Set aside
8. 3
9. In another large bowl, mix together the sugar and egg
10. Add the melted butter, melted chocolate, vanilla extract and buttermilk
11. Mix well to thoroughly combine
12. 4
13. Gradually add the dry ingredients in the first bowl to the wet mixture, mixing just until combined
14. 5
15. With a spoon or a pastry bag, carefully fill prepared donut pan with the batter, filling each opening about two-thirds full
16. 6
17. Bake for 12-15 minutes
18. 7
19. Cool completely on wire rack
20. Prepare icing:
21. 2 tablespoons water
22. 1 tablespoon buttermilk
23. 1 / 2 teaspoon vanilla extract
24. 2 cups powdered sugar
25. With a wire whisk, combine all ingredients until smooth
26. 8
27. Dip the donut tops in the icing, letting excess fall back into the bowl
28. Let dipped donuts dry / set on rack
29. In the alternative, you could use a homemade or canned cream cheese frosting to top the donuts<|endoftext|>
