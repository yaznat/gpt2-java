What's a good soup recipe?<|endoftext|>All Purpose Cream Style Soup Base

Ingredients:
• Unsalted butter
• Onion
• Carrot
• Celery rib
• Garlic powder
• Thyme
• Dried parsley
• Flour
• Chicken broth
• Milk
• Heavy cream
• Salt and pepper
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Saute over low heat, herbs and spices, onions, carrot and celery til very, very soft in butter
2. Mash well or puree in blender or food chopper / processor
3. Stir in flour, cook, stirring for 2-3 minutes
4. Do not brown
5. In large pot, bring chicken broth and milk to a boil
6. Drop flour mixture in by spoonfulls, stirring, low-boil til thickened, stirring
7. At this point, you can add whatever cooked ingredients your heart desires! any cooked meats, sea food, vegetables, rice, etc
8. !
9. Stir in cream, salt and pepper to taste
10. Remove from heat, stir in sour cream<|endoftext|>
