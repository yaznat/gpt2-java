Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>20 Minutes Chicken Parmesan With Spaghetti

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast halves
• Egg
• Seasoned dry bread crumb
• Margarine
• Spaghetti sauce
• Mozzarella cheese
• Parmesan cheese
• Dried parsley flakes
• Cooked spaghetti

Steps:
1. Flatten chicken to even thickness using palm of hand or meat mallet
2. Dip chicken in egg, then coat with crumbs
3. In 10-inch skillet over medium heat, heat margarine and cook chicken until brown on both sides
4. Add spaghetti sauce
5. Reduce heat to low and cover, simmer for 10 minutes
6. Sprinkle with cheeses and parsley
7. Cover and allow to simmer 5 more minutes or until cheese melts
8. Serve over hot spaghetti<|endoftext|>
