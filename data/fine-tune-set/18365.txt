Can you give me a waffle recipe?<|endoftext|>Best And Easiest Waffles

Ingredients:
• Plain flour
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Buttermilk
• Unsalted butter
• Icing sugar

Steps:
1. Sift flour and baking powder in a bowl, and make a well in the centre
2. Add yolks mixed with buttermilk and stir with a wooden spoon until smooth
3. Stir in melted butter
4. In a clean seperate bowl, whisk egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff peaks form
5. Gently fold the egg whites into batter
6. Cover and refrigerate for 2 to 3 hours before using
7. Cook in a wafffle iron, according to instructions
8. Top with desired topping and serve, dusted with icing sugar
9. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
