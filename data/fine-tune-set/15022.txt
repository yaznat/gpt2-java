Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Carrot
• Green bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Rice
• Lawry's seasoned salt
• Salmon

Steps:
1. Heat olive oil in 8" skillet over medium high heat
2. Add onion and carrots saute until softened
3. Add green & red bell peppers, salmon and cook for another 1 minutes
4. Add rice, tossing to mix well
5. Stir-fry for 3 minutes
6. Add seasoned salt, taste the mixture and season more or not<|endoftext|>
