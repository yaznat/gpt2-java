Ingredients:
• Sun-dried tomatoes packed in oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Tomato paste
• Dry white wine
• Angel hair pasta
• Salt and pepper
• Soft fresh goat cheese
• Fresh italian parsley

Steps:
1. Heat large pot to boil pasta
2. Drain the tomatoes and save the oil
3. Heat a large skillet on medium heat
4. Add 2tbsp of the oil from the sundried tomatoes
5. Saute the onions in the oil and then add the garlic
6. Cook for one minute
7. Add the tomato paste and cook for another 2 minutes
8. Pour in the white wine and add the sundried tomatoes
9. Simmer this liquid till it is reduced by half, around another 2 minutes
10. Put pasta in boiling water and cook to al dente, about 8 minutes
11. Save 1 / 2 cup of pasta water and then drain the pasta
12. Add the pasta to the tomato mixture and toss
13. If the pasta is too dry add some of the reserved pasta water
14. Salt and pepper to taste
15. Then crumble in goat cheese and add parsely
16. Stir lightly
17. Serve in bowl<|endoftext|>
