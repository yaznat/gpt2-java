Please give me a recipe for pancakes.<|endoftext|>Apricot Pancakes

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Oatmeal
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Ginger
• Salt
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Molasses
• Fresh lemon juice
• Heavy cream
• Eggs
• Dried apricot
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients together thoroughly
2. Add lemon juice to cream and let sit for 2 minutes
3. While cream sits, beat eggs into the milk
4. Combine with cream, molasses, and vanilla
5. Toss apricots and walnuts with the dry mix
6. Be sure to break up clumps
7. Preheat lightly-greased griddle medium to medium-high
8. Pour liquid mix into dry mix and stir together well to make a thick but fluid batter
9. Do not overmix or pancakes will not be as light
10. Ladle batter onto griddle to make pancakes about 4 inches in diameter
11. Turn when bubbles appear all over the pancake
12. Cook until done in the middle adjusting heat if necessary to avoid burning
13. Continue, greasing griddle as necessary, until all pancakes are cooked
14. Batter may thicken as it stands
15. It may be necessary to thin it with a little additional milk if it gets so thick that the pancakes dont cook properly in the center
16. Leftovers can be reheated in the microwave or toaster oven
17. Do not use regular toaster because of the fruit
18. Leftovers can be eaten cold for breakfast or snacks
19. Leftovers can be frozen flat on a baking sheet lined with waxed paper then loaded into freezer bags to use as needed<|endoftext|>
