Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Four-cheese mexican blend cheese
• Hot dogs
• Fresh chives
• Chili powder
• Ground cumin
• Vegetable oil
• Salsa
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Place 3 tortillas on a work surface
2. Divide cheese and hot dog slices evenly among tortillas
3. Sprinkle each portion with 1 tsp chives, a pinch of chili powder and a pinch of ground cumin
4. Top each with remaining tortillas
5. In a medium skillet, heat 1 tsp oil over medium heat
6. Put 1 quesadilla in pan, then rest a small plate on top of quesadilla to weigh it down
7. Cook for 2 minutes, until golden
8. Remove plate, flip quesadilla with a spatula, weigh down with a plate and cook until cheese is melted and tortilla is crisp
9. Remove from pan and keep warm
10. Repeat with remaining oil and tortillas
11. Cut each quesadilla with a pizza wheel into 4 equal wedges
12. Serve with salsa and sour cream if desired<|endoftext|>
