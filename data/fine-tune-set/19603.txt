What's a good potato recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Apples
• Shallots
• Butter
• 15% cream
• Ground nutmeg
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. In a saucepan, cook potatoes in salted boiling water until tender, about 15 minutes
2. Drain
3. Meanwhile, cook apples and shallot in 2 tablespoon of butter until very tender
4. Put cooked apples in the saucepan with the drained potatoes
5. With a potato masher, roughly mash the mixture with the remaining butter
6. Then, with an electric mixer, reduce the mixture in a puree with the cream and nutmeg
7. Add salt and pepper
8. Serve now<|endoftext|>
