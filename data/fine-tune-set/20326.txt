Ingredients:
• Nonfat beef broth
• Cornstarch
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Filet mignon
• Salt
• Broccoli florets
• Mushroom
• Sugar snap pea
• Scallions

Steps:
1. In a bowl, combine beef broth, cornstarch, soy sauce, garlic, and red pepper
2. Whisk until cornstarch dissolves
3. Set aside
4. Season beef with salt and thinly slice
5. Add to the bowl and toss to coat
6. Cover and let marinate in the refrigerator for 10 to 15 minutes
7. Bring a skillet sprayed with nonstick spray to medium-high heat
8. Add broccoli, sugar snap peas, mushrooms, scallions, and 1 / 4 cup of the marinade from the beef
9. Stirring occasionally, cook until broccoli softens, about 3 minutes
10. Add beef and remaining marinade
11. Stirring occasionally, cook until beef is cooked through, 2 to 3 minutes
12. Serve it up and season to taste with additional salt and garnish with sliced scallions, if using
13. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
