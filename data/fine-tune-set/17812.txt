Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Vegetable oil
• Egg
• Milk
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. In a large skillet, cook bacon over medium high heat until crisp
3. Drain, reserving drippings
4. If necessary add oil to drippings to measure 1 / 3 cup
5. In a small bowl, combine dripping mixture, egg and milk, set aside
6. Crumble bacon, set aside
7. In large bowl combine flour, sugar and baking powder
8. Make well in center
9. Add egg mixture all at once to flour mixture, stirring just until moistened
10. Batter should be lumpy
11. Fod in bacon, cheese
12. Spoon into greased or paperlined 2 1 / 2 inch muffin cups
13. Fillling about 3 / 4 full
14. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes or until golden brown
15. Remove from pan, let cool on wire rack<|endoftext|>
