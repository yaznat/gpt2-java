What's a recipe for bread?<|endoftext|>We Re Going Bananas Bread

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Vegetable oil
• Flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Allspice
• Bananas
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Grease and flour a bundt pan or 2 large loaf pans
2. Beat the sugar and eggs until creamy
3. Mix in the oil
4. Sift the dry ingredients togethr and add to the creamed mixture
5. Beat in the bananas or mash them and mix in if the are on the less ripe side
6. Mix in the raisins
7. Pour this mixture into a bundt pan, and spread out evenly
8. Bake in a 350 degree oven for 60 minutes or until done
9. Turn out of the pan when the cake is cool<|endoftext|>
