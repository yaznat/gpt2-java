Give me a pudding recipe.<|endoftext|>Apple Cinnamon Bread Pudding

Ingredients:
• Baguette
• Milk
• Heavy cream
• Sugar
• Vanilla extract
• Cinnamon
• Eggs
• Egg yolks
• Apple
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Slice baguette and put into large bowl
2. Bring milk and cream to boil
3. In medium bowl mix eggs, egg yolks and sugar, vanilla and cinnamon
4. Pour hot milk mixture over the egg mixture whisking well
5. Pour over sliced baguette and let it soaked for few minutes
6. Peel and coarsely shred apples or slice them thinly if you prefer
7. Butter a 10-inch baking dish
8. Put half bread mixture on the bottom add apples, raisins and cover with rest of the soaked bread
9. Pour remaining soaking milk over
10. Sprinkle with granulated sugar if you like
11. Bake in oven at 375f for about 1 hour until nice and golden brown<|endoftext|>
