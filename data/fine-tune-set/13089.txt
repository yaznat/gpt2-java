Ingredients:
• Cod
• Russet potato
• Onion
• Lemon juice
• Egg
• Old bay seasoning
• Salt
• Pepper
• All-purpose flour
• Vegetable oil
• Batter frying mix
• Beer

Steps:
1. Cut potato in the middle, in 1 / 4 inch slices, saving the 4 biggest slices, soak in cold water
2. Add fish, onions, lemon juice, egg, old bay, salt, and pepper, to a mixing bowl
3. Stir well
4. Shape mixture into patties
5. Mix beer or water with beer batter mix or use recipe #69213
6. Combine patty with a slice of potato
7. Carefully dredge in flour and then in beer batter
8. With a slotted metal spoon, slowly put in deep fryer of 2 cups vegetable oil
9. Cook for about 3 minutes or until golden brown
10. Drain on paper towels<|endoftext|>
