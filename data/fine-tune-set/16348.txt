Ingredients:
• Shrimp
• Bacon
• Butter flavor shortening
• Parmesan cheese
• Garlic clove
• Lemon juice

Steps:
1. Wrap each shrimp with a bacon slice and secure with 2 wooden picks
2. Stir together the melted shortening, cheese, garlic and lemon juice
3. Brush mixture on shrimp
4. Place shrimp on a baking sheet and broil, about 5 inches from the heat, for 4 minutes per side or until bacon is crisp<|endoftext|>
