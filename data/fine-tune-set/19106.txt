Give me a ice cream recipe.<|endoftext|>Ben Jerry S Butter Pecan Ice Cream

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Pecan halves
• Salt
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Heavy cream
• Milk

Steps:
1. Melt the butter in a heavy skillet over low heat
2. Add the pecans and salt and saute, stirring constantly, until the pecans start to turn brown
3. Drain the butter into a small bowl
4. Transfer the pecans to another bowl and let cool
5. Whisk the eggs in a mixing bowl until light and fluffy, 1 to 2 minutes
6. Whisk in the sugar, a little at a time, then continue whisking until completely blended, about 1 minute more
7. Pour in the cream and milk, and whisk to blend
8. Add the melted butter and blend
9. Transfer the mixture to an ice cream maker and freeze following the manufacturer's directions
10. After the ice cream stiffens, add the pecans, then continue freezing until done<|endoftext|>
