Ingredients:
• Bisquick baking mix
• Milk
• Orange juice
• Vegetable oil
• Egg

Steps:
1. Beat all ingredients together until well blended
2. Pour 1 cup batter onto hot waffle iron, greased
3. Bake until steaming slows and waffle is golden brown
4. Makes 3 9 inch waffles<|endoftext|>
