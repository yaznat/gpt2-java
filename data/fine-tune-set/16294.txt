Ingredients:
• Jalapeno peppers
• Cream cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Cajun seasoning
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Cut jalapenos in half lengthwise
2. Remove seeds and center membranes
3. In a small mixing bowl, combine the cream cheese, cheddar cheese and cajun seasoning
4. Stuff about 1 1 / 2 teaspoonfuls into each pepper half
5. Cut bacon strips in half widthwise
6. In a large skillet cook bacon until partially cooked
7. Wrap a bacon piece around each pepper
8. Secure with a toothpick
9. Place on a wire rack in a shallow baking pan
10. Bake uncovered at 350 for 25-30 minutes or until bacon is crisp
11. Discard the toothpicks and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
