What's a good taco recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef Bean Oven Tacos

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Black beans
• Taco seasoning
• Diced tomatoes
• Taco shells
• Cheese
• Corn

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 350f
2. Brown the ground beef with the chopped onion
3. Once browned, drain well
4. Stir in the black beans, taco seasoning, diced tomatoes and let simmer for 5 minutes
5. Line up 8 taco shells in a 7x11-inch baking dish
6. Spoon meat mixture into the taco shells
7. Top with a pinchfuls of cheese
8. Bake in the preheated oven for 10 minutes
9. Serve beef & bean oven tacos with side of veggies<|endoftext|>
