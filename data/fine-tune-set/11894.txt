What's a recipe for curry?<|endoftext|>Caribbean Vegetable Chicken Curry

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Dijon mustard
• Sesame oil
• Curry powder
• Vegetable oil
• Garlic
• Fresh gingerroot
• Hot chili pepper
• Allspice
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Onion
• Leek
• Homemade vegetable stock
• Tomato puree
• Potatoes
• Sweet potato
• Carrots
• Butternut squash
• Pineapple juice
• Milk
• Chickpeas
• Plantains
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Pat chicken dry
2. In small bowl, combine mustard, sesame oil and 1 tbsp
3. Curry powder
4. Preheat barbecue or broiler and grill chicken for 5 minutes per side
5. Cut into 2-inch chunks
6. Reserve
7. Heat oil in large, deep skillet or dutch oven
8. Add garlic, ginger and hot chile pepper
9. Cook for 30 seconds
10. Add remaining 2 tbsp
11. Curry powder, allspice, cinnamon and nutmeg
12. Cook for 30 seconds longer
13. Add onion, leek and stock
14. Cook until liquid evaporates
15. Add pureed tomatoes and bring to boil
16. Add potatoes and sweet potato and cook, covered, for 10 minutes
17. Add carrots, squash, pineapple juice, plantains, chickpeas and chicken and cook, covered, for 30 minutes longer
18. Taste and adjust seasonings if necessary
19. To serve, sprinkle with cilantro<|endoftext|>
