Give me a recipe for cake.<|endoftext|>Abraham Lincoln S Favorite Cake

Ingredients:
• Egg whites
• Sugar
• Blanched almonds
• Butter
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Buttermilk
• Vanilla
• Almond extract

Steps:
1. Beat egg whites until frothy
2. Add 1 cup sugar gradually until egg whites are stiff peaks
3. Finely chop almonds
4. Cream the butter and remaining 1 cup sugar until light and fluffy
5. Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together
6. Add vanilla extract and almond extract to buttermilk
7. Combine nuts with flour mixture
8. Add wet to dry by thirds
9. Fold beaten egg whites into batter
10. Place greased wax paper in tube pan
11. Turn batter into a 10-inch tube pan
12. Bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for about 1 hour or until cake tests done
13. Frost with boiled icing<|endoftext|>
