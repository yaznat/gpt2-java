Ingredients:
• Pork loin
• Barbecue sauce
• Ketchup
• Water
• Garlic powder
• Red onion
• Diced tomatoes
• Fresh cilantro
• Lemon
• Salt and pepper
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. Pork
2. Place pork in a pot, ketchup, barbecue sauce, garlic powder and water, simmer for an hour and a half, until the meat is very soft
3. You can use a slow cooker
4. Salad
5. Place all ingredients in a bowl stir well
6. To serve
7. Heat 2 tortillas and place 2 tablespoons of meat and a teaspoon of salad, and ready to enjoy<|endoftext|>
