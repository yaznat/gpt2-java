Ingredients:
• Tequila
• Green chartreuse liqueur
• Lime juice
• Ice

Steps:
1. Pour all ingredients over ice in tightly covered container
2. Shake until very cold
3. Pour into serving glass
4. Garnish with lime slice, if desired<|endoftext|>
