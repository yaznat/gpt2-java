Can you give me a quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Fajita Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Flank steaks
• Garlic
• Cooking oil
• Lime
• Worcestershire sauce
• Liquid smoke
• White onion
• Green bell peppers
• Cheddar cheese
• Flour tortilla
• Guacamole
• Salsa
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Cut flank steak into strips 1 / 4-inch wide and 3 inches long and marinate in worcestershire sauce, liquid smoke, juice of 1 lime and garlic for 20 minutes
2. While meat is marinating, cut onion and bell pepper into 1 / 4-inch wide strips and heat in non-stick skillet with oil until they begin to sweat
3. Remove them to a paper plate to drain
4. Drain meat and brown in same skillet until done
5. Remove to paper plate to drain
6. Wipe out the skillet so it is dry and begin to reheat it over a medium heat
7. Take one tortilla and put a small amount of grated cheese, 1 / 4 of the meat, onion and bell pepper to suit and cover with another small amount of grated cheese and top with a tortilla
8. Carefully put "tortilla sandwich" in the heated dry skillet and cook until cheese melts
9. Flip and cook on other side same amount of time
10. Tortilla will take on a lovely "tanned" appearance and become just slightly crispy
11. This dinner sounds involved but is actually quite simple and very quick
12. Kids and adults both will love it served with iced tea, pico de gallo, guacamole and salsa
13. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
