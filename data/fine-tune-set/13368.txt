What's a good chicken recipe?<|endoftext|>Al Kabsa Traditional Saudi Rice Chicken Dish

Ingredients:
• Butter
• Chicken
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Tomato puree
• Chopped tomatoes
• Carrots
• Whole cloves
• Nutmeg
• Ground cumin
• Ground coriander
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Hot water
• Chicken stock cube
• Basmati rice
• Raisins
• Slivered almonds
• Saffron
• Green cardamoms
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground allspice
• White pepper
• Dried limes

Steps:
1. Melt butter in a large stock pot, casserole or dutch oven
2. Add chicken pieces, onion & garlic & saut until onion is tender
3. Stir in tomato puree & simmer over a low heat for a couple of minutes
4. Add tomatoes, carrots, cloves, all the spices & salt and pepper
5. Cook for a couple of minutes
6. Add the water & maggi cube
7. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and cover
8. Simmer over low heat for 30 minutes
9. Add rice to the pot & stir carefully
10. Re-cover & simmer for 35 - 40 minutes - adding the raisins for the last 10 minutes - or until rice is tender
11. Place the rice on a large serving dish, topped with the chicken & garnished with almonds
12. Serve with a fresh mixed salad - preferably with a little lime vinaigrette
13. Saudis like their kabsa with a hot sauce called 'shattah'
14. N
15. B: you can also 'finish' the chicken pieces off in the oven by cooking them for 25 - 30 minutes at 180 c whilst the rice is cooking<|endoftext|>
