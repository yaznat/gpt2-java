Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Butter
• Chicken
• Celery ribs
• Onion
• Garlic
• Granny smith apples
• Red bell pepper
• Curry powder
• Cinnamon
• Cumin
• All-purpose flour
• Chicken broth
• Fresh parsley
• Cooked basmati rice

Steps:
1. Heat oil and 1 tblsp butter over moderately-high heat until foam subsides
2. Brown chicken, patted dry and seasoned with salt
3. Transfer to a plate with a slotted spoon
4. Pour off fat from casserole, add remaining 2 tblsps butter and cook celery, onion and garlic over moderately-low heat, until softened
5. Add apples and cook 5 minutes
6. Add bell pepper, curry, cinnamon and cumin and cook 1 minutes
7. Stir in flour and cook over moderate heat 3 minutes
8. Add broth and chicken and cook, covered and at a bare simmer, 15-20 minutes, or until chicken is cooked and tender
9. Transfer chicken to a plate with a slotted spoon
10. Boil sauce 3-5 minutes, or until thickened slightly, and season
11. Pour sauce over chicken and sprinkle with parsley<|endoftext|>
