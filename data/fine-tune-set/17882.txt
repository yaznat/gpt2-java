Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Unsalted butter
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Unbleached all-purpose flour
• Glaze
• Heavy cream
• Water
• Fine sea salt
• Gelatin powder

Steps:
1. Brownies:
2. Heat oven to 325 degrees f
3. Line a 9-by-13-inch pan with foil and then butter well
4. In the top of a double boiler over medium heat, melt chocolate and butter, being sure the water in the bottom of the double boiler does not touch the top pan
5. Stir chocolate and butter until completely melted
6. Then stir in sugar
7. Blend well
8. Remove from heat
9. In a separate bowl, lightly whisk eggs
10. Stir eggs into chocolate mixture
11. Then add flour, stirring until completely blended
12. Spread batter into prepared pan and bake for 25-30 minutes
13. Completely cool brownies in pan
14. Pour caramel glaze over brownies and cool in fridge until caramel sets
15. Slice into bars, about 2 inches square
16. Salted caramel glaze:
17. In a small saucepan over low heat, warm heavy cream until hot, but not boiling
18. In a separate tall saucepan, combine sugar and water
19. Place over medium-high heat
20. Do not stir as sugar dissolves and mixture reaches a dark amber stage
21. Add cream, butter, and salt
22. Stir gently until mixture is smooth and well combined
23. Remove from heat and add gelatin, stirring to combine
24. Use immediately<|endoftext|>
