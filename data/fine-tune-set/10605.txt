Ingredients:
• Wild mushroom
• Tomatoes
• Prosciutto
• Salt and pepper
• Olive oil
• Garlic
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Rosemary sprigs
• Angel hair pasta
• Whipping cream
• Butter
• Parmesan cheese
• Fresh basil

Steps:
1. Rinse the mushrooms and pat dry
2. Cut off and reserve stems
3. Cut the mushroom caps into thin strips
4. You should have about 4 cups of mushrooms
5. Cut the tomatoes into 1 / 2 inch cubes
6. Cut the prosciutto into very thin strips
7. Heat oil in skillet and add mushrooms, rosemary, pepper, and crushed red pepper and salt
8. Cook, stirring for about 3 minutes, then add the garlic and prosciutto for another 1-2 minutes
9. Meanwhile, cook pasta in boiling water to desired doneness
10. Reserve 1 / 2 cup of the cooking liquid, drain pasta, and return to pot
11. Add tomatoes to the mushroom mixture and stir
12. Add cream and bring to a boil
13. Add the pasta, reserved pasta liquid, butter, 1 / 4 cup of the parmesan, and basil
14. Toss to blend
15. Serve with remaining parmesan cheese<|endoftext|>
