Ingredients:
• Blueberries
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Egg white
• Sour cream
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Vanilla
• Brown sugar
• Cinnamon
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f grease and flour one 8x12 pan, or use cooking spray
2. Cream butter and sugar, add eggs, egg white, sour cream and vanilla and mix well
3. In a separate bowl, mix flour, baking powder, and baking soda
4. Add to creamed mixture and mix well
5. In another small bowl, combine topping ingredients until well mixed
6. Set aside
7. Spread 1 / 2 of dough in bottom of prepared baking pan
8. Top with blueberries or cherries
9. Spread rest of dough on top
10. Sprinkle topping over dough
11. Bake at 325f for 50 to 60 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean<|endoftext|>
