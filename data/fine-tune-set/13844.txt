Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Pork chops
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Apple juice
• Spicy brown mustard
• Honey
• Ground black pepper
• Medium egg noodles

Steps:
1. Heat oil in skillet
2. Cook chops 10 minute or until browned
3. Add soup, apple juice, mustard, honey and black pepper
4. Heat to a boil
5. Cover and cook over low heat 5 minute or until chops are done
6. Serve with noodles<|endoftext|>
