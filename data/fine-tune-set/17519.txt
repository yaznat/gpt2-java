Ingredients:
• Blueberry muffin mix
• Peaches
• Sugar
• Flour
• Ground cinnamon
• Butter

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
2. Drain peaches and blueberries
3. Spread fruit on paper towels to drain
4. Combine sugar, flour and cinnamon in a small bowl
5. Cut in butter and set aside
6. Mix muffin batter as directed, except do not fold in blueberries
7. Spread batter in a greased 8 inch square pan
8. Top with drained blueberries and peach slices
9. Sprinkle the cinnamon mixture over the fruit
10. Bake for 25 minutes or until golden brown<|endoftext|>
