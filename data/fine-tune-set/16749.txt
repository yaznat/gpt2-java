What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Chocolate Swirl Milkshakes

Ingredients:
• Vanilla ice cream
• Milk
• Vanilla extract
• Chocolate fudge topping
• Maraschino cherries

Steps:
1. Combine ice-cream, milk and vanilla in a blender
2. Process until thick and smooth, about 1 minute
3. Divide fudge syrup among parfait glasses, covering the bottom and drizzling syrup in a spiral pattern around inside of each glass
4. Pour equal amounts of ice-cream mixture into each glass
5. Swirl some chocolate fudge syrup over each serving
6. Place 1 cherry on top of each milkshake<|endoftext|>
