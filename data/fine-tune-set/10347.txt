Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Butter
• Salt
• Egg
• Almonds
• Lemon peel
• Blanched almonds

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 325
2. Place cookie sheet in oven to preheat
3. Grease 10 or 9 inch springform pan
4. In large bowl, blend all crust ingredients at low speed until dough forms
5. If desired, refrigerate for easier handling
6. Divide dough in half
7. Spread half in bottom of greased pan to form crust
8. In small bowl, combine all filling ingredients except whole almonds
9. Blend well
10. Spread over crust to within 1 / 2 inch of sides of pan
11. Between 2 sheets of waxed paper, press remaining dough to 10 or 9 inch circle
12. Remove top sheet of waxed paper
13. Place dough over filling
14. Remove waxed paper
15. Press dough into place
16. Top with whole almonds
17. Place cake on preheated cookie sheet
18. Bake at 325 for 65-75 minutes or until top is light golden brown
19. Cool 15 minutes
20. Remove sides of pan
21. Cool completely<|endoftext|>
