What's a recipe for fried rice?<|endoftext|>Easy Chinese Egg Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Egg
• Cooked rice
• Green onions
• Sesame oil
• Salt

Steps:
1. Heat oil in wok or pan
2. Add egg and scramble
3. Add rice and saut until rice clumps are loosened
4. Add salt to taste
5. Saut in green onion and sesame oil for fragrance
6. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
