What's a recipe for custard?<|endoftext|>Egg Custard Pie

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Salt
• Flour
• Milk
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Nutmeg
• 9" unbaked pie shell

Steps:
1. Beat egg yolks
2. Add sugar, salt and flour
3. Scald milk and butter
4. Pour into sugar mixture
5. Beat egg whites fold into mixture
6. Pour into pie shell and bake at 325 for approximately 30 minutes or until firm<|endoftext|>
