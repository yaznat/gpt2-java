Ingredients:
• Butter
• Brown sugar
• Granulated sugar
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Unbleached white flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• White chocolate chips
• Macadamia nuts

Steps:
1. Make sure the oven keeps the right temperature
2. The cookies may need a minute or two more or less than stated
3. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
4. Line baking sheets with parchment paper
5. Measure flour, baking soda and salt in a large bowl and blend together
6. Set aside
7. In a large mixing bowl, preferably a heavy-duty mixer, and using the paddle or beater attachment, beat butter until creamy
8. Add brown sugar and beat again
9. Scrape down the bowl and add the granualted sugar
10. Beat until fluffy
11. Add eggs one at a time, beating after each addition
12. Add vanilla, beat and scrape again
13. On low speed, add flour mixture 2 cups at a time, and mix well
14. Mix in white chocolate and macadamia nuts
15. Mix only until blended
16. Using a cookie scoop, place dough on prepared baking sheets, spacing evenly to allow for spreading
17. Bake at 350 degrees for 10-12 minutes or until lightly browned
18. Do not over bake these cookies
19. They continue to bake for a few minutes after they are removed from the oven
20. Transfer to cooling racks
21. Store in air tight container or plastic bag to stay soft and moist
22. Makes 48 warm and gooey cookies<|endoftext|>
