Please give me a recipe for a cocktail.<|endoftext|>I Am Tired Of Living Cocktail

Ingredients:
• Light rum
• Tequila
• Grand marnier
• Grenadine
• Tea bag
• Powdered sugar
• Maraschino cherries
• Cracked ice

Steps:
1. Frost two 8-ounce crystal glasses in your freezer for at least 30 minutes or so
2. Brew the tea and chill it in the freezer
3. Stir in the powdered sugar after it cools
4. In a separate carafe, blend the rum, tequila, grand marnier and the grenadine
5. Pour this mix into the two glasses, an equal amount in each
6. Fill each glass the rest of the way with the chilled tea
7. Garnish with a couple of the cherries on a toothpick in each glass<|endoftext|>
