Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Red wine vinegar
• Salt & fresh ground pepper
• Hard-boiled eggs
• Celery
• Red onions
• Sweet gherkin
• Mayonnaise
• Dijon mustard
• Fresh parsley leaves

Steps:
1. Cover potatoes with 1 inch of water in stockpot or dutch oven
2. Bring to simmer over medium-high heat
3. Reduce heat to medium and simmer, stirring once or twice to ensure even cooking, until potatoes are tender, 25 to 30 minutes for medium potatoes or 15 to 20 minutes for new potatoes
4. Drain
5. Cool potatoes slightly and peel if you like
6. Cut potatoes into 3 / 4-inch cubes while still warm, rinsing knife occasionally in warm water to remove starch
7. Place warm potato cubes in large bowl
8. Add vinegar, 1-2 teaspoon salt, and 1 / 4 teaspoon pepper and toss gently
9. Cover bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate until cool, about 20 minutes
10. When potatoes are cool, toss with remaining ingredients and season with salt and pepper to taste
11. Serve
12. Imediately or cover and refrigerate
