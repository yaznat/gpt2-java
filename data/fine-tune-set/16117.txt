Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Red onion
• Chorizo sausage
• Manchego cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. To prepare the onions, lightly brush them with the teaspoon of olive oil
2. Cook on a grill pan or indoor grill for one or two minutes until the onions have started getting a little soft and have a few grill marks
3. Remove from grill and break slices into rings
4. To prepare the chorizo, place a skillet over medium high heat until hot, not smoking
5. Add the chorizo and cook about 1 minute on each side
6. Drain the sausage on a paper towel to remove the excess grease
7. To assemble the quesadilla, start the tortilla that will become the bottom
8. Top with a tenth of the cheese
9. Sprinkle with 1 / 5 of the onions, then the sausage
10. Cover that with another tenth of the cheese and top with another tortilla
11. Repeat step 3 with the next 8 tortilla to assemble a total of 5 quesadillas
12. To cook the quesadillas, heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat
13. Brush lightly with some of the tablespoon of olive oil
14. Place quesadilla in the pan and cook for 2 or three minutes until bottom tortilla is browned and the cheese on bottom has melted and begun to dribble
15. Flip the quesadilla and continue to cook an additional 2 or three minutes
16. Remove quesadilla to plate, cover with clean cloth and place in oven while preparing the others
17. Before serving cut in halves or quarters<|endoftext|>
