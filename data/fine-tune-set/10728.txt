Ingredients:
• Pumpkin
• Olive oil flavored cooking spray
• Salt and pepper
• Dry penne pasta
• Bacon
• Basil pesto
• Parmesan cheese

Steps:
1. Pre-heat oven to 200c
2. Line a baking tray with non-stick baking paper, and place the pumpkin in a single layer onto the prepared tray
3. Spray with olive oil spray and season with salt and pepper
4. Bake pumpkin in oven for around 25 minutes or until tender
5. Bring a large pan of lightly salted water to the boil, then cook pasta according to the packet directions, until al dente
6. Drain and return to the pan
7. Saute the bacon in a shallow non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat for 5 minutes, stirring often
8. Add pesto to pasta and toss until coated, then add pumpkin and bacon and toss again until well combined
9. Divide among serving bowls and sprinkle with parmesan cheese
10. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
