Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Egg
• Oatmeal
• Bacon bits
• A. 1. original sauce
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Place ground beef in a medium mixing bowl
2. In a small skillet, saute onion until slightly carmelized
3. Add to ground beef
4. Add remaining ingredients and mix well
5. Shape into four patties
6. Grill for about 6 minutes per side or until juices run clear<|endoftext|>
