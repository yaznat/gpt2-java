Ingredients:
• Skirt steaks
• Pepper
• Salt
• Garlic
• Sugar
• Beef stock
• Teriyaki sauce
• Soy sauce
• Hoisin sauce
• Onion
• Red pepper

Steps:
1. Cut the steak across the grain into 1 / 4 inch slices
2. In a bowl, combine pepper, salt, garlic and sugar, stir
3. Put your steak slices in the mixture and toss
4. Put steak into crockpot, add beef broth and stir
5. Add teriyaki, soy and hoison sauce, stir
6. Add chopped onions and sliced red pepper, stir
7. Cover and cook on low for 5-7 hours depending on your crock pot
8. Serve over noodles or rice
9. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
