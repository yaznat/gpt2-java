Ingredients:
• Chorizo sausages
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh jalapeno pepper
• Tortillas
• Cooking oil
• Monterey jack cheese
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. For filling, in a medium skillet cook chorizo or sausage, onion, and garlic until meat is brown and onion is tender
2. Drain off fat
3. Pat chorizo mixture with paper towels to remove as much additional fat as possible
4. Stir in jalapeno pepper
5. Set aside
6. Brush one side of 3 tortillas with half of the cooking oil
7. Place tortillas, oiled sides down, on a large baking sheet
8. Spread the chorizo filling over tortillas on baking sheet
9. Combine cheese and cilantro or parsley
10. Sprinkle over filling
11. Top with the remaining tortillas
12. Brush with the remaining oil
13. Place quesadillas on the rack of an uncovered grill directly over medium heat
14. Grill for 3 to 4 minutes or until filling is heated through and tortillas are starting to brown, turning once halfway through grilling
15. To serve, cut quesadillas into wedges
