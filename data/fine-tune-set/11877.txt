Ingredients:
• Onion
• Canola oil
• Curry powder
• Cumin
• Coriander
• Cinnamon
• Frozen spinach
• Butternut squash
• Chickpeas
• Diced tomatoes
• Light coconut milk
• Salt and pepper
• Cilantro
• Basmati rice

Steps:
1. Place cubed butternut squash on a foil lined baking sheet, sprayed with nonstick cooking spray
2. Sprinkle generously with curry powder, salt and pepper, and roast at 350 for 35-45 minutes, or until tender
3. You still want them to hold their shape when added to the curry, and not be mush: >
4. While squash cooks, heat 1-2 tsp of canola oil over medium heat
5. Add onions and a little bit of salt, and saut 2-3 minutes, or until they begin to soften
6. Next, add the curry powder, cumin, corriander and cinnamon, and stir to coat the onions evenly with spices
7. Add tomatoes with their juices, and the chickpeas, stir to combine
8. Next, add the spinach, stirring to coat with cooking liquid
9. When all the spinach is added to the pan, cover and simmer until just wilted, about 3 minutes
10. Add coconut milk and gently stir to combine
11. Finally, add the roasted butternut squash to the liquid, and stir to coat
12. Top with fresh chopped cilantro and serve over basmati rice<|endoftext|>
