Ingredients:
• Low sodium beef broth
• Dry red wine
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Dried tarragon
• Black pepper
• Cooking spray
• Beef tenderloin steaks
• Shallots
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Combine first 6 ingredients, stirring with a whisk
2. Heat a nonstick skillet coated with cooking spray over medium-high heat
3. Add steaks
4. Cook 3 minutes on each side or until desired degree of doneness
5. Remove from pan
6. Keep warm
7. Add shallots to pan
8. Saut 1 minute
9. Add broth mixture
10. Bring to a boil
11. Cook until reduced to 1 / 4 cup
12. Serve sauce with the steaks
13. Sprinkle with parsley<|endoftext|>
