Give me a pancake recipe.<|endoftext|>Banana Flax Pancakes With Blueberry Sauce

Ingredients:
• Egg
• Canola oil
• Honey
• Skim milk
• Banana
• Complete pancake and waffle mix
• Walnuts
• Ground flax seeds
• Salt
• Fresh blueberries
• Sugar

Steps:
1. In small bowl, mix together egg, oil, honey, milk and banana
2. Set aside
3. In large bowl, combine pancake mix, ground walnuts, ground flax seed and salt
4. Add egg mixture to dry ingredients and stir together
5. Spoon batter onto hot skillet
6. Cook until bubbles start to appear on surface and flip
7. Cook until light, golden brown
8. Blueberry sauce
9. In nonstick saucepan, combine blueberries and sugar
10. Cook until blueberries are warm and softened
11. Spoon warm blueberries on top of each pancake and serve<|endoftext|>
