What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Breast Of Chicken Flambe With Brandied Cherry Sauce 4 2

Ingredients:
• Chicken breasts
• Butter
• Paprika
• Salt
• White pepper
• Cherry brandy
• Bing cherries
• Burgundy wine
• Sugar
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Preparing bing cherry sauce:
2. Drain juice from cherries
3. Combine juice with wine, sugar, cornstarch, and salt
4. Mix thoroughly
5. Bring mixture to a boil until sauce thickens
6. Remove from heat
7. Stir in the drained cherries
8. Season chicken breast with salt, white pepper, paprika, and brush with butter
9. Bake for 30 minutes at 325f or until cooked and tender
10. Place chicken in a medium size ovenproof serving dish and cover with sauce
11. Have table set, candles lit, all accompaniments on table, and turn off lights when ready to flamb
12. How to flambe:
13. Pour 1 / 4 ounce of brandy over dish and place in middle of table
14. Using long wooden matches, ignite chicken breast
15. When flame goes out and liquor has burned off, you are ready to serve<|endoftext|>
