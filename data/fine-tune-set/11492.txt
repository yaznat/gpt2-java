Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• White sugar
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Instant chocolate pudding mix
• Amish starter
• Vegetable oil
• Milk
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Lightly grease two 9x5 inch loaf pans
3. In a large mixing bowl, stir together all-purpose flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, chocolate pudding
4. Make a well in the center of this mixture
5. Add amish friendship bread starter, vegetable oil, milk, eggs and vanilla extract
6. Mix well
7. Pour batter into prepared loaf pans
8. 2-9' loaf pans
9. Bake in preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into center of the loaf comes out clean, about 60 minutes
10. Cool on a wire rack for 10 minutes before removing from pan<|endoftext|>
