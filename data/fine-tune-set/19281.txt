Ingredients:
• Bananas
• Chocolate chips
• Creamy peanut butter
• Wooden popsicle sticks

Steps:
1. Peel& halve bananas
2. Insert sticks
3. Freeze on plate 1 hour
4. Mix pb / chocolate in microwave-safe dish& heat till melted& smooth
5. Coat bananas
6. Re-freeze 1 hour, then remove from freezer& wrap in aluminum foil<|endoftext|>
