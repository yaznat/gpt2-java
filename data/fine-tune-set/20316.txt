What's a recipe for stir fry?<|endoftext|>Chinese Cabbage Snow Pea And Mushroom Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Light soy sauce
• Ketjap manis
• Sesame oil
• Caster sugar
• Peanut oil
• White onion
• Almonds
• Ginger
• Oyster mushrooms
• Shiitake mushrooms
• Snow peas
• Chinese cabbage

Steps:
1. Combine soy sauce, kecap manis, sesame oil and sugar
2. Heat peanut oil in wok over high heat
3. Add onion and almonds stir-fry 1 minute
4. Add ginger, mushrooms and stir fry 2 minutes
5. Add snow peas, cabbage and sauce mixture
6. Stir-fry 2 minutes
7. Serve with steamed rice<|endoftext|>
