• Evaporated milk
• German chocolate cake mix
• Margarine

Steps:

2. Preheat oven to 350
3. Combine caramels and 1 / 3 cup evaporated milk in saucepan
4. Cook over low heat until melted
5. Combine cake mix, margarine, 1 / 3 cup evaporated milk, and nuts
6. Stir by hand until dough is well mixed
7. Press half of the dough into pan
8. Bake for 6 minutes
9. Remove from oven
10. Sprinkle chocolate chips over crust
11. And spread caramel over top of that
12. Crumble remaining dough over top of caramel layer
13. Bake for 15 - 25 minutes
14. Cool completely
