Ingredients:
• Milk
• Salt
• Light brown sugar
• Bacon drippings
• Active dry yeast
• Water
• Egg
• All-purpose flour
• Whole wheat flour
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Combine scalded milk, salt, 2 tablespoons of the brown sugar, and bacon drippings
2. Cool
3. Combine yeast, 2 tablespoons brown sugar, and warmed water
4. When yeast mixture begins to foam, add to cooled milk mixture
5. Add beaten egg, all purpose flour and whole wheat flour
6. Beat for 3 minutes
7. Cover and let rise for 45 minutes then stir in the bacon and beat for 2 minutes
8. Pour into a greased 2-quart casserole or two glass loaf pans about 9 x 5 x 3-inches
9. Let rise until batter reaches top of pan, about 30 minutes
10. Slash top diagonally a few times with a sharp knife
11. Bake at 375 for 35 to 40 minutes<|endoftext|>
