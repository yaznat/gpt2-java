Give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Bittersweet Chocolate Brownies Macrina Bakery

Ingredients:
• Unsweetened chocolate
• Unsalted butter
• Granulated sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Combine chocolate and butter in a medium bowl
2. Cover with plastic wrap and place in microwave oven on medium for 3 minutes
3. Stir well
4. Cool to room temperature
5. Preheat oven to 325f lightly oil an 8-by-9-inch or 9-inch-square baking pan
6. Place sugar and flour in a mixer bowl and mix 30 seconds on low speed
7. Increase speed to medium and add eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each
8. With mixer on low speed, drizzle in the cooled, melted chocolate mixture
9. Increase speed to medium and mix about 1 minute or until the batter is mousse-like in texture
10. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and spread it evenly with a rubber spatula
11. Bake on center rack of oven 30 to 35 minutes or until the center is just set
12. Cool on a wire rack<|endoftext|>
