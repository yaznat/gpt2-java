Ingredients:
• Avocado
• Sour cream
• Cream cheese
• Taco seasoning
• Salt

• Tomatoes
• Four-cheese mexican blend cheese

Steps:

3. Mix with sour cream
4. Stir mashed avocado into sour cream and cream cheese mixture
5. Add taco seasoning, salt, and mrs
6. Dash
7. Mix to taste
8. Add more seasoning to taste if desired
9. Pour into flat bottomed serving dish
10. Top with diced tomato and cheese
