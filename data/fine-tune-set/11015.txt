What's a good sandwich recipe?<|endoftext|>Alan Nowell And Paula Deen Jumbo Lump Crab Cakes Sandwiches

Ingredients:
• Mayonnaise
• Egg
• Dijon mustard
• Hot chili sauce
• Green onions
• Dill
• Parsley
• Breadcrumbs
• Red pepper
• Lemon, zest of
• Salt
• Pepper
• Lump crabmeat
• All-purpose flour
• Peanut oil

Steps:
1. Whisk together the first four ingredients in a large bowl
2. Add the rest of the ingredients through black pepper and mix well
3. Gently fold in crab meat
4. Divide into 6 patties and coat with flour
5. Saute in the peanut oil over medium heat for 4-6 minutes per side
6. Serve on a bun with remoulade sauce<|endoftext|>
