Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Garlic cloves
• Red chilies
• Beef mince
• Chinese five spice powder
• Kidney beans
• Chopped tomatoes
• Strong black coffee
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper
• Taco shells

Steps:
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion, garlic and chillies for 3-4 minutes until begining to soften
2. Add the mince and five spice powder and cook for a further 3-4 minutes, stirring, until the meat begins to brown
3. Add the kidney beans, tomatoes and coffee
4. Bring to the boil and simmer for 20 minutes until the mixture is thick and fairly dark
5. Season to taste
6. Fill the taco shells with shredded lettuce, then pile in the chilli mixture
7. Top with a spoonful of soured cream, if liked and a shake of paprika and serve with a leafy green salad<|endoftext|>
