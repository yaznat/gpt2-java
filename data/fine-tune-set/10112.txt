What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Authentic Midwestern Hot Dog Chili Hot Dog Sauce

Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Water
• Onions
• Chili powder
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Salt
• Ground allspice
• Worcestershire sauce
• Garlic powder
• Cinnamon
• Cumin
• Bay leaves
• Vinegar
• Tomato paste
• Chocolate

Steps:
1. Add raw beef to cold water and stir until beef separates to a fine texture
2. Add remaining ingredients
3. Stir and simmer uncovered for at least 3 hours
4. At the beginning of the last hour, pan may be covered after desired consistency is reached
5. Remove bay leaves before serving<|endoftext|>
