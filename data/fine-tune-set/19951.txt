Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Unsalted butter
• Oysters
• Green onions
• Garlic
• Cayenne pepper
• Salt
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Half-and-half

Steps:
1. In 10" skillet, fry bacon until crisp
2. Crumble it
3. Drain all but 2 teaspoons bacon grease from skillet
4. Add bacon back to pan
5. Add 1 tablespoons unsalted butter to reserved 2 tsp bacon grease in skillet over med-high heat
6. Add oysters, green onions, and garlic
7. Saute until edges of oysters start to curl, about 1-2 minutes
8. Add remaining 2 tablespoons butter, cayenne and salt
9. Remove from heat and set aside
10. Whisk together eggs, half and half and a pinch of salt
11. Clean pan and heat over medium heat
12. Add 2 tsp unsalted butter and 2 tsp vegetable oil to hot pan
13. Add egg mixture and cook, lifting edges to let uncooked egg run under omelette, so that top is not too wet
14. Flip once and cook 10 seconds
15. Flip omelette onto large plate
16. Put oyster mixture in the middle and fold over omelette to serve<|endoftext|>
