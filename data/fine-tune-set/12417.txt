Ingredients:
• Onion
• Celery
• Butter
• Southern style hash browns
• Chicken broth
• Garlic salt
• Black pepper
• Heavy cream
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onion
• Bacon

Steps:
1. Saute onions and celery in butter until soft
2. Season with salt and pepper
3. Place celery and onions in a slow cooker
4. Add hashbrown potatoes, chicken broth and seasonings
5. Cover and cook on low for 6 - 8 hours
6. Add cream and continue to cook for another hour
7. If too thick, add a little milk or water
8. Garnish each soup bowl with chopped green onion, cheese and bacon<|endoftext|>
