Please give me a recipe for chicken.<|endoftext|>Alice Chicken

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Worcestershire sauce
• Bacon
• Butter
• Fresh mushrooms
• Green onion
• Monterey jack cheese
• Honey mustard dressing

Steps:
1. Place chicken in a glass dish or bowl
2. Poke each breast with a fork several times
3. Pour worcestershire sauce over and turn to coat them well
4. Cover dish or bowl and refrigerate for at least 1 hour
5. Place bacon in a large, deep skillet
6. Cook over medium high heat until evenly brown
7. Drain, and set aside
8. Remove most of the fat from the skillet, but do not wipe it clean
9. In same skillet used to cook bacon, melt the butter
10. Add mushrooms and green onions, and saute for about 10 minutes, or until soft
11. Remove from pan and set aside
12. Preheat oven to broil
13. Remove chicken from marinade and discard any remaining liquid
14. Broil chicken breasts for about 5 minutes each side
15. When the cooking of the chicken is almost complete, top each breast with 2 slices bacon and grated cheese
16. Continue to broil until cheese has melted and remove from oven
17. Serve with mushroom mixture and a variety of salad dressings for topping<|endoftext|>
