Ingredients:
• Sugar
• All-purpose flour
• Milk
• Eggs
• Whipping cream
• Apple pie filling
• Vanilla
• Ground cinnamon

Steps:
1. In large saucepan, combine sugar and flour
2. Stir in milk
3. Cook and stir until slightly thickened and bubbly
4. Stir about 1 cup of the hot mixture into eggs
5. Return egg mixture to saucepan
6. Cook and stir 2 minutes or more
7. Cover and chill
8. Stir in whipping cream, pie filling, vanilla and cinnamon
9. Freeze ina 4- or 5-quart ice-cream freezer according to manufacturer's directions<|endoftext|>
