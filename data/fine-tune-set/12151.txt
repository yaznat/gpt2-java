What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Blueberry Pie

Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Salt
• Apples
• Blueberries
• Lemon juice
• Pastry for double-crust pie
• Margarine

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425f
2. In a large bowl, stir together sugar, cornstarch and salt
3. Add apples, blueberries and lemon juice
4. Toss to coat the fruit
5. Turn into pastry lined 9" pie plate
6. Dot with margarine
7. Add top crust
8. Seal and flute edge
9. Pierce top crust evenly with a fork to allow steam to escape
10. Bake in oven until crust is browned and filling is bubbly<|endoftext|>
