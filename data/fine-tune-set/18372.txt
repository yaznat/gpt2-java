Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Best Ever Easy Waffles

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Baking soda
• Milk
• Lemon juice
• Eggs
• Butter

Steps:
1. Sift together flour, baking powder, salt, and baking soda
2. Stir in milk, lemon juice, and melted butter
3. Stir in beaten egg
4. Cook on greased waffle iron<|endoftext|>
