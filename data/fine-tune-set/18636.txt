Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Walnuts
• Graham cracker crumbs
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Apple
• Lemon, zest of
• Fresh lemon juice
• Icing sugar

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees f
2. Butter 10 inch springform pan
3. In large bowl, beat eggs with sugar until thick and pale
4. Combine walnuts, crumbs, baking powder salt and cinnamon and fold into egg mixture
5. Do not overmix
6. Fold in apple, lemon zest and lemon juice
7. Pour into pan, smoothing top
8. Bake about 40 minutes
9. Cool cake and then remove from springform pan
10. For glaze: combine icing sugar with 4 tsps lemon juice
11. Add more juice if necessary for proper drizzling consistency
12. Spoon over cake
13. Sprinkle with remaining walnuts<|endoftext|>
