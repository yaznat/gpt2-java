Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Salt
• Pepper
• Water
• Condensed chicken gumbo soup
• Ketchup
• Prepared mustard

Steps:
1. Cook meat and onion together until meat is browned
2. Add remaining ingredients and mix
3. Cover
4. Simmer gently over low heat 30 minutes
5. Stir occasionally
6. Spoon into 8 buns<|endoftext|>
