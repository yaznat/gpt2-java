Ingredients:
• Sea scallops
• Bacon
• Maple syrup
• Barbecue sauce
• Wooden skewer

Steps:
1. Soak long wooden skewers in water for about 20 minutes
2. Preheat bbq to about 250f
3. Mix bbq sauce and maple syrup
4. Wrap bacon around each scallop and thread onto a skewer
5. Place each skewer on the grill
6. Sear for about 2 minutes each side
7. Spoon or brush bbq sauce / maple syrup mixture over entire skewer each turn
8. Grill and turn every 2 minutes
9. Remove from grill after about 8 minutes
