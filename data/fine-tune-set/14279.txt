Ingredients:
• Eggplant
• Tofu
• Gingerroot
• Jalapeno pepper
• Green onions
• Garlic clove
• Soy sauce
• Lime, juice of
• Sugar
• Flour
• Sesame oil
• Cilantro
• Vegetable oil
• Water

Steps:
1. Heat 1t oil in large frying pan over medium high heat
2. Cook tofu in oil for 3-4 minutes per side, flipping once, until golden and crisp
3. Remove tofu from pan
4. In heated pan with residual oil, saute eggplant cubes until fully cooked but not soggy
5. This may take 5-10 minutes depending on how large you cut the eggplant
6. Remove eggplant from pan
7. Meanwhile, prepare sauce
8. In a 1 or 2 cup measuring cup, stir together soy sauce, lime juce, sugar, flour, sesame oil
9. Add enough water to make about 2 / 3 cup liquid
10. May add more or less water if you like an especially thick or thin sauce
11. Add 1t of oil to the hot pan
12. Cook ginger in hot oil for about 1 minute, then add jalapeno, green onion, and garlic
13. Cook for about 2 minutes more
14. Add cooked tofu and eggplant to pan with ginger mix
15. Cook and stir for about one more minute
16. Pour sauce mixture into pan, bring to boil and cook, stirring, until thickened
17. This will only take a minute or two
18. Add chopped cilantro to pan and stir to combine
19. Serve with plenty of hot cooked rice<|endoftext|>
