Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Flour
• Salt
• Almond extract
• Slivered almonds

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Melt butter and then add sugar in a mixing bowl
3. Beat in eggs one at a time
4. Add extract
5. Add flour and salt, mixing well
6. Pour batter into a large cast-iron skillet that has been lined with foil
7. Sprinkle with almonds
8. Bake for 30 minutes
9. Cool completely and remove foil<|endoftext|>
