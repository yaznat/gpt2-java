Give me a recipe for waffles.<|endoftext|>Cream Waffles

Ingredients:
• Whipping cream
• Flour
• Ice water
• Butter
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Whip the cream until stiff
2. Fold in the flour, then the ice water
3. Chill 1 hour
4. Melt the butter and stir into the batter
5. Spoon a little batter into each section of a heated waffle iron and bake until brown
6. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and serve hot<|endoftext|>
