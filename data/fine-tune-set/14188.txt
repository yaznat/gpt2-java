Ingredients:
• Baked tofu
• Rice vinegar
• Sucanat
• Sake
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Salt
• Canola oil
• Carrots
• White sesame seeds
• Toasted sesame oil

Steps:
1. If using baked tofu skip to slicing instructions
2. If using usu-age tofu - bring a small saucepan of water to a boil
3. Add tofu and gently simmer over medium heat, turning occasionally, for 1 minute
4. Drain to remove excess oil
5. Cut tofu diagonally in half and slice each half into thin strips
6. Combine the vinegar, sucanat, sake, soy sauce, and salt in a small bowl
7. Stir until the sugar has dissolved
8. Heat the oil in a large skillet over high heat
9. Add carrots and tofu strips and saut until the carrots are crisp-tender, about 3 minutes
10. Reduce heat to medium-low and add the soy mixture
11. Cook carrots and tofu for 2 more minutes, or until tender
12. Turn off heat, stir in the sesame seeds, and drizzle with toasted sesame oil
13. Transfer to small serving dish<|endoftext|>
