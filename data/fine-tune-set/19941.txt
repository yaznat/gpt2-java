Ingredients:
• Peanut oil
• Salt
• Fresh bean sprout
• Eggs
• Green onion
• Fresh cilantro
• Oyster sauce
• Water

Steps:
1. Heat a wok or large skillet
2. Add oil, salt and bean sprouts
3. Cook for 1 minute, then pour in the beaten eggs and add the green onions and cilantro
4. Allow to cook for 2 minutes, then, when it still looks moist but is cooked through, with a spatula turn half of the omelette over the other half
5. Drizzle the omelette with the oyster sauce mixture and serve<|endoftext|>
