Steps:
1. Fried rice:
2. Cook peas, corn, and carrots in microwave for 2-4 minutes
3. Cool and add to rice
4. Place 1 - 2 t oil in wok or non-stick pan
5. Saut rice, vegetables and onion briefly
6. Top with 1t butter and cook until hot
7. Add soy sauce and saut until incorporated
8. Serve immediately
9. Top with shrimp sauce
10. Shrimp sauce:
11. Add ingredients, in order given, to food processor or blender
