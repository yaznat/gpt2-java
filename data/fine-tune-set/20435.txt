What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Ann S Brunch Casserole Low Carb

Ingredients:
• Swiss cheese
• Cheddar cheese
• Fresh mushrooms
• Yellow onion
• Green pepper
• Butter
• Cubed ham
• Bacon
• Salt
• Pepper
• Eggs
• Milk
• Whole wheat flour

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Butter 9x13 baking dish
3. Combine cheeses, arrange half in the dish
4. Saute onion, green pepper abd fresh mushrooms in 1 / 4 c butter
5. Let most all the moisture cook out of mushrooms
6. Arrange sauted mixture over the cheese, then sprinkle with ham
7. Top with remaining cheese
8. Beat eggs with milk, flour, salt and pepper
9. Pour over gently other ingredients
10. Top with crumbled bacon
11. Bake 45-60 mkinutes until center is firm
12. Cool 5 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
