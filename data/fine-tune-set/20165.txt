What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Beef And Broccoli Stir Fry

Ingredients:
• Soy sauce
• Dry sherry
• Honey
• Garlic
• Orange zest
• Flank steak
• Head broccoli
• Oil
• Cornstarch
• Salt and pepper
• Cooked white rice

Steps:
1. In large bowl mix together the first 5 ingredients
2. Add meat and mix coating well
3. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour preferably 4 hours
4. In large pot of boiling salted water add broccoli and blanch for 2 minutes
5. Drain and rinse under cold water
6. Heat oil in heavy large wok or skillet over high heat
7. Drain meat well but reserve marinade
8. Add cornstarch to reserved marinade and mix until smooth then set aside
9. Add meat to wok and stir fry until almost cooked about 2 minutes
10. Add broccoli and stir fry until tender crisp, about 2 minutes
11. Add reserved marinade mixture and bring to a boil
12. Cook until sauce thickens and coats meat and broccoli, stirring constantly about 2 minutes
13. Season to taste with salt and pepper
14. Serve over rice<|endoftext|>
