What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Crispy Ranch Fries

Ingredients:
• French-fried potatoes
• Ranch dressing mix
• Ground black pepper
• Mayonnaise
• Ketchup
• Sweet pickle relish

Steps:
1. Arrange french fries in a single layer on a greased 15 x 10 inch by 1 inch baking pan
2. Sprinkle with 2 tablespoons dip mix and pepper, then toss fries lightly to coat
3. Bake at 450 degrees for 15 to 20 minutes or until lightly browned
4. While fries are baking, in a small bowl mix together mayonnaise, ketchup, remaining dip mix, and sweet pickle relish
5. Serve with fries<|endoftext|>
