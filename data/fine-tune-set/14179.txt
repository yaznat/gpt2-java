Generate a recipe for tofu.<|endoftext|>Buta Dofu Pork With Tofu

Ingredients:
• Pork steak
• Oil
• Onion
• Garlic
• Fresh ginger
• Green onion
• Firm tofu
• Chicken broth
• Sugar
• Soy sauce
• Cornstarch

Steps:
1. Slice pork into thin strips
2. Heat oil in a heavy frying pan and brown pork well
3. Add onion, garlic and ginger and cook until the onion has softened
4. Add cubed tofu and broth and cook 10 minutes
5. Season with sugar and soy sauce
6. Cook covered, 5 minutes
7. Add cornstarch which has been mixed with a little water and stir gently until thickened
8. Let steam for a minute
9. Add sliced green onion and steam for a minute or so
10. May garnish with chopped green onions just before serving if desired<|endoftext|>
