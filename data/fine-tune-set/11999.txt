Can you give me a pie recipe?<|endoftext|>S W O C Pie Cookies

Ingredients:
• Milk chocolate chips
• Shortening
• Raisins
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Using a double boiler, melt chocolate chips and shortening together
2. Stir until smooth then remove from heat
3. Stir in raisins and almonds
4. Drop by tablespoonfuls onto waxed paper lined sheets
5. Chill until ready to serve<|endoftext|>
