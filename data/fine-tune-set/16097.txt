Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breast
• Chicken broth
• Bell pepper
• Onion
• Garlic clove
• Salsa
• Taco seasoning
• Fat-free refried beans
• Light cheese
• Whole wheat tortillas

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Saute chicken in broth for 2 minutes
3. Add peppers, onions, garlic, salsa and taco seasoning
4. Cook until veggies are soft
5. Place tortillas on a baking sheet and spread refried beans onto half of each trotilla
6. , top with chicken and veggie mixture and shredded cheese
7. Fold each tortilla over and bake at 350 degrees for 10-15 minutes
8. Serve with fat free sour cream and salsa<|endoftext|>
