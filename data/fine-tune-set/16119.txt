Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Onion
• Chorizo sausage
• Red potatoes
• Salsa
• Corn kernel
• Fresh coriander
• Salt
• Monterey jack cheese
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Cook onions& sausage in oil, in a skillet on medium-high heat for 5-7 mins, stirring occasionally
2. Add diced potatoes, cook for 5 mins or until golden, increase time by 7-10 mins if required
3. Stir in salsa, corn, chopped coriander& salt
4. Spoon 1 cup onto half of each tortilla, sprinkle with cheese, fold uncovered half over filling half
5. Bake in oven at 350 until cheese melted or in skillet for a few mins on each side<|endoftext|>
