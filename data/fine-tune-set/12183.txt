What's a good pie recipe?<|endoftext|>Apple Jell O Pie

Ingredients:
• Cooking apples
• Raspberry gelatin powder
• Sugar
• Flour
• Cinnamon
• Margarine
• 9 in. unbaked pastry shells

Steps:
1. Arrange apple slices in pie shell
2. Sprinkle gelatin powder over the apples
3. Mix sugar, flour, cinnamon and margarine until crumbly
4. Spread crumb topping over apples
5. Bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes to one hour<|endoftext|>
