Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Fu Manchu Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Eggs
• Green onions
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Garlic
• Fresh gingerroot
• Red pepper
• Pepperoni
• Frozen peas
• Soy sauce
• Cooked long-grain rice

Steps:
1. Heat a little of the oil in a wok or large skillet
2. Remove from pan and set aside
3. Heat the remaining oil in the pan and stir-fry the chicken, garlic, ginger and pepper over a high heat for 5 minutes
4. Stir in the pepperoni, peas, onions, rice and eggs and continue to cook, stirring, for a further 3-4 minutes until piping hot, season with the soy sauce and serve<|endoftext|>
