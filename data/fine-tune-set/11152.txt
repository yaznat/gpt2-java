What's a recipe for a sandwich?<|endoftext|>Baked Breakfast Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Bread
• Cheese
• Tomatoes
• Bacon
• Onion
• Celery
• Green peppers
• Salt
• Pepper
• Eggs
• Milk
• Mustard

Steps:
1. Trim and butter bread
2. Arrange eight slices buttered side down in 9x13x2 pan
3. Cover with sharp grated cheese
4. Brown bacon, onion, celery, green pepper, salt and pepper together and pour on top of cheese
5. Arrange tomato slices on top of bacon mixture
6. Cover with remaining 8 slices bread
7. Beat together the eggs, milk and mustard and pour over bread
8. Top with more grated cheese
9. Let stand overnight
10. Bake at 350 for 1 hour<|endoftext|>
