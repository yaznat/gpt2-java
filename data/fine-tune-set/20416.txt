Give me a casserole recipe.<|endoftext|>Aloopsie Casserole

Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Kielbasa
• Red onion
• Coleslaw mix
• Sauerkraut
• Tomato soup
• Rice

Steps:
1. Cook rice according to package directions
2. While the rice is cooking, brown the beef and onion together
3. When beef and rice are done mix them together along with the kielbasa sausage
4. In a separate bowl mix the coleslaw and sauerkraut together
5. Place on the bottom of a greased 9x13" baking dish
6. Spread the meat and rice mixture over the coleslaw and top with the tomato soup
7. Cover and bake 1 hour at 350 degrees<|endoftext|>
