Can you give me a pasta recipe?<|endoftext|>Angel Chicken Pasta

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Butter
• Italian salad dressing mix
• White wine
• Condensed golden mushroom soup
• Cream cheese with chives
• Angel hair pasta

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325f
2. In large saucepan melt butter
3. Add dressing mix, wine and soup
4. Mix in cream cheese and stir until smooth
5. Place chicken in baking dish and cover with sauce
6. Bake for 60 minutes
7. Ten minutes before chicken is done cook pasta
8. Serve chicken and sauce over pasta<|endoftext|>
