Ingredients:
• Graham cracker squares
• Pecans
• Milk
• Banana cream instant pudding
• Bananas
• Whipped topping

Steps:
1. Crush the graham crackers into coarse crumbs, mix in the chopped pecans
2. Place the cold milk in a medium size bowl and sprinkle with pudding
3. Beat with a wire whisk for 2 minutes
4. In 4 wine glasses or parfait glasses, spoon in a couple of tablespoons crumb mixture, top with 4 slices of banana and pudding
5. Repeat layers once
6. Cover each glass with plastic wrap and refrigerate until ready to use
7. To serve, top each with a dallop of whipped topping and 1 additional slice of banana

9. Place on paper towels and pat dry<|endoftext|>
