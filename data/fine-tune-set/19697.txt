Ingredients:
• Sweet potatoes
• Olive oil
• Garlic granules
• Parmesan cheese
• Sea salt

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 450
2. Cut up sweet potatoes into wedges, rounds or thin strips
3. In a large bowl add the fries
4. Coat them with oil
5. You may substitute just water if desired
6. Then add the garlic granules, parmesan and sea salt
7. Spay a cookie sheet with pam or oil and lay fries out on top
8. Bake for 10 mins then turn them over
9. Put back in the oven for 10 more minutes
10. If you have thicker fries you may need an additional 5-10 minutes baking time<|endoftext|>
