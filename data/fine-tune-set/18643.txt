Ingredients:
• Milk
• Eggs
• Vanilla extract
• Brown sugar
• White bread cubes
• Raisins
• Rum
• Apples
• Cinnamon
• Butter

Steps:
1. In a large bowl, combine milk, eggs, vanilla and 1 / 2 cup of the brown sugar
2. Stir in bread cubes
3. Let stand for at least 30 minutes
4. Stirring occasionally
5. In a small bowl, combine raisins and rum or apple juice
6. Set aside
7. In another bowl, toss together apples, cinnamon and remaining brown sugar
8. Add raisin and apple mixtures to the softened bread, mixing well
9. Spread evenly in a greased 8x8x2 baking pan
10. Drizzle with the melted butter
11. Bake in a 375 degree fahrenheit oven for 40 to 50 minutes or until golden brown and apples are tender
12. Serve warm or cold<|endoftext|>
