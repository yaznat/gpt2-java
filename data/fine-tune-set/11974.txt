Ingredients:
• Cooked brown rice
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Green pepper
• Cooked chicken
• Flour
• Curry powder
• Salt
• Water
• Lemon juice
• Tomato sauce
• Garlic

Steps:
1. Cook onion and green pepper in oil until tender
2. Add chicken
3. Saut briefly
4. In separate bowl mix flour, curry powder and salt
5. Slowly stir in water and lemon juice to make paste
6. Add tomato sauce and garlic and pour mixture over chicken mixture
7. Mix
8. To freeze, freeze in ziploc plastic bag
9. Freeze rice in separate ziploc bag
10. To serve, thaw
11. Place rice in casserole dish
12. Place chicken mixture over top of rice
13. Heat through on 350, about 30 min<|endoftext|>
