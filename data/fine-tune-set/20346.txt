Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Frozen vegetables
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Condensed cream of mushroom & garlic soup
• Salt & pepper
• Cooking oil
• Cornbread mix

Steps:
1. Brown onion and meat in the oil, drain any excess moisture
2. Remove from heat and add both cans of soup and the veggies of your choice and stir
3. Salt and pepper to taste
4. Pour mixture into a greased 9x9 inch baking pan
5. Spoon the prepared cornbread mix over the top and spread even
6. Bake in a preheated 350 degree oven for 35-45 minutes, or until cornbread is done<|endoftext|>
