Ingredients:
• Water
• Sugar
• Soda crackers
• Margarine
• Cream of tartar
• Cinnamon
• Nutmeg
• Pastry for double-crust pie

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Line pie tin with bottom crust
3. Combine the rest of the ingredients and place in crust
4. Cover with top crust, crimp edges attractively and pierce evenly all over with a fork to release the steam
