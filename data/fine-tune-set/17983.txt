Please give me a recipe for brownies.<|endoftext|>Blond Zucchini Brownies

Ingredients:
• Margarine
• Hot water
• Brown sugar
• Vanilla
• Zucchini
• Nuts
• Flour
• Baking powder
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Egg
• Butterscotch chips

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
2. Use the hot water to melt the margarine
3. Add brown sugar & beat well
4. Cool
5. Add egg and vanilla beat well
6. Mix dry ingredients together and add to sugar mixture
7. Stir in zucchini & nuts
8. Pour mixture into greased & floured 9 x 9 inch pan, spinkle with butterscotch chips and bake 20-25 minutes<|endoftext|>
