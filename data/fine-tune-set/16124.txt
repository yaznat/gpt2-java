What's a good quesadilla recipe?<|endoftext|>Colourful Quesadillas

Ingredients:
• Tortillas
• Miracle whip light
• Sun-dried tomato
• Artichoke heart
• Shallot
• Black beans
• Garlic powder
• Chili pepper flakes
• Spinach
• Gouda cheese
• Part-skim mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Spread miracle whip on one half of the tortilla, on that same half sprinkle with sundried tomatoes
2. Gently squeeze most of the water from the artichoke hearts
3. Cut artichoke hearts in half and cut each half into 4 pieces
4. Sprinkle artichoke heart pieces, shallots, black beans, garlic powder, and pepper flakes on same half of the tortilla
5. Top with shredded spinach
6. Grate cheese and sprinkle cheese on top of spinach
7. Fold tortilla over so fillings are covered
8. On medium heat, place quesadilla in a non stick frying pan and heat till golden brown, flip quesadilla onto the other side and heat until golden brown and cheese is melted
9. Place quesadilla on a plate and cut into 4 wedges
10. Enjoy with sour cream and salsa
11. If doubling, feel free to cover the whole surface of one tortilla with fillings and place the second tortilla over top
12. Cut into 8 wedges for 2 servings<|endoftext|>
