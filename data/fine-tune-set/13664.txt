Give me a recipe for pork.<|endoftext|>A Little Of Everything Pork Chops

Ingredients:
• Pork chops
• Onion
• Celery ribs
• Green pepper
• Stewed tomatoes
• Ketchup
• Cider vinegar
• Brown sugar
• Worcestershire sauce
• Lemon juice
• Beef bouillon cube
• Cornstarch
• Water

Steps:
1. Place pork chops in crock pot
2. Add remaining ingredients, except the water and cornstarch
3. Cover and cook on low for 5 to 6 hours
4. 30 minutes before serving, dissolve cornstarch in water and stir into crock pot
5. Cover and cook on high for 30 minutes until sauce is slightly thickened<|endoftext|>
