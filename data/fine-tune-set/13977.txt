What's a good lamb recipe?<|endoftext|>Bucatini Lamb Eggplant Bucatini Melanzane Ed Agnello

Ingredients:
• Eggplant
• Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
• Milk
• Ground lamb
• Egg
• Salt
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Italian seasoning
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Mushroom marinara sauce
• Oregano
• Romano cheese
• Bucatini pasta
• Flat leaf parsley

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 350f
2. Line a baking sheet with foil or parchment paper
3. Bring a large pot of water to a boil
4. Sprinkle eggplant with salt
5. Allow to drain in a colander for 30 minutes
6. Combine milk and breadcrumbs in a bowl
7. Allow to rest for 10 minutes
8. Mix lamb, egg, breadcrumbs, salt, black ground pepper, crushed red pepper flakes, italian seasoning, oregano, and parsley in a large bowl
9. Roll into 1 inch balls
10. Place on prepared baking sheet
11. Bake for 15-20 minutes and set aside
12. Heat olive oil in a large skillet
13. Add eggplant, cook until tender
14. Add garlic, cook 1 minute
15. Add sauce and season with salt and pepper
16. Add meatballs to sauce
17. Simmer 10-15 minutes
18. Cook pasta according to package directions and drain
19. Add pasta to sauce
20. Mix well
21. Sprinkle with grated cheese before serving<|endoftext|>
