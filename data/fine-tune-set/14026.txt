Ingredients:
• Onion
• Olive oil
• Shoulder lamb chops
• Salt
• Pepper
• Gingerroot
• Ground cumin
• Garlic cloves
• Cinnamon stick
• Cilantro
• Prune
• Honey
• Lime juice
• Sesame seeds

Steps:
1. In large frying pan, saute onion in olive oil until limp
2. Push to sides of pan
3. In same pan, brown chops well on both sides
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Add ginger, cumin, garlic and cinnamon
6. Saute 1 minute
7. Transfer to crock pot
8. Deglaze pan with 2 tablespoons water
9. Add to pot
10. Scatter cilantro and prunes over lamb chops
11. Cover
12. Cook on low for 6 hours
13. Stir in honey and lime juice
14. Heat to serving temperature
15. Sprinkle with sesame seeds<|endoftext|>
