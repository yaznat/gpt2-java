Please give me a recipe for a wrap.<|endoftext|>Beef And Mozzarella Wraps

Ingredients:
• Lean ground meat
• Wonton wrappers
• Pam cooking spray
• Onion
• Celery
• Garlic salt
• White pepper
• Dry mustard
• Olive oil
• Tomato sauce
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
• Mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Spray pam in two 12 cup muffin pans
2. Place one wonton wrappers in each cup
3. Bake 375 for 7 minutes
4. Leave in muffin pans to cool
5. In frying pan, add ground meat, onions, celery, garlic salt, pepper and dry mustard
6. Mix well
7. Add oil, if meat is lean or it is ground turkey
8. Lightly brown
9. Drain
10. Add tomato sauce and sugar
11. Mix
12. Add two beaten eggs and bread crumbs
13. Mix well
14. Distribute evenly in 24 muffin cups
15. Bake 375 20 minutes
16. Last 5 minutes of cooking, sprinkle mozzarella cheese on top of cups<|endoftext|>
