Ingredients:
• Butter
• Sugar
• Eggs
• Cake flour
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Sour cream
• Almond extract
• Lemon extract
• Orange extract
• Vanilla extract
• Amaretto
• Orange marmalade
• Apricot preserves
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Beat butter at the medium speed of an electric mixer about 2 minutes or until it is soft and creamy
2. Gradually add the sugar, beating at medium speed for 5 to 7 minutes
3. Add the eggs, one at a time, beating between each addition
4. Beat just until the yellow disappears
5. Combine the flour, baking soda and salt
6. Add to the butter mixture alternately with the sour cream, beginning and ending with the flour mixture
7. Mix at low speed just until blended after each addition
8. Stir in flavorings and 1 / 2 cup amaretto
9. Pour the batter into a greased and floured 12-cup bundt pan
10. Bake at 325 degrees for 1 hour and 15 minutes or until a wooden pick inserted into the center comes out clean
11. Cool in a pan on a wire rack for 10 to 15 minutes
12. Remove from the pan, and let cool completely on the wire rack
13. Combine the remaining 1 / 4 cup of amaretto, marmalade, and preserves in a small saucepan
14. Cook over medium heat until marmalade and preserves melt, stirring frequently
15. Drizzle over the cake
16. Sprinkle with the almonds<|endoftext|>
