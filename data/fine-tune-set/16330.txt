Ingredients:
• Whole mushroom
• Bacon
• Barbecue sauce

Steps:
1. Soak bamboo skewers 30 minutes
2. Cut bacon strips in half--they'll be just long enough to wrap around the mushroom one time
3. Wrap each mushroom with a bacon strip and thread on skewer
4. Grill on medium heat till bacon is done, basting with barbecue sauce
5. This usually takes around 10-15 minutes
6. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
