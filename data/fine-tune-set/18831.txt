Give me a custard recipe.<|endoftext|>Baked Egg Custard

Ingredients:
• Eggs
• Sugar
• Salt
• Nutmeg
• Vanilla
• Milk

Steps:
1. Bring milk just to a boil and add beaten eggs and remaining ingredients
2. Whisk well
3. Pour into a buttered casserole dish and bake in a 350 preheated oven for 30 minutes
4. Enjoy<|endoftext|>
