What's a good bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Amish Cornbread

Ingredients:
• Amish batter
• Eggs
• Milk
• Sugar
• Flour
• Cornmeal
• Oil
• Baking soda
• Salt
• Baking powder

Steps:
1. Combine all above in large bowl and beat at medium speed for 2 minutes
2. Add: oil, baking soda, salt, and baking powder mix well and pour in well greased 9" pan
3. Bake at 425f 25-30 minutes<|endoftext|>
