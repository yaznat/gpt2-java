Can you give me a ice cream recipe?<|endoftext|>Breyers Ice Cream Cherry Cheesecake

Ingredients:
• Graham cracker crumbs
• I can't believe it's not butter spread
• Sugar
• Cream cheese
• Vanilla extract
• Vanilla ice cream
• Cherry pie filling

Steps:
1. In medium bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs, i can't believe it's not butter spread and 3 tablespoons sugar
2. Press into 9-inch springform pan
3. Freeze 15 minutes
4. Meanwhile, in large bowl with electric mixer beat cream cheese, remaining 2 / 3 cup sugar and vanilla until smooth
5. Set aside
6. Into springform pan, spoon breyers ice cream, pressing to form an even layer
7. Evenly spread on cream cheese mixture
8. Cover and freeze 4 hours or overnight
9. Freeze serving platter 30 minutes before serving
10. Top cheesecake with cherry pie filling
11. Dip knife or metal spatula in hot water, then wipe dry and run around edge of cake to loosen
12. Remove ring from pan
13. On chilled serving platter, arrange cake<|endoftext|>
