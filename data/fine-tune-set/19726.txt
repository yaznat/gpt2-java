What's a recipe for fries?<|endoftext|>Burger N Fries Pot Pie

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• All-purpose flour
• Diced tomatoes
• Shredded cheddar cheese
• French-fried potatoes

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 400
2. In a large nonstick skillet, cook beef and onion over med-high heat for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until beef is thoroughly cooked
3. Drain very well
4. Sprinkle flour over beef mixture
5. Cook 1 minute, stirring constantly
6. Stir in tomatoes
7. Heat to boiling
8. Remove from heat
9. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper
10. In an ungreased 1 1 / 2 quart casserole, spread beef mixture
11. Sprinkle with cheese
12. Arrange frozen potatoes evenly in single layer on top
13. Bake, uncovered, about 20 minutes or until potatoes are golden brown
14. Let stand 5 minutes before serving<|endoftext|>
