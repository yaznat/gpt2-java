Ingredients:
• Angel hair pasta
• Butter
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Chicken broth
• Cherry tomatoes
• Milk
• Fresh lemon juice
• Capers
• Baby spinach
• Nutmeg
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Cornstarch
• Shrimp
• Fresh mozzarella cheese
• Kosher salt
• Fresh parsley

Steps:
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Drain and keep warm
3. In a large skillet heat butter and olive oil over medium heat
4. Add garlic and stir for about a minute so as not to brown
5. Stir in broth, tomatoes, milk, lemon juice, capers, and spinach
6. Season with nutmeg and black pepper
7. Cook for 10 minutes or so, then sprinkle with cornstarch and stir
8. Break up tomatoes by smooshing them, if desired
9. Stir in shrimp and heat through
10. Taste and adjust seasonings including a bit of salt, if needed
11. Add mozzarella to pasta and toss a bit
12. Pour sauce over pasta and garnish with parsley<|endoftext|>
