Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Diced tomatoes
• Chili powder
• Black beans
• Corn
• Fresh cilantro

Steps:
1. Dice chicken into 1 / 2 inch chunks
2. Combine chicken, undrained tomatoes, and chili powder in a mdeium saucepan
3. Cook over medium heat, about 5 minutes or until chicken is done and sauce is slightly thickened
4. Add remaining ingredients and simmer 5 to 7 minutes or until heated through
5. Garnish with shredded cheese, diced avocados, radishes, or green onions, if desired
6. Makes 4-1 cup servings<|endoftext|>
