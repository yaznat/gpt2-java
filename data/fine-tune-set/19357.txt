Ingredients:
• Flour
• Sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Butter
• Eggs
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Nutmeg
• Cinnamon

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Spray pan lightly with cooking oil
3. In bowl mix together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt
4. Add butter, eggs, milk, vanilla, nutmeg, and cinnamon
5. Beat until well blended
6. Fill each donut hole 2 / 3 full
7. Bake 8 minutes or until top springs back when lightly touched
8. Cool
9. Remove from pan and dip into glaze
10. Decorate with sprinkles, nuts, shaved chocolate or coconut
11. Donuts may also be dipped into cinnamon and sugar instead of the glaze
12. When the pan has cooled, wipe clean with cloth or paper towel and repeat process
13. Yields approximately 36 mini donuts
14. Glaze: 1 cup powdered sugar mixed with 2 tblsp
