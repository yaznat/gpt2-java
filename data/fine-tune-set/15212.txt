What's a good pizza recipe?<|endoftext|>Biancoverde Greens On White Pizza

Ingredients:
• Prepared pizza crust
• Garlic paste
• Mozzarella cheese
• Asiago cheese
• Part-skim ricotta cheese
• White truffle oil
• Arugula
• Olive oil
• Salt
• Fresh ground pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
2. Spread the pizza crust with the garlic paste, then top with the mozzarella and asiago, and spoon dollops of the ricotta over all
3. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until cheese is melted
4. Remove from the oven
5. Drizzle the arugula lightly with olive oil and toss, then place immediately on top of pizza
6. Return to the oven for one minute only, just for the heat to wilt the arugula
7. Drizzle the truffle oil over the top and season with salt and pepper
8. Slice and serve immediately<|endoftext|>
