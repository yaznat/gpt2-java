Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Chicken stock
• Brown sugar
• Tomato sauce
• Onion
• Minced garlic cloves
• Worcestershire sauce
• Liquid smoke
• Hot sauce
• Cumin
• Seasoning
• Flour
• Hamburger buns

Steps:
1. Heat stock in shallow pan to a low simmer
2. Add chicken to stock
3. Poach chicken for 5 minutes each side
4. Remove chicken to plate to cool
5. Reserve stock for sauce
6. In a large bowl mix together all sauce ingredients, except the flour
7. Add mixed ingredients to simmering stock, reserved from poaching the chicken
8. Let liquid reduce for 5 minutes
9. Cut cooled chicken into strips
10. Add chicken into the simmering liquid
11. Shred chicken until it looks like pulled pork
12. Add the tbsp of flour
13. Let cook until sauce thickens
14. Serve on a lightly toasted onion roll and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
