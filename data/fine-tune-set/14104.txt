What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Amber S Japan Style Tofu Meal

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Tamari
• Rice vinegar
• Fresh ginger
• Water
• Tomato juice
• Vegetable oil
• Quinoa
• Green beans
• Miso
• Cornstarch
• Scallion
• Alfalfa sprouts

Steps:
1. Slice the tofu into 4 horizontal slabs
2. Gently press them between a tea towel to remove some of their moisture
3. Then slice into strips
4. Stir together the marinade ingredients in a shallow dish
5. Add the tofu strips, and flip gently to coat
6. Let marinate for 20 minutes- 1 hour
7. Meanwhile, cook your quinoa, and let it rest, partially covered, off the heat when it's done
8. When the quinoa is cooked, you can also start steaming the beans and preparing the tofu:
9. Whisk everything for the sauce, except the miso, in a small saucepan
10. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat
11. Drain any extra marinade from the tofu strips, transfer them to the pan and fry till golden-brown
12. Set aside
13. Add the reserved marinade to the pan with the sauce ingredients and place it over medium heat
14. Quickly bring to a boil, whisking well to remove lumps of cornstarch
15. After it cooks for two minutes, remove from heat and stir in the miso
16. To serve, arrange 4 tofu strips on a bed of quinoa on each plate
17. Spoon sauce over top, and sprinkle with scallion and sprouts
18. Divvy up the beans on the side, and serve
19. Hope you enjoy!<|endoftext|>
