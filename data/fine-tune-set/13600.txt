Ingredients:
• Beef sirloin
• Onion
• Fresh parsley
• Paprika
• Salt
• Ground cumin
• Fresh ground white pepper
• Vegetable oil

Steps:
1. Combine the onion, parsley, paprika, 1 teaspoons salt, 1 / 2 teaspoons cumin, the white pepper and oil in a large baking dish
2. Add the beef and toss to thoroughly to coat
3. Cover and let marinate, in refrigerator, for at least 2 hours but preferably 8
4. Preheat grill to high
5. Arrange kebabs on hot grate and grill, turning once with tongs, until the meat is cooked to taste, 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium
6. Moroccans tend to eat their beef well done
7. To serve, place 1 tablespoons each of salt and cumin, in serparate tiny bowls on table, along with optional shallot relish, harissa and bread
8. Let each person season their kebabs with the salt and cumin<|endoftext|>
