Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Celery ribs
• Carrots
• Onions
• Green beans
• Garlic cloves
• Dried basil
• Smoked paprika
• Salt
• Pepper
• Beef stock
• Tomatoes
• Beef
• All-purpose flour

Steps:
1. Place the diced meat and flour in a plastic bag
2. Toss the meat to coat thoroughly in the flour
3. Add in extra flour if required
4. Set aside
5. Prepare vegetables and place in the bottom of the crock pot
6. Add herbs, garlic, salt and pepper to the veges
7. Add the meat to the crock pot
8. Add the can of tomatoes
9. If using peeled tomatoes-roughly chop them first
10. Add the stock / wine / water
11. Cook on high setting for approx 4 hours or low setting for about 8 hours
12. Serve with toast, noodles or rice
13. Before serving if the gravy is not thick enough add some cornflour or arrowroot mixed with a small amount of water to the casserole and cook until thickened<|endoftext|>
