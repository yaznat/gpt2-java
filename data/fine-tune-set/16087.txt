Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Chicken
• Bacon
• Ranch dressing
• Red pepper flakes
• Monterey jack cheese

Steps:
1. Spread 2 tbsp ranch dressing on 2 tortillas
2. Put cheese, chicken, and bacon on top of the ranch
3. Sprinkle red pepper flakes on each one
4. Top with remaining tortilla
5. Heat pan
6. Spray with pam
7. Place quesadilla in pan
8. Cook each side 1-2 minutes until cheese melts and tortilla in slightly browned<|endoftext|>
