What's a good chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Atomic Canuck Chili

Ingredients:
• Lean ground beef
• Onion
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Garlic powder
• Seasoned pepper
• Mushroom pieces
• Baked beans
• Kidney beans
• Tomato paste
• White sugar
• Carrots
• Celery
• Green bell pepper
• Red bell pepper
• Jalapeno peppers
• Beer
• Barbecue sauce
• Hot sauce

Steps:
1. In a large skillet over medium heat, brown ground beef together with onion, crushed red pepper, garlic powder, and seasoned pepper
2. Drain the fat, and place the mixture into a slow cooker
3. In the slow cooker, stir together beef mixture, mushrooms, baked beans, kidney beans with liquid, tomato paste, sugar, carrots, celery, peppers, beer, and barbeque sauce
4. Season with hot sauce and more crushed red pepper
5. Cover and cook on low for 4 to 5 hours<|endoftext|>
