Ingredients:
• Potatoes
• Bacon
• Cheddar cheese
• Green onions
• Oil
• Salt
• Sour cream

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375f
2. Cook the diced bacon until crispy, remove from bacon fat and drain on paper towel
3. Forget everything you were ever taught about peeling potatoes, and peel them with a knife leaving a good bit of potato on the skin
4. Retain center of potatoes for another recipe, or slice them into wedges and cook in the same manner as the potato skins
5. Deep fry the potato skins until crispy and golden
6. Drain thoroughly and salt to taste
7. Spread the potato skins evenly on a pizza pan
8. Sprinkle evenly with the green onions, cheese and bacon
9. Bake for 10-15 minutes until hot and cheddar is melted
10. Serve hot with sour cream for dipping<|endoftext|>
