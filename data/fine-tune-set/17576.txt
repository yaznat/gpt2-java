Ingredients:
• Brewed coffee
• Molasses
• Dark rum
• Whipped cream
• Nutmeg

Steps:
1. Combine the coffee and molasses in a saucepan
2. Heat, stirring, until molasses is dissolved and coffee is very hot
3. Pour 1 tablespoon of rum into each of 6 mugs
4. Add the coffee
5. Top with whipped cream and sprinkle with nutmeg
6. Do not stir before drinking<|endoftext|>
