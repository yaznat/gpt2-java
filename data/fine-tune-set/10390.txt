What's a good cookie recipe?<|endoftext|>Amaretto Cookies No Bake

Ingredients:
• Vanilla wafers
• Granulated sugar
• Cocoa
• White karo
• Almonds
• Amaretto liqueur

Steps:
1. Mix the first six ingredients together until well blended
2. Form dough into 1 / 2" balls and roll in colored granulated sugar
3. Place in a metal cookie tin on either parchment or waxed paper to ferment for about 3 days
4. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
