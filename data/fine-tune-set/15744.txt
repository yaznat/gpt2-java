What's a recipe for a taco?<|endoftext|>Chipotle Pork Soft Tacos With Pineapple Salsa

Ingredients:
• Pineapple
• Apple
• Shallot
• Cilantro
• Fresh lime juice
• Ground cumin
• Salt
• Canola oil
• Yellow onion
• Garlic cloves
• Pork tenderloin
• Reduced-sodium fat-free chicken broth
• Cider vinegar
• Dried oregano
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Cherry tomatoes
• Chipotle chiles
• Corn tortillas

Steps:
1. To prepare salsa, combine the first 7 ingredients in a medium bowl
2. Stir until well blended
3. Cover and chill
4. To prepare tacos, heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat
5. Add onion to pan
6. Cook 2 minutes or until tender
7. Add garlic
8. Cook 30 seconds
9. Add pork to pan
10. Cook 4 minutes or until pork loses its pink color, stirring occasionally
11. Stir in broth and next 7 ingredients
12. Cover, reduce heat, and simmer 10 minutes
13. Uncover and simmer 10 minutes or until liquid is nearly evaporated
14. Warm tortillas according to package directions
15. Serve pork mixture with tortillas and salsa<|endoftext|>
