Ingredients:
• Balsamic vinegar
• Olive oil
• Onion
• Lemons
• Fresh rosemary
• Garlic cloves
• Rib eye steaks
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. In a 1-gallon food storage bag, mix first 6 ingredients
2. Pierce steaks with fork and add to marinade
3. Let sit 15 minutes
4. Meanwhile, heat large nonstick skillet
5. Remove steaks from marinade
6. Season with salt and pepper
7. Add to skillet and cook 4-5 minutes per side for medium-rare
8. Remove to a plate
9. Add 3 tablespoons water to the marinade
10. Pour into skillet along with drippings on the plate from steak
11. Boil until reduced to 3 tablespoons, about 2 minutes
12. Remove lemon and garlic from sauce
13. Cut steaks into serving pieces and top with sauce<|endoftext|>
