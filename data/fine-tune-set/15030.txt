Can you give me a fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Shrimp And Egg Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Green onions
• White onion
• Sesame oil
• Chinese five spice powder
• Medium shrimp
• Garlic clove
• Mushrooms
• Egg
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Cooked brown rice
• Bean sprouts
• Carrot
• Red pepper

Steps:
1. If using frozen shrimp, defrost under cold running water, then clean, de-vein, and remove tails of shrimp
2. If using cooked shrimp or crab, add it when the veggies are almost done
3. Heat 1 tablespoon of sesame oil in wok on medium high heat
4. When oil is hot, add shrimp and stir-fry until pink
5. Then remove them from wok
6. Add garlic and white onion to wok, and after they are soft add the other veggies
7. You can really use just about any veggie with this
8. When veggies are mostly done, move them to the sides of the wok, creating a hole of bare pan on the bottom
9. Pour in beaten egg
10. Let it scramble a bit and break it into smaller pieces
11. Add rice to the wok, and return shrimp to the wok
12. Add the green onions, and the five spice powder and soy sauce to taste
13. Add 1 tablespoon more oil to the rice
14. Stir to fry the rice and combine the seasonings
15. Add vegetable oil if you need more oil<|endoftext|>
