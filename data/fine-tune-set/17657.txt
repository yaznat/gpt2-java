Please give me a recipe for muffins.<|endoftext|>Another Low Calorie Bran Muffin Recipe

Ingredients:
• Wheat bran
• Whole wheat flour
• Skim milk
• Vinegar
• Baking soda
• Baking powder
• Vanilla extract
• Salt
• Splenda granular
• Molasses
• Unsweetened applesauce
• Egg
• Raisins

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees f
2. Spray muffin cups with pam
3. Add skim milk to vinegar to make 1 cup soured milk and set aside for 5 minutes
4. Add soured milk to bran
5. Let stand for 10 minutes
6. Mix together applesauce, egg, splenda, molasses and vanilla and then add to milk / bran mixture
7. Mix together flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt
8. Add flour mixture to bran / milk mixture, and stir just until blended
9. Fold in raisins and spoon batter into muffin tins
10. Bake for 17 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of a muffin comes out clean
11. Cool in pan for 5 minutes, then remove from pan and enjoy!<|endoftext|>
