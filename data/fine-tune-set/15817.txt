What's a good burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Blt Burritos

Ingredients:
• Lettuce
• Fresh tomatoes
• Bacon
• Mayonnaise
• Pepper
• Flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Combine the first five ingredients and mix well
2. Spoon down the center of each tortilla, then fold the ends and side over the filling and roll up
3. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
