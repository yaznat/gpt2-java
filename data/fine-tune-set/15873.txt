Can you give me a burrito recipe?<|endoftext|>Cheese And Hot Dog Burritos

Ingredients:
• Flour tortillas
• Hot dogs
• Cheddar cheese
• Ketchup

Steps:
1. Heat wieners as directed on package
2. Heat tortillas
3. Place 1 hot wiener on lower third of each tortilla
4. Sprinkle each wiener with 1 / 4 cup of the cheese, and about 1 tbs ketchup or taco sauce
5. Fold in sides and roll up<|endoftext|>
