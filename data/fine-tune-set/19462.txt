Give me a recipe for potatoes.<|endoftext|>5 Minute Potato

Ingredients:
• Red potatoes
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic
• Butter
• Cheese
• Salt
• Pepper

Steps:
1. Wash and cube the potato
2. Put the extra virgin olive oil, garlic, salt and pepper on top and mix together well, so that each peace gets an even amount
3. Microwave for 1 minute and 15 seconds
4. Add butter and cheese
5. Microwave for another 15 seconds<|endoftext|>
