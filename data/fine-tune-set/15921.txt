Ingredients:
• 6-inch flour tortillas
• Beef
• Refried beans
• Salsa
• Cheese

Steps:
1. Preheat sandwich maker
2. Lightly mist wells with non-stick cooking spray
3. Place a tortilla in the sandwich maker, top with 1 tbsp
4. Each: meat, beans, salsa, and cheese
5. Top with remaining tortilla
6. Close and cook for about 3 minutes
7. Serve with a dollop of sour cream and some shredded lettuce<|endoftext|>
