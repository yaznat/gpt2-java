Ingredients:
• Chicken stock
• Low sodium soy sauce
• Sherry wine
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Distilled white vinegar
• Sesame oil
• Peanut oil
• Fresh ginger
• Crushed red pepper flakes

Steps:
1. In a small bowl, combine the stock with the soy sauce, sherry, sugar, cornstarch slurry, vinegar and sesame oil
2. Stir to dissolve the sugar
3. In a medium saucepan, heat the peanut oil until shimmering
4. Add the ginger and crushed red pepper and cook over high heat, stirring, until fragrant and golden
5. Add the stock mixture and boil over high heat until thickened and glossy, about 1 minute<|endoftext|>
