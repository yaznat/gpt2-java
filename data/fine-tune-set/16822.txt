Give me a recipe for a milkshake.<|endoftext|>Peach And Orange Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Peach yogurt
• Orange juice
• Peaches
• Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Pour milk, yogurt & orange juice into a blender & process gently until combined
2. Add peach slices & process until smooth
3. Pour into 2 tall glasses & enjoy!<|endoftext|>
