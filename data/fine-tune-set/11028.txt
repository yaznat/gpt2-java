Give me a sandwich recipe.<|endoftext|>Amazing Marinated Tofu Sandwich

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Soy sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Lime, juice of
• Honey
• Firm tofu
• Whole wheat bread
• Cheddar cheese
• Mayonnaise
• Mustard
• Lettuce
• Fresh basil
• Onion

Steps:
1. To make the marinade, combine the oil, soy sauce, garlic, lime juice, and honey
2. Mix it with a fork until combined
3. Place the tofu in the marinade until ready to cook
4. Cook the tofu in a pan with a little of the marinade, no oil is necessary as it is already in the marinade
5. Cook till golden brown
6. While the tofu is cooking, lightly toast the bread
7. Then spread the mayonnaise on one slice and the mustard on the other
8. When the tofu is cooked, place it on one slice of bread, put the cheddar on top, and place it bread-side down in the pan
9. Place the lid on the pan to let the cheese melt
10. When the cheese has melted, remove the sandwich, place any veggies on top of the cheese, and place the last piece of bread on top
11. Serve warm!<|endoftext|>
