Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Red onion
• Red pepper
• Garlic cloves
• Sea salt
• Sweet potato
• Lime
• Diced tomatoes
• Black beans
• Jalapeno pepper
• Cumin
• Chili powder
• Cocoa powder
• Cilantro leaf

Steps:
1. Warm the oil in a large pan over medium heat and add the onion, red pepper, garlic, and salt
2. Saute until soft, about 4 minutes
3. Add the sweet potato and lime zest, cook 10-15 minutes more, continuing to stir occasionally
4. Add the tomatoes, black beans, jalapeno, lime juice, cumin, chili powder, and cocoa
5. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook for 10 minutes
6. Or omit the oil, throw everything in a crock pot, and cook on high for 6 hours
7. Serve over brown rice with lime wedges and cilantro<|endoftext|>
