What's a good beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Beans With Ground Beef

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Bell pepper
• Ground beef
• Fajita seasoning mix
• Pepper
• Bacon
• Pork & beans
• Ranch style beans
• Masterpiece original barbecue sauce
• Picante sauce
• Ketchup
• Brown sugar
• Cilantro

Steps:
1. Saute onion and bell pepper in butter
2. In separate pan brown ground beef
3. Season with fajita seasoning and pepper
4. Drain
5. Put in crock pot or large pan and add remainder of ingredients
6. Stir
7. Cook for two hours in the crock pot or large pan<|endoftext|>
