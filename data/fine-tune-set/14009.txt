What's a recipe for lamb?<|endoftext|>Croatian Bbq Lamb Liver

Ingredients:
• Lamb liver
• Milk
• Salt
• Pepper
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove

Steps:
1. Cut liver into 1 cm thick stakes, removing small veins
2. Soak liver in milk for 1 hour
3. Drain the milk dry with paper towel, soak in oil and put on fire
4. Livers are done when they are rose inside
5. Salt after otherwise livers will be hard
6. Serve with fresh cut onion rings
7. At the end, just pour over some olive oil over the livers and add some minced garlic<|endoftext|>
