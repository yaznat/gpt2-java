Ingredients:
• Ground beef
• Onion
• Ground cumin
• Chili powder
• Salt
• Pepper
• Refried beans
• Cheddar cheese
• Picante sauce
• Burrito-size flour tortillas

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef and 1 / 2 of the onion along with cumin, chili powder, salt and pepper
2. Drain
3. Add refried beans to meat and mix well
4. Warm the tortillas for a few seconds in the microwave to make them more flexible
5. Spread approximately 2 / 3 c meat mixture on each tortilla
6. Top each with remaining onion, 1 / 4 c cheese and 2-3 t picante sauce
7. Roll burrito style and wrap in foil and freeze
8. To heat, warm in 350 degree oven for 15 minutes if thawed and 25-30 minutes if frozen<|endoftext|>
