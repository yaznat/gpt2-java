What's a recipe for coffee?<|endoftext|>Any Fruit Coffee Cake

Ingredients:
• Fruit
• Lemon juice
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• All-purpose flour
• Baking powder
• Ground cinnamon
• Ground mace
• Margarine
• Egg
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Walnuts

Steps:
1. For the first ingredient: you may use raspberries or 2 cups chopped, peeled apples, apricots, peaches, pineapple, or blueberries
2. In a medium saucepan combine fruit and 1 / 2 cup water
3. Simmer, covered, for 5 minutes or till fruit juice is tender
4. Stir in lemon juice
5. Mix the 1 / 2 cup sugar and cornstarch
6. Stir into fruit mixture
7. Cook and stir till thickened
8. Cool
9. Mixing bowl: stir together 1 1 / 2 cups flour, 1 / 2 cup sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, mace, and 1 / 4 teaspoon salt
10. Cut in 1 / 2 cup margarine or butter till mixture resembles fine crumbs
11. Combine egg, milk and vanilla
12. Add to flour mixture, mixing till blended
13. Spread half of the batter in a greased 9x9x2 inch baking pan
14. Spread the cooked fruit over batter
15. Spread remaining batter over fruit
16. Combine the 1 / 4 cup sugar and 1 / 4 cup flour, cut in the 2 tablespoons margarine till mixture resembles course crumbs
17. Stir in nuts
18. Sprinkle nut mixture over batter in pan
19. Bake in a 350f oven for 40 to 45 minutes or till done
20. Cool<|endoftext|>
