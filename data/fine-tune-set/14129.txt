What's a good tofu recipe?<|endoftext|>Baked Italian Style Tofu Toaster Oven

Ingredients:
• Firm tofu
• Egg
• Italian style breadcrumbs
• Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat the toaster oven to 350'f
2. Line your toaster oven pan with tin foil and spray generously with coconut oil
3. I use coconut because it doesn't go rancid like other oils when they are heated
4. Open the tofu packages and drain
5. Break the egg in a shallow dish and beat it until combined
6. Put the bread crumbs in a shallow dish
7. Cut each of the tofu blocks in half the long way
8. This will give you 4 blocks total
9. If you like it thinner cut them into 3 so you have 6 pieces
10. Dip in the egg, then the bread crumbs
11. Salt and pepper the tops
12. Place on the prepared pan and spray each piece with oil generously
13. Bake for 25-30 minutes
14. Check them periodically to make sure they aren't burning
15. Serve with your favourite spaghetti sauce and pasta
16. You can even make them into a sandwich with toppings
17. This is very versatile
18. Bon appetit!<|endoftext|>
