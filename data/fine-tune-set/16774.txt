What's a recipe for a milkshake?<|endoftext|>Extreme Vanilla Malted Milkshake

Ingredients:
• Milk
• Vanilla
• Malted milk balls
• Vanilla ice cream
• Confectioners' sugar

Steps:
1. Place the milk, vanilla and malted milk balls in the blender and blend until combined
2. Add in the ice cream and blend until smooth and thickened
3. If desired add in some confectioners sugar to sweeten<|endoftext|>
