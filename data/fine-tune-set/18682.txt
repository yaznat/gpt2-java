Ingredients:
• Sugar
• Flour
• Cocoa
• Eggs
• Butter
• Vanilla
• Nuts

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
2. Combine sugar, flour and cocoa in bowl
3. Stir in eggs, melted butter, vanilla and nuts
4. Place large pan of hot water in oven
5. Pour batter in buttered 9" square cake pan
6. Place in larger pan, water should come halfway up the side
7. Bake 1 hour, 20 minutes
8. Pudding is done when surface is lightly cracked and centre springs back when touched
9. Remove dish from larger pan to cool
10. Pudding has custard like consistency when it cools
11. Serve lukewarm or cut cold into squares<|endoftext|>
