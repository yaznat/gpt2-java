What's a recipe for rice?<|endoftext|>Amazing Mexican Rice

Ingredients:
• Vegetable oil
• Yellow onion
• Rice
• Garlic clove
• Chicken broth
• Salt and pepper
• Chipotle chiles
• Corn

Steps:
1. Over medium heat, saute onion in oil
2. Add rice and garlic
3. Saute until the rice looks clear
4. Add salt, pepper, and chicken broth and turn heat up to high
5. Stir well and bring to a boil
6. Mix in chipotle diced tomatoes and corn
7. Turn heat to low
8. Cook covered for 20 minutes<|endoftext|>
