Generate a recipe for tea.<|endoftext|>Absolutely The Best Southern Sweet Tea

Ingredients:
• Tea bags
• Water
• Granulated sugar

Steps:
1. Hold tea bags lined up evenly by the strings and make a loop knot to hold them together
2. Pull off paper and staple
3. Set aside
4. In a one gallon pitcher, add very hot water
5. Stir in the sugar till dissolved
6. Add tea bags and dunk into water a few times, then leave them in the pitcher
7. Cover with lid of pitcher and refrigerate
8. Wait until it is very cold
9. The flavor changes until it reaches the 'cold' state
10. Drink and enjoy<|endoftext|>
