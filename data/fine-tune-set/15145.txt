Ingredients:
• Shell noodles
• Butter
• Condensed cheddar cheese soup
• Evaporated milk
• Chicken bouillon cube
• Season-all salt
• Onion powder
• Black pepper
• Dry mustard
• Sharp cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Cook the pasta in a pot of boiling water until al dente and set aside
2. Melt butter in a medium sauce pan
3. Slowly stir in milk & cheese soup until well blended
4. Next add the bouillon, seasonings, & shredded cheese
5. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring, until mixture is smooth and bubbly
6. Do not allow to burn
7. Spray a 9x9 baking dish with cooking spray then add the pasta
8. Pour the cheese mixture over the pasta then stir
9. Bake in 375 oven for 20-25 minutes
10. Serve hot from baking dish<|endoftext|>
