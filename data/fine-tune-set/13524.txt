Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Orange Beef

Ingredients:
• Navel oranges
• Green onion
• Sugar
• Cornstarch
• Reduced sodium soy sauce
• Garlic cloves
• Fresh gingerroot
• Boneless beef top sirloin steaks
• Canola oil
• Cooked rice

Steps:
1. Finely grate the peel from 2 oranges
2. Set aside
3. Cut thin strips of orange peel from the remaining orange for garnish
4. Set aside
5. Squeeze juice from all oranges
6. Cut onions to separate the white and green parts
7. Thinly slice the white parts
8. Cut green parts into 1 inch lengths for garnish
9. For sauce, in a small bowl, combine sugar and cornstarch
10. Stir in orange juice until smooth
11. Stir in the soy sauce, garlic, ginger, grated orange peel and white parts of onions
12. Set aside
13. In a large nonstick skillet or wok coated with cooking spray, stir fry beef in oil until no longer pink
14. Stir the sauce and add to the pan
15. Bring to a boil
16. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened
17. Serve with rice
18. Garnish with orange peel and remaining onions
19. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
