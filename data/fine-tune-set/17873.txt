Ingredients:
• Almond joy candy bars
• Butter
• Sugar
• Vanilla
• Eggs
• All-purpose flour
• Salt
• Almonds

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 350f
2. Grease 9-inch square pan
3. In medium saucepan over low heat, melt candy bars and shortening, stirring occasionally until chocolate and shortening are melted
4. Add sugar and vanilla
5. Blend well
6. Add eggs 1 at a time, beating well after each addition
7. Stir in flour, salt and chopped almonds
8. Mix well
9. Spread in greased pan
10. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes or until top springs back when touched lightly in center
11. Cool completely
12. Cut into bars<|endoftext|>
