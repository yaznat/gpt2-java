What's a good sushi recipe?<|endoftext|>Sandwich Sushi

Ingredients:
• White bread
• Creamed corn
• Smoked ham
• Carrot
• Lebanese cucumber
• Canned salmon
• Mayonnaise
• Iceberg lettuce
• Avocado

Steps:
1. Place bread onto a flat surface
2. Use a rolling pin to flatten bread slightly
3. Spread corn over 4 slices, leaving a 2cm strip free of filling along 1 edge
4. Top with ham, carrot and cucumber
5. Roll up to enclose filling
6. Cut each sandwich into 3 rounds
7. Place salmon and mayonnaise into a bowl
8. Mix gently to combine
9. Arrange lettuce over remaining 4 slices of bread, leaving a 2cm strip free of filling along 1 edge
10. Top with salmon mixture and avocado
11. Roll up to enclose filling
12. Cut each sandwich into 3 rounds
13. Wrap all sandwiches in plastic wrap
14. Store in the fridge until ready to pack into lunch boxes<|endoftext|>
