Ingredients:
• Hot dog buns
• Butter
• Eggs
• Vanilla
• Granulated sugar
• Milk

Steps:
1. Place buns in lightly greased pan with sides, crust side up
2. Melt butter & pour over buns
3. Mix remaining ingredients & pour over all
4. Bake at 350 for 30 minutes<|endoftext|>
