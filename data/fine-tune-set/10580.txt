Give me a pasta recipe.<|endoftext|>Angel Hair Pasta With Basil Tomato Sauce And Chicken

Ingredients:
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Garlic cloves
• Shallot
• Roma tomatoes
• Salt and pepper
• Fresh basil
• Whole wheat angel hair pasta
• Water
• Parmesan cheese
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts

Steps:
1. Saut garlic and shallot in oil over medium heat until garlic golden and shallot wilted
2. Add tomatoes and season with salt, pepper, and basil
3. Chop cooked chicken into bite size pieces
4. Add pasta, water, and chicken to sauce, stir gently in to coat
5. Move to serving platter, sprinkle with parmesan cheese, and serve<|endoftext|>
