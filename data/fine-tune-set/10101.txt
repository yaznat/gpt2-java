Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Style Quinoa Salad With Chili Orange Dressing

Ingredients:
• Orange zest
• Orange juice
• Soy sauce
• Honey
• Sesame oil
• Chili sauce
• Black pepper
• Quinoa
• Water chestnuts
• Red bell pepper
• Snow peas
• Scallions

Steps:
1. Prepare the dressing by whisking together the first 7 ingredients
2. Rinse the quinoa very throughly to remove the bitter natural resin
3. Bring 2 cups of water to boil and add quinoa in a steady stream to prevent clumping
4. Return to the boil and and reduce heat to low
5. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes until it is tender
6. Look for the white line around the seed - it's the germ and when it bursts the grain is cooked
7. If water remains in the pot, remove the lid and boil and stir until the remaining liquid evaporates, being careful not to overcook
8. Remove from heat and let stand covered for 5 minutes
9. Fluff with a fork and let in cool
10. In a serving bowl combine the quinoa with the last 4 ingredients
11. Add dressing and toss until combined
12. Chill for 1 hour before serving<|endoftext|>
