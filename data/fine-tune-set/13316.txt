Ingredients:
• Dried chipotle powder
• Garlic powder
• Garlic cloves
• Onion powder
• Ground cumin
• Lemon pepper
• Chili powder
• Ground allspice
• Boneless skinless chicken breast half
• Olive oil

Steps:
1. Mix first 8 ingredients in a gallon-size zipper-top bag and seal the bag
2. Shake the bag to combine the spices thoroughly
3. Cut chicken into 1 / 2 inch wide strips and place in the bag
4. Shake the bag to coat the chicken completely
5. Refrigerate for a minimum of 1hr
6. Heat the skillet over medium heat
7. Add the chicken pieces and cook until lightly browned on the first side, 4 to 5 minutes
8. Turn the chicken and cook until no longer pink in the center, another 4 to 5 minutes
9. Serve over rice or salad<|endoftext|>
