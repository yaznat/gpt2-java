Ingredients:
• Long-grain white rice
• Eggs
• Salt
• Salad oil
• Onion
• Garlic
• Green bell pepper
• Mushroom
• Cooked ham
• Dry roasted salted peanut
• Soy sauce

Steps:
1. Rub cooked rice with wet hands so all grains are separated
2. Set aside
3. In a small bowl, lightly beat together eggs and salt
4. Place a wok over medium heat
5. When wok is hot, add 1 tablespoon of the oil, when oil is hot, add eggs and cook, stirring occasionally, until soft curds form
6. Remove from wok and set aside
7. Increase heat to medium-high and add 1 tablespoon more oil to wok
8. When oil is hot, add onion and garlic, stir-fry until onion is soft, then add bell pepper, mushrooms, ham and peanuts
9. Stir-fry until heated through, about 2 minutes
10. Remove from wok and set aside
11. Pour remaining 2 tablespoons oil into wok, when hot, add rice and stir-fry until heated through, about 2 minutes
12. Stir in ham mixture and soy, add eggs
13. Stir mixture gently until eggs are in small pieces<|endoftext|>
