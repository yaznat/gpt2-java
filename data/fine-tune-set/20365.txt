Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken thighs
• Cauliflower
• Condensed cheddar cheese soup
• Chicken broth
• Salt
• Onion powder
• Black pepper
• Hot sauce
• Heavy cream
• Shredded cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. Cut chicken into chunks
2. Coat crock pot with cooking spray
3. Add all ingredients except cream and shredded cheese
