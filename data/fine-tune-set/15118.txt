Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Garlic clove
• Boboli whole wheat pizza crust
• Baby arugula
• Goat cheese
• Walnuts
• Balsamic vinegar

Steps:
1. Pre heat oven to 450f
2. Mix olive oil and garlic in a small bowl and let sit for 5 - 10 minutes for flavors to blend
3. Brush pizza crust with garlic oil, spread arugula over top fallowed by the goat cheese and walnuts
4. Bake 10-14 minutes
5. Sprinkle baked pizza with 1 to 1 1 / 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
6. Slice and enjoy<|endoftext|>
