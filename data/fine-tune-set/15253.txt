What's a recipe for pizza?<|endoftext|>Breakfast Pizzas

Ingredients:
• Bacon
• Sliced mushrooms
• Eggs
• Refrigerated crescent dinner rolls
• Cream cheese
• Basil pesto
• Mozzarella cheese
• Italian plum tomatoes
• Green onions
• Cooking spray

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 375f
2. Spray large cookie sheet with cooking spray
3. In 10 in skillet, cook bacon until crisp
4. Drain on paper towels
5. Crumble bacon and set aside
6. Reserve 1 tablespoon bacon drippings in skillet
7. Add mushrooms to drippings and saute for about 5 minutes until lightly brown
8. Drain on paper towels and set aside
9. Scramble eggs in butter or 1 tablespoon bacon drippings
10. Remove from pan and set aside
11. Unroll dough and separate into 4 rectangles
12. Press each into 8x4- inch rectangle, firmly pressing preforations to seal
13. Cut each in half crosswide, making 8 squares
14. Place 1 / 2 inch apart on cookie sheet
15. Mix cream cheese and pesto and spread 2 teaspoons on each square
16. Top evenly with cooked mushrooms and scrambled eggs
17. Sprinkle 1 / 2 cup of the mozzarella cheese on top
18. Arrange tomato slices over cheese
19. Sprinkle with onions, crumbled bacon and remaining 1 / 2 cup cheese
20. Bake 13 to 15 minutes or until crust is golden brown
21. Serve warm<|endoftext|>
