What's a recipe for a casserole?<|endoftext|>Amy S Hash Browns Casserole

Ingredients:
• Onion
• Butter
• Salt
• Pepper
• Cream of mushroom soup
• Sour cream
• Velveeta cheese
• Frozen hash browns
• Corn flakes

Steps:
1. Saute onion in 1 / 2 cup butter
2. Combine onion / butter mixture in a bowl with salt, pepper, cream of mushroom soup, sour cream, velveeta cheese, and hash browns
3. Mix until combined
4. Pour mixture into a greased 9x13 pan
5. Cover with foil and bake at 350 degrees fahrenheit for 45 minutes to1 hour
6. Remove foil and stir well
7. Melt the 1 / 4 cup butter and combine with the cornflakes
8. Toss well, until coated
9. Spread on top of potato mixture
