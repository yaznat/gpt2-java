Can you give me a beef recipe?<|endoftext|>Awesome N Easy Beef Stroganoff

Ingredients:
• Boneless steak
• Onion soup mix
• Beef bouillon cubes
• Flour
• Olive oil
• Water
• Red wine
• Sour cream
• Sliced mushrooms
• Fettuccine pasta

Steps:
1. Cut steak into bite size pieces
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in heavy skillet
3. Add beef and brown
4. Sprinkle with flour and cook an additional minute
5. Add water, wine, soup mix and bouillon and stir
6. Transfer to casserole dish with lid and bake at 325f for 3 hours
7. Ensure it does not go dry by adding wine or water
8. Fry mushrooms in pan with 1 tbsp olive oil
9. Add mushrooms to mixture and return to oven for an hour
10. Cook noodles as directed
11. Remove mixture from oven and stir in sour cream
12. Serve over noodles<|endoftext|>
