What's a recipe for muffins?<|endoftext|>Autumn Pumpkin Spice Muffins

Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour
• Sugar
• Brown sugar
• Baking powder
• Salt
• Cinnamon
• Ginger
• Baking soda
• Canned pumpkin
• Butter
• Buttermilk
• Eggs

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 400f
2. Grease 12 muffin tins or lined cups
3. In a large bowl: combine all ingredients except for pumpkin, butter, buttermilk, and eggs
4. In seperate bowl, combine the rest of ingredients
5. Mix into the flour mxture until moistened
6. Spoon the batter into the cups
7. Bake about 20 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean
8. Let cool before removing from the tins<|endoftext|>
