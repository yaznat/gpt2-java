Give me a pizza recipe.<|endoftext|>Basic Cheese Pizza

Ingredients:
• Dry yeast
• Water
• Sugar
• Bread flour
• Salt
• Olive oil
• Tomato puree
• Tomato paste
• Garlic clove
• Fresh basil
• Fresh mozzarella cheese

Steps:
1. Dough:
2. In a glass or plastic bowl, combine yeast, water, and sugar
3. Stir to dissolve the yeast and let the yeast "bloom" for 15 minutes
4. Stir in 1 cup flour, add salt, and then stir in another cup of flour
5. Dump mixture onto kneading board and work in last cup of flour, kneading until dough is soft and elastic, but not sticky
6. Form dough into a ball
7. In another bowl, pour in the 1 tbsp olive oil and spread around
8. Coat ball of dough with oil and cover bowl with a damp towel and let dough rise for 40 minutes
9. Punch down dough and knead on board about 2 minutes
10. Dough is now ready to spread in the pan
11. To avoid sticking of crust, lightly spray pizza pan with olive oil or vegetable oil spray and then work dough to pan - this dough is enough for 1 14-in pizza with a thin bottom crust and enough dough around the edge to munch
12. Sauce:
13. Combine pureed tomatoes, tomato paste, minced garlic, and basil
14. Spread onto prepared pizza dough
15. Top with sliced mozzarella cheese and bake at 500f for 11-13 minutes<|endoftext|>
