What's a recipe for chicken?<|endoftext|>5 Ingredient Sunday Chicken

Ingredients:
• Boneless skinless chicken breasts
• Bacon
• Ham
• Cream of celery soup
• Evaporated milk
• Garlic powder
• White pepper

Steps:
1. Cut each chicken breast into 1 / 3's
2. Place in bottom of non-stick sprayed crock pot
3. Top with bacon that has been cut into small slices, , and thin slices of chopped sliced ham
4. Sprinkle garlic and white pepper
5. In seperate bowl combine soup and evaporated milk
6. Pour over the chicken and cover
7. Cook on high for 4 hours then low one hour stirring once when you switch it to low
8. I offered a choice of rice or mashed potatoes to serve this over
9. My family likes both<|endoftext|>
