Can you give me a bread recipe?<|endoftext|>Almond Glazed Poppy Seed Bread

Ingredients:
• Flour
• Salt
• Baking powder
• Eggs
• Milk
• Butter
• Oil
• Sugar
• Poppy seeds
• Almond extract
• Vanilla
• Orange juice
• Powdered sugar

Steps:
1. Combine bread ingredients and beat 2 minutes
2. Pour into 2 large, greased loaf pans
3. Bake at 350f degrees for 1 hour
4. Cool 10 minutes and remove from pans
5. Mix glaze ingredients together and spoon on top of bread<|endoftext|>
