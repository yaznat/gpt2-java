Please give me a recipe for an omelette.<|endoftext|>Spanish Omelette Tortilla De Patatas Spain

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Baking potato
• Yellow onion
• Garlic cloves
• Eggs
• Salt
• Lemon

Steps:
1. Heat the olive oil in a 9-inch skillet and add the potato slices carefully, because the salt will make the oil splatter
2. Try to keep the potato slices separated so they will not stick together
3. Cook, turning occasionally, over medium heat for 5 minutes
4. Add the onions and garlic and cook until the potatoes are tender
5. Drain into a colander, leaving about 3 tablespoons of oil in the skillet
6. Meanwhile, in a large bowl, whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt
7. Add the potatoes, and stir to coat with the egg
8. Add the egg-coated potatoes to the very hot oil in the skillet, spreading them evenly to completely cover the base of the skillet
9. Lower the heat to medium and continue to cook, shaking the pan frequently, until mixture is half set
10. Use a plate to cover the skillet and invert the omelette away from the hand holding the plate
11. Add 1 tablespoon oil to the pan and slide the omelette back into the skillet on its uncooked side
12. Cook until completely set
13. Allow the omelette to cool, and then cut it into wedges
14. Season it with salt and sprinkle with lemon juice to taste if using
15. Serve warm or at room temperature<|endoftext|>
