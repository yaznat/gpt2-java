Ingredients:
• Cooking spray
• Onion
• Red bell pepper
• Garlic
• Extra firm tofu
• Soy sauce
• Black pepper
• Baby spinach
• Tomatoes
• Pita bread

Steps:
1. Spray a large skillet with cooking spray then heat until hot
2. Add the onion, peppers, & garlic and cook over medium heat for 2 minutes
3. Add the tofu, soy sauce, & pepper to the vegetables, stir
4. Continue to cook another 3-4 minutes or until the tofu is heated through
5. Remove from the heat & cool slightly
6. Mix the tomatoes & spinach in with the tofu & vegetables then stuff each pita half with the mixture
7. Serve immediately<|endoftext|>
