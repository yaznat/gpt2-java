Ingredients:
• Udon noodles
• Canola oil
• Panko breadcrumbs
• Firm tofu
• Egg yolk
• Mixed mushrooms
• Fresh ginger
• Garlic clove
• Baby bok choy
• Oyster sauce
• Hoisin sauce

Steps:
1. Bring a saucepan of water to a boil
2. Add the udon and cook until tender, 5 minutes
3. Drain
4. Toss with 1 teaspoon of the canola oil
5. Meanwhile, put the panko in a large, resealable plastic bag and crush into fine crumbs
6. In a shallow bowl, gently toss the tofu with the egg yolk
7. Transfer the tofu to the bag and coat with the panko
8. Heat the remaining 1 / 2 cup of oil in a wok until just smoking
9. Add the tofu and stir-fry over high heat until crisp, 2 to 3 minutes
10. Using a slotted spoon, transfer to a paper towel-lined plate
11. Pour off all but 1 / 4 cup of the oil and return the wok to high heat
12. Add the mushrooms and stir-fry until lightly browned, about 3 minutes
13. Add the ginger, garlic and bok choy and stir-fry for 5 minutes
14. Add the udon and oyster and hoisin sauces and stir-fry for 2 minutes
15. Add the tofu and toss
16. Transfer to a bowl and serve<|endoftext|>
