What's a good fried rice recipe?<|endoftext|>Ground Beef Fried Rice

Ingredients:
• Extra lean ground beef
• Green onions
• Celery ribs
• Bean sprouts
• Fresh mushrooms
• Red bell pepper
• Gingerroot
• Cooked rice
• Soy sauce
• Salt & freshly ground black pepper

Steps:
1. Brown ground beef in large skillet
2. Drain
3. Stir in onions, celery, bean sprouts, mushrooms, red bell pepper and gingerroot
4. Cook over medium-high heat 5 minutes or until vegetables are crisp-tender, stirring frequently
5. Stir in rice and soy sauce
6. Season with salt and black pepper to taste
7. Heat thoroughly, stirring occasionally<|endoftext|>
