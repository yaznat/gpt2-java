Give me a stew recipe.<|endoftext|>Beef Beer Stew

Ingredients:
• Stew meat
• Condensed golden mushroom soup
• Beer
• Celery ribs
• Carrots
• Potatoes
• Onions
• Fresh mushrooms
• Flour
• Canola oil
• Salt & pepper

Steps:
1. Flour and season stew meat
2. Add oil to large pot and brown stew meat
3. Stir in soup and beer
4. Add cut up veggies
5. Cover and simmer on low for 3 hours
6. Hint: we desire more gravy so i double the soup and beer
7. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
