Ingredients:
• Goat cheese
• Cottage cheese
• Mozzarella cheese
• Fresh ground black pepper
• Flour tortillas
• Onions
• Tomatoes
• Fresh spinach
• Cilantro
• Chives
• Jalapeno peppers
• Garlic cloves
• Parsley

Steps:
1. Preheat your oven to 425f
2. In a blender, puree the goat cheese, cottage cheese, half of the mozzarella cheese, and pepper to taste
3. Spread half of the cheese mixture on each tortilla to 1 / 2" inside the edge
4. Sprinkle half of each remaining ingredient over each tortilla
5. Place one tortilla on a cookie sheet
6. Place the other on top of it
7. Sprinkle the top with the remaining mozzarella
8. Bake 8-10 minutes, or until cheese begins to brown
9. Slice into four wedges and serve warm
10. Enjoy!<|endoftext|>
