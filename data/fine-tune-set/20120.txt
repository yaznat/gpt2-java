What's a good stir fry recipe?<|endoftext|>Asian Beef Stir Fry With Snow Peas And Rice

Ingredients:
• Cornstarch
• Soy sauce
• Water
• Red wine
• Sugar
• Flank steaks
• Oyster sauce
• Sesame oil
• Sesame paste
• Garlic
• Chicken broth
• White rice
• Ginger powder
• Beef
• Crushed red pepper flakes
• Green onions
• Frozen snow peas

Steps:
1. The night before: combine the following in a medium size bowl in this order: cornstarch, stir in soy sauce, gradually add water, red wine, and sugar
2. Slice beef against the grain in strips approximately 2 long
3. Add to marinade bowl as you cut
4. Toss with a fork until well coated
5. Cover and leave in fridge overnight
6. Combine the following in another small bowl or container: cornstarch
7. Blend in oyster sauce, sesame oil, sugar, water, ginger-sesame sauce, garlic, and chicken broth
8. Cover and leave in fridge overnight
9. When you get home for dinner: combine rice and water in a large microwave safe pot or casserole dish
10. Cover and microwave at high for 10 minutes, then medium for 10 minutes
11. Heat oil in a large nonstick fry pan or wok at med-high
12. Add ginger, drained marinated beef, and chili flakes
13. Slice green onions and add to pan as you cut
14. Toss until meat browns
15. Add stir fry sauce and snow peas
16. Toss until sauce thickens slightly and snow peas are tender but crisp<|endoftext|>
