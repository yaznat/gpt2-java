Can you give me a chili recipe?<|endoftext|>Black Bean And Chocolate Chili

Ingredients:
• Olive oil
• Onions
• Garlic cloves
• Celery
• Jalapeno chile
• Cumin
• Oregano
• Cinnamon
• Ground cloves
• Ground coriander
• Chili powder
• Ground black pepper
• Salt
• Bell peppers
• Black beans
• Diced tomatoes
• Fresh lime juice
• Soy sauce
• Semisweet chocolate
• Vegan cheddar cheese

Steps:
1. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat
2. Add onions and garlic and saut until onions become soft and translucent, about 10 minutes
3. Add the celery and chile, cover and cook for another five minutes more
4. Reduce the heat and stir in spices, along with the bell peppers
5. Cover again and cook for another five minutes
6. Stir often to keep the spices from burning
7. If the pan gets too dry add a little liquid from the diced tomatoes, or some water
8. Next, add the black beans, tomatoes, lime juice and soy sauce
9. Let the chili simmer on low for 5 to 10 minutes, until it thickens slightly and flavors combine
10. Stir in the chocolate
11. When chocolate has melted, taste to adjust the salt and pepper
12. Serve with topping of your choice
13. I like a bit of vegan cheese, sour "cream" and scallions
14. Chili can also makes a great filling to serve wrapped in flour tortillas, or in taco shells if you prefer<|endoftext|>
