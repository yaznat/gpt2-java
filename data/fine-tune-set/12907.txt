Give me a steak recipe.<|endoftext|>Blackened Steak Salad

Ingredients:
• Paprika
• Ground black pepper
• Salt
• Garlic powder
• Cayenne pepper
• Dried oregano
• Dried thyme
• Olive oil
• Balsamic vinegar
• Dijon mustard
• Mixed baby greens
• Bell pepper
• Red onion
• Tomatoes
• Extra virgin olive oil
• Minced garlic cloves
• Beef steaks
• Butter
• Sliced mushrooms
• Blue cheese

Steps:
1. For spice mixture:
2. Mix all ingredients in small bowl
3. For salad:
4. Whisk oil, vinegar and mustard in large bowl to blend
5. Season with salt and pepper
6. Add greens, bell pepper and onion and toss to coat
7. Divide salad between plates
8. For steaks:
9. Brush the steaks with evoo infused with chopped garlic all over before dipping in the spice mix
10. Spread spice mixture on plate
11. Coat both sides of steaks with spice mixture
12. Dip both sides of steaks into melted butter
13. Heat heavy large skillet over high heat until very hot
14. Add steaks and sliced mushrooms, cook until desired doneness, about 2 minutes per side for medium-rare
15. The shrooms may need more time, use your judgement
16. Transfer meat to cutting board
17. Let stand 2 minutes
18. Thinly slice steaks crosswise
19. Arrange slices atop salads
20. Sprinkle with cheese
21. Garnish with tomato and avocado and serve<|endoftext|>
